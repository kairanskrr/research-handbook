# Wohlin 2021：Case Study Research in Software Engineering — It is a Case, and it is a Study, but is it a Case Study?

- 资料：`wohlin2021case`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infsof.2021.106514)（开放获取，CC BY 4.0）
- 版本：Information and Software Technology 133:106514，2021。读的是出版社版本，取自布莱金厄理工学院机构库 [DiVA](https://bth.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1527888/FULLTEXT01.pdf)。
- 说明：作者检查了 7 个软件工程期刊上最近 100 篇自称"案例研究"的论文，判断它们是否符合案例研究的定义。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Close to 50% of the articles investigated were judged as not being case studies according to the definition of a case study."
- 要点：被检查的论文中，接近一半按定义不算案例研究。

### E2

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："Studies of only historical information, for example data or software, in any form does not meet the definitions of a case study."
- 要点：作者认为，只研究历史信息（如已有的数据或软件）的研究不符合案例研究的定义。

### E3

- 位置：§4 Results
- 原文："Typical examples claiming to be case studies, but not meeting the definitions are: (1) Evaluations or illustrations on a limited scale, for example, in a laboratory. These could be labelled evaluations or illustrations given their primary objective, and (2) Studies of existing information in open source repositories, defect databases of other database sources. These ought to be labelled as archival analysis."
- 要点：自称案例研究但不符合定义的典型情况：一是小规模的评估或演示（例如在实验室里），应叫"评估"或"演示"；二是对开源仓库、缺陷库等已有信息的研究，应叫"档案分析"。

### E4

- 位置：§4 Results
- 原文："Finally, if the investigator participates in the case studied, then it ought to be labelled as action research"
- 要点：研究者参与到所研究的案例中时，应当叫行动研究。

### E5

- 位置：§6 Conclusions
- 原文："A case study is an empirical investigation of a case, using multiple data collection methods, to study a contemporary phenomenon in its real-life context, and with the investigator(s) not taking an active role in the case investigated."
- 要点：作者建议的定义：案例研究是对一个案例的实证考察，使用多种数据收集方法，在真实环境中研究一个当下的现象，研究者不在案例中扮演主动角色。

### E6

- 位置：§6 Conclusions
- 原文："We have also emphasised multiple data collection methods to clarify that triangulation is essential. [...] To interview more than one person does not make it a case study."
- 要点：作者强调多种数据收集方法，因为三角验证是必需的；访谈了不止一个人，并不因此就成了案例研究。

### E7

- 位置：§6 Conclusions
- 原文："In particular, researchers should provide a reference to the definition used."
- 要点：研究者应当注明自己采用的是哪个定义。
