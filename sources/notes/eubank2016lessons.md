# Eubank 2016：Lessons from a Decade of Replications at the Quarterly Journal of Political Science

- 资料：`eubank2016lessons`，[原文 PDF（杜克大学 DukeSpace 机构库）](https://dukespace.lib.duke.edu/items/319e52d7-0932-4d21-89bb-85df233fbcbd)
- 版本：PS: Political Science & Politics 49(2): 273–276，2016；机构库提供出版版 PDF。作者 2012 年 9 月至 2015 年 11 月任该刊复现助理。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：引言
- 原文："Since its inception in 2005, the Quarterly Journal of Political Science (QJPS) has sought to encourage this type of transparency by requiring that all submissions be accompanied by a replication package consisting of data and code for generating results."
- 要点：《政治学季刊》（QJPS）自 2005 年创刊起，就要求所有投稿附上能生成结果的数据和代码。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："It finds that of the 24 empirical papers subjected to in-house replication review since September 2012, only four packages did not require any modifications. Most troubling, 14 packages (58%) had results in the paper that differed from those generated by the author's own code."
- 要点：2012 年 9 月以来接受内部复现审查的 24 篇实证论文中，只有 4 份复现包不需要任何修改；14 份（58%）论文中的结果和作者自己的代码跑出来的不一样。

### E3

- 位置：In-House Replication
- 原文："Of the remaining 20 papers, 13 had code that would not execute without errors, eight failed to include code for results that appeared in the paper"
- 要点：其余 20 篇中，13 篇代码运行出错，8 篇缺少论文中某些结果的代码。

### E4

- 位置：In-House Replication
- 原文："This process--although it varies by paper--takes approximately six hours of labor and costs roughly $180 per paper"
- 要点：这种内部审查每篇约需 6 小时、约 180 美元。

### E5

- 位置：Advice to Authors
- 原文："Test files on a different computer, preferably with a different operating system."
- 要点：建议在另一台电脑（最好是不同操作系统）上测试复现文件。

### E6

- 位置：Advice to Authors
- 原文："The majority of replication problems emerge because authors either modified their code but failed to update their manuscript or made an error while transcribing their results into their paper."
- 要点：多数复现问题来自改了代码却没更新论文，或把结果抄进论文时抄错。

### E7

- 位置：Randomizations and Simulations
- 原文："Set a random-number generator seed in their code so that it consistently generates the exact results in the paper."
- 要点：用到随机性的分析，要在代码中设定随机数种子，保证每次跑出论文中的确切结果。
