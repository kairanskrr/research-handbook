# 国际传播学会：Open Scholarship 页面

- 资料：`icaopenscholarship`，[原文网页](https://www.icahdq.org/page/OpenScholarship)
- 版本：国际传播学会（ICA）官网"开放学术"页面。官网对脚本返回人机验证页，读的是 Internet Archive 2025-09-18 的快照（web.archive.org/web/20250918203718/...）。位置写页面小标题。

### E1

- 位置：页首（Claes de Vreese 署名的介绍）
- 原文："ICA has adopted the term Open Scholarship in recognition of the epistemological and methodological diveristy in our field."
- 要点：ICA 用"开放学术"这个说法，是承认本领域在认识论和方法上的多样性。

### E2

- 位置：ICA Perspectives on Open Scholarship
- 原文："Conceptually, communication scholars discussed, for example, whether open science standards of reproducibility and replicability may be at odds with certain types of qualitative scholarship; procedurally, some problematized the additional labor associated with manuscript preparation and review; and from an ethical point of view, some suggested open science practices to be colonial in that they privilege academic voices over the subaltern voices of researched subjects."
- 要点：2020 年 ICA 年会上的争论：可复现、可重复的标准是否和某些定性研究冲突；准备稿件和审稿的额外工作量；以及开放科学是否偏向学者的声音、压过被研究者的声音。

### E3

- 位置：ICA Perspectives on Open Scholarship
- 原文："Proponents of open science practices suggest that transparency will help ensure robustness in communication scholarship, and opponents argue that these practices privilege some forms of scholarship while diminishing others."
- 要点：支持者认为透明能保证研究稳健；反对者认为这些做法抬高一些研究形式、贬低另一些。

### E4

- 位置：Open Science Badges at ICA
- 原文："ICA supports Open Science Badges to acknowledge open science practices. These voluntary badges are offered for those who would like to share data and materials and preregister research, and signal to the reader that the content has been made available in a persistent location."
- 要点：ICA 支持自愿申请的开放科学徽章，表彰共享数据、材料和做预注册的研究。

### E5

- 位置：Availability of Data and Materials Statement
- 原文："Where ethically feasible, ICA encourages authors to make all data on which the conclusions of the paper rely available to readers. Authors are required to include a Data Availability Statement in their article."
- 要点：在伦理允许时，ICA 鼓励公开论文结论所依据的全部数据；ICA 期刊要求文章包含数据可得性声明。

### E6

- 位置：Open Science Badges at ICA
- 原文："During the paper submission process, authors can also mark their desired badges. These badges will appear in the conference print program."
- 要点：年会投稿时也可以申请徽章，徽章会印在会议手册上。
