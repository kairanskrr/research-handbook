# Crater 和 Lievense 2018：Scale-up of industrial microbial processes

- 资料：`crater2018scaleup`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1093/femsle/fny138)，PMC5995164
- 版本：FEMS Microbiology Letters 365(13):fny138，2018 年。开放获取（CC BY-NC 4.0）。两位作者来自美国生物技术公司 Genomatica，文章基于他们在工业项目中的经验。通过 Europe PMC 全文接口读取。位置写章节。

### E1

- 位置：What is scale-up and why does it matter?
- 原文："A process developed in a laboratory (e.g. 0.5–10 L fermentors) must be translated into a full manufacturing scale process (e.g. 20 000–2000 000 L fermentors), with scale factors ranging anywhere from thousands to millions."
- 要点：实验室工艺（例如 0.5～10 升发酵罐）要转到生产规模（例如 2 万～200 万升），放大倍数从几千到几百万。

### E2

- 位置：What is scale-up and why does it matter?
- 原文："The time required to transition from lab-scale to manufacturing is typically 3–10 years."
- 要点：从实验室规模到生产，通常要 3～10 年。

### E3

- 位置：Guiding principles, Begin with the end in mind
- 原文："One cannot simply enlarge lab-scale equipment and duplicate lab-scale conditions at large-scale"
- 要点：不能简单地把实验室设备放大、在大规模上照搬实验室条件。

### E4

- 位置：Guiding principles, Begin with the end in mind
- 原文："Instead, beginning with the end in mind, a skilled project team that really understands large-scale processes prepares a detailed conceptual design of the envisioned manufacturing process and plant before the first lab experiments are done."
- 要点：作者主张在做第一个实验室实验之前，就由懂大规模工艺的团队做出设想中的生产工艺和工厂的详细概念设计。

### E5

- 位置：Key challenges and improvement opportunities
- 原文："A common fundamental error is to select the microbial host strain and develop it without regard for its suitability for large-scale production."
- 要点：一个常见的根本错误，是选择和改造微生物宿主菌株时不考虑它是否适合大规模生产。

### E6

- 位置：A road map for successful scale-up
- 原文："Lab-scale process development must be conducted under conditions that mimic, as close as possible, the intended large-scale manufacturing process."
- 要点：实验室规模的工艺开发，要在尽量模拟大规模生产的条件下进行。

### E7

- 位置：Key considerations for fermentation scale-up
- 原文："There are many parameters that impact performance, and most of these are subject to change during scale-up."
- 要点：影响发酵表现的参数很多，其中多数在放大过程中会变。

### E8

- 位置：Table 1
- 原文："Validate all industrial raw materials in lab/pilot studies ahead of procurement"
- 要点：采购之前，先在实验室或中试中验证所有工业级原料。

### E9

- 位置：A road map for successful scale-up
- 原文："Piloting, usually at 1% to 10% of full manufacturing scale, provides unique benefits and further risk reduction"
- 要点：中试通常在生产规模的 1%～10% 进行，能进一步降低风险。

### E10

- 位置：Guiding principles for process scale-up
- 原文："Based on our experiences, here are three guiding principles that are critical to the successful scale-up of industrial microbial processes."
- 要点：三条原则来自作者自己的经验。
