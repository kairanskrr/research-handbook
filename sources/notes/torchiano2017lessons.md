# Torchiano 等人 2017：Lessons Learnt in Conducting Survey Research

- 资料：`torchiano2017lessons`，[arXiv:1702.05744](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.05744)
- 版本：arXiv v3（2017-02-24），发表于 CESI@ICSE 2017

### E1

- 位置：§II Survey Research: Basic Terms and Concepts
- 原文："surveys do not entail an active intervention component – differently than, e.g., an experiment where one or more factors are applied and controlled."
- 要点：问卷调查是观察性的，不主动施加干预；这与施加并控制因素的实验不同。

### E2

- 位置：§III-A-1，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："Too often, they use survey research to focus on aspects that can be quantiﬁed by numbers. Such a quantiﬁcation then can lead to the illusion of treating the data as if it was revealed via experimentations, seeing causal dependencies where there might be none and over-interpreting the ﬁndings."
- 要点：新手常只关注能量化的方面，于是产生错觉，把问卷数据当成实验数据，看到并不存在的因果关系，过度解读结果。

### E3

- 位置：§III-A-1，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："That is, instead of using surveys to ask normative questions ("What should we do?"), they should ask descriptive questions ("What is happening in your environment?"), and especially explanatory questions ("Why is this happening?")."
- 要点：问卷不要问规范性问题（"我们该怎么做"），而要问描述性问题（"你那里在发生什么"），尤其是解释性问题（"为什么会这样"）。

### E4

- 位置：§III-A-2，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："The question "Who can best provide you with the information you need?" should drive the selection rather than "Who are the subjects most likely available to participate?". It is easier to handle a small response rate than useless responses."
- 要点：选目标总体时，要问"谁最能提供我需要的信息"，而不是"谁最可能有空参加"；回应率低比回答没用更好处理。

### E5

- 位置：§III-B-1 Identifying the Unit of Analysis
- 原文："Surveying a random sample composed of developers is different from surveying developers from a random sample of software houses." [...] "Having the wrong units of analysis may render the survey data useless for the addressed questions."
- 要点：随机抽开发者与从随机抽到的公司中调查开发者是两回事；分析单元选错，数据可能对研究问题毫无用处。

### E6

- 位置：§III-C Sampling
- 原文："Suitable sampling frames are rarely available in SE research."
- 要点：软件工程研究很少有合适的抽样框。

### E7

- 位置：§III-C Sampling
- 原文："not present any bias on including on its database preferentially only subsets of the target population. Unequal criteria for including search units mean unequal sampling opportunities,"
- 要点：好的名单来源不应偏向收录目标总体的某些子群；收录标准不一，各人被抽到的机会就不同。

### E8

- 位置：§III-C Sampling
- 原文："it is important to establish signiﬁcantly higher sample sizes, considering the expectation of a very low participation rate (typically less than 10%)."
- 要点：面向业界人士的自愿问卷，参与率通常低于 10%，计划的发放量要大得多。

### E9

- 位置：§III-D-1 Simplicity，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："As much as possible, prefer to design short questions regarding a single concept and avoid double-barrelled questions, which can confound the answers." [...] "Passive voices and double negative questions should be avoided"
- 要点：题目尽量短、一题只问一个概念，避免"一题两问"；避免被动语态和双重否定。

### E10

- 位置：§III-D-1 Simplicity，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："two distinct constructs are merged in a single question is: "In your opinion, do you agree or disagree that code refactoring is a need as well as code smell detection?""
- 要点：反例：把重构和代码异味检测两个概念塞进一道题。

### E11

- 位置：§III-D-2 Clarity，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："An example of a biased item is: "Do you prefer working in projects following agile methods or those following usual non-agile approaches?". Avoiding bias can be achieved by only phrasing questions that do not suggest likely answers or responses."
- 要点：带倾向的题目会暗示答案（例子把非敏捷说成"通常的"做法）；只用不暗示答案的措辞。

### E12

- 位置：§III-D-2 Clarity，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："Such sensitive questions can include in which company the respondents work, what respondents’ income are, or opinions about their organisation or their management." [...] "if useful to answer the particular research questions, the relation should be made clear to the respondents."
- 要点：敏感问题（所在公司、收入、对单位和管理层的看法等）可能让人退出；确实需要时，要向回答者说明它和研究问题的关系。

### E13

- 位置：§III-D-3 Effortless，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："the questions should deal with recent events rather than events that lie far in the past"
- 要点：题目应问近期的事，不问很久以前的事。

### E14

- 位置：§III-D-4 Responsiveness，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："Whenever an ordinal scale is used, it is important to label exactly all the options." [...] "We also learnt that it is useful to use Likert scales when a construct can be represented as a general agreement with a statement. These come, however, as a measure with limited precision."
- 要点：用定序量表时，每个选项都要标上明确的文字；李克特量表适合测"是否同意某个说法"，但精度有限。

### E15

- 位置：§III-D-4 Responsiveness，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："A better question would be "What is your experience with X?" with an ordinal response set ranging from "none" to "very high". In our experience, the best option is to use ranges as shown in Table I where clear reference points are deﬁned."
- 要点：问经验时，比"我对 X 很有经验（同意/不同意）"更好的是"你的 X 经验如何"；最好用有明确参照点的区间（如"1 到 3 年"）。

### E16

- 位置：§III-E-1 Controlling participation，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："it is impossible to control who will read the invitation message, thus, hindering the control of the recruited sample." [...] "In such a case, it was not possible to compute any response rates and an extra effort was required to compare the features of the responding population to that of the target population."
- 要点：通过邮件列表、论坛等广撒网，无法控制谁看到邀请，也算不出回应率，还要额外比较回答者和目标总体的特征。

### E17

- 位置：§III-E-1 Controlling participation，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："One way to deal with this problem is via technical mechanisms as most web-based questionnaire tools allow the deﬁnition of unique tokens to the population."
- 要点：可以用问卷工具给每个受邀者发唯一的访问码，防止邀请被随意转发。

### E18

- 位置：§III-E-2 Stimulating participation，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："However, if a tool is already mentioned in the invitation, then people not using it tend to self-exclude themselves: it brings to an under-estimation of the adoption rate."
- 要点：邀请里提到某个工具，不用它的人往往自行不参加，使测得的采用率失真（原文说是低估）。

### E19

- 位置：§III-E-2 Stimulating participation，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："it is possible to offer rewards (e.g. rafﬂes, payments, sharing results); this makes the less motivated participants more likely to be involved, thus avoiding a possible selfexclusion bias."
- 要点：抽奖、报酬、分享结果等奖励能让积极性较低的人也参与，避免自我排除偏差。

### E20

- 位置：§III-E-2 Stimulating participation，Lessons Learnt
- 原文："while a ﬁrst reminder (typically between 4 and 8 days) may signiﬁcantly increase the response rate, a third reminder can improve the response rate by a small amount only. Avoid reminding those who already have participated"
- 要点：第一次提醒（通常在 4 到 8 天后）能明显提高回应率，第三次提醒作用很小；不要提醒已经填过的人。
