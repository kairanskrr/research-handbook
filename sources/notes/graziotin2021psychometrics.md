# Graziotin 等人 2021：Psychometrics in Behavioral Software Engineering: A Methodological Introduction with Guidelines

- 资料：`graziotin2021psychometrics`，[arXiv:2005.09959](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.09959)
- 版本：arXiv v4（2021-06-08）；正式版发表于 ACM TOSEM 31(1)，2021

### E1

- 位置：§1.1 Misconceptions with psychological tests in software engineering research
- 原文："when a validated test from psychology is adopted by software engineering researchers, its items are often modified, causing the destruction of its psychometric reliability and validity properties."
- 要点：软件工程研究者采用心理学中验证过的量表时，常常改动题目，从而破坏了它的信度和效度。

### E2

- 位置：§1.1 Misconceptions with psychological tests in software engineering research
- 原文："we used a single Likert item scale to represent productivity." [...] "the productivity scale itself was not validated, making the results less valuable from a psychometric perspective"
- 要点：作者自我检讨：早年研究用一道李克特题测生产率，这个量表没有验证过，使结果的价值打了折扣。

### E3

- 位置：§6.0.1 Item facility
- 原文："Item facility for Likert items can be calculated with the mean value of the item."
- 要点：在心理测量的题目分析中，李克特题的"题目难易度"用该题的平均值计算。

### E4

- 位置：§13 Limitations of Psychometrics
- 原文："It might truly take years to developing and validating a measurement instrument. We highly recommend searching the literature for existing, psychometrically validated tests before developing one."
- 要点：开发并验证一个测量工具可能要好几年；自己开发之前，先在文献里找现成的、验证过的量表。
