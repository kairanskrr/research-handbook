# Graziotin 等人 2021：Psychometrics in Behavioral Software Engineering: A Methodological Introduction with Guidelines

- 资料：`graziotin2021psychometrics`，[arXiv:2005.09959](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.09959)
- 版本：arXiv v4（2021-06-08）；正式版发表于 ACM TOSEM 31(1)，2021

### E1

- 位置：§1.1 Misconceptions with psychological tests in software engineering research
- 原文："when a validated test from psychology is adopted by software engineering researchers, its items are often modified, causing the destruction of its psychometric reliability and validity properties."
- 要点：软件工程研究者采用心理学中验证过的量表时，常常改动题目，从而破坏了它的信度和效度。

### E2

- 位置：§1.1 Misconceptions with psychological tests in software engineering research
- 原文："we used a single Likert item scale to represent productivity." [...] "the productivity scale itself was not validated, making the results less valuable from a psychometric perspective"
- 要点：作者自我检讨：早年研究用一道李克特题测生产率，这个量表没有验证过，使结果的价值打了折扣。

### E3

- 位置：§6.0.1 Item facility
- 原文："Item facility for Likert items can be calculated with the mean value of the item."
- 要点：在心理测量的题目分析中，李克特题的"题目难易度"用该题的平均值计算。

### E4

- 位置：§13 Limitations of Psychometrics
- 原文："It might truly take years to developing and validating a measurement instrument. We highly recommend searching the literature for existing, psychometrically validated tests before developing one."
- 要点：开发并验证一个测量工具可能要好几年；自己开发之前，先在文献里找现成的、验证过的量表。

### E5

- 位置：§3 Pool items（结尾）
- 原文："a better strategy would be to develop six to ten items per envisioned factor, or sub-construct."
- 要点：作者建议每个设想的因子（子构念）编 6 到 10 道题。

### E6

- 位置：§5 Pilot test
- 原文："All results confirm the indicated value of n = 30 as reasonable minimum recommendation for a pilot study for a psychological test."
- 要点：作者引 Johanson 和 Brooks 的分析：心理测验的试测，30 人是合理的最低建议。

### E7

- 位置：§7 Factor analysis
- 原文："EFA attempts to uncover patterns and clusters of items by exploring predictions, while CFA attempts to confirm hypotheses on existing factors"
- 要点：探索性因子分析找题目聚成哪些类；验证性因子分析检验事先假设的因子结构。

### E8

- 位置：§7.2 Confirmatory Factor Analysis
- 原文："it is common to split the sample for conducting EFA on a part of it and CFA on the remaining part" / "Most authors, however, prefer conducting EFA only and rely on future independent studies towards a better psychometrics evaluation of a tool."
- 要点：样本够大时，常把样本分成两半，一半做探索性、一半做验证性因子分析；但多数作者只做探索性，把进一步检验留给以后的独立研究。

### E9

- 位置：§7.2 Confirmatory Factor Analysis
- 原文："the estimation of the goodness-of-fit in CFA is a long lasting debate"
- 要点：验证性因子分析的拟合度怎样评判，长期存在争论。

### E10

- 位置：§9 Reliability
- 原文："Several factors, as defined in the SEPT [3], affect the reliability of a measurement instrument, especially adding or removing items, changing wording or intervals of items"
- 要点：按《教育与心理测验标准》，增删题目、改措辞或选项间隔，都会影响量表的信度。

### E11

- 位置：§10 Validity
- 原文："The degree to which evidence and theory support the interpretation of test scores for proposed uses of tests."
- 要点：《教育与心理测验标准》对效度的定义：证据和理论在多大程度上支持按预定用途解释测验分数。

### E12

- 位置：§11 Fairness in testing and test bias
- 原文："A test is fair when it reflects the same constructs for all participants, and its scores have the same meaning for all individuals of the population"
- 要点：公平的测验对所有参与者测的是同一个构念，分数对总体中每个人意义相同。

### E13

- 位置：§11.1 Item bias
- 原文："A presence of DIF does not necessarily indicate bias but unexpected behavior on an item"
- 要点：出现题目功能差异（DIF）不一定就是偏差，只说明该题在不同组表现异常，需要查原因。
