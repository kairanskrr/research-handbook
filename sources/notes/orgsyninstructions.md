# Organic Syntheses：Instructions for Authors（作者须知）

- 资料：`orgsyninstructions`，[原文](https://www.orgsyn.org/instructions.aspx)
- 版本：《Organic Syntheses》（有机合成方法期刊，1921 年创刊，文章免费阅读）官网的作者须知页面，2026-10-06 读取。网页不分页，位置写小标题或段落内容。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："All organic chemists have experienced frustration at one time or another when attempting to repeat reactions based on experimental procedures found in journal articles."
- 要点：几乎每个有机化学家都有过照着期刊里的实验步骤重复反应却做不出来的挫败经历。

### E2

- 位置：开头
- 原文："To ensure reproducibility, Organic Syntheses requires experimental procedures written with considerably more detail as compared to the typical procedures found in other journals and in the "Supporting Information" sections of papers. In addition, each Organic Syntheses procedure is carefully "checked" for reproducibility in the laboratory of a member of the Board of Editors."
- 要点：为了保证可重复，该刊要求实验步骤比一般期刊和支持信息写得详细得多，而且每个步骤都要在一位编委的实验室里重做核查。

### E3

- 位置：Checking of procedures
- 原文："At least two successful runs of each step must be completed, at least one of which must be on exactly the submitting author's scale"
- 要点：核查时，每一步至少要成功做两次，其中至少一次与投稿人的规模完全相同。

### E4

- 位置：Checking of procedures
- 原文："In general, the yields and levels of selectivity obtained by the checking authors must be no less than ca. 5% below that reported by the submitting authors."
- 要点：核查者得到的产率和选择性，一般不能比投稿人报告的低约 5% 以上。

### E5

- 位置：Checking of procedures
- 原文："the Organic Syntheses editor and their coworker(s) are now credited as "Checking Authors" on all articles and should be included in the list of authors in citations to published Organic Syntheses articles."
- 要点：核查的编委和合作者现在作为"核查作者"署名，引用时要列入作者。

### E6

- 位置：Scale / reproducibility 段
- 原文："it is required that each reaction or experimental operation will have been carried out at least twice on exactly the scale described in the procedure" / "It is recommended that the reaction(s) and operations be tested by a coworker not been involved in the project as an "internal check""
- 要点：投稿前每个反应或操作必须按所写规模至少做两次；建议请一位没参与项目的同事做"内部核查"。

### E7

- 位置：Scale / reproducibility 段
- 原文："A range in yield over several runs of more than 10% may indicate that there are variables affecting the reaction that have not been identified"
- 要点：多次实验产率相差超过 10%，可能说明还有没找到的影响因素。

### E8

- 位置：Common Omissions
- 原文："The source of all starting materials and reagents must be provided and the purity of commercial reagents should be included."
- 要点：所有原料和试剂都要写来源，商品试剂要写纯度。

### E9

- 位置：Common Omissions
- 原文："Room temperature and vacuum pressures must be defined explicitly."
- 要点：室温和真空度要写出具体数值。

### E10

- 位置：Reaction procedure
- 原文："For reactions carried out at room temperature provide the temperature in °C (i.e., do not report as "rt")."
- 要点：室温反应要写具体摄氏度，不要只写"rt"。

### E11

- 位置：Reaction procedure
- 原文："Describe the appearance of the reaction mixture (color, homogeneous or not, etc.) and describe all significant changes in appearance during the course of the reaction (color changes, gas evolution, appearance of solids, exotherms, etc.)."
- 要点：要描述反应混合物的外观及反应中的所有明显变化（颜色、放气、析出固体、放热等）。

### E12

- 位置：Characterization（产物纯度）
- 原文："provide analytical data establishing that the purity of the isolated product is at least 97%. Note that this data should be obtained for the material on which the yield of the reaction is based, not for a sample that has been subjected to additional purification"
- 要点：该刊要求分离产物纯度至少 97%，且纯度数据要来自计算产率的那批产物，而不是另外再纯化过的样品。

### E13

- 位置：Safety（Note 1 的规定措辞）
- 原文："Prior to performing each reaction, a thorough hazard analysis and risk assessment should be carried out with regard to each chemical substance and experimental operation on the scale planned and in the context of the laboratory where the procedures will be carried out."
- 要点：每做一个反应之前，都要针对每种化学品、每个操作、计划的规模和所在实验室的条件，做全面的危害分析和风险评估。

### E14

- 位置：Common Omissions
- 原文："if a product of one step is used in the next step without purification, then a Note is required describing the purification of a sample and characterization data for both the purified sample and the crude material used in the next step."
- 要点：上一步产物不纯化就直接用于下一步时，要另外说明怎样纯化一份样品，并给出纯化样品和粗品两者的表征数据。

### E15

- 位置：Reaction Procedure
- 原文："Indicate the order of addition and time for addition of all reagents and how each is added (via syringe, addition funnel, etc.). Indicate the temperature of the reaction mixture (preferably internal temperature)."
- 要点：写明所有试剂的加料顺序、加料用时和加法（注射器、恒压滴液漏斗等）；写反应液温度，最好是内温。

### E16

- 位置：Reaction Procedure
- 原文："Be careful to describe clearly all cooling and warming cycles, including initial and final temperatures and the time interval involved."
- 要点：所有降温和升温过程都要写清起止温度和所用时间。

### E17

- 位置：Reaction Procedure
- 原文："Indicate how the reaction can be monitored to determine the extent of conversion of reactants to products. In the case of reactions monitored by TLC, provide details in a Note, including eluent, Rf values, and method of visualization."
- 要点：写明怎样监测反应进度；用薄层色谱监测时写展开剂、比移值和显色方法。

### E18

- 位置：Reaction Workup
- 原文："Describe the composition and volume of quenching agent, and time and temperature for addition." / "For extractions, the number of washes and the volume of each should be indicated as well as the size of the separatory funnel. For concentration of solutions after workup, indicate the method and pressure and temperature used."
- 要点：淬灭剂的组成、体积、加入时间和温度；萃取的次数、每次体积和分液漏斗大小；浓缩的方法、压力和温度都要写。

### E19

- 位置：Reagents（用量的有效数字和溶液浓度）
- 原文："Likewise, "5.00 equiv" implies that an amount of excess reagent must be controlled to a precision of 0.01 equiv. The concentration of solutions should be expressed in terms of molarity or normality, and not percent"
- 要点：有效数字要合理，写"5.00 equiv"就意味着要控制到 0.01 当量；溶液浓度用物质的量浓度或当量浓度，不用百分比。

### E20

- 位置：Scale / reproducibility 段
- 原文："In any case where a reagent is employed in significant excess (e.g., more than 1.5 equiv), a Note should be included explaining why an excess of that reagent is necessary. If possible, the Note should indicate the effect of using amounts of reagent less than that specified in the procedure."
- 要点：试剂明显过量（如超过 1.5 当量）时要解释原因，最好说明用量减少会怎样。

### E21

- 位置：Characterization（纯度证据）
- 原文："quantitative NMR (qNMR), GC, or HPLC analyses involving measurements versus an internal standard will also be accepted."
- 要点：用内标的定量核磁、气相色谱或高效液相色谱测纯度也可以接受。

### E22

- 位置：Scale / reproducibility 段
- 原文："It is appropriate to report the weight, yield, and purity of the product of each step in a procedure as a range. Yields should be rounded off to the nearest percent"
- 要点：每一步产物的质量、产率和纯度可以按范围报告；产率取整到百分之一。

### E23

- 位置：Common Omissions
- 原文："The amount of product generated in a prior step must be more than or equal to the amount used as starting material in a subsequent step."
- 要点：前一步得到的产物量，必须不少于下一步用作原料的量。

### E24

- 位置：Discussion section
- 原文："The scope and limitations of the method should be discussed, and it is generally desirable to include a table of examples." / "Competing methods for accomplishing the same overall transformation should be described and referenced."
- 要点：讨论部分要讲方法的适用范围和局限，最好附例子表；还要介绍并引用完成同一转化的竞争方法。

### E25

- 位置：Common Omissions
- 原文："Full details on procedures for purification must be provided"
- 要点：纯化步骤必须写全。
