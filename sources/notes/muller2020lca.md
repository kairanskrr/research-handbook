# Müller 等人 2020：A Guideline for Life Cycle Assessment of Carbon Capture and Utilization

- 资料：`muller2020lca`，[原文](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fenrg.2020.00015/full)，DOI：10.3389/fenrg.2020.00015
- 版本：Frontiers in Energy Research 第 8 卷第 15 篇，2020 年，知识共享署名许可（CC BY）开放获取。是 2018 年《二氧化碳利用技术经济评估与生命周期评估指南》（Global CO2 Initiative 等）中生命周期评估部分的摘要版，适用对象是碳捕集与利用技术。网页无页码，位置写小标题。2026-10-06 读取。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："The guideline has been development in a collaborative process involving over 40 experts and builds upon existing LCA standards and guidelines."
- 要点：这份指南由 40 多位专家合作制定，建立在已有的生命周期评估标准和指南之上。

### E2

- 位置：General Introduction to Life Cycle Assessment
- 原文："Life cycle assessment is a methodology to account for the environmental impacts of a product or service throughout its entire life cycle"
- 要点：生命周期评估是计算一个产品或服务在整个生命周期中环境影响的方法。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："However, the reduction of environmental impacts cannot be taken for granted: high energetic co-reactants such as hydrogen (Sternberg and Bardow, 2016) or epoxide are often needed to activate the chemically inert CO2"
- 要点：用二氧化碳做原料不一定减少环境影响，因为常要用氢气等高能量的共反应物来活化惰性的二氧化碳。

### E4

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："As a result, LCA studies on CCU technologies are often not comparable, e.g., because of differences in functional units, i.e., the relative basis for which environmental impacts are assessed, or system boundaries."
- 要点：由于功能单位（计算环境影响的比较基准）或系统边界不同，碳捕集与利用的生命周期评估研究常常无法相互比较。

### E5

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："For example, for CO2-based methanol, LCA studies in the literature reported cradle-to-gate carbon footprints between −1.7 and +9.7 kg of CO2, eq per kg of methanol."
- 要点：以二氧化碳制甲醇为例，文献报告的"从原料到出厂"碳足迹在每千克甲醇 −1.7 到 +9.7 千克二氧化碳当量之间。

### E6

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："However, harmonization of assumptions regarding supply of electricity and CO2 reduced the variation such that all carbon footprints had the same sign and could be clearly distinguished from fossil-based methanol."
- 要点：把电力和二氧化碳供应的假设统一后，差异缩小到所有碳足迹同号，并能和化石路线甲醇清楚区分。

### E7

- 位置：General Introduction to Life Cycle Assessment（技术成熟度低的研究）
- 原文："In contrast, low TRL processes usually have higher energy demand or solvent consumption because of not yet established heat integration and/or process optimization. At the same time, low TRL processes lack auxiliary processes such as product purification steps after reaction. Thus, LCA studies on lab-scale processes can both under- or over-estimate environmental impacts."
- 要点：技术成熟度低的工艺还没做热集成和优化，能耗、溶剂消耗偏高，又缺少反应后提纯等辅助工序，所以基于实验室规模的生命周期评估可能高估也可能低估环境影响。

### E8

- 位置：General Introduction to Life Cycle Assessment（技术成熟度低的研究）
- 原文："In early development stages, LCA is most useful to identify hot spots for environmental improvement via contribution followed by a sensitivity analysis."
- 要点：在早期开发阶段，生命周期评估最大的用处是找出环境影响的"热点"，再做敏感性分析。

### E9

- 位置：Scope Definition › Defining Functional Units for CCU Technologies
- 原文："Instead, the functional unit shall be defined so that the technical performance in the defined application of the products becomes comparable, e.g., compare detergents based on the washing performance and not based on mass."
- 要点：新产品和传统产品化学结构不同时，功能单位要按实际用途中的性能来定，例如洗涤剂按洗涤效果比，而不是按质量比。

### E10

- 位置：Life Cycle Interpretation（碳中和与负排放的解读）
- 原文："However, the negative LCA results does only reflect a comparison. In particular, negative LCA results do not necessarily imply that the CCU product is carbon neutral or even has negative emissions over its life cycle."
- 要点：生命周期评估得到负值只是比较的结果，不一定说明产品在整个生命周期是碳中和的或负排放的。

### E11

- 位置：Reporting
- 原文："The main report shall report all assumptions, data for calculation, methods, results and limitations as transparently and detailed as possible."
- 要点：主报告要尽可能透明、详细地写出全部假设、计算数据、方法、结果和局限。
