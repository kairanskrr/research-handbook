# 口述历史协会 2018：OHA Principles and Best Practices

- 资料：`oha2018principles`，[伦理声明](https://oralhistory.org/oha-statement-on-ethics/)，[最佳实践](https://oralhistory.org/best-practices/)
- 版本：美国口述历史协会（Oral History Association，OHA）2018 年 10 月通过的一套文件，包括核心原则、伦理声明、最佳实践、给受访者的说明等。本摘录取自其中两个网页：OHA Statement on Ethics（伦理声明）和 Oral History Best Practices（最佳实践），2026-10-06 读取。位置写网页名和小节。引文中的脚注编号用 `[...]` 略去。

### E1

- 位置：伦理声明，Preparation and Communication
- 原文："Prior to beginning the interview, the interviewer obtains the narrator’s informed consent, which means, most generally, documenting the knowing agreement of the narrator to participate in the process and overall project"
- 要点：访谈开始前，要取得受访者（narrator）的知情同意，也就是把受访者在知情的前提下同意参与这件事记录下来。

### E2

- 位置：伦理声明，Preparation and Communication
- 原文："While oral historians strive to protect the narrator, they are careful, at every point in the process, not to make promises that they cannot keep."
- 要点：口述历史研究者要尽力保护受访者，但在每个环节都不做自己兑现不了的承诺。

### E3

- 位置：伦理声明，Preparation and Communication
- 原文："The interviewer clearly communicates the goals of the project, the potential risks of participating in it, and the proviso that, once accessible[...], the oral history can be used[...] in any number of ways, by any number of potential users."
- 要点：要讲清项目目标、参与的潜在风险，以及访谈一旦公开，可能被任何人以各种方式使用。

### E4

- 位置：伦理声明，Collaboration: The Oral History Interview
- 原文："Options for narrator review include the right to delete, restrict, and/or redact portions of an interview; the ability to add clarifications and correct mistakes; and the choice to keep the interview closed to the public until a set date or to decline to release it to the public in the first place."
- 要点：受访者审阅访谈时可以删除、限制或涂抹部分内容，补充说明、更正错误，也可以选择到某个日期前不公开，或干脆不公开。

### E5

- 位置：伦理声明，Collaboration: The Oral History Interview
- 原文："During the course of the interview itself, oral historians attempt to minimize potential harm to the narrator, communicate the narrator’s right to refuse to answer questions, and honestly describe their institutional, professional, political, and other affiliations, as well as obligations and demands."
- 要点：访谈中要尽量减少对受访者的伤害，告知对方有权拒绝回答，并如实说明自己的机构、职业、政治等关系和所承担的义务。

### E6

- 位置：伦理声明，Using Oral Sources
- 原文："This means users of oral sources should provide analyses, including when edited or excerpted, that remain true to the words and meanings offered by the narrator and take care to not quote words out of context or otherwise contort the original meaning."
- 要点：使用口述资料时，即使经过编辑或节选，也要忠于受访者的原话和原意，不断章取义。

### E7

- 位置：最佳实践，Preparation
- 原文："During initial preparation, oral historians should locate an appropriate repository[...] to house the project’s finished oral histories and other documentation."
- 要点：项目一开始就要找好保存访谈成果和相关文件的收藏机构（档案馆、图书馆等）。

### E8

- 位置：最佳实践，Access and Use
- 原文："recognizing the subjectivity of the interview, including, when possible, verification of information presented as factual"
- 要点：使用者要认识到访谈是主观的；对其中当作事实陈述的内容，能核实的要核实。

### E9

- 位置：最佳实践，Preparation
- 原文："First-time interviewers and others involved in oral history projects should seek training"
- 要点：第一次做访谈的人应该先接受培训。

### E10

- 位置：最佳实践，Access and Use
- 原文："take all steps practicable to abide by any restrictions set forth by the narrator, while also making clear that certain legal challenges—such as subpoenas or open-record requests—may make some restrictions unenforceable"
- 要点：收藏机构要尽量遵守受访者提出的限制，但也要讲明：传票、信息公开申请等法律程序可能让某些限制无法执行。

### E11

- 位置：最佳实践，Preservation
- 原文："transfer the original recording from whatever device was used, make an appropriate number of redundant digital copies,[...] and store those in different physical locations, as soon as possible after any interview is completed"
- 要点：每次访谈结束后尽快把原始录音导出，做多份备份，分别存放在不同的物理地点。

### E12

- 位置：伦理声明，Collaboration: The Oral History Interview
- 原文："Power operates in every human engagement, and no less so in oral history interviewing."
- 要点：任何人际交往中都有权力关系，口述历史访谈也不例外。

### E13

- 位置：伦理声明，Collaboration: The Oral History Interview
- 原文："This step sets oral history apart[...] from other[...] methodologies in that it ensures the narrator’s account enters the public record"
- 要点：让受访者审阅并同意公开，是口述历史和其他访谈方法不同的地方：受访者的讲述会进入公共记录。

### E14

- 位置：最佳实践，Interviewing
- 原文："interviewers must respect the rights of interviewees to refuse to discuss certain subjects, to restrict access to the interview, or, under certain circumstances, to choose a pseudonym."
- 要点：受访者有权拒绝谈某些话题、限制访谈的查阅范围，在某些情况下可以用化名。
