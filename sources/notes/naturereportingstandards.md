# Nature Portfolio：Reporting standards and availability of data, materials, code and protocols

- 资料：`naturereportingstandards`，[原文](https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/reporting-standards)
- 版本：Nature Portfolio 编辑政策页面，2026-10 读取。

### E1

- 位置：页首
- 原文："A condition of publication in a Nature Portfolio journal is that authors are required to make materials, data, code, and associated protocols promptly available to readers without undue qualifications."
- 要点：在 Nature Portfolio 期刊发表的条件之一是：作者要及时向读者提供材料、数据、代码和相关流程，不附加不合理的条件。

### E2

- 位置：Reporting requirements for physical sciences research
- 原文："For physical sciences, we require authors of research articles in some specific areas to provide details of characterization, or experimental and analytical design in a reporting summary" / "Solar cells" / "Claims of lasing"
- 要点：物理科学方面，Nature Portfolio 要求某些特定方向的作者在报告摘要表中提供表征或实验与分析设计的细节。页面列出的方向是太阳能电池（Solar cells）和激光声明（Claims of lasing）。

### E3

- 位置：页首
- 原文："Any restrictions on the availability of materials or information must be disclosed to the editors at the time of submission."
- 要点：材料或信息的获取如有任何限制，投稿时必须告诉编辑。
