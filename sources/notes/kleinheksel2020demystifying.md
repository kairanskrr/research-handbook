# Kleinheksel 等 2020：Demystifying Content Analysis

- 资料：`kleinheksel2020demystifying`，[原文（PubMed Central 全文）](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7055418/)
- 版本：American Journal of Pharmaceutical Education 第 84 卷第 1 期，文章号 7113，2020 年（DOI 10.5688/ajpe7113，已通过 Crossref 核对）；作者 Kleinheksel、Rockich-Winston、Tawfik、Wyatt。面向药学教育研究者的方法入门文章，属于健康职业教育领域。位置写 PMC 全文的小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Content analysis is a method designed to identify and interpret meaning in recorded forms of communication by isolating small pieces of the data that represent salient concepts and then applying or creating a framework to organize the pieces in a way that can be used to describe or explain a phenomenon."
- 要点：内容分析是从记录下来的交流中识别和解读意义的方法：把代表关键概念的小段数据分离出来，再套用或建立一个框架把它们组织起来，用来描述或解释一个现象。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Content analysis was originally introduced as a strictly quantitative method, recording counts to measure the observed frequency of pre-identified targets in consumer research."
- 要点：内容分析最初是严格的定量方法，统计事先确定的目标出现的频次。

### E3

- 位置：Qualitative Content Analysis
- 原文："In much of the literature, manifest content analysis is defined as describing what is occurring on the surface, what is and literally present, and as “staying close to the text.”" / "It is content that can be recognized and counted with little training."
- 要点：显性内容分析描述文本表面、字面上存在的东西，"贴着文本"；这类内容稍加训练就能识别和计数。

### E4

- 位置：Qualitative Content Analysis
- 原文："The practical distinction between quantitative and qualitative manifest content analysis is the intention behind the analysis. The quantitative method seeks to generate a numerical value to either cite prevalence or use in statistical analyses, while the qualitative method seeks to identify a construct or concept within the text using specific words or phrases for substantiation, or to provide a more organized structure to the text being described."
- 要点：定量和定性内容分析的实际区别在意图：定量的要得到数值，用来说明普遍程度或做统计；定性的要在文本中识别一个概念并用原文词句佐证，或给文本更有条理的结构。

### E5

- 位置：Qualitative Content Analysis
- 原文："Latent content analysis is most often defined as interpreting what is hidden deep within the text. In this method, the role of the researcher is to discover the implied meaning in participants' experiences."
- 要点：隐性内容分析解读文本深处隐含的意义，研究者的任务是发现言外之意。

### E6

- 位置：Creating and Using Codes
- 原文："If studying a phenomenon that has already been described through an existing framework, codes can be created a priori using theoretical frameworks or concepts identified in the literature. If there is no existing framework to apply, codes can emerge during the analytical process."
- 要点：如果现象已经有现成框架描述过，可以事先根据理论或文献中的概念建编码；没有现成框架时，编码可以在分析中产生。

### E7

- 位置：Creating and Using Codes
- 原文："While there is no one way to decide what qualifies as a meaning unit, researchers typically define units of meaning differently depending on what kind of analysis is being conducted." / "In written text, such as student reflections or course evaluation data, the researcher must decide if the text should be divided into phrases or sentences, or remain as paragraphs."
- 要点：意义单元怎么切没有唯一的办法，取决于分析类型；书面文本要决定按短语、句子还是段落切分。

### E8

- 位置：Manifest Content Analysis
- 原文："A codebook (Table 2) is created with the following columns: name of code, code description, and examples of the code. This codebook helps an individual researcher to approach their analysis systematically, but it can also facilitate coding by multiple coders who would apply the same rules outlined in the codebook to the coding process."
- 要点：编码手册至少有编码名称、编码说明和例子三栏；它让单人分析更系统，也让多名编码者按同样的规则编码。

### E9

- 位置：Manifest Content Analysis
- 原文："To ensure that all coders are working in tandem, they must establish inter-rater reliability as part of their training process." / "After reviewing the codebook and receiving instruction, everyone on the team individually codes the same piece of data."
- 要点：多人编码时，要在培训过程中建立编码者间信度：大家读完编码手册、听完讲解后，各自独立给同一份材料编码。

### E10

- 位置：Manifest Content Analysis
- 原文："While calculating percentage agreement has sometimes been used to establish inter-rater reliability, most publication editors require more rigorous statistical analysis (eg, Krippendorf's alpha, or Cohen's kappa)." / "the choice depends on the number of coders, the sample size, and the type of data to be analyzed."
- 要点：百分比一致率有时被用来建立信度，但多数期刊编辑要求更严格的统计量，如 Krippendorff's alpha 或 Cohen's kappa；选哪个取决于编码者人数、样本量和数据类型。

### E11

- 位置：How to Choose a Methodological Approach to Content Analysis
- 原文："To determine which approach a researcher should take in their content analysis, two decisions need to be made. First, researchers must determine their goal for the analysis. Second, the researcher must decide where they believe meaning is located."
- 要点：选哪种内容分析，要做两个决定：分析的目标是什么，你认为意义在哪里（表面还是深处）。

### E12

- 位置：Other Aspects to Consider，Navigating Terminology
- 原文："Hsieh and Shannon proposed three qualitative approaches to content analysis: conventional, directed, and summative." / "In conventional content analysis, the researcher does not use preconceived categories because existing theory or literature are limited. In directed content analysis, the researcher attempts to further describe a phenomenon already addressed by theory, applying a deductive approach and using identified concepts or codes from exiting research to validate the theory. In summative content analysis, a descriptive approach is taken, identifying and quantifying words or content in order to describe their context."
- 要点：Hsieh 和 Shannon 把定性内容分析分成三种：常规式（理论不足时不用预设类别）、定向式（用已有理论的概念做演绎编码，进一步描述或验证理论）、总结式（识别并统计词语或内容，以描述其语境）。

### E13

- 位置：Other Aspects to Consider，Ensuring Quality
- 原文："In manifest content analysis, reliability is necessary but not sufficient to establish validity. Because there is little judgment required of the coders, lack of high inter-rater agreement among coders will render the data invalid. Additionally, coder fatigue is a common threat to manifest content analysis because the coding is clerical and repetitive in nature."
- 要点：显性内容分析中，信度是效度的必要而非充分条件；编码几乎不需判断，所以编码者一致性不高，数据就无效；编码重复枯燥，编码者疲劳是常见威胁。

### E14

- 位置：Other Aspects to Consider，Ensuring Quality
- 原文："For latent pattern content analysis, validity and reliability are inversely related. Greater reliability is achieved through more detailed coding rules to improve consistency, but these rules may diminish the accessibility of the coding to consumers of the research."
- 要点：隐性内容分析中，信度和效度此消彼长：规则写得越细，一致性越高，但编码结果可能离读者的理解越远。

### E15

- 位置：Other Aspects to Consider，Ensuring Quality
- 原文："Stability is another threat, which occurs when coders make different judgments as time passes. To reduce this risk, allowing for recoding at a later date can increase the consistency and stability of the codes."
- 要点：稳定性也是威胁：同一编码者随时间推移判断会变；过一段时间重新编码一遍，能提高一致性和稳定性。

### E16

- 位置：Other Aspects to Consider，Ensuring Quality
- 原文："Researchers can increase the reproducibility of their codebook by creating a detailed audit trail, including descriptions of the methods used to create and define the codes, materials used for the training of the coders, and steps taken to ensure inter-rater reliability."
- 要点：留下详细的审计记录能提高编码手册的可复现性：编码怎样建立和定义、培训编码者用了什么材料、为保证编码者间信度做了哪些步骤。

### E17

- 位置：Other Aspects to Consider，Ensuring Quality
- 原文："The terms “reliability” and “validity” are typically not used because they reflect the positivist quantitative paradigm. In qualitative research, the preferred term is “trustworthiness,” which is comprised of the concepts of credibility, transferability, dependability, and confirmability"
- 要点：定性研究一般不用"信度""效度"，而用"可信赖性"，它包括可信度、可迁移性、可靠性和可确认性。
