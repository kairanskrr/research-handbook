# Carlsen & Glenton 2011：What about N? A methodological study of sample-size reporting in focus group studies

- 资料：`carlsen2011what`，[原文](https://bmcmedresmethodol.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1471-2288-11-26)
- 版本：BMC Medical Research Methodology 11:26（2011），开放获取（知识共享署名许可 2.0）。健康服务研究。2026-10-17 读的期刊网页全文。

### E1

- 位置：Background
- 原文："A focus group or focus interview is commonly defined as a method of collecting research data through moderated group discussion based on the participants' perceptions and experience of a topic decided by the researcher. Focus groups differ from group interviews in that the emphasis is on the interaction between the participants rather than between the moderator or researcher and the participants."
- 要点：焦点小组通常定义为：围绕研究者定的话题，通过有人主持的小组讨论收集参与者的看法和经历；它和小组访谈的区别在于重点是参与者之间的互动。

### E2

- 位置：Background
- 原文："health researchers are repeatedly encouraged to use focus groups to evaluate health services, to elicit the views of key stakeholders or decision makers or to explore the views of marginalised groups that typically would not respond to a postal survey or would be intimidated by a conventional interview situation. Focus group interviews are also recommended as a pre- or post-study to prepare or interpret data from surveys or trial studies."
- 要点：健康研究常用焦点小组评估服务、了解关键相关方的看法、了解不回邮寄问卷或怕正式访谈的边缘群体；也常放在问卷或试验之前或之后，用来准备或解读数据。

### E3

- 位置：Background
- 原文："Most of this guidance recommends that the focus group should be the unit of analysis in focus group-studies. In line with this, sample size should refer to number of groups and not the total number of participants in a study."
- 要点：多数指南建议以小组为分析单位，所以样本量指的是小组数，而不是参与者总数。

### E4

- 位置：Background
- 原文："Guidance on group size is common and seldom goes beyond a minimum of 4 and a maximum of 12 participants per group"
- 要点：关于每组人数的建议，很少超出 4 到 12 人的范围。

### E5

- 位置：Background
- 原文："Sandelowski, in her discussion of sample size in qualitative research, emphasizes that both too few and too many groups can lower the quality of focus group studies"
- 要点：小组太少或太多都会降低焦点小组研究的质量。

### E6

- 位置：Background
- 原文："In general they recommend from two to five groups per category of participants. However, the authors usually underline that these are only rules of thumb and that the number of focus groups depends on the complexity of the research question and the composition of the groups."
- 要点：教科书一般建议每类参与者做 2 到 5 组，但强调这只是经验法则，组数取决于问题的复杂程度和小组构成。

### E7

- 位置：Abstract，Results
- 原文："The number of focus groups conducted varied greatly (mean 8.4, median 5, range 1 to 96). Thirty seven (17%) studies attempted to explain the number of groups."
- 要点：在 2008 年开放获取健康期刊的 220 篇焦点小组研究中，组数差别很大（中位数 5，范围 1 到 96），只有 17% 试图解释为什么做这么多组。

### E8

- 位置：Abstract，Results
- 原文："Among those stating that they had reached a point of saturation, several appeared not to have followed principles from grounded theory where data collection and analysis is an iterative process until saturation is reached."
- 要点：自称达到饱和的研究里，有好几项看来并没有边收集边分析，不符合饱和概念本来的做法。

### E9

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："There is also an ethical side to sample size: an excessive number of interviews means placing a burden on patients or health workers that is not legitimised by added scientific value and can thereby be seen as unethical."
- 要点：样本量也有伦理的一面：访谈做得过多，给参与者带来负担却没有增加科学价值，可以看作不合伦理。
