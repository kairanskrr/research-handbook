# Mastrandrea 等 2010：Guidance Note for Lead Authors of the IPCC Fifth Assessment Report on Consistent Treatment of Uncertainties

- 资料：`mastrandrea2010ipcc`，[原文](https://www.ipcc.ch/site/assets/uploads/2017/08/AR5_Uncertainty_Guidance_Note.pdf)
- 版本：政府间气候变化专门委员会（Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change，IPCC）2010 年 11 月发布的 4 页指南，写给第五次评估报告（AR5）的主要作者。位置写段落编号。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 页，引言
- 原文："These guidance notes are intended to assist Lead Authors of the Fifth Assessment Report (AR5) in the consistent treatment of uncertainties across all three Working Groups."
- 要点：这份指南是写给 IPCC 第五次评估报告主要作者的，用来统一三个工作组处理不确定性的方式。

### E2

- 位置：第 1 页，两种度量
- 原文："Confidence in the validity of a finding, based on the type, amount, quality, and consistency of evidence (e.g., mechanistic understanding, theory, data, models, expert judgment) and the degree of agreement. Confidence is expressed qualitatively."
- 要点：第一种度量是"信度"（confidence）：根据证据的类型、数量、质量、一致性和各方一致程度来判断，用文字定性表达。

### E3

- 位置：第 1 页，两种度量
- 原文："Quantified measures of uncertainty in a finding expressed probabilistically (based on statistical analysis of observations or model results, or expert judgment)."
- 要点：第二种度量是用概率表达的定量不确定性，依据观测或模型结果的统计分析，或专家判断。

### E4

- 位置：第 9 段
- 原文："A level of confidence is expressed using five qualifiers: "very low," "low," "medium," "high," and "very high.""
- 要点：信度分五级：很低、低、中等、高、很高。

### E5

- 位置：第 9 段
- 原文："Confidence should not be interpreted probabilistically, and it is distinct from "statistical confidence.""
- 要点：信度不是概率，也不同于统计学里的"置信度"。

### E6

- 位置：第 10 段及表 1
- 原文："A statement that an outcome is "likely" means that the probability of this outcome can range from 66% (fuzzy boundaries implied) to 100% probability." / "Virtually certain 99-100% probability Very likely 90-100% probability Likely 66-100% probability About as likely as not 33 to 66% probability Unlikely 0-33% probability Very unlikely 0-10% probability Exceptionally unlikely 0-1% probability"
- 要点：可能性用固定词表达，每个词对应一个概率区间，例如"很可能"（likely）对应 66%–100%，"极不可能"（very unlikely）对应 0–10%。

### E7

- 位置：第 10 段
- 原文：""About as likely as not" should not be used to express a lack of knowledge"
- 要点："可能性大约一半"不能用来表示"不知道"。

### E8

- 位置：第 10 段
- 原文："When there is sufficient information, it is preferable to specify the full probability distribution or a probability range"
- 要点：信息足够时，最好直接给出完整的概率分布或概率区间，而不是只用词语。

### E9

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Be prepared to make expert judgments in developing key findings, and to explain those judgments by providing a traceable account"
- 要点：作者要做专家判断，并给出"可追溯的说明"，交代判断依据。

### E10

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："Be aware of a tendency for a group to converge on an expressed view and become overconfident in it."
- 要点：要警惕团体讨论时意见趋同、变得过度自信。

### E11

- 位置：第 6 段
- 原文："Experts tend to underestimate structural uncertainty arising from incomplete understanding of or competing conceptual frameworks for relevant systems and processes."
- 要点：专家容易低估结构不确定性，即对系统理解不完整或存在多种竞争框架带来的不确定性。

### E12

- 位置：第 4 段
- 原文："Be aware that the way in which a statement is framed will have an effect on how it is interpreted (e.g., a 10% chance of dying is interpreted more negatively than a 90% chance of surviving)."
- 要点：同一结论换一种说法，读者的理解就不同。

### E13

- 位置：第 1 页，引言
- 原文："Author teams are therefore encouraged to provide information on the tails of distributions of key variables"
- 要点：鼓励给出关键变量分布尾部（小概率但后果大的结果）的信息。

### E14

- 位置：第 10 段
- 原文："there is evidence that readers may adjust their interpretation of this likelihood language according to the magnitude of perceived potential consequences."
- 要点：读者会根据后果的严重程度调整对这些可能性用词的理解。
