# 美国教育研究协会 2011：Code of Ethics

- 资料：`aera2011code`，[原文 PDF](https://www.aera.net/Portals/38/docs/About_AERA/CodeOfEthics(1).pdf)
- 版本：美国教育研究协会（American Educational Research Association，AERA）伦理准则，2011 年 2 月由 AERA 理事会批准。位置写条款编号。

### E1

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："It has as its primary goal the welfare and protection of the individuals and groups with whom education researchers work."
- 要点：准则的首要目标是保护教育研究者与之打交道的个人和群体的福祉。

### E2

- 位置：13.04 Informed Consent With Children，(a)
- 原文："In undertaking research with children, education researchers obtain the consent of a parent or a legally authorized guardian."
- 要点：研究涉及儿童时，要取得父母或法定监护人的同意。

### E3

- 位置：13.04 (b)
- 原文："In undertaking research with children, education researchers obtain the assent (that is, the agreement) of children to participate, to the extent that the children are capable of providing such assent"
- 要点：在儿童有能力表达的范围内，还要取得儿童本人的赞同（assent）。

### E4

- 位置：13.04 (c)
- 原文："Education researchers recognize that waivers of consent from a child and a parent or guardian require approval from institutional review boards or, in the absence of such boards, from another authoritative body with expertise on the ethics of research."
- 要点：免除儿童及其家长的同意，必须经机构审查委员会（或其他有伦理专长的权威机构）批准。

### E5

- 位置：13.01 (d)
- 原文："In undertaking research with vulnerable populations (e.g., children, youth, special needs students, recent immigrant populations), education researchers take special care to ensure that the voluntary nature of the research is understood and that consent or assent is not coerced."
- 要点：研究儿童、青少年、特殊需要学生等弱势人群时，要特别确保对方明白参加是自愿的，同意或赞同不是被迫的。

### E6

- 位置：13.03 Informed Consent of Students and Subordinates
- 原文："When undertaking research at their own institutions or organizations with research participants who are students or subordinates, education researchers take special care to protect the prospective participants from adverse consequences of declining or withdrawing from participation."
- 要点：在本单位以自己的学生或下属为参与者时，要特别保护他们不因拒绝或退出而受到不利影响。

### E7

- 位置：14.02 Dual Relationships
- 原文："In planning research, education researchers select research participants with whom they have no other relationship (e.g., teacher, supervisor, mentor, or employer). If research opportunities or circumstances require selection of research participants with whom education researchers have another such relationship, researchers take particular care to ensure that consent to participate is voluntary and free of coercion."
- 要点：规划研究时，应选择和自己没有其他关系（如师生、上下级）的参与者；不得不选时，要特别确保同意是自愿、没有强迫的。

### E8

- 位置：13 Informed Consent（总述）
- 原文："Education researchers recognize the possibility of undue influence or subtle pressures on research participants that may derive from researchers' expertise or authority, and they take this into account in designing informed consent procedures."
- 要点：研究者的专业身份或权威可能给参与者带来不当影响或微妙压力，设计知情同意程序时要考虑这一点。

### E9

- 位置：14.06 Data Sharing，(a)
- 原文："Education researchers share data and pertinent documentation as a regular practice."
- 要点：把共享数据和相关文档作为常规做法。

### E10

- 位置：14.06 (c)
- 原文："Education researchers anticipate data sharing as an integral part of a research plan whenever data sharing is feasible."
- 要点：只要可行，就把数据共享作为研究计划的一部分事先考虑。

### E11

- 位置：15. Authorship Credit，(c)
- 原文："Education researchers ensure that principal authorship, authorship order, and other publication credits are based on the relative scientific or professional contributions of the individuals involved, regardless of their status."
- 要点：第一作者和署名顺序按各人的相对贡献定，与地位无关。

### E12

- 位置：15 (d)
- 原文："A student is usually listed as principal author on any multiple-authored publication that substantially derives from the student's dissertation or thesis."
- 要点：主要来自学生学位论文的多作者论文，学生通常是第一作者。

### E13

- 位置：13.06 Use of Recording Technology
- 原文："Education researchers obtain informed consent from research participants, students, employees, clients, or others prior to photographing, videotaping, filming, or recording them in any form"
- 要点：给参与者、学生等拍照、录像、录音之前，要先取得知情同意。
