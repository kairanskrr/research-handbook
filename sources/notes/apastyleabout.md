# APA Style：About APA Style

- 资料：`apastyleabout`，[原文](https://apastyle.apa.org/about-apa-style)
- 版本：网页（页面注明最后更新 2023 年 3 月），使用 Internet Archive 2024 年快照。

### E1

- 位置：Where did APA Style come from?
- 原文："APA Style originated in 1929, when a group of psychologists, anthropologists, and business managers convened and sought to establish a simple set of procedures, or style guidelines, that would codify the many components of scientific writing to increase the ease of reading comprehension."
- 要点：APA 格式始于 1929 年，目的是统一科学写作的各个部分，让论文更容易读。

### E2

- 位置：Does APA Style cover everything about writing?
- 原文："APA Style covers the aspects of scholarly writing most pertinent to writing in psychology, nursing, business, communications, engineering, and related fields. It specifically addresses the preparation of draft manuscripts being submitted for publication in a journal and the preparation of student papers being submitted for a course assignment."
- 要点：APA 格式面向心理学、护理、商学等领域，专门针对投给期刊的稿件和学生课程论文。

### E3

- 位置：Why is APA Style needed?
- 原文："Authors plan for ethical compliance and report critical details of their research protocol to allow readers to evaluate findings and other researchers to potentially replicate the studies."
- 要点：APA 格式不只是排版：它要求作者报告研究方案的关键细节，让读者能评估结果、别人能重做研究。
