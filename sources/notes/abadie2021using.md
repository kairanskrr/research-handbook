# Abadie 2021：Using Synthetic Controls: Feasibility, Data Requirements, and Methodological Aspects

- 资料：`abadie2021using`，[原文](https://hdl.handle.net/1721.1/144417)
- 版本：Journal of Economic Literature 59(2)，2021，391–425 页（DOI 10.1257/jel.20191450）。本条摘录取自麻省理工学院开放获取库 DSpace@MIT 收录的出版社定稿版本。

### E1

- 位置：§5 Contextual Requirements，Size of the Effect and Volatility of the Outcome
- 原文："the impact of "small" interventions with effects of a magnitude similar to the volatility of the outcome are difficult to detect. Even a large effect may be difficult to detect if the volatility of the outcome is also large."
- 要点：效应和结果变量本身的波动差不多大时很难发现；结果波动很大时，大效应也可能看不出来。

### E2

- 位置：§5 Contextual Requirements，Availability of a Comparison Group
- 原文："Units that adopt an intervention similar to the one adopted by the unit of interest should not be included in the donor pool because they are affected by the intervention, very much like the unit of interest." / "it is important to restrict the donor pool to units with characteristics that are similar to the affected unit."
- 要点：采取过类似干预的单位不能进对照单位池；对照单位池要限于特征和处理单位相近的单位。

### E3

- 位置：§5 Contextual Requirements，Convex Hull Condition
- 原文："there may not exist a combination of untreated units that provide a credible approximation to the treated units, and the conventional synthetic control estimator should not be used in that case."
- 要点：如果找不到一组未处理单位的组合能可信地近似处理单位，就不应该用常规的合成控制估计。

### E4

- 位置：§6 Data Requirements，Sufficient Pre-intervention Information
- 原文："The credibility of a synthetic control estimator depends in great part on its ability to steadily track the trajectory of the outcome variable for the affected unit before the intervention." / "when designing a synthetic control study, it is of crucial importance to collect information on the affected unit and the donor pool for a large pre-intervention window."
- 要点：合成控制是否可信，很大程度上看它能否在干预前稳定地跟上处理单位的结果走势；设计研究时要收集足够长的干预前时期的数据。
