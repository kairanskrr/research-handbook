# Vandenbroucke et al. 2007：Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology (STROBE): Explanation and Elaboration

- 资料：`vandenbroucke2007strobe`，[PLoS Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.0040297)（CC BY）
- 版本：PLoS Med 4(10): e297，2007。STROBE 的配套解释文章。

### E1

- 位置：Box 1. Main study designs covered by STROBE
- 原文："In cohort studies, the investigators follow people over time. They obtain information about people and their exposures at baseline, let time pass, and then assess the occurrence of outcomes."
- 要点：队列研究：先记录一群人的暴露情况，随时间跟踪，再看结局是否发生。

### E2

- 位置：Box 1. Main study designs covered by STROBE
- 原文："In case-control studies, investigators compare exposures between people with a particular disease outcome (cases) and people without that outcome (controls)."
- 要点：病例对照研究：比较得了某病的人（病例）和没得的人（对照）过去的暴露情况。

### E3

- 位置：Box 1. Main study designs covered by STROBE
- 原文："In cross-sectional studies, investigators assess all individuals in a sample at the same point in time, often to examine the prevalence of exposures, risk factors or disease."
- 要点：横断面研究：在同一时间点调查样本中的所有人，常用来看暴露、危险因素或疾病的患病率。

### E4

- 位置：Box 1. Main study designs covered by STROBE
- 原文："A difficulty that can occur in any design but is particularly clear in cross-sectional studies is to establish that an exposure preceded the disease"
- 要点：横断面研究尤其难以确定暴露发生在疾病之前。

### E5

- 位置：Box 3. Bias
- 原文："Bias and confounding are not synonymous. Bias arises from flawed information or subject selection so that a wrong association is found. Confounding produces relations that are factually right, but that cannot be interpreted causally because some underlying, unaccounted for factor is associated with both exposure and outcome"
- 要点：偏倚和混杂不是一回事。偏倚来自信息或研究对象选择有缺陷；混杂是关联本身是真的，但因为有一个与暴露和结局都相关、却没考虑到的因素，不能解释为因果。

### E6

- 位置：Item 8 (Data sources/measurement), Explanation
- 原文："Measurement error and misclassification of exposures or outcomes can make it more difficult to detect cause-effect relationships, or may produce spurious relationships. Error in measurement of potential confounders can increase the risk of residual confounding"
- 要点：暴露或结局测量不准、分类错误，可能让真实的因果关系更难发现，也可能制造虚假的关系；混杂因素测不准，会留下"残余混杂"。

### E7

- 位置：Item 19 (Limitations), Explanation
- 原文："The inability to precisely measure true values of an exposure tends to result in bias towards unity: the less precisely a risk factor is measured, the greater the bias."
- 要点：暴露测不准，往往会让估计出的效应被拉向"没有关联"；测得越不准，偏差越大。

### E8

- 位置：Item 20 (Interpretation), Explanation
- 原文："The doubling in the risk of ovarian cancer associated with eating 2 to 4 eggs per week is not immediately credible, since dietary [...] habits are associated with a large number of lifestyle factors as well as SES"
- 要点：每周吃 2 到 4 个鸡蛋与卵巢癌风险加倍有关联，这个结果不能直接相信，因为饮食习惯与大量生活方式因素和社会经济地位相关。

### E9

- 位置：Item 16 (Main results), Explanation
- 原文："we and others do not advise selecting confounders based solely on statistical significance testing"
- 要点：不建议只凭统计显著性检验来挑选要调整的混杂因素。

### E10

- 位置：Item 8 (Data sources/measurement), Explanation
- 原文："Rather than simply citing validation studies (as in the first example), we advise that authors give the estimated validity or reliability, which can then be used for measurement error adjustment or sensitivity analyses"
- 要点：不要只引用验证研究，而要给出测量方法的效度或信度估计值，便于做测量误差校正或敏感性分析。

### E11

- 位置：Item 13 (Participants), Explanation
- 原文："the association between young maternal age and leukaemia in offspring, which has been observed in some case-control studies [...] was explained by differential participation of young women in case and control groups. Young women with healthy children were less likely to participate than those with unhealthy children"
- 要点：例子：孩子健康的年轻母亲更不愿意参加研究，于是"年轻母亲与孩子白血病"的关联其实是参与差异造成的。

### E12

- 位置：STROBE 清单，Item 20
- 原文："Give a cautious overall interpretation of results considering objectives, limitations, multiplicity of analyses, results from similar studies, and other relevant evidence"
- 要点：结合目标、局限、分析次数多少、类似研究的结果等，对结果做谨慎的整体解释。

### E13

- 位置：Item 13 (Participants), Explanation
- 原文："Although low participation does not necessarily compromise the validity of a study, transparent information on participation and reasons for non-participation is essential."
- 要点：参与率低不一定损害研究的效度，但必须如实报告参与情况和不参与的原因。

### E14

- 位置：Box 1. Main study designs covered by STROBE
- 原文："Controls represent the cohort or population of people from which the cases arose."
- 要点：病例对照研究里，对照代表的是"产生病例的那个队列或人群"。

### E15

- 位置：Box 1. Main study designs covered by STROBE
- 原文："Some cross-sectional studies are analytical and aim to quantify potential causal associations between exposures and disease." / "A difficulty that can occur in any design but is particularly clear in cross-sectional studies is to establish that an exposure preceded the disease"
- 要点：有些横断面研究是分析性的，想量化暴露和疾病之间可能的因果关联；但横断面研究尤其难以确定暴露发生在疾病之前。

### E16

- 位置：Box 1. Main study designs covered by STROBE
- 原文："Investigators may assess several different outcomes, and examine exposure and outcome variables at multiple points during follow-up."
- 要点：队列研究可以同时评估多个结局，并在随访中多次测量暴露和结局。

### E17

- 位置：Item 4 (Study design), Explanation
- 原文："We recommend that authors refrain from simply calling a study ‘prospective’ or ‘retrospective’ because these terms are ill defined" / "Most importantly, we recommend that authors describe exactly how and when data collection took place."
- 要点：不要只给研究贴"前瞻性""回顾性"的标签，这两个词定义不清；最重要的是写清数据具体是怎样、在什么时候收集的。

### E18

- 位置：Item 6 (Participants), Explanation
- 原文："In general, controls should reflect the population from which the cases arose."
- 要点：一般来说，对照应当反映产生病例的那个人群。

### E19

- 位置：Item 6 (Participants), Explanation
- 原文："Controls with other diseases may have advantages over population-based controls, in particular for hospital-based cases, because they better reflect the catchment population of a hospital, have greater comparability of recall and ease of recruitment. However, they can present problems if the exposure of interest affects the risk of developing or being hospitalized for the control condition(s)"
- 要点：用患其他病的人作对照（医院对照），对来自医院的病例有好处：更能代表医院的服务人群，回忆的可比性更好，也容易招募；但如果所研究的暴露会影响对照所患疾病的发病或住院，就会出问题。

### E20

- 位置：Box 2. Matching in case-control studies
- 原文："Matching is intuitively appealing, but the complexities involved have led methodologists to advise against routine matching in case-control studies."
- 要点：匹配直觉上很吸引人，但由于其中的复杂性，方法学者不建议在病例对照研究中例行匹配。

### E21

- 位置：Box 2. Matching in case-control studies
- 原文："Matching will introduce irremediable bias if the matching variable is not a confounder but in the causal pathway between exposure and disease. For example, in vitro fertilization is associated with an increased risk of perinatal death, due to an increase in multiple births and low birth weight infants" [...] "Matching on plurality or birth weight will bias results towards the null, and this cannot be remedied in the analysis."
- 要点：如果匹配变量不是混杂因素，而是位于暴露到疾病的因果通路上，匹配会带来无法补救的偏倚。例子：试管婴儿与围产期死亡风险增加有关，原因是多胎和低出生体重增加；按胎数或出生体重匹配，会把结果拉向无关联，分析时无法补救。

### E22

- 位置：Box 2. Matching in case-control studies
- 原文："A crude analysis of the data will produce odds ratios that are usually biased towards unity if the matching factor is associated with the exposure. The solution is to perform a matched or stratified analysis"
- 要点：匹配因素和暴露相关时，不考虑匹配的粗分析得到的比值比通常偏向 1；应做匹配分析或分层分析。

### E23

- 位置：STROBE 清单，Item 7 (Variables)
- 原文："Clearly define all outcomes, exposures, predictors, potential confounders, and effect modifiers. Give diagnostic criteria, if applicable."
- 要点：清楚定义所有结局、暴露、预测因素、潜在混杂因素和效应修饰因素；适用时给出诊断标准。

### E24

- 位置：Box 4. Grouping
- 原文："Readers should be informed if cut-points are selected post hoc from several alternatives. In particular, if the cut-points were chosen to minimise a P value the true strength of an association will be exaggerated"
- 要点：如果分组切点是事后从几个备选中挑的，要告诉读者；特别是为了让 P 值最小而选切点，会夸大关联强度。

### E25

- 位置：Item 8 (Data sources/measurement), Explanation
- 原文："if an interviewer first questions all the cases and then the controls, or vice versa, bias is possible because of the learning curve; solutions such as randomising the order of interviewing may avoid this problem."
- 要点：访谈者先问完所有病例再问对照（或反过来），会因为"越问越熟练"带来偏倚；随机安排访谈顺序可以避免。

### E26

- 位置：Box 3. Bias
- 原文："In case-control studies information bias occurs if cases recall past exposures more or less accurately than controls without that disease, or if they are more or less willing to report them (also called ‘recall bias’). ‘Interviewer bias’ can occur if interviewers are aware of the study hypothesis and subconsciously or consciously gather data selectively" / "Some form of blinding of study participants and researchers is therefore often valuable."
- 要点：病例对照研究里，病例回忆或愿意报告过去暴露的程度和对照不同，就是回忆偏倚；访谈者知道研究假设时，可能有意无意地选择性收集数据（访谈者偏倚）。所以对参与者和研究人员做某种形式的设盲常常很有价值。

### E27

- 位置：Box 3. Bias
- 原文："Patients receiving a drug that causes non-specific stomach discomfort may undergo gastroscopy more often and have more ulcers detected than patients not receiving the drug – even if the drug does not cause more ulcers." / "One way to assess its influence is to measure the intensity of medical surveillance in the different study groups, and to adjust for it in statistical analyses."
- 要点：吃某种会引起胃部不适的药的人，做胃镜更多，于是查出更多溃疡，即使药本身不导致溃疡（检出偏倚）。可以测量各组接受医疗检查的强度，并在分析中调整。

### E28

- 位置：Box 3. Bias
- 原文："Such bias may be countered by using cases and controls that were referred in the same way to the diagnostic service"
- 要点：病例和对照经同样的转诊途径进入诊断服务，可以抵消这类选择偏倚。

### E29

- 位置：Box 5. Confounding
- 原文："Investigators should think beforehand about potential confounding factors. This will inform the study design and allow proper data collection by identifying the confounders for which detailed information should be sought. Restriction or matching may be used."
- 要点：研究者应事先想好潜在混杂因素，这能指导研究设计，并确定哪些混杂因素需要详细收集数据；设计上可以用限制或匹配。

### E30

- 位置：Box 5. Confounding
- 原文："In the analysis phase, investigators may use stratification or multivariable analysis to reduce the effect of confounders."
- 要点：分析阶段可以用分层或多变量分析减少混杂的影响。

### E31

- 位置：Item 16 (Main results), Explanation
- 原文："Decisions about excluding or including variables should be guided by knowledge, or explicit assumptions, on causal relations. Inappropriate decisions may introduce bias, for example by including variables that are in the causal pathway between exposure and disease"
- 要点：模型里放不放某个变量，应由因果关系的知识或明确的假设来决定；决定不当会引入偏倚，例如把暴露到疾病因果通路上的变量放进模型。

### E32

- 位置：Item 16 (Main results), Explanation
- 原文："If the decision to include a variable in the model was based on the change in the estimate, it is important to report what change was considered sufficiently important to justify its inclusion."
- 要点：如果按"加入后估计值变化多少"来决定是否纳入变量，要报告多大的变化才算足以纳入。

### E33

- 位置：Item 12 (Statistical methods), Explanation
- 原文："investigators should pre-determine analyses at least for the primary study objectives in a study protocol." / "When a study is reported, authors should tell readers whether particular analyses were suggested by data inspection."
- 要点：至少主要研究目标的分析要事先写进研究方案；报告时要说明哪些分析是看了数据之后才想到的。

### E34

- 位置：Item 16 (Main results), Explanation
- 原文："Readers can compare unadjusted measures of association with those adjusted for potential confounders and judge by how much, and in what direction, they changed. Readers may think that ‘adjusted’ results equal the causal part of the measure of association, but adjusted results are not necessarily free of random sampling error, selection bias, information bias, or residual confounding"
- 要点：同时给出未调整和调整后的估计，读者能看出调整让结果变了多少、往哪个方向变；调整后的结果不等于"因果部分"，仍可能有随机误差、选择偏倚、信息偏倚或残余混杂。

### E35

- 位置：Box 6. Missing data: problems and possible solutions
- 原文："Although such ‘complete-case’ analyses are unbiased in many circumstances, they can be biased and are always inefficient" / "The plausibility of assumptions made in missing data analyses is generally unverifiable." / "Such analyses are therefore best viewed in the spirit of sensitivity analysis"
- 要点：只分析数据完整的人，很多情况下无偏，但可能有偏，而且总会损失效率；缺失数据分析的假设一般无法验证，所以最好把这类分析当作敏感性分析看待。

### E36

- 位置：Item 12 (d) Cohort study: loss to follow-up, Explanation
- 原文："Investigators therefore need to decide, ideally at the stage of planning the study, how they will deal with loss to follow-up."
- 要点：最好在规划研究时就决定怎样处理失访。

### E37

- 位置：Item 12 (e) Sensitivity analyses, Explanation
- 原文："Sensitivity analyses are useful to investigate whether or not the main results are consistent with those obtained with alternative analysis strategies or assumptions" / "Issues that may be examined include the criteria for inclusion in analyses, the definitions of exposures or outcomes"
- 要点：敏感性分析用来检查换一种分析策略或假设时，主要结果是否一致；可以检查纳入标准、暴露或结局的定义等（原文还列了调整哪些混杂、缺失数据的处理、可能的选择偏倚和测量偏倚、定量变量的处理）。

### E38

- 位置：Item 12 (e) Sensitivity analyses, Explanation
- 原文："In 1959 Cornfield et al. famously showed that a relative risk of 9 for cigarette smoking and lung cancer was extremely unlikely to be due to any conceivable confounder, since the confounder would need to be at least nine times as prevalent in smokers as in non-smokers"
- 要点：1959 年 Cornfield 等人指出，吸烟与肺癌的相对危险度为 9，几乎不可能由任何可以想到的混杂因素造成，因为这个混杂因素在吸烟者中的流行程度至少要是非吸烟者的 9 倍。

### E39

- 位置：Item 12 (e) Sensitivity analyses, Explanation
- 原文："One important, perhaps under recognised, use of sensitivity analysis is when a study shows little or no association between an exposure and an outcome and it is plausible that confounding or other biases toward the null are present."
- 要点：研究发现暴露和结局几乎没有关联、而混杂或其他偏倚可能把结果拉向"无关联"时，敏感性分析尤其有用，这一用途可能被低估了。

### E40

- 位置：Item 14 (Descriptive data), Explanation
- 原文："In case-control studies potential confounders cannot be judged by comparing cases and controls. Control persons represent the source population and will usually be different from the cases in many respects."
- 要点：病例对照研究里，不能靠比较病例和对照来判断混杂；对照代表源人群，通常在很多方面都和病例不同。

### E41

- 位置：Item 19 (Limitations), Explanation
- 原文："It is important not only to identify the sources of bias and confounding that could have affected results, but also to discuss the relative importance of different biases, including the likely direction and magnitude of any potential bias"
- 要点：讨论局限时，不只要列出可能的偏倚和混杂来源，还要讨论各种偏倚的相对重要性，包括可能的方向和大小。

### E42

- 位置：Item 20 (Interpretation), Explanation
- 原文："Authors should address the real range of uncertainty in estimates, which is larger than the statistical uncertainty reflected in confidence intervals."
- 要点：估计值真实的不确定范围，比置信区间反映的统计不确定性更大，作者要讨论。

### E43

- 位置：Item 20 (Interpretation), Explanation
- 原文："These criteria should not, however, be applied mechanically. For example, some have argued that relative risks below 2 or 3 should be ignored" / "Although a causal effect is more likely with a relative risk of 9, it does not follow that one below 3 is necessarily spurious."
- 要点：Bradford Hill 准则不应机械套用；有人主张忽略相对危险度低于 2 或 3 的结果，但相对危险度低于 3 不一定就是假的。

### E44

- 位置：Item 10 (Study size), Explanation
- 原文："If an analysis is performed on data that were already available for other purposes, the main question is whether the analysis of the data will produce results with sufficient statistical precision to contribute substantially to the literature, and sample size considerations will be informal." / "Do not bother readers with post hoc justifications for study size or retrospective power calculations"
- 要点：用为别的目的已经收集好的数据做分析时，关键是结果精度够不够，样本量考虑可以是非正式的；不要事后为样本量找理由或做事后功效计算。

### E45

- 位置：Item 8 (Data sources/measurement), Example 2
- 原文："Samples pertaining to matched cases and controls were always analyzed together in the same batch and laboratory personnel were unable to distinguish among cases and controls"
- 要点：例子：匹配的病例和对照的样本总在同一批次检测，实验室人员分不出哪个是病例、哪个是对照。

### E46

- 位置：Box 6. Missing data: problems and possible solutions
- 原文："Inserting a missing category indicator for a confounder may increase residual confounding"
- 要点：给混杂因素加一个"缺失"类别，可能增加残余混杂。

### E47

- 位置：Item 20 (Interpretation), Explanation
- 原文："Variables used to measure SES (income, education, or occupation) are surrogates for other undefined and unmeasured exposures, and the true confounder will by definition be measured with error"
- 要点：用收入、教育、职业测量社会经济地位，只是替代指标，真正的混杂因素必然测不准。
