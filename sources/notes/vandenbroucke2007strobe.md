# Vandenbroucke et al. 2007：Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology (STROBE): Explanation and Elaboration

- 资料：`vandenbroucke2007strobe`，[PLoS Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.0040297)（CC BY）
- 版本：PLoS Med 4(10): e297，2007。STROBE 的配套解释文章。

### E1

- 位置：Box 1. Main study designs covered by STROBE
- 原文："In cohort studies, the investigators follow people over time. They obtain information about people and their exposures at baseline, let time pass, and then assess the occurrence of outcomes."
- 要点：队列研究：先记录一群人的暴露情况，随时间跟踪，再看结局是否发生。

### E2

- 位置：Box 1. Main study designs covered by STROBE
- 原文："In case-control studies, investigators compare exposures between people with a particular disease outcome (cases) and people without that outcome (controls)."
- 要点：病例对照研究：比较得了某病的人（病例）和没得的人（对照）过去的暴露情况。

### E3

- 位置：Box 1. Main study designs covered by STROBE
- 原文："In cross-sectional studies, investigators assess all individuals in a sample at the same point in time, often to examine the prevalence of exposures, risk factors or disease."
- 要点：横断面研究：在同一时间点调查样本中的所有人，常用来看暴露、危险因素或疾病的患病率。

### E4

- 位置：Box 1. Main study designs covered by STROBE
- 原文："A difficulty that can occur in any design but is particularly clear in cross-sectional studies is to establish that an exposure preceded the disease"
- 要点：横断面研究尤其难以确定暴露发生在疾病之前。

### E5

- 位置：Box 3. Bias
- 原文："Bias and confounding are not synonymous. Bias arises from flawed information or subject selection so that a wrong association is found. Confounding produces relations that are factually right, but that cannot be interpreted causally because some underlying, unaccounted for factor is associated with both exposure and outcome"
- 要点：偏倚和混杂不是一回事。偏倚来自信息或研究对象选择有缺陷；混杂是关联本身是真的，但因为有一个与暴露和结局都相关、却没考虑到的因素，不能解释为因果。

### E6

- 位置：Item 8 (Data sources/measurement), Explanation
- 原文："Measurement error and misclassification of exposures or outcomes can make it more difficult to detect cause-effect relationships, or may produce spurious relationships. Error in measurement of potential confounders can increase the risk of residual confounding"
- 要点：暴露或结局测量不准、分类错误，可能让真实的因果关系更难发现，也可能制造虚假的关系；混杂因素测不准，会留下"残余混杂"。

### E7

- 位置：Item 19 (Limitations), Explanation
- 原文："The inability to precisely measure true values of an exposure tends to result in bias towards unity: the less precisely a risk factor is measured, the greater the bias."
- 要点：暴露测不准，往往会让估计出的效应被拉向"没有关联"；测得越不准，偏差越大。

### E8

- 位置：Item 20 (Interpretation), Explanation
- 原文："The doubling in the risk of ovarian cancer associated with eating 2 to 4 eggs per week is not immediately credible, since dietary [...] habits are associated with a large number of lifestyle factors as well as SES"
- 要点：每周吃 2 到 4 个鸡蛋与卵巢癌风险加倍有关联，这个结果不能直接相信，因为饮食习惯与大量生活方式因素和社会经济地位相关。

### E9

- 位置：Item 16 (Main results), Explanation
- 原文："we and others do not advise selecting confounders based solely on statistical significance testing"
- 要点：不建议只凭统计显著性检验来挑选要调整的混杂因素。

### E10

- 位置：Item 8 (Data sources/measurement), Explanation
- 原文："Rather than simply citing validation studies (as in the first example), we advise that authors give the estimated validity or reliability, which can then be used for measurement error adjustment or sensitivity analyses"
- 要点：不要只引用验证研究，而要给出测量方法的效度或信度估计值，便于做测量误差校正或敏感性分析。

### E11

- 位置：Item 13 (Participants), Explanation
- 原文："the association between young maternal age and leukaemia in offspring, which has been observed in some case-control studies [...] was explained by differential participation of young women in case and control groups. Young women with healthy children were less likely to participate than those with unhealthy children"
- 要点：例子：孩子健康的年轻母亲更不愿意参加研究，于是"年轻母亲与孩子白血病"的关联其实是参与差异造成的。

### E12

- 位置：STROBE 清单，Item 20
- 原文："Give a cautious overall interpretation of results considering objectives, limitations, multiplicity of analyses, results from similar studies, and other relevant evidence"
- 要点：结合目标、局限、分析次数多少、类似研究的结果等，对结果做谨慎的整体解释。

### E13

- 位置：Item 13 (Participants), Explanation
- 原文："Although low participation does not necessarily compromise the validity of a study, transparent information on participation and reasons for non-participation is essential."
- 要点：参与率低不一定损害研究的效度，但必须如实报告参与情况和不参与的原因。
