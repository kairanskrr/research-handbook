# Simmons, Nelson & Simonsohn 2018：False-Positive Citations

- 资料：`simmons2018false`，[原文 PDF（作者主页）](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/26-Simmons-Nelson-Simonsohn-2018.pdf)
- 版本：Perspectives on Psychological Science 13(2):255–259。作者回顾 2011 年《False-Positive Psychology》一文的写作和被引用情况。

### E1

- 位置：第 256 页
- 原文："we also recommended that authors be required to have at least 20 observations per cell" / "20-per-cell is a comically low threshold, insufficient to detect in a representative sample that men are heavier than women"
- 要点：2011 年文章曾建议每个实验组至少 20 个观测。作者后来说，这个门槛低得可笑：每组 20 人连"男性比女性重"都检测不出来。

### E2

- 位置：第 258 页
- 原文："Preregistration does not restrict the ability to conduct exploratory analyses; it merely allows the researcher and the reader to properly distinguish between analyses that were planned and exploratory." / "It is perfectly acceptable to simply pose a research question and describe exactly how you intend to answer it, and it is perfectly acceptable to publish a finding that did not conform to your original prediction."
- 要点：预注册不限制探索性分析，只是让人分清哪些分析是事先计划的、哪些是探索的。预注册时可以只提研究问题、写清怎么回答；结果和预测不符，也完全可以发表。
