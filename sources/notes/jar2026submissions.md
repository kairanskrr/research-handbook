# JAR 2026：Submissions（Basic Requirements 与 Submission Checklist）

- 资料：`jar2026submissions`，[投稿页](https://www.jar-online.net/en/submissions)，[基本要求 PDF](https://www.jar-online.net/sites/default/files/2025-12/JAR_submissions_basic-requirements_2026.pdf)，[投稿清单 PDF](https://www.jar-online.net/sites/default/files/2025-12/JAR_submission_checklist_2026.pdf)
- 版本：《艺术研究期刊》（Journal for Artistic Research，JAR，艺术研究学会 Society for Artistic Research 主办）2026 年版投稿要求和投稿清单，以及投稿页说明（2026-10-06 读取）。只代表这一家期刊的要求。

### E1

- 位置：Basic Requirements，Suitability a
- 原文："the exposition exposes artistic practice as research. This goes beyond simply documenting, describing, or writing about work. It engages with questions and claims about knowledge within practice."
- 要点：投稿要把艺术实践作为研究"展陈"出来，不只是记录、描述或谈论作品，而要处理关于实践中知识的问题和主张。

### E2

- 位置：Basic Requirements，Length
- 原文："We do not measure suitable length in words, as the journal also accommodates time-based media; instead, we require that a reader be able to access all essential aspects of the exposition in the period of an hour of investigation."
- 要点：JAR 不按字数衡量长度，而是要求读者能在大约一小时内看到展陈的全部要点。

### E3

- 位置：Basic Requirements，PhD Research
- 原文："Simply copying and pasting large sections of the thesis into a journal submission, without contextualization, will not be accepted."
- 要点：把博士论文大段照搬进投稿、不做重新定位，不会被接受。

### E4

- 位置：Basic Requirements，PhD Research
- 原文："We are open to unpublished material emerging during PhD research, while requiring authors to show sensitivity to the difference in readership and intention between thesis and exposition."
- 要点：JAR 接受博士研究中产生的未发表材料，但要求作者意识到论文和展陈的读者与目的不同。

### E5

- 位置：Basic Requirements，Design
- 原文："The design should support the exposition of practice as research and not simply \"style\" a page."
- 要点：页面设计要服务于把实践作为研究展陈出来，而不只是装饰页面。

### E6

- 位置：Submission checklist，Media files and images
- 原文："files must be uploaded into the RC rather than embedded using external links, which can break over time. Author's must ensure adequate image/sound/video quality for web display"
- 要点：媒体文件要上传到研究库，不要用会随时间失效的外部链接；图像、声音、视频质量要足够网页展示。

### E7

- 位置：Submission checklist，Media files and images
- 原文："Authors should select the appropriate mode of documentation and display for your research (e.g., video vs. images, slide show vs. single image) and test whether media files work on other computers."
- 要点：作者要为研究选择合适的记录和展示方式（视频还是图片、幻灯片还是单图），并在别的电脑上测试媒体文件能否正常播放。

### E8

- 位置：Submission checklist，Copyright
- 原文："Copyright: should be secured and noted as needed."
- 要点：版权要事先取得，并按需要注明。

### E9

- 位置：投稿页，General Information
- 原文："Our format for publishing artistic research, the ‘exposition’, invites authors to combine text, image, film, and audio material on expandable web pages, challenging the dominance of writing in traditional academic research."
- 要点：JAR 发表艺术研究的形式叫"展陈"（exposition），作者可以在可扩展的网页上组合文字、图像、影片和声音。

### E10

- 位置：投稿页，General Information
- 原文："At present, the editorial review process typically takes between eight months and a year from submission to publication."
- 要点：JAR 说明，目前从投稿到发表一般需要八个月到一年。
