# Kozlowski 2022：On the Topic of Substrate Scope

- 资料：`kozlowski2022substrate`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.orglett.2c03246)
- 版本：Organic Letters 2022, 24, 7247–7249，社论（Editorial）。作者时任《Organic Letters》主编（宾夕法尼亚大学化学系）。出版社网站免费阅读；出版社网站拦截脚本，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2025-07-07 保存的出版社网页。社论注明观点只代表作者本人，不一定代表美国化学会。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Increasingly, we have been seeing submissions with very large numbers of substrates (>50), and even a submission where the entire scope was relegated to the Supporting Information."
- 要点：编辑部越来越多收到底物超过 50 个的投稿，甚至有把整个底物范围都放进支持信息的。

### E2

- 位置：开头（作者 2022 年 9 月在推特上发起的投票）
- 原文："I engaged the organic chemistry community via Twitter on September 8 th of this year with a poll asking what a reasonable number of examples might be" / "there is clearly overwhelming support for <30 examples (88% of 1909 votes)."
- 要点：作者在推特上做的投票中，1909 票里 88% 支持少于 30 个例子。

### E3

- 位置：开头（投票结论）
- 原文："The overwhelming conclusion is that a small scope is acceptable provided that it is diverse , and the more so the better."
- 要点：结论是：底物范围小一点可以接受，前提是足够多样，越多样越好。

### E4

- 位置：Figure 2 的说明段
- 原文："The red substrates below the line would be far better choices for scope as they answer questions about sterics (2-methyl), functional group compatibility (4-hydroxy, 2-pyridyl, 3-pyridyl), and reactivity of the carbonyl (ketone, alkyl aldehyde, activated ketone)."
- 要点：好的底物各自回答一个问题：位阻、官能团兼容性、羰基反应性等。

### E5

- 位置：Figure 2 的说明段
- 原文："Screening of additives is an alternative means of assessing compatibility with different functional groups that can also reduce the amount of work associated with development of a new reaction method."
- 要点：筛选添加剂是评估官能团兼容性的另一种办法，还能减少工作量。

### E6

- 位置：Drug-like Structures or Not?
- 原文："I agree that such molecules can be helpful especially to our colleagues in pharma, but they should not be a prerequisite until such a time as they are freely available."
- 要点：药物分子等复杂底物有帮助，但在它们能免费获得之前，不应是发表的前提条件。

### E7

- 位置：Substrates That Do Not Work
- 原文："For the reasons above, it is hard, as an author, to put lots of negative examples into an article because of fear that reviewers will not be positive."
- 要点：作者因为担心审稿人不满，很难在论文里放很多负面例子。

### E8

- 位置：Substrates That Do Not Work
- 原文："I recommend including selected examples of low-yielding or nonyielding reactions in either the manuscript text or tables to provide a general sense of what does and does not work. Additional tables of ALL unsuccessful substrates can be added to the Supporting Information."
- 要点：建议在正文或表中放几个低产率或不反应的例子，让读者大致知道什么行、什么不行；所有失败底物可以列表放进支持信息。

### E9

- 位置：Reviewers
- 原文："We ask reviewers to keep in mind the above principles as well (more is not always better; negative results are not always bad) when they review submissions for Organic Letters."
- 要点：请审稿人记住：例子多不一定好，负面结果不一定坏。

### E10

- 位置：Reviewers（引用的审稿意见）
- 原文："Substrates should be chosen to highlight some important fact about a reaction, not just fill a table."
- 要点：选底物是为了说明反应的某个重要事实，不是为了填满表格。

### E11

- 位置：Reasonable Substrate Scope Examples
- 原文："For methods discoveries, we generally recommend 10–20 diverse substrates including some that are unreactive or give low yields."
- 要点：对方法学快报，《Organic Letters》一般建议 10–20 个多样的底物，其中包括一些不反应或低产率的。

### E12

- 位置：Reasonable Substrate Scope Examples
- 原文："Even so, there may be instances where additional substrates are desirable, so this recommendation is by no means meant to be definitive."
- 要点：这条建议不是硬性规定，有时多一些底物是有必要的。

### E13

- 位置：Reasonable Substrate Scope Examples
- 原文："There may be instances where an extensive substrate scope in a Full Article does indeed add value in terms of showing reproducibility/robustness, delineating the subtle interplay of multiple modifications, and further providing data to drive machine learning efforts."
- 要点：在完整论文里，大的底物范围有时确实有价值，例如展示可重复性和稳健性、为机器学习提供数据。

### E14

- 位置：Substrates That Do Not Work（引用的读者评论）
- 原文："I want to know what happened with the obvious substrate that is missing (like if all your aryls only have meta or para substituents, what did ortho do?)."
- 要点：读者想知道"明显该试却没出现"的底物怎样了，比如只有间位、对位取代时，邻位怎样。
