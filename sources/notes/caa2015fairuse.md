# 美国大学艺术协会 2015：Code of Best Practices in Fair Use for the Visual Arts

- 资料：`caa2015fairuse`，[原文 PDF](https://www.collegeart.org/pdf/fair-use/best-practices-fair-use-visual-arts.pdf)
- 版本：2015 年 2 月。美国大学艺术协会（College Art Association，CAA）发布，项目负责人 Patricia Aufderheide 和 Peter Jaszi（美国大学）。只适用于美国版权法下的合理使用。原文文本中首字下沉造成的拆分字母（如 "T he"）已合并。位置写小标题和页码。

### E1

- 位置：When the Code Does and Does Not Apply，第 6–7 页
- 原文："Fair use is part of US copyright law, and the Code applies to the activities of members of the visual arts community who use copyrighted works in the US in furtherance of their professional goals. [...] The Code does not apply to reproductions, distributions, | CODE OF BEST PRACTICES performances, and other uses outside the United States"
- 要点：合理使用是美国版权法的一部分；这份准则只适用于在美国的使用，不适用于美国以外。

### E2

- 位置：One: Analytic Writing，Principle，第 9 页
- 原文："In their analytic writing about art, scholars and other writers (and, by extension, their publishers) may invoke fair use to quote, excerpt, or reproduce copyrighted works, subject to certain limitations:"
- 要点：在分析性写作中，学者（及其出版社）可以援引合理使用来引用、节选或复制受版权保护的作品，但有限制条件。

### E3

- 位置：One: Analytic Writing，Limitations，第 9 页
- 原文："The writer’s use of the work, whether in part or in whole, should be justified by the analytic objective, and the user should be prepared to articulate that justification."
- 要点：使用（无论部分或全部）要由分析目的支撑，并准备好说明理由。

### E4

- 位置：One: Analytic Writing，Limitations，第 9 页
- 原文："The writer’s analytic objective should predominate over that of merely representing the work or works used."
- 要点：分析目的要压过"单纯展示作品"的目的。

### E5

- 位置：One: Analytic Writing，Limitations，第 9 页
- 原文："the size and resolution of the published reproduction should not exceed that appropriate to the analytic objective."
- 要点：图像的尺寸和分辨率不超过分析目的所需。

### E6

- 位置：One: Analytic Writing，Limitations，第 10 页
- 原文："The writing should provide attribution of the original work as is customary in the field, to the extent possible."
- 要点：按本领域惯例注明原作信息。

### E7

- 位置：Why the Code?，第 6 页
- 原文："reported that the practices of many professionals in the visual arts are constrained due to the pervasive perception that permissions to use third-party materials are required even where a confident exercise of fair use would be appropriate."
- 要点：CAA 委托的调查报告发现：很多视觉艺术从业者误以为一定要取得许可，即使本可以有把握地使用合理使用，因而工作受限。

### E8

- 位置：When the Code Does and Does Not Apply，第 7 页
- 原文："fair use cannot be invoked to avoid paying such fees."
- 要点：藏品方作为"唯一来源"收取的费用不基于版权，不能用合理使用来免除。

### E9

- 位置：When the Code Does and Does Not Apply，第 7 页
- 原文："Fair use and, therefore, the Code, also do not apply to the extent that a license agreement or other contract controls reproduction or other use."
- 要点：如果有许可协议或合同约束复制和使用，合理使用和本准则都不适用。

### E10

- 位置：Three: Making Art，Limitations，第 11 页
- 原文："Artists should avoid uses of existing copyrighted material that do not generate new artistic meaning, being aware that a change of medium, without more, may not meet this standard."
- 要点：艺术家使用他人作品要产生新的艺术意义；只是换个媒介可能不够。

### E11

- 位置：Three: Making Art，Limitations，第 11 页
- 原文："When copying another’s work, an artist should cite the source, whether in the new work or elsewhere (by means such as labeling or embedding), unless there is an articulable aesthetic basis for not doing so."
- 要点：复制他人作品时要注明来源，除非有说得出的美学理由。

### E12

- 位置：Message from the CAA President，第 2 页
- 原文："surveying nearly 12,000 CAA members on their use of copyrighted materials, conducting 100 interviews"
- 要点：准则的依据：调查近 12,000 名 CAA 会员、做了 100 次访谈。

### E13

- 位置：When the Code Does and Does Not Apply，第 7 页
- 原文："Bridgeman, however, does not on its face apply to still photographs of three-dimensional works, such as sculpture, architecture, and performance art."
- 要点：美国 Bridgeman 判例（平面作品的忠实翻拍不受版权保护）不当然适用于雕塑、建筑、表演艺术的照片。

### E14

- 位置：When the Code Does and Does Not Apply，第 7 页
- 原文："Similarly, the Code is not a guide to—and, again, is not needed for—the use of material that is in the public domain."
- 要点：公有领域作品不需要本准则。
