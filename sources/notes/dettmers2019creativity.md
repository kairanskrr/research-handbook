# Dettmers 2019：Creativity in Academia

- 资料：`dettmers2019creativity`，[原文](https://timdettmers.com/2019/09/03/creativity-in-academia/)
- 版本：作者博客，2019-09-03

### E1

- 位置：正文中段
- 原文："Expertise is important and a requirement for creativity. You need to be able to understand what are valid ideas and which are not."
- 要点：专业知识是创造力的前提：你得能分辨哪些想法成立、哪些不成立。

### E2

- 位置：正文中段
- 原文："They feel like failures if their ideas fail. But the process of hammering on ideas and not making any progress is the first part of creativity."
- 要点：很多研究者在想法失败时觉得自己失败了；但反复琢磨一个想法却毫无进展，本身就是创造过程的第一阶段。

### E3

- 位置：正文中段
- 原文："The second step is often abandoning the idea for some time." / "But this step can be a critical element of creativity."
- 要点：第二步常常是暂时放下这个想法，这一步可能正是创造的关键。

### E4

- 位置：正文中段
- 原文："Instead, it is much more effective to work on many ideas."
- 要点：同时手里有多个想法，比只盯着一个更有效。

### E5

- 位置：正文中段
- 原文："I think to have a sane creative process, it is essential to acknowledge and even embrace this long-winded exhausting struggle with multiple rounds of failure as an essential part of creativity."
- 要点：要有健康的创造过程，就要接受、甚至拥抱漫长而反复失败的挣扎，把它当作创造的一部分。
