# WMA 2024：Declaration of Helsinki – Ethical Principles for Medical Research Involving Human Participants

- 资料：`wma2024helsinki`，[原文](https://www.wma.net/policies-post/wma-declaration-of-helsinki/)
- 版本：世界医学会（World Medical Association，WMA）第 75 届大会 2024 年 10 月修订版。网页版没有段落编号，位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："The World Medical Association (WMA) has developed the Declaration of Helsinki as a statement of ethical principles for medical research involving human participants, including research using identifiable human material or data."
- 要点：《赫尔辛基宣言》是涉及人的医学研究的伦理原则，也包括使用可识别的人体材料或数据的研究。

### E2

- 位置：General Principles
- 原文："These purposes can never take precedence over the rights and interests of individual research participants."
- 要点：研究目的永远不能凌驾于受试者个人的权利和利益之上。

### E3

- 位置：General Principles
- 原文："Physicians who combine medical research with medical care should involve their patients in research only to the extent that this is justified by its potential preventive, diagnostic or therapeutic value and if the physician has good reason to believe that participation in the research will not adversely affect the health of the patients who serve as research participants."
- 要点：既看病又做研究的医生，只有在研究有潜在的预防、诊断或治疗价值，且有充分理由相信参加研究不会损害病人健康时，才能让自己的病人参加研究。

### E4

- 位置：Research Ethics Committees
- 原文："The protocol must be submitted for consideration, comment, guidance, and approval to the concerned research ethics committee before the research begins."
- 要点：研究方案必须在研究开始前交给研究伦理委员会审查批准。

### E5

- 位置：Research Ethics Committees
- 原文："When collaborative research is performed internationally, the research protocol must be approved by research ethics committees in both the sponsoring and host countries."
- 要点：跨国合作研究，发起国和实施国的伦理委员会都要批准。

### E6

- 位置：Free and Informed Consent
- 原文："each potential participant must be adequately informed in plain language of the aims, methods, anticipated benefits and potential risks and burdens, qualifications of the researcher, sources of funding, any potential conflicts of interest"
- 要点：要用通俗的语言告诉每位可能的受试者研究目的、方法、预期获益、风险和负担、研究者资质、资金来源、利益冲突等。

### E7

- 位置：Free and Informed Consent
- 原文："the physician or another qualified individual must then seek the potential participant’s freely given informed consent, formally documented on paper or electronically."
- 要点：确认对方理解后，取得自愿的知情同意，并以纸质或电子方式正式记录。

### E8

- 位置：Free and Informed Consent
- 原文："When seeking informed consent for participation in research the physician or other researcher must be particularly cautious if the potential participant is in a dependent relationship with them or may consent under duress. In such situations, the informed consent must be sought by an appropriately qualified individual who is independent of this relationship."
- 要点：如果可能的受试者和医生之间有依赖关系，或可能在压力下同意，要特别小心；这时知情同意应由与这层关系无关的合格人员去取得。

### E9

- 位置：Research Registration, Publication, and Dissemination of Results
- 原文："Medical research involving human participants must be registered in a publicly accessible database before recruitment of the first participant."
- 要点：涉及人的医学研究，必须在招募第一位受试者之前在公开数据库登记。

### E10

- 位置：Research Registration, Publication, and Dissemination of Results
- 原文："Negative and inconclusive as well as positive results must be published or otherwise made publicly available. Sources of funding, institutional affiliations, and conflicts of interest must be declared in the publication."
- 要点：阴性、不确定的结果和阳性结果一样都要公开；论文中要声明资金来源、所属机构和利益冲突。

### E11

- 位置：Free and Informed Consent
- 原文："Physicians or other qualified individuals must obtain free and informed consent from research participants for the collection, processing, storage, and foreseeable secondary use of biological material and identifiable or re-identifiable data."
- 要点：采集、处理、保存生物材料和可识别（或可重新识别）的数据，以及可预见的二次使用，都要取得自愿的知情同意。

### E12

- 位置：Free and Informed Consent
- 原文："A research ethics committee must approve the establishment and monitor ongoing use of such databases and biobanks."
- 要点：建立这类数据库和生物样本库要经研究伦理委员会批准，后续使用也要受它监督。

### E13

- 位置：Free and Informed Consent
- 原文："Where consent is impossible or impracticable to obtain, secondary research on stored data or biological material may be done only after consideration and approval of a research ethics committee."
- 要点：无法或难以取得同意时，用已保存的数据或生物材料做二次研究，必须先经研究伦理委员会审查批准。
