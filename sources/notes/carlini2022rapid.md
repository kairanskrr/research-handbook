# Carlini 2022：Rapid Iteration in Machine Learning Research

- 资料：`carlini2022rapid`，[原文](https://nicholas.carlini.com/writing/2022/rapid-iteration-machine-learning-research.html)
- 版本：作者主页博客，2022-06-19，2026-10-05 读取。

### E1

- 位置：开头段
- 原文："I can only do good research when I can quickly iterate on ideas."
- 要点：作者认为只有能快速迭代想法时，才能做出好研究。

### E2

- 位置："(Machine learning) research does not support rapid iteration" 一节
- 原文："Any time where it takes longer than ~a second for a script to start giving useful output, I find that my ability to be productive starts to drop off."
- 要点：等待结果的时间一长，效率就明显下降。

### E3

- 位置："(Machine learning) research does not support rapid iteration" 一节
- 原文："So I took a few days out of my PhD research time and wrote a tool to make my workflow quite a bit faster."
- 要点：作者专门花几天时间做工具来缩短等待，此后多年一直受益。
