# Hopewell et al. 2025：CONSORT 2025 explanation and elaboration: updated guideline for reporting randomised trials

- 资料：`hopewell2025consortee`，[BMJ](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2024-081124)；开放获取全文见 [PubMed Central PMC11995452](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11995452/)
- 版本：BMJ 2025;389:e081124。CONSORT 2025 声明的配套解释文章，逐项说明每个清单条目的含义、理由和实证依据。网页版无页码，位置写清单条目编号。

### E1

- 位置：Item 17a，Box 5（Treatment allocation in trials）
- 原文："Firstly, when properly implemented, it eliminates selection bias, balancing both known and unknown prognostic factors, in the assignment of treatments."
- 要点：随机分配做得正确时，能消除分组时的选择偏倚，使已知和未知的预后因素在组间均衡。

### E2

- 位置：Item 9（Trial design），Explanation
- 原文："the main alternative designs are multi-arm parallel, crossover, cluster, and factorial designs, with each having their own specific CONSORT extensions"
- 要点：除两组平行设计外，主要的其他设计有多臂平行、交叉、整群和析因设计，各有专门的 CONSORT 扩展版。

### E3

- 位置：Item 7（Specific objectives），Explanation
- 原文："The PICO framework, which requires defining the patient population (P); the experimental intervention (I); the comparator intervention or condition (C); and the outcome or outcomes (O) of interest, has been proposed to help define the research question."
- 要点：PICO 框架要求写清病人人群（P）、试验干预（I）、对照干预或条件（C）和关注的结局（O），用来帮助界定研究问题。

### E4

- 位置：Item 7，Explanation
- 原文："The objectives should be phrased using neutral wording (eg, “to compare the effect of treatment A versus treatment B on outcome X for persons with condition Y”) rather than in terms of a particular direction of effect."
- 要点：研究目的要用中性措辞（比较 A 和 B 对 Y 病人结局 X 的效果），不要预设效果的方向。

### E5

- 位置：Item 7，Explanation
- 原文："Authors should clarify whether the aim is to establish superiority of the experimental intervention, or non-inferiority or equivalence, as compared with the comparator intervention."
- 要点：要说清目的是证明试验干预更优（优效），还是不差于（非劣效）或等效于对照干预。

### E6

- 位置：Item 7，Explanation
- 原文："Finally, trials can be designed to study the effect of the experimental intervention under different conditions, often described on a spectrum from ideal conditions (explanatory trial) to standard clinical care conditions (pragmatic trial)."
- 要点：试验可以在不同条件下研究干预效果，从理想条件（解释性试验）到常规临床条件（实用性试验）是一条连续谱。

### E7

- 位置：Item 14（Outcomes），Explanation
- 原文："The primary outcome is the prespecified outcome considered to be of greatest importance to relevant stakeholders (such as patients, policy makers, clinicians, and funders) and should be the one used in the sample size calculation (item 16)."
- 要点：主要结局是事先规定、对病人、决策者、医生、资助方等最重要的结局，样本量也应按它来算。

### E8

- 位置：Item 14，Explanation
- 原文："Most trials have a single primary outcome. Having several primary outcomes can incur potential problems of interpretation associated with multiplicity of analyses"
- 要点：大多数试验只有一个主要结局；设多个主要结局会带来多重分析的解释问题。

### E9

- 位置：Item 14，Explanation
- 原文："For each outcome, it is important to detail: (1) the specific measurement variable" [...] "(2) the specific participant level analysis metric" [...] "(3) the method of aggregation" [...] "and (4) the measurement time point of interest for analysis."
- 要点：每个结局都要写清四件事：具体测量什么变量、每个受试者用什么形式的数据分析（如相对基线的变化）、组内怎样汇总（如均值、比例）、在哪个时间点分析。

### E10

- 位置：Item 14，Explanation
- 原文："A core outcome set is an agreed standardised set of outcomes that should be measured and reported, as a minimum, in all clinical trials in specific areas of health or health care."
- 要点：核心结局集是某个领域所有临床试验至少都应测量和报告的一组公认标准结局。

### E11

- 位置：Item 7（Specific objectives），Explanation
- 原文："Trials whose primary objective is to evaluate benefits of an intervention may not be powered to detect harms, but authors should still report whether they considered harms outcomes when planning the trial."
- 要点：以评价获益为主要目的的试验，可能没有足够把握度检测伤害，但仍应报告计划时是否考虑了伤害结局。

### E12

- 位置：Item 14，Explanation
- 原文："Evidence shows important discrepancies between the outcome reported in the registry or protocol and outcomes reported in final publications, frequently in favour of statistically significant results."
- 要点：有证据表明，登记或方案里的结局与最终论文报告的结局常常不一致，而且常常偏向统计学显著的结果。

### E13

- 位置：Item 10（Changes to trial），Explanation
- 原文："A comparison of protocols and publications of 102 randomised trials found that 62% of trial reports had at least one primary outcome that was changed, introduced, or omitted compared with the protocol."
- 要点：一项比较 102 个随机试验方案与论文的研究发现，62% 的论文至少有一个主要结局与方案相比被改动、新增或删掉。

### E14

- 位置：Item 16a（Sample size），Explanation
- 原文："A trial should therefore be sufficiently large to have a high probability (power) of identifying a clinically important difference of a prespecified size that meets a criterion of statistical significance, if such a difference exists."
- 要点：试验要足够大，使得如果真有一个事先规定大小的、有临床意义的差异，试验有很高的概率（把握度）把它检测出来。

### E15

- 位置：Item 16a，Explanation
- 原文："Elements of the sample size calculation that need to be specified are the primary outcome (and time point) on which the calculation was based" [...] "the statistical test; the α (type I error) value and whether it is two sided; the statistical power (or the β (type II error) value); and the resulting target sample size per trial group"
- 要点：样本量计算要写明：依据哪个主要结局（和时间点）、各组预期结局值（即目标差异）、统计检验、α 值及是否双侧、把握度，以及每组的目标样本量。

### E16

- 位置：Item 16a，Explanation
- 原文："Details should be given of any inflation of the sample size made for attrition or non-adherence during the study."
- 要点：为失访或不依从而放大样本量的，要写明细节。

### E17

- 位置：Item 16a，Explanation
- 原文："larger sample sizes are needed to detect smaller differences. Moreover, the inverse relationship is not linear: very small differences require enormous sample sizes to have good power to detect."
- 要点：差异越小，需要的样本越大；而且不是线性关系，很小的差异需要极大的样本。

### E18

- 位置：Item 16a，Explanation
- 原文："There is no value in conducting and reporting a post hoc calculation of statistical power using the results of a trial, for example, as a pretext to explain non-significant findings; this may even mislead and confuse readers."
- 要点：用试验结果事后计算把握度没有价值（比如拿来解释不显著的结果），还可能误导读者。

### E19

- 位置：Item 17a，Explanation
- 原文："Authors should specify the method of sequence generation, such as a random-number table or a computerised random number generator."
- 要点：要写明序列是怎样生成的，例如随机数字表或计算机随机数生成器。

### E20

- 位置：Item 17a，Explanation
- 原文："“random” is sometimes used inappropriately in the literature to describe trials in which non-random, “deterministic” allocation methods were used, such as alternation, hospital numbers, or date of birth." / "Even the term “quasi-random” is unacceptable for describing such trials."
- 要点：交替分配、按住院号或出生日期分配都不是随机方法，不能称为"随机"，连"准随机"也不能用。

### E21

- 位置：Item 17b（Type of randomisation），Explanation
- 原文："In trials of several hundred participants or more, simple randomisation can usually be trusted to generate similar numbers in the two trial groups" [...] "For smaller trials of fewer than around 200 participants," [...] "some form of restricted randomisation procedure to help achieve balance between groups in size or characteristics may be useful"
- 要点：几百人以上的试验，简单随机化通常就能让两组人数相近；不到约 200 人的小试验，用某种限制性随机化帮助组间均衡可能有用。

### E22

- 位置：Item 17b，Box 6（Randomisation and minimisation）
- 原文："Although the order of interventions varies randomly within each block, a person running the trial could deduce some of the next treatment allocations if they discovered the block size." / "Blinding the interventions, using larger block sizes, and randomly varying the block size can ameliorate this problem."
- 要点：区组随机化中，知道区组大小的人能猜出接下来的部分分配；对干预设盲、用更大的区组、随机变换区组大小可以缓解。

### E23

- 位置：Item 17b，Box 6
- 原文："Stratification by centre is common in multicentre trials. Stratification requires some form of restriction, such as blocking within strata. Stratification without some form of restriction is ineffective."
- 要点：多中心试验常按中心分层；分层必须配合某种限制（如层内区组），否则无效。

### E24

- 位置：Item 17b，Box 6
- 原文："Minimisation offers the only acceptable alternative to randomisation, and some have argued that it is superior."
- 要点：最小化法是随机化唯一可接受的替代方法。

### E25

- 位置：Item 17a，Explanation
- 原文："Successful randomisation hinges on two steps: generation of an unpredictable allocation sequence and concealment of this sequence from the investigators enrolling participants (item 18)."
- 要点：随机化成功取决于两步：生成不可预测的分配序列，并对负责入组的研究者隐藏这个序列。

### E26

- 位置：Item 18（Allocation concealment），Explanation
- 原文："The decision to accept or reject a participant should be made, and informed consent should be obtained from the participant, in ignorance of the next assignment in the sequence."
- 要点：决定是否纳入一位受试者、取得其知情同意时，都应不知道下一个分配是什么。

### E27

- 位置：Item 18，Explanation
- 原文："Centralised or third party assignment is especially desirable." / "Use of a pharmacy or central computer or telephone randomisation system are common techniques."
- 要点：集中分配或第三方分配最理想；常用药房、中央计算机或电话随机系统。

### E28

- 位置：Item 18，Explanation
- 原文："Enclosing assignments in sequentially numbered, opaque, sealed envelopes can be a good allocation concealment mechanism if it is developed and monitored diligently." / "Investigators should ensure that the envelopes are opaque when held to the light, and are opened sequentially and only after the participant’s name and other details are written on the appropriate sequentially numbered sealed envelope."
- 要点：按顺序编号的不透光密封信封，认真制作和监督时可以是好的隐藏方法；要保证对着光看不透，按顺序拆，并且先在信封上写好受试者姓名等信息再拆。

### E29

- 位置：Item 19（Implementation），Explanation
- 原文："investigators should strive for complete separation of the people involved with generation and allocation concealment from the people who implement assignments."
- 要点：负责生成序列和做分配隐藏的人，应与执行分配的人完全分开。

### E30

- 位置：Item 18，Explanation
- 原文："Trials in which the allocation sequence had been inadequately or unclearly concealed yielded larger estimates of treatment effects than did trials in which authors reported adequate allocation concealment."
- 要点：分配隐藏不充分或不清楚的试验，估计出的治疗效果比隐藏充分的试验更大。

### E31

- 位置：Item 18，Explanation
- 原文："Allocation concealment should not be confused with blinding (item 20). Allocation concealment seeks to prevent selection bias (box 5), protects the assignment sequence before and until allocation, and can always be successfully implemented." / "In contrast, blinding seeks to prevent ascertainment bias, protects the sequence after allocation, and cannot always be implemented."
- 要点：分配隐藏不同于盲法：分配隐藏防选择偏倚，保护分配之前的序列，总能做到；盲法防测量偏倚，保护分配之后的信息，不一定能做到。

### E32

- 位置：Item 20a（Blinding），Explanation
- 原文："The term “blinding” (masking) refers to withholding information about the assigned interventions from people involved in the trial who may potentially be influenced by this knowledge."
- 要点：盲法是对试验中可能受影响的人隐瞒分配到的干预。

### E33

- 位置：Item 20a，Explanation
- 原文："Unblinded outcome assessors may differentially assess subjective outcomes, and unblinded data analysts may introduce bias through the choice of analytical strategies, such as the selection of favourable time points or outcomes and by decisions to remove patients from the analyses."
- 要点：没设盲的结局评估者可能对主观结局有差别地评估；没设盲的数据分析者可能通过挑选有利的时间点、结局或剔除病人引入偏倚。

### E34

- 位置：Item 20a，Explanation
- 原文："In certain trials, especially surgical trials, blinding of participants and healthcare providers is often difficult or impossible, but blinding of data collectors and outcome assessors for both benefits and harms is often achievable and recommended."
- 要点：手术等试验常常无法对受试者和医护设盲，但对数据收集者和结局评估者设盲往往做得到，也推荐这样做。

### E35

- 位置：Item 20b，Explanation
- 原文："they should provide information about the mechanism used to establish blinding (eg, placebo identical to the experimental intervention, sham intervention, sham surgery)."
- 要点：要说明设盲的手段，例如与试验干预外观一致的安慰剂、假干预、假手术。

### E36

- 位置：Item 20b，Explanation
- 原文："Use of a fixed code will increase the risk of unblinding because unblinding a participant could result in unblinding several or all trial participants."
- 要点：用固定编码（如 A=组 1）会增加破盲风险，因为揭开一个人就可能揭开多人甚至所有人。

### E37

- 位置：Item 20a，Box 7（Blinding terminology）
- 原文："a study of 200 randomised trials reported as double blind demonstrated 18 different combinations of groups actually blinded when the authors of these trials were surveyed" / "Authors should instead explicitly report the blinding status of the people involved for whom blinding may influence the validity of a trial."
- 要点：一项调查了 200 个自称"双盲"试验作者的研究发现，实际设盲的人群有 18 种不同组合；所以应明确写出每类相关人员是否设盲。

### E38

- 位置：Item 3（Protocol and statistical analysis plan），Explanation
- 原文："The protocol should be signed off by the trial steering committee before the allocation of any participants and data collection. Similarly, the full statistical analysis plan should be signed off by the trial steering committee before the dataset is closed for analysis."
- 要点：方案应在分配任何受试者、收集数据之前由试验指导委员会签署定稿；完整的统计分析计划应在数据库锁定、开始分析前签署定稿。

### E39

- 位置：Item 16b（Interim analyses and stopping），Explanation
- 原文："If an intervention demonstrates exceptional efficacy, the study might require early termination on ethical grounds. To mitigate this concern, assessing results as data accumulates is advisable, ideally through an independent data monitoring committee (DMC), sometimes referred to as a data and safety monitoring board (DSMB)."
- 要点：干预效果特别突出时，出于伦理可能要提前终止试验；所以宜在数据积累过程中评估结果，最好由独立的数据监查委员会来做。

### E40

- 位置：Item 16b，Explanation
- 原文："Further to stopping early for efficacy, interim analyses can be used to evaluate (1) futility, to assess whether a trial is likely to meet its objectives; or (2) safety, to assess whether there is evidence for increased risk of harms"
- 要点：期中分析除了因有效而提前停止，还可以评估无效（试验是否可能达到目的）和安全性（是否有伤害风险增加的证据）。

### E41

- 位置：Item 16b，Explanation
- 原文："Various group sequential statistical approaches exist to adjust for multiple looks (ie, analyses) at the data, and these should be predetermined in the trial protocol"
- 要点：有多种成组序贯方法可以校正多次查看数据，这些方法应事先写进试验方案。

### E42

- 位置：Item 10，Explanation
- 原文："Authors should report the nature and timing of protocol changes because changes made at different times (eg, before or after breaking the blinding) might be associated with different risks of bias."
- 要点：要报告方案改动的内容和时间，因为在不同时间（如揭盲前或揭盲后）做的改动，偏倚风险不同。

### E43

- 位置：Item 16b，Explanation
- 原文："For instance, examining data from a trial at five interim analyses using a P value of 0.05 would elevate the overall false-positive rate closer to 19% rather than the expected 5%."
- 要点：做五次期中分析、每次都用 P=0.05 作标准，总的假阳性率会升到接近 19%，而不是预期的 5%。

### E44

- 位置：Item 21b（Analysis population），Explanation
- 原文："To preserve the benefits of randomisation, all randomised participants are included in the analysis and retained in the group to which they were allocated. Meeting these two conditions defines an intention-to-treat analysis—which is widely recommended as the preferred analysis strategy."
- 要点：为保住随机化的好处，所有随机化的受试者都纳入分析，并留在原分配组；满足这两点就是意向性分析，普遍推荐作为首选。

### E45

- 位置：Item 21b，Explanation
- 原文："Excluding participants may compromise the randomisation and lead to biased estimates of treatment effects if appropriate methods are not used." / "authors should clearly define which participants are included in each analysis and in which intervention group and avoid terms such as “modified intention-to-treat” or “per protocol” analysis."
- 要点：剔除受试者可能破坏随机化、使效果估计有偏；要写清每项分析纳入了谁、算在哪组，不要只用"改良意向性分析""符合方案分析"这类标签。

### E46

- 位置：Item 21c（Missing data），Explanation
- 原文："While a few missing outcomes will not cause a problem, many trials have more than 10% of randomised patients with missing outcomes."
- 要点：少量缺失没问题，但很多试验超过 10% 的随机化病人缺失结局。

### E47

- 位置：Item 21b，Explanation
- 原文："Striving for intention-to-treat analysis by imputing values for participants with missing outcomes may lead to use of inadequate methods such as last observation carried forward."
- 要点：为了凑成意向性分析去填补缺失结局，可能用到"末次观测值结转"这类不恰当的方法。

### E48

- 位置：Item 21a（Statistical methods），Explanation
- 原文："it is often advisable to make adjustments for stratification variables" / "In the context of randomised trials, the decision to make adjustments should not be based on whether there are baseline covariates that are statistically significantly different between randomised groups."
- 要点：通常宜对分层变量做校正；是否校正某个协变量，不应根据它在组间是否有显著差异来决定。

### E49

- 位置：Item 21d（Additional analyses），Explanation
- 原文："It is incorrect to infer a subgroup effect (interaction) from one significant (in one subgroup) and one non-significant P value (in another subgroup)." / "Post hoc subgroup comparisons (analyses done after looking at the data) are especially likely not to be confirmed by further studies."
- 要点：一个亚组显著、另一个不显著，不能据此说存在亚组差异；看过数据后才做的亚组比较，尤其难被后续研究证实。

### E50

- 位置：Item 25（Baseline data），Explanation
- 原文："Significance testing of baseline differences is not recommended and should not be reported." / "providing the randomisation has not been subverted or comprised, any differences are caused by chance."
- 要点：不建议对基线差异做显著性检验，也不应报告；只要随机化没被破坏，基线差异都是偶然造成的。

### E51

- 位置：Item 26，Explanation
- 原文："Results should be reported for all planned primary and secondary outcomes and at each time point, not just for analyses that were statistically significant or thought to be interesting."
- 要点：所有计划的主要和次要结局、每个时间点都要报告，不能只报显著或"有意思"的。

### E52

- 位置：Item 26（Outcomes and estimation），Explanation
- 原文："For all outcomes, authors should provide a CI to indicate the precision (uncertainty) of the estimated effect size." / "results should not be reported solely as P values."
- 要点：所有结局都要给出效应量的置信区间，不要只报 P 值。

### E53

- 位置：Item 26，Explanation
- 原文："When the primary outcome is binary, both the relative effect (risk ratio (relative risk) or odds ratio), and the absolute effect (risk difference) should be reported (with CIs)"
- 要点：主要结局是二分类时，相对效应（风险比或比值比）和绝对效应（风险差）都要报告，并附置信区间。

### E54

- 位置：Item 22a（Participant flow），Explanation
- 原文："Erroneous conclusions can be reached if participants are excluded from analysis, and imbalances in such omissions between groups may be especially indicative of bias."
- 要点：把受试者排除出分析可能得出错误结论；两组排除人数不均衡尤其提示偏倚。

### E55

- 位置：Item 29（Interpretation），Explanation
- 原文："some discussion sections can be overly optimistic when discussing the trial findings (interpretation bias; spin)."
- 要点：有些讨论部分对结果过分乐观，即解释偏倚（"美化"）。

### E56

- 位置：Item 20a，Explanation
- 原文："Regardless of whether blinding is possible, authors can and should always state who was blinded"
- 要点：不管能不能设盲，都应写明谁被设盲了。

### E57

- 位置：Item 2，Explanation（引用世界卫生组织 2015 年关于公开试验结果的声明）
- 原文："the key outcomes are to be made publicly available within 12 months of study completion by posting to the results section of the primary clinical trial registry."
- 要点：世界卫生组织 2015 年的声明要求，研究结束后 12 个月内把主要结果发布到一级试验登记库的结果栏目。

### E58

- 位置：Item 20a，Explanation
- 原文："In pragmatic trials (trials that try to make the experience as close as real life so as to understand real world effectiveness), blinding of participants and healthcare providers would decrease the pragmatism of the trials, since patients in real life are not blinded."
- 要点：实用性试验对受试者和医护设盲会降低其贴近真实世界的程度，因为现实中病人不设盲。

### E59

- 位置：Item 20a，Explanation
- 原文："For example, blinding of data collectors and outcome assessors is unlikely to matter for objective outcomes, such as death from any cause."
- 要点：对全因死亡这类客观结局，数据收集者和评估者是否设盲不太可能有影响。

### E60

- 位置：Item 20b，Explanation
- 原文："Thus, given the uncertainty this type of information provides, the usefulness of tests of blinding has been questioned."
- 要点：试验结束时询问受试者猜测分组的"盲法检验"，其用处受到质疑。
