# AMS 2006：The Culture of Research and Scholarship in Mathematics: Rates of Publication

- 资料：`amsculture2006rates`，[原文](https://www.ams.org/profession/leaders/culture/CultureStatement06.pdf)（ams.org 拦截脚本时用 [Internet Archive 快照](http://web.archive.org/web/20240612181818/http://www.ams.org/profession/leaders/culture/CultureStatement06.pdf)）
- 版本：美国数学会职业委员会 2006 年声明，一页 PDF，2026-10-06 读取。

### E1

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Mathematicians tend to publish at rates that are modest compared to some other sciences. The majority of mathematical research is published in refereed research journals rather than conference proceedings or books."
- 要点：和一些其他科学相比，数学家发表得不多；大部分数学研究发表在有审稿的研究期刊上，而不是会议论文集或书。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："And, since an article typically represents a mature treatise on a mathematical question, and since mathematics research is not considered time-sensitive, delays in publication are common."
- 要点：一篇数学论文通常是对一个问题的成熟论述，数学研究也不被认为有时效性，所以发表延迟很常见。

### E3

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："A study of the 40 mathematicians winning Sloan Fellowships in 2005-2006 shows that 70% published an average of two or fewer articles per year in the five years preceding their award."
- 要点：2005–2006 年获斯隆研究奖的 40 位数学家中，70% 在获奖前五年平均每年发表不超过两篇。

### E4

- 位置：第 5 段
- 原文："When judging the work of most mathematicians, the key measure of value for a research program is the quality of publications rather than rate."
- 要点：评价数学家的工作，关键是论文质量而不是发表速度。

### E5

- 位置：第 4 段
- 原文："In fact, of all items covered by Mathematical Reviews in the years 2001-2005, fully 80% were from refereed journals."
- 要点：2001–2005 年《数学评论》（Mathematical Reviews）收录的条目中，80% 来自有审稿的期刊。
