# Björk & Solomon 2013：The publishing delay in scholarly peer-reviewed journals

- 资料：`bjork2013publishing`，[原文](https://web.archive.org/web/20131008075840/http://openaccesspublishing.org/oa11/article.pdf)
- 版本：作者自存稿（作者网站 openaccesspublishing.org，经 Internet Archive 2013-10-08 存档读取）；正式发表于 Journal of Informetrics 7(4): 914–923, 2013，DOI 10.1016/j.joi.2013.09.001。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Using a stratified random sample we studied average publishing delays in 2700 papers published in 135 journals sampled from the Scopus citation index."
- 要点：研究从 Scopus 引文数据库分层随机抽了 135 本期刊的 2700 篇论文，统计平均出版周期。

### E2

- 位置：§2 Methods
- 原文："We decided to merge some of these latter groups based on our subjective assumption of similarity in reviewing culture and publication speed resulting in nine groups. These include arts/humanities, biomedicine, business/economics, chemistry, earth science, engineering, mathematics, physics, and social sciences." / "we selected 20 articles working backward from the last article published in 2012."
- 要点：期刊被作者主观合并成九组（含工程、地球科学、社会科学），没有更细的学科；每本期刊取 2012 年最后刊出的 20 篇往前数。

### E3

- 位置：§3 Results
- 原文："The first time period reflects the delay due to the peer review and revision process used by a journal and the second the length of the publishing process, backlog due to publication page limits and potentially other factors."
- 要点："投稿到录用"反映审稿和修改的时间，"录用到刊出"反映出版流程、版面限制造成的积压等花的时间。

### E4

- 位置：附录表 Publication Time in Months by Discipline，Engineering 行
- 原文："Engineering Mean 5.00 4.30"
- 要点：工程组：投稿到录用平均 5.00 个月，录用到刊出平均 4.30 个月（表中投稿到刊出合计 9.30 个月）。

### E5

- 位置：§4 Discussion
- 原文："There was also considerably more variation among journals within a discipline and size group than among disciplines and size groups."
- 要点：同一学科组、同一规模组内各期刊之间的差别，比学科组之间的差别大得多。

### E6

- 位置：附录表 Publication Time in Months by Discipline，Social Science 行
- 原文："Social Science Mean 6.17 7.93"
- 要点：社会科学组：投稿到录用平均 6.17 个月，录用到刊出平均 7.93 个月（表中投稿到刊出合计 14.10 个月）。
