# Nanda 2025：My Research Process: Key Mindsets - Truth-Seeking, Prioritisation, Moving Fast

- 资料：`nanda2025mindsets`，[原文](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/cbBwwm4jW6AZctymL)
- 版本：AI Alignment Forum 帖子，2025-04-27，2026-10-05 读取。位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Prioritisation
- 原文："You need to be ruthless about dropping less promising directions where necessary. But beware switching costs - if you switch all the time without exploring anything properly you’ll learn nothing!"
- 要点：该放弃的方向要果断放弃，但也要警惕切换成本：总在换方向、什么都没好好探索，就什么也学不到。

### E2

- 位置：Prioritisation
- 原文："I like to explicitly write goals down and regularly check in that they’re being achieved"
- 要点：把目标明确写下来，定期检查是否在朝它推进。

### E3

- 位置：Prioritisation
- 原文："Goal: What is the overall north star of the project? (generally measured in months) [...] Sub-goal: What is my current bit of the project working towards (measured in weeks) [...] Objective: What is the concrete short-term outcome I am aiming for right now (measured in days, e.g. 1 week)"
- 要点：三层目标：项目总目标（以月计）、当前子目标（以周计）、眼下的具体目标（以天计）。

### E4

- 位置：Prioritisation
- 原文："I recommend actually writing a plan, and estimate how long each step will take, at least for the current research stage you’re in. You don’t need to take it very seriously, and you’ll totally deviate a ton."
- 要点：至少为当前阶段写一份计划并估计每步用时；计划不必死守，偏离很正常，但写计划能逼你把项目想清楚。

### E5

- 位置：Prioritisation
- 原文："If you feel stuck, set a 5 minute timer and brainstorm possible things you could do!"
- 要点：卡住时，定 5 分钟计时，列出所有可以做的事。

### E6

- 位置：Prioritisation
- 原文："Prioritising and executing are different mental modes and should not be done simultaneously."
- 要点：排优先级和埋头执行是两种状态，不要同时进行；要分别留出反思时间和执行时间。

### E7

- 位置：Prioritisation
- 原文："Having a weekly review can be incredibly useful - where you zoom out and check in on what’s going on, any current issues, etc."
- 要点：每周做一次回顾，跳出细节检查进展和问题。

### E8

- 位置：Prioritisation
- 原文："What’s consumed the most time recently? [...] What’s blocked me? [...] What mistakes have I made, and how could I systematically change my approach so it doesn’t happen again in future?"
- 要点：周回顾可用的问题：最近什么最花时间？什么卡住了我？犯了什么错，怎样系统地避免再犯？

### E9

- 位置：Prioritisation
- 原文："Some get stuck in rabbit holes and need to get better at moving on. Others get caught in analysis paralysis and never do anything, because they’re always waiting for the (non-existent) perfect opportunity."
- 要点：两种相反的毛病：有人钻牛角尖不肯放手，有人想太多迟迟不动手。先认清自己偏向哪一种。

### E10

- 位置：Moving Fast
- 原文："Definition: A feedback loop is the process from having an experiment idea and to results. Tight feedback loops are when the time taken is short."
- 要点：反馈回路指从有了实验想法到看到结果的过程；这段时间越短，回路越紧。

### E11

- 位置：Moving Fast
- 原文："It is far better to do a quick and dirty experiment to get some preliminary signs of life than an extremely long and expensive experiment that will produce conclusive data but only after weeks of work."
- 要点：先做快而粗糙的实验看到初步迹象，远好于花几周做一个昂贵但结论明确的实验。

### E12

- 位置：Moving Fast
- 原文："Thus, a key question to ask yourself is: if this direction is doomed, how could I discover this as fast as humanly possible?"
- 要点：快速失败：问自己"如果这个方向注定不行，怎样最快发现？"

### E13

- 位置：Moving Fast
- 原文："One thing that might be helpful is to log how you spend your time and then reflect on it, and ways you might be able to go faster next time."
- 要点：记录时间花在哪里，事后复盘下次如何更快。

### E14

- 位置：Moving Fast
- 原文："It’s easy to e.g. be sloppy in the name of speed and introduce many bugs that cost you time in the long-run."
- 要点：作者也承认求快的代价：以速度为名变得马虎，会引入错误，长期反而更费时间。

### E15

- 位置：Prioritisation
- 原文："But even then, feel free to deviate - mentors typically have good research priors, but you know way more about your specific problem than them, which can be enough to make better decisions than even a very senior researcher"
- 要点：导师的研究直觉通常很好，但你对自己这个具体问题了解得更多，有时足以做出比资深研究者更好的决定。

### E16

- 位置：Truth Seeking
- 原文："Asking another researcher, especially a mentor is a great source of perspective"
- 要点：找别的研究者、特别是导师谈，是获得新视角、发现其他解释的好办法。
