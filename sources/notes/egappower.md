# EGAP 方法指南：10 Things You Need to Know About Statistical Power

- 资料：`egappower`，[原文网页](https://methods.egap.org/guides/assessing-designs/power_en.html)
- 版本："治理与政治证据网络"（Evidence in Governance and Politics，EGAP）方法指南，作者 Alexander Coppock；2026 年 10 月访问。位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§2 Why You Need It
- 原文："Negative results from underpowered studies can be hard to interpret: Is there really no effect? Or is the study just not able to figure it out? Positive results from an underpowered study can also be misleading: conditional upon being statistically significant, an estimate from an underpowered study probably overestimates treatment effects."
- 要点：功效不足的研究，阴性结果难以解释（真没效应，还是测不出来？）；阳性结果也可能误导，显著的估计很可能高估了效应。

### E2

- 位置：§5 When to Believe Your Power Analysis
- 原文："The good news is that it is easy to find out how much your conclusions depend on your assumptions: simply vary your assumptions and see how the conclusions on power vary."
- 要点：功效计算依赖假设；改变假设，看功效结论怎样变化，就知道结论有多依赖假设。

### E3

- 位置：§2 Why You Need It
- 原文："Many disciplines have settled on a target power value of 0.80." / "While this convention is a useful benchmark, be sure that you are comfortable with the risks associated with an 80% expected success rate."
- 要点：许多学科以 0.80 为目标功效；这只是参照，要想清楚能否接受 80% 的预期成功率意味着的风险。

### E4

- 位置：§6 How to Use Simulation to Estimate Power
- 原文："We can tell our computers to literally run the experiment thousands of times and simply count how frequently our experiment comes up significant."
- 要点：可以用模拟估计功效：让计算机把实验跑上几千遍，数一数有多少次结果显著。
