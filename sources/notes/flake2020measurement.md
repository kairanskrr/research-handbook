# Flake & Fried 2020：Measurement Schmeasurement: Questionable Measurement Practices and How to Avoid Them

- 资料：`flake2020measurement`，[原文 PDF（PsyArXiv 预印本）](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/hs7wm/)
- 版本：经同行评审、未经编辑加工的预印本；正式版发表于 Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science 3(4):456–465。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："we define questionable measurement practices (QMPs) as decisions researchers make that raise doubts about the validity of the measures, and ultimately the validity of study conclusions."
- 要点：可疑测量做法（QMP）：研究者在测量上做的、让人怀疑测量是否有效、进而怀疑研究结论是否成立的决定。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："This is difficult because psychological constructs like depression, personality traits, attitudes, or cognitive abilities often do not permit direct observation." / "Neither rigorous research design, advanced statistics, nor large samples can correct such false inferences."
- 要点：抑郁、人格特质、态度、认知能力这类心理学构念往往无法直接观察。如果测量本身测不准想测的东西，再严谨的设计、再高级的统计、再大的样本也救不回来。

### E3

- 位置：Questioning the Validity of Psychological Science
- 原文："We can think of no single psychological construct for which there exists exactly one rigorously validated measure that is universally accepted by the field" / "data collected with a 10-item questionnaire can be summarized in one sum-score 1,023 different ways by summing different subsets of items"
- 要点：几乎没有哪个心理学概念只有一个公认的测量方法。一份 10 道题的问卷，光是挑哪些题加总，就有 1023 种算法，这本身就是"分叉路径"。

### E4

- 位置：Question 6: Do you create a measure on the fly?
- 原文："Using a measure exactly as described elsewhere, and providing a citation to the original source, decreases ambiguity and the potential for flexibility."
- 要点：照原样使用已有量表并注明出处，能减少含糊和可操作的灵活空间。

### E5

- 位置：Question 6: Do you create a measure on the fly?
- 原文："of the 43 item-based scales they reviewed, 34 (79%) appeared to be ad-hoc and were used with no other supporting validity information in the original study."
- 要点：Shaw 等人检查一个大型多实验室重复项目用到的量表：43 个中有 34 个（79%）像是临时编的，原研究里没有其他效度证据。

### E6

- 位置：Table 1
- 原文："Do you modify the measure? If so, how and why?"
- 要点：作者列出的透明报告问题之一：你改动了量表吗？怎么改的，为什么改？

### E7

- 位置：Question 1: What is your construct?
- 原文："If the construct is not defined clearly at the outset, the ambiguity in what is being measured will make navigating all the future measurement decisions difficult and present many opportunities to exploit this ambiguity, knowingly or otherwise."
- 要点：一开始没把构念定义清楚，后面每个测量决定都难做，也留下了有意无意利用这种含糊的机会。

### E8

- 位置：Question 2: Why and how do you select your measure?
- 原文："helps prevent thinking two instruments measure the same construct because they have similar names (i.e., the jingle fallacy), or assuming two measures assess different constructs because they have different names (i.e., the jangle fallacy)"
- 要点：名字相似不等于测同一个构念（jingle 谬误），名字不同也不等于测不同构念（jangle 谬误）。

### E9

- 位置：Question 3: What measure(s) did you use?
- 原文："Of 433 scales used in studies published in JPSP, 40% had no accompanying information regarding their source, 19% were reported without the number of items, and 9% without the response scale" / "(Flake, et al., 2017)"
- 要点：Flake 等人 2017 年检查《人格与社会心理学杂志》（JPSP）论文中的 433 个量表：40% 没说来源，19% 没报告题数，9% 没报告作答选项。

### E10

- 位置：Question 4: How do you quantify your measure?
- 原文："Developing a priori decision rules for removing items or stimuli and/or grouping them is ideal."
- 要点：最好事先定好删哪些题、怎样分组的规则。

### E11

- 位置：Question 5: Do you modify the scale?
- 原文："18 measures were the result of combining two separate measures into one on the basis of the reliability coefficient α" / "Flake et al., (2017) noted, in their review of 500 measures used in articles published in JPSP"
- 要点：Flake 等人检查的 500 个测量中，有 18 个是凭 α 系数把两个独立量表合并成一个。

### E12

- 位置：Question 5: Do you modify the scale?
- 原文："the main point is not that such modifications are never called for, but that if they remain undeclared and unjustified, they threaten the validity of the inferences we can draw from a given study."
- 要点：改量表不是绝对不行，问题在于不声明、不说明理由。

### E13

- 位置：Question 6: Do you create a measure on the fly?
- 原文："at a minimum, researchers should disclose why they created the measure, and justify why they use an ad-hoc measure over using an existing measure in case such a measure exists. Further, if there is no or little validity evidence for that measure, researchers should discuss it as a limitation of the study."
- 要点：自编量表至少要说明为什么自编、为什么不用已有量表；效度证据很少或没有时，要作为局限讨论。

### E14

- 位置：Questioning the Validity of Psychological Science
- 原文："data collected with a 10-item questionnaire can be summarized in one sum-score 1,023 different ways by summing different subsets of items"
- 要点：一份 10 道题的问卷，光是挑哪些题加总，就有 1023 种算法。

### E15

- 位置：Summary
- 原文："even if we increase the sample size of our depression trials, adequately power our studies, pre-register our analytic strategies, and stop p-hacking — we can still be left wondering if we were ever measuring depression at all."
- 要点：即使样本够大、功效够、做了预注册、不做 p-hacking，如果测量有问题，仍然不知道自己测的是不是抑郁。

### E16

- 位置：Questionable Measurement Practices 一节（作者引用 Shadish 等 2002 的四类效度）
- 原文："describe four types of validity that contribute to the overall validity of a conclusion: internal validity, external validity, statistical conclusion validity, and construct validity" / "Internal validity captures the aspects of a study’s design that support causal claims about the relations between variables." / "External validity concerns the generalizability of findings across different populations and settings." / "Statistical conclusion validity captures whether conclusions from a statistical analysis are correct." / "construct validity encompasses how the constructs in the study are operationalized"
- 要点：Shadish 等人把影响结论整体效度的分为四类：内部效度（支持变量间因果主张的设计）、外部效度（结果能否推广到不同人群和情境）、统计结论效度（统计分析得出的结论是否正确）、构念效度（研究中的概念怎样被变成可测的东西）。
