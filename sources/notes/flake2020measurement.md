# Flake & Fried 2020：Measurement Schmeasurement: Questionable Measurement Practices and How to Avoid Them

- 资料：`flake2020measurement`，[原文 PDF（PsyArXiv 预印本）](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/hs7wm/)
- 版本：经同行评审、未经编辑加工的预印本；正式版发表于 Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science 3(4):456–465。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："we define questionable measurement practices (QMPs) as decisions researchers make that raise doubts about the validity of the measures, and ultimately the validity of study conclusions."
- 要点：可疑测量做法（QMP）：研究者在测量上做的、让人怀疑测量是否有效、进而怀疑研究结论是否成立的决定。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："This is difficult because psychological constructs like depression, personality traits, attitudes, or cognitive abilities often do not permit direct observation." / "Neither rigorous research design, advanced statistics, nor large samples can correct such false inferences."
- 要点：抑郁、人格特质、态度、认知能力这类心理学构念往往无法直接观察。如果测量本身测不准想测的东西，再严谨的设计、再高级的统计、再大的样本也救不回来。

### E3

- 位置：Questioning the Validity of Psychological Science
- 原文："We can think of no single psychological construct for which there exists exactly one rigorously validated measure that is universally accepted by the field" / "data collected with a 10-item questionnaire can be summarized in one sum-score 1,023 different ways by summing different subsets of items"
- 要点：几乎没有哪个心理学概念只有一个公认的测量方法。一份 10 道题的问卷，光是挑哪些题加总，就有 1023 种算法，这本身就是"分叉路径"。

### E4

- 位置：Question 6: Do you create a measure on the fly?
- 原文："Using a measure exactly as described elsewhere, and providing a citation to the original source, decreases ambiguity and the potential for flexibility."
- 要点：照原样使用已有量表并注明出处，能减少含糊和可操作的灵活空间。

### E5

- 位置：Question 6: Do you create a measure on the fly?
- 原文："of the 43 item-based scales they reviewed, 34 (79%) appeared to be ad-hoc and were used with no other supporting validity information in the original study."
- 要点：Shaw 等人检查一个大型多实验室重复项目用到的量表：43 个中有 34 个（79%）像是临时编的，原研究里没有其他效度证据。

### E6

- 位置：Table 1
- 原文："Do you modify the measure? If so, how and why?"
- 要点：作者列出的透明报告问题之一：你改动了量表吗？怎么改的，为什么改？
