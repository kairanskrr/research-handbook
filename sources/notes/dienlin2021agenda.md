# Dienlin 等人 2021：An Agenda for Open Science in Communication

- 资料：`dienlin2021agenda`，[原文 PDF（White Rose 开放存档）](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/158440/7/jqz052.pdf)
- 版本：Journal of Communication 71(1), 1–26，2021（2020 年在线发表），DOI 10.1093/joc/jqz052，知识共享 CC BY 4.0 开放获取；位置写正文小节名。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："we propose an agenda for adopting open science practices in Communication, which includes the following seven suggestions: (1) publish materials, data, and code; (2) preregister studies and submit registered reports; (3) conduct replications; (4) collaborate; (5) foster open science skills; (6) implement Transparency and Openness Promotion Guidelines; and (7) incentivize open science practices."
- 要点：作者为传播学提出七条开放科学建议：公开材料、数据和代码；预注册并投注册报告；做重复研究；合作；培养开放科学技能；实施"透明与开放促进指南"（TOP 指南）；激励开放科学做法。

### E2

- 位置：引言
- 原文："The field of Communication has not yet conducted a large-scale replication project. However, because we employ similar methods to the fields that have already acknowledged these problems, there is reason to believe we face similar issues."
- 要点：传播学还没做过大规模重复项目；但它用的方法和已经承认有可重复性问题的学科相似，有理由认为也有类似问题。

### E3

- 位置：Replicability in Communication
- 原文："studies in leading journals in Communication show evidence of the QRPs discussed above (Matthes et al., 2015)"
- 要点：对传播学主要期刊的研究发现了可疑研究做法的证据。

### E4

- 位置：Replicability in Communication
- 原文："Of all p-values reported in 693 empirical Communication papers published between 2010 and 2012, 8.8% were incorrect; of those, 75% were too low (Vermeulen & Hartmann, 2015)."
- 要点：2010–2012 年 693 篇传播学实证论文报告的 p 值中，8.8% 有误；有误的当中 75% 偏低。

### E5

- 位置：Replicability in Communication
- 原文："from which we can extrapolate that effects are typically tested with 167 participants. However, for most study designs such a small sample size would be insufficient to reliably detect small or moderate effects"
- 要点：作者根据 Rains 等人 2018 年对传播学元分析的回顾推算，效应一般是在 167 名参与者上检验的；对大多数设计来说，这样的样本不足以可靠地检测小到中等的效应。

### E6

- 位置：Open science practices，3. Conduct replications
- 原文："In Communication, they represent approximately 1.8% of all published research (Keating & Totzkay, 2019)."
- 要点：在传播学，直接重复研究约占已发表研究的 1.8%。

### E7

- 位置：Open science practices，2. Preregister studies and submit registered reports
- 原文："In Communication, at the time of writing, registered reports are accepted by Communication Research Reports, Computational Communication Research, and Journal of Media Psychology."
- 要点：写作时（2020 年前后），传播学接受注册报告的期刊有 Communication Research Reports、Computational Communication Research 和 Journal of Media Psychology。

### E8

- 位置：Open science practices，1. Publish materials, data, and code
- 原文："We recommend that researchers share study materials (e.g., items, stimuli, protocols, instructions, or codebooks for content- and meta-analyses), data (e.g., raw, aggregated, processed, or synthetic), code (e.g., data-gathering, -preparation, or -analysis)"
- 要点：建议共享研究材料（包括内容分析的编码手册）、数据和代码（包括数据收集代码）。

### E9

- 位置：Objections（隐私与版权）
- 原文："Specific raw data such as news articles, video material, or data scraped from online platforms sometimes cannot be made public, for example because of copyright reasons"
- 要点：新闻报道、视频、从平台抓取的数据等原始数据，有时因版权等原因不能公开。

### E10

- 位置：Objections（隐私与版权）
- 原文："Second, researchers can prevent others from identifying participants by (a) removing direct or indirect identifiers (e.g., date of birth), (b) binning (i.e., turning continuous values into categories), or (c) aggregating."
- 要点：防止参与者被认出的办法：删除直接或间接标识信息（如出生日期）、把连续数值分组、汇总。

### E11

- 位置：Objections（隐私与版权）
- 原文："A third solution to both privacy and copyright issues is non-consumptive data use (van Atteveldt et al., 2019), which means providing access to the data without physically copying it (e.g., by means of onsite visits)."
- 要点：第三种办法是"非消耗性使用"：让别人能访问数据，但不把数据复制出去（例如到现场使用）。

### E12

- 位置：Open science and qualitative research
- 原文："we argue that open science practices can increase the quality of qualitative research primarily by improving transparency and traceability."
- 要点：开放科学做法主要通过提高透明度和可追溯性来提高定性研究的质量。

### E13

- 位置：Open science and qualitative research
- 原文："Hence, preregistration of qualitative research can be fruitful, too: It increases transparency, tracks flexibility and modification during the research process, and, when submitted as a registered report, prevents publication bias"
- 要点：定性研究也可以预注册：提高透明度、记录研究过程中的调整；以注册报告形式投稿还能防止发表偏差。

### E14

- 位置：Objections（结尾）
- 原文："Our agenda is not all or nothing: Even an incremental adoption will bring important benefits to our field, and some progress is better than no progress."
- 要点：这些建议不是"全做或不做"，部分采用也有好处。

### E15

- 位置：Replicability in Communication
- 原文："Similarly, there exist several accounts of publication bias in Communication (Franco, Malhotra, & Simonovits, 2014; Keating & Totzkay, 2019), which indicates a preference for novel and statistically significant results."
- 要点：传播学也有发表偏差的记录，偏爱新颖的、统计显著的结果。

### E16

- 位置：引言
- 原文："Although we focus mostly on confirmatory and quantitative research, we also (d) reflect on open science practices relevant to other approaches such as qualitative research."
- 要点：这篇文章主要针对验证性的定量研究，也讨论了定性研究。
