# Olah & Carter 2017：Research Debt

- 资料：`olah2017debt`，[原文](https://distill.pub/2017/research-debt/)
- 版本：Distill 文章，2017。网页，位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Interpretive Labor
- 原文："In these one-to-many cases, each member of the audience pays the cost of understanding, even though the cost of explaining stays the same."
- 要点：一对多的写作中，解释的成本只付一次，理解的成本每个读者都要付一次；作者多花力气讲清楚，能为所有读者省力。

### E2

- 位置：Clear Thinking
- 原文："Conversely, if we can’t explain an idea well, that’s often a sign that we don’t understand it as well as we could."
- 要点：讲不清一个想法，往往说明自己还没真正理解它。

### E3

- 位置：Research Distillation
- 原文："It’s tempting to think of explaining an idea as just putting a layer of polish on it, but good explanations often involve transforming the idea."
- 要点：好的解释不只是表面润色，往往需要重新组织、改造想法本身。
