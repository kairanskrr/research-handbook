# 美国环境保护署：CADDIS（因果分析/诊断决策信息系统）

- 资料：`epa2026caddis`，[原文](https://www.epa.gov/caddis)
- 版本：美国环境保护署（U.S. EPA）官方网站，各页显示 "Last updated on January 22, 2026"，2026-10-08 读取。引用的页面：Basic Information About CADDIS、Volume 1 About Causal Assessment、Step 5. Identify Probable Causes、About Scoring the Evidence。位置写页面名和小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Basic Information About CADDIS，What is CADDIS?
- 原文："CADDIS, or the Causal Analysis/Diagnosis Decision Information System, is an on-line application designed to help users conduct causal assessments, primarily in stream" / "It provides a logical, step-by-step framework for stressor identification"
- 要点：CADDIS 是美国环境保护署的在线系统，帮助用户做因果评估，主要针对溪流生态系统，提供一套按步骤识别"压力因素"（造成生物受损的原因）的框架。

### E2

- 位置：About Causal Assessment，A Philosophical System
- 原文："For example, we may determine that an effluent is the likely cause of an impairment using abduction. We then can deduce that eliminating the effluent would result in biotic recovery. Environmental monitoring could then be used inductively to infer that effluent was indeed the cause."
- 要点：例子：先推断某股排放出水最可能是受损的原因；再推出"去掉这股出水，生物应当恢复"；之后用环境监测来确认它确实是原因。

### E3

- 位置：About Causal Assessment，The Issue
- 原文："these methods might evaluate if silt can decrease caddisflies, instead of whether silt caused decreased caddisflies at a specific site. CADDIS addresses causation for specific, individual cases."
- 要点：多数因果分析方法回答一般性问题（泥沙能不能让石蛾减少），CADDIS 回答具体个案（这个地点的石蛾减少是不是泥沙造成的）。

### E4

- 位置：About Causal Assessment，Weighing of Evidence
- 原文："We believe that all relevant evidence should be considered." / "Common sources include site observations, regional monitoring studies, environmental manipulations, laboratory experiments, and general scientific knowledge."
- 要点：所有相关证据都要考虑；常见来源有现场观察、区域监测研究、环境操纵实验、实验室实验和一般科学知识。

### E5

- 位置：About Causal Assessment，Rejection
- 原文："we recognize that one can more confidently eliminate than accept a causal hypothesis. For example, suppose an effect occurs downstream of a source. This provides weak supporting evidence for emissions from that source as a cause. However, if the effect occurs upstream of the source, rejection with confidence is possible." / "Therefore, we begin weighing evidence by rejecting as many causes as possible."
- 要点：排除一个因果假设比接受它更有把握。效应出现在排放源下游，只是支持它的弱证据；效应出现在源的上游，就能有把握地排除它。所以先尽量排除候选原因。

### E6

- 位置：Step 5. Identify Probable Causes，Overview
- 原文："This is why several candidate causes are listed at the beginning of the causal analysis process." / "Then, each candidate cause is compared to every other candidate cause to determine which causes led to the specific effects."
- 要点：因果分析一开始就列出几个候选原因，最后把每个候选原因和其他所有候选原因相互比较。

### E7

- 位置：Step 5. Identify Probable Causes，Overview
- 原文："In some situations, no cause is identified or the confidence in conclusions will be too low to support management action." / "A classification of each candidate cause as refuted, diagnosed, probable, unlikely or uncertain"
- 要点：有时找不出原因，或结论的把握太低、不足以支持管理行动；第 5 步的产出包括把每个候选原因归为：已排除、已诊断、可能、不太可能、不确定。

### E8

- 位置：About Scoring the Evidence，Overview
- 原文："the highest scores are given to the types of evidence: That use data from the site, That are based on more than one association, That closely link the proximate cause and the effect."
- 要点：得分最高的证据类型是：用本地点的数据、基于不止一个关联、把直接原因和效应紧密连起来的证据。

### E9

- 位置：Step 5. Identify Probable Causes，Evaluating the Quantity and Quality of Evidence
- 原文："Increasing the number of types or pieces of evidence decreases the likelihood that any one faulty study or data set will mislead you."
- 要点：增加证据的种类和数量，可以降低被某一项有问题的研究或数据集误导的可能。
