# Tetley 1999：Mixed jurisdictions: common law vs civil law (codified and uncodified) (Part I)

- 资料：`tetley1999mixed`，[原文 PDF](https://unidroit.org/english/publications/review/articles/1999-3-tetley1-e.pdf)
- 版本：Uniform Law Review / Revue de droit uniforme 第 4 卷第 3 期（1999），DOI 10.1093/ulr/4.3.591，第 591 页起，国际统一私法协会（UNIDROIT）出版并在其官网公开的 PDF，2026-10-11 读取。作者 William Tetley 时任加拿大麦吉尔大学法学教授。位置写文章第 V 部分的小节号。作者比较两大法系时主要以法国民法为例，是 1999 年的概括。

### E1

- 位置：§V.1 Order of priority: jurisprudence and doctrine
- 原文："A major difference between the civil law and common law is that priority in civil law is given to doctrine (including the codifiers' reports) over jurisprudence, while the opposite is true in the common law."
- 要点：两大法系的一大区别：大陆法系把学说（doctrine，包括法典起草者的报告）放在判例（jurisprudence）之前，普通法正相反。

### E2

- 位置：§V.4 Jurisprudence: function
- 原文："Common law jurisprudence sets out a new specific rule to a new specific set of facts and provides the principal source of law, while civil law jurisprudence applies general principles, and is only a secondary source of law of explanation."
- 要点：普通法的判例针对新的具体事实定出新的具体规则，是主要法源；大陆法系的判例适用一般原则，只是次要的、起解释作用的法源。

### E3

- 位置：§V.5 Stare decisis 及脚注 115
- 原文："The English doctrine of stare decisis compels lower courts to follow decisions rendered in higher courts" / "Stare decisis is unknown to civil law, where judgments rendered by judges only enjoy the "authority of reason"." / "In practice, however, the Cour de cassation is feared by judges of lower courts."
- 要点：英国的遵循先例原则要求下级法院遵循上级法院的判决；大陆法系没有这一原则，判决只享有"说理的权威"。作者在脚注里补充，实际上下级法院的法官很怕（法国）最高法院 Cour de cassation（正文未采用这句脚注）。

### E4

- 位置：§V.7 Statutes: functions
- 原文："Civil law codes provide the core of the law" / "general principles are systematically and exhaustively exposed in codes"
- 要点：大陆法系的法典是法律的核心，一般原则在法典中成体系、无遗漏地写出。

### E5

- 位置：§V.9 Interpretation of laws
- 原文："In civil law jurisdictions, the first step in interpreting an ambiguous law, according to MAZEAUD, is to discover the intention of the legislator by examining the legislation as a whole, including the "travaux préparatoires", as well as the provisions more immediately surrounding the obscure text."
- 要点：在大陆法系，按 Mazeaud 的说法，解释含义不清的法律，第一步是整体考察立法（包括立法准备材料和含义不清的条文周围的条款），找出立法者的意图。

### E6

- 位置：§V.2 Doctrine: functions
- 原文："The civil law doctrine's function is "to draw from this disorganised mass [cases, books and legal dictionaries] the rules and the principles which will clarify and purge the subject of impure elements, and thus provide both the practice and the courts with a guide for the solution of particular cases in the future.""
- 要点：大陆法系学说的作用（引 David 和 Brierley）：从判例、书籍和法律词典这些杂乱的材料里整理出规则和原则，给实务和法院今后解决具体案件提供指引。
