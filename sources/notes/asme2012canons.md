# ASME：The ASME Criteria for Interpretation of the Canons

- 资料：`asme2012canons`，[原文](https://www.asme.org/wwwasmeorg/media/ResourceFiles/AboutASME/Get%20Involved/Advocacy/Policy-Publications/CRITERIA-FOR-INTERPRETATION-OF-THE-CANONSJune2012.pdf)
- 版本：美国机械工程师学会（ASME）《伦理准则》基本条款的解释准则，2012 年 6 月版，ASME 网站免费 PDF。位置写条款编号。

### E1

- 位置：2.f.(1)
- 原文："Technical Communications for publication (theses, articles, papers, reports, etc.) which are based on research involving more than one individual (including students and supervising faculty, industrial supervisor/researcher, or other co-workers) must recognize all significant contributors. Co-authors listed on proposed and accepted publications should have entered the joint authorship arrangement by mutual consent prior to submittal of the document for publication"
- 要点：多人参与的研究（包括学生和导师、企业主管或研究人员）写成论文、学位论文、报告时，要承认所有重要贡献者；合著者应在投稿前经双方同意确定署名安排。

### E2

- 位置：2.g
- 原文："Engineers shall not engage in plagiarism. The act of substantially using another's ideas or written materials without due credit and advance notification, is unethical."
- 要点：不得抄袭；大量使用别人的想法或文字而不注明出处，是不道德的。

### E3

- 位置：4.l
- 原文："Before undertaking work for others in which Engineers may make improvements, plans, designs, inventions, or other records which may justify seeking copyrights, patents, or proprietary rights, Engineers shall enter into positive agreements regarding the rights of respective parties."
- 要点：替别人做可能产生改进、设计、发明（可能申请版权、专利或专有权）的工作之前，要先和各方明确约定权利归属。

### E4

- 位置：4.j.(1)
- 原文："They will not disclose confidential information concerning the business affairs or technical processes of any present or former employer or client or bidder under evaluation without consent, unless required by law or court order."
- 要点：未经同意，不得泄露现任或前任雇主、客户的商业事务或技术工艺等保密信息，法律或法院要求除外。

### E5

- 位置：4.m
- 原文："Engineers shall admit their own errors when proven wrong and refrain from distorting or altering the facts to justify their mistakes or decisions."
- 要点：被证明错了就承认，不歪曲事实为自己的错误辩护。

### E6

- 位置：1.c.(3)
- 原文："Whenever Engineers observe conditions, directly related to their employment, which they believe will endanger public safety or health, they shall inform their supervisor and the proper authority of the situation."
- 要点：发现与工作直接相关、可能危及公众安全或健康的情况，要报告上级和有关主管部门。
