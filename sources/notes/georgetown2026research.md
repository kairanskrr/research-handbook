# Georgetown Law Writing Center 2026：You Have Your Scholarly Paper Topic, Now . . . Get the Research Done

- 资料：`georgetown2026research`，[原文 PDF（美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心，免费）](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/academics/wp-content/uploads/sites/58/2026/10/You-Have-Your-Scholarly-Paper-Topic-Now-Get-the-Research-Done_Kelsey-Handschuh-2026-Revisions_FINAL-1.pdf)
- 版本：美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心讲义，Laura Klein 初稿（法学院图书馆员 Sara Sampson 协助），Kelsey Handschuh 2026 年修订（图书馆员 Jill Smith 协助）。写给**美国法学院学生**写学术论文；举的数据库（Westlaw、Lexis、HeinOnline 等）和立法史资料都是美国的。位置写讲义的小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：I.A.1 Formulate a Research Strategy
- 原文："Your research strategy should incorporate two considerations: (1) the sources you want to consult and the order in which you expect to consult them, and (2) your deadlines for research and writing--both self-imposed and externally imposed."
- 要点：检索策略包括两点：要查哪些资料、按什么顺序查；检索和写作的截止时间（自己定的和外部规定的）。

### E2

- 位置：I.A.1
- 原文："Commit your research strategy to paper, write it down, and consult it often as you proceed. That way, diverging from the plan requires a conscious decision on your part."
- 要点：把检索策略写下来，边做边对照；这样偏离计划时，必须是你有意识做出的决定。

### E3

- 位置：I.A.1
- 原文："One way to keep your research organized is to create a research log. You should design this research log in a way that makes sense to you, but helpful things to incorporate include where you found the source, how you found the source," / "what you found, and why you are examining the source."
- 要点：建一份检索日志，记下：资料在哪里找到、怎样找到、找到了什么、为什么要看它。

### E4

- 位置：I.A.2 Conduct a Preemption Check
- 原文："It's important to conduct the preemption check at this early stage--after you've selected a topic but before you've developed your thesis--so that you can focus all your subsequent research on developing a unique thesis."
- 要点：在选定题目之后、确定论点之前，就做"抢先检查"（查别人是否已写过同样的论点），这样后面的检索都能服务于一个独特的论点。

### E5

- 位置：I.A.2
- 原文："Remember that you are almost guaranteed to find articles on your topic--that does not mean you have been preempted."
- 要点：几乎一定能找到同题目的文章，这不等于你被抢先了。

### E6

- 位置：I.B Consulting Secondary Sources
- 原文："After you have solidified your research plan, consider beginning with secondary sources to conduct background research for your paper."
- 要点：定好检索计划后，可以先从二手资料入手做背景研究。

### E7

- 位置：I.B.1–2
- 原文："These sources are descriptive, meaning they simply explain the state of the law and do not analyze or critique legal theories." / "analytical sources, such as a law review article, evaluate the state of the law and may make suggestions for what the law should be."
- 要点：法律百科、专著、法律重述等是描述性的二手资料，只说明法律现状；法律评论文章等分析性资料则评价法律现状，可能提出法律应当怎样。

### E8

- 位置：I.C.1 Use Headnotes and Citing References/Sources
- 原文："Headnotes are editor-written descriptions of the major issues of law in the case."
- 要点：判例摘要（headnotes）是数据库编辑写的、对案件主要法律问题的描述，不是判决本身。

### E9

- 位置：I.D What to Do When You're Stuck
- 原文："Research is recursive, so retracing your steps once you've learned more is all part of the process."
- 要点：检索是反复进行的；了解更多之后回头重走一遍，是正常过程。

### E10

- 位置：I.E.3 Consider Contrary Sources
- 原文："Be sure, however, to consider carefully the countervailing arguments that have been made, and address those in your paper."
- 要点：认真考虑已有的反对论证，并在文中回应。

### E11

- 位置：II. Knowing When to Stop--or at Least Pause
- 原文："First, you must ask yourself whether you have researched the most-cited cases and articles on your topic. Next you must be certain that you have checked recent publications. Last, but certainly not least, you must be certain that you can support the essential components of your thesis."
- 要点：判断能否暂停检索的三条：查过本题被引最多的判例和文章；查过最新发表物；论点的关键部分都有支撑。
