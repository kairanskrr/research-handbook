# Kabeshov, Bergonzini & Engkvist 2026：A Return to the Logic-First Spirit of Corey's Retrosynthetic Analysis

- 资料：`kabeshov2026retrosynthesis`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.6c00702)（[PubMed Central 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13232869/)）
- 版本：ACS Central Science 2026, 12(5), 562–564，评论文章，CC BY 4.0 开放获取。作者来自阿斯利康（AstraZeneca）哥德堡研发中心。原文文本取自 Europe PMC 全文 XML。

### E1

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Chemists look at a target molecule and ask not only "What reaction should I do next?" but, more fundamentally, "What is the best way to disconnect this structure into meaningful fragments?""
- 要点：逆合成分析就是看着目标分子，问"怎样把它拆成有意义的片段"。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Human experts often begin with broad strategic disconnections and only later decide which exact functional groups, reagents, or tactical adjustments will make those ideas work in the laboratory."
- 要点：有经验的化学家通常先定大的拆分策略，再决定具体官能团、试剂和战术调整。

### E3

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："Data-driven systems can now learn from very large reaction sets, propose plausible one-step disconnections, and search multistep routes far more effectively than earlier rule-only systems."
- 要点：计算机辅助合成规划系统可以从大量反应数据中学习，提出单步拆分并搜索多步路线。

### E4

- 位置：第 6 段
- 原文："Once a promising strategic route is identified, chemists can map it back into concrete reagents, select protecting groups, choose among buyable building blocks, and adapt the plan to feasibility, selectivity, scale, or platform constraints."
- 要点：找到有前景的策略路线后，还要由化学家落实成具体试剂、保护基、可买到的原料，并按可行性、选择性、规模调整。
