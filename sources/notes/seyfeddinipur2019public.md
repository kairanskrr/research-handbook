# Seyfeddinipur 等 2019：Public access to research data in language documentation: Challenges and possible strategies

- 资料：`seyfeddinipur2019public`，[原文](http://hdl.handle.net/10125/24901)
- 版本：Language Documentation & Conservation 13: 545–563（开放获取，CC BY-NC 4.0）；30 位作者，来自多国大学、语言档案馆和国际机构

### E1

- 位置：§2.1 Challenges
- 原文："Determining who has the cultural and legal authority to provide consent may be complex where individuals, families, or communities hold rights to specific stories, songs, dances, or other cultural expressions."
- 要点：故事、歌曲等可能分属个人、家族或社区，弄清谁有权同意可能很复杂。

### E2

- 位置：§2.2 Strategies
- 原文："Ownership of material and questions around access options need to be discussed early, both with individuals and the wider community, with discussion continuing on a regular basis"
- 要点：材料归谁、谁能访问，要尽早和个人及整个社区讨论，并定期继续讨论。

### E3

- 位置：§2.2 Strategies
- 原文："Who can listen to, view, or read particular materials, and what does it mean if anyone in the world could do this?"
- 要点：讨论访问级别时可以问：谁能听、看、读这些材料？如果全世界的人都能看，意味着什么？

### E4

- 位置：§2.2 Strategies
- 原文："Workshops can be held to discuss these topics."
- 要点：可以办讨论会来谈这些问题。

### E5

- 位置：§2.2 Strategies
- 原文："Where written consent is not suitable, speakers' agreement can be recorded orally, as can relevant discussions with the wider community."
- 要点：不适合书面同意时，可以录下发音人的口头同意，以及和社区的相关讨论。

### E6

- 位置：§2.2 Strategies
- 原文："Individual and community views of consent can change over time"
- 要点：个人和社区对同意的看法会随时间变化。

### E7

- 位置：§2.2 Strategies
- 原文："informed consent should include discussion of the level of access (open, or restricted in some way), and this discussion should be included as part of the collection's metadata."
- 要点：知情同意要讨论访问级别（开放或某种限制），讨论结果写进合集的元数据。

### E8

- 位置：§2.2 Strategies
- 原文："It may be appropriate to err on the side of caution and restrict access, at least in the early stages of research."
- 要点：至少在研究早期，宁可谨慎，先限制访问。

### E9

- 位置：§3.1 Challenges
- 原文："In some jurisdictions research permits are required in order to conduct a language documentation project, and the permits may place explicit restrictions on access to research data."
- 要点：有些地方做语言记录需要研究许可，许可可能明确限制数据的访问。

### E10

- 位置：§3.2 Strategies
- 原文："Intellectual Property law typically vests copyright in language documentation materials with the individuals who made the recordings"
- 要点：按知识产权法，录音的版权一般归录音的人（研究者），而不是说话的人。

### E11

- 位置：§5.2.2 Possible strategies
- 原文："From the outset of a project, researchers should work with archives to address issues of licensing and access, to develop a succession plan stating who will be responsible for materials in the future, and to make plans for future treatment of restricted materials."
- 要点：项目一开始就和档案馆商量许可和访问，定好继任计划（将来谁负责这些材料），以及受限材料以后怎么处理。

### E12

- 位置：§5.2.2 Possible strategies
- 原文："it is too easy for researchers or archives to use `community sensitivity' as an excuse for not making their records available"
- 要点：研究者或档案馆很容易拿"社区敏感"当借口，不公开记录。

### E13

- 位置：§6.2.1
- 原文："in small societies it may be impossible to anonymize speakers."
- 要点：在小社会里，可能根本没法把发音人匿名。

### E14

- 位置：§6.2.1
- 原文："we recommend using restricted access only with a clearly specified embargo period, after which the restrictions can be lifted."
- 要点：在不违背社区意愿时，限制访问应设明确的封存期，到期可以解除。
