# 美国疾病控制与预防中心、美国国立卫生研究院 2020：Biosafety in Microbiological and Biomedical Laboratories, 6th Edition

- 资料：`cdc2020bmbl`，[原文 PDF](https://www.cdc.gov/labs/pdf/SF__19_308133-A_BMBL6_00-BOOK-WEB-final-3.pdf)
- 版本：第 6 版，2020 年 6 月修订。美国政府出版物。CDC 官网对脚本返回 403，通过 Internet Archive 快照（`web.archive.org/web/2024id_/...`）下载同一份 PDF。位置写章节名。

### E1

- 位置：Foreword
- 原文："We wish to emphasize that the sixth edition of BMBL remains an advisory document recommending best practices for the safe conduct of work in biomedical and clinical laboratories from a biosafety perspective. The BMBL is not intended to be a regulatory document although we recognize that some may use it in that way."
- 要点：第 6 版 BMBL 是建议性文件，推荐生物医学和临床实验室安全工作的最佳做法；它不打算作为法规文件，尽管有人会这样用它。

### E2

- 位置：Foreword
- 原文："The core principle of this document is protocol-driven risk assessment; it is not possible for a single document to identify all of the possible combinations of risks and mitigations feasible in biomedical and clinical laboratories."
- 要点：本书的核心原则是"按具体实验方案做风险评估"；没有一份文件能列尽所有风险和应对措施的组合。

### E3

- 位置：Section II, Biological Risk Assessment
- 原文："The biological risk assessment process is used to identify the hazardous characteristics of an infectious or potentially infectious agent or material, if known; the activities that can result in a person’s exposure to an agent; the likelihood that such exposure will cause an LAI; and the probable consequences of such an infection."
- 要点：生物风险评估要弄清四件事：病原或材料有什么危害特性；哪些操作会让人接触到它；接触后引起实验室获得性感染的可能性；感染的可能后果。

### E4

- 位置：Section II, Biological Risk Assessment
- 原文："Risk assessment requires careful judgment and is an important responsibility for directors and principal investigators (PI) of microbiological and biomedical laboratories. Institutional leadership and oversight resources, such as Institutional Biosafety Committees (IBCs) or equivalent resources, animal care and use committees, biological safety professionals, occupational health staff, and laboratory animal veterinarians also share in this responsibility."
- 要点：风险评估需要审慎判断，是实验室主任和课题组负责人的重要责任；机构生物安全委员会、动物管理委员会、生物安全专业人员等也分担这一责任。

### E5

- 位置：Section III, Biosafety Levels
- 原文："The four primary Biosafety Levels (BSLs) for laboratories described in Section IV of BMBL consist of combinations of facility design features and safety equipment (primary and secondary barriers), facility practices and procedures, and personal protective equipment."
- 要点：四个生物安全等级，每级都是设施设计和安全设备（一级、二级屏障）、操作规程、个人防护装备的组合。

### E6

- 位置：Section III, Biosafety Level 1
- 原文："Biosafety Level 1 (BSL-1) standard practices, safety equipment, and facility specifications are generally appropriate for undergraduate and secondary educational training and teaching laboratories and for other laboratories that work with defined and characterized strains of viable biological agents not known to consistently cause disease in healthy adult humans."
- 要点：一级适用于教学实验室，以及使用已明确鉴定、已知不会稳定地让健康成人致病的活菌株的实验室。

### E7

- 位置：Section III, Biosafety Level 2
- 原文："Biosafety Level 2 (BSL-2) standard practices, safety equipment, and facility specifications are applicable to laboratories in which work is performed using a broad-spectrum of biological agents and toxins that are associated with causing disease in humans of varying severity."
- 要点：二级适用于使用各类能让人患上轻重不等疾病的病原和毒素的实验室。

### E8

- 位置：Section III, Biosafety Level 2
- 原文："Work done with any human, animal, or plant-derived specimens (e.g., blood, body fluids, tissues, or primary cell lines), where the presence of a biological agent or toxin may be unknown, can often be safely conducted under conditions typically associated with BSL-2."
- 要点：使用人、动物或植物来源的样品（血液、体液、组织、原代细胞系），而不知道其中是否有病原或毒素时，通常可以在二级条件下安全操作。

### E9

- 位置：Section III, Biosafety Level 3
- 原文："Biosafety Level 3 (BSL-3) standard practices, safety equipment, and facility specifications are applicable to laboratories in which work is performed using indigenous or exotic biological agents with a potential for respiratory transmission and those that may cause serious and potentially lethal infection."
- 要点：三级适用于可能经呼吸道传播、可能造成严重甚至致命感染的病原。

### E10

- 位置：Section III, Biosafety Level 4
- 原文："Biosafety Level 4 (BSL-4) standard practices, safety equipment, and facility specifications are applicable primarily for laboratories working with dangerous and exotic biological agents that pose a high individual risk of life-threatening disease that may be transmitted via the aerosol route and for which there is no available vaccine or therapy."
- 要点：四级主要适用于危险的外来病原：个人感染后危及生命的风险高、可经气溶胶传播、没有疫苗或疗法。

### E11

- 位置：Section III, Biosafety Levels
- 原文："It is important to note that the four Biosafety Levels described below are not to be confused and equated with Agent Risk Groups as described in the National Institutes of Health Guidelines for Research Involving Recombinant or Synthetic Nucleic Acid Molecules (NIH Guidelines). [...] However, just because a biological agent is listed as a Risk Group 3 agent, it does not mean the activities conducted with that biological agent must occur in a BSL-3 laboratory."
- 要点：生物安全等级不能和 NIH 准则里病原的"风险组"混为一谈；一种病原属于风险组 3，不等于相关操作一定要在三级实验室做。

### E12

- 位置：Section III, Biosafety Levels
- 原文："For example, handling large volumes or high concentrations of a biological agent or toxin may require additional practices outlined in Sections IV and V of BMBL."
- 要点：处理大体积或高浓度的病原或毒素，可能需要额外的防护措施。

### E13

- 位置：Section I, Introduction
- 原文："Reading only selected sections will not adequately prepare even an experienced laboratory worker to handle potentially infectious agents safely."
- 要点：只读其中几节，即使是有经验的实验人员，也不足以做好安全处理潜在感染性病原的准备。
