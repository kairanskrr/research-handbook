# IEEE Author Center：Post-Publication Policies

- 资料：`ieeeauthorposting`，[原文](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-journal-author/publishing-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/post-publication-policies/)
- 版本：IEEE 期刊作者中心网页，2026-10-06 读取。位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Article Sharing and Posting Policies — Preprint
- 原文："Authors who have submitted or plan to submit their articles to IEEE may retain their posted preprints in the following locations:" / "arXiv.org, TechRxiv.org, or any not-for-profit preprint server approved by the Publication Services and Products Board (PSPB)" / "IEEE does not consider this to be a form of prior publication."
- 要点：投 IEEE 期刊前或投稿后，作者可以把预印本留在 arXiv、TechRxiv 等认可的非营利预印本服务器上，IEEE 不把这算作"已发表"。

### E2

- 位置：Article Sharing and Posting Policies — Author-submitted article
- 原文："Upon acceptance, the previously posted article must be replaced with either (1) the full citation to the IEEE work with the DOI, or (2) the accepted version of the article with the DOI and an appropriate copyright notice"
- 要点：论文被录用后，之前公开的版本要换成带 DOI 的完整引用，或换成带 DOI 和版权声明的录用版本。

### E3

- 位置：Article Sharing and Posting Policies — Published article (VOR)
- 原文："For articles that are not published under an open access license and that use the standard IEEE Copyright Form, the author may not post the VOR online"
- 要点：非开放获取、签了标准 IEEE 版权表的论文，作者不能在网上公开 IEEE 排版的最终正式版（Version of Record，VOR）。
