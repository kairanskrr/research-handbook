# Thurston 1994：On proof and progress in mathematics

- 资料：`thurston1994proof`，[原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9404236)
- 版本：Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society 30(2):161–177，1994；arXiv 版 math/9404236v1。

### E1

- 位置：§1 开头
- 原文："This question brings to the fore something that is fundamental and pervasive: that what we are doing is finding ways for people to understand and think about mathematics."
- 要点：作者认为数学家在做的事，根本上是找到让人理解和思考数学的方法，而不只是证明定理。

### E2

- 位置：§1，四色定理的例子
- 原文："Rather, it reflected a continuing desire for human understanding of a proof, in addition to knowledge that the theorem is true."
- 要点：围绕计算机证明四色定理的争议，反映的是人们除了想知道定理成立，还想理解证明。

### E3

- 位置：§3 How is mathematical understanding communicated?
- 原文："Nonetheless, most of the audience at an average colloquium talk gets little of value from it."
- 要点：作者观察到，一般的学术报告会（colloquium），大部分听众收获不多。

### E4

- 位置：§3
- 原文："When a significant theorem is proved, it often (but not always) happens that the solution can be communicated in a matter of minutes from one person to another within the subfield."
- 要点：在同一个子领域内，重要定理的证明思路常常几分钟就能当面讲清楚。

### E5

- 位置：§3
- 原文："One-on-one, people use wide channels of communication that go far beyond formal mathematical language."
- 要点：一对一交流时，人们用的沟通方式远超正式的数学语言。

### E6

- 位置：§4 What is a proof?
- 原文："I didn't really understand what a "proof" was. By going to seminars, reading papers, and talking to other graduate students, I gradually began to catch on."
- 要点：作者读研时并不明白"证明"是什么，是通过参加讨论班、读论文、和其他研究生交流慢慢明白的。

### E7

- 位置：§4
- 原文："You look at other papers in the field, and you see what facts they quote without proof, and what they cite in their bibliography."
- 要点：看领域里其他论文不加证明直接引用哪些事实、引用哪些文献，就能知道这个领域公认的基础。

### E8

- 位置：§4
- 原文："There were published theorems that were generally known to be false, or where the proofs were generally known to be incomplete."
- 要点：有些已发表的定理，圈内都知道是错的或证明不完整。

### E9

- 位置：§4
- 原文："People are usually not very good in checking formal correctness of proofs, but they are quite good at detecting potential weaknesses or flaws in proofs."
- 要点：人不太擅长检查证明的形式正确性，但很擅长发现证明中可能的薄弱处和漏洞。

### E10

- 位置：§4
- 原文："The kind of change I would advocate is that mathematicians take more care with their proofs, making them really clear and as simple as possible so that if any weakness is present it will be easy to detect."
- 要点：作者主张把证明写得清楚、尽量简单，让任何薄弱处都容易被发现。

### E11

- 位置：§4
- 原文："It's just that the reliability does not primarily come from mathematicians formally checking formal arguments; it comes from mathematicians thinking carefully and critically about mathematical ideas."
- 要点：数学结果的可靠性主要不是来自逐条检查形式论证，而是来自数学家仔细、批判地思考其中的想法。

### E12

- 位置：§5 What motivates people to do mathematics?
- 原文："That's not true: the social setting is extremely important."
- 要点：数学研究不是纯粹的个人活动，社会环境极其重要。

### E13

- 位置：§5
- 原文："I think that our strong communal emphasis on theorem-credits has a negative effect on mathematical progress."
- 要点：作者认为数学界过于看重"定理归属的功劳"，对数学进步有负面影响。

### E14

- 位置：§5
- 原文："The people who see the way to proving theorems are doing it in the context of a mathematical community; they are not doing it on their own."
- 要点：证明定理的人是在数学共同体中做到的，不是独自完成的。

### E15

- 位置：§3
- 原文："We mathematicians need to put far greater effort into communicating mathematical ideas. To accomplish this, we need to pay much more attention to communicating not just our definitions, theorems, and proofs, but also our ways of thinking."
- 要点：数学家要更努力地交流想法，不只交流定义、定理和证明，也要交流思考方式。
