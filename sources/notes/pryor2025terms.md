# Pryor：Philosophical Terms and Methods

### E1

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / What Is an Argument?
- 原文："An important thing to remember is that even if a given argument is bad, its conclusion might still be true"
- 要点：一个论证不好，不等于它的结论是假的。

### E2

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / An Argument's Structure
- 原文："When you're arguing, you'll usually take certain theses for granted (these are the premises of your argument) and attempt to show that if one accepts those premises, then one ought also to accept the argument's conclusion."
- 要点：论证就是把某些论点当作前提，说明接受这些前提的人也应当接受结论。

### E3

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / Different Flavors of Argument
- 原文："Mathematical proofs are good examples of this. Most of the arguments philosophers concern themselves with are also — or purport to be — arguments of this flavor."
- 要点：演绎论证（前提真则结论必真）的典型是数学证明；哲学家处理的论证大多也是、或自称是这一类。

### E4

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / What Makes a Deductive Argument Good?
- 原文："Are its premises true and worth accepting?" / "Does its conclusion really follow from the premises?" / "These are completely independent issues."
- 要点：评价演绎论证要问两个彼此独立的问题：前提是否为真、值得接受；结论是否真的从前提推出。

### E5

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / Valid Arguments
- 原文："To tell whether an argument is valid, figure out what the form of the argument is, and then try to think of some other argument of that same form and having true premises but a false conclusion. If you succeed, then every argument of that form must be invalid."
- 要点：检验有效性的办法：找出论证形式，再找一个同形式、前提真而结论假的论证；找到了，这个形式就无效。

### E6

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / Implicit or Hidden Premises
- 原文："Sometimes a premise is left out because the author takes it to be obvious, as in the engineer argument and the exploding car argument. But sometimes the missing premise is very contentious, as in the abortion argument."
- 要点：没写出来的前提，有时只是作者觉得显然，有时却非常有争议。

### E7

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / Sound Arguments
- 原文："An argument is sound just in case it's valid and all its premises are in fact true."
- 要点：可靠（sound）= 有效 + 所有前提实际为真。

### E8

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / Sound Arguments
- 原文："When you're doing philosophy, it is never enough simply to say that you disagree with someone's conclusion, or that their conclusion is wrong. If your opponent's conclusion is wrong, then there must be something wrong with their argument, and you need to say what you think it is."
- 要点：只说不同意对方结论不够；结论错，论证就一定有地方错，要指出错在哪。

### E9

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / Persuasive Arguments
- 原文："Their premises should be uncontentious — or at least, less contentious than the argument's conclusion."
- 要点：有说服力的论证，前提应当没有争议，至少比结论争议小。

### E10

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / Persuasive Arguments
- 原文："In any event, an argument's premises should be credible independently of its conclusion — in the sense that it'd be possible for someone to reasonably accept the premises even if they had not already accepted the conclusion."
- 要点：前提要能独立于结论而可信：一个还没接受结论的人也能合理地接受前提。

### E11

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / Begging the Question
- 原文："A good rule of thumb is the following: if an argument contains a premise or step that would not be accepted by a reasonable person who is initially prone to doubt the argument's conclusion, then the argument begs the question."
- 要点：判断"窃取论题"（循环论证）的经验法则：如果某个前提或步骤，一个起初倾向怀疑结论的讲理的人不会接受，论证就窃取了论题。

### E12

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / Conclusive or Not?
- 原文："often (perhaps always) we won't be in positions where the premises of an argument are absolutely indubitable and immune to philosophical challenge. So although we're working with deductive arguments, these arguments usually won't decisively settle the debates we're considering."
- 要点：哲学论证的前提很少绝对无可置疑，所以即使是演绎论证，通常也不能一锤定音。

### E13

- 位置：What is the Philosophical Method? / What do philosophers do?
- 原文："They try to unearth hidden assumptions in their opponents' arguments. They try to show that their opponents' views are inconsistent or lead to absurd results"
- 要点：哲学家会挖出对方论证里隐藏的假设，或说明对方观点不一致、会导出荒谬结果。

### E14

- 位置：What is the Philosophical Method? / Appeals to authority/popularity and the burden of proof
- 原文："Where the burden of proof lies will sometimes depend on the "dialectical situation," that is, on the kind of debate the parties are having."
- 要点：举证责任在谁，有时取决于"论辩情境"，即双方在进行哪种争论。

### E15

- 位置：What is the Philosophical Method? / Appeals to authority/popularity and the burden of proof
- 原文："a mere statement that the renowned Professor X holds a certain position carries no argumentative weight of its own."
- 要点：仅仅说明某位著名教授持有某个立场，本身没有论证分量。

### E16

- 位置：What is the Philosophical Method? / Shifting the burden of proof
- 原文："Arguments by analogy often exemplify such shifts in the burden of proof, because they are hostage to unnoticed or unacknowledged disanalogies."
- 要点：类比论证受制于没注意到或没承认的"不相似之处"。

### E17

- 位置：What is the Philosophical Method? / Charity
- 原文："We should exercise "charity" when interpreting our opponent's arguments. That is, we should try to understand them in the strongest versions we can — even if that requires helping the original authors out in some ways."
- 要点：宽容原则：按对方论证最强的版本去理解，必要时帮原作者补强。

### E18

- 位置：What is the Philosophical Method? / Charity
- 原文："We can help our own view the most by engaging with and responding to the strongest arguments we know the other side can give."
- 要点：回应对方能给出的最强论证，对自己的观点帮助最大。

### E19

- 位置：Distinctions and Definitions / Equivocating
- 原文："Equivocation is a bad form of argument where some of the key terms can be understood in multiple ways, and the plausibility of the argument depends on reading the term differently in different places."
- 要点：偷换概念（equivocation）：关键词有多种理解，论证看起来成立只是因为在不同地方读成了不同意思。

### E20

- 位置：Distinctions and Definitions / (Different Kinds of) Definitions
- 原文："Another kind of activity philosophers can go in for is stipulating that they're going to use some expression in a specific way, which might not be the way other people use the word, or understand it in other contexts." / "It's important not to confuse these."
- 要点：除了分析我们已有的概念，哲学家也可以"约定"一个词在本文中的用法；两种活动不要混淆。

### E21

- 位置：Distinctions and Definitions / (Different Kinds of) Definitions
- 原文："She wants to know what we're thinking and talking about when we think and talk about belief and death. She's seeking an analysis of our pre-existing concepts."
- 要点：问"什么是信念""什么是死亡"时，要的不是约定，而是对已有概念的分析。

### E22

- 位置：Distinctions and Definitions / Thought-Experiments and Counter-Examples
- 原文："One way we test analyses is by trying to come up with counter-examples"
- 要点：检验一个概念分析的办法之一是找反例。

### E23

- 位置：Distinctions and Definitions / Thought-Experiments and Counter-Examples
- 原文："Perhaps it's not in fact technologically possible to freeze a person and revive him again. This is not important." / "So if it's possible even in principle for someone to be frozen, and for his biological processes to stop, without his thereby dying, then Professor Smith's proposed analysis is wrong."
- 要点：分析声称对一切可能情形成立，所以只要原则上可能的反例就能推翻它，技术上做不到无关紧要。

### E24

- 位置：Distinctions and Definitions / Thought-Experiments and Counter-Examples
- 原文："We should recognize and acknowledge that such intuitions aren't sacrosanct. Sometimes they're wrong." / "But in general, there is a presumption that our pre-theoretical intuitions are true, and we should throw them out only if we have very strong reasons for doing so."
- 要点：直觉不神圣，有时会错；但一般推定理论之前的直觉为真，只有理由很强时才抛弃。

### E25

- 位置：Distinctions and Definitions / Thought-Experiments and Counter-Examples
- 原文："you haven't in fact described a coherent possibility, and so you haven't succeeded in offering a genuine counter-example to his claim." / "Sometimes it can be hard to tell whether you've described a coherent possibility or not."
- 要点：描述的情形本身不融贯（如"圆的正方形"），就不是真正的反例；有时很难判断是否融贯。

### E26

- 位置：Distinctions and Definitions / Thought-Experiments and Counter-Examples
- 原文："In general, we want to distinguish between questions about what it is to be X and questions about how we find out that something is X."
- 要点：要区分"什么是 X"和"怎样查出某物是 X"两类问题。

### E27

- 位置：What is the Philosophical Method? / Working from the middle
- 原文："In philosophy, there's hardly anything that's really immune to questioning. And the more supporting weight you want some assumption to bear, the more ways philosophers will find to challenge it."
- 要点：哲学里几乎没有不受质疑的东西；你让一个假设承担的分量越大，别人找到的质疑办法就越多。

### E28

- 位置：What is the Philosophical Method? / Working from the middle
- 原文："I usually won't mean your objection must be fatal to the argument. Maybe the argument can be fixed up in some way to work around it."
- 要点："好的反驳"不一定是致命的；论证也许可以修补绕过它。

### E29

- 位置：Philosophical Arguments / Sound Arguments（引自其 Philosophical Glossary）
- 原文："You should not say that Locke has proven some claim, or shown or demonstrated that something is the case, unless you think that Locke's arguments for his claim are successful."
- 要点：除非你认为论证成功，否则不要说某人"证明了"某事，应写"论证了""试图证明"。
