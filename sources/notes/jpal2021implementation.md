# J-PAL 研究资源：Implementation monitoring

- 资料：`jpal2021implementation`，[原文网页](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/resource/implementation-monitoring)
- 版本：J-PAL 研究资源页，作者 Michael Gibson，2021 年 2 月更新；2026 年 10 月访问。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："If aspects of the program are not implemented correctly, researchers may falsely conclude that the program had no effect, when in reality, the program wasn’t actually implemented as designed."
- 要点：项目没有按设计执行，研究者可能误以为项目没有效果。

### E2

- 位置：Selecting indicators
- 原文："Research teams should consider a few key indicators that focus on things the implementing partner can control."
- 要点：监测时选少数几个关键指标，聚焦实施伙伴能控制的环节。

### E3

- 位置：Indicators to monitor
- 原文："Compliance can be monitored by comparing a list of respondents who were treated to a list of respondents who were randomly assigned to the treatment group."
- 要点：把实际接受干预的名单和随机分到处理组的名单对照，就能监测依从情况。

### E4

- 位置：Indicators to monitor
- 原文："Evaluation-driven effects occur when respondents change their behavior in response to the evaluation instead of the intervention."
- 要点：受访者因为"被评估"而不是因为干预本身改变行为，叫评估驱动效应。

### E5

- 位置：Indicators to monitor
- 原文："To inform ex-post analysis and reporting, thoroughly document any threats to internal validity that were found during the course of the intervention."
- 要点：干预期间发现的内部效度威胁都要详细记录，供事后分析和报告。

### E6

- 位置：Indicators to monitor
- 原文："To inform ex-post generalizability and scaling-up collect data on the costs of the intervention and contextual details."
- 要点：为判断结果能否推广，要收集干预成本和背景信息。

### E7

- 位置：Monitoring methods
- 原文："they are most useful when research staff show up unannounced—and can induce Hawthorne effects (Evans, 2014)."
- 要点：实地走访不打招呼时最有用，但也可能引发霍桑效应（被观察而改变行为）。
