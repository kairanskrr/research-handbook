# Open Science Collaboration 2015：Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science

- 资料：`osc2015estimating`，[原文 PDF（邓迪大学机构库，作者接受稿）](https://discovery.dundee.ac.uk/ws/files/7385883/RPP_SCIENCE_2015.pdf)
- 版本：发表于 Science 349(6251)，aac4716。这里用的是同行评审后的接受稿，页码以稿件页眉"Reproducibility Project N"为准。

### E1

- 位置：摘要，第 2 页
- 原文："We conducted replications of 100 experimental and correlational studies published in three psychology journals using high-powered designs and original materials when available. Replication effects were half the magnitude of original effects, representing a substantial decline."
- 要点：项目重做了 3 本心理学期刊上的 100 项实验和相关研究，用统计功效高的设计、尽量用原材料。重做得到的效应量平均只有原研究的一半。

### E2

- 位置：摘要，第 2 页
- 原文："Ninety-seven percent of original studies had significant results. Thirty-six percent of replications had significant results"
- 要点：原研究 97% 结果显著，重做后只有 36% 显著。

### E3

- 位置：引言，第 3 页
- 原文："A direct replication may not obtain the original result for a variety of reasons: Known or unknown differences between the replication and original study may moderate the size of an observed effect, the original result could have been a false positive, or the replication could produce a false negative."
- 要点：重做没得到原结果，原因可能有三种：两次研究的条件有差别、原结果是假阳性、重做的结果是假阴性。所以一次重做失败不等于原研究一定错了。

### E4

- 位置：Method，第 4 页
- 原文："In total, 100 replications were completed by 270 contributing authors."
- 要点：100 项重做由 270 位作者合作完成，是心理学大型协作项目的例子。

### E5

- 位置：摘要，第 2 页
- 原文："Correlational tests suggest that replication success was better predicted by the strength of original evidence than by characteristics of the original and replication teams."
- 要点：能不能重做成功，更多取决于原研究证据本身有多强，而不是原团队或重做团队的特点。

### E6

- 位置：Sampling frame and study selection，第 6 页
- 原文："On average, the to-be-replicated articles contained 2.99 studies"
- 要点：被选中的论文平均每篇包含约 3 项研究。心理学论文常在一篇里报告多项研究。
