# von Elm et al. 2007：The Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology (STROBE) Statement

- 资料：`vonelm2007strobe`，[PLoS Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.0040296)（CC BY）
- 版本：PLoS Med 4(10): e296，2007。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Randomised trials cannot answer all important questions about a given intervention. For example, observational studies are more suitable to detect rare or late adverse effects of treatments, and are more likely to provide an indication of what is achieved in daily medical practice"
- 要点：随机试验回答不了所有问题；观察性研究更适合发现罕见或迟发的不良反应，也更能反映日常医疗中的实际效果。

### E2

- 位置：Aims and Use of the STROBE Statement
- 原文："The STROBE Statement is a checklist of items that should be addressed in articles reporting on the 3 main study designs of analytical epidemiology: cohort, case-control, and crosssectional studies."
- 要点：STROBE 是一份清单，适用于分析流行病学的三种主要设计：队列研究、病例对照研究、横断面研究。

### E3

- 位置：Aims and Use of the STROBE Statement
- 原文："The intention is solely to provide guidance on how to report observational research well: these recommendations are not prescriptions for designing or conducting studies. Also, while clarity of reporting is a prerequisite to evaluation, the checklist is not an instrument to evaluate the quality of observational research."
- 要点：STROBE 只管怎样报告，不规定怎样设计和实施研究，也不是评价研究质量的工具。

### E4

- 位置：STROBE Components
- 原文："The STROBE Statement is a checklist of 22 items that we consider essential for good reporting of observational studies"
- 要点：STROBE 清单共 22 项。
