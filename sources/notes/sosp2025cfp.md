# SOSP 2025：Call for Papers

- 资料：`sosp2025cfp`，[原文](https://sigops.org/s/conferences/sosp/2025/cfp.html)
- 版本：第 31 届 ACM 操作系统原理研讨会（SOSP 2025）征稿启事，2026-10-08 读取。位置写网页小标题。只作为系统方向一个顶级会议某一年的例子。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："In keeping with SOSP tradition, we will favor work that explores new territory, continues a significant research dialogue, or reflects on experience with or measurements of state-of-the-art implementations."
- 要点：按 SOSP 传统，偏爱开拓新领域、延续重要研究对话，或反思最先进实现的使用经验和测量的工作。

### E2

- 位置：Submission Criteria
- 原文："Propose and implement an interesting, compelling solution;" / "Demonstrate the practicality and benefits of the solution;" / "Be accessible to the broader systems community."
- 要点：好论文要提出并实现一个有说服力的方案，证明它实用、有好处，并让更广的系统社区读得懂。

### E3

- 位置：Submission Criteria
- 原文："We encourage submission of groundbreaking work in significant new directions, with the understanding that the evaluation criteria for papers addressing new problems may be different from those continuing a line of work in a more established area."
- 要点：鼓励开创新方向的工作；研究新问题的论文，评价标准可能不同于延续成熟方向的论文。

### E4

- 位置：Author Response Period
- 原文："Authors must limit their responses to (a) correcting factual errors in the reviews or (b) directly addressing questions posed by reviewers. In particular, responses must not include new experiments or data, describe additional work completed since submission, or promise additional work to follow."
- 要点：作者回复只能纠正审稿中的事实错误或直接回答审稿人的问题，不能加新实验或数据，也不能承诺后续工作。

### E5

- 位置：格式要求
- 原文："Submissions may have at most 12 pages of technical content, including all text, figures, tables, etc. Bibliographic references are not included in the 12-page limit."
- 要点：技术内容最多 12 页（含图表），参考文献不计。

### E6

- 位置：Anonymity
- 原文："If your submission reports on experiences with a system at your institution or organization, you should refer to the system anonymously but describe the properties of the system that are needed to appreciate the work (e.g., size of the user base, volume of requests, etc.)."
- 要点：报告本单位系统的经验时，系统名要匿名，但要描述理解工作所需的系统特征，如用户规模、请求量。

### E7

- 位置：录用
- 原文："Papers selected by the program committee will receive conditional acceptance, with the final acceptance subject to revisions and approval by a program committee member acting as a shepherd." / "encouraged to supply source code and raw data to help others replicate and understand their results, as part of an artifact evaluation process."
- 要点：入选论文先有条件录用，经指导人认可修改后才最终录用；鼓励作者通过制品评估提供源代码和原始数据。

### E8

- 位置：Anonymity
- 原文："If the name of your project or system is already known to the community (e.g., through arXiv, technical reports, talks, social media posts, or other uses), your SOSP submission must use an anonymized name."
- 要点：项目或系统的名字已在社区公开过，投稿时必须换匿名名字。

### E9

- 位置：开头
- 原文："SOSP takes a broad view of systems and solicits contributions from many fields of systems practice, including: operating systems, file and storage systems, distributed systems, cloud computing, mobile and edge systems, secure and reliable systems, systems aspects of big data and machine learning"
- 要点：SOSP 对"系统"取广义，征集操作系统、文件和存储系统、分布式系统、云计算、移动和边缘系统、安全可靠的系统、大数据和机器学习的系统问题等方向的工作。
