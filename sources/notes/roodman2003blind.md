# Roodman 2003：Blind Analysis in Particle Physics

- 资料：`roodman2003blind`，[原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0312102)
- 版本：PHYSTAT2003 会议论文（SLAC），arXiv:physics/0312102v1。作者后来与 Klein 合写了综述 Klein & Roodman 2005（Annual Review of Nuclear and Particle Science），那篇没有找到确定合法的开放全文，本项目改用这篇会议论文。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："A blind analysis is a measurement which is performed without looking at the answer. Blind analyses are the optimal way to reduce or eliminate experimenter's bias, the unintended biasing of a result in a particular direction."
- 要点：盲分析就是不看答案做测量，是减少"实验者偏差"（无意中把结果推向某个方向）的最好办法。

### E2

- 位置：§2 Experimenter's Bias
- 原文："Experimenter's bias is defined as the unintended influence on a measurement towards prior results or theoretical expectations."
- 要点：实验者偏差指测量无意中被推向以前的结果或理论预期。

### E3

- 位置：§2 Experimenter's Bias
- 原文："If the cuts are set with knowledge of how that choice affects the results, experimenter's bias could occur."
- 要点：如果在知道选择条件（cut）会怎样影响结果的情况下设定它，就可能产生实验者偏差。

### E4

- 位置：§2 Experimenter's Bias
- 原文："The numerical result, and how well it agrees with prior measurements or the Standard Model, contains no real information about the internal correctness of the measurement."
- 要点：结果的数值以及它和以前测量或标准模型符不符合，不能说明测量本身做得对不对。

### E5

- 位置：§1 Introduction，Dunnington 的例子
- 原文："A notable early use of a blind analysis in physics was in a measurement of the e/m of the electron, by Dunnington [...] Dunnington asked his machinist to arbitrarily choose an angle [...] Only when the analysis was complete, and Dunnington was ready to publish a result, did he accurately measure the hidden angle."
- 要点：早期例子：Dunnington 测电子荷质比时，让机械师随意设定一个关键角度，直到分析完成、准备发表时才去精确测量它。

### E6

- 位置：§3.1 Hidden Signal Box
- 原文："The hidden signal box technique explicitly hides the signal region until the analysis is completed."
- 要点："隐藏信号框"方法：分析完成前，把信号可能出现的区域藏起来不看。

### E7

- 位置：§3.2 Hidden Answer
- 原文："For precision measurements of parameters, a different technique for avoiding bias must be used. In this case, hiding the answer is often the appropriate method."
- 要点：精密测量参数时，常用的办法是隐藏答案（比如给结果加一个未知的偏移量）。

### E8

- 位置：§3.4 Other Blind Methods
- 原文："Other kinds of analyses are difficult to adapt to the methods described."
- 要点：并不是所有分析都容易套用这些盲分析方法。

### E9

- 位置：§3.4 Other Blind Methods
- 原文："One method is to fix the analysis on a sub-sample of the data, and then used the identical method on the full data sample."
- 要点：另一种办法：先在一小部分数据上把分析方法定死，再原样用到全部数据上。

### E10

- 位置：§4 Conclusion
- 原文："While the collaboration had initial reservations about the blind analysis technique, it has now become a standard method for BABAR"
- 要点：BABAR 合作组起初对盲分析有保留，后来把它变成了标准做法。
