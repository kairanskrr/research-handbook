# CABI Agriculture and Bioscience：Evidence Synthesis 投稿要求

- 资料：`cabiagbioevidencesynthesis`，[原文](https://cabiagbio.biomedcentral.com/submission-guidelines/preparing-your-manuscript/evidence-synthesis)
- 版本：CABI 出版的开放获取期刊《CABI Agriculture and Bioscience》投稿指南中"证据综合"（Evidence Synthesis）文章类型的说明。原网址现在跳转到出版社的期刊主页，原文经互联网档案馆（Internet Archive）2025 年 5 月 27 日的快照读取。

### E1

- 位置：Evidence Synthesis，文章类型说明
- 原文："These differ from traditional literature reviews in that they should report on structured evidence syntheses (such as systematic reviews or systematic maps) that follow an a-priori protocol."
- 要点：这类文章和传统文献综述不同：报告的是按事先定好的方案做的结构化证据综合，例如系统综述或系统地图。

### E2

- 位置：Evidence Synthesis，稿件要求
- 原文："All evidence synthesis submissions must be accompanied by a 'RepOrting standards for Systematic Evidence Syntheses' (ROSES) form."
- 要点：所有证据综合投稿都必须附一份 ROSES 表格。

### E3

- 位置：Evidence Synthesis，Abstract 中的 Protocol registration
- 原文："We expect systematic reviews and maps to have followed an a-priori protocol (methodology) and recommend that this is made publically available prior to starting your evidence synthesis." / "agriRxiv, the preprint service for agriculture and allied sciences, for example, accepts evidence synthesis protocols."
- 要点：期刊希望系统综述和系统地图按事先的方案做，并建议在开始前公开方案；例如农业预印本平台 agriRxiv 接收证据综合的方案。
