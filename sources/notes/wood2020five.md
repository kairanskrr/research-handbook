# Wood 2020：Five tips for starting (and continuing) a PhD

- 资料：`wood2020five`，[原文](https://blogs.ed.ac.uk/ellie-wood/2020/09/04/five-tips-for-starting-and-continuing-a-phd/)
- 版本：爱丁堡大学博客，2020-09-04

### E1

- 位置：Tip 1（阅读笔记）
- 原文："It’s useful to keep track of what you’ve been reading because you won’t remember all of it, but you will want to come back to a lot of it."
- 要点：记录读过的东西，因为你记不住全部，但以后会经常回头查。

### E2

- 位置：Tip 4（利用读博的灵活性）
- 原文："What things do you want to do outside of your PhD (sports, reading non-PhD-related books, joining local clubs and groups, always protecting weekends off) to actively maintain a healthy work-life balance (which is better for both your wellbeing, and the state of your thesis)?"
- 要点：主动安排研究之外的生活（运动、读闲书、参加社团、保护周末），保持工作与生活的平衡，这对身心和论文都更好。

### E3

- 位置：Tip 4
- 原文："Are there times when you’re going to be working extra hard (like fieldwork)? How do you want to balance that with rest and recuperation afterwards"
- 要点：预见到要特别辛苦的阶段（如野外调查），提前想好之后怎么休息恢复。

### E4

- 位置：Tip 5（和其他博士生交流）
- 原文："help you feel connected to and supported by your colleagues and peers."
- 要点：和其他博士生交流，能让你感到与同伴相连、被支持。
