# Song & Chung 2010：Observational Studies: Cohort and Case-Control Studies

- 资料：`song2010observational`，[PubMed Central 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2998589/)
- 版本：Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery 126(6): 2234–2242，2010（PMC 上的作者稿）。面向整形外科医生的方法入门文章，例子来自外科。

### E1

- 位置：引言
- 原文："in an observational study, the investigator does not intervene and rather simply “observes”"
- 要点：观察性研究里，研究者不施加干预，只是"观察"。

### E2

- 位置：COHORT STUDIES
- 原文："Cohort studies are particularly advantageous for examining rare exposures because subjects are selected by their exposure status. Additionally, the investigator can examine multiple outcomes simultaneously. Disadvantages include the need for a large sample size and the potentially long follow-up duration of the study design resulting in a costly endeavor."
- 要点：队列研究按暴露选人，特别适合研究罕见暴露，还能同时看多个结局；缺点是样本量大、随访可能很长，成本高。

### E3

- 位置：CASE-CONTROL STUDIES, Study Design；Table 4
- 原文："Case-control studies are well suited to investigate rare outcomes or outcomes with a long latency period because subjects are selected from the outset by their outcome status." / "Rates of disease in exposed and unexposed individuals cannot be determined"
- 要点：病例对照研究一开始就按结局选人，适合研究罕见结局或潜伏期长的结局；但算不出暴露组和非暴露组的发病率。

### E4

- 位置：COHORT STUDIES（与病例系列的区别）
- 原文："Yet, unless a second comparative group serving as a control is present, these studies are defined as case-series."
- 要点：没有第二个作对照的比较组，只跟踪一组人的研究是病例系列，不是队列研究。

### E5

- 位置：Methodological Issues, Selection of Subjects in Cohort Studies
- 原文："A critical characteristic of subject selection is to have both the exposed and unexposed groups be selected from the same source population" / "Subjects who are not at risk for developing the outcome should be excluded from the study."
- 要点：队列研究选人的关键：暴露组和非暴露组要来自同一个源人群；不可能发生该结局的人应排除。

### E6

- 位置：Attrition Bias (Loss to follow-up)
- 原文："Any systematic differences related to the outcome or exposure of risk factors between those who drop out and those who stay in the study must be examined, if possible, by comparing individuals who remain in the study and those who were loss to follow-up or dropped out."
- 要点：要尽可能比较留下的人和失访的人，检查两者在结局或暴露方面有没有系统差异。

### E7

- 位置：Selection of Cases
- 原文："In a case-control study, it is imperative that the investigator has explicitly defined inclusion and exclusion criteria prior to the selection of cases." / "most likely cases from the hospital sample will represent a more severe form of the disease than those in the community."
- 要点：选病例之前必须明确定好纳入和排除标准；从医院选的病例，病情往往比社区里的病例更重。

### E8

- 位置：Selection of Controls
- 原文："The precision of the findings may improve by having up to about three or four controls per case."
- 要点：每个病例配到大约三四个对照，结果的精度可能提高。

### E9

- 位置：Bias in Case-Control Studies
- 原文："A good study design will implement a standardized interview in a non-judgemental atmosphere with well-trained interviewers to reduce interviewer bias."
- 要点：用训练有素的访谈者、在不带评判的氛围中做标准化访谈，可以减少访谈者偏倚。

### E10

- 位置：COHORT STUDIES（回顾性队列）
- 原文："The primary disadvantage of this study design is the limited control the investigator has over data collection. The existing data may be incomplete, inaccurate, or inconsistently measured between subjects."
- 要点：回顾性队列的主要缺点是研究者对数据收集几乎没有控制，现有数据可能不完整、不准确，或各人测量方式不一致。
