# Centre for Reviews and Dissemination：PROSPERO 网站说明页（2023 年存档）

- 资料：`crd2023prospero`，[PROSPERO](https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/prospero/)；本文读的是 Internet Archive 2023 年 1 月 19 日快照：http://web.archive.org/web/20230119125846/https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/prospero/
- 版本：快照页面包含首页、"About PROSPERO"、登记须知和登记资格自查页面的文字。PROSPERO 网站之后改版，现行页面需要运行脚本才能显示，未能读取；现行规则以官网为准。

### E1

- 位置：Registering a review on PROSPERO
- 原文："The aim is to provide a comprehensive listing of systematic reviews registered at inception, to help avoid unplanned duplication. By promoting transparency in the process and enabling comparison of reported review findings with what was planned in the protocol PROSPERO also aims to minimise the risk of bias in systematic review."
- 要点：PROSPERO 收录刚启动时就登记的系统综述，目的是避免无计划的重复；并且让人可以对照"计划做的"和"最后报告的"，以减少偏倚。

### E2

- 位置：Registering a review on PROSPERO
- 原文："PROSPERO has been developed and is managed by the Centre for Reviews and Dissemination (CRD) at the University of York and is funded by the UK's National Institute for Health Research (NIHR)."
- 要点：PROSPERO 由英国约克大学评价与传播中心建设和管理，英国国家卫生研究院资助。

### E3

- 位置：Inclusion criteria
- 原文："PROSPERO includes details of any planned or on-going systematic review that has a health related outcome."
- 要点：PROSPERO 收录有健康相关结局的、计划中或进行中的系统综述。

### E4

- 位置：Requirements for registration
- 原文："A full protocol should be ready before registering with PROSPERO" / "Submissions must be made before data extraction commences (from October 2019)"
- 要点：登记前要写好完整方案；必须在开始数据提取之前提交（2019 年 10 月起）。

### E5

- 位置：Integrity of data and prospective registration
- 原文："Reviews should ideally be registered before screening against eligibility criteria commences."
- 要点：最好在按纳入标准筛选文献之前就登记。

### E6

- 位置：Requirements for registration，PROSPERO does not accept
- 原文："Systematic reviews without an outcome of clear relevance to the health of humans" / "Scoping reviews" / "Literature reviews that use a systematic search" / "Systematic reviews assessing sports performance as an outcome"
- 要点：不收：结局与人类健康没有明确关系的综述、范围综述、只是用了系统检索的文献综述、以运动成绩为结局的综述等。

### E7

- 位置：登记资格自查页
- 原文："PROSPERO will not accept registrations unless there is more than one person conducting the review."
- 要点：综述团队少于两人，不予登记。

### E8

- 位置：登记资格自查页
- 原文："We therefore do not accept registrations from systematic reviews that will not be made available to others e.g. projects that are internal to an organization or company, or masters dissertations if it is known that these will not be shared."
- 要点：结果不会公开的综述不予登记，例如机构内部项目、已知不会公开的硕士论文。

### E9

- 位置：首页通知
- 原文："Students: We do not (have the resources to) register reviews done as part of training courses, modules or other 'mini' reviews."
- 要点：作为培训课程、课程模块作业的"迷你"综述，不予登记。

### E10

- 位置：When to register your review
- 原文："Do not register too early. Your systematic review protocol should be complete before you submit your registration request." / "Registering reviews that are never performed is unhelpful to the research community and may discredit the research team."
- 要点：不要登记得太早：方案写完再登记；登记了却不做，对学界无益，也会损害团队声誉。

### E11

- 位置：Other considerations
- 原文："To avoid duplication of records, Cochrane protocols should not be registered separately with PROSPERO."
- 要点：Cochrane 综述的方案会自动上传，不要再单独登记。
