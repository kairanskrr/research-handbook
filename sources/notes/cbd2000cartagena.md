# 《生物多样性公约》秘书处 2000：Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety to the Convention on Biological Diversity: Text and Annexes

- 资料：`cbd2000cartagena`，[原文 PDF](https://www.cbd.int/doc/legal/cartagena-protocol-en.pdf)
- 版本：《卡塔赫纳生物安全议定书》英文文本，2000 年 1 月 29 日通过，2003 年 9 月 11 日生效；秘书处 2000 年蒙特利尔出版的文本单行本。位置写条款号。

### E1

- 位置：Article 1
- 原文："the objective of this Protocol is to contribute to ensuring an adequate level of protection in the field of the safe transfer, handling and use of living modified organisms resulting from modern biotechnology that may have adverse effects on the conservation and sustainable use of biological diversity, taking also into account risks to human health, and specifically focusing on transboundary movements."
- 要点：议定书的目标：对可能危害生物多样性的现代生物技术改性活生物体，在安全转移、处理和使用方面确保足够的保护，同时考虑对人体健康的风险，重点是跨境转移。

### E2

- 位置：Article 2.1
- 原文："Each Party shall take necessary and appropriate legal, administrative and other measures to implement its obligations under this Protocol."
- 要点：各缔约方要采取法律、行政等措施履行议定书义务。

### E3

- 位置：Article 3(g)
- 原文："“Living modified organism” means any living organism that possesses a novel combination of genetic material obtained through the use of modern biotechnology;"
- 要点：改性活生物体指通过现代生物技术获得了新的遗传物质组合的任何活生物体。

### E4

- 位置：Article 3(i)
- 原文："“Modern biotechnology” means the application of: a. In vitro nucleic acid techniques, including recombinant deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA) and direct injection of nucleic acid into cells or organelles, or b. Fusion of cells beyond the taxonomic family, that overcome natural physiological reproductive or recombination barriers and that are not techniques used in traditional breeding and selection;"
- 要点：现代生物技术指体外核酸技术（包括重组 DNA、把核酸直接注入细胞），或超出科的细胞融合；它们突破自然的繁殖或重组屏障，不是传统育种技术。

### E5

- 位置：Article 3(b)
- 原文："“Contained use” means any operation, undertaken within a facility, installation or other physical structure, which involves living modified organisms that are controlled by specific measures that effectively limit their contact with, and their impact on, the external environment;"
- 要点："封闭使用"指在设施或其他实体结构内进行、用具体措施有效限制改性活生物体接触和影响外部环境的操作。

### E6

- 位置：Article 6.2
- 原文："the provisions of this Protocol with respect to the advance informed agreement procedure shall not apply to the transboundary movement of living modified organisms destined for contained use undertaken in accordance with the standards of the Party of import."
- 要点：运往进口方、按进口方标准封闭使用的改性活生物体，跨境转移时不适用"事先知情同意"程序。

### E7

- 位置：Article 7.1
- 原文："Subject to Articles 5 and 6, the advance informed agreement procedure in Articles 8 to 10 and 12 shall apply prior to the first intentional transboundary movement of living modified organisms for intentional introduction into the environment of the Party of import."
- 要点：改性活生物体首次跨境转移、目的是有意释放到进口方环境中的，事先要走"事先知情同意"程序。

### E8

- 位置：Article 18.2(b)
- 原文："Living modified organisms that are destined for contained use clearly identifies them as living modified organisms; and specifies any requirements for the safe handling, storage, transport and use, the contact point for further information, including the name and address of the individual and institution to whom the living modified organisms are consigned; and"
- 要点：运往封闭使用的改性活生物体，随附文件要清楚标明它是改性活生物体，写明安全处理、储存、运输和使用的要求，以及收货个人和机构的名称、地址等联系信息。
