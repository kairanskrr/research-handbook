# Mahoney 2005：Clarifying Comparative-Historical Methodology

- 资料：`mahoney2005clarifying`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.998194)
- 版本：Qualitative Methods（美国政治学会定性方法分会通讯，后改名 Qualitative & Multi-Method Research）3(1), pp. 19–22，2005 年春季；Zenodo 开放存档（CC BY-NC-ND 4.0）。作者当时在美国布朗大学。这是对 Kittel 一文的回应，代表作者个人观点。位置写通讯页码。

### E1

- 位置：第 20 页
- 原文："Comparative-historical analysis stands between these two alternatives; it is a mode of research in which scholars compare multiple cases in the effort to formulate causal generalizations about those cases."
- 要点：比较历史分析介于单案例研究和大样本研究之间：比较多个案例，以概括出关于这些案例的因果结论。

### E2

- 位置：第 20 页
- 原文："Rather, the generalizations are bounded by particular scope conditions that define comparable cases."
- 要点：这些因果概括不是普遍规律，而是受"适用范围条件"限定，这些条件规定了哪些案例可以拿来比较。

### E3

- 位置：第 21 页
- 原文："Still, if one is working with a very small N (e.g., N = 2 or 3), then one would have to draw probabilistic inferences by relying primarily on within-case analysis rather than cross-case comparison."
- 要点：案例很少（例如两三个）时，推断主要靠案例内部的分析，而不是案例之间的比较。

### E4

- 位置：第 20 页
- 原文："In the field of comparative historical analysis, substantive knowledge accumulation has occurred across diverse areas, including studies of revolutions, welfare states, and democracy and authoritarianism"
- 要点：比较历史分析在革命、福利国家、民主与威权等多个课题上积累了实质性知识。
