# Kramer、Guillory & Hancock 2014：Experimental evidence of massive-scale emotional contagion through social networks

- 资料：`kramer2014experimental`，[原文（PubMed Central）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4066473/)
- 版本：《美国国家科学院院刊》（PNAS）第 111 卷第 24 期，第 8788–8790 页，DOI 10.1073/pnas.1320040111。该文后来附有编辑关注声明，见 [verma2014editorial](verma2014editorial.md)。

### E1

- 位置：Significance
- 原文："We show, via a massive (N = 689,003) experiment on Facebook, that emotional states can be transferred to others via emotional contagion, leading people to experience the same emotions without their awareness."
- 要点：研究在 Facebook 上对 689,003 人做了实验。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："In an experiment with people who use Facebook, we test whether emotional contagion occurs outside of in-person interaction between individuals by reducing the amount of emotional content in the News Feed."
- 要点：实验的处理是减少用户动态消息（News Feed）里带情绪的内容。

### E3

- 位置：Materials and Methods
- 原文："As such, it was consistent with Facebook’s Data Use Policy, to which all users agree prior to creating an account on Facebook, constituting informed consent for this research."
- 要点：作者认为，实验符合所有用户注册时同意的 Facebook 数据使用政策，这就构成了对本研究的知情同意。
