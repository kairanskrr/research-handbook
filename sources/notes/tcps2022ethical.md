# 加拿大三大研究资助机构 2022：Tri-Council Policy Statement: Ethical Conduct for Research Involving Humans（TCPS 2 (2022)）

- 资料：`tcps2022ethical`，[第 2 章](https://ethics.gc.ca/eng/tcps2-eptc2_2022_chapter2-chapitre2.html)、[第 6 章](https://ethics.gc.ca/eng/tcps2-eptc2_2022_chapter6-chapitre6.html)
- 版本：加拿大卫生研究院（CIHR）、自然科学与工程研究理事会（NSERC）、社会科学与人文研究理事会（SSHRC）联合发布的《三理事会政策声明》2022 年版，加拿大政府研究伦理网站 ethics.gc.ca 官方网页，2026-10-10 读取。

### E1

- 位置：第 2 章，Article 2.1，Application
- 原文："Pilot studies fall within this Policy's definition of research requiring REB review. For the purposes of this Policy, pilot studies are smaller versions of the main study (e.g., fewer participants, shorter duration)."
- 要点：在这份政策里，预研究（正式研究的缩小版，参与者更少、时间更短）属于需要研究伦理委员会（REB）审查的研究。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 章，Article 2.1，Application
- 原文："Undertaking pilot studies in research is distinct from the initial exploratory phase of research, which may involve contact with individuals or communities, but which does not require REB review"
- 要点：预研究不同于研究最初的探索阶段；探索阶段可能接触个人或社区，但不需要伦理委员会审查。

### E3

- 位置：第 6 章，Article 6.11
- 原文："Researchers shall submit their research proposals, including proposals for pilot studies, for REB review and approval of their ethical acceptability prior to the start of recruitment of participants, data collection, access to data or collection of human biological materials. REB review is not required for the initial exploratory phase, which is intended to establish research partnerships or to inform the design of a research proposal, and may involve contact with individuals or communities."
- 要点：研究者必须在招募参与者、收集数据之前，把研究方案（包括预研究方案）交伦理委员会审查批准；为建立合作关系或为设计方案做的初步探索不需要审查。

### E4

- 位置：第 6 章，Article 6.11，Application，Pilot Studies
- 原文："The level of REB review for pilot studies should follow a proportionate approach to research ethics review"
- 要点：预研究的审查级别按"相称审查"的原则定，即风险越低，审查可以越简化。

### E5

- 位置：第 6 章，Article 6.12
- 原文："In keeping with a proportionate approach to research ethics review, the selection of the level of REB review shall be determined by the level of foreseeable risks to participants: the lower the level of risk, the lower the level of scrutiny (delegated review); the higher the level of risk, the higher the level of scrutiny (full board review)."
- 要点："相称审查"指按参与者可预见的风险大小定审查级别：风险越低，审查越简化（委托审查）；风险越高，审查越严格（全体委员会审查）。
