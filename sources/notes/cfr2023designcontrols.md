# 美国联邦法规 21 CFR 820.30 Design controls（2023 年版，现已删除）

- 资料：`cfr2023designcontrols`，[原文 PDF](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2023-title21-vol8/pdf/CFR-2023-title21-vol8-sec820-30.pdf)
- 版本：美国《联邦法规汇编》（Code of Federal Regulations，CFR）第 21 编第 820.30 节，2023 年 4 月 1 日版，来自美国政府出版局 govinfo 网站。这是旧的质量体系法规中的"设计控制"条款；新的质量管理体系法规实施后，现行版本中 §820.30 已标为"保留"（[Reserved]），见 `cfr2026qmsr`。位置写条款项号。

### E1

- 位置：§820.30(a)
- 原文："Each manufacturer of any class III or class II device, and the class I devices listed in paragraph (a)(2) of this section, shall establish and maintain procedures to control the design of the device in order to ensure that specified design requirements are met."
- 要点：旧规定要求 III 类、II 类器械和部分 I 类器械的制造商建立设计控制程序，确保达到规定的设计要求。

### E2

- 位置：§820.30(c) Design input
- 原文："Each manufacturer shall establish and maintain procedures to ensure that the design requirements relating to a device are appropriate and address the intended use of the device, including the needs of the user and patient."
- 要点：设计输入：设计要求要恰当，覆盖器械的预期用途，包括使用者和病人的需要。

### E3

- 位置：§820.30(e) Design review
- 原文："The procedures shall ensure that participants at each design review include representatives of all functions concerned with the design stage being reviewed and an individual(s) who does not have direct responsibility for the design stage being reviewed, as well as any specialists needed."
- 要点：每次设计评审都要有相关各方的代表，还要有一位不直接负责这一阶段的人，以及需要的专家。

### E4

- 位置：§820.30(f) Design verification
- 原文："Design verification shall confirm that the design output meets the design input requirements."
- 要点：设计验证：确认设计输出满足设计输入要求。

### E5

- 位置：§820.30(g) Design validation
- 原文："Design validation shall ensure that devices conform to defined user needs and intended uses and shall include testing of production units under actual or simulated use conditions."
- 要点：设计确认：确保器械符合规定的用户需要和预期用途，要在实际或模拟使用条件下测试生产出来的器械。

### E6

- 位置：§820.30(i) Design changes
- 原文："Each manufacturer shall establish and maintain procedures for the identification, documentation, validation or where appropriate verification, review, and approval of design changes before their implementation."
- 要点：设计变更在实施前要识别、记录、确认（或验证）、评审和批准。

### E7

- 位置：§820.30(j) Design history file
- 原文："The DHF shall contain or reference the records necessary to demonstrate that the design was developed in accordance with the approved design plan and the requirements of this part."
- 要点：设计历史文件（Design History File，DHF）要包含或指向必要的记录，证明设计是按批准的计划和法规要求完成的。
