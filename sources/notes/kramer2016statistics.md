# Kramer、Paparozzi、Stroup 2016：Statistics in a Horticultural Journal: Problems and Solutions

- 资料：`kramer2016statistics`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.21273/JASHS03747-16)
- 版本：Journal of the American Society for Horticultural Science 141(5): 400–406，2016，CC BY-NC-ND 开放获取。作者逐篇检查了该刊 2014 年全部各期和 2015 年第 1 期共 86 篇论文的统计问题。原文文本取自 Internet Archive 保存的出版社网页。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Slightly fewer than half appeared to have problems."
- 要点：被检查的 86 篇论文里，略少于一半看起来有统计问题。

### E2

- 位置：Results and Discussion，Statistical problems
- 原文："The most common problem (30 articles) was inappropriate analysis of data from multiple dependent variables on the same unit of observation."
- 要点：最常见的问题（30 篇）是：在同一个观测单位上测了多个指标，却没有恰当地分析它们。

### E3

- 位置：Results and Discussion，Statistical problems
- 原文："In 11 cases, inspection of the figures revealed an obvious relationship between the mean and variance. Typically, the variance increased with larger means, yet the statistical analysis used a method that requires the assumption of no mean-variance relationship. This suggests a larger problem of failure to verify assumptions."
- 要点：11 篇从图上就能看出方差随均值增大，却用了要求"方差和均值无关"的方法，说明没有检查统计假定。

### E4

- 位置：Results and Discussion，Statistical problems
- 原文："The second most frequent problem listed in Table 2 , (seven instances) concerned inconsistencies between how the data were described (the study design) and how they were analyzed. For example, there may have been constraints on the randomization of the observations, such as blocking in a randomized complete block design, by locations (plots of land) or by occasions (different years), but the analysis used a method that failed to account for these sources of variation."
- 要点：设计里有区组、地点、年份这些对随机化的限制，分析时却没有把这些变异来源放进模型（7 篇）。

### E5

- 位置：Results and Discussion，Statistical problems
- 原文："This requires a confidence interval, or at least a properly estimated standard error of the difference (not a standard error of the mean—they are not interchangeable)."
- 要点：要说明处理差多少，需要置信区间，至少要有正确估计的"差值的标准误"，它和"均值的标准误"不能互换。

### E6

- 位置：Results and Discussion，Statistical problems
- 原文："In many common designs (e.g., any design with blocking), there is no straightforward way to determine the standard error of the difference from the standard error of the mean."
- 要点：在很多常见设计（例如任何有区组的设计）里，没法从均值标准误直接推出差值标准误。

### E7

- 位置：Results and Discussion，Software packages
- 原文："Authors and reviewers should recognize that statistical software is a means of implementing a statistical analysis, not a statistical method in itself."
- 要点：统计软件只是实现分析的工具，本身不是统计方法。

### E8

- 位置：Results and Discussion，Underlying reasons
- 原文："One good example of this is the concept and implementation of random effects, such as blocks and studies at multiple locations or occasions. These factors were typically modeled as fixed effects until software became available in the early 1990s to model them correctly as random effects."
- 要点：区组、多地点、多次重复的试验这类因素，过去通常当固定效应处理，直到 20 世纪 90 年代初有了软件，才能正确地当随机效应建模。

### E9

- 位置：Results and Discussion，Underlying reasons
- 原文："In other cases, reviewers whose knowledge on statistics is dated may provide well-meaning, but inappropriate feedback regarding statistical aspects of the manuscript being reviewed, or, even worse, incorrectly reject the manuscript thinking the statistics are flawed when in fact they are legitimate, just not understood by the reviewer."
- 要点：统计知识过时的审稿人，可能给出好心但不恰当的意见，甚至误以为统计有错而拒稿，其实方法是对的，只是审稿人不了解。

### E10

- 位置：Recommendations，Collaboration
- 原文："plan before you plant! This is crucial: the statistical thinking that goes into planning a study—before any data are collected—whether it is a formally designed experiment, a survey, or an observational study, is the most important use of statistics in research."
- 要点：种之前先计划。收集数据之前做研究计划时的统计思考，是统计在研究中最重要的用途。

### E11

- 位置：Results and Discussion，Software packages
- 原文："Sometimes a method was given, but the software used was clearly not capable of implementing the analysis described (e.g., use of SAS PROC GLM to analyze data with random model effects)."
- 要点：有的论文写了方法，但所用软件过程根本做不了这种分析，例如用 SAS 的 PROC GLM 分析含随机效应的数据。

### E12

- 位置：Methods
- 原文："Eighty-six articles from JASHS (all issues in 2014 plus issue 1 in 2015) were examined to characterize the kinds of statistical methodology used and associated problems."
- 要点：检查了该刊 2014 年全部各期加 2015 年第 1 期，共 86 篇论文。

### E13

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："we suggest that researchers in this field actively seek out opportunities to improve and update their statistical knowledge throughout their careers and engage a statistician as a collaborator early when unfamiliar methods are needed to design or analyze a research study."
- 要点：作者建议园艺研究者在整个职业生涯中不断更新统计知识；设计或分析需要不熟悉的方法时，尽早请统计学者作为合作者。
