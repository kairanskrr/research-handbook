# Schuurmans et al. 2026：Stirring the Debate: How Mixing Influences Reproducibility and Efficiency in Synthetic Organic Chemistry

- 资料：`schuurmans2026stirring`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.5c01825)（[PubMed Central 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12871880/)）
- 版本：ACS Central Science 2026, 12(1), 7 起，观点文章，CC BY 4.0 开放获取。作者来自阿姆斯特丹大学、埃因霍温理工大学、马斯特里赫特大学。原文文本取自 Europe PMC 全文 XML。

### E1

- 位置：搅拌与加料一节
- 原文："Standardizing experimental protocols is therefore critical for reproducibility. Authors should explicitly report stirring conditions, vessel geometries, and addition techniques, as these can profoundly affect outcomes."
- 要点：作者应明确报告搅拌条件、容器形状和加料方式，它们可能严重影响结果。

### E2

- 位置：搅拌与加料一节
- 原文："This is why slow, controlled, dropwise additions are a common practice in organic chemistry: they limit local concentration spikes, allow heat to dissipate gradually, and help maintain selectivity."
- 要点：慢慢滴加能避免局部浓度过高、让热量逐渐散掉、保持选择性。

### E3

- 位置：搅拌与安全一节
- 原文："Inadequate agitation can lead to localized hotspots, potentially causing solvent boiling, decomposition, or runaway reactions."
- 要点：搅拌不足会造成局部过热，可能导致溶剂沸腾、分解甚至反应失控。

### E4

- 位置：磁力搅拌器位置一节
- 原文："Ananikov and co-workers revealed that even when conducting identical reactions side by side on the same stir plate, differences in vial position and height can significantly affect outcome."
- 要点：文中介绍：Ananikov 等人发现，同一个搅拌器上并排做同样的反应，瓶子的位置和高度不同就会明显影响结果。

### E5

- 位置：5. Conclusions
- 原文："However, as systems increase in complexity, involve multiple phases, or scale to larger volumes, mixing becomes a decisive factor influencing not only the reaction rate and selectivity but also safety and reproducibility."
- 要点：体系变复杂、有多相或放大后，混合会成为影响反应速率、选择性、安全和可重复性的决定因素。
