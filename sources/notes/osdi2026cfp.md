# OSDI '26：Call for Papers

- 资料：`osdi2026cfp`，[原文](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26/call-for-papers)
- 版本：第 20 届 USENIX 操作系统设计与实现研讨会（OSDI '26，2026 年 7 月）征稿启事，2026-10-08 读取。位置写网页小标题。只作为系统方向一个顶级会议某一年的例子。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："OSDI emphasizes innovative research and quantified or insightful experiences in systems design and implementation."
- 要点：OSDI 看重创新研究，以及系统设计和实现中经过量化或有洞见的经验。

### E2

- 位置：Submitting a Paper
- 原文："Submissions will be judged on novelty, significance, interest, clarity, relevance, and correctness. An excellent paper will:" / "Motivate a significant problem" / "Propose an interesting and compelling solution" / "Demonstrate the practicality and benefits of the solution"
- 要点：评审看新颖性、重要性、趣味性、清晰度、相关性和正确性；好论文要提出重要问题、给出有说服力的方案，并证明方案实用、有好处。

### E3

- 位置：Deadline and Submission Instructions
- 原文："Papers should be the right length, which may be less than 12 single-spaced pages. Reviewers will be encouraged to down-rank overly padded papers. However, papers of 6 pages or less are unlikely to receive full consideration."
- 要点：投稿上限 12 页；鼓励长度合适，注水的论文会被压低评分，但 6 页及以下的论文不太可能得到充分考虑。

### E4

- 位置：Submission Tracks
- 原文："OSDI '26 offers two reviewing tracks, the Research track (comparable to previous OSDIs) and the Operational Systems track." / "Submissions to the Operational Systems track should describe the design, implementation, analysis, and experience of operational systems." / "We encourage submissions that disprove or strengthen existing assumptions, deepen the understanding of existing problems, and validate known techniques at new scales or in new environments."
- 要点：设研究和"运行系统"两个审稿轨道；运行系统轨道写已部署系统的设计、实现、分析和经验，欢迎推翻或加强已有假设、在新规模或新环境下验证已知技术的工作。

### E5

- 位置：开头，本届主要变化
- 原文："Removes the author response period" / "Replaces the revise-and-resubmit process with a conditional accept decision"
- 要点：OSDI '26 取消了作者回复期，并用"有条件录用"取代了修改重投。

### E6

- 位置：Submitting a Paper，预印本
- 原文："technical reports, talks, and social media posts, is permitted. However, your OSDI submission must use an anonymized name for your project or system that differs from any used in such contexts."
- 要点：允许在 arXiv、技术报告、报告和社交媒体上先公开，但投稿里的项目或系统要换一个匿名名字。

### E7

- 位置：Deadline and Submission Instructions，补充材料
- 原文："PC members are not required to read supplementary material when reviewing the paper, so each paper should stand alone without it."
- 要点：审稿人不必读补充材料，论文本身必须自成一体。

### E8

- 位置：有条件录用
- 原文："This decision indicates a heavyweight shepherding process in which a paper will be rejected unless the authors make a set of changes determined by the program committee during the review period."
- 要点：有条件录用意味着要在指导人监督下完成委员会规定的修改，否则仍会被拒。
