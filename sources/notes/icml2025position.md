# ICML 2025：Call For Position Papers

- 资料：`icml2025position`，[原文](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPositionPapers)
- 版本：ICML 2025（国际机器学习会议）立场论文赛道征稿启事，页尾注明 2025-01-28 更新；2026-10-17 读取。位置写网页小标题。只代表机器学习方向这一个会议当年的规定。

### E1

- 位置：页首（ICML 2025 Position Paper CFP）
- 原文："Position papers make an argument for a viewpoint or perspective about what should be done, in contrast to research track papers, which report on advances that have already been accomplished."
- 要点：立场论文为"应该怎么做"的一种看法提出论证；研究论文则报告已经取得的进展。

### E2

- 位置：页首（ICML 2025 Position Paper CFP）
- 原文："submissions to the position paper track will be judged primarily on whether they present a compelling position that warrants greater exposure within the machine learning community (regardless of whether a reviewer agrees with the position)."
- 要点：立场论文主要看它的立场是否有说服力、值得让更多同行看到；审稿人同不同意这个立场不是评判标准。

### E3

- 位置：页首（ICML 2025 Position Paper CFP）
- 原文："The goal of this track is to highlight papers that stimulate (productive, civil) discussion on timely topics that need our community’s input." / "Controversial topics are welcome."
- 要点：这个赛道要的是能激发建设性、文明讨论的及时话题，欢迎有争议的话题。

### E4

- 位置：页首（ICML 2025 Position Paper CFP）
- 原文："Position papers should meet standard ICML expectations for scholarship, including the use of evidence and reasoning to support claims, inclusion of relevant background and context, and the attribution of others’ work via appropriate citations."
- 要点：立场论文同样要达到学术标准：用证据和推理支撑主张，交代背景，恰当引用别人的工作。

### E5

- 位置：Policies and Requirements
- 原文："The Title should state the position and start with “Position:”." / "The Abstract must identify the paper as a position paper and briefly state the position (e.g., “This position paper argues that <statement of the position>.”)" / "The Introduction must state the position, using bold text."
- 要点：标题要说出立场，摘要要用一句话说明立场，引言要用粗体写出立场。

### E6

- 位置：Policies and Requirements
- 原文："These hypothetical paper titles do state a position:" / "\"Position: Stop Research on Psychic Properties of Machine Learning\"" / "while these versions do not:" / "\"Position: A Perspective on Psychic Quantum Atelic Learning\""
- 要点：说出立场的标题是一个主张（"停止某类研究"），只点出话题的标题（"关于某话题的一种看法"）不算。

### E7

- 位置：Policies and Requirements
- 原文："The paper must include an “Alternative Views” section in the main body of the paper (not an appendix) that describes and addresses one or more viable (not strawmen) positions that are opposed to the paper’s position."
- 要点：正文必须有"不同观点"一节，描述并回应一种或多种站得住的（不是稻草人式的）反对立场。

### E8

- 位置：Policies and Requirements；Reviewing
- 原文："Papers that describe new research without advocating a position are not responsive to this call and should instead be submitted to the main paper track."
- 要点：只描述新研究、不主张某个立场的论文不属于这个赛道，应投主赛道。

### E9

- 位置：Reviewing，Position
- 原文："Examples include (but are not limited to) an argument in favor of or against a particular research direction (not a particular algorithm or solution), a call to action, a value statement, a policy proposal, or a recommendation for changes to how we conduct and evaluate research."
- 要点：立场可以是支持或反对某个研究方向（不是某个具体算法）、行动呼吁、价值主张、政策建议，或建议改变做研究和评估研究的方式。

### E10

- 位置：Reviewing
- 原文："Support: The paper supports its position with clear reasoning and evidence where appropriate." / "Significance: The paper demonstrates that the topic is important, in terms of scope, impact, timeliness, risks, benefits, etc."
- 要点：审稿标准包括"支撑"（用清楚的推理、在合适处用证据支撑立场）和"重要性"（说明话题在范围、影响、时效、风险和收益上为什么重要）。

### E11

- 位置：Reviewing
- 原文："Discussion potential: The topic is likely to inspire constructive, useful discussion within the ICML community. The reviewer need not agree with the stated position."
- 要点：审稿标准"讨论潜力"：话题可能引发建设性的讨论；审稿人不必同意论文的立场。

### E12

- 位置：Reviewing
- 原文："Communication quality: The paper is well organized and clearly written." / "Context: The paper includes a discussion of (and citations to) literature and events relevant to the stated position."
- 要点：审稿标准还有"表达质量"（组织好、写得清楚）和"背景"（讨论并引用与立场相关的文献和事件）。

### E13

- 位置：Policies and Requirements
- 原文："The formatting requirements (including 8-page limit), double-blind reviewing, policies (including plagiarism, dual submission, use of generative AI, ethics review, etc.), and deadlines for position papers are identical to those of the main conference"
- 要点：立场论文的格式（8 页上限）、双盲审稿、各项政策和截止日期都和主会议相同。

### E14

- 位置：页首（ICML 2025 Position Paper CFP）
- 原文："We encourage you to browse the position papers that were published at ICML 2024 for examples (and ideas to build on, or from which to offer an alternative position)."
- 要点：征稿启事建议先读上一年发表的立场论文，作为例子，也可以在它们的基础上提出新想法或相反的立场。
