# Patterson, Snyder & Ullman 1999：Evaluating Computer Scientists and Engineers for Promotion and Tenure

- 资料：`cra1999evaluating`，[原文](https://cra.org/resources/best-practice-memos/evaluating-computer-scientists-and-engineers-for-promotion-and-tenure/)
- 版本：美国计算研究协会（Computing Research Association，CRA）"最佳实践备忘录"，1999 年 8 月经 CRA 理事会批准，2026-10-05 读取网页版。位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："conference publication is preferred in the field, and computational artifacts —software, chips, etc. —are a tangible means of conveying ideas and insight."
- 要点：在计算机领域，会议发表更受重视；软件、芯片等"制品"本身也是传达思想的实在方式。

### E2

- 位置：Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field
- 原文："The reason conference publication is preferred to journal publication, at least for experimentalists, is the shorter time to print (7 months vs 1-2 years), the opportunity to describe the work before one’s peers at a public presentation, and the more complete level of review (4-5 evaluations per paper compared to 2-3 for an archival journal)"
- 要点：实验方向偏爱会议的理由（1999 年）：发表快（7 个月对 1～2 年）、能当面向同行报告、每篇审稿意见更多（4～5 份对 2～3 份）。

### E3

- 位置：Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field
- 原文："For experimentalists conference publication is preferred to journal publication, and the premier conferences are generally more selective than the premier journals"
- 要点：对实验方向，会议优先于期刊；顶级会议通常比顶级期刊更难中。

### E4

- 位置：Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field
- 原文："Though conference publication is highly regarded in the theoretical community, there is a long tradition of completing, revising, and extending conference papers for submission and publication in archival journals."
- 要点：理论方向也看重会议，但有把会议论文补全、扩展后再投期刊的长期传统。

### E5

- 位置：Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field
- 原文："A key research tradition is to share artifacts with other researchers to the greatest extent possible."
- 要点：实验方向的一个重要传统是尽可能把制品（软件、系统等）分享给其他研究者。

### E6

- 位置：Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field（引 Fred Brooks）
- 原文："Since anyone can create something new [in a synthetic field], that alone does not establish a contribution. Rather, one must show that the creation is better."
- 要点：计算机是"人造"学科，造出新东西本身不算贡献，必须证明它更好。

### E7

- 位置：Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field
- 原文："Publication in the prestige conferences is inferior to the prestige journals only in having significant page limitations and little time to polish the paper."
- 要点：顶级会议相对顶级期刊的不足：篇幅限制严、打磨论文的时间少。
