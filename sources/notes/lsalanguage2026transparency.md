# Language（美国语言学会期刊）：Research transparency 与 Preparing your materials

- 资料：`lsalanguage2026transparency`，[原文](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/language/information/journal-policies/research-transparency)
- 版本：美国语言学会（Linguistic Society of America，LSA）旗舰期刊 *Language* 在剑桥大学出版社网站上的两页作者说明：Journal policies → Research transparency，以及 Author instructions → [Preparing your materials](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/language/information/author-instructions/preparing-your-materials)；另引 LSA 网站的 [Language 介绍页](https://www.linguisticsociety.org/lsa-publications/language/submission-guidelines)。2026-10-08 读取，页面未标版本日期。位置写网页小标题。只代表这一本期刊当前的要求。

### E1

- 位置：Research transparency，Data availability
- 原文："All manuscripts submitted to this journal must contain a Data Availability Statement , explaining where and how readers can access the materials underpinning the research published in the manuscript."
- 要点：投给 *Language* 的所有稿件都必须有"数据可得性声明"，说明读者在哪里、怎样获取支撑研究的材料。

### E2

- 位置：Research transparency，Data availability
- 原文："Whenever possible, we also encourage authors to make all data and qualitative materials underpinning their research available to readers through an appropriate repository" / "Appropriate repositories are ones that provide persistent identifiers and have robust preservation policies"
- 要点：鼓励（不是强制）尽可能把数据和定性材料放进合适的存储库；合适的存储库提供永久标识符、有可靠的保存政策。

### E3

- 位置：Research transparency，Data availability
- 原文："If you cannot make your data publicly available due to ethical or legal concerns, please explain this in your Data Availability Statement."
- 要点：因伦理或法律原因不能公开数据的，在数据可得性声明里说明。

### E4

- 位置：Research transparency，Code and model availability
- 原文："We strongly encourage authors to share any analytical code or models used for the research reported in their manuscript."
- 要点：强烈鼓励共享分析代码和模型。

### E5

- 位置：Preparing your materials，Data requirements and referencing
- 原文："Data sources should be clearly referenced and, if publicly available, cited as such"
- 要点：数据来源要清楚注明；公开的数据要正式引用。

### E6

- 位置：Preparing your materials，Data requirements and referencing（Large-scale empirical or experimental studies）
- 原文："articles reporting results of empirical studies such as quantitative analyses or experimental results should allow replication by other researchers based upon the article's contents and references, and possibly Supplementary Materials"
- 要点：报告定量分析或实验结果的文章，应能让别人凭文章内容、参考文献和补充材料重做。

### E7

- 位置：Preparing your materials，Data requirements and referencing（Articles reporting judgements）
- 原文："Authors of articles reporting judgments (e.g., acceptability or meaning judgments) should be prepared to further substantiate their judgments if called into question by referees or Editors"
- 要点：报告语感判断（如可接受度、意义判断）的作者，在审稿人或编辑质疑时要准备进一步拿出依据。

### E8

- 位置：LSA 网站 Language 介绍页
- 原文："Language is the flagship journal of the Linguistic Society of America." / "Starting in 2026, Language is a fully open access journal."
- 要点：*Language* 是美国语言学会的旗舰期刊；从 2026 年起完全开放获取。
