# 英国萨里郡 2010：萨里考古学会《发掘记录手册》

- 资料：`surrey2010excavation`，[原文（萨里考古学会官网 PDF）](https://d10.surreyarchaeology.org.uk/sites/surreyarchaeology.org.uk/files/Excavation%20Recording%20Manual.pdf)
- 版本：Surrey Archaeological Society（英国萨里考古学会），*Excavation Recording Manual*，研究委员会主席 David Bird 2010 年 5 月作序，学会官网公开 PDF（29 页）。这是一个地方考古学会给会员用的记录规范，不是全英国的统一标准。位置写手册页码和栏目名。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 页，Introduction
- 原文："The Research Committee wishes to encourage use of a common method of site recording in Surrey." / "The system which is described here has been developed and used over a number of years by Society members who consulted, amongst other sources, the Essex County Council and Museum of London Archaeology Service manuals."
- 要点：萨里考古学会希望郡内发掘使用统一的记录方法；这套系统参考了埃塞克斯郡议会和伦敦博物馆考古服务部的手册。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 页，Context Record Sheet，General Note 与 Context Number
- 原文："Where a field is listed as 'Required', it is not permitted to leave it blank. If there is no information appropriate to enter there, it must at least contain a dash or 'n/a' or ' none'." / "This will be a unique number from a sequence which may be extended from year to year and is issued from the Context Record. It is essential that no duplication of context numbers occurs. The sequence may contain gaps; batches of numbers may conveniently be reserved for different areas of the site."
- 要点：标为"必填"的栏不能空着，没有内容也要写横线、n/a 或 none；单位编号从统一的登记册领取，绝不能重复，可以有空号，也可以给不同发掘区预留一批号。

### E3

- 位置：第 2–5 页，Context Record Sheet 各栏
- 原文："Matrix description (Colour, Composition, Texture, Inclusions)" / "Stratigraphic Relationships Required" / "It is important to enter reciprocal information on all the appropriate sheets (e.g. if 1 is above 2, then 2 must also be recorded as below 1)." / "Plans; Sections/Elevations/Profiles; Sample Numbers; Photos" / "Interpretation Required"
- 要点：单位记录表上有土质描述（颜色、成分、质地、包含物）、地层关系、与平面图/剖面图/样品/照片的交叉编号、解释等栏；地层关系要在相关各张表上对应填写（1 在 2 之上，2 的表上就要写在 1 之下）。
