# 美国国家职业安全卫生研究所 2009：Approaches to Safe Nanotechnology

- 资料：`niosh2009approaches`，[原文（Internet Archive 快照）](http://web.archive.org/web/20260726080036/https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/docs/2009-125/pdfs/2009-125.pdf)
- 版本：美国国家职业安全卫生研究所（NIOSH）出版物 DHHS (NIOSH) Publication No. 2009-125，2009 年。cdc.gov 对脚本访问返回 403，读取的是 Internet Archive 2026-07-26 快照中的 PDF。

### E1

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："Engineered nanoparticles are materials purposefully produced with at least one dimension between 1 and 100 nanometers."
- 要点：工程纳米颗粒指有意制备、至少一个维度在 1–100 纳米之间的材料。

### E2

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："Until the results from research studies can fully elucidate the characteristics of nanoparticles that may pose a health risk, precautionary measures are warranted."
- 要点：在研究弄清哪些纳米颗粒特性可能危害健康之前，有必要采取预防措施。

### E3

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："Nanomaterials have the greatest potential to enter the body through the respiratory system if they are airborne and in the form of respirable-sized particles (nanoparticles)."
- 要点：纳米材料一旦飘在空气中、成为可吸入的颗粒，最可能经呼吸道进入人体。

### E4

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："Current knowledge indicates that a well-designed exhaust ventilation system with a high-efficiency particulate air (HEPA) filter should effectively remove nanomaterials."
- 要点：设计良好、带高效空气过滤器（HEPA）的排风系统应能有效去除纳米材料。

### E5

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："Examples of good practices include cleaning of work areas using HEPA vacuum pickup and wet wiping methods, preventing the consumption of food or beverages in workplaces where nanomaterials are handled, providing hand-washing facilities"
- 要点：良好做法包括：用 HEPA 吸尘器和湿擦清洁工作区，在操作纳米材料的地方禁止饮食，提供洗手设施。

### E6

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："It should be recognized that exposure limits recommended for nonnanoscale particles may not be health protective for nanoparticle exposures"
- 要点：针对非纳米颗粒制定的接触限值，对纳米颗粒未必能保护健康。

### E7

- 位置：8.3.3 Work practices
- 原文："Storing dispersible nanomaterials, whether suspended in liquids or in a dry particle form in closed (tightly sealed) containers whenever possible."
- 要点：易分散的纳米材料，不论是液体悬浮还是干粉，尽量存放在密封容器里。

### E8

- 位置：8.3.3 Work practices
- 原文："Dry sweeping or air hoses should not be used to clean work areas."
- 要点：不要用干扫或气枪吹的方式清洁工作区。

### E9

- 位置：6.1 Fire and Explosion Risk
- 原文："Decreasing the particle size of combustible materials can increase combustion potential and combustion rate, leading to the possibility of relatively inert materials becoming highly reactive in the nanometer size range."
- 要点：可燃材料粒径变小，燃烧可能性和燃烧速率会增加；相对惰性的材料到纳米尺度可能变得高度活泼。

### E10

- 位置：8.3.3 Work practices
- 原文："Avoiding handling nanomaterials in the open air in a 'free particle' state."
- 要点：避免在敞开环境中以"自由颗粒"状态操作纳米材料。
