# Shepperd 2018：Inferencing into the void: problems with implicit populations

- 资料：`shepperd2018inferencing`，[arXiv:1810.07392](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.07392)
- 版本：对 Falessi 等人 2018 的评论，收入 Feldt 等人 2018 在 Empirical Software Engineering 上的"四篇评论"；arXiv 预印本

### E1

- 位置：§2 Summary of F++
- 原文："By contrast, I, Carolyn Mair and Magne Jørgensen have recently completed a series of experiments with more than 400 professional participants [7]. Clearly the challenges vary, but equally clearly it's not impossible to conduct experiments with large sample sizes."
- 要点：作者和合作者完成过一系列有 400 多名专业人员参加的实验；招募专业人员的难度因人而异，但做大样本实验并非不可能。

### E2

- 位置：§3 First principles
- 原文："This informality also leads to an under-appreciation of the fact that in software engineering the population is not restricted to the human participants (be they professional or otherwise) but also tasks in particular settings."
- 要点：要推广的"总体"不只是参与者，还包括特定环境下的任务。

### E3

- 位置：§4 Examples
- 原文："The point from all three examples is that we should consider the representativeness of participants, tasks and setting. Equally important we need to be aware of how they interact."
- 要点：要同时考虑参与者、任务和环境的代表性，以及它们之间的相互作用。

### E4

- 位置：§5 Summary
- 原文："there is clear value in using students especially for preliminary experiments. But if the target population is some class of professional, it is hard to see why, if practically possible, it would be disadvantageous to use professional participants. The likelihood that this might entail more effort should not be a reason for not undertaking such empirical work."
- 要点：用学生做初步实验很有价值；但目标总体是某类专业人员时，只要可行，就应该用专业人员，"更费事"不应成为不做的理由。
