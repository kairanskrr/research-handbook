# Lamport 1995：How to Write a Proof

- 资料：`lamport1995how`，[原文](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/lamport-how-to-write.pdf)
- 版本：作者主页公开的 DEC 系统研究中心（Systems Research Center）报告版（1993 年 2 月，1993 年 12 月修订），正式发表于 American Mathematical Monthly 102(7), 1995。位置写节号。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Mathematical Proofs
- 原文："Anecdotal evidence suggests that as many as a third of all papers published in mathematical journals contain mistakes--not just minor errors, but incorrect theorems and proofs."
- 要点：作者引述的传闻性证据（非系统统计）：数学期刊上多达三分之一的论文含有错误，不只是小错，而是错误的定理和证明。

### E2

- 位置：§2.1 The High-Level Proof
- 原文："The proof sketch serves as a "road map" to the proof, helping the reader understand intuitively why the proof works."
- 要点：证明概要是证明的"路线图"，帮读者直观理解证明为什么成立。

### E3

- 位置：§4.1 My Experience
- 原文："I had written five pages when I realized that Kelley's proof was wrong."
- 要点：作者为教学改写一本经典拓扑教材中的半页证明，写到第五页时发现原证明是错的。

### E4

- 位置：§4.1 My Experience
- 原文："In a properly written proof, where every use of an assumption or a proof step is explicit, simple text searching reveals exactly where every hypothesis is used."
- 要点：每次使用假设都写明的证明，用文本搜索就能查到每个假设用在哪里；需要弱化假设时很方便。

### E5

- 位置：§4.2 Writing Structured Proofs
- 原文："Most errors come from not carrying out the proof to enough levels."
- 要点：大多数错误来自证明没有展开到足够细的层次。

### E6

- 位置：§4.2 Writing Structured Proofs
- 原文："My own rule of thumb is to expand the proof until the lowest level statements are obvious, and then continue for one more level."
- 要点：经验法则：一直展开到最底层的每句话都显然成立，然后再多展开一层。

### E7

- 位置：§4.2 Writing Structured Proofs
- 原文："The assertion "this case is similar to the previous one" is not acceptable; one is forced to find the appropriate general step that makes the proof of both cases easy."
- 要点："这种情况与前一种类似"不被接受；要找出能同时处理两种情况的一般性步骤。

### E8

- 位置：§4.2 Writing Structured Proofs
- 原文："I recommend that authors provide two versions of their proofs: a very detailed one for themselves, the referees, and interested colleagues; and a less detailed one for paper publication."
- 要点：建议准备两个版本的证明：给自己、审稿人和感兴趣同行的详细版，以及正式发表的简略版。

### E9

- 位置：§4.2 Writing Structured Proofs
- 原文："Structured proofs are longer than conventional ones. Although the formatting is partly responsible, structured proofs are longer mainly because they include more detail."
- 要点：结构化证明更长，主要是因为包含了更多细节，而不只是格式造成的。
