# Carletto, Gourlay & Winters 2013：From Guesstimates to GPStimates: Land Area Measurement and Implications for Agricultural Analysis

- 资料：`carletto2013guesstimates`，[原文（世界银行开放知识库）](https://hdl.handle.net/10986/15910)，DOI [10.1596/1813-9450-6550](https://doi.org/10.1596/1813-9450-6550)
- 版本：World Bank Policy Research Working Paper 6550，2013 年 7 月，许可 CC BY 3.0 IGO，2026-10-08 从世界银行开放知识库下载 PDF。工作论文（未经期刊同行评议的版本），观点只代表作者，不代表世界银行。数据来自"生活水平测量研究—农业综合调查"（LSMS-ISA）在马拉维、乌干达、坦桑尼亚、尼日尔的调查。位置写小节或摘要。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："commonly employed self-reported land area measures used in most analysis are not only potentially measured with error, but these errors may be correlated with agricultural outcomes."
- 要点：大多数分析常用的农户自报土地面积，不但可能有测量误差，而且误差可能和农业结果相关。

### E2

- 位置：§4 Conclusions
- 原文："The data indicate that small plot areas are generally over reported by farmers while larger plot areas are generally under reported."
- 要点：数据显示，农户一般多报小地块的面积，少报大地块的面积。

### E3

- 位置：§4 Conclusions
- 原文："This suggests that in assessing agricultural relationships using control variables will not address measurement error."
- 要点：分析农业关系时，加控制变量解决不了这种测量误差。

### E4

- 位置：§4 Conclusions
- 原文："The final conclusion then reinforces the arguments made by Gibson and McKenzie (2007) that GPS information should become a routine part of survey data collection since they tend to provide better measure and help to identify causal relationships."
- 要点：作者的结论是，GPS 信息应成为调查数据收集的常规部分，因为它测得更好，也有助于识别因果关系。

### E5

- 位置：§1，脚注 3
- 原文："Traversing is completed using a tape and compass and is considered the gold standard of land measurement, but its implementation is time-consuming and costly." / "Traversing is therefore infeasible in the context of national household surveys."
- 要点：用皮尺和罗盘绕地块边界测量（导线测量）被视为土地测量的金标准，但费时费钱，全国性住户调查里做不到。

### E6

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Measures employing Global Positioning Systems, on the other hand, while not perfect especially on smaller plots, are likely to provide more precise measures and errors less correlated with agricultural outcomes."
- 要点：GPS 测量尤其在小地块上并不完美，但可能更精确，误差也和农业结果关系更小。

### E7

- 位置：§1
- 原文："Meeting these objectives is possible because of recently collected, multicountry data coming out of the Living Standards Measurement Study--Integrated Surveys on Agriculture (LSMS-ISA)." / "We use data from four of the most recently completed surveys--Malawi, Uganda, Tanzania and Niger."
- 要点：研究用的是"生活水平测量研究—农业综合调查"（LSMS-ISA）在马拉维、乌干达、坦桑尼亚、尼日尔四国的数据。

### E8

- 位置：§1
- 原文："Finally, we compare the use of self-reported and GPS land measures in estimating the inverse farm-size productivity relationship to determine the implications for measurement error on agricultural analysis."
- 要点：作者比较用自报面积和 GPS 面积估计"农场越小、单位面积产量越高"这一反向关系的结果，看测量误差对农业分析的影响。
