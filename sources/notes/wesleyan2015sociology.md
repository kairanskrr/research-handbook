# 美国卫斯理大学社会学系 2015：Expectations for Promotion to Associate Professor Conferring Tenure

- 资料：`wesleyan2015sociology`，[原文 PDF](https://www.wesleyan.edu/acaf/Tenure-and-Promotion-Expectations/SOC_2015.pdf)
- 版本：美国卫斯理大学（Wesleyan University）社会学系的终身教职晋升要求，2010 年 5 月制定、2015 年 4 月修订，共 2 页。只代表这一个系的要求，用来说明"书和论文都可以作为主要成果"这种做法确实存在。位置写小节。

### E1

- 位置：Scholarship（晋升副教授）
- 原文："At the time of the tenure review, Sociology expects that the candidate will have produced a substantial, coherent body of high quality, scholarly work constituting a significant contribution to the candidate’s area of expertise. This may take the form of a book or a series of articles."
- 要点：终身教职评审时，该系期望候选人有一批分量足、成体系的高质量成果，形式可以是一本书，也可以是一系列论文。

### E2

- 位置：Scholarship（晋升副教授）
- 原文："If the major scholarly accomplishment is represented by a book, Sociology will typically expect that the book be published or accepted for publication with a well-regarded press."
- 要点：如果主要成果是一本书，该系通常期望它已在声誉好的出版社出版或被接受出版。

### E3

- 位置：Scholarship（晋升副教授）
- 原文："Candidates will also be expected to have made evident progress on a second project, as demonstrated through conference papers, public lectures, or published or accepted articles, and a well-conceived statement of a research agenda."
- 要点：候选人还应在第二个项目上有明显进展，并有一份构思清楚的研究计划陈述。
