# Czaykowska-Higgins 2009：Research Models, Community Engagement, and Linguistic Fieldwork: Reflections on Working within Canadian Indigenous Communities

- 资料：`czaykowskahiggins2009research`，[原文](http://hdl.handle.net/10125/4423)
- 版本：Language Documentation & Conservation 3(1): 15–50（开放获取）；作者是加拿大维多利亚大学的语言学者，文章基于她在加拿大原住民社区的工作经验

### E1

- 位置：§4.1 Establishing relationships
- 原文："the most important prerequisite to and sine qua non of any kind of linguistic fieldwork" [...] "is establishing and maintaining solid, respectful, reciprocal, and trusting working relationships"
- 要点：作者认为，任何语言田野工作最重要的前提，是建立并维持稳固、互相尊重、互惠和信任的工作关系。

### E2

- 位置：§4.1 Establishing relationships
- 原文："In most Canadian Indigenous communities, for instance, the elders of the community are always central in the research partnership."
- 要点：在大多数加拿大原住民社区，长者总是研究合作中的核心。

### E3

- 位置：§4.1 Establishing relationships
- 原文："consultation has to take place regularly and often"
- 要点：协商要经常、定期进行。

### E4

- 位置：§4.1 Establishing relationships
- 原文："it is possible to overlook or underestimate the need and the time needed for common sense and sensitivity."
- 要点：赶论文、赶评职称时，容易低估经营关系所需的时间。

### E5

- 位置：§4.2 Determining roles
- 原文："those members of the language-using community who work with linguists to document and describe their languages are naturally seen to be researchers and experts in their own right"
- 要点：在社区为本的语言研究中，和语言学者一起记录语言的社区成员本身就被看作研究者和专家。

### E6

- 位置：§4.2 Determining roles
- 原文："the role of a linguist should not" [...] "be that of a knight in shining armor who rides into a community to rescue it."
- 要点：语言学者不该把自己当成骑马进村、拯救社区的英雄。

### E7

- 位置：§4.3 Institutional issues
- 原文："working out an agreed-upon process for undertaking the research; determining the kinds of protocols that all partners are comfortable with"
- 要点：合作研究的成果包括商定研究流程、确定各方都接受的规程。

### E8

- 位置：§4.3 Institutional issues
- 原文："developing work plans for the various people involved in doing the research; developing Memoranda of Understanding"
- 要点：还包括给各方制定工作计划、签订谅解备忘录。
