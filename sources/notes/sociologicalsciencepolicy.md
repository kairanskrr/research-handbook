# Sociological Science：审稿流程、复现政策与期刊介绍（网页）

- 资料：`sociologicalsciencepolicy`，[Editorial and Review Process](https://sociologicalscience.com/for-authors/editorial-and-review-process/)、[Reproducibility Policy](https://sociologicalscience.com/for-authors/reproducibility-policy/)、[Journal Information](https://sociologicalscience.com/about/journal-information/)
- 版本：开放获取的社会学期刊 Sociological Science（由非营利的 Society for Sociological Science 出版）官网三个页面，2026 年 10 月 6 日读取。这是一本刻意与传统审稿方式不同的期刊，页面中对"社会学期刊的主流做法"的描述是该刊自己的看法。位置写页面名和小节。

### E1

- 位置：Editorial and Review Process，开头
- 原文："The dominant model de facto requires a majority (or sometimes even a unanimity) of reviewers to “vote” in favor of a paper before it will be published. Reviewers – who have little if any accountability to the journal or the author – can wield enormous influence over both the fate and content of papers. This practice has led to a bias toward errors of omission, to multiple rounds of revisions, and to ever-increasing review times."
- 要点：该刊认为，社会学期刊的主流模式实际上要多数（有时全体）审稿人"投票"赞成才能发表，审稿人对论文命运和内容影响很大，导致多轮修改、审稿时间越来越长。

### E2

- 位置：Editorial and Review Process，开头
- 原文："Authors will never be asked to “revise and resubmit” their papers; after the initial review, all subsequent decisions will be made by the Editorial Staff without soliciting further reviews." / "Finally, authors will be notified of the journal’s editorial decision within 30 days of submission."
- 要点：该刊从不要求"修改后重投"；初审之后由编辑决定，不再送审；投稿后 30 天内通知决定。

### E3

- 位置：Reproducibility Policy，开头
- 原文："Starting with submissions received after April 1, 2023, authors of articles relying on statistical or computational methods will be required to deposit replication packages as a condition of publication in Sociological Science. Replication packages must contain both the statistical code and — when legally and ethically possible — the data required to fully reproduce the reported results."
- 要点：自 2023 年 4 月 1 日之后收到的投稿起，用统计或计算方法的论文必须存放复现包才能发表；复现包要含统计代码，以及在法律和伦理允许时完整复现结果所需的数据。

### E4

- 位置：Reproducibility Policy，开头
- 原文："Under many legitimate circumstances, data cannot legally or ethically be made available to readers. When authors cannot make their data available, they must explain why in the main text of the paper. In such cases, making code and other materials available is still required, unless doing so would violate legal or ethical constraints."
- 要点：很多正当情况下数据不能公开；这时必须在正文里说明原因，代码和其他材料仍须公开（除非违反法律或伦理约束）。

### E5

- 位置：Reproducibility Policy，开头
- 原文："Researchers using qualitative data, such as interviews or participant observation data, are not required to submit a replication package. We encourage authors to make qualitative data available when possible, and urge them to consider whether materials such as interview protocols or coding schemes can be shared."
- 要点：使用访谈、参与观察等定性数据的研究不要求提交复现包；鼓励在可能时公开定性数据，并考虑能否共享访谈提纲、编码方案等材料。

### E6

- 位置：Reproducibility Policy，FAQ：What if authors are analyzing publicly available datasets?
- 原文："Alternatively, replication packages must include (a) a link to the official web site where the data set(s) are stored; (b) the release version and/or release date of the data the researcher used, in case the data producers make corrections later; and (c) the code necessary to extract the variables (since users often rename variables at the same time as they extract)."
- 要点：分析公开数据集时，如果不附原始数据，复现包须包括：官方数据网址、所用数据的发布版本或日期（以防数据方后来修正）、提取变量的代码。

### E7

- 位置：Reproducibility Policy，FAQ：Does this policy require researchers to document their results in a specific way?
- 原文："At the bare minimum, we strongly recommend that documentation clearly demarcate which sections of code correspond to which specific tables and figures presented in the paper."
- 要点：最起码要在文档里标明哪段代码对应论文里的哪张表、哪幅图。

### E8

- 位置：Reproducibility Policy，Policy on Findings from Experimental Data，(2) Pre-registration
- 原文："Sociological Science requires authors of papers that include originally collected experimental data and present findings from those experiments as testing hypotheses (i.e., as “confirmatory” rather than “exploratory” research) to indicate explicitly whether those experiments were pre-registered."
- 要点：自己收集实验数据、并把结果当作假设检验（验证性而非探索性）来呈现的论文，必须明确说明实验是否预注册。

### E9

- 位置：Reproducibility Policy，FAQ：Do reviewers or editors check the accuracy of the replication packages?
- 原文："No. The existence of a replication package does not imply that the package has been vetted."
- 要点：该刊不检查复现包能否复现；有复现包不等于它被审核过。

### E10

- 位置：Journal Information，Principles
- 原文："Second, since the ratio of novel insights to the number of written words should be high, Sociological Science discourages excessive citations to prior work, needless literature reviews, and tangential discussions."
- 要点：该刊要求新见解与字数之比要高，不鼓励过多引用、不必要的文献综述和离题的讨论。

### E11

- 位置：Journal Information，Principles
- 原文："Science advances through a dialogue between theory and evidence. Neither should be privileged in the editorial process. In Sociological Science, papers that primarily identify or resolve empirical puzzles have equal standing with papers that primarily address conceptual puzzles."
- 要点：科学在理论与证据的对话中前进，两者都不应被优待；主要解决经验难题的论文和主要处理概念难题的论文地位相同。
