# SODA 2025：Submissions

- 资料：`soda2025cfp`，[原文](https://www.siam.org/conferences-events/siam-conferences/soda25/submissions/)
- 版本：ACM-SIAM 离散算法研讨会（SODA 2025）投稿页面。siam.org 对脚本返回 403，本条取自 Internet Archive 2024-11-06 对官方页面的存档（https://web.archive.org/web/20241106211859/https://www.siam.org/conferences-events/siam-conferences/soda25/submissions/）。SODA 2026 的投稿页没有存档，所以用 2025 年的。位置写网页小标题。只作为理论方向一个会议某一年的例子。

### E1

- 位置：页面标题
- 原文："ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms (SODA25)"
- 要点：SODA 全称 ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms。

### E2

- 位置：Important Submission Information
- 原文："Submissions are due by 11:59 p.m. on July 5, 2024, anywhere on earth." / "There is a single submission deadline for both registering and submitting a paper; there is not a separate abstract deadline."
- 要点：2024 年 7 月 5 日截稿（按地球上最晚的时区算）；只有一个截止时间，没有单独的摘要截止。

### E3

- 位置：Submission Format
- 原文："There is no page limit and authors are encouraged to use the "full version" of their paper as the submission." / "The submission should contain within the initial ten pages following the title page a clear presentation of the merits of the paper" / "The submission should be addressed to a broad spectrum of theoretical computer science and discrete mathematics researchers." / "Proofs must be provided which can enable the main mathematical claims of the paper to be fully verified." / "material other than the abstract, references, and the first ten pages will be read at the committee's discretion"
- 要点：没有页数上限，鼓励投完整版；标题页之后的前 10 页讲清论文价值；写给广泛的理论计算机和离散数学研究者；必须提供能完整核验主要数学结论的证明；摘要、参考文献和前 10 页之外的内容，委员会自行决定读不读。

### E4

- 位置：Anonymous Submissions
- 原文："The conference will employ a fairly lightweight double-blind reviewing process." / "Nothing should be done in the name of anonymity that weakens the submission or makes the job of reviewing the paper more difficult."
- 要点：采用"相当轻量"的双盲审稿；不能为了匿名削弱论文。

### E5

- 位置：Simultaneous Submissions
- 原文："Simultaneous submissions of the results to another conference with a published proceedings is not allowed."
- 要点：不能把同样的结果同时投给另一个有论文集的会议。

### E6

- 位置：Simultaneous Submissions
- 原文："At the time that the author signs SODA's copyright transfer agreement, the author must affirm that no copyright transfer agreement permitting the publication of a similar paper in a journal or elsewhere has been signed."
- 要点：作者签 SODA 的版权转让协议时，要声明没有签过允许在期刊或别处发表相似论文的版权转让协议。
