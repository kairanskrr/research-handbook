# 查尔斯·达尔文大学 2025：Distress Protocol（模板）

- 资料：`cdu2025distress`，[原文](https://www.cdu.edu.au/files/2025-09/Distress%20Protocol%20Template.docx)
- 版本：澳大利亚查尔斯·达尔文大学（Charles Darwin University）人类研究伦理委员会公开的"情绪困扰应对方案"模板（Word 文件，2025 年 9 月上传）；模板注明最初根据 Draucker、Martsolf、Poole 2009 年的论文和原住民健康与医学研究理事会伦理委员会的模板改编。2026-10-10 读取。

### E1

- 位置：1. How will the risk be identified by the researcher?
- 原文："Is visibly upset or distressed (e.g., crying, teary, or any other noticeable change in behaviour ranging from relaxed to uncomfortable)."
- 要点：研究者要留意受访者是否明显难过或不安，例如哭泣、流泪，或从放松变得不自在。

### E2

- 位置：2. Distress Response，a) Immediate action
- 原文："Verbally acknowledge that the participant appears upset and ask them to confirm their capacity to continue." / "If the participant confirms they are triggered/upset, the researcher will offer to take a break from the interview." / "If the participant confirms they would like a break, the researcher will also remind the participant the interview can also cease." / "If the participant confirms their capacity to continue, the researcher will offer to not discuss the particular triggering subject."
- 要点：当场说出你注意到对方不舒服，问对方还能不能继续；对方确认难受，就提出暂停；暂停时提醒对方也可以结束访谈；对方能继续，就提出可以不再谈引起痛苦的话题。

### E3

- 位置：2. Distress Response，b) Referrals
- 原文："Refer participants to appropriate community-based support services, mental health professionals, or crisis support lines."
- 要点：把受访者转介到合适的社区支持服务、心理健康专业人员或危机热线。

### E4

- 位置：3. What will the researcher do if the interview is ended early?；4. What will the researcher do if the interview stops at the normal stage?
- 原文："The researcher will cease the interview and will follow up with the participant via email to check they are no longer distressed." / "Ask the participant at the end of the interview how they are feeling." / "Follow up with the participant later that day/following morning."
- 要点：访谈提前结束时，事后发邮件确认对方已不再难受；正常结束时，也在结尾问对方感觉如何，并在当天晚些时候或第二天早上再联系一次。

### E5

- 位置：6. Documentation
- 原文："Document all instances of distress: Maintain confidential records of all instances of distress, including the date, time, nature of the distress, and the response provided."
- 要点：每次出现情绪困扰都要保密记录：日期、时间、困扰的性质、你怎样回应。

### E6

- 位置：Background；文末来源说明
- 原文："This protocol outlines procedures for addressing distress experienced by participants, researchers, and/or community members involved in research projects." / "The template was initially developed from ‘Developing Distress Protocols for Research on Sensitive Topics’ by Claire Burke Draucker, Donna S. Martsolf, and Candice Poole"
- 要点：这份模板面向一般研究项目中的参与者、研究者和社区成员，不专指口述历史；它最初根据 Draucker、Martsolf、Poole 的《为敏感话题研究制定情绪困扰应对方案》改编。
