# AERA：Publications FAQs

- 资料：`aerapublicationsfaq`，[原文](https://www.aera.net/Publications/Publications-FAQs)
- 版本：美国教育研究协会官网"出版常见问题"页面，2026 年 10 月读取。

### E1

- 位置：AERA Online Paper Repository — How can I access past conference papers?
- 原文："AERA’s Online Paper Repository is an open access clearinghouse, with abstracts, of scientific presentations given at AERA annual meetings since 2010." / "AERA does not publish conference proceedings"
- 要点：AERA 有开放获取的在线论文库，收 2010 年以来年会报告的摘要（不少附全文）；AERA 不出版会议论文集。

### E2

- 位置：AERA Online Paper Repository — Can I submit a paper I presented at the Annual Meeting to a journal for publication consideration?
- 原文："Authors retain full copyright of their work when uploading a conference paper to AERA’s Online Repository , so they are free to submit it to journals for publication consideration after the annual meeting."
- 要点：上传到论文库的年会论文，作者保留版权，会后可以投期刊。
