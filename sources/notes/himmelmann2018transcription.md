# Himmelmann 2018：Meeting the transcription challenge

- 资料：`himmelmann2018transcription`，[原文](http://hdl.handle.net/10125/24806)
- 版本：Language Documentation & Conservation Special Publication 15（Reflections on Language Documentation 20 Years after Himmelmann 1998），pp. 33–40，开放获取；作者为德国科隆大学语言学者

### E1

- 位置：§1
- 原文："Factors smaller than 10 (i.e. ten minutes are necessary to transcribe and translate one minute of recording) are rarely mentioned, and factors as high as 150 and higher are not unrealistic in the case of complex multiparty conversations."
- 要点：转写加翻译一分钟录音，很少有人说少于十分钟；复杂的多人对话，150 倍甚至更高也不算离谱。

### E2

- 位置：§1
- 原文："More often than not, segmentation units above the word (i.e. prosodic units and/or syntactic phrases) are not explicitly discussed or justified, and are thus difficult to reconstruct and evaluate for users of a documentation."
- 要点：词以上的切分单位（韵律单位、句法短语）常常不加说明，使用者难以还原和评价。

### E3

- 位置：§2
- 原文："Transcription involves interpretation and hence considerable enrichment of the acoustic signal."
- 要点：转写包含解释，会给声音信号加进大量信息。

### E4

- 位置：§2
- 原文："the tendency for the native speaker to paraphrase what is said rather than repeating it more or less directly. There is also the tendency to edit out elements typical of spoken language such as particles, hesitations and the like."
- 要点：母语者协助转写时，倾向于转述而不是照原话重复，还会删掉语气词、犹豫等口语成分。

### E5

- 位置：§2
- 原文："transcriptions of the same recording produced by different transcribers/transcription teams will differ in some details."
- 要点：不同转写人转写同一段录音，细节会不同。

### E6

- 位置：§4 脚注 6
- 原文："current practice is often wanting even on the most basic level of including the information of who was involved in the transcription"
- 要点：目前的做法常常连"谁参与了转写"这种最基本的信息都没记。

### E7

- 位置：§4
- 原文："Usually no attempt is made to include a larger group of community members in these decisions at the transcription stage."
- 要点：转写阶段的正字法等决定，通常没有请更多社区成员参与。

### E8

- 位置：§4
- 原文："The production of such materials involves standardization on many levels: determining base forms for lexical entries, orthographic conventions (e.g., how clitics are written), and so on. Many of these decisions are also part of the transcription process."
- 要点：编词典、识字课本要做很多标准化决定，例如词条的基本形式、拼写约定；其中很多决定在转写时就已经做了。
