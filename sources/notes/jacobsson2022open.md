# Jacobsson 等人 2022：An open-access database and analysis tool for perovskite solar cells based on the FAIR data principles

- 资料：`jacobsson2022open`，[原文](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41560-021-00941-3)
- 版本：Nature Energy 7, 107–115（2022；2021-12-13 在线发表），开放获取（CC BY 4.0）。

### E1

- 位置：Main
- 原文："However, most laboratories are not able to adhere to the FAIR data principles, especially in the applied science fields."
- 要点：作者认为，多数实验室做不到 FAIR 数据原则，应用科学领域尤其如此。

### E2

- 位置：Main
- 原文："However, the largest hurdle is the diversity and complexity of the datasets involved. For instance, sample properties are often influenced by the sample history."
- 要点：最大的障碍是数据多样又复杂，例如样品性质常受样品"经历"（制备和处理历史）影响。

### E3

- 位置：Details of the database
- 原文："We have manually gone through every paper found in the Web of Science with the search phrase ‘perovskite solar’ up to the end of February 2020 (that is, over 15,000 papers). In total, we have manually extracted data for over 42,400 devices."
- 要点：作者团队人工查阅了截至 2020 年 2 月 Web of Science 中检索"perovskite solar"得到的 1.5 万多篇论文，提取了 4.24 万多个器件的数据。

### E4

- 位置：Example uses of the Perovskite Database
- 原文："A problem faced while developing perovskite solar cells, which is in no way unique for the perovskite field, are cell-to-cell and batch-to-batch variations. Those can be large, thus masking otherwise statistically significant differences."
- 要点：电池之间、批次之间的差异是钙钛矿电池研究中的问题（不只这个领域有）；差异可能很大，会掩盖本来统计上显著的差别。

### E5

- 位置：Example uses of the Perovskite Database
- 原文："There are also laboratory-to-laboratory variations, and what appears to make a significant difference in one laboratory may not be relevant in another. This is usually ascribed to undescribed, unexplored, unknown or hidden parameters"
- 要点：还有实验室之间的差异：在一个实验室有显著作用的因素，换个实验室可能无关紧要；这通常被归因于没写出来、没研究过或未知的"隐藏参数"。

### E6

- 位置：Example uses of the Perovskite Database
- 原文："Those could be things such as glove box volume, precise atmospheric composition during fabrication, minor or unintended variations in precursor stoichiometry, chemical impurities and so on to mention a few hypotheses."
- 要点：作者举的可能的隐藏参数：手套箱体积、制备时的气氛成分、前驱体配比的细微或无意变化、化学杂质等（作者称之为假设）。

### E7

- 位置：Future expansion of the database
- 原文："In a typical project, there may be hundreds and sometimes thousands of devices made before the paper is written. Despite this, the average number of devices for which we could extract data was fewer than six per publication with original device data."
- 要点：一个典型项目在写论文前可能做了成百上千个器件，但在含原始器件数据的论文中，平均每篇能提取到数据的器件不到 6 个（这是作者这批文献的统计）。

### E8

- 位置：Future expansion of the database
- 原文："A common pattern is that one parameter is changed in few steps, and for each of those steps data for the best device could be found."
- 要点：常见的情况是：一个参数改几档，每一档只能找到最好那个器件的数据。

### E9

- 位置：Future expansion of the database
- 原文："Sharing data in this way gives it new life and draws additional attention to the original publication, it is a way to comply with the demands for openness more frequently seen from taxpayers, funding agencies and publishers"
- 要点：这样共享数据，能让数据被再利用、给原论文带来更多关注，也能满足纳税人、资助机构和出版方对开放的要求。
