# Vardi 2009：Conferences vs. Journals in Computing Research

- 资料：`vardi2009conferences`，[原文](https://cacm.acm.org/opinion/conferences-vs-journals-in-computing-research/)
- 版本：Communications of the ACM 52(5)，2009，主编寄语。原网站拒绝脚本访问，经互联网档案馆 2025-09-09 快照读取。

### E1

- 位置：正文第 2 段
- 原文："As far as I know, we are the only scientific community that considers conference publication as the primary means of publishing our research results."
- 要点：计算机可能是唯一一个以会议为主要发表渠道的学科。

### E2

- 位置：正文第 6 段
- 原文："The reviewing process performed by program committees is done under extreme time and workload pressures, and it does not rise to the level of careful refereeing."
- 要点：程序委员会的审稿在极大的时间和工作量压力下完成，达不到仔细审阅的水平。

### E3

- 位置：正文第 6 段
- 原文："There is some expectation that conference papers will be followed up by journal papers, where careful refereeing will ultimately take place. In truth, only a small fraction of conference papers are followed up by journal papers."
- 要点：人们期待会议论文之后再出期刊版、接受仔细审稿，但实际上只有一小部分会这样做。

### E4

- 位置：正文第 7 段
- 原文："physicists ensure fast dissemination by depositing preprints at www.arxiv.org and by having a very fast review cycle."
- 要点：快速传播不一定要靠会议：物理学家靠在 arXiv 发预印本加上快速审稿做到这一点。
