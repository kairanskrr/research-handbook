# Varian 1997：How to Build an Economic Model in Your Spare Time

- 资料：`varian1997build`，[原文 PDF](https://people.ischool.berkeley.edu/~hal/Papers/how.pdf)
- 版本：作者主页公开稿。位置写小节号。

### E1

- 位置：§1.1 Getting ideas
- 原文："I think that you should look for your ideas outside the academic journals--in newspapers, in magazines, in conversations, and in TV and radio programs."
- 要点：作者建议到期刊以外找想法：报纸、杂志、谈话、广播电视。

### E2

- 位置：§1.2 Is your idea worth pursuing?
- 原文："The first test is to try to phrase your idea in a way that a non-economist can understand."
- 要点：检验想法的第一步：能不能用外行听得懂的话说出来。

### E3

- 位置：§1.3 Don't look at the literature too soon
- 原文："Eventually you should do a thorough literature review, of course, but I think that you will do much better if you work on your idea for a few weeks before doing a systematic literature search."
- 要点：最终要做全面的文献检索，但作者建议先自己琢磨几周再系统查文献。

### E4

- 位置：§1.4 Building your model
- 原文："Lucky for you, all economics models look pretty much the same. There are some economic agents. They make choices in order to advance their objectives. The choices have to satisfy various constraints so there's something that adjusts to make all these choices consistent."
- 要点：作者说经济学模型看起来都差不多。基本结构是：有一些行为人，为了自己的目标做选择；选择受约束；有某种东西会调整，使大家的选择相互一致。

### E5

- 位置：§1.4 Building your model
- 原文："Most students think that the next stage is to prove a theorem or run a regression. No! The next stage is to work an example." / "Write down the simplest possible model you can think of, and see if it still exhibits some interesting behavior. If it does, then make it even simpler."
- 要点：先算具体例子，而不是急着证定理或跑回归；写出能想到的最简单的模型，如果它仍有有趣的现象，就再简化。

### E6

- 位置：§1.6 Making mistakes
- 原文："This process--simplify to get the result, complexify to see how general it is--is a good way to understand your model."
- 要点：先简化得到结果，再推广看它有多一般，这样来回往复是理解模型的好办法。

### E7

- 位置：§1.8 Giving a seminar
- 原文："the more you can talk [...] about your work, the better the final paper will be. This is because a talk forces you to get to the point."
- 要点：越多地讲自己的工作，最终的论文越好，因为报告逼你直奔重点。

### E8

- 位置：§1.8 Giving a seminar
- 原文："When you are done with your talk you should take a few minutes to jot down some notes: what was difficult for people to understand? What questions did they ask? What suggestions did they make? What references did they give you?"
- 要点：报告结束后马上记下：哪里听众难懂、问了什么、提了什么建议、推荐了哪些文献。

### E9

- 位置：§1.11 When to stop
- 原文："You can tell when your work is getting ready for publication by the reactions in the seminars: people stop asking questions."
- 要点：研讨会上大家不再提问时，说明工作快可以发表了。

### E10

- 位置：§1.8 Giving a seminar / Controlling the audience
- 原文："Spell out each aspect of your result in excruciating detail so no one can possibly misunderstand. When you do this you will certainly get questions like "Will this generalize to n agents?" or "Have you corrected for heteroskedasticity?""
- 要点：讲第一个结果时要把每个细节讲透，这时一定会有人当场提问；作者建议借此建立听众对你的信任，再往下讲。
