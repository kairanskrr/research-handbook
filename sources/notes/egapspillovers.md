# EGAP 方法指南：10 Things to Know About Spillovers

- 资料：`egapspillovers`，[原文网页](https://methods.egap.org/guides/data-collection/spillovers_en.html)
- 版本："治理与政治证据网络"（EGAP）方法指南，作者 Alexander Coppock；2026 年 10 月访问。位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§1 What they are
- 原文："Spillovers refer to a broad class of instances in which a given subject is influenced by whether other subjects are treated."
- 要点：溢出指一个对象受到"别的对象是否接受了干预"的影响。

### E2

- 位置：§1 What they are
- 原文："Politics: Election monitoring at some polling stations may displace fraud to neighboring polling stations." / "Economics: Lowering the cost of production for one firm may change the market price faced by other firms."
- 要点：例子：在部分投票站监督选举，舞弊可能转移到邻近投票站；降低一家企业的生产成本，可能改变其他企业面对的市场价格。

### E3

- 位置：§2 If ignored, spillovers may "bias" treatment effect estimates
- 原文："In this case, positive spillovers cause a negative bias and vice-versa. This is because when spillovers are positive, the control group mean is inflated, so the difference-in-means is smaller than it otherwise would have been."
- 要点：正向溢出抬高对照组均值，使两组差距变小，造成向下的偏差；负向溢出反之。

### E4

- 位置：§2 If ignored, spillovers may "bias" treatment effect estimates
- 原文："Often, evaluators are trying to estimate what would happen if a program were rolled out to everyone. Evidence from an RCT that ignores spillover could greatly over or underestimate the total effects of the intervention."
- 要点：评估者常想知道项目推广到所有人时会怎样；忽视溢出的随机试验可能大大高估或低估总效应。

### E5

- 位置：§3 Most experimental analyses implicitly or explicitly assume that there are no spillovers
- 原文："The assumption that there are no spillovers is known as the non-interference assumption; it is part of a somewhat more elaborate assumption sometimes referred to as the Stable Unit Treatment Value Assumption (or SUTVA)"
- 要点：大多数实验分析默认没有溢出，即"无干扰假设"，它是"稳定单位处理值假设"的一部分。

### E6

- 位置：§8 Sometimes you can avoid spillovers with "buffer rows"
- 原文："One approach to addressing the problem of spillovers is to ensure that other units’ treatment assignments cannot interfere with potential outcomes, by including “buffer rows” between experimental units."
- 要点：一种办法是在实验单位之间留出"隔离带"，让彼此的分组无法相互影响。

### E7

- 位置：§9 There are other design-based approaches for detecting spillover effects
- 原文："First, it requires the researcher to stipulate a non-interference assumption ex ante, so there can be no question of fiddling around with interference assumptions until a statistically significant result pops up."
- 要点：多层随机设计要求事先写定溢出范围的假设，避免事后反复调整假设直到出现显著结果。

### E8

- 位置：§9 There are other design-based approaches for detecting spillover effects
- 原文："As ever, the difficulty is that the non-interference assumption used in the design stage could be wrong."
- 要点：难点在于，设计时设定的溢出范围本身可能是错的。
