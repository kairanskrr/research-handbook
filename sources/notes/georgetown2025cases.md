# Georgetown Law Writing Center 2025：Using Cases in Legal Analysis: Methods and Examples

- 资料：`georgetown2025cases`，[原文 PDF（美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心，免费）](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/academics/wp-content/uploads/sites/58/2025/08/Using_Cases_in_Legal_Analysis_Methods_and_ExamplesFCD.pdf)
- 版本：美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心讲义，Alice Hsieh 2012 年初稿，Justin New 2025 年修订。写给**美国法学院学生**，背景是美国这样的普通法体系；例子多来自法律备忘录。位置写讲义的小标题。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："All of these uses of cases are relevant in a common law system where judges create binding law through cases"
- 要点：讲义说明，这些用判例的方法适用于法官通过判例创造有约束力法律的普通法体系。

### E2

- 位置：Identifying and Briefing Cases
- 原文："The briefing method is useful when selecting and preparing cases for analysis, as it allows you to dissect a case for later reassembly."
- 要点：判例摘要法（case briefing）便于挑选和准备要分析的判例：先把案件拆开，之后再重新组合。

### E3

- 位置：Identifying and Briefing Cases
- 原文："Issue presented to the court." / "Facts of the case." / "Procedural posture of the case." / "Holding, including the answer to the issue and the court's reasoning." / "Commentary, or dicta, provided by the court." / "Analytical approach or pattern used by the court."
- 要点：摘要可用的小标题：提交法院的争点；案件事实；程序阶段；裁判要旨（holding，含对争点的回答和法院的理由）；法院的附带评论（dicta）；法院采用的分析方法或模式。讲义还列了审查标准、该领域的通行规则两项。

### E4

- 位置：Identifying and Briefing Cases
- 原文："Be mindful of the particular issue you are addressing and do not be afraid to engage with your argument's weaknesses."
- 要点：挑判例时紧扣你要处理的问题，不要回避自己论证的弱点。

### E5

- 位置：Cases Interpreting Statutes
- 原文："If the statute has codified common law, then cases existing before the codification are good law and useful in interpreting the statute. If the statute has partially or wholly rejected common law, then previous cases are likely not good law, but may at least be useful in determining why the statute reads as it does."
- 要点：制定法把普通法规则编纂进来时，编纂前的判例仍有效、有助于解释；制定法部分或全部否定了普通法时，之前的判例可能不再有效，但有助于理解条文为什么这样写。

### E6

- 位置：Cases Interpreting Statutes
- 原文："If you are working with a statute, it is not enough to quote the statute and apply your case. Rather, your analysis should include the key language from case law that interprets and updates the statute."
- 要点：分析制定法，不能只引条文；要纳入解释和发展该条文的判例中的关键表述。

### E7

- 位置：Cases Used to Synthesize a Rule
- 原文："Use this approach when you need to weave together case law to articulate a rule." / "Based on these three cases, a synthesized rule might state"
- 要点：需要把多个判例编织起来、表述出一条规则时，用"综合规则"的方法；讲义举例：三个判例（两个支持请求、一个驳回）综合出一条规则。

### E8

- 位置：Cases Demonstrating Interpretive Patterns Or Legal Trends
- 原文："Because the courts may be split in how they interpret a certain area of law--with some using sociological methods, others recognizing the role of medicine in the law, others factoring in history, and so on--identifying trends can be a useful mode of argumentation."
- 要点：法院在某领域的解释可能分裂（有的用社会学方法，有的考虑医学，有的考虑历史），识别这些趋势本身可以成为论证方式。
