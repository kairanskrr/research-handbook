# 美国现代语言协会 2012：Guidelines for Evaluating Work in Digital Humanities and Digital Media

- 资料：`mla2012digital`，[原文](https://www.mla.org/About-Us/Governance/Committees/Committee-Listings/Professional-Issues/Committee-on-Information-Technology/Guidelines-for-Evaluating-Work-in-Digital-Humanities-and-Digital-Media)
- 版本：2012 年 1 月修订，2012 年 2 月经 MLA 执行委员会批准

### E1

- 位置：Document and Explain Your Work
- 原文："conference proceedings are among the most prestigious publications in computer science, whereas they are generally deemed to be a lesser form of publication in the humanities"
- 要点：会议论文集在计算机领域地位很高，在人文学科一般被看作次一等的发表。

### E2

- 位置：Document and Explain Your Work
- 原文："Faculty members who work in digital media or digital humanities should be prepared to make explicit the results, theoretical underpinnings, and intellectual rigor of their work."
- 要点：做数字人文的人要准备说清工作的成果、理论基础和学术严谨性。

### E3

- 位置：Document and Explain Your Work
- 原文："describe the process underlying creation of work in digital media (e.g., the creation of infrastructure as well as content) and their particular contributions"
- 要点：要说明数字作品的制作过程和自己的具体贡献。

### E4

- 位置：Ask about Evaluation and Support
- 原文："When candidates for faculty positions first negotiate the terms of their jobs, they should ask how credit for digital work will be considered"
- 要点：求职时就要问清数字工作在评价中怎样计算。

### E5

- 位置：页尾
- 原文："These guidelines were last revised by the Committee on Information Technology in January 2012 and approved by the MLA Executive Council at its February 2012 meeting."
- 要点：版本信息。
