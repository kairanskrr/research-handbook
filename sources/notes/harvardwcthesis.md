# 哈佛学院写作中心：Thesis

- 资料：`harvardwcthesis`，[原文](https://writingcenter.fas.harvard.edu/thesis)
- 版本：哈佛学院写作中心 Strategies for Essay Writing 系列网页，未署作者；原网址对命令行访问返回 403，经 Internet Archive 2024 年 2 月快照读取

### E1

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："A strong thesis is arguable, which means a thoughtful reader could disagree with it and therefore needs your careful analysis of the evidence to understand how you arrived at this claim."
- 要点：好论点是可争辩的：有思考的读者可能不同意，因此需要你仔细分析证据来说服。

### E2

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："If your thesis is descriptive, then you will not need to convince your readers of anything—you will be naming or summarizing something your readers can already see for themselves."
- 要点：描述性的论点不需要说服任何人，只是在概括读者自己能看到的东西。

### E3

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："If your thesis is too narrow, you won’t be able to explore your topic in enough depth to say something interesting about it. If your thesis is too broad, you may not be able to support it with evidence from the available sources."
- 要点：论点太窄，说不出有意思的东西；太宽，现有材料支撑不了。

### E4

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："When you frame that question or problem for your readers, you are telling them what is at stake in your argument—why your question matters and why they should care about the answer."
- 要点：提出问题时，就是在告诉读者论证的意义何在：问题为什么重要，读者为什么该关心答案。

### E5

- 位置：Questions to ask about your thesis
- 原文："Is the thesis supportable with evidence from the text rather than with generalizations or outside research?"
- 要点：自查：论点能否用文本证据支撑，而不是靠泛泛之论或外部研究？

### E6

- 位置：Questions to ask about your thesis
- 原文："Would anyone want to read a paper in which this thesis was developed? That is, can you explain what this paper is adding to our understanding of a problem, question, or topic?"
- 要点：自查：有人想读吗？能说清这篇文章给对某个问题的理解增加了什么吗？
