# NHERI@UTexas 2020：Safety Plan

- 资料：`nheriutexas2020safety`，[原文](https://utexas.designsafe-ci.org/media/filer_public/02/b1/02b1b9f1-9b83-4fe7-ad82-7acc4c959a50/nheriutexas_safety_plan-20200826.pdf)
- 版本：Version 2.3，2020 年 8 月。美国国家科学基金会资助的自然灾害工程研究基础设施（NHERI）在得克萨斯大学奥斯汀分校的设备站点（用大型移动激振车做现场试验）的安全手册。

### E1

- 位置：§1.1 Management Statement of Safety Policy
- 原文："Our primary research methodology incorporates the use of large-scale mobile seismic sources, or shaker trucks, in the field at research sites across the country."
- 要点：该站点的主要研究方法是在美国各地的现场使用大型移动震源（激振车）。

### E2

- 位置：§1.1 Management Statement of Safety Policy
- 原文："A typical NHERI@UTexas project is the culmination of intensive preparation over the course of months or sometimes years. Funding, organization, scheduling and myriad logistical issues can define a strictly limited window of opportunity for successful completion of the project."
- 要点：该站点的一个典型项目要经过数月、有时数年的准备；经费、组织、排期和后勤问题可能让完成项目的时间窗口非常有限。

### E3

- 位置：§1.7 Job Hazard Analysis
- 原文："Job hazard analysis is a technique that focuses on job tasks as a way to identify hazards before they occur."
- 要点：工作危害分析是一种以具体作业任务为单位、在危害发生前把它识别出来的方法。

### E4

- 位置：§2.2.1 Safety Preparation Before Field Tests
- 原文："The Principal Investigator of the user team must perform a survey of the proposed test site to identify all potential hazards of the site and the potential hazards that may be caused by the shaker before field tests, and provide the NHERI@UTexas Project Management Team (PMT) with a description of planned experiments one month before testing commences."
- 要点：现场试验前，用户团队的项目负责人必须踏勘场地，识别场地本身和激振车可能带来的危害，并在试验开始前一个月把试验计划交给站点的项目管理团队。

### E5

- 位置：§2.2.1 Safety Preparation Before Field Tests
- 原文："However, the responsibility for the design of the experiment with regards to safety of the test site and structures in and near by the test site rests on the user, and the Principal Investigator of the user team is responsible for obtaining permits for site access and site liability insurance."
- 要点：试验设计中涉及场地及周边结构安全的责任在用户一方；用户团队的项目负责人负责办理场地准入许可和场地责任保险。

### E6

- 位置：§2.2.2 Responsibility In The Field
- 原文："If vibration-sensitive structures (especially inhabited buildings) are within 50 ft of the shake point, users must consult the owner for permission to shake and are strongly encouraged to perform pre-construction and post-construction photo surveys of these structures."
- 要点：激振点 50 英尺内有对振动敏感的结构（尤其是有人住的建筑）时，必须征得业主同意，并强烈建议在试验前后给这些结构拍照留档。

### E7

- 位置：§1.12 Safety Self-Inspections
- 原文："During field operations the Chief Scientist and/or Chief Engineer will conduct a daily "tailgate meeting" safety briefing with all personnel involved in field work."
- 要点：现场作业期间，每天由首席科学家或首席工程师召集全体现场人员开安全简会（"车尾会"）。

### E8

- 位置：§1.12 Safety Self-Inspections
- 原文："will call an immediate cessation of work and hold a tailgate meeting with all personnel present to determine the corrective action required, and to modify the testing schedule accordingly. A safe and deliberate workflow must always take precedence over schedule concerns."
- 要点：出现判断失误、事故、设备故障等情况时，立即停工开会，确定纠正措施并调整试验计划；安全、稳妥的流程永远优先于进度。

### E9

- 位置：§1.12 Safety Self-Inspections
- 原文："Never make assumptions regarding safety; other people may not be aware that something is wrong or that they are being unsafe."
- 要点：不要对安全想当然；别人可能没意识到出了问题，或没意识到自己处在危险中。
