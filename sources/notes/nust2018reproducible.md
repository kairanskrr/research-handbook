# Nüst 等 2018：Reproducible research and GIScience: an evaluation using AGILE conference papers

- 资料：`nust2018reproducible`，[PMC 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6047504/)，doi:10.7717/peerj.5072
- 版本：PeerJ 6:e5072（2018），开放获取（CC BY 4.0）。作者是德国、西班牙、奥地利、荷兰等地的地理信息科学学者。研究对象是欧洲地理信息实验室协会（Association of Geographic Information Laboratories in Europe，AGILE）年会上获最佳论文提名的 32 篇论文（2010 年及 2012–2017 年），并对作者做了问卷。通过 Europe PMC 读取全文。位置写章节。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："the Association of Geographic Information Laboratories in Europe (AGILE), which has organised annual conferences on GIScience topics since 1998"
- 要点：AGILE 自 1998 年起每年举办地理信息科学（GIScience）会议。

### E2

- 位置：Materials & Methods，The paper corpus
- 原文："Since 2007, the AGILE conference has had a full paper track (cf. Pundt & Toppen, 2017) and a short paper track with blind peer review. The latter is published for free on the AGILE website."
- 要点：自 2007 年起，AGILE 会议设有长论文通道和短论文通道（原文写作 "a short paper track with blind peer review"）；短论文在 AGILE 网站免费发布。

### E3

- 位置：Materials & Methods，The paper corpus
- 原文："The corpus contains 32 documents: 20 full papers (7.9% of 253 full papers since 2007) and 12 short papers"
- 要点：评估样本共 32 篇：20 篇长论文（占 2007 年以来 253 篇长论文的 7.9%）和 12 篇短论文。

### E4

- 位置：Results，Assessment of reproducibility
- 原文："None of the papers reach the highest level of reproducibility in any category. [...] Especially problematic is the high number of papers (19) with level 0 for data, meaning that the specific data is not only unavailable but it is not re-createable from the information in the paper."
- 要点：没有一篇论文在任何一项上达到最高可复现等级；19 篇在数据一项上是 0 级，即数据既拿不到，也无法根据论文信息重新生成。

### E5

- 位置：Materials & Methods，Definition and criteria
- 原文："The level 0 unavailable means that there is insufficient documentation in the paper, or the information is only available upon request"
- 要点：评估中，"只能应要求提供"也被算作 0 级（拿不到）。

### E6

- 位置：Results，Author feedback (survey)
- 原文："Out of a total of 82 authors, 22 filled in the survey"
- 要点：82 位作者中有 22 位填写了问卷。

### E7

- 位置：Results，Author feedback (survey)
- 原文："Table 6 shows that the most frequently mentioned reasons were legal restrictions and lack of time"
- 要点：作者们最常提到的阻碍是法律限制和没有时间。

### E8

- 位置：Discussion，Improving day-to-day research in GIScience
- 原文："It seems that current practises in scientific publications lack full access to data and code. Instead, only methods and results are documented in writing."
- 要点：目前的论文普遍没有完整提供数据和代码，只用文字描述方法和结果。

### E9

- 位置：Discussion，Suggestions to authors
- 原文："Ultimately, proprietary software is a deal-breaker for reproducibility"
- 要点：作者认为，专有（商业闭源）软件最终会成为可复现性的致命障碍。
