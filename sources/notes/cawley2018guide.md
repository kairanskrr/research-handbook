# Cawley 2018：A Guide and Advice for Economists on the U.S. Junior Academic Job Market (2018–2019 Edition)

- 资料：`cawley2018guide`，[AEA 网站 PDF](https://www.aeaweb.org/content/file?id=869)
- 版本：2018 年 9 月 21 日版（2018–19 求职季）。只讲美国学术就业市场；具体日程和机制每年可能变化。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："The most important determinant of your success on the job market is the quality of your job market paper and how well you present it."
- 要点：求职成败最重要的决定因素，是求职论文（job market paper）的质量和你讲它讲得好不好。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："You should focus your time and effort towards improving your paper, your interview skills, and your job talk (your 90-minute seminar based on the job market paper)."
- 要点：把时间花在改论文、练面试和准备求职报告上；求职报告是基于求职论文的 90 分钟研讨会报告。

### E3

- 位置：Preparing to Go on the Job Market
- 原文："The most important indicator of your readiness is whether you will have a good job market paper completed early in the Fall." / "Try to complete your job market paper by the end of summer."
- 要点：是否准备好上市场，最重要的标志是秋季初能否完成一篇好的求职论文；争取在夏末前完成。

### E4

- 位置：Preparing to Go on the Job Market
- 原文："Organize an informal workshop with the other graduate students who will be on the job market and practice presenting your job market paper."
- 要点：和同届求职的同学组织非正式讨论会，反复练习讲求职论文。

### E5

- 位置：Interviewing at the ASSA Meetings
- 原文："Candidates with empirical job market papers should expect a careful examination and detailed discussion of their identification strategy and empirical methods."
- 要点：求职论文是实证研究的，面试时会被仔细追问识别策略和实证方法。

### E6

- 位置：Interviewing at the ASSA Meetings
- 原文："Interviewers want to make sure that the job candidate is responsible for the idea behind the job market paper and the decisions made in it."
- 要点：面试官想确认，求职论文的想法和其中的关键决定确实出自候选人本人。

### E7

- 位置：Applying for Academic Jobs
- 原文："The materials you transmit must include a cover letter, your CV, and your job market paper."
- 要点：申请学术职位时，提交的材料必须包括求职信、简历和求职论文。
