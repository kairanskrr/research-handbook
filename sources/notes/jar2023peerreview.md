# JAR：Peer Reviewing and Artistic Research（网页）与 Peer Review Form 2023

- 资料：`jar2023peerreview`，[同行评议说明页](https://www.jar-online.net/en/peer-reviewing-and-artistic-research)，[评审表 2023 版（docx）](https://jar-online.net/sites/default/files/2023-03/JAR_Peer-Review_Form_2023.docx)
- 版本：《艺术研究期刊》（JAR）的同行评议说明网页（2026-10-06 读取）和给评审人用的评审表 2023 版。只代表这一家期刊的做法。位置写网页小标题或评审表的节号。

### E1

- 位置：网页，JAR's Peer Review process
- 原文："JAR employs a 'single blind' review process. The reviewers are anonymous while the names of the authors are known. In artistic research a 'double blind' review process is not workable since artwork often carries the ‘signature’ of those who created it."
- 要点：JAR 用单盲评审：评审匿名，作者姓名公开；因为作品常带有创作者的"签名"，双盲在艺术研究里行不通。

### E2

- 位置：网页，JAR's Peer Review process
- 原文："We recommend that prospective authors also consult the form in order to understand how their submission is reviewed."
- 要点：JAR 建议作者也看看评审表，了解自己的稿件会被怎样评审。

### E3

- 位置：评审表，Appendix A，第 3 节
- 原文："Whether or not the submission contains a description of the question, issue or problem that is explored, and if not, if such an omission matters; Whether or not the submission shows evidence of innovation in content, form or technique in relation to a genre of practice, and if not, if such an omission matters;"
- 要点：评审要看：是否描述了所探究的问题；是否在内容、形式或技术上相对某一实践类型有创新；缺了的话，这个缺失是否要紧。

### E4

- 位置：评审表，Appendix A，第 3 节
- 原文："Whether or not the submission is contextualized and the context is referenced, which may include social, artistic and/or theoretical issues, and if not, if such an omission matters; Whether or not the submission provides new (kinds of) knowledge, interpretation, insights or experiences, and if not, if such an omission matters; Whether or not the submission’s methodology is adequate and thorough, and if not, if such an omission matters."
- 要点：还要看：是否做了背景定位并注明出处（社会、艺术或理论方面）；是否提供了新的知识、解读、洞见或体验；方法是否恰当、周密；缺了的话是否要紧。

### E5

- 位置：评审表，Appendix A，第 3 节
- 原文："Ultimately, a submission may successfully expose practice as research despite not adhering to conventional academic criteria for the assessment of research. If applicable, please state where the breaching of such criteria is detrimental to the submission."
- 要点：投稿即使不符合常规学术评价标准，也可能成功地把实践展陈为研究；评审要指出的是，哪里违背标准确实损害了稿件。

### E6

- 位置：评审表，第 1 节
- 原文："Ethical or legal concerns may e.g. arise from the practice with human or other animal subjects, the use of intellectual property or copyright, incidences of plagiarism or any other issue that you may find relevant."
- 要点：伦理或法律问题可能来自：涉及人或其他动物的实践、知识产权或版权的使用、抄袭等。

### E7

- 位置：评审表，Appendix A，第 2 节
- 原文："JAR considers the form of a submission to be as important as its content."
- 要点：JAR 认为稿件的形式和内容同样重要。

### E8

- 位置：评审表，Appendix B
- 原文："Pitfall: Seek to find all flaws in the submission, in part to show your expertise as a reviewer. Recommendation: Look for reasons to support a submission. Despite its flaws, does it point in new directions or expose promising insights?"
- 要点：评审的误区之一是挑出所有毛病来显示自己内行；建议是找支持稿件的理由：尽管有缺点，它是否指向新方向、给出有希望的洞见。

### E9

- 位置：评审表，Appendix B
- 原文："Pitfall: Be less critical because you support the art that is presented.Recommendation: Even work you like can benefit from a critical reading."
- 要点：另一误区：因为喜欢作品就放松批评；建议是，喜欢的作品也值得批判地阅读。
