# Mark、Gudith、Klocke 2008：The Cost of Interrupted Work: More Speed and Stress

- 资料：`mark2008cost`，[原文 PDF](https://www.ics.uci.edu/~gmark/chi08-mark.pdf)
- 版本：CHI 2008（ACM 人机交互大会）短文，作者主页提供的 PDF，2026-10-07 读取，pdftotext 提取。位置写节名。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："We found that context does not make a difference but surprisingly, people completed interrupted tasks in less time with no difference in quality. Our data suggests that people compensate for interruptions by working faster, but this comes at a price: experiencing more stress, higher frustration, time pressure and effort."
- 要点：被打断时人们完成任务反而更快、质量没有差别；但这是靠加快速度补偿的，代价是更大的压力、挫败感、时间紧迫感和付出的努力。打断的内容和手头任务是否相关，没有差别。

### E2

- 位置：Experimental Design and Procedure
- 原文："Forty-eight subjects participated." / "We simulated an office environment in the lab and chose an email task, common in information work." / "Interruption frequency was set to two minutes"
- 要点：实验有 48 名参与者，在实验室模拟办公环境，任务是回复电子邮件；打断每两分钟一次。

### E3

- 位置：Discussion and Conclusions
- 原文："After only 20 minutes of interrupted performance people reported significantly higher stress, frustration, workload, effort, and pressure."
- 要点：只在被打断的条件下工作 20 分钟，参与者报告的压力、挫败感、工作负荷、努力和紧迫感就明显更高。

### E4

- 位置：Discussion and Conclusions
- 原文："Our results showed that any interruption introduces a change in work pattern and is not related to context per se."
- 要点：任何打断都会改变工作方式，和打断内容是否与手头任务相关无关。
