# Nelson, Simmons & Simonsohn 2018：Psychology's Renaissance

- 资料：`nelson2018psychology`，[原文 PDF（作者主页）](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Nelson-Simmons-Simonsohn-2018.pdf)
- 版本：Annual Review of Psychology 69:511–534。页码为期刊页码。

### E1

- 位置：摘要，第 511 页
- 原文："In 2010–2012, a few largely coincidental events led experimental psychologists to realize that their approach to collecting, analyzing, and reporting data made it too easy to publish false-positive findings."
- 要点：2010–2012 年的几件事让实验心理学家意识到：他们收集、分析、报告数据的方式，让假阳性结果太容易发表。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction，第 512 页
- 原文："Authors voluntarily posting their data. Top journals routinely publishing replication attempts, both failures and successes. Hundreds of researchers preregistering their studies. [...] Enormous increases in sample sizes. Some top journals requiring the full disclosure of measures, conditions, exclusions, and the rules for determining sample sizes."
- 要点：作者列出这几年的变化：作者主动公开数据；顶级期刊发表重复研究（成功和失败的都发）；数百位研究者预注册；样本量大幅增加；部分顶级期刊要求完整披露测量、实验条件、剔除的数据和样本量的确定规则。

### E3

- 位置：The Renaissance Begins: 2010–2012，第 512–513 页
- 原文："Social psychology's most prestigious journal, the Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, published an article by Daryl Bem (2011) in which he presented nine experiments supporting a transparently outlandish claim—that people can be influenced by an unforeseeable future event."
- 要点：引发反思的事件之一：社会心理学顶级期刊发表了 Bem 的 9 个实验，支持"人会受到无法预见的未来事件影响"这一明显荒谬的结论。

### E4

- 位置：The Renaissance Begins，第 513 页
- 原文："an article reporting the surprisingly severe consequences of selectively reporting data and analyses, a practice that we later called p-hacking. In that article, we showed that conducting multiple analyses on the same data set and then reporting only the one(s) that obtained statistical significance (e.g., analyzing multiple measures but reporting only one) can dramatically increase the likelihood of publishing a false-positive finding."
- 要点：p-hacking 的定义：对同一份数据做多种分析，只报告显著的那个（例如测了多个指标只报一个）。这会大大提高发表假阳性结果的可能。

### E5

- 位置：The Renaissance Begins，第 513 页
- 原文："about 65% of respondents indicated that they had dropped a dependent variable when reporting a study."
- 要点：John 等人 2012 年的调查里，约 65% 的受访心理学研究者承认，报告研究时曾略去某个因变量（结果指标）。

### E6

- 位置：P-Hacking Explains an Old Paradox，第 514 页
- 原文："They would instead explore the data further, examining, for example, whether outliers were interfering with the effect, whether the effect was significant within a subset of participants or trials, or whether it emerged when the dependent variable was coded differently."
- 要点：第一次分析不显著时，研究者通常不会放弃，而是继续"看看"：去掉异常值、只看一部分参与者、换一种方式计算因变量。这些就是 p-hacking 的常见形式。

### E7

- 位置：P-Hacking Explains an Old Paradox，第 516 页
- 原文："we showed how acceptable levels of p-hacking could raise false-positive rates to unacceptable levels (e.g., from 5% to 61%; see Figure 1)."
- 要点：在模拟中，几种当时被认为"可以接受"的灵活做法叠加起来，能把假阳性率从 5% 提高到 61%。

### E8

- 位置：False Positives Are Bad，第 518 页
- 原文："P-hacking is a pervasive problem precisely because researchers usually do not realize that they are doing it or appreciate that what they are doing is consequential"
- 要点：p-hacking 普遍存在，正是因为研究者通常意识不到自己在这样做，或者不觉得这有什么后果。它不需要恶意。

### E9

- 位置：P-Hacking Explains an Old Paradox，第 514 页
- 原文："On the other hand, the overwhelming majority of published studies are underpowered and, thus, theoretically unlikely to obtain results that are statistically significant"
- 要点：一方面发表的结果几乎都显著，另一方面绝大多数研究统计功效不足、理论上本不该常常显著。作者认为 p-hacking 解释了这个矛盾。

### E10

- 位置：Preventing P-Hacking: Disclosure，第 518 页
- 原文："At the bare minimum, this means requiring authors to disclose all of their measures, manipulations, and exclusions, as well as how they determined their sample sizes"
- 要点：最低限度的防线是披露：报告全部测量指标、全部实验操纵、所有剔除的数据，以及样本量是怎么定的。

### E11

- 位置：第 519 页脚注 11
- 原文："We report how we determined our sample size, all data exclusions (if any), all manipulations, and all measures in the study"
- 要点：作者建议放进论文的标准披露声明（常被称为"21 字声明"）。

### E12

- 位置：Preventing P-Hacking: Preregistration，第 519 页
- 原文："Preregistrations are time-stamped plans for data analysis written before any data are analyzed."
- 要点：预注册是在分析任何数据之前写好、带时间戳的分析计划。

### E13

- 位置：Replications，第 523 页
- 原文："Again, 36% were conclusive successes; however, among the remaining 64%, only 25% were conclusive failures, and 49% were inconclusive, i.e., neither failures nor successes" / "This high share of inconclusive results reflects the fact that many of the 100 replications were underpowered"
- 要点：对开放科学合作组织的 100 项重做，用能区分"无定论"的方法重新判断：36% 明确成功，其余 64% 里只有 25% 明确失败，49% 无定论。无定论多，是因为不少重做本身样本也不够大。

### E14

- 位置：P-Hacking Explains an Old Paradox，第 516 页
- 原文："It is tempting to take comfort in the fact that psychology publications usually contain more than one experiment" / "If a researcher is willing to file-drawer even a small number of studies, a false-positive four-study article becomes easy enough to produce"
- 要点：心理学论文通常包含不止一个实验。但多个实验并不能自动保证结论可靠：只要愿意把少数不理想的研究放进抽屉不发表，一篇四个实验都"成功"的假阳性论文并不难做出来。

### E15

- 位置：The Renaissance Begins，第 514 页
- 原文："In 2011, psychologist Brian Nosek began organizing several collaborative replication efforts, in which multiple independent labs attempted to replicate previously published research" / "He and Jeffrey Spies also worked to develop an online platform, the Open Science Framework (OSF), which was originally released in 2012; it allowed researchers to more transparently record, share, and report their work."
- 要点：2011 年起 Nosek 组织了多个实验室合作重复已发表研究的项目；他和 Spies 还开发了开放科学框架（OSF）这个在线平台，2012 年上线，让研究者能更透明地记录、共享和报告研究。

### E16

- 位置：Innocent Errors: Unintentional Errors，第 525 页
- 原文："They found that 54% of articles contained at least one error, and 12% had at least one error that altered the statistical significance of a reported result."
- 要点：Bakker & Wicherts 检查心理学期刊里报告的统计结果，54% 的论文至少有一处错误，12% 至少有一处错误改变了结果是否显著。
