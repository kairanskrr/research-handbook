# Mensh & Kording 2017：Ten simple rules for structuring papers

- 资料：`mensh2017ten`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005619)
- 版本：PLOS Computational Biology 13(9): e1005619，开放获取。页码为 PDF 页码。

### E1

- 位置：Rule 1，第 2 页
- 原文："Focus on a single message; papers that simultaneously focus on multiple contributions tend to be less convincing about each and are therefore less memorable."
- 要点：一篇论文聚焦一个核心信息；同时主打多个贡献，每个都不够有说服力，也不容易被记住。

### E2

- 位置：Rule 1，第 2 页
- 原文："Your communication efforts are successful if readers can still describe the main contribution of your paper to their colleagues a year after reading it."
- 要点：检验沟通是否成功：读者一年后还能向同事讲出你论文的主要贡献。

### E3

- 位置：Rule 3，第 3 页
- 原文："This structure reduces the chance that the reader will wonder “Why was I told that?” (if the context is missing) or “So what?” (if the conclusion is missing)."
- 要点："背景—内容—结论"结构避免读者问"为什么告诉我这个"（缺背景）或"所以呢"（缺结论）。

### E4

- 位置：Rule 3，第 3 页
- 原文："Applying C-C-C at the paragraph scale, the first sentence defines the topic or context, the body hosts the novel content put forth for the reader’s consideration, and the last sentence provides the conclusion to be remembered."
- 要点：这个结构在全文和段落两个尺度都适用：段首给话题，中间给内容，段尾给要记住的结论。

### E5

- 位置：Rule 3，第 3 页
- 原文："They do not care about the chronological path by which you reached a result; they just care about the ultimate claim and the logic supporting it"
- 要点：读者不关心你按什么时间顺序得到结果，只关心最终的主张和支撑它的逻辑；不要按时间顺序写论文。

### E6

- 位置：Rule 6，第 6 页
- 原文："The introduction should not contain a broad literature review beyond the motivation of the paper."
- 要点：引言围绕"缺口"展开：一步步收窄到文献中缺什么、为什么重要，最后一段概括本文如何填补；不要在引言里做泛泛的文献综述。

### E7

- 位置：Rule 7，第 6 页
- 原文："sketch out the logical structure of how your results support your claim and convert this into a sequence of declarative statements that become the headers of subsections within the results section"
- 要点：把"结果如何支撑主张"的逻辑写成一串陈述句，作为结果部分各小节的标题。

### E8

- 位置：Rule 8，第 7 页
- 原文："The discussion section explains how the results have filled the gap that was identified in the introduction, provides caveats to the interpretation, and describes how the paper advances the field by providing new opportunities."
- 要点：讨论部分说明缺口如何被填补、解释有哪些局限、对领域有什么推进。

### E9

- 位置：Rule 9，第 7 页
- 原文："The title, abstract, and figures are viewed by far more people than the rest of the paper, and the methods section is read least of all. Budget accordingly."
- 要点：标题、摘要和图被看得最多，按读者数量分配时间。

### E10

- 位置：Rule 10，第 8 页
- 原文："In many cases, trashing entire paragraphs and rewriting is a faster way to produce good text than incremental editing."
- 要点：不要太舍不得自己写的东西；整段删掉重写，往往比一点点改更快写出好文字。

### E11

- 位置：Rule 10，第 8 页
- 原文："if you, as the writer, cannot describe the entire outline of a paper to a colleague in a few minutes, then clearly a reader will not be able to."
- 要点：如果你几分钟内讲不清整篇论文的提纲，读者也不可能讲清，说明故事还需要提炼。

### E12

- 位置：Rule 5，第 4 页
- 原文："The abstract is, for most readers, the only part of the paper that will be read."
- 要点：对大多数读者来说摘要是唯一会读的部分，所以摘要必须讲完整个故事。

### E13

- 位置：Rule 2，第 2 页
- 原文："Avoid abbreviations and acronyms so that readers do not have to go back to earlier sections to identify them."
- 要点：为不了解你工作的读者写作：清楚定义术语，少用缩写。

### E14

- 位置：Rule 10，第 8 页
- 原文："Non-specific feedback and unenthusiastic reviews often imply that the reviewers did not “get” the big picture story line."
- 要点：笼统、不热情的反馈往往说明读者没抓住主线；很具体的反馈通常指出某段逻辑不够。

### E15

- 位置：Rule 5，第 4 页
- 原文："The context must communicate to the reader what gap the paper will fill." / "The content (“Here we”) first describes the novel method or approach that you used to fill the gap or question. Then you present the meat—your executive summary of the results." / "the conclusion interprets the results to answer the question that was posed at the end of the context section."
- 要点：摘要按"背景—内容—结论"写：背景说明论文要填补什么缺口；内容先说用了什么新方法，再给结果的概要；结论解释结果，回答背景末尾提出的问题。

### E16

- 位置：Rule 5，第 4 页
- 原文："This structure helps you avoid the most common mistake with the abstract, which is to talk about results before the reader is ready to understand them."
- 要点：摘要最常见的错误，是在读者还没准备好理解时就谈结果。

### E17

- 位置：Rule 1，第 2 页
- 原文："The title is the ultimate refinement of the paper’s contribution. Thinking about the title early—and regularly returning to hone it—can help not only the writing of the paper but also the process of designing experiments or developing theories."
- 要点：标题是对论文贡献的最终提炼；尽早想标题并反复打磨，对写作和设计实验都有帮助。

### E18

- 位置：Rule 6，第 6 页
- 原文："The last paragraph of the introduction is special: it compactly summarizes the results, which fill the gap you just established."
- 要点：引言最后一段要简要概括结果，说明它们怎样填补了前面提出的缺口。

### E19

- 位置：Rule 7，第 6 页
- 原文："figures are often viewed by readers who skip directly from the abstract in order to save time. Thus, the title of the figure should communicate the conclusion of the analysis, and the legend should explain how it was done."
- 要点：很多读者看完摘要直接看图，所以图的标题要说出分析的结论，图注要说明是怎么做的。

### E20

- 位置：Rule 7，第 6–7 页
- 原文："Each paragraph convinces the reader of the answer given in its last sentence. This makes it easy to find the paragraph in which a suspicious conclusion is drawn and to check the logic of that"
- 要点：结果部分每段先提出要回答的问题，最后一句给出答案；这样读者容易找到可疑结论出在哪一段，并检查那一段的逻辑。

### E21

- 位置：Rule 9，第 7 页
- 原文："Make an outline. We like to write one informal sentence for each planned paragraph."
- 要点：先写提纲，每个计划中的段落写一句非正式的话。

### E22

- 位置：Rule 8，第 7 页
- 原文："Each of the following paragraphs in the discussion section starts by describing an area of weakness or strength of the paper. It then evaluates the strength or weakness by linking it to the relevant literature."
- 要点：讨论部分的段落各讲论文的一个弱点或长处，并结合相关文献评价它。
