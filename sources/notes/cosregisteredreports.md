# Center for Open Science：Registered Reports

- 资料：`cosregisteredreports`，[原文](https://www.cos.io/initiatives/registered-reports)

### E1

- 位置：Participating Journals
- 原文："Currently, over 300 journals use the Registered Reports publishing format either as a regular submission option or as part of a single special issue."
- 要点：目前有 300 多本期刊采用注册报告（作为常规投稿类型，或在某期专刊中使用）。

### E2

- 位置：Participating Journals
- 原文："a good way to begin is to (a) read the specific author guidelines included in the list of participating journals below, (b) complete this template protocol and then (c) expand the template protocol into a full Stage 1 manuscript."
- 要点：入门做法：先读目标期刊的作者指南，填好方案模板，再扩写成完整的第一阶段稿件。
