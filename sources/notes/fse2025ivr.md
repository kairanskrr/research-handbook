# FSE 2025：Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers

- 资料：`fse2025ivr`，[原文](https://conf.researchr.org/track/fse-2025/fse-2025-ideas-visions-and-reflections)
- 版本：FSE 2025（ACM 软件工程基础国际会议）"想法、展望与反思"赛道征稿启事；2026-10-17 读取。位置写网页小标题。只代表软件工程这一个会议当年的规定。

### E1

- 位置：Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers
- 原文："Bold visions of new directions that may not yet be supported by solid results but rather by a strong and well-motivated scientific intuition."
- 要点：展望类论文提出新方向，可以还没有扎实的结果支撑，而靠有充分理由的科学直觉。

### E2

- 位置：Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers
- 原文："Bold revisits of current research directions that may be somehow misguided."
- 要点：反思类论文可以大胆重新审视某些可能走偏了的现有研究方向。

### E3

- 位置：Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers
- 原文："Submissions must clearly motivate and illustrate initial evidence (type 1) or a rationale (types 2 and 3) for changing current practice and/or research in software engineering."
- 要点：投稿必须说清为什么要改变现有实践或研究：新想法类给初步证据，展望和反思类给理由。

### E4

- 位置：Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers
- 原文："Evaluation results are not required for FSE-IVR papers. Preliminary results for submissions of the first type or a sketch of an evaluation plan for submissions of the second type, could help the reviewers understand the scope of the work better."
- 要点：不要求评估结果；但初步结果或评估计划的草图能帮审稿人理解工作的范围。

### E5

- 位置：Out of Scope
- 原文："A submission should not be an FSE full research submission lacking an evaluation, or a disguised advertisement for previously published results: An FSE research track submission with slightly less evaluation or lower quality is not a good FSE-IVR paper."
- 要点：缺少评估的完整研究论文、变相宣传已发表成果的稿件，都不算好的这类论文。

### E6

- 位置：Evaluation
- 原文："Value: the problem is worth exploring" / "Impact: the potential for disruption of current practice" / "Originality: the novelty of the paper insight" / "Scholarship: appropriate consideration of relevant literature" / "Quality: overall paper quality"
- 要点：所有论文按价值、影响、原创性、学术性（恰当考虑相关文献）和整体质量评审。

### E7

- 位置：Evaluation
- 原文："Visions of the future (type 2) and Reflections on the past (type 3) papers will also be evaluated according to:" / "Validity: soundness of the rationale"
- 要点：展望和反思类论文还要看"效度"，也就是理由是否站得住。

### E8

- 位置：How to Submit
- 原文："must not exceed four pages (not including a one-page bibliography)"
- 要点：篇幅不超过四页（另加一页参考文献）。

### E9

- 位置：Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers
- 原文："FSE-IVR publications lay the foundation for strong full-paper publications later on."
- 要点：这个赛道的论文为以后的完整论文打基础。
