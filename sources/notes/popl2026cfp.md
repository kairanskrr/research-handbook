# POPL 2026：PACMPL Issue POPL 2026 Call for Papers

- 资料：`popl2026cfp`，[原文](https://popl26.sigplan.org/track/POPL-2026-popl-research-papers)
- 版本：ACM SIGPLAN 程序设计语言原理研讨会（Principles of Programming Languages，POPL）2026 研究论文征稿启事，会议官网，2026-10-08 读取。网页，位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Scope
- 原文："Both theoretical and experimental papers are welcome, on topics ranging from formal frameworks to experience reports. We seek submissions that make principled, enduring contributions to the theory, design, understanding, implementation, or application of programming languages."
- 要点：POPL 理论和实验论文都收，从形式化框架到经验报告；征集对程序语言的理论、设计、理解、实现或应用有原理性、持久贡献的论文。

### E2

- 位置：Evaluation Criteria
- 原文："All papers will be judged on significance, originality, relevance, correctness, and clarity."
- 要点：审稿看重要性、原创性、相关性、正确性和清晰度。

### E3

- 位置：Double-Blind Reviewing
- 原文："This means that identities of authors will not be made visible to reviewers until after conditional-acceptance decisions have been made, and then only for the conditionally-accepted papers."
- 要点：POPL 2026 采用完全双盲：直到有条件录用决定之后，审稿人才看到作者身份，而且只看到被录用论文的。

### E4

- 位置：Double-Blind Reviewing，Supplementary material
- 原文："Authors are permitted to provide supplementary material (e.g., detailed proofs, proof scripts, system implementations, or experimental data) along with their submission, which reviewers may (but are not required to) examine."
- 要点：可以随投稿提交补充材料，例如详细证明、证明脚本、系统实现、实验数据；审稿人可以看，但不必看。

### E5

- 位置：Double-Blind Reviewing，Dissemination of work under submission
- 原文："Authors are welcome to disseminate their ideas and post draft versions of their paper(s) on their personal website, institutional repository, or arXiv (reviewers will be asked to turn off arXiv notifications during the review period)." / "nothing should be done in the name of anonymity that weakens the quality of the submission."
- 要点：可以在个人网站、机构库或 arXiv 上发草稿（审稿期间审稿人会被要求关掉 arXiv 提醒）；不能为了匿名而降低投稿质量。

### E6

- 位置：Evaluation Process
- 原文："A response must be concise, addressing specific points raised in the reviews; in particular, it must not introduce new technical results."
- 要点：作者回复要简洁，针对审稿意见的具体问题；不能在回复里提出新的技术结果。

### E7

- 位置：Evaluation Process
- 原文："To conform with ACM requirements for journal publication, all POPL papers will be conditionally accepted" / "That the changes are sufficient will be confirmed by the original reviewers prior to acceptance to POPL."
- 要点：为符合 ACM 对期刊发表的要求，POPL 的论文都是先有条件录用，之后由原审稿人确认修改。

### E8

- 位置：Submission Guidelines
- 原文："Each paper should have no more than 25 pages of text, excluding bibliography, using the PACMPL format"
- 要点：正文不超过 25 页，不含参考文献。

### E9

- 位置：Artifact Evaluation for Accepted Papers
- 原文："Artifact submission is strongly encouraged but voluntary and will not influence the final decision regarding the papers."
- 要点：强烈鼓励但不强制提交制品，制品评估不影响论文是否录用。
