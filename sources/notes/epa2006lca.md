# 美国环境保护署 2006：Life Cycle Assessment: Principles and Practice

- 资料：`epa2006lca`，[原文 PDF](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPDF.cgi?Dockey=P1000L86.PDF)
- 版本：EPA/600/R-06/060，2006 年 5 月。由 Scientific Applications International Corporation（SAIC）为 EPA 国家风险管理研究实验室编写。位置写 PDF 页码。

### E1

- 位置：Chapter 1，PDF 第 9 页
- 原文："Life cycle assessment is a "cradle-to-grave" approach for assessing industrial systems. "Cradle-to-grave" begins with the gathering of raw materials from the earth to create the product and ends at the point when all materials are returned to the earth."
- 要点：生命周期评价是"从摇篮到坟墓"的评估方法：从开采原材料开始，到所有材料回归自然为止。

### E2

- 位置：Chapter 1，PDF 第 10 页
- 原文："The LCA process is a systematic, phased approach and consists of four components: goal definition and scoping, inventory analysis, impact assessment, and interpretation"
- 要点：生命周期评价分四部分：确定目标和范围、清单分析、影响评价、结果解释。

### E3

- 位置：Chapter 1，PDF 第 11 页
- 原文："An LCA allows a decision maker to study an entire product system hence avoiding the suboptimization that could result if only a single process were the focus of the study."
- 要点：生命周期评价看整个产品系统，避免只盯一个环节造成的"局部最优"。

### E4

- 位置：Chapter 1，PDF 第 11 页
- 原文："For example, when selecting between two rival products, it may appear that Option 1 is better for the environment because it generates less solid waste than Option 2. However, after performing an LCA it might be determined that the first option actually creates larger cradle-to-grave environmental impacts when measured across all three media (air, water, land)"
- 要点：例子：方案 1 固体废物少，看起来更环保；但做完生命周期评价，可能发现它在空气、水、土地三方面的总影响反而更大。

### E5

- 位置：Chapter 1，PDF 第 13 页
- 原文："Performing an LCA can be resource and time intensive. Depending upon how thorough an LCA the user wishes to conduct, gathering the data can be problematic, and the availability of data can greatly impact the accuracy of the final results."
- 要点：做生命周期评价费时费力，数据难收集，能拿到什么数据会大大影响结果的准确性。

### E6

- 位置：Chapter 1，PDF 第 13 页
- 原文："LCA will not determine which product or process is the most cost effective or works the best."
- 要点：生命周期评价不能告诉你哪个方案最省钱、效果最好。

### E7

- 位置：Chapter 2，PDF 第 19 页
- 原文："Careful selection of the functional unit to measure and display the LCA results will improve the accuracy of the study and the usefulness of the results."
- 要点：仔细选择功能单位（用来衡量和展示结果的基准），能提高研究的准确性和结果的用处。

### E8

- 位置：Chapter 2，PDF 第 19 页，选择功能单位的例子
- 原文："An LCA study comparing two types of wall insulation to determine environmental preferability must be evaluated on the same function, the ability to decrease heat flow."
- 要点：比较两种墙体保温材料时，必须按同一功能（减少热流的能力）来比较，而不是按相同面积和厚度。

### E9

- 位置：Chapter 2，PDF 第 26 页
- 原文："If assumptions are omitted, the final results may be taken out of context or easily misinterpreted."
- 要点：不写明假设，最终结果可能被断章取义或误读。

### E10

- 位置：Chapter 4，PDF 第 60 页
- 原文："According to ISO 14042, any judgment of preferability is a subjective judgment regarding the relative importance of one impact category over another."
- 要点：按 ISO 14042，判断哪个方案更好，涉及哪类影响更重要，这是主观判断。
