# NDSS 2026：Call for Papers

- 资料：`ndss2026cfp`，[原文](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss2026/submissions/call-for-papers/)
- 版本：NDSS Symposium 2026（网络与分布式系统安全研讨会）征稿启事，2026-10-08 读取。位置写网页小标题。只作为安全方向一个会议某一年的例子。

### E1

- 位置：Paper Submission Information，Major Revision
- 原文："Major Revision: Papers in this category are considered promising but need additional work (e.g., new implementations, experiments, and/or proofs)." / "within six weeks after notification"
- 要点：有"大修"结果：论文有希望但还要补工作（新实现、实验或证明），可在通知后六周内修改重投。

### E2

- 位置：Ethical Considerations
- 原文："Authors must proactively anticipate and address potential negative consequences of both conducting and publishing their research. Submissions may be rejected regardless of scientific merit if they fail to adequately address ethical concerns."
- 要点：作者必须主动预见并处理做研究和发表研究可能带来的负面后果；伦理问题处理不当，不论科学价值都可能被拒。

### E3

- 位置：Ethical Considerations
- 原文："IRB exemptions may not be sufficient grounds for proper mitigation of ethical concerns." / "If the paper reports a potentially high-impact vulnerability, the authors should report or at least discuss their plan for responsible disclosure."
- 要点：伦理审查委员会的豁免不一定足以说明伦理问题已妥善处理；报告高影响漏洞的论文要报告或至少讨论负责任披露的计划。

### E4

- 位置：Ethical Considerations
- 原文："Each paper may optionally include an “Ethics Considerations” section immediately preceding the reference section."
- 要点：NDSS 2026 的"伦理考量"小节是可选的。

### E5

- 位置：Areas/Topics of Interest，SoK 论文
- 原文："Such papers go beyond summarizing previous research (like in surveys); they also include a thorough examination and analysis of existing approaches, identify gaps and limitations"
- 要点：知识系统化论文不只是总结已有研究，还要深入分析已有方法、找出空白和局限。

### E6

- 位置：Paper Formatting
- 原文："Technical papers submitted for NDSS Symposium must not exceed 13 pages, excluding the “Ethics Considerations” section, references, or appendices"
- 要点：正文不超过 13 页，伦理考量、参考文献和附录不计入。

### E7

- 位置：Artifact Evaluation
- 原文："Authors of accepted papers are strongly encouraged to open-source their artifacts and to submit them for an artifact evaluation."
- 要点：强烈鼓励录用论文开源制品并参加制品评估。

### E8

- 位置：Paper Submission Information
- 原文："NDSS Symposium 2026 will have two review cycles: a summer submission cycle and a fall submission cycle."
- 要点：2026 年这届有夏、秋两轮投稿。
