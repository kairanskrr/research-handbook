# Haddaway 等人 2018：ROSES RepOrting standards for Systematic Evidence Syntheses

- 资料：`haddaway2018roses`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13750-018-0121-7)（Environmental Evidence 7:7，开放获取，CC BY）
- 版本：2018 年 3 月发表的期刊论文。摘录读自出版社 Springer 的开放获取 PDF 全文（https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1186/s13750-018-0121-7.pdf，2026-10-10 下载）；E1、E2 最初取自 DOAJ 收录的该文摘要，与全文中的摘要一致。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："However, due to its development for systematic reviews in healthcare, PRISMA has limited applicability for reviews in conservation and environmental management. We highlight 12 key problems with the application of PRISMA to this field, including an overemphasis on meta-analysis and no consideration for other synthesis methods."
- 要点：PRISMA 是为医疗卫生系统综述制定的，用到保护和环境管理领域的综述有局限；作者列出 12 个问题，例如过分强调元分析、不考虑其他综合方法。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："We introduce ROSES (RepOrting standards for Systematic Evidence Syntheses), a pro forma and flow diagram designed specifically for systematic reviews and systematic maps in the field of conservation and environmental management."
- 要点：ROSES 是专为保护和环境管理领域的系统综述和系统地图设计的报告表格和流程图。

### E3

- 位置：Key differences between ROSES and PRISMA，第 4 页
- 原文："Our proposed pro forma for systematic review and map protocols and reports can be found in Additional files 1, 2, respectively, along with the template flow diagram in Additional file 3."
- 要点：ROSES 分两份表格：一份用于系统综述和系统地图的方案，一份用于最终报告；另有一份流程图模板。

### E4

- 位置：Key differences between ROSES and PRISMA，第 5 页
- 原文："Thirdly, we have departed from the format of PRISMA by not only providing reporting guidance in a checklist form, but also including requirements to record summary information (i.e. meta-data) that describes the key steps in the conduct of reviews."
- 要点：和 PRISMA 不同，ROSES 除了核对清单，还要求作者记录描述综述各关键步骤的概要信息（元数据）。

### E5

- 位置：Background，第 4 页
- 原文："reviewers could also record and present in a standard form important summary details, such as the number of search results obtained before duplicate removal."
- 要点：概要信息的例子：去重之前一共检索到多少条结果。

### E6

- 位置：Benefits of ROSES，第 6 页
- 原文："ROSES focuses on the earlier and middle stages of the review process, i.e. searching, screening, data extraction and critical appraisal, whilst there is limited detail regarding synthesis."
- 要点：ROSES 的重点是检索、筛选、数据提取和质量评判这些前中期步骤，对最后的综合写得不细。

### E7

- 位置：Benefits of ROSES，第 6 页
- 原文："However, ROSES is considerably longer than the current PRISMA forms and it will, therefore, undoubtedly take more time for authors to complete." / "We strongly recommend that authors begin to complete ROSES pro forma as early as possible."
- 要点：ROSES 表格比 PRISMA 长得多，填起来更费时间；作者强烈建议尽早开始填。
