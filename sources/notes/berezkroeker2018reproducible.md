# Berez-Kroeker 等 2018：Reproducible research in linguistics: A position statement on data citation and attribution in our field

- 资料：`berezkroeker2018reproducible`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1515/ling-2017-0032)
- 版本：Linguistics 56(1)，开放获取（CC BY-NC-ND 4.0）；原文文本取自美国国家科学基金会公共访问库 par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10055458

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："represents the collective views of some 41 colleagues"
- 要点：这份立场声明代表约 41 位同行的共同看法。

### E2

- 位置：§2
- 原文："The difference between reproducible research and replicable research is that the latter produces new data, which can then ostensibly be analyzed for either confirmation or disconfirmation of previous results; the former provides access to the original data for independent analysis."
- 要点：可重复研究（replicable）是重新收集新数据；可复现研究（reproducible）是让别人拿到原始数据独立分析。

### E3

- 位置：§2
- 原文："Clearly, linguists cannot expect their colleagues to replicate data collection conditions"
- 要点：语言学者没法指望同行重造一次当初的数据收集条件。

### E4

- 位置：§3
- 原文："In a survey of one hundred descriptive grammars from a ten-year span between 2003 and 2012, Gawne and colleagues (2017) found that even with the benefit of years of pervasive discussion of data management methods in language documentation, very few authors in this genre make their methods or data sources explicit in their writing."
- 要点：对 2003—2012 年 100 部描写语法的调查发现，很少有作者写明方法或数据来源。

### E5

- 位置：§3
- 原文："In short, we are in danger of being a social science asking its audience to take our word for it."
- 要点：语言学有变成"请读者相信我们说的话"的危险。

### E6

- 位置：§2
- 原文："Transparency about methods of data collection and analysis may include: a description of the conditions under which data were collected"
- 要点：方法透明包括写清数据是在什么条件下收集的。

### E7

- 位置：§2
- 原文："transparency about how to locate relevant subparts of a data set (page numbers, corpus line numbers, time-codes of starting and ending points in an audio or video recording, etc.)"
- 要点：来源透明包括让读者能定位到数据的具体部分：页码、语料行号、录音录像的起止时间码。

### E8

- 位置：§2 脚注 6
- 原文："we also acknowledge that this must be done with sensitivity toward the privacy concerns of the people whose language is being recorded"
- 要点：细粒度引用要顾及被录音者的隐私。

### E9

- 位置：§4.2
- 原文："authors should include in their publications an explicit statement that data come from introspection, either their own or that of others."
- 要点：数据来自内省（自己或他人的语感判断）时，要在文中明确说明。

### E10

- 位置：§4.2
- 原文："In order for data to be citable, it should be stored in an accessible location, preferably a data archive or some other repository with a demonstrated commitment to both preserving the data and making them accessible for many years to come."
- 要点：数据要能被引用，就要存在可访问的地方，最好是承诺长期保存和开放的数据档案馆。

### E11

- 位置：§4.2
- 原文："though restricted data is citable (and should be cited by those who are permitted to access it), only unrestricted data can be re-used and cited"
- 要点：受限数据也可以引用，但只有不受限的数据才能被再利用。

### E12

- 位置：§4.3
- 原文："Data work is intellectually valuable, and linguists who do data work need to be recognized for their work during professional evaluation."
- 要点：数据工作有学术价值，应在职业评价中得到承认。

### E13

- 位置：§5
- 原文："We recommend that individual linguists proactively seek to develop a relationship with a data archive."
- 要点：建议每位语言学者主动和一家数据档案馆建立联系。

### E14

- 位置：§3
- 原文："precious endangered language data can disappear, and expensive experiments may be recreated out of ignorance."
- 要点：珍贵的濒危语言数据可能消失，昂贵的实验可能因不知情而被重做。

### E15

- 位置：§4.2
- 原文："Data should be available in formats that do not require proprietary software and that will be usable or portable as technology changes."
- 要点：数据格式不应依赖专有软件，要能随技术变化继续使用。
