# Atiq & Marmor 2025：The Nature of Law（斯坦福哲学百科）

- 资料：`atiq2025nature`，[原文](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/lawphil-nature/)
- 版本：Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy 条目 "The Nature of Law"，2001 年首发，2025-04-29 实质修订，作者 Emad Atiq、Andrei Marmor（均为美国康奈尔大学）。2026-10-08 读取。位置写条目小节。条目讨论的主要是英美分析法学传统。

### E1

- 位置：§2 The Methodology of Jurisprudence
- 原文："There are five main approaches to these issues that we’ll discuss in turn." / "One view takes the subject of investigation to be a concept of law, and jurisprudence to be a form of conceptual analysis." / "A fifth view, associated principally with Dworkin’s work, takes legal theory to be a fundamentally “interpretive” enterprise that is both descriptive and evaluative."
- 要点：法理学方法有五种主要路径，其中一种把法理学看作对"法"这个概念的分析，另一种（主要与 Dworkin 相关）把法律理论看作既描述又评价的"解释性"事业。

### E2

- 位置：§2.1 Conceptual Analysis
- 原文："Their goal is to characterize the concept in a way that is compatible with our intuitions about it"
- 要点：概念分析的目标，是以符合我们直觉的方式刻画"法"这个概念。

### E3

- 位置：§2.1 Conceptual Analysis
- 原文："The account’s success turns on whether it yields intuitively correct results about particular cases and does so in an explanatorily satisfying way as opposed to an ad hoc manner."
- 要点：一个解说成功与否，看它能否对具体案例给出直觉上正确的结果，而且是以有解释力的方式，而不是临时拼凑。

### E4

- 位置：§2.1 Conceptual Analysis
- 原文："A key question this approach raises concerns the source and reliability of the ‘raw data’ for the theory—namely, our basic conceptual intuitions."
- 要点：这种做法的关键问题是理论的"原始数据"——我们的概念直觉——从哪里来、是否可靠。

### E5

- 位置：§2.2 Metaphysical Reduction
- 原文："One reductionist approach, prominently defended by Brian Leiter (2007), is known as “naturalized jurisprudence.”" / "But a distinguishing characteristic is the insistence on a purely empirical methodology and rejection of intuitionistic or a priori methods"
- 要点：Leiter 等人主张的"自然化法理学"坚持纯经验的方法，拒绝依靠直觉或先验的方法。

### E6

- 位置：§2.5 Constructive Interpretation
- 原文："Recall that legal practitioners, on Dworkin’s portrayal, interpret the social practices within their system in a way that’s guided by both considerations of “fit” and “justification”" / "However, the precise criteria for morally evaluating competing theories on the interpretivist approach remain unclear."
- 要点：按 Dworkin 的说法，解释法律实践要同时考虑"契合"（与实践相符）和"证成"（在道德上说得过去）；但在这种路径下，用什么具体标准从道德上评价相互竞争的理论，仍不清楚。

### E7

- 位置：§2.5 结尾
- 原文："In the end, “methodological pluralism” may be the most apt characterization of the state of play in jurisprudence."
- 要点：法理学的现状，也许最贴切的描述是"方法论多元"。
