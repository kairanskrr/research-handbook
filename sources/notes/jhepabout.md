# JHEP：About（Journal of High Energy Physics 简介与编辑方针）

- 资料：`jhepabout`，[原文](https://jhep.sissa.it/jhep/help/JHEP/JHEP_about.jsp)
- 版本：Journal of High Energy Physics（JHEP，高能物理期刊）的"JHEP about"网页，2026-10-08 读取，页面未注明日期。期刊归意大利国际高等研究院（SISSA）所有，由 SISSA 负责审稿，Springer 出版。只代表这本期刊。

### E1

- 位置：General；Scope
- 原文："JHEP encompasses the following areas of theoretical and experimental physics: Collider Physics Underground and Large Array Physics Quantum Field Theory Gauge Field Theories Symmetries String and Brane Theory"
- 要点：JHEP 的收稿范围覆盖理论和实验物理的多个方向，其中包括量子场论、规范场论和弦与膜理论。

### E2

- 位置：Editorial policy
- 原文："The criteria for acceptance are scientific quality, originality and relevance." / "Editors are asked to apply a strict quality filter in selecting contributions for publication."
- 要点：接收标准是科学质量、原创性和相关性；编辑要严格把关。

### E3

- 位置：Editorial policy，机器学习论文
- 原文："Editors/Referees are asked to apply the same standards of rigour and deductive reasoning that characterize theoretical physics when assessing submissions in the field of machine learning applications in particle physics."
- 要点：评审粒子物理中的机器学习应用论文时，编辑和审稿人要采用理论物理特有的严谨和演绎推理标准。
