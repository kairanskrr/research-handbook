# IEEE SA：Develop Standards

- 资料：`ieeesadevelop`，[原文](https://standards.ieee.org/develop/)
- 版本：IEEE 标准协会（IEEE Standards Association，IEEE SA）网页，2026-10-06 读取。

### E1

- 位置：页首
- 原文："Standards drive technological innovation, fuel growth of global markets, expand consumer choice, support interoperability and help protect public health and safety."
- 要点：IEEE SA 认为标准推动技术创新、支持不同厂商设备之间的互通，并有助于保护公众健康和安全。

### E2

- 位置：页首
- 原文："IEEE Standards are developed using a time-tested, effective and trusted process. This process is broken down into a six stage life cycle."
- 要点：IEEE 标准按一个分六个阶段的固定流程制定。

### E3

- 位置：Our Principles — IEEE Standards Development Principles
- 原文："These principles provide a community for voluntary cooperation among interested parties and stakeholders, enable technical excellence, global interoperability, and innovation to foster economic growth and societal prosperity."
- 要点：标准制定靠相关各方自愿合作；原则包括直接参与、正当程序、广泛共识、平衡、透明、开放等。

### E4

- 位置：Standards Board Committees
- 原文："Patent Committee (PatCom) – Recommends updates to the IEEE SA patent policy, provides educational material related to the patent policy, and reviews conformity with the patent policy procedures."
- 要点：IEEE SA 设有专利委员会，负责专利政策；专利和标准的关系是标准制定中的正式议题。
