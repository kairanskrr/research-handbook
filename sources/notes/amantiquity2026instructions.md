# American Antiquity 投稿说明：Preparing your materials（剑桥大学出版社网站，2026 年 10 月访问）

- 资料：`amantiquity2026instructions`，https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-antiquity/information/author-instructions/preparing-your-materials
- 版本：美国考古学会期刊 American Antiquity 在出版方剑桥大学出版社网站上的投稿说明，2026 年 10 月访问。网页没有写发布日期，内容可能更新。

### E1

- 位置：References and in-text citations
- 原文："As of October 2026, the SAA journals use Chicago Manual of Style's Author-Date referencing style." / "In the text, the author's surname, date of publication and, if required, a page number, should be given. For example: "(Sauveterre 1998, 20)"."
- 要点：截至 2026 年 10 月（原文"As of October 2026"，没有说从哪天开始改），SAA 期刊用《芝加哥手册》的作者–年份制；文中写作者姓、出版年，需要时加页码，例如 (Sauveterre 1998, 20)。

### E2

- 位置：Authorship and contributorship
- 原文："All authors listed on any papers submitted to this journal must be in agreement that the authors listed would all be considered authors according to disciplinary norms, and that no authors who would reasonably be considered an author have been excluded."
- 要点：所有列出的作者都要同意：按学科惯例这些人都算作者，也没有漏掉理应署名的人。

### E3

- 位置：CRediT taxonomy for contributors
- 原文："When submitting a manuscript, the corresponding author will be prompted to provide further details concerning contributions to the manuscript using the CRediT taxonomy ." / "All parties who have contributed to the scholarly work, but do not meet the full criteria for authorship, should be recognised with their contributions described in terms of the CRediT taxonomy."
- 要点：投稿时通讯作者要用 CRediT（贡献者角色分类）说明各人的贡献；有贡献但不够作者标准的人，也要用 CRediT 写明贡献、给予承认。
