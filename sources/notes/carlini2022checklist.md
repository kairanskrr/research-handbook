# Carlini 2022：Research Paper Release Checklist

- 资料：`carlini2022checklist`，[原文](https://nicholas.carlini.com/writing/2022/paper-release-checklist.html)
- 版本：作者主页，2022-01-30

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Every time I make one (or someone tells me a mistake they made that I could easily see myself making) I write it down and add it to a checklist."
- 要点：每犯一次可避免的错误，就把它写进清单，下次发布前逐项检查。

### E2

- 位置：Secondary checklist for public releases
- 原文："All authors are listed on the paper." / "Author names are spelled correctly, with correct affiliations." / "Un-comment the acknowledgments section, and any author contribution section."
- 要点：公开发布前检查：作者是否列全、姓名和单位是否正确、致谢和作者贡献部分是否恢复（匿名投稿时常被注释掉）。
