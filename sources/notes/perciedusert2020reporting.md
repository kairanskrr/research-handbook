# Percie du Sert 等人 2020：Reporting animal research: Explanation and elaboration for the ARRIVE guidelines 2.0

- 资料：`perciedusert2020reporting`，[原文](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3000411)
- 版本：PLoS Biology 18(7): e3000411, 2020，开放获取，2026-10-06 读取。ARRIVE 2.0 的逐条解释文件。位置写条目编号。

### E1

- 位置：Item 1. Study design，1a
- 原文："1a. The groups being compared, including control groups. If no control group has been used, the rationale should be stated."
- 要点：要写清比较了哪些组，包括对照组；没有设对照组，要说明理由。

### E2

- 位置：Item 1a，Explanation
- 原文："Negative controls are used to determine whether a difference between groups is caused by the intervention (e.g., wild-type animals versus genetically modified animals, placebo versus active treatment, sham surgery versus surgical intervention). Positive controls can be used to support the interpretation of negative results or determine if an expected effect is detectable."
- 要点：阴性对照用来判断组间差异是不是由干预造成的（如野生型对基因改造动物、安慰剂对药物、假手术对手术）；阳性对照用来帮助解释阴性结果，或确认预期的效应能否被测出来。

### E3

- 位置：Box 1. Glossary，Experimental unit
- 原文："Experimental unit: Biological entity subjected to an intervention independently of all other units, such that it is possible to assign any two experimental units to different treatment groups."
- 要点：实验单位是独立接受干预的生物学个体，任意两个实验单位都可以被分到不同处理组。

### E4

- 位置：Item 1b，Explanation
- 原文："Conflating experimental units with subsamples or repeated measurements can lead to artificial inflation of the sample size. For example, measurements from 50 individual cells from a single mouse represent n = 1 when the experimental unit is the mouse."
- 要点：把子样本或重复测量当成实验单位，会人为夸大样本量；例如实验单位是小鼠时，从一只小鼠测 50 个细胞，n 仍然等于 1。

### E5

- 位置：Item 1b，Explanation
- 原文："Reporting n = 50 in this case is an example of pseudoreplication. It underestimates the true variability in a study, which can lead to false positives and invalidate the analysis and resulting conclusions"
- 要点：这种情况报告 n = 50 就是"伪重复"，会低估真实变异，导致假阳性，使分析和结论失效。

### E6

- 位置：Item 1b，Explanation
- 原文："However, the experimental unit may be the cage or the litter (e.g., a diet administered to a whole cage, or a treatment administered to a dam and investigated in her pups), or it could be part of the animal"
- 要点：实验单位不一定是单只动物：也可能是一笼、一窝动物，或动物身体的一部分。

### E7

- 位置：Item 4. Randomisation，Explanation
- 原文："Selecting an animal ‘at random’ (i.e., haphazardly or arbitrarily) from a cage is not statistically random, as the process involves human judgement."
- 要点：从笼子里"随便"抓一只动物不是统计意义上的随机，因为其中有人的判断。

### E8

- 位置：Item 5. Blinding，条目和 Explanation
- 原文："Describe who was aware of the group allocation at the different stages of the experiment (during the allocation, the conduct of the experiment, the outcome assessment, and the data analysis)." / "Researchers often expect a particular outcome and can unintentionally influence the experiment or interpret the data in such a way as to support their preferred hypothesis. Blinding is a strategy used to minimise these subjective biases."
- 要点：要写清实验各阶段（分组、实施、评估结局、分析数据）谁知道分组；研究者常常预期某个结果，可能无意中影响实验或解读数据，盲法就是用来减少这种主观偏倚的。

### E9

- 位置：Item 1a，Explanation（后半）
- 原文："Selectively excluding some experimental groups (for example, because the data are inconsistent or conflict with the narrative of the paper) is misleading and should be avoided."
- 要点：因为数据不一致或和论文的叙述冲突，就有选择地去掉某些实验组，是误导性的做法，应当避免。

### E10

- 位置：Item 1b，Explanation
- 原文："Within a design, biological and technical factors will often be organised hierarchically, such as cells within animals and mitochondria within cells, or cages within rooms and animals within cages."
- 要点：实验设计里，生物学因素和技术因素常常分层嵌套：细胞在动物里、线粒体在细胞里，或动物在笼子里、笼子在房间里。

### E11

- 位置：Item 1a，Explanation
- 原文："Negative controls are used to determine whether a difference between groups is caused by the intervention" / "Positive controls can be used to support the interpretation of negative results or determine if an expected effect is detectable."
- 要点：阴性对照用来判断组间差异是不是处理造成的；阳性对照用来帮助解释阴性结果，或确认实验能测出预期的效应。

### E12

- 位置：Item 13. Objectives，Explanation
- 原文："A typical exploratory study may measure multiple outcomes and look for patterns in the data or relationships that can be used to generate hypotheses. It may also be a pilot study, which aims to inform the design or feasibility of larger subsequent experiments."
- 要点：探索性研究测多个结局，在数据里找规律来提出假设；也可以是预实验，为后续更大的实验的设计和可行性提供信息。

### E13

- 位置：Item 13. Objectives，Explanation
- 原文："an experiment designed to detect a hypothesised effect will likely need to be analysed with inferential statistics, and a statistical estimation of the sample size will need to be performed a priori" / "Hypothesis-testing studies also have a predefined primary outcome measure"
- 要点：检验假设的实验要用推断统计分析，样本量要事先估算，并且有一个事先确定的主要结局指标。

### E14

- 位置：Item 2b，Explanation
- 原文："For any type of experiment, it is crucial to explain how the sample size was determined." / "Low power has three effects: first, within the experiment, real effects are more likely to be missed; second, when an effect is detected, this will often be an overestimation of the true effect size"
- 要点：任何实验都要说明样本量是怎么定的。统计功效低的后果：真实的效应更容易漏掉；即使测到了效应，也常常高估了它的大小。

### E15

- 位置：Item 2b，Box 3 及 Explanation
- 原文："Sample size calculation is based on a mathematical relationship between the following parameters: effect size, variability, significance level, power, and sample size." / "Consultation with a statistician is recommended, especially when the experimental design is complex or unusual."
- 要点：样本量计算基于效应量、变异程度、显著性水平、统计功效和样本量之间的数学关系；设计复杂或不常见时，建议咨询统计人员。

### E16

- 位置：Item 2b，Explanation
- 原文："When the objective of the preliminary study is primarily logistic or operational (e.g., to improve procedures and equipment), the number of animals needed is generally small. In such cases, power calculations are not appropriate" / "Pilot studies alone are unlikely to provide adequate data on variability for a power calculation for future experiments."
- 要点：以改进操作和设备为主要目的的预实验，所需样本一般很少，不适合做功效计算；单靠预实验，也不太可能为后续实验的功效计算提供足够的变异数据。

### E17

- 位置：Item 3a，Explanation
- 原文："To ensure scientific rigour, the criteria should be defined before the experiment starts and data are collected" / "Criteria for excluding samples or data include failure to meet quality control standards, such as insufficient sample volumes, unacceptable levels of contaminants, poor histological quality, etc."
- 要点：纳入和排除标准要在实验开始、收集数据之前定好；排除样品或数据的标准例如：没达到质控标准，如样品量不够、污染超标、组织切片质量差。

### E18

- 位置：Item 3a，Explanation
- 原文："At the very least, these criteria should be documented in a laboratory notebook and reported in manuscripts, explicitly stating that the criteria were defined before any data"
- 要点：排除标准至少要写进实验记录本，并在论文里报告，明确说明这些标准是在收集数据之前定的。

### E19

- 位置：Item 3b，Explanation
- 原文："In some instances, it may be scientifically justifiable to remove outlying data points from an analysis, such as obvious errors in data entry or measurement with readings that are outside a plausible range." / "providing the reasoning for removing data points enables the distinction to be made between responsible data cleaning and data manipulation"
- 要点：有时剔除离群数据点在科学上说得通，如明显的录入错误、读数超出合理范围；写明剔除理由，读者才能区分负责任的数据清理和数据操纵。

### E20

- 位置：Item 3b，Explanation
- 原文："Explicitly state when built-in models in statistics packages have been used to remove outliers"
- 要点：用了统计软件自带的离群值检验去掉数据，要明确写出来。

### E21

- 位置：Item 4，Box 4
- 原文："Blocking is a method of controlling natural variation among experimental units. This splits up the experiment into smaller subexperiments (blocks), and treatments are randomised to experimental units within each block" / "This takes into account nuisance variables that could potentially bias the results (e.g., cage location, day or week of procedure)."
- 要点：区组设计把实验拆成几个小的子实验（区组），在每个区组内随机分配处理，用来控制可能造成偏倚的无关变量，比如笼子位置、做实验的日子或周次。

### E22

- 位置：Item 4b，Explanation
- 原文："Strategies to report include standardising (keeping conditions the same, e.g., all surgeries done by the same surgeon), randomising (e.g., the sampling or measurement order), and blocking or counterbalancing"
- 要点：减少组间系统差异的办法：标准化（条件保持一致）、随机化（如取样或测量的顺序）、区组或平衡安排。

### E23

- 位置：Item 5，Explanation
- 原文："If this is not possible for every stage of an experiment (see Box 5), it should always be possible to conduct at least some of the stages blind. This has implications for the organisation of the experiment and may require help from additional personnel" / "Blinding is especially important during outcome assessment, particularly if there is a subjective element (e.g., when assessing behavioural changes or reading histological slides)"
- 要点：不能每个阶段都设盲时，至少有些阶段总能做到盲，这可能需要别人帮忙；评估结果时尤其要设盲，特别是带主观判断的评估，如看组织切片。

### E24

- 位置：Item 5，Explanation
- 原文："There are, however, compelling data from systematic reviews showing that nonblinded outcome assessment leads to the treatment effects being overestimated, and the lack of bias-reducing measures such as randomisation and blinding can contribute to as much as 30%–45% inflation of effect sizes"
- 要点：系统综述的数据显示，缺少随机和盲法等减少偏倚的措施，可能让效应量虚高多达 30%～45%。

### E25

- 位置：Item 6b，Explanation
- 原文："Studies that claim to test a hypothesis but do not specify a predefined primary outcome measure or those that change the primary outcome measure after data were collected (also known as primary outcome switching) are liable to selectively report only statistically significant results" / "Registering a protocol in advance protects the researcher against concerns about selective outcome reporting (also known as data dredging or p-hacking)"
- 要点：声称检验假设却没有事先确定主要结局，或收集数据后换主要结局，容易只挑显著的结果报告；事先登记研究方案，可以免于"选择性报告结局"（也叫数据挖掘或 p 值操纵）的嫌疑。

### E26

- 位置：Item 7a，Explanation
- 原文："The statistical analysis methods implemented will reflect the goals and the design of the experiment; they should be decided in advance before data are collected"
- 要点：统计分析方法要反映实验目的和设计，在收集数据之前定好。

### E27

- 位置：Item 10a，Explanation
- 原文："If a complete experiment is repeated on a different day or under different conditions, report the results of all repeats rather than selecting data from representative experiments."
- 要点：完整的实验在不同日子或不同条件下重复过，要报告所有重复的结果，而不是挑"代表性实验"的数据。

### E28

- 位置：Item 9a，Explanation
- 原文："Provide enough detail to enable others to replicate the methods and highlight any quality assurance and quality control used" / "When available, cite the Research Resource Identifier (RRID) for reagents and tools used"
- 要点：方法要写到别人能重复的程度，并说明用了哪些质量保证和质控措施；有研究资源标识符（RRID）的试剂和工具要引用它。

### E29

- 位置：Item 1a，Explanation
- 原文："A pilot study, such as one designed to test the feasibility of a procedure, might also not require a control group."
- 要点：检验操作可行性的预实验，可能不需要对照组。

### E30

- 位置：Item 6b，Explanation
- 原文："For exploratory studies, it is not necessary to identify a single primary outcome, and often multiple outcomes are assessed"
- 要点：探索性研究不必确定单一的主要结局，常常评估多个结局。

### E31

- 位置：Item 7a，Explanation
- 原文："Factors deemed to affect the variability of the outcome measure should also be handled in the analysis, for example, as a blocking factor (e.g., batch of reagent or experimenter)"
- 要点：可能影响结局变异的因素（如试剂批次、操作者），在分析时也要处理，例如作为区组因素。

### E32

- 位置：Item 12b，Explanation
- 原文："Include information on the rationale for choosing a particular species and explain how the outcome measures assessed are relevant to the condition under study and how the model was validated. Stating that an animal model is commonly used in the field is not appropriate, and a well-considered, detailed rationale should be provided."
- 要点：要写清为什么选这个物种、所测指标和所研究的疾病有什么关系、模型怎样验证过；只说"这个模型在本领域常用"不够，要给出想清楚的、具体的理由。

### E33

- 位置：Item 12a，Explanation
- 原文："Scientific background information for an animal study should demonstrate a clear evidence gap and explain why an in vivo approach was warranted. Systematic reviews of the animal literature provide the most convincing evidence that a research question has not been conclusively addressed, by showing the extent of current evidence within a field of research. They can also inform the choice of animal model by providing a comprehensive overview of the models used along with their benefits and limitations"
- 要点：背景要说明存在明确的证据空白、为什么必须用活体动物；动物文献的系统综述最能说明问题还没解决，也能帮你选模型，因为它会列出用过的模型及其优缺点。

### E34

- 位置：Box 3. Information used in a power calculation
- 原文："The effect size is estimated as a biologically relevant change in the primary outcome measure between the groups under study. This can be informed by similar studies and involves scientists exploring what magnitude of effect would generate interest and would be worth taking forward into further work. In preclinical studies, the clinical relevance of the effect should also be taken into consideration."
- 要点：效应量按主要结局指标上"有生物学意义的变化"来定，可参考类似研究，想清楚多大的效应才值得往下做；临床前研究还要考虑这个效应在临床上有没有意义。

### E35

- 位置：Box 3
- 原文："A target power between 80% and 95% is normally deemed acceptable, which entails a risk of false negative between 5% and 20%."
- 要点：目标统计功效一般定在 80%～95%，对应 5%～20% 的假阴性风险。

### E36

- 位置：Box 3
- 原文："Pilot studies alone are unlikely to provide adequate data on variability for a power calculation for future experiments. Systematic reviews and previous studies are more appropriate sources of information on variability"
- 要点：只靠预实验，很难得到够用的变异度数据来算后续实验的样本量；系统综述和以往研究是更合适的来源。

### E37

- 位置：Item 2b，Explanation
- 原文："Sample sizes that are too small (i.e., underpowered studies) produce inconclusive results, whereas sample sizes that are too large (i.e., overpowered studies) raise ethical issues over unnecessary use of animals and may produce trivial findings that are statistically significant but not biologically relevant"
- 要点：样本太小，结果不确定；样本太大，多用了动物，有伦理问题，还可能得到统计显著但没有生物学意义的结果。

### E38

- 位置：Item 2b，Explanation
- 原文："Low power has three effects: first, within the experiment, real effects are more likely to be missed; second, when an effect is detected, this will often be an overestimation of the true effect size; and finally, when low power is combined with publication bias, there is an increase in the false positive rate in the published literature"
- 要点：功效低有三个后果：真实效应更容易漏掉；测到的效应常常被高估；再加上发表偏倚，已发表文献里的假阳性会增多。

### E39

- 位置：Box 3
- 原文："If no power calculation was used to determine the sample size, state this explicitly and provide the reasoning that was used to decide on the sample size per group. Regardless of whether a power calculation was used or not, when explaining how the sample size was determined take into consideration any anticipated loss of animals or data"
- 要点：没做功效分析，要明说，并给出每组样本量的依据；不论做没做，定样本量时都要考虑预计会损失的动物或数据。

### E40

- 位置：Box 3
- 原文："For example, the number of animals needed for antibody or tissue production is determined by the amount required and the production ability of an individual animal." / "When the objective of the preliminary study is primarily logistic or operational (e.g., to improve procedures and equipment), the number of animals needed is generally small. In such cases, power calculations are not appropriate"
- 要点：有些研究事先算功效不合适，例如为生产抗体或组织而用的动物，数量取决于需要多少；以摸索流程、调试设备为主的预实验，动物数一般很少，也不适合做功效分析。

### E41

- 位置：Item 4，Explanation
- 原文："Selecting an animal ‘at random’ (i.e., haphazardly or arbitrarily) from a cage is not statistically random, as the process involves human judgement." / "Inferential statistics based on nonrandomised group allocation are not valid"
- 要点：从笼里"随手"抓一只不是统计意义上的随机，因为里面有人的判断；没有随机分组，推断统计就不成立。

### E42

- 位置：Item 4，Explanation
- 原文："Examples of appropriate randomisation methods include online random number generators (e.g., https://www.graphpad.com/quickcalcs/randomize1/) or a function like Rand() in spreadsheet software such as Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice."
- 要点：合适的随机方法包括在线随机数生成器，或电子表格软件里的 Rand() 函数。

### E43

- 位置：Box 4. Considerations for the randomisation strategy
- 原文："All animals/samples are simultaneously randomised to the treatment groups without considering any other variable. This strategy is rarely appropriate, as it cannot ensure that comparison groups are balanced for other variables that might influence the result of an experiment." / "This splits up the experiment into smaller subexperiments (blocks), and treatments are randomised to experimental units within each block"
- 要点：简单随机不考虑其他变量，很少是合适的做法，因为它不能保证各组在可能影响结果的其他变量上平衡；区组随机是把实验分成若干小的子实验（区组），在每个区组内随机分配处理。

### E44

- 位置：Box 4
- 原文："If blocking factors are used in the randomisation, they should also be included in the analysis."
- 要点：随机时用了区组因素，分析时也要把它放进去。

### E45

- 位置：Box 4，Examples of nuisance variables
- 原文："Time or day of the experiment" / "Litter, cage, or fish tank" / "Investigator or surgeon—different level of experience in the people administering the treatments, performing the surgeries, or assessing the results may result in varying stress levels in the animals or duration of anaesthesia"
- 要点：可以纳入随机策略的干扰变量包括：实验的时间或日期；窝、笼、鱼缸；操作者或手术者（经验不同可能让动物应激程度或麻醉时间不同）。

### E46

- 位置：Item 4b，Explanation
- 原文："For example, rodents in cages at the top of the rack may be exposed to higher light levels, which can affect stress" / "Strategies to report include standardising (keeping conditions the same, e.g., all surgeries done by the same surgeon), randomising (e.g., the sampling or measurement order), and blocking or counterbalancing (e.g., position of animal cages or tanks on the rack)"
- 要点：例如笼架顶层的啮齿类动物光照更强，可能影响应激；控制办法包括标准化（如所有手术由同一人做）、随机化（如取样或测量顺序）、区组或平衡安排（如笼子在架上的位置）。

### E47

- 位置：Item 5，Explanation
- 原文："If this is not possible for every stage of an experiment (see Box 5), it should always be possible to conduct at least some of the stages blind. This has implications for the organisation of the experiment and may require help from additional personnel—for example, a surgeon to perform interventions, a technician to code the treatment syringes for each animal, or a colleague to code the treatment groups for the analysis."
- 要点：做不到每个阶段都设盲，至少总能让部分阶段设盲；这需要在组织上安排，可能要别人帮忙，例如由外科医生做干预、技术员给每只动物的注射器编码、同事给分析用的分组编码。

### E48

- 位置：Box 5，During allocation
- 原文："Allocation concealment refers to concealing the treatment to be allocated to each individual animal from those assigning the animals to groups, until the time of assignment. Together with randomisation, allocation concealment helps minimise selection bias"
- 要点：分组隐藏是指在分配那一刻之前，负责分组的人不知道下一只动物会接受哪种处理；它和随机分组一起减少选择偏倚。

### E49

- 位置：Box 5，During the outcome assessment
- 原文："Blinding is especially important during outcome assessment, particularly if there is a subjective element (e.g., when assessing behavioural changes or reading histological slides)" / "If the person assessing the outcome cannot be blinded to the group allocation (e.g., obvious phenotypic or behavioural differences between groups), some, but not all, of the sources of bias could be mitigated by sending data for analysis to a third party"
- 要点：结局评估阶段设盲尤其重要，特别是有主观成分时（如评估行为变化、读组织切片）；评估者无法设盲时（如各组表型差别明显），把数据交给第三方分析，可以减少部分偏倚。

### E50

- 位置：Box 5，During the data analysis
- 原文："This type of blinding is often neglected but is important, as the analyst makes many semisubjective decisions such as applying data transformation to outcome measures, choosing methods for handling missing data, and handling outliers." / "Data can be coded prior to analysis so that the treatment group cannot be identified before analysis is completed."
- 要点：分析阶段的盲法常被忽视，但分析者要做很多半主观的决定（数据变换、缺失值、离群值）；可以在分析前给数据编码，分析完才揭晓组别。

### E51

- 位置：Item 3，Explanation
- 原文："To ensure scientific rigour, the criteria should be defined before the experiment starts and data are collected"
- 要点：纳入和排除标准要在实验开始、收集数据之前定好。

### E52

- 位置：Item 3a，Explanation
- 原文："At the very least, these criteria should be documented in a laboratory notebook and reported in manuscripts, explicitly stating that the criteria were defined before any data was collected."
- 要点：至少要把这些标准记在实验记录本里，在论文中写明它们是在收集任何数据之前定的。

### E53

- 位置：Item 3b，Explanation
- 原文："Reporting exclusions and attritions provides valuable information to other investigators evaluating the results or who intend to repeat the experiment or test the intervention in other species. It may also provide important safety information for human trials (e.g., exclusions related to adverse effects)."
- 要点：报告排除和中途损失的动物，对评估结果、重复实验或在其他物种上测试的人都有用，还可能为人体试验提供重要的安全信息（如因不良反应而排除的动物）。

### E54

- 位置：Item 6b，Explanation
- 原文："In a hypothesis-testing experiment, the primary outcome measure answers the main biological question. It is the outcome of greatest importance, identified in the planning stages of the experiment and used as the basis for the sample size calculation"
- 要点：在验证假设的实验里，主要结局指标回答核心问题，在计划阶段就确定，并作为计算样本量的依据。

### E55

- 位置：Item 6a，Explanation
- 原文："Failure to disclose all the outcomes that were measured introduces bias in the literature, as positive outcomes (e.g., those statistically significant) are reported more often"
- 要点：不报告测过的全部指标，会让文献产生偏倚，因为阳性结果被报告得更多。

### E56

- 位置：Item 13，Explanation
- 原文："Knowing whether a study is exploratory or hypothesis-testing is critical to its interpretation." / "In contrast, exploratory research investigates many possible effects and is likely to yield more false positive results because some will be positive by chance."
- 要点：研究是探索性的还是验证假设的，决定了怎样解读结果；探索性研究考察很多可能的效应，其中一些会碰巧阳性，所以假阳性更多。

### E57

- 位置：Item 13，Explanation
- 原文："Hypothesis-testing studies may collect additional information for exploratory purposes; it is important to distinguish which hypotheses were prespecified and which originated after data inspection"
- 要点：验证性研究也可以顺带收集探索用的信息，但要分清哪些假设是事先定的，哪些是看了数据才有的。

### E58

- 位置：Item 17a，Explanation
- 原文："For hypothesis-testing studies, interpretations should be restricted to the primary outcome (see Item 6. Outcome measures). Exploratory results derived from additional outcomes should not be described as conclusive, as they may be underpowered and less reliable."
- 要点：验证性研究的结论应限于主要结局；其他指标得到的探索性结果可能功效不足、不够可靠，不应写成定论。

### E59

- 位置：Item 8a，Explanation
- 原文："The species, strain, substrain, sex, weight, and age of animals are critical factors that can influence most experimental results" / "(e.g., a mouse referred to as an adult can vary in age from 6 to 20 weeks"
- 要点：物种、品系、亚系、性别、体重和年龄会影响大多数实验结果；例如"成年小鼠"可以是 6 到 20 周龄，所以要写具体年龄。

### E60

- 位置：Item 8b，Explanation
- 原文："For example, animals of the same strain but from different sources, or animals obtained from the same source but at different times, may be genetically different"
- 要点：同一品系、不同来源的动物，或同一来源、不同时间购入的动物，遗传上可能不同。

### E61

- 位置：Item 15，Explanation
- 原文："The environment determines the health and wellbeing of the animals, and every aspect of it can potentially affect their behavioural and physiological responses, thereby affecting research outcomes"
- 要点：饲养环境决定动物的健康和福利，环境的每个方面都可能影响动物的行为和生理反应，从而影响研究结果。

### E62

- 位置：Item 15，Explanation
- 原文："If no environmental enrichment was provided, this should be clearly stated with justification. Similarly, scientific justification needs to be reported for withholding food and water and for singly housing animals"
- 要点：没有提供环境丰富化，要写明并说明理由；禁食禁水、单笼饲养也要给出科学理由。

### E63

- 位置：Item 9a、9b，Explanation
- 原文："Animal handling can be a source of stress, and the specific method used (e.g., mice picked up by tail or in cupped hands) can affect research outcomes" / "Along with innate circadian rhythms, these can affect research outcomes such as behavioural, physiological, and immunological parameters"
- 要点：抓取动物的方式（如提尾巴还是用手捧）会造成应激、影响结果；光照周期、给药和测量的时间，加上动物本身的昼夜节律，会影响行为、生理和免疫指标。

### E64

- 位置：Item 14，Explanation
- 原文："This includes ensuring that they have the relevant approval for their study from an appropriate ethics committee and/or regulatory body before the work starts."
- 要点：作者要确保在研究开始之前，已经得到相应伦理委员会和（或）监管机构的批准。

### E65

- 位置：Item 16c，Explanation
- 原文："Humane endpoints are predetermined morphological, physiological, and/or behavioural signs that define the circumstances under which an animal will be removed from an experimental study."
- 要点：人道终点是事先定好的形态、生理或行为征象，一旦出现，动物就要退出实验。

### E66

- 位置：Item 19，Explanation
- 原文："Registering a protocol before the start of the experiment enables researchers to demonstrate that the hypothesis, approach, and analysis were planned in advance and not shaped by data as they emerged; it enhances scientific rigour and protects the researcher against concerns about selective reporting of results"
- 要点：实验开始前登记方案，可以证明假设、方法和分析是事先计划的，不是看着数据调整的；这能提高严谨性，也让研究者免受"选择性报告"的质疑。

### E67

- 位置：Item 19，Explanation
- 原文："A protocol should consist of (1) the question being addressed and the key features of the research that is proposed, such as the hypothesis being tested, the primary outcome measure (if applicable), and the statistical analysis plan; and (2) the laboratory procedures to be used to perform the planned experiment."
- 要点：方案应包括：（1）研究问题和关键设计，如要检验的假设、主要结局指标、统计分析计划；（2）实验要用的操作步骤。

### E68

- 位置：Item 19，Explanation
- 原文："Other online resources include the Open Science Framework, which is suitable to deposit PHISPS (Population; Hypothesis; Intervention; Statistical Analysis Plan; Primary; Outcome Measure; Sample Size Calculation) protocols and provide researchers with the flexibility to embargo the preregistration, keep it from public view until the research is published"
- 要点：开放科学框架（Open Science Framework）适合存放包含研究对象、假设、干预、统计分析计划、主要结局指标、样本量计算的方案，并可以设置保密期，在论文发表前不公开。

### E69

- 位置：Item 19，Explanation
- 原文："When there have been deviations from the protocol, describe the rationale for these changes in the publication so that readers can take this into account when assessing the findings."
- 要点：偏离了方案，要在论文里写明改动的理由，让读者评估结果时考虑进去。

### E70

- 位置：Item 18，Explanation
- 原文："Similarly, human clinical trials initiated based on insufficient or misleading animal research evidence increase research waste and negatively influence the risk-benefit balance for research participants"
- 要点：依据不足或有误导性的动物研究证据启动人体临床试验，会增加科研浪费，也会让受试者承担的风险和收益失衡。

### E71

- 位置：Item 8a，Explanation
- 原文："Reporting the weight or the sex of the animals used may not feasible for all studies. For example, sex may be unknown for embryos or juveniles" / "If reporting these characteristics can be reasonably expected for the species used and the experimental setting but are not reported, provide a justification."
- 要点：不是所有研究都能报告体重或性别，例如胚胎或幼体的性别可能未知；如果按物种和实验情况应当能报告却没报告，要说明理由。

### E72

- 位置：Item 10b，Explanation
- 原文："In hypothesis-testing studies using inferential statistics, investigators frequently confuse statistical significance and small p-values with biological or clinical importance"
- 要点：研究者常把统计显著、P 值小，误当成有生物学或临床意义。
