# Vinitsky：Ways to Engage Deeply with a Paper

- 资料：`vinitskypaper`，[原文](https://www.eugenevinitsky.com/posts/paper-games)
- 版本：个人博客，年份待确认

### E1

- 位置：A template
- 原文："The goal of this template is to help you avoid the situation where you've been reading a paper for an hour or two but come away realizing you've in fact learned nothing."
- 要点：读论文模板的目的，是避免读了一两个小时却什么都没学到。

### E2

- 位置：Why am I reading this paper?
- 原文："Fill in this section to convince yourself that it's worthwhile to invest the time into reading this paper. If you can't fill it in, maybe you shouldn't actually read the paper?"
- 要点：读之前先写下"为什么要读这篇"；写不出来，也许就不该读。

### E3

- 位置：Summary
- 原文："1 sentence summary [...] Paragraph summary [...] Complete summary (I do this for vanishingly few papers)"
- 要点：总结分三档：一句话、一段话、完整总结；完整总结只对极少数论文做。

### E4

- 位置：Unresolved question
- 原文："What bit of the paper didn't you understand? You can link this to a main sheet so that maybe you can come back to it later and see that you get it now."
- 要点：记下没读懂的地方，汇总到一处，以后回头再看。

### E5

- 位置：Unresolved question
- 原文："Once you've completed this section, you might consider stopping if the "Why am I reading this paper" section was not super compelling. Everything below requires a lot more work and should not be done for most papers."
- 要点：做完总结和疑问后，如果读这篇的理由不够有力，就可以停下；后面更费力的部分大多数论文不需要做。

### E6

- 位置：Paper games
- 原文："Imagine there is a panel grilling you on the paper, what question might they ask you that you wouldn't be able to answer?"
- 要点：想象一个评审小组在追问你这篇论文，哪个问题你答不上来？

### E7

- 位置：Paper games
- 原文："What experiment would I have run to make this paper more convincing?"
- 要点：问自己：我会补做什么实验，让这篇论文更有说服力？

### E8

- 位置：Paper games
- 原文："If I were to do a new project based on what's in this paper, what would it be?"
- 要点：问自己：如果基于这篇论文做一个新项目，会做什么？
