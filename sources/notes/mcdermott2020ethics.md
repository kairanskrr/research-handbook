# McDermott & Hatemi 2020：Ethics in field experimentation: A call to establish new standards to protect the public from unwanted manipulation and real harms

- 资料：`mcdermott2020ethics`，[原文（PubMed Central）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7720186/)
- 版本：PNAS 117(48): 30014–30021，2020，观点文章（Perspective）；PubMed Central 免费全文。作者立场鲜明，是对田野实验伦理的批评。位置写段落。

### E1

- 位置：引言（摘要之后第 1 段）
- 原文："a review of all articles published in the preeminent political science journals from 2013 to 2017 found that almost none of the field experiments in that period reflected compliance with the current ethical requirements that govern research with human participants"
- 要点：作者引用的一项综述发现，2013–2017 年顶级政治学期刊发表的田野实验，几乎都没有体现出遵守现行人类受试者伦理要求。

### E2

- 位置：New Risks from Large-Scale Social Science Research: Social Pressure Manipulations
- 原文："In seeking to identify what increases or depresses voter turnout, for example, scores of studies have undertaken large-scale interventions in real elections"
- 要点：为研究什么会提高或压低投票率，许多研究在真实选举中做了大规模干预。

### E3

- 位置：New Risks from Large-Scale Social Science Research: Social Pressure Manipulations
- 原文："Several studies have targeted very large minority populations in such ventures, large enough to change electoral outcomes, by sending racially charged group-conflict messages and other anxiety-inducing stimuli."
- 要点：有些研究针对规模大到足以改变选举结果的少数族裔群体，发送带种族冲突色彩、令人焦虑的信息。

### E4

- 位置：New Requirements and a New Standard: Respect for Societies
- 原文："This proposed fourth basic principle, respect for societies, requires addressing the potential effects manipulations can have on both local and large-scale societal outcomes."
- 要点：作者提议增加第四条基本原则"尊重社会"，要求考虑干预对地方和整个社会的影响。

### E5

- 位置：New Requirements and a New Standard: Respect for Societies
- 原文："we suggest that, for any field experiment on a real-life society, the relevant publics should be consented and debriefed."
- 要点：作者主张：任何在真实社会中做的田野实验，都应征得相关公众同意并在事后告知。
