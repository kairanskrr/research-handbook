# Anderson & Shattuck 2012：Design-Based Research: A Decade of Progress in Education Research?

- 资料：`anderson2012design`，[作者稿（阿萨巴斯卡大学机构库）](https://auspace.athabascau.ca/handle/2149/3184)
- 版本：作者接受稿（Word 文件），存于第一作者所在的阿萨巴斯卡大学机构知识库；正式版发表于 Educational Researcher 41(1): 16–25，2012。位置写作者稿的小标题。

### E1

- 位置：引言
- 原文："DBR is a methodology designed by and for educators that seeks to increase the impact, transfer, and translation of education research into improved practice. In addition, it stresses the need for theory building and development of design principles that guide, inform, and improve both practice and research in educational contexts."
- 要点：基于设计的研究（design-based research）是由教育者设计、为教育者服务的方法，目标是让研究更好地转化为实践改进，同时强调建构理论、提炼设计原则。

### E2

- 位置：What is DBR?
- 原文："Being situated in a real educational context."
- 要点：特征之一：在真实的教育环境中进行。

### E3

- 位置：What is DBR? — Involving multiple iterations
- 原文："This evolution, through multiple iterations is but one of the challenges of the methodology in that it is difficult to know when (or if ever) the research program is completed."
- 要点：需要多轮迭代，这带来一个难题：很难知道研究什么时候（甚至是否）算做完。

### E4

- 位置：What is DBR? — Involving a collaborative partnership between research(s) and practitioner(s)
- 原文："In action research the educator is both researcher and teacher (Kuhn & Quigley, 1997). The partnership in a design-based study recognizes that teachers are usually too busy and often ill trained to conduct rigorous research."
- 要点：行动研究里，教育者既是研究者又是教师；基于设计的研究则是研究者和教师合作，因为教师通常太忙、也常缺少做严谨研究的训练。

### E5

- 位置：Comparison to Action Research
- 原文："Finally, we repeat that action research is normally carried on by the teacher(s) alone, thus not benefitting from the expertise and energy of a research and design team that characterizes DBR."
- 要点：行动研究通常由教师自己做，没有基于设计的研究那样的研究和设计团队。

### E6

- 位置：Problems with DBR
- 原文："They argued that "if a researcher is intimately involved in the conceptualization, design, development, implementation, and re-searching of a pedagogical approach, then ensuring that researchers can make credible and trustworthy assertions is a challenge" (p. 10)."
- 要点：作者引用 Barab 和 Squire 的批评：研究者全程参与一种教学方法的构想、设计、实施和研究，要保证结论可信是个挑战。

### E7

- 位置：Problems with DBR
- 原文："Herrington, McKenney, Reeves, and Oliver (2007) addressed this concern head-on in an article demonstrating ways in which DBR can be used as a basis for a doctoral dissertation over a four year period."
- 要点：针对迭代多、周期难界定的问题，作者提到有文章专门讨论怎样在四年里把基于设计的研究做成博士论文。

### E8

- 位置：Results
- 原文："There was no meta-analysis which is not surprising given that the studies generally lacked control group comparisons, in line with DBR philosophy."
- 要点：作者分析的 47 篇高被引文章里没有元分析；这些研究普遍没有对照组，这和该方法的理念一致。

### E9

- 位置：Results
- 原文："Unlike quantitative studies most DBR studies do not produce measureable effect sizes that demonstrate "what works.""
- 要点：和定量研究不同，大多数基于设计的研究不产出能证明"什么有效"的效应量。

### E10

- 位置：What is DBR? — Use of mixed methods
- 原文："DBR is largely agnostic when it comes to epistemological challenges to choice of methodologies used and typically involves mixed methods using a variety of research tools and techniques."
- 要点：基于设计的研究在方法选择上不拘一格，通常用混合方法和多种研究工具。
