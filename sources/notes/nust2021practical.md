# Nüst & Pebesma 2021：Practical Reproducibility in Geography and Geosciences

- 资料：`nust2021practical`，[作者接受稿（EarthArXiv）](https://eartharxiv.org/repository/object/3216/download/6465/)，doi:10.1080/24694452.2020.1806028
- 版本：Annals of the American Association of Geographers 111(5)，2021（2020 年 10 月在线发表）。读取的是作者接受稿，CC BY 4.0 许可。作者来自德国明斯特大学地理信息研究所。位置写小节名。

### E1

- 位置：Reproducible workflows > Creating reproducible workflows
- 原文："A script-based workflow means that a user can execute a full analysis, i.e., starting from raw data up to visualisations for publication, without any manual intervention."
- 要点：基于脚本的工作流程，指从原始数据到发表用的图，整个分析不需要任何手工操作就能跑完。

### E2

- 位置：Reproducible workflows > Creating reproducible workflows
- 原文："practicing version pinning in the respective configuration files, allow freezing the logical component in a specific state."
- 要点：在配置文件里锁定软件包版本，可以把软件环境固定在某个状态。

### E3

- 位置：Reproducible workflows > Creating reproducible workflows
- 原文："All parts of an (executable) research compendium must be adequately licensed to allow use and extension (cf. Stodden 2009), and use open formats (Marwick 2015)."
- 要点：研究材料包的各部分都要有合适的许可，并用开放格式。

### E4

- 位置：Examples
- 原文："They extend earlier collections of cases in geography (Brunsdon 2016), spatial data collection and analysis in ecology (Lewis, Wal, and Fifield 2018), spatial statistics"
- 要点：作者引用的案例来自地理学、生态学的空间数据收集和分析、空间统计和地球科学，说明这些问题跨越多个学科。

### E5

- 位置：Examples
- 原文："such as setting a seed to avoid problems with non-deterministic results."
- 要点：作者肯定被复现研究的做法之一：设定随机种子，避免结果不确定。

### E6

- 位置：Challenges for practical reproducibility in geography and geosciences
- 原文："They argue that cartography is often considered a design process and an art, but this should not be at the cost of reproducibility, e.g., due to manual tweaking of visual appearances."
- 要点：制图常被当作设计和艺术，但不应因为手工调整外观而牺牲可复现性。

### E7

- 位置：Challenges for practical reproducibility in geography and geosciences
- 原文："Online services pose a challenge for reproducibility, because they may change over time or disappear." / "Still, the code to access geoservices, the requests sent as well as the retrieved responses must all be stored"
- 要点：在线地理服务可能变化或消失；访问它们的代码、发出的请求和得到的返回结果都要保存。

### E8

- 位置：Challenges for practical reproducibility in geography and geosciences
- 原文："Prevailing GIS software is GUI based, proprietary, or both. To fix these limitations, either these tools must be updated to provide an executable workflow, i.e., recording a trace of the user interactions (cf. Brunsdon 2016), or researchers need to switch to open tools to achieve a unified toolchain"
- 要点：主流地理信息系统软件靠图形界面点击操作、闭源，或两者兼有；要么软件能记录用户操作形成可执行流程，要么研究者换用开放工具。

### E9

- 位置：Challenges for practical reproducibility in geography and geosciences
- 原文："Data collection and creation of visualisations can be reproducible and should be, because maps are commonly used for interaction with study participants during data collection, and for communicating results."
- 要点：即使是定性地理信息系统研究，数据收集和制图也可以、而且应当可复现，因为地图常用于和参与者互动、传达结果。

### E10

- 位置：Challenges for practical reproducibility in geography and geosciences
- 原文："Made-up data prevents de-anonymisation and can be tailored to illustrate the method."
- 要点：用编造的模拟数据可以防止重新识别个人，又能演示方法。
