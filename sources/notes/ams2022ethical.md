# AMS：Policy Statement on Ethical Guidelines

- 资料：`ams2022ethical`，[原文](https://www.ams.org/about-us/governance/policy-statements/sec-ethics)
- 版本：美国数学会伦理准则，2005 年通过，2019、2022 年修订；网页，2026-10-06 读取。

### E1

- 位置：I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- 原文："The knowing presentation of another person's mathematical discovery as one's own constitutes plagiarism and is a serious violation of professional ethics. Plagiarism may occur for any type of work, whether written or oral and whether published or not."
- 要点：明知是别人的数学发现却当作自己的，就是抄袭；无论书面还是口头、发表还是没发表都算。

### E2

- 位置：I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- 原文："To give appropriate credit, even to unpublished materials, materials on websites, and announced results (because the knowledge that something is true or false is valuable, however it is obtained);"
- 要点：即使是没发表的材料、网站上的材料、只宣布过的结果，也要恰当致谢引用。

### E3

- 位置：I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- 原文："To publish full details of results that are announced without unreasonable delay, because claiming a result in advance of its having been achieved with reasonable certainty injures the community by restraining those working toward the same goal;"
- 要点：宣布了结果，就要及时发表完整细节；在没有合理把握前抢先宣布，会阻碍同一目标上的其他人。

### E4

- 位置：I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- 原文："To correct in a timely way or to withdraw work that is erroneous."
- 要点：发现工作有错，要及时更正或撤回。

### E5

- 位置：I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- 原文："All the authors listed for a paper, however, must have made a significant contribution to its content, and all who have made such a contribution must be offered the opportunity to be listed as an author."
- 要点：署名的每个作者都必须对内容有实质贡献；每个有实质贡献的人都应有机会署名。

### E6

- 位置：I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- 原文："A claim of independence may not be based on ignorance of widely disseminated results."
- 要点：不能因为不了解已广泛传播的结果，就声称自己是独立发现的。

### E7

- 位置：II. Social Responsibility of Mathematicians
- 原文："The appearance of bias in reviewing, refereeing, or in funding decisions must be scrupulously avoided, particularly where decisions may affect one's own research, that of colleagues, or of one's students. When conflicts of interest occur, one should withdraw from the decision-making process."
- 要点：审稿和评审中要严格避免偏袒的嫌疑；有利益冲突时应退出决策。

### E8

- 位置：II. Social Responsibility of Mathematicians
- 原文："Similarly, a referee's report is normally provided with the understanding that the name of the writer be withheld from certain interested parties, and the referee must be anonymous unless otherwise indicated in advance."
- 要点：审稿报告的作者身份对相关方保密，审稿人是匿名的，除非事先另有说明。

### E9

- 位置：IV. Publications
- 原文："Editors are responsible for the timely refereeing of articles and must judge articles by the state of knowledge at the time of submission. Editors should accept a paper for publication only if they are reasonably certain the paper is correct."
- 要点：编辑要按投稿时的知识状态评判论文，只有在合理确信论文正确时才接收。

### E10

- 位置：IV. Publications
- 原文："Both editors and referees must respect the confidentiality of materials submitted to them unless these materials have previously been made public, and above all may not appropriate to themselves ideas in work submitted to them"
- 要点：编辑和审稿人要对稿件保密（除非已公开），尤其不能把稿件里的想法据为己有。

### E11

- 位置：IV. Publications
- 原文："The published article should bear the date on which the manuscript was originally submitted to the journal for publication, together with the dates of any revisions."
- 要点：发表的文章应注明最初投稿日期和修改日期。

### E12

- 位置：III. Education and Granting of Degrees
- 原文："In particular, a Ph.D. degree is a certification of both mathematical knowledge and independent achievement in mathematics."
- 要点：数学博士学位既证明数学知识，也证明在数学上有独立成就。

### E13

- 位置：III. Education and Granting of Degrees
- 原文："Mathematicians and organizations involved in advising doctoral students should fully inform them about the employment opportunities, both within and outside academia, that may be available to them upon completion of their degrees."
- 要点：指导博士生的人应向学生充分介绍学术界内外的就业机会。

### E14

- 位置：开头（准则说明）
- 原文："These are guidelines, not a collection of rigid rules."
- 要点：这是指导原则，不是一套死板的规则。
