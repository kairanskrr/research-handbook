# Gagnier et al. 2013：The CARE guidelines: consensus-based clinical case reporting guideline development

- 资料：`gagnier2013care`，[Journal of Medical Case Reports](https://doi.org/10.1186/1752-1947-7-223)，[PMC 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3844611/)（开放获取）
- 版本：J Med Case Rep 2013;7:223。同一指南同时发表在多家期刊。

### E1

- 位置：Background
- 原文："Case reports may generate hypotheses for future clinical studies, prove useful in the evaluation of global convergences of systems-oriented approaches, and guide the individualization and personalization of treatments in clinical practice."
- 要点：病例报告可以为后续临床研究提出假设，也能指导个体化治疗。

### E2

- 位置：Background
- 原文："For example, our understanding of the relationship between thalidomide and congenital abnormalities and the use of propranolol for the treatment of infantile hemangiomas began with case reports."
- 要点：沙利度胺与先天畸形的关系、普萘洛尔治疗婴儿血管瘤，最初都来自病例报告。

### E3

- 位置：Background
- 原文："Written without the benefit of reporting guidelines, case reports often are insufficiently rigorous to be aggregated for data analysis, inform research design, or guide clinical practice."
- 要点：没有报告规范时，病例报告往往不够严谨，难以汇总分析或指导研究和临床。

### E4

- 位置：Results，informed consent
- 原文："We believe that authors have an ethical duty to obtain informed consent from the patient to publish patient information in a case report."
- 要点：在病例报告中发表病人信息，作者有伦理义务取得病人的知情同意。

### E5

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："This 13-item checklist provides a framework to satisfy the need for completeness and transparency for published case reports."
- 要点：CARE 是一份 13 项的病例报告清单。

### E6

- 位置：Limitations
- 原文："Second, we recognize that causality determinations are a challenge for case reports even when following reporting guidelines."
- 要点：即使按规范写，病例报告也很难判断因果。

### E7

- 位置：Conclusions
- 原文："Unlike randomized controlled trials, case reports are individual reports related to the care of individual patients where the sample size is one."
- 要点：病例报告是关于单个病人诊疗的报告，样本量为一。
