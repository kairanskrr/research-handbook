# ACM 2020：Artifact Review and Badging（Version 1.1）

- 资料：`acm2020artifact`，[原文](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/artifact-review-and-badging-current)
- 版本：v1.1，2020-08-24。ACM 网站拒绝脚本访问，原文文本取自 Internet Archive 快照 `https://web.archive.org/web/20260102131040/https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/artifact-review-and-badging-current`。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："By "artifact" we mean a digital object that was either created by the authors to be used as part of the study or generated by the experiment itself. For example, artifacts can be software systems, scripts used to run experiments, input datasets, raw data collected in the experiment, or scripts used to analyze results."
- 要点：制品（artifact）指作者为研究制作的、或实验产生的数字对象，例如软件系统、运行实验的脚本、输入数据、实验得到的原始数据、分析脚本。

### E2

- 位置：Artifacts Evaluated – Functional v1.1
- 原文："The artifacts associated with the research are found to be documented, consistent, complete, exercisable, and include appropriate evidence of verification and validation."
- 要点："已评估–可运行"级别要求制品有文档、与论文一致、完整、能运行，并附有验证的证据。

### E3

- 位置：Artifacts Evaluated – Reusable v1.1
- 原文："they are very carefully documented and well-structured to the extent that reuse and repurposing is facilitated"
- 要点："已评估–可复用"级别在可运行之上，还要求文档细致、结构良好，便于别人复用和改作他用。

### E4

- 位置：Artifacts Available v1.1
- 原文："Author-created artifacts relevant to this paper have been placed on a publically accessible archival repository. A DOI or link to this repository along with a unique identifier for the object is provided." / "Personal web pages are not acceptable for this purpose."
- 要点："可获取"级别要求把制品放到公开的长期存档库，并给出 DOI 或带唯一标识的链接；个人网页不算。

### E5

- 位置：Results Validated
- 原文："The main results of the paper have been obtained in a subsequent study by a person or team other than the authors, using, in part, artifacts provided by the author." / "The main results of the paper have been independently obtained in a subsequent study by a person or team other than the authors, without the use of author-supplied artifacts."
- 要点："结果已复现"指他人用作者的制品得到了主要结果；"结果已重现"指他人不用作者的制品、独立得到了主要结果。

### E6

- 位置：Artifacts Evaluated – Functional，Notes（Complete）
- 原文："Proprietary artifacts need not be included. If they are required to exercise the package then this should be documented, along with instructions on how to obtain them."
- 要点：专有的部分可以不包含，但如果运行需要它们，要写明并说明怎么获取。

### E7

- 位置：Artifacts Evaluated
- 原文："Artifacts need not be made publicly available to be considered for this badge. However, they do need to be made available to reviewers."
- 要点：申请"已评估"徽章不要求公开制品，但要提供给评审人。

### E8

- 位置：开头
- 原文："well-formed and documented artifacts allow others to build directly upon the previous work through reuse and repurposing."
- 要点：结构良好、有文档的制品让别人能直接在你的工作之上继续做。

### E9

- 位置：开头
- 原文："An experimental result is not fully established unless it can be independently reproduced."
- 要点：实验结果只有能被独立复现，才算真正确立。

### E10

- 位置：开头
- 原文："such review processes are encouraged, but remain completely optional for ACM journals and conferences, and when they are made available, it is recommended that participation by authors also be made optional."
- 要点：制品评审受到鼓励，但对会议、期刊和作者都是自愿的。

### E11

- 位置：Badging
- 原文："We recommend that three separate badges related to artifact review be associated with research articles in ACM publications: Artifacts Evaluated, Artifacts Available and Results Validated."
- 要点：三类徽章：制品已评估、制品可获取、结果已验证，相互独立。

### E12

- 位置：Artifacts Evaluated – Reusable v1.1
- 原文："very carefully documented and well-structured to the extent that reuse and repurposing is facilitated."
- 要点："可复用"级：文档和结构好到别人能直接复用、改作他用。

### E13

- 位置：Artifacts Available v1.1
- 原文："Author-created artifacts relevant to this paper have been placed on a publically accessible archival repository. [...] Personal web pages are not acceptable for this purpose."
- 要点："可获取"：放在公开的长期存档库里，并给出 DOI 或链接；个人主页不算。

### E14

- 位置：Results Validated
- 原文："exact replication or reproduction of results is not required, or even expected. Instead, the results must be in agreement to within a tolerance deemed acceptable for experiments of the given type."
- 要点："结果已验证"不要求数字完全一致，只要在该类实验可接受的误差内一致、不改变主要结论。
