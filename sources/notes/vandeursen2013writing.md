# van Deursen 2013：Some Research Paper Writing Recommendations

- 资料：`vandeursen2013writing`，[原文](https://avandeursen.com/2013/07/10/research-paper-writing-recommendations/)
- 版本：作者博客文章，2013-07-10。网页，位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Identify the Contributions
- 原文："A contribution is anything the world did not know before this paper, but which we now do know thanks to this paper."
- 要点：贡献的定义：在这篇论文之前世界不知道、因为这篇论文现在知道了的东西。

### E2

- 位置：Identify the Contributions
- 原文："Each contribution is an outcome, not the process of doing something. Contributions are things, not effort."
- 要点：贡献是成果，不是过程或工作量。"花六个月人工分析了 50 万条提交记录"不是贡献，从中得到的发现才是。

### E3

- 位置：Structure the Paper
- 原文："Furthermore, it is essential to separate background material from own contributions."
- 要点：把背景材料和自己的贡献明确分开，让读者和审稿人一眼看出什么是新的。

### E4

- 位置：Communicate the Contributions
- 原文："Thus, contributions should not just be strong, they should also be communicable."
- 要点：审稿讨论时大家以贡献为单位讨论论文，所以贡献不仅要强，还要讲得清、让非专家也能理解。

### E5

- 位置：Share Contributions Early
- 原文："Therefore, it is crucial to offer help to others, volunteering to read preliminary drafts of papers"
- 要点：主动帮别人读初稿，换来别人读你的初稿，互相帮助。
