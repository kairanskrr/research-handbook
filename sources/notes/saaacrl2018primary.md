# SAA 与 ACRL/RBMS 2018：Guidelines for Primary Source Literacy

- 资料：`saaacrl2018primary`，[原文 PDF](https://www2.archivists.org/sites/all/files/Guidelines%20for%20Primary%20Souce%20Literacy_AsApproved062018_1.pdf)（[标准页面](https://www2.archivists.org/standards/guidelines-for-primary-source-literacy)）
- 版本：美国档案工作者协会（SAA）与美国大学与研究图书馆协会（Association of College and Research Libraries，ACRL）珍本与手稿分会（Rare Books and Manuscripts Section，RBMS）联合工作组编写，2018 年 2 月经 ACRL 理事会、2018 年 6 月经 SAA 理事会批准。官网免费 PDF，2026-10-06 读取。位置写节名（Introduction、Core Ideas、Learning Objectives 编号、Appendix 1 词汇表条目）。文件主要写给教人使用一手资料的图书馆员、档案工作者和教师，也说学生和研究者可以用来自学。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："They require critical analysis due to their creators' intents and biases; the variety of contexts in which they have been created, preserved, and made accessible; and the gaps, absences, and silences that may exist in the materials."
- 要点：一手资料需要批判性分析，原因有三：创作者的意图和偏见；资料被创作、保存、开放利用的各种背景；资料中可能存在的缺口、缺失和沉默。

### E2

- 位置：Appendix 1，Discipline 条
- 原文："Disciplines often have specific views on the types of sources that should be used in research and what constitutes a primary source."
- 要点：不同学科对该用哪类资料、什么算一手资料，常有各自的看法。

### E3

- 位置：Core Ideas，Analytical Concepts
- 原文："Interpretation of sources occurs on a continuum from the creation of the source to its utilization by the current user, and includes mediation by librarians, archivists, and database creators or designers."
- 要点：对资料的解释，从资料产生一直延续到今天的使用者，中间还经过图书馆员、档案工作者和数据库设计者的"中介"。

### E4

- 位置：Core Ideas，Theoretical Concepts
- 原文："Users must seek to understand resulting silences and absences by critically considering what sources were never created, what sources may no longer exist, and what sources are collected, as well as communities' abilities to engage in these activities."
- 要点：要理解档案中的沉默和缺失，就要想：哪些资料从未被创造，哪些已不存在，哪些被收藏了，以及不同群体有没有能力去做这些事。

### E5

- 位置：Core Ideas，Theoretical Concepts
- 原文："Collections in cultural heritage institutions reflect and reinforce societal power structures."
- 要点：文化遗产机构的收藏反映并强化社会的权力结构。

### E6

- 位置：Core Ideas，Theoretical Concepts
- 原文："Collections and databases are always mediated in some way, and exhibits, digital collections, and guides or other access tools reflect the selection, reproduction, and presentation decisions of many individuals"
- 要点：收藏和数据库总是经过某种中介；展览、数字化收藏、指南等检索工具，反映了许多人在挑选、复制和呈现上的决定。

### E7

- 位置：Core Ideas，Practical Considerations
- 原文："They will need strategies for capturing and managing research data, including transcription, photography, and downloads."
- 要点：使用者需要有办法采集和管理研究资料，包括转录、拍照和下载。

### E8

- 位置：Learning Objectives 1.C、1.D
- 原文："Draw on primary sources to generate and refine research questions." / "Understand that research is an iterative process and that as primary sources are found and"
- 要点：可以借一手资料提出和改进研究问题；研究是反复往返的过程，找到和分析资料后，问题可能会变。

### E9

- 位置：Learning Objectives 2.D
- 原文："Understand that historical records may never have existed, may not have survived, or may not be collected and/or publicly accessible. Existing records may have been shaped by the selectivity and mediation of individuals such as collectors, archivists, librarians, donors, and/or publishers, potentially limiting the sources available for research."
- 要点：历史记录可能从未存在、没有留存、没被收藏或不公开；现存记录也可能被收藏者、档案工作者、捐赠者、出版者等的挑选所塑造。

### E10

- 位置：Learning Objectives 2.C
- 原文："Distinguish between catalogs, databases, and other online resources that contain information about sources, versus those that contain digital versions, originals, or copies of the sources themselves."
- 要点：分清两类资源：只提供资料信息的目录和数据库，和提供资料本身（数字版、原件或复制件）的资源。

### E11

- 位置：Learning Objectives 3.B
- 原文："Identify and communicate information found in primary sources, including summarizing the content of the source and identifying and reporting key components such as how it was created, by whom, when, and what it is."
- 要点：读一手资料时，要能概括内容，并说清它是怎样产生的、谁产生的、何时产生、是什么。

### E12

- 位置：Learning Objectives 3.C
- 原文："Understand that a primary source may exist in a variety of iterations, including excerpts, transcriptions, and translations, due to publication, copying, and other transformations."
- 要点：同一份一手资料可能有多个版本：节选、转录、译本等。

### E13

- 位置：Learning Objectives 4.B
- 原文："Critically evaluate the perspective of the creator(s) of a primary source, including tone, subjectivity, and biases, and consider how these relate to the original purpose(s) and audience(s) of the source."
- 要点：评估创作者的视角（语气、主观性、偏见），并想想它们和资料原本的目的、读者有什么关系。

### E14

- 位置：Learning Objectives 4.D
- 原文："As part of the analysis of available resources, identify, interrogate, and consider the reasons for silences, gaps, contradictions, or evidence of power relationships in the documentary record"
- 要点：分析资料时，要找出并追问文献记录中的沉默、缺口、矛盾和权力关系的痕迹，想想原因。

### E15

- 位置：Learning Objectives 4.E
- 原文："Factor physical and material elements into the interpretation of primary sources including the"
- 要点：解释一手资料时，要把它的物理和材料特征也考虑进去。

### E16

- 位置：Learning Objectives 5.A
- 原文："Examine and synthesize a variety of sources in order to construct, support, or dispute a research argument."
- 要点：考察并综合多种资料，用来建立、支撑或反驳一个论点。

### E17

- 位置：Learning Objectives 5.C
- 原文："Cite primary sources in accordance with appropriate citation style guidelines or according to repository practice and preferences (when possible)."
- 要点：按合适的引用格式引用一手资料，可能时也遵从收藏机构的惯例和偏好。

### E18

- 位置：Learning Objectives 5.B、5.D
- 原文："Use primary sources in a manner that respects privacy rights and cultural contexts." / "Adhere to copyright and privacy laws when incorporating primary source information in a research or creative project."
- 要点：使用一手资料要尊重隐私和文化背景，遵守版权法和隐私法。

### E19

- 位置：Core Ideas，Ethical Concepts
- 原文："They must understand how these concepts affect their ability to use primary sources in their work, and they should responsibly consider how their scholarship may potentially affect the creators, donors, owners, and readers of these primary sources."
- 要点：要明白法律、隐私、捐赠协议、版权等怎样影响自己使用资料，并负责任地考虑自己的研究可能对资料的创作者、捐赠者、所有者和读者产生什么影响。

### E20

- 位置：Appendix 1，Authenticity 条
- 原文："Authenticity alone does not automatically imply that the content of a record is reliable."
- 要点：一份记录是真的，不等于它的内容可靠。

### E21

- 位置：Appendix 1，Silences 条
- 原文："Gaps or missing pieces in the historical record, often caused by those who were unable to write their own records, or whose records were not considered valuable or were suppressed by the dominant culture. Should be distinguished from merely lack of holdings in a particular repository."
- 要点："沉默"指历史记录里的缺口，常因有些人无法留下记录，或他们的记录不被看重、被压制；它要和"某个馆恰好没收藏"区分开。

### E22

- 位置：Appendix 1，Research Question 条
- 原文："A good research question is answerable, arguable, and is not too broad or too narrow." / "The research question determines what information is needed, and may help the researcher define which sources are considered "primary" for a given project."
- 要点：好的研究问题可回答、可论证、不太宽也不太窄；研究问题决定需要什么信息，也帮助确定哪些资料算一手资料。

### E23

- 位置：Appendix 1，Privacy 条
- 原文："Professionals may restrict or redact records because of applicable laws, institutional risk management decisions, donor agreements, or professional ethics."
- 要点：档案和图书馆专业人员可能因为法律、机构风险管理、捐赠协议或职业伦理，限制查阅或遮盖记录的部分内容。

### E24

- 位置：Appendix 1，Copyright 条
- 原文："Copyright law varies by country."
- 要点：各国版权法不同。

### E25

- 位置：Appendix 1，Evidence 条
- 原文："The strength of supporting evidence and the approach by which it is gathered and applied to a claim impact the credibility of the claim"
- 要点：证据有多强、怎样收集、怎样用来支撑主张，都影响主张的可信度。

### E26

- 位置：Appendix 1，Public Domain 条
- 原文："Privacy and other ethical considerations should still be weighed when using materials in the public domain."
- 要点：即使材料已进入公有领域（不受版权限制），使用时仍要权衡隐私和其他伦理问题。

### E27

- 位置：Appendix 1，Mediation 条
- 原文："Librarians and archivists also provide mediation for documents through finding aids and catalog records, as well as their organization and arrangement of materials."
- 要点：图书馆员和档案工作者通过检索指南、目录记录以及对材料的组织排列，也在资料和使用者之间起着"中介"作用。
