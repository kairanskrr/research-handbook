# UKRIO 2023：Procedure for the Investigation of Misconduct in Research（第 2 版）

- 资料：`ukrio2023procedure`，[原文](https://ukrio.org/wp-content/uploads/Procedure-for-the-Investigation-of-Misconduct-in-Research_Accessible.pdf)
- 版本：英国科研诚信办公室（UK Research Integrity Office，UKRIO，英国的一家慈善机构）发布，作者 James Parry、Nicola Sainsbury；第 2.0 版，2023-03-10（无障碍版 PDF）。是供研究机构参照或采用的范本程序，不是法律；各机构实际用的程序以本机构规定为准。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction，第 1 页
- 原文："The Procedure for the Investigation of Misconduct in Research is at the heart of, and crucial to, the aims of our charity, the UK Research Integrity Office (UKRIO)." / "The Procedure described here is designed as a model for research organisations to follow for the investigation of allegations of misconduct in research." / "organisations can use the Procedure as a benchmark when creating or revising institutional processes to investigate allegations of research misconduct or adopt it in full or in part."
- 要点：英国科研诚信办公室是一家慈善机构；这份程序是给研究机构调查不端举报用的范本，机构可以拿它作参照来制定或修订自己的程序，也可以全部或部分采用。

### E2

- 位置：Purpose, Scope and Standards，第 9 段（第 12 页）
- 原文："A key role in the Procedure is that of the Named Person. This is the individual nominated by the Organisation (see paragraphs 236-237) to have responsibility for receiving any allegations of misconduct in research"
- 要点：程序里的关键角色是机构指定的"负责人"（Named Person），负责接收所有科研不端举报。

### E3

- 位置：Receipt of Allegations stage，第 38 段（第 23 页）
- 原文："A person making an allegation or complaint will not be penalised, provided that it is done without malice and in good faith, reasonably believing it to be true."
- 要点：只要举报不带恶意、出于善意、合理地相信属实，举报人不会因此受罚。

### E4

- 位置：Receipt of Allegations stage，第 39 段（第 23 页）
- 原文："Anyone may raise a concern relating to research misconduct; it is not limited to members of the organisation. The complainant may, in the first instance and where appropriate, attempt to address the issue with either the individual concerned or an appropriate senior colleague rather than raising a concern via this Procedure; they may also wish to seek advice from the confidential liaison point within the institution."
- 要点：任何人都可以提出对科研不端的担忧，不限于本机构的人；合适时可以先找当事人或合适的资深同事解决，也可以先向机构内的保密联络人咨询。

### E5

- 位置：Receipt of Allegations stage，第 40 段（第 23 页）
- 原文："it is recognised that members of staff or students may fear that their own position could be jeopardised if they raise a particular concern directly." / "Depending on what is stated in an Organisation's Whistleblowing Procedure, a member of staff or a student may choose to raise a concern in the first instance with the confidential liaison point within their institution or other appropriate points referenced in the Whistleblowing Procedure and ask that person to bring the matter forward on their behalf."
- 要点：担心直接举报会影响自己的员工或学生，可以视本机构举报人保护规定的说法，先向保密联络人等提出，请对方代为转达。
