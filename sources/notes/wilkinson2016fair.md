# Wilkinson 等人 2016：The FAIR Guiding Principles for scientific data management and stewardship

- 资料：`wilkinson2016fair`，[原文](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618)
- 版本：Scientific Data 3, 160018（2016），开放获取（CC BY 4.0）。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Distinct from peer initiatives that focus on the human scholar, the FAIR Principles put specific emphasis on enhancing the ability of machines to automatically find and use the data, in addition to supporting its reuse by individuals."
- 要点：FAIR 原则除了方便人重用数据，还特别强调让计算机能自动找到并使用数据。

### E2

- 位置：引言
- 原文："This article describes four foundational principles—Findability, Accessibility, Interoperability, and Reusability"
- 要点：FAIR 是四条基本原则的首字母：可查找、可获取、可互操作、可重用。

### E3

- 位置：Box 2: The FAIR Guiding Principles
- 原文："F1. (meta)data are assigned a globally unique and persistent identifier"
- 要点：数据和元数据要有全球唯一、长期有效的标识符。

### E4

- 位置：Box 2: The FAIR Guiding Principles
- 原文："R1.1. (meta)data are released with a clear and accessible data usage license" / "R1.2. (meta)data are associated with detailed provenance"
- 要点：发布数据要附明确的使用许可，并带有详细的来源信息（数据是怎么产生的）。

### E5

- 位置：The FAIR Guiding Principles in detail
- 原文："One such example is highly sensitive or personally-identifiable data, where publication of rich metadata to facilitate discovery, including clear rules regarding the process for accessing the data, provides a high degree of ‘FAIRness’ even in the absence of FAIR publication of the data itself."
- 要点：FAIR 不等于全部公开：敏感数据即使本身不公开，只要公开详细的元数据和获取规则，也能达到较高的 FAIR 程度。

### E6

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："A diverse set of stakeholders—representing academia, industry, funding agencies, and scholarly publishers—have come together to design and jointly endorse a concise and measureable set of principles that we refer to as the FAIR Data Principles."
- 要点：来自学术界、产业界、资助机构和学术出版方的各方代表，共同设计并认可了一套简洁、可衡量的原则，称为 FAIR 数据原则。
