# Vaux 等人 2012：Replicates and repeats—what is the difference and is it significant?

- 资料：`vaux2012replicates`，[原文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3321166/)
- 版本：EMBO Reports 13(4): 291–296, 2012，作者 Vaux、Fidler、Cumming，PubMed Central 开放全文，2026-10-06 读取。位置写段落内容或小标题。

### E1

- 位置：引言
- 原文："As biological experiments can be complicated, replicate measurements are often taken to monitor the performance of the experiment, but such replicates are not independent tests of the hypothesis, and so they cannot provide evidence of the reproducibility of the main results." / "data from replicates cannot be used to draw inferences about the validity of a hypothesis, and therefore should not be used to calculate CIs or P values"
- 要点：生物实验复杂，常做重复测量来监控实验表现；但这种重复不是对假设的独立检验，不能证明主要结果可重复，也不应拿来计算置信区间（CI）或 P 值。

### E2

- 位置：骨髓集落实验的例子
- 原文："We have made just one comparison, so n = 1, no matter how many replicate plates we count. To make an inference that can be generalized to all WT mice and Bdl−/− mice, we need to repeat our experiments a number of times, making several independent comparisons using several mice of each type."
- 要点：细胞都来自一只野生型小鼠和一只基因敲除小鼠时，无论数多少个平板，n 都等于 1；要推广到所有这类小鼠，必须用多只小鼠做多次独立比较。

### E3

- 位置：Sidebar A，Fundamental principle 3
- 原文："A conclusion can only apply to the population from which you took the random sample of independent measurements"
- 要点：结论只能推广到你抽取独立样本的那个总体。

### E4

- 位置：Why bother with replicates at all?
- 原文："Replicates serve as internal quality checks on how the experiment was performed. If, for example, in the experiment described in Table 1 and Fig 1, one of the replicate plates with saline-treated WT bone marrow contained 100 colonies, you would immediately suspect that something was wrong. You could check the plate to see if it had been mislabelled. You might look at the colonies using a microscope and discover that they are actually contaminating colonies of yeast."
- 要点：重复测量的作用是检查实验本身做得好不好。例如某个对照平板意外长出 100 个集落，你会立刻怀疑出了问题，去查是否贴错标签、是否是污染的酵母。

### E5

- 位置：定量逆转录聚合酶链反应（qRT-PCR）的例子
- 原文："The three 10 μl aliquots each came from the same, single, mRNA preparation, so we can only make inferences about the contents of that particular tube."
- 要点：三份样品都取自同一份 mRNA 制备物，只能对那一管的内容下结论。

### E6

- 位置：Sidebar A，Fundamental principle 2
- 原文："Experimental design, at its simplest, is the art of varying one factor at a time while controlling others"
- 要点：实验设计最基本的做法是：一次只改变一个因素，其他因素保持不变。

### E7

- 位置：So, what is wrong?
- 原文："Terms such as ‘required’, ‘essential’ and ‘obligatory’ are not relative, yet are still often incorrectly used when partial effects are seen."
- 要点："必需""不可缺少"这类词没有程度之分，但只看到部分效应时，人们仍常错误地使用它们。

### E8

- 位置：Sidebar A，Fundamental principle 4
- 原文："Values from an outlying replicate can be omitted if a convincing explanation is found, although repeating part or all of the experiment is a safer strategy. Results from an independent sample, however, can only be left out in exceptional circumstances"
- 要点：重复测量中的离群值有令人信服的解释时可以剔除，但重做部分或全部实验更稳妥；独立样本的结果只有在特殊情况下才能剔除。

### E9

- 位置：Why bother with replicates at all?
- 原文："Replicates serve as internal quality checks on how the experiment was performed."
- 要点：重复测量的作用是检查实验本身做得好不好。
