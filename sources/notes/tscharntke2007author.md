# Tscharntke 等人 2007：Author Sequence and Credit for Contributions in Multiauthored Publications

- 资料：`tscharntke2007author`，[原文](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.0050018)
- 版本：PLoS Biology 5(1): e18, 2007，开放获取，2026-10-06 读取。作者来自生态与环境科学领域。正文没有小标题，位置写段落。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："Traditionally, the first author contributes most and also receives most of the credit, whereas the position of subsequent authors is usually decided by contribution, alphabetical order, or reverse seniority."
- 要点：传统上，第一作者贡献最大、得到的认可也最多；其后作者的顺序按贡献、字母顺序或资历倒序排列。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Following informal practices in the biomedical sciences, the last author often gets as much credit as the first author, because he or she is assumed to be the driving force, both intellectually and financially, behind the research."
- 要点：按生物医学领域的非正式惯例，最后一位作者常和第一作者得到同样多的认可，因为人们认为他/她在思路和经费上都是研究的推动者。

### E3

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Evaluation committees and funding bodies often take last authorship as a sign of successful group leadership and make this a criterion in hiring, granting, and promotion. This practice is unofficial, and hence not always followed"
- 要点：评审委员会和资助机构常把最后作者看作成功领导课题组的标志，并用于招聘、资助和晋升；但这是非正式惯例，并不总被遵守。

### E4

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："authors often adopt different methods of crediting contributions for the following authors, because of very different traditions across countries and research fields"
- 要点：不同国家、不同领域传统差别很大，第一作者之后的署名顺序怎样体现贡献，做法各不相同。

### E5

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："We suggest that the approach taken should be stated in the acknowledgements section"
- 要点：作者建议在致谢里说明本文采用了哪种署名排序方式。
