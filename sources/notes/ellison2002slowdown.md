# Ellison 2002：The Slowdown of the Economics Publishing Process

- 资料：`ellison2002slowdown`，[NBER 工作论文版 PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w7804/w7804.pdf)；正式版发表于 Journal of Political Economy 110(5)
- 版本：NBER Working Paper 7804（2000 年 7 月）。数据截至 1999 年。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 1 段
- 原文："Thirty or forty years ago papers in the top economics journals were typically accepted within six to nine months of their submission. Today it is much more common for journals to ask that papers be extensively revised, and on average the cycle of reviews and revisions consumes about two years."
- 要点：三四十年前，顶级经济学期刊的论文通常投稿后六到九个月就被接收；到作者写作时（约 2000 年），期刊更常要求大幅修改，审稿和修改平均要花大约两年。

### E2

- 位置：§2，Figure 1 前后
- 原文："At Econometrica and the Review of Economic Studies we see review times lengthening from 6-12 months in the early seventies to 24-30 months in the late nineties."
- 要点：Econometrica 和 Review of Economic Studies 的审稿周期，从 1970 年代初的 6–12 个月延长到 1990 年代末的 24–30 个月。
