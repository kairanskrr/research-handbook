# 英国教育研究协会 2024：Ethical Guidelines for Educational Research (5th ed.)

- 资料：`bera2024ethical`，[原文 PDF](https://www.bera.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Ethical-Guidelines-for-Educational-Research-5th-edition.pdf)
- 版本：英国教育研究协会（British Educational Research Association，BERA）《教育研究伦理指南》第 5 版，2024 年 4 月，CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 许可，可免费下载。位置写指南的段落编号（第 1–83 段）。

### E1

- 位置：Fundamentals，第 7 页
- 原文："The intended audience for these guidelines is anyone undertaking educational research – whether their job description includes research, or they conduct research within the field (for example, while studying for a qualification or with the intention of improving practice). This includes both independent researchers and practitioners based in educational institutions of any kind."
- 要点：指南面向所有做教育研究的人，包括为了拿学位或改进自己教学而做研究的人，以及在各类教育机构工作的实践者。

### E2

- 位置：第 11 段（Consent）
- 原文："Researchers should think about whether they should approach gatekeepers before directly approaching participants, and whether they should adopt an institution's own ethical approval and safeguarding procedures; this is usually a requirement."
- 要点：直接接触参与者之前，先考虑是否要先联系"守门人"（例如学校负责人）；通常还要求遵守该机构自己的伦理审批和儿童保护程序。

### E3

- 位置：第 18 段
- 原文："Institutional leaders may agree to take part, acting as gatekeepers on behalf of members (such as teachers and students in schools). In order to ensure that all participants are as fully informed as possible about the benefits and potential costs of the study, researchers should offer both information and support."
- 要点：大规模研究中，机构负责人可能代表教师和学生同意参加；研究者仍要向每个参与者提供信息和支持，让他们尽量了解研究的好处和代价。

### E4

- 位置：第 23 段
- 原文："Principles of consent apply to children and young people as well as to adults. This is termed assent for those under the age of legal consent [...] in the setting/culture."
- 要点：同意的原则同样适用于儿童和青少年；对未达法定同意年龄的人，称为"赞同"（assent）。

### E5

- 位置：第 23 段
- 原文："Information sheets and consent forms should be appropriately designed for participants who may differ in such factors as age, reading ability and attention span."
- 要点：信息说明和同意书要按参与者的年龄、阅读能力、注意力时长来设计。

### E6

- 位置：第 24 段
- 原文："This may involve gaining the consent of those responsible for children, such as parents, guardians or others in loco parentis."
- 要点：可能还需要取得对儿童负有责任的人（父母、监护人等）的同意。

### E7

- 位置：第 26 段
- 原文："Generally, opt-in procedures for gaining consent are preferred. However, opt-out procedures are acceptable in some circumstances. Researchers have a responsibility to consult local legislation and consider local practice if opt-out procedures are envisaged."
- 要点：一般优先用"主动同意"（opt-in）；某些情况下可以用"默认参加、可以退出"（opt-out），但要查当地法律和惯例。

### E8

- 位置：第 16 段
- 原文："In circumstances in which some members of a group (such as students in a class or their parents/guardians) have not given consent to participate, for example in class observation, researchers should decide whether this was an active refusal of consent, in which case they would need to respect this and find a practical solution."
- 要点：例如课堂观察时班上有学生或家长没有同意，研究者要判断这是不是主动拒绝；如果是，必须尊重，并想出可行的办法。

### E9

- 位置：第 17 段
- 原文："If the research aims to understand the roles of individuals within the group, then these non-consenting members must not be included in the research. However, it may still be feasible and ethically permissible to record consenting individuals' interactions with anonymised non-consenting individuals."
- 要点：研究要了解群体中个人的角色时，不同意的成员不能被纳入研究；但记录同意者与匿名化的不同意者之间的互动，仍可能可行。

### E10

- 位置：第 19 段
- 原文："An important consideration is the extent to which a researcher's reflective research into their own practice impinges upon others – for example, in the case of power relationships arising from the dual roles of teacher/ lecturer/manager and researcher, and their impact on students and colleagues."
- 要点：研究自己的教学实践时，要考虑"教师（或管理者）兼研究者"的双重身份带来的权力关系，以及对学生和同事的影响。

### E11

- 位置：第 19 段
- 原文："These may be addressed appropriately by, for example, making the researcher role very explicit, involving an independent third party in the research process and seeking agreement for politically"
- 要点：处理双重身份的办法举例：把研究者身份讲得非常明确、让独立的第三方参与研究过程。

### E12

- 位置：第 20 段
- 原文："This can occur, for example, when the researcher and potential participant are family members, or if the researcher is the participant's teacher/lecturer. Researchers need to consider carefully how to deal with such situations and, if possible, should reassure potential participants that non-participation is entirely acceptable."
- 要点：研究者是参与者的老师时，参与者可能无法自愿地给出同意；研究者要仔细处理，并尽量让对方放心：不参加完全没问题。

### E13

- 位置：第 35 段
- 原文："For example, in an experimental design (including a randomised controlled study), the intervention made available to one group, while being unavailable to the control or comparison group, may be viewed as desirable. In mitigation, for example, an intervention found to be effective can typically be offered to control groups after the end of a trial."
- 要点：实验中只有一组得到干预，可能被看作不公平；缓解办法之一是试验结束后把被证明有效的干预提供给对照组。

### E14

- 位置：第 48 段
- 原文："In some cases, such as revelations of abuse or proposed acts of terrorism, researchers may be under statutory duty to disclose confidential information to relevant authorities."
- 要点：例如得知虐待等情况时，研究者可能负有法定义务向有关部门披露保密信息。

### E15

- 位置：第 48 段
- 原文："There are circumstances in which confidentiality may need to be broken, and information sheets and consent forms should state this."
- 要点：有些情况下必须打破保密承诺，这一点要事先写进信息说明和同意书。

### E16

- 位置：第 69 段
- 原文："All results of interventions and evaluations, including those that are negative, should be reported. Evaluations should preferably be registered beforehand with an official body that maintains a platform for this purpose (indeed, many sponsors require this)."
- 要点：所有干预和评估的结果，包括负面结果，都应报告；评估最好事先在官方登记平台登记，很多资助方也这样要求。

### E17

- 位置：第 67 段
- 原文："Ideally, researchers will make shareable anonymised versions of data available for secondary analysis."
- 要点：理想情况下，研究者应提供可共享的匿名化数据，供他人做二次分析。

### E18

- 位置：第 76 段（Authorship）
- 原文："Where research has involved collaboration across different roles or professions – for example, between education researchers who are academics and those who are teachers or other practitioners – then anyone who has made a substantive contribution should be credited as a co-author."
- 要点：学者和中小学教师等实践者合作的研究，凡有实质贡献的人都应列为合著者。

### E19

- 位置：第 77 段
- 原文："Academic status or any other indicator of seniority does not determine first authorship. Rather, the order of authorship should reflect relative leadership and contributions made. Alternatively, co-authors may agree to a simple alphabetic listing of their names."
- 要点：学术地位或资历不决定谁是第一作者；署名顺序应反映主导作用和贡献大小，也可以约定按字母顺序。

### E20

- 位置：Aspirations of educational researchers，第 9 页
- 原文："Concepts such as 'data', 'reliability', 'validity', 'credibility', 'trustworthiness', 'subjectivity' and 'objectivity' may therefore be understood and legitimately applied in different ways."
- 要点：教育研究者群体跨学科、方法多样，"数据""信度""效度""可信度"等概念可以有不同的、都正当的理解和用法。

### E21

- 位置：第 56 段（Methods）
- 原文："These guidelines should not be interpreted as privileging particular research methods over others: the Association respects the diverse range of possible methods."
- 要点：指南不偏向任何一种研究方法，尊重方法的多样性。

### E22

- 位置：About BERA，第 4 页
- 原文："We run a major international conference each year alongside a diverse and engaging series of events, and publish high quality research in our peer-reviewed journals, reports, book series and the groundbreaking BERA Blog."
- 要点：BERA 每年举办大型国际会议，出版同行评审期刊、报告、丛书和博客。

### E23

- 位置：第 37 段
- 原文："Researchers should consider the impact of their research on the lives and workloads of participants, particularly when researching vulnerable or over-researched populations."
- 要点：研究者要考虑研究给参与者的生活和工作量带来的负担，特别是弱势人群或被研究得太多的人群。

### E24

- 位置：第 52 段
- 原文："For example, a group of teachers engaging in a process of action research as part of curriculum renewal should inform the school management of their intentions."
- 要点：例如几位教师在课程改革中做行动研究，应告知学校管理层。

### E25

- 位置：Fundamentals，第 8 页
- 原文："It is recognised that educational researchers whose work is conducted under the auspices of an educational institution will be required to seek ethical review and clearance from that institution."
- 要点：在教育机构名下做研究的人，需要经过该机构的伦理审查并获得许可。
