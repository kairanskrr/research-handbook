# 美国宗教学会 2019：AAR Religious Literacy Guidelines: What U.S. College Graduates Need to Understand about Religion

- 资料：`aar2019literacy`，[原文](https://aarweb.org/resource/aar-religious-literacy-guidelines/)
- 版本：美国宗教学会（American Academy of Religion，AAR）2019 年发布的指南，讲美国两年制和四年制大学毕业生应具备的宗教素养。它是教学指南，不是研究方法规范。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Prologue
- 原文："The AAR is the world’s largest association of scholars who research or teach topics related to religion. There are over 8,000 members comprised largely of faculty at colleges, universities, and theological schools in North America"
- 要点：AAR 自称是研究或讲授宗教相关主题的学者的最大协会，有 8,000 多名会员，多为北美高校和神学院的教师。

### E2

- 位置：Approaches to Religion Found in Two- and Four-Year Colleges and Universities / Religious Studies
- 原文："There are many different methods that can fall under the category of the academic study of religion (e.g., literary-critical, anthropological, sociological, historical, etc.). There are also sub-disciplines whose approach to the study of religion is congruent with religious studies. These include the sociology of religion, anthropology of religion, and psychology of religion, among others."
- 要点：宗教的学术研究包括多种方法（文学批评、人类学、社会学、历史学等），宗教社会学、宗教人类学、宗教心理学等分支也与宗教学的路径一致。

### E3

- 位置：Religious Studies
- 原文："There is distinction between the confessional or prescriptive expression of a religious belief or practice and the study of diverse religious expressions"
- 要点：宗教学的共同主张之一：表达某种宗教信仰（宣信的、规范性的）和研究各种宗教表达，是两回事。

### E4

- 位置：Religious Studies
- 原文："Religions are internally diverse"
- 要点：宗教内部是多样的（宗教学的共同主张之一，另有"宗教总在演变""宗教嵌在文化和历史之中"）。

### E5

- 位置：Other Approaches / Faith-based
- 原文："Faith-based teaching about religion promotes an explicit belief and/or practice of religion."
- 要点：基于信仰的宗教教学推广某种明确的信仰或实践。

### E6

- 位置：Other Approaches / Faith-based
- 原文："The faith-based approach may or may not incorporate critical analytical tools, such as cultural and historical context, to deepen a tradition’s self-understanding by reflecting on its internal diversity."
- 要点：基于信仰的路径可能用、也可能不用批判分析工具（如文化和历史背景）来加深一个传统的自我理解。

### E7

- 位置：Executive Summary 能力列表
- 原文："Distinguish confessional or prescriptive statements made by religions from descriptive or analytical statements"
- 要点：宗教素养的一项能力：把宗教作出的宣信性、规范性陈述，与描述性、分析性陈述区分开。

### E8

- 位置：Other Approaches / Experiential
- 原文："This approach can be compatible with a religious studies approach, especially when framed as ethnographic research."
- 要点：走访宗教人士和场所的体验式路径，若按民族志研究来组织，可以和宗教学路径兼容。
