# Lyons 2013：Discovering the Significance of 5σ

- 资料：`lyons2013discovering`，[原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.1284)
- 版本：arXiv:1310.1284v1，2013。作者署名单位为帝国理工学院和牛津大学粒子物理系。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："It has become the convention in Particle Physics that in order to claim a discovery of some form of New Physics, the chance of just the background having a statistical fluctuation at least as large as the observed effect is equivalent to the area beyond 5σ in one tail of a normalised Gaussian distribution, or smaller"
- 要点：在粒子物理中，宣布发现新物理的惯例是：仅由本底涨落产生同样大效应的概率，不超过标准正态分布单侧 5σ 以外的面积。

### E2

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："Indeed, journals are very reluctant to allow the word ‘discovery’ to appear in the title, abstract or conclusions of a paper, unless the p-value is that small."
- 要点：p 值没小到这个程度，期刊很不愿意让"发现"一词出现在标题、摘要或结论里。

### E3

- 位置：§2，Look Elsewhere Effect
- 原文："If the background fluctuation is required to be at the observed mass, this probability is called the local p-value. If, however, the restriction on the location of the background fluctuation is removed, this is instead the global p-value, and is larger than the local one because of the LEE."
- 要点：只算"在观察到的位置出现涨落"的概率，叫局部 p 值；允许涨落出现在任何位置，叫全局 p 值，它更大。这就是"别处效应"（Look Elsewhere Effect，LEE）。

### E4

- 位置：§2，Look Elsewhere Effect
- 原文："It is clearly important to decide in advance of the analysis what procedure will be used; and to specify in any publication exactly how the global p-values are defined."
- 要点：要在分析之前定好用什么方法，并在论文里写清全局 p 值是怎样定义的。

### E5

- 位置：§2 Why 5σ?
- 原文："Statisticians who hear about the 5σ criterion are very skeptical about it being sensible."
- 要点：统计学家听说 5σ 标准，很怀疑它是否合理。

### E6

- 位置：§4 Conclusions
- 原文："It would be very useful if we could distance ourselves from the attitude of `Require 5σ for all discovery claims'."
- 要点：作者主张不要对所有发现都一刀切地要求 5σ。

### E7

- 位置：§3 Why not 5σ?
- 原文："There are several reasons why it is not sensible to use a uniform criterion of 5σ for all searches for new physics."
- 要点：对所有新物理搜索都用统一的 5σ 标准并不合理，因为别处效应、先验可信度、系统误差的影响因实验而异。
