# Nanda 2025：My Research Process: Understanding and Cultivating Research Taste

- 资料：`nanda2025taste`，[原文](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/Ldrss6o3tiKT6NdMm)
- 版本：AI Alignment Forum，2025-05-01，系列文章第 3 篇。作者的领域是机器学习可解释性，以下只摘跨学科成立的部分。原文中加粗的词在去掉 HTML 标签后与后面的标点之间多出一个空格（如 "a mentor ,"），引文照提取文本原样摘录。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："While I believe research taste is very real, incredibly valuable, and a key differentiator I look for, I don't think it's mystical or innate. Talent plays an important role, but taste is largely learned, and with the right mindset you can learn faster."
- 要点：研究品味真实且重要，但不神秘、不是天生的，主要靠后天学习。

### E2

- 位置：What is research taste?
- 原文："Research is full of key decisions that will affect the future of the project, without an obvious way to find the right answer: from choosing the research problem itself, to identifying which anomalies are and are not worth exploring, distinguishing an experiment that will be compelling from one that’ll have inconclusive results, etc."
- 要点：作者把品味定义得更宽：不只选题，还包括研究全过程中没有标准答案的关键决策，比如哪些异常值得追、哪个实验更有说服力。

### E3

- 位置：Where does taste come from?
- 原文："If you're new to research, feeling like you lack "taste" is completely normal and expected. You don't need perfect judgment to start."
- 要点：刚入门觉得自己没品味完全正常，不需要完美的判断力才能开始。

### E4

- 位置：How to learn it?
- 原文："The core problem is you just don't get that much data [...] Generally the shorter a feedback loop is the more data you will get."
- 要点：品味难学的核心是反馈太少；反馈回路越短，得到的"数据"越多。

### E5

- 位置：How to learn it?
- 原文："To get more data the easiest way is to lean on sources of supervised data: ideally a mentor , or seeing what worked in papers . You can also get more from each data point - analyse it in detail before setting the feedback, predict your mentor’s answers before they give them , etc."
- 要点：加速方法：借助导师和论文；每次反馈前先预测导师会怎么说。

### E6

- 位置：Decomposing Research Taste › Conviction & Confidence
- 原文："Confidence doesn't mean correctness . Generally people reach the level of having conviction far before they reach the level of having correct intuitions"
- 要点：自信不等于正确；人们往往先有了信念，很久之后才有准确的直觉。

### E7

- 位置：同上
- 原文："Try to write down prior predictions , and why you think an idea is good, pursue it, and reflect on what happened ."
- 要点：事先写下预测和"为什么觉得这个想法好"，做完后回顾结果。

### E8

- 位置：Cultivating Research Taste › Getting more data
- 原文："Especially early on, projects where you can test intermediate hypotheses or get partial results relatively quickly can accelerate learning more than moonshots with year-long feedback loops."
- 要点：早期优先做能较快得到中间结果的项目，比反馈周期长达一年的"登月"项目学得更快。

### E9

- 位置：Conclusion: Patience and Process
- 原文："It's perfectly normal, and indeed expected, to rely heavily on external guidance (like mentors or established research directions) early in your career."
- 要点：职业早期高度依赖导师或成熟研究方向的指导，是正常且预期之中的。
