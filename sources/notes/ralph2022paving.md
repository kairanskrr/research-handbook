# Ralph & Baltes 2022：Paving the Way for Mature Secondary Research: The Seven Types of Literature Review

- 资料：`ralph2022paving`，[arXiv:2205.01163](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.01163)
- 版本：arXiv v2（ESEC/FSE 2022）。

### E1

- 位置：§2.1 Ad Hoc Reviews
- 原文："An ad hoc literature review is simply a discussion of some literature, as contained in most research papers as part of a background or related work section. [...] Ad hoc reviews use purposive sampling; that is, researchers purposefully select papers or studies that are useful, relevant, or support their arguments."
- 要点：非系统综述就是对一些文献的讨论，大多数论文的"相关工作"都属于这一类。论文是作者有目的地挑的：有用、相关，或支持自己的观点。

### E2

- 位置：§2.1 Ad Hoc Reviews
- 原文："Ad hoc reviews are often appropriate, for example, to support theory development, identify promising research topics, or prepare for a comprehensive exam. However, they suffer from important limitations: their unsystematic nature introduces sampling bias and defies replication. This may lead to cherry-picking of evidence supporting authors' arguments. Therefore, ad hoc reviews are inappropriate for supporting empirical statements such as 'x causes y' [...]"
- 要点：非系统综述适合发展理论、寻找有前景的选题、备考等；但有抽样偏差、无法复现，容易只挑支持自己的证据，所以不适合用来支撑"X 导致 Y"这类实证结论。

### E3

- 位置：§2.2.5 Scoping Reviews
- 原文："What is often called a 'systematic mapping study' in SE is usually called a scoping review elsewhere [...]. Scoping reviews are often primarily descriptive. They count the number of studies on a topic."
- 要点：软件工程里的"映射研究"，在医学等领域叫"范围综述"（scoping review），以描述和计数为主。

### E4

- 位置：§2.2.5 Scoping Reviews
- 原文："Scoping reviews include a similar search but typically do not provide sufficient quantitative synthesis to answer important empirical questions. Therefore, scoping reviews do not inform evidence-based practice like meta-analyses and case surveys do."
- 要点：作者认为范围综述（映射研究）通常综合不够，回答不了重要的实证问题。

### E5

- 位置：§3.1 Recommendations
- 原文："Software engineering needs more meta-analysis, case surveys, and meta-synthesis, but fewer scoping and rapid reviews. [...] predominately descriptive scoping reviews are essentially works-in-progress. They belong in workshops, poster sessions and short paper tracks. [...] If the literature does support some kind of synthesis, the scoping review is like a pilot study for a more ambitious systematic review."
- 要点：作者建议多做元分析、案例调查、元综合，少做范围综述和快速综述。以描述为主的范围综述"本质上是半成品"，更适合 workshop、海报或短文；如果文献足够做综合，范围综述就像是更完整的系统综述的预研。

### E6

- 位置：§2.2.3 Case Survey (aka Case Meta-analysis)
- 原文："A case survey’s primary studies are (typically qualitative) case studies in the broadest sense (i.e., a scholarly account of some events). Experience reports and grey literature may or may not be included, depending on the study’s purposes."
- 要点：案例调查的原始研究是最广义的案例研究，即对某些事件的学术性记述；经验报告和灰色文献（未经正式出版渠道发表的材料）是否纳入，取决于研究目的。

### E7

- 位置：§2.2.3
- 原文："Unlike meta-synthesis, however, a case survey transforms qualitative accounts into a quantitative dataset that supports null-hypothesis testing. Case surveys share the philosophy of meta-analysis (positivism), not meta-synthesis (constructivism). They typically begin with a priori hypotheses and an a priori coding scheme."
- 要点：与元综合不同，案例调查把定性记述转换成可以做零假设检验的定量数据集；它的理念接近元分析（实证主义）；通常从事先定好的假设和编码方案出发。

### E8

- 位置：§2.2.3
- 原文："The researcher reads each case and extracts data into the coding scheme, often using simple dichotomous variables like ‘did the team have retrospective meetings? [yes/no]’" / "The resulting dataset is often too sparse for regression modeling, so researchers use simple bivariate correlations to test hypotheses"
- 要点：研究者逐个阅读案例，把信息填进编码方案，常用简单的是/否变量（如"团队开没开回顾会议"）；得到的数据集往往太稀疏，做不了回归建模，所以常用简单的两变量相关来检验假设。

### E9

- 位置：§2.2.3
- 原文："Now case meta-analysis is widely used in management and information systems" / "SE case surveys remain rare." / "Case surveys have great potential in SE because case studies are so common."
- 要点：案例调查在管理学和信息系统领域用得很广；在软件工程里这类研究仍然少见；因为软件工程里案例研究很多，作者认为它很有潜力。

### E10

- 位置：§3 Discussion
- 原文："With their sparse data sets, case surveys need many case studies."
- 要点：因为数据稀疏，案例调查需要大量案例研究。

### E11

- 位置：§3 Discussion 与 §3.1 Recommendations
- 原文："Meta-analysis and case surveys synthesize the results of many studies to answer specific empirical (often causal) questions about the world." / "Software engineering needs more meta-analysis, case surveys, and meta-synthesis, but fewer scoping and rapid reviews."
- 要点：元分析和案例调查综合许多研究的结果，回答关于现实世界的具体实证问题（常常是因果问题）；作者建议软件工程多做元分析、案例调查和元综合。

### E12

- 位置：§2.2.4 Critical Reviews
- 原文："A critical review analyzes a sample of primary studies to support an argument or critique." / "critical reviews in software engineering often investigate methodological topics"
- 要点：批判性综述分析一批原始研究，用来支撑一个论点或一项批评；软件工程里的批判性综述常常讨论方法论问题。

### E13

- 位置：§2.2.4
- 原文："Stol et al. [31]’s critical review of the use of grounded theory in SE criticizes method slurring; that is, claiming to have used a research methodology that was not actually used to create illusory legitimacy."
- 要点：例子：Stol 等人批评软件工程中的"方法冒用"（method slurring），即声称用了某种研究方法、实际上没用，以制造虚假的正当性。

### E14

- 位置：§2.2.4
- 原文："Critical reviews are not for supporting evidence-based practice, or summarizing the evidence for a theory. Critical reviews are part of the meta-scientific discourse; i.e., the conversations a scientific community has internally about how it conducts research."
- 要点：批判性综述不是为了支撑循证实践或总结某个理论的证据，而是元科学讨论的一部分，即一个科学群体内部关于"我们怎么做研究"的对话。

### E15

- 位置：§2.2.4
- 原文："Second, for many critical reviews, including all relevant primary studies is impossible and unnecessary." / "a random sample of papers from a selection of top journals and conferences is sufficient because critical reviews do not assess causal claims"
- 要点：很多批判性综述不可能、也没必要纳入所有相关研究；从若干顶级期刊和会议随机抽样就够了，因为它不评估因果主张，发表偏差无关紧要。

### E16

- 位置：§2.2.4
- 原文："Analysis performed within a critical review can be quantitative, qualitative or both. However, critical reviews typically adopt a critical stance; that is, they go beyond mere description and offer specific critiques of the work being reviewed."
- 要点：批判性综述的分析可以是定量的、定性的或两者兼有，但通常持批判立场：不止于描述，而要对所审视的工作提出具体批评。

### E17

- 位置：§3 Discussion
- 原文："they focus on an empirical topic (e.g. cognitive biases in SE); not a meta-scientific topic (e.g. construct validity); therefore, they are not configured, from the outset, to deliver useful metascientific critique."
- 要点：范围综述（映射研究）关注的是实证主题（如软件工程中的认知偏差），而不是元科学主题（如构念效度），所以从一开始就不是为提供元科学批评设计的。

### E18

- 位置：§3.1 Recommendations
- 原文："Meta-science in SE has been hamstrung by reviewers and venues insisting that every paper reports an empirical study, regardless of whether its research question is empirical."
- 要点：作者认为，审稿人和刊物坚持每篇论文都要报告一项实证研究（不管它的研究问题是不是实证问题），束缚了软件工程的元科学研究。

### E19

- 位置：§3.1 Recommendations
- 原文："First, when elucidating a ubiquitous problem (e.g. poor construct validity) citing specific examples of papers making that mistake is unnecessary. The credibility gained is not worth the costs to community cohesion, interpersonal relationships, and future collaborations. Authors should explain this reasoning and reviewers should accept this compromise."
- 要点：阐明一个普遍存在的问题时，没必要点名引用犯了这个错的具体论文：由此增加的可信度，抵不上对学术群体凝聚力、人际关系和未来合作的损害；作者应说明这样做的理由，审稿人应接受。

### E20

- 位置：§3.1 Recommendations
- 原文："Second, reviewers should not misapply the “find ALL relevant studies” or “exclude low quality studies” criteria to critical reviews."
- 要点：审稿人不应把"找全所有相关研究""排除低质量研究"这类标准错误地套用到批判性综述上。

### E21

- 位置：§3.2 Limitations
- 原文："This is not an empirical paper. It is meta-science: part of our community’s internal discourse about what we are doing, why, and how to do it."
- 要点：作者说明自己这篇论文不是实证论文，而是元科学：研究群体内部关于"我们在做什么、为什么做、怎么做"的讨论。
