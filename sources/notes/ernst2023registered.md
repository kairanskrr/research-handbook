# Ernst & Baldassarre 2023：Registered Reports in Software Engineering

- 资料：`ernst2023registered`，[原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03649)
- 版本：arXiv 预印本 v1（2023 年 2 月）；正式版发表于 Empirical Software Engineering 28(2):55，2023。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："In software engineering research, registered reports were first introduced in the International Conference on Mining Software Repositories (MSR) in 2020. They are now established in three conferences and two pre-eminent journals, including this one (EMSE)."
- 要点：软件工程里，注册报告 2020 年最早出现在挖掘软件仓库国际会议（MSR）；到 2023 年，已有三个会议和两本期刊（含 Empirical Software Engineering）采用。

### E2

- 位置：§2.1
- 原文："Many journals now support the format, with the Empirical Software Engineering Journal (EMSE) supporting them as of 2020." / "As of 2022, the ACM journal Transactions on Software" [...] "Engineering and Methodology (TOSEM) hosts a Registered Paper initiative"
- 要点：Empirical Software Engineering 期刊从 2020 年起支持注册报告；从 2022 年起，ACM 的 TOSEM 期刊有"Registered Paper"项目。

### E3

- 位置：§3
- 原文："The modification from the typical RR approach at the Empirical Software Engineering Journal (EMSE) is that Stage 1 is managed as a conference track. Current options include the International Conference on Mining Software Repositories, the International Conference on Software Maintenance and Evolution, and the Empirical Software Engineering and Measurement Conference."
- 要点：Empirical Software Engineering 期刊的做法是：第一阶段由会议的专门轨道审稿，目前可选 MSR、ICSME（软件维护与演化国际会议）和 ESEM（实证软件工程与度量会议）；第二阶段的完整论文投期刊。
