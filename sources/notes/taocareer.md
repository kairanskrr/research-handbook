# Tao：Career advice（职业建议系列网页）

- 资料：`taocareer`，[原文](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/career-advice/)
- 版本：Terence Tao（陶哲轩）博客 "What's new" 上的 Career advice 系列页面，2026-10-06 读取。每页是一条独立的建议，位置写页面标题。

### E1

- 位置：Be sceptical of your own work
- 原文："In particular, the method may also be able to prove much stronger statements which are known to be false, which would imply that there is a flaw in the method."
- 要点：如果一个方法看似轻松解决了问题，检查它是否也能"证明"已知为假的更强命题；能的话，方法有漏洞。

### E2

- 位置：Be sceptical of your own work
- 原文："if you are trying to prove some ambitious claim, you might try to first look for a counterexample; either you find one, which saves you a lot of time and may well be publishable in its own right, or else you encounter some obstruction, which should give some clue as to what one has to do in order to establish the claim positively"
- 要点：要证一个大胆的命题，先试着找反例：找到了能省下大量时间（反例本身也可能值得发表）；找不到，遇到的障碍会提示证明该怎么做。

### E3

- 位置：Be sceptical of your own work
- 原文："A good way to stress-test this sort of false argument is to try to run the same argument without the initial assumption that X is false."
- 要点：检验反证法证明的一个办法：去掉"假设 X 不成立"这个前提再跑一遍论证；如果仍能推出矛盾，问题出在论证本身。

### E4

- 位置：Be sceptical of your own work
- 原文："A final type of warning sign is if your approach is based on adapting a method that has been used frequently in the past by many experts in the field, but with an additional twist of your own that all the previous experts somehow “missed”."
- 要点：危险信号：你的做法是在专家们常用的方法上加了一个"所有专家都没想到"的小改动；这个改动很可能藏着专家们早已知道并避开的缺陷。

### E5

- 位置：Be sceptical of your own work
- 原文："Isolate a toy model case x of major problem X."
- 要点：作者描述的真正解决大问题的典型过程，第一步是分离出一个玩具模型（简化的特殊情形）。

### E6

- 位置：On the importance of partial progress
- 原文："One of the secrets to mathematical problem solving is that one needs to place a high value on partial progress, as being a crucial stepping stone to fully solving the problem."
- 要点：解数学问题的秘诀之一是重视部分进展，它是完全解决问题的关键垫脚石。

### E7

- 位置：On the importance of partial progress
- 原文："The point is that even if the technique is doomed to fail, the precise point in the argument at which it fails can be very instructive"
- 要点：即使一种技术注定失败，它在论证中具体失败在哪一步也很有启发。

### E8

- 位置：Ask yourself dumb questions – and answer them!
- 原文："For instance, given a standard lemma in a subject, you can ask what happens if you delete a hypothesis, or attempt to strengthen the conclusion"
- 要点：对一个标准引理，可以问：去掉一个假设会怎样？结论能不能加强？

### E9

- 位置：Learn the limitations of your tools
- 原文："Thus, knowing a library of counterexamples, or easily analysed model situations, is very important, as well as knowing the type of obstructions that your tool can deal with, and which ones it has no hope of resolving."
- 要点：要掌握一批反例和容易分析的模型情形，并知道你的工具能处理哪些障碍、哪些完全无望。

### E10

- 位置：Learn the limitations of your tools
- 原文："While unintuitive, one should also discuss the known limitations of a tool when writing a paper that uses that tool."
- 要点：论文里应该讨论所用工具的已知局限。

### E11

- 位置：There’s more to mathematics than rigour and proofs
- 原文："The point of rigour is not to destroy all intuition; instead, it should be used to destroy bad intuition while clarifying and elevating good intuition."
- 要点：严谨不是为了消灭直觉，而是消灭坏直觉、澄清和提升好直觉。

### E12

- 位置：There’s more to mathematics than rigour and proofs
- 原文："It is only with a combination of both rigorous formalism and good intuition that one can tackle complex mathematical problems; one needs the former to correctly deal with the fine details, and the latter to correctly deal with the big picture."
- 要点：严格形式和好直觉缺一不可：前者处理细节，后者把握全局。

### E13

- 位置：Don’t base career decisions on glamour or fame
- 原文："A famous unsolved problem is almost never solved ab nihilo."
- 要点：著名难题几乎从不是凭空解决的，要先在更简单的模型问题上积累技术、直觉、部分结果和文献。

### E14

- 位置：Don’t prematurely obsess on a single “big problem” or “big theory”
- 原文："spend most of your time on more feasible “low-hanging fruit”, which will build up your experience, mathematical power, and credibility for when you are ready to tackle the more ambitious projects."
- 要点：在还没有可靠的发表记录和稳定职位时，大部分时间应花在更可行的问题上，积累经验、能力和信誉，再去碰宏大的问题。

### E15

- 位置：Don’t prematurely obsess on a single “big problem” or “big theory”，附录 "on publishing proofs of famous open problems"
- 原文："Can you state, in high-level terms (i.e. rising above all the technical details and computations), why the argument works?"
- 要点：发布重大结果前自问：能否脱开技术细节，用高层的话说清论证为什么成立？

### E16

- 位置：Learn and relearn your field
- 原文："Another useful way to learn more about one’s field is to take a key paper in that field, and perform a citation search on that paper (i.e. search for other papers that cite the key paper)."
- 要点：了解领域的一个办法：拿一篇关键论文做引文检索，查有哪些论文引用了它。

### E17

- 位置：Learn and relearn your field
- 原文："Do you know why each of the hypotheses are necessary?"
- 要点：对一个基本引理，要知道每个假设为什么必要。

### E18

- 位置：Write down what you’ve done
- 原文："Eventually I resolved to write down (preferably on a computer) a sketch of any interesting argument I came across – not necessarily at a publication level of quality, but detailed enough that I could then safely forget about the details, and readily recover the argument from the sketch whenever the need arises."
- 要点：把遇到的有意思的论证写成草稿，不必达到发表水平，但要详细到日后能据此恢复整个论证。

### E19

- 位置：Use the wastebasket
- 原文："Even an embarrassingly wrong piece of work (and I have a number of these, which fortunately have never made it as far as publication) should be stored privately somewhere, because you never know whether something salvageable can be extracted from it"
- 要点：即使是错得难堪的工作也要私下存档，也许以后能从中挽救出有用的东西。

### E20

- 位置：Solving mathematical problems
- 原文："A good rule of thumb is that if you cannot adequately explain the solution of a problem to a classmate, then you haven’t really understood the solution yourself"
- 要点：如果不能把解法给同学讲清楚，说明你自己还没真正理解。

### E21

- 位置：Be sceptical of your own work
- 原文："Realise that special case y is related to (or at least analogous to) a problem z in another field of mathematics. Look up the literature on z, and ask experts in that field for the latest perspectives on that problem."
- 要点：作者描述的典型过程中，一步是发现特殊情形与另一领域的某个问题相关或相似，然后去查那个问题的文献、请教那个领域的专家。

### E22

- 位置：Be sceptical of your own work
- 原文："with many of the intermediate steps described here being significant publishable papers in their own right"
- 要点：解决大问题的过程中，许多中间步骤本身就是有分量、可发表的论文。
