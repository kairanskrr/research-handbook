# Casler 2015：Fundamentals of Experimental Design: Guidelines for Designing Successful Experiments

- 资料：`casler2015fundamentals`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.2134/agronj2013.0114)
- 版本：Agronomy Journal 107(2): 692–705，2015。出版社网站上免费阅读（Unpaywall 标注为 CC BY-NC-ND 的开放获取文章）。脚本访问出版社网站被拦截，原文文本取自 Internet Archive 2025-07-07 的快照。本文是发表在农学期刊上、写给农学研究者的试验设计综述。位置写小标题或段落内容。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Four basic tenets or pillars of experimental design— replication, randomization, blocking, and size of experimental units— can be used creatively, intelligently, and consciously to solve both real and perceived problems in comparative experiments."
- 要点：试验设计有四根支柱：重复、随机化、区组、实验单位的大小。要有意识地用它们解决比较试验里的问题。

### E2

- 位置：Table 1（术语定义）
- 原文："the smallest unit to which a treatment is applied"
- 要点：实验单位（experimental unit）是施加处理的最小单位。

### E3

- 位置：Table 1（术语定义）
- 原文："the practice of applying each treatment to multiple and mutually independent experimental units"
- 要点：重复（replication）是把每个处理施加到多个彼此独立的实验单位上。

### E4

- 位置：Replication 一节
- 原文："Replicate observations must occur at a spatial and temporal scale that matches the application of the treatments"
- 要点：重复必须发生在与施加处理相同的空间和时间尺度上。

### E5

- 位置：Replication 一节，烧荒试验的例子
- 原文："the researcher chooses to conduct the experiment on two 1-ha plots or experimental units, one burned and one an unburned control. [...] Even though the statistical analysis makes sense and is completely valid, the science is flawed—the experimental design does not allow an unequivocal interpretation of the results."
- 要点：例子：只用两块 1 公顷的地，一块烧、一块不烧，在每块地里取很多土样。统计分析本身没错，但科学上有缺陷：设计不允许对结果做出明确解释。

### E6

- 位置：Replication 一节，烧荒试验的例子
- 原文："Hurlbert (1984) coined the term pseudoreplication for this situation in which experimental treatments are replicated but at a scale that is not sufficient to provide a valid scientific interpretation of the results, i.e., complete confounding of treatments with experimental units"
- 要点：Hurlbert 1984 把这种情况叫做"伪重复"：处理有重复，但重复的尺度不够，处理和实验单位完全混在一起，无法给出有效的科学解释。

### E7

- 位置：Replication 一节，烧荒试验的例子
- 原文："replication in time, e.g., across years, could be conducted at the proper scale but only if the treatments are independently randomized to experimental units in each year, i.e., the experiment is repeated on a new section of prairie in each year."
- 要点：在时间上重复（跨年份）也可以，但前提是每年都把处理重新独立随机分配给实验单位，也就是每年在一块新的草原上重做。

### E8

- 位置：Number of replicates 一节，资源分配
- 原文："It is always clear from these exercises that the number of locations and/or years should be maximized relative to the number of replicates (e.g., McCann et al., 2012). The only situation that favors maximizing the number of replicates is when all the treatment × environment interactions are zero, which is rarely the case."
- 要点：按方差分量做资源分配的计算显示，相对于每个地点内的重复数，应该优先增加地点数和年份数；只有在处理和环境之间完全没有交互作用时，才值得优先增加重复，而这种情况很少见。

### E9

- 位置：Replication 一节
- 原文："This is analogous to the very common practice of repeating experiments across multiple locations and years for the purpose of assessing treatment differences under different environmental circumstances. As long as no attempt is made to attribute simple effects of interactions to unreplicated environmental variables, which cannot be conclusively resolved, the researcher is relatively safe from criticism."
- 要点：在多个地点和年份重复试验、看处理在不同环境下的差异，是很常见的做法。只要不把交互作用归因到没有重复的环境变量上（这无法下定论），就不容易被批评。

### E10

- 位置：Number of replicates 一节
- 原文："Based on my experiences teaching and consulting on this topic, this is the single most important and most often ignored topic in experimental design. [...] Despite this fact, few agronomic researchers have ever conducted a meaningful power analysis with the goal of designing a better experiment."
- 要点：作者认为，很少有农学研究者为了设计更好的试验而认真做过统计功效分析（这是作者根据教学和咨询经验的判断）。

### E11

- 位置：Replication 一节，特殊情况
- 原文："Many on-farm experiments represent a classic example of this situation. Farmers and outreach personnel see value in trying different treatments on a very large scale but see little value in replication of those treatments. Researchers organizing these types of field experiments have three options: (i) conduct the experiments on multiple farms, using farms as blocks; (ii) use control-plot designs in which one control treatment is interspersed with the other treatments, optimally in an every-other-plot pattern; or (iii) use a combination of both approaches."
- 要点：农场试验里，农民和推广人员愿意大面积试不同处理，却不太看重重复。研究者有三种办法：在多个农场做、把农场当区组；用对照小区设计，把对照插在其他处理之间，最好隔一块放一块；或者两者结合。

### E12

- 位置：Replication 一节，参与式研究
- 原文："The most traditional application involves the use of a “mother” trial, conducted with proper replication and randomization on an experimental research station, accompanied by numerous “daughter” trials conducted by various landholders (Fig. 7)."
- 要点：参与式研究中的"母—子试验"设计：在试验站做一个有正规重复和随机化的"母试验"，同时由许多农户各自做"子试验"。

### E13

- 位置：Randomization 一节
- 原文："Randomization helps to ensure that those unknown or unexpected sources of variation do not introduce biases, confounding, or elimination of valid hypothesis tests during the course of the experiment."
- 要点：随机化保证那些未知或意外的变异来源不会带来偏倚、混杂，或者让有效的假设检验做不成。

### E14

- 位置：Blocking 一节
- 原文："Blocking is utilized in experimental designs for one or both of two purposes: (i) for precision, to create groups of experimental units that are more homogeneous than would occur with random sampling of the entire population of experimental units; or (ii) for convenience, to allow different sizes of experimental units when larger plots or larger experimental areas are required for the application of one factor compared with other factors."
- 要点：区组有两个用途：一是提高精度，把更相近的实验单位分成一组；二是方便操作，让某个因素可以用更大的小区。

### E15

- 位置：Blocking 一节
- 原文："I can cite examples of the use of randomized complete block designs (RCBD) being used to successfully recover from post-establishment disturbances to colleagues' experiments, including military maneuvers (three different incidents), a house mover, wayward herbicide sprayers (twice), a motorcycle gang, an angry and vengeful former employee, and partying students."
- 要点：作者举例说，随机完全区组设计帮同事的试验从各种意外破坏中挽救回来：军事演习、搬房子的车、喷错的除草剂、摩托车帮、报复的前员工、开派对的学生。区组像一份保险。

### E16

- 位置：Blocking 一节，裂区设计
- 原文："They are frequently used strictly for convenience, when the whole-plot factor requires larger experimental units than the subplot factor(s). Common examples include tillage treatments, irrigation treatments, and planting dates."
- 要点：裂区设计常常是为了方便：某个因素（主区因素）需要更大的地块。常见例子是耕作、灌溉和播期。

### E17

- 位置：Blocking 一节
- 原文："Because many experimental research stations consist of large and visually uniform fields, bidirectional blocking is an excellent insurance policy against guessing wrong on the blocking arrangement."
- 要点：很多试验站的田块很大、看起来很均匀，两个方向上都设区组，可以防止区组方向猜错。

### E18

- 位置：Blocking 一节
- 原文："when the number of treatments is >20, all researchers should consider the general principle that more blocks and smaller blocks are nearly always more efficient than complete blocks"
- 要点：处理数超过 20 个时，更多、更小的区组几乎总比完全区组更有效。

### E19

- 位置：结论部分，给年轻研究者的建议
- 原文："While field sites may appear uniform, experience tells us that they are probably nonuniform and that patterns of spatial variability cannot be predicted without years of trial and error."
- 要点：田块看起来均匀，其实多半不均匀；空间变异的模式要多年摸索才能预测。

### E20

- 位置：结论部分，给年轻研究者的建议
- 原文："Become reasonably proficient in experimental design and data analysis or become good friends with someone who has this qualification and can benefit from working with you."
- 要点：自己要相当熟悉试验设计和数据分析，或者和懂这些、也能从合作中受益的人交朋友。

### E21

- 位置：结论部分，给年轻研究者的建议
- 原文："Always conduct a thorough power analysis before designing every experiment, even if you have to make guesses about parameter estimates."
- 要点：每次设计试验前都做统计功效分析，即使参数只能靠猜。

### E22

- 位置：结论部分
- 原文："Researchers who have the benefit of long-term research results, accumulated from many years of research at a particular site or on a defined group of subjects, have access to a wealth of data that can be used for planning purposes."
- 要点：在同一地点积累了多年结果的研究者，有大量数据可以用来规划新试验（估计方差、做功效分析）。

### E23

- 位置：结论部分
- 原文："Rarely can these questions be answered when P values are high, resulting in unpublishable results and wasted time and money."
- 要点：P 值高的时候，很难分清是处理真的没差别、设计有问题，还是试验受了干扰，结果常常发表不了，时间和钱都浪费了。

### E24

- 位置：Replication 一节，区组和年份作为随机效应
- 原文："Alternatively, care should be taken in designing such experiments to allow the blocking factor to be treated as a random effect, e.g., in which each block could be considered a random observation from a larger population of levels."
- 要点：设计时要考虑让区组（例如地点、年份）可以当作随机效应，即把每个区组看成从更大总体中随机抽到的一个。

### E25

- 位置：Blocking 一节，不完全区组设计
- 原文："In particular, these designs have become very common in tree breeding programs that are typically located in mountainous regions with little or no level or flat topography."
- 要点：α 设计和行列设计在林木育种项目里很常见，因为这些项目多在山区，几乎没有平地。

### E26

- 位置：结论部分，给年轻研究者的建议
- 原文："Begin by designing small experiments with relatively large experimental units, as large as you feel you can handle with labor and machinery."
- 要点：刚开始时，先设计小规模的试验，实验单位尽量大，大到人力和机械能应付为止。

### E27

- 位置：Number of replicates 一节，资源分配
- 原文："It is always clear from these exercises that the number of locations and/or years should be maximized relative to the number of replicates"
- 要点：按方差分量做资源分配的计算显示，相对于每个地点内的重复数，应该优先增加地点数和年份数。

### E28

- 位置：Replication 一节，Number of replicates
- 原文："Power is the probability of rejecting a null hypothesis that is, in fact, false."
- 要点：统计功效是"原假设其实是错的时候，试验能拒绝它"的概率。

### E29

- 位置：Replication 一节，功效分析示例
- 原文："Such a graphical display easily allows any researcher to choose one of multiple design arrangements that meet the expected target for power, which is often set at 0.8 (80%)."
- 要点：把不同重复数、取样数下的功效画成图，就能挑出达到目标功效的设计；目标常定为 0.8（80%）。

### E30

- 位置：Replication 一节，资源分配
- 原文："Classical resource allocation theory is based on having accurate estimates of the variance components from previous experiments."
- 要点：经典的资源分配理论（决定地点、年份、重复各做多少）依赖以往试验得到的准确方差分量估计。

### E31

- 位置：Table 2 标题
- 原文："Mixed models analysis provides direct estimates of the five variance components in the table, using restricted maximum likelihood estimation methods."
- 要点：对多地点、多年份的随机完全区组试验，混合模型分析（用限制性最大似然法）可以直接估计出各个方差分量。

### E32

- 位置：Randomization 一节
- 原文："Randomization is a strict mathematical process in which a random number generator is used to order the treatments. Interspersion is a subjective concept in which the researcher strives to achieve balance and avoid “clumping” of certain treatments or replicates of treatments."
- 要点：随机化是用随机数生成器排处理顺序的严格过程；"穿插"是研究者主观追求均衡、避免某些处理扎堆。

### E33

- 位置：Randomization 一节
- 原文："Sometimes randomization can result in experimental designs that appear to have undesirable clumping or patterns. These patterns can result in potential bias or confounding to treatment effects if there are underlying spatial variation patterns that are correlated with the randomization plan"
- 要点：随机化有时会排出扎堆的布局；如果田里的空间变异恰好和这种布局相关，就可能带来偏倚或混杂。

### E34

- 位置：Randomization 一节
- 原文："Spatially balanced complete block designs (van Es et al., 2007) were developed to solve this potential problem, helping to ensure that both randomization and interspersion are key components of any design."
- 要点：空间均衡完全区组设计就是为解决这个问题而提出的，让随机化和穿插两者兼顾。

### E35

- 位置：Blocking 一节
- 原文："In addition, much like randomization, blocking can be thought of as an insurance policy against disturbances that may or may not arise during the course of the experiment."
- 要点：区组和随机化一样，可以看作防范试验期间意外干扰的保险。

### E36

- 位置：Size of experimental units 一节（Smith 定律）
- 原文："The concept is based on Smith's Law (Smith, 1938), which derived from the general observation of a negative asymptotic relationship between variance (on a per-unit or single-plot basis) and plot size"
- 要点：Smith 定律：单个小区的方差随小区面积增大而下降，并逐渐趋于平稳。

### E37

- 位置：Size of experimental units 一节
- 原文："For small initial plot sizes, any change in plot size is expected to have a large effect on mean unit variance. Conversely, for researchers who are using relatively large plots, relatively large changes in plot size may have little or no impact on the mean unit variance."
- 要点：小区本来就小，改面积对方差影响大；小区本来就大，再大幅改面积也几乎没影响。

### E38

- 位置：Size of experimental units 一节
- 原文："For any given experiment, our choice of plot size depends on seed and land availability and prior estimates of the slope of the log-linear curve in Fig. 11, estimated for that particular field"
- 要点：作者自己课题组定小区大小，看种子和土地够不够，以及这块地以往估出的方差—面积曲线斜率。

### E39

- 位置：Blocking 一节，裂区设计
- 原文："A common misconception of the split-plot randomization is that they represent an experimental design per se, e.g., a split-plot with Factor A as the whole-plot factor and Factor B as the subplot factor. In fact, this represents an incomplete description of the experimental design because it is missing the basic design for Factor A, the whole-plot factor."
- 要点：只写"A 为主区、B 为副区的裂区设计"是不完整的，还缺主区因素 A 本身用的基本设计（例如随机完全区组）。

### E40

- 位置：Replication 一节，特殊情况
- 原文："These designs fell out of favor when it became clear that devoting half of the experimental units to a single treatment or cultivar was highly inefficient (Melton and Finckner, 1967), and modern spatial analysis methods were just emerging (Gaeton and Guyon, 2010). Nevertheless, they remain a very viable option for small on-farm experiments."
- 要点：对照小区设计（对照隔一块放一块）把一半实验单位给了对照，效率很低，后来不流行了；但对小型农场试验仍很可行。

### E41

- 位置：Replication 一节，增广设计
- 原文："Additional check cultivars are added at random to individual blocks to augment error estimation and to estimate the spatial effects on a finer or smaller scale"
- 要点：在增广设计里，随机地往各区组加入对照品种（check），用来估计误差和较小尺度的空间效应。

### E42

- 位置：Replication 一节
- 原文："As long as no attempt is made to attribute simple effects of interactions to unreplicated environmental variables, which cannot be conclusively resolved, the researcher is relatively safe from criticism."
- 要点：只要不把交互作用归因到没有重复的环境变量上，就不容易被批评。

### E43

- 位置：Replication 一节，农场试验
- 原文："Researchers organizing these types of field experiments have three options: (i) conduct the experiments on multiple farms, using farms as blocks; (ii) use control-plot designs in which one control treatment is interspersed with the other treatments, optimally in an every-other-plot pattern; or (iii) use a combination of both approaches."
- 要点：农场试验三种办法：多个农场当区组；对照小区穿插在其他处理之间；或两者结合。
