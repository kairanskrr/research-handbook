# EGAP 方法指南：10 Things to Know About Survey Experiments

- 资料：`egapsurveyexperiments`，[原文网页](https://methods.egap.org/guides/data-collection/survey-experiments_en.html)
- 版本："治理与政治证据网络"（EGAP）方法指南，网页元数据署名 Christopher Grady；2026 年 10 月访问。位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§1 What is a survey experiment
- 原文："A survey experiment is an experiment conducted within a survey."
- 要点：调查实验就是在问卷调查里做的实验。

### E2

- 位置：§2 Why do a survey experiment
- 原文："Other preferences, such as racist attitudes and illegal behaviors, may be sensitive — preferences with which respondents do not want to be publicly associated. Direct questioning techniques may thus understate the prevalence of these preferences."
- 要点：种族歧视态度、违法行为等敏感问题，直接问会低估其普遍程度。

### E3

- 位置：§3 Conjoint experiments
- 原文："In a typical conjoint experiment, researchers repeatedly ask respondents to choose between two distinct options and randomly vary the characteristics of these two options."
- 要点：联合分析实验让受访者反复在两个选项间选择，选项的各项特征随机变化。

### E4

- 位置：§5 Endorsement experiments
- 原文："Because endorsement experiments preempt the need for respondents to self-report their support for a controversial object, they are especially useful in politically sensitive contexts."
- 要点：背书实验不需要受访者直接表态支持有争议的对象，所以在政治敏感环境中特别有用。

### E5

- 位置：§6 List Experiments
- 原文："List experiments (also known as the item count technique) measure a sensitive attitude or behavior when researchers expect respondents to falsify it if it is solicited using a direct question."
- 要点：列举实验用于测量直接问时受访者可能撒谎的敏感态度或行为。

### E6

- 位置：§9 Considerations
- 原文："researchers need to be careful about survey satisficing, which occurs when respondents put in minimal effort to understand and answer a survey question."
- 要点：要注意受访者敷衍作答（只花最少力气理解和回答问题）。

### E7

- 位置：§10 Limitations
- 原文："When preferences are measured — and treatments are delivered — in a survey setting, there is no guarantee that the survey-experimental findings will translate into the real world."
- 要点：在问卷里测出的结果不一定能推到真实世界。
