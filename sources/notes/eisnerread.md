# Eisner 2009：How to Read a Technical Paper

- 资料：`eisnerread`，[原文](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~jason/advice/how-to-read-a-paper.html)
- 版本：网页，署名 2009 年，最后修改 2018-08-05

### E1

- 位置：Multi-pass reading
- 原文："Skim the paper first, skipping over anything that would take much mental effort. Just get an idea of where the paper is going, why it was written, what's old hat and what's new to you."
- 要点：先略读，跳过费脑的部分，只弄清论文要讲什么、为什么写、哪些是旧东西、哪些对你是新的。

### E2

- 位置：Write as you read
- 原文："Write as you read. This keeps your attention focused and makes you engage with the paper."
- 要点：边读边写，能让注意力集中，迫使你主动投入。

### E3

- 位置：Low-level notes
- 原文："restate unclear points in your own words [...] come up with examples that illustrate the author's ideas, and examples that would be problematic for the author [...] challenge the paper's claims or methods"
- 要点：在论文上做批注：用自己的话重述没讲清的地方，举出支持作者和难住作者的例子，质疑论文的主张和方法。

### E4

- 位置：High-level notes
- 原文："Low-level notes aren't enough. Also keep high-level notes about papers. You should try to distill the paper down: summarize the things that interested you, contrast with other papers, and record your own questions and ideas for future work."
- 要点：光有批注不够，还要写高层次笔记：提炼论文、和其他论文对比、记下自己的问题和后续想法。

### E5

- 位置：High-level notes
- 原文："At a minimum, you should re-explain the ideas in your own words: produce some text that is aimed at your future self. You should be able to reread this later and quickly reconstruct your understanding of the paper."
- 要点：至少要用自己的话把思想重新讲一遍，写给将来的自己，以后重读时能很快恢复理解。

### E6

- 位置：High-level notes（引 Michael Mitzenmacher）
- 原文："Read creatively. Reading a paper critically is easy, in that it is always easier to tear something down than to build it up. Reading creatively involves harder, more positive thinking. What are the good ideas in this paper?"
- 要点：批判地读容易，创造性地读更难：要找出论文里的好想法，思考它们还能怎样推广和改进。

### E7

- 位置：Which parts to focus on
- 原文："I do think that when you are learning a new area, you should read at least some papers extremely thoroughly. That means knowing what every sentence and every subscript is doing, so that you really learn all of the techniques used in the paper."
- 要点：进入新领域时，至少要把几篇论文读得极其透彻，弄懂每一句话、每一个下标在做什么。

### E8

- 位置：Which parts to focus on — Experiments
- 原文："then you should go back and carefully examine the experimental design, including the choice of data. Were the experiments fair? Do they support the claims? What's really going on? Are the conclusions likely to generalize beyond this experimental scenario?"
- 要点：如果在意论文的结论（方法是否有效、要不要用它），就要仔细检查实验设计和数据选择：实验公平吗？支持论文的主张吗？结论能推广到这个实验场景之外吗？

### E9

- 位置：Set aside time
- 原文："There is no shame in reading slowly. It still takes me several hours to absorb a paper on something that I genuinely don't know well."
- 要点：读得慢不丢人；即使是资深研究者，读不熟悉领域的论文也要好几个小时。

### E10

- 位置：What to read
- 原文："put yourself in an author's shoes; what phrases might they have used?"
- 要点：检索时站在作者的角度想：他们会用什么词来描述这个问题？

### E11

- 位置：What to read
- 原文："backward references: follow the bibliography to earlier papers [...] forward references: see who else has cited the work"
- 要点：找到一篇相关论文后，向后查它的参考文献，向前查谁引用了它。

### E12

- 位置：What to read
- 原文："has someone else already listed the right papers for you? [...] course syllabi [...] literature review chapters from dissertations"
- 要点：先看看别人是否已经替你列好了论文：综述文章、课程大纲、学位论文的文献综述章节等。

### E13

- 位置：What to read
- 原文："read a lot of abstracts (and skim the papers as needed) before deciding which papers are best to read"
- 要点：先广泛读摘要，再决定哪些论文最值得精读。

### E14

- 位置：Which parts to focus on
- 原文："If you don't know who to ask, find other papers that cite this one (via Google Scholar) and see what they say about this paper."
- 要点：判断不了一篇论文哪里重要时，可以找引用它的论文，看别人怎么评价它。
