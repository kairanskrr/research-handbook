# Eisner 2010：Write the Paper First

- 资料：`eisnerfirst`，[原文](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~jason/advice/write-the-paper-first.html)
- 版本：网页，署名 2010 年

### E1

- 位置：Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — What needs to be done?
- 原文："Writing the literature review will help you design your experiments. It may influence what datasets you use, what comparisons you do, and what you are trying to prove you can do that other people can't."
- 要点：写文献综述能帮你设计实验：决定用什么数据、和谁比较、要证明自己能做到别人做不到的什么。

### E2

- 位置：Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — What needs to be done?
- 原文："However, don't write the lit review first. Write out your own ideas before comparing them with the literature. This increases the chance that you'll find new angles on the problem."
- 要点：但不要先写文献综述；先把自己的想法写出来，再和文献对比，这样更可能找到新角度。

### E3

- 位置：Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — Is it a good topic?
- 原文："Show people a draft of the first 2-3 pages before you do any experiments. If they tell you that your basic idea will evoke skepticism or yawns from reviewers, it's better to know that before you waste your time."
- 要点：做实验之前，先把论文前两三页给别人看；如果他们认为基本想法会让审稿人怀疑或觉得无聊，最好在浪费时间之前知道。

### E4

- 位置：Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — What needs to be done?
- 原文："Assuming the idea is indeed a good one, then writing a draft makes you sharpen the message of the paper. Then you can figure out what work needs to be done to support exactly that message."
- 要点：写草稿能逼你把要传达的论点磨清楚，再据此确定需要做哪些工作来支撑这个论点。

### E5

- 位置：Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — What needs to be done?
- 原文："Writing the experimental section is possible even before you've done the experiments. Explain the full experimental design. Make empty result tables with row and column headers and explanatory captions."
- 要点：实验没做之前也能写实验部分：写出完整设计，画出带行列标题和说明的空结果表。

### E6

- 位置：Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — What needs to be done?
- 原文："Honest writing may lead you to realize that proving your point requires more work than you'd thought (which is why you'd better write early)."
- 要点：诚实地写下来，可能会让你意识到证明自己的论点需要比预想更多的工作，所以要早写。

### E7

- 位置：The document is a focus for discussion with others
- 原文："By showing others the draft at this stage, you'll get important feedback before you invest time in the "wrong" work."
- 要点：在这个阶段给别人看草稿，能在你投入时间做"错误"的工作之前得到重要反馈。

### E8

- 位置：The document is like a code specification
- 原文："Of course, neither coding nor research is purely top-down—in practice, there's feedback. Just as coding will make you rethink parts of the spec, certainly experimentation will make you revise parts of the paper."
- 要点：研究不是纯粹自上而下的；实验会反过来让你修改论文草稿，计划要随之更新。

### E9

- 位置：Writing is the best use of limited time / Clear writing increases your odds of acceptance
- 原文："Clear motivation and exposition are more important than results for getting your paper accepted."
- 要点：Eisner 认为，对论文能否被接收，清楚的动机和表述比结果更重要；混乱的稿子是致命的，少几个实验不是。

### E10

- 位置：The document is a focus for discussion with others
- 原文："Your draft can describe your motivation, formal problem, model, algorithms, and experiments before you actually build anything."
- 要点：在真正动手做之前，草稿就可以写出动机、问题、模型、算法和实验设计，并拿给别人提意见。

### E14

- 位置：Writing is the best use of limited time / Clear writing increases your odds of acceptance
- 原文："A well-known senior academic told me that they review papers by reading as far as they can understand, then assigning a score based on how far they got."
- 要点：有资深学者审稿时读到看不懂为止，按读到多远打分。Eisner 不推荐这种做法，但它说明了写不清楚的代价。
