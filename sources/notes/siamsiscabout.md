### E1

- 位置：期刊简介
- 原文："SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing (SISC) contains research articles on numerical methods and techniques for scientific computation. Papers in this journal address computational issues relevant to the solution of scientific or engineering problems and include computational results demonstrating the effectiveness of the proposed techniques."
- 要点：SISC 刊登科学计算的数值方法和技术；论文处理和解决科学或工程问题有关的计算问题，并附有计算结果，展示所提技术的有效性。
