# AIAA：Editorial Policy Statement on Numerical and Experimental Accuracy

- 资料：`aiaaeditorialpolicy`，[原文](https://aiaa.org/EditorialPolicyStatement)
- 版本：美国航空航天学会（AIAA）网页，2026-10-06 读取。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："The AIAA journals will not accept for publication any manuscript reporting (1) numerical solutions of an engineering problem that fails to adequately address the accuracy of the computed results or (2) experimental results unless the accuracy ofthe data is adequately presented."
- 要点：AIAA 期刊不接收没有充分讨论计算结果精度的数值论文，也不接收没有充分给出数据精度的实验论文。

### E2

- 位置：开头
- 原文："The implementation of this policy statement will be at the discretion of the Editor-in-Chief of each journal and the Associate Editor evaluating the submitted manuscript."
- 要点：这份政策怎样执行，由各期刊主编和负责该稿的副主编决定。

### E3

- 位置：Experimental Accuracy，Estimation of Bias Uncertainty
- 原文："The bias uncertainty of the results (systematic component of the unexplained variance) should be estimated. In addition, the method of estimation should be described."
- 要点：要估计系统性的偏差不确定度，并说明估计方法。

### E4

- 位置：Experimental Accuracy，Estimation of Precision Uncertainty
- 原文："The repeatability of the results (random component of the unexplained variance) should be quantified. Also, the method of determining the repeatability should be specified."
- 要点：要量化结果的重复性（随机部分），并说明怎样确定的。

### E5

- 位置：Experimental Accuracy，Statement of Total Uncertainty
- 原文："Authors should identify any known potential sources of bias and precision uncertainty that have not been explicitly included in the estimates of total uncertainty."
- 要点：已知但没有计入总不确定度的偏差来源和随机来源，也要指出来。

### E6

- 位置：Experimental Accuracy，Statement of Experimental Design
- 原文："the experimental conditions and order of acquisition should be presented or concisely described."
- 要点：要写出实验条件和数据采集的顺序，方便别人评估不确定度、重做实验或做仿真对比。

### E7

- 位置：Numerical Accuracy，Statement of Code Verification Activities
- 原文："If commercially available computer codes are an important element in the simulations presented, references for publicly available codetesting should be given."
- 要点：仿真中用到商业软件的，要引用公开的代码测试资料。

### E8

- 位置：Experimental Accuracy，Coverage Factor
- 原文："The coverage factor used for all expanded uncertainty estimates should be specified."
- 要点：所有扩展不确定度都要写明用的包含因子。

### E9

- 位置：Estimation of Precision Uncertainty
- 原文："The repeatability of the results (random component of the unexplained variance) should be quantified. Also, the method of determining the repeatability should be specified. Unusual results, whether explained or unexplained, should be identified."
- 要点：要量化结果的重复性并说明方法；异常结果，不论能否解释，都要指出来。

### E10

- 位置：Coverage Factor
- 原文："The coverage factor used for all expanded uncertainty estimates should be specified. That is, the author(s) should associate a specific level of confidence with each uncertainty value"
- 要点：所有扩展不确定度都要写明包含因子，也就是给每个不确定度配上明确的置信水平。
