# The College of New Jersey 政治学系：Disciplinary Standards for Reappointment, Tenure, and Promotion in Political Science

- 资料：`tcnj2023polisci`，[原文](https://academicaffairs.tcnj.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/171/2023/05/Political-Science-Disciplinary-Standards.pdf)
- 版本：美国新泽西学院政治学系的续聘、终身教职和晋升学科标准；正文最后修订于 2023 年 4 月 5 日，系主任 2022 年 6 月、院长 2022 年 9 月、教务长 2023 年 5 月签署。读的是学校网站 PDF 在 Internet Archive 的存档（https://web.archive.org/web/20250325153514/https://academicaffairs.tcnj.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/171/2023/05/Political-Science-Disciplinary-Standards.pdf）。

### E1

- 位置：Scholarship 部分
- 原文："In evaluating an applicant's scholarship, there is no precise formula to weigh a book vs. articles, but generally a book would be considered the equivalent of two to four articles."
- 要点：该系评估科研成果时，书和文章没有精确换算公式，一般一本书算两到四篇文章。

### E2

- 位置：Scholarship 部分
- 原文："Conference papers, which typically lack peer review, are an important type of scholarly activity, but are regarded primarily as evidence of ongoing scholarly activity and a precursor to journal submission and acceptance."
- 要点：会议论文通常没有同行评审，主要被看作研究在持续进行、准备投期刊的证据。

### E3

- 位置：Scholarship 部分
- 原文："Conference papers published as part of a conference Proceedings (rare for our discipline) should be similarly explained and the extent of peer review must be clarified."
- 要点：收进会议论文集正式出版的情况在政治学很少见；如果有，候选人要说明经过了多少同行评审。
