# Falessi 等人 2018：Empirical Software Engineering Experts on the Use of Students and Professionals in Experiments

- 资料：`falessi2018empirical`，[布鲁内尔大学机构库中的作者版本](https://bura.brunel.ac.uk/bitstream/2438/14575/1/Fulltext.pdf)
- 版本：Empirical Software Engineering 23(1), 2018；作者接受稿

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："We, on the contrary, strongly believe that no population (students, professionals, or others) can be deemed better than another in absolute terms. [Conclusion] Using students as participants remains a valid simplification of reality needed in laboratory contexts."
- 要点：作者认为没有哪一类参与者（学生、专业人员或其他）绝对优于另一类；在实验室情境中，用学生作参与者仍是一种合理、必要的对现实的简化。

### E2

- 位置：§4.1.1 Misleading terminology
- 原文："ST1 - Classifying experiment participants using a binary scale (students or professional) is an inappropriate approach."
- 要点：用"学生/专业人员"二分法来刻画实验参与者是不合适的：二者会重叠，学生也可能有工作经验。

### E3

- 位置：§4.1.2 Threats to validity trade-off
- 原文："The experimental paradigm requires a trade-off between internal and external validity. It is infeasible to maximize both internal and external validity at the same time."
- 要点：实验要在内部效度和外部效度之间权衡，二者不可能同时达到最大。

### E4

- 位置：§4.1.3（便利样本）
- 原文："When experiments are conducted in a company, the participants are also a convenient sample because they are not selected randomly from the population of all developers and very often they are not even a random sample of the developers at the company."
- 要点：在公司里做实验，专业人员同样是便利样本：既不是从全体开发者中随机抽的，往往也不是该公司开发者的随机样本。

### E5

- 位置：§6.2 Added statements
- 原文："ST9 - We should think about population and validity already before conducting the experiment, at the time when we are planning to use convenience sampling."
- 要点：在计划使用便利样本时，就要事先想清楚总体和效度，而不是做完实验再回头找理由。（专家调查中 100% 同意）

### E6

- 位置：§6.2 Added statements
- 原文："ST16 - Papers should provide a precise description of the reasons why the use of students as subjects in the study was appropriate under the specific circumstances of the experiment reported. On the other hand, reviewers should criticize the use of students not as a general statement valid for all experiments, but by detailing why it was inappropriate under the specific circumstances in the experiment presented."
- 要点：论文要具体说明在本实验的情境下用学生为什么合适；审稿人批评用学生时，也要具体说明在本实验中为什么不合适，而不是一概而论。

### E7

- 位置：§4.2.1 Hard to get, even harder to improve
- 原文："This means that in one case, only 33% of the committed participants finally showed up for the experiment. In this author's experience, "no shows" happen more often with professionals than with students."
- 要点：一位作者的经验：某次实验只有 33% 承诺参加的专业人员真正到场；专业人员爽约比学生更常见。

### E8

- 位置：§7 Towards a characterization of experience
- 原文："The R3 scheme characterizes Real, Relevant, and Recent experience, hence the name R3."
- 要点：作者建议按"真实经验、相关经验、近期经验"三个维度刻画参与者，而不是只贴"学生"或"专业人员"的标签。

### E9

- 位置：§9 Discussion
- 原文："Thus, students are clearly neither always nor never suitable as proxies for professional developers; it depends on different contexts and with different types of population."
- 要点：学生既不是总能、也不是总不能代替专业开发者，取决于情境和目标总体。
