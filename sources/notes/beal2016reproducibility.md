# Beal 等人 2016：Reproducibility of Fluorescent Expression from Engineered Biological Constructs in E. coli

- 资料：`beal2016reproducibility`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0150182)，PMC4777433
- 版本：PLoS ONE 11(3):e0150182，2016 年。开放获取（CC BY 4.0）。通过 Europe PMC 全文接口读取。研究依托 2014、2015 年国际基因工程机器大赛（iGEM）的跨实验室测量。位置写章节。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："We present results of the first large-scale interlaboratory study carried out in synthetic biology, as part of the 2014 and 2015 International Genetically Engineered Machine (iGEM) competitions. Participants at 88 institutions around the world measured fluorescence from three engineered constitutive constructs in E. coli."
- 要点：这是合成生物学第一项大规模跨实验室研究，借 2014、2015 年 iGEM 大赛进行；全球 88 个机构的参与者测量了大肠杆菌中三个工程化组成型表达构建体的荧光。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Few participants were able to measure absolute fluorescence, so data was analyzed in terms of ratios."
- 要点：很少有参与者能测绝对荧光值，所以只能按比值分析。

### E3

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："while host strain did not affect expression ratios, choice of instrument did."
- 要点：宿主菌株不影响表达比值，但用哪台仪器有影响。

### E4

- 位置：Results, Absolute vs. Relative Units
- 原文："Highly inconsistent calibration is effectively no better than simply using arbitrary units to begin with."
- 要点：校准做得极不一致，实际上不比一开始就用任意单位好。

### E5

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："the limits encountered in this study highlight the need for adoption of calibrated measurements producing standardized units (e.g., [12, 13]) and for protocol approaches that can reduce the impact of “cultural art” in laboratory methods (i.e., the undocumented or undocumentable differences in how an apparently identifical measurement is executed differently between two individuals or laboratories, and in how the data is handled to turn “raw” observations into reported values)"
- 要点：需要用校准过的测量得到标准单位，并用规范的操作方法减少"手艺"的影响，即两个人或两个实验室做同一测量时那些没写下来或写不下来的差别，以及把原始观测变成报告数值的处理差别。

### E6

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："A major barrier to transition from principle to practice, however, is the frequent sensitivity of biological systems to small changes in their cellular or environmental context"
- 要点：从原理走向应用的一大障碍，是生物系统常常对细胞或环境条件的微小变化很敏感。
