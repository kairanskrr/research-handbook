# Stodden 等 2013：Setting the Default to Reproducible: Reproducibility in Computational and Experimental Mathematics

- 资料：`stodden2013setting`，[原文](https://icerm.brown.edu/topical_workshops/tw12-5-rcem/icerm_report.pdf)
- 版本：ICERM（布朗大学计算与实验数学研究所）2012 年 12 月研讨会报告，2013 年 2 月 16 日版。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："With the increasingly central role of computation in scientific discovery this means communicating all details of the computations needed for others to replicate the experiment, i.e. making available to others the associated data and code."
- 要点：计算在科学发现中越来越核心，因此要公开别人重复实验所需的全部计算细节，即公开数据和代码。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Often there is no record of the workflow process or the code actually used to obtain the published results, let alone a record of the false starts."
- 要点：计算工作常常没有记录工作流程和实际用来得出结果的代码，更别说失败的尝试。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："provided the first opportunity for a broad cross section of computational scientists and mathematicians, including pure mathematicians who focus on experimental mathematics or computer-assisted proofs, to discuss these issues"
- 要点：研讨会参与者包括计算科学家和数学家，也包括做实验数学、计算机辅助证明的纯数学家。

### E4

- 位置：Journals 一节
- 原文："Very rigorous verification and validity testing, along with a full disclosure of computational details, should be required of papers making important assertions, such as the computer-assisted proof of a long-standing mathematical result"
- 要点：做出重要论断的论文（例如对长期未解数学问题的计算机辅助证明），应被要求做严格的验证并完整公开计算细节。

### E5

- 位置：Appendix C The State of Experimental Mathematics
- 原文："(d) Have symbolic manipulations been validated, say by using two different symbolic manipulation packages? Such checks are often required, because even the best symbolic and numeric computation packages have bugs and limitations"
- 要点：符号计算结果要验证，例如用两个不同的符号计算软件互相核对；最好的软件也有缺陷和局限。

### E6

- 位置：Conclusion
- 原文："The goal of computational reproducibility is to provide a solid foundation to computational science, much like a rigorous proof is the foundation of mathematics."
- 要点：计算可复现性之于计算科学，就像严格证明之于数学。
