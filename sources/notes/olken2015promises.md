# Olken 2015：Promises and Perils of Pre-analysis Plans

- 资料：`olken2015promises`，[AEA 文章页（含摘要）](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.29.3.61)
- 版本：Journal of Economic Perspectives 29(3)。全文 PDF 被网站拦截，本摘录只依据 AEA 文章页上公开的摘要。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："A pre-analysis plan pre-specifies in a precise way the analysis to be run before examining the data. A researcher can specify variables, data cleaning procedures, regression specifications, and so on."
- 要点：预分析计划是在看数据之前，精确写下要做哪些分析：用哪些变量、怎样清洗数据、跑哪些回归等。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："If the regressions are pre-specified in advance and researchers are required to report all the results they pre-specify, data-mining problems are greatly reduced."
- 要点：事先写定回归、并要求报告所有预先写定的结果，可以大大减少"数据挖掘"问题。

### E3

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："I also explore some of the limitations and costs of such plans. I then review a few pieces of evidence that suggest that, in many contexts, the benefits of using pre-specified analysis plans may not be as high as one might have expected initially."
- 要点：预分析计划也有局限和成本；作者认为在很多情形下，它的好处可能没有最初设想的那么大。

### E4

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："For the most part, I will focus on the relatively narrow issue of pre-analysis for randomized controlled trials."
- 要点：文章主要讨论随机对照试验的预分析计划。
