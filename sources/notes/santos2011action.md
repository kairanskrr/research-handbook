# dos Santos & Travassos 2011：Action Research Can Swing the Balance in Experimental Software Engineering

- 资料：`santos2011action`，[原文（arXiv 1306.2414）](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.2414)
- 版本：*Advances in Computers* 第 83 卷（2011）中的一章，作者在 arXiv 公开的版本（77 页）。

### E1

- 位置：§2.2 Action Research Process
- 原文："a kind of social research with experimental basis that is conceived and conducted in close association with an action or a collective problem resolution where researchers and participants are involved in a cooperative way"
- 要点：引 Thiollent 的定义：行动研究是一种有实验基础的社会研究，与某项行动或集体解决问题的过程紧密结合，研究者和参与者以合作的方式共同参与。

### E2

- 位置：§2.2（典范行动研究过程，基于 Davison 等人 2004）
- 原文："(1) Diagnostic: consists of exploring the research field, stakeholders and their expectations holistically." / "(2) Planning: stage where actions are defined to the faced circumstances." / "(3) Intervention: corresponds to the planned actions implementation." / "(4) Evaluation: stage where the interventions effect are analyzed considering the theoretical background used as basis to the actions definition." / "(5) Reflection: involves the dissemination of acquired knowledge among participants and other organization departments."
- 要点：典范的五阶段：诊断（全面了解研究领域、利益相关者及其期望）、计划（针对情况确定行动）、干预（实施计划的行动）、评估（依据制定行动时所用的理论分析干预效果）、反思（在参与者和组织其他部门中传播所获得的知识）。

### E3

- 位置：§2.2
- 原文："the research environment requires a contract/agreement to legitimize the actions and possible benefits for both parties (researchers and organization)" / "The other additional step regards the criteria to finish the study. This should occur whenever both parties involved are satisfied with the outcome."
- 要点：研究需要一份合同或协议，使行动正当化并明确双方的利益；研究在双方都对结果满意时结束。

### E4

- 位置：§2.2
- 原文："the researcher can return to the diagnosis stage after an initial attempt to plan the study has not been completed because of a lack of better problem description"
- 要点：各阶段之间可以回退，例如问题描述不清导致计划做不下去时，可以回到诊断阶段。

### E5

- 位置：§2.2
- 原文："The importance of making the criteria explicit before conducting the research is also emphasized by Avison et al. (1999) who claim" / "that without it the evaluation of the results tends to be compromised, and eventually what is being described may be action (but not research) or research (but not action)."
- 要点：研究开始前就要把标准写明；否则结果评估会打折扣，最后描述的可能只是"行动而不是研究"，或"研究而没有行动"。

### E6

- 位置：§2.2
- 原文："Action Research does not obtain the repeatability property present in natural sciences, but it is sufficient to clarify the processes and models that allowed the researcher to make the interpretations and derive the conclusions."
- 要点：行动研究达不到自然科学那样的可重复性，但足以讲清研究者得出解释和结论所依据的过程和模型。

### E7

- 位置：§2.2.1 Other Action Research Characteristics
- 原文："Action Research differentiates itself from routine practice because it is proactive and strategically driven by theories and research techniques."
- 要点：行动研究与日常工作的区别在于，它由理论和研究技术主动、有策略地驱动。

### E8

- 位置：§5.1.1 Learning and Difficulties in Using the Action Research Methodology
- 原文："in certain cases, it can be difficult to perceive the difference between an Action Research study and a Case Study. This difficulty is even more noticeable when there is more emphasis on observation than on action at certain moments."
- 要点：有时很难分清行动研究和案例研究，尤其是某些阶段观察多于行动的时候。

### E9

- 位置：§5.1.1
- 原文："over the refactoring study meetings described in the previous section where one of the authors played both professional and researcher roles"
- 要点：作者自己的重构研究中，一位作者同时担任项目中的专业人员和研究者两种角色。

### E10

- 位置：§5.1.1
- 原文："The previous definition of what data should be collected shields the researcher from unexpected events allowing one to focus on the monitoring of actions and the participating in interventions." / "Obviously, unplanned events will happen but the Action Research methodology can accommodate this kind of deviation. Nevertheless, it still necessary to register these events to allow the researcher to justify them."
- 要点：事先定好要收集哪些数据，能让研究者在意外发生时仍专注于监控行动和参与干预；计划外的事一定会发生，方法可以容纳，但要记录下来，以便说明理由。

### E11

- 位置：§5.1.1
- 原文："Automated forms (e.g., voice recording) provide the researcher with additional time to conduct the interventions."
- 要点：自动化的数据收集方式（如录音）能给研究者腾出时间去实施干预。

### E12

- 位置：§5.1.3 Ethical Issues
- 原文："researchers should not put the problem solution in an unfavourable position in relation to their academic interests as the initial agreement between the parties (researchers and organization) should always aim at addressing a real problem."
- 要点：研究者不应为了自己的学术利益而让解决问题处于次要地位，双方最初的协议应始终以解决真实问题为目标。

### E13

- 位置：§5.1.3 Ethical Issues
- 原文："Being a collaborative research in a real context, the ethical issues involved in an Action Research study become even more important." / "those involved feel compelled to participate as several research activities overlap their professional responsibilities." / "it should also be offered to these professionals that their individual data will not be published" / "the freedom in consenting cannot be always achieved"
- 要点：行动研究是在真实环境中的合作研究，伦理问题更加重要。研究在参与者自己的单位里进行、与本职工作重叠，参与者会觉得不得不参加；应承诺不发表个人数据；自由同意并不总能做到。

### E14

- 位置：§5.2.1 Engineering Software and Building Theories
- 原文："Due to the way in which theories are built in Action Research, they tend to be of level 1 (minor working relationships that are concrete and directly based on observations) or level 2 (theories of the middle range involving some abstractions but that are still closely linked to observations)."
- 要点：行动研究产出的理论往往停留在较低层次：直接基于观察的具体工作关系，或有一定抽象、但仍紧贴观察的中层理论。

### E15

- 位置：§5.2.2 Research Rigour and the Results Relevance
- 原文："One is the control level applied when conducting the study, aimed at minimizing researcher bias and the influence of other variables over the outcomes." / "The second is how theoretical knowledge is kept explicit during the research actions"
- 要点：行动研究的严谨性有两个方面：一是研究中的控制程度，用来减少研究者偏见和其他变量对结果的影响；二是在研究行动中让理论知识保持明确。

### E16

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："the initial findings also revealed that the rigour and control of such studies should improve in Software Engineering."
- 要点：作者对软件工程行动研究文献的调查发现，这类研究的严谨性和控制还需要提高。
