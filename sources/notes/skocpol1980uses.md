# Skocpol & Somers 1980：The Uses of Comparative History in Macrosocial Inquiry

- 资料：`skocpol1980uses`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0010417500009282)
- 版本：Comparative Studies in Society and History 22(2), pp. 174–197，1980 年；出版社 Cambridge University Press 官网免费提供全文 PDF。两位作者当时在美国哈佛大学，文章早期版本在 1979 年美国社会学会年会"历史社会学方法"分会上报告。位置写期刊页码。这是两位作者对比较历史研究方法的归纳，不是学会规范。

### E1

- 位置：第 175 页
- 原文："Notwithstanding such attempts at homogenization, there are, in fact, at least three distinct logics-in-use of comparative history." / "One of them, which we shall label comparative history as macro-causal analysis, actually does resemble multivariate hypothesis-testing."
- 要点：比较历史研究实际上至少有三种不同的用法；其中一种叫"宏观因果分析"，确实类似多变量的假设检验。

### E2

- 位置：第 182 页
- 原文："This third variant of comparative history is, indeed, a kind of multivariate analysis to which scholars turn in order to validate causal statements about macro-phenomena for which, inherently, there are too many variables and not enough cases. Macro-analytic comparative historians proceed by selecting or referring to aspects of historical cases in order to set up approximations to controlled comparisons."
- 要点：宏观因果分析用来检验关于宏观现象的因果说法，这类现象天生"变量太多、案例太少"；研究者挑选历史案例的某些方面，搭出近似的"对照比较"。

### E3

- 位置：第 183 页
- 原文："On the one hand, Macro-analysts can try to establish that several cases having in common the phenomenon to be explained also have in common the hypothesized causal factors, although the cases vary in other ways that might have seemed causally relevant."
- 要点：一种设计（密尔的"求同法"）：几个都出现了待解释现象的案例，也都有假设的原因，而它们在其他看似相关的方面各不相同。

### E4

- 位置：第 183 页
- 原文："On the other hand, Macro-analysts can contrast cases in which the phenomenon to be explained and the hypothesized causes are present to other ("negative") cases in which the phenomenon and the causes are both absent, although they are as similar as possible to the "positive" cases in other respects." / "Taken alone, this second approach is more powerful for establishing valid causal associations than is the "Method of Agreement."
- 要点：另一种设计（密尔的"求异法"）：把现象和原因都出现的案例，和两者都没出现、但其他方面尽量相似的"反面"案例对比；单独使用时，它比求同法更有力。

### E5

- 位置：第 193–194 页
- 原文："The problem is that perfectly controlled comparisons are never really feasible." / "and history rarely, if ever, provides exactly the cases needed for controlled comparisons."
- 要点：完美的对照比较从来做不到；历史几乎从不恰好提供对照比较所需的案例。

### E6

- 位置：第 194 页
- 原文："Moreover, macro-causal analysis remains a powerful tool for criticizing and invalidating mistaken theories."
- 要点：宏观因果分析仍是批评和否定错误理论的有力工具。

### E7

- 位置：第 195 页
- 原文："The point is that the method itself is no substitute for theory, and cannot in itself guarantee reliable and valid conclusions."
- 要点：方法本身不能代替理论，也不能单靠它保证结论可靠有效。

### E8

- 位置：第 195 页
- 原文："The answer is that, because they are largely inductively established, comparative-historical causal arguments cannot be readily generalized beyond the cases actually discussed."
- 要点：比较历史的因果论证主要靠归纳得出，不能轻易推广到文中实际讨论的案例之外。

### E9

- 位置：第 193 页
- 原文："But the Contrast-oriented approach can also be theoretically very misleading. For virtually any themes can be brought to bear upon the case materials without being put to any explicit test and without being openly identified as a proto-theory."
- 要点："对比式"比较历史在理论上可能很误导：几乎任何主题都能套到案例材料上，既不经明确检验，也不公开承认它其实是一种雏形理论。
