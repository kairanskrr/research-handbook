# Henderson 等人 2013：Threats to Validity in the Design and Conduct of Preclinical Efficacy Studies

- 资料：`henderson2013threats`，[原文](https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.1001489)
- 版本：PLoS Medicine 10(7): e1001489, 2013，开放获取，2026-10-06 读取。对 26 份临床前动物实验设计指南的系统综述。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract，Methods and Findings
- 原文："From these, we identified 26 guidelines that met our eligibility criteria—most of which were directed at neurological or cerebrovascular drug development. Together, these guidelines offered 55 different recommendations. Some of the most common recommendations included performance of a power calculation to determine sample size, randomized treatment allocation, and characterization of disease phenotype in the animal model prior to experimentation."
- 要点：纳入 26 份指南，大多针对神经和脑血管药物研发，共 55 条建议；最常见的包括：用功效分析定样本量、随机分配处理、实验前先描述清楚动物模型的疾病表型。

### E2

- 位置：Editors' Summary，Why Was This Study Done?
- 原文："Internal threats to validity are factors that confound reliable inferences about treatment–effect relationships in preclinical research." / "Construct threats to validity arise when researchers mischaracterize the relationship between an experimental system and the clinical disease it is intended to represent." / "External threats to validity are unseen factors that frustrate the transfer of treatment–effect relationships from animal models to patients."
- 要点：内部效度威胁：干扰"处理—效应"推断的因素；构念效度威胁：错误理解了实验系统和它要代表的临床疾病之间的关系；外部效度威胁：阻碍"处理—效应"关系从动物模型转移到病人身上的未知因素。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："For instance, a researcher might err in using only rotational behavior in animals to represent human parkinsonism—a condition with a complex clinical presentation including tremor and cognitive symptoms."
- 要点：例子：只用动物的旋转行为代表人类帕金森病是错误的，因为人类帕金森病的临床表现复杂，包括震颤和认知症状。

### E4

- 位置：Box 1. Construct Validity and Preclinical Research
- 原文："First, preclinical researchers might use treatments, animal models, or outcome assessments that are poorly matched to the clinical setting, as when preclinical studies use an acute disease model to represent a chronic disease in human beings."
- 要点：构念效度的一种威胁：处理、模型或结局评估和临床情形不匹配，例如用急性病模型代表人类的慢性病。

### E5

- 位置：Box 1
- 原文："For example, researchers intending to represent intravenous drug administration can introduce a threat to construct validity if, when performing tail vein administration in rats, they inadvertently administer a drug subcutaneously."
- 要点：另一种威胁是操作没做对，例如本想尾静脉给药模拟静脉注射，却不小心打到了皮下。

### E6

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Responses in an inbred mouse, for example, may be particular to the strain, thus limiting generalizability to other mouse models or patients." / "Researchers often address threats to external validity by replicating treatment effects in multiple model systems, or using multiple treatment formulations."
- 要点：近交系小鼠的反应可能只属于这个品系，难以推广到其他模型或病人；研究者常通过在多个模型系统中重复处理效应来应对外部效度威胁。

### E7

- 位置：Results，Threats to Internal Validity, Construct Validity, and External Validity
- 原文："Nine concerned matching the procedures used in preclinical studies—such as timing of drug delivery—to those planned for clinical studies."
- 要点：25 条构念效度建议中，有 9 条是让临床前研究的操作（如给药时机）和计划中的临床研究相匹配。

### E8

- 位置：Results，Threats to Internal Validity, Construct Validity, and External Validity
- 原文："The most common was the recommendation that researchers reproduce their treatment effects in more than one animal model type, followed closely by independent replication of experiments"
- 要点：外部效度方面最常见的建议是在不止一种动物模型中重现处理效应，其次是独立重复实验。

### E9

- 位置：Editors' Summary，What Did the Researchers Do and Find?
- 原文："The most commonly included recommendations of this type called for the use of power calculations to ensure that sample sizes are large enough to yield statistically meaningful results, random allocation of animals to treatment groups, and “blinding” of researchers who assess outcomes to treatment allocation. Among the 25 recommendations that addressed threats to construct validity, the most commonly included recommendations called for characterization of the properties of the animal model before experimentation and matching of the animal model to the human manifestation of the disease."
- 要点：内部效度方面最常见的建议：功效分析、随机分配、对结局评估者设盲；构念效度方面最常见的建议：实验前描述清楚动物模型的特性、让模型和人类疾病的表现相匹配。

### E10

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："Whether failure to implement the procedures described above explains the frequent discordance between preclinical effect sizes and those in clinical trials is unclear."
- 要点：没有落实这些做法，是否就能解释临床前和临床试验效应量常常不一致，目前还不清楚。

### E11

- 位置：Results，Guideline Characteristics
- 原文："Most were derived from workshop discussions, and only three described a clear methodology for their development."
- 要点：这些指南大多来自研讨会讨论，只有 3 份说明了清楚的制定方法。

### E12

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："Validity threats can be specific to disease domains, models, or intervention platforms. For instance, confounding of anesthetics with disease response presents a greater validity threat in cardiovascular preclinical studies than in cancer, since anesthetics can interact with cardiovascular function but rarely interfere with tumor growth."
- 要点：效度威胁可能因疾病领域、模型或干预类型而不同；例如麻醉药会影响心血管功能、很少干扰肿瘤生长，所以麻醉药的混杂在心血管研究中威胁更大。

### E13

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："First, they aim to demonstrate causal relationships between an investigational agent (treatment) and a disease-related phenotype or phenotype proxy (effect) in an animal model." / "For example, biased outcome assessment due to experimenter expectation can lead to spurious inferences about treatment response." / "The second aim of preclinical efficacy studies is to support generalization of treatment–effect relationships to human patients."
- 要点：临床前有效性研究有两个目标：一是在动物模型中证明受试药物（处理）和疾病相关表型（效应）之间的因果关系，例如研究者的预期会让结局评估产生偏倚、得出错误的推断；二是支持把这种"处理—效应"关系推广到病人身上。
