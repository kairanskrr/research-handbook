# Mokoena 等人 2026：What is the adsorption efficiency and capacity of biodegradable polymer blend foams filled with carbonaceous materials in the removal of heavy metals from water? A systematic review protocol

- 资料：`mokoena2026adsorption`，[原文（Europe PMC）](https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC13151128)，DOI [10.1186/s13750-026-00384-x](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13750-026-00384-x)
- 版本：Environmental Evidence 15:4，2026，开放获取（CC BY 4.0），作者单位在南非（University of the Free State）。2026-10-08 经 Europe PMC 读取全文。这是一份系统综述方案，只代表一个团队的做法。位置写小节标题。

### E1

- 位置：Methods
- 原文："The systematic review and the present protocol will be in alignment with the guidelines by the Collaboration for Environmental Evidence (CEE) [...] and the ROSES checklist"
- 要点：这份系统综述及其方案按环境证据协作组（CEE）的指南和 ROSES 清单来做。

### E2

- 位置：Methods，Searching for articles
- 原文："The review will search through and retrieve information from both grey and peer-reviewed sources, like journal articles, books, book chapters, conference papers and reports, that include laboratory and pilot scale investigations."
- 要点：检索灰色文献和同行评审文献，包括实验室规模和中试规模的研究。

### E3

- 位置：Study validity assessment
- 原文："This checklist will assess internal validity (e.g., study design and control of confounding factors), external validity (e.g., relevance and generalizability to other contexts), and reporting transparency (e.g., completeness and clarity of methods and results)."
- 要点：按 CEE 指南改编的清单评判每项纳入研究的内部效度（设计、控制混杂因素）、外部效度（能否推广到别的情境）和报告透明度。

### E4

- 位置：Study validity assessment，Risk of bias
- 原文："Studies should clearly account for potential confounding parameters, like the presence of competing ions and the complexity of water matrices."
- 要点：纳入研究应说明可能的干扰因素，例如水中有竞争离子、水体基质复杂。
