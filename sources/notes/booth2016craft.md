# Booth, Colomb & Williams 2016：The Craft of Research（第四版）

- 资料：`booth2016craft`，原书不公开。读的是两份注明改编自本书的大学页面：[Brandeis 大学写作项目讲义 Articulating Your Research Topic](https://www.brandeis.edu/writing-program/resources/faculty/handouts/articulating-research-topic.html)；[宾州州立大学约克校区图书馆 Spark Collection 页面 The Research Process](https://sites.psu.edu/spark/research-process/)
- 版本：Brandeis 讲义注明 "Credit: University Writing Center, 2020. Adapted from Wayne C. Booth et al., The Craft of Research, Fourth Edition (Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 2016), 44-46."；宾州州立大学页面注明模板 "adapted from The Craft of Research, 3rd edition"。两者都是改编，不是原书原文。整理内容引用时应写作"Brandeis 写作项目根据 Booth 等人改编"，不要写成原书的话。位置写页面小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Brandeis 讲义，开头段
- 原文："The process below can help you become more confident articulating your project by breaking it down into three smaller steps"
- 要点：讲义把说清楚研究课题拆成三个小步骤，帮初学者在还没想清楚时也能表述自己的项目。

### E2

- 位置：Brandeis 讲义，Step 1: Name Your Topic
- 原文："If you are beginning a project with only a topic and maybe the glimmerings of a good question or two, start by naming your project:" / "I am trying to learn about/working on/studying _______________."
- 要点：第一步，说出主题：用"我在研究/我在了解 ____"这个句式填上你的主题。

### E3

- 位置：Brandeis 讲义，Step 2: Add an Indirect Question
- 原文："Now add an indirect question that indicates what you do not know or understand about your topic:" / "because I want to find out who/what/when/where/whether/why/how _______________."
- 要点：第二步，加一个间接问句，说明你对这个主题还有什么不知道或不明白：用"因为我想弄清谁/什么/何时/何地/是否/为什么/如何 ____"。

### E4

- 位置：Brandeis 讲义，Step 3: Answer So What? by Motivating Your Question
- 原文："This step tells you whether your question might interest not just you but others. To do that, add a second indirect question that explains why you asked your first question." / "in order to help my reader better understand _______________."
- 要点：第三步回答"那又怎样"：再加一个间接问句，说明你为什么问第一个问题，用"以便帮助读者更好地理解 ____"；这一步检验问题是否不只对你自己有意思。

### E5

- 位置：Brandeis 讲义，Step 3 中的例子
- 原文："I am working on Lincoln's beliefs about predestination and their influence on his reasoning" / "because I want to find out how his belief in destiny and God's will influenced his understanding of the causes of the Civil War," / "in order to help my reader understand how his religious beliefs may have influenced his military decisions."
- 要点：例子：我在研究林肯的宿命论信念及其对他思考的影响，因为我想弄清他对天命的信念如何影响他对内战起因的理解，以便帮助读者理解他的宗教信念可能如何影响了他的军事决策。

### E6

- 位置：Brandeis 讲义，Step 3 之后
- 原文："Regularly test your progress by asking a roommate, relative or friend to force you to flesh out those three steps. Even if you can't take them all confidently, you'll know where you are and where you still have to go."
- 要点：定期请室友、亲戚或朋友逼你把三步说完整；即使还不能都说得有把握，也能知道自己走到了哪一步、还差什么。

### E7

- 位置：Brandeis 讲义，To summarize
- 原文："What you are writing about: "I am working on the topic of…"" / "What you don’t know about it: "because I want to find out…"" / "Why you want your reader to know and care about it: "in order to help my reader understand better…""
- 要点：总结：三步分别说明你写什么（主题）、你对它还不知道什么（问题）、为什么读者应该知道并关心（意义）。

### E8

- 位置：宾州州立大学页面，1 Name Your Topic
- 原文："Try to be specific enough so you can find, read, and synthesize the information in the time you have."
- 要点：主题要足够具体，让你能在现有时间内找到、读完并综合相关资料。

### E9

- 位置：宾州州立大学页面，2 Add an Indirect Question
- 原文："Find questions that you find interesting. You can do this by finding background information on your topic." / "What is its history? How does it fit within a larger context? How is it categorized? What are points of agreement or disagreement? Where is the research on this topic heading?"
- 要点：找问题的办法是先查主题的背景资料，并追问：它的历史是什么？放在更大背景中处于什么位置？怎样分类？哪些地方有共识、哪些有分歧？这方面研究正往哪里走？

### E10

- 位置：宾州州立大学页面，3 Motivate Your Question
- 原文："This part of the planning process argues why a reader should care about your research. And it’s the most challenging step to articulate. You may not have a conclusive answer until you’ve completed a first draft"
- 要点：说明意义（读者为什么要关心）是三步中最难表述的一步，可能要等写完初稿才有确定答案。
