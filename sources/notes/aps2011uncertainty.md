# Physical Review A 编辑部 2011：Editorial: Uncertainty Estimates

- 资料：`aps2011uncertainty`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.83.040001)
- 版本：Physical Review A 83, 040001，2011 年 4 月 29 日，署名 The Editors（美国物理学会 Physical Review A 编辑部）。一页社论，出版社网站免费阅读。只代表这一本期刊编辑部当时的要求。位置写段落。

### E1

- 位置：第 1–2 段
- 原文："The purpose of this Editorial is to discuss the importance of including uncertainty estimates in papers involving theoretical calculations of physical quantities." / "It is not unusual for manuscripts on theoretical work to be submitted without uncertainty estimates for numerical results."
- 要点：社论讨论理论计算论文为什么要给出不确定度估计；编辑部说，理论论文投来时数值结果不带不确定度估计并不少见。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："In contrast, papers presenting the results of laboratory measurements would usually not be considered acceptable for publication in Physical Review A without a detailed discussion of the uncertainties involved in the measurements."
- 要点：相比之下，报告实验测量的论文如果不详细讨论测量不确定度，Physical Review A 通常不会接收。

### E3

- 位置：第 3 段，理论论文的六类
- 原文："Theoretical papers can be broadly classified as follows:" / "1. Development of new theoretical techniques or formalisms. 2. Development of approximation methods, where the comparison with experiment, or other theory, itself provides an" / "assessment of the error in the method of calculation. 3. Explanation of previously unexplained phenomena, where a semiquantitative agreement with experiment is already" / "significant. 4. Proposals for new experimental arrangements or configurations, such as optical lattices. 5. Quantitative comparisons with experiment for the purpose of (a) verifying that all significant physical effects have been" / "taken into account, and/or (b) interpolating or extrapolating known experimental data. 6. Provision of benchmark results intended as reference data or standards of comparison with other less accurate methods."
- 要点：社论把理论论文大致分成六类：发展新的理论技巧或形式体系；发展近似方法（和实验或其他理论的比较本身就评估了误差）；解释此前没解释的现象（和实验半定量地符合就已有意义）；提出新的实验方案；和实验定量比较（检验重要物理效应是否都考虑到了，或内插、外推已知实验数据）；提供基准结果作为参考数据。

### E4

- 位置：第 4 段
- 原文："It is primarily papers in the last two categories that require a careful assessment of the theoretical uncertainties."
- 要点：主要是最后两类（和实验定量比较、提供基准结果）需要仔细评估理论不确定度。

### E5

- 位置：第 4 段
- 原文："The uncertainties can arise from two sources: (a) the degree to which the numerical results accurately represent the predictions of an underlying theoretical formalism, for example, convergence with the size of a basis set, or the step size in a numerical integration, and (b) physical effects not included in the calculation from the beginning, such as electron correlation and relativistic corrections."
- 要点：理论不确定度有两个来源：数值结果在多大程度上准确代表理论本身的预言（例如随基组大小或积分步长是否收敛）；以及计算一开始就没包含的物理效应（例如电子关联、相对论修正）。

### E6

- 位置：第 4 段
- 原文："The aim is to estimate the uncertainty, not to state the exact amount of the error or provide a rigorous bound."
- 要点：目标是估计不确定度，不是给出误差的确切大小或严格上界。

### E7

- 位置：第 5 段
- 原文："Papers presenting the results of theoretical calculations are expected to include uncertainty estimates for the calculations whenever practicable, and especially under the following circumstances:" / "1. If the authors claim high accuracy, or improvements on the accuracy of previous work. 2. If the primary motivation for the paper is to make comparisons with present or future high precision experimental" / "measurements. 3. If the primary motivation is to provide interpolations or extrapolations of known experimental measurements."
- 要点：报告理论计算结果的论文，只要可行就应给出不确定度估计；尤其是声称高精度或比以前更准、主要目的是和现在或将来的高精度实验比较、主要目的是内插或外推已知实验数据这三种情况。

### E8

- 位置：第 5 段
- 原文："There are many cases where it is indeed not practical to give a meaningful error estimate for a theoretical calculation; for example, in scattering processes involving complex systems."
- 要点：很多情况下确实无法给理论计算一个有意义的误差估计，例如复杂体系的散射过程。
