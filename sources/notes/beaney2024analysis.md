# Beaney & Raysmith 2024：Analysis（斯坦福哲学百科全书）

- 资料：`beaney2024analysis`，[原文](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/analysis/)
- 版本：《斯坦福哲学百科全书》（Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy，免费在线）条目，作者 Michael Beaney、Thomas Raysmith；2003 年 4 月 7 日首发，2024 年 7 月 18 日实质修订。位置写节号。本文件是第 106 号任务的本地摘录。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Analysis has always been at the heart of philosophical method, but it has been understood and practised in many different ways."
- 要点："分析"一直是哲学方法的核心，但理解和做法多种多样。

### E2

- 位置：§2 Ancient Conceptions of Analysis
- 原文："the paradox of analysis, concerning how an analysis can be both correct and informative"
- 要点："分析悖论"：一个分析怎样既正确、又能提供新信息。

### E3

- 位置：§6 Conceptions of Analysis in Analytic Philosophy
- 原文："It might also be seen as reflected in the approach to the analysis of concepts that seeks to specify the necessary and sufficient conditions for their correct employment."
- 要点：一种概念分析的做法是给出正确使用一个概念的充分必要条件。

### E4

- 位置：§6 Conceptions of Analysis in Analytic Philosophy
- 原文："the definition of 'knowledge' as 'justified true belief' is perhaps the most famous example"
- 要点：最著名的例子是把"知识"定义为"得到辩护的真信念"。

### E5

- 位置：§6 Conceptions of Analysis in Analytic Philosophy
- 原文："The specification of necessary and sufficient conditions may no longer be seen as the primary aim of conceptual analysis, especially in the case of philosophical concepts such as 'knowledge', which are fiercely contested; but consideration of such conditions remains a useful tool in the analytic philosopher's toolbag."
- 要点：给出充分必要条件可能不再被视为概念分析的首要目标，尤其是"知识"这类争议激烈的概念；但考虑这类条件仍是有用的工具。
