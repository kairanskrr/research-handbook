# 国际传播学会：第 76 届年会（ICA26）投稿指南与投稿类型

- 资料：`ica2026conference`，[投稿指南](https://www.icahdq.org/page/conf-submission-guidelines)、[投稿类型](https://www.icahdq.org/page/conf-submission-types)
- 版本：国际传播学会（ICA）第 76 届年会（2026 年 6 月 4–8 日，南非开普敦）的两个网页。官网对脚本返回人机验证页，读的是 Internet Archive 快照：投稿指南 2025-10-02（web.archive.org/web/20251002022040/...），投稿类型 2025-09-05（web.archive.org/web/20250905202618/...）。投稿类型页的部分文字仍沿用上一届（如提到 ICA25）。src 文件把两页正文拼在一起，用方括号标出页名。位置写页名和小标题。

### E1

- 位置：投稿指南，Exclusive Submissions
- 原文："Each unique paper/proposal may be submitted to only one Division or Interest Group, OR to the theme sessions."
- 要点：每篇论文只能投给一个分会或兴趣小组，或者投主题会场，不能一稿多投。

### E2

- 位置：投稿指南，Exclusive Submissions
- 原文："Each *submitter* (whomever is entering the individual submission in the system) is limited to three (3) first author peer-reviewed submissions."
- 要点：每位提交人最多提交三篇以第一作者身份送审的投稿。

### E3

- 位置：投稿指南，Ethical Considerations
- 原文："If material in your presentation has been published, presented, or accepted for publication or presentation, this must be disclosed in your paper or proposal and may make your submission ineligible, depending on the Division/Interest Group."
- 要点：内容已发表、已报告或已被接收的，必须在稿件中说明；按各分会规定，可能因此不能参评。

### E4

- 位置：投稿类型，Full Paper
- 原文："Full Paper 8,000 words maximum, not including tables and references, unless Division or Interest Group guidelines state otherwise."
- 要点：全文投稿最多 8000 词，不含表格和参考文献，分会另有规定的除外。

### E5

- 位置：投稿类型，Preregistration
- 原文："Preregistration The design and analysis plan for the reported research is preregistered in a public, open-access repository."
- 要点：投稿类型里有"预注册"一类：研究的设计和分析计划已在公开的开放存档库中预注册。

### E6

- 位置：投稿类型，Works in Progress
- 原文："Works in progress reflect ongoing research that has not yet reached full completion stage but has a structure and enough substance (an argument, methods, a reference list, preliminary results, or preliminary conclusions) for review."
- 要点：可以投"进行中的研究"：尚未完成，但有论点、方法、参考文献、初步结果或初步结论，足以送审。

### E7

- 位置：投稿指南，Ethical Considerations
- 原文："If your submission or proposal is accepted for an ICA conference, you have a commitment to register for and attend the conference and perform your assigned role."
- 要点：投稿被接收后，作者有义务注册、参会并完成分配给自己的任务。

### E8

- 位置：投稿指南，Ethical Considerations
- 原文："Covert fragmented publication (salami strategy) – Occurs when a complex study is broken down into two or more components and each component is analyzed and published as a separate paper."
- 要点：把一项复杂研究拆成几块、各自当作独立论文发表而不说明（"切香肠"），属于隐蔽的自我抄袭。

### E9

- 位置：投稿指南，General Guidelines
- 原文："The main ICA rules are the standards, and then Divisions and Interest Groups can be more strict, so please reach each Div/IG's calls carefully."
- 要点：ICA 总规则是底线，各分会和兴趣小组可以更严格，要仔细读各自的征稿启事。

### E10

- 位置：投稿指南，Deadline 与 Conference attendance
- 原文："All submissions and proposals must be completed online no later than 3 November @ 12:00 noon ICA headquarters time (EDT)." [...] "If your paper or proposal is accepted for presentation at the annual conference, you will be notified in mid-January"
- 要点：ICA26 的投稿截止日是 11 月 3 日中午（美国东部夏令时），录用结果在次年 1 月中旬通知。

### E11

- 位置：投稿类型，Interdivisional
- 原文："All submissions will be reviewed by members of a committee comprising reviewers from all 33 divisions and interest groups."
- 要点：跨分会投稿由来自全部 33 个分会和兴趣小组的审稿人组成的委员会审稿（可见 ICA 共有 33 个分会和兴趣小组）。

### E12

- 位置：投稿类型，Research Escalator Extended Abstract
- 原文："The Research Escalator session is a mentorship opportunity for early career scholars, those new to ICA, or scholars with a work-in-progress to receive feedback and support on a project that is being developed"
- 要点："研究扶梯"会场是给青年学者、初次参加 ICA 的人或有在研项目的人获得反馈和指导的机会。
