# McInnes 等 2018：PRISMA-DTA 声明（JAMA）

- 资料：`mcinnes2018preferred`，[原文（JAMA 出版社页面）](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2670259)；出版社页面对脚本返回 403，本摘录读的是 Internet Archive 2025-01-02 的存档：https://web.archive.org/web/20250102084459/https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2670259
- 版本：McInnes MDF、Moher D、Thombs BD 等与 PRISMA-DTA Group，"Preferred Reporting Items for a Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of Diagnostic Test Accuracy Studies: The PRISMA-DTA Statement"，JAMA 2018;319(4):388–396，DOI 10.1001/jama.2017.19163。出版社在页面上把本文标为免费阅读（contentStatus "Free"）。作者主要来自加拿大渥太华。位置写节名。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Although systematic reviews of diagnostic test accuracy studies share elements with those of intervention studies, there are important differences. Study design and measures of effect differ from those of randomized clinical trials. Accuracy can differ between studies due to differences in patients, setting, prior testing, and use of different reference standards. Consequently, the methods for evaluating risk of bias, summarizing results, and exploring variability for diagnostic test accuracy studies differ from those used for intervention studies."
- 要点：诊断准确性综述和干预综述有重要不同：研究设计和效应指标都和随机对照试验不同；准确性会因病人、场合、先前检查和参考标准不同而在研究间变化；因此评价偏倚风险、汇总结果、探讨变异的方法都不同。

### E2

- 位置：Results（新增条目 D2 的说明）
- 原文："Meta-analyses of diagnostic test accuracy studies typically require multivariate models (eg, bivariate and hierarchical summary receiver operating characteristic), which allow for the tradeoff between sensitivity and specificity due to the positivity threshold, for potential correlation between estimates of sensitivity and specificity across studies, and for variability through the inclusion of random effects." / "Traditional univariate methods ignore this correlation and can give misleading results."
- 要点：诊断准确性元分析通常要用多变量模型（如双变量模型），它能处理敏感度和特异度随判阳性界值此消彼长、两者在研究间可能相关、以及研究间的变异；传统单变量方法忽略这种相关，可能给出误导性结果。
