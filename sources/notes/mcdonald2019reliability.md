# McDonald, Schoenebeck & Forte 2019：Reliability and Inter-rater Reliability in Qualitative Research

- 资料：`mcdonald2019reliability`，[作者主页版本](https://yardi.people.si.umich.edu/pubs/Schoenebeck_Reliability_CSCW19.pdf)
- 版本：Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction 3 (CSCW), 1–23, 2019，DOI 10.1145/3359174；本次读取的是作者主页上的版本（页眉排版为预印本格式）。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction；第二、三段引文出自 §2.2
- 原文："IRR is a statistical measure of agreement between two or more coders of data. IRR can be confusing because it merges a quantitative method, which has roots in positivism and objective discovery, with qualitative methods that favor an interpretivist view of knowledge." / "Common statistical calculations used in IRR include Cohen's kappa" / "Krippendorff's alpha"
- 要点：编码者间信度（IRR）是衡量两名以上编码者一致程度的统计量，常用 Cohen's Kappa、Krippendorff's Alpha 等；它把源于实证主义的定量方法和偏向诠释主义的定性方法混在一起，所以容易引起困惑。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："notably, IRR is rare, occurring in around 1/9 of qualitative papers."
- 要点：在 2016–2018 年 CSCW 和 CHI 的定性研究论文中，只有约九分之一计算了编码者间信度。

### E3

- 位置：§4.3 Approaches to Reliability by Data Collection Method
- 原文："Of the 251 papers, 179 (71.3%) described conducting interviews"
- 要点：在作者分析的 251 篇定性论文中，71.3% 用了访谈。

### E4

- 位置：§2.2 Reliability in Qualitative Research
- 原文："there is general consensus that simply calculating percent agreement between coders is not acceptable because it does not account for the possibility of agreement by chance."
- 要点：普遍认为只算编码者之间的百分比一致率不可接受，因为它没考虑碰巧一致的可能。

### E5

- 位置：§2.2 Reliability in Qualitative Research
- 原文："other techniques for communicating reliability include member checking (e.g., confirming/reviewing results with participants or other members of the community), re-interviewing participants, triangulation of data with secondary data sources (e.g., interviews plus field notes, photographs, etc.), making research process transparent" [...] "and communicating positionality of the researchers."
- 要点：除编码者间信度外，体现可靠性的做法还有：参与者核查、再次访谈、与其他数据来源互相印证、公开研究过程、说明研究者的立场。

### E6

- 位置：§2.2 Reliability in Qualitative Research
- 原文："Many qualitative researchers reject validity and reliability altogether in favor of concepts like dependability, confirmability, credibility, and transferability that resonate with interpretivist accounts"
- 要点：许多定性研究者完全不用效度和信度的说法，而改用可依赖性、可确认性、可信度、可迁移性等符合诠释主义的概念。

### E7

- 位置：§2.3 Critiques and Limitations of Reliability
- 原文："quantitative researchers have sometimes made "the mistake of evaluating qualitative research reports using the standards of quantitative research," expecting IRR regardless of the nature of the qualitative research"
- 要点：定量研究者有时会犯"用定量标准评价定性研究"的错误，不管研究性质如何都要求编码者间信度。

### E8

- 位置：§2.3 Critiques and Limitations of Reliability
- 原文："Although statistical measures can help confirm that interpretations are consistent between coders, they are not a substitute for interpretation and making meaning from the data."
- 要点：统计量能帮助确认编码者之间解读一致，但不能代替对数据的解读。

### E9

- 位置：§2.3 Critiques and Limitations of Reliability
- 原文："we argue that approaches to establishing and measuring reliability should be aligned with epistemological traditions the researcher draws on."
- 要点：怎样建立和衡量可靠性，应当与研究者所依据的认识论传统一致。

### E10

- 位置：§1 Introduction（贡献概述）
- 原文："Pragmatic examples include when developing codes is part of the process, when there is a single researcher, when researchers are embedded in the research context, where analysis is driven by participants' own interpretations of their data, or when coding requires little interpretation."
- 要点：不适合追求一致性或计算信度的情形：编码本身是分析过程的一部分、只有一位研究者、研究者身处研究情境之中、分析由参与者自己的解读驱动、编码几乎不需要解读。

### E11

- 位置：§1 Introduction（贡献概述）
- 原文："The use of agreement or IRR should be consistent with methodological choices and analytical goals, which might include ensuring consistency across multiple coders, for applying existing codebooks, and when researchers aim to report quantitative results."
- 要点：适合追求一致性或计算信度的情形：要保证多名编码者一致、套用已有编码手册、要报告定量结果。

### E12

- 位置：§5.3.4 Describe results quantitatively
- 原文："If findings hinge on frequency counts, there is a strong argument that IRR should be used."
- 要点：如果发现依赖频次统计，就有充分理由计算编码者间信度。

### E13

- 位置：§5.1.2 Expert researcher
- 原文："Many techniques such as memoing, reflection, and review are designed to support the single researcher process without requiring agreement"
- 要点：写分析备忘、反思、复查等做法，就是为单人研究设计的，不需要多人一致。

### E14

- 位置：§5.2.3 Identifying points of disagreement
- 原文："Disagreement can also prompt more reflection that ultimately results in a stronger codebook and coding process."
- 要点：编码者之间的分歧能促使反思，最终得到更好的编码手册和编码过程。

### E15

- 位置：§5.3.6 Confirmation Bias
- 原文："Using multiple coders and agreement processes can help reduce individual biases of a particular researcher."
- 要点：多人编码并寻求一致，有助于减少个别研究者的偏见。

### E16

- 位置：§5.4.2 Passive voice
- 原文："passive voice can obfuscate the role of researchers in performing the analysis."
- 要点：方法部分用被动语态，可能掩盖研究者在分析中的作用。

### E17

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Drawing on a meta-analysis of a representative sample of CSCW and HCI publications from 2016-2018" / "notably, IRR is rare, occurring in around 1/9 of qualitative papers."
- 要点：作者分析了 2016–2018 年 CSCW 和人机交互发表物的代表性样本；其中定性论文只有约九分之一计算了编码者间信度。
