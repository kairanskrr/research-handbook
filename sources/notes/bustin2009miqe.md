# Bustin 等人 2009：The MIQE Guidelines: Minimum Information for Publication of Quantitative Real-Time PCR Experiments

- 资料：`bustin2009miqe`，[原文网页](https://academic.oup.com/clinchem/article/55/4/611/5631762)，DOI 10.1373/clinchem.2008.112797
- 版本：Clinical Chemistry 55(4):611–622，2009 年。Europe PMC 标注出版社网站全文"出版 12 个月后免费"。出版社网站对脚本返回 403，通过 Internet Archive 2019 年 12 月对期刊旧网址（clinchem.aaccjnls.org/content/55/4/611）的快照读取全文。2025 年已有修订版 MIQE 2.0（见 `bustin2025miqe`）。位置写章节号。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract, Background
- 原文："Currently, a lack of consensus exists on how best to perform and interpret quantitative real-time PCR (qPCR) experiments. The problem is exacerbated by a lack of sufficient experimental detail in many publications, which impedes a reader’s ability to evaluate critically the quality of the results presented or to repeat the experiments."
- 要点：怎样做和解读实时荧光定量 PCR 实验缺乏共识；很多论文实验细节不够，读者没法评价结果质量，也没法重复实验。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract, Content
- 原文："MIQE is a set of guidelines that describe the minimum information necessary for evaluating qPCR experiments. Included is a checklist to accompany the initial submission of a manuscript to the publisher."
- 要点：MIQE 规定了评价定量 PCR 实验所需的最少信息，附有一份投稿时随稿提交的清单。

### E3

- 位置：Abstract, Content
- 原文："Full disclosure of all reagents, sequences, and analysis methods is necessary to enable other investigators to reproduce results."
- 要点：要让别人能重复结果，必须完整公开全部试剂、序列和分析方法。

### E4

- 位置：引言
- 原文："Specifically, information about sample acquisition and handling, RNA quality and integrity, reverse-transcription details, PCR efficiencies, and analysis parameters are frequently omitted, whereas sample normalization is habitually carried out against single reference genes without adequate justification."
- 要点：论文常常漏掉样品获取和处理、RNA 质量和完整性、反转录细节、PCR 扩增效率和分析参数；归一化习惯性地只用一个内参基因，又没有充分理由。

### E5

- 位置：8.1 Normalization
- 原文："Normalization against a single reference gene is not acceptable unless the investigators present clear evidence for the reviewers that confirms its invariant expression under the experimental conditions described. The optimal number and choice of reference genes must be experimentally determined and the method reported"
- 要点：只用一个内参基因归一化是不可接受的，除非给出清楚证据证明它在该实验条件下表达不变；内参基因用几个、用哪个，要通过实验确定并报告方法。

### E6

- 位置：7.3 Controls and quantification calibrators
- 原文："NTCs should be included on each plate or batch of samples, and conditions for data rejection be established."
- 要点：每块板或每批样品都要设无模板对照，并事先定好剔除数据的条件。

### E7

- 位置：2.1 Analytical sensitivity
- 原文："Experimental results less than the theoretically possible LOD should never be reported. It also follows that results of “0” are meaningless and misleading."
- 要点：低于理论检出限的结果绝不应报告；报告"0"没有意义而且误导。

### E8

- 位置：Conclusions（Table 1 说明）
- 原文："Items deemed essential (E) are required to allow reviewers to evaluate the work and other investigators to reproduce it."
- 要点：清单里标为"必需"的条目，是审稿人评价工作、别人重复工作所必需的。

### E9

- 位置：2. Conceptual Considerations
- 原文："It is also necessary to be aware that most quantitative RNA data are not absolute, but relative."
- 要点：大多数 RNA 定量数据是相对的，不是绝对的。

### E10

- 位置：2. Conceptual Considerations
- 原文："The reporting of results for nonvalidated commercial assays and assays that have been validated only in silico are strongly discouraged."
- 要点：强烈不建议报告用未经验证的商品化检测、或只在计算机上验证过的检测得到的结果。
