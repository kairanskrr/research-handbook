# 世界卫生组织 2026：One Health（情况说明书）

- 资料：`who2026onehealth`，[原文](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/one-health)
- 版本：世界卫生组织（WHO）情况说明书，页面日期 2026 年 5 月 8 日，2026-10-06 读取。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Key facts
- 原文："One Health is an approach to optimize the health of humans, animals, plants and ecosystems by integrating these fields, rather than keeping them separate."
- 要点："同一健康"（One Health）是把人、动物、植物和生态系统的健康放在一起考虑、而不是分开处理的方法。

### E2

- 位置：Key facts
- 原文："More than 60% of emerging infectious diseases that are reported globally come from animals, both wild and domestic."
- 要点：全球报告的新发传染病中，60% 以上来自动物（野生和家养）。

### E3

- 位置：Overview（相关领域列表）
- 原文："zoonotic diseases, which are infectious diseases that are caused by germs that spread between animals and people, such as Ebola, avian influenza and rabies"
- 要点：人畜共患病是由在动物和人之间传播的病原引起的传染病，如埃博拉、禽流感、狂犬病。
