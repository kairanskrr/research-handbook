# Hutchinson 2015：The Doctrinal Method: Incorporating Interdisciplinary Methods in Reforming the Law

- 资料：`hutchinson2015doctrinal`，[作者版本 PDF（昆士兰科技大学机构库 QUT ePrints）](https://eprints.qut.edu.au/93564/3/93564.pdf)；正式版 DOI：10.5553/ELR.000055
- 版本：Erasmus Law Review 2015 年第 3 期，第 130–138 页。作者 Terry Hutchinson 当时在**澳大利亚**昆士兰科技大学法学院任教。文章考察澳大利亚的法学博士论文、2013 年澳大利亚刑法改革期刊文章和法律改革委员会报告。读到的是机构库里的作者版本，页码与期刊正式版不同，位置写小节号。

### E1

- 位置：§2 Clarifying the Basics: What is doctrinal research?
- 原文："a critical conceptual analysis of all relevant legislation and caselaw to reveal a statement of the law relevant to the matter under investigation"
- 要点：规范研究的基本特征：对所有相关立法和判例做批判性的概念分析，得出与所研究问题相关的法律陈述。

### E2

- 位置：§2
- 原文："This `conceptual analysis critique' is based on an understanding of the rules of precedent between the court jurisdictions, the rules of statutory interpretation, the tacit discipline knowledges such as the difference between civil and criminal jurisdictions, and various tests of liability, along with the acknowledged reasoning methods, borrowed from philosophy and logic, such as induction and deduction."
- 要点：这种分析建立在几类知识上：各级法院之间的先例规则、制定法解释规则、学科中的默会知识（如民事和刑事管辖的区别、各种责任认定标准），以及归纳、演绎等推理方法。

### E3

- 位置：§2
- 原文："Whether the doctrinal method can ever be stated in a formulaic way is problematic. At its heart it is fluid. It is difficult to reduce to an algorithm."
- 要点：规范方法能否写成固定公式是有疑问的；它本质上是流动的，很难化约成一套算法。

### E4

- 位置：§3.1 及脚注 33
- 原文："A basic search string resulted in a retrieved list displaying 60 items. When book reviews, speeches, and government publications were omitted, then only 32 refereed journal articles remained as a relevant subset of the database." / "These were located on the AustLII database using the search term `crim* w/10 (reform* OR recommend*)' within the Australian journal titles published in 2013. The search was conducted on 16th February 2015."
- 要点：作者报告了检索的数据库（澳大利亚法律信息研究所的 AustLII）、检索式、范围（2013 年澳大利亚期刊）、检索日期（2015 年 2 月 16 日），以及筛选过程：60 条结果，去掉书评、演讲和政府出版物后剩 32 篇同行评审文章。

### E5

- 位置：§3.1
- 原文："The doctrinal methodology design was tacit. Not so any nondoctrinal methods. Surveys, for example, were outlined and explained using appropriate tables."
- 要点：在这 32 篇文章里，规范方法的设计都是隐含的；非规范方法（如问卷调查）则有说明，还配了表格。

### E6

- 位置：§3.1
- 原文："While only two of the articles were reporting that the authors had themselves undertaken surveys, interviews or statistical projects,34 18 used statistics published elsewhere in discussing the basis of concerns."
- 要点：32 篇中只有 2 篇报告作者自己做了调查、访谈或统计项目；18 篇使用了别处发表的统计数据来说明问题的依据。

### E7

- 位置：§3.1
- 原文："All the articles analysed pertinent secondary literature with only two including an explicit acknowledgement of the literature review." / "Relevant texts, journal articles and law reform publications are referenced where applicable to the discussion rather than brought together under a formal literature review heading."
- 要点：所有文章都分析了相关二手文献，但只有 2 篇明确提到文献综述；相关文献是在讨论中随处引用，而不是集中放在"文献综述"标题下。

### E8

- 位置：§3.1
- 原文："Only two of the articles had as their main objective a comparison of the law between jurisdictions. However, 23 of the articles include a comparative review of the existing law particularly for the Australian state jurisdictions as well as pertinent examples from international jurisdictions."
- 要点：只有 2 篇以法域间比较为主要目标，但 23 篇包含对现行法的比较性回顾（主要是澳大利亚各州，也有外国的相关例子）。

### E9

- 位置：§3.1
- 原文："The current expectation in the literature is that there will be some statement of the legal jurisdictional status quo or an acknowledgement of obvious discrepancies in practice elsewhere."
- 要点：目前文献中的预期是：要交代各法域的现状，或至少承认其他地方做法上的明显差异。

### E10

- 位置：§3.1
- 原文："Many of the articles (23) include recommendations for reforming the law informed by the evidence presented." / "Five of the articles simply critiqued the existing laws."
- 要点：23 篇文章根据所呈现的证据提出了法律改革建议；5 篇只是批评现行法律。

### E11

- 位置：§3.1
- 原文："Studies of reform in other areas of law such as tort law or corporate law or equity might well be more legalistic in approach."
- 要点：作者承认的局限：只看了刑法改革；侵权法、公司法、衡平法等领域的改革研究可能更偏法条分析。

### E12

- 位置：§3.2 Interdisciplinarity and the Work of the Law Reform Commissions（引 Barnett）
- 原文："`they need to see the entire picture and identify the real problem(s) before launching into a search for policy solutions'"
- 要点：做法律改革时，要先看清全貌、找到真正的问题，再去寻找政策方案。

### E13

- 位置：§3.1
- 原文："The Arthurs Report had recognised that comparative and historical research involving legal rules was likely to lead to `new' solutions."
- 要点：加拿大 Arthurs 报告认为，对法律规则做比较和历史研究，可能带来"新"的解决办法。

### E14

- 位置：§3.1
- 原文："All the articles used a doctrinal research methodology to some extent."
- 要点：被考察的 32 篇文章都在一定程度上使用了规范研究方法。
