# Kyveryga 2019：On-Farm Research: Experimental Approaches, Analytical Frameworks, Case Studies, and Impact

- 资料：`kyveryga2019onfarm`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.2134/agronj2019.11.0001)
- 版本：Agronomy Journal 111(6)，2019，农场试验专刊的导言，CC BY-NC-ND 开放获取。原文文本取自作者单位艾奥瓦大豆协会网站存放的 PDF。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Precision Agriculture Technology Tools and Farmer Motivation through On-Farm Research
- 原文："On-farm research has expanded rapidly during the last two decades, especially in developed countries. This increase is attributed to an increase in the wider use of precision agriculture (PA) technologies."
- 要点：过去二十年，农场试验在发达国家扩展很快，作者把原因归于精准农业技术的普及。

### E2

- 位置：Precision Agriculture Technology Tools and Farmer Motivation through On-Farm Research
- 原文："Technologies in the PA tool box such as yield monitoring, Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS), light bars, auto guidance, and variable rate devices enable farmers and researchers to conduct on-farm experiments at a field scale, in multiple locations and at much lower cost."
- 要点：产量监测、卫星导航、自动驾驶、变量作业等设备，让农民和研究者能以整块田的尺度、在多个地点、以低得多的成本做农场试验。

### E3

- 位置：Precision Agriculture Technology Tools and Farmer Motivation through On-Farm Research
- 原文："In-depth interviews of 40 participants of the Nebraska On-Farm Research Network and qualitive summaries of the survey data revealed that farmers conducted on-farm trials mainly because of the potential to increase profit, general curiosity and the need for unbiased results."
- 要点：专刊中一项美国内布拉斯加州的研究，深度访谈了当地农场试验网络的 40 名参与者：农民做农场试验，主要是为了增加收益、出于好奇，以及想要不偏不倚的结果。

### E4

- 位置：Precision Agriculture Technology Tools and Farmer Motivation through On-Farm Research
- 原文："farmers expressed the desire to be more involved in all phases of conducting an on-farm research project."
- 要点：同一项研究中，农民表示希望更多地参与农场试验的各个阶段。

### E5

- 位置：Analytical Approaches for On-Farm Trials
- 原文："One of the critical components of on-farm research is to effectively analyze and summarize study outcomes and communicate results to farmers and agronomists."
- 要点：农场试验的关键之一，是把结果分析好、汇总好，并讲给农民和农艺师听。
