# World Health Organization：Trial registration（国际临床试验注册平台网页）

- 资料：`whotrialregistration`，[原文](https://www.who.int/tools/clinical-trials-registry-platform/network/trial-registration)
- 版本：世界卫生组织（World Health Organization，WHO）国际临床试验注册平台（International Clinical Trials Registry Platform，ICTRP）网页，2026 年 10 月访问。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：How to register a trial
- 原文："You cannot register a trial with WHO. The WHO ICTRP is not a clinical trials registry." / "To register a trial, submit the details directly to any one of the Primary Registries in the WHO Registry Network"
- 要点：WHO 本身不是登记库，不能在 WHO 登记；要直接向 WHO 登记网络中的任一一级登记库提交。

### E2

- 位置：How to register a trial
- 原文："it is only necessary for your trial to be registered once, in either a Primary Registry in the WHO Registry Network or an ICMJE approved registry."
- 要点：满足 WHO 的透明和发表要求，只需在一个 WHO 一级登记库或 ICMJE 认可的登记库登记一次。

### E3

- 位置：How to register a trial，NOTE
- 原文："Regulatory, legal, ethical, funding and other requirements for oversight and conduct of clinical trials differ from country to country."
- 要点：各国对临床试验的监管、法律、伦理、资助等要求不同。
