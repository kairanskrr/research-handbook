# Alvesson & Sandberg 2020：The Problematizing Review: A Counterpoint to Elsbach and Van Knippenberg's Argument for Integrative Reviews

- 资料：`alvesson2020problematizing`，[City St George's 大学开放仓库（City Research Online）作者接受稿 PDF](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/34063/1/JMS.review.article.Alvesson%26%20Sandberg.pdf)（[仓库记录页](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/34063/)，[出版社页面](https://doi.org/10.1111/joms.12582)）
- 版本：Journal of Management Studies 57(6)，2020，第 1290–1304 页。读的是作者所在机构仓库公开的作者接受稿（Accepted Version），仓库声明可用于个人研究、学习和非营利用途。接受稿没有期刊页码，位置写小标题。

> 为什么用这篇：作为 `alvesson2013has` 的补充。它把问题化的六步原则写得更细（每一步说明了要做什么），给出了一个领导力研究的例子，并且作者自己列出了问题化方法可能受到的批评。

### E1

- 位置：AN ALTERNATIVE: THE PROBLEMATIZING REVIEW
- 原文："the primary aim of the problematizing review is to re-evaluate existing understandings of phenomena, with a particular view to challenging and reimagining our current ways of thinking about them"
- 要点：问题化式综述的主要目的，是重新评估对现象的已有理解，挑战并重新设想我们目前的思考方式。

### E2

- 位置：The ideal of reflexivity
- 原文："In a problematizing review, the author is guided by a constant consideration and occasional questioning of assumptions, perspectives and vocabularies in order to come up with, test and possibly suggest alternative ideas and ways of thinking about a phenomenon or domain."
- 要点：问题化式综述的作者要不断留意、并不时质疑已有的假设、视角和用语，以便提出、检验并可能提出看待现象的另一种思路。

### E3

- 位置：The ideal of reflexivity
- 原文："For example, the overwhelming majority of the leadership literature proceeds from `the assumption that the employees sampled innately need or desire leadership' (Hunter et al., 2007, p. 436). However, one could see this need, desire or interest in leadership among employees as a false assumption, or as a more open issue, and even suggest that some people do not see themselves as followers and want to be spared leadership at work"
- 要点：例子：绝大多数领导力研究默认员工天生需要或想要领导；问题化可以把这当作错误假设或开放问题，甚至提出有些人并不把自己看作追随者，希望在工作中不被领导。

### E4

- 位置：Not accumulating but problematizing
- 原文："Rather than undertaking a surface reading of a large volume of available studies, a deep reading of the foundational texts, and of a moderate number of representative texts of a field, enables the author to better identify, articulate and challenge problematic, taken-for-granted assumptions in a specific domain"
- 要点：与其浅读大量研究，不如深读一个领域的奠基文献和适量代表性文献，这样更容易找出、写明并挑战那些被视为理所当然的假设。

### E5

- 位置：Not accumulating but problematizing
- 原文："(1) identifying a domain of literature; (2) identifying and articulating assumptions underlying this domain; (3) evaluating them and focusing on more problematic or limiting elements; (4) developing an alternative assumption ground with the potential to become the start of a novel theoretical contribution; (5) considering it in relation to its audience (what is seen as new, credible and interesting); and (6) evaluating the alternative assumption ground"
- 要点：六步的细化版：第 3 步要重点关注较有问题或有局限的假设；第 4 步提出的替代假设应有可能成为新理论贡献的起点；第 5 步要考虑读者会觉得什么是新的、可信的、有趣的。

### E6

- 位置：Not accumulating but problematizing
- 原文："The last of these is important in order to assess the possibility of having a new, counterintuitive conversation with others in the field that will inspire new ways of thinking on a subject"
- 要点：最后一步（评估替代假设）很重要，用来判断能否和同行展开一场新的、反直觉的讨论，激发对这个主题的新思考。

### E7

- 位置：Not accumulating but problematizing
- 原文："Identifying or constructing a domain of literature provides a way in to picking some texts, but careful reading of these may inspire a revision of the literature domain that will be the final target of the research question."
- 要点：先确定文献领域只是挑选文本的入口，细读之后可能会修改领域范围，最终研究问题针对的领域可能与起初不同。

### E8

- 位置：FINAL REMARKS
- 原文："the problematizing review we have proposed may be criticized for several disadvantages and problems, such as for underutilizing a whole set of studies in a knowledge area targeted for a review publication; for focusing on underlying assumptions that are hard to detect; for allowing too much discretion to the researcher doing the review work; and for being too demanding."
- 要点：作者承认的局限：可能没有充分利用一个领域的大量研究；关注的假设难以发现；给研究者太多自由裁量；要求太高。

### E9

- 位置：FINAL REMARKS
- 原文："We also acknowledge that there may be a problem if more energy goes into challenging assumptions than into working out and refining or testing well-founded and productive ideas" / "The pursuit of novelty and everyone wanting to develop their own theory is not unproblematic."
- 要点：如果花在挑战假设上的精力多于完善和检验已有的可靠想法，也会有问题；人人追求新意、都想提出自己的理论，并非没有弊端。

### E10

- 位置：Less is more（该节结尾）
- 原文："The idea with the problematizing review is, of course, not to dismiss everything that has been done or to problematize for its own sake. It is also important to highlight high-quality studies and ideas."
- 要点：问题化不是否定前人的一切，也不是为质疑而质疑；同样要指出高质量的研究和想法。
