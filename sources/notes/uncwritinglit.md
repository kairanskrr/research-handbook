# 北卡罗来纳大学教堂山分校写作中心：Literature (Fiction)

- 资料：`uncwritinglit`，[原文](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/literature/)
- 版本：写作中心免费讲义（CC BY-NC-ND 4.0）；2026 年 10 月读取

### E1

- 位置：Demystifying the process
- 原文："literary analyses (or papers that offer an interpretation of literary texts) rely on the assumption that stories, poems, and plays must mean something."
- 要点：文学分析的前提是作品有意义可以阐释。

### E2

- 位置：Demystifying the process
- 原文："To say that there is no one answer is not to say that anything we decide to say about a literary text is valid, interesting, or valuable. Interpretations of literature are often opinions, but not all opinions are equal."
- 要点：没有唯一答案，不等于怎么说都行；阐释有高下之分。

### E3

- 位置：Demystifying the process
- 原文："use careful reasoning to explain how that evidence relates to the main points of the interpretation."
- 要点：好的阐释要用缜密的推理说明证据和论点的关系。

### E4

- 位置：Demystifying the process
- 原文："avoid the obvious (in other words, it won’t argue a conclusion that most readers could reach on their own from a general knowledge of the story)"
- 要点：好的阐释要避开显而易见的结论。

### E5

- 位置：2. Explore potential topics
- 原文："A problem, on the other hand, is something that bugs you or that doesn’t seem to add up."
- 要点："问题"指文本里让你困惑、说不通的地方，可以从这里找题目。

### E6

- 位置：4. Write out a working thesis
- 原文："A changing thesis statement is a good sign that you’re starting to say more interesting and complex things on your subject."
- 要点：工作论点在变，是好迹象。

### E7

- 位置：5. Make an extended list of evidence
- 原文："As you make your notes keep track of page numbers so you can quickly find the passages in your book again"
- 要点：做笔记时记下页码。

### E8

- 位置：9. Interpret your evidence
- 原文："If you’re quoting passages without interpreting them, you’re not demonstrating your reasoning skills or helping the reader."
- 要点：只引不析，等于没有论证。

### E9

- 位置：General hints
- 原文："Your thesis should be able to answer the brutal question “so what?”"
- 要点：论点要回答"那又怎样"。

### E10

- 位置：General hints
- 原文："Literature scholars have identified connections between literature and the following: economics, family dynamics, education, religion, mortality, law, politics, sexuality, history, psychology, the environment, technology, animality, citizenship, and migration, among others."
- 要点：文学研究者把文学和经济、宗教、法律、政治、历史、环境等更大的议题联系起来。

### E11

- 位置：General hints
- 原文："Avoid going into a topic with a preconceived notion of what you’ll find."
- 要点：不要带着预设的结论进入题目。

### E12

- 位置：2. Explore potential topics
- 原文："A pattern can be the recurrence of certain kinds of imagery, vocabulary, formal elements (like rhyme and meter), or events."
- 要点："模式"指意象、词汇、形式要素或事件的反复出现。

### E13

- 位置：2. Explore potential topics
- 原文："chances are good that other people will have been perplexed by these moments as well and will be interested to see how you make sense of it all."
- 要点：让你困惑的地方，别人多半也困惑过，会想看你怎样解释。

### E14

- 位置：3. Select a topic with a lot of evidence
- 原文："narrow down your list by identifying how much evidence or how many specific details you could use to investigate each potential issue."
- 要点：在几个候选题目之间，按能用的证据和细节有多少来筛选。

### E15

- 位置：3. Select a topic with a lot of evidence
- 原文："Don’t launch into a topic without considering all the options first because you may end up with a topic that seemed promising initially but that only leads to a dead end."
- 要点：先比较各个选项再动手，否则可能选了一个起初看着好、最后走进死胡同的题目。

### E16

- 位置：5. Make an extended list of evidence
- 原文："you also want to avoid the temptation to arrive at definite conclusions about your topic."
- 要点：收集证据阶段，不要急着下定论。

### E17

- 位置：5. Make an extended list of evidence
- 原文："These other examples might provide a context or some useful contrasts that could illuminate my evidence relating to Victor."
- 要点：和主题不直接相关的段落，也可能提供语境或对照，帮助解释核心证据。

### E18

- 位置：6. Select your evidence
- 原文："First, select the facts which bear the closest relation to your thesis statement. Second, choose the pieces of evidence you’ll be able to say the most about."
- 要点：挑证据的两条标准：和论点关系最近；你能就它说得最多。

### E19

- 位置：7. Refine your thesis
- 原文："This step and the previous step (selecting evidence) are actually best done at the same time, since selecting your evidence and defining the focus of your paper depend upon each other."
- 要点：挑证据和修改论点最好同时进行，两者相互依赖。

### E20

- 位置：4. Write out a working thesis；7. Refine your thesis
- 原文："Victor Frankenstein’s tragic ambition was fueled by a faulty education." / "Victor Frankenstein’s education in unnaturally isolated environments fosters his tragic ambition."
- 要点：讲义的例子：工作论点从"错误的教育助长了悲剧性的野心"，改成更具体的"在不自然的孤立环境中受教育，助长了他的悲剧性野心"。

### E21

- 位置：8. Organize your evidence
- 原文："The ideas that tie these clusters of evidence together can then become the claims that you’ll make in your paper."
- 要点：把相似的证据归成组，把每组串起来的想法就是文中的分论点。

### E22

- 位置：General hints，Make your thesis relevant to your readers
- 原文："Avoid writing a paper that identifies a pattern in a story but doesn’t quite explain why that pattern leads to an interesting interpretation."
- 要点：不能只指出一个模式，还要说明这个模式为什么能引出有意思的阐释。

### E23

- 位置：General hints
- 原文："Avoid going into a topic with a preconceived notion of what you’ll find. Be prepared to challenge your own ideas about what gender, race, or class mean in a particular text."
- 要点：不要带着预设结论进入题目；准备好挑战自己对性别、种族、阶级等概念在具体文本中含义的看法。

### E24

- 位置：General hints，Make your thesis specific
- 原文："A paper about education in general in Frankenstein will probably not get my reader’s attention as much as a more specific topic about the impact of the learning environment on the main character."
- 要点：泛泛谈"教育"不如具体到"学习环境对主人公的影响"。

### E25

- 位置：5. Make an extended list of evidence
- 原文："A good interpretive paper does not need to cover all elements of the story"
- 要点：好的阐释论文不必覆盖作品的所有要素。

### E26

- 位置：Demystifying the process
- 原文："To say that there is no one answer is not to say that anything we decide to say about a literary text is valid, interesting, or valuable."
- 要点：没有唯一答案，不等于怎么说都成立。

### E27

- 位置：5. Make an extended list of evidence
- 原文："even just reaching a higher state of confusion is a good indicator that you’re treating your topic in a complex way"
- 要点：越读越困惑，也可能说明你在深入地处理题目。
