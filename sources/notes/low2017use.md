# Low & Meghir 2017：The Use of Structural Models in Econometrics

- 资料：`low2017use`，[原文](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.31.2.33)
- 版本：Journal of Economic Perspectives 31(2): 33–58，2017。E1、E2 取自美国经济学会官网文章页上的摘要；E3 起读的是全文，即伦敦大学学院机构库（UCL Discovery）保存的期刊排版版 PDF（https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10089195/1/jep.31.2.33.pdf）。文章介绍结构模型在实证分析中的作用，代表作者观点。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："The central payoff of a structural econometric model is that it allows an empirical researcher to go beyond the conclusions of a more conventional empirical study that provides reduced-form causal relationships."
- 要点：结构计量模型的主要好处，是让研究者超出只给出"简约式"因果关系的常规实证研究的结论。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Structural models identify mechanisms that determine outcomes and are designed to analyze counterfactual policies, quantifying impacts on specific outcomes as well as effects in the short and longer run."
- 要点：结构模型识别决定结果的机制，用来分析没有实施过的（反事实）政策，量化它对具体结果的短期和长期影响。

### E3

- 位置：引言，第 33 页
- 原文："The set of assumptions under which the model inferences are valid should be clear: indeed, the clarity of the assumptions is what gives value to structural models."
- 要点：模型推断在哪些假设下成立，必须写清；作者认为，假设清楚正是结构模型的价值所在。

### E4

- 位置：Standard Errors and Post-Estimation Checks，第 53 页
- 原文："First, calculate parameter standard errors." / "Second, use the finite difference approach in the first check to show how moments change with estimated structural parameters. This information helps make estimation more transparent, by showing which parameters are pinned down by which moments."
- 要点：估计完参数还要做检查：先算参数的标准误；再展示哪个参数由数据的哪个统计特征（矩）确定，让估计更透明。

### E5

- 位置：Standard Errors and Post-Estimation Checks，第 53 页
- 原文："A further test compares simulated predictions with additional moments or reduced form evidence, preferably not targeted in estimation."
- 要点：进一步的检验，是拿模型模拟出的预测，去和估计时最好没用到的数据特征或简约式结果比较。
