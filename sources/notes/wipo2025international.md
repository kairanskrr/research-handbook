# WIPO 2025：An International Guide to Patent Case Management for Judges，中国章 4.1.4

- 资料：`wipo2025international`，[原文](https://www.wipo.int/patent-judicial-guide/en/full-guide/china/4.1.4)
- 版本：世界知识产权组织（WIPO）2025 年版网页全文，采用知识共享署名 4.0 许可（CC BY 4.0），2026-10-11 读取。中国章由 Du Weike、He Juan 两位中国法官撰写。位置写小节号。

### E1

- 位置：§4.1.4 Guiding case system
- 原文："Article 18 paragraph 2 of the Law on the Organization of the People's Courts provides that "the Supreme People's Court may publish guiding cases.""
- 要点：《人民法院组织法》第 18 条第 2 款规定，最高人民法院可以发布指导性案例。

### E2

- 位置：§4.1.4 Guiding case system（概述 2015 年《〈关于案例指导工作的规定〉实施细则》第 9–11 条）
- 原文："At all levels, in trials of cases that are similar to the guiding cases issued by the Supreme People's Court in terms of basic facts and the application of the law, the courts shall make decisions by reference to the rules of the decision in the applicable guiding cases." / "Guiding cases shall be cited as reasons for a decision but not as the basis for the decision."
- 要点：各级法院审理基本案情和法律适用与最高人民法院指导性案例相似的案件，应当参照相应指导性案例的裁判规则作出裁判；指导性案例作为裁判理由引述，不作为裁判依据。
