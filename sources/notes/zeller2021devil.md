# Zeller 2021：The Devil's Guide to Incremental Research

- 资料：`zeller2021devil`，[原文](https://andreas-zeller.info/2021/04/15/the-devils-guide-to-incremental-research.html)
- 版本：作者博客，2021-04-15，原为推文串。全文是反讽：以"魔鬼的建议"形式列出凑论文的套路，读者应当反着读。

### E1

- 位置：THE CHERRY SHUFFLE
- 原文："Run it again and again until it once outperforms the competition. Report these “better” results. Save the seed for later reproduction, and quietly drop the results of the earlier experiments."
- 要点：（反讽）方法带随机性，就一遍遍重跑，直到某一次胜过对手，只报告这一次，悄悄丢掉其他结果。

### E2

- 位置：THE AUTOTUNE
- 原文："Auto-tune them towards your benchmark until you are significantly “better” than the untuned competition."
- 要点：（反讽）把自己方法的参数针对测试集调到最好，再和没调参的对手比。

### E3

- 位置：THE ACHILLES HEEL
- 原文："Your achievements go into the title, limitations into a footnote."
- 要点：（反讽）成果写进标题，局限藏进脚注。

### E4

- 位置：THE FISHING EXPEDITION
- 原文："Use a hypothesis like “The more X in a file, the more Y in it, too.” Whether X and Y are comments, loops, bugs, or emojis - anything will work as a “novel insight”."
- 要点：（反讽）随便拿两个量找相关，总能找到能发表的"新发现"。

### E5

> 已作废：与 [E8](#e8) 重复（原文被 E8 完整包含）。

- 位置：倒数第三段（Be efficient）
- 原文："Do not register your study. Do not keep logs of experiments or decisions. Do not keep a history. Do not test your code. Do not validate your results. Do not share anything."
- 要点：（反讽）不预先登记研究、不记实验日志、不测代码、不验证结果、不共享。反过来读就是一份良好做法清单。

### E6

- 位置：THE RUBE GOLDBERG
- 原文："take some simple technique and attach some huge machinery – say, a machine learner and a data corpus. Invariably, this will improve results somewhat, and you can claim you are “better”!"
- 要点：（反讽）给简单方法套上庞大的装置，总能提升一点，于是号称"更好"，却不交代代价。

### E7

- 位置：开头
- 原文："patterns that maximize the number of publications despite limited significance"
- 要点：作者要点名的是一类"配方"：能让论文数量最大化，但研究意义有限。

### E8

- 位置：倒数第三段
- 原文："Do not register your study. Do not keep logs of experiments or decisions. Do not keep a history. Do not test your code. Do not validate your results. Do not share anything. None of this is misconduct!"
- 要点：（反讽）不预注册、不记实验日志、不留版本历史、不测试代码、不验证结果、不共享，这些"都不算学术不端"，但都在损害研究的可信度。
