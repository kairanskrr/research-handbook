# Brown & Fehige 2023：Thought Experiments（斯坦福哲学百科全书）

- 资料：`brown2023thought`，[原文](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/thought-experiment/)
- 版本：《斯坦福哲学百科全书》条目，作者 James Robert Brown、Yiftach Fehige；1996 年首次发布，2023 年 11 月 28 日实质修订。位置写节号。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Thought experiments are basically devices of the imagination."
- 要点：思想实验本质上是想象的工具。

### E2

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Few would object to thought experiments that serve to illustrate complex states of affairs, or those that are used in educational contexts. The situation is different, however, with respect to the appropriation of imagined scenarios to investigate reality"
- 要点：用思想实验来说明复杂情形或用于教学，很少有人反对；用想象的情境去研究现实，则有争议。

### E3

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Secondly, they are used in many disciplines, including biology, economics, history, mathematics, philosophy, and physics (although, interestingly, not with the same frequency in each)."
- 要点：思想实验用于生物、经济、历史、数学、哲学、物理等多个学科，但各学科用得多少不同。

### E4

- 位置：§4.1 The Skeptical Objection
- 原文："Intuition pumps are often abused, though seldom deliberately"
- 要点：（条目引用 Dennett 1985）"直觉泵"式的思想实验常被滥用，虽然很少是故意的。

### E5

- 位置：§4.1 The Skeptical Objection
- 原文："Wilkes wants philosophy "to use science fact rather than science fiction or fantasy" (Wilkes 1988, p. 1)"
- 要点：Wilkes 主张哲学用科学事实，而不是科幻或幻想。

### E6

- 位置：§4.1 The Skeptical Objection
- 原文："We also agree that the inferences drawn in thought experimenting are highly problematic if the hypothetical scenario "is inadequately described" (Wilkes 1988, p. 8)."
- 要点：条目作者同意：假想情境描述不充分时，从思想实验得出的推论问题很大。

### E7

- 位置：§4.1 The Skeptical Objection
- 原文："That is to say that the more detailed the imaginary scenario in the relevant aspects is, the better the thought experiment"
- 要点：假想情境在相关方面描述得越详细，思想实验越好。

### E8

- 位置：§4.1 The Skeptical Objection
- 原文："her suggestion that thought experimental scenarios would have to satisfy current scientific knowledge about the relevant entities featured in a thought experiment is highly implausible."
- 要点：条目作者认为，要求思想实验情境必须符合现有科学知识，这一主张很不可信。

### E9

- 位置：§4.1 The Skeptical Objection
- 原文："This is indeed problematic because intuitions can be highly misleading"
- 要点：直觉可能严重误导人。
