# Brown & Fehige 2023：Thought Experiments（斯坦福哲学百科全书）

- 资料：`brown2023thought`，[原文](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/thought-experiment/)
- 版本：《斯坦福哲学百科全书》条目，作者 James Robert Brown、Yiftach Fehige；1996 年首次发布，2023 年 11 月 28 日实质修订。位置写节号。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Thought experiments are basically devices of the imagination."
- 要点：思想实验本质上是想象的工具。

### E2

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Few would object to thought experiments that serve to illustrate complex states of affairs, or those that are used in educational contexts. The situation is different, however, with respect to the appropriation of imagined scenarios to investigate reality"
- 要点：用思想实验来说明复杂情形或用于教学，很少有人反对；用想象的情境去研究现实，则有争议。

### E3

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Secondly, they are used in many disciplines, including biology, economics, history, mathematics, philosophy, and physics (although, interestingly, not with the same frequency in each)."
- 要点：思想实验用于生物、经济、历史、数学、哲学、物理等多个学科，但各学科用得多少不同。
