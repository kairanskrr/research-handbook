# 美国哲学学会 2022：Statement on Best Practices in Journal Publishing

- 资料：`apaphil2022journal`，[原文](https://www.apaonline.org/page/journalpractices)
- 版本：美国哲学学会（American Philosophical Association，注意不是同样简称 APA 的美国心理学会）的声明，由学术出版工作组起草，2022 年 2 月经理事会批准；前一版 2011 年 11 月批准。官网对脚本拦截，原文取自 Internet Archive 快照。是建议，不是强制规定。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Guidelines for Journals / Communicating with Authors
- 原文："It is strongly recommended that journals use triply anonymous refereeing. The author’s name and institutional affiliation should not be revealed to the editor or to the referees, and the names of the referees and their institutional affiliation should not be made known to the author."
- 要点：强烈建议期刊采用"三重匿名"审稿：编辑和审稿人都不知道作者是谁、来自哪里，作者也不知道审稿人是谁。

### E2

- 位置：Guidelines for Journals / Communicating with Authors
- 原文："All submissions should be read anonymously before a decision to desk reject is made."
- 要点：编辑直接拒稿（不送外审）之前，也应在匿名状态下读过稿件。

### E3

- 位置：Guidelines for Authors / Manuscript Preparation
- 原文："Prior to submitting a manuscript to a journal for review, an author should check the journal’s website."
- 要点：投稿前，作者应查看期刊网站。

### E4

- 位置：Guidelines for Authors / Manuscript Preparation
- 原文："The journal’s acceptance rate, average time for manuscript review, and average time from acceptance to publication."
- 要点：期刊网站上通常可以查到的信息之一：接收率、平均审稿时间、接收到出版的平均时间（同一列表还有期刊关注的领域、文章类型和篇幅、匿名审稿的稿件准备要求）。

### E5

- 位置：Guidelines for Authors / Manuscript Preparation
- 原文："Authors should make use of such information to ensure that their choice of a journal is appropriate and that they have properly prepared their manuscript for anonymous review."
- 要点：作者应利用这些信息选对期刊，并把稿件按匿名审稿的要求准备好。

### E6

- 位置：Guidelines for Authors / Manuscript Submission/Resubmission
- 原文："In submitting a manuscript for review, an author is indicating that it is not under review or forthcoming in any other publication."
- 要点：投稿即表示这篇稿件没有在别处审稿或待发表（不能一稿多投）。

### E7

- 位置：Guidelines for Authors / Requests for Information
- 原文："[...] authors should not hesitate to confirm the status of their manuscript’s review after a period of four months, unless the journal has already specified that the review process is likely to take longer."
- 要点：投稿四个月后，作者可以放心去询问审稿进度，除非期刊已说明审稿会更久。

### E8

- 位置：Guidelines for Referees / Conflicts of Interest and Anonymity
- 原文："Since many philosophers post their papers on the web prior to submitting them for publication, referees are often able to identify authors by a web search. Referees should not attempt to identify the authors of papers they are reviewing."
- 要点：很多哲学家投稿前把论文放到网上，审稿人常能搜到作者；审稿人不应试图找出作者是谁。

### E9

- 位置：Guidelines for Referees / Conflicts of Interest and Anonymity
- 原文："Referees should identify any potential conflicts of interest to editors. They should, in particular, tell editors when they know the identity of the author."
- 要点：审稿人应向编辑说明潜在利益冲突，特别是知道作者身份时要告诉编辑。

### E10

- 位置：Guidelines for Referees / Professional Responsibility
- 原文："The professional responsibility to referee falls on all members of the profession. The expectation is that refereeing is unpaid service to the profession from which all benefit."
- 要点：审稿是所有同行的职业责任，是无偿的服务。

### E11

- 位置：Guidelines for Referees / Appropriate Response Times
- 原文："Potential referees should respond to invitations to review within one week at most."
- 要点：收到审稿邀请，最多一周内答复。

### E12

- 位置：Copyright and Open Access / Consideration of Previously Posted Papers
- 原文："This practice is becoming more common among philosophers. In philosophy of science, the University of Pittsburgh provides the PhilSci Archive, an electronic archive for preprints."
- 要点：投稿前先把论文放到网上（如预印本库）在哲学家中越来越常见；科学哲学有匹兹堡大学的 PhilSci Archive 预印本库。

### E13

- 位置：Copyright and Open Access / Consideration of Previously Posted Papers
- 原文："[...] journal editors should, in general, be willing to consider papers which have been released to pre-print archives."
- 要点：在满足版权等条件时，期刊编辑一般应愿意考虑已在预印本库公开的论文。

### E14

- 位置：Guidelines for Journals / Communicating with (Potential) Referees
- 原文："Although the practice of having authors recommend referees may help save editors time, it should not be used. The potential for conflicts of interest outweighs the benefits."
- 要点：不应让作者推荐审稿人，利益冲突的风险大于好处。

### E15

- 位置：Guidelines for Journals / Communicating with (Potential) Referees
- 原文："Referees should be encouraged to not give undue weight to minor stylistic flaws, as they may be due to authors having English as a second language."
- 要点：审稿人不应过分看重小的文字瑕疵，作者可能英语不是母语。
