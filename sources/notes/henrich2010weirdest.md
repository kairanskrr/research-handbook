# Henrich, Heine & Norenzayan 2010：The weirdest people in the world?

- 资料：`henrich2010weirdest`，[原文 PDF（德国数据论坛 RatSWD 工作论文 No. 139）](https://www.konsortswd.de/wp-content/uploads/RatSWD_WP_139.pdf)
- 版本：工作论文版（2010 年 4 月），正式版发表于 Behavioral and Brain Sciences 33(2–3):61–83。位置写小节。

### E1

- 位置：Long Abstract
- 原文："Behavioral scientists routinely publish broad claims about human psychology and behavior in the world's top journals based on samples drawn entirely from Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich and Democratic (WEIRD) societies."
- 要点：行为科学家常常只用来自"西方、受过教育、工业化、富裕、民主"（WEIRD）社会的样本，就在顶级期刊上对"人类"下宽泛的结论。

### E2

- 位置：Long Abstract
- 原文："WEIRD subjects are particularly unusual compared with the rest of the species—frequent outliers."
- 要点：比较各人群的数据后发现，WEIRD 被试和全人类相比恰恰很特殊，常常是离群的一端。

### E3

- 位置：§1.1 The Behavioral Sciences Database is Narrow
- 原文："68% of subjects came from the US, and a full 96% of subjects were from Western industrialized countries" / "This means that 96% of psychological samples come from countries with only 12% of the world's population."
- 要点：Arnett 2008 分析 2003–2007 年六个心理学分支的顶级期刊：68% 的被试来自美国，96% 来自西方工业化国家，而这些国家只占世界人口的 12%。

### E4

- 位置：§1.1 The Behavioral Sciences Database is Narrow
- 原文："67% of the American samples (and 80% of the samples from other countries) were composed solely of undergraduates in psychology courses"
- 要点：在社会心理学顶级期刊上，67% 的美国样本（以及 80% 的其他国家样本）完全由上心理学课的本科生组成。

### E5

- 位置：§1.2 Researchers Often Assume their Findings are Universal
- 原文："In top journals such as Nature and Science researchers frequently extend their findings from undergraduates to the species—often declaring this generalization in their titles."
- 要点：在 Nature、Science 等顶级期刊上，研究者常把本科生身上的发现推广到全人类，甚至写进标题。

### E6

- 位置：§2.1 Visual Perception（Müller-Lyer 错觉）
- 原文："The results show substantial differences among populations, with American undergraduates anchoring the extreme end of the distribution" / "the San foragers of the Kalahari were unaffected by the so-called "illusion""
- 要点：例子：一种经典视错觉在美国本科生身上最强，而卡拉哈里的桑人采集者完全不受影响。连视知觉都会因人群而异。

### E7

- 位置：§6.3 Recommendations
- 原文："Journal editors and reviewers should press authors to both explicitly discuss and defend the generalizability of their findings. Claims and confidence regarding generalizability must scale with the strength of the empirical defense."
- 要点：编辑和审稿人应要求作者明确讨论、论证结论能推广到多大范围；推广的说法要和证据强度相称。

### E8

- 位置：§6.3 Recommendations
- 原文："Journal editors should require explicit and detailed information on subject pool composition"
- 要点：期刊应要求作者详细报告被试来源和构成。
