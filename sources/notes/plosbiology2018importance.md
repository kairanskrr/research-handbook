# PLOS Biology 编辑部 2018：The importance of being second

- 资料：`plosbiology2018importance`，[原文](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.2005203)
- 版本：PLoS Biology 16(1): e2005203，2018 年 1 月 29 日，社论，开放获取（CC BY）。这是一家期刊（PLOS Biology）的政策，不是各期刊通行的规定。2026-10-08 读取。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："we are formalizing a policy whereby manuscripts that confirm or extend a recently published study ("scooped" manuscripts, also referred to as complementary) are eligible for consideration at PLOS Biology."
- 要点：PLOS Biology 正式规定：证实或扩展了一项刚发表研究的稿件（"被抢先"的稿件，也叫互补研究）可以投稿、会被考虑。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Being scooped is loosely defined as when two independent groups studying the same system produce the same or similar results, and one group publishes their work first. Being scooped is often considered to devalue the second, complementary study; many journals will reject it citing lack of novelty."
- 要点：被抢先，大致是两个独立的组得到相同或相似的结果，一组先发表；后发的研究常被认为贬值，很多期刊会以缺乏新颖性拒稿。

### E3

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："we consider that two papers from two groups independently identifying the same phenomenon in parallel increase the confidence in the results of the work."
- 要点：该刊认为，两个组独立发现同一现象，会提高人们对结果的信心。

### E4

- 位置：第 5 段
- 原文："authors of a complementary study have six months from the publication or posting (to a preprint server) of the first article to submit their manuscript to PLOS Biology. We hope that authors will use these six months to fully support and potentially extend the results of the first article."
- 要点：在该刊，互补研究的作者从第一篇发表或挂上预印本起有六个月投稿；编辑希望作者用这段时间充分支持、并尽量扩展第一篇的结果。

### E5

- 位置：第 5 段
- 原文："All submissions must still meet our editorial requirements for depth of study and potential impact."
- 要点：被抢先的稿件仍须达到该刊对研究深度和潜在影响的要求。
