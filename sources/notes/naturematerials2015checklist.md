# Nature Materials 2015 社论：A checklist for photovoltaic research

- 资料：`naturematerials2015checklist`，[原文](https://www.nature.com/articles/nmat4473)
- 版本：Nature Materials 14, 1073（2015），社论，2015-10-22 发表，可免费阅读。社论不分节，位置写段落序号（正文第几段）。

### E1

- 位置：导语
- 原文："To aid the reproducibility of published results for photovoltaic devices, from now on we will ask authors of relevant manuscripts to complete a checklist of key technical information that must be reported."
- 要点：为了让光伏器件的结果可复现，Nature Materials 要求相关稿件的作者填写一份关键技术信息清单。

### E2

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："discussions between experts in solar cells and editors from the Nature family of journals have converged in the identification of key technical and procedural information about the characterization of photovoltaic devices based on any material system."
- 要点：太阳能电池专家和 Nature 系列期刊编辑讨论后，确定了表征光伏器件（不限材料体系）需要报告的关键技术和流程信息。

### E3

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Most of the technical and statistical information requested in the checklist — such as current–voltage scan conditions, the size of the solar cells and the number of tested samples — applies to any study reporting the characterization of a photovoltaic device."
- 要点：清单要的大部分技术和统计信息（如电流—电压扫描条件、电池面积、测试样品数）适用于任何报告光伏器件表征的研究。

### E4

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："the checklist asks authors to clarify whether the performance of their devices has been confirmed by independent certification laboratories."
- 要点：清单要求作者说明器件性能是否经过独立认证实验室确认。

### E5

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："This and other best practices — such as the reporting of average efficiency values alongside best-device performance, and the inclusion of information on the maximum device stability verified in the lab — should help to provide a fair picture of the potential of photovoltaic materials"
- 要点：报告最好器件性能的同时报告平均效率、给出实验室验证过的最长稳定性等做法，有助于公正地反映光伏材料的潜力。

### E6

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："extra care should be taken by authors in describing the adopted characterization procedure and in providing statistical evidence of performance reproducibility."
- 要点：没有拿到认证的新材料结果也可以发表，但作者要格外仔细地描述表征流程，并提供性能可重复的统计证据。

### E7

- 位置：第 4 段
- 原文："characterization procedures optimized for a specific photovoltaic technology may not be straightforwardly applicable to other systems."
- 要点：为某种光伏技术优化的表征流程，不一定能直接用到别的体系。
