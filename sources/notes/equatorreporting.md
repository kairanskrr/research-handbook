### E1

- 位置：About us
- 原文："The EQUATOR (Enhancing the QUAlity and Transparency Of health Research) Network is an international initiative that seeks to improve the reliability and value of published health research literature by promoting transparent and accurate reporting and wider use of robust reporting guidelines."
- 要点：EQUATOR 网络是一个国际组织，通过推广透明准确的报告和可靠的报告规范，提高健康研究文献的可靠性和价值。

### E3

- 位置：What is a reporting guideline?
- 原文："“A checklist, flow diagram, or structured text to guide authors in reporting a specific type of research, developed using explicit methodology.”"
- 要点：报告规范的定义：用明确方法制定的清单、流程图或结构化文本，指导作者报告某一类研究。
