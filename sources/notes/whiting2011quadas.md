# Whiting 等 2011：QUADAS-2（Annals of Internal Medicine，只读了摘要）

- 资料：`whiting2011quadas`，[PubMed 摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22007046/)
- 版本：Whiting PF、Rutjes AW、Westwood ME 等与 QUADAS-2 Group，"QUADAS-2: a revised tool for the quality assessment of diagnostic accuracy studies"，Ann Intern Med 2011;155(8):529–536，DOI 10.7326/0003-4819-155-8-201110180-00009。第一作者单位为英国布里斯托大学。本文只读了 PubMed 上的摘要（用 NCBI E-utilities 取得），未读全文。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："In 2003, the QUADAS tool for systematic reviews of diagnostic accuracy studies was developed." / "QUADAS-2 was developed." / "This tool comprises 4 domains: patient selection, index test, reference standard, and flow and timing. Each domain is assessed in terms of risk of bias, and the first 3 domains are also assessed in terms of concerns regarding applicability."
- 要点：QUADAS 工具 2003 年开发，QUADAS-2 是它的修订版。QUADAS-2 有四个领域：患者选择、待评价检查、参考标准、流程与时间；每个领域评偏倚风险，前三个领域还评适用性方面的担忧。
