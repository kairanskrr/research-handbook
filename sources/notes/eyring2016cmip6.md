# Eyring 等 2016：Overview of the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 (CMIP6) experimental design and organization

- 资料：`eyring2016cmip6`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-9-1937-2016)
- 版本：Geoscientific Model Development 9, 1937–1958, 2016，开放获取。只读取了期刊网页上的摘要和引言首段。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："By coordinating the design and distribution of global climate model simulations of the past, current, and future climate, the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project (CMIP) has become one of the foundational elements of climate science."
- 要点：耦合模式比较计划（CMIP）协调全球气候模式对过去、现在和未来气候的模拟设计和数据分发，是气候科学的基础之一。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："The DECK and CMIP historical simulations, together with the use of CMIP data standards, will be the entry cards for models participating in CMIP."
- 要点：模式要参加 CMIP，必须跑几组共同的基准试验和历史模拟，并采用 CMIP 的数据标准。

### E3

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Participation in CMIP6-Endorsed MIPs by individual modelling groups will be at their own discretion and will depend on their scientific interests and priorities."
- 要点：各建模组自愿决定参加哪些专题比较计划。

### E4

- 位置：§1 引言
- 原文："The objective of CMIP is to better understand past, present, and future climate change in a multi-model context."
- 要点：CMIP 的目标是在"多模式"的框架下理解气候变化。
