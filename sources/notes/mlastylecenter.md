# 美国现代语言协会：MLA Style Center（In-Text Citations: An Overview；Plagiarism and Academic Dishonesty；Works Cited: A Quick Guide）

- 资料：`mlastylecenter`，[原文](https://style.mla.org/)
- 版本：MLA 格式官网的免费页面，依据《MLA 手册》第 9 版；2026 年 10 月读取

### E1

- 位置：In-Text Citations: An Overview
- 原文："In-text citations are brief, unobtrusive references that direct readers to the works-cited-list entries for the sources you consulted"
- 要点：文中引注是简短的提示，指向文末"引用文献"列表中的条目。

### E2

- 位置：In-Text Citations: An Overview
- 原文："if a specific part of a work is quoted or paraphrased and the work includes a page number, line number, time stamp, or other way to point readers to the place in the work where the information can be found, that location marker must be included in parentheses."
- 要点：引用或转述具体段落时，必须在括号里给出页码、行号或时间码等位置。

### E3

- 位置：Works Cited: A Quick Guide
- 原文："When the source being documented forms part of a larger whole, the larger whole can be thought of as a container that holds the source."
- 要点："容器"概念：被引作品属于更大的整体时（如短篇小说收在选集里），那个整体就是容器。

### E4

- 位置：Plagiarism and Academic Dishonesty
- 原文："In published work, if you reuse ideas or phrases that you used in prior work and do not cite your prior work, you have plagiarized."
- 要点：发表时重用自己以前的观点或句子而不引用，也是抄袭。

### E5

- 位置：Plagiarism and Academic Dishonesty
- 原文："Paraphrasing someone’s ideas or arguments or copying someone’s unique wording without giving proper credit is plagiarism."
- 要点：转述别人的观点或论证、照搬别人独特的措辞而不注明，都是抄袭。
