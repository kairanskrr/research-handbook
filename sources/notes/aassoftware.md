# AAS Journals：Policy Statement on Software

- 资料：`aassoftware`，[原文](https://journals.aas.org/policy-statement-on-software/)
- 版本：美国天文学会（American Astronomical Society，AAS）期刊的软件政策网页，2026-10-06 读取（页面注明 2024 年 2 月更新）。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："AAS Journals have adopted a policy that reflects the importance of software to the astronomical community, and the need for clear communication about such software which ensures that credit is appropriately given to its authors."
- 要点：AAS 期刊的软件政策，承认软件对天文界的重要性，要保证软件作者得到应有的认可。

### E2

- 位置：Guidelines for citation of software
- 原文："Ideally, both forms of citation should be included. The former extends credit to the authors for their publication and tells the reader where to learn about the software. The latter gives the reader access to the exact version of the software used in the project."
- 要点：引用软件最好两种方式都用：引描述软件的论文（给作者认可、告诉读者去哪里了解），再引软件本身的 DOI（让读者拿到你用的确切版本）。

### E3

- 位置：Guidelines for software articles
- 原文："We highly recommend that authors release code described in an article under an appropriate open source license"
- 要点：强烈建议作者用合适的开源许可证发布论文中描述的代码，并存档拿到 DOI。

### E4

- 位置：Guidelines for software articles
- 原文："Such articles need not include research results produced using the software"
- 要点：AAS 期刊接受专门介绍软件的论文，不要求包含用该软件得到的研究结果。

### E5

- 位置：Review of Software
- 原文："The AAS journals do not ask referees to directly review the software described in papers published in our journals."
- 要点：AAS 期刊不要求审稿人直接审查论文所描述的软件。
