# Might：How to peer review scientific work

- 资料：`mightreview`，[原文](https://matt.might.net/articles/how-to-peer-review/)
- 版本：博客文章，写给研究生。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Review the manuscript as you would want your own reviewed."
- 要点：核心原则：像你希望别人审你的稿那样去审别人的稿。

### E2

- 位置：Recognize conflicts
- 原文："If you're not sure whether you have a conflict, disclose that to the editor or program chair so that they can decide."
- 要点：不确定是否有利益冲突时，告诉编辑或程序委员会主席，由他们决定。

### E3

- 位置：Don't review like a grad student
- 原文："Reviews written by graduate students are among the most negative."
- 要点：研究生写的审稿意见往往最负面；建议先列出论文的优点再批评。

### E4

- 位置：Acknowledge visceral reactions
- 原文："Acknowledging your gut instinct will help you avoid confirmation bias"
- 要点：记下第一印象及其原因，有助于避免确认偏误（只找支持自己第一印象的证据）。

### E5

- 位置：Offer constructive criticism
- 原文："When you criticize an aspect of a manuscript, offer advice on how to fix it."
- 要点：批评某处时，同时给出怎么改的建议。

### E6

- 位置：Offer constructive criticism
- 原文："If you recommend rejection, what specifically could have changed your mind to argue for acceptance?"
- 要点：建议拒稿时，说明具体什么能改变你的判断。

### E7

- 位置：Hold the snark
- 原文："Peer reviews are not the right venue to show off your wit."
- 要点：审稿意见不是卖弄机智的地方。

### E8

- 位置：Keep it impersonal
- 原文："Don't let the track record of the authors sway your opinion in either direction."
- 要点：不要让作者过去的成绩影响你的判断，无论正面还是负面。

### E9

- 位置：Anonymize but don't hide
- 原文："Anonymity in peer review is meant as a shield, not a sword."
- 要点：匿名是盾牌，不是武器。

### E10

- 位置：Scholarly conflict
- 原文："I recommend informing the program chair or editor and recusing yourself from discussion of such manuscripts"
- 要点：遇到别人独立解决了你正在做的问题的稿件，告知编辑或主席并回避讨论。
