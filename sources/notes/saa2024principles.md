# 美国考古学会 2024：SAA Principles of Archaeological Ethics

- 资料：`saa2024principles`，[原文 PDF](https://www.saa.org/common/Uploaded%20files/saadocs/CareerPractice/saa_ethics.pdf)
- 版本：美国考古学会（Society for American Archaeology，SAA）2024 年 3 月 7 日通过的九条考古伦理原则（1996 年首版，2016 年增加第 9 条，2024 年修订）。PDF 含英文和西班牙文，本摘录只用英文部分，位置写原则编号。

### E1

- 位置：Principle No. 1: Stewardship
- 原文："Stewardship involves the collaborative management of the archaeological record for the benefit of all people."
- 要点："守护"（stewardship）指为了所有人的利益，合作管理考古记录。

### E2

- 位置：Principle No. 1: Stewardship
- 原文："Archaeologists work for the long-term conservation and protection of the archaeological record, while respecting the rights and beliefs of Indigenous and descendant peoples to the representation of their heritage as manifested in the archaeological record."
- 要点：考古学家致力于长期保护考古记录，同时尊重原住民和后裔群体对自身遗产如何被呈现的权利和信仰。

### E3

- 位置：Principle No. 2: Responsibility
- 原文："Archaeologists must abide by all laws and regulations in addition to collaborating, to the extent possible, with groups affected by their work to achieve results in a mutually agreeable and beneficial manner."
- 要点：考古学家必须守法，并尽可能和受其工作影响的群体合作，达成双方都认可、都受益的结果。

### E4

- 位置：Principle No. 3: Commercialization
- 原文："The commercial sale or authentication of archaeological objects and other data for profit must not be practiced by archaeologists."
- 要点：考古学家不得为牟利而买卖考古物品，也不得为其做商业鉴定。

### E5

- 位置：Principle No. 3: Commercialization
- 原文："framing the archaeological record as an invaluable and nonrenewable resource for telling the story of the past and present."
- 要点：考古记录是讲述过去和现在的宝贵且不可再生的资源。

### E6

- 位置：Principle No. 4: Public Education and Community Engagement
- 原文："(3) listen to and incorporate the knowledge and concerns of impacted communities."
- 要点：考古学家要倾听受影响社区的知识和关切，并把它们纳入工作。

### E7

- 位置：Principle No. 5: Preservation of the Archaeological Record
- 原文："The collections and documentation (digital/physical) generated through archaeological research are part of the archaeological record and as such should be treated in accord with the principles of stewardship."
- 要点：考古研究产生的藏品和记录（数字的、实体的）本身就是考古记录的一部分，要按守护原则对待。

### E8

- 位置：Principle No. 5: Preservation of the Archaeological Record
- 原文："Archaeologists have the responsibility to ensure that the record is archived, curated, and published, according to prevailing professional standards."
- 要点：考古学家有责任按现行专业标准，确保记录得到归档、保管和发表。

### E9

- 位置：Principle No. 6: Reporting
- 原文："Archaeologists have a responsibility to share and publish results and data in a timely and, so much as possible, open access manner."
- 要点：要及时分享和发表结果与数据，并尽可能开放获取。

### E10

- 位置：Principle No. 6: Reporting
- 原文："An interest in preserving and protecting sensitive information about the past and minimizing unintended consequences must be taken into account when publishing and distributing knowledge."
- 要点：发表时必须考虑保护有关过去的敏感信息，尽量减少意外后果。

### E11

- 位置：Principle No. 7: Training and Resources
- 原文："An archaeologist’s education should include research design and planning that prioritizes analysis of existing collections, justifies new fieldwork that maximizes conservation of existing resources, and ensures proper curation and dissemination of results."
- 要点：考古训练应包括这样的研究设计：优先分析已有藏品；新的田野工作要说明理由，并最大程度保护现存资源；确保成果得到妥善保管和传播。

### E12

- 位置：Principle No. 7: Training and Resources
- 原文："Archaeology is a multidisciplinary field that requires a holistic approach to training, research, and professional conduct."
- 要点：考古学是多学科领域，训练、研究和职业行为都需要综合性的做法。

### E13

- 位置：Principle No. 5: Preservation of the Archaeological Record
- 原文："Archaeologists should be cognizant that many descendant and Indigenous communities view the archaeological record as part of their heritage and should be included in the decisions regarding documentation and the pursuit of intellectual knowledge."
- 要点：许多后裔群体和原住民社区把考古记录视为自己的遗产，应当让他们参与有关记录和研究的决定。
