# Lejaeghere 等人 2016：Reproducibility in density functional theory calculations of solids

- 资料：`lejaeghere2016reproducibility`，[原文（美国国家标准与技术研究院 NIST 发布的 PDF）](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=919278)
- 版本：Science 351(6280), aad3000（2016）。读取的是 NIST 出版物库提供的全文 PDF（含研究摘要页和正文）。

### E1

- 位置：Research Article Summary — Rationale
- 原文："More than 15,000 DFT papers are published each year, and DFT is now increasingly used in an automated fashion to build large databases or apply multiscale techniques with limited human supervision."
- 要点：每年发表的密度泛函理论论文超过 1.5 万篇；它越来越多地被自动化使用，用来建大型数据库或做多尺度计算，人工监督有限。

### E2

- 位置：Research Article Summary — Rationale
- 原文："A plethora of DFT computer codes are available, many of them differing considerably in their details of implementation, and each yielding a certain ‘precision’ relative to other codes."
- 要点：密度泛函理论计算程序很多，实现细节差别很大，彼此算出的结果有一定差异。

### E3

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："We report the results of a community-wide effort that compared 15 solid-state codes, using 40 different potentials or basis set types, to assess the quality of the Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof equations of state for 71 elemental crystals."
- 要点：这项社区合作比较了 15 个固体计算程序、40 种势或基组，计算 71 种单质晶体的状态方程。

### E4

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："We conclude that predictions from recent codes and pseudopotentials agree very well, with pairwise differences that are comparable to those between different high-precision experiments. Older methods, however, have less precise agreement."
- 要点：较新的程序和赝势之间结果非常一致，两两差异和不同高精度实验之间的差异相当；较老的方法一致性差一些。

### E5

- 位置：正文，赝势方法与全电子方法比较的一段
- 原文："The considerable difference in the older potentials, even for the predefined structures in this relatively simple test set, provides a compelling argument to use only the most recent potential files of a given code."
- 要点：旧版势文件差异明显，这是只用某个程序最新版势文件的有力理由。

### E6

- 位置：正文，Fig. 4 讨论
- 原文："A complete specification would have to include code defaults and hard-coded values, so a reasonable compromise was chosen."
- 要点：要完整说明一次计算，连程序的默认值和写死在代码里的参数都得写上；作者只能折中，在补充表格里列出最重要的数值设置。
