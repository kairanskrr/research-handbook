# APS Journals：Guidelines for Data Availability Statements in Physical Review

- 资料：`apsdataavailability`，[原文](https://journals.aps.org/authors/data-availability-statements)
- 版本：APS 期刊作者指南网页，2026-10-06 读取。APS 2024 年宣布这项政策，先在 Physical Review D 等期刊实行，再推广到全部 Physical Review 期刊。

### E1

- 位置：Overview of Policies
- 原文："Statement required: All published articles must include a Data Availability Statement (DAS)."
- 要点：所有发表的论文都必须有"数据可获取性声明"。

### E2

- 位置：Overview of Policies
- 原文："Authors are strongly encouraged to share data and software publicly and according to FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) principles at publication."
- 要点：强烈鼓励作者在发表时按 FAIR 原则（可发现、可获取、可互操作、可重用）公开数据和软件。

### E3

- 位置：Overview of Policies
- 原文："Publicly shared data and software must be cited in the reference list, and the citation must be included in the data availability statement."
- 要点：公开的数据和软件必须写进参考文献，并在声明里引用。

### E4

- 位置：Overview of Policies
- 原文："If data can’t be made public, authors must agree to share data privately with editors, reviewers, and readers upon reasonable request."
- 要点：数据不能公开时，作者必须同意在合理请求下私下提供给编辑、审稿人和读者。

### E5

- 位置：What Qualifies as Research Data?
- 原文："Research data and software includes any factual material necessary to verify or replicate study results"
- 要点：研究数据和软件包括核实或重做研究结果所需的一切事实材料，作者自己写的代码也算。

### E6

- 位置：Examples of Data Availability Statements，Software is publicly available
- 原文："The data that support the findings of this study were generated by numerical simulations. The source code and parameters used to generate the simulations is publicly available"
- 要点：数值模拟类论文的声明示例：数据由模拟产生，模拟用的源代码和参数已公开。
