# Berez-Kroeker 等 2018：The Austin Principles of Data Citation in Linguistics

- 资料：`berezkroeker2018austin`，[原文](https://site.uit.no/linguisticsdatacitation/austinprinciples/)
- 版本：Version 1.0（2018），CC BY 4.0

### E1

- 位置：3. Evidence
- 原文："Linguists should cite the data upon which scholarly claims are based."
- 要点：学术主张依据了哪些数据，就要引用这些数据。

### E2

- 位置：3. Evidence
- 原文："Authors should ensure that data collection and processing methods are transparent"
- 要点：数据收集和处理方法要透明。

### E3

- 位置：5. Access
- 原文："Linguistic data should be as open as possible, in order to facilitate reproducibility; and as closed as necessary"
- 要点：语言数据应尽可能开放，必要时才限制（出于伦理、法律和言语社区的约束）。

### E4

- 位置：2. Credit and Attribution
- 原文："One way to do this is through citations that list individual contributors and their roles."
- 要点：可以在引用中列出各位贡献者及其角色。

### E5

- 位置：4. Unique Identification
- 原文："linguists should look for services that provide the means for identification in the form of a Persistent Identifier (PID). For digital data, examples of these include Digital Object Identifiers (DOI) and Handles."
- 要点：选数据库时要找能提供持久标识符（如 DOI）的服务。

### E6

- 位置：7. Specificity and Verifiability
- 原文："Citations should specify which version of the data is being referenced."
- 要点：引用要写明数据的版本。

### E7

- 位置：7. Specificity and Verifiability
- 原文："Data citations should make it easy for a curious reader to find the specific datum or subset of data within the larger dataset that support a claim."
- 要点：引用要让读者能找到支撑主张的那一条或那一部分数据。

### E8

- 位置：6. Persistence
- 原文："Linguists should confirm that the archives or repositories where they are storing their data have written policies pertaining to persistence of data, metadata, and identifiers."
- 要点：要确认存放数据的档案馆有书面的长期保存政策。
