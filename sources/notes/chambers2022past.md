# Chambers & Tzavella 2022：The past, present and future of Registered Reports

- 资料：`chambers2022past`，[原文 PDF（卡迪夫大学机构库，作者接受稿）](https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/145541/1/Chambers%26Tzavella2021-MS.pdf)
- 版本：发表于 Nature Human Behaviour 6:29–42。这里用作者接受稿，位置写小节和稿件页码（近似）。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Registered Reports are a form of empirical publication in which study proposals are peer reviewed and pre-accepted before research is undertaken."
- 要点：注册报告是一种发表形式：研究开始之前，研究方案先经过同行评审并被预先接收。

### E2

- 位置：引言，稿件第 3 页
- 原文："proposals that are favourably assessed receive in principle acceptance (IPA), which commits the journal to publishing the final paper regardless of whether the hypotheses are supported, provided the authors adhere to their approved protocol and interpret the results in line with the evidence."
- 要点：方案通过评审后获得"原则性接收"：只要作者按批准的方案做、按证据解读结果，不管假设是否得到支持，期刊都会发表。

### E3

- 位置：Present，Bias control 一段，稿件第 7 页
- 原文："a recent analysis of 296 hypotheses published across 127 RRs in different fields found that 60% of RRs report null results, approximately five times greater than the rate in regular articles" / "In psychology this difference is even more striking, with a new study finding that just 4% of regular articles failed to confirm the first hypothesis compared with 56% for RRs"
- 要点：注册报告里报告阴性（不显著）结果的比例远高于普通论文。心理学中，普通论文只有 4% 没有证实第一个假设，注册报告是 56%。

### E4

- 位置：Present，Bias control 一段，稿件第 7 页
- 原文："it is possible that authors, knowing that their study will be published regardless of whether their hypotheses are supported, might employ the RR format to test riskier hypotheses."
- 要点：作者提醒，这个差异不一定全是因为偏差被消除了；也可能是作者用注册报告去检验更冒险的假设。

### E5

- 位置：Present，Limitations and drawbacks 一段，稿件第 12 页
- 原文："they are particularly well suited to hypothesis-driven studies and are not designed to improve the robustness or transparency of purely exploratory science"
- 要点：注册报告适合检验假设的研究，不是为纯探索性研究设计的。

### E6

- 位置：Present，Stage 1 delay and bureaucratic tennis 一段，稿件第 13 页
- 原文："the Stage 1 review time typically adds a period of several months between submitting a Stage 1 manuscript and the commencement of the research."
- 要点：第一阶段审稿通常让研究推迟几个月才能开始，对合同期短或经费要求马上收数据的人是障碍。

### E7

- 位置：Present，关于注册报告可及性的一段，稿件第 6 页
- 原文："the often substantial sample sizes needed for RRs to achieve minimum levels of statistical power required by many journals, combined with the time taken for Stage 1 review (see Limitations and drawbacks), could act as a deterrent"
- 要点：很多期刊对注册报告有最低统计功效要求，所需样本量往往很大，加上第一阶段审稿耗时，可能让资源有限的研究者望而却步。

### E8

- 位置：Box 1. Top tips for authors，稿件第 13 页
- 原文："A well-justified sampling plan that meets journal requirements (e.g. 90% statistical power) should be formulated"
- 要点：给作者的建议：制定有充分理由、满足期刊要求的抽样计划，例如达到 90% 的统计功效。
