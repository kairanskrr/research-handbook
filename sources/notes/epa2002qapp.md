# 美国环境保护署 2002：Guidance for Quality Assurance Project Plans (EPA QA/G-5)

- 资料：`epa2002qapp`，[原文 PDF](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2015-06/documents/g5-final.pdf)（EPA 官网对脚本返回 403，本项目通过 Internet Archive 快照读取）
- 版本：EPA/240/R-02/009，2002 年 12 月。位置写 PDF 页码（从封面算起）。

### E1

- 位置：Chapter 1，PDF 第 9 页
- 原文："The QA Project Plan documents the results of a project's technical planning process, providing in one place a clear, concise, and complete plan for the environmental data operation and its quality objectives and identifying key project personnel."
- 要点：质量保证项目计划把项目的技术规划结果写在一处：数据工作怎样做、质量目标是什么、关键人员是谁。

### E2

- 位置：Chapter 1，PDF 第 10 页
- 原文："But in all cases, the QA Project Plan should be completed and approved before the project is started."
- 要点：无论哪种情况，质量保证项目计划都要在项目开始前完成并获批。

### E3

- 位置：Chapter 1，PDF 第 10 页
- 原文："It is EPA policy that all work funded by EPA in which environmental data will be collected, evaluated, used, or reported (including the use of existing data and modeling), or which involves the design, construction, and operation of environmental technology, have approved QA Project Plans"
- 要点：EPA 的政策是：凡 EPA 资助、涉及收集、评价、使用或报告环境数据（包括用已有数据和建模）或涉及环境技术的工作，都要有获批的质量保证项目计划。

### E4

- 位置：Chapter 1，PDF 第 13 页
- 原文："Following the principle of the graded approach, if an element is not applicable, then indicate why it is not relevant."
- 要点：按"分级"原则，计划中某一项不适用时，要写明为什么不适用。

### E5

- 位置：Chapter 2，Element A6，PDF 第 25 页
- 原文："For a modeling project, for example, indicate whether this is a model application project, i.e., evaluating existing models to determine whether they can perform the needed modeling to predict a future environmental condition in useable outputs, or whether this is a project to develop a new model because you know no applicable model exists."
- 要点：建模项目要说明是评估、应用已有模型，还是因为没有合适的模型而开发新模型。

### E6

- 位置：Chapter 2，Element B3，PDF 第 36 页
- 原文："A unique project numbering system helps to track each sample through the entire process to ensure samples are not switched accidentally."
- 要点：用唯一的样品编号系统，可以全程追踪每个样品，避免样品被弄混。

### E7

- 位置：Chapter 2，Element B3，PDF 第 36 页
- 原文："The term "chain-of-custody" is often used to designate legal custody procedures that enable tracing the possession and handling of a "sample" [...] during transfer, i.e., from sample collection through laboratory analysis, so that its physical possession is known at all points of the project."
- 要点：监管链指一套程序，可以追溯样品从采集到实验室分析的整个转交过程中由谁持有、怎样处理。

### E8

- 位置：Chapter 2，Element B3，PDF 第 36 页
- 原文："This may include sample custody tags and written documentation to be signed by each person who handles the samples, and the use of a container sealed with custody tape. If such a procedure will be followed, describe it fully so that it can be done. Chain-of-custody procedures may be specified for projects where the data may be used in court as evidence."
- 要点：监管链可能包括样品标签、每个经手人签字的书面记录和用封条密封的容器；数据可能作为法庭证据时，项目可能会要求监管链程序。

### E9

- 位置：Chapter 2，Element B5，PDF 第 39 页
- 原文："QC activities are those technical activities routinely performed, not to eliminate or minimize errors, but to measure or estimate their effect."
- 要点：质量控制活动的目的不是消除误差，而是测量或估计误差的影响。

### E10

- 位置：Chapter 2，Element B5，PDF 第 39 页
- 原文："Blanks are "clean" samples used to measure the sources of contamination at different collection and measurement stages."
- 要点：空白样是"干净"的样品，用来测量采样和测定各阶段带入的污染。

### E11

- 位置：Chapter 2，Element B5，PDF 第 39 页
- 原文："Bias is systematic error. A variety of QC samples can be used to determine the degree of bias, such as analysis of samples with a known concentration of the contaminant of concern. These are known as standards, matrix spike samples, and matrix-specific QC samples."
- 要点：偏差是系统误差，可以用已知浓度的样品（标准样、基质加标样等）来确定偏差有多大。

### E12

- 位置：Chapter 2，Element B5，PDF 第 39 页
- 原文："Imprecision is random error, observed as different results from repeated measurements of the same or identical samples. Replicate samples and split samples are commonly used to denote the level of precision in the measurement or collection system."
- 要点：不精密是随机误差，表现为重复测同一样品结果不同；常用平行样和分样来说明精密度。

### E13

- 位置：Chapter 2，Element B5，PDF 第 39 页
- 原文："For each measurement activity, identify those QC checks that will be followed in this project, and indicate at what frequency each will occur."
- 要点：对每项测量，都要写明做哪些质量控制检查、多久做一次。

### E14

- 位置：Appendix B 术语表，PDF 第 71 页
- 原文："field blank -- a clean analyte-free sample which is carried to the sampling site and then exposed to sampling conditions, returned to the laboratory, and treated as an environmental sample."
- 要点：现场空白是不含目标物的干净样品，带到采样现场、暴露于采样条件，再带回实验室，按环境样品处理。

### E15

- 位置：Appendix B 术语表，PDF 第 72 页
- 原文："matrix spike sample -- a sample prepared by adding a known amount of the target analyte to a specified amount of a matrix. Spiked samples are used, for example, to determine the effect of the matrix on a method's recovery efficiency."
- 要点：基质加标样是往一定量的样品基质中加入已知量的目标物，用来判断基质对方法回收率的影响。

### E16

- 位置：Appendix B 术语表，PDF 第 69 页
- 原文："collocated samples -- two or more portions collected at the same point in time and space so as to be considered identical. These samples are also known as field replicates and should be identified as such."
- 要点：同点样是在同一时间、同一地点采的两份以上、可视为相同的样品，也叫现场平行样。

### E17

- 位置：Appendix B 术语表，PDF 第 71 页
- 原文："environmental technology -- an all-inclusive term used to describe pollution control devices and systems, waste treatment processes and storage facilities, and site remediation technologies and their components that may be used to remove pollutants or contaminants from, or to prevent them from entering, the environment. Examples include wet scrubbers (air), soil washing (soil), granulated activated carbon unit (water), and filtration (air, water)."
- 要点：环境技术泛指污染控制设备和系统、废物处理与储存设施、场地修复技术，例如湿式洗涤器（空气）、土壤淋洗（土壤）、颗粒活性炭装置（水）、过滤（空气、水）。
