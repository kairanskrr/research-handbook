# 美国政治学会 2020：Principles and Guidance for Human Subjects Research

- 资料：`apsa2020humansubjects`，[原文 PDF](https://www.apsanet.org/Portals/54/diversity%20and%20inclusion%20prgms/Ethics/Final_Principles%20with%20Guidance%20with%20intro.pdf)
- 版本：美国政治学会（APSA）理事会 2020 年 4 月 4 日通过；12 条原则，每条附说明（Guidance）。APSA 官网免费提供 PDF。位置写原则编号和小标题。

### E1

- 位置：开头说明
- 原文："On April 4, 2020, at the 2020 Spring Council Meeting, the APSA Council approved and adopted the following Principles and Guidance for Human Subjects Research."
- 要点：这份原则由 APSA 理事会于 2020 年 4 月 4 日通过。

### E2

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："These principles are not intended to be rules, requirements, or prohibitions, and they are not presented as a checklist for ethical research."
- 要点：这些原则不是规则、要求或禁令，也不是伦理研究的打勾清单。

### E3

- 位置：General Principles, 2
- 原文："Political science researchers have an individual responsibility to consider the ethics of their research related activities and cannot outsource ethical reflection to review boards, other institutional bodies, or regulatory agencies."
- 要点：研究者本人要为研究伦理负责，不能把伦理思考"外包"给审查委员会或监管机构。

### E4

- 位置：Power, 4b
- 原文："When conducting research with powerful parties, including some public officials, other actors, institutions, and corporations, covert or deceptive research with more than minimal harm may sometimes be ethically permissible."
- 要点：研究有权力的一方（部分公职人员、机构、企业）时，带有超过最低风险的隐蔽或欺骗性研究，有时在伦理上可以允许。

### E5

- 位置：Guidance (Power)
- 原文："The new regulations no longer offer an exemption for research on elected and public officials."
- 要点：美国 2019 年 1 月起施行的新规定，不再对研究民选官员和公职人员给予豁免。

### E6

- 位置：Consent, 5
- 原文："Political science researchers should generally seek informed consent from individuals who are directly engaged by the research process, especially [...] research involves more than minimal risk of harm or if it is plausible to expect that engaged individuals would withhold consent if consent were sought"
- 要点：一般应征得直接参与者的知情同意，特别是风险超过最低程度、或可以预料对方若被询问会拒绝时。

### E7

- 位置：Guidance (Harm)
- 原文："a breach of confidentiality that reveals the identity of a dissident or her associates who were not studied;"
- 要点：评估伤害时要考虑传播研究结果造成的伤害，例如泄密暴露了异见人士或其未被研究的同伴的身份。

### E8

- 位置：Confidentiality, 9d
- 原文："If research materials are shared, researchers should ensure that the material is sufficiently redacted so that even well-informed parties cannot infer the identity of any person to whom specific statements or information can be attributed."
- 要点：如果共享研究材料，要充分删改，让即使了解内情的人也推断不出某句话是谁说的。

### E9

- 位置：Guidance (Confidentiality)
- 原文："Even if an editor requires data sharing as a condition of publication, this does not release the researcher from the ethical obligation to protect the confidentiality of their participants."
- 要点：即使编辑把共享数据作为发表条件，研究者保护参与者保密的伦理义务也不因此解除。

### E10

- 位置：Guidance (Confidentiality)
- 原文："Threats to confidentiality can come in many forms, including carelessness, data sharing, cybersecurity failures, freedom of information (FOIA) requests, subpoenas, and mandatory reporting."
- 要点：泄密可能来自粗心、数据共享、网络安全事故、信息公开申请、法院传票和强制报告等。

### E11

- 位置：Impact, 10
- 原文："In general, political science researchers should not compromise the integrity of political processes for research purposes without the consent of individuals that are directly engaged by the research process."
- 要点：一般来说，未经直接参与者同意，不应为研究目的损害政治过程的完整性。

### E12

- 位置：Guidance (Impact), Minimal Risks
- 原文："In democratic systems with widespread protections of political rights, such petition signing likely entails minimal risk of harm. However, in authoritarian systems, petition signers may plausibly be viewed as unwelcome critics of the government and might be targeted for reprisal."
- 要点：同样是邀请公民签请愿书，在政治权利受保护的民主体制下风险很低；在威权体制下，签名者可能被视为批评者而遭报复。

### E13

- 位置：Guidance (Impact), Minimal Risks
- 原文："An example of a study in which consent is not sought (and plausibly cannot be sought) but plausibly involves minimal risk, is one in which researchers place Facebook ads reproducing campaign advertising. Such interventions are minimal risk to individuals if they are similar to what individuals encounter in their usual lives and they are of minimal social risk if they are not done at a scale liable to alter electoral outcomes and do not inject false or misleading information into political processes."
- 要点：以在脸书（Facebook）上投放仿制竞选广告为例：干预与人们日常所见相似，对个人才算最低风险；规模不足以改变选举结果、又不向政治过程注入虚假信息，对社会才算最低风险。

### E14

- 位置：Guidance (Impact), Third parties
- 原文："collaborations do not transfer all ethical responsibility to the third party."
- 要点：和政府、选举机构等第三方合作，不等于把伦理责任全部转给对方。

### E15

- 位置：Guidance (Impact), Approvals
- 原文："Approval from electoral or other authorities can help provide justification but does not obviate researchers from all ethical considerations."
- 要点：选举机构等当局的批准可以提供部分理由，但不能免除研究者的全部伦理考量。

### E16

- 位置：Impact, 10d
- 原文："In publications and presentations, researchers should report likely impacts on the experiences of participants, any impacts on broader political processes, and whether and from whom they sought consent."
- 要点：发表时要报告对参与者的可能影响、对更广泛政治过程的影响，以及是否、向谁征求了同意。

### E17

- 位置：Laws, Regulations, and Prospective Review, 11
- 原文："When a research project is conducted in more than one jurisdiction, or when a scholar is based in one jurisdiction and conducts research in another, the researcher should be aware of relevant laws and regulations in each setting."
- 要点：在多个司法辖区做研究，或人在甲地、研究在乙地时，要了解每个地方的相关法律法规。

### E18

- 位置：Guidance (Trauma)
- 原文："Trauma may be more likely when research involves war or sexual violence, but trauma may emerge in a wide range of research settings."
- 要点：涉及战争或性暴力的研究更可能造成创伤，但创伤也可能出现在很多其他研究情境中。

### E19

- 位置：Confidentiality, 9b
- 原文："In some cases, it might be appropriate for researchers to not promise confidentiality (for example, research on powerful elites or oral histories in which the participants prefer to be identified). In these cases, researchers should clearly communicate the lack of confidentiality to the participants"
- 要点：有些情况下可以不承诺保密，例如研究有权势的精英，或参与者希望署名的口述史；这时要向参与者讲清楚不保密。

### E20

- 位置：Guidance (Impact), Impact arising from use of research
- 原文："there is generally no concern or harm if a book reaches conclusions unfavorable to a policy or political actor and these conclusions affect opinion and votes."
- 要点：研究结论对某项政策或某个政治人物不利、进而影响舆论和选票，一般不构成这条原则所说的问题。

### E21

- 位置：Impact, 10a
- 原文："Relevant considerations include when the research presents minimal risk of negatively affecting individual experience or impacting political outcomes, when researchers are studying powerful actors [...] or institutions, or when deception or misrepresentation is otherwise justifiable."
- 要点：未经同意而影响政治过程的研究，在什么情况下可能合适，要考虑：风险是否最低、研究对象是否为有权力的行为者或机构、欺骗是否另有正当理由。
