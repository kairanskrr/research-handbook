# 美国民族音乐学会 2008：Position Statement on Ethnographic Research & Institutional Review Boards

- 资料：`sem2008irb`，[原文](https://www.ethnomusicology.org/page/PS_IRB)（官网对脚本返回 403，原文文本取自 Internet Archive 2024 年快照）
- 版本：SEM 理事会 2008 年 1 月 16 日通过。针对美国大学的伦理审查委员会；文中引用的是 2018 年修订前的美国联邦"共同规则"（Common Rule）。网页。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：SEM Policy Guidelines for Institutional Review Boards
- 原文："on anonymity of research subjects and the insistence on destroying documentation at a project’s end are particularly antithetical to the kind of work ethnomusicologists and other ethnographers do."
- 要点：美国伦理审查规则强调匿名、要求项目结束后销毁记录，这与民族音乐学的工作方式格格不入（SEM 的立场）。

### E2

- 位置：SEM Policy Guidelines for Institutional Review Boards
- 原文："Each local situation dictates the culturally appropriate means of obtaining permission for conducting research and for documenting that research with audio and visual recording media."
- 要点：怎样取得研究许可和录音录像许可，要看当地文化中什么方式合适。

### E3

- 位置：SEM Policy Guidelines for Institutional Review Boards
- 原文："Research documentation, in the form of field notes, recordings, photographs, videos, and publications, is important not only to the immediate research project but to the historical record and should be archived and preserved"
- 要点：田野笔记、录音、照片、视频等研究记录，对历史记录也很重要，应当归档保存。
