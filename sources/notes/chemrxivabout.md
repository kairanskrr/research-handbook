# ChemRxiv：About ChemRxiv 与 FAQs for ChemRxiv Authors

- 资料：`chemrxivabout`，[About](https://chemrxiv.org/engage/assets/public/chemrxiv/term/about.htm)，[作者常见问题](https://chemrxiv.org/engage/assets/public/chemrxiv/term/author-faq.htm)
- 版本：ChemRxiv 官网两个说明页面。官网对脚本访问返回 403，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 快照（About：2025-08-12；作者常见问题：2025-10-15）。

### E1

- 位置：About，第 1 段
- 原文："ChemRxiv (pronounced 'chem-archive') is a free submission, distribution, and archive service for unpublished preprints in chemistry and related areas."
- 要点：ChemRxiv 是化学及相关领域未发表预印本的免费提交、发布和存档服务。

### E2

- 位置：About，筛查说明
- 原文："We conduct a basic screening for plagiarism, offensive language, non-scientific, and inappropriate content, and we will exclude materials that may pose a health or security risk if identified. Beyond this screening, the files are unedited and are not peer reviewed."
- 要点：ChemRxiv 只做基本筛查（抄袭、冒犯性语言、非科学和不当内容，以及可能带来健康或安全风险的材料），之外不编辑、不经同行评审。

### E3

- 位置：About，Scope
- 原文："Analytical chemistry" / "Organic chemistry" / "Physical chemistry" / "Theoretical and computational chemistry"
- 要点：投稿时要从一组分类中选择，包括分析化学、有机化学、物理化学、理论与计算化学等（共 16 类）。

### E4

- 位置：About，The ChemRxiv partnership
- 原文："ChemRxiv is co-owned and collaboratively managed by the American Chemical Society (ACS)"
- 要点：ChemRxiv 由美国化学会等化学学会共同拥有和管理（同段列出的还有中国化学会、日本化学会、德国化学会和英国皇家化学会）。

### E5

- 位置：作者常见问题，How is the content screened?
- 原文："This process is carried out by PhD chemists, but it is not considered peer review. We make no assessment of the accuracy, completeness, or importance of the science presented."
- 要点：筛查由有博士学位的化学家做，但不算同行评审，不评价内容是否正确、完整、重要。

### E6

- 位置：作者常见问题，Can I retract my content on ChemRxiv?
- 原文："Individual pieces of research content posted to ChemRxiv are citable and part of the scholarly record, so they cannot be removed from the site except in extremely rare circumstances."
- 要点：发到 ChemRxiv 的内容可被引用、属于学术记录，除极少数情况外不能删除。

### E7

- 位置：作者常见问题，期刊政策
- 原文："Although most journals accept content posted as a preprint prior to submission, some journals or publishers may place restrictions on the posting of certain prepublication content. We recommend that you check the policies of any publishers to which you intend to submit your work for formal publication, in advance of uploading content to ChemRxiv."
- 要点：ChemRxiv 说大多数期刊接受投稿前发过预印本的稿件，但有的期刊有限制；建议上传前先查目标出版社的政策。
