# Columbia Public Health 2023：Content Analysis

- 资料：`columbia2023contentanalysis`，[原文网页](https://www.publichealth.columbia.edu/research/population-health-methods/content-analysis)
- 版本：哥伦比亚大学梅尔曼公共卫生学院"人群健康方法"（Population Health Methods）系列的公开方法介绍页，页面元数据显示最后修改于 2023 年 3 月 30 日。面向公共卫生研究者。位置写网页小标题。注意：该页说"一般 80% 是可接受的信度"，没说明是哪种指标；Lombard 等人和 Krippendorff 都反对只用百分比一致率，正文不采用这一句。

### E1

- 位置：Overview
- 原文："Content analysis is a research tool used to determine the presence of certain words, themes, or concepts within some given qualitative data (i.e. text). Using content analysis, researchers can quantify and analyze the presence, meanings, and relationships of such certain words, themes, or concepts."
- 要点：内容分析用来确定某些词、主题或概念在文本中是否出现，并可以量化、分析它们的出现、含义和相互关系。

### E2

- 位置：Overview
- 原文："Researchers can then make inferences about the messages within the texts, the writer(s), the audience, and even the culture and time of surrounding the text."
- 要点：研究者可以据此推断文本中的信息、作者、受众，乃至文本所处的文化和时代。

### E3

- 位置：Description
- 原文："Sources of data could be from interviews, open-ended questions, field research notes, conversations, or literally any occurrence of communicative language (such as books, essays, discussions, newspaper headlines, speeches, media, historical documents)."
- 要点：数据可以来自访谈、开放式问题、田野笔记、对话，或任何交流性语言，如书籍、讨论、新闻标题、演讲、媒体、历史文件。

### E4

- 位置：Types of Content Analysis
- 原文："There are two general types of content analysis: conceptual analysis and relational analysis. Conceptual analysis determines the existence and frequency of concepts in a text. Relational analysis develops the conceptual analysis further by examining the relationships among concepts in a text."
- 要点：内容分析有两大类：概念分析确定概念在文本中是否出现、出现多少次；关系分析进一步考察概念之间的关系。

### E5

- 位置：Conceptual Analysis，General steps，第 1 步
- 原文："Decide the level of analysis: word, word sense, phrase, sentence, themes"
- 要点：先决定分析层次：词、词义、短语、句子还是主题。

### E6

- 位置：Conceptual Analysis，General steps，第 2 步
- 原文："Decide how many concepts to code for: develop a pre-defined or interactive set of categories or concepts. Decide either: A. to allow flexibility to add categories through the coding process, or B. to stick with the pre-defined set of categories."
- 要点：决定编码哪些概念：要么在编码过程中允许增加类别，要么坚持事先定好的类别。

### E7

- 位置：Conceptual Analysis，General steps，第 3 步
- 原文："Decide whether to code for existence or frequency of a concept. The decision changes the coding process."
- 要点：决定编码的是概念"是否出现"还是"出现几次"，这会改变编码过程。

### E8

- 位置：Conceptual Analysis，General steps，第 4–5 步
- 原文："What level of implication is to be allowed? Words that imply the concept or words that explicitly state the concept?" / "Develop rules for coding your texts. After decisions of steps 1-4 are complete, a researcher can begin developing rules for translation of text into codes. This will keep the coding process organized and consistent."
- 要点：要决定允许多大程度的隐含（只算明说的，还是暗示的也算），然后写出把文本转换成编码的规则，让编码有条理、前后一致。

### E9

- 位置：Conceptual Analysis，General steps，第 6 步
- 原文："Decide what to do with irrelevant information: should this be ignored (e.g. common English words like “the” and “and”), or used to reexamine the coding scheme in the case that it would add to the outcome of coding?"
- 要点：事先决定怎样处理和研究问题无关的内容：忽略，还是用来重新检查编码方案。

### E10

- 位置：Relational Analysis
- 原文："Select text for analysis carefully by balancing having enough information for a thorough analysis so results are not limited with having information that is too extensive so that the coding process becomes too arduous and heavy to supply meaningful and worthwhile results."
- 要点：选文本时要平衡：信息要足够做彻底的分析，又不能多到编码不堪重负。

### E11

- 位置：Reliability and Validity，Validity，Conclusions
- 原文："Software can obtain an accurate count of that word's occurrence and frequency, but not be able to produce an accurate accounting of the meaning inherent in each particular usage."
- 要点：软件能准确数出一个词出现了多少次，却分不清每次用法的含义（例如 "mine" 既是代词，也是地雷，也是矿井）。

### E12

- 位置：Disadvantages of Content Analysis
- 原文："Is often devoid of theoretical base, or attempts too liberally to draw meaningful inferences about the relationships and impacts implied in a study" / "Tends too often to simply consist of word counts" / "Often disregards the context that produced the text, as well as the state of things after the text is produced"
- 要点：内容分析常见的缺点：缺乏理论基础、推断过度，常常只是数词频，忽视文本产生的背景。
