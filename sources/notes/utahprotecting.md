# University of Utah TLO：Protecting Your Research

- 资料：`utahprotecting`，[原文](https://technologylicensing.utah.edu/news-events/resources-opportunities/protecting-your-research)
- 版本：犹他大学技术许可办公室网页，2026-10-06 读取。内容按美国法律和该校政策写成。

### E1

- 位置：What counts as public disclosure — Journal publications
- 原文："Journal publications: The clock starts when the article publishes, not when you submit it. The peer review process remains confidential, giving you time to file patent protection."
- 要点：期刊论文从正式发表时才算公开，投稿和保密审稿期间不算，这段时间可以去申请专利。

### E2

- 位置：What counts as public disclosure — Online preprint servers
- 原文："Online preprint servers: Unlike journal submissions, these create immediate public disclosure the moment you upload."
- 要点：预印本一上传就构成公开。

### E3

- 位置：What counts as public disclosure — Conference presentations
- 原文："Conference presentations: Both the presentation itself and any published abstracts count as public disclosures."
- 要点：会议报告本身和公开的摘要都算公开。

### E4

- 位置：What counts as public disclosure — Thesis and dissertation defenses
- 原文："Thesis and dissertation defenses: These are considered public disclosures by law, even if only university personnel attend."
- 要点：在美国，学位论文答辩依法算公开，即使只有校内人员参加。

### E5

- 位置：Provisional applications
- 原文："Provisional applications are informal placeholders that can be filed quickly - often within days. The TLO can file your manuscript, poster, or even PowerPoint slides directly with the USPTO to establish your priority date."
- 要点：美国的"临时申请"可以在几天内提交，甚至直接用论文稿、海报或幻灯片，先占住优先权日期。
