# Hussey & Hughes 2020：Hidden Invalidity Among 15 Commonly Used Measures in Social and Personality Psychology

- 资料：`hussey2020hidden`，[预印本（PsyArXiv）](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/7rbfp)；[正式版（开放获取）](https://doi.org/10.1177/2515245919882903)
- 版本：PsyArXiv 预印本 PDF；正式版发表于 Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science 3(2):166–184（2020）。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："When the scales were assessed using the modal practice of considering only internal consistency, 89% of them appeared to possess good validity. Yet when validity was assessed comprehensively (via internal consistency, immediate and delayed test-retest reliability, factor structure, and measurement invariance for age and gender groups), only 4% demonstrated good validity." / "We comprehensively assessed structural validity in a uniquely large and varied data set (N = 144,496 experimental sessions) to investigate the psychometric properties of some of the most widely used self-report measures (k = 15 questionnaires, 26 scales)"
- 要点：用 14 万多次实验会话的数据检验社会与人格心理学常用的 15 份问卷（26 个分量表）：只看内部一致性，89% 看起来效度良好；综合检验内部一致性、重测信度、因子结构和跨年龄、性别的测量不变性后，只有 4% 良好。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："The first, the substantive phase, involves identifying and defining a construct" / "Quantitative analyses (e.g., item and factor analyses; assessments of consistency, stability, and measurement invariance) are used to determine the psychometric properties of the measure. The third phase, the external phase, involves examining if the measure appropriately represents the construct via checks for convergent and discriminant validity with other measures"
- 要点：构念验证分三个阶段：内容阶段（定义构念、编题）、结构阶段（题目分析、因子分析、一致性、稳定性、测量不变性）、外部阶段（与其他测量的聚合和区分效度等）。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："they found an overreliance on Cronbach's α as the sole source of evidence for structural validity"
- 要点：Flake 等人 2017 年的检查发现，研究者过度依赖 Cronbach α 作为结构效度的唯一证据。

### E4

- 位置：Purpose of the Present Study
- 原文："the most common test (α) relies on numerous assumptions that can be assessed only by less commonly applied analyses (e.g., within a CFA context)"
- 要点：最常用的 α 依赖许多假设，这些假设只能用较少人做的分析（如验证性因子分析）来检验。

### E5

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："if evidence for measurement invariance is not obtained--which is typically the case--then it is difficult to determine if a given measure reflects the same construct across samples, contexts, and conditions"
- 要点：没有测量不变性的证据（通常就是没有），就难以判断一个量表在不同样本、情境中测的是不是同一个构念。

### E6

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："In short, replicability does not equal validity."
- 要点：结果可重复，不等于测量有效。

### E7

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："we refer to the first practice as v-hacking: selectively choosing and reporting a combination of metrics, including their implementations and cutoffs, and taking advantage of other degrees of experimenter freedom so as to improve the apparent validity of measures."
- 要点："效度操纵"（v-hacking）：有选择地挑指标、算法和阈值来报告，让量表看起来更有效。

### E8

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："We are not advocating for the introduction of some set of universally applied metrics or cutoff values for those metrics."
- 要点：作者并不主张设一套所有人通用的指标和阈值。

### E9

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："Researcher degrees of freedom could be greatly constrained by expanding the practice of preregistration to also include choices concerning the assessment of structural validity (e.g., metrics, cutoffs, measurement models, and decision-making strategies)."
- 要点：把结构效度的评估方案（指标、阈值、测量模型、决策规则）也写进预注册，可以大大减少研究者的灵活空间。

### E10

- 位置：Purpose of the Present Study
- 原文："many self-report scales are being used in contexts, and with samples, that differ from those in which they were originally validated."
- 要点：很多自评量表被用在与原验证研究不同的情境和样本中。
