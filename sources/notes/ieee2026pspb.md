# IEEE 2026：IEEE Publication Services and Products Board Operations Manual

- 资料：`ieee2026pspb`，[原文](https://pspb.ieee.org/images/files/files/opsmanual.pdf)
- 版本：IEEE 出版服务与产品委员会（Publication Services and Products Board，PSPB）运行手册，2026 年 6 月 25 日修订版，2026-10-06 下载 PDF。第 2.7 节全文收录了《IEEE 伦理准则》（IEEE Policies 7.8，2020 年 6 月版）。位置写小节号和页码。

### E1

- 位置：§2.7，转载 IEEE Policies 7.8《IEEE 伦理准则》第 1 条，第 23–24 页
- 原文："to hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public, to strive to comply with ethical design and sustainable development practices, to protect the privacy of others, and to disclose promptly factors that might endanger the public or the environment;"
- 要点：IEEE 伦理准则第 1 条：公众的安全、健康和福祉放在首位；遵守合乎伦理的设计；保护他人隐私；发现可能危害公众或环境的因素要及时披露。

### E2

- 位置：§2.7，《IEEE 伦理准则》第 3 条，第 24 页
- 原文："to avoid real or perceived conflicts of interest whenever possible, and to disclose them to affected parties when they do exist;"
- 要点：尽量避免实际的或看起来存在的利益冲突；确实存在时，要向受影响的各方披露。

### E3

- 位置：§2.7，《IEEE 伦理准则》第 5 条，第 24 页
- 原文："to seek, accept, and offer honest criticism of technical work, to acknowledge and correct errors, to be honest and realistic in stating claims or estimates based on available data, and to credit properly the contributions of others;"
- 要点：欢迎并给出诚实的技术批评；承认并改正错误；根据已有数据陈述结论或估计时要诚实、实事求是；恰当承认他人的贡献。

### E4

- 位置：§8.1.7.F，第 91 页
- 原文："it is common in technical publishing for material to be presented at various stages of its evolution, such as publishing early ideas in a workshop or as a draft article posted as a Preprint (see Subsection 8.1.9.A.2.d), more developed work in a conference, and fully developed contributions as journal or transactions articles."
- 要点：IEEE 认可"逐步发表"：早期想法发在研讨会或预印本上，较成熟的工作发会议，完全成熟的成果发期刊或汇刊（Transactions）。

### E5

- 位置：§8.1.7.F，第 91–92 页
- 原文："IEEE recognizes the importance of this evolutionary publication process as a significant means of scientific communication and fully supports this publishing paradigm, but IEEE requires that this evolutionary process be fully referenced." / "all the articles published in the periodical contain substantial additional technical material with respect to the conference article or articles of which they represent an evolution."
- 要点：IEEE 支持把会议论文发展成期刊论文，但要求完整引用前面的版本，而且期刊版本必须比会议论文多出实质性的技术内容。

### E6

- 位置：§8.2.1.A.1，第 104 页
- 原文："Made a significant intellectual contribution to the theoretical development, system or experimental design, prototype development, and/or the analysis and interpretation of data associated with the work contained in the article;"
- 要点：IEEE 署名条件之一：在理论推导、系统或实验设计、原型开发、数据分析与解释中，至少一项做出重要的智力贡献。原型开发被明确列为可以构成署名资格的贡献。

### E7

- 位置：§8.2.1.A.1，第 104 页
- 原文："Contributed to drafting the article or reviewing and/or revising it for intellectual content; and" / "Approved the final version of the article as accepted for publication, including references."
- 要点：署名还必须同时满足：参与起草或在内容上修改论文；认可最终录用版本（包括参考文献）。几条要同时满足。

### E8

- 位置：§8.2.1.A.2，第 105 页
- 原文："In articles with multiple authorship, the order of the authors shall be at the discretion of the authors."
- 要点：IEEE 不规定作者排序规则，由作者自己决定。

### E9

- 位置：§8.2.1.B.3，第 106 页
- 原文："Methods and materials should be described in sufficient detail to permit evaluation and replication."
- 要点：方法和材料要写到足以让别人评估和重做的程度。

### E10

- 位置：§8.2.1.B.2，第 106 页
- 原文："Financial support of the work being reported and of the authors should be clearly acknowledged in the article, as well as any potential conflict of interest."
- 要点：资助来源和任何潜在的利益冲突，都要在论文里写清楚。

### E11

- 位置：§8.2.1.B.9，第 106 页
- 原文："IEEE defines plagiarism as the use of someone else's work (for example, use of text, prior ideas, processes, results, or algorithms) without explicitly acknowledging the original author and/or source."
- 要点：IEEE 对抄袭的定义：使用他人的文字、想法、流程、结果或算法，却不明确注明原作者或出处。

### E12

- 位置：§8.2.1.B.12，第 107 页
- 原文："If authors have used their own previously published work(s) as a basis for a new submission, they are required to cite the previous work(s) and very briefly indicate how the new submission offers substantive novel contributions beyond those of the previously published work(s)."
- 要点：在自己已发表工作的基础上写新稿，必须引用旧作，并简要说明新稿有哪些实质性的新贡献。

### E13

- 位置：§8.2.1.B.16，第 107 页
- 原文："An author, in offering an article for presentation at an IEEE conference, or accepting an invitation to present an article, is expected to be present at the meeting to deliver the article." / "IEEE reserves the right to exclude an article from distribution after the conference (e.g., not placed on IEEE Xplore) if the article is not presented at the conference."
- 要点：IEEE 会议论文要求作者到会报告；没有报告的论文，IEEE 可以不把它放进会后发行（例如不收进 IEEE Xplore 数据库）。

### E14

- 位置：§8.2.1.B.8，第 106 页
- 原文："Authors have an obligation to correct errors promptly."
- 要点：作者有义务及时更正错误。

### E15

- 位置：§4.3.7 TechRxiv Editorial Advisory Board Charter，第 58 页
- 原文："The TechRxiv Editorial Advisory Board is an organizational unit of, and reports to, PSPB." / "is an open, moderated preprint server for unpublished research in general areas of engineering technology and practice, particularly as they relate to the IEEE Field of Interest."
- 要点：TechRxiv 是 IEEE 管理的开放预印本网站，面向工程技术领域的未发表研究。
