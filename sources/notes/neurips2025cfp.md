# NeurIPS 2025：Call for Papers

- 资料：`neurips2025cfp`，[原文](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers)
- 版本：NeurIPS 2025 征稿启事，2026-10-05 读取。位置写网页小标题。只作为机器学习方向一个顶级会议的例子。

### E1

- 位置：页首日期列表
- 原文："Abstract submission deadline: May 11, 2025 AoE [...] Full paper submission deadline: May 15, 2025 AOE"
- 要点：先交摘要，几天后交全文，截止时间按"地球上任何地方"（AoE）时区计算。

### E2

- 位置：Paper Formatting Instructions
- 原文："The main text of a submitted paper is limited to nine content pages, including all figures and tables."
- 要点：正文限 9 页（含图表）。

### E3

- 位置：Paper Checklist
- 原文："authors are required to complete a paper checklist."
- 要点：投稿必须填写论文检查清单（涵盖可复现性、透明度、伦理和社会影响）。

### E4

- 位置：Double-blind Reviewing
- 原文："All submissions must be anonymized and may not contain any identifying information that may violate the double-blind reviewing policy."
- 要点：双盲评审：投稿（含补充材料和代码链接）不能暴露作者身份。

### E5

- 位置：Preprints
- 原文："The existence of non-anonymous preprints (on arXiv or other online repositories, personal websites, social media) will not result in rejection. [...] Reviewers will be instructed not to actively look for such preprints"
- 要点：在 arXiv 等处发了署名的预印本不会因此被拒；审稿人被要求不要主动去搜。

### E6

- 位置：Preprints
- 原文："Note that public versions of the submission should not say "Under review at NeurIPS" or similar."
- 要点：公开版本不要写"正在 NeurIPS 审稿中"之类的话。

### E7

- 位置：Dual Submissions
- 原文："Papers previously presented at workshops are permitted, so long as they did not appear in conference proceedings"
- 要点：在没有正式论文集的研讨会（workshop）上报告过的论文可以投；进了论文集的就算已发表。

### E8

- 位置：Author Responses
- 原文："Authors will have one week to view and respond to initial reviews."
- 要点：作者有一周时间阅读并回复初审意见。

### E9

- 位置：Publication of Accepted Submissions
- 原文："Reviews, meta-reviews, and any discussion with the authors will be made public for accepted papers"
- 要点：录用论文的审稿意见和讨论会公开。

### E10

- 位置：Publication of Accepted Submissions
- 原文："We strongly encourage accompanying code and data to be submitted with accepted papers when appropriate"
- 要点：鼓励录用论文同时提交代码和数据。

### E11

- 位置：Author Responses
- 原文："Authors may not submit revisions of their paper or supplemental material, but may post their responses as a discussion in OpenReview."
- 要点：回复期内不能提交修改后的论文或补充材料，只能在审稿平台上以讨论的形式发回复。

### E12

- 位置：Author Responses
- 原文："After the initial response period, authors will be able to respond to any further reviewer/AC questions and comments by posting on the submission's forum page."
- 要点：初次回复期之后，作者还可以在论文的讨论页上回答审稿人和领域主席的追问。

### E13

- 位置：Author Responses
- 原文："After the notification deadline, accepted and opted-in rejected papers will be made public and open for non-anonymous public commenting. Their anonymous reviews, meta-reviews, author responses and reviewer responses will also be made public."
- 要点：结果公布后，录用的论文和自愿公开的被拒论文会公开，连同审稿意见、元审稿意见和作者回复。

### E14

- 位置：Dual Submissions
- 原文："The NeurIPS policy on dual submissions applies for the entire duration of the reviewing process."
- 要点：一稿多投的禁令在整个审稿过程中都有效。
