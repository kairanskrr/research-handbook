# Ernst：How to write a technical paper or a research paper

- 资料：`ernstwrite`，[原文](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~mernst/advice/write-technical-paper.html)
- 版本：作者主页文章，2005 年 4 月，最后更新 2024-07-01。网页，位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：开头（目录之前的引言部分）
- 原文："Equally importantly, writing papers and giving talks will clarify your thinking and thereby improve your research."
- 要点：写论文、做报告会迫使你更深入地理解自己的想法，从而改进研究本身。

### E2

- 位置：Know your message, and stay on message
- 原文："As a general rule, your paper needs to convince the audience of three key points. If any of these is missing or unclear, the paper will not be compelling."
- 要点：论文要让读者相信三点：问题重要；问题难（显而易见的方法和已有方法不够）；你解决了它。还要说明你的贡献是新的。

### E3

- 位置：Know your message, and stay on message / Which details to include
- 原文："A common mistake is to focus on what you spent the most time on."
- 要点：不要按时间顺序叙述你试过的所有事，也不要按花费时间分配篇幅；论文要告诉读者成功的结果，并说服他们这些结论成立。

### E4

- 位置：Make the organization and results clear
- 原文："Another way of saying this is that you should give away the punchline. A technical paper is not a joke or a mystery novel."
- 要点：一开始就亮出结论，技术论文不是笑话或悬疑小说，读者不该遇到意外，只该看到对已介绍想法的深入展开。

### E5

- 位置：Make the organization and results clear
- 原文："Instead, state the point first and then support it."
- 要点：在章节和段落层面同样先说观点，再给支撑。

### E6

- 位置：Make the organization and results clear
- 原文："However, I prefer to write these sections early in the process (and then revise them as needed), because they frame the paper."
- 要点：有人习惯最后写摘要和引言；Ernst 主张早写、再按需修改，因为它们框定了整篇论文。

### E7

- 位置：Writing style
- 原文："Avoid puffery, self-congratulation, superlatives, and subjective or value judgments: give the objective facts and let the reader judge."
- 要点：不要自吹、不要用最高级和主观评价，给出客观事实让读者判断；满篇"新颖""显著"往往是在掩盖证据不足。

### E8

- 位置：Getting started: overcoming writer's block and procrastination
- 原文："The key idea is to write something, and you can improve it later."
- 要点：克服空白页的关键是先写点东西，以后再改；可以先口头讲、写提纲、写随想笔记、分块写。

### E9

- 位置：Know your message, and stay on message / Which details to include
- 原文："If any part of the paper does not support your main point, then delete or change that part."
- 要点：不支持主要观点的部分，无论多正确，都要删掉或改写。

### E10

- 位置：Brevity
- 原文："Make every word count."
- 要点：逐节、逐段、逐句、逐词检查，凡是不支持论点的都删掉。

### E11

- 位置：Make the organization and results clear
- 原文："Some people like to write the abstract, and often also the introduction, last. Doing so makes them easier to write, because the rest of the paper is already complete and can just be described."
- 要点：有人习惯最后写摘要和引言，因为那时正文已经完成，照着描述就行，写起来更容易。
