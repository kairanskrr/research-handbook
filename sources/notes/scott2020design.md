# Scott, Wenderoth & Doherty 2020：Design-Based Research: A Methodology to Extend and Enrich Biology Education Research

- 资料：`scott2020design`，[原文（PubMed Central）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8711803/)，DOI [10.1187/cbe.19-11-0245](https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.19-11-0245)
- 版本：CBE—Life Sciences Education 19(3): es11，2020；开放获取，PubMed Central 全文。位置写文中小标题。

### E1

- 位置：What Is Design-Based Research?
- 原文："Consequently, investigators using design-based research approach educational inquiry much as an engineer develops a new product: First, the researchers identify a problem that needs to be addressed (e.g., a particular learning challenge that students face)."
- 要点：用基于设计的研究，就像工程师开发新产品：先找出一个需要解决的问题，例如学生面临的某个学习难点。

### E2

- 位置：What Is Design-Based Research?
- 原文："Though we have portrayed four discrete phases to design-based research, there is often overlap of the phases as the research progresses (e.g., testing and evaluating can occur simultaneously)."
- 要点：作者把一轮画成设计、检验、评估、反思四个阶段，但实际上各阶段常常重叠，例如检验和评估可以同时进行。

### E3

- 位置：What Is Design-Based Research?
- 原文："3) Design-based research should generate design principles that guide the development and implementation of future instructional tools"
- 要点：作者转述 Sandoval 2014 的"认识论承诺"之三：要产出设计原则，指导今后教学工具的开发和实施。

### E4

- 位置：What Is Design-Based Research?
- 原文："4) Design-based research should be enacted using extended, iterative teaching experiments in classrooms."
- 要点：承诺之四：要在课堂里做持续较长时间、反复迭代的教学实验。

### E5

- 位置：How Is Design-Based Research Different from an Experimental Approach?
- 原文："In design-based research, both researchers and instructors are engaged in all stages of the research from conception to reflection"
- 要点：在基于设计的研究里，研究者和教师从构想到反思都参与每个阶段。

### E6

- 位置：How Is Design-Based Research Different from an Experimental Approach?
- 原文："In this case, if the research questions being investigated produce generalizable results that have the potential to impact teaching broadly, then this is consistent with a design-based research approach"
- 要点：研究者同时是任课教师时，如果研究问题能产出可推广、可能广泛影响教学的结果，就符合基于设计的研究。

### E7

- 位置：How Is Design-Based Research Different from an Experimental Approach?
- 原文："However, when the research questions are self-reflective about how a researcher/instructor can improve his or her own classroom practices, this aligns more closely with "action research,""
- 要点：如果研究问题是反思"我怎样改进自己的课堂"，就更接近行动研究。

### E8

- 位置：How Is Design-Based Research Different from an Experimental Approach?
- 原文："This involves collecting data from multiple sources as the research progresses, including how the instructional tools were implemented, aspects of the implementation process that failed to go as planned, and how the instructional tools or implementation process was modified."
- 要点：研究过程中要从多个来源收集数据，包括工具实际怎样实施、哪些地方没按计划进行、工具或实施过程怎样被修改。

### E9

- 位置：How Is Design-Based Research Different from an Experimental Approach?
- 原文："In experimental research, the intervention is fixed throughout the experimental period, with any revisions occurring only after the experiment has concluded."
- 要点：在实验研究中，干预在整个实验期间固定不变，修改只能在实验结束后进行。

### E10

- 位置：How Is Design-Based Research Different from an Experimental Approach?
- 原文："By contrast, design-based research takes a more flexible approach that allows instructional tools to be modified in situ as they are being implemented"
- 要点：基于设计的研究更灵活，允许教学工具在实施过程中就地修改。

### E11

- 位置：How Is Design-Based Research Different from an Experimental Approach?
- 原文："According to Sandoval (2014) , "Design research, as a means of uncovering causal processes, is oriented not to finding effects but to finding functions , to understanding how desired (and undesired) effects arise through interactions in a designed environment" (p. 30)."
- 要点：作者引用 Sandoval 2014：设计研究不是为了找"效果"，而是为了找"作用方式"，理解想要的（和不想要的）效果怎样在设计好的环境中产生。

### E12

- 位置：Identifying the Learning Problem
- 原文："The process of identifying a learning problem usually emerges from a researcher's own experiences (in or outside a classroom) or from previous research that has been described in the literature"
- 要点：学习问题通常来自研究者自己的经验，或来自已有文献。

### E13

- 位置：Identifying the Learning Problem
- 原文："To remain true to Sandoval's first epistemic commitment, a learning problem must advance a theory of learning"
- 要点：按 Sandoval 的第一条承诺，选的学习问题必须能推进某种学习理论。

### E14

- 位置：Design-Based Research Cycle 1, Phase 1: Designing Instructional Tools
- 原文："Indeed, this alignment between theory, the nature of the instructional tools, and the ways students are assessed is central to ensuring rigorous design-based research"
- 要点：理论、教学工具和评估学生的方式三者对得上，是保证这类研究严谨的核心。

### E15

- 位置：Phase 2: Testing the Instructional Tools
- 原文："During this phase, if necessary, the design team may make adjustments to the tools as they are being used to account for these unanticipated conditions"
- 要点：检验阶段如有必要，设计团队可以在使用中调整工具，以应对没预料到的情况。

### E16

- 位置：Phase 3: Evaluating the Instructional Tools
- 原文："This can be done using products produced by students (e.g., homework, lab reports), attitudinal gains measured with surveys, participation rates in activities, interview testimonials, classroom discourse practices, and formative assessment or exam data"
- 要点：评估可以用学生作品（作业、实验报告）、问卷测得的态度变化、活动参与率、访谈、课堂话语，以及形成性评估或考试数据。

### E17

- 位置：Phase 3: Evaluating the Instructional Tools
- 原文："Regardless of the source, evidence must be in a form that supports a systematic analysis that could be scrutinized by other researchers"
- 要点：无论数据来自哪里，证据的形式都要支持系统分析，能让其他研究者审查。

### E18

- 位置：Phase 4: Reflecting on the Instructional Tools and Their Implementation
- 原文："How was the theory underpinning the research advanced by the research endeavor (theoretical outcome)? Did the instructional tools support student learning about the learning problem (practical outcome)? What were the critical features of the design solution that supported student learning (design principles)?"
- 要点：回溯分析要回答三个问题：理论推进了什么（理论成果）？工具是否帮助了学生学习（实践成果）？设计中哪些关键特征起了作用（设计原则）？

### E19

- 位置：Practical Outcome (Epistemic Commitment 2)
- 原文："When we reviewed notes of the Spring 2017 implementation scheme, we saw that the pretest was due at the end of the first day of class after students had been exposed to ion flux ideas in class and in a reading/video assignment about ion flow, which may be one reason for the students' high performance on the pretest."
- 要点：作者回看 2017 年春季的实施记录，发现前测是在第一天上课、学生已经接触相关内容之后才截止的，这可能是前测成绩异常高的原因之一。

### E20

- 位置：Practical Outcome (Epistemic Commitment 2)
- 原文："It also indicated we needed to close pretests before the first day of class for a more accurate measure of students' incoming ideas and the effectiveness of the instructional tools employed at the beginning of the unit."
- 要点：由此他们知道，下一轮要在第一天上课前就关闭前测。

### E21

- 位置：Design Principles (Epistemic Commitment 3)
- 原文："Therefore, design-based research should produce design principles that describe characteristics of learning environments that researchers and instructors can use to develop instructional tools specific to their local contexts"
- 要点：设计原则要描述学习环境的特征，让其他研究者和教师能据此开发适合自己情境的教学工具。

### E22

- 位置：Design Principles (Epistemic Commitment 3)
- 原文："Consequently, the design principles must balance specificity with adaptability so they can be used broadly to inform instruction"
- 要点：设计原则要在具体和可调整之间取得平衡，才能广泛地指导教学。

### E23

- 位置：Design Principles (Epistemic Commitment 3)
- 原文："2) Instructional tools should explicitly articulate the principle of interest, with specific guidance on how that principle is applied in context."
- 要点：作者第一轮得出的设计原则之一举例：教学工具要明确讲出目标原理，并具体指导怎样在情境中应用它。

### E24

- 位置：Collaborations Can Be Difficult to Maintain
- 原文："Moreover, the iterative nature of design-based research requires these collaborations to persist for an extended period of time. Attending to these challenges is an important part of forming the design team and identifying the different roles researchers and instructors will play in the research."
- 要点：迭代的性质要求合作维持很长时间；组建团队时就要正视这些困难，并明确研究者和教师各自的角色。

### E25

- 位置：Design-Based Research Experiments Are Resource Intensive
- 原文："Because of the large variety of data streams that could be collected in a design-based research experiment—and the resources required to collect and analyze them—it is critical that the research team identify a priori how specific data streams, and the methods of their analysis, will provide the evidence necessary to address the theoretical and practical objectives of the research"
- 要点：可收集的数据种类很多、成本也高，所以研究团队必须事先确定：哪些数据、用什么分析方法，来为理论和实践目标提供证据。

### E26

- 位置：Concerns with Experimental Rigor
- 原文："One way design-based researchers address this concern is by "specifying theoretically salient features of a learning environment design and mapping out how they are predicted to work together to produce desired outcomes" ( Sandoval, 2014 , p. 19)."
- 要点：回应严谨性质疑的一个办法（引 Sandoval 2014 的"猜想图"思路）：列出设计中理论上关键的特征，并画出预计它们怎样共同产生期望的结果。

### E27

- 位置：Concerns with Experimental Rigor
- 原文："Through this process, researchers explicitly show before they begin the work how their theory of learning is embodied in the instructional tools to be tested, the specific data the tools will produce for analysis, and what outcomes will be taken as evidence for success."
- 要点：这样，研究者在动手之前就讲明：学习理论怎样体现在工具里、工具会产生哪些可分析的数据、什么结果算成功的证据。

### E28

- 位置：Design-Based Research Experiments Are Resource Intensive
- 原文："The focus of design-based research on studying learning ecologies to uncover mechanisms of learning requires that researchers collect multiple data streams through time, which often necessitates significant temporal and financial resources"
- 要点：要揭示学习机制，就要长时间收集多种数据，常常需要大量时间和经费。

### E29

- 位置：Design-Based Research Fosters Collaborations across Disciplines
- 原文："It also gives instructors agency to make informed adjustments to the instructional tools during implementation that improve their practical applications while remaining true to the goals of the research"
- 要点：让教师成为团队一员，使他们能在实施中做有依据的调整，既更实用，又不偏离研究目标。

### E30

- 位置：How Is Design-Based Research Different from an Experimental Approach?
- 原文："In an experimental approach, the researcher is responsible for making all the decisions about how the experiment will be implemented and analyzed, while the instructor facilitates the experimental treatments."
- 要点：在实验研究中，研究者决定实验怎样实施和分析，教师只负责执行实验处理。

### E31

- 位置：What Is Design-Based Research?
- 原文："This leads to another research cycle of designing, testing, evaluating, and reflecting to refine the instructional tools in support of student learning."
- 要点：一轮结束后进入下一轮"设计、检验、评估、反思"，继续改进教学工具。
