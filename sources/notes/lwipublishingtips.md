# 美国法律写作学会：Publishing Tips

- 资料：`lwipublishingtips`，[原文 PDF](https://www.lwionline.org/sites/default/files/Publishing-Tips.pdf)
- 版本：美国法律写作学会（Legal Writing Institute，美国法律写作教师的专业组织）网站上的投稿指南，共 8 页，未注明日期，自称"持续更新的稿件"。讲的是向**美国法律评论**投稿。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Deciding Which Journals to Submit to
- 原文："There are hundreds of law reviews. Most law reviews are student run."
- 要点：美国有数百家法律评论，大多数由学生运营。

### E2

- 位置：Deciding Which Journals to Submit to
- 原文："You can submit your article to multiple law reviews at the same time."
- 要点：可以把一篇文章同时投给多家法律评论。

### E3

- 位置：Deciding Which Journals to Submit to
- 原文："A few journals, e.g., the Journal of Legal Education, require exclusive submissions"
- 要点：少数期刊（如《法学教育杂志》）要求独家投稿，不能同时投别处。

### E4

- 位置：Deciding Which Journals to Submit to
- 原文："The vast majority of journals, however, do not require or indicate a preference for exclusive submissions."
- 要点：绝大多数期刊不要求、也不表示偏好独家投稿。

### E5

- 位置：Expediting Your Submission
- 原文："In practice, many top journals may not read your manuscript until they get an "expedited review" request."
- 要点：实际上，很多顶级期刊可能要等收到"加速审阅"请求才读你的稿子。

### E6

- 位置：Expediting Your Submission
- 原文："If you receive multiple publication offers, you should accept one offer and reject your other offer(s)."
- 要点：收到多个录用时，接受一个，拒绝其余。

### E7

- 位置：What to Include in a Cover Letter
- 原文："SSRN downloads (if a lot), inclusion on SSRN Top Ten Downloads lists"
- 要点：投稿信里可以写文章已获得的关注，例如 SSRN 下载量（如果很多）、进入 SSRN 下载排行。

### E8

- 位置：The Publication Process
- 原文："The time from when you accept a publication offer to when the article is published can range from a few months to a couple of years (in extreme cases)."
- 要点：从接受录用到正式发表，可能是几个月，极端情况下要一两年。

### E9

- 位置：Expediting Your Submission
- 原文："You should do your best to keep track of the law reviews that have rejected your article, so as to avoid expediting your article with a law review that has already rejected your article."
- 要点：记清哪些期刊已经拒稿，免得向它们发加速请求。

### E10

- 位置：Using ExpressO and/or Scholastica
- 原文："Expresso and Scholastica are the two most used online submission systems by which legal scholars can submit articles to law reviews"
- 要点：ExpressO 和 Scholastica 是向美国法律评论投稿最常用的两个在线系统。
