# Schäfer, Lückemeier & Glorius 2024：Improving reproducibility through condition-based sensitivity assessments

- 资料：`schafer2024sensitivity`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1039/d4sc03017f)（[PubMed Central 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11382186/)）
- 版本：Chemical Science 2024, 15, 14548–14555，观点文章（perspective），CC BY 3.0 开放获取。德国明斯特大学 Glorius 课题组。原文文本取自 Europe PMC 全文 XML。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction 第 3 段
- 原文："While the general outcome of chemical reactions tends to align with existing literature, there is often variability in quantitative results such as yield, selectivity, and purity." / "Even with detailed protocols provided in ESI, reaction parameters may differ between labs with different chemists conducting the experiments"
- 要点：反应的大方向一般和文献一致，但产率、选择性、纯度等数字常有出入；即使支持信息写得详细，不同实验室、不同人做时条件也可能不同。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction（灵敏度筛查的设计）
- 原文："To do so, single reaction parameters are varied in both positive and negative directions, while all other parameters are kept constant. The resulting impact on the yield provides a valuable clue regarding which parameters to prioritize when troubleshooting reproducibility issues."
- 要点：灵敏度筛查的做法：一次只把一个条件往高和往低两个方向改，其余不变，看产率怎样变，从而知道排查时先查哪个条件。

### E3

- 位置：General parameters
- 原文："Frequently neglected parameters in published laboratory procedures encompass stirring rate, reaction duration (including "overnight" reactions), and the purity of substrates and reagents."
- 要点：发表的步骤里常被忽略的条件有：搅拌速度、反应时长（包括"过夜"）、底物和试剂的纯度。

### E4

- 位置：General parameters
- 原文："They repurified their carboxylic acid starting material, enhancing its purity from 98.9% to 99.9%. This small adjustment, as revealed by their sensitivity screen, led to a notable +23% increase in yield"
- 要点：文中介绍的一个例子：Bull 等人把羧酸原料从 98.9% 重新纯化到 99.9%，产率提高了 23%。

### E5

- 位置：Homogeneous catalysis and cross-coupling reactions
- 原文："Gooßen and colleagues investigated the impact of different commercial palladium precatalyst batches and found considerable yield discrepancies among batches"
- 要点：文中介绍：Gooßen 等人发现不同批次的商品钯预催化剂给出的产率差别相当大。

### E6

- 位置：Introduction（对筛查范围的修正）
- 原文："we acknowledge that our proposed range of ±10% concentration was frequently insufficient for detecting substantial yield changes beyond the detection limit of the analytical method employed. The widespread adoption of at least a ±50% concentration variation by many groups"
- 要点：作者承认原先建议的浓度变化 ±10% 常常不够，很多课题组改用至少 ±50%。

### E7

- 位置：Conclusion and outlook
- 原文："for an in-depth evaluation of reactions it should be followed up by more detailed approaches such as Design of experiments (DoE), mechanistic analysis, and advanced process optimisation."
- 要点：灵敏度筛查只是初步工具，深入评估还要用实验设计、机理研究等方法。

### E8

- 位置：Introduction 第 4 段
- 原文："Conducting Design of Experiment (DoE) studies to explore every aspect of a reaction reveals its sensitivity, but this approach is likely the most time-consuming and costly."
- 要点：用实验设计方法全面考察反应最能揭示灵敏度，但可能最费时费钱。

### E9

- 位置：Introduction（灵敏度筛查的来历）
- 原文："In response to this challenge, the sensitivity screen was developed as a general tool to tackle the aforementioned reproducibility problems." / "Our innovation was embraced by the scientific community and has since been widely adopted for applications beyond the originally suggested ones."
- 要点：灵敏度筛查是作者课题组为解决可重复性问题提出的通用工具，后来被广泛采用。
