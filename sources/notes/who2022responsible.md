# 世界卫生组织 2022：Global guidance framework for the responsible use of the life sciences: mitigating biorisks and governing dual-use research

- 资料：`who2022responsible`，[原文 PDF](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/362313/9789240056107-eng.pdf)
- 版本：2022 年，ISBN 978-92-4-005610-7。许可：CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO。WHO 机构库对脚本只返回网页框架，通过 Internet Archive 快照（`web.archive.org/web/2023id_/...`）下载同一份 PDF。位置写章节或词汇表。

### E1

- 位置：Glossary, Dual-use research of concern
- 原文："Dual-use research of concern (DURC) describes research that is conducted for peaceful and beneficial purposes, but could easily be misapplied to do harm with no, or only minor, modification."
- 要点：值得关注的两用研究，指出于和平、有益目的开展，但不加修改或只稍加修改就很容易被用来造成伤害的研究。

### E2

- 位置：Glossary, Dual-use research
- 原文："Research conducted for peaceful and beneficial purposes that has the potential to produce knowledge, information, methods, products or technologies that could also be intentionally misused to endanger the health of humans, nonhuman animals, plants and agriculture, and the environment."
- 要点：两用研究指出于和平、有益目的开展，但产生的知识、信息、方法、产品或技术也可能被故意滥用，危害人、动物、植物、农业和环境的研究。

### E3

- 位置：Glossary, Biosafety
- 原文："Containment principles, technologies, measures and practices that are implemented to prevent unintentional exposure to biological agents or their inadvertent release."
- 要点：生物安全（biosafety）指防止人无意中接触病原、防止病原意外泄漏的防护原则、技术和做法。

### E4

- 位置：Glossary, Biosecurity
- 原文："Biosecurity aims to prevent their unauthorized access, loss, theft, misuse, diversion or release."
- 要点：生物安保（biosecurity）的目的是防止病原、数据、设备等被未经授权取得、丢失、被盗、滥用、挪作他用或释放。

### E5

- 位置：Executive summary
- 原文："Robust biorisk management relies on three core pillars: biosafety, laboratory biosecurity and the oversight of dual-use research."
- 要点：生物风险管理有三根支柱：生物安全、实验室生物安保、两用研究的监管。

### E6

- 位置：Section 5.3, Box 6 Checklist for scientists, STEP 1
- 原文："Outputs: Potential risks of work are identified and assessed before beginning the work."
- 要点：给科研人员的清单第一步：开始工作之前，先识别和评估潜在风险。

### E7

- 位置：Section 5.3, Box 6 Checklist for scientists, STEP 1
- 原文："Could the information, data and research methods generated by the proposed work be misused to cause harm? What mitigation strategies have been put into place to reduce this risk?"
- 要点：要问自己：这项工作产生的信息、数据和方法会不会被滥用来造成伤害？准备了什么措施降低这种风险？

### E8

- 位置：Section 5.3, Box 6 Checklist for scientists, STEP 1
- 原文："Can a different methodology, experimental design or different organism be used to make the experiment safer or less of a biosecurity risk?"
- 要点：能不能换一种方法、实验设计或生物体，让实验更安全、生物安保风险更低？

### E9

- 位置：Section 5.3, Box 6 Checklist for scientists, STEP 1
- 原文："Is the proposed work falling under the scope of export controls?"
- 要点：这项工作是否属于出口管制的范围？

### E10

- 位置：Section 5.3, Box 6 Checklist for scientists, STEP 1
- 原文："Does the proposed work follow institutional guidelines, or national or regional legislation, regulations or guidelines for safe, secure and responsible research?"
- 要点：这项工作是否遵守本机构的规定，以及国家或地区的法律、法规和指南？

### E11

- 位置：Section 5.3, Box 6 Checklist for scientists, STEP 4
- 原文："Risk mitigation strategies cannot reduce risks to zero unless the work or research is not undertaken."
- 要点：除非不做这项研究，否则风险缓解措施不能把风险降到零。

### E12

- 位置：Section 5.3, Box 6 Checklist for scientists, Note
- 原文："Although the checklists identify examples of considerations targeted at different stakeholders, biorisk management is a shared responsibility between different stakeholders."
- 要点：生物风险管理是各方共同的责任。
