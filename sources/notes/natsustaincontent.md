# Nature Sustainability：Content Types

- 资料：`natsustaincontent`，[原文](https://www.nature.com/natsustain/content)
- 版本：《自然·可持续发展》（Nature Sustainability）期刊的文章类型说明页，2026-10-17 读取。位置写网页小标题。只代表这一本期刊；Nature 系列其他期刊的说明相近但不完全相同。

### E1

- 位置：Perspective
- 原文："A Perspective is intended to provide a forum for authors to discuss models and ideas from a personal viewpoint. They are more forward looking and/or speculative than Reviews and may take a narrower field of view. They may be opinionated but should remain balanced and are intended to stimulate discussion and new approaches."
- 要点：观点文章（Perspective）从个人视角讨论模型和想法，比综述更面向未来、更带推测性；可以有鲜明观点，但要保持平衡，目的是激发讨论和新做法。

### E2

- 位置：Perspective
- 原文："Perspectives may also advocate a controversial position or present a speculative hypothesis. Two articles advocating opposite sides in a research controversy are normally published as Perspectives."
- 要点：观点文章可以主张一个有争议的立场或提出推测性假说；研究争议中正反两方的文章通常都以观点文章发表。

### E3

- 位置：Perspective
- 原文："Most Perspective articles are invited by the editors, though we are open to consider proposals."
- 要点：大多数观点文章是编辑邀请的，但也接受作者提议。

### E4

- 位置：Perspective
- 原文："The synopsis should outline the topics that will be covered, how the material will be organized, include an example of figure (even in sketch form), list any recent, key publications in the area, and state clearly what the contribution of the article is (new model, new conceptual framework, etc.)."
- 要点：主动投稿要先交一到两页提要：写清要讲的话题、怎样组织、一张示例图、近期关键文献，以及文章的贡献是什么（新模型、新概念框架等）。

### E5

- 位置：Perspective，Format
- 原文："Main text – up to 5,000 words." / "References – up to 60." / "Perspectives are peer reviewed."
- 要点：正文最多 5000 词，参考文献最多 60 条，要经过同行评审。

### E6

- 位置：Comment
- 原文："These pieces are intended to be agenda-setting, authoritative and informed, and can be provocative articles calling for action on timely issues [...] If so, they must road-map a proposed solution in detail, not simply snapshot a problem."
- 要点：评论文章（Comment）可以是呼吁行动的文章，但必须详细给出解决方案的路线图，而不只是描述一个问题。
