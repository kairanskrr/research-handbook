# Sage：Music & Science 投稿说明

- 资料：`sage2024musicscience`，[原文](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/MNS)（Sage 出版的开放获取期刊，SEMPRE 会员享折扣）
- 版本：网页。原站对脚本访问返回 403，本条摘录取自 Internet Archive 2024-01-14 的快照（http://web.archive.org/web/20240114200115/https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/MNS）。

### E1

- 位置：4.7 Data
- 原文："Music & Science requests authors to submit any primary data used in their research articles alongside their article submissions for publication in the online version of the journal, or to provide detailed information in their articles on how the data can be accessed." / "Examples of data types include but are not limited to statistical data files, replication code, text files, audio files, images, videos, appendices, and additional charts and graphs necessary to understand the original research."
- 要点：要求作者随文提交原始数据，或在文中详细说明怎样获取；数据包括统计数据文件、复现代码、文本、音频、图像、视频等。

### E2

- 位置：4.7 Data
- 原文："In making your dataset available, please ensure that it is appropriately labelled (e.g., it should be easy to identify data pertaining to particular variables, etc.) in order that a reviewer or reader is in a position to replicate the analyses that you have undertaken."
- 要点：数据要标注清楚，让审稿人或读者能重做你的分析。

### E3

- 位置：4.1 Peer review policy
- 原文："Music & Science follows a double-anonymous peer-review process through to the point of publication, at which point the reviewers have the option of remaining anonymous or being named. A minimum of two reviewers are selected to review a submission."
- 要点：双盲审稿，至少两位审稿人。

### E4

- 位置：4.8 Research ethics and participant consent
- 原文："Authors are also required to state in the methods section whether participants provided informed consent and whether the consent was written or verbal"
- 要点：要在方法部分说明被试是否知情同意，以及是书面还是口头同意。
