# Huang 2021：How to come up with research ideas?

- 资料：`huang2021ideas`，[原推文](https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1423499757591400448)（通过 [Thread Reader 镜像](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1423499757591400448.html) 读取）
- 版本：推文串，2021-08-06，共 9 条。作者是计算机视觉研究者，例子偏向该领域，以下只摘通用的做法。

### E1

- 位置：第 3 条，*Relax assumptions*
- 原文："Identify the underlying assumptions of existing work and try relaxing them to make it work in more unconstrained settings."
- 要点：找出已有工作背后的假设，尝试放宽，让方法在限制更少的条件下也能用。

### E2

- 位置：第 4 条，*Make more assumptions*
- 原文："Take a general approach and tailor it to your SPECIFIC problem. You can then leverage all the domain knowledge (i.e., make more assumptions) to improve the method."
- 要点：反过来，把通用方法用到具体问题上，利用领域知识（增加假设）改进它。

### E3

- 位置：第 5–6 条，*Combine two ideas/problems*、*Grab a powerful hammer and find all the nails*
- 原文："Pay attention to new emerging tools in the community. Apply and adapt them on your problem."
- 要点：结合两个想法；关注领域里新出现的工具，把它用到自己的问题上。

### E4

- 位置：第 8 条，*Stress test the state-of-the-art*
- 原文："Don't simply run on the fixed, boring benchmark datasets. Try it out on diverse, unconstrained examples and see how it fails. It's a great way to identify limitations of existing work. This is where your work can fill the gap."
- 要点：对最先进的方法做压力测试：换到多样、无约束的例子上看它怎么失败，从中找到已有工作的局限和你能填补的空白。
