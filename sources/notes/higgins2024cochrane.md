# Higgins et al. (eds.) 2024：Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions

- 资料：`higgins2024cochrane`，[第 1 章](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-01)，[第 24 章](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-24)
- 版本：6.5 版，Cochrane，2024，官网免费在线阅读。第 1 章（Lasserson, Thomas & Higgins，2021 年 8 月更新）；第 24 章（Reeves 等人，2019 年 10 月更新）。位置写章节号。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 章，Key Points
- 原文："Systematic reviews aim to minimize bias through the use of pre-specified research questions and methods that are documented in protocols, and by basing their findings on reliable research."
- 要点：系统综述通过事先写在方案里的研究问题和方法，以及只依据可靠的研究，来减少偏倚。

### E2

- 位置：第 1 章，Key Points
- 原文："People who might make – or be affected by – decisions around the use of interventions should be involved in important decisions about the review."
- 要点：会做决策或受决策影响的人，应参与综述的重要决定。

### E3

- 位置：第 1 章，§1.1
- 原文："a systematic review should typically be undertaken before embarking on new primary research."
- 要点：开始新的原始研究之前，一般应先做系统综述。

### E4

- 位置：第 1 章，§1.1
- 原文："completing a high-quality systematic review is often demanding and time-consuming."
- 要点：完成一篇高质量的系统综述往往费力又费时。

### E5

- 位置：第 1 章，§1.2
- 原文："For a question about the effects of an intervention, the PICO approach is usually used, which is an acronym for Population, Intervention, Comparison(s) and Outcome."
- 要点：问干预效果的问题，一般用 PICO 框架：人群、干预、对照、结局。

### E6

- 位置：第 1 章，Key Points
- 原文："Systematic reviews should be conducted by a team that includes domain expertise and methodological expertise, who are free of potential conflicts of interest."
- 要点：系统综述应由既懂专业又懂方法、没有潜在利益冲突的团队来做。

### E7

- 位置：第 24 章，Key Points
- 原文："Potential biases are likely to be greater for non-randomized studies compared with randomized trials when evaluating the effects of interventions, so results should always be interpreted with caution"
- 要点：评价干预效果时，非随机研究的潜在偏倚可能比随机试验大，解释结果时要始终谨慎。

### E8

- 位置：第 24 章，§24.1
- 原文："Some of this evidence, especially about harms of interventions, will often need to come from NRSI."
- 要点：有些证据，尤其是关于干预危害的证据，常常要来自非随机研究。

### E9

- 位置：第 24 章，§24.1
- 原文："nutritionists may be interested in the health-related effects of a diet that includes a minimum of five portions of fruit or vegetables per day (‘five-a-day’), an aetiological question. On the other hand, public health professionals may be interested in the health-related effects of interventions to promote a change in diet to include ‘five-a-day’, an effectiveness question."
- 要点：营养学家关心"每天至少吃五份果蔬"这种饮食本身对健康的影响，这是病因问题；公共卫生人员关心"推动人们改成这样吃"的干预有什么效果，这是效果问题。两者是不同的问题。

### E10

- 位置：第 24 章，Key Points
- 原文："We recommend that eligibility criteria, data collection and assessment of included studies place an emphasis on specific features of study design (e.g. which parts of the study were prospectively designed) rather than ‘labels’ for study designs (such as case-control versus cohort)."
- 要点：判断非随机研究，要看具体的设计特征（比如哪些部分是前瞻设计的），而不是只看"病例对照""队列"这类标签。

### E11

- 位置：第 1 章，§1.1
- 原文："Cochrane reviews use primary research to generate new knowledge about the effects of an intervention (or interventions) used in clinical, public health or policy settings."
- 要点：Cochrane 综述研究的干预，既有临床的，也有公共卫生和政策层面的。

### E12

- 位置：第 1 章，§1.1
- 原文："This Handbook is about systematic reviews on the effects of interventions, and specifically about methods used by Cochrane to undertake them."
- 要点：这本手册讲干预效果的系统综述，具体讲 Cochrane 用的方法。
