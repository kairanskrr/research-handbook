# Higgins et al. (eds.) 2024：Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions

- 资料：`higgins2024cochrane`，[第 1 章](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-01)，[第 24 章](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-24)
- 版本：6.5 版，Cochrane，2024，官网免费在线阅读。第 1 章（Lasserson, Thomas & Higgins，2021 年 8 月更新）；第 24 章（Reeves 等人，2019 年 10 月更新）。位置写章节号。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 章，Key Points
- 原文："Systematic reviews aim to minimize bias through the use of pre-specified research questions and methods that are documented in protocols, and by basing their findings on reliable research."
- 要点：系统综述通过事先写在方案里的研究问题和方法，以及只依据可靠的研究，来减少偏倚。

### E2

- 位置：第 1 章，Key Points
- 原文："People who might make – or be affected by – decisions around the use of interventions should be involved in important decisions about the review."
- 要点：会做决策或受决策影响的人，应参与综述的重要决定。

### E3

- 位置：第 1 章，§1.1
- 原文："a systematic review should typically be undertaken before embarking on new primary research."
- 要点：开始新的原始研究之前，一般应先做系统综述。

### E4

- 位置：第 1 章，§1.1
- 原文："completing a high-quality systematic review is often demanding and time-consuming."
- 要点：完成一篇高质量的系统综述往往费力又费时。

### E5

- 位置：第 1 章，§1.2
- 原文："For a question about the effects of an intervention, the PICO approach is usually used, which is an acronym for Population, Intervention, Comparison(s) and Outcome."
- 要点：问干预效果的问题，一般用 PICO 框架：人群、干预、对照、结局。

### E6

- 位置：第 1 章，Key Points
- 原文："Systematic reviews should be conducted by a team that includes domain expertise and methodological expertise, who are free of potential conflicts of interest."
- 要点：系统综述应由既懂专业又懂方法、没有潜在利益冲突的团队来做。

### E7

- 位置：第 24 章，Key Points
- 原文："Potential biases are likely to be greater for non-randomized studies compared with randomized trials when evaluating the effects of interventions, so results should always be interpreted with caution"
- 要点：评价干预效果时，非随机研究的潜在偏倚可能比随机试验大，解释结果时要始终谨慎。

### E8

- 位置：第 24 章，§24.1
- 原文："Some of this evidence, especially about harms of interventions, will often need to come from NRSI."
- 要点：有些证据，尤其是关于干预危害的证据，常常要来自非随机研究。

### E9

- 位置：第 24 章，§24.1
- 原文："nutritionists may be interested in the health-related effects of a diet that includes a minimum of five portions of fruit or vegetables per day (‘five-a-day’), an aetiological question. On the other hand, public health professionals may be interested in the health-related effects of interventions to promote a change in diet to include ‘five-a-day’, an effectiveness question."
- 要点：营养学家关心"每天至少吃五份果蔬"这种饮食本身对健康的影响，这是病因问题；公共卫生人员关心"推动人们改成这样吃"的干预有什么效果，这是效果问题。两者是不同的问题。

### E10

- 位置：第 24 章，Key Points
- 原文："We recommend that eligibility criteria, data collection and assessment of included studies place an emphasis on specific features of study design (e.g. which parts of the study were prospectively designed) rather than ‘labels’ for study designs (such as case-control versus cohort)."
- 要点：判断非随机研究，要看具体的设计特征（比如哪些部分是前瞻设计的），而不是只看"病例对照""队列"这类标签。

### E11

- 位置：第 1 章，§1.1
- 原文："Cochrane reviews use primary research to generate new knowledge about the effects of an intervention (or interventions) used in clinical, public health or policy settings."
- 要点：Cochrane 综述研究的干预，既有临床的，也有公共卫生和政策层面的。

### E12

- 位置：第 1 章，§1.1
- 原文："This Handbook is about systematic reviews on the effects of interventions, and specifically about methods used by Cochrane to undertake them."
- 要点：这本手册讲干预效果的系统综述，具体讲 Cochrane 用的方法。

### E13

- 位置：第 10 章，Key Points
- 原文："Potential advantages of meta-analyses include an improvement in precision, the ability to answer questions not posed by individual studies, and the opportunity to settle controversies arising from conflicting claims. However, they also have the potential to mislead seriously, particularly if specific study designs, within-study biases, variation across studies, and reporting biases are not carefully considered."
- 要点：元分析的好处是提高精确度、回答单个研究没问过的问题、平息相互矛盾的结论；但如果不仔细考虑研究设计、研究内部的偏倚、研究间差异和报告偏倚，它也可能严重误导。

### E14

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.1 "Do not start here!"
- 原文："results of meta-analyses can be very misleading if suitable attention has not been given to formulating the review question; specifying eligibility criteria; identifying and selecting studies; collecting appropriate data; considering risk of bias; planning intervention comparisons; and deciding what data would be meaningful to analyse."
- 要点：如果前面的问题界定、纳入标准、检索筛选、数据收集、偏倚风险、比较设计没做好，元分析结果可能非常误导人。不要一上来就做统计。

### E15

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.1，MECIR C62（强制）
- 原文："Undertake (or display) a meta-analysis only if participants, interventions, comparisons and outcomes are judged to be sufficiently similar to ensure an answer that is clinically meaningful. Meta-analyses of very diverse studies can be misleading, for example where studies use different forms of control."
- 要点：只有当研究对象、干预、对照、结局足够相似、合并后的答案有临床意义时，才做元分析；把差异很大的研究合并（例如对照组形式不同）可能误导。

### E16

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.3
- 原文："Do not do a meta-analysis. A systematic review need not contain any meta-analyses. If there is considerable variation in results, and particularly if there is inconsistency in the direction of effect, it may be misleading to quote an average value for the intervention effect."
- 要点：系统综述不一定要有元分析。结果差异很大，尤其效应方向不一致时，报一个平均效应可能误导。

### E17

- 位置：第 12 章，§12.1
- 原文："Legitimate reasons include limited evidence; incompletely reported outcome/effect estimates, or different effect measures used across studies; and bias in the evidence. Other commonly cited reasons for not using meta-analysis are because of too much clinical or methodological diversity, or statistical heterogeneity [...] However, meta-analysis methods should be considered in these circumstances, as they may provide important insights if undertaken and interpreted appropriately."
- 要点：不做元分析的正当理由：证据太少；结局或效应估计报告不全、各研究用的效应指标不同；证据本身有偏倚。常被提到的"差异太大、异质性太高"不一定是不做的理由，做得恰当、解释得当，元分析仍可能给出重要信息。

### E18

- 位置：第 12 章，Key Points
- 原文："Synthesis methods that involve vote counting based on statistical significance have serious limitations and are unacceptable."
- 要点：按"显著的有几篇、不显著的有几篇"来计票的综合方法有严重缺陷，不可接受。

### E19

- 位置：第 12 章，表 12.1.a
- 原文："Meta-analysis is not possible with no studies, or only one study."
- 要点：没有研究或只有一项研究时，无法做元分析。

### E20

- 位置：第 7 章，§7.1.2
- 原文："Early appraisal tools were structured as quality 'scales', which combined information on several features into a single score. However, this approach was questioned after it was revealed that the type of quality scale used could significantly influence the interpretation of the meta-analysis results"
- 要点：早期的质量评价工具是"打总分"的量表；后来发现，选用哪种量表会明显改变对元分析结果的解读，这种做法因此受到质疑。

### E21

- 位置：第 7 章，Key Points
- 原文："An assessment of the internal validity of studies included in a Cochrane review should emphasize the risk of bias in their results, that is, the risk that they will over-estimate or under-estimate the true intervention effect."
- 要点：评价纳入研究时，重点是结果的偏倚风险，即高估或低估真实干预效果的风险。

### E22

- 位置：第 8 章，Key Points
- 原文："Each assessment using the RoB 2 tool focuses on a specific result from a randomized trial."
- 要点：RoB 2 每次评价的是随机试验中的某一个具体结果，而不是整篇试验。

### E23

- 位置：第 8 章，§8.2.3
- 原文："bias arising from the randomization process; bias due to deviations from intended interventions; bias due to missing outcome data; bias in measurement of the outcome; and bias in selection of the reported result."
- 要点：RoB 2 的五个偏倚领域：随机化过程、偏离预定干预、结局数据缺失、结局测量、报告结果的选择。

### E24

- 位置：第 8 章，Key Points
- 原文："Judgements can be 'Low', or 'High' risk of bias, or can express 'Some concerns'." / "The overall risk of bias for the result is the least favourable assessment across the domains of bias."
- 要点：判断分为"低风险""有些担忧""高风险"；整体判断取各领域中最差的那一个。

### E25

- 位置：第 8 章，Key Points
- 原文："Answers to signalling questions and judgements about risk of bias should be supported by written justifications."
- 要点：每个信号问题的回答和偏倚判断都要写下理由。

### E26

- 位置：第 8 章，§8.2.1
- 原文："It is important not to select results to assess based on the likely judgements arising from the assessment."
- 要点：不要按"可能评出什么结论"来挑选要评价的结果。

### E27

- 位置：第 8 章，§8.2.2
- 原文："the estimated effect of assignment to intervention would be the most appropriate to inform a health policy question about whether to recommend an intervention in a particular health system [...] whereas the estimated effect of adhering to the intervention as specified in the trial protocol would be the most appropriate to inform a care decision by an individual patient"
- 要点："被分配到干预组"的效果（意向性治疗效果）适合回答卫生政策问题；"按方案坚持干预"的效果适合回答个体患者的决策。评价前要先说清关心哪一种。

### E28

- 位置：第 25 章，Key Points
- 原文："Review authors should specify important confounding domains and co-interventions of concern in their protocol."
- 要点：纳入非随机研究时，要在方案里事先写明重要的混杂因素和需要关注的合并干预。

### E29

- 位置：第 25 章，Key Points
- 原文："At the start of a ROBINS-I assessment of a study, review authors should describe a 'target trial', which is a hypothetical pragmatic randomized trial of the interventions compared in the study, conducted on the same participant group and without features putting it at risk of bias."
- 要点：用 ROBINS-I 评价一项非随机研究，先描述一个"目标试验"：在同一人群中比较同样干预、没有偏倚隐患的假想随机试验，再拿实际研究去比。

### E30

- 位置：第 25 章，Key Points
- 原文："judgements for each bias domain, and for overall risk of bias, can be 'Low', 'Moderate', 'Serious' or 'Critical' risk of bias."
- 要点：ROBINS-I 的判断分四级：低、中、严重、极严重。

### E31

- 位置：第 25 章，§25.1
- 原文："Evaluating risk of bias in results of NRSI requires both methodological and content expertise. The process is more involved than for randomized trials"
- 要点：评价非随机研究的偏倚风险既要懂方法也要懂专业，比评价随机试验更费事。

### E32

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.4.3
- 原文："It is generally recommended that meta-analyses are undertaken using risk ratios (taking care to make a sensible choice over which category of outcome is classified as the event) or odds ratios."
- 要点：二分类结局一般建议用风险比（要想清楚哪一类算"事件"）或比值比来做元分析。

### E33

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.4.3
- 原文："The odds ratio is the hardest summary statistic to understand and to apply in practice, and many practising clinicians report difficulties in using them. There are many published examples where authors have misinterpreted odds ratios from meta-analyses as risk ratios."
- 要点：比值比最难理解，很多临床医生用不好；已发表文献中常有人把元分析的比值比误读成风险比。

### E34

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.4.3
- 原文："It is often sensible to use one statistic for meta-analysis and to re-express the results using a second, more easily interpretable statistic."
- 要点：常见的做法是用一种指标做合并，再换算成另一种更好懂的指标（如绝对风险差）来呈现。

### E35

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.4.3
- 原文："Selecting an effect measure based on what is the most consistent in a particular situation is not a generally recommended strategy, since it may lead to a selection that spuriously maximizes the precision of a meta-analysis estimate."
- 要点：不要看数据后挑"最一致"的效应指标，这可能人为夸大合并结果的精确度。

### E36

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.5.1
- 原文："Selection of summary statistics for continuous data is principally determined by whether studies all report the outcome using the same scale (when the mean difference can be used) or using different scales (when the standardized mean difference is usually used)."
- 要点：连续结局：各研究用同一量表时用均数差；用不同量表时一般用标准化均数差。

### E37

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.2.1 与 §10.3
- 原文："In the second stage, a summary (combined) intervention effect estimate is calculated as a weighted average of the intervention effects estimated in the individual studies." / "larger studies, which have smaller standard errors, are given more weight than smaller studies"
- 要点：元分析的合并结果是各研究效应的加权平均；常用方法里，标准误小的大研究权重更大。

### E38

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.4.1
- 原文："the assumption is usually made that the true effect of intervention (in both magnitude and direction) is the same value in every study (i.e. fixed across studies)." / "Instead of assuming that the intervention effects are the same, we assume that they follow (usually) a normal distribution."
- 要点：固定效应模型假定每项研究的真实效应都相同；随机效应模型假定各研究的真实效应不同，服从（通常是正态的）分布。

### E39

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.4.1
- 原文："The decision between fixed- and random-effects meta-analyses has been the subject of much debate, and we do not provide a universal recommendation."
- 要点：选固定效应还是随机效应一直有争论，手册不给统一建议。

### E40

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.4.1
- 原文："Since it is generally considered to be implausible that intervention effects across studies are identical (unless the intervention has no effect at all), this leads many to advocate use of the random-effects model."
- 要点：一般认为各研究的干预效应完全相同不太可能，所以很多人主张用随机效应模型。

### E41

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.4.1
- 原文："Others have argued that a fixed-effect analysis can be interpreted in the presence of heterogeneity, and that it makes fewer assumptions than a random-effects meta-analysis."
- 要点：也有人认为有异质性时固定效应结果仍可解释，而且它的假设比随机效应少。

### E42

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.4.1
- 原文："The choice between a fixed-effect and a random-effects meta-analysis should never be made on the basis of a statistical test for heterogeneity."
- 要点：绝不要根据异质性检验的结果来决定用固定效应还是随机效应。

### E43

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.4.1
- 原文："If there is additionally some funnel plot asymmetry (i.e. a relationship between intervention effect magnitude and study size), then this will push the results of the random-effects analysis towards the findings in the smaller studies."
- 要点：随机效应模型给小研究更多权重；如果漏斗图不对称（效应大小和研究规模有关），随机效应结果会被推向小研究的结论。

### E44

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.4.1
- 原文："A pragmatic approach is to plan to undertake both a fixed-effect and a random-effects meta-analysis, with an intention to present the random-effects result if there is no indication of funnel plot asymmetry."
- 要点：一个务实的做法：事先计划两种模型都做，若没有漏斗图不对称的迹象，就报告随机效应结果。

### E45

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.1
- 原文："Statistical heterogeneity manifests itself in the observed intervention effects being more different from each other than one would expect due to random error (chance) alone."
- 要点：统计异质性指各研究观察到的效应之间的差异，超出了单靠随机误差能解释的程度。

### E46

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.2
- 原文："while a statistically significant result may indicate a problem with heterogeneity, a non-significant result must not be taken as evidence of no heterogeneity."
- 要点：异质性检验（卡方检验）显著可能提示有问题；但不显著绝不能当作"没有异质性"的证据。

### E47

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.2
- 原文："This describes the percentage of the variability in effect estimates that is due to heterogeneity rather than sampling error (chance)."
- 要点：I² 表示效应估计的变异中，有多大比例来自异质性而不是抽样误差。

### E48

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.2
- 原文："Thresholds for the interpretation of the I 2 statistic can be misleading, since the importance of inconsistency depends on several factors." / "0% to 40%: might not be important; 30% to 60%: may represent moderate heterogeneity*; 50% to 90%: may represent substantial heterogeneity*; 75% to 100%: considerable heterogeneity*."
- 要点：用固定阈值解读 I² 可能误导；手册给的粗略参考（针对随机试验的元分析）：0–40% 可能不重要，30–60% 可能中等，50–90% 可能较大，75–100% 很大；区间有意重叠，还要结合效应大小和方向、异质性证据的强弱来看。

### E49

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.2，MECIR C63（强制）
- 原文："Authors should recognize that there is much uncertainty in measures such as I 2 and Tau 2 when there are few studies. Thus, use of simple thresholds to diagnose heterogeneity should be avoided."
- 要点：研究少时，I² 和 Tau² 很不确定，不要用简单阈值诊断异质性。

### E50

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.4.2
- 原文："The confidence interval from a random-effects meta-analysis describes uncertainty in the location of the mean of systematically different effects in the different studies. It does not describe the degree of heterogeneity among studies, as may be commonly believed."
- 要点：随机效应结果的置信区间说的是"平均效应在哪里"的不确定性，不是研究之间差异有多大。

### E51

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.4.3
- 原文："we encourage their use when the number of studies is reasonable (e.g. five or more [...] and there is no clear funnel plot asymmetry."
- 要点：研究数量合理（例如五项以上）且漏斗图没有明显不对称时，鼓励报告预测区间，用它表示各研究真实效应的分布范围。

### E52

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.3
- 原文："Severe apparent heterogeneity can indicate that data have been incorrectly extracted or entered into meta-analysis software. For example, if standard errors have mistakenly been entered as SDs for continuous outcomes"
- 要点：异质性异常大时，先检查数据有没有提取或录入错误，例如把标准误当成标准差录入。

### E53

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.3
- 原文："Reliable conclusions can only be drawn from analyses that are truly pre-specified before inspecting the studies' results, and even these conclusions should be interpreted with caution. Explorations of heterogeneity that are devised after heterogeneity is identified can at best lead to the generation of hypotheses."
- 要点：只有在看结果之前事先规定的亚组分析等，才能得出可靠结论（也要谨慎）；发现异质性之后才想出来的分析，最多只能提出假设。

### E54

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.3
- 原文："In general it is unwise to exclude studies from a meta-analysis on the basis of their results as this may introduce bias." / "It is advisable to perform analyses both with and without outlying studies as part of a sensitivity analysis"
- 要点：不要因为某项研究的结果"不合群"就把它剔除；可以在敏感性分析里分别做包含和不包含它的分析。

### E55

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.3
- 原文："This is not a substitute for a thorough investigation of heterogeneity."
- 要点：用随机效应模型不能代替认真调查异质性的来源。

### E56

- 位置：第 10 章，Key Points
- 原文："Sensitivity analyses should be used to examine whether overall findings are robust to potentially influential decisions."
- 要点：用敏感性分析检查：换一种可能影响结果的决定，总体结论是否还站得住。

### E57

- 位置：第 13 章，§13.1
- 原文："'statistically significant' results that suggest an intervention works are more likely than 'statistically non-significant' results to be available, available rapidly, available in high impact journals and cited by others, and hence more easily identifiable for systematic reviews."
- 要点："显著有效"的结果比"不显著"的结果更容易发表、发得更快、发在高影响力期刊、被更多引用，因而更容易被综述找到。

### E58

- 位置：第 13 章，Key Points
- 原文："It may be necessary to consult multiple bibliographic databases, trials registers, manufacturers, regulators and study authors or sponsors."
- 要点：为减少遗漏，可能要查多个文献数据库、试验登记库，并联系厂商、监管机构、研究作者或资助方。

### E59

- 位置：第 13 章，§13.3.4.3
- 原文："the funnel plot should be seen as a generic means of displaying small-study effects: a tendency for the intervention effects estimated in smaller studies to differ from those estimated in larger studies"
- 要点：漏斗图展示的是"小研究效应"：小研究估计的效应与大研究不同的倾向。发表偏倚只是可能原因之一。

### E60

- 位置：第 13 章，§13.3.4.4
- 原文："As a rule of thumb, tests for funnel plot asymmetry should be used only when there are at least 10 studies included in the meta-analysis, because when there are fewer studies the power of the tests is low. Only 28% of a random sample of 300 systematic reviews indexed in 2020 included a meta-analysis with at least 10 studies"
- 要点：经验法则：元分析至少有 10 项研究时才做漏斗图不对称检验，否则检验效能太低。2020 年收录的 300 篇系统综述随机样本中，只有 28% 含有至少 10 项研究的元分析。

### E61

- 位置：第 13 章，§13.3.4.4
- 原文："funnel plot asymmetry should not be considered to be diagnostic for the presence of non-reporting bias ."
- 要点：漏斗图不对称不能用来诊断发表偏倚（未报告偏倚）。

### E62

- 位置：第 13 章，§13.3.4.6
- 原文："they compare the fixed-effect and random-effects estimates of the intervention effect. If the estimates are similar, then any small-study effects have little effect on the intervention effect estimate."
- 要点：担心小研究效应时，比较固定效应和随机效应的估计；两者相近，说明小研究效应对结果影响不大。

### E63

- 位置：第 13 章，Key Points
- 原文："A thorough assessment of selective non-reporting or under-reporting of results in the studies identified is likely to be the most valuable."
- 要点：评估证据缺失带来的偏倚，最有价值的是逐项核查已找到的研究有没有选择性地不报告或少报告结果。

### E64

- 位置：第 14 章，Key Points
- 原文："The GRADE approach specifies four levels of the certainty for a body of evidence for a given outcome: high, moderate, low and very low."
- 要点：GRADE 对某个结局的整体证据给出四级确定性：高、中、低、极低。

### E65

- 位置：第 14 章，Key Points
- 原文："GRADE assessments of certainty are determined through consideration of five domains: risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication bias. For evidence from non-randomized studies and rarely randomized studies, assessments can then be upgraded through consideration of three further domains."
- 要点：GRADE 看五个方面：偏倚风险、不一致性、间接性、不精确性、发表偏倚；非随机研究的证据还可以根据另外三个方面上调。

### E66

- 位置：第 14 章，§14.2
- 原文："In GRADE, a body of evidence from randomized trials begins with a high-certainty rating while a body of evidence from NRSI begins with a low-certainty rating."
- 要点：GRADE 中，随机试验的证据从"高"起评，非随机研究从"低"起评。

### E67

- 位置：第 14 章，§14.2，MECIR C74（强制）
- 原文："Ideally, two people working independently should assess the certainty of the body of evidence and reach a consensus view on any downgrading decisions."
- 要点：最好两人独立评定证据确定性，对降级决定达成共识。

### E68

- 位置：第 14 章，§14.2，MECIR C75（强制）
- 原文："Justify and document all assessments of the certainty of the body of evidence (e.g. downgrading or upgrading using GRADE)."
- 要点：每一次升级、降级都要写明理由并记录下来。

### E69

- 位置：第 14 章，Key Points
- 原文："'Summary of findings' tables include a row for each important outcome (up to a maximum of seven)."
- 要点："结果总结表"每个重要结局一行，最多七个结局。

### E70

- 位置：第 III 章，Key Points
- 原文："Cochrane review protocols and reviews should comply with the PRISMA 2020 and PRISMA for Protocols reporting guidelines."
- 要点：Cochrane 的综述方案和综述报告应符合 PRISMA 2020 和 PRISMA 方案版（PRISMA for Protocols）。

### E71

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.4.4
- 原文："many methods of meta-analysis are based on large sample approximations, and are unsuitable when events are rare."
- 要点：很多元分析方法基于大样本近似，事件罕见时不适用，要谨慎选方法。

### E72

- 位置：第 10 章，Key Points
- 原文："Variation across studies (heterogeneity) must be considered, although most Cochrane reviews do not have enough studies to allow for the reliable investigation of its causes."
- 要点：必须考虑研究间的差异，但大多数 Cochrane 综述的研究数量不够，难以可靠地查明差异的原因。

### E73

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.3，MECIR C69（强制）
- 原文："If a fixed-effect analysis is used, the confidence intervals ignore the extent of heterogeneity. If a random-effects analysis is used, the result pertains to the mean effect across studies."
- 要点：用固定效应时，置信区间没有反映异质性；用随机效应时，结果指的是各研究效应的平均值。两种情况下都要讨论明显异质性的含义。

### E74

- 位置：第 14 章，§14.2
- 原文："Review authors will generally grade evidence from sound non-randomized studies as low certainty, even if ROBINS-I is used."
- 要点：即使用了 ROBINS-I，做得好的非随机研究的证据一般也评为低确定性。

### E75

- 位置：第 10 章，§10.10.3
- 原文："Also, investigations of heterogeneity when there are very few studies are of questionable value."
- 要点：研究很少时，调查异质性的来源价值存疑。
