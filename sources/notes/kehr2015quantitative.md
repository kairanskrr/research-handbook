# Kehr & Kowatsch 2015：Quantitative Longitudinal Research: A Review of IS Literature, and a Set of Methodological Guidelines

- 资料：`kehr2015quantitative`，[原文 PDF](https://www.alexandria.unisg.ch/entities/publication/ffeb99ad-44cf-4501-be14-2999c9171dae)（圣加仑大学机构知识库中的会议定稿版）；会议页面 https://aisel.aisnet.org/ecis2015_cr/94
- 版本：ECIS 2015（第 23 届欧洲信息系统会议）完整论文，18 页。页码为论文页脚页码。讨论的是信息系统领域以人为对象的定量纵向研究。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 1 页
- 原文："Also, a doubtless explanation and prediction of IS-related phenomena requires a chronological perspective, given that causes precede effects"
- 要点：原因发生在结果之前，所以要解释和预测现象，需要按时间先后来观察。这是纵向研究能为因果推断提供线索的原因。

### E2

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 2 页
- 原文："our work is organized around a conceptualization of Singer and Willett (2003a), defining longitudinal research as repeated observations of the same units on the same outcomes over a certain amount of time."
- 要点：纵向研究的定义：在一段时间内，对同一批单位的同一批结果变量重复观察。

### E3

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 2 页
- 原文："authors refer to longitudinal research when retrospectively assessing outcomes through one observation only, i.e. when requesting subjects to remember and report past changes"
- 要点：有的研究只观察一次、让对象回忆过去的变化，也自称纵向研究；作者不采用这种宽泛的定义。

### E4

- 位置：§4.1 Guideline 1，第 5 页
- 原文："Thus, theoretical considerations on the expected fluctuation of the outcome variable must precede any decision on a particular study design, such as (1) the number and space of repeated measurements, or (2) the selection of an adequate time metric."
- 要点：在决定测量次数、间隔和用什么度量时间之前，先要从理论上想清楚结果变量预期会怎样波动。

### E5

- 位置：§4.1 Guideline 1，第 6 页
- 原文："metric of time may include any measure that is non-reversible, e.g. age and grades, money spent, or hours of system use"
- 要点：时间的度量不一定是日历时间，任何不可逆的量（年龄、年级、花费、系统使用时长）都可以作为时间尺度。

### E6

- 位置：§4.1 Guideline 1，第 6 页
- 原文："If such groundwork is missing, scholars could still consider qualitative prestudies to correctly anticipate oscillation (Ployhart and Ward, 2011), or align wave space and frequency to the fluctuation of the fastest changing variable"
- 要点：没有现成理论可依时，可以先做定性预研究来估计变化节律，或者按变化最快的那个变量来安排测量间隔和频率。

### E7

- 位置：§4.2 Guideline 2，第 7 页
- 原文："This class of research questions indeed requires at least three measurement occasions to correctly and extensively describe linear growth, and even more waves are required to identify non-linear change over time"
- 要点：要描述变化的形态（例如线性增长），至少需要三次测量；要识别非线性变化，需要更多次。

### E8

- 位置：§4.2 Guideline 2，第 7 页
- 原文："However, there is a feasible class of research questions aiming to detect whether or why a change exists at all." / "Typically, pretest-posttest designs with two measurement occasions are applied in such a context"
- 要点：只想知道"有没有变化、为什么变化"的问题，常用两次测量的前测—后测设计。

### E9

- 位置：§4.2 Guideline 2，第 7 页
- 原文："However, these designs have been subject to continuous criticism alluding to reliability and validity artifacts" / "As a silver bullet to these issues, scholars typically apply randomized control group trials, assuming that measurement errors will equally affect all groups, whereas true change will reflect in differences of change between samples with identical pre-conditions"
- 要点：两次测量的设计一直被批评难以区分真实变化和测量误差；常用办法是加随机对照组：测量误差对各组影响相同，真实变化体现为组间变化量的差异。

### E10

- 位置：§4.3 Guideline 3，第 8 页
- 原文："However, higher effectiveness may also be confounded by a simple training effect – users accustomed to the existing usability issues and learned to bypass them, resulting in a shorter time needed to fulfill the task."
- 要点：例子：反复测同一批用户完成任务的效率，效率提高可能只是因为用户熟悉了、学会了绕开问题（练习效应），而不是产品真的变好了。

### E11

- 位置：§4.3 Guideline 3，第 8 页
- 原文："If subjects remember their answers from preceding measurement occasions, they will probably aspire to repeat their responses to keep consistency, despite real changes, resulting in false-negative findings" / "Furthermore, repeated measurements increase the likelihood of subjects correctly guessing the study purpose, leading to experimenter demand effects"
- 要点：被试记得上一轮的答案，会为了前后一致而重复，掩盖真实变化；多次测量也让被试更容易猜到研究目的，按研究者的期望作答。

### E12

- 位置：§4.3 Guideline 3，第 9 页
- 原文："When studying why a change occurs, we suggest introducing a control group of similar individuals as a silver bullet to these issues."
- 要点：研究"为什么变化"时，建议引入条件相似的对照组来应对上述效度威胁。

### E13

- 位置：§4.3 Guideline 3，第 9 页
- 原文："In fact, attrition is an expectable and, in most cases, unavoidable issue when dealing with longitudinal data" / "First, sample sizes decrease, diminishing statistical power and the amount of available analysing techniques (ibid.). Second, systematic differences between respondents and non-respondents may threaten internal and external validity"
- 要点：流失（对象中途退出）在纵向研究中几乎不可避免。后果一是样本变小、统计功效下降；二是留下的和退出的对象之间可能有系统性差异，威胁内部和外部效度。

### E14

- 位置：§4.3 Guideline 3，第 9 页
- 原文："If, for any reason, all subjects with low perceived acceptance had left the study at some point of time, an artificial elevation in average acceptance would have resulted."
- 要点：例子：如果接受度低的人都中途退出了，平均接受度就会被人为抬高。

### E15

- 位置：§4.3 Guideline 3，第 9 页
- 原文："Thus, one should anticipate and expect attrition by including more participants than needed (Ployhart and Vandenberg, 2010), by adopting response enhancement techniques like incentives (Anseel et al., 2010) or by reducing the amount and spaces of waves to a reasonable number."
- 要点：应对流失：招募比所需更多的对象；用激励等手段提高回应率；把测量轮数和间隔控制在合理范围。

### E16

- 位置：§4.3 Guideline 3，第 9 页
- 原文："In prior IS literature investigating change over time, however, most studies did not explicitly account for attrition (20 out of 21), but simply excluded incomplete data sets"
- 要点：作者回顾的 21 项信息系统纵向研究中，20 项没有明确处理流失，只是把不完整的数据直接删掉。

### E17

- 位置：§4.4 Guideline 4，第 10 页
- 原文："In contrast to cross-sectional data, longitudinal data is interdependent, i.e. data gathered from a successive wave can be, to a certain extent, predicted by data gathered at a preceding wave" / "interdependent measurement values most likely violate the assumption of uncorrelated residuals"
- 要点：纵向数据前后相依，后一轮的数据在一定程度上可以由前一轮预测；这会违反很多常用统计方法（如方差分析）"残差互不相关"的前提。

### E18

- 位置：§4.4 Guideline 4，第 10 页
- 原文："Other scholars, in contrast, relied on descriptive statistics (Vitari and Ravarini, 2009), correlation analysis (Kim, 2009a), or structure equation modeling (e.g. Kim et al., 2009; Venkatesh and Sykes, 2013), potentially reporting inflated relationships between analysed variables due to autocorrelation."
- 要点：用普通的相关分析等方法处理纵向数据，可能因为自相关而夸大变量之间的关系。

### E19

- 位置：§4.4 Guideline 4，第 10–11 页
- 原文："Repeated measures GLM (e.g. repeated measures ANOVA, paired-samples t-test, trend analysis by regression) is applicable for any research question referring to the group level" / "Hierarchical linear modeling (HLM), in contrast, enables researchers to model interunit differences in intraunit changes using a multiple level approach"
- 要点：只关心群体层面的变化，可用重复测量的一般线性模型（如重复测量方差分析、配对 t 检验）；要研究不同个体的变化轨迹有何差异，可用分层线性模型（多层模型）。
