# ASCE Library：Data Availability Statement

- 资料：`ascedataavailability`，[原文](https://ascelibrary.org/page/dataavailability)（ASCE 网站拦截脚本访问，本摘录读自 Internet Archive 2022-06-21 快照：http://web.archive.org/web/20220621123856/https://ascelibrary.org/page/dataavailability）
- 版本：美国土木工程师学会（ASCE）期刊的数据可得性声明政策页面，2022 年快照。现行版本可能已更新，投稿时以目标期刊页面为准。

### E1

- 位置：Data Availability Statement，第 1 段
- 原文："ASCE is introducing a new policy requiring authors to specify the availability of data, computational models, code, and other electronic materials used in work submitted for publication."
- 要点：ASCE 要求作者说明投稿工作所用数据、计算模型、代码和其他电子材料能否获得。

### E2

- 位置：Data Availability Statement，第 2 段
- 原文："When submitting a new manuscript, authors will include a new section titled “Data Availability Statement” before the “Acknowledgments.” Within this section, authors will select one or more of the following statements verbatim"
- 要点：投稿时在致谢前加一节"数据可得性声明"，从给定的几种说法中原样选一种或几种。

### E3

- 位置：Data Availability Statement，可选说法列表
- 原文："Some or all data, models, or code that support the findings of this study are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request." / "Some or all data, models, or code generated or used during the study are proprietary or confidential in nature and may only be provided with restrictions."
- 要点：可选说法包括"可向通讯作者合理索取"和"属于专有或保密材料，只能有条件提供"等。

### E4

- 位置：Data Availability Statement，列表之后
- 原文："Available data and code must be cited in the reference section. Authors may update or change their statement if information changes during the course of peer review. It may not be changed after the paper is accepted."
- 要点：公开的数据和代码要列入参考文献；声明在审稿期间可以改，录用后不能再改。

### E5

- 位置：References 之后
- 原文："All authors are required to make data and materials available to the editors and reviewers during the peer review process if requested."
- 要点：审稿期间，编辑和审稿人要求时，作者必须提供数据和材料。

### E6

- 位置：Where should my data go?
- 原文："ASCE will publish Supplemental Materials along with a paper; however, the file types are limited and not appropriate for large data sets or code. Further, Supplemental Materials do not have their own DOI and cannot be cited on their own."
- 要点：ASCE 的补充材料文件类型有限，不适合大数据集和代码；补充材料没有自己的 DOI，不能单独引用。

### E7

- 位置：What should we share?
- 原文："Data used to generate figures or tables should be included."
- 要点：生成图表所用的数据应当包括在共享内容里。

### E8

- 位置：Will my data or code be peer reviewed?
- 原文："No. If authors make data, models, and code available, reviewers and editors may or may not review those files and provide feedback."
- 要点：ASCE 不对数据和代码做同行评审；审稿人和编辑可能看，也可能不看。

### E9

- 位置：What if my data is proprietary?
- 原文："Authors may not claim that data is proprietary just because they do not want to share it. ASCE encourages authors who enter agreements to collect or analyze proprietary data to negotiate terms for making the data used in a paper available to readers (e.g., publish anonymously or without identifying information)."
- 要点：不能因为不想共享就声称数据是专有的；ASCE 鼓励作者在签数据协议时就谈好，让论文所用数据能以匿名等方式提供给读者。

### E10

- 位置：Where should my data go?
- 原文："Ideally, the repository chosen will provide authors with a Digital Object Identifier (DOI) for the data. DOIs provide a persistent link to the data and avoids link rot."
- 要点：最好选能给数据分配 DOI（数字对象标识符）的仓库，DOI 是持久链接，能避免链接失效。

### E11

- 位置：Where should my data go?
- 原文："More general data repositories include: Dryad, Figshare, DesignSafe, Open Science Framework, Harvard Dataverse, Zenodo, HydroShare"
- 要点：ASCE 列出的通用数据仓库包括 Dryad、Figshare、DesignSafe、Open Science Framework、Harvard Dataverse、Zenodo、HydroShare。
