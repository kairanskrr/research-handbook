# Bulley & Şahin 2021：Practice Research（Report 1: What is practice research? / Report 2: How can practice research be shared?）

- 资料：`bulley2021practice`，[原文 PDF](https://eprints-gro.gold.ac.uk/30222/1/2021_Bulley-Sahin_Practice-Research-reports.pdf)，DOI: 10.23636/1347
- 版本：2021 年 4 月 1 日，英国实践研究顾问组（Practice Research Advisory Group UK，PRAG-UK）发布，Research England 资助，CC BY-NC-ND 4.0。依据 2019–2021 年对英格兰 62 位实践研究者、理论学者、科研支持人员和政策制定者的访谈、问卷和调查。位置写段落编号。

### E1

- 位置："What methods did we use?"，第 3 页
- 原文："Practice research is when practice is the significant method conveyed in a research output."
- 要点：实践研究：实践是研究成果所传达的主要方法。

### E2

- 位置：第 1.1.1.3 段
- 原文："Excellent practice may not necessarily translate into excellent practice research, and vice versa.11 For practice to be practice research the research has to be articulated."
- 要点：好的实践不一定是好的实践研究，反之亦然；实践要成为实践研究，研究必须被说清楚。

### E3

- 位置：第 1.1.1.3 段，脚注 11（引 Knudsen 2018）
- 原文："[d]oing good research is not about making successful works: it is about succeeding in generating new insights."
- 要点：做好研究不在于做出成功的作品，而在于产生新的洞见。

### E4

- 位置：第 1.2.2.6 段
- 原文："For the vast majority of practice research outputs, it is not possible for research audiences to experience the practice first hand, and as a result ‘documentation’ is employed as a proxy for practice."
- 要点：绝大多数实践研究成果，读者无法亲身体验实践本身，只能靠"记录"（documentation）代替。

### E5

- 位置：第 1.2.2.1 段
- 原文："Our findings indicate that in most cases, an effective means to share ways of knowing in practice research is by joining a research narrative to a practice."
- 要点：他们的发现：多数情况下，把"研究叙述"和实践结合起来，是分享实践研究中认识的有效方式。

### E6

- 位置：第 1.2.2.10 段
- 原文："Practice researchers should not feel a requirement to present their research in non-linear, cross-media and complex item types: in some instances a journal article or book chapter may be the most appropriate avenue for a research narrative."
- 要点：不必觉得一定要用非线性、跨媒介的复杂形式；有时期刊论文或书的一章就是写研究叙述最合适的地方。

### E7

- 位置：第 1.4.1.6 段
- 原文："The different components of these practice research outputs (commonly involving a research narrative alongside documentation of practice) were usually dispersed across multiple locations, including commercial video and sound hosting platforms, research repositories, blogs, and personal web pages."
- 要点：受访者报告：实践研究成果的各部分常分散在商业视频音频平台、机构库、博客、个人网页等多处。

### E8

- 位置："What methods did we use?"，第 3 页；"Scope"
- 原文："sixty two contemporary practice researchers, theorists, research support professionals and policymakers [...] we have limited our scope to the boundaries of England."
- 要点：报告依据 62 位实践研究者、理论学者、科研支持人员和政策制定者；范围限于英格兰。

### E9

- 位置：第 1.5.4.2 段
- 原文："care has to be taken to provide adequate descriptive texts for multimedia content and transcriptions should be provided where possible."
- 要点：多媒体内容要配足够的描述文字，尽量提供文字稿，方便有障碍的读者。

### E10

- 位置：第 1.5.4.4 段
- 原文："Practice research often makes use of multimedia documentation which can have particularly complex copyright and licensing implications."
- 要点：实践研究常用多媒体记录，版权和授权问题特别复杂。

### E11

- 位置：第 1.5.4.7 段
- 原文："it is sometimes the case that practice researchers do not consider the ethical implications of human participants in practice research projects."
- 要点：实践研究者有时没有考虑项目中人类参与者的伦理问题。

### E12

- 位置：第 1.5.4.8 段
- 原文："ensuring that where people have been involved in the production of these things, that they are cited and they are mentioned."
- 要点：参与制作的人，都要被列出、被提到。

### E13

- 位置：第 1.4.1.5 段
- 原文："there are substantial challenges in sharing practice publicly due to ethical concerns (for example in nursing, or in projects exploring loss and bereavement)"
- 要点：出于伦理考虑，公开分享某些实践（例如护理、丧亲主题的项目）有很大困难。

### E14

- 位置：第 1.2.2.7 段，脚注 144
- 原文："See the use of ‘exposition’ in the Research Catalogue, a repository of research attached to JAR (Journal for Artistic Research)"
- 要点："Research Catalogue"是附属于《艺术研究期刊》（Journal for Artistic Research，JAR）的研究库，用"展陈"（exposition）的形式发表。
