# Bulley & Şahin 2021：Practice Research（Report 1: What is practice research? / Report 2: How can practice research be shared?）

- 资料：`bulley2021practice`，[原文 PDF](https://eprints-gro.gold.ac.uk/30222/1/2021_Bulley-Sahin_Practice-Research-reports.pdf)，DOI: 10.23636/1347
- 版本：2021 年 4 月 1 日，英国实践研究顾问组（Practice Research Advisory Group UK，PRAG-UK）发布，Research England 资助，CC BY-NC-ND 4.0。依据 2019–2021 年对英格兰 62 位实践研究者、理论学者、科研支持人员和政策制定者的访谈、问卷和调查。位置写段落编号。

### E1

- 位置："What methods did we use?"，第 3 页
- 原文："Practice research is when practice is the significant method conveyed in a research output."
- 要点：实践研究：实践是研究成果所传达的主要方法。

### E2

- 位置：第 1.1.1.3 段
- 原文："Excellent practice may not necessarily translate into excellent practice research, and vice versa.11 For practice to be practice research the research has to be articulated."
- 要点：好的实践不一定是好的实践研究，反之亦然；实践要成为实践研究，研究必须被说清楚。

### E3

- 位置：第 1.1.1.3 段，脚注 11（引 Knudsen 2018）
- 原文："[d]oing good research is not about making successful works: it is about succeeding in generating new insights."
- 要点：做好研究不在于做出成功的作品，而在于产生新的洞见。

### E4

- 位置：第 1.2.2.6 段
- 原文："For the vast majority of practice research outputs, it is not possible for research audiences to experience the practice first hand, and as a result ‘documentation’ is employed as a proxy for practice."
- 要点：绝大多数实践研究成果，读者无法亲身体验实践本身，只能靠"记录"（documentation）代替。

### E5

- 位置：第 1.2.2.1 段
- 原文："Our findings indicate that in most cases, an effective means to share ways of knowing in practice research is by joining a research narrative to a practice."
- 要点：他们的发现：多数情况下，把"研究叙述"和实践结合起来，是分享实践研究中认识的有效方式。

### E6

- 位置：第 1.2.2.10 段
- 原文："Practice researchers should not feel a requirement to present their research in non-linear, cross-media and complex item types: in some instances a journal article or book chapter may be the most appropriate avenue for a research narrative."
- 要点：不必觉得一定要用非线性、跨媒介的复杂形式；有时期刊论文或书的一章就是写研究叙述最合适的地方。

### E7

- 位置：第 1.4.1.6 段
- 原文："The different components of these practice research outputs (commonly involving a research narrative alongside documentation of practice) were usually dispersed across multiple locations, including commercial video and sound hosting platforms, research repositories, blogs, and personal web pages."
- 要点：受访者报告：实践研究成果的各部分常分散在商业视频音频平台、机构库、博客、个人网页等多处。

### E8

- 位置："What methods did we use?"，第 3 页；"Scope"
- 原文："sixty two contemporary practice researchers, theorists, research support professionals and policymakers [...] we have limited our scope to the boundaries of England."
- 要点：报告依据 62 位实践研究者、理论学者、科研支持人员和政策制定者；范围限于英格兰。

### E9

- 位置：第 1.5.4.2 段
- 原文："care has to be taken to provide adequate descriptive texts for multimedia content and transcriptions should be provided where possible."
- 要点：多媒体内容要配足够的描述文字，尽量提供文字稿，方便有障碍的读者。

### E10

- 位置：第 1.5.4.4 段
- 原文："Practice research often makes use of multimedia documentation which can have particularly complex copyright and licensing implications."
- 要点：实践研究常用多媒体记录，版权和授权问题特别复杂。

### E11

- 位置：第 1.5.4.7 段
- 原文："it is sometimes the case that practice researchers do not consider the ethical implications of human participants in practice research projects."
- 要点：实践研究者有时没有考虑项目中人类参与者的伦理问题。

### E12

- 位置：第 1.5.4.8 段
- 原文："ensuring that where people have been involved in the production of these things, that they are cited and they are mentioned."
- 要点：参与制作的人，都要被列出、被提到。

### E13

- 位置：第 1.4.1.5 段
- 原文："there are substantial challenges in sharing practice publicly due to ethical concerns (for example in nursing, or in projects exploring loss and bereavement)"
- 要点：出于伦理考虑，公开分享某些实践（例如护理、丧亲主题的项目）有很大困难。

### E14

- 位置：第 1.2.2.7 段，脚注 144
- 原文："See the use of ‘exposition’ in the Research Catalogue, a repository of research attached to JAR (Journal for Artistic Research)"
- 要点："Research Catalogue"是附属于《艺术研究期刊》（Journal for Artistic Research，JAR）的研究库，用"展陈"（exposition）的形式发表。

### E15

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.2.1.5 段
- 原文："For artist researcher Carole Gray, problem formation for practitioners does not necessarily take place in a neat or predictable way, but emerges over time according to the needs of the [...] practice and the evolving sense of what a project is. That is not to say that the fundamental research questions necessarily change, but more that the final problems are not decided until the practice-led project is completed."
- 要点：艺术家研究者 Carole Gray 认为，实践者的问题不一定整齐、可预测地形成，而是随实践需要和对项目的理解逐步浮现；基本研究问题不一定变，但最终的问题要到项目完成才定下来。

### E16

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.3.2.3 段
- 原文："One of the other challenges that commonly faces practice research PhD students, as highlighted by Redhead, is that the locus of inquiry or use of specific methods may shift as a project is underway."
- 要点：实践研究博士生常遇到的一个难题：项目进行中，探究的焦点或所用方法可能会转移。

### E17

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.2.2.9 段
- 原文："As Corby observes when explaining the process of institutional assessment for practice research outputs: “We can start guessing but it’s not our job to guess. It’s the job of the researcher to articulate what is the innovation.”"
- 要点：Corby 谈机构评估实践研究成果时说：评审可以猜，但猜不是评审的工作；说清创新在哪里是研究者的工作。

### E18

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.2.2.13 段
- 原文："Unless relevant to describing the practice as method, there is no need for additional philosophical justification of practice as method, just as there is no need to justify the existence of qualitative or quantitative research techniques in other areas of research."
- 要点：除非和描述方法直接相关，否则不需要额外用哲学来论证"实践可以作为方法"，就像别的领域不需要论证定性或定量方法为何存在。

### E19

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.2.2.13 段
- 原文："with journal editors, PhD supervisors and research assessors observing the excessive length of time taken by practice researchers to get to the discussion of the research itself, prefacing their work with long philosophical and ontological explanations of how practice is the significant method within the research output."
- 要点：期刊编辑、博士导师和评审者观察到，实践研究者常先写很长的哲学和本体论解释，很久才进入研究本身。

### E20

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.2.2.14 段
- 原文："“I think the simpler the language, the more powerful the proposition. What you don’t want to do is explain a little idea in big words. You want to explain a big idea in little words.”"
- 要点：Brown 说：语言越简单，主张越有力；要用小词讲大想法，不要用大词讲小想法。

### E21

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.1.3 段
- 原文："‐ (Summary / Abstract) ‐ Context / Introduction ‐ Research inquiry / Questions in practice ‐ Method statements ‐ Practice, description of practice, or documentation of practice (as a proxy) ‐ Insights / Discussion of ways of knowing ‐ Conclusion and references"
- 要点：PRAG-UK 给出的实践研究成果通用结构示例：（摘要）、背景/引言、研究探究/实践中的问题、方法说明、实践本身或其描述或记录、洞见/认识方式的讨论、结论和参考文献。

### E22

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.1.2 段
- 原文："it is useful to include an example of how a practice research output may be structured in cases where a research [...] narrative is not embodied in the practice."
- 要点：这个结构示例适用于研究叙述没有体现在实践本身中的情况。

### E23

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.1.5 段
- 原文："does a non-linear presentation form cause accessibility issues for certain audiences? Will a non-linear presentation cause problems for long-term preservation? Does a non-linear presentation affect the ability of the research to be interoperable and discoverable?"
- 要点：用非线性方式呈现前先问：会不会给某些读者造成无障碍问题？会不会影响长期保存？会不会影响研究被检索和互通？

### E24

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.2.1 段
- 原文："As theorist and researcher Michael Biggs summarises, practice “isn’t turned into research by being documented, it doesn’t have to be documented; but it’s efficient to document it, if possible.”"
- 要点：Biggs 说，实践不是因为被记录才变成研究，也不一定要记录；但能记录的话，记录是高效的做法。

### E25

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.2.3 段
- 原文："it is important to differentiate between an overall practice research output and documentation as a proxy for practice within a research output."
- 要点：要区分完整的实践研究成果，和成果中作为实践替代物的记录。

### E26

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.2.4 段
- 原文："“I tell my students to always look at when they get to a point in their PhD research where they need to capture and start owning things, particularly if they’re making things that have potential copyright.”"
- 要点：Jane Grant 提醒学生：留意博士研究到了什么阶段需要把东西记录下来、开始确立所有权，尤其是做的东西可能涉及版权时。

### E27

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.2.5 段
- 原文："Among the vast array of tools used by practice researchers to create documentation as a proxy for their practice, the most common we heard reference to were: film and digital cameras, notebooks, file sharing systems, sound recorders, post-production software, and code repositories."
- 要点：受访者最常提到的记录工具：胶片和数码相机、笔记本、文件共享系统、录音设备、后期制作软件、代码仓库。

### E28

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.2.6 段
- 原文："One general way forward with this issue is to create representative excerpts of practice (such as pages from musical scores or excerpts from films), that can then be shared under an appropriate Creative Commons license."
- 要点：完整记录因版权、授权或经济原因不能公开时，一个办法是做有代表性的节选（如乐谱几页、影片片段），用合适的知识共享许可分享。

### E29

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.2.7 段
- 原文："One of the most common pieces of feedback we had from our interviewees regarding receiving practice research as an audience, was the need for documentation (as a proxy for practice) to be of reasonable format and length."
- 要点：受访者作为读者最常提的反馈之一：记录的格式和长度要合理。

### E30

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.2.8 段
- 原文："Where it is not possible for documentation to be employed as a proxy for practice (due to financial constraints, conceptual issues or licensing issues for example), a description of practice can function to elucidate the practice as part of a research narrative."
- 要点：因经费、观念或授权原因无法用记录代替实践时，可以在研究叙述里用文字描述实践。

### E31

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.3.6 段
- 原文："Jane Boggan, Panel Adviser for Main Panel D for REF 2014 and REF 2021, states: “Where does this piece of work fit? If you’re going to make the case that this is a new insight, unless you’ve got a sense of where this sits in the context of other work in that field, it’s very difficult to say whether it’s new or not.”"
- 要点：REF 艺术与人文评审组顾问 Boggan 说：如果不清楚一件作品在该领域其他工作中处于什么位置，就很难说它是不是新的。

### E32

- 位置：Report 2，第 2.1.1.7 段
- 原文："Vital resources that can be drawn upon as reference for those seeking structural guidance of this kind are the British Library’s EThOS service, Open Access Theses and Dissertations (OATD), Networked Digital Library of Theses and Dissertations, and the DART-Europe E-Theses Portal, all of which provide examples of hundreds of practice research PhDs completed over the last twenty years."
- 要点：想参考实践研究博士论文结构，可查英国国家图书馆 EThOS、OATD、NDLTD、DART-Europe 等学位论文库，里面有过去二十年完成的数百篇实践研究博士论文。

### E33

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.5.4.7 段
- 原文："As Chow notes, it is sometimes the case that practice researchers do not consider the ethical implications of human participants in practice research projects. Chow has found in undertaking his own practice research projects that training in methods from social sciences and qualitative research has been valuable"
- 要点：实践研究者有时没有考虑人类参与者的伦理问题；Chow 发现社会科学和定性研究方法的训练对他很有帮助。

### E34

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.5.4.10 段
- 原文："Areas which merit consideration include, but are not limited to, working with vulnerable groups, working with sensitive themes such as loss and trauma, experiments involving human and non-human subjects, and physical and mental health considerations."
- 要点：值得考虑的伦理领域包括：和弱势群体合作、涉及丧失和创伤等敏感主题、涉及人和非人类对象的实验、身心健康问题。

### E35

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.5.4.10 段
- 原文："In forming the guidance and standards for ethical practice research, there is much to be learnt from the protocols already established in other research fields, particularly the social sciences"
- 要点：制定实践研究的伦理规范时，可以向其他领域（尤其社会科学）已有的规程学习。

### E36

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.5.4.5 段
- 原文："As professional practice is often funded outside of the institution, its dissemination within practice research outputs can cause adverse economic and intellectual property issues for practice researchers."
- 要点：职业创作常由校外资助，把它放进公开的实践研究成果里，可能给研究者带来经济和知识产权上的不利影响。

### E37

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.2.2.5 段
- 原文："From our interviewees we found that dependent on the audience and the framing of the practice, practice can be practice research, or practice."
- 要点：受访者的看法是：取决于受众和怎样框定，同一实践可以是实践研究，也可以只是实践。

### E38

- 位置：Report 1，第 1.2.1.5 段，Practice-Based Research 小标题下
- 原文："Candy’s description does not entirely accord with the use of the term in other disciplines. In clinical practice, practice-based research is used commonly, especially in psychotherapy, public health and family medicine. [...] “in simple terms, refers to research conducted within practice settings.”"
- 要点：Candy 的说法和其他学科的用法不完全一致；在临床领域（心理治疗、公共卫生、家庭医学）practice-based research 也很常用，指在实践场景中做的研究。
