# Mendez 2020：Enlisting the Gatekeeper: Chain-Referral and Elite Access in Foreign Policy Analysis

- 资料：`mendez2020enlisting`，[原文](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/103025/4/Mendez_2020_Foreign_Policy_Analysis_Case_Study.pdf)
- 版本：SAGE Research Methods Cases，2020，DOI 10.4135/9781529711318；作者存放在伦敦政治经济学院（LSE）机构库的出版版本。作者 Alvaro Mendez 讲他研究"哥伦比亚计划"（Plan Colombia）时访谈美国和哥伦比亚决策者的经验，是一个人的案例经验，不是系统研究。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract；Project Overview and Context
- 原文："The author found that non-random "snowball sampling" or chain-referral can begin with the gatekeepers, who themselves give access to elite interviewees. In the context of a process-tracing study, this is a potent methodology. In short, the key to getting "snowball access" rolling is the interpersonal relationships that the researcher should establish with the first few persons who grant an interview." / "A single method solved both problems, and aided the author to interview more than half of the decision makers known to have had a hand in the making of Plan Colombia"
- 要点：作者的经验：非随机的"滚雪球"抽样（链式引荐）可以从"守门人"开始，由他们引荐精英受访者；在过程追踪研究里很有效；关键是和最早接受访谈的几个人建立关系。靠这个办法，他访谈了已知参与制定"哥伦比亚计划"的决策者中的一半以上。

### E2

- 位置：Research Design--The Case for Snowballing into Elite Interviews
- 原文："random sampling is hit-or-miss if "the causal processes of interest are very specific episodes of decisionmaking at the elite level, where a limited set of actors are involved in deliberations, decisions, and actions regarding a particular political outcome" (Tansey, 2007, p. 769). Thus, it is that more and more methodology theorists are acknowledging the validity of non-probability sampling of prospective elite interviewees."
- 要点：Mendez 转引 Tansey 2007 第 769 页：如果关心的是精英层面非常具体的决策过程、只有少数人参与，随机抽样就是碰运气；越来越多的方法学者承认对精英受访者做非概率抽样是有效的。

### E3

- 位置：Practical Lessons Learned，第 6、7 条
- 原文："A gentler way of signaling your status would be to use your institution's email address or letterhead in all correspondence." / "Mention your best accomplishments in casual conversation or in a covering letter."
- 要点：联系受访者时，所有往来都用单位的邮箱或信笺；在附信或闲谈里提一下自己最好的成果，以示自己值得认真对待。
