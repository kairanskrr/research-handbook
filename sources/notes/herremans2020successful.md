# Herremans 2020：How to be a successful PhD student?

- 资料：`herremans2020successful`，[原文](https://medium.com/data-science/how-to-be-a-successful-phd-student-3f3a4eb48be8)
- 版本：Medium（Towards Data Science）文章，2020-04-13；经 r.jina.ai 阅读器读取于 2026-10-05。正文是一组无编号的要点，位置写所在要点的开头。

### E1

- 位置：要点"Avoid sending this all too common email..."
- 原文："Always alert all co-authors on manuscripts well in advance before submitting them. While supervisors love receiving news that a new (draft) paper is ready, a better approach would be to share conference manuscripts at least two weeks in advance so that your senior co-authors can plan their time."
- 要点：投稿前要提前通知所有合作者；会议论文至少提前两周发出，让资深合作者能安排时间。

### E2

- 位置：要点"As a junior member of the team, pair up..."
- 原文："Communicate very regularly with this person, and report on larger milestones with your supervisor, so as to maintain a proper research direction."
- 要点：新人可以和一位资深成员（最好是博士后）结对；日常和他频繁沟通，较大的里程碑再向导师汇报。

### E3

- 位置：要点"Thoroughly proofread your manuscript..."
- 原文："Thoroughly proofread your manuscript before sending it to a supervisor or co-author."
- 要点：稿件发给导师或合作者之前，自己先仔细校对，让对方把时间花在研究意见上。

### E4

- 位置：要点"All authors listed on a manuscript..."
- 原文："All authors listed on a manuscript need to have made a significant contribution to the work. This could be through coding, ideation, editing, advising, among others. Do not just include friends as co-authors. In addition, everyone needs to have read the final version of the manuscript before submitting."
- 要点：署名作者都必须对工作有实质贡献，不要挂朋友的名；投稿前所有作者都要读过最终稿。

### E5

- 位置：要点"If your manuscript contains software code..."
- 原文："Labs should be collaborative, not competitive."
- 要点：实验室内部应该合作而不是竞争，例如把代码共享给组里同学。

### E6

- 位置：要点"Own your project."
- 原文："Own your project. When you meet with your supervisor, show up with all tasks completed, and ideally, the next step in mind or already started. [...] Do not just show up expecting your supervisor to give you small pre-chewed tasks."
- 要点：项目是你自己的：见导师时把任务做完，最好已经想好甚至开始了下一步，不要等导师分配嚼碎的小任务。
