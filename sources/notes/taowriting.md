# Tao：On writing（写论文建议系列网页）

- 资料：`taowriting`，[原文](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/advice-on-writing-papers/)
- 版本：Terence Tao（陶哲轩）博客 "What's new" 上的 On writing 系列页面，2026-10-06 读取。每页是一条独立的建议，位置写页面标题。

### E1

- 位置：Create lemmas
- 原文："One should write the statement of a lemma in a way that makes it easy to use, rather than easy to prove."
- 要点：引理的陈述要写得便于使用，而不是便于证明。

### E2

- 位置：Create lemmas
- 原文："Lemmas also provide a good opportunity to explicitly “recap” all the running hypotheses, assumptions, and notational conventions that have already been introduced in the argument"
- 要点：引理是重新列明当前所有假设和记号约定的好时机。

### E3

- 位置：Describe the results accurately
- 原文："A paper should neither understate nor overstate its main results."
- 要点：论文对主要结果既不要说小，也不要说大。

### E4

- 位置：Describe the results accurately
- 原文："Conversely, if there are unsatisfactory aspects to the result (e.g. hypotheses too strong, or conclusions a little weaker than expected) these should also be stated honestly and openly, e.g. “We do not know if hypothesis H is actually necessary”."
- 要点：结果不尽如人意的地方（假设太强、结论偏弱）要坦白写出来。

### E5

- 位置：Describe the results accurately
- 原文："If for some reason you need to assert a non-trivial statement without proof or citation, it should be made clear that you are doing so"
- 要点：不加证明也不加引用地断言一个非平凡的命题时，要明确告诉读者。

### E6

- 位置：Give appropriate amounts of detail
- 原文："A paper should dwell at length (using plenty of English) on the most important, innovative, and crucial components of the paper, and be brief on the routine, expected, and standard components of the paper."
- 要点：最重要、最新、最关键的部分要详细写（多用文字解释），常规、标准的部分要简短。

### E7

- 位置：Use good notation
- 原文："A good rule of thumb is that any expression or concept which is used three or more times will probably benefit from introducing some notation to capture that expression or concept; conversely, an expression which is only used once probably does not need its own special notation."
- 要点：经验法则：用到三次以上的表达式或概念值得引入记号；只用一次的通常不需要。

### E8

- 位置：Use good notation
- 原文："One should strive to make one’s choices of notation compatible and consistent with notation already in the literature"
- 要点：记号要尽量与已有文献一致。

### E9

- 位置：Use good notation
- 原文："Notation which is used globally should be defined in a notation section near the front of the paper, or in the introduction; notation which is only used locally (e.g. within a single section, or within a proof of a single lemma) should be defined close to where it is used"
- 要点：全文通用的记号在前面集中定义；只在局部用的记号在用到的地方附近定义。

### E10

- 位置：Use good notation
- 原文："Try to avoid notation which is overly “cute” or “clever”."
- 要点：避免过于花哨、"聪明"的记号。

### E11

- 位置：On "local" and "global" errors in mathematical papers, and how to detect them
- 原文："This simple example illustrates the importance of getting key terms defined precisely in a mathematical argument."
- 要点：一个表达式有两种解释、而论证前后各用了一种时，错误无法定位到某一步；关键术语必须精确定义。

### E12

- 位置：On "local" and "global" errors in mathematical papers, and how to detect them
- 原文："The other way to find an error in a proof is to obtain a “high level” or “global” objection, showing that the proof, if valid, would necessarily imply a further consequence that is either known or strongly suspected to be false."
- 要点：找错的另一条路是"全局"反驳：说明这个证明如果成立，会推出已知为假或极可能为假的结论（例如存在反例）。

### E13

- 位置：On "local" and "global" errors in mathematical papers, and how to detect them
- 原文："Other examples of this type include proofs of some conclusion that mysteriously never use in any essential way a crucial hypothesis"
- 要点：证明从头到尾没有实质用到某个关键假设，也是全局错误的信号。

### E14

- 位置：On "local" and "global" errors in mathematical papers, and how to detect them
- 原文："It is generally good practice to try to structure a proof to be fault tolerant with respect to local errors"
- 要点：证明要尽量组织得能容忍局部错误：某一步出错时，整篇论文不至于全盘崩溃，还能留下有价值的部分结果。

### E15

- 位置：On "local" and "global" errors in mathematical papers, and how to detect them
- 原文："But when one is testing an argument for errors or other objections, then it is perfectly acceptable to use heuristics, hand-waving, intuition, or other non-rigorous means to locate and describe errors."
- 要点：构造证明要尽可能严谨；但检查论证找错时，用启发式、直觉等不严格的手段完全可以。

### E16

- 位置：Write a rapid prototype first
- 原文："First, one writes a bare-bones “skeleton” or “prototype” of the paper as quickly as possible; this prototype has the (approximate) statements of all the key lemmas, propositions, and theorems, as well as all key definitions, but all the proofs are omitted, or sketched in very informal “notes to self”."
- 要点：先尽快写出论文骨架：所有关键定义、引理、定理的（大致）陈述，证明先省略或只写给自己看的草记。

### E17

- 位置：Submit to an appropriate journal
- 原文："It is always tempting to submit a paper to a prestigious journal, but if the paper makes only a borderline case for publication in this journal, then the net result may be a lengthy process, critical reviews holding the paper to a very high standard, and ultimate rejection of the paper."
- 要点：勉强够得上顶级期刊的论文投过去，结果可能是漫长的流程、极高标准的审稿，最后被拒。

### E18

- 位置：Submit to an appropriate journal
- 原文："It is also a good idea to check that the editorial board of the journal you are submitting to contains at least one member who is expert enough in the fields that your paper is in that he or she can judge its quality appropriately and send it to a good referee."
- 要点：投稿前查一下期刊编委会里有没有懂你方向的人，能判断质量、找到合适的审稿人。

### E19

- 位置：Submit to an appropriate journal
- 原文："Is the paper written so that it is easy to check that the arguments are correct?"
- 要点：判断论文配投什么级别期刊的第一个问题：论文是否写得让人容易检查论证正确。

### E20

- 位置：Submit to an appropriate journal
- 原文："Generally speaking, it is not recommended to simultaneously submit two unrelated papers to the same journal"
- 要点：一般不建议同时向同一家期刊投两篇无关的论文。

### E21

- 位置：Submit a final draft, not a first draft
- 原文："Even if you are for some reason in a hurry to publish your paper, a poorly prepared paper will have a greater chance of rejection, may incur demands for major revisions, and will in general slow down the processing of your paper to the extent that it would have been faster to wait until the paper was in a more final form before submission."
- 要点：即使着急发表，准备不充分的论文更容易被拒或被要求大修，反而更慢；不如等成熟了再投。
