# 英国特许考古学家协会 2023：Standard and universal guidance for archaeological excavation

- 资料：`cifa2023excavation`，[标准 PDF](https://www.archaeologists.net/sites/default/files/2024-11/CIfA-Standard-Archaeological-Excavation_2023.pdf)，[通用指南 PDF](https://www.archaeologists.net/sites/default/files/2024-11/CIfA-Universal-Guidance-Archaeological-Excavation_2023.pdf)
- 版本：英国特许考古学家协会（Chartered Institute for Archaeologists，CIfA）2023 年 12 月发布的《考古发掘标准》（1 页）和《考古发掘通用指南》。这是对其会员的职业要求，各国另有本地法规和指南。位置写文件名和条款号。

### E1

- 位置：标准，开头
- 原文："All archaeological excavations will result in a report, published accounts where appropriate, and a stable, ordered, accessible archive."
- 要点：每次考古发掘都要产出一份报告、适当时公开发表的成果，以及一套稳定、有序、可查阅的档案。

### E2

- 位置：标准，Definition of archaeological excavation
- 原文："Archaeological excavation involves a programme of intrusive fieldwork with defined research objectives which examines, records and interprets archaeological deposits, features and structures"
- 要点：考古发掘是一种侵入性的田野工作，有明确的研究目标，要检查、记录并解释遗存。

### E3

- 位置：通用指南，2.4
- 原文："The proposed project team should have access to suitable expertise and appropriate reference material to assess the significance of remains and any materials recovered"
- 要点：项目团队要能调动合适的专业知识和参考资料，评估遗存和出土材料的意义。

### E4

- 位置：通用指南，2.12 (i)
- 原文："methods of recording, including spatial data collection standards"
- 要点：项目设计书要写明记录方法，包括空间数据的采集标准。

### E5

- 位置：通用指南，2.12 (o)
- 原文："staffing (including specialists, both external and in-house)"
- 要点：项目设计书要写明人员安排，包括内部和外部的专家。

### E6

- 位置：通用指南，5.3
- 原文："the report should normally contain sufficient data and references to the project archive to permit interpretations to be challenged."
- 要点：报告要包含足够的数据和指向项目档案的出处，让别人能质疑其中的解释。

### E7

- 位置：通用指南，2.2
- 原文："The project design should set out the proposed scheme of investigation in enough detail that all relevant parties can understand and agree what will be done"
- 要点：项目设计书（project design）要把调查方案写得足够详细，让各方都能理解并同意要做什么。

### E8

- 位置：通用指南，2.12 (l)
- 原文："archiving strategy, including reference to data management plans, selection strategy and local repository requirements"
- 要点：项目设计书要包含归档策略：数据管理计划、哪些材料保留，以及当地收藏机构的要求。

### E9

- 位置：通用指南，2.2
- 原文："It should provide a benchmark against which the results of the work can be monitored and measured. Templates should therefore be used with care."
- 要点：项目设计书是之后检查、衡量工作成果的基准；所以套模板要小心。

### E10

- 位置：通用指南，2.12 (h)
- 原文："methods of investigation, including environmental sampling and scientific dating strategies, where appropriate"
- 要点：项目设计书要写明调查方法，包括环境采样和科学测年的策略（适用时）。

### E11

- 位置：通用指南，2.9
- 原文："Any variations to the project design should be circulated and understood within the project team and agreed in writing by all relevant parties."
- 要点：对项目设计书的任何改动，都要在团队内传达，并由各相关方书面同意。

### E12

- 位置：通用指南，1.4
- 原文："Due regard should also be given to the jurisdiction-specific guidance on archaeological excavation to ensure the work undertaken is in accordance with the legislation and policy of the country or jurisdiction of the place of work."
- 要点：还要遵守工作所在国家或地区的本地指南、法律和政策。

### E13

- 位置：通用指南，3.3
- 原文："consideration should be given to the processing of environmental samples at the fieldwork stage, where appropriate, to support the assessment of significance and preservation."
- 要点：适当时，在野外阶段就处理环境样品，帮助判断遗址的重要性和保存状况。

### E14

- 位置：通用指南，3.4
- 原文："Following (or where suitable, during) fieldwork, the findings should be assessed against the project design to determine the extent to which the research aims have been met or could be met and to identify any new research questions to be incorporated in an updated project design."
- 要点：野外工作之后（合适时在进行中），要对照项目设计书评估发现：研究目标达成了多少、还能达成多少，并找出新的研究问题，写进更新后的设计书。

### E15

- 位置：通用指南，4.5
- 原文："The report should include a statement of the quantity and perceived quality of the data in the site archive, a statement of the archaeological potential of the data to answer the project research aims, and recommendations for the analysis and data storage and curation requirements."
- 要点：发掘后评估报告要说明：现场档案里数据的数量和质量、这些数据回答研究目标的潜力，以及对后续分析和数据保存的建议。

### E16

- 位置：通用指南，4.3
- 原文："If material is identified which would merit further study, the project design should be updated, even if the research aims remain unchanged."
- 要点：评估时发现值得深入研究的材料，就要更新项目设计书，即使研究目标没变。

### E17

- 位置：通用指南，5.3
- 原文："the report should normally contain sufficient data and references to the project archive to permit interpretations to be challenged. Similarly, reports should normally integrate the results of specialist analysis with the site sequence, to ensure that important data are not overlooked"
- 要点：报告要有足够的数据和档案出处，让别人能质疑解释；还要把专家分析结果和遗址的地层序列整合起来，免得漏掉重要数据。

### E18

- 位置：通用指南，5.4
- 原文："Subject to any reasonable contractual requirements on confidentiality, copies of the report should be submitted to an appropriate national or local record in accordance with the stated timeframe outlined in the project design."
- 要点：在合理的保密约定之外，报告要按设计书写的时间，提交给国家或地方的记录机构。

### E19

- 位置：通用指南，5.5
- 原文："The digital report should be made available online to ensure that the results of the project are readily available to support public dissemination, future research and/or decisions about the site or asset."
- 要点：电子版报告要放到网上，方便公众、后来的研究和有关遗址的决策使用。

### E20

- 位置：通用指南，1.5
- 原文："Departures from the universally applicable or jurisdiction-specific guidance should be undertaken with caution. Clear justification is required, with the reasons formally documented"
- 要点：偏离通用或本地指南要谨慎，要有清楚的理由并正式记录。

### E21

- 位置：通用指南，3.2
- 原文："It should be clear that the nature and scale of post-investigation analyses, publications and the archive will be tailored to the significance and research potential of the assets."
- 要点：发掘后的分析、发表和档案的规模，要和遗存的重要性、研究潜力相称。
