# Zeller 2012：What makes good research?

- 资料：`zeller2012good`，[原文](https://andreas-zeller.info/2012/10/16/what-makes-good-research.html)
- 版本：作者博客，2012-10-16。文中转述 Günter Hotz 的"好研究三轴模型"。

### E1

- 位置：正文，Hotz model of good research
- 原文："It is actually fairly simple. There are three criteria, which are orthogonal. [...] First, was it hard? [...] is this something which required years of training and perspiration, or is this something anyone on the street could have come up with? Second, is it elegant? Is this something which solves a specific problem, or is this something that you can apply over and over again to new problems? Third, is it useful? Is this of value only to the academic community, or is this something people outside can use to create value or make money?"
- 要点：判断好研究有三条互相独立的标准：难不难（是否需要多年训练和汗水才能做到）、优不优雅（能否反复用于新问题）、有没有用（学术圈之外能否用它创造价值）。

### E2

- 位置：同上
- 原文："If you're good on one of the axes, you are doing good research. If you're good on two axes, we'll hire you."
- 要点：在任何一条轴上做得好，就是好研究。

### E3

- 位置：正文，lessons 第二、三点及结尾
- 原文："Difficulty, elegance, usefulness – pick any two."
- 要点：三条很难同时做到：有用往往要牺牲优雅，优雅意味着简单。

### E4

- 位置：正文结尾
- 原文："You have every right to choose the axis (or axes!) on which you plan to excel. Different people make different choices, but remember: The axes are orthogonal, and no axis dominates the others."
- 要点：可以自己选择在哪条轴上追求卓越；三条轴没有高低之分。
