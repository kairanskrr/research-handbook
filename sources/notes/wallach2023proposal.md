# Wallach 等人 2023：Proposal and extensive test of a calibration protocol for crop phenology models

- 资料：`wallach2023proposal`，[原文（出版社，开放获取）](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13593-023-00900-0)，DOI [10.1007/s13593-023-00900-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13593-023-00900-0)
- 版本：Agronomy for Sustainable Development 43:46, 2023，开放获取（CC BY 4.0），2026-10-08 经法国 CIRAD 机构库 Agritrop 的 PDF 读取。研究在国际农业模型比对与改进项目（AgMIP）框架内完成，19 个建模团队参加，对象是小麦物候模型（预测作物发育期的模型）。位置写小节或摘要。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："calibration of crop phenology models is difficult and there is no consensus on the best approach."
- 要点：作物物候模型的校准很难，最佳做法没有共识。

### E2

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："Calibration normally only concerns a fairly small subset of the parameters in a crop model, but is essentially always necessary because mechanistic models are only approximations, without universally valid parameter values"
- 要点：校准通常只涉及作物模型里一小部分参数，但几乎总是必需的，因为机理模型只是近似，没有普遍适用的参数值。

### E3

- 位置：§2.1 Data sets；§2.3
- 原文："For both data sets, the calibration and evaluation subsets had neither site nor year in common, so the evaluation is a rigorous test of how well a modeling team can simulate phenology for out-of-sample environments." / "At no time were the evaluation data shown to participants, neither in previous studies nor in the present study."
- 要点：校准数据和检验数据的地点、年份都不重合，所以检验能严格反映模型对新环境的预测能力；检验数据从不给参加者看。

### E4

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："A second major innovation of the protocol is the definition of documentation tables for each step of the calibration procedure, which can be used both for communication within a modeling team and to inform users of the calibrated model."
- 要点：这套校准方案为每一步规定了文档表，既用于团队内部沟通，也告诉模型使用者校准是怎么做的。

### E5

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："It has been found, for example, that there is a wide diversity of calibration approaches between modeling teams furnished with identical data, even between modeling teams using the same model structure"
- 要点：已有研究发现，拿到相同数据的建模团队，校准方法差别很大，即使用的是同一个模型结构。

### E6

- 位置：§3.3，Table 8 说明；§3.3 正文
- 原文："showing root mean squared error (RMSE) for the calibrated model and for two benchmark models" / "Almost all modeling teams did better than the two benchmark models for all stages, both for usual calibration and protocol calibration"
- 要点：评价时把校准后模型的均方根误差和两个简单基准模型比较；几乎所有团队在所有发育期都好于基准模型。

### E7

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："There are therefore two main tracks to improvement of crop phenology model predictions. The first is through improvement of model structure through improved understanding of the underlying processes, and the second is through improvement of model calibration"
- 要点：改进作物物候模型预测有两条主要路子：一是靠对过程的更好理解改进模型结构，二是改进模型校准。
