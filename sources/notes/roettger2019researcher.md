# Roettger 2019：Researcher degrees of freedom in phonetic research

- 资料：`roettger2019researcher`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.5334/labphon.147)
- 版本：Laboratory Phonology 10(1)，开放获取；出版社网站拦截脚本，原文文本取自 Internet Archive 快照

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："It is argued that phonetic research faces a large number of researcher degrees of freedom due to its scientific object"
- 要点：语音学研究有大量"研究者自由度"，即分析时可以做的各种选择。

### E2

- 位置：§5.2
- 原文："We should explore our data. Yes. Yet we should not pretend we are testing concrete hypotheses when doing so."
- 要点：可以探索数据，但不要把探索说成在检验事先的假设。

### E3

- 位置：§5.3
- 原文："A preregistration is a time-stamped document in which researchers specify exactly how they plan to collect their data and how they plan to conduct their confirmatory analyses."
- 要点：预注册是带时间戳的文件，事先写明怎样收集数据、怎样做验证性分析。

### E4

- 位置：§5.4
- 原文："if we have measured and analyzed ten phonetic parameters to establish whether the final syllable of a word is more prominent than the prefinal syllable, we should provide this information"
- 要点：如果测了十个语音参数，就要如实报告。

### E5

- 位置：§2
- 原文："there are good reasons to believe that they are, more often than not, unintentional."
- 要点：这类问题多数时候不是故意的。

### E6

- 位置：注释
- 原文："Note that there are only a few journals on quantitative linguistics that have adopted registered reports."
- 要点：（2019 年时）采用注册报告的定量语言学期刊还很少。
