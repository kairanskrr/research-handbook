# franzke 等人 2020：Internet Research: Ethical Guidelines 3.0

- 资料：`franzke2020internet`，[原文 PDF](https://aoir.org/reports/ethics3.pdf)
- 版本：网络研究者协会（Association of Internet Researchers，AoIR）第三版网络研究伦理指南（IRE 3.0），2019 年 10 月 6 日经会员一致通过，2020 年发布，83 页，知识共享 BY-NC-SA 4.0 许可。位置写章节号。

### E1

- 位置：0. Preview
- 原文："IRE 3.0 is written especially for researchers, students, IRB members or technical developers who face ethical concerns during their research or are generally interested in Internet Research Ethics."
- 要点：这份指南写给在研究中遇到伦理问题的研究者、学生、伦理审查委员会成员和技术开发者。

### E2

- 位置：§2.3.4 Judgment Calls: Guidelines, Not Recipes
- 原文："Taken together, all of this means that the best we can do is develop "guidelines, not recipes" (IRE 1.0, p. 3)."
- 要点：网络研究伦理只能给出"指南"，不能给出照做就行的"菜谱"，需要研究者自己判断。

### E3

- 位置：§2.3.5 Asking the Right Questions（回顾 IRE 2.0）
- 原文："people were sharing more and more information online in what amounted to public or quasi-public fora (the latter protected, e.g., by passwords, registered profile requirements, etc.). But they often nonetheless expected that these exchanges were somehow private either individually private or in some form of group privacy. Even though these expectations were not warranted by the technical realities of a given forum or SNS, especially deontological ethics calls for respecting these expectations,"
- 要点：人们在公开或半公开的网络空间发言，却常常以为这些交流是私密的；即使技术上并非如此，义务论伦理也要求尊重这种隐私期待。

### E4

- 位置：§3.1.2 Informed Consent
- 原文："Such consent is manifestly impracticable in the case of Big Data projects, however, resulting in a serious ethical dilemma."
- 要点：在大数据项目里，逐一取得知情同意明显做不到，这造成了严重的伦理两难。

### E5

- 位置：§3.1.2 Informed Consent
- 原文："Most commonly, researchers pseudonymize their data separating keys from the actual dataset and also make sure to justify both any questions using/processing sensitive data and/or how risk in this process has been dealt with (e.g. storage, aggregation of data, publication of aggregates)."
- 要点：最常见的做法是把数据假名化，把对照表和数据分开保存，并说明为什么要处理敏感数据、风险怎样处理（存储、聚合、只发表汇总结果等）。

### E6

- 位置：§3.1.2 Informed Consent
- 原文："Another way of trying to mitigate risk and resolve the dilemma is by reserving the acquisition of informed consent to the dissemination stage of a project, i.e., by asking for informed consent from specific subjects for the publication of a quote or other data that might make them and their personal information identifiable (e.g., through a string search or more sophisticated data-matching techniques)."
- 要点：另一种办法是到发表阶段再取得同意：要引用某人的原话或其他可能让人认出他的数据时（例如别人拿原话去搜索就能找到本人），向这个人征得同意。

### E7

- 位置：§3.2.7 Assumptions, Questions, Issues, Procedures for Data Acquisition, Analysis, Storage, Dissemination
- 原文："These questions emphasize the hard fact that the best current practices and techniques can only "de-identify" data, i.e., not perfectly anonymize data. Therefore, emphasis on data security is even more important"
- 要点：目前最好的技术也只能"去标识化"，做不到完美匿名，所以数据安全更加重要。

### E8

- 位置：§3.2.2 Legal Aspects，Terms and Conditions
- 原文："These terms and conditions suggest that researchers relying on Twitter-based datasets must continually check if any tweets have been deleted from the platform, and then remove them for their research dataset accordingly"
- 要点：按当时 Twitter 的服务条款，用 Twitter 数据的研究者要持续检查哪些推文已被删除，并从自己的数据集中删掉。

### E9

- 位置：§3.2.2 Legal Aspects，Terms and Conditions
- 原文："For example, Facebook and LinkedIn prohibit the automated scraping of data from their platform, limiting researchers' ability to collect needed data through such means"
- 要点：Facebook 和 LinkedIn 禁止自动抓取，限制了研究者收集数据的途径。

### E10

- 位置：脚注 11（§3.1.2 Informed Consent）
- 原文："In some cases, transforming the quotations (Markham, 2012) and images (Tiidenberg, 2018; Warfield, Hoholuk, Vincent & Camargo, 2019), or creating samples of discourse that are composites from various participants may mitigate risk."
- 要点：改写引文和图片，或把多位参与者的话合成一段示例，有时可以降低风险。

### E11

- 位置：§2.3.5 Asking the Right Questions（回顾 IRE 2.0）
- 原文："informed consent may need to be sought not only from individuals, but also "from communities and online system administrators?""
- 要点：知情同意可能不只要向个人征求，还要向社区和网站管理者征求。

### E12

- 位置：§3.2.7 Assumptions, Questions, Issues, Procedures for Data Acquisition, Analysis, Storage, Dissemination
- 原文："The need to be aware of the problematic representativeness of even large datasets of social media users as compared to overall populations etc."
- 要点：即使是很大的社交媒体用户数据集，和总体人群相比也有代表性问题。
