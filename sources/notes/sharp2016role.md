# Sharp, Dittrich & de Souza 2016：The Role of Ethnographic Studies in Empirical Software Engineering

- 资料：`sharp2016role`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSE.2016.2519887)；作者接受稿见 [ITU 机构库](https://researcher.itu.dk/ws/files/82419396/The_Role_of_Ethnographic_Studies_in_Empirical_Software_Engineering.pdf)（亦见 Open University 机构库 [ORO 45166](https://oro.open.ac.uk/45166/)）
- 版本：IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering 42(8)，2016。读的是作者接受稿（Accepted Manuscript），与正式版可能有出入；位置按小节标注，页码为稿件页码。
- 说明：面向软件工程研究者介绍民族志：它的主要特征、研究设计的五个维度，以及它在实证软件工程中能起的作用。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 1 页
- 原文："In the context of Computer Science ethnography has been adopted within the CSCW (Computer-Supported Cooperative Work) and HCI communities to conduct studies of the workplace and inform the design of computer applications"
- 要点：在计算机领域，民族志已被计算机支持的协同工作（CSCW）和人机交互（HCI）社区采用，用来研究工作场所、指导应用设计。

### E2

- 位置：§2.1，第 3 页脚注 1
- 原文："First, people tend to say what they think their interviewer wants to hear. Second, they are inclined to create a rationalised account which may (or may not) reflect what is wanted, and to have selective memory."
- 要点：只靠访谈不够：受访者倾向于说访谈者想听的话，倾向于给出合理化的说法，记忆也有选择性。

### E3

- 位置：§2.3，第 4 页
- 原文："There are four main features of ethnographic research: the members’ point of view, a focus on the ordinary detail of life, the analytical stance, and production of ‘thick descriptions’ for academic accountability."
- 要点：民族志的四个主要特征：从成员的视角看问题；关注日常生活的细节；分析的立场；产出"厚描述"，对学术负责。

### E4

- 位置：§2.3，The members' point of view，第 4 页
- 原文："The distinguishing feature of ethnography is its focus on the informants’ point of view, i.e., ethnography aims to understand what is, or is not, relevant, important, and painful for the informant."
- 要点：民族志最突出的特征是关注信息提供者自己的视角：对他们来说什么重要、什么不重要、什么让他们头疼。

### E5

- 位置：§2.3，The members' point of view，第 4 页
- 原文："Therefore, ethnographies do not need to last long anymore: in general, a couple of weeks is enough time to produce good results [84]. Assuming that studies require a long time to be conducted is a very common myth about ethnographic research."
- 要点：研究与自己文化相近的群体时，民族志不必很长，一般几周就能出好结果；"民族志一定要做很久"是常见误解。

### E6

- 位置：§2.3，The members' point of view，第 4 页
- 原文："For instance, in Example Agile the ethnographic study of agile practices was conducted over a period of three weeks, with one week of full-time observation followed by two one-day visits over the following two weeks."
- 要点：例子：对敏捷实践的民族志研究持续三周：一周全天观察，之后两周各去一天。

### E7

- 位置：§2.3，The members' point of view，第 4 页
- 原文："On the other hand, the ethnographer must be aware of his own assumptions and biases."
- 要点：研究自己熟悉的文化时，研究者必须意识到自己的假设和偏见。

### E8

- 位置：§2.3，The ordinary detail of life，第 4 页
- 原文："This flexibility is often confounded with lack of rigour, a second myth about ethnographic research. [...] “The ethnographer enters the field with an open mind not an empty head.”"
- 要点：民族志的数据收集计划需要灵活，但灵活不等于不严谨；研究者带着开放的心态进入现场，而不是脑袋空空。

### E9

- 位置：§2.3，The analytical stance，第 5 页
- 原文："Ethnographers are not journalists who solely report what they have observed in a particular situation."
- 要点：民族志研究者不是只报道所见所闻的记者。

### E10

- 位置：§2.3，The analytical stance，第 5 页
- 原文："In other words, there is a misconception that ethnography is purely descriptive; that it is only concerned with the description of what has been seen in the field. This is not correct."
- 要点：认为民族志只是描述现场所见，是一种误解；它还要做分析。

### E11

- 位置：§2.3，The analytical stance，第 5 页
- 原文："An ethnographic study in empirical software engineering will aim not simply to observe activity but to determine rationalities and explanations: what causes frustration, stops progress, or makes the team work well"
- 要点：软件工程中的民族志不只是观察活动，还要找出背后的道理和解释：什么让人沮丧、什么阻碍进展、什么让团队运转良好。

### E12

- 位置：§2.3，Thick descriptions for academic accountability，第 5 页
- 原文："In other words, because of its analytical nature, the results of an ethnographic study are suitable for analytical generalization, but not suitable for statistical generalization."
- 要点：民族志的结果适合做分析性推广，不适合做统计推广。

### E13

- 位置：§2.3，Thick descriptions for academic accountability，第 5 页
- 原文："[Even if] one swallow does not make a summer, ... one swallow does prove the existence of swallows. And careful dissection of even one swallow may provide a great deal of reliable information about swallow anatomy"
- 要点：引 Anzai & Simon：一只燕子不成夏天，但一只燕子足以证明燕子存在；仔细解剖一只燕子也能得到大量可靠的燕子解剖知识。

### E14

- 位置：§2.4，Participant or non-participant observation，第 6 页
- 原文："Observation is key to ethnographic studies and both participant and non-participant observation are legitimate forms of ethnography. Observation is almost always complemented with other forms of data collection such as interviews or document analysis."
- 要点：观察是民族志的核心，参与式和非参与式观察都可以；观察几乎总要辅以访谈、文档分析等其他数据收集方式。

### E15

- 位置：§2.4，Participant or non-participant observation，第 6 页
- 原文："Participant observation in this context is more often interpreted as taking on a meaningful role within the community and engaging with the community’s everyday business. For instance, when observing software engineering practices, companies are very wary of letting non-employees access their sourcecode repositories"
- 要点：在软件工程中，参与式观察通常指在群体中承担一个有意义的角色、参与日常事务；企业往往不愿让外人访问代码仓库。

### E16

- 位置：§2.4，Participant or non-participant observation，第 6 页
- 原文："In this case the researcher might take over documentation tasks [129] or support the project through administration [75]."
- 要点：例如研究者可以承担文档工作，或帮项目做行政支持。

### E17

- 位置：§2.4，The duration of the field study，第 6 页
- 原文："These examples illustrate that the researcher does not need to be located full-time at the study site, but that fewer days may be spent at the site, allowing time to start analyzing the data during the field study time."
- 要点：研究者不必全职驻场；去现场的天数少一些，可以在实地研究期间就开始分析数据。

### E18

- 位置：§2.4，Space and location，第 6 页
- 原文："In global and open source software projects, online environments play a crucial role, and the concept of a field site changes: alongside the physical workplaces, the virtual environment needs to be regarded as a fieldsite"
- 要点：在全球分布和开源项目中，线上环境也是研究现场。

### E19

- 位置：§2.4，The ethnographers' intent，第 7 页
- 原文："Practitioners being studied may expect help with improving their practices, or addressing their problems and be puzzled if the researchers do not help them to improve, as they are perceived as peers with access to relevant knowledge about methods and tools."
- 要点：被研究的从业者可能期待研究者帮他们改进，研究者不帮忙时他们会感到困惑。

### E20

- 位置：§2.4，The ethnographers' intent，第 7 页
- 原文："It is therefore important that the researcher both is open about her research interest and her intent and also takes into account the way her research might impact the observed software development practice."
- 要点：研究者要公开自己的研究兴趣和意图，并考虑研究可能对被观察的实践产生的影响。

### E21

- 位置：§4.2 When is ethnography appropriate，第 12 页
- 原文："Ethnography puts cooperative, social and human aspects of software engineering practice in the centre, and is therefore very well-suited to any research question focusing on these aspects."
- 要点：民族志把合作、社会和人的因素放在中心，非常适合关注这些方面的研究问题。

### E22

- 位置：§4.2，第 12 页
- 原文："For example, asking ‘How do software practitioners develop systems using XP?’ rather than ‘Is single programmer coding more productive than pair programming?’"
- 要点：例子：适合民族志的问题是"从业者怎样用极限编程开发系统"，而不是"单人编程是否比结对编程更高效"。

### E23

- 位置：§4.2，第 13 页
- 原文："Radical changes in tools, techniques, methods and processes, cannot be supported by ethnographic research. Ethnographic methods rely on existing practices."
- 要点：民族志依赖现有实践，无法支撑工具、方法和流程的彻底变革。

### E24

- 位置：§4.3 Why a software engineer should consider being the ethnographer，第 13 页
- 原文："From the ethnographer’s point of view, a software engineer may be tempted to make assumptions or to be judgmental [115]. From the practitioners’ point of view, they will expect someone knowledgeable in the area to offer guidance"
- 要点：由软件工程师来做民族志的缺点：研究者容易想当然或评判别人；从业者会期待懂行的人给出指导。

### E25

- 位置：§4.3，第 13 页
- 原文："Another is that access to the informants’ discussions and rationalities will be more meaningful, which in turn leads to more meaningful analysis."
- 要点：由软件工程师来做的优点之一：更能理解信息提供者的讨论和想法，分析也更有意义。
