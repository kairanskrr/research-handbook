# 美国历史学会 2023：Statement on Standards of Professional Conduct（职业行为准则）

- 资料：`aha2023standards`，[原文（Internet Archive 快照）](https://web.archive.org/web/20240604095630/https://www.historians.org/jobs-and-professional-development/statements-standards-and-guidelines-of-the-discipline/statement-on-standards-of-professional-conduct)
- 版本：美国历史学会（American Historical Association，AHA）准则，2005 年全面修订，最近一次修订为 2023 年 1 月。官网对脚本访问返回 403，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2024-06-04 的快照（旧网址 `historians.org/jobs-and-professional-development/statements-standards-and-guidelines-of-the-discipline/statement-on-standards-of-professional-conduct`）。位置写准则的节号和标题。

### E1

- 位置：§2 Shared Values of Historians
- 原文："By “documents,” historians typically mean all forms of evidence—not just written texts, but artifacts, images, video, statistics, oral recollections, the built and natural environment, and many other things—that have survived as records of former times."
- 要点：历史学家说的"文献"（documents）泛指一切留存下来的过去的记录：文字、实物、图像、影像、统计、口头回忆、建成环境和自然环境等，不只是文字。

### E2

- 位置：§2 Shared Values of Historians
- 原文："This distinction between primary and secondary sources is among the most fundamental that historians make. Drawing the boundary between them is a good deal more complicated than it might seem, since determining whether a document is primary or secondary largely depends on the questions one asks of it."
- 要点：区分一手资料和二手资料是历史学最基本的区分之一；但界线不简单，一份文献算一手还是二手，很大程度上取决于你拿它问什么问题。

### E3

- 位置：§2 Shared Values of Historians
- 原文："It enables other historians to retrace the steps in an argument to make sure those steps are justified by the sources."
- 要点：注释和参考文献等学术"装置"（scholarly apparatus）让别的历史学家能重走论证的每一步，核对每一步是否有资料支撑。

### E4

- 位置：§2 Shared Values of Historians
- 原文："The sloppier their apparatus, the harder it is for other historians to trust their work."
- 要点：注释做得越马虎，同行越难信任这项研究。

### E5

- 位置：§2 Shared Values of Historians
- 原文："Doing justice to those views means to some extent trying (never wholly successfully) to see their worlds through their eyes."
- 要点：要公正对待过去的人的看法，就要尽量（永远做不到完全）用他们的眼睛看他们的世界。

### E6

- 位置：§3 Scholarship
- 原文："They should report their findings as accurately as possible and not omit evidence that runs counter to their own interpretation."
- 要点：要尽可能准确地报告发现，不能略去与自己解释相反的证据。

### E7

- 位置：§3 Scholarship
- 原文："Historians should acknowledge the receipt of any financial support, sponsorship, or unique privileges (including special access to research material) related to their research, especially when such privileges could bias their research findings."
- 要点：受到的资助、赞助或特殊便利（包括特许查阅资料）都要说明，尤其是这些便利可能让结论产生偏向时。

### E8

- 位置：§3 Scholarship
- 原文："Certain kinds of research, certain forms of employment, and certain techniques (for instance, in conducting oral history interviews) sometimes entail promises about what a historian will and will not do with the resulting knowledge. Historians should honor all such promises."
- 要点：有些研究（例如口述历史访谈）会涉及对资料用途的承诺；历史学家必须遵守这些承诺。

### E9

- 位置：§3 Scholarship
- 原文："They should define any confidentiality requirements before their research begins, and give public notice of any conditions or rules that may affect the content of their work."
- 要点：保密要求要在研究开始前讲清楚；可能影响研究内容的条件或规则要公开说明。

### E10

- 位置：§3 Scholarship
- 原文："They should be careful to avoid any actions that might prejudice access for future historians."
- 要点：不要做任何可能妨碍后来的历史学家查阅资料的事。

### E11

- 位置：§4 Plagiarism
- 原文："Borrowing unexamined primary source references from a secondary work without citing that work is likewise inappropriate."
- 要点：从二手著作里抄来自己没有核对过的一手资料出处，却不注明那本二手著作，同样不当。

### E12

- 位置：§4 Plagiarism
- 原文："A basic rule of good note-taking requires every researcher to distinguish scrupulously between exact quotation and paraphrase."
- 要点：做笔记的基本规则：严格区分原文照抄和转述。

### E13

- 位置：§8 Reputation and Trust
- 原文："“Forthcoming”: a completed manuscript has been accepted by a press or journal. “Under contract to . . .”: a press and an author have signed a contract for a book in progress, but the final manuscript has not yet been submitted."
- 要点：AHA 建议的在研成果状态用语："即将出版"指完整书稿已被出版社或期刊接受；"已签约"指书还在写，已和出版社签了合同，但还没交终稿。

### E14

- 位置：§2 Shared Values of Historians
- 原文："Because the record of the past is so fragmentary, absolute historical knowledge is denied us."
- 要点：过去留下的记录是零碎的，所以不可能得到绝对的历史知识。

### E15

- 位置：§3 Scholarship
- 原文："Historians should document their findings and be prepared to make available their sources, evidence, and data, including any documentation they develop through interviews."
- 要点：要记录自己的发现，并准备好公开资料、证据和数据，包括访谈中形成的记录。

### E16

- 位置：§2 Shared Values of Historians
- 原文："We honor the historical record, but understand that its interpretation constantly evolves as historians analyze primary documents in light of the ever-expanding body of secondary literature that places those documents in a larger context."
- 要点：对历史记录的解释会不断变化，因为历史学家要借助不断增加的二手研究，把一手文献放进更大的背景中去分析。

### E17

- 位置：§2 Shared Values of Historians
- 原文："They do not fabricate evidence."
- 要点：历史学家不捏造证据。

### E18

- 位置：§3 Scholarship
- 原文："They should always acknowledge assistance received from colleagues, students, research assistants, and others, and give due credit to collaborators."
- 要点：要始终说明从同事、学生、研究助理等人那里得到的帮助，并给合作者应得的认可。

### E19

- 位置：§4 Plagiarism
- 原文："The plagiarist’s standard defense—that he or she was misled by hastily taken and imperfect notes—is plausible only in the context of a wider tolerance of shoddy work."
- 要点：抄袭者常辩解说是匆忙记下的笔记误导了自己；这种辩解只有在普遍容忍马虎工作的环境里才说得过去。

### E20

- 位置：§8 Reputation and Trust
- 原文："Historians should not list among the completed achievements on their resumes degrees or honors they have never earned, jobs they have never held, articles or books they have never written or published, or any comparable misrepresentations of their creative or professional work."
- 要点：简历上不能把没获得的学位荣誉、没担任过的职位、没写成或没发表的文章和书列为已完成的成果。

### E21

- 位置：§3 Scholarship
- 原文："Although they recognize the legitimacy of restricting access to some sources for national security, proprietary, and privacy reasons, they have a professional interest in opposing unnecessary restrictions whenever appropriate."
- 要点：历史学家承认出于国家安全、商业产权和隐私而限制查阅某些资料是正当的，但在适当时也要反对不必要的限制。

### E22

- 位置：§2 Professional Integrity
- 原文："Any changes to a primary source or published secondary work, whether digital or print, should be noted."
- 要点：对一手资料或已发表著作做的任何改动（数字的或印刷的），都要注明。

### E23

- 位置：§2 Professional Integrity
- 原文："The ease with which digitally preserved records are reproduced and accessed facilitates this practice but makes accurate citation even more vital."
- 要点：数字化记录容易复制和获取，这让准确引用更加重要。

### E24

- 位置：§2 Professional Integrity
- 原文："contributing in a fair-minded way to ongoing scholarly and public debates over what those sources tell us about the past and also what they fail to illuminate."
- 要点：职业的历史研究要公正地参与讨论：资料告诉了我们什么，也包括它们照不亮什么。

### E25

- 位置：§3 Scholarship
- 原文："Historians should not misrepresent their sources. They should report their findings as accurately as possible and not omit evidence that runs counter to their own interpretation."
- 要点：不得歪曲资料；尽可能准确地报告发现，不略去与自己解释相反的证据。

### E26

- 位置：§3 Scholarship
- 原文："Historians sometimes appropriately agree to restrictive conditions about the use of particular sources."
- 要点：历史学家有时会正当地同意某些资料的使用限制条件。
