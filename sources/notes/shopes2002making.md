# Shopes 2002：Making Sense of Oral History

- 资料：`shopes2002making`，[原文 PDF](https://historymatters.gmu.edu/mse/oral/oral.pdf)，[网页版](https://historymatters.gmu.edu/mse/oral/what.html)
- 版本：Linda Shopes（美国口述历史协会前会长）为乔治·梅森大学"History Matters"网站"Making Sense of Evidence"系列写的指南，PDF 创建于 2002 年。2026-10-06 读取。位置写小节名。

### E1

- 位置：Interpreting Oral History
- 原文："For all their considerable value, oral history interviews are not an unproblematic source. Although narrators speak for themselves, what they have to say does not."
- 要点：口述访谈很有价值，但不是没有问题的资料。受访者在替自己说话，可他们说的内容不会自己解释自己。

### E2

- 位置：Interpreting Oral History
- 原文："The first step in assessing an interview, then, is to consider the reliability of the narrator and the verifiability of the account."
- 要点：评估一份访谈，第一步是考虑讲述者是否可靠、讲述能否核实。

### E3

- 位置：Interpreting Oral History
- 原文："The narrator's relationship to the events under discussion, personal stake in presenting a particular version of events, physical and mental state at the time of the events under discussion and at the moment of the interview, as well as the overall attention and care the narrator brings to the interview and the internal consistency of the account all figure into the narrator's reliability as a source."
- 要点：判断讲述者是否可靠要看：他和事件的关系、讲某个版本对他有什么利害、事件发生时和访谈时的身心状态、对访谈的用心程度、讲述前后是否一致。

### E4

- 位置：Interpreting Oral History
- 原文："The veracity of what is said in an interview can be gauged by comparing it both with other interviews on the same subject and with related documentary evidence."
- 要点：可以把访谈和同一题目的其他访谈、相关文献对照，来判断说法是否属实。

### E5

- 位置：Interpreting Oral History
- 原文："If, however, it conflicts with other evidence or is incompatible with it, the historian needs to account for the disparities" / "Might intervening events--for example, ideological shifts between the time of the events under discussion and the time of the interview or subsequent popular cultural accounts of these events--have influenced later memories?"
- 要点：访谈与其他证据冲突时，历史学者要解释差异，例如：事后的思潮变化或流行文化中的讲法，是否影响了后来的记忆？

### E6

- 位置：Interpreting Oral History
- 原文："Although Kennedy was elected with just 49.7% of the vote in the fall of 1960, almost two-thirds of all Americans remembered voting for him when they were asked about it in the aftermath of his assassination."
- 要点：肯尼迪 1960 年只得了 49.7% 的选票，但在他遇刺后被问到时，近三分之二的美国人记得自己投了他。

### E7

- 位置：Interpreting Oral History
- 原文："An interview is inevitably an act of memory, and while individual memories can be more or less accurate, complete, or truthful, in fact interviews routinely include inaccurate and imprecise information, if not outright falsehoods. Narrators frequently get names and dates wrong, conflate disparate events into a single event, recount stories of questionable truthfulness."
- 要点：访谈就是回忆；访谈里常有不准确、不精确的信息，甚至不实之词。受访者常记错人名日期，把几件事混成一件。

### E8

- 位置：Interpreting Oral History
- 原文："Narrators manipulated the facts of Trastulli's death to render it less senseless and more comprehensible to them" / "errors, inventions, and myths lead us through and beyond facts to their meanings"
- 要点：Portelli 研究过意大利工人特拉斯图利之死的各种讲述：讲述者改动了事实，让这场死亡在他们看来不那么无谓、更好理解；"错误、虚构和神话"能带我们穿过事实，看到事实的意义。

### E9

- 位置：Interpreting Oral History
- 原文："an interview is a storied account of the past recounted in the present, an act of memory shaped as much by the moment of telling as by the history being told."
- 要点：访谈是在当下讲述的、关于过去的故事；回忆既受所讲的历史影响，也受讲述时刻的影响。
