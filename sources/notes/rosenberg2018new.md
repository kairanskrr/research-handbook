# Rosenberg & Watkins 2018：New Policy to Specify Availability of Data, Models, and Code

- 资料：`rosenberg2018new`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)WR.1943-5452.0000998)（本摘录读自 Internet Archive 2022-03-02 快照中的全文页面：http://web.archive.org/web/20220302124549/https://ascelibrary.org/doi/full/10.1061/(ASCE)WR.1943-5452.0000998）
- 版本：Journal of Water Resources Planning and Management 144(9): 01618001，编辑按语（Editor's Note）。

### E1

- 位置：正文第 1 段
- 原文："To improve the availability and reproducibility of work published in the Journal of Water Resources Planning and Management, the Editorial Board is initiating a new policy to require authors to specify the availability of data, computational models, code, and other electronic materials used in work submitted for publication."
- 要点：2018 年，ASCE《水资源规划与管理期刊》编委会开始要求作者说明数据、计算模型、代码和其他电子材料能否获得。
