# Carlini 2024：My research idea logfile, 2016-2019

- 资料：`carlini2024logfile`，[原文](https://nicholas.carlini.com/writing/2024/my-research-logfile.html)
- 版本：作者主页博客，2024-01-21，2026-10-05 读取。

### E1

- 位置：开头段
- 原文："I maintain a single file called ideas.txt, where I just append a new entry every time I think of something that might be an interesting topic for a paper."
- 要点：作者只维护一个想法文件，每想到可能成为论文的点子就追加一条。

### E2

- 位置：A bit more detail
- 原文："And when I'm in the middle of working on one project, if I think of something that's really exciting, I can just write it down and get back to the task at hand."
- 要点：做项目途中冒出新想法，先记下来再回到手头工作，避免被新想法带跑。

### E3

- 位置：A bit more detail
- 原文："This filtering of ideas is useful because it means I've thought the idea was good at least twice: once when I wrote it down, and once when I read it."
- 要点：隔一段时间再读想法记录，能更客观地筛选：一个想法要在记下时和重读时都觉得好才值得做。
