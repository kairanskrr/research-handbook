# Percie du Sert 等人 2020：The ARRIVE guidelines 2.0: Updated guidelines for reporting animal research

- 资料：`perciedusert2020arrive`，[原文](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3000410)
- 版本：PLoS Biology 18(7): e3000410, 2020，开放获取（CC0），2026-10-06 读取。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："The ARRIVE guidelines (Animal Research: Reporting of In Vivo Experiments) were originally developed in 2010 to improve the reporting of animal research. They consist of a checklist of information to include in publications describing in vivo experiments to enable others to scrutinise the work adequately, evaluate its methodological rigour, and reproduce the methods and results."
- 要点：ARRIVE（动物研究：活体实验报告指南）2010 年首次发布，是一份清单，列出描述活体动物实验的论文应写的信息，让别人能审查、评估方法是否严谨，并重复方法和结果。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："the “ARRIVE Essential 10,” which constitutes the minimum requirement, and the “Recommended Set,” which describes the research context."
- 要点：2.0 版把条目分成两组："核心 10 项"是最低要求；"推荐项"补充研究背景。

### E3

- 位置：Introducing ARRIVE 2.0
- 原文："It includes details on the study design, the sample size, measures to reduce subjective bias, outcome measures, statistical methods, the animals, experimental procedures, and results."
- 要点：核心 10 项包括：研究设计、样本量、减少主观偏倚的措施、结局指标、统计方法、动物信息、实验步骤和结果。

### E4

- 位置：Why good reporting is important
- 原文："Factors that contribute to poor reproducibility include flawed study design and analysis, variability and inadequate validation of reagents and other biological materials, insufficient reporting of methodology and results, and barriers to accessing data."
- 要点：导致难以复现的因素包括：研究设计和分析有缺陷、试剂和其他生物材料不稳定且验证不足、方法和结果报告不充分、数据难以获取。

### E5

- 位置：Why good reporting is important
- 原文："This includes details on randomisation (reported in only 30%–40% of publications), blinding (reported in only approximately 20% of publications), sample size justification (reported in less than 10% of publications)"
- 要点：作者引用的研究显示，抽样的动物研究论文中，只有 30%～40% 报告了随机分组，约 20% 报告了盲法，不到 10% 说明了样本量的依据。

### E6

- 位置：Why good reporting is important
- 原文："Approval for experiments involving animals is generally based on a harm–benefit analysis, weighing the harms to the animals involved against the benefits of the research to society. If the research is not reported in enough detail, even when conducted rigorously, the benefits may not be realised, and the harm–benefit analysis and public trust in the research are undermined."
- 要点：动物实验的审批一般基于"伤害—收益分析"；研究即使做得严谨，报告不够详细，收益也可能落空，这个分析和公众信任都会受损。

### E7

- 位置：Introducing ARRIVE 2.0（后半）
- 原文："While the primary focus of the guidelines has been on the reporting of animal studies, ARRIVE also has other applications earlier in the research process, including in the planning and design of in vivo experiments."
- 要点：ARRIVE 主要用于报告，但也可以在更早阶段用来规划和设计活体实验。

### E8

- 位置：Why good reporting is important
- 原文："They apply to any area of research using live animal species and are especially pertinent to describe comparative research in the laboratory or other formal test setting."
- 要点：ARRIVE 适用于所有使用活体动物的研究领域，尤其适合实验室里的比较研究。

### E9

- 位置：Introducing ARRIVE 2.0
- 原文："This includes the ethical statement, declaration of interest, protocol registration, and data access, as well as more detailed information on the methodology such as animal housing, husbandry, care, and monitoring."
- 要点：推荐项包括伦理声明、利益声明、研究方案注册、数据获取方式，以及饲养、照料、监测等更细的方法信息。

### E10

- 位置：Introducing ARRIVE 2.0
- 原文："While reporting animal research in adherence to all 21 items of ARRIVE 2.0 represents best practice, the classification of the items into 2 groups is intended to facilitate the improved reporting of animal research by allowing an initial focus on the most critical issues." / "The first step should be to ensure compliance with the ARRIVE Essential 10 as a minimum requirement."
- 要点：21 项全部报告是最佳做法；分成两组是为了先抓最关键的；第一步至少要做到核心 10 项。

### E11

- 位置：Why good reporting is important
- 原文："This is illustrated by a randomised controlled trial at PLOS ONE, designed to test the effect of requesting a completed ARRIVE checklist in the manuscript submission process. This single editorial intervention, which did not include further verification from journal staff, failed to improve the disclosure of information in published papers"
- 要点：PLOS ONE 的一项随机对照试验发现，投稿时只要求交一份填好的 ARRIVE 清单、编辑部不进一步核查，并没有让已发表论文报告得更完整。
