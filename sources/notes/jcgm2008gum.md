# JCGM 100:2008：Evaluation of measurement data — Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement（GUM）

- 资料：`jcgm2008gum`，[原文](https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/2071204/JCGM_100_2008_E.pdf)
- 版本：计量指南联合委员会（JCGM）2008 年第一版（即 GUM 1995 加少量更正），国际计量局（BIPM）网站免费提供。位置写条款号。

### E1

- 位置：0.1
- 原文："When reporting the result of a measurement of a physical quantity, it is obligatory that some quantitative indication of the quality of the result be given so that those who use it can assess its reliability. Without such an indication, measurement results cannot be compared, either among themselves or with reference values given in a specification or standard."
- 要点：报告物理量的测量结果，必须给出结果质量的定量说明，让使用者能判断它有多可靠；没有这种说明，测量结果之间、测量结果和规范或标准中的参考值之间都无法比较。

### E2

- 位置：3.3.2
- 原文："In practice, there are many possible sources of uncertainty in a measurement, including: [...] inadequate knowledge of the effects of environmental conditions on the measurement or imperfect measurement of environmental conditions; [...] finite instrument resolution or discrimination threshold; [...] approximations and assumptions incorporated in the measurement method and procedure; j) variations in repeated observations of the measurand under apparently identical conditions."
- 要点：不确定度来源很多，例如对环境条件的影响了解不够或环境条件测得不准、仪器分辨力有限、测量方法中的近似和假设、看似相同条件下重复观测的差异。

### E3

- 位置：2.3.2、2.3.3
- 原文："Type A evaluation (of uncertainty) method of evaluation of uncertainty by the statistical analysis of series of observations [...] Type B evaluation (of uncertainty) method of evaluation of uncertainty by means other than the statistical analysis of series of observations"
- 要点：A 类评定：对一系列观测值做统计分析来评定不确定度；B 类评定：用统计分析以外的办法评定。

### E4

- 位置：3.3.3
- 原文："These categories apply to uncertainty and are not substitutes for the words “random” and “systematic”."
- 要点：A 类、B 类是按评定方法分的，不能拿来代替"随机"和"系统"这两个词。

### E5

- 位置：3.4.7
- 原文："Blunders in recording or analysing data can introduce a significant unknown error in the result of a measurement. [...] Measures of uncertainty are not intended to account for such mistakes."
- 要点：记录或分析数据时的过失会带来大的未知误差；不确定度不负责涵盖这类错误。

### E6

- 位置：3.4.8
- 原文："Although this Guide provides a framework for assessing uncertainty, it cannot substitute for critical thinking, intellectual honesty and professional skill. The evaluation of uncertainty is neither a routine task nor a purely mathematical one; it depends on detailed knowledge of the nature of the measurand and of the measurement."
- 要点：指南只提供框架，代替不了批判性思考、诚实和专业技能；评定不确定度不是例行公事，也不只是数学，要靠对被测量和测量过程的深入了解。

### E7

- 位置：7.1.4
- 原文："when reporting the result of a measurement and its uncertainty, it is preferable to err on the side of providing too much information rather than too little."
- 要点：报告测量结果和不确定度时，宁可信息给多，不要给少。

### E8

- 位置：7.1.4
- 原文："list all uncertainty components and document fully how they were evaluated;"
- 要点：列出所有不确定度分量，并完整记录每个分量是怎样评定的。

### E9

- 位置：7.2.3
- 原文："give the value of k used to obtain U [or, for the convenience of the user of the result, give both k and uc( y)];"
- 要点：用扩展不确定度 U 报告时，要给出求 U 用的包含因子 k（或同时给出 k 和合成标准不确定度）。

### E10

- 位置：3.2.4
- 原文："It is assumed that the result of a measurement has been corrected for all recognized significant systematic effects and that every effort has been made to identify such effects."
- 要点：GUM 的前提是：测量结果已经对所有已识别的重要系统效应做了修正，并且已尽力找出这些效应。
