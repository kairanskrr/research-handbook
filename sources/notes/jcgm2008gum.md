# JCGM 100:2008：Evaluation of measurement data — Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement（GUM）

- 资料：`jcgm2008gum`，[原文](https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/2071204/JCGM_100_2008_E.pdf)
- 版本：计量指南联合委员会（JCGM）2008 年第一版（即 GUM 1995 加少量更正），国际计量局（BIPM）网站免费提供。位置写条款号。

### E1

- 位置：0.1
- 原文："When reporting the result of a measurement of a physical quantity, it is obligatory that some quantitative indication of the quality of the result be given so that those who use it can assess its reliability. Without such an indication, measurement results cannot be compared, either among themselves or with reference values given in a specification or standard."
- 要点：报告物理量的测量结果，必须给出结果质量的定量说明，让使用者能判断它有多可靠；没有这种说明，测量结果之间、测量结果和规范或标准中的参考值之间都无法比较。

### E2

- 位置：3.3.2
- 原文："In practice, there are many possible sources of uncertainty in a measurement, including: [...] inadequate knowledge of the effects of environmental conditions on the measurement or imperfect measurement of environmental conditions; [...] finite instrument resolution or discrimination threshold; [...] approximations and assumptions incorporated in the measurement method and procedure; j) variations in repeated observations of the measurand under apparently identical conditions."
- 要点：不确定度来源很多，例如对环境条件的影响了解不够或环境条件测得不准、仪器分辨力有限、测量方法中的近似和假设、看似相同条件下重复观测的差异。

### E3

- 位置：2.3.2、2.3.3
- 原文："Type A evaluation (of uncertainty) method of evaluation of uncertainty by the statistical analysis of series of observations [...] Type B evaluation (of uncertainty) method of evaluation of uncertainty by means other than the statistical analysis of series of observations"
- 要点：A 类评定：对一系列观测值做统计分析来评定不确定度；B 类评定：用统计分析以外的办法评定。

### E4

- 位置：3.3.3
- 原文："These categories apply to uncertainty and are not substitutes for the words “random” and “systematic”."
- 要点：A 类、B 类是按评定方法分的，不能拿来代替"随机"和"系统"这两个词。

### E5

- 位置：3.4.7
- 原文："Blunders in recording or analysing data can introduce a significant unknown error in the result of a measurement. [...] Measures of uncertainty are not intended to account for such mistakes."
- 要点：记录或分析数据时的过失会带来大的未知误差；不确定度不负责涵盖这类错误。

### E6

- 位置：3.4.8
- 原文："Although this Guide provides a framework for assessing uncertainty, it cannot substitute for critical thinking, intellectual honesty and professional skill. The evaluation of uncertainty is neither a routine task nor a purely mathematical one; it depends on detailed knowledge of the nature of the measurand and of the measurement."
- 要点：指南只提供框架，代替不了批判性思考、诚实和专业技能；评定不确定度不是例行公事，也不只是数学，要靠对被测量和测量过程的深入了解。

### E7

- 位置：7.1.4
- 原文："when reporting the result of a measurement and its uncertainty, it is preferable to err on the side of providing too much information rather than too little."
- 要点：报告测量结果和不确定度时，宁可信息给多，不要给少。

### E8

- 位置：7.1.4
- 原文："list all uncertainty components and document fully how they were evaluated;"
- 要点：列出所有不确定度分量，并完整记录每个分量是怎样评定的。

### E9

- 位置：7.2.3
- 原文："give the value of k used to obtain U [or, for the convenience of the user of the result, give both k and uc( y)];"
- 要点：用扩展不确定度 U 报告时，要给出求 U 用的包含因子 k（或同时给出 k 和合成标准不确定度）。

### E10

- 位置：3.2.4
- 原文："It is assumed that the result of a measurement has been corrected for all recognized significant systematic effects and that every effort has been made to identify such effects."
- 要点：GUM 的前提是：测量结果已经对所有已识别的重要系统效应做了修正，并且已尽力找出这些效应。

### E11

- 位置：3.1.1
- 原文："A measurement therefore begins with an appropriate specification of the measurand, the method of measurement (B.2.7), and the measurement procedure (B.2.8)."
- 要点：测量从明确三件事开始：被测量是什么、用什么测量方法、按什么测量程序。

### E12

- 位置：3.1.3（含例子）
- 原文："The measurand should be defined with sufficient completeness with respect to the required accuracy so that for all practical purposes associated with the measurement its value is unique." / "If the length of a nominally one-metre long steel bar is to be determined to micrometre accuracy, its specification should include the temperature and pressure at which the length is defined."
- 要点：被测量要按所需的准确程度定义得足够完整，使它的值在实际用途上是唯一的。例：要把一根约一米长的钢棒长度测到微米级，定义里就要写明在什么温度和压力下的长度。

### E13

- 位置：D.1.1
- 原文："incomplete definition of the measurand introduces into the uncertainty of the result of a measurement a component of uncertainty"
- 要点：被测量定义得不完整，本身就会给测量结果带来一个不确定度分量。

### E14

- 位置：3.4.2
- 原文："all relevant quantities should be varied to the fullest practicable extent so that the evaluation of uncertainty can be based as much as possible on observed data."
- 要点：所有相关的量都应尽可能实际改变一下，让不确定度评定尽量建立在观测数据上。

### E15

- 位置：3.4.2
- 原文："A well-designed experiment can greatly facilitate reliable evaluations of uncertainty and is an important part of the art of measurement."
- 要点：设计良好的实验能让不确定度评定可靠得多，这是测量技艺的重要部分。

### E16

- 位置：3.4.2
- 原文："The mathematical model should always be revised when the observed data, including the result of independent determinations of the same measurand, demonstrate that the model is incomplete."
- 要点：观测数据（包括对同一被测量的独立测定结果）表明测量模型不完整时，就要修改模型。

### E17

- 位置：3.4.3
- 原文："the experimentally observed variability of its output values, as measured by their observed standard deviation, is often compared with the predicted standard deviation obtained by combining the various uncertainty components"
- 要点：判断测量系统是否正常，常把实际观测到的标准差，和把各不确定度分量合成后预测的标准差相比较。

### E18

- 位置：3.2.3
- 原文："Systematic error, like random error, cannot be eliminated but it too can often be reduced." / "a correction (B.2.23) or correction factor (B.2.24) can be applied to compensate for the effect."
- 要点：系统误差和随机误差一样无法完全消除，但常常可以减小；对识别出的系统效应可以施加修正值或修正因子来补偿。

### E19

- 位置：3.2.4 注 1
- 原文："Often, measuring instruments and systems are adjusted or calibrated using measurement standards and reference materials to eliminate systematic effects; however, the uncertainties associated with these standards and materials must still be taken into account."
- 要点：仪器常用测量标准和标准物质来调整或校准，以消除系统效应；但这些标准和物质本身的不确定度仍要计入。

### E20

- 位置：3.2.3 注
- 原文："The terms “error” and “uncertainty” should be used properly and care taken to distinguish between them."
- 要点："误差"和"不确定度"两个词要用对，注意区分。

### E21

- 位置：4.2.7
- 原文："If the random variations in the observations of an input quantity are correlated, for example, in time, the mean and experimental standard deviation of the mean as given in 4.2.1 and 4.2.3 may be inappropriate estimators"
- 要点：如果重复观测之间有相关（例如随时间相关、有漂移），用普通的平均值和平均值的实验标准差来估计可能不合适。

### E22

- 位置：4.3.7
- 原文："one can only assume that it is equally probable for Xi to lie anywhere within it (a uniform or rectangular distribution of possible values" / "If the difference between the bounds, a+ − a−, is denoted by 2a, then Equation (6) becomes u2(xi) = a2/3"
- 要点：只知道一个量落在某个区间里、别的什么都不知道时，只能假设它在区间内任何位置的可能性相同，即均匀（矩形）分布。区间半宽为 a 时，方差 u² = a²/3，即标准不确定度 a/√3。

### E23

- 位置：5.1.2
- 原文："Each u(xi) is a standard uncertainty evaluated as described in 4.2 (Type A evaluation) or as in 4.3 (Type B evaluation)."
- 要点：合成时，每个输入量的标准不确定度可以来自 A 类评定，也可以来自 B 类评定，一起代入传播公式。

### E24

- 位置：5.2.1
- 原文："If some of the Xi are significantly correlated, the correlations must be taken into account."
- 要点：如果输入量之间有显著相关，合成不确定度时必须考虑相关性。

### E25

- 位置：3.3.7
- 原文："an expanded uncertainty U is obtained by multiplying the combined standard uncertainty uc by a coverage factor k."
- 要点：扩展不确定度 U 等于合成标准不确定度乘以包含因子 k。

### E26

- 位置：7.2.6
- 原文："It usually suffices to quote uc(y) and U [as well as the standard uncertainties u(xi) of the input estimates xi] to at most two significant digits"
- 要点：合成标准不确定度和扩展不确定度（以及各输入量的标准不确定度）一般最多给两位有效数字。

### E27

- 位置：3.4.8
- 原文："Although this Guide provides a framework for assessing uncertainty, it cannot substitute for critical thinking, intellectual honesty and professional skill."
- 要点：指南只提供框架，代替不了批判性思考、诚实和专业技能。

### E28

- 位置：0.1
- 原文："Without such an indication, measurement results cannot be compared, either among themselves or with reference values given in a specification or standard."
- 要点：没有对结果质量的定量说明，测量结果之间、测量结果和标准中的参考值之间都无法比较。
