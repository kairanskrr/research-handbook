# ADSA、ASAS、PSA 2020：Guide for the Care and Use of Agricultural Animals in Research and Teaching（第 4 版）

- 资料：`fass2020agguide`，[原文 PDF](https://www.asas.org/docs/default-source/default-document-library/agguide_4th.pdf)
- 版本：第 4 版，2020 年，美国乳品科学学会（ADSA）、美国动物科学学会（ASAS）、家禽科学学会（PSA）联合发布，CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 许可，免费下载。俗称"Ag Guide"（农业动物指南）。早期版本由 FASS（Federation of Animal Science Societies，动物科学学会联合会）发布，故沿用常见叫法。位置写章节和小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Preface
- 原文："This is the fourth edition of the Guide for the Care and Use of Agricultural Animals in Research and Teaching, commonly known as the Ag Guide." / "The Ag Guide is published jointly by the American Dairy Science Association (ADSA), the American Society of Animal Science (ASAS), and the Poultry Science Association (PSA)."
- 要点：这是《农业动物研究与教学照料和使用指南》第 4 版，俗称 Ag Guide；由美国乳品科学学会、美国动物科学学会和家禽科学学会联合发布。

### E2

- 位置：Preface
- 原文："Must indicates that the animal care and use must be as stated; should indicates that the animal care and use ought to be as indicated unless otherwise justified; recommend indicates an appropriate way of doing things but leaves room for other approaches that achieve the same result."
- 要点：指南里 must 表示必须照做；should 表示除非有正当理由，否则应照做；recommend 表示一种合适做法，但允许用其他能达到同样效果的办法。

### E3

- 位置：Preface
- 原文："It is essential that IACUCs and others who oversee animal care practices exercise professional judgment to encourage appropriate animal welfare outcomes to be achieved, rather than merely seeking to apply specific housing and management requirements."
- 要点：监管动物照料的委员会要运用专业判断，追求实际的福利结果，而不只是机械套用具体的饲养和管理条文。

### E4

- 位置：Preface
- 原文："Although many of the species chapters have standards for such things as floor space, feeder space, and drinker allocations because these metrics have been verified by scientific study"
- 要点：很多物种章节给出了地面面积、料槽和饮水器分配等标准，因为这些指标经过了科学研究验证。

### E5

- 位置：Chapter 1: Institutional Policies（开头）
- 原文："The use of animals for research and teaching, including production research, is governed by numerous federal and local laws, regulations, and standards. In the United States, the Animal Welfare Act (AWA, 1990) and Title 9 Part 1A of the Code of Federal Regulations (USDA, 1985) apply, whereas in Europe, the European Convention for the Protection of Vertebrate Animals used for Experimental and Other Scientific Purposes (ETS123; Council of Europe, 1986) and subsequent amendments apply (Directive 2010/63/EU; European Union, 2010)."
- 要点：研究和教学用动物（包括生产研究）受多种法律法规管辖：美国适用《动物福利法》和联邦法规第 9 编，欧洲适用欧洲委员会的实验动物保护公约及欧盟 2010/63/EU 指令等。

### E6

- 位置：Chapter 1，Monitoring the Care and Use of Agricultural Animals
- 原文："The chief executive officer, institutional official, or responsible administrative official of the institution should appoint a committee (known as the institutional animal care and use committee or IACUC) to monitor the care and use of agricultural animals in agricultural research and teaching activities within the institution."
- 要点：机构应任命一个"机构动物照料与使用委员会"（IACUC），监督本机构农业研究和教学中农业动物的照料与使用。

### E7

- 位置：Chapter 1，Monitoring the Care and Use of Agricultural Animals
- 原文："a scientist who has experience in agricultural research or teaching involving agricultural animals;" / "a veterinarian who has training and experience in agricultural animal medicine and who is licensed or eligible to be licensed to practice veterinary medicine;"
- 要点：委员会成员应包括有农业动物研究或教学经验的科学家，以及受过农业动物医学训练、有执业资格的兽医（另有非科学背景成员和机构外成员等）。

### E8

- 位置：Chapter 1，Protocol Review
- 原文："Animal use protocol(s) (AUP) describing these activities must be reviewed before the initiation of the research or teaching activity to determine whether the proposed care and use of animals is appropriate and humane."
- 要点：动物使用方案必须在研究或教学开始前审查，判断拟定的照料和使用是否恰当、人道。

### E9

- 位置：Chapter 1，Protocol Review
- 原文："Availability or appropriateness of alternative procedures or models (e.g., less invasive procedures, cell or tissue culture, or computer simulations) for the proposed research or teaching activity." / "It should be noted, however, that hands-on training involving animals is an important component of agricultural research and teaching (Vemulapalli et al., 2017); additionally, there is no substitute of another animal or simulation for production research."
- 要点：审查时要考虑替代方法（如细胞培养、计算机模拟）；但指南指出，动手操作动物是农业研究和教学的重要部分，生产研究没有别的动物或模拟可以替代。

### E10

- 位置：Chapter 1，Protocol Review
- 原文："justification for the species and strain of animal used; justification for the number of animals used; description of procedures that may cause discomfort, distress, or pain"
- 要点：方案要说明为什么用这个物种和品系、为什么用这么多动物，并描述可能造成不适、痛苦或疼痛的操作。

### E11

- 位置：Chapter 2: Agricultural Animal Health Care，Euthanasia
- 原文："Protocols for euthanasia should follow the specifications for "acceptable" or "acceptable with conditions" techniques as described in the American Veterinary Medical Association (AVMA) Guidelines for the Euthanasia of Animals (AVMA, 2020) and include the method for confirming that death has occurred." / "The current edition of the AVMA guidelines should be considered the primary standard for euthanasia."
- 要点：安乐死方案应采用美国兽医协会（AVMA）《动物安乐死指南》中"可接受"或"有条件可接受"的方法，并写明怎样确认死亡；AVMA 指南的现行版是安乐死的主要标准。

### E12

- 位置：Chapter 3: Husbandry, Housing, and Biosecurity（高致病性病原体研究）
- 原文："Research or other activities with high-consequence livestock and poultry pathogens causing high morbidity and mortality (e.g., tuberculosis, foot and mouth disease) or the vectors carrying these pathogens (e.g., mosquitoes, ticks) must be conducted in biocontainment."
- 要点：涉及高发病率、高死亡率的畜禽病原体（如结核、口蹄疫）或其传播媒介的研究，必须在生物防护设施里做。

### E13

- 位置：Chapter 1: Institutional Policies（开头）
- 原文："Scientists, veterinarians, and others must assume responsibility for animal welfare and uphold the rigor and integrity of agricultural animal research and instruction."
- 要点：科学家、兽医等人必须对动物福利负责，并维护农业动物研究和教学的严谨与诚信。

### E14

- 位置：Chapter 1，Special Considerations，Genetically Engineered, Gene Edited, and Cloned Animals
- 原文："Guidelines for research involving genetically engineered (GE) and gene edited (GEd) animals do not differ materially from those that apply to conventional animals used in research except under special conditions. The published scientific literature has not established the need for unique guidelines."
- 要点：除特殊情况外，基因工程和基因编辑动物的研究指南和常规研究动物没有实质区别；已发表的文献没有证明需要专门的指南。

### E15

- 位置：Preface
- 原文："The Ag Guide comprises subject-oriented chapters covering subjects that pertain to all the agricultural animal species (chapters 1 to 5) and species chapters, which fall under the purview of the respective association(s) dedicated to the species (chapters 6 to 13)."
- 要点：第 1–5 章是适用于所有农业动物的专题章节，第 6–13 章是按物种写的章节。
