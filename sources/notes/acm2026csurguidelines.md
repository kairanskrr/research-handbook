# ACM Computing Surveys：Author Guidelines

- 资料：`acm2026csurguidelines`，[原页面](https://dl.acm.org/journal/csur/author-guidelines)
- 版本：ACM 网站拦截脚本访问，以下内容核对自 [Internet Archive 2026-07-11 快照](http://web.archive.org/web/20260711105913/https://dl.acm.org/journal/csur/author-guidelines)。
- 背景：ACM Computing Surveys 是计算机领域最主要的综述期刊。

### E1

- 位置：Types of Papers，Long Survey Paper
- 原文："A paper that summarizes and organizes recent research results in a novel way that integrates and adds understanding to work in the field. A survey article assumes a general knowledge of the area; it emphasizes the classification of the existing literature, developing a perspective on the area, and evaluating trends."
- 要点：综述论文要用新的方式归纳和组织近期研究，帮助读者更深地理解这个领域。它假定读者对领域已有基本了解，重点是：对已有文献分类、形成对领域的视角、评估发展趋势。

### E2

- 位置：Types of Papers，Tutorial Paper
- 原文："A paper that organises and introduces work in the field. A tutorial paper assumes its audience is inexpert; it emphasizes the basic concepts of the field and provides concrete example that embody these concepts."
- 要点：教程不同于综述：教程面向外行，重点讲基本概念，并给出具体例子。

### E3

- 位置：Types of Papers，Short Survey paper
- 原文："Short survey papers are perspective pieces/essays that are well-focused and written by a leader in the field that usually presents a personal point of view critiquing widespread notions pertaining to a field."
- 要点：短综述是领域内资深学者写的观点文章，通常针对领域内流行的看法提出个人的批评性观点。

### E4

- 位置：Paper Length
- 原文："Long survey papers must not exceed 35 pages, including references, when formatted using the Surveys style."
- 要点：长综述不超过 35 页（含参考文献）。
