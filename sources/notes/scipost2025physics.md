# SciPost Physics：About（Acceptance Criteria）

- 资料：`scipost2025physics`，[原文](https://scipost.org/SciPostPhys/about)
- 版本：SciPost 基金会（荷兰）出版的开放获取期刊 SciPost Physics 的"About"网页。原站有机器人验证，取自 Internet Archive 2025-12-04 的存档 https://web.archive.org/web/20251204175909/https://scipost.org/SciPostPhys/about 。只代表这本期刊当时的要求。

### E1

- 位置：Acceptance Criteria，Expectations
- 原文："To be considered for publication in SciPost Physics, a submission has to meet at least one of the expectations and all of the general acceptance criteria listed below" / "Expectations (at least one required) - the paper must: Provide a novel and synergetic link between different research areas. Open a new pathway in an existing or a new research direction, with clear potential for multi-pronged follow-up work Detail a groundbreaking theoretical/experimental/computational discovery Present a breakthrough on a previously-identified and long-standing research stumbling block"
- 要点：投稿要满足至少一条"期望"和全部一般标准。四条期望：在不同研究领域之间建立新的联系；在已有或新的方向上开辟新路径、明显能引出后续工作；报告开创性的理论、实验或计算发现；在公认的长期难题上取得突破。

### E2

- 位置：Acceptance Criteria，General acceptance criteria
- 原文："General acceptance criteria (all required) - the paper must: Be written in a clear and intelligible way, free of unnecessary jargon, ambiguities and misrepresentations Provide sufficient details (inside the bulk sections or in appendices) so that arguments and derivations can be reproduced by qualified experts" / "Contain a clear conclusion summarizing the results (with objective statements on their reach and limitations) and offering perspectives for future work."
- 要点：一般标准（全部必须满足）包括：写得清楚，没有不必要的行话、歧义和不实陈述；给出足够细节（正文或附录），让合格的专家能重现论证和推导；结论要总结结果，客观说明适用范围和局限，并展望后续工作。

### E3

- 位置：Submission and Editorial Process
- 原文："Minimal number of reports : at least 2 substantial reports must have been received; all points raised must have been addressed either in resubmissions or in author replies before a recommendation for publication can be formulated."
- 要点：至少收到两份实质性的审稿报告，并且报告提出的所有问题都在修改稿或作者回复中处理过，才能提出发表建议。
