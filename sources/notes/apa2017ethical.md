# American Psychological Association 2017：Ethical Principles of Psychologists and Code of Conduct

- 资料：`apa2017ethical`，[原文 PDF](https://www.apa.org/ethics/code/ethics-code-2017.pdf)
- 版本：2002 年通过，含 2010 年和 2016 年修订，2017 年 1 月 1 日生效。位置写条款编号。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction and Applicability
- 原文："Membership in the APA commits members and student affiliates to comply with the standards of the APA Ethics Code and to the rules and procedures used to enforce them."
- 要点：美国心理学会（APA）会员和学生会员都要遵守这部准则。

### E2

- 位置：Standard 8.01 Institutional Approval
- 原文："When institutional approval is required, psychologists provide accurate information about their research proposals and obtain approval prior to conducting the research. They conduct the research in accordance with the approved research protocol."
- 要点：需要机构审批时，研究开始前要如实提交方案、获得批准，并按批准的方案执行。

### E3

- 位置：Standard 8.02(a) Informed Consent to Research
- 原文："(2) their right to decline to participate and to withdraw from the research once participation has begun"
- 要点：知情同意要告诉参与者的内容包括：可以拒绝参加，开始后也可以退出。条款共列了八项（目的、时长、风险、保密限度、报酬、联系人等）。

### E4

- 位置：Standard 8.04(b)
- 原文："When research participation is a course requirement or an opportunity for extra credit, the prospective participant is given the choice of equitable alternative activities."
- 要点：参加研究是课程要求或可以换课程加分时，必须给学生提供同等的替代活动。

### E5

- 位置：Standard 8.07(a) Deception in Research
- 原文："Psychologists do not conduct a study involving deception unless they have determined that the use of deceptive techniques is justified by the study's significant prospective scientific, educational, or applied value and that effective nondeceptive alternative procedures are not feasible."
- 要点：只有研究价值足够大、且没有可行的不欺骗替代方案时，才可以在研究中使用欺骗。

### E6

- 位置：Standard 8.07(c)
- 原文："Psychologists explain any deception that is an integral feature of the design and conduct of an experiment to participants as early as is feasible, preferably at the conclusion of their participation, but no later than at the conclusion of the data collection, and permit participants to withdraw their data."
- 要点：用了欺骗，要尽早向参与者说明（最好在其参与结束时，最迟在数据收集结束时），并允许他们撤回自己的数据。

### E7

- 位置：Standard 8.08(a) Debriefing
- 原文："Psychologists provide a prompt opportunity for participants to obtain appropriate information about the nature, results, and conclusions of the research, and they take reasonable steps to correct any misconceptions that participants may have"
- 要点：研究结束后要及时向参与者说明研究的性质、结果和结论，并纠正他们可能产生的误解（即"事后说明"）。

### E8

- 位置：Standard 8.12(b) Publication Credit
- 原文："Principal authorship and other publication credits accurately reflect the relative scientific or professional contributions of the individuals involved, regardless of their relative status. Mere possession of an institutional position, such as department chair, does not justify authorship credit."
- 要点：署名顺序要反映实际贡献大小，与职位高低无关。仅仅担任系主任之类的职位，不构成署名理由。

### E9

- 位置：Standard 8.12(c)
- 原文："Except under exceptional circumstances, a student is listed as principal author on any multiple-authored article that is substantially based on the student's doctoral dissertation. Faculty advisors discuss publication credit with students as early as feasible"
- 要点：主要基于学生博士论文的多作者论文，除特殊情况外，学生应为第一作者；导师要尽早和学生讨论署名。

### E10

- 位置：Standard 8.14(a) Sharing Research Data for Verification
- 原文："After research results are published, psychologists do not withhold the data on which their conclusions are based from other competent professionals who seek to verify the substantive claims through reanalysis"
- 要点：论文发表后，有资质的同行为了重新分析、核实结论而索要数据时，不得拒绝（前提是能保护参与者隐私）。

### E11

- 位置：Standard 9.02(b) Use of Assessments
- 原文："Psychologists use assessment instruments whose validity and reliability have been established for use with members of the population tested."
- 要点：使用的测评工具，要在被测人群中已经确立了效度和信度。

### E12

- 位置：Standard 8.10(b) Reporting Research Results
- 原文："If psychologists discover significant errors in their published data, they take reasonable steps to correct such errors in a correction, retraction, erratum, or other appropriate publication means."
- 要点：发现已发表数据有重大错误，要通过更正、撤稿等方式纠正。
