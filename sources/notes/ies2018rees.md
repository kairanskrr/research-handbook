# 美国教育科学研究所 2018：Celebrating the Launch of the Registry of Efficacy and Effectiveness Studies (REES)

- 资料：`ies2018rees`，[原文](https://ies.ed.gov/learn/blog/celebrating-launch-registry-efficacy-and-effectiveness-studies-rees)
- 版本：IES 官网博客，2018 年 10 月 31 日。介绍教育效果研究会（Society for Research on Educational Effectiveness，SREE）开发的研究登记平台 REES。位置写博客中的问答小标题。

### E1

- 位置：正文第一段
- 原文："REES is a registry of causal impact studies in education developed with a grant from the National Center for Education Research (NCER). REES will increase transparency, improve the replicability of studies, and provide easy access to information about completed and ongoing studies."
- 要点：REES 是教育领域因果效应研究的登记平台，目的是提高透明度和可重复性，方便查到已完成和进行中的研究。

### E2

- 位置：正文第一段
- 原文："In the FY 2019 Requests for Applications (RFAs) for the Education Research and Special Education Research Grants Programs, IES recommended that applicants describe a plan for pre-registering their studies in both the project narrative (as part of the research) as well as the data management plan."
- 要点：IES 在 2019 财年的教育研究资助申请指南中，建议申请人在项目陈述和数据管理计划里写明预注册计划。

### E3

- 位置：What kinds of studies can be registered?
- 原文："It accommodates a range of study designs including randomized controlled trials, quasi-experimental designs, regression discontinuity designs, and single case designs."
- 要点：可以登记随机对照试验、准实验、断点回归和单一被试设计等研究。

### E4

- 位置：What information should be included in a study entry?
- 原文："A REES entry includes basic study information and a pre-analysis plan."
- 要点：登记内容包括研究的基本信息和一份预分析计划。

### E5

- 位置：What if a study entry needs to be changed?
- 原文："All updates to an entry will be time-stamped. Original entries and updated entries will be publically available."
- 要点：登记内容的每次修改都带时间戳，原始版本和修改版本都公开。
