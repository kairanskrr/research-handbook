# Huang 2023：How to decide what to work on?

- 资料：`huang2023decide`，[原推文](https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1628521223838654464)（通过 [Thread Reader 镜像](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1628521223838654464.html) 读取）
- 版本：推文串，2023-02-22，共 7 条。

### E1

- 位置：第 2 条，*Find your position*
- 原文："Understand your unfair advantages over others, what the community cares about, and how you can best contribute. Avoid crowding the ball (aka chasing the hottest problem in the field)."
- 要点：弄清自己相对别人的独特优势、领域关心什么、自己怎样贡献最大；不要扎堆追最热的问题。

### E2

- 位置：第 3 条，*Identify intrinsic motivation*
- 原文："Work on problems and real applications that you genuinely care about."
- 要点：做自己真正在乎的问题和实际应用。

### E3

- 位置：第 4 条，*Anticipate disruptive technologies*
- 原文："Instead of focusing on what everyone is working on NOW, anticipate the major problems LATER when disruptive technologies (e.g., new sensors, algorithms, models, data) emerge."
- 要点：与其盯着大家现在在做的，不如预判新技术出现后会冒出哪些大问题。

### E4

- 位置：第 5 条，*Imagine success*
- 原文："At the begging of your project, be an amateur and dream big! Don't be intimidated by all the technical challenges just yet."
- 要点：项目开始时像外行一样敢想，先不要被技术困难吓住。（原文 "begging" 应为 "beginning"，照录。）

### E5

- 位置：第 6 条，*Validate the idea fast*
- 原文："Not all the ideas will pan out. Design small experiments to validate your hypothesis and assumptions. If you fail, fail FAST!"
- 要点：不是每个想法都能成；设计小实验尽快验证假设，要失败就快点失败。
