# AEC 等 2020：The Vienna Declaration on Artistic Research

- 资料：`aec2020vienna`，[原文 PDF](https://aec-music.eu/media/2021/05/viennadeclarationonarfinalversion24june20_20201216163421.pdf)（发布页：https://aec-music.eu/publication/vienna-declaration-on-artistic-research/）
- 版本：2020 年 6 月 24 日定稿，两页。由欧洲音乐院校协会（AEC）等十个欧洲艺术高等教育和艺术研究组织联合署名的政策文件，对象是政策制定者和资助机构。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Artistic Research (AR) is practice-based, practice-led research in the arts which has developed rapidly in the last twenty years globally"
- 要点：艺术研究（artistic research）是艺术领域中基于实践、由实践引导的研究，过去二十年在全球快速发展。

### E2

- 位置：Key features of Artistic Research
- 原文："Excellent AR is research through means of high level artistic practice and reflection; it is an epistemic inquiry, directed towards increasing knowledge, insight, understanding and skills."
- 要点：优秀的艺术研究通过高水平的艺术实践和反思进行，是一种以增加知识、洞见、理解和技能为目标的认识探究。

### E3

- 位置：Infrastructure & Access to Funding
- 原文："AR incorporates many aspects and features that are not, or not solely, text based, such as artefacts, movements and sounds."
- 要点：艺术研究包含许多不是（或不只是）文字的东西，如作品、动作、声音。

### E4

- 位置：Evaluation & Recognition
- 原文："AR is validated through peer review covering the range of disciplinary competences addressed by the work."
- 要点：艺术研究通过同行评议来认定，评议者要覆盖作品涉及的各项学科能力。

### E5

- 位置：Claim for Action
- 原文："To support and work towards the establishment of AR as an independent category within the Frascati Manual"
- 要点：签署方呼吁把艺术研究列为 Frascati 手册中的独立类别。

### E6

- 位置：Infrastructure & Access to Funding
- 原文："AR in general does not have equal access to research funding as other fields of research or is not at all eligible to apply for research grants or scholarships."
- 要点：在一些国家，艺术研究没有和其他领域同等的资助渠道，甚至无权申请。

### E7

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："The Vienna Declaration is intended as a policy document addressing political decision makers, funding bodies,"
- 要点：这是一份写给政策制定者和资助机构的政策文件。

### E8

- 位置：Key features of Artistic Research
- 原文："AR is undertaken in all art practice disciplines - including architecture, design, film, photography, fine art, media and digital arts, music and the performing arts"
- 要点：艺术研究存在于所有艺术实践门类：建筑、设计、电影、摄影、美术、媒体与数字艺术、音乐和表演艺术。

### E9

- 位置：文末署名
- 原文："AEC, CILECT / GEECT, Culture Action Europe, Cumulus, EAAE, ELIA, EPARM, EQ-Arts, MusiQuE, SAR"
- 要点：宣言由十个组织联合署名（原文只列缩写）；据宣言导言，署名方包括艺术高等教育院校的学科组织、艺术研究组织、两个艺术领域的国际质量保障机构和国际政策组织。
