# 美国国家癌症研究所：Dietary Assessment Primer（饮食评估入门）

- 资料：`nci2025dietprimer`，[美国国家癌症研究所网站](https://epi.grants.cancer.gov/dietary-assessment-primer/)
- 版本：读了 "Key Concepts about Measurement Error"、"Types of Measurement Error"、"Effects of Measurement Error"、"Learn More about Misreporting" 四页，各页标注最后更新于 2025 年 12 月 29 日。

### E1

- 位置：Key Concepts about Measurement Error
- 原文："Because dietary intake can rarely be directly observed, we typically rely on self-report instruments, such as 24-hour dietary recalls (24HR), food records, food frequency questionnaires (FFQ), screeners"
- 要点：饮食很少能直接观察，通常要靠自我报告工具：24 小时膳食回顾、膳食记录、食物频率问卷、简易筛查问卷。

### E2

- 位置：Key Concepts about Measurement Error
- 原文："In dietary assessment, we are often interested in assessing usual dietary intake (i.e., long-term average) rather than intake on a given day or other short-term time period (i.e., acute intake)."
- 要点：饮食评估关心的常常是"平常摄入量"（长期平均），而不是某一天或短期的摄入。

### E3

- 位置：Effects of Measurement Error, Regression Models
- 原文："When a single dietary exposure is measured with within-person random error only (i.e., without systematic error), the estimate of the" [...] "association between the exposure and the health or disease outcome is always attenuated (i.e., biased toward the null)."
- 要点：只有一个饮食暴露、且只有个体内随机误差时，暴露与健康结局的关联估计总会被削弱（偏向"无关联"）。

### E4

- 位置：Effects of Measurement Error, Regression Models
- 原文："When a single dietary exposure contains both systematic and within-person random error, the estimate of the association with the outcome may be biased in either direction from the null, i.e., it may be attenuated or inflated."
- 要点：既有系统误差又有随机误差时，关联估计可能偏向任一方向：被削弱，也可能被夸大。

### E5

- 位置：Effects of Measurement Error, Regression Models
- 原文："the estimated association between the main dietary exposure and the outcome may be attenuated or inflated because of possible" [...] "residual confounding with other dietary covariates also measured with error."
- 要点：模型里同时放了其他也测不准的饮食变量时，主要饮食暴露的关联估计可能被削弱或夸大，因为存在残余混杂。

### E6

- 位置：Learn More about Misreporting
- 原文："Misreporting in self-report dietary assessment is considered to be unavoidable and can include both under- and overreporting."
- 要点：自我报告的饮食评估中，误报被认为不可避免，既有少报也有多报。

### E7

- 位置：Learn More about Misreporting
- 原文："Alternatively, a sensitivity analysis can determine whether findings differ based on inclusion or exclusion of energy misreporters. Researchers should carefully review data and exercise caution in discarding data."
- 要点：可以做敏感性分析，看纳入或排除能量误报者时结果是否不同；研究者应仔细检查数据，删数据要谨慎。

### E8

- 位置：Learn More about Misreporting
- 原文："Energy underreporting has been shown to be associated with various personal characteristics, but most consistently with body mass index."
- 要点：能量少报与多种个人特征有关，最一致的是体重指数。

### E9

- 位置：Effects of Measurement Error, Table 1
- 原文："Averaging repeat observations, or statistical modeling if number of repeats is limited"
- 要点：对付个体内随机误差的办法：对重复测量取平均；重复次数有限时用统计建模。
