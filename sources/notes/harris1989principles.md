### E1

- 位置：Chapter 6, Deposits as units of stratification
- 原文："Archaeological stratification, on this account, is an irreversible process. Once a unit of stratification - either a layer or interface - is formed, it is thereafter subject only to alteration and decay: it cannot be made again."
- 要点：考古地层的形成不可逆；一个地层单位（堆积层或界面）一旦形成，只会被改变或消失，不能再造。

### E2

- 位置：Chapter 5, The laws of archaeological stratigraphy, Law of Superposition
- 原文："In a series of layers and interfacial features, as originally created, the upper units of stratification are younger and the lower are older, for each must have been deposited on, or created by the removal of, a pre-existing mass of archaeological stratification."
- 要点：叠压定律：在保持原状的地层中，上面的单位较晚，下面的较早。

### E3

- 位置：Chapter 12, An outline for stratigraphic recording on excavations
- 原文："In the presence of obvious stratification, the stratigraphic method of excavation must be used."
- 要点：有明显地层时，必须按地层挖（而不是按人为的固定深度）。

### E4

- 位置：Chapter 3, Techniques of archaeological excavation
- 原文："By imposing the arbitrary strategy of excavation on sites with clear stratification, archaeologists destroy the primary data they seek"
- 要点：在地层清楚的遗址上按人为深度挖，等于毁掉自己要找的原始数据。

### E5

- 位置：Chapter 12
- 原文："Starting from the latest units and working downwards to the earlier units of stratification, all the units must be numbered." / "It is sufficient to have only one series of numbers in the records."
- 要点：从最晚的单位往下挖到较早的单位，每个单位都要编号；记录里只用一套连续编号就够了。

### E6

- 位置：Chapter 12
- 原文："These are most easily recorded on pre-printed sheets (e.g. Fig. 59). Three relationships will be sought: which units lie above, which lie below, and which units can be stratigraphically correlated."
- 要点：地层关系最好记在预先印好的表格上；要找三种关系：哪些在上面、哪些在下面、哪些可以对应为同一单位。

### E7

- 位置：Chapter 12
- 原文："Before the excavation of the actual unit has commenced, a plan should be made of its surface." / "On complex sites with many overlapping deposits, the single-layer plan should be used and every unit should be planned."
- 要点：动手挖一个单位之前，先画它表面的平面图；在堆积复杂、相互重叠的遗址上，要用单层平面图，每个单位都画。

### E8

- 位置：Chapter 9, Archaeological plans
- 原文："If the site has little stratification, the composite plan is the first, and probably the last, choice. On complex sites, the single-layer plan is the basic requirement, from which composite plans can later be made."
- 要点：地层简单的遗址用综合平面图就行；复杂遗址则以单层平面图为基础，之后再合成综合图。

### E9

- 位置：Chapter 5
- 原文："Using this method during an excavation (Fig. 10), a sequence can be built up on paper as the work progresses."
- 要点：发掘过程中就可以一边挖一边在纸上搭建地层序列图。

### E10

- 位置：Chapter 11, Stratigraphic sequences and post-excavation analyses
- 原文："Residual remains. These objects were made at a much earlier time than the formation of the layer in which they were found." / "Infiltrated remains. These objects were made at a later time than the formation of the deposit in which they were found"
- 要点：残留物比所在地层早得多；渗入物比所在地层晚，是后来混进去的。

### E11

- 位置：Chapter 11
- 原文："Whether they are indigenous, infiltrated or residual does not affect the recording of artefacts on archaeological excavations. In fact, they must all be recorded in the same manner, if their character is to be distinguished later."
- 要点：不管出土物是原生的、渗入的还是残留的，野外都要用同样的方式记录，之后才能区分。

### E12

- 位置：Chapter 11
- 原文："it is the least old object (or objects) which must be nearest to the date of the layer itself; it gives in other words a terminus post quem, which means that the date of the layer must be after the date of the objects' manufacture" / "This axiom is based on the assumption that layers can be sealed from any later intrusions"
- 要点：地层中最晚的器物最接近地层年代，给出一条"不早于"的界限（terminus post quem）：地层一定晚于这件器物的制造时间；前提是地层没有被后来的东西侵入。

### E13

- 位置：Chapter 6
- 原文："The chronological date of a unit of stratification can never change its position in the stratigraphic sequence of a site"
- 要点：一个地层单位的绝对年代，永远改变不了它在地层序列中的位置。
