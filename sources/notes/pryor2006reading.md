# Pryor：Guidelines on Reading Philosophy

- 资料：`pryor2006reading`，[原文](https://www.jimpryor.net/teaching/guidelines/reading.html)
- 版本：Jim Pryor 个人主页上的读文献指南，页面标注最后更新于 2006 年 8 月 10 日；以知识共享许可发布。写给本科哲学课学生。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Skim the Article to Find its Conclusion and Get a Sense of its Structure
- 原文："A good way to begin when you're trying to read a difficult article is to first skim the article to identify what the author's main conclusion is."
- 要点：读难懂的文章，先略读，找出作者的主要结论。

### E2

- 位置：Skim the Article to Find its Conclusion and Get a Sense of its Structure
- 原文："Try to identify these auxiliary arguments, and the claims they're intended to support; and try to avoid mistaking one of these auxiliary arguments for the author's main argument."
- 要点：分辨出辅助论证（为某个前提辩护的论证）以及它们支持的主张，不要把辅助论证误当成主论证。

### E3

- 位置：Skim the Article to Find its Conclusion and Get a Sense of its Structure
- 原文："The author's main conclusion is that Y's objection to the skeptic does not work. (Notice: the main conclusion isn't that skepticism is true.)"
- 要点：例子：一篇文章的主要结论是"Y 对怀疑论的反驳不成立"，而不是"怀疑论是对的"；要读准作者到底主张什么。

### E4

- 位置：Go Back and Read the Article Carefully
- 原文："Take special notice of any unargued assumptions you think the author is relying on."
- 要点：特别留意作者依赖的、没有论证过的假设。

### E5

- 位置：Go Back and Read the Article Carefully
- 原文："In your notes, you might make a quick outline of the article's major argumentative "pieces." Draw arrows to diagram how you think those pieces fit together."
- 要点：笔记里给文章的主要论证"零件"列个提纲，画箭头表示它们怎样组合。

### E6

- 位置：Go Back and Read the Article Carefully
- 原文："You should expect to read a philosophy article more than once. I've been doing philosophy for more than ten years and I still have to read articles many times before I fully understand them."
- 要点：一篇哲学文章要读不止一遍；Pryor 做了十多年哲学，仍要读很多遍才完全读懂。

### E7

- 位置：Evaluate the Author's Arguments
- 原文："Does he appeal to some premise which you think is false? (Why do you think it is false?) Is there some assumption which the author does not make explicit, but which you think is false? Does his argument equivocate or beg the question?"
- 要点：评价论证时问：有没有你认为错误的前提？有没有没明说但错误的假设？论证是否偷换概念或循环论证？

### E8

- 位置：Evaluate the Author's Arguments
- 原文："You may never reach any definitive conclusion. But each time you try to make sense of the debate, you'll find you understand the issues a little bit better. That's the way we make progress in philosophy."
- 要点：可能永远得不到定论，但每次梳理争论都会理解得更深一点；Pryor 说哲学就是这样进步的。

### E9

- 位置：Evaluate the Author's Arguments
- 原文："(This is why you often hear philosophers saying, "Even if we assume such-and-such for argument's sake, I still think X's argument fails, because...")"
- 要点：可以为了论证暂时接受某个前提，集中攻击更重要的前提；哲学家常说"即使为论证起见假定……，X 的论证仍然不成立"。

### E10

- 位置：Skim the Article to Find its Conclusion and Get a Sense of its Structure
- 原文："Philosophers often provide auxiliary arguments, arguments for important premises they appeal to in support of their main conclusion."
- 要点：哲学家常给出辅助论证，即为主结论所依赖的重要前提辩护的论证。

### E11

- 位置：Evaluate the Author's Arguments
- 原文："You'll start to get a sense of how the different views relate to each other and what their pros and cons are."
- 要点：下功夫之后，你会逐渐看出不同观点之间的关系和各自的优缺点。

### E12

- 位置：Go Back and Read the Article Carefully
- 原文："Consider various interpretations of what he says. Are there any important ambiguities that his argument fails to take account of?"
- 要点：考虑作者话语的多种解读；他的论证有没有忽视的重要歧义？

### E13

- 位置：Evaluate the Author's Arguments
- 原文："In such cases, you may want to provisionally accept one of the argument's premises, and move on to focus on other premises, which you think are more important or which are easier to criticize."
- 要点：可以暂时接受某个前提，集中火力批评更重要或更容易批评的其他前提。
