# Thompson、Ellison、Wood 2002：Harmonized guidelines for single-laboratory validation of methods of analysis（IUPAC Technical Report）

- 资料：`thompson2002harmonized`，[原文 PDF](https://publications.iupac.org/pac/2002/pdf/7405x0835.pdf)
- 版本：Pure and Applied Chemistry 74(5): 835–855，2002。国际纯粹与应用化学联合会（International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry，IUPAC）的技术报告，由 IUPAC 工作组起草，ISO、AOAC International 参与研讨。IUPAC 官网 PDF 对脚本返回验证页，原文取自 Internet Archive 保存的同一 PDF；报告声明在注明出处的条件下允许转载。位置写章节。面向分析实验室的方法验证，不是期刊审稿标准。

### E1

- 位置：3. Method validation, uncertainty, and quality assurance
- 原文："Method validation makes use of a set of tests that both test any assumptions on which the analytical method is based and establish and document the performance characteristics of a method, thereby demonstrating whether the method is fit for a particular analytical purpose."
- 要点：方法验证用一组检验，既检验分析方法所依赖的假设，又确定并记录方法的性能指标，从而说明方法是否适合某个具体的分析目的。

### E2

- 位置：3. Method validation, uncertainty, and quality assurance
- 原文："Typical performance characteristics of analytical methods are: applicability, selectivity, calibration, trueness, precision, recovery, operating range, limit of quantification, limit of detection, sensitivity, and ruggedness. To these can be added measurement uncertainty and fitness-for-purpose."
- 要点：典型的性能指标有适用范围、选择性、校准、正确度、精密度、回收率、工作范围、定量限、检出限、灵敏度和耐用性，还可加上测量不确定度和"是否适合用途"。

### E3

- 位置：Appendix A12. Fitness for purpose
- 原文："Fitness for purpose is the extent to which the performance of a method matches the criteria, agreed between the analyst and the end-user of the data, that describe the end-user's needs."
- 要点："适合用途"指方法性能在多大程度上满足分析者和数据使用者事先商定的、描述使用者需要的标准。

### E4

- 位置：1.1 Background
- 原文："“Full” validation for an analytical method is usually taken to comprise an examination of the characteristics of the method in an interlaboratory method performance study (also known as a collaborative study or collaborative trial)."
- 要点："完整"验证通常指在多个实验室参加的方法性能研究（协同研究）中考察方法的各项指标。

### E5

- 位置：1.1 Background
- 原文："However, it is not always practical or necessary to provide full validation of analytical methods. In such circumstances a “single-laboratory method validation” may be appropriate."
- 要点：完整验证并不总是可行或必要，这时可以做单个实验室的方法验证。

### E6

- 位置：Appendix A11. Ruggedness
- 原文："The ruggedness of a method is tested by deliberately introducing small changes to the procedure and examining the effect on the results."
- 要点：检验耐用性的办法，是故意给操作步骤加一些小变化，看结果受多大影响。
