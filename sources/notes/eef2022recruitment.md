# 英国教育捐赠基金会 2022：Guidance on Recruitment and Retention

- 资料：`eef2022recruitment`，[原文 PDF](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/public/files/Recruitment_and_retention_guidance_FINAL..pdf)
- 版本：2022 年 10 月更新。英国教育捐赠基金会（Education Endowment Foundation，EEF，资助英格兰学校随机对照试验的慈善机构）写给其资助项目的实施团队和评估团队的招募与保留指南。只适用于 EEF 资助的试验，涉及的法律（GDPR 等）是英国的。位置写章节标题。

### E1

- 位置：2. Creating a recruitment strategy
- 原文："Recruiting to trials is time intensive and is often one of the biggest risks for a trial. If insufficient schools are recruited onto the study, a low sample size would reduce its sensitivity to detect an effect, and the trial may not be able to go ahead."
- 要点：给试验招募学校很费时间，常常是试验最大的风险之一；招不够学校，样本小、测不出效应，试验可能做不成。

### E2

- 位置：What are the timelines for recruitment?
- 原文："Delivery teams have told us that it takes longer to recruit schools to an evaluation than to their normal programme."
- 要点：实施团队反映，为评估研究招募学校，比为平时的项目招募更花时间。

### E3

- 位置：What are the timelines for recruitment?
- 原文："Be aware of busy times of years for schools, for example it is particularly difficult to recruit schools in the weeks preceding and including the SATs and GCSEs. Also be mindful of when schools are able to make decisions, for example for a project that requires a lot of staff time or big changes to standard practice, schools may be less able to make such a commitment late in the academic year"
- 要点：注意学校一年中的忙季，例如全国统考（SATs、GCSEs）前和考试期间特别难招；需要大量教师时间或大改常规做法的项目，学年末学校更难做出承诺。

### E4

- 位置：What are the timelines for recruitment?
- 原文："For most projects that start delivery in schools in September, EEF would usually expect recruitment to begin in the prior January."
- 要点：大多数 9 月开始在学校实施的项目，EEF 通常期望在前一年 1 月就开始招募。

### E5

- 位置：How many schools will be targeted?
- 原文："It is not unusual for projects to need to contact dozens of schools for every school they eventually recruit."
- 要点：每最终招到一所学校，要联系几十所学校，这并不少见。

### E6

- 位置：Why is this offer worthwhile?
- 原文："It is imperative schools understand they are signing up to the evaluation and may be allocated to the intervention or control group, and are committing to take part in the evaluation regardless."
- 要点：必须让学校明白：他们报名的是评估，可能被分到干预组或对照组，无论分到哪组都承诺参加评估。

### E7

- 位置：3. Ethical approval
- 原文："The ethics review process usually takes 3-6 weeks but varies between organisations."
- 要点：伦理审查通常需要 3–6 周，各机构不同。

### E8

- 位置：c) Parent information sheet and withdrawal form
- 原文："Opt-out consent is not a valid legal basis under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)"
- 要点：在欧盟《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR）下，"默认同意、可以退出"不是处理个人数据的有效法律依据。

### E9

- 位置：c) Parent information sheet and withdrawal form
- 原文："Thus, parents or legal guardians – and/ or, where appropriate, pupils – should always be given the opportunity to withdraw their child's (or, if applicable, their own) data from processing. Typically, schools send these out to parents at the start of the autumn term or at the end of the summer term, depending on the timeline of the project."
- 要点：总要给家长（适当时也给学生）撤回数据的机会；学校通常在秋季学期开始或夏季学期末把说明发给家长。

### E10

- 位置：c) Parent information sheet and withdrawal form
- 原文："For participants who are 13 years or older, they themselves, rather than their parents, should be given the right to object to data processing."
- 要点：13 岁及以上的学生，应由本人（而不是家长）行使反对数据处理的权利。

### E11

- 位置：Pre-testing
- 原文："Pre-testing should be completed before randomisation takes place. If schools are randomised, allocated to the control group, and then asked to complete testing, they might refuse to take part, or the data may be biased once schools/pupils are aware of their allocation."
- 要点：前测要在随机分组前完成；否则被分到对照组的学校可能拒绝测试，知道分组后数据也可能有偏差。

### E12

- 位置：5. Retention
- 原文："If schools or pupils decline to take part in data collection or withdraw from outcome data collection during the study (known as "outcome attrition"), this will reduce the sample size and can introduce biases."
- 要点：学校或学生不参加结果数据收集（结果流失），会减少样本量，还可能带来偏差。

### E13

- 位置：5. Retention
- 原文："It can be more difficult to retain control schools in the post-test, as they have had little contact with the delivery team and will not have engaged very much with the project since recruitment."
- 要点：对照组学校和项目团队接触少，后测时更难留住。

### E14

- 位置：Pre-intervention survey to establish usual practice
- 原文："Especially for targeted interventions, pupils in control schools are often given additional support or training of a different kind."
- 要点：尤其是针对性干预，对照组学校的学生也常常得到其他形式的额外支持或训练，所以要事先了解对照组的"常规做法"。

### E15

- 位置：Keeping a recruitment log
- 原文："This log will be used to create a participant flow diagram which will become part of the final report. Please keep a record of all schools contacted and reasons for non-participation."
- 要点：招募日志用来画进入最终报告的参与者流程图；要记录联系过的所有学校和不参加的原因。

### E16

- 位置：5. Retention
- 原文："The validity of the findings from a trial is illustrated by the padlock rating5 which is heavily influenced by outcome attrition (i.e., did the school provide post-test data or not)."
- 要点：EEF 用"挂锁评级"表示试验结论的可靠程度，这一评级受结果流失（学校有没有交后测数据）影响很大。
