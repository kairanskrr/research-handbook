# Rossner 和 Yamada 2004：What's in a picture? The temptation of image manipulation

- 资料：`rossner2004what`，[原文（PubMed Central）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2172141/)
- 版本：Journal of Cell Biology 166(1):11–15，2004 年，免费全文。两位作者当时是该刊的执行编辑和编辑。原文文本读取自 Europe PMC 全文接口，2026-10-06。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：引言
- 原文："Many such manipulations, however, constitute inappropriate changes to your original data, and making such changes can be classified as scientific misconduct."
- 要点：很多对数字图像的修改属于对原始数据的不当改动，可能被认定为科研不端。

### E2

- 位置：Journal guidelines，The Journal of Cell Biology
- 原文："No specific feature within an image may be enhanced, obscured, moved, removed, or introduced. The grouping of images from different parts of the same gel, or from different gels, fields, or exposures must be made explicit by the arrangement of the figure (e.g., using dividing lines) and in the text of the figure legend."
- 要点：《细胞生物学杂志》的规定：图像中任何特定部分都不能被增强、遮盖、移动、删除或添加；来自同一块胶不同部位、不同胶、不同视野或不同曝光的图像拼在一起，要用分隔线标出并在图注说明。

### E3

- 位置：Journal guidelines，The Journal of Cell Biology
- 原文："Adjustments of brightness, contrast, or color balance are acceptable if they are applied to the whole image and as long as they do not obscure or eliminate any information present in the original. Nonlinear adjustments (e.g., changes to gamma settings) must be disclosed in the figure legend."
- 要点：亮度、对比度、色彩平衡的调整，只要作用于整张图、不遮盖或消除原图信息，就可以接受；非线性调整（如改伽马值）必须在图注里说明。

### E4

- 位置：Blots and gels，Gross misrepresentation
- 原文："Deleting a band from a blot, even if you believe it to be an irrelevant background band, is a misrepresentation of your data"
- 要点：从印迹图上删掉一条带，即使你认为它只是无关的背景带，也是歪曲数据。

### E5

- 位置：Blots and gels，Cleaning up background
- 原文："It is very tempting to use the tool variously known as ‘Rubber Stamp’ or ‘Clone Stamp’ in Photoshop to clean up unwanted background in an image (Fig. 4). Don't do it."
- 要点：不要用图像软件的"仿制图章"之类工具清理背景。

### E6

- 位置：Blots and gels，Splicing lanes together
- 原文："But it is within usual guidelines to remove a complete lane from a gel and splice the remaining lanes together. This alteration should be clearly indicated, however, by leaving a thin white or black line between the gel pieces that have been juxtaposed." / "it is unacceptable to juxtapose pieces from different gels to compare the levels of proteins or nucleic acids. Rerun all of the samples on the same gel!"
- 要点：去掉整条泳道、把其余泳道拼起来，一般是允许的，但要在拼接处留一条细的白线或黑线标明；把不同胶的片段拼在一起比较蛋白或核酸水平不可以，应把所有样品在同一块胶上重跑。

### E7

- 位置：Micrographs，Linear vs. nonlinear adjustments
- 原文："Some authors mistakenly change the contrast of an experimental compared with a control photo, or change individual panels in a time course, or use different contrast levels when making merged images compared with the original images."
- 要点：常见错误：实验组和对照组照片的对比度调得不一样，时间序列里单独调某一张，合并图和原图用不同的对比度。

### E8

- 位置：Other data-management issues
- 原文："It is crucially important to keep your original digital or analog data exactly as they were acquired and to record your instrument settings."
- 要点：原始图像数据要按采集时的样子原样保存，并记录仪器设置。

### E9

- 位置：Blots and gels，Brightness/contrast adjustments
- 原文："Think about this when you are doing the experiment and perform multiple exposures to get the bands at the density you want, without having to overadjust digitally the brightness and contrast of the scanned image."
- 要点：做实验时就多拍几个曝光，得到想要的条带深浅，而不是事后在电脑上过度调整亮度和对比度。

### E10

- 位置：Other data-management issues
- 原文："Examples include selective acquisition of data by adjusting the settings on your microscope or imager, selecting and reporting a very unusual result as being representative of the data, or hiding negative results that may contradict your conclusions."
- 要点：其他歪曲数据的做法：通过调整显微镜或成像仪设置有选择地采集数据，把极不寻常的结果当作"代表性"结果报告，隐藏与结论矛盾的阴性结果。

### E11

- 位置：Conclusion
- 原文："For every adjustment that you make to a digital image, it is important to ask yourself, ‘Is the image that results from this adjustment still an accurate representation of the original data?’"
- 要点：每做一次图像调整，都问自己：调整后的图像还准确代表原始数据吗？
