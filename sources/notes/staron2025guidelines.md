# Staron 2025：Guidelines for Conducting Action Research Studies in Software Engineering

- 资料：`staron2025guidelines`，[原文](https://www.e-informatyka.pl/EISEJ/papers/2025/1/5/)
- 版本：e-Informatica Software Engineering Journal 19(1), 250105, 2025，DOI 10.37190/e-Inf250105。开放获取（PDF 页脚标注 CC BY-SA 4.0）。作者是 Springer 2020 年专著 *Action Research in Software Engineering* 的作者，本文在该书基础上整理出指南；该书不是开放获取，本项目未读。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："The action research addresses the challenges and dilemmas many software engineering researchers face" / "how to introduce and simultaneously study new technology."
- 要点：行动研究针对的难题是：如何一边引入新技术，一边研究它。

### E2

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："Researchers must be more observers or participants but not executors of actions and interventions at the company." / "Experiments are driven by hypotheses and, therefore, prescribe controllability, which favors isolated, small (even toy) problems as the core of experimentation. Design science research focuses on the artifacts rather than improving the practice of the collaborating company."
- 要点：案例研究中研究者是观察者或参与者，而不是公司里干预的执行者；实验由假设驱动、要求可控，偏向孤立的小问题；设计科学研究关注制品本身，而不是改进合作公司的实践。

### E3

- 位置：§2.2 Existing action research guidelines（转述 Davison 等人 2004 的典范行动研究原则）
- 原文："Did both the researcher and the client agree that CAR was the appropriate approach" / "Did the client make an explicit commitment to the project?" / "Were the roles and responsibilities of the researcher and client organization members specified explicitly?" / "Were project objectives and evaluation measures specified explicitly?" / "Were the data collection and analysis methods specified explicitly?"
- 要点：研究者与委托方协议原则：双方是否都同意行动研究适合这个情况；委托方是否明确承诺；双方的角色和职责、项目目标和评估指标、数据收集和分析方法是否都明确写下。

### E4

- 位置：§2.2（转述 Davison 等人 2004）
- 原文："Did the researcher conduct an independent diagnosis of the organizational situation?" / "Were the planned actions based explicitly on the results of the diagnosis?" / "Was this reflection followed by an explicit decision on whether or not to proceed through an additional process cycle?" / "Did the client approve the planned actions before they were implemented?" / "Was the organization situation assessed comprehensively both before and after the intervention?"
- 要点：循环过程原则和"通过行动带来改变"原则中的检查项：研究者是否独立诊断了组织状况；计划的行动是否明确基于诊断结果；反思后是否明确决定要不要再走一个循环；行动实施前是否经委托方批准；干预前后是否都全面评估了组织状况。

### E5

- 位置：§2.2
- 原文："These guidelines also do not consider the necessity for researchers to be embedded in the organizations but often imply that the researchers are external and introduce a change in the organization. This is not how action research is done in software engineering" / "research must be embedded in the organization, as without it, the change is often abandoned."
- 要点：Staron 认为，信息系统领域的指南往往默认研究者是外来的；而在软件工程中，研究必须嵌入组织内部，否则改变往往会被放弃。

### E6

- 位置：§2.1 Studies in action research；Figure 2
- 原文："The action research methodology presented in that book focuses on a five-step cyclic model, which is similar to the view of action research of Baskerville et al." / "This paper follows the same model, although the guidelines are applicable for the action research models with an arbitrary number of phases."
- 要点：Staron 的专著采用五步循环模型（图 2：诊断、行动计划、采取行动、评估、学习），本文沿用，但指南也适用于阶段数不同的模型。

### E7

- 位置：§3 Context of action research
- 原文："In action research, the fact that the researchers are embedded in the organization means that they are either directly employed at the company or that they have the same access to the company as the employees"
- 要点：嵌入组织指研究者要么直接受雇于公司，要么拥有和员工一样的访问权限。

### E8

- 位置：§3.1 What constitutes action research
- 原文："An example of such an intervention can be changing how the company writes its commit messages by adding the ID of the user story that is implemented" / "at least some research team members should have a research degree" / "at least some action team members should be practitioners from the host company."
- 要点：干预的例子：要求提交信息里写上所实现的用户故事编号。行动团队中要有受过研究训练的成员，也要有来自该公司的从业者。

### E9

- 位置：§3.2 Situations where action research is not appropriate
- 原文："if we do not have access to the company and no company representative is part of the research team, we must choose another research methodology." / "When researchers cannot commit to the project for the long term, we should also choose another methodology." / "We should also not engage when we cannot contribute to the practice and we are after publications, not impacting the industry."
- 要点：不适合做行动研究的情况：进不了公司、团队里没有公司代表；研究者不能长期投入；只想发论文、无法对实践有所贡献。

### E10

- 位置：§4.1 Project set-up，Guideline 2 与 Guideline 3
- 原文："The researchers should specify their research results, publications, and engagement expectations. The practitioners should be transparent about their expectations regarding resources, the ability to conduct interventions, and collaboration with academic partners." / "Be ready to pivot on your research methodology if the situation is not optimal for action research." / "In most cases, to get more familiar with the organization, it is better to start with a case study"
- 要点：开始前双方要说清各自的期望：研究者说明想得到的研究成果、发表和投入程度；从业者说明资源、能否实施干预、和学术方合作的期望。情况不适合时要准备换研究方法；多数情况下先做案例研究熟悉组织更好。

### E11

- 位置：§4.2 Host environment，Guideline 5 与 Guideline 6
- 原文："approach management first in hierarchical organizations, and start with the team in empowered organizations." / "The researchers must respect the rules, principles, and obligations of the host organization as if they were employed there." / "researchers must be able to generalize their results to such a level that does not harm the company and is transferable to other contexts."
- 要点：等级制组织先找管理层，自主型组织先找团队；研究者要像员工一样遵守组织的规定和义务；发表时要把结果概括到既不伤害公司、又能迁移到其他情境的程度。

### E12

- 位置：§4.3 Interventions，Guideline 7
- 原文："Every intervention should be planned, lead to observable effects, and properly evaluated." / "Quantitative effects can be collected by measuring products, processes, or designs, while qualitative effects can be observed through interviews, workshops, and post-mortem analyses." / "We must measure the baselines and conduct qualitative data collection before the intervention."
- 要点：每次干预都要事先计划、产生可观察的效果，并被认真评估；定量效果靠测量产品、过程或设计，定性效果靠访谈、研讨会和事后复盘；干预之前就要测基线并收集定性数据。

### E13

- 位置：§4.3，Guideline 8 与 Guideline 9
- 原文："Interventions in each cycle should be atomic." / "These disruptions are confounding factors, making it difficult to determine whether the observed results are due to the intervention or these other changes." / "Practitioners in the action team must be able to conduct the intervention." / "Practitioners should intervene within the scope of their work rather than attempting to change or advocate for the practices of other teams or organizations." / "Conducting a mock-up study or creating a pilot product to demonstrate a tool outside of current practices is not action research, as it does not introduce observable changes; it is merely an offline study."
- 要点：每个循环的干预要尽量小而单一；干预拖得越长，越容易被其他工作打断，这些干扰是混杂因素，让人分不清效果来自干预还是别的变化。行动团队中的从业者必须有能力实施干预，并在自己的工作范围内干预，而不是去改变其他团队的做法。在现有实践之外做模拟研究或演示原型不是行动研究。

### E14

- 位置：§4.4 Planning, conducting and analyzing，Guideline 10 与 Guideline 11
- 原文："We must set the stop criteria and ensure we reach them." / "We need to know when the intervention was successful and when it was not." / "Every intervention must be compared to a baseline, so ensure that there is one."
- 要点：事先定好停止标准，事先知道怎样算干预成功、怎样算不成功；每次干预都要和基线比较。

### E15

- 位置：§4.4，Guideline 11 之后
- 原文："when we want to validate the effect of introducing modern code reviews, we should measure the performance of code reviews before this introduction in terms of speed, duration, and number of reviews." / "If pre-intervention data collection is necessary, we should be prepared to mitigate the Hawthorne effect"
- 要点：例如要验证引入现代代码评审的效果，就要在引入之前测量评审的速度、时长和次数；干预前收集数据时，要准备应对霍桑效应（人们知道自己被观察时会改变行为）。

### E16

- 位置：§4.4，Guideline 12
- 原文："ensure that when the intervention is done, there are no confounders like reorganizations, new team onboarding, product changes, or other events that could affect the result. If they are, be prepared to document these events and study their effects or pivot"
- 要点：干预期间要留意重组、新成员加入、产品变化等可能影响结果的事件；出现了就要记录下来并研究其影响，或者调整方向。

### E17

- 位置：§4.4，Guideline 13
- 原文："Design and plan interventions based on causes identified in the diagnosing phase." / "The action team can introduce new theories (e.g., from processes to products), change the scope of the cycle (e.g., from methods to tools), adjust the team (e.g., introduce new roles), or re-design the intervention so that it fits both the organization and the theory."
- 要点：根据诊断阶段找出的原因设计干预；每个循环之间可以调整理论、范围、团队，或重新设计干预。

### E18

- 位置：§4.4，Guideline 14
- 原文："Be ready to stop the intervention if it causes harm to the organization" / "We should remember that even negative or inconclusive results are of certain value to the research community."
- 要点：干预如果对组织造成伤害，要随时准备停止；负面或不确定的结果对研究界也有价值。

### E19

- 位置：§4.4，Guideline 15
- 原文："Depending on the specific context and goals of the research, the process might conclude after two or three cycles or extend to ten or more cycles." / "they can get stuck in solving no longer important problems."
- 要点：行动研究可能两三个循环就结束，也可能持续十个以上；长期不变的团队有可能陷在已经不重要的问题上。

### E20

- 位置：§4.5 Reporting，Guideline 18
- 原文："Characterize the context of the study, including undertaken actions/interventions." / "If the action team has to choose whether to reveal the organization's name or its characteristics, the characteristics provide more value to other researchers."
- 要点：要描述研究的情境，包括采取了哪些干预；如果只能在公开组织名称和公开组织特征之间二选一，特征对其他研究者更有价值。

### E21

- 位置：§4.6 Ethical considerations for action research studies
- 原文："This can include private conversations, e-mails, or commit messages containing sensitive information." / "we should always consult the company's management and legal department before spreading information that could be sensitive" / "When presenting such results in a larger forum, it is essential to anonymize the data by removing names and identifying details of individuals or teams."
- 要点：研究者可能接触到私人对话、邮件、含敏感信息的提交记录；传播可能敏感的信息前要咨询公司管理层和法务；在更大范围展示结果时要去掉个人或团队的姓名和可识别细节。

### E22

- 位置：§4.6，Guideline 19 与 Guideline 20
- 原文："Both researchers and practitioners tend to generalize the results too quickly. The fact that an improvement worked for one organization does not guarantee it will work for another." / "When we specify the theory upfront, we use it to interpret the results, which helps avoid overinterpretation, speculation, or even the Hawthorne effect."
- 要点：研究者和从业者都容易过快推广结果，在一个组织有效不代表在另一个组织也有效；事先确定理论并用它解释结果，有助于避免过度解读。

### E23

- 位置：§5 Conclusions
- 原文："Although these guidelines are probably relevant to industrial action research (in vivo action research), they have not yet been validated in that context."
- 要点：作者承认这些指南尚未在工业行动研究中得到验证。
