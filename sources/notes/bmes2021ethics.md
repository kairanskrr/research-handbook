# BMES 2021：BMES Code of Ethics

- 资料：`bmes2021ethics`，[原文 PDF](https://www.bmes.org/hubfs/Files/BMES_Code_of_Ethics_2021_-_Revised_October_2021.pdf)
- 版本：美国生物医学工程学会（Biomedical Engineering Society，BMES）伦理准则，2021 年 10 月修订版，2026-10-06 下载。位置写准则条目编号。

### E1

- 位置：序言
- 原文："This BMES Code of Ethics is not a binding statement of law" / "The principles herein define the specific conduct to which a biomedical engineer should conform to meet the ethical standards of our profession, beyond the requirements set by regulatory bodies."
- 要点：这份准则不是法律；它规定的是生物医学工程师在监管机构要求之外，还应遵守的职业伦理。

### E2

- 位置：1.1.1
- 原文："Assume personal responsibility for understanding the local regulations pertaining to biomedical research and technology development, and adhere to the letter and spirit of applicable regulation"
- 要点：弄懂本地关于生物医学研究和技术开发的法规是自己的责任，要遵守法规的字面要求和精神。

### E3

- 位置：2.1.3
- 原文："Keep meticulous, comprehensive, and accurate records throughout the process of discovery and design to prevent errors, increase transparency, and improve reproducibility."
- 要点：在研究和设计的全过程中，保持细致、完整、准确的记录，用来防止出错、增加透明度、提高可复现性。

### E4

- 位置：2.1.4
- 原文："Exercise due diligence when studying or developing biomedical technology (e.g., seek review from collaborators or consultants when working outside areas of proficiency, keep up to date on current methods and practices, and undertake thorough background searches)."
- 要点：研究和开发生物医学技术要尽职：超出自己专长时请合作者或顾问把关，跟上现行方法，做充分的背景检索。

### E5

- 位置：2.2.2
- 原文："Strive to make biomedical data and methodology publicly accessible following project completion or proprietary development (subject to privacy constraints on human data)"
- 要点：项目结束或专有开发完成后，在人体数据隐私限制之内，尽量公开数据和方法。

### E6

- 位置：2.3.1
- 原文："Treat human subjects as intrinsically valuable rather than instrumental in service of research and development goals"
- 要点：把受试者当作本身有价值的人，而不是为研发目标服务的工具。

### E7

- 位置：2.4.1
- 原文："Use animals in quantities and in levels of distress that are justified by the potential benefits the biomedical research may have for advancing the public health."
- 要点：使用动物的数量和让动物承受的痛苦程度，要能被研究对公共健康的潜在益处所证明是合理的。

### E8

- 位置：3.1.2
- 原文："Design and refine biomedical devices explicitly with the broadest possible range of humans in mind (e.g., spanning age, sex, size, ability, and other fundamental physiological characteristics)."
- 要点：设计器械时要明确考虑尽可能广泛的人群，包括不同年龄、性别、体型、能力和其他基本生理特征。

### E9

- 位置：3.2.2
- 原文："Ensure that artificially intelligent systems and data-driven models developed with the potential for use in public health decisions are validated on their use cases and that their limitations, scope, and data sources are known and clearly defined to practitioners and institutions (e.g., algorithmic medical diagnostics)."
- 要点：可能用于公共健康决策的人工智能系统和数据驱动模型，要在实际用途上得到验证，并向使用者和机构讲清局限、适用范围和数据来源。

### E10

- 位置：5.1.1
- 原文："Declare conflicts of interest transparently to relevant parties, both financial and intellectual"
- 要点：向相关各方透明地声明利益冲突，包括经济上的和学术观点上的。

### E11

- 位置：5.1.2
- 原文："Communicate to the media and public with scrupulous honesty (e.g., clearly express warranted levels of confidence in conclusions and put expected efficacy in context) to avoid misleading the public (e.g., giving false hopes for treatment"
- 要点：对媒体和公众说话要严格诚实，讲清结论有多大把握、预期疗效放在什么背景下理解，避免给病人虚假的治疗希望。
