# Lachat et al. 2016：STROBE-nut: An Extension of the STROBE Statement

- 资料：`lachat2016strobenut`，[PLoS Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1002036)（CC BY）
- 版本：PLoS Med 13(6): e1002036，2016。STROBE 针对营养流行病学的扩展。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："In total, 24 recommendations for nutritional epidemiology were added to the STROBE checklist."
- 要点：在 STROBE 清单基础上，为营养流行病学增加了 24 条建议。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Methods to assess dietary intake include (i) food frequency questionnaires, principally used to assess long-term average intakes; (ii) 24-hour recall as a memory-based short-term dietary assessment method; (iii) a food diary, which prospectively collects dietary intake data"
- 要点：评估饮食的方法包括：主要用来评估长期平均摄入的食物频率问卷；靠记忆的短期方法 24 小时回顾；前瞻记录的膳食日记等。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Only a few ideal reference measures are currently available, i.e., stable isotopes like doubly labeled water for energy intake, the recovery biomarker 24-hour urinary nitrogen excretion for protein intake, and 24-hour urinary potassium excretion for potassium intake. For practical reasons, however, nonideal reference measures such as 24-hour dietary recalls or food records are often used."
- 要点：理想的参照测量很少，只有双标水（能量）、24 小时尿氮（蛋白质）、24 小时尿钾（钾）等；实际中常用 24 小时回顾或膳食记录这类不理想的参照。

### E4

- 位置：nut-9
- 原文："It should be clear how misreporting (including under- and overreporting) was defined and addressed in the analysis. Potential selection bias due to exclusion of misreporters should be assessed by comparing participant characteristics"
- 要点：要写清误报（少报和多报）怎样定义、分析中怎样处理；排除误报者可能带来选择偏倚，应比较排除前后参与者的特征。

### E5

- 位置：nut-12.2
- 原文："Adjustment for total energy or energy from food can also mitigate the dietary assessment measurement error"
- 要点：对总能量或食物能量做调整，也能减轻饮食评估的测量误差。

### E6

- 位置：nut-8.6
- 原文："Describe and reference the validation study of the dietary or nutritional assessment method, including the reference method(s) used, when it was conducted, and in which population."
- 要点：描述并引用饮食评估方法的验证研究，包括参照方法、做验证的时间和人群。
