# ASME：Papers Presented at a Conference

- 资料：`asmeconfpapers`，[原文](https://www.asme.org/publications-submissions/journals/information-for-authors/journal-guidelines/papers-presented-at-a-conference)
- 版本：美国机械工程师学会（ASME）期刊作者指南页面，2026-10-06 读取。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Conferences
- 原文："ASME currently accepts submissions of papers that have been previously presented at a conference proceeding. Authors are required to identify the conference as part of the submission. A description of modifications made to the paper post-presentation should be included in the Comments box."
- 要点：ASME 期刊接受已在会议上发表过的论文投稿，但要注明是哪个会议，并说明会后做了哪些修改。

### E2

- 位置：Conferences
- 原文："The review and publication of a paper that was previously presented at a conference is at the discretion of the Journal Editor."
- 要点：这类论文是否送审、是否发表，由期刊主编决定。

### E3

- 位置：ASME Conferences
- 原文："To avoid dual submission, papers from an ASME conference can only be submitted to a journal after Author Notification of Acceptance."
- 要点：为避免重复投稿，ASME 会议的论文要等收到会议录用通知后才能投 ASME 期刊。

### E4

- 位置：ASME Conferences
- 原文："Conference papers submitted to an ASME Journal undergo an independent peer-review with the opportunity for the author to provide revision, expansion, and updates based upon the reviews."
- 要点：投到 ASME 期刊的会议论文要重新独立审稿，作者可以根据审稿意见修改、扩充和更新。

### E5

- 位置：ASME Conferences
- 原文："A reference to the conference publication should be included as a footnote and reference in the journal paper."
- 要点：期刊版要在脚注和参考文献里引用会议版。
