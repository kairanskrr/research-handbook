# Bertout、Baneux、Robertson-Plouch 2021：Recommendations for Ethical Review of Veterinary Clinical Trials

- 资料：`bertout2021recommendations`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.3389/fvets.2021.715926)
- 版本：Frontiers in Veterinary Science 8:715926，2021 年，开放获取的短综述（Mini Review）。讨论的是美国的情况。作者声明了利益冲突（见 E9）。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："While the laws and standards are clear for humans and for laboratory animals, the laws and standards for clinical research for client-owned animals are not as well-defined."
- 要点：人体研究和实验动物研究的法律标准都很清楚，客户自有动物临床研究的法律标准却不够明确。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Herein, "veterinary clinical studies" refer to clinical studies enrolling privately-owned companion animal patients (pet dogs, cats, horses, etc.,) through veterinary clinics or hospitals, similar to clinical trials with human participants. The term does not refer to studies in laboratory animals owned by a research facility."
- 要点：文中"兽医临床研究"指通过兽医诊所或医院招募私人拥有的伴侣动物（宠物狗、猫、马等）的临床研究，类似人体临床试验；不包括研究机构自己拥有的实验动物。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："because companion animal species naturally develop a number of diseases or conditions that parallel those found in humans, studies involving privately-owned pets can help fuel advances in both human and veterinary medicine."
- 要点：伴侣动物会自然患上很多和人类相似的疾病，所以用宠物做的研究能同时推动人医和兽医。

### E4

- 位置：Review，Differences in Laws and Regulations for Human and Animal Clinical Research
- 原文："For veterinary clinical studies with client-owned animals, the laws pertaining to research are scant."
- 要点：（在美国）针对客户自有动物临床研究的法律很少。

### E5

- 位置：Review（伦理审查的缺口）
- 原文："However, the AWA laws and regulations and the IACUC review do not address some of the most critical elements of clinical research, including, as an example, the process of informed consent."
- 要点：美国《动物福利法》及其法规和动物照料与使用委员会（IACUC）的审查，没有覆盖临床研究最关键的一些环节，例如知情同意。

### E6

- 位置：Review（伦理审查的缺口）
- 原文："There are both similarities and differences in the informed consent in human studies and in animal studies, with animal studies bearing a distinction that consent is by the owner, rather than the human participant or parent of a pediatric patient."
- 要点：动物研究的知情同意由主人给出，而不是由受试者本人或患儿父母给出，这是它和人体研究的区别。

### E7

- 位置：Review（伦理审查的缺口）
- 原文："Many journals justifiably require a statement from the authors confirming that the research being submitted for publication was approved by an ethical review committee prior to study conduct. Consequently, in the absence of an ethical review, veterinary medical doctors employed in private practice may face rejection of their original research manuscripts by journals."
- 要点：很多期刊要求作者声明研究在开展前已获伦理委员会批准；私人诊所的兽医如果没有伦理审查，原创论文可能被拒。

### E8

- 位置：Review（伦理审查的缺口）
- 原文："those doctors who are completing residencies in private practices are often applying for specialty board certification; these boards typically have a first-author publication requirement as part of the credentialing process"
- 要点：在私人诊所做住院医师培训的兽医常要申请专科认证，这些专科委员会一般要求有第一作者论文。

### E9

- 位置：Conflict of Interest
- 原文："JB co-owns a veterinary clinical research organization (CASTR Alliance, Inc.) that provides clinical research services, including ethical review, for pharma- or biotech- sponsored veterinary clinical studies."
- 要点：第一作者共同拥有一家提供兽医临床研究服务（包括伦理审查）的公司；本文建议扩大独立伦理审查委员会的作用，读者应留意这层利益关系。

### E10

- 位置：Veterinary Clinical Studies Committee
- 原文："The AVMA policy indicates that the VCSC should ensure that informed consent is obtained from owners and that the animals enrolled in the studies are protected from conflict-of-interest issues."
- 要点：美国兽医协会（AVMA）的政策提出"兽医临床研究委员会"，它应确保取得主人的知情同意，并保护入组动物不受利益冲突影响。
