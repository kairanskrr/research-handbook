# Hunt 2002：Against Presentism

- 资料：`hunt2002against`，[原文（Internet Archive 快照）](https://web.archive.org/web/20220918163213/https://www.historians.org/publications-and-directories/perspectives-on-history/may-2002/against-presentism)
- 版本：Lynn Hunt 时任美国历史学会会长，发表于 AHA 刊物 *Perspectives on History* 2002 年 5 月号的会长专栏。官网对脚本访问返回 403，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2022-09-18 的快照（旧网址 `historians.org/publications-and-directories/perspectives-on-history/may-2002/against-presentism`）。文章不分节，位置写段落主题。

### E1

- 位置：开头部分
- 原文："Yet presentism besets us in two different ways: (1) the tendency to interpret the past in presentist terms; and (2) the shift of general historical interest toward the contemporary period and away from the more distant past."
- 要点："当下主义"（presentism）有两种表现：一是用今天的概念和标准去解释过去；二是研究兴趣整体偏向近现代，远离更久远的过去。

### E2

- 位置：讨论道德优越感的段落
- 原文："Interpreting the past in terms of present concerns usually leads us to find ourselves morally superior"
- 要点：用今天的关切去解释过去，往往让我们觉得自己道德上更优越。

### E3

- 位置：最后一段
- 原文："But it is possible to remind ourselves of the virtues of maintaining a fruitful tension between present concerns and respect for the past."
- 要点：当下主义没有现成的解法，但可以提醒自己：在今天的关切和对过去的尊重之间保持一种有益的张力。

### E4

- 位置：最后一段
- 原文："The emergence of new concerns in the present invariably reveals aspects of historical experience that have been occluded or forgotten."
- 要点：今天出现的新关切，也会揭示过去被遮蔽或遗忘的历史经验。现实关切本身不是坏事。
