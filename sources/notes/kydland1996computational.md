# Kydland & Prescott 1996：The Computational Experiment: An Econometric Tool

- 资料：`kydland1996computational`，[原文](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.10.1.69)
- 版本：Journal of Economic Perspectives 10(1): 69–85，1996。本文只读了美国经济学会官网文章页上公开的摘要，没有读到全文。据 Hansen & Heckman 1996 摘要，校准方法由这两位作者倡导。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Performing experiments using actual people at the level of national economies is obviously impractical but constructing a model economy and computing the economic behavior of the model's people is."
- 要点：在整个国家经济的层面上拿真人做实验显然不可行，但可以建一个模型经济，计算模型里的人怎样行动。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："This essay specifies the steps in designing a computational experiment to address some well-posed quantitative question. The computational experiment is an econometric tool used in the task of deriving the quantitative implications of theory."
- 要点：文章给出设计计算实验的步骤，用来回答一个提得清楚的定量问题；计算实验是推出理论的定量含义的工具。
