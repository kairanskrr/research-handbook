# OECD 1998：OECD Principles on Good Laboratory Practice (as revised in 1997)

- 资料：`oecd1998glp`，[原文 PDF](https://one.oecd.org/document/env/mc/chem(98)17/en/pdf)（官网对脚本返回 403，文本取自 Internet Archive 快照，内容为同一份 PDF）
- 版本：经济合作与发展组织（Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development，OECD）《良好实验室规范原则和合规监测》系列第 1 号，文件号 ENV/MC/CHEM(98)17，1998 年发布，免费公开。位置写章节号。

### E1

- 位置：Section I, 2.1
- 原文："Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) is a quality system concerned with the organisational process and the conditions under which non-clinical health and environmental safety studies are planned, performed, monitored, recorded, archived and reported."
- 要点：良好实验室规范是一套质量体系，管非临床健康与环境安全研究怎样计划、实施、监督、记录、归档和报告。

### E2

- 位置：Section I, 1. Scope
- 原文："Unless specifically exempted by national legislation, these Principles of Good Laboratory Practice apply to all non-clinical health and environmental safety studies required by regulations for the purpose of registering or licensing pharmaceuticals, pesticides, food and feed additives, cosmetic products, veterinary drug products and similar products, and for the regulation of industrial chemicals."
- 要点：适用范围是法规要求的、用于药品、农药、食品和饲料添加剂、化妆品、兽药等注册或许可，以及工业化学品监管的非临床安全研究（国家立法另有豁免的除外）。

### E3

- 位置：Preface
- 原文："These Principles of GLP were adopted by the OECD Council in 1981, as an Annex to the Council Decision on the Mutual Acceptance of Data in the Assessment of Chemicals"
- 要点：这套原则由 OECD 理事会 1981 年通过，是《化学品评估数据互认决定》的附件。

### E4

- 位置：Foreword
- 原文："The GLP regulations for non-clinical laboratory studies published by the US Food and Drug Administration in 1976 provided the basis for the work of the Expert Group"
- 要点：OECD 原则的基础是美国食品药品监督管理局 1976 年发布的非临床实验室研究良好实验室规范法规。

### E5

- 位置：Preface
- 原文："If individual countries can confidently rely on test data developed in other countries, duplicative testing can be avoided, thereby saving time and resources."
- 要点：目的之一是让各国互相认可试验数据，避免重复试验。

### E6

- 位置：Section II, 1.2
- 原文："The Study Director is the single point of study control and has the responsibility for the overall conduct of the study and for its final report."
- 要点：每项研究有一位研究负责人（Study Director），对研究的整体实施和最终报告负总责。

### E7

- 位置：Section II, 2.1
- 原文："This individual(s) should not be involved in the conduct of the study being assured."
- 要点：做质量保证的人不能参与他所检查的那项研究。

### E8

- 位置：Section II, 8.1
- 原文："For each study, a written plan should exist prior to the initiation of the study."
- 要点：每项研究开始前都要有书面的研究计划。

### E9

- 位置：Section II, 8.1
- 原文："Deviations from the study plan should be described, explained, acknowledged and dated in a timely fashion by the Study Director and/or Principal Investigator(s) and maintained with the study raw data."
- 要点：偏离研究计划的地方要及时描述、解释、确认并注明日期，和原始数据一起保存。

### E10

- 位置：Section II, 8.3
- 原文："All data generated during the conduct of the study should be recorded directly, promptly, accurately, and legibly by the individual entering the data. These entries should be signed or initialled and dated."
- 要点：所有数据由录入人直接、及时、准确、清楚地记录，并签名或签缩写、注明日期。

### E11

- 位置：Section II, 8.3
- 原文："Any change in the raw data should be made so as not to obscure the previous entry, should indicate the reason for change and should be dated and signed or initialled by the individual making the change."
- 要点：修改原始数据不能盖住原来的记录，要写明理由、日期和修改人签名。

### E12

- 位置：Foreword
- 原文："Its principles are required to be followed by test facilities carrying out studies to be submitted to national authorities for the purposes of assessment of chemicals and other uses relating to the protection of man and the environment."
- 要点：要把研究结果交给国家主管部门做评估的试验机构，必须遵守这些原则。

### E13

- 位置：Section II, 10.1
- 原文："a) The study plan, raw data, samples of test and reference items, specimens, and the final report of each study;"
- 要点：研究计划、原始数据、受试物和对照物样品、标本、最终报告都要归档，保存期限由主管部门规定。

### E14

- 位置：Foreword
- 原文："Organisation and were adopted by Council on 26th November, 1997 [C(97)186/Final], which formally amended Annex II of the 1981 Council Decision."
- 要点：修订后的原则由 OECD 理事会于 1997 年 11 月 26 日通过，取代了 1981 年决定的附件。
