# Hennink, Kaiser & Weber 2019：What Influences Saturation? Estimating Sample Sizes in Focus Group Research

- 资料：`hennink2019what`，[原文（PubMed Central 作者稿）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6635912/)
- 版本：Qualitative Health Research 29(10):1483–1496（2019）；读的是 PubMed Central 上的作者稿（NIHMS1031312），2026-10-17 访问。公共卫生。研究数据是美国亚特兰大南亚裔成人关于饮食、运动和糖尿病的 10 场焦点小组。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Four focus groups were sufficient to identify a range of new issues (code saturation), but more groups were needed to fully understand these issues (meaning saturation)."
- 要点：在这项研究里，四组就足以发现大部分新问题（编码饱和），但要充分理解这些问题需要更多组（意义饱和）。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Group stratification influenced meaning saturation, whereby one focus group per stratum was needed to identify issues; two groups per stratum provided a more comprehensive understanding of issues, but more groups per stratum provided little additional benefit."
- 要点：分层影响意义饱和：每层一组能发现问题，每层两组理解得更全面，再多则收益很小。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction 末尾
- 原文："we assessed the sample size needed to reach code saturation, which we define as the point when no additional issues are identified in data and the codebook has stabilized. Second, we assessed the sample size needed to reach meaning saturation, which we define as the point at which we fully understand the issues identified and when no further insights or nuances are found."
- 要点：编码饱和指不再出现新问题、编码手册稳定下来；意义饱和指已充分理解这些问题，不再有新的见解或细微差别。

### E4

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："The first focus group generated 60% of all new codes with a sharp decline thereafter, regardless of the order in which focus groups were conducted."
- 要点：不论各组的先后顺序如何，第一组就产生了 60% 的新编码，之后急剧下降。

### E5

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："the prevalence of issues in data does not indicate their significance in understanding the study phenomenon."
- 要点：一个问题在数据中出现得多不多，不代表它对理解研究现象重不重要。

### E6

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："researchers doing a focus group study stratified by one characteristic (e.g., sex) would need to conduct two groups to include both strata but should ideally conduct two groups for each of these strata - thereby making a sample size of 4 focus groups."
- 要点：按一个特征（如性别）分层时，至少要两组才能覆盖两层，理想是每层两组，共四组。

### E7

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："Guest et al (2016) show that a homogenous study population where focus groups are not stratified can reach saturation in three to six focus groups"
- 要点：据 Hennink 等人转述，Guest 等人发现，人群同质、不分层时，三到六组可以达到饱和。

### E8

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："Therefore, providing universal sample size recommendations for focus groups studies is not useful." / "often this is achieved by identifying a range rather a fixed number when a sample size is proposed in advance (e.g., 4–6 focus groups)."
- 要点：给焦点小组研究定一个通用的组数没有用；事先提出样本量时，常写一个范围（如 4 到 6 组）而不是固定数字，以便在收集中灵活调整。
