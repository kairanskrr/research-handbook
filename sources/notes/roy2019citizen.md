# Roy & Edwards 2019：Citizen Science During the Flint, Michigan Federal Water Emergency: Ethical Dilemmas and Lessons Learned

- 资料：`roy2019citizen`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.5334/cstp.154)
- 版本：《Citizen Science: Theory and Practice》第 4 卷，2019 年，开放获取。作者是参与揭露美国密歇根州弗林特饮用水铅污染的弗吉尼亚理工大学团队成员，文章是他们对自身经历的案例回顾，立场鲜明，文中批评的个人和组织对部分说法有异议。位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："A citizen science collaboration among Flint residents, the Virginia Tech "Flint Water Study" team, and others first exposed contamination in Flint's water supply in 2015."
- 要点：2015 年，弗林特居民、弗吉尼亚理工大学团队等的公民科学合作，最先揭露了弗林特供水的污染。

### E2

- 位置：Uncovering the Flint Water Crisis through Citizen Science
- 原文："Virginia Tech provided the technical plan, analytical support, and funding, while Flint residents donated their local knowledge, homes as test sites, and hard work."
- 要点：大学提供技术方案、分析支持和经费；居民贡献本地知识、把自己家当采样点，并出力。

### E3

- 位置：Uncovering the Flint Water Crisis through Citizen Science
- 原文："the citizen coordinated citywide sampling event focused on water lead, which ultimately involved 269 geographically distributed Flint homes."
- 要点：由居民协调的全市水中铅采样，最终覆盖了分布在全市的 269 户家庭。

### E4

- 位置：Characteristics of quality citizen science projects
- 原文："Freitag and colleagues (2016) list 12 strategies to enhance credibility of citizen science projects, including prior expertise of project leaders, substantial training of citizen scientists, science advising to strengthen data collection, peer-oversight between citizen scientists, quality assurance protocols, and cross-comparison of citizen science data with data from professionals."
- 要点：作者引用 Freitag 等人提出的提高公民科学可信度的策略，包括培训参与者、科学指导、质量保证程序、和专业人员的数据交叉比对等。

### E5

- 位置：Persistent attacks on merited expertise
- 原文："If scientists ignore individuals with local knowledge and expertise, the results can be disastrous"
- 要点：科学家忽视拥有本地知识和经验的人，后果可能是灾难性的。

### E6

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Possible abuses of citizen science documented in Flint explored in this case study include: 1) collection of non-representative data that created unjustified fear among residents about the safety of water used for bathing and showering"
- 要点：作者记录的问题之一：收集没有代表性的数据，让居民对洗澡用水的安全产生了没有根据的恐慌。
