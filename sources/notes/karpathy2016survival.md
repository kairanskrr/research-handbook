# Karpathy 2016：A Survival Guide to a PhD

- 资料：`karpathy2016survival`，[原文](http://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/)
- 版本：作者博客，2016-09-07，2026-10-05 读取。位置写小节名和段落开头的加粗小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Adviser / Student adviser relationship
- 原文："It’s important to understand the nature of the relationship: the adviser-student relationship is a symbiosis; you have your own goals and want something out of your PhD, but they also have their own goals, constraints and they’re building their own career."
- 要点：师生关系是共生关系：你有自己的目标，导师也有自己的目标、约束和职业发展。

### E2

- 位置：Adviser / Student adviser relationship
- 原文："Therefore, it is very helpful to understand your adviser’s incentive structures"
- 要点：了解导师的激励结构（怎样被评价、怎样拿经费、怎样资助你），能避免或缓解大部分师生摩擦。

### E3

- 位置：Adviser / Axes of variation
- 原文："Some will try to exercise a lot of influence on the details of your work and some are much more hands off."
- 要点：导师风格差异很大：有的管得很细，有的基本放手；见面频率、回邮件速度等也各不相同。

### E4

- 位置：Adviser / Impressing an adviser
- 原文："The ideal student from their perspective is someone with interest and passion, someone who doesn’t need too much hand-holding, and someone who takes initiative - who shows up a week later having done not just what the adviser suggested, but who went beyond it; improved on it in unexpected ways."
- 要点：导师眼中理想的学生有主动性：一周后带回来的不只是导师建议的东西，还在此基础上有所改进。

### E5

- 位置：Adviser / Consider the entire lab
- 原文："In most cases you will also end up collaborating with some of the senior PhD students or postdocs and they will play a role very similar to that of your adviser."
- 要点：你通常会和高年级博士生或博士后合作，他们的作用和导师很像。

### E6

- 位置：Research topics / Resistance
- 原文："If you feel this way during your phd you should have the courage to sometimes ignore your adviser."
- 要点：导师也会判断失误；如果你确信导师错了，有时要有勇气不听导师的。

### E7

- 位置：Research topics / Resistance
- 原文："I ended up working on a problem he wasn’t very excited about and, surprise, he turned out to be very right and I wasted a few months."
- 要点：作者自己没听导师的建议，结果证明导师是对的，浪费了几个月。坚持己见是有输有赢的赌注。

### E8

- 位置：Writing papers 一节，段落 "An internal deadlines 2 weeks prior"
- 原文："Not many labs do this, but luckily Fei-Fei is quite adamant about an internal deadline 2 weeks before the due date in which you must submit at least a 5-page draft with all the final experiments (even if not with final numbers) that goes through an internal review process identical to the external one (with the same review forms filled out, etc)."
- 要点：作者的导师要求在截稿前两周设内部截止日期：交一份至少 5 页、包含全部最终实验（数字可以不是最终的）的初稿，并按与正式审稿相同的流程做内部评审。

### E9

- 位置：同上
- 原文："I found this practice to be extremely useful because forcing yourself to lay out the full paper almost always reveals some number of critical experiments you must run for the paper to flow and for its argument flow to be coherent, consistent and convincing."
- 要点：逼自己提前排出整篇论文，几乎总会暴露出几个必须补做的关键实验。

### E10

- 位置：Writing code 一节，段落 "Think of the future you"
- 原文："I guarantee you that you will come back to your code base a few months later (e.g. to do a few more experiments for the camera ready version of the paper), and you will feel completely lost in it. I got into the habit of creating very thorough readme.txt files in all my repos (for my personal use) as notes to future self on how the code works, how to run it, etc."
- 要点：几个月后你一定会回到自己的代码（例如为论文终稿补实验），并完全看不懂；作者养成了在每个仓库写详细 readme 的习惯，作为给未来自己的说明：代码怎么工作、怎么运行。
