# NeurIPS 2025：Call for Position Papers

- 资料：`neurips2025position`，[原文](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPositionPapers)
- 版本：NeurIPS 2025（神经信息处理系统大会）立场论文赛道征稿启事；2026-10-17 读取。位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：页首（NeurIPS 2025 Call for Position Papers）
- 原文："We want to hear your ideas. What is the field getting right? Getting wrong? How can our work be used for positive or destructive ends in government, military, medicine, public policy, or law? What are open problems that we should focus more on? Or less?"
- 要点：征稿启事列出立场论文可以回答的问题：领域做对了什么、做错了什么，研究成果可能被怎样用，哪些开放问题该多关注或少关注。

### E2

- 位置：Policies and Requirements
- 原文："Alternative Views: The paper can (if appropriate) include an “Alternative Views” section in the main body of the paper (not in an appendix) that describes and addresses one or more viable (not strawmen) positions that are opposed to the paper’s position."
- 要点：NeurIPS 2025 把"不同观点"一节写成"可以（在合适时）包含"，而不是必须。

### E3

- 位置：Reviewing
- 原文："Alternative views: The paper describes and addresses one or more viable (not strawmen) positions that are opposed to the paper’s position (if relevant)."
- 要点：审稿标准里的"不同观点"一条加了"（如果相关）"。

### E4

- 位置：Policies and Requirements
- 原文："Paper Checklist: No checklist is required for Position Papers."
- 要点：NeurIPS 2025 的立场论文不需要填主赛道的论文检查清单。
