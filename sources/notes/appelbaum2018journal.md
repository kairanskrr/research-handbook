# Appelbaum et al. 2018：Journal Article Reporting Standards for Quantitative Research in Psychology

- 资料：`appelbaum2018journal`，[原文 PDF（APA 官网公开）](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/releases/amp-amp0000191.pdf)
- 版本：American Psychologist 73(1):3–25。APA 出版与传播委员会工作组报告，即定量研究报告标准（JARS–Quant）。

### E1

- 位置：摘要，第 3 页
- 原文："Examples of modifications include division of hypotheses, analyses, and conclusions into 3 groupings (primary, secondary, and exploratory)"
- 要点：新版标准把假设、分析和结论分成三类：主要的、次要的、探索性的。

### E2

- 位置：Table 1，Method — Sample size, power, and precision
- 原文："Describe the sample size, power, and precision, including • Intended sample size • Achieved sample size, if different from intended sample size • Determination of sample size, including • Power analysis, or methods used to determine precision of parameter estimates • Explanation of any interim analyses and stopping rules employed"
- 要点：方法部分要报告计划样本量、实际样本量、样本量是怎么定的（功效分析或精度计算），以及中途分析和停止收数据的规则。

### E3

- 位置：Table 1，Method — Measures
- 原文："Define all primary and secondary measures and covariates, including measures collected but not included in this report."
- 要点：要定义所有主要和次要测量指标，包括收集了但没有在本文报告的指标。

### E4

- 位置：Table 1，Method — Psychometrics
- 原文："Provide information on validated or ad hoc instruments created for individual studies, for example, psychometric and biometric properties." / "Estimate and report values of reliability coefficients for the scores analyzed (i.e., the researcher's sample), if possible."
- 要点：要说明用的是已验证的量表还是为本研究临时编的，报告其测量学性质；尽可能报告本研究样本上的信度系数。

### E5

- 位置：Table 1，Method — Data diagnostics
- 原文："Criteria for post-data collection exclusion of participants, if any"
- 要点：要报告收集数据后剔除参与者的标准。

### E6

- 位置：Table 1，Discussion
- 原文："Provide a statement of support or nonsupport for all hypotheses whether primary or secondary"
- 要点：讨论部分要对所有假设（主要和次要的）逐一说明是否得到支持。

### E7

- 位置：Table 1，Title page and author note
- 原文："Registration information if the study has been registered"
- 要点：研究做过注册的，要在作者注释里给出注册信息。

### E8

- 位置：正文末尾，关于表格用法的一段
- 原文："the ways in which the tables might be used (e.g., as a formal checklist, as a reference list for editors, authors, and reviewers) are not specified."
- 要点：标准没有规定这些表格的用法，期刊可以拿它做正式清单，也可以只当参考。

### E9

- 位置：摘要，第 3 页
- 原文："Several new modules are included that report standards for observational studies, clinical trials, longitudinal studies, replication studies, and N-of-1 studies."
- 要点：新版增加了几个模块，分别针对观察性研究、临床试验、纵向研究、重复研究和单被试（N-of-1）研究。

### E10

- 位置：Changes in Table 1，Psychometrics
- 原文："the term reliability should refer to test scores in particular samples and not to tests or testing instruments. Likewise, the term validity should refer not to a test but to the proposed interpretations of test scores"
- 要点：信度指特定样本上的分数，不是量表本身的属性；效度指对分数的某种解释，不是量表本身的属性。

### E11

- 位置：Changes in Table 1，Psychometrics
- 原文："Best practice is to estimate both reliability and validity, when possible, within the researcher's sample or samples (i.e., the scores analyzed)."
- 要点：最好在自己的样本上估计信度和效度。

### E12

- 位置：Table 1，Method — Psychometrics
- 原文："Provide information on validated or ad hoc instruments created for individual studies, for example, psychometric and biometric properties." / "Estimate and report values of reliability coefficients for the scores analyzed (i.e., the researcher's sample), if possible. Provide estimates of convergent and discriminant validity where relevant."
- 要点：说明用的是已验证的还是为本研究临时编的量表；尽可能报告本研究样本上的信度系数，相关时报告聚合效度和区分效度。

### E13

- 位置：Table 1，Method — Psychometrics
- 原文："Test–retest coefficients in longitudinal studies in which the retest interval corresponds to the measurement schedule used in the study"
- 要点：纵向研究要报告重测系数，重测间隔要与研究的测量安排一致。
