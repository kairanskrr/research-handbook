# NIST：FY 2008 Budget Request Details — National Earthquake Hazards Reduction Program

- 资料：`nist2008nehrp`，[原文](https://www.nist.gov/congressional-and-legislative-affairs/fy08-national-earthquake-hazards-reduction-program)
- 版本：美国国家标准与技术研究院（NIST）2008 财年预算申请说明中的"国家地震减灾计划（NEHRP）"一节；网页 2009 年创建，2025 年更新，读取于 2026-10-06。反映的是 2007—2008 年前后的情况。

### E1

- 位置：Challenge
- 原文："Currently, a critical gap exists between the results produced by basic research and the implementation of that knowledge in the field. New construction materials and techniques and building codes and standards do not reflect the current state-of-knowledge."
- 要点：NIST 当时认为，基础研究成果和工程实际应用之间存在关键差距；新的建筑材料与技术、建筑规范和标准没有反映当前的认识水平。

### E2

- 位置：Proposed Program；Expected Impacts
- 原文："Identify implementation gaps between basic research results and design guidance and national model building code provisions;" / "Updated model building codes and standards that incorporate the latest scientific research aimed at reducing the risk to life and property from earthquakes;"
- 要点：NIST 的计划包括找出基础研究成果与设计指南、全国示范建筑规范条文之间的落地差距；预期成果之一是吸收最新科学研究的更新版示范建筑规范和标准。
