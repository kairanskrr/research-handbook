# Weissgerber 等人 2015：Beyond Bar and Line Graphs: Time for a New Data Presentation Paradigm

- 资料：`weissgerber2015beyond`，[原文](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.1002128)
- 版本：PLOS Biology 13(4):e1002128，2015 年，开放获取。原文文本用 pdftotext 从期刊 PDF 提取，2026-10-06。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Bar and line graphs of continuous data are ‘visual tables’ that typically show the mean and standard error (SE) or standard deviation (SD). This is problematic for three reasons. First, many different data distributions can lead to the same bar or line graph"
- 要点：连续数据画成柱状图或折线图，只显示均值和标准误或标准差；问题之一是，很多不同的数据分布会画出一模一样的柱状图。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Bar graphs of paired data erroneously suggest that the groups being compared are independent and provide no information about whether changes are consistent across individuals"
- 要点：配对数据画成柱状图，会让人误以为各组是独立的，也看不出每个个体的变化方向是否一致。

### E3

- 位置：Recommendations for a New Data Presentation Paradigm
- 原文："The best option for small datasets is to show the full data, as summary statistics are only meaningful if there are enough data to summarize."
- 要点：小样本数据最好的展示方式是把全部数据点画出来，因为数据够多时汇总统计量才有意义。

### E4

- 位置：Introduction；Are Your Figures Worth a Thousand Words?
- 原文："We systematically reviewed all full-length, original research articles published in the top 25% of physiology journals between January 1 and March 31, 2014 (n = 703)" / "85.6% of papers included at least one bar graph." / "13.4% of articles included at least one univariate scatterplot"
- 要点：作者系统回顾了 2014 年 1–3 月生理学排名前 25% 期刊上的 703 篇原创研究论文：85.6% 至少有一张柱状图，只有 13.4% 至少有一张单变量散点图。

### E5

- 位置：Are Your Figures Worth a Thousand Words?
- 原文："Showing the SE rather than the SD magnifies the apparent visual differences between groups."
- 要点：画标准误而不画标准差，会在视觉上放大组间差异。
