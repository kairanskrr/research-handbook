# Zimmermann 等人 2020：Techno-Economic Assessment Guidelines for CO2 Utilization

- 资料：`zimmermann2020tea`，[原文](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fenrg.2020.00005/full)，DOI：10.3389/fenrg.2020.00005
- 版本：Frontiers in Energy Research 第 8 卷第 5 篇，2020 年 1 月 31 日，知识共享署名许可（CC BY）开放获取。是 Global CO2 Initiative（美国密歇根大学）等机构 2018 年发布的《二氧化碳利用技术经济评估与生命周期评估指南》中技术经济评估部分的摘要版。适用对象是碳捕集与利用（Carbon Capture and Utilization，CCU）技术。网页无页码，位置写小标题。2026-10-06 读取。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："No generally accepted techno-economic assessment (TEA) standard has evolved, and assessment studies often result in “apples vs. oranges” comparisons, a lack of transparency and a lack of comparability to other studies."
- 要点：在碳捕集与利用领域，技术经济评估还没有公认的标准，评估研究常常拿不可比的东西作比较，不透明，也没法和其他研究对照。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："For CCU, TEA is reported to be commonly used in industrial companies following internal standards—however usually remains unpublished."
- 要点：据报道，企业常按内部标准做碳捕集与利用的技术经济评估，但通常不发表。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Most academic TEAs in CCU follow chemical engineering text books such as Peters et al. (2003); Sinnott and Towler (2009), and Turton et al. (2012)."
- 要点：学术界做这类技术经济评估，大多照化学工程教科书的方法做。

### E4

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："the feedback of more than 50 experts from industry, academia and policy"
- 要点：这份指南吸收了 50 多位来自产业界、学术界和政策界专家的意见。

### E5

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Goal › Perspectives and Principles of Assessment Goals
- 原文："First and foremost, all assessments need to be based on process concepts that are technologically plausible; for example, proposed concepts do not violate the laws of thermodynamics. Before the assessment, a “sanity check,” for example, checking kinetics as well as mass and energy balances, needs to be conducted"
- 要点：评估的前提是工艺方案在技术上说得通，例如不违反热力学定律；评估前要先做"合理性检查"，核对动力学和物料、能量衡算。

### E6

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Goal › Perspectives and Principles of Assessment Goals
- 原文："The study context, especially comparison to what, location, time horizon, scale and partners"
- 要点：评估目标里要写清研究背景，特别是和什么比、在哪里、时间跨度、规模和合作方。

### E7

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Goal › Perspectives and Principles of Assessment Goals
- 原文："Commissioners and authors of the study (e.g., funding organization, university, company, individual)"
- 要点：评估目标里要写明谁委托、谁做的研究（资助机构、大学、企业、个人）。

### E8

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Scope › Including or excluding CCU upstream processes in system boundaries
- 原文："Therefore, such exclusion cannot result in zero cost input flows, as it is unlikely that suppliers provide CO2 or H2 or electricity without charge."
- 要点：把上游工序排除在系统边界外，不等于这些输入可以按零成本计算，供应商不会免费提供二氧化碳、氢气或电。

### E9

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Inventory › Data Quality
- 原文："If practitioners cannot improve data quality to a satisfactory level, they might not be able to answer the questions posed in the goal. Thus, they should either adjust goal and scope according to data availability or discontinue the study."
- 要点：数据质量提不到够用的程度，就回答不了目标里的问题；这时要么按能拿到的数据调整目标和范围，要么停止这项研究。

### E10

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Inventory › Data Quality
- 原文："Practitioners shall state any problems with the acquisition of confidential data clearly."
- 要点：获取保密数据遇到的问题，要明确写出来。

### E11

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Inventory › Collecting Data
- 原文："When collecting data from different sources, practitioners should carry out harmonization, which means keeping uniformity and aligning assumptions (e.g., adapting data to the same year, continuous use of lower heating value)."
- 要点：从不同来源收集数据时，要统一口径、对齐假设，例如换算到同一年份、始终用低位热值。

### E12

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Inventory › Collecting Data
- 原文："If the technology maturity is low, experts have to include learning curves and improvements for the system elements, for both reference product systems as well as the benchmark systems"
- 要点：技术成熟度低时，要把学习曲线和改进考虑进去，而且对被评估系统和对照系统都要这样做。

### E13

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Inventory › Other Key CCU Inputs › Hydrogen as an input
- 原文："Assuming an optimistic future hydrogen production technology or a larger than currently feasible production scale in the base case scenario"
- 要点：常见陷阱之一：在基准情景里假设乐观的未来制氢技术，或比目前可行规模更大的生产规模。

### E14

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Inventory › Other Key CCU Inputs › Electricity as an input
- 原文："Assuming free or negative electricity prices in the base case scenario."
- 要点：常见陷阱之一：在基准情景里假设电价为零或为负。

### E15

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Inventory › Deriving a CO2 Price
- 原文："One typical pitfall when deriving a CO2 price from literature is mixing up the cost of CO2 captured, and the cost of CO2 avoided."
- 要点：从文献取二氧化碳价格时，一个典型陷阱是把"捕集每吨二氧化碳的成本"和"每减排一吨二氧化碳的成本"混为一谈。

### E16

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Calculation › Best Practices
- 原文："practitioners shall organize indicator calculation separately from inventory (in a different file or sheet) but link to it allowing indicators to update following changes of the inventory."
- 要点：指标计算要和数据清单分开放（不同文件或工作表），但相互链接，清单一改，指标自动更新。

### E17

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Interpretation › Uncertainty and Sensitivity Analysis
- 原文："Practitioners shall provide conclusions, limitations, and a basis for recommendations in the report—which actively build on the analysis of uncertainty and sensitivity."
- 要点：报告里的结论、局限和建议，要建立在不确定性分析和敏感性分析之上。

### E18

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Interpretation › Interpretation of Indicators
- 原文："Indicators, in particular the ones without an inherent comparison, shall be interpreted by comparing the indicator value of the product system in focus to one or multiple benchmark values."
- 要点：指标（特别是本身不含比较的指标）要和一个或多个对照值比较后再解读。

### E19

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Reporting › Content
- 原文："In particular, the report shall present all assumptions, data, methods, results, recommendations, and limitations transparently and as detailed as possible given the audience and style of the report."
- 要点：报告要透明地、尽可能详细地写出全部假设、数据、方法、结果、建议和局限。

### E20

- 位置：TEA Guidelines › Reporting › Reporting Styles
- 原文："Finally, the report should list the names and backgrounds of the practitioners that carried out the study and include a description of whether and how a review was conducted."
- 要点：报告应列出做评估的人的姓名和背景，并说明是否经过审查、怎样审查。
