# Studia Logica：Aims and Scope

- 资料：`studialogicaaims`，[原文](https://link.springer.com/journal/11225/aims-and-scope)
- 版本：Studia Logica（1953 年在波兰创刊，Springer 出版）的"Aims and scope"网页。原站有脚本验证，取自 Internet Archive 2025-12-03 的存档 https://web.archive.org/web/20251203224712/https://link.springer.com/journal/11225/aims-and-scope 。只代表这本期刊。

### E1

- 位置：Aims and scope，第 3 段
- 原文："The key criterion for acceptance of papers to be published in Studia Logica will not be the scope of presented research but its method: they are required to contain significant and original results concerning formal systems and their properties."
- 要点：接收的关键标准不是研究的题目范围，而是方法：论文必须包含关于形式系统及其性质的重要、原创的结果。

### E2

- 位置：Aims and scope，第 3 段
- 原文："Studia Logica strive for a balance between mathematical techniques and philosophical relevance."
- 要点：期刊力求在数学技术和哲学相关性之间取得平衡。
