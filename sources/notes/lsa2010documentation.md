# 美国语言学会 2010：Resolution Recognizing the Scholarly Merit of Language Documentation

- 资料：`lsa2010documentation`，[原文](https://lsa.membershipsoftware.org//Files/Advocacy/201001_Resolution_Scholarly_Merit_Lang_Documentation.pdf)
- 版本：2010 年 1 月年会通过，2011 年 5 月会员表决通过

### E1

- 位置：第 1 条 Whereas
- 原文："Whereas the practice of linguistic fieldwork is shifting to a more collaborative endeavor firmly based on ethical responsibilities to speech communities"
- 要点：语言田野工作正在转向以对言语社区的伦理责任为基础的合作。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 条 Whereas
- 原文："the range of scholarly work to include not only grammars, dictionaries, and text collections, but also archives of primary data, electronic databases, corpora, critical editions of legacy materials, pedagogical works designed for the use of speech communities, software, websites, or other digital media"
- 要点：学术成果的范围扩大到语法书、词典、文本集，以及原始数据档案、语料库、给社区用的教学材料、软件等。

### E3

- 位置：决议
- 原文："Therefore the Linguistic Society of America supports the recognition of these materials as scholarly contributions to be given weight in the awarding of advanced degrees and in decisions on hiring, tenure, and promotion of faculty."
- 要点：美国语言学会支持在学位授予、招聘、终身教职和晋升中把这些材料算作学术贡献。
