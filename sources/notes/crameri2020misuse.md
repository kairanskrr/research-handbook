# Crameri, Shephard & Heron 2020：The misuse of colour in science communication

- 资料：`crameri2020misuse`，[原文（Nature Communications，开放获取）](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-19160-7)，doi:10.1038/s41467-020-19160-7
- 版本：Nature Communications 11:5444（2020），CC BY 4.0 许可。作者是地球科学方向的学者，讨论各学科科学图件的配色。位置写段落。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："colour maps that visually distort data through uneven colour gradients or are unreadable to those with colour-vision deficiency remain prevalent in science. These include, but are not limited to, rainbow-like and red–green colour maps."
- 要点：颜色梯度不均匀、让色觉障碍者无法读懂的配色在科学界仍很普遍，包括彩虹色和红绿配色。

### E2

- 位置：引言第 2 段
- 原文："when colours are used incorrectly, this can lead to the effective manipulation of data (e.g., by highlighting some data over others), the oversight of the needs of those with colour vision deficiencies, and the removal of meaning when printed in black and white"
- 要点：用错颜色等于在操纵数据（突出一部分数据）、忽略色觉障碍读者，黑白打印时还会丢失含义。

### E3

- 位置：引言第 5 段
- 原文："In rainbow colour maps, the yellow is the brightest colour and attracts the eye the most" / "Hence, such an arrangement of colours can unfairly highlight a particular section of the parameter space while obscuring other parts"
- 要点：彩虹色中黄色最亮、最抓眼，但它既不在两端也不在中间，会不公平地突出一段数值、掩盖其他部分。

### E4

- 位置：引言（色觉障碍一段）
- 原文："Colour maps that include both red and green colours with similar lightness cannot be read by a large fraction of the readership" / "The general estimate is that worldwide 0.5% of women and 8% of men are subject to a colour-vision deficiency"
- 要点：亮度相近的红绿配色，很大一部分读者读不出来；通常估计全世界 0.5% 的女性和 8% 的男性有色觉障碍。

### E5

- 位置：引言（"For the casual reader" 一段）
- 原文："Colour map choice is often made passively and is not subject to the same scrutiny as other data methodologies."
- 要点：配色常常是被动选的（用软件默认），不像其他数据方法那样受到审视。

### E6

- 位置：Box 2（科学配色的来源）
- 原文："With the main aim being cartography, the online tool offers sequential, diverging and categorical (or in other words, qualitative) colour maps"
- 要点：ColorBrewer 是面向制图的免费配色工具，提供顺序、发散、类别三类配色。

### E7

- 位置：正文"Scientific data should be displayed..." 一段
- 原文："A perceptually uniform colour map weights the same data variation equally all across the dataspace, while other colour maps (such as rainbow) interpret some small data variation to be more important than others"
- 要点：感知均匀的配色让同样的数据差异在整个数值范围内看起来一样大；彩虹色则会放大某些小差异。
