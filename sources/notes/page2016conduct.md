# Page 等人 2016：Conduct, Oversight, and Ethical Considerations of Clinical Trials in Companion Animals with Cancer

- 资料：`page2016conduct`，[PMC 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4913608/)
- 版本：Journal of Veterinary Internal Medicine 30(2):527–535，2016 年（DOI 10.1111/jvim.13916），开放获取（CC BY-NC）。2014 年 11 月在美国兽医协会（AVMA）召开的研讨会的共识报告，针对伴侣动物肿瘤临床试验。位置写小标题或表格。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："The ethical conduct and oversight of clinical research involving client‐owned companion animals require the proper management of concerns and expectations that may differ from both laboratory animal research oversight and clinical trial conduct in humans."
- 要点：用客户自有伴侣动物做临床研究，伦理问题既不同于实验动物研究，也不同于人体临床试验。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Although clear and established policy guidelines for the use and care of laboratory animals in research exist, many of these guidelines are limited in their direct applicability to client‐owned animals included in clinical trials."
- 要点：实验动物的规范很成熟，但很多不能直接套用到参加临床试验的客户自有动物身上。

### E3

- 位置：Table 1，Guiding ethical principles
- 原文："The consent process must be honest, thorough, and well‐communicated and the pet owner must have adequate time to consider participation without real or perceived coercion or conflict of interest from any member of the investigative team."
- 要点：知情同意过程必须诚实、完整、沟通清楚；主人要有充分时间考虑，不受研究团队任何人实际或可感知的强迫或利益冲突影响。

### E4

- 位置：Consenting Process – Best Practices And Ethics Recommendations
- 原文："Full disclosure of the study purpose, associated risks and benefits, study design and interventions required, funding source for the study, incentives to the owner, and conflict of interest (COI) statements from the study staff and investigators are required elements to address during the consent process. The recommended best practice for the consenting process is that it be managed by a neutral individual (nurse, nonsponsor supported veterinarian) with a witness present."
- 要点：同意过程中必须讲清：研究目的、风险和获益、设计和要做的干预、资金来源、给主人的补贴、研究人员的利益冲突。推荐由中立人员（护士、不受资助方支持的兽医）主持，并有见证人在场。

### E5

- 位置：Consenting Process – Best Practices And Ethics Recommendations
- 原文："All financial subsidies or incentives should be carefully and fully described. A description of what is not subsidized is equally important."
- 要点：所有经济补贴都要讲清楚；哪些不补贴，同样重要。

### E6

- 位置：Study Design – Best Practices and Ethics Recommendations
- 原文："A potential ethical conflict occurs when an animal is placed at risk, real or perceived, in return for a therapeutic incentive. The owner is placed in a financially vulnerable position when study completion is required before receiving reimbursement for veterinary care."
- 要点：用治疗补贴换取让动物承担风险，是潜在的伦理冲突；要求完成研究才报销诊疗费用，会让主人在经济上处于弱势。

### E7

- 位置：Study Design – Best Practices and Ethics Recommendations
- 原文："All patients enrolled in clinical trials, whether studied with or without a control group, must receive best supportive care."
- 要点：所有入组的动物，不论有无对照组，都必须得到最佳支持治疗（缓解症状、维持生活质量的治疗）。

### E8

- 位置：Study Design – Best Practices and Ethics Recommendations（终点）
- 原文："In addition, euthanasia is a well‐known confounder for determination of overall survival in animal patients enrolled in clinical trials." / "Elective euthanasia, when a patient in a self‐supportive, clinically stable status is euthanized at the owner's request, must be considered carefully when examining outcome data from clinical trials."
- 要点：在动物临床试验里，安乐死是判断总生存期时众所周知的混杂因素；病情稳定时应主人要求实施的"选择性安乐死"，分析结局数据时必须仔细考虑。

### E9

- 位置：Study Design – Best Practices and Ethics Recommendations
- 原文："Commercially sponsored trials, donor‐sponsored trials, and hospital‐subsidized trials may not receive a scientific peer review separate from an evaluation by the Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC), the mandate of which does not always include such scientific merit review."
- 要点：企业、捐赠者或医院资助的试验，可能只经过动物照料与使用委员会（IACUC）的审查，而 IACUC 的职责不一定包括科学价值评审。

### E10

- 位置：Table 1，Guiding ethical principles
- 原文："Results from all trials should be published to ensure reproducibility and avoid redundancy."
- 要点：所有试验的结果都应发表，以保证可复现、避免重复。

### E11

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Clinical trials involving companion animals with naturally occurring cancers have been conducted for over 50 years with the intent of improving both animal and human health."
- 要点：用自然发生癌症的伴侣动物做临床试验已有 50 多年，目的是同时改善动物和人的健康。

### E12

- 位置：Consenting Process – Best Practices And Ethics Recommendations
- 原文："The informed consent document should describe the aspects of the study in simple, concise, and easy‐to‐understand language."
- 要点：知情同意书要用简单、简洁、易懂的语言描述研究。

### E13

- 位置：Clinical Trial Approval Policies – Best Practice Recommendations
- 原文："Several institutions have adopted the model of a clinical review board (CRB) to evaluate the merit, feasibility, and compliance with ethical standards for clinical trials in client‐owned animals."
- 要点：一些机构设立了临床审查委员会，评估客户自有动物临床试验的价值、可行性和伦理合规。

### E14

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："A workshop bringing together a diverse group of stakeholders was convened at the American Veterinary Medical Association (AVMA) headquarters in Washington, DC in November 2014 to confirm guiding ethical principles (Table 1) and develop ‘Best Practice Recommendations’ for clinical trial conduct and oversight in veterinary oncology."
- 要点：2014 年 11 月，一个由多方人士参加的研讨会在美国兽医协会（AVMA）华盛顿总部召开，确认伦理原则并制定兽医肿瘤临床试验的"最佳实践建议"。
