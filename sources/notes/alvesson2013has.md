# Alvesson & Sandberg 2013：Has Management Studies Lost Its Way? Ideas for More Imaginative and Innovative Research

- 资料：`alvesson2013has`，[City St George's 大学开放仓库（City Research Online）全文 PDF](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/17992/1/Alvesson_et_al-2013-Journal_of_Management_Studies.pdf)（[仓库记录页](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/17992/)，[出版社页面](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-6486.2012.01070.x)）
- 版本：Journal of Management Studies 50(1)，2013 年 1 月，第 128–152 页（2012 年在线发表）。读的是作者所在机构仓库存放的出版社正式版，仓库标注为 Creative Commons Attribution（CC BY）许可，OpenAlex 也记录出版社版为 CC BY。位置写期刊页码。

> 为什么用这篇：任务原定的 Sandberg & Alvesson 2011（Organization 18(1)）和 Alvesson & Sandberg 2011（Academy of Management Review 36(2)）都查不到合法公开全文：Lund 大学研究门户和 LUP 只有题录和摘要、没有文件；Unpaywall 和 OpenAlex 都标为非开放；网上能搜到的 PDF 在课程网站和第三方网站上，来源不明，没有使用。这篇是同两位作者的开放获取论文，直接定义了"找空白"（gap-spotting），并给出问题化（problematization）方法的六步原则，内容与 2011 两篇一致，文中也引用了那两篇。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract，第 128 页
- 原文："We contend that a primary reason behind this paradoxical shortage is the near total dominance of incremental gap-spotting research in management. This domination is even more paradoxical as it is well known that gap-spotting rarely leads to influential theories."
- 要点：作者认为，管理学高影响力研究稀缺，主要原因是渐进式的"找空白"研究几乎一统天下，而找空白很少产生有影响力的理论。

### E2

- 位置：Gap-Spotting: The Key Problem to the Paradoxical Shortage of Interesting Studies，第 131 页
- 原文："It is by identifying or constructing gaps in existing literature that most management researchers formulate their research questions and develop their theories." / "Such gap-spotting research means that the assumptions underlying existing literature for the most part remain unchallenged in the formulation of research questions."
- 要点：找空白，就是通过找出或构造已有文献中的空白来提出研究问题；这样做时，已有文献背后的假设基本没有受到挑战。

### E3

- 位置：Gap-Spotting: The Key Problem to the Paradoxical Shortage of Interesting Studies，第 131 页
- 原文："In gap-spotting, researchers refer positively or mildly critically to earlier studies with the purpose of `extend(ing) this literature' (Westphal and Khanna, 2003, p. 363), to `address this gap in the literature' (Musson and Tietze, 2004, p. 1301), to `fill this gap'"
- 要点：找空白的典型说法是"扩展这一文献""回应文献中的这一空白""填补这一空白"，对前人研究是正面或温和批评的态度。

### E4

- 位置：Gap-Spotting: The Key Problem to the Paradoxical Shortage of Interesting Studies，第 131–132 页
- 原文："Neither is it simply about finding a gap in a given body of literature. Instead, it often involves constructing gaps by bringing together different bodies of literature in complex and sometimes creative ways"
- 要点：找空白不只是在一批文献里发现空白，常常是把不同的文献放在一起，以复杂、有时颇有创意的方式"构造"出空白。

### E5

- 位置：Gap-Spotting: The Key Problem to the Paradoxical Shortage of Interesting Studies，第 132 页
- 原文："Nor do we deny the possibility that gap-spotting research can lead to important contributions. However, because gap-spotting research does not deliberately and ambitiously question the assumptions underlying established literature, it rarely leads to the development of new high-ranking theories."
- 要点：作者不否认找空白也能有重要贡献；但因为它不刻意质疑已有文献的假设，很少产生新的重要理论。

### E6

- 位置：What Makes a Theory Interesting and Influential?，第 131 页
- 原文："In other words, if a theory doesn't challenge some of an audience's assumptions significantly, it is unlikely to receive attention and become influential – even if it has been extremely rigorously developed."
- 要点：一个理论如果没有明显挑战读者原有的某些假设，即使做得极其严谨，也不太可能受到关注、产生影响。

### E7

- 位置：Gap-Spotting: The Key Problem to the Paradoxical Shortage of Interesting Studies，第 131 页
- 原文："It is important to note also that assumption-challenging research needs to be connected to established literature in order to be seen as meaningful"
- 要点：挑战假设的研究也必须和已有文献联系起来，别人才会觉得它有意义。

### E8

- 位置：A Need to Consider Alternative Methodologies for Theory Development，第 144–145 页
- 原文："An alternative to this is to formulate research questions by problematizing some dominant assumptions in existing research (Davis, 1971)." / "`Real' problematization also involves questioning the assumptions underlying one's own meta-theoretical position."
- 要点：问题化是找空白之外的另一条路：通过质疑已有研究中占主导的假设来提出研究问题；真正的问题化还要质疑自己所持立场背后的假设。

### E9

- 位置：A Need to Consider Alternative Methodologies for Theory Development，第 145 页
- 原文："This ready-made or pseudo `problematization' only reproduces the assumptions of the framework inspiring the researcher and is unlikely to lead to particularly novel and interesting ideas"
- 要点：只是拿自己偏爱的现成理论立场去批评别人，是"伪问题化"：它只是在重复自己那套框架的假设，不太可能带来新颖有趣的想法。

### E10

- 位置：A Need to Consider Alternative Methodologies for Theory Development，第 145 页
- 原文："To be able to problematize assumptions through such a dialectical interrogation, the following methodological principles are central: (1) to identify a domain of literature; (2) to identify and articulate assumptions underlying this domain; (3) to evaluate them; (4) to develop an alternative assumption ground; (5) to consider it in relation to its audience; and (6) to evaluate the alternative assumption ground."
- 要点：问题化的六步：（1）确定一个文献领域；（2）找出并写明这个领域背后的假设；（3）评估这些假设；（4）提出另一套替代假设；（5）结合读者来考虑这套替代假设；（6）评估这套替代假设。

### E11

- 位置：A Need to Consider Alternative Methodologies for Theory Development，第 145 页
- 原文："Successful problematization is of course also very much a matter of creativity, intuition, reading inspiring texts that offer critical insights (but without being accepted as a new fixed framework), talking to other people, having specific experiences, or making observations that may trigger new thinking etc. Although we have no strong belief in rational, logical, or mechanistic procedures for problematization, we do think some structure can be helpful."
- 要点：作者承认问题化很依赖创造力、直觉、阅读、与人交谈和个人经历，六步不是机械程序，只是有帮助的一点结构。

### E12

- 位置：A Need to Consider Alternative Methodologies for Theory Development，第 144 页
- 原文："There are no guarantees, of course, and most research is almost per definition not capable of accomplishing something that is seen as really interesting, as this is something that clearly stands above the average or normal."
- 要点：这些方法没有保证；按定义，大多数研究都做不到真正"有趣"，因为有趣意味着明显高于一般水平。

### E13

- 位置：A Need to Consider Alternative Methodologies for Theory Development，第 146 页
- 原文："It is the unanticipated and the unexpected – the things that puzzle the researcher – that are of particular interest in the encounter." / "The ideal research process then includes two key elements: (a) to create a mystery, and (b) to solve it"
- 要点：另一条挑战假设的路是利用经验材料：特别关注让研究者困惑的意外之处，理想过程是先"制造一个谜"，再"解开它"。

### E14

- 位置：Notes，注释 3
- 原文："It is of course necessary to have a reasonable knowledge of the area one is working within and do some positioning in relation to at least significant existing studies."
- 要点：即使走问题化路线，也要对自己所在领域有合理了解，并至少和重要的已有研究做一些定位比较。
