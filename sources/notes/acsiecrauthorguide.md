# 美国化学会：Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research 作者指南

- 资料：`acsiecrauthorguide`，[原文](https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=iecred)
- 版本：美国化学会（American Chemical Society，ACS）期刊《Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research》（下称 I&EC Research）的作者指南网页，页面标注 "Last updated: October 05, 2026"，2026-10-06 读取。只代表这一份期刊的要求。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Scope of the Journal
- 原文："papers may deal with process design and development (for example, synthesis and design methods, systems analysis, process control, schemes for data correlation, modeling and scale-up procedures, etc.)"
- 要点：这份期刊接收过程设计与开发方面的论文，包括建模和放大方法等。

### E2

- 位置：Manuscript Types › Topical Categories
- 原文："Kinetics, Catalysis, and Reaction Engineering; Materials and Interfaces; Process Systems Engineering; Separations; Thermodynamics, Transport, and Fluid Mechanics"
- 要点：期刊按主题分栏目，包括动力学、催化与反应工程，材料与界面，过程系统工程，分离，热力学、传递与流体力学等。

### E3

- 位置：Scope of the Journal
- 原文："Papers that simply report data or calculations will be declined for publication."
- 要点：只报告数据或计算结果的论文会被拒。

### E4

- 位置：Scope of the Journal
- 原文："Manuscripts most likely to be declined after Editorial review are those with English deficiencies, those lacking conclusions that are clearly significant and novel, and those with contributions that are judged unlikely to attract the interest of a general applied chemistry or chemical engineering readership."
- 要点：最可能在编辑初审就被拒的稿件：英语有问题、结论缺乏明显的重要性和新意、读者面太窄。

### E5

- 位置：Prior Publication Policy
- 原文："Posting of the original submitted manuscript to a pre-print server is acceptable. Please note the use of a pre-print server explicitly in the cover letter"
- 要点：可以把投稿原稿放到预印本服务器上，但要在投稿信里明确说明。

### E6

- 位置：Prior Publication Policy
- 原文："Publishing material that has been used in reports to research sponsors is acceptable provided one or more of the authors holds the copyright."
- 要点：用在给资助方的报告里的材料可以发表，前提是至少一位作者拥有版权。

### E7

- 位置：Research Data Policy
- 原文："All ACS journals strongly encourage authors to make the research data underlying their articles publicly available at the time of publication."
- 要点：美国化学会所有期刊都强烈鼓励作者在论文发表时公开支撑论文的研究数据。

### E8

- 位置：Data Requirements
- 原文："Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research does not have specific data requirements."
- 要点：I&EC Research 没有专门的数据要求。

### E9

- 位置：Safety Considerations
- 原文："Authors must emphasize any unexpected, new, and/or significant hazards or risks associated with the reported work. This information should be in the Experimental Section of a full article and included in the main text of a communication or letter."
- 要点：作者必须突出写明工作中任何意外的、新的或重大的危害和风险；完整论文写在实验部分。

### E10

- 位置：Patent Activities and Intellectual Property
- 原文："Authors are responsible for ensuring that all patent activities and intellectual property issues are satisfactorily resolved prior to first publication (ASAP or in issue). Acceptance and publication will not be delayed for pending or unresolved issues of this nature."
- 要点：作者要在首次发表前处理好专利和知识产权问题；期刊不会因为这类问题推迟录用和发表。

### E11

- 位置：Conflict of Interest Disclosure
- 原文："the Corresponding Author must provide a statement on behalf of all authors of the manuscript, describing all potential sources of bias, including affiliations, funding sources, and financial or management relationships, that may constitute conflicts of interest."
- 要点：通讯作者要代表全体作者声明所有可能的偏倚来源，包括单位、资助来源以及经济或管理关系。
