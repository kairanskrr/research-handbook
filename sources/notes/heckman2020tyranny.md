# Heckman & Moktan 2020：Publishing and Promotion in Economics: The Tyranny of the Top Five

- 资料：`heckman2020tyranny`，[IZA 讨论稿 PDF](https://docs.iza.org/dp11868.pdf)；正式版发表于 Journal of Economic Literature 58(2)
- 版本：IZA Discussion Paper No. 11868（2018 年 10 月）。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Analyzing the job histories of tenure-track economists hired by the top 35 U.S. economics departments, we find that T5 publications have a powerful influence on tenure decisions and rates of transition to tenure."
- 要点：分析美国前 35 名经济系的预聘制（tenure-track）教师的履历，发现五大期刊发表对能否获得终身教职影响很大。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Pursuit of T5 publications has become the obsession of the next generation of economists. However, the T5 screen is far from reliable. A substantial share of influential publications appear in non-T5 outlets. Reliance on the T5 to screen talent incentivizes careerism over creativity."
- 要点：作者认为，追求五大期刊已成为年轻经济学家的执念；但这道筛子远不可靠，很多有影响的论文发表在五大之外；靠它选人，会鼓励功利而不是创造。

### E3

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："At the same time, the number of submissions to the T5 and the length of submitted papers have increased5 with concomitant growth in rejection rates and delays in the refereeing process."
- 要点：五大期刊的投稿数量和论文篇幅都在增加，随之而来的是拒稿率上升、审稿拖延。

### E4

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："Economists at highly ranked departments with established reputations are increasingly not publishing in T5 or field journals8 and more often post papers online in influential working paper series, which are highly cited, but not counted as T5s."
- 要点：名校的知名经济学家越来越多地不投期刊，而是把论文发在有影响力的工作论文系列上；这些论文引用很高，但不算五大发表。
