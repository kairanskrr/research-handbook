# NC3Rs：The 3Rs

- 资料：`nc3rsthe3rs`，[原文](https://nc3rs.org.uk/who-we-are/3rs)
- 版本：英国国家动物研究替代、优化与减少中心（National Centre for the Replacement, Refinement and Reduction of Animals in Research，NC3Rs）网页，2026-10-06 读取。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：What are the 3Rs?
- 原文："The principles of the 3Rs (Replacement, Reduction and Refinement) were developed over 50 years ago providing a framework for performing more humane animal research. Since then they have been embedded in national and international legislation and regulations on the use of animals in scientific procedures"
- 要点：3R 原则（替代、减少、优化）提出于 50 多年前，是让动物研究更人道的框架，此后被写进各国和国际上关于实验动物使用的法律法规。

### E2

- 位置：Definitions of the 3Rs（基本定义）
- 原文："Avoiding or replacing the use of animals in areas where they otherwise would have been used." / "Minimising the number of animals used consistent with scientific aims." / "Minimising the pain, suffering, distress or lasting harm that research animals might experience."
- 要点：替代：在本来要用动物的地方，避免或换掉动物；减少：在满足科学目的的前提下尽量少用动物；优化：尽量减轻实验动物可能经历的疼痛、痛苦、不适或持久伤害。

### E3

- 位置：Definitions of the 3Rs（"减少"的更新定义）
- 原文："Appropriately designed and analysed animal experiments that are robust and reproducible, and add to the knowledge base."
- 要点：NC3Rs 更新后的"减少"定义：动物实验设计和分析得当、结果稳健可复现，并真正增加知识。

### E4

- 位置：Replacement
- 原文："Full replacement refers to methods that replace the use of animals for research and testing purposes. It includes the use of human volunteers, tissues and cells, mathematical and computer models, and established cell lines"
- 要点：完全替代的方法包括：人类志愿者、组织和细胞、数学和计算机模型、已建立的细胞系。
