# Boote & Jones 2013：Protocols for AgMIP Crop Model Improvement Teams

- 资料：`boote2013protocols`，[原文（AgMIP 官网 PDF）](https://agmip.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Protocols-for-AgMIP-Crop-Model-Improvement-Teams.pdf)
- 版本：2013-05-14，5 页，国际农业模型比对与改进项目（AgMIP）在官网"Protocols and Reports"页公开，2026-10-08 读取。是作物模型改进团队的组织建议，不是期刊论文。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Framework for Proposed Crop Model Improvement Activities
- 原文："In all cases, it is important to also collect good weather data, soil characteristics, crop and soil measurements in those various experiments designed to collect data to improve crop models or test new production technologies."
- 要点：为改进作物模型或检验新生产技术而设计的试验，都要同时收集好的气象数据、土壤特性和作物、土壤测量数据。

### E2

- 位置：Framework for Proposed Crop Model Improvement Activities
- 原文："Collected data needs to be stored in a way that it is available for the future community of scientists who may see a future need."
- 要点：收集的数据要以将来的研究者也能取用的方式保存。
