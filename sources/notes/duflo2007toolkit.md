# Duflo, Glennerster & Kremer 2007：Using Randomization in Development Economics Research: A Toolkit

- 资料：`duflo2007toolkit`，[原文 PDF（NBER）](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/t0333/t0333.pdf)
- 版本：NBER Technical Working Paper 333（2006 年 12 月），后收入 Handbook of Development Economics 第 4 卷（2007）。NBER 网站免费提供工作论文 PDF。位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§3 导言
- 原文："most of the projects involving randomization differ from this model in several important ways. They are in general conducted with implementation partners (governments, NGOs, or private companies) who are implementing real-world programs and are interested in finding out whether they work or how to improve them."
- 要点：和实验室条件下的临床试验不同，发展经济学的随机研究大多与实施伙伴（政府、非政府组织、企业）合作，评估真实运行的项目。

### E2

- 位置：§3.1 Partners
- 原文："introducing randomization in real-world programs almost always requires working with a partner who is in charge of actually implementing the program."
- 要点：在真实项目里引入随机化，几乎总要和真正负责实施项目的伙伴合作。

### E3

- 位置：§3.1 Partners
- 原文："When possible, working with governments offers several advantages. First, it can allow for much wider geographic scope. Second, the results may be more likely to feed into the policy process." / "However, NGOs and firms offer a much more flexible environment, where it can be easier for the researchers to monitor the implementation of the research design."
- 要点：和政府合作覆盖面更广、结果更容易进入政策；非政府组织和企业更灵活，研究者更容易监督设计的执行。

### E4

- 位置：§3.3.1 Oversubscription Method
- 原文："A natural opportunity for introducing randomization occurs when there are limited resources or implementation capacity and demand for a program or service exceeds supply. In this case, a natural and fair way to ration resources is to select those who will receive the program by lottery among eligible candidates."
- 要点：资源有限、申请人多于名额时，在合格者中抽签既自然又公平，也就引入了随机化。

### E5

- 位置：§3.3.2 Randomized Order of Phase-In
- 原文："Randomizing the order of phase-in can allow evaluation of program effects in contexts where it is not acceptable for some groups or individuals to receive no support."
- 要点：随机决定分批推行的先后，可以在"不能让任何人一直得不到帮助"的场合做评估。

### E6

- 位置：§3.3.2 Randomized Order of Phase-In
- 原文："When planning a phase-in design, the time between phases should be sufficient to encompass any treatment lag." / "Randomized phase-in becomes problematic when the comparison group is affected by the expectation of future treatment."
- 要点：分批推行时，两批之间的间隔要长于效果显现所需的时间；对照组如果因为预期以后会轮到自己而改变行为，设计就有问题。

### E7

- 位置：§3.3.3 Within-Group Randomization
- 原文："The drawback of such designs is that they increase the likelihood that the comparison group is contaminated."
- 要点：在同一单位内部随机（如同一学校不同年级），对照组更容易被"污染"。

### E8

- 位置：§3.3.4 Encouragement Designs
- 原文："Rather than randomize over the treatment itself, researchers randomly assign subjects an encouragement to receive the treatment."
- 要点：鼓励设计不随机分配干预本身，而是随机分配"鼓励接受干预"。

### E9

- 位置：§4.2 Grouped Errors
- 原文："for a given sample size, an increase in the number of individuals sampled per cluster increases the precision much less than increasing the number of clusters being randomized."
- 要点：总样本量一定时，增加每个群里的人数，对精度的提升远不如增加随机分配的群的个数。

### E10

- 位置：§4.6 Power calculations in practice
- 原文："despite all the precision of these formulas, power calculations involve substantial guess work in practice." / "The best way to obtain guesses about these parameters is in general to use previously collected data, ideally from the same country or region."
- 要点：功效计算公式虽精确，实际上靠大量估计；估计参数最好用已有数据，最好来自同一国家或地区。

### E11

- 位置：§4.6 Power calculations in practice
- 原文："As a rule of thumb for a policy intervention evaluation, this should be the smallest effect size that is large enough such that the intervention would be cost effective if it were to be scaled up."
- 要点：评估政策干预时，想检测的效应大小可以设为"推广后仍然划算"的最小效应。

### E12

- 位置：§4.6 Power calculations in practice
- 原文："Some argue that since the study has little chance to deliver conclusive results, it is not worthwhile to conduct it." / "The greatest incremental precision on a given question comes from the first few observations. Moreover, results from several low power studies can be combined in a meta-analysis which will have more power."
- 要点：低功效研究值不值得做有争议：有人认为得不出结论就不值得做；作者指出最初几项研究带来的精度增益最大，几项低功效研究也可以合并做元分析。

### E13

- 位置：§4.4 Control Variables
- 原文："Note that controlling for covariates affected by the treatment would bias the estimate of the treatment effect by capturing part of its impact." / "Note that this choice must in principle be specified in advance to avoid the risk of specification searching."
- 要点：控制受干预影响的变量会使效应估计有偏；控制哪些变量原则上要事先写定，以免设定搜索。

### E14

- 位置：§4.5 Stratification
- 原文："The randomization ensures that treatment and control groups will be similar in expectation. But stratification is used to ensure that along important observable dimensions this is also true in practice in the sample."
- 要点：随机化保证两组"平均来说"相似；分层随机保证在重要的可观测特征上，这次抽到的样本也确实相似。

### E15

- 位置：§5.1 Level of Randomization
- 原文："First, as discussed in section 4.2, the larger the groups that are randomized, the larger the total sample size needed to achieve a given power." / "Second, however, spillovers from treatment to comparison groups can bias the estimation of treatment effects. In such case, the randomization should occur at a level that captures these effects."
- 要点：随机化的单位越大，需要的总样本越多；但如果处理组会影响对照组，随机化应放在能把这种影响包住的层级上。

### E16

- 位置：§5.1 Level of Randomization
- 原文："It is much easier for a research team to ensure that villages are being treated according to an initial random assignment than to monitor individuals."
- 要点：按村随机比按个人随机更容易保证执行：研究团队监督村子是否按分组接受干预，比监督每个人容易得多。

### E17

- 位置：§5.3.1 Conducting Baseline Surveys
- 原文："In principle, randomization renders baseline surveys unnecessary, since it ensures the treatment and comparison groups are similar in expectation." / "When the intervention is expensive and data collection is relatively cheap, conducting a baseline will save money. When the intervention is cheap but data collection is expensive, it may be more cost effective to run a larger experiment without conducting a baseline."
- 要点：原则上随机化使基线调查不是必需的；干预贵、调查便宜时做基线省钱，干预便宜、调查贵时，不做基线、扩大样本可能更划算。

### E18

- 位置：§5.3.1 Conducting Baseline Surveys
- 原文："Second, a baseline survey provides an opportunity to check that the randomization was conducted appropriately. Third, collecting baseline data offers an opportunity to test and refine data collection procedures."
- 要点：基线调查还能检查随机化是否做对，并试用、改进数据收集流程。

### E19

- 位置：§5.3.1 Conducting Baseline Surveys
- 原文："The alternative strategy of collecting "pre-intervention data" retrospectively in the postsurvey will usually be unacceptable, because even if the program does not affect those variables it may well affect recall of those variables."
- 要点：在后期调查中回忆式补收"干预前数据"通常不可接受：项目可能影响人们对这些变量的回忆。

### E20

- 位置：§5.3.2 Using Administrative Data
- 原文："However, this could introduce biases as a difference in measured outcomes between treatment and comparison areas could reflect different data collection methodologies."
- 要点：处理组用项目数据、对照组另做调查，两组结果的差异可能只是测量方法不同造成的。

### E21

- 位置：§6.2 Partial Compliance
- 原文："One must compare all those initially allocated to the treatment group to all those initially randomized to the comparison group, whatever their actual behavior and their actual treatment status. The analysis cannot exclude subjects or cut the sample according to behavior that may have been affected by the random assignment."
- 要点：必须按最初的随机分组比较两组全体，不管实际是否接受了干预；不能按可能受分组影响的行为剔除或切分样本。

### E22

- 位置：§6.2.1 From Intention To Treat to Average Treatment Effects
- 原文："In many contexts, the intention-to-treat estimate is actually a parameter of interest."
- 要点：很多情况下，意向处理效应本身就是想要的量（例如政策推广后总有人不接受）。

### E23

- 位置：§6.2.1 From Intention To Treat to Average Treatment Effects
- 原文："Under the monotonicity and the independence assumptions, the Wald estimator gives us the effect of the treatment on those whose treatment status was affected by the instrument, which is known as the local average treatment effect (LATE)"
- 要点：在单调性和独立性假设下，用随机分组作工具变量得到的是"因分组而改变是否接受干预的人"的效应，即局部平均处理效应。

### E24

- 位置：§6.2.1 From Intention To Treat to Average Treatment Effects
- 原文："In this case, the Wald estimate is the effect of the treatment on the treated."
- 要点：对照组无人接受干预时，这一估计就是"处理组中实际接受者的处理效应"。

### E25

- 位置：§6.2.2 When is IV Not Appropriate
- 原文："This, however, is inappropriate, because children in treatment schools who were not sent to the balsakhi may have benefited from the fact that their class is now smaller for most of the day, and their weakest peers have left the classrooms."
- 要点：处理组里没直接接受辅导的孩子也可能受益（班级变小，最弱的同学被抽走），这时用接受率去除意向处理效应来推算是不恰当的。

### E26

- 位置：§6.3 Externalities
- 原文："If they are local, randomization at the group level can allow for estimation of the total program effect on the group. If externalities do not operate across groups, group-level randomization is sufficient to identify overall treatment effects. It cannot, however, decompose the direct and spillover effects."
- 要点：溢出是局部的、不跨群时，按群随机能估计项目的总效应，但分不开直接效应和溢出效应。

### E27

- 位置：§6.3 Externalities
- 原文："A first technique is to purposefully vary the level of exposure to a treatment within a group."
- 要点：要测溢出，一种做法是有意改变群内接受干预的比例。

### E28

- 位置：§6.4 Attrition
- 原文："Random attrition will only reduce a study's statistical power; however, attrition that is correlated with the treatment being evaluated may bias estimates."
- 要点：随机的样本流失只降低功效；和干预相关的流失会使估计有偏。

### E29

- 位置：§6.4 Attrition
- 原文："Even if attrition rates are similar in treatment and comparison groups, it remains possible that the attritors were selected differently in the treatment and comparison groups."
- 要点：即使两组流失率相近，两组流失的人也可能是不同类型的人。

### E30

- 位置：§6.4 Attrition
- 原文："This makes attrition a very difficult problem to solve ex post and that implies managing attrition during the data collection process is essential." / "it is important to collect good information in the baseline on how to find them later on (for example the names of neighbors and relatives that can be interviewed if the respondent cannot be found)."
- 要点：样本流失事后很难补救，必须在收数据时就管住；基线时就要收集日后找到人的信息（如邻居和亲属的名字）。

### E31

- 位置：§6.4 Attrition
- 原文："A first step in the analysis of an evaluation must always be to report attrition levels in the treatment and comparison groups and to compare attritors with non-attritors using baseline data"
- 要点：分析的第一步，必须报告两组各自的流失率，并用基线数据比较流失者和留下的人。

### E32

- 位置：§7.1 Grouped Data
- 原文："show the cluster-correlated Huber-White estimator performs poorly when the number of clusters is small (less than 50), leading to over-rejection of the null hypothesis of no effect." / "When the number of clusters is small, hypothesis tests can also be generated using randomization inference"
- 要点：作者引用的模拟研究显示，群少于 50 个时，常用的聚类稳健标准误表现差，容易错误地拒绝"没有效应"；群少时可以用随机化推断做检验。

### E33

- 位置：§7.2 Multiple Outcomes
- 原文："all the variables collected in a prospective evaluation were, presumably, collected because they were considered potential outcome variables. All should therefore be reported whether they are significant or not."
- 要点：前瞻性评估中收集的变量都是当作可能的结果变量收集的，因此无论显著与否都应报告。

### E34

- 位置：§7.3 Subgroups
- 原文："Ideally, researchers should know when designing the evaluation protocol, either through a priori reasoning or through knowledge gained from other studies, which of the possible subgroups should be investigated separately and make this decision clear ex ante."
- 要点：理想情况下，设计方案时就依据理论或已有研究确定要单独分析哪些子群体，并事先写明。
