# Mayring 2000：Qualitative Content Analysis

- 资料：`mayring2000qualitative`，[原文（FQS 开放获取 HTML）](https://www.qualitative-research.net/index.php/fqs/article/view/1089/2385)
- 版本：Forum Qualitative Sozialforschung / Forum: Qualitative Social Research 第 1 卷第 2 期，Art. 20，2000 年 6 月（页面注明 2009 年 2 月修订）；CC BY 4.0。全文按段落编号 [1]–[28]，位置写段落号。出自德国的心理学与社会科学定性研究传统。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction，[2]
- 原文："The main idea of the procedure of analysis is thereby, to preserve the advantages of quantitative content analysis as developed within communication science and to transfer and further develop them to qualitative-interpretative steps of analysis."
- 要点：定性内容分析的出发点，是保留传播学里定量内容分析的长处，再把它们用到定性、诠释性的分析步骤上。

### E2

- 位置：§1 Introduction，[4]
- 原文："The object of (qualitative) content analysis can be all sort of recorded communication (transcripts of interviews, discourses, protocols of observations, video tapes, documents ...)."
- 要点：内容分析的对象可以是任何被记录下来的交流：访谈转录稿、话语、观察记录、录像、文件等。

### E3

- 位置：§1 Introduction，[5]
- 原文："Qualitative content analysis defines itself within this framework as an approach of empirical, methodological controlled analysis of texts within their context of communication, following content analytical rules and step by step models, without rash quantification."
- 要点：定性内容分析是在文本的交流情境中、按规则和分步模型做的、受方法控制的实证分析，不急于量化。

### E4

- 位置：§3 Basic Ideas of Content Analysis，[7]
- 原文："Rules of analysis: The material is to be analyzed step by step, following rules of procedure, devising the material into content analytical units." / "Categories in the center of analysis: The aspects of text interpretation, following the research questions, are putted into categories, which were carefully founded and revised within the process of analysis (feedback loops)."
- 要点：材料要按程序规则一步步分析，并切分成分析单元；分析的中心是类别，类别依研究问题而定，在分析中通过反馈循环反复修订。

### E5

- 位置：§3 Basic Ideas of Content Analysis，[7]
- 原文："For estimating the inter-coder reliability we use in qualitative content analysis (in contrary to quantitative content analysis) only trained members of the project team and we reduce the standard of coder agreement (COHENS Kappa over .7 would be sufficient)."
- 要点：Mayring 的定性内容分析只用受过训练的课题组成员估计编码者间信度，并降低标准（Cohen's Kappa 0.7 以上即可），这与定量内容分析不同。

### E6

- 位置：§4 Procedures，[8]
- 原文："We developed a number of procedures of qualitative content analysis (cf. MAYRING 2000) amongst which two approaches are central: inductive category development and deductive category application."
- 要点：定性内容分析的两个核心做法是：归纳地发展类别，演绎地应用类别。

### E7

- 位置：§4.1 Inductive category development，[9]
- 原文："Classical quantitative content analysis has few answers to the question from where the categories come, how the system of categories is developed"
- 要点：经典的定量内容分析很少回答"类别从哪里来"。

### E8

- 位置：§4.1 Inductive category development，[12]
- 原文："The main idea of the procedure is, to formulate a criterion of definition, derived from theoretical background and research question, which determines the aspects of the textual material taken into account. Following this criterion the material is worked through and categories are tentative and step by step deduced. Within a feedback loop those categories are revised, eventually reduced to main categories and checked in respect to their reliability. If the research question suggests quantitative aspects (e.g. frequencies of coded categories) can be analyzed."
- 要点：归纳发展类别的做法：先依据理论背景和研究问题定一个"选取标准"，决定看材料的哪些方面；按这个标准逐段处理材料，暂定类别；在反馈循环中修订、归并成主类别，并检查信度；研究问题需要时，也可以分析编码类别的频次等定量方面。

### E9

- 位置：§4.2 Deductive category application，[15]
- 原文："Then main idea here is to give explicit definitions, examples and coding rules for each deductive category, determining exactly under what circumstances a text passage can be coded with a category. Those category definitions are putted together within a coding agenda."
- 要点：演绎应用类别的做法：给每个事先定好的类别写明确定义、例子和编码规则，准确规定什么情况下一段文本可以编入这个类别；这些定义汇总成编码手册（coding agenda）。

### E10

- 位置：§4.2，Fig. 3 之后，[17]
- 原文："Category definitions, prototypical text passages, and rules for distinguishing different categories were formulated in respect to theory and material, are completed step by step, and are revised with the process of analysis."
- 要点：类别定义、典型例句和区分相邻类别的规则，依据理论和材料写成，逐步补全，并随分析修订。

### E11

- 位置：§5 Computer Programs，[18]
- 原文："Especially within the last years several computer programs had been developed within the framework of qualitative analysis to support (not to replace) steps of text interpretation"
- 要点：计算机程序是用来支持、而不是取代文本解读步骤的。

### E12

- 位置：§7 Discussion，[27]
- 原文："The procedures of qualitative content analysis seem less appropriate, if the research question is highly open-ended, explorative, variable and working with categories would be a restriction, or if a more holistic, not step-by-step ongoing of analysis is planned."
- 要点：如果研究问题高度开放、探索性强、会不断变化，用类别会成为束缚，或者打算做整体性而非分步的分析，定性内容分析就不太合适。

### E13

- 位置：§7 Discussion，[28]
- 原文："The research question and the characteristics of the material should have the priority in the decision about adapted methods."
- 要点：选方法时，研究问题和材料的特点应当优先。
