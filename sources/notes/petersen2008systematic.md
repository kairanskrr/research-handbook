# Petersen et al. 2008：Systematic Mapping Studies in Software Engineering

- 资料：`petersen2008systematic`，[原文 PDF（作者主页）](https://www.cse.chalmers.se/~feldt/publications/petersen_ease08_sysmap_studies_in_se.pdf)
- 版本：EASE 2008 论文。页码为 PDF 文件的页序。

### E1

- 位置：§2，PDF 第 2 页
- 原文："The essential process steps of our systematic mapping study are definition of research questions, conducting the search for relevant papers, screening of papers, keywording of abstracts and data extraction and mapping"
- 要点：映射研究五步：确定研究问题、检索、筛选、给摘要标注关键词、提取数据并画出映射图。

### E2

- 位置：§2.4，PDF 第 4 页
- 原文："Keywording is done in two steps. First, the reviewers read abstracts and look for keywords and concepts that reflects the contribution of the paper. [...] When this is done, the set of keywords from different papers are combined together to develop a high level understanding about the nature and contribution of the research. [...] When abstracts are of too poor quality to allow meaningful keywords to be chosen, reviewers can choose to study also the introduction or conclusion sections of the paper."
- 要点：分类方案靠"关键词标注"建立：先读摘要，找出反映贡献的关键词；再把各篇的关键词合并、聚类，形成分类。摘要信息不够时，可以看引言或结论。

### E3

- 位置：§3.2，PDF 第 7 页
- 原文："a systematic review aims at establishing the state of evidence [...] the systematic reviews we have found focus on identifying best practices based on empirical evidence [...]. This is not a goal for systematic maps"
- 要点：目标不同：系统综述要确定证据的现状、找出有实证支持的做法；映射研究不以此为目标。

### E4

- 位置：§3.2，PDF 第 7 页
- 原文："In maps, the articles are not evaluated regarding their quality as the main goal is not to establish the state of evidence." / "In a systematic mapping study, more articles can be considered as they don't have to be evaluated in such detail."
- 要点：映射研究一般不评估单篇论文的质量，所以能覆盖更多论文、梳理更大的领域；系统综述要评估质量，范围通常更窄，工作量更大。

### E5

- 位置：§3.2，PDF 第 8 页
- 原文："a systematic review focusing on papers using some particular method might introduce a bias when it comes to presenting the overall research area."
- 要点：只收用某种研究方法的论文，再拿来描述整个领域，会带来偏差。

### E6

- 位置：§4，PDF 第 9 页
- 原文："A mapping study that goes deeper into the papers can become more like a systematic review. The two type of studies can be considered as different points on a continuum."
- 要点：映射研究读得越深，就越接近系统综述。两者是一条连续谱上的不同位置。

### E7

- 位置：摘要（PDF 第 1 页）；§1（PDF 第 2 页）
- 原文："Systematic maps and reviews are different in terms of goals, breadth, validity issues and implications. Thus, they should be used complementarily and require different methods (e.g., for analysis)." / "In this paper we clarify and expand upon the differences between systematic review and systematic mapping studies and argue for a broader set of situations where the latter is appropriate."
- 要点：映射研究和系统综述在目标、广度、效度和意义上都不同，应当互相补充；作者主张映射研究的适用场景比以往认为的更广。

### E8

- 位置：§2.5，PDF 第 5 页
- 原文："In our study, we used a bubble plot to report the frequencies, shown in Figure 3."
- 要点：映射结果可以用气泡图展示各类别交叉处的论文数量。
