# medRxiv：About medRxiv

- 资料：`medrxivabout`，[About medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/about-medrxiv)
- 版本：网页，2026-10-06 访问。

### E1

- 位置：About medRxiv
- 原文："medRxiv (pronounced "med-archive") is a free online archive and distribution server for complete but unpublished manuscripts (preprints) in the medical, clinical, and related health sciences."
- 要点：medRxiv 是医学、临床和相关健康科学的免费预印本平台。

### E2

- 位置：About medRxiv
- 原文："Preprints are preliminary reports of work that have not been certified by peer review. They should not be relied on to guide clinical practice or health-related behavior and should not be reported in news media as established information."
- 要点：预印本没有经过同行评审，不应用来指导临床实践或健康行为，新闻媒体也不应把它当作定论报道。

### E3

- 位置：About medRxiv
- 原文："MedRxiv is not intended for case reports (single or in series), narrative reviews, scoping reviews or systematic reviews without meta-analyses"
- 要点：medRxiv 不收病例报告、叙述性综述、范围综述和不含荟萃分析的系统综述等。

### E4

- 位置：About medRxiv
- 原文："Once posted on medRxiv, manuscripts receive a digital object identifier (DOI), so are discoverable, citable, and indexed by numerous search engines and third-party services and cannot be removed."
- 要点：稿件一旦发布就有永久标识符、可被引用和检索，而且不能撤下。
