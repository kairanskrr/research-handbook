# Lombard, Snyder-Duch & Bracken 2010：Practical Resources for Assessing and Reporting Intercoder Reliability in Content Analysis Research Projects

- 资料：`lombard2010practical`，[原文网页](http://matthewlombard.com/reliability/)
- 版本：作者的公开在线指南，最后更新 2010 年 6 月 1 日；是三位作者 2002 年发表在 Human Communication Research 上的论文（Lombard, Snyder-Duch & Bracken 2002）的配套资源，含该文指南的修订版。位置写网页的小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：1. What is this site about, who created it, and why?
- 原文："reported a study that characterized the assessment and reporting of reliability in 200 studies in the mass communication literature between 1994 and 1998." [...] "we found that only 69% (n = 137) of the articles contained any report of intercoder reliability"
- 要点：作者 2002 年的研究查了 1994–1998 年大众传播文献中的 200 项研究，只有 69%（137 篇）报告了编码者间信度。

### E2

- 位置：2. What is intercoder reliability?
- 原文："Intercoder reliability is the widely used term for the extent to which independent coders evaluate a characteristic of a message or artifact and reach the same conclusion."
- 要点：编码者间信度指几位编码者各自独立判断一条信息的某个特征时，得出相同结论的程度。

### E3

- 位置：3. Why should content analysis researchers care about intercoder reliability?
- 原文："It is widely acknowledged that intercoder reliability is a critical component of content analysis, and that although it does not insure validity, when it is not established properly, the data and interpretations of the data can not be considered valid."
- 要点：编码者间信度是内容分析的关键部分；有信度不保证有效，但没有恰当地确立信度，数据和解读就不能算有效。

### E4

- 位置：4. How should content analysis researchers properly assess and report intercoder reliability?，第 1 步
- 原文："If percent agreement is selected (and this is not recommended), use a second index that accounts for agreement expected by chance."
- 要点：不建议用百分比一致率；如果用了，要再加一个考虑了碰巧一致的指标。

### E5

- 位置：第 4 节，第 1 步
- 原文："Note that the selection of index or indices must precede data collection and evaluation of intercoder reliability."
- 要点：用哪个信度指标，必须在收集数据、评估信度之前选定。

### E6

- 位置：第 4 节，第 3 步
- 原文："Coefficients of .90 or greater are nearly always acceptable, .80 or greater is acceptable in most situations, and .70 may be appropriate in some exploratory studies for some indices."
- 要点：系数 0.90 以上几乎总能接受，0.80 以上多数情况下可接受，某些探索性研究中对某些指标 0.70 也可能合适。

### E7

- 位置：第 4 节，第 5 步
- 原文："Coding must be done independently and without consultation or guidance; if possible, the researcher should not be a coder."
- 要点：信度测试的编码必须各自独立完成，不能商量或被指导；可能的话研究者本人不要当编码者。

### E8

- 位置：第 4 节，第 5 步
- 原文："The size of this sample can vary depending on the project but a good rule of thumb is 30 units"
- 要点：预测试样本的经验值是 30 个单元。

### E9

- 位置：第 4 节，第 6 步
- 原文："it should not be less than 50 units or 10% of the full sample, and it rarely will need to be greater than 300 units"
- 要点：正式信度样本不少于 50 个单元或全样本的 10%，很少需要超过 300 个。

### E10

- 位置：第 4 节，第 8 步
- 原文："Provide this minimum information: The size of and the method used to create the reliability sample, along with a justification of that method." [...] "The intercoder reliability level for each variable, for each index selected." [...] "How disagreements in the reliability coding were resolved in the full sample."
- 要点：至少要报告：信度样本的大小、抽取方法和理由；每个变量、每个指标的信度值；分歧怎样解决等。

### E11

- 位置：第 4 节
- 原文："Do NOT use only percent agreement to calculate reliability."
- 要点：不要只用百分比一致率计算信度。

### E12

- 位置：第 4 节
- 原文："Do NOT use overall reliability across variables, rather than reliability levels for each variable, as a standard for evaluating the reliability of the instrument."
- 要点：不要用所有变量合在一起的总体信度来评价编码工具，要逐个变量报告。

### E13

- 位置：第 4 节
- 原文："Do NOT use Cronbach's alpha, Pearson's r, or other correlation-based indices that standardize coder values and only measure covariation."
- 要点：不要用 Cronbach's alpha、Pearson 相关系数等基于相关的指标，它们只衡量两人打分是否一起高低变化，不衡量编码是否相同。

### E14

- 位置：5. Which measure(s) of intercoder reliability should researchers use?
- 原文："there is no consensus on a single, "best" one."
- 要点：哪一个信度指标"最好"，没有共识。

### E15

- 位置：第 5 节
- 原文："Krippendorff's alpha is well regarded and very flexible (it can be used with multiple coders, accounts for different sample sizes and missing data, and can be used for ordinal, interval and ratio level variables)"
- 要点：Krippendorff's alpha 评价好、适用面广：可用于多名编码者，能处理不同样本量和缺失数据，也能用于有序和数值型变量。

### E16

- 位置：第 4 节，第 4 步
- 原文："assess reliability informally with a small number of units which ideally are not part of the full sample (or census) of units to be coded, and refine the instrument and coding instructions until the informal assessment suggests an adequate level of agreement."
- 要点：培训编码者时，先用少量（最好不在正式样本里的）单元非正式地测信度，反复修改编码表和说明，直到一致程度足够。
