# Nosek et al. 2018：The preregistration revolution

- 资料：`nosek2018preregistration`，[原文（PubMed Central 开放全文）](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5856500/)
- 版本：PNAS 115(11):2600–2606。网页版，位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："An effective solution is to define the research questions and analysis plan before observing the research outcomes—a process called preregistration."
- 要点：预注册的定义：在看到研究结果之前，先定好研究问题和分析计划。

### E2

- 位置：Mental Constraints on Distinguishing Predictions and Postdictions
- 原文："The problem with this is understood as post hoc theorizing or hypothesizing after the results are known"
- 要点：把事后解释当成事先预测，就是"看到结果再提假设"（hypothesizing after the results are known，HARKing）。

### E3

- 位置：Mental Constraints on Distinguishing Predictions and Postdictions
- 原文："good intentions are not sufficient to overcome the fallibility of memory, motivated reasoning, and cognitive biases that can occur outside of conscious awareness or control"
- 要点：光有好的意愿不够。记忆会出错，人会不自觉地往想要的方向推理，这些偏差常常发生在意识之外。

### E4

- 位置：Preregistration Distinguishes Prediction and Postdiction
- 原文："Preregistration does not favor prediction over postdiction; its purpose is to make clear which is which."
- 要点：预注册不是说事先预测比事后探索更高级，而是让人分清哪个是哪个。

### E5

- 位置：Challenge 1: Changes to Procedure During Study Administration
- 原文："Jolene can transparently report changes that were made and why." / "Compared with the situation in which Jolene did not preregister at all, preregistration with reported deviations provides substantially greater confidence in the resulting statistical inferences."
- 要点：实际做研究时偏离计划很常见。只要如实报告改了什么、为什么改，有预注册加上偏差说明，仍比完全不预注册可信得多。

### E6

- 位置：Challenge 6: A Program of Research
- 原文："all outcomes of that analysis plan must be reported to avoid the problem of selective reporting."
- 要点：预注册之后，计划中的所有分析结果都要报告，否则仍然是选择性报告。

### E7

- 位置：Making Preregistration the Norm — Advancing the Motive for Preregistration
- 原文："With Registered Reports, authors submit their research question and methodology to the journal for peer review before observing the outcomes of the research." / "At second-stage review, reviewers do not evaluate the perceived importance of the outcomes. Rather, they evaluate the quality of study execution and adherence to the preregistered plan."
- 要点：注册报告：作者在看到结果之前把研究问题和方法交给期刊审稿；第二阶段审稿不评判结果是否"重要"，只看研究做得好不好、是否按计划执行。

### E8

- 位置：Challenge 2: Discovery of Assumption Violations During Analysis（决策树一段）
- 原文："Preregistration does not eliminate the possibility of poor statistical practices, but it does make them detectable."
- 要点：预注册不能杜绝糟糕的统计做法，但能让这些做法被发现。

### E9

- 位置：Preregistration Distinguishes Prediction and Postdiction
- 原文："there is not yet sufficient experimental evidence establishing its superiority for reproducibility."
- 要点：作者承认：预注册能提高可重复性，理论上很有道理，也有一些相关性证据，但还没有足够的实验证据。
