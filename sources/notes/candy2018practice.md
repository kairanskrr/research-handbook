# Candy & Edmonds 2018：Practice-Based Research in the Creative Arts: Foundations and Futures from the Front Line

- 资料：`candy2018practice`，[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1162/LEON_a_01471)；全文 PDF 取自悉尼科技大学机构库 OPUS（[记录页](http://hdl.handle.net/10453/134306)，标为开放获取）
- 版本：Linda Candy、Ernest Edmonds，*Leonardo* 第 51 卷第 1 期，第 63–69 页，2018 年，MIT Press。两位作者依据指导艺术、设计、数字媒体跨学科博士研究 35 年以上的经验写成。位置写小标题和期刊页码。

### E1

- 位置：引言，第 63 页
- 原文："We draw on examples spanning over 35 years of experience in supervising interdisciplinary PhD research programs in the arts, design and digital media."
- 要点：文章依据作者 35 年以上指导艺术、设计、数字媒体跨学科博士研究的例子。

### E2

- 位置：引言，第 63 页
- 原文："A basic principle of practice-based research is that not only is practice embedded in the research process but research questions arise from the process of practice, the answers to which are directed toward enlightening and enhancing practice."
- 要点：基于实践的研究的一个基本原则：实践嵌在研究过程里，研究问题也从实践过程中产生，答案用来启发和改进实践。

### E3

- 位置：引言，第 63 页
- 原文："This form of research is usually set in a [...] specific context, yet it must also reach beyond the particular cases if it is to be perceived as contributing to knowledge in any way."
- 要点：这类研究通常放在一个具体情境里，但要算作知识贡献，必须超出个案。

### E4

- 位置：Practice-Based Research Defined and Differentiated，第 63–64 页
- 原文："Moreover, research and practice are different, and it is ill advised to use the terms in ways that suggest they are interchangeable"
- 要点：研究和实践是两回事，不宜把两个词当成可以互换来用。

### E5

- 位置：Different Approaches to Practice-Related Research，第 65 页
- 原文："For practicebased researchers, making an artifact is pivotal, and the insights from making, reflecting and evaluating may be fed back directly into the artifact itself."
- 要点：对基于实践的研究者，做作品是关键；从制作、反思、评估中得到的洞见可以直接反馈到作品里。

### E6

- 位置：Practice-based Research and the PhD，第 65 页
- 原文："If, on the other hand, the research is in the art domain and the way that we perceive the artifact is of central concern, then there may be no alternative to providing that video. The role of artifact as art object is not illustrating anything but rather is a subject of interest in itself."
- 要点：几何论文里的旋转视频只是辅助说明；但如果研究关心的是人怎样感知作品，就可能非提供视频不可——这时作品不是插图，本身就是研究对象。

### E7

- 位置：Practice-based Research and the PhD，第 65 页
- 原文："To be able to achieve a full appreciation of the creative works themselves, PhD examiners need to have access to a form that conveys as near as possible a genuine sense of the experience of the works."
- 要点：答辩评审需要以尽可能接近真实体验的形式接触作品。

### E8

- 位置：Practice-based Research and the PhD，第 65 页
- 原文："typically, this is achieved by viewing exhibitions of works and live performances and, where that is not possible, recordings of music, films, photographs of paintings, etc."
- 要点：通常是看展览和现场演出；做不到时，用音乐录音、影片、画作照片等。

### E9

- 位置：Practice-based Research and the PhD，第 65 页
- 原文："In fact, it may not be possible to lodge the artifact itself in the university library as is normally required and in these cases, the submission of sufficiently good documentation for the complete work to be fully understood is necessary to meet the PhD requirements."
- 要点：作品本身可能无法存进大学图书馆，这时必须提交足够好的记录，让完整作品能被充分理解。

### E10

- 位置：Practice-based Research and the PhD，第 65 页
- 原文："That said, the creative works cannot be expected to speak for themselves in the context of a PhD submission. For that reason, they should, indeed must, be accompanied by some form of textual analysis or explanation to support the candidate's position and to demonstrate critical reflection."
- 要点：在博士提交材料里，不能指望作品自己说话；必须配上文字分析或说明。

### E11

- 位置：Practice-based Research and the PhD，第 65 页
- 原文："As such it is a vital part of the research outcomes and cannot be viewed simply as an optional extra. The role of the written thesis is to share the understandings achieved through the research and to affirm that it is important to have a clear structure that sets out the aims, background, methods and outcomes of the research."
- 要点：书面论文是研究成果的重要部分，不是可有可无的附件；要有清楚的结构，写明目标、背景、方法和成果。

### E12

- 位置：Practice-based Research and the PhD，第 65 页
- 原文："This is where the candidate shows how the work relates to the state of the art in the field and that the work is in some way \"new.\" They also have to facilitate an understanding of just what the knowledge is, for example by explaining how to approach or view the new creative work."
- 要点：论文要说明作品与领域现状的关系、它新在哪里，并帮助读者理解知识是什么，例如说明该怎样去看这件新作品。

### E13

- 位置：Practice-based Research and the PhD，第 65 页
- 原文："This means that practice-based doctoral submissions must include a substantial contextualization of the creative work, by way of a \"literature\" review."
- 要点：基于实践的博士提交材料必须通过一种"文献"综述，对作品做充分的背景定位。

### E14

- 位置：The Artifact in Practice-Based Research，第 66 页
- 原文："This raises the need to ensure that, when research results are communicated, the relevant norms and tests are made explicit. As a minimum, a commentary is needed that frames the context in which the artwork is to be understood, including the research norms and tests."
- 要点：报告研究结果时，要把所用的规范和检验标准讲明；至少要有一段评述，交代理解作品的情境，包括研究的规范和检验标准。

### E15

- 位置：Contributions to New Knowledge and the Practice-Based PhD，第 66 页
- 原文："As we have stated previously, it is important to be clear that knowledge that is new for the practitioner alone is not included in any definition of PhD practice-based research."
- 要点：只对实践者本人是新的知识，不属于任何基于实践的博士研究定义里的知识。

### E16

- 位置：Contributions to New Knowledge and the Practice-Based PhD，第 66 页
- 原文："Knowledge arising from practice-based research is embedded in a range of outcomes: understandings about audience experience, strategies for designing engaging art systems, taxonomies of emergent behavior and models of collaboration, to take a few examples. And of course, there are the works themselves"
- 要点：基于实践的研究产生的知识体现在多种成果里：对观众体验的理解、设计策略、分类、合作模式等，当然还有作品本身。

### E17

- 位置：Contributions to New Knowledge and the Practice-Based PhD，第 66 页
- 原文："On the other hand, the creative work exists within a context: an artwork alone, without text, cannot be seen as a research outcome."
- 要点：作品存在于情境之中；没有文字的作品本身不能算研究成果。

### E18

- 位置：Contributions to New Knowledge and the Practice-Based PhD，第 66 页
- 原文："Some kinds of new knowledge are derived from empirical studies of audiences and art systems, while others are more speculative and exploratory."
- 要点：有些新知识来自对观众和艺术系统的实证研究，有些更具推测性和探索性。

### E19

- 位置：Example Contributions，Tebby 一例，第 67 页
- 原文："Later, Susan Tebby [17] carried out research into the use of various formal procedures and patterns in making art. [...] Without realizing the artworks, it would not have been possible to grasp these implications. In this case, the role of practice was both central and unavoidable."
- 要点：Tebby 的博士研究探讨在艺术创作中使用各种形式化程序和图式；，不把作品做出来就无法把握这些含义；实践的作用既核心又不可替代。

### E20

- 位置：Example Contributions，Tebby 一例，第 67 页
- 原文："The submission of the PhD consisted of a thesis together with an exhibition of the artworks generated during and through the research. The examiners spent a significant amount of time in the exhibition, which illuminated the understandings that they had obtained from reading the text. The exhibition was fully documented and a full set of 35mm slides (the medium of the day), lodged in the library with the text."
- 要点：提交材料是论文加展览；评审在展览里花了大量时间；展览被完整记录，幻灯片和论文一起存进图书馆。

### E21

- 位置：Example Contributions，Bell 一例，第 67 页
- 原文："consisted of an iterative process of making (writing computer code), looking (evaluating) and revising. The writing of code was, of course, quite an objective process, in which algorithms postulated to be interesting were implemented. The evaluation, on the other hand, was entirely subjective. Although others were consulted, the central figure in the evaluation process was the artist himself"
- 要点：Bell 的研究过程是"制作（写代码）—看（评估）—修改"的反复循环；评估完全主观，虽然也征询了别人，但评估的核心是艺术家本人。

### E22

- 位置：Example Contributions，Johnston 一例，第 68 页
- 原文："Indeed, the research goals and opportunities were only discovered out of practice. The evaluation element came about in trying to see what the implications were for the performing musicians; that was done by observing and discussing their practice in the context of these new forms."
- 要点：Johnston 的研究目标和机会是在实践中才发现的；评估部分通过观察演奏者、和他们讨论来完成。

### E23

- 位置：Example Contributions，Seevinck 一例，第 68 页
- 原文："The central results were embodied in artworks that facilitated and encouraged emergent thinking in members of the participating audience. It was necessary to demonstrate that this was, in fact, the case; so, as well as delivering the results of the research in both text and documentedartifact forms, the results of evaluations of participant responses were reported."
- 要点：Seevinck 的核心成果体现在促进参与观众"涌现式"思考的作品里；要证明确实如此，所以除了文字和作品记录，还报告了对参与者反应的评估结果。

### E24

- 位置：Conclusions，第 68 页
- 原文："This means that reporting the research in a PhD submission requires a written thesis that might well include a description of how a submitted artifact should be apprehended, as well as other evidence that demonstrates that the results are new, not just to the practitioner researcher, but to the wider world."
- 要点：博士提交材料需要书面论文，可能要说明该怎样去体会提交的作品，并提供证据说明结果不只对研究者本人是新的，对更广的世界也是新的。

### E25

- 位置：Practice-Based Research Defined and Differentiated，第 63 页
- 原文："In design research, for example, the emphasis is on understanding the nature of practice and how to improve it rather than creating and reflecting on new artifacts."
- 要点：在设计研究中，重点是理解实践的性质、怎样改进它，而不是创作并反思新作品。
