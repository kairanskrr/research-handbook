# Comrie, Haspelmath & Bickel 2015：The Leipzig Glossing Rules

- 资料：`leipzig2015glossing`，[原文](https://www.eva.mpg.de/lingua/pdf/Glossing-Rules.pdf)
- 版本：规则正文为 2008 年 2 月修订版，文件最后修改于 2015 年 5 月 31 日；德国马普进化人类学研究所语言学系与莱比锡大学语言学系联合制定，免费 PDF

### E1

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："Interlinear morpheme-by-morpheme glosses give information about the meanings and grammatical properties of individual words and parts of words."
- 要点：逐词素的行间注释说明每个词和词的组成部分的意义和语法属性。

### E2

- 位置：Rule 1: Word-by-word alignment
- 原文："Interlinear glosses are left-aligned vertically, word by word, with the example."
- 要点：注释行和例句逐词左对齐。

### E3

- 位置：Rule 2: Morpheme-by-morpheme correspondence
- 原文："Segmentable morphemes are separated by hyphens, both in the example and in the gloss. There must be exactly the same number of hyphens in the example and in the gloss."
- 要点：可切分的词素在例句行和注释行都用连字符隔开，两行的连字符数必须相同。

### E4

- 位置：Rule 2
- 原文："Clitic boundaries are marked by an equals sign, both in the object language and in the gloss."
- 要点：附着成分（依附在别的词上的成分）的边界用等号标出。

### E5

- 位置：Rule 3: Grammatical category labels
- 原文："Grammatical morphemes are generally rendered by abbreviated grammatical category labels, printed in upper case letters (usually small capitals)."
- 要点：语法词素用大写（通常是小型大写字母）的语法范畴缩写标注。

### E6

- 位置：Rule 4: One-to-many correspondences
- 原文："When a single object-language element is rendered by several metalanguage elements (words or abbreviations), these are separated by periods." / "Latin insul-arum island-GEN.PL 'of the islands'"
- 要点：一个成分要用几个词或缩写来注释时，用句点连接。

### E7

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："Glosses are part of the analysis, not part of the data."
- 要点：注释属于分析，不属于数据。

### E8

- 位置：About the rules
- 原文："most authors will feel the need to add (or modify) certain conventions (especially category labels)."
- 要点：多数作者会需要增加或修改某些约定，尤其是范畴标签。

### E9

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："The main purpose that is assumed here is the presentation of an example in a research paper or book. When an entire corpus is tagged, somewhat different" [...] "considerations may apply"
- 要点：这套规则主要针对论文和书里的例句；给整个语料库加标注时，考虑会有所不同。

### E10

- 位置：About the rules
- 原文："The Leipzig Glossing Rules have been developed jointly by the Department of Linguistics of the Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology (Bernard Comrie, Martin Haspelmath) and by the Department of Linguistics of the University of Leipzig (Balthasar Bickel)."
- 要点：规则由德国马普进化人类学研究所语言学系和莱比锡大学语言学系联合制定。

### E11

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："the main purpose of this document is to make the most widely used conventions explicit."
- 要点：这份文件的主要目的，是把最通行的注释约定写明。

### E12

- 位置：About the rules
- 原文："an appendix with a proposed" [...] "of abbreviated category labels."
- 要点：规则附有一份建议使用的语法范畴缩写表。
