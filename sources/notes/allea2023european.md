# ALLEA 2023：The European Code of Conduct for Research Integrity（修订版）

- 资料：`allea2023european`，[原文](https://allea.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/European-Code-of-Conduct-Revised-Edition-2023.pdf)
- 版本：2023 年修订版。欧洲科学与人文学院联合会（European Federation of Academies of Sciences and Humanities，ALLEA）制定，CC BY 4.0 许可，DOI 10.26356/ECOC。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Principles
- 原文："Reliability in ensuring the quality of research, reflected in the design, methodology, analysis, and use of resources." / "Honesty in developing, undertaking, reviewing, reporting, and communicating research in a transparent, fair, full, and unbiased way."
- 要点：四条基本原则中的两条：可靠（设计、方法、分析和资源使用保证研究质量）；诚实（从开展到报告和传播，都要透明、公平、完整、无偏）。

### E2

- 位置：§1 Principles
- 原文："Respect for colleagues, research participants, research subjects, society, ecosystems, cultural heritage, and the environment." / "Accountability for the research from idea to publication"
- 要点：另两条原则：尊重（同事、参与者、研究对象、社会和环境）；负责（从想法到发表，对研究负责）。

### E3

- 位置：§3.1 Research Misconduct
- 原文："Fabrication is making up data or results and recording them as if they were real." / "Falsification is manipulating research materials, equipment, images, or processes, or changing, omitting, or suppressing data or results without justification." / "Plagiarism is using other people’s work or ideas without giving proper credit to the original source."
- 要点：学术不端的传统定义是 FFP：伪造（编造数据或结果）、篡改（无正当理由地操纵材料或改动、删除、隐瞒数据或结果）、剽窃（使用他人的成果或想法而不恰当注明出处）。

### E4

- 位置：§3.1，其他不可接受的做法
- 原文："Misusing statistics, for example to inappropriately suggest statistical significance." / "Withholding research data or results without justification." / "Citing selectively or inaccurately."
- 要点：FFP 之外也不可接受的做法：滥用统计制造显著性；无正当理由扣留数据或结果；选择性或不准确地引用。

### E5

- 位置：§3.1，其他不可接受的做法
- 原文："Chopping up research results with the specific aim of increasing the number of research publications" / "Re-publishing substantive parts of one’s own earlier publications, including translations, without duly acknowledging or citing the original"
- 要点：为增加论文数量而拆分结果（"切香肠"）；重复发表自己以前论文的实质内容（包括翻译）却不注明，即"自我剽窃"。

### E6

- 位置：§3 开头
- 原文："It damages the research processes, degrades relationships among researchers, undermines trust in and the credibility of research, wastes resources, and may expose research participants and subjects, users, society, or the environment to unnecessary harm."
- 要点：违背良好研究规范的后果：损害研究过程和研究者之间的关系，削弱研究的可信度，浪费资源，还可能让参与者、社会和环境受到不必要的伤害。

### E7

- 位置：§2.7 Publication, Dissemination, and Authorship
- 原文："authorship itself is based on: (1) a significant contribution to the design of the research, relevant data collection, its analysis, and/ or interpretation; (2) drafting and/or critical reviewing the publication; (3) approval of the final publication; and (4) agreeing to be responsible for the content of the publication"
- 要点：署名的四个条件：对设计、数据收集、分析或解释有实质贡献；起草或批判性修改论文；同意最终版本；同意对论文内容负责。

### E8

- 位置：§2.7
- 原文："Authors formally agree on the sequence of authorship" / "Authors acknowledge important work and contributions of those who do not meet the criteria for authorship, including collaborators, assistants, and funders who have enabled the research."
- 要点：作者要正式商定署名顺序；不够署名条件但有贡献的人（合作者、助手、资助方）要在致谢中写明。

### E9

- 位置：§3.1，其他不可接受的做法
- 原文："Manipulating authorship or denigrating the role of other researchers in publications."
- 要点：操纵署名、贬低其他研究者在论文中的作用，属于不可接受的做法。

### E10

- 位置：§2.7
- 原文："Authors disclose any financial and nonfinancial conflicts of interest as well as sources of support for the research or the publication."
- 要点：披露所有经济和非经济利益冲突，以及资助来源。

### E11

- 位置：§2.7
- 原文："Authors and publishers promptly issue corrections or retract publications, if necessary"
- 要点：发现错误时，及时发更正，必要时撤稿。

### E12

- 位置：§2.7
- 原文："negative results can be as relevant as positive findings for publication and dissemination."
- 要点：负面结果和正面结果一样值得发表和传播。

### E13

- 位置：§2.7
- 原文："Authors are transparent in their communication, outreach, and public engagement about assumptions and values influencing their research as well as the robustness of the evidence, including remaining uncertainties and knowledge gaps."
- 要点：对外交流时，要说清研究的假设、证据有多可靠，以及还有哪些不确定和未知。

### E14

- 位置：§2.3 Research Procedures
- 原文："in a way that is compatible with the accepted norms of the discipline and facilitates verification or replication, where applicable"
- 要点：报告结果和方法时，要符合学科惯例，并便于他人核查或重复。

### E15

- 位置：§2.5 Data Practices and Management
- 原文："ensure appropriate stewardship, curation, and preservation of all data, metadata, protocols, code, software, and other research materials for a reasonable and clearly stated period." / "access to data is as open as possible, as closed as necessary"
- 要点：在合理且明确的期限内妥善保存数据、代码等研究材料；数据"尽可能开放，必要时封闭"。

### E16

- 位置：§2.4 Safeguards
- 原文："Researchers, research institutions, and organisations comply with relevant codes, guidelines, and regulations." / "Researchers recognise and weigh potential harms and risks relating to their research and its applications and mitigate possible negative impacts."
- 要点：遵守相关规范和法规；识别并权衡研究及其应用可能带来的伤害和风险，并设法减轻。

### E17

- 位置：§2.5
- 原文："Researchers inform research participants about how their data will be used, reused, accessed, stored, and deleted, in compliance with GDPR."
- 要点：告诉参与者，他们的数据将怎样使用、再利用、访问、存储和删除（按欧盟《通用数据保护条例》）。

### E18

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："applies to research in all scientific and scholarly fields."
- 要点：这份准则适用于所有学科的研究。

### E19

- 位置：§3.1，其他不可接受的做法
- 原文："Hiding the use of AI or automated tools in the creation of content or drafting of publications."
- 要点：把"在创作内容或起草论文时隐瞒使用人工智能或自动化工具"列为不可接受的做法。

### E20

- 位置：§2.7 Publication, Dissemination, and Authorship
- 原文："authors are given credit for issuing corrections post-publication"
- 要点：作者在发表后主动发更正，应当得到认可。
