# Freelon 2018：Computational Research in the Post-API Age

- 资料：`freelon2018computational`，[作者主页版本](http://dfreelon.org/publications/2018_Computational_research_in_the_postAPI_age.pdf)
- 版本：Political Communication 35(4), 665–668，2018，DOI 10.1080/10584609.2018.1477506；本次读取的是作者主页上的接收稿（标注 2018-08-20，"Forthcoming in Political Communication"），共 6 页。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："The closure of the Pages API eliminated all terms of service (TOS)-compliant access to Facebook content."
- 要点：2018 年 4 月 Facebook 关闭公共主页接口后，已没有任何符合服务条款的办法获取 Facebook 内容。

### E2

- 位置：开头
- 原文："When companies can restrict or eliminate API access at any time, for any reason, and without any recourse, computational researchers and students need to seriously consider how to proceed."
- 要点：公司可以随时、以任何理由限制或取消接口访问，研究者无从申诉，必须认真考虑怎么办。

### E3

- 位置：开头
- 原文："First, they should learn how to scrape the web; and second, they should understand the potential consequences of violating platforms’ TOS by doing so."
- 要点：作者建议研究者学会网页抓取，同时弄清抓取可能违反平台服务条款带来的后果。

### E4

- 位置：Terms of service，Use authorized methods whenever possible
- 原文："Before scraping a site that prohibits it, exhaust all possible means of procuring your data via officially-sanctioned means."
- 要点：在抓取禁止抓取的网站之前，先用尽所有官方许可的取数途径。

### E5

- 位置：Terms of service，Do not confuse TOS compliance with human subjects compliance or privacy protection
- 原文："The purpose of TOS is to protect the companies that create them."
- 要点：服务条款是为了保护制定它的公司。

### E6

- 位置：Terms of service，Do not confuse TOS compliance with human subjects compliance or privacy protection
- 原文："By employing TOS-compliant methods, you are respecting the business prerogatives of the company that created the platform you are studying, but you may or may not be respecting the dignity and privacy of the platform’s users."
- 要点：遵守服务条款只是尊重了公司的商业权利，不等于尊重了平台用户的尊严和隐私。

### E7

- 位置：Terms of service，Use authorized methods whenever possible
- 原文："Twitter’s API allows generous prospective collection of tweets but sharply limits retrospective collection. Therefore, researchers might set up a server-based data collection workflow that can quickly be initialized as events of theoretical interest occur."
- 要点：当时 Twitter 接口对"事后回溯"限制很严，对"事中实时收集"较宽松，所以可以事先搭好收集流程，事件一发生就开始收。

### E8

- 位置：Terms of service，Understand the risks of violating TOS
- 原文："Until it is resolved, researchers should bear in mind the potential (if unlikely) consequences of even small-scale TOS violations."
- 要点：在相关诉讼有结果之前，即使小规模违反服务条款，也要想到可能（虽然不太可能）的后果。

### E9

- 位置：Terms of service，Understand the risks of violating TOS
- 原文："the only data it allows to be shared are unique object IDs from which the (undeleted) original data can be reconstituted."
- 要点：当时 Twitter 只允许共享推文编号，别人可以凭编号重新获取尚未删除的原始数据。
