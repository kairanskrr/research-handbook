# Shaw 2002：What Makes Good Research in Software Engineering?

- 资料：`shaw2002makes`，[原文](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~Compose/ftp/shaw-fin-etaps.pdf)
- 版本：International Journal of Software Tools for Technology Transfer 4(1)，2002，作者主页 PDF。只摘录跨学科成立的部分。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："Scientific and engineering research fields can be characterized by identifying what they value: [...] What kinds of questions are "interesting"? [...] What kinds of results help to answer these questions, and what research methods can produce these results? [...] What kinds of evidence can demonstrate the validity of a result, and how are good results distinguished from bad ones?"
- 要点：一个研究领域看重什么，可以用三个问题刻画：什么问题值得问、什么结果能回答它、什么证据能证明结果成立。

### E2

- 位置：§2.2 Types of Research Results
- 原文："Research yields new knowledge. This knowledge is expressed in the form of a particular result."
- 要点：研究产出新知识，知识以某种具体的结果形式表达出来。

### E3

- 位置：§2.1 Types of Research Questions
- 原文："Research questions may be about methods for developing software, about methods for analyzing software, about the design, evaluation, or implementation of specific systems, about generalizations over whole classes of systems, or about the sheer feasibility of a task."
- 要点：研究问题有不同类型：怎么做、怎么分析评估、某个具体对象怎么样、一类对象的一般规律、某件事是否可能。

### E4

- 位置：§2.2 Types of Research Results
- 原文："The result may be a specific procedure or technique for software development or for analysis. It may be more general, capturing a number of specific results in a model; such models are of many degrees of precision and formality. Sometimes, the result is the solution to a specific problem or the outcome of a specific analysis. Finally, as Brooks observed, observations and rules of thumb may be good preliminary results."
- 要点：结果也有不同类型：方法或技术、概括许多结果的模型、具体问题的解答或分析、观察和经验法则。

### E5

- 位置：§1.2.3 Broad View of Research
- 原文："Findings: well-established scientific truths, judged by truthfulness and rigor; [...] Observations: reports on actual phenomena, judged by interestingness; [...] Rules of thumb: generalizations, signed by their author but perhaps incompletely supported by data, judged by usefulness"
- 要点：Shaw 转述 Brooks 的三层结果：确证的发现（看真实和严谨）、观察（看是否有意思）、经验法则（看是否有用）。

### E6

- 位置：§1.2.3 Broad View of Research
- 原文："Observations and rules of thumb provide valuable guidance for practice when findings are not available. They also help to understand and area and lay the groundwork for the research that will, in time, yield findings."
- 要点：在没有确证发现时，观察和经验法则也有价值，并为以后得出确证发现打基础。

### E7

- 位置：§2.3 Types of Research Validations
- 原文："Good research requires not only a result, but also clear and convincing evidence that the result is sound. This evidence should be based on experience or systematic analysis, not simply persuasive argument or textbook examples."
- 要点：好研究不仅要有结果，还要有清楚、令人信服的证据；证据应来自实际经验或系统分析，而不是说服性的论述或教科书式的例子。

### E8

- 位置：§2.3 Types of Research Validations
- 原文："A significant number of ICSE submissions depend only on blatant assertion to demonstrate the validity of their results, or offer no evidence at all; such submissions are only rarely accepted."
- 要点：只靠断言、或完全不给证据的投稿很少被录用。

### E9

- 位置：§4 Summary
- 原文："The strategy of a research project should select a result, an approach to obtaining the result, and a validation strategy appropriate to the research question."
- 要点：研究策略要让"结果类型、获得方法、验证方式"三者都与研究问题匹配。

### E10

- 位置：§4 Summary
- 原文："major results that influence practice rely on accumulation of evidence from many projects. Each individual paper thus provides incremental knowledge, and collections of related research projects and reports provide both confirming and cumulative evidence."
- 要点：重大结果依赖许多项目的证据积累；单篇论文提供的是增量知识。
