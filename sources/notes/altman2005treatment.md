# Altman & Bland 2005：Treatment allocation by minimisation

- 资料：`altman2005treatment`，[原文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC556084/)
- 版本：BMJ 2005;330(7495):843，"Statistics Notes"（统计笔记）专栏（DOI 10.1136/bmj.330.7495.843）。本条摘录取自美国国家医学图书馆 PubMed Central 收录的全文。

### E1

- 位置：正文第 1 段
- 原文："The only widely acceptable alternative approach is minimisation" / "a method of ensuring excellent balance between groups for several prognostic factors, even in small samples. With minimisation the treatment allocated to the next participant enrolled in the trial depends (wholly or partly) on the characteristics of those participants already enrolled. The aim is that each allocation should minimise the imbalance across multiple factors."
- 要点：最小化法即使在小样本里也能让各组在多个预后因素上很均衡；下一个入组者分到哪组，（全部或部分）取决于已入组者的特征，目的是让每次分配都使多个因素上的不平衡最小。

### E2

- 位置：正文第 3–4 段
- 原文："The first participant is allocated a treatment at random. For each subsequent participant we determine which treatment would lead to better balance between the groups in the variables of interest." / "(Note that two or more categories need to be constructed for continuous variables.)"
- 要点：第一个人随机分配；之后每来一个人，看分到哪组能让关心的变量更均衡。连续变量要先分成两类或更多类。

### E3

- 位置：正文第 5–6 段
- 原文："We formalise this by summing over the four variables the numbers of participants with the same characteristics as this new recruit already in the trial" / "Imbalance is minimised by allocating this person to the group with the smaller total (or at random if the totals are the same)."
- 要点：具体算法：对每一组，把已入组者中和新来者在各个因素上特征相同的人数加起来；把新来者分到总数较小的组，总数相同时随机分。

### E4

- 位置：正文第 7 段
- 原文："We use weighted randomisation so that there is a high chance (eg 80%) of each participant getting the treatment that minimises the imbalance. The use of a random element will slightly worsen the overall imbalance between the groups, but balance will be much better for the chosen variables than with simple randomisation. A random element also makes the allocation more unpredictable, although minimisation is a secure allocation system when used by an independent person."
- 要点：可以加入随机成分：以较高概率（例如 80%，原文举的例子）分到使不平衡最小的那组。这样均衡性略差，但仍比简单随机化好得多，而且分配更难预测。
