# American Sociological Review：投稿指南

- 资料：`asrsubmission`，[Sage 投稿指南 PDF](https://www.sagepub.com/docs/default-source/msg/ASR)（网页版：https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/ASR）
- 版本：美国社会学会旗舰期刊《美国社会学评论》（American Sociological Review，ASR）的投稿指南，由出版社 Sage 发布，2026 年 10 月 6 日下载（网页版对脚本返回验证页，用的是同内容的 PDF）。位置写指南小节。

### E1

- 位置：What We Publish
- 原文："ASR papers tend to combine very strong research designs and analytic methods, general interest framing, and theory development that transcends subfields of sociology. As editors, when we are evaluating whether or not to send a paper out for review, we have three criteria in mind—quality of methods, general sociological appeal, and breadth."
- 要点：ASR 论文通常结合很强的研究设计和分析方法、面向一般读者的问题框架，以及超越社会学分支领域的理论发展；编辑决定是否送审时看三点：方法质量、对社会学的普遍吸引力、广度。

### E2

- 位置：Manuscript Preparation
- 原文："ASR Articles may not exceed 15,000 words in length including text, references, and footnotes (excluding tables and figures)."
- 要点：ASR 论文不超过 15,000 词，含正文、参考文献和脚注，不含图表。

### E3

- 位置：Ethics
- 原文："Submission of a manuscript to another professional journal while it is under review by the ASR is regarded by the ASA as unethical."
- 要点：稿件在 ASR 审稿期间又投给别的期刊，美国社会学会视为不道德。

### E4

- 位置：Anonymized Manuscript
- 原文："ASR uses anonymous peer reviewers for manuscript evaluation. Delete or rewrite any text that identifies you as the author: when citing your own work, please write “Smith (1992) concluded...,” but do not write “I concluded (Smith 1992)....”"
- 要点：ASR 采用匿名审稿；要删去或改写能认出作者的文字，引用自己的工作时写"Smith (1992) 认为……"，不写"我认为 (Smith 1992)……"。

### E5

- 位置：Anonymized Manuscript，引用格式
- 原文："Citations in the text should provide the last name of the author(s) and year of publication. Include page numbers for direct quotes or specific passages."
- 要点：正文引用写作者姓和年份；直接引用或指向具体段落时要加页码（格式如 Ramirez and Weiss 1979:239–40）。

### E6

- 位置：References
- 原文："References for data sets should include a persistent identifier, such as a Digital Object Identifier (DOI)."
- 要点：引用数据集要包含持久标识符，例如 DOI。

### E7

- 位置：Manuscript Submission
- 原文："ASR does not consider submissions of papers previously rejected at the journal."
- 要点：ASR 不再考虑曾被本刊拒绝的稿件。
