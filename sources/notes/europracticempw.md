# Europractice：MPW Prototyping

- 资料：`europracticempw`，[原文](https://europractice-ic.com/mpw-prototyping/)
- 版本：Europractice（欧盟支持的芯片原型制造服务机构）网页，2026-10-06 读取。位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：页首
- 原文："The cost of producing a new ASIC for a dedicated application within a small market can be high, if directly produced by a commercial foundry. Europractice has reduced the prototyping cost, especially for ASIC prototyping, by two techniques:" / "Multi Project Wafer Runs (MPW) or"
- 要点：直接找代工厂做一颗专用芯片（ASIC）很贵；多项目晶圆（MPW）等方式可以降低原型制造成本。

### E2

- 位置：Selecting and Accessing Technology
- 原文："Once the technology is chosen, customers need detailed information about it, such as design rules, electrical parameters and standard cell libraries. All this information is put together by the foundry in the so-called ‘Design Kit’. Customers can obtain access to it by signing the appropriate NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) or DKLA (Design Kit License Agreement)."
- 要点：设计芯片需要代工厂的"设计套件"（含设计规则、电气参数、标准单元库），要先签保密协议或许可协议才能拿到。

### E3

- 位置：Fabricating and Packaging Prototypes
- 原文："After the design phase has finished, the results can be submitted via the Design registration system. This will initiate a verification process, involving Electrical Rule Checking (ERC) and Design Rule Checking (DRC)." / "the corrected specifications will be delivered to the foundry where prototypes will be fabricated during an MPW or a mini@sic run. Finally, the resulting prototypes will be shipped to the customer for the test and validation phase."
- 要点：芯片原型的流程：设计完成后提交，经电气规则检查和设计规则检查，交给代工厂在某一轮多项目晶圆上制造，最后把原型寄回给设计者测试验证。

### E4

- 位置：Fabricating and Packaging Prototypes
- 原文："Europractice usually delivers unpackeged and untested prototypes."
- 要点：寄回的原型通常未封装、未测试，测试要由设计者自己完成。
