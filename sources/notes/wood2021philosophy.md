# Wood 2021：Philosophy and Christian Theology（斯坦福哲学百科全书）

- 资料：`wood2021philosophy`，[原文](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/christiantheology-philosophy/)
- 版本：《斯坦福哲学百科全书》条目，作者 William Wood（牛津大学神学系），2021 年 10 月 15 日首次发布。只讨论基督教神学与哲学的关系；条目列出历史上几种看待两者关系的模式（整合、合作、分离、冲突等），"合作说"是其中一种。位置写节号。

### E1

- 位置：§1 开头
- 原文："Similarly, with respect to their methods of inquiry, philosophers and theologians alike interpret authoritative texts, deploy arguments, and marshal evidence to support their conclusions."
- 要点：在研究方法上，哲学家和神学家都解读权威文本、提出论证、组织证据来支持结论。

### E2

- 位置：§1 开头
- 原文："Here one might insist that Christian theological claims are grounded by appeals to “faith” or “authority”, whereas philosophical claims are grounded by appeals to “reason”. This contrast is promising when suitably developed, but it is not as sharp as one might initially suppose."
- 要点：常见说法是神学诉诸"信仰"或"权威"，哲学诉诸"理性"；作者认为这个对比有道理，但没有想象中那么分明。

### E3

- 位置：§1.2.1 Cooperation
- 原文："On the Cooperation account, theology differs from philosophy chiefly because theology assumes the truth of divine revelation, whereas philosophy does not."
- 要点：按"合作说"，神学和哲学的主要区别是神学预设神圣启示为真，哲学不预设。

### E4

- 位置：§1.2.1 Cooperation
- 原文："Theologians can appeal to revelation—scripture and authoritative Church tradition—in order to generate new lines of inquiry, and can treat revealed truths as evidence in their investigations. For their part, philosophers must appeal only to premises and evidence that are in principle available to any rational inquirer."
- 要点：按合作说，神学家可以诉诸启示（经文和权威的教会传统），把它当证据；哲学家只能诉诸原则上任何理性探究者都能获得的前提和证据。
