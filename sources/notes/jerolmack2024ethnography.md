# Jerolmack, Murphy & Reyes 2024：Ethnography in the open science and digital age（社论）

- 资料：`jerolmack2024ethnography`，[PMC 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10978767/)（Frontiers in Sociology 9:1392012，doi:10.3389/fsoc.2024.1392012）
- 版本：2024 年 3 月发表的专题社论，开放获取（CC BY）。三位作者是美国的社会学者，为一组关于民族志与开放科学、数字田野、田野伦理的专题文章写的导言。只有一页，位置写段落。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："Academic gatekeepers are increasingly demanding that fieldnotes, interview transcripts, and other data be shared in the name of “open science.” Popular ethnographies have had their scholarly rigor impugned by journalists and the public."
- 要点：学术把关者越来越多地以"开放科学"之名要求共享田野笔记、访谈转录稿等数据；一些有名的民族志著作的学术严谨性受到记者和公众质疑。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："While the “replication crisis” in social science has catalyzed a movement for transparency (e.g., registering hypotheses in advance; sharing data), it is not clear that positivistic standards of verification translate to the interpretive enterprise of ethnography"
- 要点：社会科学的"可重复性危机"催生了透明化运动（如事先登记假设、共享数据），但实证主义的核验标准能否套用到解释性的民族志上，并不清楚。

### E3

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Some, like Lubet (2018), contend that ethnographers must name sources, fact-check, and perhaps even share raw data to secure readers' trust and facilitate falsifiability"
- 要点：一方（如 Lubet 2018）认为民族志研究者必须说出信息来源、做事实核查，甚至共享原始数据，才能赢得读者信任、让结论可被证伪。

### E4

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："some (e.g., Reyes, 2018; Stuart, 2020) warn that demands for open science may further marginalize scholars who study vulnerable populations (where data transparency is dangerous) or who lack the resources that facilitate data transparency (e.g., hiring fact checkers)."
- 要点：另一方（如 Reyes 2018、Stuart 2020）警告：开放科学的要求可能进一步边缘化研究弱势人群的学者（对这些人群，数据透明是危险的），以及缺乏资源（如雇事实核查员）的学者。

### E5

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Between these poles, others have floated flexible “standards for transparency that are consistent” with ethnographers' “commitment to their subjects and interpretive scholarship” (Murphy et al., 2021, p. 41)—e.g., partial disclosure of people or places, sharing the coding scheme, or online appendices with supplementary data"
- 要点：两极之间，有人提出灵活的透明标准，与对研究对象的承诺和解释性学术相容，例如部分披露人或地点、共享编码方案、用在线附录提供补充数据。

### E6

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："As social life is increasingly lived online, it becomes unclear where the boundaries of the “field site” should be drawn and whether ethnographic conventions—methodological and ethical—are directly transferrable to the study of digital spaces"
- 要点：社会生活越来越多地发生在网上，"田野"的边界该划在哪里、民族志在方法和伦理上的惯例能否直接搬到数字空间，都不清楚了。

### E7

- 位置：第 5 段
- 原文："There can be no “one size fits all” answer to these developments and debates."
- 要点：这些争论没有"一刀切"的答案。

### E8

- 位置：第 6 段
- 原文："Khan et al. contend that the college students whose sexual practices they studied would be less likely to disclose personal details, and that so much information would have to be masked to maintain confidentiality that the remaining data would be meaningless."
- 要点：专题中 Khan 等人认为：如果要公开数据，他们研究其性行为的大学生就不太愿意吐露个人细节；而且为了保密要遮掉的信息太多，剩下的数据将毫无意义。
