# 美国化学安全与危害调查委员会 2011：Texas Tech University Laboratory Explosion（案例研究）

- 资料：`csb2011texastech`，[原文](https://www.csb.gov/assets/1/20/CSB_Study_TTU_.pdf)（事件页面：https://www.csb.gov/texas-tech-university-chemistry-lab-explosion/ ）
- 版本：美国化学安全与危害调查委员会（U.S. Chemical Safety and Hazard Investigation Board，CSB）案例研究，编号 2010-05-I-TX，2011 年发布。美国联邦政府出版物，官网免费下载。2026-10-06 用 curl 下载 PDF，pdftotext 提取。事件发生在得克萨斯理工大学化学与生物化学系（不是化学工程系）。

### E1

- 位置：§1.0 Introduction，第 1 页
- 原文："On January 7, 2010, a graduate student within the Chemistry and Biochemistry Department at Texas Tech University (Texas Tech) lost three fingers, his hands and face were burned, and one of his eyes was injured after the chemical he was working with detonated."
- 要点：2010 年 1 月 7 日，得克萨斯理工大学化学与生物化学系一名研究生在处理的化合物发生爆炸，失去三根手指，手和脸烧伤，一只眼睛受伤。

### E2

- 位置：§4.0 Incident Description，第 3 页
- 原文："they decided to scale-up the synthesis of NHP to make approximately 10 grams. [...] The PIs of the research were not consulted on the decision to scale up."
- 要点：学生决定把这种含能化合物的合成量放大到约 10 克，放大的决定没有征求两位课题负责人的意见。

### E3

- 位置：§4.0 Incident Description，第 4 页
- 原文："Based on experience, the two students had discovered that smaller amounts of the compound would not ignite or explode on impact when wet with water or hexane, and they assumed the hazards of larger quantities of NHP would be controlled in a similar manner."
- 要点：学生凭经验发现少量化合物用水或己烷润湿后受撞击不会着火或爆炸，就假定量大时也能同样控制住危险。

### E4

- 位置：§3.0 Pre-incident Events，第 3 页
- 原文："Safety restrictions, such as a 100 milligram limit on the amount of compound permitted to be synthesized, were verbally communicated"
- 要点：合成量不超过 100 毫克这类安全限制，只是口头传达的。

### E5

- 位置：§3.0 Pre-incident Events，第 3 页
- 原文："none of the students the CSB interviewed as part of its investigation, including those directly involved in the incident, stated they were aware of a strict 100 milligram limit."
- 要点：调查委员会访谈的学生（包括直接当事人）都没有说自己知道有严格的 100 毫克上限。

### E6

- 位置：§4.0 Incident Description，第 4 页
- 原文："No formal hazard evaluation13 was conducted to analyze the effectiveness of using either water or hexane to mitigate the potential explosive hazards associated with the quantity of NHP synthesized the day of the incident."
- 要点：对于当天合成的量，没有做正式的危害评估来分析用水或己烷能不能控制爆炸危险。（原文"evaluation"后的 13 是脚注编号。）

### E7

- 位置：§4.0 脚注 13，第 4 页
- 原文："A hazard evaluation is an organized effort to identify and analyze the significance of hazardous situations associated with a process or activity (CCPS, 2008, p. 15)"
- 要点：危害评估是有组织地识别和分析某个过程或活动中危险情形有多严重的工作。

### E8

- 位置：§6.2 Hazard Determination，第 8 页
- 原文："While not all standards and practices used in the chemical process industry can be directly applied to an academic laboratory environment, drawing applicable parallels to harness the field's experience and knowledge of hazardous activities involving chemicals can be useful."
- 要点：化工过程行业的标准和做法不能全部直接搬到学校实验室，但借鉴其中适用的经验是有用的。

### E9

- 位置：§9.0 Key Lessons，第 3 条，第 17 页
- 原文："Current standards on hazard evaluations, risk assessments, and hazard mitigation are geared toward industrial settings and are not fully transferable to the academic research laboratory environment."
- 要点：现有的危害评估、风险评估和危害控制标准面向工业环境，不能完全照搬到学校研究实验室。

### E10

- 位置：§9.0 Key Lessons，第 4 条，第 17 页
- 原文："Research-specific written protocols and training are necessary to manage laboratory research risk."
- 要点：管理实验室研究风险，需要针对具体研究的书面规程和培训。

### E11

- 位置：§9.0 Key Lessons，第 6 条，第 17 页
- 原文："Near-misses and previous incidents provide opportunities for education and improvement only if they are documented, tracked, and communicated to drive safety change."
- 要点：未遂事件和以往事故，只有被记录、追踪、传达，才能成为学习和改进的机会。

### E12

- 位置：§7.0 Lessons Not Learned From Previous Incidents and Near-Misses，第 13 页
- 原文："The second previous incident involved a scale-up situation within the same research groups; a student unintentionally used the wrong units of measure and created an excess of a known energetic material."
- 要点：约三年前同一课题组发生过一次放大相关的未遂事件：学生用错了计量单位，做出了过量的已知含能材料。

### E13

- 位置：§7.0，第 13 页
- 原文："the near-miss was not reported to anyone outside of the research groups."
- 要点：那次未遂事件没有向课题组以外的任何人报告。

### E14

- 位置：§6.2.3 Lack of Formal Documentation and Communication，第 12 页
- 原文："Prior to the incident, weekly group meetings between students and PIs were held, but the focus was primarily on experimental results, not actual research activities and the safety implications of the work."
- 要点：事故前每周都有组会，但主要谈实验结果，不谈实际怎么做和工作的安全影响。

### E15

- 位置：§4.0 脚注 7，第 3 页
- 原文："Laboratory notebooks were not dated and did not always indicate amounts of reactants used during synthesis."
- 要点：实验记录本没有写日期，也不总是记下合成时用的反应物用量。

### E16

- 位置：§4.0 Incident Description，第 4 页
- 原文："Several individuals from the lab indicated that the decision to wear goggles was a personal choice which they based on how dangerous an activity was perceived to be."
- 要点：实验室里几个人说，戴不戴护目镜是个人选择，取决于自己觉得操作有多危险。

### E17

- 位置：3.0 Pre-incident Events，第 3 页
- 原文："Safety restrictions, such as a 100 milligram limit on the amount of compound permitted to be synthesized, were verbally communicated by the two Principal Investigators (PIs) of the research to some students."
- 要点：每次最多合成 100 毫克这类安全限制，只是两位课题负责人口头告诉了部分学生。

### E18

- 位置：第 3 页脚注 7
- 原文："The events leading up to the January 7 incident were documented mainly with interviews conducted by the CSB because little written documentation was available." / "Laboratory notebooks were not dated and did not always indicate amounts of reactants used during synthesis."
- 要点：事故经过主要靠 CSB 的访谈还原，因为书面记录很少；实验记录本没有写日期，也不总是记下合成时用了多少反应物。

### E19

- 位置：6.2.3 Lack of Formal Documentation and Communication，第 13 页
- 原文："Lab notebooks, often seen as a primary means of documenting research plans and activities, were not consistently reviewed by PIs and varied greatly among students"
- 要点：记录本本应是记录研究计划和活动的主要手段，但课题负责人并不定期查看，学生之间记法差别很大。

### E20

- 位置：6.2.3，第 13 页
- 原文："weekly group meetings between students and PIs were held, but the focus was primarily on experimental results, not actual research activities and the safety implications of the work."
- 要点：每周组会主要讨论实验结果，不讨论实际操作和它的安全影响。

### E21

- 位置：7.0 Lessons Not Learned From Previous Incidents and Near-Misses，第 13～14 页
- 原文："a student unintentionally used the wrong units of measure and created an excess of a known energetic material."
- 要点：此前同一课题组有学生用错单位，做出了过量的已知含能材料（这次是未遂事件）。

### E22

- 位置：6.2.2 No Training for Research-Specific Safety Risks，第 12 页
- 原文："the safety training needs of undergraduates, who often perform "cookbook" laboratory work with well-understood and defined risks, differ greatly from those of students performing research"
- 要点：本科生多做风险已知、照着做的实验，做研究的学生需要的安全训练和他们很不一样。

### E23

- 位置：6.2.3，第 13 页
- 原文："Students in the energetic labs were not required to obtain permission or seek approval from their PIs prior to changing research experiment variables, including subjectively determined "small" increases of material synthesized and temperature variations."
- 要点：学生改实验条件（包括自己判断的"少量"增加合成量、改温度）之前，不需要征得课题负责人同意。

### E24

- 位置：第 3 页脚注 6
- 原文："At a university, the PI is typically a professor in charge of the research, students, postdoctoral researchers, and technicians working in the laboratory."
- 要点：在大学里，课题负责人一般是负责这项研究以及实验室里学生、博士后和技术员的教授。

### E25

- 位置：7.0，第 14 页
- 原文："Entities looking to improve safety should encourage the reporting of such incidents, even when injuries or damage do not result"
- 要点：想改进安全的单位，应鼓励报告这类事件，即使没有造成伤害或损失。
