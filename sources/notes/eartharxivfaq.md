# EarthArXiv：Frequently Asked Questions

- 资料：`eartharxivfaq`，[原文](https://eartharxiv.github.io/faq.html)
- 版本：EarthArXiv 常见问题页面（CC BY 2.0），2026-10-06 读取。部分问答写于 AGU 推出预印本平台 ESSOAr 前后，内容未注明日期。位置写问题标题。

### E1

- 位置：How might preprints and post-prints (peer reviewed articles) affect citations?
- 原文："You will be unable to delete the preprint file, but you can update or modify it."
- 要点：预印本发出后不能删除，只能更新。

### E2

- 位置：How might preprints and post-prints (peer reviewed articles) affect citations?
- 原文："The preprint and the peer reviewed article are considered distinct works - thus, unique DOIs - and citations can be made to either."
- 要点：预印本和正式发表的论文是两个作品，各有 DOI，都可以被引用。

### E3

- 位置：How do I know if the journal I am submitting to will not object to a preprint/postprint
- 原文："we strongly encourage authors to also check the specific copyright and sharing information provided by their journal of choice."
- 要点：要自己查目标期刊的版权和共享政策。

### E4

- 位置：How do I know if the journal I am submitting to will not object to a preprint/postprint
- 原文："if you have submitted your manuscript to EarthArXiv after submitting it to a journal, and the journal rejects publication of your manuscript your published preprint will remain public on EarthArXiv."
- 要点：期刊拒稿后，预印本仍然公开。

### E5

- 位置：How do I know if the journal I am submitting to will not object to a preprint/postprint
- 原文："check that all the journals that your article might cascade through before ultimately being published accept preprints"
- 要点：发预印本前，把可能依次投的所有期刊都查一遍，确认它们接受预印本。

### E6

- 位置：Is there now a competition between ESSOAr and EarthArXiv?
- 原文："The AGU's Earth and Space Science Open Archive (ESSOAr) presents an alternative place to publish."
- 要点：AGU 的地球与空间科学开放存档（ESSOAr）是另一个可选的预印本平台。

### E7

- 位置：Can I submit related materials along with my paper, e.g. data and code?
- 原文："EarthArXiv does not currently host the supplementary materials, but we do provide options to link your preprint to materials hosted elsewhere."
- 要点：EarthArXiv 不存补充材料，数据和代码要放到别处再链接。
