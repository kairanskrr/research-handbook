# Ofosu & Posner 2023：Pre-Analysis Plans: An Early Stocktaking

- 资料：`ofosu2023preanalysis`，[原文 PDF（LSE 机构库）](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/112747/)
- 版本：Perspectives on Politics 21(1): 174–190，2023（2021 年在线发表），开放获取（CC BY 4.0）。位置写小节。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："We analyze a representative sample of 195 PAPs registered on the Evidence in Governance and Politics (EGAP) and American Economic Association (AEA) registration platforms to assess whether PAPs registered in the early days of pre-registration (2011 [...] 2016)"
- 要点：作者分析了在 EGAP 和 AEA 两个登记平台上登记的 195 份预分析计划的代表性样本（登记时间 2011–2016 年）。

### E2

- 位置：引言
- 原文："Graduate students are now taught that registering a PAP is a de rigeur part of undertaking their research projects."
- 要点：作者观察到，研究生现在被教导：登记预分析计划是做研究项目的必备环节。

### E3

- 位置：Empirical Approach
- 原文："The overwhelming majority of the 195 PAPs we coded were from field (63%), survey (27%), or lab (4%) experiments; observational studies comprised just 4% of our sample."
- 要点：样本中 63% 是田野实验，27% 是调查实验，4% 是实验室实验，观察性研究只占 4%。

### E4

- 位置：Empirical Approach
- 原文："The survey was conducted in 2018"
- 要点：作者另做的研究者调查在 2018 年进行。

### E5

- 位置：Empirical Approach
- 原文："was sent to all affiliated researchers in the EGAP and Innovations for Poverty Action (IPA) research networks (N=664). We received 155 responses"
- 要点：调查发给 EGAP 和扶贫行动创新组织（IPA）两个网络的全部 664 名研究者，收回 155 份。

### E6

- 位置：Do PAPs Reduce the Scope for Fishing?
- 原文："In 44% of PAPs, the number of pre-specified control variables was judged to be unclear"
- 要点：44% 的预分析计划没有写清要放哪些控制变量。

### E7

- 位置：Do PAPs Reduce the Scope for Fishing?
- 原文："However, in the fourteen instances in our sample where deviations occurred, the change was noted in only one case."
- 要点：样本中 14 篇论文的模型偏离了计划，只有 1 篇说明了这种改动。

### E8

- 位置：Do PAPs Reduce the Scope for Fishing?
- 原文："Twenty-five percent of PAPs specified how they would deal with missing values or attrition; 13% specified how they would deal with noncompliance"
- 要点：只有 25% 的计划写了怎样处理缺失值或样本流失，13% 写了怎样处理不依从。

### E9

- 位置：Do PAPs Reduce the Scope for Post-Hoc Hypothesis Adjustment?
- 原文："the median paper in our sample neglected to report 25% of the hypotheses that had been pre-specified in the PAP."
- 要点：样本中位数论文漏报了计划中 25% 的假设。

### E10

- 位置：Do PAPs Reduce the Scope for Post-Hoc Hypothesis Adjustment?
- 原文："But authors that presented results based on hypotheses that were not pre-registered failed to mention this in 82% of cases."
- 要点：报告了未登记假设的论文中，82% 没有说明这些假设不在计划里。

### E11

- 位置：Objections to PAPs: Too Time Consuming
- 原文："Eighty-eight percent of researchers in our potential PAP users' survey reported devoting a week or more to writing the PAP for a typical project"
- 要点：在这项 2018 年调查中，88% 的研究者说一个典型项目的预分析计划要写一周或更久。

### E12

- 位置：Objections to PAPs: Too Time Consuming
- 原文："when there is a limited window of opportunity to initiate an experiment before an election takes place or a new policy comes into force--such delays can make it impossible to undertake the project at all"
- 要点：赶在选举或新政策生效前做实验时，写计划造成的延误可能让项目做不成。

### E13

- 位置：Objections to PAPs: Too Time Consuming
- 原文："64% agreed with the statement that "it takes a considerable amount of time, but it is worth it.""
- 要点：64% 的受访研究者同意"很花时间，但值得"。

### E14

- 位置：Objections to PAPs: Limit Flexibility and Scope for New Discoveries
- 原文："The more common approach--and the approach we advocate--is to freely undertake exploratory investigations that go beyond the PAP, clearly labeling the results of such investigations in the paper as coming from analyses that were not pre-specified, with an explanation provided for why they were added."
- 要点：作者主张：可以自由做计划外的探索分析，但要在论文中明确标出哪些不是预先设定的，并解释为什么加。
