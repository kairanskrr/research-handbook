# Freeman 2013：How to do research

- 资料：`freeman2013research`，[原文 PDF](https://people.csail.mit.edu/billf/publications/How_To_Do_Research.pdf)
- 版本：讲义，2013-03-06，共 3 页。页码为 PDF 页序。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 页
- 原文："advice can go on a bumper sticker: “Slow down to speed up”."
- 要点：第一条建议："慢下来才能快"。

### E2

- 位置：第 1 页
- 原文："Take things slowly. Verify your assumptions. [...] As you do experiments, only change one thing at a time, so you know what the outcome of the experiment means."
- 要点：研究里没人告诉你正确答案，所以要放慢、核实假设，每次实验只改一个地方，才知道结果说明什么。

### E3

- 位置：第 1 页
- 原文："Please don’t tell me “it doesn’t work”. [...] What I want to hear instead is something like, “I’ve narrowed down the problem to step B."
- 要点：不要只说"不行"，要说明已经把问题缩小到哪一步、排除了哪些原因。

### E4

- 位置：第 1 页
- 原文："It’s the simplest toy model that captures the main idea."
- 要点：找能抓住核心思想的最简单玩具模型（toy model），用它建立直觉。

### E5

- 位置：第 1 页
- 原文："But we also need a rudder for steering–that’s stepping back periodically to make sure we’re working on the right thing."
- 要点：除了埋头苦干，还要"掌舵"：定期退后一步，确认自己在做对的事。

### E6

- 位置：第 3 页
- 原文："Is there a simpler case we can make it work?"
- 要点：遇到失败时追问：能不能先在更简单的情形下让它成功？

### E7

- 位置：第 3 页
- 原文："Progress can include: “I’ve shown why this doesn’t work”, [...] or “I spent the whole time reading because I know I have to understand this before I can make any progress.”"
- 要点：进展的含义很宽：说明了为什么不行、把任务简化到能跑通、为理解问题而读书，都算进展。

### E8

- 位置：第 2 页
- 原文："The strong student starts doing what the advisor has asked, sees that it doesn’t work, looks around within some epsilon ball of the original proposal to [...] what does work, and reports that solution."
- 要点：弱学生照导师的方案做，失败了就汇报失败；强学生发现不行后，会在原方案附近找能行的做法，再汇报这个解法。

### E9

- 位置：第 3 页
- 原文："I love to hear about progress when I meet with students, but note that I have a very general notion of progress."
- 要点：导师对"进展"的理解很宽：说明为什么不行、简化问题、读书弄懂背景都算。

### E10

- 位置：第 3 页
- 原文："Please don’t hide from me. Let’s talk. I like it when you track me down and insist that we talk"
- 要点：不要躲着导师；导师出差时也欢迎学生主动找上门要求谈话。
