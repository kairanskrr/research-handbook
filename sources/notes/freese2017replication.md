# Freese & Peterson 2017：Replication in Social Science

- 资料：`freese2017replication`，[SocArXiv 预印本](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/5bck9)（正式版：Annual Review of Sociology 43:147–165，doi:10.1146/annurev-soc-060116-053450）
- 版本：作者 2017 年 5 月发布在 SocArXiv 上的稿件（与正式发表版的页码不同，表格未附）。作者是美国斯坦福大学和西北大学的社会学者。位置写小节标题。注意：这是 2017 年的综述，文中说"社会学还没有相应的运动"，之后已有期刊出台复现政策（见 `sociologicalsciencepolicy`）。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Across the medical and social sciences, new discussions about replication have been transforming research practices. Sociologists, however, have been largely absent from these discussions."
- 要点：医学和社会科学关于可重复性的讨论正在改变研究做法，但社会学者大体缺席。

### E2

- 位置：引言
- 原文："A passing glance at sociology might even suggest that replication has become a vanishing art: sociology’s two flagship journals have published 27 articles over their history with “replication” in the title, but only one in the last 25 years."
- 要点：社会学两本旗舰期刊历史上标题含"replication"的文章共 27 篇，近 25 年只有 1 篇。

### E3

- 位置：Forms of Replication in Quantitative Social Science
- 原文："(1) Verifiability involves taking the results of an original study as the object of inquiry and typically asks limited questions regarding whether the same results are obtained by doing the same analyses on the same data. (2) Tests of robustness conduct a reanalysis on the original data using alternative specifications to see if the target finding is merely the result of analytic decisions. (3) Tests of repeatability involve collecting new data to determine whether key result of a study can be observed by using the original procedures to collect new data. Finally, (4) for inquiries into generalizability, the original study provides a premise for other work that trying to evaluate whether similar findings may be consistently observed across different methods or settings."
- 要点：定量社会科学里的"重复"分四种：可核验性（同样数据、同样分析能否得到同样结果）、稳健性（换一种分析设定结果还在不在）、可重复性（按原程序收集新数据能否再观察到）、可推广性（在不同方法或场景下是否一致）。

### E4

- 位置：Verifiability
- 原文："In quantitative social science, data are usually not collected by a study’s authors, and a complicated chain of actors may separate the origins of data from its analysis. For instance, skepticism of findings by McPherson et al. (2006) about increasing social isolation in the United States led to the discovery of 41 people which the survey center providing the data had incorrectly coded as having no confidants (McPherson et al 2008)."
- 要点：定量社会科学的数据通常不是论文作者自己收集的，数据来源和分析之间隔着一长串环节。例子：有人质疑"美国人越来越孤立"的结论，结果发现提供数据的调查中心把 41 人错误编码为"没有可倾诉的人"。

### E5

- 位置：Verifiability
- 原文："The convention in sociology has largely been to treat the availability of data for verification as an ethical matter to be handled by individual requests." / "In sociology, Young and Horvath (2015) reported more than half of authors have refused to provide data or code to graduate students trying to verify 53 findings as a class exercise."
- 要点：社会学的惯例大多是把"提供数据供核验"当作个人伦理问题，靠个别索取处理。Young 和 Horvath（2015）报告：研究生在课堂练习中尝试核验 53 项结果，超过一半的作者拒绝提供数据或代码。

### E6

- 位置：Verifiability
- 原文："Authors are usually urged to deposit data in independent repositories, developed and maintained by archiving professionals, rather than leaving long-term data availability contingent on authors maintaining their personal websites. Major repositories include the Open Science Framework, OpenICPSR, and Harvard’s Dataverse initiative."
- 要点：作者通常被敦促把数据存进由专业人员维护的独立存储库，而不是放在个人网站上；主要存储库有 Open Science Framework、openICPSR、哈佛 Dataverse。

### E7

- 位置：Verifiability
- 原文："The larger point is that good reasons may be given for why data cannot be made publicly available, but it is hard to argue against expecting researchers to be transparent about what the conditions of data availability are, so that readers can know whether work might be verified independently in principle when assessing its credibility."
- 要点：数据不能公开可以有正当理由，但研究者至少应说清数据在什么条件下可得，让读者知道这项研究原则上能否被独立核验。

### E8

- 位置：Verifiability
- 原文："documentation that makes work transparent to others is likely the best way of ensuring it will be able to be understood by collaborators and, perhaps most urgently, by oneself later in the long gestation that characterizes many research projects"
- 要点：让别人看得懂的记录，也是让合作者、尤其是让自己在漫长的研究周期后仍能看懂的最好办法；许多研究项目的周期很长。

### E9

- 位置：Robustness
- 原文："An abiding concern is that published findings represent a best-case scenario among all the arbitrary and debatable decisions made over the course of analyzing data. This might be due to “phacking,” where a researcher runs different analyses until they find support for their preferred hypothesis (Simonsohn et al. 2014), or to “HARKing” in which hypotheses presented as a priori were actually developed after results were known (Kerr 1998)."
- 要点：一直存在的担忧是：发表的结果只是分析中众多可争议选择里"最好看"的那一种，原因可能是 p-hacking（反复换分析直到支持想要的假设），或 HARKing（看到结果后才提出、却写成事先提出的假设）。

### E10

- 位置：Generalizability
- 原文："For example, the General Social Survey keeps the same wording of core items from year after year, and the International Social Survey Programme coordinates the asking of the same survey questions across member nations." / "All these follow, in different ways, a dictum we have heard attributed to Otis Dudley Duncan, “If you want to measure change, don’t change the measure.”"
- 要点：美国综合社会调查（General Social Survey）每年保持核心题目的措辞不变；国际社会调查项目在各成员国问同样的问题。这些做法遵循一句据说出自 Duncan 的格言："想测量变化，就别改变测量。"

### E11

- 位置：Generalizability
- 原文："Most quantitative social science uses data that were not collected by the authors of the paper, and were instead assembled as large-scale public goods for a research community."
- 要点：大多数定量社会科学研究用的数据不是作者自己收集的，而是作为研究共同体的大型公共资源建立起来的。

### E12

- 位置：Generalizability
- 原文："Our own impression is that, when psychologists are faced with divergent results from studies asking the same question, they tend to first consider how the designs of the two studies are different, whereas sociologists in the same situation tend to first consider how the samples for the two studies differ."
- 要点：作者的印象是：面对同一问题的不同结果，心理学家倾向于先看两项研究的设计有何不同，社会学家倾向于先看两项研究的样本有何不同。

### E13

- 位置：Generalizability
- 原文："Experimental psychologists regularly use deliberately non-representative samples that are only interesting because they are presented as broadly illuminating about generalized human psychological processes; sociology focuses more on population samples or historical cases for which results might be taken as instructive even if they do not generalize farther than their target population."
- 要点：实验心理学常用有意不具代表性的样本，研究的是一般的人类心理过程；社会学更关注总体样本或历史案例，即使结果不能推广到目标总体之外，也被认为有启发。

### E14

- 位置：Generalizability
- 原文："To the extent this is true, what empirical work tests is not so much propositions as such but ideas about the scope in which the propositions apply."
- 要点：如果一般性社会学命题在某些条件下成立、在另一些条件下不成立，那么实证研究检验的与其说是命题本身，不如说是命题适用的范围。

### E15

- 位置：Replication in Qualitative Social Science
- 原文："Qualitative social science is often presented as firstly an interpretive enterprise. In sociology, it is typically concerned with issues of motivation and meaning that give the verstehen approach its lasting appeal. It may be that “replication” is simply the wrong language to apply to qualitative studies."
- 要点：定性社会科学常被视为首先是一种解释性的事业，在社会学里通常关心动机和意义；"重复"这个说法也许根本不适合用在定性研究上。

### E16

- 位置：Replication in Qualitative Social Science
- 原文："Fieldwork methods often involve considerations of confidentiality that prompt researchers to use pseudonyms and even change details to prevent identification of parties or settings, which can strongly limit possibilities for fact-checking."
- 要点：田野研究出于保密，常用化名甚至改动细节，防止人和地点被认出，这会大大限制事实核查的可能。

### E17

- 位置：Replication in Qualitative Social Science
- 原文："Murphy and Jerolmack (2016) note an example in which Bosk (2008) reflected on his classic ethnography of medical errors from three decades earlier. Because he changed the gender of the only female medical resident in the group he studied, others were not able to consider whether gender had shaped interactions."
- 要点：例子：Bosk 在一项关于医疗差错的经典民族志里，改掉了研究对象中唯一一位女住院医师的性别，结果别人无法考察性别是否影响了互动。

### E18

- 位置：Replication in Qualitative Social Science
- 原文："Desmond (2016) recently offers the intriguing alternative of hiring an independent, named factchecker who was given access to otherwise confidential materials."
- 要点：Desmond（2016）的做法是：聘请一位独立、公开署名的事实核查员，让其查看原本保密的材料。

### E19

- 位置：Replication in Qualitative Social Science
- 原文："He advocates an iterative procedure in which qualitative researchers “replicate” observations through a number of distinct cases (following language of Yin [2002]). After each case, the research questions evolve to become more sophisticated. The process ends once the researcher subjectively feels “saturated” and believes that additional sites will yield little new information."
- 要点：Small（2009）主张迭代的做法：在多个不同案例中"重复"观察，每做完一个案例，研究问题就变得更精细；当研究者主观上觉得"饱和"、认为再增加地点也得不到多少新信息时，过程结束。

### E20

- 位置：Replication in Qualitative Social Science
- 原文："Unlike most forms of quantitative data, fieldnotes lose much of their value when divorced from the notetaker."
- 要点：和大多数定量数据不同，田野笔记一旦脱离记录者，就失去了大部分价值。

### E21

- 位置：Conclusion，Replication policy in sociology
- 原文："At the same time, many sociologists pride ourselves on the way that being the most undisciplined social science promotes creativity." / "Code and data sharing guidelines for quantitative sociology should move forward even if there is no counterpart for qualitative sociology."
- 要点：许多社会学者以本学科是"最不守规矩的社会科学"、因而有创造力为傲；作者主张，定量社会学的代码和数据共享规范应向前推进，即使定性社会学还没有对应的规范。
