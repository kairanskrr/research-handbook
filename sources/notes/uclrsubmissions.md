# 芝加哥大学法律评论：投稿须知

- 资料：`uclrsubmissions`，[原文网页](https://lawreview.uchicago.edu/submissions)
- 版本：2026 年 10 月 6 日读取。《芝加哥大学法律评论》（University of Chicago Law Review）是美国芝加哥大学法学院的学生编辑期刊。位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Submissions to The Law Review
- 原文："accepts submissions through Scholastica."
- 要点：该刊通过 Scholastica（一个投稿管理平台）收稿。

### E2

- 位置：Selection
- 原文："The Law Review occasionally solicits feedback on submissions from scholars who are expert in their field."
- 要点：该刊有时会请领域专家对来稿提意见。

### E3

- 位置：Expedited Review
- 原文："Please let us know if you have received an offer of publication from another journal and would like us to expedite our review of your article."
- 要点：如果已收到别的期刊的录用，可以请求该刊加快审阅。

### E4

- 位置：Withdrawal
- 原文："We appreciate if you are able to let us know that you have decided to withdraw your article from consideration."
- 要点：决定撤稿时，希望作者告知。

### E5

- 位置：Light Edit Policy
- 原文："The editors believe strongly that both the argument and the voice of a manuscript should be the creations of the author."
- 要点：该刊奉行"轻度编辑"，认为论证和文风应属于作者。
