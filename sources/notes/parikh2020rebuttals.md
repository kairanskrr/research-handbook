# Parikh, Batra & Lee 2020：How we write rebuttals

- 资料：`parikh2020rebuttals`，[原文](https://deviparikh.substack.com/p/how-we-write-rebuttals-dc84742fece1)
- 版本：博客文章，2020。基于人工智能领域会议的经验。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："The core guiding principle is that the rebuttal should be thorough, direct, and easy for the Reviewers and Area Chair (RACs) to follow."
- 要点：回复的核心原则：全面、直接、让审稿人和领域主席容易看懂。

### E2

- 位置：谁读回复
- 原文："The AC, who is likely even less familiar with your work, and a good guiding principle is to assume that all they will read is the set of reviews and the rebuttal."
- 要点：回复有两类读者：审稿人和领域主席（AC）；后者可能只读审稿意见和回复。

### E3

- 位置：谁读回复
- 原文："In our experience, most new members of the research community focus on (1), but ignore (2)."
- 要点：新手往往只顾审稿人，忽略了做决定的领域主席。

### E4

- 位置：谁读回复
- 原文："would a neutral third-party be able to tell if the reviewer concerns were addressed purely based on your rebuttal (without reading the paper or the reviews again)?"
- 要点：检验标准：一个中立的第三方只读你的回复，能否判断审稿人的问题是否已解决。

### E5

- 位置：写作流程
- 原文："Itemize reviewer comments. We use a handy spreadsheet to organize individual comments/questions/concerns raised by each reviewer."
- 要点：先把每位审稿人的每条意见逐条列进表格，找出共同问题、避免遗漏。

### E6

- 位置：写作流程
- 原文："Write concisely but don’t worry about space. Cover every point and trim / prioritize them later."
- 要点：初稿先覆盖每一点，再按重要性删减到篇幅限制内。

### E7

- 位置：Tip 2
- 原文："Tip 2. Order matters. Start with the biggest concerns that you have good answers for and work your way to less clear-cut responses and minor points."
- 要点：先回答你有把握的最大问题，再到不那么明确的问题和小问题。

### E8

- 位置：Tip 3
- 原文："Tip 3. Let reviewers speak for themselves, then respond directly. Quote the core of the reviewer’s question or concern concisely and completely. Then before saying anything else, respond to it directly."
- 要点：先简要引用审稿人的问题，然后第一句就直接回答，再展开解释。

### E9

- 位置：Tip 12
- 原文："Tip 12. Stats speak louder than words. Rather than argue with RACs, give them data/stats to back your claim up."
- 要点：与其争论，不如拿数据说话。

### E10

- 位置：Tip 13
- 原文："Tip 13. Don’t promise, do. Instead of saying “We will discuss Singh et al. in the paper.”, provide a discussion in the rebuttal."
- 要点：不要只承诺“会加上”，直接在回复里把内容写出来。

### E11

- 位置：Tip 15
- 原文："Tip 15. Be transparent."
- 要点：做不到的（例如会议不允许新实验、算力不够）就如实说明。

### E12

- 位置：Tip 9
- 原文："Tip 9. But get credit for details you already included. That said, if something a reviewer asked for was already in the paper, say so."
- 要点：审稿人要求的内容论文里已经有，就指出位置并在回复中复述。

### E13

- 位置：Tip 1
- 原文："Tip 1. Start positive. Provide a summary of the reviews, highlighting positive things that reviewers said about your work."
- 要点：开头先总结审稿意见中的正面评价，别让读者忘了论文的优点。

### E14

- 位置：Tip 18
- 原文："So decide whether you’d like to be argumentative and risk your reviewer taking a strong stand against the paper, or if you’d like to work towards a common ground."
- 要点：回复也是人与人的交流：争辩可能让审稿人更坚定地反对，寻找共识更有效。

### E15

- 位置：Tip 16
- 原文："Tip 16. Shine a spotlight on reviewers acting in bad-faith."
- 要点：对明显不负责任或无依据的审稿意见，可以指出来，让其他人适当降低其权重。

### E16

- 位置：开头（引 Bill Freeman 的比喻）
- 原文："The reality, Bill describes, is more like a large crowded marketplace."
- 要点：真实的同行评审更像拥挤的集市：每个人时间和注意力都有限、信息不完整。
