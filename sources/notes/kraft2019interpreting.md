# Kraft 2019：Interpreting Effect Sizes of Education Interventions

- 资料：`kraft2019interpreting`，[原文 PDF](https://edworkingpapers.com/sites/default/files/Interpreting%20Effect%20Sizes%20-%20August%2019%20FINAL_0.pdf)
- 版本：EdWorkingPaper No. 19-10，2019 年 8 月更新版（布朗大学安嫩伯格研究所的公开工作论文）。正式版发表于 Educational Researcher 49(4)，2020；正式版付费，这里只读了工作论文版。位置写工作论文页码。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Researchers commonly interpret effect sizes by applying benchmarks proposed by Cohen over a half century ago. However, effects that are small by Cohen's standards are large relative to the impacts of most field-based interventions."
- 要点：研究者常用半个多世纪前 Cohen 提出的标准解读效应量；但按 Cohen 标准算"小"的效应，和大多数现场干预的效果相比其实已经很大。

### E2

- 位置：第 2–3 页
- 原文："The default approach to evaluating the magnitude of effect sizes is to apply a set of benchmarks proposed by Jacob Cohen over a half century ago (0.2 Small, 0.5 Medium, 0.8 Large) (Cohen, 1969)."
- 要点：默认做法是用 Cohen 的标准：0.2 小、0.5 中、0.8 大。

### E3

- 位置：第 3 页
- 原文："However, Cohen's standards are based on a handful of small, tightly controlled lab experiments in social psychology from the 1960s performed largely on undergraduates."
- 要点：Cohen 的标准来自 20 世纪 60 年代少数几个以本科生为主、控制严格的小型社会心理学实验室实验。

### E4

- 位置：第 3 页
- 原文："Researchers design studies without sufficient statistical power to detect realistic effect sizes."
- 要点：沿用过大的标准带来的后果之一：研究者设计的研究统计功效不足，测不出现实中的效应量。

### E5

- 位置：第 6 页
- 原文："For example, Lortie-Forgues and Inglis (2019) found an average effect size of only 0.06 SD among 141 RCTs funded by IES and the UK-based Education Endowment Foundation."
- 要点：Kraft 引用 Lortie-Forgues 和 Inglis 2019 年的研究：美国教育科学研究所和英国教育捐赠基金会资助的 141 项随机对照试验，平均效应量只有 0.06 个标准差。

### E6

- 位置：第 8 页
- 原文："But translations are not interpretations. They are simple unit conversions that leave the interpretation to the reader and allow considerable room for disagreement."
- 要点：把效应量换算成"几个月的学习"等单位只是换算，不是解读，解读仍留给读者，分歧空间很大。

### E7

- 位置：第 9 页（准则 1）
- 原文："It is incumbent on researchers reporting effect sizes to clarify which type their statistic describes, and it is important that research consumers do not assume effect sizes inherently represent causal relationships."
- 要点：报告效应量的研究者要说清它是相关关系还是因果效应；读者也不要默认效应量就是因果关系。

### E8

- 位置：第 10 页（准则 2）
- 原文："Even among measures of student achievement, effect sizes for researcher-designed and specialized topic tests aligned with the treatment are often two to four times larger than effects on broad standardized state tests"
- 要点：研究者自编的、和干预内容高度对应的测验，效应量常是州统一标准化考试的 2–4 倍。

### E9

- 位置：第 11 页（准则 2）
- 原文："Outcomes assessed immediately after an intervention ends are likely to show larger effects than outcomes captured months or years later"
- 要点：干预一结束就测，效应往往比几个月或几年后测更大。

### E10

- 位置：第 13 页（准则 3）
- 原文："Targeted interventions and small-scale efficacy trials generally produce larger effect sizes than universal interventions because they target study participants that are most likely to benefit and because there is less variation in outcomes among smaller, non-representative samples"
- 要点：针对特定人群的干预和小规模试验，效应量一般比面向所有人的干预大，因为对象最可能受益，样本也更同质。

### E11

- 位置：第 13 页（准则 3）
- 原文："Whenever possible, researchers should present effects standardized at the student level, irrespective of the level of treatment or the unit of analysis."
- 要点：尽量用学生个体层面的标准差来标准化效应量，不论干预或分析的单位是什么。

### E12

- 位置：第 16 页（准则 3）
- 原文："Expect studies that report the effect of offering an intervention to have smaller effect sizes than studies that report the effect of receiving an intervention."
- 要点："提供干预"的效应（意向治疗）通常小于"实际接受干预"的效应。

### E13

- 位置：第 17 页（准则 4）
- 原文："Effect sizes from lower-cost interventions are more impressive than similar effects from more costly programs."
- 要点：效应相近时，成本低的干预更值得重视。

### E14

- 位置：第 17 页（准则 5）
- 原文："One of the most consistent findings in the education literature is that effects decrease when smaller targeted programs are taken to scale"
- 要点：教育研究里最一致的发现之一是：小规模的针对性项目推广到大范围后，效果会下降。

### E15

- 位置：第 20–21 页
- 原文："I propose the following benchmarks for effect sizes from causal studies of pre-K–12 education interventions evaluating effects on student achievement: less than 0.05 is Small, 0.05 to less than 0.20 is Medium, and 0.20 or greater is Large. These proposed benchmarks are based on the distribution of 1,942 effect sizes from 747 RCTs evaluating education interventions with standardized test outcomes"
- 要点：针对学前到 12 年级、以学业成绩为结果的因果研究，Kraft 提出新标准：小于 0.05 为小，0.05 到小于 0.20 为中，0.20 及以上为大；依据是 747 项以标准化测验为结果的随机对照试验中的 1,942 个效应量。

### E16

- 位置：第 21 页
- 原文："As shown in Table 1, these values divide the overall distribution, with a median of 0.10 SD, into approximate thirds"
- 要点：这些效应量的中位数是 0.10 个标准差，新标准大致把它们三等分。

### E17

- 位置：第 21 页
- 原文："One in four effect sizes from RCTs of education interventions with standardized test outcomes described in Table 1 are zero or negative, with many more small, positive effects that cannot be distinguished from zero."
- 要点：这些试验中四分之一的效应量为零或为负，还有许多小的正效应与零区分不开。

### E18

- 位置：第 22 页
- 原文："Effect sizes from studies with samples greater than 2,000 students are several times smaller than studies with 100 students or fewer (medians of 0.03 vs. 0.24 SD)."
- 要点：样本超过 2,000 名学生的研究，效应量比 100 人及以下的小几倍（中位数 0.03 对 0.24）。

### E19

- 位置：第 22 页
- 原文："RCTs funded by the U.S. Department of Education, which requires scholars to pre-register their research design and report their findings, have a median effect size of 0.03 across 139 effect sizes from 49 RCTs."
- 要点：美国教育部资助、要求预注册设计并报告结果的 49 项随机对照试验，139 个效应量的中位数是 0.03。

### E20

- 位置：第 22–23 页
- 原文："These patterns suggest that effects of 0.15 or even 0.10 SD should be considered large and impressive when they arise from large-scale field experiments that are pre-registered and examine broad achievement measures."
- 要点：对于预注册、大规模、用综合性成绩测量的现场实验，0.15 甚至 0.10 个标准差就应视为大而可观的效应。

### E21

- 位置：第 26 页（结论）
- 原文："Effect sizes that are equal in magnitude are rarely equal in importance."
- 要点：大小相同的效应量，重要性很少相同。

### E22

- 位置：第 5 页
- 原文："Most basically, effect sizes are a measure of differences in means between two subgroups divided by the standard deviation of the measure of interest"
- 要点：效应量最基本的含义：两组平均值之差除以所测指标的标准差。
