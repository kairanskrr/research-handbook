# O.Nyumba 等人 2018：The use of focus group discussion methodology: Insights from two decades of application in conservation

- 资料：`nyumba2018use`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.12860)；[埃克塞特大学机构库的 PDF](https://ore.exeter.ac.uk/repository/handle/10871/32495)
- 版本：Methods in Ecology and Evolution 9(1):20–32（2018），开放获取（知识共享署名许可）。生态与保护生物学中的社会科学方法。2026-10-17 读的是机构库 PDF；位置写小节编号和期刊页码。PDF 文字提取时短横线变成了乱码，摘录中补回为"–"，其余逐字照录。作者署名为 Tobias O.Nyumba 等人，文末建议引用格式写作 Ochieng NT 等人。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 21 页
- 原文："However, its popularity and application has grown across a wide range of disciplines including education (Flores & Alonso, 1995), communication and media studies (Lunt & Livingstone, 1996), sociology (Morgan, 1996), feminist research (Wilkinson, 1998, 1999), health research (Wilkinson, 1998) and marketing research"
- 要点：焦点小组用在教育、传播与媒介研究、社会学、女性主义研究、健康研究和市场研究等许多学科。

### E2

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 21 页
- 原文："In contrast, in a focus group discussion, researchers adopt the role of a "facilitator" or a "moderator." In this setting, the researcher facilitates or moderates a group discussion between participants and not between the researcher and the participants."
- 要点：在焦点小组里，研究者是主持人，引导的是参与者之间的讨论，而不是研究者和参与者之间的问答。

### E3

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 21 页
- 原文："Consequently, there are cases where authors have confused and conflated these two distinctive methods (Parker & Tritter, 2006)."
- 要点：有些作者把焦点小组和访谈这两种不同的方法混为一谈。

### E4

- 位置：§2.1 Research design，第 22 页
- 原文："Thereafter, participant identification is perhaps the most critical step since the technique is largely based on group dynamics and synergistic relationships among participants to generate data"
- 要点：确定参与者可能是最关键的一步，因为这种方法主要靠组内动态和参与者之间的相互激发来产生数据。

### E5

- 位置：§2.1 Research design，第 23 页
- 原文："One potential drawback in focus group discussion is the lack of guarantee that all those recruited will attend the discussion. To overcome this, Rabiee (2004) recommends that researchers may over-recruit by 10–25%."
- 要点：招到的人不一定都会来，所以可以多招 10% 到 25%。

### E6

- 位置：§2.1 Research design，第 23 页
- 原文："With more than 12 members, the group becomes difficult to manage and may disintegrate into two or even three small groups, each having their own independent discussion."
- 要点：超过 12 人，小组就难以管理，可能分裂成两三个各说各的小圈子。

### E7

- 位置：§2.2 Data collection，第 23 页
- 原文："Focus group discussion requires a team consisting of a skilled facilitator and an assistant (Burrows & Kendall, 1997; Krueger, 1994)." / "the assistant's role includes observing non-verbal interactions and the impact of the group dynamics, and documenting the general content of the discussion"
- 要点：焦点小组需要一名熟练的主持人加一名助手；助手观察非语言互动和小组动态的影响，并记录讨论的大致内容。

### E8

- 位置：§2.2 Data collection，第 23 页
- 原文："Participants are likely to suffer from fatigue when discussions are longer."
- 要点：讨论时间过长，参与者容易疲劳。

### E9

- 位置：§3.7 Online focus groups，第 24 页
- 原文："However, these discussion platforms are only accessible to participants with access to the Internet and are prone to technical problems such as poor or loss of connectivity and failure to capture non-verbal data"
- 要点：线上焦点小组只能覆盖能上网的人，还容易出现断线等技术问题，并且难以记录非语言信息。

### E10

- 位置：§5 Results，第 25 页
- 原文："The number of participants per focus group ranged from 2 to 21 with a median of 10 participants. More than half of the studies (n = 101) did not report the sample size, whereas 55% (n = 93) did not report group size and 32% (n = 54) did not mention the number of focus group discussion sessions"
- 要点：在综述的 170 项保护研究中，每组 2 到 21 人、中位数 10 人；一半以上没报告样本量，55% 没报告每组人数，32% 没写做了几场。

### E11

- 位置：§7 Discussion，第 27 页
- 原文："However, the technique is also subject to "careless or inappropriate use," potential data manipulation, and exploitation of participants when researchers tend to assume that group consent represents individual consent (Kitzinger & Barbour, 1999)."
- 要点：这种方法也可能被滥用，例如研究者把小组的同意当作每个人的同意。

### E12

- 位置：§10 Advantages and caveats，第 28 页
- 原文："Since focus group discussion depends on participants' dynamics, it should be avoided where participants are uneasy with each other or where social stigmatisation due to the disclosure may arise" / "Focus group discussion provides depth and insight, but cannot produce useful numerical results, hence must not be used where statistical data are required"
- 要点：参与者彼此不自在、或者说出来会招致社会歧视时，不应使用焦点小组；它提供深度和洞见，但给不出有用的数字结果，需要统计数据时不能用它。

### E13

- 位置：§10 Advantages and caveats，第 29 页
- 原文："However, focus group participants are sometimes reluctant to deal with sensitive topics in a discussion setting compared with an individual interview or a survey (18). Researchers must be aware of this constraint when planning and framing the group discussion questions."
- 要点：和个别访谈或问卷相比，参与者有时不愿在小组里谈敏感话题，设计问题时要考虑到这一点。

### E14

- 位置：§11 Recommendations，第 29 页
- 原文："Ability to remain impartial by getting involved while maintaining verbal and non-verbal objectivity."
- 要点：主持人要参与其中，同时在言语和肢体上保持中立。

### E15

- 位置：§11 Recommendations，第 30 页
- 原文："These include dominance effect (a dominant individual shapes the discussion), halo effect (the perceived status of a group member influences the discussion), groupthink (the members in a group tend to think similarly to maintain group cohesion) among several others (Mukherjee et al., 2015). The facilitator (and/or assistant) should keep a keen eye out to spot and address such biases in the data collection phase."
- 要点：小组讨论有群体偏差：主导效应（一个强势的人左右讨论）、光环效应（某人的身份影响讨论）、群体思维（为保持和气而趋同）等；主持人和助手要在收集数据时留意并处理。

### E16

- 位置：§11 Recommendations，第 30 页
- 原文："The review revealed that 144 out of 170 studies used focus group discussion alongside other techniques in the same study. In most of these studies, it was extremely difficult to tease out what component of the results and inferences were derived from the focus group discussion alone."
- 要点：170 项研究中有 144 项把焦点小组和其他方法一起用，多数很难分清哪些结果来自焦点小组。
