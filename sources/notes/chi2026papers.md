# ACM CHI 2026：Papers 征稿与作者、审稿指南

- 资料：`chi2026papers`，[征稿页](https://chi2026.acm.org/authors/papers/)；同站的 [Guide to a Successful Submission](https://chi2026.acm.org/guide-to-a-successful-submission/)、[Papers Review Process](https://chi2026.acm.org/papers-review-process/)、[Guide to Reviewing Papers](https://chi2026.acm.org/guide-to-reviewing-papers/)、[Contributions to CHI](https://chi2026.acm.org/submission-guides/contributions-to-chi/)、[CHI Anonymization Policy](https://chi2026.acm.org/chi-anonymization-policy/)
- 版本：ACM CHI 2026（ACM 人机交互大会，2026 年 4 月在巴塞罗那举行）官网上的上述 6 个网页，2026-10-08 读取。位置写"页面名 › 小标题"。只代表 CHI 2026，其他年份和其他人机交互会议的规定可能不同。

### E1

- 位置：Papers › Message from the Papers Chairs
- 原文："Acceptance is highly competitive: accepted papers will excel in originality, significance, validity, research quality, and presentation clarity."
- 要点：录用竞争激烈；录用论文要在原创性、重要性、效度、研究质量和表达清晰度上出色。

### E2

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › 开头
- 原文："a large proportion of submissions, typically around 75%, get rejected."
- 要点：通常约 75% 的投稿被拒。

### E3

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › The Review Process
- 原文："This replaces the rebuttal process used in previous years, giving authors time to revise and resubmit their work for CHI 2026."
- 要点：CHI 2026 用"修改后重投"取代了以往年份的作者回复（rebuttal）环节。

### E4

- 位置：Papers Review Process › Round 1 Review Process
- 原文："Authors that meet this minimum threshold will have 5 weeks to respond as they see fit." / "as many as 50% of R&R papers may be rejected during the second round of reviewing."
- 要点：第一轮过了门槛的论文有 5 周时间修改；进入"修改后重投"的论文，第二轮仍可能有多达一半被拒。

### E5

- 位置：Papers › Important Facts
- 原文："Short Papers: Submissions of 5,000 words or less." / "Standard-Length Papers: Submissions between 5,000 and 12,000 words (about 7,000-8,000 words on average)." / "Submissions of more than 12,000 words will be desk-rejected if their length is not justified."
- 要点：篇幅按词数算：短论文 5,000 词以内；标准论文 5,000–12,000 词（平均约 7,000–8,000 词）；超过 12,000 词又没有正当理由的直接拒稿。

### E6

- 位置：Papers › Important Facts
- 原文："reviewers/ACs are NOT required to read the appendices and supplementary materials for review."
- 要点：审稿人和领域主席不必读附录和补充材料，理解贡献所需的重要信息都要放在正文里。

### E7

- 位置：Papers › Step 1. Write Your Paper › Research Quality
- 原文："design papers will be judged as rigorous according to the expectations of the design community, not by the expectations of the computational interaction community"
- 要点：严谨与否按论文所属子社区的规范评判，例如设计类论文按设计社区的标准，而不按计算交互社区的标准。

### E8

- 位置：Papers › Policy on Research Involving Human Participants and Subjects
- 原文："Any research in submitted manuscripts that involves human subjects must go through the appropriate ethics review requirements that apply to the authors’ research environment." / "authors are asked to submit a short note to reviewers that provides this context."
- 要点：涉及人类参与者的研究，必须通过作者所在研究环境要求的伦理审查；各地要求不同，要给审稿人附一段简短说明。

### E9

- 位置：Papers › Policy on Use of Large Language Models
- 原文："Text generated from a large-scale language model (LLM), such as ChatGPT, must be marked where such tools are used for purposes beyond editing the author’s own text"
- 要点：大语言模型生成的文字，凡用途超出修改作者自己的文字，都要标明。

### E10

- 位置：Papers › Step 3. Select a Subcommittee
- 原文："you can state a preference of up to two subcommittees whose mandates you believe your topic fits into."
- 要点：投稿时最多选两个主题子委员会。

### E11

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › The Review Form
- 原文："Significance: why do the contribution and benefit matter, and how much?" / "Research quality: How clear are the authors’ processes? How rigorous is the research (for the subfield’s definition of rigor)?"
- 要点：审稿表的评判项包括重要性、原创性、研究质量（按该子领域对严谨的定义）、表达清晰度和相关工作。

### E12

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Making a Significant Contribution
- 原文："Of these, the contribution and its significance are the most important"
- 要点：各项评判中，贡献及其重要性最重要。

### E13

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Ensuring High-Quality Research
- 原文："For this reason it is risky to leave quality issues (for example, evaluating a design) until the last minute."
- 要点：审稿人常以质量问题为由拒稿，所以怎样证明质量（例如怎样评估一个设计）要在计划阶段就想好。

### E14

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Ensuring High-Quality Research
- 原文："They often criticize authors for conducting studies without adequate theoretical basis" / "you should explain not only what you did, but also why you did it"
- 要点：审稿人常批评研究缺少理论依据、设计选择没有说明理由；不只写做了什么，还要写为什么这样做。

### E15

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Transparency
- 原文："Contributions that are technology-oriented (e.g., a new technique or algorithm) and contributions that are quantitative studies (i.e., experiments with statistically analyzed results) are expected to be verifiable, reproducible"
- 要点：技术类贡献和定量研究，要求可验证、可复现、可重现。

### E16

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Transparency
- 原文："Contributions that follow a qualitative research approach (i.e., which most of the time incorporate researchers’ interpretation as part of the method) should be transparent about the various decisions made; the positionality of the authors and/or researchers"
- 要点：定性研究要透明地交代各项决定、研究者的立场（positionality），以及研究设计和报告的步骤。

### E17

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Transparency
- 原文："The authors should make explicit how the themes were identified or constructed from the data, and whether each conclusion was drawn from outstanding instances or general trends among participants."
- 要点：要写清主题是怎样从数据里得出的，每个结论来自个别突出的例子还是参与者中的普遍趋势。

### E18

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Transparency › Sharing research material
- 原文："These resources are most reliably shared by posting to a publicly available open-access repository with a persistent identifier"
- 要点：代码、数据、访谈提纲等材料，最可靠的分享方式是放到带永久标识符的公开存储库。

### E19

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Transparency › Sharing research material
- 原文："In these cases, we advise you to share as much as possible and explicitly state in your paper why the rest cannot be shared."
- 要点：敏感数据或专有代码不能分享时，尽量多分享，并在论文里写明其余部分为什么不能分享。

### E20

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Transparency › Sharing research material
- 原文："When research involves asking questions of human subjects, it is helpful for reviewers, and later readers, to know what questions (and with what wording) were asked."
- 要点：向参与者提问的研究，要让读者知道问了什么、原话怎么问的。

### E21

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Describing the Work Clearly and Concisely
- 原文："Remember that, unlike journal papers, CHI Papers are reviewed mostly on an ‘as-is’ basis"
- 要点：CHI 论文基本按投稿时的样子评审；修改期很短，需要大改的会被拒。

### E22

- 位置：Contributions to CHI › Systems, Tools, Architectures, and Infrastructure
- 原文："(Note that a good systems paper does not necessarily require a user study, but it does require an appropriate and reasonable validation.)"
- 要点：好的系统论文不一定要做用户研究，但一定要有合适、合理的验证。

### E23

- 位置：Contributions to CHI › Development or Refinement of Interface Artifacts or Techniques
- 原文："Do you include a rigorous and convincing validation of the artifact/technique (e.g., empirical study, usability study, field study as appropriate), where you clearly show not only that incremental gains exist, but that the gains are of practical significance?"
- 要点：界面制品或交互技术要有严格、有说服力的验证（实证研究、可用性研究、现场研究等），不只证明有提升，还要证明提升有实际意义。

### E24

- 位置：Contributions to CHI › Understanding Users
- 原文："Do you identify the population you are studying and account for the diversity (or lack thereof) of the population?"
- 要点：理解用户类研究要说明研究的人群是谁，以及这个人群是否多样。

### E25

- 位置：Guide to Reviewing Papers › Replicating Work
- 原文："Constructive replication can be a significant contribution to human-computer interaction"
- 要点：建设性的复现研究本身可以是人机交互的重要贡献。

### E26

- 位置：Guide to Reviewing Papers › Prior Publication
- 原文："non-archival venues, such as workshop presentations, posters, and CHI’s own Late-Breaking Work do not count as prior publications."
- 要点：研讨会报告、海报、CHI 的最新进展（Late-Breaking Work）等非存档形式，不算已发表。

### E27

- 位置：CHI Anonymization Policy
- 原文："Authors are required to cite their own work in the third person"
- 要点：引用自己以前的工作要用第三人称。

### E28

- 位置：CHI Anonymization Policy
- 原文："CHI does not discourage non-archival publication of work prior to or during the review process, but recognizes that complete anonymization becomes more difficult in that context."
- 要点：CHI 不反对在审稿前或审稿中公开发布（如 arXiv），但承认这会让完全匿名更难。

### E29

- 位置：CHI Anonymization Policy
- 原文："too detailed descriptions of where user studies were conducted, where authors received IRB approvals, or figures that include faces of authors"
- 要点：会暴露身份的信息包括：过于详细的用户研究地点、在哪里通过伦理审查、带作者面孔的图片。

### E30

- 位置：Papers Review Process › Update for CHI 2026: Assisted Desk Reject (ADR) Process
- 原文："Grossly insufficient data to support claims." / "A disproportionately small HCI contribution given the paper length."
- 要点：CHI 2026 新增的"协助直拒"标准包括：数据远不足以支撑主张；相对篇幅，人机交互贡献过小。

### E31

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Maximizing Impact
- 原文："You should keep a critical eye on who is and who is not included in your work and ensure that you make claims accordingly."
- 要点：要留意研究纳入了谁、没纳入谁，主张的范围要和这一点相符。

### E32

- 位置：Contributions to CHI › Introduction 及各小标题
- 原文："Below you will find some of the types of contributions a paper can make to HCI, and the associated criteria that a reviewer may use to assess this type of contribution."
- 要点：CHI 列出了若干贡献类型及各自的评审标准，小标题依次为：界面制品或技术的开发与改进、理解用户、系统与工具、方法学、理论、创新与愿景、论证、验证与复现。

### E33

- 位置：Papers › Step 3. Select a Subcommittee
- 原文："CHI 2026 expects to receive over 5,000 submissions as of 2025."
- 要点：CHI 2026 预计收到 5,000 多篇投稿。

### E34

- 位置：Papers › Your Paper at the Conference
- 原文："Authors of accepted papers are required to present their work at CHI 2026"
- 要点：录用论文的作者必须到会报告。

### E35

- 位置：Papers › Important Dates
- 原文："Thursday, September 4, 2025 : Abstract/metadata deadline" / "Thursday, September 11, 2025 : Full paper deadline" / "Thursday, January 15, 2026 : Decision Notification"
- 要点：CHI 2026：2025 年 9 月 4 日交摘要和元数据，9 月 11 日交全文，2026 年 1 月 15 日通知结果。

### E36

- 位置：Papers › Step 4. Complete Submission
- 原文："Listed authors cannot be changed after the abstract/metadata deadline."
- 要点：摘要和元数据截止后，作者名单不能再改。

### E37

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Ensuring High-Quality Research
- 原文："if you are providing an incremental improvement to a well-known technique, such as a new or refined type of menu capable of reducing errors in selecting menu items, you will probably run a careful and convincing experiment to measure error rates with this and other types of menu." / "If you have come up with a system to support a new way of collaborative writing, you may try to evaluate it in real-world conditions"
- 要点：证明质量的方式随贡献类型而变：改进一种已知技术（如能减少选错的新菜单），通常做严谨的实验比较出错率；支持新协作写作方式的系统，可能在真实条件下评估。

### E38

- 位置：Contributions to CHI › Development or Refinement of Interface Artifacts or Techniques
- 原文："Do you describe the artifact or technique in sufficient detail for others to replicate it?"
- 要点：界面制品或技术要描述得足够详细，让别人能重做出来。

### E39

- 位置：Contributions to CHI › Argument
- 原文："Is the argument well-founded and well-argued? Is it supported by facts?" / "Does it incorporate other points of view and perspectives, and does it consider them in a balanced fashion?"
- 要点：论证类论文要论据扎实、有事实支撑，并平衡地考虑其他观点。

### E40

- 位置：Contributions to CHI › 各贡献类型的小标题
- 原文："Development or Refinement of Interface Artifacts or Techniques" / "Systems, Tools, Architectures, and Infrastructure" / "Innovation, Creativity, and Vision" / "Validation and Replication Validation and replication studies consolidate what the CHI field knows."
- 要点：Contributions to CHI 页面列出的贡献类型包括：界面制品或技术的开发与改进、理解用户、系统与工具（含架构和基础设施）、方法学、理论、创新与愿景、论证、验证与复现。

### E41

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › The Review Form
- 原文："Originality: What new ideas or approaches are introduced?" / "Relevant previous work: Is prior work adequately reviewed?"
- 要点：审稿表还要评原创性、表达清晰度和相关工作是否充分综述。

### E42

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Ensuring High-Quality Research
- 原文："Reviewers often cite problems with quality as the reason to reject a submission."
- 要点：审稿人常以研究质量问题为由拒稿。

### E43

- 位置：Guide to a Successful Submission › Ensuring High-Quality Research
- 原文："A further concern is lack of justification for design choices and not explaining why certain design features have been included."
- 要点：审稿人另一个常见意见是设计选择没有说明理由。

### E44

- 位置：Papers Review Process › Update for CHI 2026: Assisted Desk Reject (ADR) Process
- 原文："CHI 2026 introduces an Assisted Desk Reject (ADR) step, collaboratively performed by SCs and ACs." / "ADR applies when a paper is out of scope or so far from acceptable as to make external reviews unnecessary."
- 要点：CHI 2026 新增"协助直拒"，由子委员会主席和领域主席共同执行；用于超出范围或离录用标准太远、不必送外审的论文。

### E45

- 位置：Guide to Reviewing Papers › References
- 原文："the debate about what makes a good CHI paper has been going on as long as the CHI conference has existed" / "Greenberg, S. and Buxton, B. 2008. Usability evaluation considered harmful (some of the time)."
- 要点：审稿指南把 Greenberg & Buxton 2008 列为"什么是好的 CHI 论文"这场争论的参考读物。
