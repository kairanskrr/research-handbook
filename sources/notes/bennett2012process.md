# Bennett & Checkel 2012：Process Tracing: From Philosophical Roots to Best Practices

- 资料：`bennett2012process`，[Simons Papers in Security and Development No. 21/2012（西蒙菲莎大学机构库，Internet Archive 2017 年快照）](https://web.archive.org/web/20170816174541/http://summit.sfu.ca/system/files/iritems1/14884/SimonsWorkingPaper21.pdf)
- 版本：工作论文版，西蒙菲莎大学国际研究学院 2012 年 6 月发布，声明"All papers can be downloaded free of charge"。文中注明它是 Bennett & Checkel 主编的 Process Tracing in the Social Sciences: From Metaphor to Analytic Tool（剑桥大学出版社，2015）第 1 章的预发版。位置写工作论文页码。

### E1

- 位置：I. Introduction，第 5 页
- 原文："Contemporary political scientists and sociologists have converged on the view that these puzzles, and many more on the current scholarly and policy agendas, demand answers that combine social and institutional structure and context with individual agency and decisionmaking."
- 要点：政治学者和社会学者都认为，很多问题需要把社会和制度结构、背景与个人能动和决策结合起来回答。

### E2

- 位置：II. Defining Process Tracing，第 7 页
- 原文："The term process tracing originated in the field of cognitive psychology in the United States in the late 1960s or early 1970s." / "In 1979, the Stanford University political scientist Alexander L. George appropriated the term process tracing to describe the use of evidence from within case studies to make inferences about historical explanations"
- 要点：过程追踪一词源于 1960 年代末或 1970 年代初美国的认知心理学；1979 年政治学者 George 借用它，指用案例内部证据推断历史解释。

### E3

- 位置：II. Defining Process Tracing，第 10 页
- 原文："define process tracing as the analysis of evidence on processes, sequences, and conjunctures of events within a case for the purposes of either developing or testing hypotheses about causal mechanisms that might causally explain the case."
- 要点：过程追踪是分析一个案例内部的过程、事件顺序和事件交汇的证据，用来提出或检验关于因果机制的假设。

### E4

- 位置：II. Defining Process Tracing，第 10 页
- 原文："process tracing examines the deductive observable implications of hypothesized causal mechanisms within a case to test whether these might in fact explain the case" / "Or, it inductively uses evidence from within a case to develop hypotheses that might explain the case"
- 要点：过程追踪既可以演绎（检验假设机制在案例内的可观察含义），也可以归纳（从案例证据中提出假设）。

### E5

- 位置：II. Defining Process Tracing，第 11 页
- 原文："Data set observations are "an array of scores on specific variables for a designated sample of cases," and these observations provide the basis for statistical analyses. Causal process observations are "observations on context, process, or mechanism" and are used in within-case analyses such as process tracing"
- 要点：数据集观察是一批案例在变量上的取值，用于统计分析；因果过程观察是关于背景、过程或机制的观察，用于过程追踪这类案例内分析。

### E6

- 位置：II. Defining Process Tracing，第 11 页
- 原文："Process tracing is closely related to historical explanation, as that term is used by the historian Clayton Roberts."
- 要点：过程追踪与历史学家 Clayton Roberts 所说的"历史解释"密切相关。

### E7

- 位置：II. Defining Process Tracing，第 11 页
- 原文："We emphasize instead the importance of making explicit the hypotheses about underlying causal mechanisms that are theorized to have caused an historical outcome so that these can be rigorously assessed, even at the expense of political science narratives that are more clogged"
- 要点：与历史学家不同，作者强调要把关于因果机制的假设明确写出来，以便严格检验，哪怕叙事因此更啰嗦。

### E8

- 位置：II. Defining Process Tracing，第 12 页
- 原文："It is not simply glorified historiography, nor does it proceed by the logic of frequentist statistics."
- 要点：过程追踪不只是"美化了的史学叙述"，也不按频率学派统计的逻辑进行。

### E9

- 位置：III. Bayesianism and Process Tracing，第 19 页
- 原文："each approach emphasizes that the probative value of evidence relative to competing explanations is more important than the number of pieces of evidence."
- 要点：证据相对于竞争解释的证明力，比证据的数量更重要。

### E10

- 位置：III. Bayesianism and Process Tracing，第 19 页
- 原文："Hoop tests involve evidence that is certain, but not unique; failing a hoop test disqualifies an explanation, but passing it does not greatly increase confidence in that explanation." / "Van Evera's example of a hoop test is: "Was the accused in the state on the day of the murder?" Failing this hoop test falsifies the hypothesis."
- 要点：环检验（hoop test）：证据是假设成立时必然会出现的、但不是它独有的；没通过就淘汰该解释，通过了也不大增加信心。Van Evera 的例子：案发当天被告是否在本州？不在就排除。

### E11

- 位置：III. Bayesianism and Process Tracing，第 19 页
- 原文："Whether passing a hoop test constitutes strong evidence of the truth of the hypothesis depends on how frequently the "pass" condition occurs naturally"
- 要点：通过环形检验能否算强证据，取决于"通过"的条件本来有多常见。

### E12

- 位置：III. Bayesianism and Process Tracing，第 20 页
- 原文："Smoking gun tests are unique, but not certain. Passing a smoking gun test is sufficient for strongly affirming an explanation, but passing such a test is not necessary to build confidence in an explanation."
- 要点：确凿证据检验（smoking gun）：证据为该解释独有，但不是必然出现；通过足以有力支持，没通过也不否定。

### E13

- 位置：III. Bayesianism and Process Tracing，第 20 页
- 原文："Doubly decisive tests use evidence that is both unique and certain, or that is necessary and sufficient to provide great confidence in an explanation." / "Van Evera uses the example of a bank camera that catches the faces of all those involved in robbing the bank."
- 要点：双重决定性检验：证据既独有又必然，通过即高度确信。例子：拍下所有劫匪面孔的银行摄像头。

### E14

- 位置：III. Bayesianism and Process Tracing，第 20 页
- 原文："Any one such test is not very decisive, but it is possible that a series of such tests might increase confidence in one explanation and decrease that in others, just as a suspect might be convicted on the basis of many pieces of circumstantial evidence"
- 要点：单个风中稻草检验不太有决定性，但一连串这样的检验可以提高对一个解释的信心，就像嫌疑人可以凭许多间接证据定罪。

### E15

- 位置：III. Bayesianism and Process Tracing，第 21 页
- 原文："When evidence on a particular step of a process becomes sufficiently repetitive that it is to be expected, it loses any ability to further discriminate among competing hypotheses."
- 要点：关于同一步骤的证据重复到可以预期时，就不再能区分竞争假设。

### E16

- 位置：IV. Techniques and Standards，第 21–22 页
- 原文："For phenomena on which there is little prior knowledge and for cases that are not well-explained by extant theories, process tracing proceeds primarily through inductive study." / "Here, one immerses oneself in the details of the case and tries out protohypotheses that may either quickly prove to be dead ends or become plausible and worthy of more rigorous testing."
- 要点：前人知识少、现有理论解释不了的案例，过程追踪主要靠归纳：沉浸到案例细节里，试探初步假设。

### E17

- 位置：IV. Techniques and Standards，第 22 页
- 原文："A key step here is to develop case-specific observable implications of the theories in question" / "as theories are seldom specified in such precise ways that they offer tight predictions on the observable implications that should be evident in particular cases."
- 要点：关键一步是把理论转化为针对本案例的可观察含义，因为理论很少精确到能直接给出案例里应看到什么。

### E18

- 位置：IV. Techniques and Standards，第 22 页
- 原文："researchers should seek to identify additional observable implications or what Imre Lakatos called "new facts" to test each modification to a hypothesis in order to avoid confirmation bias."
- 要点：每次修改假设，都要找新的可观察含义来检验，以免确认偏差。

### E19

- 位置：IV. Techniques and Standards，第 23 页
- 原文："it is only necessary that any new evidence from within the case be independent of the evidence that generated the hypothesis."
- 要点：检验新假设的证据可以来自同一案例，只要它独立于产生该假设的证据。

### E20

- 位置：IV. Techniques and Standards，第 23 页
- 原文："One useful technique here is to predict what the unavailable evidence will indicate once it becomes available; such predictions, if borne out, provide strong confirmatory evidence."
- 要点：对暂时拿不到的证据（如保密档案），可以事先预测它解密后会显示什么；预测应验就是有力的证据。

### E21

- 位置：IV. Techniques and Standards，第 23 页
- 原文："On the basis of interviews and a reading of primary documentation, predictions were made about socialization dynamics; these were subsequently confirmed through the release of previously classified meeting minutes"
- 要点：例子：Checkel 根据访谈和原始文件做出预测，后来被解密的会议记录证实。

### E22

- 位置：IV. Techniques and Standards，第 23 页
- 原文："The bigger the modification, the more important it is to generate and test new observable implications to guard against 'just so' stories that explain away anomalies one at a time."
- 要点：遇到反证而修改解释时，改动越大，越要提出并检验新的可观察含义，防止逐个"解释掉"异常的事后故事。

### E23

- 位置：IV. Techniques and Standards，第 23–24 页
- 原文："If we expect evidence to be hard to find or access, as when thinking social actors have gone to great lengths to hide evidence of their actions" / "or motives, then absence of evidence might not greatly lower our expectation that an entity or relationship exists."
- 要点：如果证据本来就难找（例如当事人刻意隐藏），找不到证据并不大大降低它存在的可能。

### E24

- 位置：IV. Techniques and Standards，第 24 页
- 原文："it can be applied to a few cases selected from a statistical analysis to clarify whether the direction of causal influence is indeed from the independent variable to the dependent variable, and not the reverse, and to help assess whether any observed correlations might be spurious."
- 要点：在嵌套分析中，可以对统计分析中选出的少数案例做过程追踪，弄清因果方向、判断相关是否虚假。

### E25

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，第 26 页
- 原文："Failing to consider a potentially viable explanation that readily occurs to the readers and critics of a case study can make the process tracing unconvincing."
- 要点：漏掉一个读者和批评者很容易想到的可行解释，会让过程追踪失去说服力。

### E26

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 1，第 26 页
- 原文："researchers should at a minimum assess the process tracing evidence on the explanations that political scientists, sociologists, historians, regional specialists, and functional experts have offered for the specific case under study" / "In addition, it is often useful to render in theoretical terms and do process tracing upon the understandings of actor behavior offered by participants and journalists."
- 要点：至少要检验政治学者、社会学者、历史学者、地区专家和领域专家对本案例提出过的解释；把当事人和记者对行为的理解转成理论解释来检验，也常有用。

### E27

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 1，第 26 页
- 原文："These include explanations based on actors' material power, institutional constraints and opportunities, and social norms or legitimacy"
- 要点：检查是否漏掉了主要的解释类别：物质权力、制度约束与机会、社会规范或正当性。

### E28

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 1，第 27 页
- 原文："Some may be irrelevant to the case at hand, and others may be easily dismissed in the footnotes on the basis of one or a few pieces of obvious or wellknown process-tracing evidence."
- 要点：不是每个备选解释都要详细追踪；有的与本案无关，有的凭一两条明显证据在脚注里就能排除。

### E29

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 2，第 28 页
- 原文："Some case studies accord an unduly privileged status to one explanation by granting it 'first mover advantage'" / "A far better procedure is to outline the process tracing predictions of a wide range of alternative explanations of a case in advance, and then to consider the actual evidence for and against each explanation."
- 要点：有的案例研究给一个解释"先手优势"，只在它遇到反常时才看别的解释；更好的做法是事先写出各备选解释的预测，再逐一看支持和反对的证据。

### E30

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 3，第 28 页
- 原文："When those providing evidence may have instrumental motives for putting forth particular explanations of their own or others' behavior, researchers should apply a two-step Bayesian analysis." / "First, they should attach Bayesian priors to the possible instrumental motives of those providing evidence and weigh the evidence they give in light of those priors. Then, in a second step, researchers should use the evidence that sources provide to update prior expectations on these sources' motives"
- 要点：提供证据的人可能有动机推销某种解释时，要先估计其动机来给证据打折，再用他提供的证据更新对其动机的判断。

### E31

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 3，第 29 页
- 原文："Spontaneous statements have a different evidentiary status from prepared remarks. Public statements have a different evidentiary status from private statements or statements that will remain classified for a period of time."
- 要点：即兴发言和准备好的讲话、公开声明和私下或将保密一段时间的说法，证据地位不同。

### E32

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 3，第 29 页
- 原文："Newly empowered actors in control of the archives are likely to make available only negative information about their opponents and positive information about themselves."
- 要点：新上台、掌控档案的一方，往往只公开对手的负面信息和自己的正面信息。

### E33

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 3，第 29 页
- 原文："Historians are always at risk of selectively choosing the primary and secondary sources that confirm their arguments. For this reason, it is important to consider a wide range of secondary accounts representing contending historiographical schools and explanations"
- 要点：历史学家总有挑选支持自己论点的资料的风险；所以要参考代表不同史学流派和解释的多种二手研究。

### E34

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 4，第 29 页
- 原文："Prior expectations on the strength and scope conditions of a theory require the most updating when it fails to explain a case in which it is most likely to apply, or succeeds in explaining a case in which it is least likely to apply."
- 要点：理论在"最可能成立"的案例中失败，或在"最不可能成立"的案例中成功，最需要修正对它的看法。

### E35

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 5，第 30 页
- 原文："There is no universal answer to such questions, as a justifiable starting point depends on how a researcher defines the puzzle or question they are trying to explain"
- 要点：从哪里开始追踪没有统一答案，取决于研究者怎样界定要解释的问题。

### E36

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 5，第 30 页
- 原文："A reasonable place may be a critical juncture at which an institution or practice was contingent or open to alternative paths, and actors or exogenous events determined which path it would take."
- 要点：一个合理的起点是关键节点：制度或做法当时可以走不同的路，由行为者或外部事件决定了走哪条。

### E37

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 6，第 31 页
- 原文："A single meeting or memo may prove to be the crucial piece of evidence that instantiates one explanation or undermines another. The more probative we expect evidence to be, the more time and effort we should be willing to expend to obtain it."
- 要点：一次会议、一份备忘录就可能是关键证据；预期证明力越大，越值得花时间去找。

### E38

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 6，第 32 页
- 原文："Seemingly "diverse" sources of evidence (documents, media coverage, interviews) could actually all originate from one or a few individuals with instrumental reasons to convince observers of a particular explanation."
- 要点：看似多样的来源（文件、报道、访谈）可能都出自同一两个有动机推销某种解释的人。

### E39

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 6，第 32 页
- 原文："Elizabeth Wood's excellent study of the Salvadoran civil war, for example, advances a rich, process-based argument that draws on an enormous amount of information. Yet, it was also fifteen years in the making" / "Carefully executed process tracing thus requires that researchers think at an early point about their own financial limits and temporal constraints."
- 要点：例子：Wood 关于萨尔瓦多内战的研究用了大量信息，前后花了十五年；所以要早早想清楚自己的经费和时间限制。

### E40

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 6，第 32 页
- 原文："One stops when repetition occurs. That is, a researcher should stop pursuing any one stream of evidence when it becomes so repetitive that gathering more of that same kind of evidence has a low probability of revising their estimate of the likely accuracy of alternative explanations."
- 要点：何时停止：某一类证据重复到再收集也不太会改变对各解释的判断时，就停。

### E41

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 7，第 33 页
- 原文："process tracing within single cases can in fact lead to convincing explanations if appropriate evidence is accessible." / "Yet for many inferential purposes, comparative case studies can be more powerful sources of inference than single case designs."
- 要点：有合适证据时，单案例过程追踪也能得出令人信服的解释；但对很多推断目的，比较案例更有力。

### E42

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 7，第 33 页
- 原文："As most-similar cases rarely control for all but one potentially causal factor, process tracing can also establish that other differences between the cases do not account for the difference in their outcomes."
- 要点：最相似案例很少能做到只差一个因素，过程追踪可以用来说明其他差异不能解释结果的不同。

### E43

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 8，第 34 页
- 原文："it is important to pay attention to the feeling of surprise and to follow it up with efforts to explain surprising facts theoretically."
- 要点：要重视"意外"的感觉，并努力从理论上解释意外的事实。

### E44

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 9，第 34 页
- 原文："Which actors should have known, said, and did what, and when? Who should have interacted with, worried about, or allied with whom?"
- 要点：事先写出：如果某解释成立，哪些人应该在什么时候知道、说过、做过什么，谁应该和谁来往、担心谁、和谁结盟。

### E45

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，标准 10，第 35 页
- 原文："When the evidence does not allow high levels of confidence in supporting some hypotheses and discounting others, it is important to acknowledge the level of uncertainty that remains."
- 要点：证据不足以高度确信时，要承认剩下的不确定性。

### E46

- 位置：Standards of Good Process Tracing，第 35 页
- 原文："For sure, not all ten criteria elaborated above may be relevant for any given study. However, they should serve as a starting point and checklist, thus maximizing the likelihood of conducting good process tracing."
- 要点：十条标准不一定条条适用于每项研究，但可以作为起点和检查清单。

### E47

- 位置：V. Process Tracing on General Categories of Theories，第 37 页
- 原文："One option here is to compare actors' public statements with available private deliberations that they expected would not be revealed for some time."
- 要点：判断当事人的真实想法，可以对比其公开言论和当时以为不会很快公开的私下讨论。

### E48

- 位置：V. Process Tracing on General Categories of Theories，第 38 页
- 原文："it is important to establish the provenance and authorship of public statements, and to give spontaneous and unplanned statements more weight than planned ones as indicators of genuine beliefs." / "recollections of beliefs held in the past that are backed up by documentary evidence are more credible than those lacking such supporting evidence."
- 要点：要弄清公开声明的来源和执笔人；即兴发言比事先准备的更能反映真实想法；有文件佐证的回忆比没有佐证的更可信。

### E49

- 位置：V. Process Tracing on General Categories of Theories，第 38 页
- 原文："we should expect evidence that an actor holds socially stigmatized beliefs to be harder to find than evidence that an actor shares widely accepted beliefs, so we should treat absence of evidence on the former differently from absence of evidence on the latter."
- 要点：受社会排斥的观点本来就难找到证据，找不到它和找不到普遍观点的证据，意义不同。

### E50

- 位置：VI. Conclusion，第 40 页
- 原文："the central challenge here is to avoid "lazy mechanism-based storytelling""
- 要点：核心挑战是避免"偷懒的机制式讲故事"。
