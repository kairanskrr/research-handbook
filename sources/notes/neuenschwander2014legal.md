# Neuenschwander 2014：Major Legal Challenges Facing Oral History in the Digital Age

- 资料：`neuenschwander2014legal`，[原文](https://ohda.matrix.msu.edu/2012/06/major-legal-challenges/)
- 版本：John A. Neuenschwander（著有 A Guide to Oral History and the Law，牛津大学出版社 2009）为"数字时代的口述历史"（Oral History in the Digital Age，美国博物馆与图书馆服务署资助）项目写的文章，该项目给出的引用年份为 2014。讲的是美国法律。2026-10-06 读取。位置写文章中的段落主题。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："The four major legal areas of concern prior to the digital age: release agreements, protecting restricted interviews, the privacy torts, and copyright have not changed"
- 要点：口述历史在法律上要关心的四大方面：授权协议、保护受限访谈、隐私侵权、版权。

### E2

- 位置：知情同意与授权书
- 原文："Overly broad clauses like “for such scholarly and educational purposes as the oral history program shall deem appropriate,” are simply too generic in the digital age." / "Potential uses of the interviews (in whole or in part)include but are not limited to research, Internet display, media productions, publications, educational curriculum, and museum exhibits."
- 要点："用于项目认为合适的学术和教育目的"这种笼统的用途条款在数字时代太含糊；应写具体，例如列出研究、网上展示、媒体作品、出版、教材、博物馆展览等。

### E3

- 位置：知情同意与授权书
- 原文："the growing use of video to record interviews together with increasing online displays of interviews and media productions all underscore the need to secure permission from interviewees and interviewers to display their names and images."
- 要点：录像访谈和网上展示越来越多，需要取得受访者和访谈者使用其姓名和肖像的许可。

### E4

- 位置：受限访谈与传票，波士顿学院案
- 原文："The promise that had been made to all interviewees was that their interviews would remain confidential until their death."
- 要点：波士顿学院"贝尔法斯特项目"曾向所有受访者承诺，访谈在其去世前保密。

### E5

- 位置：受限访谈与传票，波士顿学院案
- 原文："The choice to investigate criminal activity belongs to the government and is not subject to veto by academic researchers."
- 要点：美国联邦第一巡回上诉法院裁定：是否调查犯罪由政府决定，学术研究者不能否决。

### E6

- 位置：受限访谈与传票，波士顿学院案
- 原文："Ed Moloney had been directed to place a clause in each release agreement that the pledge of confidentiality would be protected “to the extent American law allows.”" / "This was not done"
- 要点：项目负责人本应在每份授权书里写明"在美国法律允许的范围内"保密，但没有写。

### E7

- 位置：受限访谈与传票，教训
- 原文："institutions do need to develop procedures to more closely scrutinize pledges of confidentiality. Such procedures should include legal risk assessments, utilizing IRB expertise where appropriate, possibly applying for a certificate of confidentiality, insuring that all parties understand the limits of the confidentiality promised, and making a commitment to mount a strong legal defense."
- 要点：机构要更严格地审查保密承诺：做法律风险评估、适当借助伦理审查委员会、考虑申请保密证书、让各方都明白保密的限度、准备好积极应诉。

### E8

- 位置：受限访谈与传票，教训
- 原文："researchers and scholars have had some success in civil cases utilizing a standard motion to quash to obtain either a withdrawal of a subpoena or the imposition of some reasonable limits on its scope." / "If the subpoena was issued as part of a criminal proceeding, however, state and federal courts have taken a position similar to that of the First Circuit in the Boston College case."
- 要点：民事案件中，研究者用撤销传票动议取得过一些成功；刑事程序中的传票，美国州和联邦法院的立场与波士顿学院案相似。

### E9

- 位置：隐私侵权
- 原文："The major privacy torts: defamation (slander and libel), false light, public disclosure of embarrassing private facts, and misappropriation of likeness (right of publicity) have always been potential liability issues for oral historians."
- 要点：主要的隐私侵权：诽谤（口头和书面）、虚假暗示（false light）、公开令人难堪的私事、盗用姓名或肖像（形象权）。

### E10

- 位置：诽谤，自查清单
- 原文："Is the individual alive and still active in his/her work or the community?" / "Would a reader’s opinion or estimation of the individual be negatively impacted by the statements or characterizations?" / "Is there supporting factual information or just an unsupported negative opinion?" / "Is there other evidence that supports or corroborates the interviewee’s statements?"
- 要点：判断诽谤风险可问四个问题：被说的人还在世、还活跃吗？读者对他的看法会变差吗？有事实支撑还是只是负面意见？有别的证据佐证吗？

### E11

- 位置：诽谤
- 原文："Most negative statements or characterizations are not defamatory. The historical record is not and should not be something that has been sanitized by its creators and keepers." / "Only after this has been done should a program consider such steps as editing, sealing or even deleting."
- 要点：大多数负面说法并不构成诽谤，历史记录不应被"消毒"；应先尝试核实，之后才考虑删改、封存或删除。

### E12

- 位置：知情同意与授权书
- 原文："Researchers in the social sciences and humanities who incur IRB review are as a matter of course required to show that they too will engage in a rigorous consenting process."
- 要点：（美国）需要经过机构伦理审查委员会（Institutional Review Board，IRB）审查的社会科学和人文研究者，照例要证明自己有严格的知情同意程序。
