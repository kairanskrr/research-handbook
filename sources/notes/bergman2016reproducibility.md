# Bergman & Danheiser 2016：Reproducibility in Chemical Research

- 资料：`bergman2016reproducibility`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201606591)
- 版本：Angewandte Chemie International Edition 2016, 55, 12548–12549，社论（Editorial）。出版社网站免费阅读；本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2025-05-31 保存的出版社 PDF。两位作者分别是加州大学伯克利分校和麻省理工学院的化学教授，Danheiser 时任《Organic Syntheses》主编。

### E1

- 位置：Sources of Irreproducibility，第 12548 页
- 原文："While a complete failure to repeat published results rarely occurs in chemistry, it is not unusual for chemists to encounter difficulty in exactly replicating reaction yields, selectivities, and other data reported in publications."
- 要点：化学里完全重复不出已发表结果的情况很少，但很难精确重复出论文报告的产率、选择性等数据并不少见。

### E2

- 位置：Sources of Irreproducibility，第 12548 页
- 原文："Instances of irreproducibility such as these are all too common, especially in synthetic chemistry."
- 要点：这类重复不出的情况太常见了，合成化学尤其如此。

### E3

- 位置：Sources of Irreproducibility，第 12548 页
- 原文："the editorial staff of the journal Organic Letters, who have found that 2–3 % of submitted manuscripts include evidence for the manual removal of peaks in NMR spectra"
- 要点：《Organic Letters》编辑部发现，投稿中有 2%～3% 有手工去掉核磁共振谱图中峰的迹象。

### E4

- 位置：Sources of Irreproducibility，第 12548 页
- 原文："Related violations of appropriate conduct include discarding data points that are not consistent with desired results, and only reporting the best yield or selectivity for a synthetic reaction."
- 要点：相关的不当行为还包括丢掉不符合预期的数据点、只报告最好的一次产率或选择性。

### E5

- 位置：Replicating Valid Results，第 12549 页
- 原文："During the period 2010–2016, 7.5 % of the articles submitted to Organic Syntheses were rejected because the yield and/or selectivity reported by the original authors could not be reproduced within a reasonable range in the laboratory of one of the editors." / "The fact that results in 1 in 13 articles proved to not be reproducible even with all of these advantages underscores the challenges associated with reproducibility in synthetic chemistry."
- 要点：2010—2016 年，投给《Organic Syntheses》的文章有 7.5% 因为编委实验室在合理范围内重复不出原作者报告的产率或选择性而被拒；即使有详细步骤、可以请教原作者，仍有约 1/13 的文章重复不出来。

### E6

- 位置：Replicating Valid Results，第 12549 页
- 原文："we believe that the majority of reproducibility problems encountered by chemists involve bona fide results, that is, results that were indeed actually obtained by the original authors."
- 要点：作者认为，化学家遇到的大多数重复问题，涉及的是原作者确实得到过的真实结果。

### E7

- 位置：Recommendations，第 12549 页
- 原文："PIs should stay in close communication with co-workers, through regular face-to-face research talks in which primary data and laboratory notebooks are examined."
- 要点：课题负责人应和组员保持密切沟通，定期当面讨论，并查看原始数据和实验记录本。

### E8

- 位置：Recommendations，第 12549 页
- 原文："in the case of a report on new synthetic chemistry, a co-worker without experience in the area might be assigned to repeat a representative example based only on the supporting information."
- 要点：报告新合成方法时，可以请一位没有该方向经验的组员只看支持信息重复一个代表性例子。

### E9

- 位置：Recommendations，第 12549 页
- 原文："While raising financial support is a critical and important contribution to a project, co-authorship should also require direct participation in the study and significant intellectual contribution to the work."
- 要点：筹集经费很重要，但署名还应要求直接参与研究并有重要的智力贡献。

### E10

- 位置：Recommendations，第 12549 页
- 原文："all co-authors are responsible for published results, including the PI."
- 要点：所有合作者都要对发表的结果负责，包括课题负责人。

### E11

- 位置：Recommendations，第 12549 页
- 原文："PIs who train their co-workers (and themselves!) to be skeptical of results, especially those that they want to believe are correct, are good role models."
- 要点：好的课题负责人会训练组员（和自己）怀疑结果，尤其是自己希望是对的结果。

### E12

- 位置：Unconscious Investigator Bias，第 12548 页
- 原文："we believe that this problem exists in the "harder" sciences as well."
- 要点：作者认为，研究者无意识的偏差在化学这类"硬"科学里同样存在。
