# 美国国会研究服务处 2025：Recent Executive Actions Related to Gain-of-Function Research and Laboratory Biosafety

- 资料：`crs2025gainoffunction`，[原文网页](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12554.html)（报告编号 IN12554）
- 版本：2025 年 5 月 19 日。美国国会研究服务处（Congressional Research Service，CRS）的简报，美国政府作品；通过 EveryCRSReport.com 的公开镜像读取。只反映 2025 年 5 月的情况。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："There currently is no overarching federal law that provides oversight of laboratory biosafety and biosecurity with enforceable legal penalties."
- 要点：美国目前没有一部统一的联邦法律、以可执行的法律处罚来监管实验室生物安全和生物安保。

### E2

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："Privately funded research is generally not covered by federal policy or agency guidance beyond what is pursuant to FSAP."
- 要点：私人资助的研究，除管制病原计划之外，一般不受联邦政策或机构指南覆盖。

### E3

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："In the United States, oversight of the life sciences , in particular laboratory biosafety and biosecurity, is exercised pursuant to a mixture of federal law, federal guidance, and self-governance, dependent on the types of experiments and biological agents being used."
- 要点：美国对生命科学（尤其实验室生物安全和安保）的监管，是联邦法律、联邦指南和自我管理的混合，看实验类型和所用病原而定。

### E4

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："It established an oversight and review process for two categories that previously had been overseen by separate policies: dual-use research of concern (DURC) and research on pathogens with enhanced pandemic potential (also known as gain - of - function research ). The 2024 Policy was scheduled to take effect on May 6, 2025, and institutions that receive federal funding had prepared to implement those requirements."
- 要点：2024 年政策把"值得关注的两用研究"和"增强大流行潜力病原研究（功能增益研究）"合并到一套监管审查程序中，原定 2025 年 5 月 6 日生效。

### E5

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："On May 5, 2025, the White House issued Executive Order (E.O.) 14292, " Improving the Safety and Security of Biological Research ," which directed OSTP to revise or replace the 2024 Policy within 120 days."
- 要点：2025 年 5 月 5 日的第 14292 号行政令，要求白宫科技政策办公室在 120 天内修订或替换 2024 年政策。

### E6

- 位置：倒数第 4 段
- 原文："NIH's notice declared that it is rescinding a previous notice, NOT-OD-25-061 , which had described the implementation of the 2024 Policy."
- 要点：NIH 撤销了之前关于落实 2024 年政策的通知。
