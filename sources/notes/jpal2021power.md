# J-PAL 研究资源：Power calculations

- 资料：`jpal2021power`，[原文网页](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/resource/power-calculations)
- 版本：阿卜杜勒·拉蒂夫·贾米尔贫困行动实验室（Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab，J-PAL）研究资源页，作者 Sabhya Gupta、Sarah Kopper，2021 年 8 月更新；2026 年 10 月访问。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Essentials
- 原文："Minimum detectable effect (MDE): The smallest effect that, if true, has (1-β)% chance of producing an estimate that is statistically significant at the α% level (Bloom 1995). In other words, the MDE is the effect size below which we may not be able to distinguish that the effect is different from zero, even if it is."
- 要点：最小可检测效应是：如果真实效应有这么大，研究有足够把握得到显著结果；比它小的效应，即使存在也可能分辨不出来。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction: Relationship between power and power components
- 原文："For example, if only half of the treatment group takes up the treatment, while no one in the control group does" [...] "the necessary sample size to detect a given effect size would be four times larger than with perfect compliance."
- 要点：处理组只有一半人接受干预、对照组无人接受时，检测同样大的效应需要的样本是完全依从时的 4 倍。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction: Relationship between power and power components
- 原文："Attrition can also reduce power (or, conversely, increase the MDE) since the study sample is smaller than previously intended."
- 要点：样本流失使实际样本变小，也会降低功效。

### E4

- 位置：Clustered designs: Power equations for clustered designs
- 原文："and an ICC of 0.07, the standard error of the treatment effect estimator (the square root term) doubles. As a result, the MDE doubles, while the required sample size quadruples."
- 要点：每群 50 人、组内相关系数仅 0.07 时，标准误就翻倍，最小可检测效应翻倍，所需样本变为 4 倍。

### E5

- 位置：Practical tips: Gathering inputs
- 原文："Note that published studies evaluating similar interventions will typically provide an optimistic estimate of a reasonable effect size."
- 要点：已发表的同类研究给出的效应大小通常偏乐观。

### E6

- 位置：Practical tips: Gathering inputs
- 原文："These standards are based on social psychology lab experiments among undergraduates, however, and have since been argued not to be realistic in real-world settings for some sectors and regions"
- 要点：Cohen 的"小、中、大效应"经验值来自以大学生为对象的社会心理学实验室研究，在一些领域和地区的真实环境中被认为不现实。

### E7

- 位置：Practical tips: Initial calculations
- 原文："Initial calculations should focus on whether the study may be feasible. After a proposed project passes a basic test of feasibility, power calculations can be refined and updated with new data."
- 要点：最初的功效计算先回答"这项研究可不可行"；通过可行性检验后，再用新数据细化。

### E8

- 位置：Practical tips: Gathering inputs
- 原文："In clustered designs, while the addition of more clusters will in general increase power more than the addition of more units to existing clusters, the former is typically more expensive to do."
- 要点：整群设计中，增加群的个数一般比在已有群里加人更能提高功效，但成本通常更高。
