# APS 2024：APS Ethics Standards

- 资料：`aps2024ethics`，[原文](https://www.aps.org/about/governance/policies-procedures/ethics-standards)
- 版本：美国物理学会（American Physical Society，APS）理事会 2024 年 12 月 16 日修订通过，2025 年 1 月 1 日生效。2026-10-06 读取网页版。它取代了 2019 年的 19.1 Guidelines on Ethics 和更早的 02.2 Guidelines for Professional Conduct。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction and Rationale，Integrity
- 原文："Integrity is foundational to science. Simply put, physicists must tell the truth."
- 要点：诚信是科学的基础，简单说就是物理学家必须说真话。

### E2

- 位置：I. Research Results，Explanations and Recommendations
- 原文："Research results should be recorded and maintained in a form that allows subsequent use, analysis, review, and reproduction to the maximum extent possible."
- 要点：研究结果的记录和保存方式，要尽可能让别人之后能使用、分析、审查和复现。

### E3

- 位置：I. Research Results，Explanations and Recommendations
- 原文："Research data and products (such as protocols, computer code, and study parameters) should be retained for a reasonable period in accordance with journal, funder, and legal mandates."
- 要点：研究数据和产物（实验规程、程序代码、研究参数等）要按期刊、资助方和法律要求保存一段合理的时间。

### E4

- 位置：II. Authorship，Ethics Standard
- 原文："Authorship should be limited to, and should not exclude, those who have made a significant contribution to the concept, design, execution, interpretation, or writeup of the research study. Authors should be able to identify and attest to their specific contribution to the work."
- 要点：署名只给、也不能漏掉在构思、设计、执行、解释或写作上有重要贡献的人；每个作者都要能说清并确认自己具体贡献了什么。

### E5

- 位置：II. Authorship，Explanations and Recommendations
- 原文："To avoid disputes over attribution of authorship, it may be helpful to decide at the start of writing the paper who will be credited as authors and in what order, who will be acknowledged as contributors, and to validate the decisions with all members of the research team."
- 要点：为避免署名纠纷，可以在开始写论文时就定下谁署名、什么顺序、谁放进致谢，并和团队所有人确认。

### E6

- 位置：II. Authorship，Explanations and Recommendations
- 原文："APS recognizes authorship agreements for large collaborations, so long as authorship is determined through a transparent process."
- 要点：APS 认可大型合作组自己的署名约定，前提是署名通过透明的程序确定。

### E7

- 位置：II. Authorship，Explanations and Recommendations
- 原文："All collaborations should establish processes for ensuring the accuracy and validity of reported results."
- 要点：所有合作都应建立保证所报告结果准确、有效的流程。

### E8

- 位置：III. Redundant Publication，Explanations and Recommendations
- 原文："Preprint servers such as arXiv are a valuable means for sharing new work. Many journals do not consider the submission of a manuscript to a preprint server as redundant publication, but it is the responsibility of the author to follow the relevant journal policies. When the manuscript has been published in a refereed journal, a link to the final document should be added to the preprint."
- 要点：arXiv 等预印本网站是分享新工作的好办法；很多期刊不把发预印本算作重复发表，但作者要自己查清期刊政策；论文正式发表后，要在预印本上加上正式版本的链接。

### E9

- 位置：Treatment of Colleagues，III. Treatment of Supervisees
- 原文："Merely providing funding is not sufficient for authorship but should be noted in the acknowledgements."
- 要点：只提供经费不够资格署名，应写进致谢。

### E10

- 位置：I. Research Results，Ethics Standard
- 原文："Fabrication and falsification do not include honest errors in scientific practice or differences in opinion about interpretation."
- 要点：诚实的错误和对结果解释的不同意见，不算捏造或篡改。

### E11

- 位置：I. Research Results，Explanations and Recommendations
- 原文："Authors are obliged to provide prompt retractions or corrections of errors in published works."
- 要点：作者有义务及时撤回或更正已发表论文中的错误。

### E12

- 位置：III. Redundant Publication，Ethics Standard
- 原文："Authors should avoid artificially increasing the number of publications by fragmenting the publication of research reports."
- 要点：不要把一项研究拆成几篇来人为增加论文数。

### E13

- 位置：III. Redundant Publication，Explanations and Recommendations
- 原文："Simultaneous submissions of the same paper to multiple journals are not allowed."
- 要点：同一篇论文不能同时投多个期刊。

### E14

- 位置：Responsibility to the Profession，IV. Social Media
- 原文："Claims of impacts to science, technology, and society beyond those that have been rigorously established can be misleading and should be avoided or clearly identified as speculation."
- 要点：向公众介绍工作时，超出已严格确立范围的影响宣称会误导人，要么不说，要么明确标为推测。

### E15

- 位置：III. Redundant Publication，Explanations and Recommendations
- 原文："Previous publication of an abstract or abbreviated presentation as part of a meeting does not usually preclude subsequent submission of a longer, more complete version for publication, but full disclosure of the existence of these materials should be included at the time of submission."
- 要点：在会议上发过摘要或简短报告，一般不妨碍之后投更完整的论文，但投稿时要说明这些材料的存在。
