# Prieto 2025：A PhD So Good It Can't Be Ignored: Passion, Practice, and Control

- 资料：`prieto2025so`，[原文](https://ahappyphd.org/posts/phd-so-good-1/)
- 版本：A Happy PhD 博客，2025（书稿节选）。文章借用 Cal Newport《So Good They Can't Ignore You》的观点讨论读博。

### E1

- 位置："passion trap" 部分
- 原文："It’s good to have preferences and tastes, but be flexible in the face of feedback from knowledgeable people (and research literature!) that tell you some other topic is more relevant and feasible for you to investigate."
- 要点：有偏好是好事，但当内行或文献告诉你另一个题目更相关、更可行时，要保持灵活。

### E2

- 位置：讨论"使命"的部分
- 原文："cool missions often require many years of trial and error to be found." / "the PhD is more about “getting the driver’s license” than making the “best lap of your life”"
- 要点：好的长期使命往往要多年试错才能找到；读博更像"考驾照"，而不是"跑出人生最佳一圈"。

### E3

- 位置："rare and valuable skills" 部分
- 原文："If rare and valuable skills (not pre-existing passion) are the key ingredient for developing a satisfying career"
- 要点：Newport 的核心观点：让职业令人满意的关键，是稀缺而有价值的技能，而不是事先就有的热情。

### E4

- 位置：刻意练习部分
- 原文："Experts don’t become experts just by innate talent or spending years on the job" / "practicing at the edge of our abilities"
- 要点：专家不是靠天赋或熬年头成为专家的，而是靠在能力边缘的刻意练习。

### E5

- 位置：刻意练习部分
- 原文："we can ask explicitly about our weaker research skills and advice for improving them."
- 要点：可以主动向导师和同事询问自己较弱的研究技能，以及如何改进。

### E6

- 位置："control traps" 部分
- 原文："prioritize positions (and supervisors) where we will learn the most"
- 要点：早期优先选择能学到最多东西的位置和导师。
