# Bakker & van Eerde 2015：An Introduction to Design-Based Research with an Example From Statistics Education

- 资料：`bakker2015introduction`，[原文 PDF（乌得勒支大学机构库）](https://dspace.library.uu.nl/handle/1874/356250)，DOI [10.1007/978-94-017-9181-6_16](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-94-017-9181-6_16)
- 版本：收入 Bikner-Ahsbahs 等编《Approaches to Qualitative Research in Mathematics Education》（Springer，2015）第 16 章，第 429–466 页；出版社版 PDF 存于作者所在的乌得勒支大学机构库，本次经 Internet Archive 2024-09-11 快照读取。位置写节号和书中页码。

### E1

- 位置：§16.1.1，第 429 页
- 原文："The purpose of this chapter is to introduce researchers, including Master and PhD students, to design-based research."
- 要点：本章专为硕士生、博士生等新手介绍基于设计的研究。

### E2

- 位置：§16.1.1，第 430 页
- 原文："DBR thus produces both useful products (e.g., educational materials) and accompanying scientific insights into how these products can be used in education"
- 要点：基于设计的研究同时产出有用的产品（如教学材料）和关于这些产品怎样在教育中使用的科学认识。

### E3

- 位置：§16.1.2.1，第 430 页
- 原文："The theoretical yield distinguishes DBR from studies that aim solely at designing educational materials through iterative cycles of testing and improving prototypes."
- 要点：有没有理论产出，是基于设计的研究和"只靠反复试用改进教学材料"的开发工作的区别。

### E4

- 位置：§16.1.2.2，第 431 页
- 原文："DBR typically has an explanatory and advisory aim, namely to give theoretical insights into how particular ways of teaching and learning can be promoted. The type of theory developed can also be of a predictive nature: Under conditions X using educational approach Y, students are likely to learn Z"
- 要点：基于设计的研究通常要解释并给出建议：怎样促进某种教与学；产出的理论也可以是预测性的，形如"在条件 X 下用方法 Y，学生可能学会 Z"。

### E5

- 位置：§16.1.2.5，第 434 页
- 原文："Hence rather than generalizing from a random sample to a population (statistical generalization), many (mainly qualitative) research approaches aim for generalization to a theory, model or concept (theoretical or analytic generalization)"
- 要点：许多研究方法不从随机样本推到总体（统计推广），而是推广到理论、模型或概念（理论推广、分析性推广）。

### E6

- 位置：§16.1.2.5，第 434 页
- 原文："Where the use of RCTs can indicate the intervention or treatment being the cause of better learning, DBR cannot claim causality with the same convincing rigor."
- 要点：随机对照试验能表明干预是学习改善的原因；基于设计的研究不能以同样令人信服的严谨程度声称因果。

### E7

- 位置：§16.1.2.6，第 435 页
- 原文："Furthermore, in DBR design is a crucial part of the research, whereas in action research the focus is on action and change, which can but need not involve the design of a new learning environment. DBR also more explicitly aims for instructional theories than does action research."
- 要点：和行动研究相比，基于设计的研究以设计为核心，并更明确地追求教学理论；行动研究关注行动和改变，不一定要设计新的学习环境。

### E8

- 位置：§16.1.2.8，第 437 页
- 原文："Because theories developed in DBR are typically tied to specific learning environments and learning goals, they are humble and hard to generalize."
- 要点：基于设计的研究产出的理论通常绑定在具体的学习环境和学习目标上，所以"谦逊"、难以推广。

### E9

- 位置：§16.1.2.9，第 438 页
- 原文："The cycles typically consist of the following phases: preparation and design phase, teaching experiment, and retrospective analysis."
- 要点：每一轮通常包括三个阶段：准备与设计、教学实验、回溯分析。

### E10

- 位置：§16.1.2.9，第 438 页
- 原文："Yet it must be general enough to be applicable in different contexts such as classrooms in other schools in other countries. In such cases we can speak of transferability."
- 要点：产出的理论虽针对特定领域，但必须足够一般，能用到其他国家其他学校的课堂里；这叫可迁移性。

### E11

- 位置：§16.1.3，第 439 页
- 原文："The hypothetical learning trajectory is made up of three components: the learning goal that defines the direction, the learning activities, and the hypothetical learning process--a prediction of how the students' thinking and understanding will evolve in the context of the learning activities."
- 要点：作者引用 Simon 1995 的定义："假设的学习轨迹"由三部分组成：学习目标、学习活动，以及对学生思维和理解将怎样变化的预测。

### E12

- 位置：§16.1.3.1，第 439 页
- 原文："An elaborated HLT thus includes mathematical learning goals, students' starting points with information on relevant pre-knowledge, mathematical problems and assumptions about students' potential learning processes and about how the teacher could support these processes."
- 要点：一份完整的假设学习轨迹包括：学习目标、学生的起点和已有知识、要用的任务，以及对学生可能怎样学、教师可以怎样支持的设想。

### E13

- 位置：§16.1.3.2，第 440 页
- 原文："Such adjustments are generally not accepted in comparative experimental research, but in DBR, changes in the HLT are made to create optimal conditions and are regarded as elements of the data corpus. This means that these changes have to be reported well and the information is stronger when changes are supported by theoretical considerations."
- 要点：教学实验中途调整计划，在比较实验里一般不被接受；在基于设计的研究里，这些调整本身就是数据的一部分，因此要好好报告，有理论依据的调整说服力更强。

### E14

- 位置：§16.1.3.3，第 440 页
- 原文："Overall, the idea behind developing an HLT is not to design the perfect instructional sequence, which in our view does not exist, but to provide empirically grounded results that others can adjust to their local circumstances."
- 要点：目标不是设计出"完美的教学序列"（作者认为不存在），而是提供有实证基础、别人能按本地情况调整的结果。

### E15

- 位置：§16.1.4.1，第 441 页
- 原文："In the first design phase, it is recommended to collect and invent a set of tasks that could be useful and discuss these with colleagues who are experienced in designing for mathematics education."
- 要点：第一轮设计时，建议收集和自创一批可能有用的任务，并和有设计经验的同行讨论。

### E16

- 位置：§16.1.4.2，第 442 页
- 原文："During a teaching experiment, data collection typically includes student work, tests before and after instruction, field notes, audio recordings of whole-class discussions, and video recordings of every lesson and of the final interviews with students and teachers."
- 要点：教学实验中通常收集：学生作品、教学前后测、现场笔记、全班讨论录音、每节课的录像，以及最后对学生和教师访谈的录像。

### E17

- 位置：§16.1.4.2，第 442 页
- 原文："We further find `mini-interviews' with students, lasting from about twenty seconds to four minutes, very useful provided that they are carried out systematically"
- 要点：作者发现，课堂上对学生做 20 秒到 4 分钟的"微型访谈"很有用，前提是有系统地做。

### E18

- 位置：§16.1.4.3，第 442 页
- 原文："The left part of the matrix summarizes the HLT and the right part is filled with excerpts from relevant transcripts, clarifying notes from the researcher as well as a quantitative impression of how well the match was between the assumed leaning as formulated in the HLT and the observed learning."
- 要点：回溯分析可用一张数据分析矩阵：左边写假设的学习轨迹（每个任务预想学生怎样反应），右边填转写摘录、研究者的说明，以及预想和实际观察到的学习吻合程度。

### E19

- 位置：§16.1.4.3，第 443 页
- 原文："With the HLT and research questions as guidelines, conjectures about students' learning and views were generated and documented, and then tested against the other episodes and other data material (student work, field notes, tests). This testing meant looking for confirmation and counter-examples."
- 要点：以假设学习轨迹和研究问题为指引，生成并记录关于学生学习的猜想，再拿其他片段和数据（学生作品、现场笔记、测验）去检验，既找支持，也找反例。

### E20

- 位置：§16.1.4.3，第 443 页
- 原文："Seemingly crucial episodes were discussed with colleagues to test whether they agreed with our interpretation or perhaps could think of alternative interpretations. This process is called peer examination."
- 要点：看起来关键的片段要拿去和同事讨论，看他们是否同意你的解释、能否想到别的解释；这叫同行检视。

### E21

- 位置：§16.1.5.1，第 444 页
- 原文："The succession of different teaching experiments makes it possible to test the conjectures developed in earlier experiments in later experiments."
- 要点：一轮接一轮的教学实验，使前一轮形成的猜想可以在后一轮中检验。

### E22

- 位置：§16.1.5.1，第 444 页
- 原文："Reports about DBR tend to be long due to the thick descriptions (Geertz 1973) required."
- 要点：基于设计的研究需要"深描"，所以报告往往很长。

### E23

- 位置：§16.1.5.2，第 444 页
- 原文："The challenge is to present the results (instruction theory, HLT, educational activities) in such a way that others can adjust them to their local contingencies."
- 要点：难点在于把结果（教学理论、学习轨迹、教学活动）呈现得让别人能按本地情况调整使用。

### E24

- 位置：§16.1.5.3，第 445 页
- 原文："Data collection by objective devices such as audio- and video registrations contribute to the internal reliability."
- 要点：用录音、录像等客观设备收集数据，有助于提高内部信度（数据收集和分析不依赖于研究者个人）。

### E25

- 位置：§16.1.5.3，第 445 页
- 原文："For measuring interrater reliability, the agreement among independent researchers, it is advised to calculate not only the percentage of agreement but also use Cohen's kappa or another measure that takes into account the probability of agreement by chance"
- 要点：衡量编码者之间的一致性时，除了一致百分比，还要算 Cohen's kappa 等考虑了偶然一致的指标。

### E26

- 位置：§16.1.5.4，第 445 页
- 原文："This means that the reader must be able to track or trace the learning process of the researchers and to reconstruct their study: failures and successes, procedures followed, the conceptual framework used, and the reasons for certain choices must all be reported."
- 要点：读者必须能追踪研究者的认识过程、重建整项研究：失败和成功、所用程序、概念框架、做某些选择的理由，都要报告。

### E27

- 位置：§16.1.2.3，第 432 页
- 原文："The products of DBR are judged on innovativeness and usefulness, not just on the rigor of the research process that is more prominent in evaluating true experiments"
- 要点：基于设计的研究的成果，按创新性和有用性来评判，而不只看研究过程的严谨性（后者在评价真实验时更突出）。

### E28

- 位置：§16.1.3.2，第 439 页
- 原文："It may happen that the teacher or researcher feels the need to adjust the HLT or instructional activity for the next lesson."
- 要点：教学实验期间，教师或研究者可能觉得需要调整下一节课的学习轨迹或活动。

### E29

- 位置：§16.1.2.5，第 434 页
- 原文："A second argument why RCT has its limitations is that a new strategy has to be designed before it can be tested, just like a Boeing airplane cannot be compared with an Airbus without a long tradition of engineering and producing such airplanes. In many cases, considerable research is needed to design innovative approaches."
- 要点：随机对照试验的局限之一：新策略要先设计出来才能检验；很多时候，设计创新的做法本身就需要大量研究。
