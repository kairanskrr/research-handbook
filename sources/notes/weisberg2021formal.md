# Weisberg 2021：Formal Epistemology（斯坦福哲学百科）

- 资料：`weisberg2021formal`，[原文](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/formal-epistemology/)
- 版本：斯坦福哲学百科（Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy，美国斯坦福大学主办，开放获取）条目，作者 Jonathan Weisberg（加拿大多伦多大学），2015 年首发，2021 年 3 月实质修订；2026-10-08 读取。位置写条目小节。这是一位作者为百科写的综述，不是学会或期刊的规定。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Formal epistemology explores knowledge and reasoning using “formal” tools, tools from math and logic. For example, a formal epistemologist might use probability theory to explain how scientific reasoning works. Or she might use modal logic to defend a particular theory of knowledge."
- 要点：形式认识论用数学和逻辑工具研究知识和推理，例如用概率论解释科学推理，或用模态逻辑为某种知识理论辩护。

### E2

- 位置：§2 The Problem of Induction，§2.2 末尾
- 原文："Formal epistemology gives us precise ways of stating how induction works. But these precise formulations do not themselves solve a problem like Hume’s, for they rely on assumptions like the probability axioms, Carnap’s assignment of prior probabilities, and Conditionalization. Still, they do help us isolate and clarify these assumptions, and then formulate various arguments in their defense. Whether formal epistemology thereby aids in the solution of Hume’s problem depends on whether these formulations and justifications are plausible, which is controversial."
- 要点：形式化给出精确的表述，但它本身不解决哲学问题，因为它依赖概率公理等假设；它的作用是把这些假设分离出来、讲清楚，再为它们论证。能否推进问题，取决于这些表述和辩护是否站得住，而这是有争议的。

### E3

- 位置：§5.1 The Zollman Effect
- 原文："Since the above model is both simple and idealized, this lack of robustness should give us pause, they argue, about its likely applicability in real-world scenarios."
- 要点：（转述 Rosenstock、Bruner 和 O'Connor 的观点）模型既简单又理想化，结果又不稳健（只在相当特定的条件下出现），就应当怀疑它能否用到现实情形。

### E4

- 位置：§6.3 The Meaning of ‘If…Then…’（信念修正理论）
- 原文："Of course, real people don’t believe everything their beliefs entail, but it helps keep things simple to make this assumption. You can think of it as an idealization: we’re theorizing about what your beliefs should look like if you were a perfect logician."
- 要点：形式模型常做理想化假设，例如假设人相信自己信念推出的一切；这是为了简单，理论讲的是"完美的逻辑学家"的信念应该是什么样。
