# 美国化学工程师学会：Code of Ethics

- 资料：`aichecodeethics`，[原文](https://www.aiche.org/about/governance/policies/code-ethics)
- 版本：美国化学工程师学会（American Institute of Chemical Engineers，AIChE）官网的伦理准则页面。官网对脚本访问返回 403，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2026-10-04 的快照（https://web.archive.org/web/20261004132831/https://www.aiche.org/about/governance/policies/code-ethics ）。准则不分节，位置写条目序号。

### E1

- 位置：导言段
- 原文："The Board of Directors of the American Institute of Chemical Engineers adopted this Code of Ethics to which it expects that the professional conduct of its members shall conform, and to which every applicant attests by signing his or her membership application."
- 要点：这份准则由学会理事会通过，约束的是学会会员；申请入会的人签字表示遵守。

### E2

- 位置：第 1 条
- 原文："Hold paramount the safety, health and welfare of the public and protect the environment in performance of their professional duties."
- 要点：履行职责时，把公众的安全、健康和福祉放在首位，并保护环境。

### E3

- 位置：第 2 条
- 原文："Formally advise their employers or clients (and consider further disclosure, if warranted) if they perceive that a consequence of their duties will adversely affect the present or future health or safety of their colleagues or the public."
- 要点：如果发现自己的工作会损害同事或公众现在或将来的健康安全，要正式告知雇主或客户，必要时考虑进一步披露。

### E4

- 位置：第 3 条
- 原文："Accept responsibility for their actions, seek and heed critical review of their work and offer objective criticism of the work of others."
- 要点：为自己的行为负责，主动寻求并认真对待别人对自己工作的批评，也客观地评论别人的工作。

### E5

- 位置：第 4 条
- 原文："Issue statements or present information only in an objective and truthful manner."
- 要点：发表意见、提供信息，都要客观真实。

### E6

- 位置：第 5 条
- 原文："Act in professional matters for each employer or client as faithful agents or trustees, avoiding conflicts of interest and never breaching confidentiality."
- 要点：在职业事务中忠实地为雇主或客户办事，避免利益冲突，绝不泄露保密信息。

### E7

- 位置：第 7 条
- 原文："Perform professional services only in areas of their competence."
- 要点：只在自己胜任的领域提供专业服务。
