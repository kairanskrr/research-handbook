# Smith、Cullis、Thompson 2005：The analysis of crop cultivar breeding and evaluation trials: an overview of current mixed model approaches

- 资料：`smith2005analysis`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0021859605005587)
- 版本：Journal of Agricultural Science 143: 449–462，2005（该刊百年综述系列）。剑桥大学出版社网站免费提供 PDF（Unpaywall 标注为开放获取）。作者来自澳大利亚新南威尔士州农业部门和英国洛桑研究所。位置写小节。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Typically, the yield data generated from crop breeding and evaluation programmes arises from series of field trials known as multi-environment trials (MET). These trials allow the investigation of varietal yield performance across a range of geographic locations and, possibly, years (seasons)."
- 要点：育种和品种评价的产量数据，一般来自一系列田间试验，叫多环境试验；它们用来考察品种在不同地点、可能还有不同年份（季节）的表现。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："These advantages include the ease with which incomplete data (not all varieties in all environments) can be handled, the ability to use more realistic within-trial models for error variation (e.g. incomplete blocks, spatial correlation models) and the ability to assume some sets of effects (e.g. variety and/or environment effects) to be random rather than fixed."
- 要点：线性混合模型分析多环境试验的好处：容易处理不完整数据（不是每个品种都在每个环境里种），能给试验内误差用更现实的模型（不完全区组、空间相关模型），还能把品种和（或）环境效应当随机效应。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："is that it provides no insight into the nature of VrE interaction. This may hinder varietal selection and recommendation decisions."
- 要点：传统方差分析的一大缺点，是看不出品种与环境交互作用的性质，可能妨碍品种选择和推荐。

### E4

- 位置：Two-stage analyses
- 原文："Many authors (Patterson & Silvey 1980; Talbot 1984; Patterson & Nabugoomu 1992) use a two-stage approach in which variety means are first obtained from the analyses of individual trials and are then combined to form the data for an overall mixed model analysis. The two-stage approach is an approximation to the more efficient one-stage approach."
- 要点：两阶段分析：先逐个试验分析得到品种均值，再合起来做总的混合模型分析。它是更有效的一阶段分析（直接用全部小区数据）的近似。

### E5

- 位置：Two-stage analyses
- 原文："Historically the two-stage approach was required due to the difficulty in obtaining individual plot data. Often this was not stored electronically but this should no longer be an issue."
- 要点：过去用两阶段分析，是因为很难拿到逐个小区的数据，常常没有电子存档；现在这不应再是问题。

### E6

- 位置：Conclusions
- 原文："A further challenge is to encourage the use of random rather than fixed variety effects. This is not an easy task, particularly as this is still a controversial topic among statisticians."
- 要点：鼓励把品种效应当随机效应而不是固定效应，并不容易，这在统计学家之间仍有争议。

### E7

- 位置：Conclusions
- 原文："the present authors believe that variety effects should be assumed to be random since this minimizes selection errors when identifying the best varieties"
- 要点：作者认为品种效应应当作随机效应，因为这样在挑选最好的品种时选错的概率最小。
