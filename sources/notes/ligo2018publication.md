# LIGO Scientific Collaboration 2018：Publication and Presentation Policy

- 资料：`ligo2018publication`，[原文](https://dcc.ligo.org/public/0026/T010168/010/T010168-10.pdf)
- 版本：LIGO-T010168-10，2018 年 2 月。LIGO 科学合作组（LIGO Scientific Collaboration，LSC，引力波探测合作组）公开的发表与报告政策。

### E1

- 位置：§2.A The LSC Author List，第 1 条
- 原文："The author list of the scientific publications of LSC observations will include all members of the LIGO Scientific Collaboration who have earned that status. The author list will be alphabetical"
- 要点：LSC 的观测论文，作者名单包括所有已取得作者资格的成员，按字母排序。

### E2

- 位置：§2.A，第 1 条
- 原文："The nominal goal of the specific implementation described in 2 is that LSC members earn author status by devoting at least 50% of their research effort to the LSC for a period of approximately one year."
- 要点：成员大约要用一年时间、把至少一半的研究精力投入 LSC，才取得作者资格。

### E3

- 位置：§2.A，第 6 条
- 原文："Individual LSC members eligible for authorship may request their names not to be included in specific papers."
- 要点：有作者资格的成员可以要求不在某篇论文上署名。

### E4

- 位置：§2.B Archival Journals，第 5 条
- 原文："Technical papers originating from individual or collaborating LSC institutions shall assign authorship in accordance with generally accepted principles."
- 要点：由单个机构或几个机构合作完成的技术论文，按一般的署名原则确定作者，不用全合作组署名。

### E5

- 位置：§2.B，第 3 条
- 原文："The primary authors can petition to the P&P committee for a limited author list of those individuals whose work substantially contributed to the paper."
- 要点：方法类论文的主要作者可以向发表委员会申请"短作者名单"，只列实质贡献者。

### E6

- 位置：§3.A Triggers for an LSC review，第 1 条
- 原文："This determination must be made prior to submission for publication or posting on any public archive (e.g., LANL gr-qc, arXiv)."
- 要点：是否需要合作组内部审查，必须在投稿或发到 arXiv 等公开平台之前确定。

### E7

- 位置：§3.B Procedures for Review，第 2 条
- 原文："The reviewer(s) confirms the most important results presented in the manuscript and provides comments on the content and recommendations on the authorship."
- 要点：内部审稿人要核实论文最重要的结果，并对内容和署名提意见。

### E8

- 位置：§4 Presentations，4.1
- 原文："Only public observational results or results that have been approved by the Collaboration"
- 要点：只有已公开的观测结果或经合作组批准的结果，才能在邀请报告和会议论文集中公开展示。

### E9

- 位置：§5 Theses，第 1 条
- 原文："An analysis claiming detection cannot be published in a thesis until an official announcement has been made by the LSC and the LIGO Directorate."
- 要点：在合作组正式宣布之前，学位论文里不能发表声称探测到信号的分析。

### E10

- 位置：§5 Theses，第 3 条
- 原文："If the thesis does not undergo a review or does not meet the above criteria, a disclaimer shall be added to the introduction, stating that the work does not reflect the scientific opinion of the LSC and it was not reviewed by the collaboration."
- 要点：没经过合作组审查的学位论文，要在引言里声明它不代表合作组的观点、未经合作组审查。

### E11

- 位置：§1 Introduction，政策目标
- 原文："encourage younger scientists and engineers to participate in the presentation and publication of results."
- 要点：政策的目标之一是鼓励年轻科学家和工程师参与报告和发表。
