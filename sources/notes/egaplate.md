# EGAP 方法指南：10 Things You Need to Know About the Local Average Treatment Effect

- 资料：`egaplate`，[原文网页](https://methods.egap.org/guides/research-questions/late_en.html)
- 版本："治理与政治证据网络"（EGAP）方法指南，作者 Peter van der Windt；2026 年 10 月访问。位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§1 What the LATE is
- 原文："In some cases it is possible to estimate the “Local Average Treatment Effect” (LATE), also known as the “Complier Average Causal Effect” (CACE). The LATE is the average treatment effect for the Compliers."
- 要点：局部平均处理效应（又称依从者平均因果效应）是"依从者"（按分组决定是否接受干预的人）的平均处理效应。

### E2

- 位置：§2 With one-sided noncompliance you need to satisfy an exclusion restriction to estimate the LATE
- 原文："The exclusion restriction (also called “excludability”) stipulates that outcomes respond to treatments, not treatment assignments."
- 要点：排除性约束：结果只对实际接受的干预有反应，而不对"被分到哪一组"本身有反应。

### E3

- 位置：§6 The LATE only reflects treatment effects among compliers
- 原文："While a LATE estimate is better than nothing, it provides a consistent estimate of the average treatment effect only for a subgroup of the population: the Compliers."
- 要点：局部平均处理效应只代表依从者这个子群体，不代表全体样本。

### E4

- 位置：§5 How to conduct statistical inference about the LATE
- 原文："Because the LATE equals the ratio of the ITT and the share of Compliers, the LATE will be equal to zero whenever the ITT equals zero."
- 要点：局部平均处理效应等于意向处理效应除以依从者比例；意向处理效应为零，它也为零。
