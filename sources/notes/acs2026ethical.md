# 美国化学会 2026：Ethical Guidelines to Publication of Chemical Research

- 资料：`acs2026ethical`，[原文](https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/ethical_guidelines)
- 版本：美国化学会（American Chemical Society，ACS）出版部编辑们制定的出版伦理准则，2026 年 6 月 15 日起生效的修订版，2026-10-06 读取。适用于 ACS 旗下期刊。

### E1

- 位置：B. Ethical Obligations of Authors，第 1 条
- 原文："Data–defined as information collected or used to generate research conclusions–must be presented with sufficient detail and/or citation to reliable and accessible sources to allow a professional in the research field to replicate the study's findings."
- 要点：数据要写得足够详细（或引用可靠、可获取的来源），让本领域的专业人员能重复出研究结果。

### E2

- 位置：B，第 3 条
- 原文："Data must be retained and made available for at least five years after publication, and authors are encouraged to deposit their data in a publicly accessible database when possible."
- 要点：数据在发表后至少保存并可提供五年；鼓励尽量存入公开数据库。

### E3

- 位置：B，第 5 条
- 原文："any unusual hazards associated with chemicals, equipment, or procedures used in an investigation must clearly be identified in the manuscript."
- 要点：研究中用到的化学品、设备或步骤有任何不寻常的危险，都必须在稿件中明确指出。

### E4

- 位置：B，第 6 条
- 原文："Authors must evaluate the potential for unintended dual-use or harmful applications of their research"
- 要点：作者必须评估研究被意外用于"两用"（既可民用也可被滥用）或有害用途的可能。

### E5

- 位置：B，第 7 条
- 原文："Fragmenting research across multiple publications should be avoided."
- 要点：应避免把一项研究拆成多篇发表。

### E6

- 位置：B，第 9 条
- 原文："Authors should submit a manuscript to only one journal at a time" / "Submitting a full paper that expands on a previously published brief report (such as a "communication" or "letter") is generally acceptable, but the earlier publication must be cited and disclosed to the editor at submission."
- 要点：一稿只能投一家期刊；在已发表的短篇快报（communication 或 letter）基础上扩展成完整论文一般可以，但要引用早先的快报并在投稿时告知编辑。

### E7

- 位置：B，第 13 条（署名）
- 原文："The co-authors of a manuscript should be all those persons who have made significant scientific contributions to the reported work and who accept shared responsibility and accountability for the integrity and accuracy of the results."
- 要点：合作者应是对工作有重要科学贡献、并愿意为结果的诚信和准确共同负责的所有人。

### E8

- 位置：B，第 13 条（署名）
- 原文："The submitting author is responsible for sharing a draft of the manuscript with each living co-author and obtaining their agreement to be listed as a co-author."
- 要点：投稿作者负责把稿件发给每位在世的合作者，并取得他们同意署名。

### E9

- 位置：B，第 13 条（署名）
- 原文："Authors may acknowledge individuals who contributed to the work but do not meet authorship criteria in the "Acknowledgments" section. This may include technical staff or data professionals."
- 要点：有贡献但不够署名条件的人（如技术人员）可以写进致谢。
