# Ruder 2020：10 Tips for Research and a PhD

- 资料：`ruder2020tips`，[原文](https://www.ruder.io/10-tips-for-research-and-a-phd/)
- 版本：作者博客，2020，2026-10-05 读取。

### E1

- 位置：Work on two things
- 原文："Instead, I've found it useful to work on two projects at once to keep my sanity in check. If you hit a wall on one project, you can spend some time working on the other."
- 要点：同时做两个项目，一个碰壁时可以转去做另一个，换个角度可能反而解开难题。

### E2

- 位置：Work on two things
- 原文："To minimise context-switching, I generally try to work on one project each day."
- 要点：为减少来回切换，每天只做其中一个项目。

### E3

- 位置：4) Collaborate
- 原文："On the contrary, being able to collaborate is an important skill that you will need later on."
- 要点：读博不是孤军奋战，合作是以后一定用得上的重要能力。

### E4

- 位置：4) Collaborate
- 原文："In collaborations, particularly if they are remote, it is important to communicate clearly and to set expectations."
- 要点：合作中（尤其远程合作）要沟通清楚、事先讲好期望。

### E5

- 位置：4) Collaborate
- 原文："If you are working on two projects, make one of them a collaboration. Collaborating with someone different from your advisor introduces you to a new perspective and will allow you to learn more than working on your own."
- 要点：和导师以外的人合作，能带来新视角，比独自工作学到更多。

### E6

- 位置：10) Play the long game
- 原文："Be generous in giving praise and attribution."
- 要点：对他人的贡献，在称赞和署名致谢上要大方。

### E7

- 位置：1) Read broadly.
- 原文："In order to have a rich repertoire to draw on for inspiration, try to cultivate diverse interests. Look beyond your immediate horizon."
- 要点：要有丰富的灵感来源，就要培养广泛的兴趣，看向自己的小领域之外。

### E8

- 位置：2) Work on two things.
- 原文："If the project is not going well, your motivation and well-being may suffer. If you hit a roadblock, you can do nothing but grind until you resolve it."
- 要点：只做一个项目的风险：项目不顺时，动力和身心状态都会受影响，卡住时只能硬磨。

### E9

- 位置：3) Be ambitious.
- 原文："You can work on a relatively safe project and one that is high-risk but also may be more impactful. The safe work ensures that you will graduate."
- 要点：同时做一个稳妥的项目和一个高风险高回报的项目；稳妥的那个保证你能毕业。

### E10

- 位置：3) Be ambitious.
- 原文："A good indication of whether something you are working on is impactful is whether you'd be excited if it was published by someone else."
- 要点：判断工作有没有影响力的一个办法：如果别人发表了它，你会不会感到兴奋。

### E11

- 位置：5) Be proactive.（开头）
- 原文："This is probably the most important piece of advice. Don't restrict yourself to the people in your immediate circle."
- 要点：作者认为最重要的一条建议：不要只局限于身边的人，要主动联系更多研究者。

### E12

- 位置：7) Keep a source of positive energy.
- 原文："External rewards such as paper acceptances are sparse, so leveraging intrinsic rewards is often necessary."
- 要点：论文被接收这样的外部奖励很稀少，所以常常需要依靠内在动力。

### E13

- 位置：7) Keep a source of positive energy.
- 原文："So it is important to build a support network that you can rely on." / "find an activity that you can fall back on to give you positive energy when things don't go as planned"
- 要点：建立可以依靠的支持网络；找一项在事情不顺时能给你正能量的活动。

### E14

- 位置：7) Keep a source of positive energy.
- 原文："In the end, the most important resource is not the amount of compute you have, but your personal well-being."
- 要点：最重要的资源不是算力，而是你自己的身心健康。

### E15

- 位置：8) Play to your strengths.
- 原文："Your ideal research topic sits at the intersection of work that is impactful, work that you are passionate about, and work that you are uniquely suited for."
- 要点：理想的课题在三者交集处：有影响力、你热爱、你特别适合做。

### E16

- 位置：10) Play the long game.
- 原文："Take care of yourself. Work hard but get enough sleep and exercise." / "the more important outcome of a PhD is a better version of yourself."
- 要点：照顾好自己，努力工作但保证睡眠和运动；读博更重要的产出是更好的自己。

### E17

- 位置：7) Keep a source of positive energy.
- 原文："The most natural way to stay positive and energised in research is to work on something that excites you and to follow your curiosity." / "In those cases, try to find a particular angle that excites you."
- 要点：保持积极最自然的方式，是做让你兴奋的事、跟随好奇心；不能自己选题时，就在题目里找一个让你兴奋的角度。
