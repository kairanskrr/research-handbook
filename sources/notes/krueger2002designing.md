# Krueger 2002：Designing and Conducting Focus Group Interviews

- 资料：`krueger2002designing`，[原文（PDF，美国东伊利诺伊大学网站上的公开副本）](https://www.eiu.edu/ihec/Krueger-FocusGroupInterviews.pdf)；明尼苏达大学下属的 SHADAC 中心也公开了同一份讲义：[FocGrp_Krueger_Oct02.pdf](https://www.shadac.org/sites/default/files/publications/FocGrp_Krueger_Oct02.pdf)
- 版本：Richard A. Krueger（明尼苏达大学教授、评估项目负责人）2002 年 10 月的讲义，共 17 页，按页脚页码引用。出自项目评估和应用研究。2026-10-17 读的是东伊利诺伊大学网站上的副本；SHADAC 链接当天有访问验证，未能下载核对。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 页，Characteristics of Focus Group Interviews
- 原文："Carefully recruited" / "5 to 10 people per group, 6-8 preferred" / "Similar types of people" / "Repeated groups"
- 要点：参与者要精心招募，每组 5 到 10 人、最好 6 到 8 人，同一组是相似的人，并且要做多组。

### E2

- 位置：第 1 页，Characteristics of Focus Group Interviews
- 原文："Moderator" / "Skillful in group discussions" / "Uses pre-determined questions" / "Establishes permissive environment"
- 要点：主持人要会带小组讨论，使用事先定好的问题，营造让人敢说话的宽松气氛。

### E3

- 位置：第 2 页，Moderator Skills
- 原文："Use an assistant moderator" / "Takes careful notes" / "Monitors recording equipment"
- 要点：要配一名助理主持人，负责仔细记笔记、看管录音设备。

### E4

- 位置：第 2 页，Moderator Skills
- 原文："Use pauses and probes" / "5 second pause" / "Probes:" / "\"Would you explain further?\"" / "\"Would you give an example?\""
- 要点：主持人要会停顿和追问：停五秒钟；追问如"能再解释一下吗""能举个例子吗"。

### E5

- 位置：第 2 页，Moderator Skills
- 原文："Control reactions to participants" / "Head nodding" / "Short verbal responses" / "(avoid \"that's good\", \"excellent\")" / "Use subtle group control" / "Experts" / "Dominant talkers" / "Shy participants" / "Ramblers"
- 要点：主持人要控制自己对发言的反应，避免说"很好""太棒了"；要不动声色地管住"专家"、话多的人，照顾害羞的人和跑题的人。

### E6

- 位置：第 3 页，Bulleted Outline（开场提纲）
- 原文："No right or wrong answers, only differing points of view" / "You don't need to agree with others, but you must listen respectfully as others share their views" / "Talk to each other"
- 要点：开场要讲清规则：没有对错，只有不同看法；不必同意别人，但要尊重地听；请大家彼此交谈。

### E7

- 位置：第 4 页，Beginning the Focus Group Discussion
- 原文："You may be assured of complete confidentiality."
- 要点：Krueger 的开场示例里对参与者说"可以保证完全保密"。

### E8

- 位置：第 6 页，Asking Questions that Yield Powerful Information
- 原文："Use open-ended questions" / "Avoid dichotomous questions" / "Use \"think back\" questions." / "Take people back to an experience and not forward to the future"
- 要点：问开放问题，避免只能答"是 / 否"的问题；多用"回想一下"的问题，把人带回到经历中，而不是让他们设想将来。

### E9

- 位置：第 6 页，Asking Questions that Yield Powerful Information
- 原文："Five Types of Questions" / "1. Opening Question (round robin)" / "2. Introductory Question" / "3. Transition Questions" / "4. Key Questions" / "5. Ending Questions" / "Sequence that goes from general to specific"
- 要点：问题分五类：开场（每人轮流答）、引入、过渡、关键、结尾；顺序从一般到具体。

### E10

- 位置：第 7 页，Ending Questions
- 原文："After the brief oral summary the question asked is:" / "\"Is this an adequate summary?\"" / "The moderator reviews the purpose of the study and then asks the participants:" / "\"Have we missed anything?\""
- 要点：结尾时主持人口头总结，问"这个总结准确吗"；再重申研究目的，问"我们漏掉什么了吗"。

### E11

- 位置：第 9 页，Note Taking
- 原文："Note taking is a primary responsibility of the assistant moderator" / "The moderator should not be expected to take written notes during the discussion."
- 要点：记笔记主要是助理主持人的事，不应指望主持人在讨论中记笔记。

### E12

- 位置：第 10 页，Systematic Analysis Process
- 原文："Conduct moderator and assistant moderator debriefing" / "Note themes, hunches, interpretations, and ideas" / "Compare and contrast this focus group to other groups"
- 要点：每组结束后马上由主持人和助理一起复盘，记下主题、直觉和想法，并和其他组比较。

### E13

- 位置：第 11 页，Focus Group Analysis Tips，Context
- 原文："Participant responses were triggered by a stimulus--a question asked by the moderator or a comment from another participant. Examine the context by finding the triggering stimulus and then interpret the comment in light of that environment."
- 要点：每句发言都是被某个刺激引出来的，可能是主持人的问题，也可能是别人的话；分析时要找到这个刺激，结合上下文解读。

### E14

- 位置：第 11 页，Focus Group Analysis Tips，Internal consistency
- 原文："Participants in focus groups change and sometimes even reverse their positions after interaction with others. When there is a shift in opinion, the researcher typically traces the flow of the conversation to determine clues that might explain the change."
- 要点：参与者和别人交流后会改变甚至反转立场；遇到这种情况，要顺着对话找出可能解释变化的线索。

### E15

- 位置：第 11 页，Focus Group Analysis Tips，Specificity
- 原文："Responses that are specific and based on experiences should be given more weight than responses that are vague and impersonal."
- 要点：具体的、基于亲身经历的回答，应比含糊、泛泛的回答更受重视。

### E16

- 位置：第 15 页，Transcribing Focus Group Interviews
- 原文："Use bold print for the moderator's statements and questions. If possible, type the name of each speaker followed by his or her comment." / "Typically it takes about eight hours to type one hour of tape."
- 要点：转录时把主持人的话标出来，尽量标明每句话是谁说的；一小时录音大约要八小时转录。

### E17

- 位置：第 15 页，Transcribing Focus Group Interviews
- 原文："For example, if there is laughter, loud voices, shouting, etc. be sure that these are noted in the transcript in parenthesis. Make note if someone was interrupted."
- 要点：转录稿里要用括号注明笑声、提高嗓门等声音，也要注明谁被打断了。
