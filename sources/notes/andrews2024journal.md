# Andrews 2024：Insights from the American Philosophical Association's Journal Surveys Project

- 资料：`andrews2024journal`，[原文](https://blog.apaonline.org/2024/06/07/insights-from-the-american-philosophical-associations-journal-surveys-project/)
- 版本：美国哲学学会博客（Blog of the APA）2024 年 6 月 7 日的文章，作者 Sam Andrews。数据来自美国哲学学会的"期刊调查"（Journal Surveys）项目：作者自愿上报投稿经历，没有经过期刊核实。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Authors can submit information about how long a journal took to review their manuscript, how good their experience was with the editors, if and what quality of comments they received, and if the manuscript was accepted for publication. Since its creation by Andy Cullison in 2009 over 9,400 surveys have been completed as of April 2024."
- 要点：作者可以上报期刊审稿用了多久、和编辑打交道的体验、收到的意见及质量、是否被接收；项目 2009 年创建，到 2024 年 4 月共收到 9,400 多份问卷。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："There are drawbacks to using author-reported surveys to glean insights into journal metrics as we do not know how accurate the surveys are and most journals do not provide the stats that would allow us to verify the survey results. For example, the acceptance rate for most journals is widely believed to be overstated in the surveys"
- 要点：作者自报的数据有缺陷：不知道准不准，多数期刊也不公布可供核对的统计；例如一般认为问卷里多数期刊的接收率被高估了。

### E3

- 位置：Setup
- 原文："However, many of these surveys are from some time ago and only 1,568 surveys (18.42%) were submitted in the last 5 years."
- 要点：清理后的 8,512 份问卷中，只有 1,568 份是最近五年提交的；作者只分析这五年（2019–2023）的数据。

### E4

- 位置：Setup
- 原文："Survey responses are spread out among 97 journals, for an average of 16.2 per journal."
- 要点：这五年的问卷分布在 97 种期刊上。

### E5

- 位置：Results of the Journal Surveys: Response Length
- 原文："Across all surveys, the average time for journals to return the initial verdict was 3.22 months. 866 surveys (55.3%) reported a response time of less than 3 months, 468 (29.8%) reported between 3-6 months and 234 (14.9%) reported longer than 6 months."
- 要点：在这些问卷中，期刊给出初审决定的平均时间是 3.22 个月；55.3% 的问卷报告不到 3 个月，29.8% 为 3–6 个月，14.9% 超过 6 个月。

### E6

- 位置：Results of the Journal Surveys: Comment Chances
- 原文："Across all surveys, 67% of surveys reported receiving 1 or more sets of reviewer comments."
- 要点：67% 的问卷报告收到了至少一份审稿意见（其余没有收到意见，例如被直接拒稿）。
