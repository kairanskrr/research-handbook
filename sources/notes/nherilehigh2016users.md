# NHERI Lehigh 2016：RTMD Experimental Facility User's Guide

- 资料：`nherilehigh2016users`，[原文](https://lehigh.designsafe-ci.org/media/cms_page_media/95/NHERI%20Lehigh%20RTMD%20EF%20User's%20Guide%202016-11-02_YsuNvbw.pdf)
- 版本：2016 年 11 月 2 日版用户指南。美国国家科学基金会资助的自然灾害工程研究基础设施（NHERI）在利哈伊大学的实时多向（RTMD）试验设施，做大尺度结构试验和混合仿真。

### E1

- 位置：Preface（第 1 段）
- 原文："The types of laboratory simulations and tests enabled by the Lehigh EF include: (1) hybrid simulation (HS) which combines large-scale physical models with computer-based numerical simulation models"
- 要点：该设施支持的试验类型之一是混合仿真：把大尺度实物模型和计算机数值模型结合起来。

### E2

- 位置：§1.6 Configuring an Experiment
- 原文："All testing proposed by the researchers will need to be reviewed by the RTMD staff prior to testing to ensure the testing protocol does not present a risk to the safety of laboratory staff and researchers of any potential for damaging the equipment." / "The researchers must also include a statement as to what is the expected damage that will occur to the test specimen during each test. No testing will commence until either the Lehigh Equipment Site PI, Co-PI, or Research Scientist of the RTMD has approved in writing the test matrix."
- 要点：研究者提出的所有试验，都要事先交设施人员审查，确认不危及人员安全、不损坏设备；研究者要说明每次试验预计试件会出现什么损伤；试验矩阵经书面批准前不得开始。

### E3

- 位置：§1.6 Configuring an Experiment
- 原文："No deviation from the test matrix is permitted without the approval of either the NHERI Lehigh EF PI, Co-PI, or RTMD Research Scientist/Engineer."
- 要点：未经批准，不得偏离已批准的试验矩阵。

### E4

- 位置：§1.6 Configuring an Experiment
- 原文："Control programs are developed and validated through hydraulics off simulations with the RTMD IT Manager." / "All aspects are tested in a safe manner before any experiment is conducted."
- 要点：控制程序要先在关闭液压的仿真模式下开发和确认；正式试验前，各个环节都要以安全的方式测试过。

### E5

- 位置：§2.7 Software Policies
- 原文："software used for any form of testing at the NHERI Lehigh RTMD EF must be validated before placed on the Real-Time Integrated Control System. The algorithms which the software is based on must be shown to be stable."
- 要点：任何试验用的软件，装上实时控制系统之前都必须经过确认，所依据的算法必须证明是稳定的。

### E6

- 位置：§1.9.1 Data Description
- 原文："The researchers will be given training and assistance by the NHERI Lehigh EF IT Systems Manager, but will responsible for uploading all of their data, including: the metadata that describes the experimental setup report; unprocessed experimental data; converted experimental data"
- 要点：研究者要自己负责上传全部数据，包括描述试验装置的元数据、未处理的原始试验数据和转换后的数据等。

### E7

- 位置：§1.9.1 Data Description
- 原文："All data will be made available raw and uncorrected, as well as in converted forms in engineering units." / "the data will be documented to enable a qualified researcher to reproduce the simulation or test and generate the derived data throughout the lifetime of data repository."
- 要点：数据既以未修正的原始形式提供，也以换算成工程单位的形式提供；数据要有足够文档，让有资质的研究者能重做仿真或试验并重新生成派生数据。

### E8

- 位置：§1.11 Payload Project Protocol
- 原文："All ongoing and newly funded projects at the NHERI Lehigh Experimental Facility are posted on the site's website to enable researchers to identify potential payload project opportunities."
- 要点：设施网站公布所有在研和新资助的项目，方便其他研究者寻找"搭载项目"机会（在别人的试验上附加自己的研究）。

### E9

- 位置：§5.1 Guidelines for Proposal Preparation
- 原文："Lehigh's site also recommends that contact be made early in the proposal process with NHERI Lehigh's RTMD EF Operations Manager, in order to aid in planning, scheduling, cost development, etc."
- 要点：建议在写项目申请的早期就联系设施运行经理，帮助规划、排期和估算费用。

### E10

- 位置：§5.3 Required Documentation
- 原文："the following two primary documents were required for completion prior to the onset of any laboratory activity for an awarded NEES research project from 2004 through 2014." / "Indemnification  Insurance  Payment terms  Termination terms  Intellectual Property rights  Publication rights" / "As of the drafting of this document (4/1/16), there is no research participation agreement template or equipment site policies compliance check document required by NHERI."
- 要点：2004—2014 年，获资助的 NEES（地震工程模拟网络）项目在开始任何试验室工作前要完成两份文件，其中"研究参与协议"包括赔偿、保险、付款、终止、知识产权、发表权等条款；截至 2016 年 4 月起草这份指南时，NHERI 没有要求这类文件（利哈伊设施保留在项目开始前加入的权利）。

### E11

- 位置：§5.4 Training
- 原文："A formal on-site training program must be completed satisfactorily prior to any use of NHERI or non-NHERI equipment, including utilization of all ATLSS laboratory equipment."
- 要点：使用任何设备之前，必须完成正式的现场培训。

### E12

- 位置：§5.5 Experiment Execution
- 原文："As a safety precaution, during testing, researchers will be asked to refrain from entering the test area."
- 要点：出于安全考虑，试验进行时研究者不得进入试验区。
