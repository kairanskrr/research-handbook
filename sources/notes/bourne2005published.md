# Bourne 2005：Ten Simple Rules for Getting Published

- 资料：`bourne2005published`，PLoS Computational Biology 1(5): e57，开放获取
- 版本：期刊社论。引文中的连字（fi、fl）已还原为普通字母。

### E1

- 位置：Rule 5
- 原文："The correct response to a paper being rejected or requiring major revision is to listen to the reviewers and respond in an objective, not subjective, manner."
- 要点：被拒或被要求大修时，正确做法是听取审稿意见，客观而非情绪化地回应。

### E2

- 位置：Rule 5
- 原文："If reviewers are unanimous about the poor quality of the paper, move on—in virtually all cases, they are right. If they request a major revision, do it and address every point they raise both in your cover letter and through obvious revisions to the text."
- 要点：审稿人一致认为质量差，就接受并继续前进；要求大修时，在回复信和正文修改中回应每一条意见。

### E3

- 位置：Rule 5
- 原文："Scientific careers are full of rejection, even for the best scientists."
- 要点：科研生涯充满拒稿，最好的科学家也一样。

### E4

- 位置：Rule 8
- 原文："Reviewing other papers will help you write better papers. To start, work with your mentors; have them give you papers they are reviewing and do the first cut at the review"
- 要点：尽早参与审稿：从帮导师写审稿初稿开始，有助于写好自己的论文。

### E5

- 位置：Rule 9
- 原文："This will define the form and level of detail and assumed novelty of the work you are doing."
- 要点：尽早决定投哪里，因为它决定论文的形式、细节程度和对新颖性的要求。

### E6

- 位置：Rule 3
- 原文："Put your energy into improving the quality of the manuscript before submission."
- 要点：精力应放在投稿前提高稿件质量上；有根本缺陷的稿件得不到有用的审稿建议。

### E7

- 位置：Rule 6
- 原文："Get a candid opinion by having the paper read by colleagues without a vested interest in the work, including those not directly involved in the topic area."
- 要点：请与工作无利害关系、甚至不在该方向的同事读稿，获得坦率意见。

### E8

- 位置：Rule 7
- 原文："Be familiar with the guide to authors and follow it, the editors and reviewers do."
- 要点：熟悉并遵守投稿指南。

### E9

- 位置：Rule 10
- 原文："It is better to publish one paper in a quality journal than multiple papers in lesser journals."
- 要点：宁可在高质量期刊发一篇，也不要在较差的期刊发多篇。

### E10

- 位置：Rule 9
- 原文："Many journals have a presubmission enquiry system available—use it."
- 要点：很多期刊提供投稿前咨询，可以利用。
