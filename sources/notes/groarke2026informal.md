# Groarke 2026：Informal Logic（斯坦福哲学百科全书）

- 资料：`groarke2026informal`，[原文](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-informal/)
- 版本：《斯坦福哲学百科全书》条目，作者 Leo Groarke；1996 年 11 月 25 日首发，2026 年 1 月 28 日实质修订。非形式逻辑（informal logic）研究日常讨论、教育、法律、医学等真实场景中的推理和论证。位置写节号。本文件是第 106 号任务的本地摘录。

### E1

- 位置：§3 Standardizing Arguments
- 原文："a first step in the analysis of a real-life argument is an attempt to "standardize" the argument by identifying its premises, conclusions, and inferences."
- 要点：分析真实论证的第一步是"标准化"：找出前提、结论和推理。

### E2

- 位置：§3 Standardizing Arguments
- 原文："discarding irrelevant and distracting digressions, repetition, and remarks ("noise") which do not play a role in the reasoning the argument contains;" / "clarifying incomplete, vague or ambiguous claims and utterances;" / "recognizing components of the argument which are not explicitly expressed."
- 要点：标准化包括：去掉与推理无关的离题话和重复（"噪音"）；澄清不完整、含糊或有歧义的说法；找出没有明说的论证成分。

### E3

- 位置：§3.1 Implicit Premises and Conclusions
- 原文："Recognizing an argument's implicit premises prepares the way for argument evaluation, for these premises, like an argument's explicit premises, need to be evaluated when the argument is assessed."
- 要点：隐含前提和明说的前提一样，评价论证时都要评价。

### E4

- 位置：§3.1 Implicit Premises and Conclusions
- 原文："One principle often used in choosing between alternative interpretations of an implicit argument component is the "Principle of Charity." It favors an interpretation of an argument which makes the argument as credible as possible."
- 要点：在隐含成分的几种解读之间选择时，常用"宽容原则"：选让论证尽可能可信的解读。

### E5

- 位置：§3.1 Implicit Premises and Conclusions
- 原文："In many situations, this can best be accomplished by assigning the argument a "logically minimum" implicit premise, understood as the weakest component needed to successfully connect the argument's premises to its conclusion."
- 要点：常见做法是补上"逻辑上最弱"的隐含前提：刚好够把前提和结论连起来的最弱成分。

### E6

- 位置：§3.3 "Supplemented" Standardizations
- 原文："It is a foundational kernel of the argument, but becomes an argument in the full sense only when it engages the dialectical tier, weighing alternative points of view, addressing the objections to the conclusion they suggest."
- 要点：（转述 Johnson 2000）前提加结论只是论证的内核；只有进入"论辩层"，权衡其他观点、回应它们对结论提出的反驳，才算完整的论证。

### E7

- 位置：§4.1 AV Criteria
- 原文："making "Acceptability, Relevance and Sufficiency" the requirements for good argument. On this account, premises are acceptable when they are true or acceptable in some other way; relevant when they provide some (i.e. any) support for the conclusion of the argument; and sufficient when they provide enough support to warrant its acceptance"
- 要点：很多非形式逻辑体系用"可接受、相关、充分"三条标准评价论证：前提为真或以其他方式可接受；对结论有支持；支持足以让人接受结论。
