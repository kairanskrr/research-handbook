# Ioannidis 2005：Why Most Published Research Findings Are False

- 资料：`ioannidis2005why`，[PLoS Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.0020124)（CC BY）
- 版本：PLoS Med 2(8): e124，2005。观点文章（Essay），结论基于一个概率模型推演，不是对已发表研究的实测。

### E1

- 位置：Corollary 4
- 原文："Corollary 4: The greater the flexibility in designs, definitions, outcomes, and analytical modes in a scientific field, the less likely the research findings are to be true. Flexibility increases the potential for transforming what would be “negative” results into “positive” results"
- 要点：设计、定义、结局和分析方式越灵活，研究发现越不可能为真；灵活性让"阴性"结果更容易被变成"阳性"。

### E2

- 位置：Corollary 5
- 原文："Corollary 5: The greater the financial and other interests and prejudices in a scientific field, the less likely the research findings are to be true. Conflicts of interest and prejudice may increase bias"
- 要点：经济和其他利益、成见越多，研究发现越不可能为真；利益冲突和成见可能增加偏倚。

### E3

- 位置：How Can We Improve the Situation?
- 原文："In some research designs, efforts may also be more successful with upfront registration of studies, e.g., randomized trials"
- 要点：对随机试验等设计，事先登记可能有助于改善问题。
