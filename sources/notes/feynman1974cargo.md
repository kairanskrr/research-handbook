# Feynman 1974：Cargo Cult Science

- 资料：`feynman1974cargo`，[原文](https://calteches.library.caltech.edu/51/2/CargoCult.htm)
- 版本：Caltech 1974 年毕业典礼演讲，Engineering and Science 杂志刊载稿（Caltech 图书馆网页版）。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Then a method was discovered for separating the ideas—which was to try one to see if it worked, and if it didn’t work, to eliminate it. This method became organized, of course, into science."
- 要点：科学的起点是一种区分想法的方法：试一试是否管用，不管用就淘汰。

### E2

- 位置：Cargo Cult Science 的比喻
- 原文："So I call these things Cargo Cult Science, because they follow all the apparent precepts and forms of scientific investigation, but they’re missing something essential, because the planes don’t land."
- 要点：形式上完全像科学、却缺了本质的东西的研究，就是"货物崇拜科学"。

### E3

- 位置：Cargo Cult Science 的比喻之后，讲科学诚信
- 原文："It’s a kind of scientific integrity, a principle of scientific thought that corresponds to a kind of utter honesty—a kind of leaning over backwards. For example, if you’re doing an experiment, you should report everything that you think might make it invalid—not only what you think is right about it"
- 要点：缺的是科学诚信：做实验时，要报告所有可能让结果不成立的因素，而不只是支持它的部分。

### E4

- 位置：同上
- 原文："If you make a theory, for example, and advertise it, or put it out, then you must also put down all the facts that disagree with it, as well as those that agree with it."
- 要点：提出理论时，要把与它不符的事实也一并写出来。

### E5

- 位置：同上
- 原文："In summary, the idea is to try to give all of the information to help others to judge the value of your contribution; not just the information that leads to judgment in one particular direction or another."
- 要点：给出全部信息，让别人能自己判断你的贡献有多大价值，而不是只给引向某个结论的信息。

### E6

- 位置：Wesson Oil 的例子
- 原文："So it’s the implication which has been conveyed, not the fact, which is true, and the difference is what we have to deal with."
- 要点：每句话都属实，但整体传达的暗示可能是误导的；科学诚信要求连暗示也要诚实。

### E7

- 位置：Millikan 油滴实验之后
- 原文："The first principle is that you must not fool yourself—and you are the easiest person to fool."
- 要点：第一原则是不要骗自己，而你自己是最容易被骗的人。

### E8

- 位置：向公众解释研究
- 原文："If you’re representing yourself as a scientist, then you should explain to the layman what you’re doing—and if they don’t want to support you under those circumstances, then that’s their decision."
- 要点：一位宇宙学家担心说"没有应用"会拿不到经费；Feynman 认为编造应用是不诚实的，应如实说明。

### E9

- 位置：发表结果
- 原文："If you’ve made up your mind to test a theory, or you want to explain some idea, you should always decide to publish it whichever way it comes out. If we only publish results of a certain kind, we can make the argument look good."
- 要点：决定检验一个理论，就要无论结果如何都发表；只发表某一类结果，会让论证显得比实际更好。

### E10

- 位置：Cornell 心理系学生的例子
- 原文："I explained to her that it was necessary first to repeat in her laboratory the experiment of the other person—to do it under condition X to see if she could also get result A—and then change to Y and see if A changed. Then she would know that the real difference was the thing she thought she had under control."
- 要点：在别人的结果上改条件之前，先在自己的条件下重复原实验；这样才知道差异真的来自你改变的那个因素。

### E11

- 位置：演讲正文，讲"科学诚信"（scientific integrity）的段落
- 原文："if you’re doing an experiment, you should report everything that you think might make it invalid—not only what you think is right about it: other causes that could possibly explain your results; and things you thought of that you’ve eliminated by some other experiment, and how they worked"
- 要点：做实验要报告所有可能使它不成立的因素：能解释结果的其他原因，以及你已经用别的实验排除了哪些、怎么排除的。

### E12

- 位置：演讲正文，讲"无论结果如何都发表"的段落
- 原文："you should always decide to publish it whichever way it comes out. If we only publish results of a certain kind, we can make the argument look good."
- 要点：决定检验一个理论，就要无论结果正反都发表；只发表某一类结果，会让论证显得比实际更好。

### E13

- 位置：演讲正文，Cornell 心理系学生做老鼠实验的例子
- 原文："it was necessary first to repeat in her laboratory the experiment of the other person—to do it under condition X to see if she could also get result A—and then change to Y and see if A changed. Then she would know that the real difference was the thing she thought she had under control."
- 要点：要研究条件 Y 的影响，先在自己的实验室重复别人在条件 X 下的实验，确认能得到同样结果，再只改成 Y。这样才知道差异真的来自你控制的那个因素。

### E14

- 位置：演讲正文，Millikan 油滴实验之后电子电荷测量史的例子
- 原文："When they got a number that was too high above Millikan’s, they thought something must be wrong—and they would look for and find a reason why something might be wrong. When they got a number closer to Millikan’s value they didn’t look so hard."
- 要点：结果不符合预期时就去找毛病，符合预期时就不再细查，这种不对称会让错误长期存在。

### E15

- 位置：演讲正文，Young 1937 年老鼠走廊实验的例子
- 原文："it uncovers the clues that the rat is really using—not what you think it’s using."
- 要点：好的实验要找出并排除真正起作用的线索，而不是想当然地认为是你以为的那个因素在起作用。

### E16

- 位置：演讲正文，科学诚信一节的小结
- 原文："the idea is to try to give all of the information to help others to judge the value of your contribution; not just the information that leads to judgment in one particular direction or another."
- 要点：报告的目的，是给出让别人能判断你工作价值的全部信息，而不只是引向某个方向的信息。

### E17

- 位置："leaning over backwards" 段
- 原文："if you’re doing an experiment, you should report everything that you think might make it invalid—not only what you think is right about it"
- 要点：做实验时，要报告所有你认为可能让结果失效的因素，而不只是支持它的部分。
