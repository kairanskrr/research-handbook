# Hernán 2018：The C-Word: Scientific Euphemisms Do Not Improve Causal Inference From Observational Data

- 资料：`hernan2018cword`，[PubMed Central 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5888052/)
- 版本：American Journal of Public Health 108(5): 616–619，2018。评论文章（commentary），代表作者个人观点。

### E1

- 位置：正文开头
- 原文："Many journal editors request authors to avoid causal language"
- 要点：很多期刊编辑要求作者避免使用因果语言。

### E2

- 位置：正文开头
- 原文："The proscription against the C-word is harmful to science because causal inference is a core task of science, regardless of whether the study is randomized or nonrandomized."
- 要点：作者认为禁用"因果"一词有害，因为不论是否随机，因果推断都是科学的核心任务。

### E3

- 位置：OF COURSE "ASSOCIATION IS NOT CAUSATION"
- 原文："The risk ratio of 0.8 does not imply that drinking a glass of wine every day lowers the risk of heart disease by 20%."
- 要点：观察到的风险比 0.8，不等于"每天喝一杯葡萄酒能让心脏病风险降低 20%"。

### E4

- 位置：OF COURSE "ASSOCIATION IS NOT CAUSATION"
- 原文："It is possible that the kind of people who drink a glass of wine per day would have a lower risk of heart disease even if they didn’t drink wine because, for example, they have high enough incomes to buy, besides wine, nutritious food and to take time off to exercise, or have better access to preventive health care."
- 要点：每天喝一杯酒的人，可能本来收入更高、吃得更好、有时间锻炼、更容易得到预防保健，所以即使不喝酒，心脏病风险也更低。

### E5

- 位置：BETTER CAUSAL QUESTIONS
- 原文："a causal analysis in observational data can be viewed as an attempt to emulate a hypothetical trial—the target trial."
- 要点：用观察性数据做因果分析，可以看作是在模拟一个假想的试验，即"目标试验"。

### E6

- 位置：BETTER CAUSAL QUESTIONS
- 原文："A key advantage of specifying the target trial is that it forces investigators to consider the intervention of interest and the time period during which it takes place. For example, a target trial could assign people to 10 years of daily wine drinking from age 55 years, or to 20 years of wine drinking from age 30 years. Each of these target trials answers a different causal question"
- 要点：写出目标试验的好处是，逼研究者想清楚关心的干预是什么、在哪个时间段发生。例如"55 岁起每天喝、喝 10 年"和"30 岁起喝 20 年"是两个不同的目标试验，回答不同的因果问题。

### E7

- 位置：BETTER CONFOUNDING ADJUSTMENT
- 原文："If the goal of the observational analysis is causal, adjustment for confounding is generally necessary." / "if the goal of the observational analysis is purely associational, no adjustment for confounding is necessary."
- 要点：目标是因果，一般要调整混杂；目标纯粹是描述关联，就不需要调整混杂。

### E8

- 位置：BETTER CONFOUNDING ADJUSTMENT
- 原文："Automatic variable selection procedures may work for prediction, but not necessarily for causal inference."
- 要点：自动筛选变量的方法可能适合预测，但不一定适合因果推断。

### E9

- 位置：CONFLATING THE MEANS AND THE ENDS
- 原文："The only part of the article in which the term “causal effect” has no place is the Results section, which should present the findings without trying to interpret them."
- 要点：论文里唯一不该出现"因果效应"的是结果部分，结果部分只呈现发现、不做解释。

### E10

- 位置：BETTER CONFOUNDING ADJUSTMENT
- 原文："there is no guarantee that a causal model incorporates all the confounders and therefore there is no guarantee that the parameter estimate for treatment can be causally interpreted, even approximately."
- 要点：没法保证模型纳入了所有混杂因素，所以也没法保证估计值能解释为因果。

### E11

- 位置：CONFLATING THE MEANS AND THE ENDS
- 原文："such a trial is considered unethical and hence forbidden. Unfortunately, that means that observational analyses become our best chance to quantify the long-term causal effect of wine on chronic diseases."
- 要点：让人长期按分组喝酒的试验不合伦理，所以观察性分析成了估计这种长期因果效应的最好机会。
