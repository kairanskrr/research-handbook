# 美国历史学会 2013：Statement on Policies Regarding the Option to Embargo Completed History PhD Dissertations

- 资料：`aha2013embargo`，[原文（Internet Archive 快照）](https://web.archive.org/web/20240601005252/https://www.historians.org/jobs-and-professional-development/statements-standards-and-guidelines-of-the-discipline/statement-on-policies-regarding-the-option-to-embargo-completed-history-phd-dissertations)
- 版本：美国历史学会（AHA）2013 年声明。官网对脚本访问返回 403，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2024-06-01 的快照（旧网址 `historians.org/jobs-and-professional-development/statements-standards-and-guidelines-of-the-discipline/statement-on-policies-regarding-the-option-to-embargo-completed-history-phd-dissertations`）。声明不分节，位置写段落序号。

### E1

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："History has been and remains a book-based discipline"
- 要点：历史学过去是、现在仍是一个以书为本的学科。

### E2

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："it is not unusual for an early-career historian to spend five or six years revising a dissertation and preparing the manuscript for submission to a press for consideration."
- 要点：刚毕业的历史学者花五六年修改博士论文、准备投给出版社的书稿，并不少见。

### E3

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："During that period, the scholar typically builds on the raw material presented in the dissertation, refines the argument, and improves the presentation itself."
- 要点：修改期间，学者通常在博士论文的材料基础上扩充、打磨论点、改进表达。

### E4

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："Many universities award tenure only to those junior faculty who have published a monograph within six years of receiving the PhD."
- 要点：许多大学只给获得博士学位后六年内出版了专著的年轻教师终身教职。

### E5

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："an increasing number of university presses are reluctant to offer a publishing contract to newly minted PhDs whose dissertations have been freely available via online sources."
- 要点：越来越多的大学出版社不愿和博士论文已在网上免费公开的新博士签出版合同。

### E6

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："The American Historical Association strongly encourages graduate programs and university libraries to adopt a policy that allows the embargoing of completed history PhD dissertations in digital form for as many as six years."
- 要点：AHA 鼓励研究生项目和大学图书馆允许历史学博士论文的电子版延迟公开，最长六年。
