# Karhulahti 2024：Positionality statements in science

- 资料：`karhulahti2024positionality`，[PMC 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11200055/)，doi:10.12688/openreseurope.17058.2
- 版本：Open Research Europe 4:62（2024，第 2 版），开放获取（CC BY）。作者是芬兰于韦斯屈莱大学的学者，这是一篇短文（essay），讨论各学科何时适合写"立场声明"。通过 Europe PMC 读取全文。位置写小节。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："positionality statements, which refer to disclosing the scientist’s position in relation to the conducted research."
- 要点：立场声明（positionality statement）是披露研究者自己与所做研究之间的关系和位置。

### E2

- 位置：What positionality does
- 原文："When the scientist itself is part of the toolset that is used to generate primary research data—for instance, ethnographic fieldnotes—the details of that “tool” are relevant to acknowledge"
- 要点：当研究者本人就是产生一手数据的"工具"（例如写民族志田野笔记）时，这个"工具"的情况应当交代。

### E3

- 位置：What positionality does
- 原文："Because engaging in fieldwork is a process where only a small part of interactions and observations can be documented, it matters who engaged and selected the fieldnote data."
- 要点：田野中只有一小部分互动和观察能被记录下来，所以是谁参与、谁挑选了写进笔记的内容，很重要。

### E4

- 位置：Two common misunderstandings
- 原文："A common misunderstanding is that scientific positionality statements must pick a set of group identities, as from a ‘shopping list’ [...] Each positionality statement should fit the methods and topics of a specific study"
- 要点：常见误解是立场声明必须像"购物清单"一样罗列各种群体身份；其实每份声明应贴合具体研究的方法和题目。

### E5

- 位置：What positionality does
- 原文："As in preregistration more generally [...] stating pre-study assumptions can help making useful analytic calibrations before it is too late."
- 要点：和预注册类似，在研究开始前写下自己的预设，有助于及早调整分析。

### E6

- 位置：Two common misunderstandings
- 原文："journals should not mandate positionality statements but treat them like other research tools: encourage them to be used wisely when fitting with the methodology and philosophy of a study."
- 要点：作者认为期刊不应强制要求立场声明，而应在适合研究方法和理念时鼓励明智使用。
