# Bourne & Korngreen 2006：Ten Simple Rules for Reviewers

- 资料：`bourne2006reviewers`，PLoS Computational Biology 2(9): e110，开放获取
- 版本：期刊社论。引文中的连字（fi、fl）已还原为普通字母。

### E1

- 位置：Rule 1
- 原文："Do Not Accept a Review Assignment unless You Can Accomplish the Task in the Requested Timeframe—Learn to Say No"
- 要点：不能按时完成就不要接审稿任务。

### E2

- 位置：Rule 2
- 原文："Do not take on the review if there is the slightest possibility of conflict of interest."
- 要点：只要有一点利益冲突的可能，就不要接；拿不准时问编辑。

### E3

- 位置：Rule 3
- 原文："Support your criticisms or praise with concrete reasons that are well laid out and logical."
- 要点：批评和表扬都要给出具体、有条理的理由。

### E4

- 位置：Rule 4
- 原文："It is always beneficial to remember that you are there to help the authors in their work, even if this means rejecting their manuscript."
- 要点：审稿人是来帮助作者的，即使结论是拒稿。

### E5

- 位置：Rule 9
- 原文："A point-by-point critique is valuable since it is easy to read and to respond to. For each point, indicate how critical it is to your accepting the paper."
- 要点：逐条列出意见，并注明每条对是否录用有多关键。

### E6

- 位置：Rule 9
- 原文："Provide a rating—fence-sitting prolongs the process unnecessarily."
- 要点：要给出明确的推荐，不要骑墙。

### E7

- 位置：Rule 9
- 原文："Further, as passionate as you might be about the subject of the paper, do not push your own opinion or hypotheses."
- 要点：不要借审稿推销自己的观点或假设。

### E8

- 位置：引言
- 原文："There is certainly a place for papers that, for example, support existing hypotheses, or provide a new or modified interpretation of an existing finding."
- 要点：新颖性常是前提但不是一切：支持已有假设、对已有发现给出新解释的论文也有价值。

### E9

- 位置：Rule 10
- 原文："However, be sure those comments are clearly supported by your review"
- 要点：给编辑的保密意见也必须有审稿正文支撑。

### E10

- 位置：Rule 6
- 原文："Including citations missed by the author (not your own) is often a short but effective way to help improve a paper."
- 要点：补充作者漏掉的文献（不是你自己的）是简单有效的帮助。

### E11

- 位置：Rule 8
- 原文："If anonymity is the rule of the journal, do not share the manuscript with colleagues unless the Editor has given the green light."
- 要点：未经编辑同意，不要把稿件给同事看。

### E12

- 位置：Rule 10
- 原文："It is also a good place to indicate your own shortcomings, biases, etc., with regard to the content of the paper"
- 要点：可以在给编辑的意见中说明自己在该内容上的不足和偏向。
