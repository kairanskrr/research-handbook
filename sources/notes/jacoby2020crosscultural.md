# Jacoby 等人 2020：Cross-Cultural Work in Music Cognition: Challenges, Insights, and Recommendations

- 资料：`jacoby2020crosscultural`，[原文（阿姆斯特丹大学机构库，出版版本）](https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/45923408/185.full.pdf)，DOI [10.1525/mp.2020.37.3.185](https://doi.org/10.1525/mp.2020.37.3.185)
- 版本：Music Perception 37(3):185–195, 2020，立场文章（position paper），作者 20 人，来自德国马克斯·普朗克实证美学研究所、美国、加拿大、英国、荷兰、澳大利亚、日本等地的大学。出版社页面标为 CC BY 开放获取；本文用的是阿姆斯特丹大学机构库公开的出版版本 PDF，2026-10-08 读取。位置写页码和小标题。

### E1

- 位置：第 186 页，引言
- 原文："Music psychology shares this sampling bias: participants are almost always recruited from WEIRD societies, experimental materials are usually drawn from Western music, and studies tend to investigate constructs such as harmonic progressions that are disproportionately relevant to Western music."
- 要点：音乐心理学也有抽样偏差：被试几乎都来自 WEIRD（西方、受过教育、工业化、富裕、民主）社会，实验材料多取自西方音乐，研究的概念（如和声进行）也偏向和西方音乐相关。

### E2

- 位置：第 186 页，引言
- 原文："First, it biases the understanding of human mechanisms for music perception and production, because WEIRD populations do not necessarily constitute a representative sample"
- 要点：这会让我们对人类感知和产生音乐的机制的理解有偏差，因为 WEIRD 人群不一定是有代表性的样本。

### E3

- 位置：第 186 页，引言
- 原文："This document outlines a list of recommendations that arose from an interdisciplinary conversation at the Max Planck Institute for Empirical Aesthetics in Frankfurt, Germany over the course of several days in October 2018. The meeting from which these recommendations emerged was not designed to generate an exhaustive checklist or set of guidelines for cross-cultural research in music, but rather to serve as a departure point for further thought." / "a major limitation of the meeting was that it was comprised predominantly of white men and women from elite Euro-American academic institutions."
- 要点：这些建议来自 2018 年 10 月在德国法兰克福马克斯·普朗克实证美学研究所的一次跨学科研讨，不是一份完整的清单或指南，只是进一步讨论的起点；作者承认与会者主要是欧美精英学术机构的白人。

### E4

- 位置：第 191 页，Empirical Methods: Issues
- 原文："Every mode of data collection represents tradeoffs among many factors, including insider knowledge or lack thereof, reproducibility, precision, bias, accuracy, logistical feasibility, cost, invasiveness, ecological validity, and pragmatics." / "In every case (e.g., human participant observation, behavioral experimentation, neuroimaging), empiricism aims for data that are as reproducible and valid as possible."
- 要点：每种收集数据的方式都要在可复现性、精度、偏差、成本、生态效度等因素之间取舍；无论参与观察、行为实验还是神经影像，目标都是数据尽可能可复现、有效。

### E5

- 位置：第 191 页，Empirical Methods: Issues
- 原文："It can be problematic to impose external concepts, notation, or measures on participants from a different musical culture. For instance, it may be problematic to ask for perceived emotions expressed by music without having established that music is used to express emotions in a given cultural context."
- 要点：把外来的概念、记谱法或测量工具强加给另一种音乐文化的被试可能有问题；例如，还没确认音乐在当地是用来表达情绪的，就让被试评音乐表达的情绪。

### E6

- 位置：第 191 页，Empirical Methods: Recommendations
- 原文："When rating scales are used, the number of scale steps could be limited to three or four to enable clear labeling of each scale step with appropriate adjectives or pictures. Where such scales are used, a control experiment is necessary to validate understanding of the scale" / "These and other materials used across cultures should be pilot tested in collaboration with experts in the culture."
- 要点：跨文化使用评分量表时，等级可以限制在三到四级，每级都配上合适的形容词或图片；使用时必须做控制实验，确认被试理解量表；跨文化使用的材料要和熟悉该文化的专家一起做预试。

### E7

- 位置：第 191 页，Empirical Methods: Recommendations
- 原文："One strategy for minimizing misinterpretation is to avoid the use of language and musical notation, with their attendant translation issues. Numerous approaches could accomplish this goal such as the use of physiological measures" / "or production tasks like tapping along with a rhythm or singing a melody"
- 要点：减少误解的一种办法是避免用语言和乐谱，例如用生理测量，或让被试跟着节奏打拍、唱一段旋律这样的产出任务。

### E8

- 位置：第 188 页，Music: Recommendations
- 原文："All scientific research involves a tradeoff between experimental control and ecological validity. The latter can be amplified by turning to paradigms that incorporate diverse musics and performance scenarios rather than relying on the most readily available and commonly used materials."
- 要点：所有科学研究都要在实验控制和生态效度之间取舍；纳入多样的音乐和表演场景，而不是只用最容易拿到、最常用的材料，可以提高生态效度。
