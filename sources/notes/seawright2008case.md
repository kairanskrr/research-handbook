# Seawright & Gerring 2008：Case Selection Techniques in Case Study Research

- 资料：`seawright2008case`，[作者 Gerring 在波士顿大学个人主页上挂的 PDF（Internet Archive 快照）](https://web.archive.org/web/2016/http://blogs.bu.edu/jgerring/files/2013/06/CaseSelection.pdf)
- 版本：Political Research Quarterly 61(2): 294–308 的出版社排版 PDF，由作者放在个人主页上；位置写期刊页码。

### E1

- 位置：引言，第 294 页
- 原文："Case selection is the primordial task of the case study researcher, for in choosing cases, one also sets out an agenda for studying those cases."
- 要点：选案例是案例研究者的首要任务，选了什么案例，就定下了怎样研究它们。

### E2

- 位置：引言，第 294 页
- 原文："In case studies of this sort, the chosen case is asked to perform a heroic role: to stand for (represent) a population of cases that is often much larger than the case itself."
- 要点：被选中的案例要承担"英雄般"的角色：代表一个往往大得多的案例总体。

### E3

- 位置：引言，第 295 页
- 原文："scholars continue to lean primarily on pragmatic considerations such as time, money, expertise, and access." / "Of course, these are perfectly legitimate factors in case selection. Yet they do not provide a methodological justification for why case A might be preferred over case B."
- 要点：学者选案例主要看时间、经费、专长和能否进入；这些理由完全正当，但不构成为什么选 A 不选 B 的方法论理由。

### E4

- 位置：引言，第 295 页
- 原文："Thus, even if cases are initially chosen for pragmatic reasons, it is essential that researchers understand retroactively how the properties of the selected cases comport with the rest of the population."
- 要点：即使最初出于实际原因选了案例，事后也必须弄清这些案例和总体中其他案例比起来是什么位置。

### E5

- 位置：Why Not Choose Cases Randomly?，第 295 页
- 原文："This result shows that for a comparative case study composed of five cases (or less), randomized case selection procedures will often produce a sample that is substantially unrepresentative of the population."
- 要点：作者的模拟显示，比较案例研究只有五个或更少案例时，随机选择常常得到明显不代表总体的样本。

### E6

- 位置：Techniques of Case Selection，第 296 页
- 原文："one desires (1) a representative sample and (2) useful variation on the dimensions of theoretical interest."
- 要点：选案例有两个目标：样本有代表性，以及在理论关心的维度上有有用的变化。

### E7

- 位置：Techniques of Case Selection，第 296 页
- 原文："the seven case study types presented in Table 1: typical, diverse, extreme, deviant, influential, most similar, and most different."
- 要点：七种选案例方式：典型、多样、极端、偏离、有影响力、最相似、最不同。

### E8

- 位置：Typical Case，第 299 页
- 原文："the researcher wants to find a typical case of some phenomenon so that he or she can better explore the causal mechanisms at work in a general, cross-case relationship."
- 要点：选典型案例，是为了深入探究一个普遍的跨案例关系背后的因果机制。

### E9

- 位置：Extreme Case，第 301 页
- 原文："Selecting cases on the dependent variable is indeed problematic if the researcher treats the resulting sample—the extreme case—as if it were representative of a population."
- 要点：按因变量（结果）选案例，如果把选出的极端案例当作总体的代表，确实有问题。

### E10

- 位置：Extreme Case，第 301 页
- 原文："Note that the extreme case method refers back to a larger sample of cases lying in the background of the analysis. These cases provide a full range of variation as well as a more representative picture of the population."
- 要点：极端案例法要参照分析背后更大的一批案例，它们提供完整的变化范围和更有代表性的总体图景。

### E11

- 位置：Deviant Case，第 302 页
- 原文："The deviant case method selects cases that, by reference to some general cross-case relationship, demonstrate a surprising value; they are poorly explained."
- 要点：偏离案例是相对于某个普遍的跨案例关系、结果出人意料、解释不了的案例。

### E12

- 位置：Deviant Case，第 302 页
- 原文："The purpose of a deviant case analysis is usually to probe for new—but as yet unspecified—explanations."
- 要点：研究偏离案例通常是为了探寻新的、尚未明确的解释。

### E13

- 位置：Most Similar，第 304 页
- 原文："In its purest form, the chosen pair of cases is similar on all the measured independent variables, except the independent variable of interest."
- 要点：最相似设计的理想形式：两个案例在所有测量到的自变量上相同，只在关心的那个自变量上不同。

### E14

- 位置：Most Different，第 306 页
- 原文："the researcher tries to identify cases where just one independent variable as well as the dependent variable covary, and all other plausible independent variables show different values." / "Analysts have usually taken the position that this research design is a weaker tool for causal inference than the most similar method"
- 要点：最不同设计：只有一个自变量和结果一起变化，其他可能的自变量都不同；分析者通常认为它比最相似设计弱。

### E15

- 位置：Complications，第 306 页
- 原文："Often, a researcher begins in an exploratory mode and proceeds to a confirmatory mode—that is, she develops a specific X/Y hypothesis." / "Unfortunately, research strategies that are ideal for exploration are not always ideal for confirmation."
- 要点：研究常从探索转为验证，但适合探索的设计不一定适合验证。

### E16

- 位置：Complications，第 306 页
- 原文："One can explain, straightforwardly, that the initial research was undertaken in an exploratory fashion and therefore was not constructed to test the specific hypothesis that is—now—the primary argument."
- 要点：一种处理办法：如实说明最初的研究是探索性的，并不是为检验现在的主要论点而设计的。
