# Easterbrook 等人 2008：Selecting Empirical Methods for Software Engineering Research

- 资料：`easterbrook2008selecting`，[原文 PDF](http://www.cs.toronto.edu/~sme/papers/2007/SelectingEmpiricalMethods.pdf)（第一作者多伦多大学主页上的作者版本）
- 版本：收入 Shull, Singer & Sjøberg 主编的 *Guide to Advanced Empirical Software Engineering*（Springer, 2008），第 285–311 页。作者版本无出版页码，位置写到小节。

### E1

- 位置：§1.0 Introduction
- 原文："These methods all have known flaws, and each can only provide limited, qualified evidence about the phenomena being studied. However, each method is flawed differently (McGrath, 1995) and viable research strategies use multiple methods, chosen in such a way that the weaknesses of each method are addressed by use of complementary methods"
- 要点：每种方法都有已知的缺陷，只能提供有限的证据；但各方法的缺陷不同，可行的研究策略是组合多种方法，让一种方法的弱点由另一种方法弥补。

### E2

- 位置：§5.6 Mixed-Methods Approaches
- 原文："Mixed method research employs data collection and analysis techniques associated with both quantitative and qualitative data. The ‘mixing’ might be within one study, by using multiple data collection techniques, or among several studies. Key decisions involve the strategy for data collection, and the sequence in which different methods are employed."
- 要点：混合方法研究同时使用与定量和定性数据相关的收集和分析技术；混合可以在一项研究内，也可以跨几项研究；关键决定是数据收集策略和各方法的先后顺序。

### E3

- 位置：§5.6 Mixed-Methods Approaches
- 原文："While mixed method research is a powerful approach to inquiry, the researcher is challenged with the need for extensive data collection, the time-intensive nature of analyzing multiple sources of data, as well as the requirement to be familiar with both quantitative and qualitative forms of research."
- 要点：混合方法很有力，但代价是数据收集量大、分析多源数据很费时间，还要求研究者同时熟悉定量和定性研究。

### E4

- 位置：§5.6 Mixed-Methods Approaches
- 原文："The Sequential explanatory strategy is characterized by the collection and analysis of quantitative data followed by the collection and analysis of qualitative data. The purpose of this strategy is typically to use qualitative results to assist in explaining and interpreting the findings of a quantitative study. It is particularly useful when unexpected results arise from the quantitative phase."
- 要点：解释性序贯设计：先收集分析定量数据，再收集分析定性数据，用定性结果解释定量发现；定量阶段出现意外结果时特别有用。

### E5

- 位置：§5.6 Mixed-Methods Approaches
- 原文："The Sequential exploratory strategy is characterized by the collection and analysis of qualitative data followed by the collection and analysis of quantitative data. Its purpose is to use quantitative data and results to assist in the interpretation of qualitative findings. This strategy is also useful for testing elements of an emerging theory resulting from a qualitative study."
- 要点：探索性序贯设计：先定性、后定量，用定量结果帮助解读定性发现，也适合检验定性研究中形成的初步理论。

### E6

- 位置：§5.6 Mixed-Methods Approaches
- 原文："This strategy uses different methods concurrently, in an attempt to confirm, cross-validate or corroborate findings. Triangulation is motivated by the fact that often ‘what people say’ could be different than ‘what people do’, and thus collecting data from multiple sources helps improve validity."
- 要点：并行三角验证设计：同时使用不同方法，互相确认、交叉验证发现；动机是"人们说的"常常和"人们做的"不一样，多来源数据有助于提高效度。

### E7

- 位置：§5.6 Mixed-Methods Approaches
- 原文："The challenge in this approach is that it may be difficult for the researcher to compare the results of two analyses or to resolve contradictions that arise in the results. In such cases a further source of evidence, or a follow up study might be necessary."
- 要点：并行设计的难点是比较两种分析的结果、化解结果之间的矛盾；必要时需要再找一个证据来源，或做一项后续研究。

### E8

- 位置：§5.6 Mixed-Methods Approaches
- 原文："Therefore, mixed methods research is more often associated with a pragmatist stance, where the emphasis is on using those methods that most effectively address the research problem."
- 要点：混合方法研究多与实用主义立场相联系，强调用最能有效解决研究问题的方法。

### E9

- 位置：§6.0 Data Collection Techniques
- 原文："Indeed, using multiple techniques allows the researcher to triangulate even within a single method. If different kinds of data support the same conclusions, it strengthens the study."
- 要点：即使只用一种研究方法，也可以用多种数据收集技术做三角验证；不同种类的数据支持同一结论，研究就更有说服力。

### E10

- 位置：§5.2 Case Studies，第 10 页
- 原文："There is much confusion in the SE literature over what constitutes a case study. The term is often used to mean a worked example. As an empirical method, a case study is something very different."
- 要点：软件工程文献中"案例研究"常被用来指一个演示用的例子；作为实证方法的案例研究是完全不同的东西。

### E11

- 位置：§5.2，第 10 页
- 原文："Case studies offer in-depth understanding of how and why certain phenomena occur, and can reveal the mechanisms by which cause-effect relationships occur."
- 要点：案例研究能深入理解现象如何、为何发生，揭示因果关系背后的机制。

### E12

- 位置：§5.2，第 10 页
- 原文："A precondition for conducting a case study is a clear research question concerned with how or why certain phenomena occur. This is used to derive a study proposition that states precisely what the study is intended to show, and to guide the selection of cases and the types of data to collect."
- 要点：做案例研究的前提是一个关于"如何"或"为什么"的清楚研究问题；由它推出研究命题，命题指导选什么案例、收什么数据。

### E13

- 位置：§5.2，第 11 页
- 原文："Case study research uses purposive sampling rather than random sampling. The aim is to select cases that are most relevant to the study proposition. Sometimes a single case is sufficient. This might be because it is a critical case for testing a well-formulated theory: if the theory holds for this case, it is likely to be true for many others."
- 要点：案例研究用目的性抽样而非随机抽样；有时一个案例就够了，例如检验一个成熟理论的关键案例：理论在这里成立，很可能在许多其他情况下也成立。

### E14

- 位置：§5.2，第 11 页
- 原文："However, a multiple case design usually offers greater validity. The different cases are best thought of as replications, rather than members of a sample."
- 要点：多案例设计通常效度更高；几个案例应当看作重复验证，而不是样本中的成员。

### E15

- 位置：§5.2，第 11 页
- 原文："In software engineering, the unit of analysis might be a company, a project, a team, an individual developer, a particular episode or event, a specific work product, etc. Choosing an appropriate unit of analysis is important, to ensure the study focuses on the intended phenomena."
- 要点：分析单元可以是公司、项目、团队、单个开发者、某次事件、某个工作产物等；选对分析单元，研究才能聚焦在想研究的现象上。

### E16

- 位置：§5.2，第 11 页
- 原文："Case study research is most appropriate for cases where the reductionism of controlled experiments is inappropriate. This includes situations where the context is expected to play a role in the phenomena (for example if the stresses of a real project affect developers’ behaviour), or where effects are expected to be wide ranging, or take a long time (e.g. weeks, months, years) to appear."
- 要点：对照实验那种"把问题拆小、隔离变量"的做法不合适时，最适合用案例研究：例如环境本身会影响现象，或者影响范围很广、要很久才显现。

### E17

- 位置：§5.2，第 11 页
- 原文："The major weakness of case studies is that the data collection and analysis is more open to interpretation and researcher bias. For this reason, an explicit framework is needed for selecting cases and collecting data."
- 要点：案例研究的主要弱点是数据收集和分析更容易受解读和研究者偏差影响，所以选案例和收数据需要明确的框架。

### E18

- 位置：§5.2，第 11 页
- 原文："For example, in confirmatory case studies, evidence builds when subsequent case studies also support the theory and/or fail to support rival theories."
- 要点：在验证型案例研究中，证据靠后续案例研究不断积累：后续研究也支持该理论，或不支持竞争理论。

### E19

- 位置：§5.3 Survey research，第 13 页
- 原文："This would sacrifice claims of representativeness (because case studies do not use representative sampling) in return for deeper insights into what happens in a small number of selected cases."
- 要点：选案例研究而不是调查，是用"代表性"换取对少数案例更深入的理解。

### E20

- 位置：§5.4 Ethnographies，第 13 页
- 原文："The goal is to study a community of people to understand how the members of that community make sense of their social interactions"
- 要点：民族志的目标是研究一个群体，理解其成员怎样理解自己的社会互动。

### E21

- 位置：§5.4，第 14 页
- 原文："He identifies a development team that allows him to observe design meetings for several weeks. He supplements his notes on what he observes with a series of individual and group interviews"
- 要点：例子：研究者找到一个允许他旁听设计会议几周的开发团队，用个人和小组访谈补充他的观察笔记。

### E22

- 位置：§5.4，第 14 页
- 原文："For this to work, the researcher must be accepted by the community as one of them, which may require a much longer duration for the study than “just a few weeks”. In software engineering research, becoming a member might only be possible if the researcher has the right technical background."
- 要点：参与式观察要求研究者被群体当成自己人，这可能需要比"几周"长得多的时间；在软件工程研究中，研究者可能需要合适的技术背景才能成为成员。

### E23

- 位置：§5.4，第 14 页
- 原文："Because of that stance, ethnographic researchers don't seek to prove hypotheses and theories, but rather create local theories to improve understanding. This philosophical stance distinguishes ethnography from case studies, surveys and field experiments."
- 要点：民族志研究者不去证明假设，而是建立局部理论来增进理解；作者认为这一立场把民族志和案例研究、调查、现场实验区分开。

### E24

- 位置：§5.4，第 14 页
- 原文："The biggest challenge in ethnographic research is to perform detailed observation, data collection and analysis while avoiding preconceptions."
- 要点：民族志最大的挑战是在详细观察、收集和分析数据时避免先入之见。

### E25

- 位置：Practical Considerations，第 20 页
- 原文："Access to the field situation may require prolonged time in establishing a relationship with the subject organization such that specific project data is made available."
- 要点：进入现场可能需要花很长时间和对方组织建立关系，对方才会提供具体的项目数据。

### E26

- 位置：Practical Considerations，第 20 页
- 原文："In return for access to the organization, the researcher usually has to give up some control. [...] Finally, obtaining permission to publish the results can be a challenge. Delays in publication are likely if the organization has concerns about inclusion of confidential data or insights in the research."
- 要点：换取进入组织的机会，研究者通常要放弃一部分控制；获得发表许可也可能困难，组织担心保密数据时发表可能被推迟。

### E27

- 位置：§7.0 Empirical Validity，第 19 页（作者转述 Creswell 2002 提出的八条策略，认为适合民族志和探索型案例研究）
- 原文："2. Member checking: go back to research participants to ensure that the interpretations of the data make sense from their perspective. [...] 5. Report discrepant information: when reporting findings, report not only those results which confirm the emerging theory, but also those which appear to present different perspectives on the findings."
- 要点：成员核查：回去找参与者，确认对数据的解读从他们的角度看说得通；报告不一致的信息：不只报告支持正在形成的理论的结果，也报告呈现不同视角的结果。
