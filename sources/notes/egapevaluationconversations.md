# EGAP 方法指南：10 Conversations that Implementers and Evaluators Need to Have

- 资料：`egapevaluationconversations`，[原文网页](https://methods.egap.org/guides/implementation/evaluation-conversations_en.html)
- 版本："治理与政治证据网络"（EGAP）方法指南，作者 Jake Bowers、Rebecca Wolfe；2026 年 10 月访问。主要写给实施方，也供评估者参考。位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§1 What is the goal of the evaluation?
- 原文："Without being clear about what you and your organization need to learn, the evaluator may not design the most appropriate evaluation method."
- 要点：实施方不讲清自己需要了解什么，评估者可能设计不出最合适的评估方法。

### E2

- 位置：§4 How can our evaluation enable learning within our organization?
- 原文："Even when the purpose is to learn, not grade, some within an organization may still be fearful of the results, as they believe their careers are tied to achieving strong results."
- 要点：即使评估是为了学习而不是打分，组织里仍有人担心结果影响自己的前途，可能不配合。

### E3

- 位置：§8 How and where shall we make the results of this study public?
- 原文："The timelines that implementers and evaluators, especially ones who come from academia, have for publishing results are likely different."
- 要点：实施方和学术界评估者公开结果的时间表很可能不同。

### E4

- 位置：§9 What if things do not go as expected or hoped?
- 原文："One approach arising from project management in business involves the creation of pre-mortems in which the implementer and evaluator begin by imagining that the evaluation has returned a null or negative result."
- 要点：可以做"事前验尸"：双方先设想评估结果为零或为负，列出可能原因，提前准备。
