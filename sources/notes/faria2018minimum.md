# Faria 等人 2018：Minimum information reporting in bio–nano experimental literature

- 资料：`faria2018minimum`，[原文（PubMed Central 作者手稿）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6150419/)
- 版本：Nature Nanotechnology 13(9):777–785，2018。读取的是 PubMed Central 上的作者接受稿（Europe PMC 资助方手稿，PMCID PMC6150419）。文中提出的标准简称 MIRIBEL。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："A significant barrier to progress in this multidisciplinary area is the variability of published literature with regards to characterisations performed and experimental details reported."
- 要点：生物—纳米交叉研究进展的一大障碍是：已发表文献做了哪些表征、报告了哪些实验细节，差别很大。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："This standard consists of specific components to be reported, divided into three categories: material characterisation, biological characterisation, and details of experimental protocols."
- 要点：MIRIBEL 标准由需要报告的具体条目组成，分三类：材料表征、生物学表征、实验流程细节。

### E3

- 位置：Material Characterisation — Synthesis and composition
- 原文："one way to ensure adequate details of composition are included for a newly published nanomaterial is to provide high-quality, reproducible steps for synthesis." / "Any methods of purification used should also be specified, as the presence of precursor residues in nanoparticle mixtures may alter biological responses."
- 要点：新发表的纳米材料，要给出高质量、可重复的合成步骤；纯化方法也要写，因为前驱体残留可能改变生物学反应。

### E4

- 位置：Material Characterisation — Size dispersity and aggregation
- 原文："Reporting the average size is insufficient to evaluate biological responses: consider the difference between particles with a uniform distribution in size from 10 to 990 nm versus those that range from 495 to 505 nm."
- 要点：只报平均粒径不够：10–990 nm 均匀分布和 495–505 nm 的两批颗粒，平均粒径都是 500 nm，性质却可能很不一样。

### E5

- 位置：Material Characterisation — Size, shape, and dimensions
- 原文："The protocol used to measure size and the “type” of size measured (e.g. geometric or hydrodynamic) should be provided"
- 要点：要写明测粒径的流程，以及测的是哪一"种"尺寸（例如几何尺寸还是水合尺寸）。

### E6

- 位置：Material Characterisation — General guidelines
- 原文："Secondly, detailing how a parameter was assessed (i.e. what technique, equipment, and protocol were used) is essential."
- 要点：每个参数是怎么测的（用什么技术、什么仪器、什么流程）必须写清。

### E7

- 位置：Material Characterisation — General guidelines
- 原文："Fourthly, characterising a property using two (or more) independent methods is of significantly more value"
- 要点：同一性质用两种或更多独立方法表征，价值大得多。

### E8

- 位置：Material Characterisation — General guidelines
- 原文："it is important to detail whether characterisation variance was determined through multiple independent measurements on the same batch of material or whether multiple batches were prepared."
- 要点：要说明表征结果的波动是同一批材料多次测量得到的，还是制备了多个批次得到的。

### E9

- 位置：Material Characterisation — General guidelines
- 原文："To the degree possible, material characterisation should be performed in a medium relevant to the fluid used in the subsequent bio–nano experiment."
- 要点：材料表征尽量在与后续实验所用液体相近的介质中做。

### E10

- 位置：Material Characterisation — General guidelines
- 原文："Thirdly, nearly all material characterisation parameters occur across a distribution, and thus including a distribution of the measured property is more informative than providing a single average value."
- 要点：几乎所有表征参数都是一个分布，给出分布比只给一个平均值信息量大。

### E11

- 位置：Experimental Protocol Details — Details of data analysis
- 原文："the number of independent experiments (n), and details of how uncertainty is expressed (e.g. standard deviation, standard error, or confidence intervals) should be provided."
- 要点：要报告独立实验次数 n，以及不确定性用什么表示（标准差、标准误还是置信区间）。

### E12

- 位置：Experimental Protocol Details — General guidelines
- 原文："Whenever possible, we recommend that raw data are provided for published bio–nano experimental reports, either as part of the article submission or in a trusted data repository"
- 要点：尽可能提供原始数据，可以随论文提交，也可以放在可信的数据仓库。

### E13

- 位置：Summary and Outlook
- 原文："proposing a standard, or agreeing that standardisation is important, is insufficient to improve the field. To be useful, any standard must be widely adopted by the community"
- 要点：提出标准、认同标准化重要，都不足以改善这个领域；标准要被广泛采用才有用。

### E14

- 位置：引言
- 原文："This checklist is designed to be used while preparing a manuscript to ensure that each component is considered as appropriate."
- 要点：配套的检查清单是在准备稿件时用的，确保每个条目都考虑到。

### E15

- 位置：Defining the Scope of a Minimum Information Standard
- 原文："One option is to consider a material as nanoengineered when it has at least one functional dimension on the nanoscale (i.e. from 1 to 100 nm)."
- 要点：一种界定办法是：至少有一个功能尺寸在纳米尺度（1–100 nm）的材料，算作纳米工程材料。

### E16

- 位置：Biological Characterisation — ARRIVE guidelines (in vivo)
- 原文："For in vivo experiments, the Animals in Research: Reporting In Vivo Experiments (ARRIVE) guidelines should be followed."
- 要点：动物实验要遵循动物研究报告指南（ARRIVE）。
