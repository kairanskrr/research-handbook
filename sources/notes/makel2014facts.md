# Makel & Plucker 2014：Facts Are More Important Than Novelty: Replication in the Education Sciences

- 资料：`makel2014facts`，[AERA 文章页](https://www.aera.net/Newsroom/Recent-AERA-Research/Facts-Are-More-Important-Than-Novelty-Replication-in-the-Education-Sciences)，[AERA 新闻稿](https://www.aera.net/tabid/15612/mid/31214/default.aspx)
- 版本：Educational Researcher 43(6)，2014 年 8 月 14 日在线发表。出版社的免费全文链接需要通过浏览器验证，脚本读不到全文；这里只引用 AERA 官网文章页上的摘要和 AERA 2014 年 8 月 14 日的新闻稿（两者都是 AERA 公开发布的文字）。位置写"摘要"或"新闻稿"。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："The present study analyzed the complete publication history of the current top 100 education journals ranked by 5-year impact factor and found that only 0.13% of education articles were replications."
- 要点：作者分析了按 5 年影响因子排名前 100 的教育期刊的全部发表历史，只有 0.13% 的文章是重复研究。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Despite increased attention to methodological rigor in education research, the field has focused heavily on experimental design and not on the merit of replicating important results."
- 要点：教育研究越来越重视方法严谨，但重心放在实验设计上，没有重视重复验证重要结果。

### E3

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："However, replications were significantly less likely to be successful when there was no overlap in authorship between the original and replicating articles."
- 要点：原研究和重复研究的作者没有重叠时，重复成功的可能性明显更低。

### E4

- 位置：新闻稿
- 原文："Nearly 68 percent (67.4 percent) of the replications successfully replicated the findings of the original study, 19.5 percent had mixed results (supporting some, but not all, findings), and 13.1 percent failed to replicate any of the original findings."
- 要点：这些重复研究中，67.4% 成功重复了原结果，19.5% 结果不一，13.1% 完全没有重复出来。

### E5

- 位置：新闻稿
- 原文："Replication studies that were conducted by completely new research teams were found to be successful 54 percent of the time. When replications were conducted by the original authors in the same publication as the original findings, 88.7 percent were successful."
- 要点：完全由新团队做的重复研究，成功率 54%；原作者在同一篇论文里做的重复，成功率 88.7%。

### E6

- 位置：新闻稿
- 原文："Overall, about half (48.2%) of all published replications are done by the research team that completed the original study."
- 要点：已发表的重复研究中，约一半（48.2%）是原研究团队自己做的。

### E7

- 位置：新闻稿
- 原文："These include revising journal editorial policies to explicitly encourage submission of replication studies, creating a new article type in journals for replications, adopting universal standards for conducting replications, and introducing replication training into graduate and undergraduate student projects."
- 要点：作者建议：期刊明确鼓励投稿重复研究、为重复研究设专门的文章类型、采用统一的重复研究标准、在研究生和本科生项目中加入重复研究训练。

### E8

- 位置：新闻稿
- 原文："The authors identified eight reasons, or biases, that help explain the lack of replication in the education research field."
- 要点：作者列出了八种导致教育研究缺少重复研究的原因或偏向。

### E9

- 位置：新闻稿
- 原文："These include submission bias, funding bias, editor/reviewer bias, journal publication policy bias, hiring bias, promotion bias, journals-analyzed bias, and “novelty equals creativity” bias."
- 要点：八种偏向包括投稿、资助、编辑/审稿人、期刊发表政策、招聘、晋升等方面的偏向，以及"新颖等于创造力"的偏向。

### E10

- 位置：新闻稿（AERA 编者注）
- 原文："The decision by AERA in 2013 to publish AERA Open as an open-access research journal provides one vehicle to encourage the publication of peer-reviewed replication studies."
- 要点：AERA 2013 年决定创办开放获取期刊 AERA Open，这是鼓励发表经同行评审的重复研究的途径之一。
