# APA Style：Journal Article Reporting Standards (JARS)

- 资料：`apajars`，[原文](https://apastyle.apa.org/jars)
- 版本：网页，原站拦截脚本抓取，使用 Internet Archive 2025 年快照。

### E1

- 位置：What are APA Style JARS?
- 原文："APA Style Journal Article Reporting Standards (APA Style jars) are a set of standards designed for journal authors, reviewers, and editors to enhance scientific rigor in peer-reviewed journal articles."
- 要点：JARS 是给期刊作者、审稿人和编辑用的报告标准，目的是提高期刊论文的科学严谨性。

### E2

- 位置：Guidelines
- 原文："Use JARS–Quant when you collect your study data in numerical form or report them through statistical analyses." / "Use JARS–Qual when you collect your study data in the form of natural language and expression." / "Use JARS–Mixed when your study combines both quantitative and qualitative methods."
- 要点：三套标准分工：数值数据和统计分析用 JARS–Quant；文字、语言形式的数据用 JARS–Qual；两者结合用 JARS–Mixed。

### E3

- 位置：What are APA Style JARS?
- 原文："jars–rec should be applied to all research, whether it is quantitative, qualitative, or mixed methods."
- 要点：另有一套关于如何讨论种族、民族和文化的标准（JARS–REC），适用于所有研究。
