# IEEE EMBS：Prepare and Submit Manuscript – IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering

- 资料：`embstbmeprepare`，[原文（Internet Archive 快照）](https://web.archive.org/web/20250115120531/https://www.embs.org/tbme/for-authors/prepare-and-submit-manuscript/)
- 版本：IEEE 医学与生物学工程学会（Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society，EMBS）主办的期刊《IEEE 生物医学工程汇刊》（IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering，TBME）投稿说明页。官网对脚本访问返回 403，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2025-01-15 的快照；页面内容之后可能有更新。位置写页面中的小节。

### E1

- 位置：页面底部期刊简介
- 原文："Papers range from engineering development in methods and techniques with biomedical applications to experimental and clinical investigations with engineering contributions."
- 要点：TBME 收的论文，从有生物医学应用的方法和技术开发，到有工程贡献的实验研究和临床研究都有。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Structured abstract (see the Template Instructions) must be less than 250 words and address the following 5 points: objective, methods, results, conclusion, and significance." / "If any of these is not clearly addressed, the manuscript may be returned without review."
- 要点：TBME 要求不超过 250 词的结构化摘要，写清目的、方法、结果、结论和意义五点；缺了任何一点，稿件可能不经审稿就被退回。

### E3

- 位置：Cover Letter
- 原文："It must disclose if there are any prior conference proceedings papers related to this work, and include a statement on why you believe the prior conference papers represent different works as compared with the submission to TBME."
- 要点：投稿信必须披露与本文相关的已发表会议论文，并说明为什么认为它们和投给 TBME 的稿件是不同的工作。

### E4

- 位置：Cover Letter 之后的说明
- 原文："Please note that we accept full papers, which have appeared in preliminary form as conference abstracts for consideration of publication." / "Note however that prior publication in an extensive conference paper may disqualify a manuscript for consideration for publication at TBME."
- 要点：只以会议摘要形式出现过的工作，可以写成全文投 TBME；但如果之前已经发表过内容充分的会议长文，可能就失去投稿资格。

### E5

- 位置：Letters
- 原文："Given that the current TBME submission to decision is about 30 days, submission of regular papers is encouraged."
- 要点：按页面当时的说法，TBME 从投稿到给出决定约 30 天。

### E6

- 位置：期刊信息（Peer Review）
- 原文："Each published article was reviewed by a minimum of two independent reviewers using a single-anonymous peer review process"
- 要点：TBME 每篇发表的文章至少经两位独立审稿人审稿，采用单盲（审稿人知道作者是谁，作者不知道审稿人）。

### E7

- 位置：Author Consent Form
- 原文："The IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society will apply sanctions against authors who are discovered to have made duplicate submissions to other journals."
- 要点：发现一稿多投，EMBS 会处罚作者（原文随后列出：立即拒稿、退回在审稿件、一年内禁止向该学会期刊投稿）。

### E8

- 位置：Disclosure of Financial Support and Author- and Project-Related Conflicts of Interest
- 原文："These requirements include financial interests of the author in any company that may be licensing, patenting, or using results from the published work at the time of or prior to the date of manuscript acceptance."
- 要点：要披露的利益关系包括：作者在可能授权、申请专利或使用这项成果的公司里的经济利益。

### E9

- 位置：Human and Animal Subjects 部分
- 原文："If the published work utilizes human subjects, authors must indicate whether the procedures followed were in accordance with the ethical standards of the responsible committee on human experimentation (institutional and national) and with the Helsinki Declaration of 1975, as revised in 2000."
- 要点：涉及人的研究，要说明程序是否符合机构和国家伦理委员会的标准以及《赫尔辛基宣言》（页面写的是 1975 年版、2000 年修订）。

### E10

- 位置：Human and Animal Subjects 部分
- 原文："If the published work utilizes animal subjects (including animal sources for in vitro research), authors must indicate whether the institutional and national guides for the care and use of laboratory animals were followed."
- 要点：涉及动物的研究（包括为体外研究取材的动物），要说明是否遵守了机构和国家的实验动物管理与使用指南。
