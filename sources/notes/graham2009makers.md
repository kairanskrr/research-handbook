# Graham 2009：Maker's Schedule, Manager's Schedule

- 资料：`graham2009makers`，[原文](http://www.paulgraham.com/makersschedule.html)
- 版本：Paul Graham 个人网站文章，2009 年 7 月，2026-10-07 读取。文章没有小标题，位置按段落话题描述。

### E1

- 位置：正文，介绍两种日程的段落
- 原文："They generally prefer to use time in units of half a day at least. You can't write or program well in units of an hour. That's barely enough time to get started."
- 要点：做东西的人（如程序员、写作者）通常喜欢以至少半天为单位安排时间；以一小时为单位写不好、编不好程序，一小时只够起个头。

### E2

- 位置：正文，"meetings are a disaster" 一段
- 原文："A single meeting can blow a whole afternoon, by breaking it into two pieces each too small to do anything hard in."
- 要点：一个会议就能毁掉整个下午，因为它把下午切成两段，每段都太短，做不了难的事。

### E3

- 位置：正文，"one meeting can sometimes affect a whole day" 一段
- 原文："If I know the afternoon is going to be broken up, I'm slightly less likely to start something ambitious in the morning."
- 要点：知道下午会被打断，上午就不太愿意开始有挑战的事。

### E4

- 位置：正文，"office hours" 一段
- 原文："These chunks of time are at the end of my working day, and I wrote a signup program that ensures all the appointments within a given set of office hours are clustered at the end. Because they come at the end of my day these meetings are never an interruption."
- 要点：作者把见人的时段集中放在工作日的末尾，这样这些会议就不会打断工作。

### E5

- 位置：结尾
- 原文："We know we have to have some number of meetings. All we ask from those on the manager's schedule is that they understand the cost."
- 要点：会议总是要开一些的；只希望安排会议的人明白它的代价。
