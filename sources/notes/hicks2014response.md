# Hicks 2014：Advice on writing an author response

- 资料：`hicks2014response`，[原文](http://www.pl-enthusiast.net/2014/09/17/advice-writing-author-response/)
- 版本：博客文章，The PL Enthusiast，2014-09-17。

### E1

- 位置：What is author response?
- 原文："Author response was originally designed to redress this wrong by allowing authors to point out factual inaccuracies in reviews."
- 要点：作者回复（rebuttal）最初是为了让作者指出审稿意见中的事实错误。

### E2

- 位置：The goal of your response
- 原文："Instead, you want to convince at least one reviewer that your paper is sufficiently above the bar that he/she will argue to accept your paper."
- 要点：回复的目标不是说服每个审稿人，而是让至少一位审稿人愿意为你的论文争取录用。

### E3

- 位置：General themes for writing the response
- 原文："Be polite and appreciative. Even if the reviewers didn’t like your paper as much as you think they should, they volunteered to review it and gave you feedback."
- 要点：要礼貌、表示感谢：审稿人是志愿付出时间给你反馈的。

### E4

- 位置：General themes for writing the response
- 原文："Do not address every concern. The goal of finding a champion implies you shouldn’t feel the need to address every concern."
- 要点：不必回应每一条意见；篇幅有限，面面俱到会冲淡重点。可以声明已考虑全部意见、会在修改中处理。

### E5

- 位置：General themes for writing the response
- 原文："The most important concerns, shared by most of the reviewers, should be addressed right away, and decisively."
- 要点：多数审稿人共同的主要问题，要放在最前面、明确地回答。

### E6

- 位置：General themes for writing the response
- 原文："Reference the key concerns in a manner close to how the reviewers phrased them, so they are easy to find."
- 要点：引用审稿人原话的方式点出要回应的问题，让对方容易找到。

### E7

- 位置：General themes for writing the response
- 原文："Remember that a reviewer probably read your paper and wrote the review a month before reading your response to the review."
- 要点：审稿人读你的回复时，可能已是写完审稿意见一个月以后，细节记不清了。

### E8

- 位置：General themes for writing the response
- 原文："Reference your own paper for answers to reviewer concerns."
- 要点：如果答案论文里已经有，指出在论文哪里。

### E9

- 位置：What is author response?
- 原文："Some forms of author response try to limit it to “factual inaccuracies.” [...] As such, author response processes I’m aware of now are more open, oftentimes having suggested rather than enforced limits, with fewer stated restrictions on what can be said."
- 要点：有的作者回复只允许指出事实错误；作者所知的回复流程如今更开放，字数常是建议而非强制，限制也更少。

### E10

- 位置：The goal of your response
- 原文："Indeed, if most of the reviewers are in favor of rejection, you have little hope."
- 要点：如果大多数审稿人倾向拒稿，回复能起的作用很小。
