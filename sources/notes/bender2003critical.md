# Bender 2003：Critical Reading

- 资料：`bender2003critical`，[原文](https://faculty.washington.edu/ebender/critical_reading.pdf)
- 版本：讲义，2003-10-17；本地 PDF `papers/pdf/bender2003critical.pdf`（PDF 字体无法提取为文本，原文文本按页面图像逐字转录）

### E1

- 位置：第 1 页，第 1 段
- 原文："Critical reading is the process of actively engaging with a text as you read it, going beyond simply following the author's arguments or explanation. It involves examining the underlying assumptions not made explicit in the text, both in the initial framing of the issue and in the reasoning from evidence to conclusions."
- 要点：批判性阅读是主动地与文本交锋，不只跟着作者的论证走，还要检查没有明说的假设，包括问题的提法和从证据到结论的推理。

### E2

- 位置：第 1 页，第 2 段
- 原文："Critical reading is not criticizing. You can read a text critically and come out the other end liking it or disliking it. In either case, you'll have a better idea of why."
- 要点：批判性阅读不等于挑刺；读完可以喜欢也可以不喜欢，但你会更清楚为什么。

### E3

- 位置：第 1 页，"For articles reporting on research"
- 原文："1. What is the research question that the article asks? 2. What assumptions/presuppositions are involved in asking/framing the question the way the authors do? 3. How were the data gathered? 4. What potential for bias was there in the gathering of the data?"
- 要点：读研究论文要问：研究问题是什么？这样提问隐含了什么假设？数据怎么收集的？收集过程中可能有什么偏差？

### E4

- 位置：第 1 页，"For articles reporting on research"
- 原文："6. What are the conclusions presented in the article? 7. What assumptions/presuppositions are involved in drawing those conclusions from that evidence?"
- 要点：还要问：结论是什么？从这些证据推出这些结论，依赖了哪些假设？

### E5

- 位置：第 1 页，第 3 段
- 原文："In answering these questions as you read, keep in mind who the author and intended audience are. [...] [NB: These questions were developed in the context of a sociolinguistics course, but I believe that they have broader applicability.]"
- 要点：回答这些问题时要记住作者是谁、写给谁看；这些问题源自社会语言学课程，但作者认为适用面更广。
