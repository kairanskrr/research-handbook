# Bligaard 等人 2016：Toward Benchmarking in Catalysis Science: Best Practices, Challenges, and Opportunities

- 资料：`bligaard2016benchmarking`，[原文（eScholarship 全文）](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/0q38h3cr)，DOI：10.1021/acscatal.6b00183
- 版本：ACS Catalysis 2016 年第 6 卷第 2590–2602 页，观点文章（Perspective）。出版方以 ACS AuthorChoice 许可开放获取；本摘录读取的是加州大学 eScholarship 托管的出版版 PDF（2026-10-06 下载，pdftotext 提取）。作者是美国能源部催化科学项目资助的十位研究者，文章说明建议只代表署名作者。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："In catalysis science, important catalyst performance metrics include activity, selectivity, and the deactivation profile, which enable comparisons between new and standard catalysts."
- 要点：在催化研究里，重要的性能指标是活性、选择性和失活情况，用来把新催化剂和标准催化剂作比较。

### E2

- 位置：§1.5，第 2593 页
- 原文："Our suggestions and conclusions are the responsibility of the signing authors and are derived from free, open, and nonconsensus-seeking discussions among a subset of the investigators supported by the Catalysis Science program, Office of Science, Basic Energy Sciences, of the U.S. Department of Energy."
- 要点：文中建议只代表署名作者，来自美国能源部催化科学项目部分受资助研究者之间不追求共识的讨论。

### E3

- 位置：§1.4，第 2591 页
- 原文："often measured in banks of nearly identical bench-scale reactors and in pilot-scale reactors."
- 要点：在工业界，催化剂在标准条件下的性能常用成排几乎相同的实验台规模反应器和中试规模反应器来测。

### E4

- 位置：§1.4，第 2592 页
- 原文："However, the criteria used, the protocols themselves, and the performance measurement results were generally not widely shared."
- 要点：但工业界用的标准、测试规程和测量结果，一般不对外广泛公开。

### E5

- 位置：§2.2.1 Best Practices（Accurate Reaction Kinetics），第 2595 页
- 原文："It is essential that measurements of turnover frequencies (TOFs, or site-normalized catalytic reaction rates), turnover numbers (TONs, or numbers of turns of a catalytic cycle, equivalent to site-normalized catalytic productivities), and selectivities be accurate, reproducible, uncorrupted by mass or heat transport limitations, and cover wide ranges of conversions."
- 要点：转换频率（按活性位点归一化的反应速率）、转换数和选择性的测量，必须准确、可重复、不受传质传热限制干扰，并覆盖较宽的转化率范围。

### E6

- 位置：§2.2.1，第 2595 页
- 原文："reported TOF values should be based on rate measurements rather than on conversion at a single time point, and the values should be checked by systematically varying the concentrations of all species present in the reactor."
- 要点：报告的转换频率应基于速率测量，而不是某一个时间点的转化率；还要系统改变反应器里各物种的浓度来检验。

### E7

- 位置：§2.2 Heterogeneous Catalysis，第 2595 页
- 原文："(1) optimum reaction conditions for one catalyst may be markedly suboptimal for another; and (2) the nature and number of the active sites may evolve during the test, complicating normalization of parameters such as rates and yields."
- 要点：比较多相催化剂有两个常见难点：对一种催化剂最优的条件对另一种可能很差；活性位点的性质和数量在测试中会变化，使速率和产率难以归一化。

### E8

- 位置：§2.2.1（Standard Materials），第 2595 页
- 原文："Cooperation between research groups in a blind, round-robin series of measurements of reaction rates, site densities, and TOF values would be valuable to characterize simple benchmark catalytic materials and reactions, to identify subtleties in such measurements, and to determine realistic error bars."
- 要点：多个课题组合作做盲法轮流测量（同一批样品轮流在各实验室测），有助于找出测量中的细微问题，并确定现实的误差范围。

### E9

- 位置：§2.2.1（Catalyst Deactivation），第 2595–2596 页
- 原文："Accelerated catalyst aging tests can be expeditious, but these tests are risky unless the causes of deactivation are known and extrapolations justified on the basis of experiments."
- 要点：加速老化试验省时间，但除非知道失活原因、并有实验支持外推，否则有风险。

### E10

- 位置：§2.3 Catalysis by Molecular and Supported Molecular Species，第 2596 页
- 原文："For example, a laboratory that tries to repeat a catalytic reaction described in the literature may find much less than the reported activity or none at all. Discrepancies may be caused by differences in reagents and protocols that are incompletely documented."
- 要点：重复文献里的催化反应，可能只得到远低于报告值的活性，甚至完全没有活性；原因可能是没有写全的试剂和操作差别。

### E11

- 位置：§2.3.1（Kinetic Measurements），第 2597 页
- 原文："Selectivity must be reported with the corresponding conversion"
- 要点：报告选择性时必须同时给出对应的转化率。

### E12

- 位置：§2.3.1（Kinetic Measurements），第 2597 页
- 原文："To avoid reporting nonintrinsic kinetics, the absence of mass/heat transfer and mixing effects must be verified."
- 要点：为了不把非本征动力学当成本征动力学报告，必须验证不存在传质、传热和混合的影响。

### E13

- 位置：§2.3.1（Kinetic Measurements），第 2597 页
- 原文："Mass balance should be verified"
- 要点：应当核对物料衡算。

### E14

- 位置：§2.3.1（Specification of Methodology），第 2597 页
- 原文："When reporting quantities such as the TON or TOF, they should be accompanied by a clear statement about how values were obtained from the raw data. This statement must include a description of how the number of active sites was counted or estimated, using some transparent and reproducible method."
- 要点：报告转换数、转换频率时，要写清它们是怎样从原始数据算出来的，包括活性位点数量是用什么透明、可重复的方法数出来或估计出来的。

### E15

- 位置：§2.3.1（Selectivity），第 2597 页
- 原文："When the selectivities of several catalysts are compared, values should be measured at the same conversion."
- 要点：比较几种催化剂的选择性时，应在相同转化率下测量。

### E16

- 位置：§2.3.1（Catalyst Deactivation），第 2597 页
- 原文："In particular, recycling studies in which product yield is measured solely at high conversion do not demonstrate catalyst stability."
- 要点：只在高转化率下测产率的循环使用实验，不能证明催化剂稳定。

### E17

- 位置：§2.3.1（Comparisons and Controls），第 2597 页
- 原文："When reporting data for a new catalyst, it is important to compare the performance with wellestablished catalysts and/or state-of-the-art catalysts measured under the same experimental conditions."
- 要点：报告新催化剂时，要在相同实验条件下和公认的或最先进的催化剂比较。（原文"wellestablished"是 PDF 提取时丢了连字符。）

### E18

- 位置：§2.3.1（Comparisons and Controls），第 2597 页
- 原文："It is essential to perform appropriate control experiments without catalyst present to establish the significance of uncatalyzed reactions"
- 要点：必须做不加催化剂的对照实验，弄清没有催化剂时反应进行到什么程度。

### E19

- 位置：§2.1.1 Best Practices（Sharing Existing Results），第 2593 页
- 原文："Making the computational input and output files available online (in addition to the output results) would significantly enhance the reader's ability to reproduce the published results."
- 要点：把计算的输入和输出文件公开放到网上，会大大提高读者复现结果的能力。

### E20

- 位置：§2.4.1 Best Practices（Standardized Reporting），第 2598 页
- 原文："Importantly, currents (or current densities) should be reported as a function of overpotential and normalized to several different catalyst parameters so that all researchers can better understand the catalyst performance."
- 要点：在电催化里，电流（或电流密度）应作为过电位的函数报告，并按几种不同的催化剂参数分别归一化。
