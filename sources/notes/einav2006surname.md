# Einav & Yariv 2006：What's in a Surname? The Effects of Surname Initials on Academic Success

- 资料：`einav2006surname`，[作者主页 PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/~leinav/pubs/JEP2006.pdf)
- 版本：Journal of Economic Perspectives 20(1)，第 175–188 页（期刊排版版，作者主页公开）。

### E1

- 位置：第 175 页（引言）
- 原文："We suspect the "alphabetical discrimination" reported in this paper is linked to the norm in the economics profession prescribing alphabetical ordering of credits on coauthored publications."
- 要点：经济学的惯例是合著论文按作者姓氏字母顺序署名。

### E2

- 位置：第 175 页（引言）
- 原文："Faculty with earlier surname initials are significantly more likely to receive tenure at top ten economics departments, are significantly more likely to become fellows of the Econometric Society"
- 要点：在前十名经济系，姓氏首字母靠前的教师更可能获得终身教职、当选计量经济学会会士。

### E3

- 位置：第 182 页脚注 8
- 原文："Psychology is one of the closest disciplines to economics, but it follows the convention of listing coauthors by contribution, rather than according to their alphabetical placement." / 第 175 页："We find no relationship between alphabetical placement and tenure status in psychology."
- 要点：心理学按贡献排序署名，而不是按字母顺序；作者用它作对照，在心理学中没有发现姓氏位置与终身教职之间的关系。

### E4

- 位置：第 184 页，Do Economists Respond to Alphabetical Bias? 一节
- 原文："About half of the papers over this time are multiauthored, and in 88 percent of the multiauthored papers, the authors are ordered alphabetically."
- 要点：1980–2002 年五大期刊上约一半论文是合著，其中 88% 按字母顺序署名。

### E5

- 位置：第 176 页（引言）
- 原文："authors with surname initials that are placed later in the alphabet are significantly less likely to participate in four- and five-author projects. Furthermore, such authors are also more likely to deviate from the accepted norm, and to write papers in which credits do not follow the alphabetical ordering."
- 要点：姓氏靠后的作者较少参加四五人的合作，也更常不按字母顺序署名。
