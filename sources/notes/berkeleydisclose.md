# UC Berkeley IPIRA：When to Disclose an Invention to OTL

- 资料：`berkeleydisclose`，[原文](https://ipira.berkeley.edu/researchers/intellectual-property-protection/when-disclose-invention-otl)
- 版本：加州大学伯克利分校知识产权与产业研究联盟（IPIRA）网页，2026-10-06 读取。内容按美国法律和该校政策写成。

### E1

- 位置：When to disclose
- 原文："Before collaboration with external partners: If you are planning to collaborate with companies or other institutions, make sure to file a disclosure first to secure the university’s rights over the invention."
- 要点：和企业或其他机构合作之前，先向学校技术许可办公室提交发明披露。

### E2

- 位置：When to disclose
- 原文："Ideally, submit a disclosure to OTL at least 1-2 months before a public enabling disclosure."
- 要点：最好在公开之前至少 1～2 个月向技术许可办公室提交发明披露。

### E3

- 位置：FAQ，Is there a grace period for filing patent applications after a public disclosure?
- 原文："Online resources often mention a “one-year grace period” for filing U.S. patent applications. However, these resources don’t consider international patent law, first-to-file litigation risk, and other factors."
- 要点：网上常说的美国"一年宽限期"没有考虑国际专利法等因素，不能依赖。

### E4

- 位置：FAQ，What are the consequences of publishing ... before disclosing it to OTL?
- 原文："A public enabling disclosure of an invention triggers a 1-year deadline for filing a patent application on that invention in the US and disqualifies filing a patent application on that invention in most non-US nations."
- 要点：公开后，在美国只剩一年申请期限，在大多数其他国家则直接失去申请资格。
