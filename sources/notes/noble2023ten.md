# Noble 2023：Ten simple rules for defining a computational biology project

- 资料：`noble2023ten`，[原文](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010786)
- 版本：PLoS Computational Biology 19(1): e1010786, 2023，开放获取，2026-10-05 读取。位置写规则编号。

### E1

- 位置：Rule 1: Write it down
- 原文："The act of writing your idea down will inevitably clarify it for you. I typically think of this writeup as a first draft of the introduction of the paper you eventually aim to write."
- 要点：把想法写下来必然会让它更清楚；这份书面说明可以看作最终论文引言的初稿。

### E2

- 位置：Rule 1: Write it down
- 原文："Unfortunately, if you get excited by an idea and immediately dive into implementing and validating it, then you can end up pursuing a half-baked idea that, on second thought, really doesn’t make sense."
- 要点：一有想法就动手实现，可能会追着一个没想清楚、其实站不住的想法跑。

### E3

- 位置：Rule 2: Define the problem you want to solve
- 原文："It is important up front to clearly delineate the scope and precise nature of the problem your work will address."
- 要点：一开始就要划清问题的范围和确切性质。

### E4

- 位置：Rule 3: Define the potential impact
- 原文："You should think carefully about who would benefit from a solution to this problem and what those benefits will be."
- 要点：想清楚谁会从解决这个问题中受益、受益什么。

### E5

- 位置：Rule 4: Summarize the related work
- 原文："A terrible trap to fall into is carrying out an entire research project only to find that the same work was already published before you began. Avoid this trap by doing your literature review up front."
- 要点：最糟的陷阱是做完整个项目才发现别人早已发表；文献调研要放在前面做。

### E6

- 位置：Rule 5: State your central hypothesis
- 原文："Boiling down your idea to a single sentence can be a powerful way to achieve focus and ensure that your project does not wander."
- 要点：把想法浓缩成一句话，能让项目聚焦、不跑偏。

### E7

- 位置：Rule 5: State your central hypothesis
- 原文："Thus, it will be worthwhile to periodically revisit your stated hypothesis and ask yourself whether it is still the driving idea behind your research."
- 要点：假设在项目中可能改变，要定期回头检查它是否仍是驱动研究的核心想法。

### E8

- 位置：Rule 7: Identify available data
- 原文："In particular, it’s important to be sure that you not only have the input data, but also the expected outputs; i.e., you need to have some way to demonstrate that your method works as expected."
- 要点：要事先确认有办法证明方法确实有效，也就是事先想好拿什么当证据。

### E9

- 位置：Rule 8: Choose appropriate validation measures
- 原文："Ask yourself what the end user cares about and what will make the work most impactful"
- 要点：选验证指标时，先问最终使用者关心什么、什么能让工作最有影响。

### E10

- 位置：Rule 10: Share your idea with colleagues
- 原文："Once you have written out your idea, share it with trusted colleagues. The goal here is to elicit feedback about whether the idea makes sense and addresses an important, unsolved problem."
- 要点：写好后给信得过的同事看，听取这个想法是否合理、是否针对一个重要且未解决的问题的反馈。

### E11

- 位置：Rule 10 之后的结语
- 原文："It is important to keep in mind that the order in which these rules are applied is not set in stone."
- 要点：这些步骤的顺序不是固定的。

### E12

- 位置：Rule 9: Set up version control
- 原文："Use a modern version control system, such as git, and use it right away. [...] You do not want to get several weeks into your project and then realize that you have no record of the initial versions of your code."
- 要点：项目一开始就用版本控制；不要等做了几周才发现早期版本没有任何记录。
