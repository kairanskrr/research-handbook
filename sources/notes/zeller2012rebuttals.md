# Zeller 2012：Patterns for writing good rebuttals

- 资料：`zeller2012rebuttals`，[原文](https://andreas-zeller.info/2012/10/01/patterns-for-writing-good-rebuttals.html)
- 版本：博客文章，2012。作者基于担任软件工程会议程序委员会主席的经验。

### E1

- 位置：Identify the champion.
- 原文："If there’s no potential champion, chances of getting your paper accepted are slim."
- 要点：如果没有潜在的支持者（champion），论文被录用的机会很小。

### E2

- 位置：Arm the champion.
- 原文："Refute every single issue raised by the detractors to give your champion extra arguments for acceptance."
- 要点：反驳反对者提出的每一条问题，给支持者在讨论时提供论据。

### E3

- 位置：Write for the committee.
- 原文："If there’s one thing you want the committee to know, say so in the very beginning (because that’s all most reviewers will read)."
- 要点：最想让委员会知道的一件事，放在最开头，因为多数人只会读开头。

### E4

- 位置：Convince.
- 原文："You don’t need to provide the full change; just sketch the change you will make."
- 要点：不必给出完整修改，但要具体勾勒你会怎么改，而不是只说“谢谢，我们会加上”。

### E5

- 位置：Choose comments wisely.
- 原文："You do not need to convince the reviewer that you’re able to fix typos, straight-forward presentation issues, language issues, or anything else that can be fixed by simple proofreading."
- 要点：错别字、简单的表述和语言问题不用在回复里争取，重点放在重大问题上。

### E6

- 位置：Write for the PC chair.
- 原文："If all reviewers misunderstood your paper, the problem is not the reviewers."
- 要点：如果所有审稿人都误解了论文，问题不在审稿人。

### E7

- 位置：Thank the reviewers.
- 原文："Reviewing is hard work and even the least significant review contains grains of truth which will be helpful for your future work."
- 要点：审稿是辛苦活；再差的审稿意见里也有对你有用的东西。回复不是发泄的地方。

### E8

- 位置：Don’t expect too much.
- 原文："Your rebuttal will not save your paper. Your rebuttal will play a role only if your paper is on the edge, or if reviewers made very obvious mistakes."
- 要点：回复救不了论文；只有论文处在录用边缘、或审稿人犯了明显错误时才起作用。

### E9

- 位置：Don’t expect too much.
- 原文："But regardless of the outcome, your rebuttal will help to improve your paper and eventually get it accepted - if not now, there’s always another deadline on the horizon."
- 要点：无论结果如何，写回复会帮你改进论文；这次不中，总还有下一个截稿日。

### E10

- 位置：Organize your rebuttal.
- 原文："Make it easy for reviewers to find their questions (and your arguments). Refer to reviewers by R1, R2, …; questions by R1Q1, R1Q2, …"
- 要点：给审稿人和问题编号，方便各自找到自己的问题和你的回答。

### E11

- 位置：Identify the undecided.
- 原文："These are reviewers who may be swayed by your arguments. [...] Focus your arguments on these reviewers."
- 要点：还没拿定主意、可能被说动的审稿人，是回复要重点争取的对象。

### E12

- 位置：Identify the detractors.
- 原文："A strong detractor can only be countered by a strong champion. Rather than trying to dissuade a strong detractor, your aim should be on arming the champion"
- 要点：强烈反对的审稿人只能靠强有力的支持者来抗衡；与其劝反对者改主意，不如给支持者提供论据。

### E13

- 位置：Write for the PC chair.
- 原文："If your reviewers have done a bad job, say so in the beginning, and the PC chair may assign you another reviewer. Stick to facts [...] that the PC chair can check in less than a minute."
- 要点：审稿人明显没做好，可以在开头指出，但只摆主席一分钟内能核实的事实。

### E14

- 位置：Choose comments wisely.
- 原文："Focus on major concerns - the ones where the reviewer assumes you may not be able to improve the paper."
- 要点：重点回应主要问题，也就是审稿人认为你可能没法改好的那些。
