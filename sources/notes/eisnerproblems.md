# Eisner：How to Find Research Problems

- 资料：`eisnerproblems`，[原文](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~jason/advice/how-to-find-research-problems.html)
- 版本：作者主页，1997 年写给宾夕法尼亚大学计算机系新博士生，页面最后修改 2015-08-30。

### E1

- 位置：正文，"small bones"
- 原文："About the smallest bone that you can find in Computer Science is a reproduction or implementation of someone else's work. While this doesn't get you points for originality, it may be useful, both to your education and to the field."
- 要点：最小的贡献是复现或实现别人的工作：不算原创，但对自己的学习和领域都可能有用。

### E2

- 位置：正文，"small bones"
- 原文："When reading papers, stay on the lookout for such bones. In particular, notice when the author may be making harmful simplifications or arbitrary choices in his/her approach. These are opportunities for you to try something different."
- 要点：读论文时留意作者做了哪些有害的简化或随意的选择，那是你可以换种做法的机会。

### E3

- 位置：正文，"big bones"
- 原文："What problems (recognized or unrecognized) are obstructing progress in my field? Can I solve them? If not, can I at least formalize them? Can I prove to my colleagues that solving them would make a difference?"
- 要点：问自己：哪些问题（公认的或没被意识到的）阻碍着领域进步？能解决吗？不能的话，能否至少把它形式化、讲清楚？

### E4

- 位置：正文，"ripe for the plucking"
- 原文："Every field has its share of problems that everyone knows are "kinda important," and that may even get mentioned a lot, but on which no one has yet made a serious attempt. If you think you spot such a problem, use your colleagues and the library to make sure it hasn't been plucked yet."
- 要点：每个领域都有"大家都知道挺重要、却没人认真做过"的问题；发现后先请教同事、查文献，确认还没人做过。

### E5

- 位置：正文，"identify new interesting problems"
- 原文："Study existing (applied) systems and note what they do badly at. If your field is interdisciplinary, ask people in the other discipline what they think is interesting."
- 要点：找新问题：研究现有系统哪里做得不好；跨学科时，去问另一个学科的人觉得什么有意思。

### E6

- 位置：同上
- 原文："In many areas, the data have a way of suggesting their own problems."
- 要点：在很多领域，数据本身会提示问题。

### E7

- 位置：Keep in mind
- 原文："serve a goal that will still seem worthy 6, 12, or 48 months from now?"
- 要点：评估耗时项目时的问题之一：它服务的目标在 6、12、48 个月后看来还值得吗？

### E8

- 位置：Finally
- 原文："Don't spend eternity on background reading. Recognize that you will have to start your work in a state of partial ignorance: you don't have time to learn everything you need to know."
- 要点：不要无休止地读背景文献；你注定要在不完全了解的状态下开始工作。

### E9

- 位置：Finally
- 原文："So start doing your own thinking early. You can alternate that with reading: just show your ideas periodically to someone who can warn you about related work and point you to relevant papers."
- 要点：尽早开始自己思考，与阅读交替进行；定期把想法给懂行的人看，让他提醒你有哪些相关工作。

### E10

- 位置：正文，"big bones"
- 原文："Sometimes finding a good solution involves changing the problem slightly."
- 要点：有时找到好解法需要把问题稍微改一改。
