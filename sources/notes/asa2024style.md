# 美国农学会、美国作物科学学会、美国土壤科学学会 2024：Publications Handbook and Style Manual

- 资料：`asa2024style`，[原文 PDF](https://www.agronomy.org/files/publications/style/style-manual.pdf)
- 版本：2024 年 2 月更新版。美国农学会（American Society of Agronomy，ASA）、美国作物科学学会（Crop Science Society of America，CSSA）、美国土壤科学学会（Soil Science Society of America，SSSA）三家学会期刊和图书的出版手册与格式手册。位置写章节。

### E1

- 位置：Chapter 4. Statistical Design and Analysis
- 原文："Included should be any information about blocking, multiple experimental unit sizes (e.g., in split and strip plots), the number of sites and years or independent runs of the experiment, the number of replicates, a description of conditions at field sites and in greenhouse or controlled environmental chambers, and how measurements were made for specific traits."
- 要点：论文要写清：区组、不同大小的实验单位（如裂区、条区）、地点数和年份数（或独立重复运行的次数）、重复数、田间和温室条件、各性状怎样测量。

### E2

- 位置：Chapter 4. Statistical Design and Analysis
- 原文："In studies where the experimental units and observational or sampling units were not the same, each should be clearly identified. The number of experimental units used and the number of samples taken from each unit should be clear to the reader."
- 要点：实验单位和观测（取样）单位不同时，两者都要写清；用了多少个实验单位、每个单位取了多少样，要让读者看得明白。

### E3

- 位置：Chapter 4. Statistical Design and Analysis
- 原文："A measure of the precision achieved, either as a standard error or a confidence interval, should be reported for all data on which conclusions are drawn."
- 要点：凡是用来下结论的数据，都要报告精度（标准误或置信区间）。

### E4

- 位置：Chapter 4，Spatial and Temporal Statistical Analyses
- 原文："Data observed at different points in space and/or time on the same experimental material are often correlated."
- 要点：在同一份试验材料上、不同位置或不同时间测得的数据，常常是相关的（不独立）。

### E5

- 位置：Chapter 1，Manuscript Handling
- 原文："For example, a paper released because it needed another year of data may be resubmitted after those data have been gathered and the results incorporated into the paper."
- 要点：例如，一篇论文因为还需要再多一年的数据而被退回，作者收集好数据、写进论文后可以再投。

### E6

- 位置：Chapter 1，Anonymous Review
- 原文："Most ASA, CSSA, and SSSA journals use a single-anonymous review process, where the authors do not know the names of the reviewers. Some use a double-anonymous process and also withhold the names of the authors from the reviewers."
- 要点：这三家学会的期刊多数用单盲审稿（作者不知道审稿人是谁），少数用双盲审稿。

### E7

- 位置：Chapter 5. Tables
- 原文："Typically, specify the crop or soil involved, the major variables presented, and the place and year."
- 要点：表格标题一般要写明作物或土壤、主要变量、地点和年份。

### E8

- 位置：Chapter 1，Research Papers 中对 Materials and Methods 的要求
- 原文："Give details of unusual experimental designs or statistical methods."
- 要点：不常见的试验设计或统计方法，要给出细节。

### E9

- 位置：Chapter 4. Statistical Design and Analysis
- 原文："Readers must be able to understand how the authors designed and conducted their studies so that the results can be judged for validity and also serve as a basis for the design of future research."
- 要点：读者必须能看懂试验怎样设计和实施，才能判断结果是否可信，并据此设计以后的研究。
