# Jeff Huang 2021：Behind the scenes: the struggle for each paper

- 资料：`huang2021struggle`，[原文](https://jeffhuang.com/struggle_for_each_paper/)
- 版本：作者主页，2021。作者（布朗大学教授）逐篇回顾自己 30 篇论文背后的经过。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："But my CV only shows a list of neatly cataloged papers: title, authors, conference. Each one appearing no different from another. But how each paper ended up published is its own story, a story about people and opportunity."
- 要点：简历上的论文看起来整整齐齐，但每篇论文是怎么发表出来的，背后都有各自的故事。

### E2

- 位置：第一篇论文的故事
- 原文："It was a long 3.5 years between when I got that first message and when it was published" / "I was rejected by every Ph.D. program I applied to before this publication"
- 要点：作者的第一篇论文从开始到发表花了 3.5 年；发表之前，他申请的博士项目全部被拒。

### E3

- 位置：结尾的反思
- 原文："The other thing I noticed is that papers that get the most citations later often got poor reviews or multiple rejections."
- 要点：后来被引最多的论文，当初常常评审意见很差，或者被拒过多次。

### E4

- 位置：结尾的反思
- 原文："But I also think about how my group has encountered a lot of rejection, and keeping up morale was sometimes difficult."
- 要点：即使是成熟的研究组，也会经历大量被拒，保持士气并不容易。
