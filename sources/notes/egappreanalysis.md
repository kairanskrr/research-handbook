# EGAP 方法指南：10 Things to Know About Pre-Analysis Plans

- 资料：`egappreanalysis`，[原文网页](https://methods.egap.org/guides/planning/pap_en.html)
- 版本："治理与政治证据网络"（Evidence in Governance and Politics，EGAP）方法指南，作者 Nuole (Lula) Chen 和 Christopher Grady；2026 年 10 月访问。位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§1 What is a Pre-Analysis Plan (PAP)
- 原文："A PAP is a document that formalizes and declares the design and analysis plan for your study. It is written before the analysis is conducted and is generally registered on a third-party website."
- 要点：预分析计划是在分析之前写好、通常登记在第三方网站上的研究设计和分析计划。

### E2

- 位置：§1
- 原文："a PAP should, at a minimum, include the full list of hypotheses that you intend to test, how you will measure variables relevant to those hypotheses, and a verifiable time stamp."
- 要点：预分析计划至少要有：全部待检验假设、相关变量怎样测量、可核实的时间戳。

### E3

- 位置：§3 Hypotheses
- 原文："with more than one hypothesis you will need to specify a procedure for handling multiple hypotheses in the inference criteria section of your PAP, either by correcting for multiple tests or by aggregating all hypotheses into an index or an omnibus test."
- 要点：假设不止一个时，要事先写明怎样处理多重检验：做校正，或合成指数、做总体检验。

### E4

- 位置：§9 Why make a PAP?
- 原文："But for experimental studies or other studies using original data collection, the researcher gets one chance to collect the data needed for the analysis."
- 要点：实验或自己收集原始数据的研究，收集数据只有一次机会，写计划能帮助事先想清楚。

### E5

- 位置：§9 Why make a PAP?
- 原文："Exploratory analyses are okay! The point of a PAP is not to prevent these unanticipated analyses, but to formalize and explain the process that led to the analyses."
- 要点：探索性分析可以做；计划的目的不是禁止计划外的分析，而是把它们的来龙去脉写清楚。

### E6

- 位置：§10 When and where do you register a PAP?
- 原文："For experimental studies, the latest you should register the PAP is before final data are collected. For observational studies, the latest you should register the PAP is before any analyses are done, including looking at descriptive statistics. You can revise PAPs through an amendment at any time."
- 要点：实验研究最晚在最终数据收集前登记；观察性研究最晚在任何分析（包括看描述统计）之前登记；之后可以随时通过修订更新。
