# AEA：Disclosure Policy

- 资料：`aea2026disclosure`，[原文](https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/policies/disclosure-policy)
- 版本：2026 年 10 月 6 日读取的网页。适用于 AEA 旗下期刊。

### E1

- 位置：Disclosure Principles
- 原文："Every submitted article should state the sources of financial support for the particular research it describes. If none, that fact should be stated."
- 要点：每篇投稿都要说明研究的资金来源；没有也要写明。

### E2

- 位置：Disclosure Principles
- 原文："Each author of a submitted article should identify each interested party from whom he or she has received significant financial support, summing to at least $10,000 in the past three years, in the form of consultant fees, retainers, grants and the like. The disclosure requirement also includes in-kind support, such as providing access to data."
- 要点：每位作者要披露过去三年从利益相关方获得的累计至少一万美元的资助（咨询费、资助等），也包括提供数据访问这类非现金支持。

### E3

- 位置：Disclosure Principles
- 原文："Each author must disclose if another party had the right to review the paper prior to its circulation."
- 要点：如果有其他方有权在论文发布前审阅论文，必须披露。

### E4

- 位置：Instructions
- 原文："Each (co)author should prepare a separate PDF document entitled “Disclosure Statement,” which must be provided with the manuscript."
- 要点：每位作者单独准备一份"披露声明"PDF，随稿件提交。

### E5

- 位置：Institutional Review Board (IRB) Approval
- 原文："For any paper involving the collection of data on human subjects the author(s) must disclose whether they have obtained Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval; if no IRB approval was obtained, the reason should be stated."
- 要点：收集人类受试者数据的论文，必须披露是否获得伦理审查委员会批准；没有的要说明原因。

### E6

- 位置：Instructions
- 原文："Failure to disclose relevant information at the submission stage may result in reversal of acceptance decisions."
- 要点：投稿时未披露相关信息，可能导致撤销录用决定。
