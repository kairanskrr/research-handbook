# Krippendorff 2004：Reliability in Content Analysis: Some Common Misconceptions and Recommendations

- 资料：`krippendorff2004reliability`，[原文（宾夕法尼亚大学 ScholarlyCommons 存放的作者稿 PDF）](https://repository.upenn.edu/handle/20.500.14332/1886)
- 版本：作者接受稿（postprint），正式版发表于 Human Communication Research 第 30 卷第 3 期，第 411–433 页，2004 年（DOI 10.1111/j.1468-2958.2004.tb00738.x，已通过 Crossref 核对）。位置写作者稿 PDF 的页码。是对 Lombard、Snyder-Duch 和 Bracken 2002 年信度指南的回应，出自传播学。作者稿用特殊字体排希腊字母，文本抽取时丢失，下面引文中的 α 按 PDF 版面补回。

### E1

- 位置：Relation Between Agreement and Reliability，第 3 页
- 原文："To be clear, agreement is what we measure; reliability is what we wish to infer from it. In content analysis, reproducibility is arguably the most important interpretation of reliability"
- 要点：一致性是测出来的东西，信度是想从中推断的东西；在内容分析里，信度最重要的含义是可复现：换一批编码者照同样的规则做，能得到同样的数据。

### E2

- 位置：Relation Between Agreement and Reliability，第 3 页
- 原文："Many methodological problems in testing reliability stem from violating the requirement for coders to be truly independent, being given coding instructions they cannot follow, or applying them to data that they fail understand."
- 要点：信度测试的很多问题来自三处：编码者并不真正独立，拿到的编码说明没法照着做，或者编码的材料他们看不懂。

### E3

- 位置：第 10 页
- 原文："The use of %-agreement should be actively discouraged, especially as a fallback criterion. Instead, I recommend that only chance-corrected agreement coefficients that satisfy (2) and (3) be used for inferring the reliability of data."
- 要点：应当明确不用百分比一致率，尤其不要拿它当退路；只用考虑了碰巧一致、并满足作者所列条件的系数来推断信度。

### E4

- 位置：第 10 页
- 原文："When all coders use only one category, there is no variation and, hence, no evidence of reliability."
- 要点：所有编码者都只用同一个类别时，数据没有变化，也就没有任何信度证据，即使百分比一致率是 100%。

### E5

- 位置：Recommendations，(i)，第 12 页
- 原文："Reliability data, the sample of data from which the trustworthiness of a population of data is to be inferred, have to be generated by coders that are widely available, follow explicit and communicable instructions (a data language), and work independently of each other. Reliability data must be representative of the data whose reliability is in question (not of the population of ultimate research interest)" / "Coders must be interchangeable, may code different subsamples of data, provided there is enough duplication or overlap."
- 要点：用来测信度的样本，要由容易找到的普通编码者、按明确可传达的说明、彼此独立地编码；这个样本要代表待检验信度的那批数据。编码者要可以互换，可以各编不同的子样本，只要有足够的重复或重叠。

### E6

- 位置：Recommendations，(ii)，第 12 页
- 原文："α can handle multiple coders, nominal, ordinal, interval, ratio, and other metrics, missing data, and small sample sizes." / "Content analyses that assess reliability in terms of any association coefficient, Pearson's r, for example, [...] Cronbach's (1951) alpha, [...] and %-agreement should be rejected as these measures are incompatible with reliability concerns in content analysis."
- 要点：Krippendorff's α 能处理多名编码者、各种测量尺度、缺失数据和小样本；作者主张，用 Pearson 相关系数等相关类系数、Cronbach's alpha 或百分比一致率来评估信度的内容分析应当被拒绝（被省略的部分还点名了 Cohen's kappa 等）。

### E7

- 位置：Recommendations，(iii)，第 12 页
- 原文："An acceptable level of agreement below which data are to be rejected as too unreliable must be chosen depending on the costs of drawing invalid conclusions from these data." / "I suggested elsewhere to require α ≥ .800, and where tentative conclusions are still acceptable, α ≥ .667" / "Except for perfect agreement, there are no magical numbers, however."
- 要点：合格线要按"得出错误结论的代价"来定；作者建议一般要求 α ≥ 0.800，只作初步结论时 α ≥ 0.667；但除了完全一致，没有神奇的数字。

### E8

- 位置：Recommendations，(iv)，第 13 页
- 原文："All distinctions that matter should be tested for their reliability. Where a system of several variables is intended to support a conclusion [...], the reliability of each variable should be measured and the smallest among them should be taken as the reliability of the whole system. Averaging the agreement measures of several variables, especially when they include easily coded clerical ones, can easily mislead researchers about the reliability of variables that matter."
- 要点：每个要紧的区分都要测信度；几个变量一起支撑结论时，逐个测，取最低的作为整体信度；把容易编的变量和难编的平均在一起会误导人。

### E9

- 位置：Recommendations，(iv)，第 13 页
- 原文："After data have been generated, reliability may be improved by discarding unreliable distinctions, recoding or lumping categories or dropping variables that do not meet the criterion adopted in (iii). Resolving disagreements by majority among three or more coders may make researchers feel better about their data, but does not affect the measured reliability"
- 要点：信度不够时，可以去掉不可靠的区分、重新编码或合并类别、删掉不达标的变量；用三人以上投票解决分歧会让人安心，但不改变测得的信度。

### E10

- 位置：Recommendations，(iii)，第 12 页
- 原文："To ensure that the measured agreement is representative of the data in question, confidence intervals should be consulted. Testing the null-hypothesis that observed agreement deviates from chance has no bearing on reliability, which concerns deviations from perfect agreement or 1.000."
- 要点：要参考置信区间，确认测得的一致性能代表这批数据；检验"一致性显著高于碰巧"对信度没有意义，信度关心的是离完全一致还差多少。
