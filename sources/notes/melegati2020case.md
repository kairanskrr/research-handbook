# Melegati & Wang 2020：Case Survey Studies in Software Engineering Research

- 资料：`melegati2020case`，[arXiv:2007.13592](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.13592)
- 版本：arXiv v1（2020-07-27），作者接受稿（postprint），正式版发表于 ESEM 2020。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："It is based on existing case studies reported in the literature and intends to harness the generalizability of survey and the depth of case study."
- 要点：案例调查以文献中已报告的案例研究为基础，想兼得调查研究的可推广性和案例研究的深度。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："we performed a systematic mapping study and analyzed 12 Software Engineering studies that used the case survey method" / "They have not applied the case survey method consistently as defined in the seminal methodological papers."
- 要点：作者用映射研究找到 12 篇自称用了案例调查的软件工程论文，发现它们没有按奠基性方法论文的定义一致地使用这种方法。

### E3

- 位置：§2 The Case Survey Method
- 原文："Larsson [19] described the method in four steps: i) select a group of existing case studies, ii) design a coding scheme to convert case descriptions into quantitative variables, iii) code the cases using multiple raters, and iv) analyze the results statistically."
- 要点：Larsson 把案例调查概括为四步：选一组已有的案例研究；设计把案例描述转换为定量变量的编码方案；由多名编码者编码；用统计方法分析。

### E4

- 位置：§2，Larsson 的 12 个步骤之"初始研究问题"与"案例选择标准"
- 原文："Larsson [19] argued that case surveys could be used to test hypotheses or explore but stressed the importance of theory to select cases properly." / "It should be based on how much data is reported rather than “how, when, or where” it was published. Besides that, a large number of cases is needed for statistical hypothesis testing."
- 要点：案例调查可以用来检验假设，也可以用来探索，但选案例要有理论依据；选择标准应看报告了多少数据，而不是在哪里、何时、如何发表；做统计检验需要大量案例。

### E5

- 位置：§2，三个关键步骤的小结
- 原文："For instance, Larsson mentioned his study on mergers and acquisitions, where he employed a minimum requirement of two pages describing the business and human issues."
- 要点：例子：Larsson 研究企业并购时，要求每个案例至少有两页描述业务和人员方面的问题，才能纳入。

### E6

- 位置：§2，"Creation of the code scheme"
- 原文："It is determined by a trade-off between “resource-saving, reliable simplicity, and information-rich complexity.”"
- 要点：编码方案取决于一种权衡：一边是省力、可靠的简单，一边是信息丰富的复杂。

### E7

- 位置：§2，"Coding the cases with multiple-raters" 与 "Measure of inter-rater reliability"
- 原文："Several authors [15, 19] suggested that at least two, preferably three raters, are employed." / "this aspect is a crucial measure of coding quality, but focusing too deep on it may be counter-productive"
- 要点：建议至少两名、最好三名编码者；编码者间一致性是衡量编码质量的关键指标，但过度纠结于它可能适得其反。

### E8

- 位置：§2，"Participation of original authors"
- 原文："Larsson [19] suggested that original case study authors could be employed as the thirdrater for their studies. Since they had contact with primary data, they may have other insights since part of the data may have been left out of the report because of space constraints."
- 要点：可以请原案例研究的作者担任第三名编码者：他们接触过一手数据，而部分数据可能因篇幅限制没有写进报告。

### E9

- 位置：§2，"Statistical analysis of the impact of specific case characteristics"
- 原文："evaluation if case study aspects such as research design, publication, and time influenced the coding."
- 要点：要检验原始案例研究的设计、发表情况和时间是否影响了编码结果。

### E10

- 位置：§2.1 Differences to other research methods
- 原文："can achieve [cross-case pattern analysis], but the resource-consuming, intensive research they require typically limits case sets to smaller sizes than are needed to benefit from advanced statistical cross-case analysis." / "are performed by the same person, with the same purpose, perspective, method, and theoretical framework."
- 要点：多案例研究也能做跨案例的规律分析，但投入太大，案例数通常达不到统计分析的需要；而且多案例研究由同一人以同样的目的、视角、方法和理论框架完成（案例调查的原始案例则不是）。

### E11

- 位置：§2.1
- 原文："a case survey study is similar to an SLR since its goal is to synthesize evidence rather than structure a research area as in an SMS. Nevertheless, case surveys are constrained to analyze studies describing cases using quantitative techniques on data obtained through a defined coding scheme."
- 要点：案例调查和系统综述一样以综合证据为目标，而不是像映射研究那样梳理领域结构；但案例调查只分析描述案例的研究，并用定量方法分析经编码方案得到的数据。

### E12

- 位置：§6 Conclusions
- 原文："The most common approach labeled as case survey is actually based on primary data collected through questionnaires or online resources such as blog posts. The other authors used the term case survey to describe multiple-case studies with a large number of cases."
- 要点：被称作"案例调查"的研究，最常见的其实是用问卷或博客等网上资料收集的一手数据；其余的是案例数较多的多案例研究。

### E13

- 位置：§5 Discussion
- 原文："But, the depth and breadth of the data about that case, generally a questionnaire answer or a blog post from only one person, weakens this claim." / "using only one source to describe a case jeopardizes one key element for case studies: triangulation."
- 要点：把一份问卷答案或一篇博客当成一个"案例"站不住：数据的深度和广度不够，而且只有一个来源，失去了案例研究的关键要素——三角互证（用多个来源相互印证）。

### E14

- 位置：§5 Discussion
- 原文："When the use deviates from the canonical approach, without proper reflection on the consequences, the validity of the research findings may be put into question."
- 要点：偏离方法的标准做法又不反思后果，研究结论的效度就可能受到质疑。
