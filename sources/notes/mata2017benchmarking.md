# Mata 和 Suhm 2017：Benchmarking Quantum Chemical Methods: Are We Heading in the Right Direction?

- 资料：`mata2017benchmarking`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201611308)
- 版本：Angewandte Chemie International Edition 56: 11011–11018，2017，CC BY-NC 开放获取。原文文本取自 Europe PMC（PMC5582598）。这是两位哥廷根大学研究者（一位理论化学、一位实验光谱）的观点文章（作者自称 essay），建议只代表作者本人，不是期刊的规定。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Theory Benchmarking Theory
- 原文："The quality of a model is thereby no longer measured through any relation to experiment, but purely to the similarity to another model. This practice has become so popular that many manuscripts dedicated to quantum chemistry benchmarks do not feature a single experimental result."
- 要点：拿理论当参照的基准测试，衡量的只是一个模型和另一个模型像不像，而不是和实验的关系；这种做法流行到很多量子化学基准论文里一个实验结果都没有。

### E2

- 位置：Theory Benchmarking Theory
- 原文："There are good reasons for theory‐only benchmarking."
- 要点：只用理论做参照的基准测试也有正当理由（例如某个量很难或无法用实验测）。

### E3

- 位置：Experiment Benchmarking Theory
- 原文："A single match between theory and experiment is almost worthless, because it could always be the result of fortuitous error cancellation. A broad, disparate data set is usually needed"
- 要点：理论和实验只吻合一次几乎没有价值，因为可能是误差碰巧相互抵消；通常需要范围广、类型多样的数据集。

### E4

- 位置：Experiment Benchmarking Theory
- 原文："If the experimental result is known beforehand to the theoretician or the theoretical prediction known to the experimentalist, there is a non‐zero risk of mutual influence"
- 要点：如果算的人事先知道实验结果，或测的人事先知道理论预测，双方就有互相影响的风险（所以作者也提倡盲测）。

### E5

- 位置：Conclusions
- 原文："Theoreticians should focus on measurable quantities, and/or a well‐defined connection between the computed quantity and the experimental data."
- 要点：做理论的人应关注能测量的量，或者让计算的量和实验数据之间有明确的对应关系。

### E6

- 位置：Conclusions
- 原文："not only mention the “successful” methods, but also take the opportunity to address the approaches that fail, in particular those with wide acceptance in the community"
- 要点：不要只报告"成功"的方法，也要讨论失败的方法，特别是被广泛使用的那些。

### E7

- 位置：Conclusions
- 原文："provide whenever possible estimates and comments on error sources and crucial approximations in the calculations carried out."
- 要点：尽可能给出误差来源和计算中关键近似的估计和说明。

### E8

- 位置：Conclusions
- 原文："They should provide all needed material to ensure reproducibility and make proper use of the Supporting Information (provide structures, energies)."
- 要点：提供保证可重复所需的全部材料，用好支持信息（给出结构和能量）。
