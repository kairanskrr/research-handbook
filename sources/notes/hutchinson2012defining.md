# Hutchinson & Duncan 2012：Defining and Describing What We Do: Doctrinal Legal Research

- 资料：`hutchinson2012defining`，[原文 PDF（Deakin Law Review 官网，开放获取）](https://ojs.deakin.edu.au/index.php/dlr/article/download/70/75/151)
- 版本：Deakin Law Review 第 17 卷第 1 期（2012），第 83–119 页。作者分别在澳大利亚昆士兰科技大学和英国伦敦城市大学任教，文章讨论的主要是**澳大利亚和英国**的情况。原文用 `...' 表示引号，照原样保留。位置写小节标题和期刊页码。

### E1

- 位置：§III Defining Legal Research Terminology，第 101 页
- 原文："1. Doctrinal research -- `Research which provides a systematic exposition of the rules governing a particular legal category, analyses the relationship between rules, explains areas of difficulty and, perhaps, predicts future developments.'"
- 要点：澳大利亚 1987 年 Pearce 委员会报告对规范研究（doctrinal research）的定义：系统阐述某一法律领域的规则，分析规则之间的关系，解释疑难之处，也许还预测未来发展。

### E2

- 位置：§III，第 101 页
- 原文："2. Reform-oriented research -- `Research which intensively evaluates the adequacy of existing rules and which recommends changes to any rules found wanting.'"
- 要点：改革导向研究：评估现有规则是否妥当，并对不足的规则提出修改建议。

### E3

- 位置：§III，第 101 页
- 原文："3. Theoretical research -- `Research which fosters a more complete understanding of the conceptual bases of legal principles and of the combined effects of a range of rules and procedures that touch on a particular area of activity.'"
- 要点：理论研究：加深对法律原则概念基础的理解。

### E4

- 位置：§III，第 102 页
- 原文："4 Fundamental research -- `Research designed to secure a deeper understanding of law as a social phenomenon, including research on the historical, philosophical, linguistic, economic, social or political implications of law.'"
- 要点：加拿大 1983 年 Arthurs 报告加了第四类"基础研究"：把法律当作社会现象来理解，包括法律的历史、哲学、语言、经济、社会和政治含义。

### E5

- 位置：§III，第 102 页
- 原文："All the reports agreed that doctrinal legal research was the most accepted methodology in the discipline of law."
- 要点：这些报告都认为，规范研究是法学中最被接受的研究方法。

### E6

- 位置：§III B Doctrinal Method as a Two Part Process，第 110 页
- 原文："Doctrinal method is normally a two-part process, because it involves first locating the sources of the law and then interpreting and analysing the text."
- 要点：规范研究通常分两步：先找到法律渊源，再解释和分析文本。

### E7

- 位置：§III B，第 110 页
- 原文："It is at the next step where the law or rule is interpreted and analysed within a specific context that the outcome becomes `contingent' or conditional on the expertise, views and methods of the individual researcher."
- 要点：第二步（在具体语境中解释和分析规则）的结果，取决于研究者个人的专业、观点和方法。

### E8

- 位置：§III B 1 The Literature Review as a Step within the Doctrinal Method，第 112 页
- 原文："One point that must be made is that doctrinal research is more than simply a literature review."
- 要点：规范研究不只是文献综述。

### E9

- 位置：§III B 1，第 113 页
- 原文："It is the location and analysis of the primary documents of the law in order to establish the nature and parameters of the law."
- 要点：规范研究的核心是找到并分析法律的一手文件，以确定法律是什么、边界在哪里。

### E10

- 位置：§III B 1，第 113 页
- 原文："It includes that intricate step of `reading, analysing and linking' the new information to the known body of law."
- 要点：规范研究包括把新信息与已知的法律体系"阅读、分析、联系起来"这一步。

### E11

- 位置：§III B 1，第 112–113 页
- 原文："`Testimony' can include the secondary literature -- texts, journal articles, government reports, policy documents, law reform documents and media reports."
- 要点：二手文献包括教科书、期刊文章、政府报告、政策文件、法律改革文件和媒体报道。

### E12

- 位置：§III B 3 Doctrinal Research Compared to Historical Research，第 117 页
- 原文："The legal researcher examines primary sources in order to draw logical conclusions about what the law is in those instances where it is not immediately self-evident from those sources. By contrast, the historical researcher examines primary sources as evidence of fact."
- 要点：法学研究者读一手资料，是为了推出"法律是什么"；历史研究者读一手资料，是把它当作事实的证据。

### E13

- 位置：§III B 3，第 117 页
- 原文："doctrinal research for the most part focuses on `privileged voices'."
- 要点：规范研究主要关注"有权威的声音"，即法官的判决和议会的立法。

### E14

- 位置：§III B 2 Is Doctrinal Research a Qualitative or a Quantitative Methodology?，第 116 页
- 原文："The outcome varies according to the expertise of the individual scholar and cannot be replicated exactly by another researcher."
- 要点：规范分析的结果因学者的专业而异，别的研究者无法精确重复。

### E15

- 位置：§II E Are HDR Students in Law Articulating Their Methodologies in Their Theses?，第 99 页
- 原文："Unfortunately the doctrinal method is often so implicit and so tacit that many working within the legal paradigm consider that it is unnecessary to verbalise the process."
- 要点：规范研究方法常常是隐含的、默会的，很多法律人认为不必把过程说出来。

### E16

- 位置：§II E，第 100 页
- 原文："Therefore, only 38 of the 60 law theses (63.3 per cent) include a discussion of the methodology as part of the thesis."
- 要点：作者 2010 年查阅澳大利亚数字论文库中的 60 篇法学研究学位论文，只有 38 篇（63.3%）讨论了研究方法。

### E17

- 位置：§II E，第 98 页
- 原文："At some point there must be a statement, however brief, concerning the method of collection of the data -- for example: `This study will include a doctrinal analysis of legislation and case law.'"
- 要点：研究学位论文中至少要有一句说明资料收集方法，例如"本研究将对立法和判例法做规范分析"。

### E18

- 位置：§III B 1，第 112 页
- 原文："perhaps this methodology is nothing more than the need for doctrinal research to follow accepted conventions, using clear rationales, and for the research to be `systematic and purposive with a robust framework'."
- 要点：规范研究的方法论，也许就是遵循公认的惯例、给出清楚的理由，做到"系统、有目的、有稳固的框架"。

### E19

- 位置：§III A Does the Research Method Differ among the Various Legal Research Genres?，第 106 页
- 原文："There is firstly the problem-based doctrinal research methodology used by practitioners and students."
- 要点：实务律师和学生用的是"以问题为导向"的规范研究（下列步骤：整理事实、识别法律问题、读背景材料、查一手材料、综合、得出初步结论）。

### E20

- 位置：§III A，第 107 页
- 原文："Academic researchers choose both the topic and the breadth and depth of investigation. The doctrinal method is similar to that being used by the practitioner or the judge, except that the academic researcher (or HDR student) is not constrained by the imperative to find a concrete answer for a client."
- 要点：学术研究者自己选择题目以及研究的广度和深度；所用规范方法与律师、法官相似，区别是不必为客户找出一个具体答案。

### E21

- 位置：§III，第 104 页（引 Minow 的法学研究贡献类型）
- 原文："VII Comparative and Historical Inquiries,"
- 要点：时任哈佛法学院院长 Minow 列出的法学研究贡献类型之一：比较与历史研究。

### E22

- 位置：§III，第 103 页（引 Minow）
- 原文："Acknowledge distinction between settled and emerging law"
- 要点：Minow 列出的"规范重述"类贡献包括：区分已经定型的法律和正在形成的法律。

### E23

- 位置：§III，第 103 页（引 Minow）
- 原文："a. Gather more than one "line" of cases, across doctrinal fields or categories, and show why they belong together or expose unjustified discrepancies"
- 要点："重新组合"类贡献：把不同领域的几条判例线索放在一起，说明它们为何属于一类，或揭示不合理的不一致。

### E24

- 位置：§II B Attempts To Measure Research Quality，第 92 页
- 原文："evaluated not only by the citation of research papers by other researchers, but also by the referencing of pertinent research by the courts or law reform bodies."
- 要点：法学研究的质量不只看同行引用，还看法院或法律改革机构是否引用；这种引用不在常用引文索引里。

### E25

- 位置：§II，第 86 页
- 原文："There is more emphasis on group, interdisciplinary and empirical research rather than there was under the older paradigm, particularly common in law faculties, of an individual legal researcher working alone."
- 要点：（澳大利亚和英国）过去二十年，法学院更强调团队、跨学科和实证研究；旧模式是法学研究者一个人独立工作。

### E26

- 位置：§II The Current Research Landscape，第 86 页
- 原文："Academics are being directed towards publication in refereed or peer reviewed journals with academic audiences, rather than practitioner journals where, arguably, their research may have more impact."
- 要点：（澳大利亚和英国）学者被引导去面向学术读者的同行评审期刊发表，而不是面向执业者的刊物。

### E27

- 位置：§III，第 103 页（引 Minow）
- 原文："a. Organize and reorganize case law into coherent elements, categories, and concepts"
- 要点：Minow 列出的"规范重述"类贡献：把判例整理成连贯的要素、类别和概念。

### E28

- 位置：§III，第 103 页（引 Minow）
- 原文："III Policy Analysis,"
- 要点：Minow 清单中的另一类贡献：政策分析。

### E29

- 位置：§III，第 104 页（引 Minow）
- 原文："VIII Jurisprudence, Philosophy of Law"
- 要点：Minow 清单中的另一类贡献：法理学、法哲学。

### E30

- 位置：§III B 2，第 116 页
- 原文："The research involved in locating the sources of the law can be easily replicated by other researchers."
- 要点：找到法律渊源这一步，其他研究者很容易重复。

### E31

- 位置：§III B，以 "What is it like and unlike?" 开头的一段，第 114 页
- 原文："but these are different because they are `legitimated' by the sovereignty of the source (parliament or court) rather than because they are the `naturally occurring', observable phenomena usually used in empirical work."
- 要点：立法和判决的分量来自制定它们的议会或法院的权威，而不是像实证研究的数据那样，是自然发生、可观察的现象。
