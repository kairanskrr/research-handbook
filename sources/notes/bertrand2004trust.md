# Bertrand, Duflo & Mullainathan 2004：How Much Should We Trust Differences-in-Differences Estimates?

- 资料：`bertrand2004trust`，[NBER 工作论文版 PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w8841/w8841.pdf)；正式版发表于 Quarterly Journal of Economics 119(1)，2004
- 版本：NBER Working Paper 8841（2002 年 3 月）。摘录依据工作论文版，正式版的数字可能略有不同。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："To illustrate the severity of this issue, we randomly generate placebo laws in state-level data on female wages from the Current Population Survey. For each law, we use OLS to compute the DD estimate of its "effect" as well as the standard error for this estimate. The standard errors are severely biased: with about 20 years of data, DD estimation finds an "effect" significant at the 5% level of up to 45% of the placebo laws."
- 要点：作者在美国当前人口调查（Current Population Survey）的州级女性工资数据里随机生成并不存在的"安慰剂法律"，用常规双重差分估计其"效应"。在约 20 年数据下，最多有 45% 的假法律在 5% 水平上"显著"，说明标准误严重偏小。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："We also suggest a third technique, based on randomization inference testing methods, which works well irrespective of sample size. This technique uses the empirical distribution of estimated effects for placebo laws to form the test distribution."
- 要点：作者建议的第三种办法：用大量安慰剂法律的估计效应构成检验所用的分布（随机化推断），样本大小都适用。
