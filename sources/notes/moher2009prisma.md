# Moher et al. 2009：Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses: The PRISMA Statement

- 资料：`moher2009prisma`，[PLoS Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1000097)（开放获取）
- 版本：PLoS Med 2009;6(7):e1000097。已被 PRISMA 2020 取代，本文只引用其中的术语定义和对方案修改的说明。

### E1

- 位置：Terminology
- 原文："A systematic review is a review of a clearly formulated question that uses systematic and explicit methods to identify, select, and critically appraise relevant research, and to collect and analyze data from the studies that are included in the review. Statistical methods (meta-analysis) may or may not be used to analyze and summarize the results of the included studies."
- 要点：系统综述针对一个界定清楚的问题，用系统、明确的方法找、选、评价研究并收集分析数据；可以用、也可以不用统计方法（元分析）。

### E2

- 位置：Terminology
- 原文："Meta-analysis refers to the use of statistical techniques in a systematic review to integrate the results of included studies."
- 要点：元分析指在系统综述中用统计方法整合纳入研究的结果。

### E3

- 位置：Box 1，Completing a Systematic Review Is an Iterative Process
- 原文："systematic reviewers may need to modify their original review protocol during its conduct. Any systematic review reporting guideline should recommend that such changes can be reported and explained without suggesting that they are inappropriate." / "Without a protocol that is publicly accessible, it is difficult to judge between appropriate and inappropriate modifications."
- 要点：做综述过程中可能需要修改方案，修改本身不是错，报告并解释即可；但没有公开的方案，别人就无法判断修改是否恰当。
