# Uhlen 等人 2016：A proposal for validation of antibodies

- 资料：`uhlen2016proposal`，[原文（PubMed Central 作者稿）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10335836/)
- 版本：Nature Methods 13(10):823–827，2016 年。国际抗体验证工作组（International Working Group for Antibody Validation，IWGAV）的建议。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：引言
- 原文："Despite their widespread use, as well as extensive and valuable discourse in the literature, a comprehensive scientific framework for antibody validation across research applications is lacking. As a result, the quality and consistency of data generated through the use of antibodies vary greatly."
- 要点：抗体用得很广，但缺少一套跨应用的验证框架，所以用抗体得到的数据质量和一致性差别很大。

### E2

- 位置：引言
- 原文："Indeed, extensive characterization of antibody performance in western blotting may indicate nothing about the performance of the same antibody in an ELISA assay" / "Therefore, approaches for antibody validation must be carried out in an application- and context-specific manner."
- 要点：抗体在蛋白质印迹里表现好，不代表在酶联免疫吸附试验里也好；验证必须按具体应用和样品情境来做。

### E3

- 位置：引言
- 原文："We suggest five conceptual pillars for validation of antibodies: (i) genetic strategies, (ii) orthogonal strategies, (iii) independent antibody strategies, (iv) expression of tagged proteins, and (v) immunocapture followed by mass spectrometry (MS). We suggest that at least one of these pillars should be used as a minimum criterion for claiming that a particular antibody has been adequately validated for a specific application."
- 要点：提出五种验证思路（基因方法、不依赖抗体的正交方法、两种独立抗体、表达带标签的蛋白、免疫捕获加质谱）；声称抗体在某种应用中已验证，至少要用其中一种。

### E4

- 位置：Genetic strategies
- 原文："However, genetic strategies cannot be used for some applications and types of samples; in particular, they cannot be used for human tissue samples and body fluids, such as plasma and serum."
- 要点：基因方法不能用于人体组织样本和血浆、血清等体液。

### E5

- 位置：Independent antibody strategies
- 原文："It is important to note that for the western blot validation assay, the whole gel should be shown, not only the area around the size of the target proteins."
- 要点：用蛋白质印迹做验证时，要展示整块胶，而不只是目标蛋白大小附近那一段。

### E6

- 位置：Suggestions for providers and users
- 原文："We therefore recommend that users of antibodies carry out at least one of the validation strategies described here in their own particular application or sample context. We also recommend that users adhere to appropriate reporting guidelines and, at a minimum, include catalog number, lot number, and perhaps RRID to ensure that any antibodies used in their research can be unambiguously identified."
- 要点：抗体使用者应在自己的应用和样品情境中至少做一种验证；论文里至少写明货号、批号，最好加上研究资源标识符，让别人能准确找到同一种抗体。

### E7

- 位置：Antibody reproducibility
- 原文："We recommend the use of Research Resource Identifiers (RRIDs), since they are free to generate, are machine readable, and may be used consistently across publishers and journals."
- 要点：推荐使用研究资源标识符（RRID）：免费、机器可读、各出版社和期刊通用。

### E8

- 位置：Suggestions for providers and users
- 原文："Producers should repeat validation experiments for each new lot to ensure that data remain relevant."
- 要点：抗体生产方应对每个新批次重复验证。

### E9

- 位置：Additional methods for antibody characterization
- 原文："Although useful to show target binding, this method will not rule out crossreactivity with proteins containing similar epitopes as the intended target."
- 要点：免疫组化常用的吸附实验能说明抗体结合了目标，但排除不了与相似表位蛋白的交叉反应。
