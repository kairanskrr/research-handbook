# Schmidt 2016：Using Archives: A Guide to Effective Research

- 资料：`schmidt2016using`，[原文](https://www2.archivists.org/usingarchives)
- 版本：美国档案工作者协会（Society of American Archivists，SAA，美国档案工作者的专业学会）官网免费公开的指南，作者 Laura Schmidt，页面标注更新于 2016-03-26，2026-10-06 读取。指南分十个网页（什么是档案、档案馆类型、查找和评估档案馆、远程申请资料、计划到访、常见使用规则、版权与限制及未整理收藏、到访档案馆、结语、附录：带注释的检索指南样例）。位置写网页标题。指南面向美国读者，举的检索工具、法律都是美国的。

### E1

- 位置：What Are Archives and How Do They Differ from Libraries?
- 原文："Materials in an archives are often unique, specialized, or rare objects, meaning very few of them exist in the world, or they are the only ones of their kind."
- 要点：档案馆里的材料常常是独一无二或极其稀少的。

### E2

- 位置：Types of Archives
- 原文："Defining your research topic and knowing what sorts of materials you are looking for will help you determine the appropriate institutions to contact."
- 要点：先界定研究题目、想清楚要找什么样的材料，才能判断该联系哪些收藏机构。

### E3

- 位置：Finding and Evaluating Archives
- 原文："Consult bibliographies and works cited sections in books on your topic. Where did the information come from?"
- 要点：查同主题著作的参考书目和引用文献，看它们的信息从哪里来。

### E4

- 位置：Finding and Evaluating Archives
- 原文："Contact other experts in the field. Which repositories did they visit for their research?"
- 要点：问同领域的专家，他们去过哪些收藏机构。

### E5

- 位置：Finding and Evaluating Archives，Finding Aids
- 原文："A finding aid (sometimes called inventory, collection listing, register, or calendar) is a text document providing a description of the contents of a collection, just like a table of contents outlines the contents of a book."
- 要点：检索指南是描述一个收藏内容的文本，像书的目录一样。

### E6

- 位置：Finding and Evaluating Archives，Finding Aids
- 原文："Finding aids sometimes provide narrative portions describing the background of a collection (how and when it was formed, how the archives acquired it, etc.), and how the archival staff has arranged or ordered the materials in the collection."
- 要点：检索指南有时会说明收藏的来历（怎样、何时形成，档案馆怎样得到它）以及材料是怎样排列的。

### E7

- 位置：Finding and Evaluating Archives，Digital Collections
- 原文："However, be aware that digital collections often reflect just a fraction of the total holdings of a repository. There may be nondigitized materials at the same institution that are also pertinent to your research."
- 要点：数字化收藏往往只是馆藏的一小部分，同一机构可能还有没数字化、但和你相关的材料。

### E8

- 位置：Finding and Evaluating Archives，Digital Collections
- 原文："Note that when searching digital collections online it can sometimes be unclear whether the items you are viewing represent a complete collection or are part of a larger collection. Try to determine the highest collection level for the most complete overview of related items."
- 要点：在线看数字化收藏时，可能分不清看到的是完整收藏还是更大收藏的一部分；要找到最高一级的收藏，了解全貌。

### E9

- 位置：Finding and Evaluating Archives，Archival Staff
- 原文："One of the most important ways to evaluate the holdings of an archives is contacting an archival professional who oversees the collections." / "I have already viewed the following [finding aids, catalogs, etc.] on your website, and thought that these specific resources would be useful for my research" / "Do you have any recommendations of other relevant materials in your collections?"
- 要点：评估一个档案馆有什么，最重要的办法之一是联系负责这些收藏的档案工作人员。指南给的邮件样例：说明研究项目，列出已经看过的检索指南和想看的材料，再问有没有其他相关材料可推荐。

### E10

- 位置：Finding and Evaluating Archives，Archival Staff
- 原文："After you have examined the catalogs, finding aids, and website of an archives, call or email the repository to confirm your findings and conclusions."
- 要点：先看完目录、检索指南和网站，再打电话或发邮件向档案馆确认。

### E11

- 位置：Finding and Evaluating Archives，Archival Staff
- 原文："In the case of an archives that does not list collections on its website (or does not have a website), contacting the staff is the only way to ascertain its holdings."
- 要点：网站上没列收藏（或没有网站）的档案馆，只能联系工作人员才知道有什么。

### E12

- 位置：Requesting Materials Remotely
- 原文："Will the archives provide scans or photocopies of the materials you wish to consult? What are the fees for those services?"
- 要点：可以问档案馆能否提供扫描件或复印件，收费多少。

### E13

- 位置：Requesting Materials Remotely
- 原文："Will the archives allow a research assistant to access materials on your behalf?"
- 要点：可以问能否由研究助理代你查阅。

### E14

- 位置：Requesting Materials Remotely
- 原文："Archivists routinely answer reference questions for researchers, so if the information you need can be retrieved in a short amount of time, there is a good chance they can relay it to you without having you come in person."
- 要点：档案工作人员经常回答研究者的咨询；能很快查到的简单信息，他们很可能直接告诉你，不用亲自跑一趟。

### E15

- 位置：Planning to Visit an Archives
- 原文："If the materials you are requesting are stored off-site, they may take several hours or days to retrieve."
- 要点：存放在馆外库房的材料，调取可能要几个小时甚至几天。

### E16

- 位置：Planning to Visit an Archives
- 原文："Check to see whether there are any limits on the amount of materials you may request or specific request times."
- 要点：事先查清一次能调阅多少、什么时间可以提交调阅申请。

### E17

- 位置：Planning to Visit an Archives
- 原文："Examine the reproduction policies of the archives. Regulations and fees for requesting photocopies, scans, digital photography, microfilming, and reproductions of photos and audio-visual materials vary among archives."
- 要点：事先查清复制规定；复印、扫描、拍照等的规定和费用，各馆不同。

### E18

- 位置：Planning to Visit an Archives
- 原文："Inquire whether any opportunities for research grants or funding are offered by the archives."
- 要点：问问档案馆有没有研究资助。

### E19

- 位置：Planning to Visit an Archives
- 原文："Schedule some additional time for the unexpected." / "Also, consider the option of a return visit to the archives in case you need to verify information, check additional materials, or pursue something you had not thought of earlier."
- 要点：给意外情况留出时间；考虑再去一次，用来核对、补查或追新线索。

### E20

- 位置：Typical Usage Guidelines in Archival Repositories，Registry and personal identification
- 原文："Some archives also require a note of recommendation or special permissions before admitting researchers."
- 要点：有些档案馆要求推荐信或特别许可才能进馆查阅。

### E21

- 位置：Typical Usage Guidelines in Archival Repositories，Use of pencil only
- 原文："This is a preservation practice in case accidental marks are made on archival materials; pencil can be erased while pen marks cannot."
- 要点：只许用铅笔，是为了万一在档案上留下痕迹还能擦掉。

### E22

- 位置：Typical Usage Guidelines in Archival Repositories，Careful handling and maintaining order
- 原文："It is also important that materials remain in the order in which the researcher received them so they can be located later and observed in their proper contexts."
- 要点：材料要保持拿到时的顺序，以后才能找到，也才能放在原来的上下文里看。

### E23

- 位置：Notes on Copyright, Restrictions, and Unprocessed Collections，Copyright
- 原文："In other words even if the archives physically owns a particular document, the copyright of the document and stipulations on how it can be used may be managed by another individual or institution."
- 要点：档案馆拥有一份文件的实物，不等于拥有它的版权；版权和使用条件可能归别人。

### E24

- 位置：Notes on Copyright, Restrictions, and Unprocessed Collections，Copyright
- 原文："It is the responsibility of researchers to find the copyright holder in order to publish or cite from the materials."
- 要点：要发表或引用这些材料，由研究者自己负责找到版权人（这里讲的是美国的情况）。

### E25

- 位置：Notes on Copyright, Restrictions, and Unprocessed Collections，Restrictions
- 原文："The donor who originally gave materials to the archives set a time limit or certain stipulations on how those materials could be used, generally due to privacy concerns or sensitive materials."
- 要点：限制查阅的一个原因是捐赠者设定了期限或使用条件，通常出于隐私或材料敏感。

### E26

- 位置：Notes on Copyright, Restrictions, and Unprocessed Collections，Restrictions
- 原文："U.S. Census records are restricted for seventy-two years before they become available to researchers to protect personal information."
- 要点：例如美国人口普查记录要封存 72 年才向研究者开放，以保护个人信息。

### E27

- 位置：Notes on Copyright, Restrictions, and Unprocessed Collections，Restrictions
- 原文："Researchers may, in some cases, gain access to restricted materials if they file a petition or request permission from the appropriate entity."
- 要点：有时可以向相关方申请，获准查阅受限材料。

### E28

- 位置：Notes on Copyright, Restrictions, and Unprocessed Collections，Unprocessed collections
- 原文："If you know of an unprocessed collection you want to use, talk to the archival staff as far ahead of your visit as possible and inquire about use policies."
- 要点：想用尚未整理的收藏，要尽早和工作人员联系，问清使用规定。

### E29

- 位置：Visiting an Archives，Prioritize your requests
- 原文："What are the materials that would be most helpful for you to view? Make plans to see those first and ask the archival staff for them promptly to ensure you have time to see them."
- 要点：先看最有用的材料，尽早提交申请，确保有时间看完。

### E30

- 位置：Visiting an Archives，Take thorough citations
- 原文："While you are working, make sure to take full citations for the materials you are viewing, including any unique identification assigned to the materials by the archives such as the call number, collection title, etc."
- 要点：边看边记完整的出处，包括档案馆给的唯一编号，例如索书号、收藏名称。

### E31

- 位置：Visiting an Archives，Take thorough citations
- 原文："If you need to go back and reference something in those materials again, or if another researcher is later trying to track your sources from a published work, this will help the archival staff locate the materials."
- 要点：完整出处让你自己回头能找到，也让读了你成果的研究者能追查到原件。

### E32

- 位置：Visiting an Archives，Point out corrections
- 原文："Mistakes or omissions sometimes occur in finding aids, websites, and descriptions of materials."
- 要点：检索指南、网站和材料描述有时有错误或遗漏。

### E33

- 位置：Appendix: Sample Annotated Finding Aid，Title Page 注释
- 原文："“Bulk dates” refers to the date range where most of the materials in the collection originate."
- 要点："主体年代"指收藏中大部分材料所属的年代范围。

### E34

- 位置：Appendix: Sample Annotated Finding Aid，Access and Use 注释及样例
- 原文："If there are any restrictions placed on an archival collection, those should be noted here. Other information in this section includes how the archives received the collection, and copyright and citation notes." / "Cite as: [item], folder, box, Corinth Chamber of Commerce records, Corinth Historical Society."
- 要点：检索指南的"查阅和使用"部分写明限制、来源、版权和建议的引用格式；样例的引用格式是"条目、文件夹、箱、收藏名、收藏机构"。

### E35

- 位置：Typical Usage Guidelines in Archival Repositories，Request forms
- 原文："Photocopies of unpublished materials provided for a researcher may require additional permissions before they are published."
- 要点：给研究者复印的未出版材料，发表前可能还需要另外取得许可。
