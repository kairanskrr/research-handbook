# 美国地质调查局 2023：Guidelines for Field-Measured Water-Quality Properties（《水质数据采集国家野外手册》A6.0 章）

- 资料：`usgs2023nfmfield`，[原文 PDF](https://pubs.usgs.gov/tm/09/a6.0/tm9a6.0.pdf)，https://doi.org/10.3133/tm9A6.0
- 版本：USGS Techniques and Methods, book 9, chapter A6.0，version 1.1（2023 年 7 月 17 日）。讲水温、溶解氧、电导率、pH 等现场指标怎样测、怎样记录。美国政府出版物，可免费阅读。位置写 PDF 页码（从封面算起）。

### E1

- 位置：§1.0 Introduction，PDF 第 9 页
- 原文："Determinations of physical or chemical properties that must be measured on site, as close as possible in time and space to the sampling of the medium."
- 要点：现场测量指的是必须在现场、在时间和空间上尽量贴近采样时测定的物理或化学性质。

### E2

- 位置：§2.1 Records，PDF 第 10 页
- 原文："Electronic and (or) paper records must be established and maintained for each uniquely identified sampling location to" / "permanently document field activities, measurement readings, instrument calibration, and any other information needed to meet programmatic or regulatory requirements"
- 要点：每个有唯一编号的采样点都要建电子或纸质记录，永久记下野外活动、读数、仪器校准等信息。

### E3

- 位置：§2.1 Records，PDF 第 10 页
- 原文："Much of the documentation process should be completed at the field site. Records should be checked independently by a field partner and (or) data administrator."
- 要点：大部分记录工作应在现场完成；记录要由野外同伴或数据管理员独立核对。

### E4

- 位置：§2.1 Records，PDF 第 10 页
- 原文："A separate paper or electronic log book must be maintained for each field instrument or replaceable sensor where instrument repair, maintenance, and calibration history information is recorded. The log book travels with the instrument."
- 要点：每台野外仪器或可更换的传感器都要有单独的日志，记录维修、保养和校准历史；日志跟着仪器走。

### E5

- 位置：§2.1 Records，PDF 第 10 页
- 原文："Use of a pencil is not acceptable" / "Erroneous or mistaken entries should be crossed out with one line and initialed."
- 要点：纸质记录不能用铅笔；写错的地方划一道线并签上姓名缩写，不要涂掉。

### E6

- 位置：§2.1 之后的仪器说明，PDF 第 12 页
- 原文："Make field measurements only with properly calibrated instruments. Instruments are required to be calibrated before each field trip and checks are required at field sites for most instruments."
- 要点：只用校准好的仪器测量；每次出野外前都要校准，多数仪器还要在现场检查。

### E7

- 位置：§2.1 之后的仪器说明，PDF 第 12 页
- 原文："If the instrument or measurement is new to you, practice your measurement technique with a mentor who has current experience and is up to date with USGS field-method protocol and procedures."
- 要点：第一次用某种仪器或做某种测量时，先跟一位有经验的人练习。

### E8

- 位置：Performance，PDF 第 12 页
- 原文："Check instrument precision and accuracy (variability and bias) periodically while at a field site"
- 要点：在现场要定期检查仪器的精密度和准确度（波动和偏差）。

### E9

- 位置：Performance，PDF 第 12 页
- 原文："or apply knowledge of the site and professional judgment to select the most representative of the final readings and document in field notes how the decision was made."
- 要点：读数不稳定时，如果凭对现场的了解从最后几次读数里挑一个最有代表性的，要在野外记录里写明是怎样决定的。
