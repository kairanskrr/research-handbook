# Cronon 等 2009：Learning to Do Historical Research: A Primer

- 资料：`cronon2009learning`，[原文](https://www.williamcronon.net/researching/)
- 版本：环境史学者 William Cronon（威斯康星大学麦迪逊分校）和他 2008 年秋季研究生讨论课的学生合写的网站，主要面向环境史，但写给所有想学历史研究的人。站点最后更新 2009 年 3 月，2026-10-06 读取。用到的页面：首页（index.htm）、What Are the Documents?（documents.htm）、Note-taking（notetaking.htm）、Manuscripts（manuscripts.htm）、Argument and Narrative（arguing.htm）。位置写页面名和小标题。

### E1

- 位置：首页，Finding the Documents 一段
- 原文："Unlike science, where one can set up a laboratory experiment or perform field investigations that describe the world as it exists right now, the past survives only as fragments that remain from a time that is no more. Studying history means collecting these fragments and figuring out what they mean."
- 要点：科学可以做实验、做现场观察；过去却已不存在，只剩下零碎的残片。研究历史就是收集这些残片，弄清它们的意思。

### E2

- 位置：首页，Finding the Documents 一段
- 原文："No matter what question you want to ask about the past, your second question should always be "What are the documents?""
- 要点：无论想问过去的什么问题，第二个问题都应该是"有哪些文献？"

### E3

- 位置：首页，Arguing and Narrating 一段
- 原文："No, your real goal is to build an argument and tell a story."
- 要点：历史研究的真正目标不是收集材料，而是建立论证、讲出故事。

### E4

- 位置：首页，Manuscripts 一段
- 原文："They exist as letters or diaries or other unique items that are held in collections called archives. [...] Historians call these unique unprinted documents manuscripts."
- 要点：有些最珍贵的历史文献从未印刷出版，它们是信件、日记等独一无二的东西，保存在档案馆的收藏里；历史学家把这些未印刷的文件叫作"手稿"（manuscripts）。

### E5

- 位置：What Are the Documents?，basic principles 列表
- 原文："While this may appear obvious to you, the corollary to this principle is that documents are always the products of a particular place and time."
- 要点：文献总是特定时间和地点的产物。

### E6

- 位置：What Are the Documents?，After the Sources: Rebuilding Your Questions
- 原文："But what this example does demonstrate is that even the most blatant lies in the documents can be interpreted to reveal important lessons about the past."
- 要点：哪怕文献里明显在撒谎（例子是一张把 1880 年代收割机画进 1863 年葛底斯堡战场的广告），经过解读也能揭示关于过去的重要信息。

### E7

- 位置：What Are the Documents?，Avoiding Common Errors: Confirmation Bias and Lack of Focus
- 原文："Researchers who work this way often fall victim to something called confirmation bias, which means that they notice only the information that confirms their preconceptions."
- 要点：只为支持自己观点而收集证据的人，容易陷入确认偏误：只看到符合预想的信息。

### E8

- 位置：What Are the Documents?，Avoiding Common Errors
- 原文："If you find convincing evidence that leads you away from your initial hypothesis, change your hypothesis."
- 要点：找到让你偏离最初假设的有力证据时，就改假设。

### E9

- 位置：What Are the Documents?，basic principles 列表
- 原文："The best relationship between questions and documents is flexible and reciprocal, not rigid and predetermined."
- 要点：问题和文献的关系应当灵活、互相塑造，而不是死板、预先定好。

### E10

- 位置：Note-taking
- 原文："Booth, Colomb, and Williams suggest that you’ll help yourself in the long run by recording complete bibliographical data even before you start taking notes."
- 要点：做笔记之前，先把完整的文献信息记下来。

### E11

- 位置：Note-taking
- 原文："You’ll want to include your ideas in your notes, but be careful to specify when you’re writing your own ideas and when you’re synthesizing the author’s."
- 要点：笔记里可以写自己的想法，但要标清哪些是自己的，哪些是在归纳作者的。

### E12

- 位置：Manuscripts，开头
- 原文："Manuscripts are the unpublished papers of an individual or an organization."
- 要点：手稿指个人或机构未出版的文件。

### E13

- 位置：Manuscripts，进入档案馆的五条提示
- 原文："Archivists have devised a special tool for navigating manuscript collections called the finding aid."
- 要点：档案管理员为手稿收藏编了一种导航工具，叫"检索指南"（finding aid）。

### E14

- 位置：Manuscripts，在档案里查找的习惯
- 原文："You can save valuable time, energy, and money by keeping a close record of the box and folder location of the materials you find. If you have not made a note of what you have seen, you may never find it again."
- 要点：把找到的材料所在的箱号、文件夹号仔细记下来；看过却没记的东西，可能再也找不到。

### E15

- 位置：Manuscripts，进入档案馆的五条提示
- 原文："Depending on your local repository’s policies, you can bring things like cameras and laptop computers. Make sure that you check on the specific policies of the repository you're using before you visit."
- 要点：能否带相机、笔记本电脑，取决于收藏机构的规定；去之前先查清。

### E16

- 位置：Manuscripts，在档案里查找的习惯
- 原文："Research questions change when you're inside an archive."
- 要点：进了档案馆，研究问题会变。

### E17

- 位置：Manuscripts，讲故事的部分
- 原文："Historians are storytellers."
- 要点：历史学家是讲故事的人；手稿能提供讲故事的细节。

### E18

- 位置：Argument and Narrative，开头
- 原文："We will first focus on arguments at their most basic level: a claim supported by evidence."
- 要点：论证最基本的形式：一个由证据支撑的主张。

### E19

- 位置：Argument and Narrative，评估一手资料的问题列表
- 原文："Who was the author’s audience? How might the intended audience affect what was or wasn’t included in the document?"
- 要点：评估一手资料要问：作者写给谁看？预设的读者会怎样影响文献写了什么、没写什么？

### E20

- 位置：Argument and Narrative，一手与二手资料
- 原文："For beginning researchers, however, it is easy to overlook primary documents because they typically take more effort to locate and interpret."
- 要点：新手容易忽视一手资料，因为它们更难找、更难解读。

### E21

- 位置：Argument and Narrative，评估二手资料
- 原文："Also examine your secondary sources to determine if the claims of the author are justified by the evidence provided."
- 要点：对二手资料也要检查：作者的主张是否被其给出的证据支持。

### E22

- 位置：Argument and Narrative，两位学者解读同一张钞票的例子
- 原文："Fiege and Mihm’s essays demonstrate how one can construct very different arguments when interpreting the same historical document."
- 要点：两位历史学家解读同一份文献（一张 1873 年的钞票），得出了很不一样的论证。

### E23

- 位置：首页，Positioning Your Argument 一段
- 原文："Doing this is called "positioning your argument," a skill that can dramatically enhance the significance of what you yourself write."
- 要点：弄清自己的观点和二手文献作者的观点是什么关系，叫"给论点定位"，能大大提升作品的意义。

### E24

- 位置：Argument and Narrative，评估一手资料的问题列表
- 原文："Is the document in fact contemporary to the period you are writing about?"
- 要点：评估一手资料要问：这份文献真的出自你研究的那个时期吗？

### E25

- 位置：Manuscripts，在档案里查找的习惯
- 原文："Even the highest-profile manuscript collection is less closely catalogued than any library."
- 要点：再有名的手稿收藏，编目也不如图书馆细。

### E26

- 位置：首页，Positioning Your Argument 一段
- 原文："Historians sometimes use the phrase "primary sources" to refer to the original documents that provide the building blocks for everything we know about the past. They use the phrase "secondary sources" to refer to the writings of scholars and other people who have used primary documents to interpret the past."
- 要点：一手资料指构成我们对过去一切认识的原始文献；二手资料指学者等人利用一手资料解释过去而写成的作品。

### E27

- 位置：Argument and Narrative，评估一手资料的问题列表
- 原文："What is the author’s background?  Might biases be present due to factors such as the author’s cultural background or socioeconomic status?"
- 要点：评估一手资料要问：作者是什么背景？文化背景、社会经济地位等会不会带来偏见？

### E28

- 位置：首页，Writing 一段
- 原文："By starting to write while you're still in the process of gathering documents, you will very likely find yourself discovering that you need additional documents that you hadn't recognized would be so important until you actually began to sketch your argument and tell your story."
- 要点：在收集文献的同时就开始写，很可能会发现还需要一些之前没意识到很重要的文献。

### E29

- 位置：Manuscripts，How to Find Manuscript Collections
- 原文："Archives sort personal papers under the names of individuals and organizations."
- 要点：档案馆按个人和机构的名字归档私人文件，所以要先找出相关的人和机构。

### E30

- 位置：Manuscripts，How to Find Manuscript Collections
- 原文："Identify dates. People live a long time. Organizations can outlive their members. Which years are most relevant to our question?"
- 要点：确定年代范围：人活得很久，机构比成员活得更久，要先想清楚哪几年和问题最相关。

### E31

- 位置：Manuscripts，How to Find Manuscript Collections
- 原文："An archive tends to collect material that aligns with its affiliation."
- 要点：档案馆倾向于收藏与自身归属相关的材料（例如参议员的文件常在其所在州的档案馆）。

### E32

- 位置：Manuscripts，Navigating the Reading Room，Plan ahead
- 原文："One of our boxes was on loan to another institution. Had we made the effort to call ahead, we could have ensured that the materials we wanted to see were available."
- 要点：作者到馆后才发现一个箱子借给了别的机构；如果事先打电话，就能确认材料在不在。

### E33

- 位置：Manuscripts，Navigating the Reading Room，Ask an archivist!
- 原文："One of the best resources you can find is the archivist who actually processed the collection you want to work with."
- 要点：最好的资源之一，是亲手整理过这个收藏的档案工作人员。

### E34

- 位置：Manuscripts，Ask Your Research Question and Lots of Others
- 原文："Take notice! For the third time we have moved away from a document that interests us because it doesn't seem to connect to our research question." / "Yet these materials raise lots of questions for us. We just haven't let ourselves ask them."
- 要点：作者发现自己第三次因为"和研究问题无关"而跳过引起兴趣的文件；其实这些材料引出了很多问题，只是他们没让自己去问。

### E35

- 位置：Manuscripts，Ask Your Research Question and Lots of Others
- 原文："Make sure you care about the question you go in with enough that you won't immediately abandon it after reading the first document. But don't be afraid to let your question transform itself in dialogue with the documents. Whatever you do, keep track of this thought process on paper. New questions or discarded ones could represent future projects."
- 要点：带着自己真正在意的问题进馆，不要看了第一份文件就放弃；但也不要怕问题在和文献的对话中改变。把思考过程写下来，新问题和放弃的问题都可能成为以后的课题。

### E36

- 位置：Manuscripts，Little Things Are Big
- 原文："For the most part, we have to learn to live with what we have. (Almost everything that's ever been written down has not ended up in an archive, remember.) Keep track of gaps and uncertainties, for future research may give you new clues."
- 要点：几乎所有写下来的东西都没有进入档案馆；只能接受现有的材料，同时记下缺口和不确定之处。

### E37

- 位置：Manuscripts，Little Things Are Big
- 原文："Lots of the correspondence in this collection has a second, third, and fourth life given to it by notes in the margins."
- 要点：很多信件因为页边批注而有了第二、第三、第四层意义。

### E38

- 位置：Manuscripts，Little Things Are Big
- 原文："We can't prove an argument with a question mark, but it might help us look at other evidence differently."
- 要点：一个问号证明不了论点，但能让你换个角度看其他证据。

### E39

- 位置：Manuscripts，Sniff Out Paper Trails
- 原文："Documents point you to the next destination."
- 要点：文献会把你引向下一个去处（别的收藏、别的档案馆）。

### E40

- 位置：Manuscripts，Sniff Out Paper Trails
- 原文："Time and money will constrain the breadth of your project, but these are the sorts of thoughts to be having to keep your research alive. Always think about the people and organizations you encounter in your secondary readings. Ask: Might these people and groups have left behind a paper trail?"
- 要点：时间和经费会限制项目范围；但要一直想：二手文献里出现的人和机构，有没有留下文字记录？

### E41

- 位置：Manuscripts，Dos and Don'ts
- 原文："Do make a list of your ideal sources, updating it as you encounter real documents."
- 要点：列一张"理想资料"清单，遇到真实文献后不断更新。

### E42

- 位置：Manuscripts，Dos and Don'ts
- 原文："Don't get discouraged if your imagined evidence doesn't live up to the real evidence you discover."
- 要点：真实找到的证据不如想象中的好，不要灰心。

### E43

- 位置：Manuscripts，Think Like A Narrator
- 原文："Beyond constructing narrative, it's a good idea to keep close track of dates. Not only does it give context to the items you find, but it also allows you to see unexpected connections."
- 要点：仔细记录日期，既给材料提供背景，也能看出意想不到的联系。

### E44

- 位置：Manuscripts，Think Like A Narrator
- 原文："This gives us a fuller picture of Nelson's life at the time that can be obscured by the fact his papers are broken into folders by topic."
- 要点：按日期把材料串起来，能看到按主题分装的文件夹所掩盖的全貌。

### E45

- 位置：Manuscripts，Dos and Don'ts
- 原文："Don't forget to put your evidence into dialogue with your research questions. This may be the most important part of the research process."
- 要点：让证据和研究问题对话，这可能是研究过程中最重要的部分。
