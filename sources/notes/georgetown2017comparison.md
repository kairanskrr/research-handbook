# Georgetown Law Writing Center 2017：How to Craft an Effective Case Comparison

- 资料：`georgetown2017comparison`，[原文 PDF（美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心，免费）](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/How-to-Craft-an-Effective-Case-Comparison.pdf)
- 版本：美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心讲义，Michel Djandji 2017 年编写。写给**美国法学院学生**。位置写讲义的小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："In the United States, like in any common law system, legal reasoning relies on the basic assumption that similar cases should be decided similarly."
- 要点：在美国以及任何普通法体系中，法律推理建立在"相似案件应相似处理"这一基本假设上。

### E2

- 位置：I. When to Use a Case Comparison
- 原文："Similar facts between the past case and the present case lead to a similar outcome (analogizing); dissimilar facts between the past case and the present case lead to a different outcome (distinguishing)."
- 要点：先前案件与当前案件事实相似，导向相似结果（类比）；事实不同，导向不同结果（区分）。

### E3

- 位置：II. Dissecting a Case Comparison
- 原文："A similarity or difference is legally significant if it relates to a term in the governing rule of law or a principle implicated by the rule or its application in this case."
- 要点：一个相似点或不同点，只有和所适用规则中的某个要件、或规则牵涉的原则有关时，才在法律上有意义。

### E4

- 位置：II，脚注 4
- 原文："For example, a writer cannot argue that because the precedential case has fact x and the current case does not have fact x, the outcome of the current case is not the outcome of the precedential case; this is mistaken negation."
- 要点：常见逻辑错误"错误否定"：不能因为先例有事实 x、本案没有 x，就推出本案结果与先例不同。

### E5

- 位置：III. What To Do with Unfavorable Facts
- 原文："These instances are called counterexamples, and a legal writer must be prepared to anticipate and rebut them."
- 要点：事实相近但结果相反的先例叫"反例"，写作者要预见并反驳它们。
