# Carnegie Mellon University EHS：Machine Shop Safety - Guideline

- 资料：`cmu2025machineshop`，[原文](https://www.cmu.edu/ehs/Workplace-Construction/documents/machine-shop-safety-policy.pdf)
- 版本：美国卡内基梅隆大学环境健康与安全办公室（EHS）机加工车间安全指南，第 15 版，2025 年 11 月修订。位置写章节编号。只代表这一所大学的规定。

### E1

- 位置：4.j（学生的责任）
- 原文："Before working on machine equipment, students must be trained and demonstrate competency to the shop manager, shop staff and/or qualified faculty."
- 要点：学生使用机器设备前，必须接受培训，并向车间管理人员或有资格的教师证明自己能胜任。

### E2

- 位置：5.a（培训）
- 原文："Have attended and successfully completed both: 1. The general shop safety course offered by EHS. 2. Specific shop and safety training for each type of machine they operate."
- 要点：要完成两种培训：学校的通用车间安全课，以及每一种要操作的机器的专门培训。

### E3

- 位置：7.a
- 原文："Always have a partner with you. At least two persons must be in the shop when machine equipment is being used"
- 要点：不能单独操作；使用机器设备时车间里至少要有两个人。

### E4

- 位置：7.b.iii
- 原文："Never wear hand protection when using tools with rotating elements or pinch points, such as drills and saws, due to the potential for getting fingers and hands caught in moving parts."
- 要点：使用钻床、锯这类带旋转部件或夹点的工具时不要戴手套，以免手指和手被卷入。

### E5

- 位置：7.c
- 原文："You must ensure that safety guards are in place on moving parts before you start working."
- 要点：开工前要确认运动部件上的防护罩已装好。

### E6

- 位置：7.o
- 原文："There are limits to what can be built in a given shop and in a given time. You can’t do it safety if you are in a hurry. If it cannot be done safely don’t start it."
- 要点：赶时间就做不安全；不能安全完成的工作就不要开始。

### E7

- 位置：7.t
- 原文："Ensure power is off, equipment is properly locked out, tagged and safety devices are in place."
- 要点：调整或维修机器前，确认已断电、上锁挂牌、安全装置就位。

### E8

- 位置：7.m
- 原文："Attend all running machines. Never leave a machine to run unattended."
- 要点：机器运转时要有人看着，不要让机器无人值守地运行。
