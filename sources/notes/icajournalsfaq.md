# 国际传播学会：Journals Frequently Asked Questions

- 资料：`icajournalsfaq`，[原文网页](https://www.icahdq.org/page/JournalsFAQ)
- 版本：国际传播学会（ICA）官网期刊常见问题页。官网对脚本返回人机验证页，读的是 Internet Archive 2025-09-12 的快照（web.archive.org/web/20250912130141/...）。位置写页面问题标题。

### E1

- 位置：How do I submit my research to an ICA journal?
- 原文："The International Communication Association publishes six major peer reviewed journals: Journal of Communication, Communication Theory, Human Communication Research, Communication, Culture & Critique, Journal of Computer-Mediated Communication, and The Annals of The International Communication Association."
- 要点：ICA 出版六本同行评审期刊。

### E2

- 位置：What style manual do ICA Journals follow?
- 原文："All submitted manuscripts must be prepared in strict accordance with the 7th edition of the Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association (APA)."
- 要点：ICA 期刊的稿件必须严格按照美国心理学会出版手册第 7 版（APA 格式）准备。
