# Errington 等人 2021：Challenges for assessing replicability in preclinical cancer biology

- 资料：`errington2021challenges`，[原文](https://elifesciences.org/articles/67995)（读取自 [Internet Archive 快照](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://elifesciences.org/articles/67995)）
- 版本：eLife 10: e67995, 2021，开放获取（CC BY），2026-10-06 读取。"癌症生物学可复现性项目"报告的复现过程中遇到的障碍。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："none of the 193 experiments were described in sufficient detail in the original paper to enable us to design protocols to repeat the experiments, so we had to seek clarifications from the original authors."
- 要点：计划复现的 193 个实验中，没有一个在原论文里写得足够详细，能直接据此设计复现方案，都要向原作者询问细节。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Third, once experimental work started, 67% of the peer-reviewed protocols required modifications to complete the research and just 41% of those modifications could be implemented."
- 要点：实验开始后，67% 已通过同行评审的方案还需要修改才能做完，而其中只有 41% 的修改能够实施。

### E3

- 位置：Challenges during experiments and peer review
- 原文："Sometimes just minor modifications were needed (e.g., changing antibody concentrations or blocking reagents to detect the protein of interest during a Western blot assay)."
- 要点：有时只需小改，例如在蛋白质印迹（Western blot）实验中调整抗体浓度或封闭试剂，才能检测到目标蛋白。

### E4

- 位置：The duration of the different phases in the project
- 原文："The gap between the start of experimental work and final data delivery was 90 weeks"
- 要点：在这个项目里，从开始做实验到交付最终数据，平均用了 90 周。

### E5

- 位置：Independent development of replication protocols
- 原文："We coded if we requested a key reagent (i.e., cell lines, plasmids, model organisms, antibodies) that was not available commercially or in a repository. We requested key reagents for 136 of the 193 experiments (70%)"
- 要点：193 个实验中有 136 个（70%）需要向原作者索要买不到、也不在资源库里的关键试剂（细胞系、质粒、模式生物、抗体）。

### E6

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："the data needed to compute effect sizes and conduct power analyses was publicly accessible for just 4 of 193 experiments. Moreover, despite contacting the authors of the original papers, we were unable to obtain these data for 68% of the experiments."
- 要点：193 个实验中，只有 4 个公开了计算效应量所需的数据；联系原作者后，仍有 68% 的实验拿不到这些数据。

### E7

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："None of the 193 experiments was described completely enough to design a replication protocol without requesting clarifying details from the original authors."
- 要点：项目计划复现的 193 个实验，没有一个描述得足够完整，能不问原作者就设计出复现方案。

### E8

- 位置：Requesting assistance from original authors
- 原文："For example, a request for specific protocol details was responded with ‘a standard procedure was used.’"
- 要点：例如，项目向原作者询问具体的实验细节，得到的回答只是"用的是标准方法"。
