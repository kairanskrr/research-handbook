# Brannon 2023：Statutory Interpretation: Theories, Tools, and Trends（美国国会研究服务部报告 R45153）

- 资料：`brannon2023statutory`，[原文（EveryCRSReport.com 公开转载的报告全文）](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R45153.html)；官方页面 https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R45153（脚本访问被拒，用公开转载版读取）
- 版本：美国国会研究服务部（Congressional Research Service）报告 R45153，作者 Valerie C. Brannon（立法律师），2018 年首发，读到的是 2023 年 3 月 10 日更新版。讲的是**美国联邦法院**怎样解释国会立法。美国国会研究服务部报告属联邦政府作品，可免费阅读。位置写报告的部分标题。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Proponents of the two main theories of statutory interpretation—purposivism and textualism—disagree about how judges can best adhere to this ideal of legislative supremacy. While purposivists argue that courts should prioritize interpretations that advance the statute's purpose, textualists maintain that judges should primarily confine their focus to the statute's text."
- 要点：美国制定法解释的两大理论：目的主义（purposivism）主张优先采用推进立法目的的解释；文本主义（textualism）主张法官主要关注条文文本。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Most frequently, judges rely on five types of interpretive tools. Although both purposivists and textualists may use any of these five tools, a judge's theory of statutory interpretation may influence the order in which these tools are applied and how much weight is given to each tool."
- 要点：法官最常用五类解释工具；两派都可能用任何一种，但解释理论会影响使用顺序和每种工具的分量。

### E3

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："First, judges often begin by looking to the ordinary meaning of the statutory text" / "Second, judges interpret specific provisions by looking to their broader statutory context" / "Third, judges may turn to the canons of construction , which are presumptions about how courts ordinarily read statutes." / "Fourth, judges may look to a statute's legislative history , or the record of Congress's deliberations when enacting a law." / "Finally, a judge might consider statutory implementation : the way a law has been applied in the past, or might be applied in the future."
- 要点：五类工具：文字的通常含义；更广的法律上下文和结构；解释准则（法院通常怎样读法条的推定）；立法史（国会制定法律时的审议记录）；法律的实施（过去怎样适用、将来可能怎样适用）。

### E4

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Nonetheless, judges may disagree about what a word's ordinary meaning is, or whether a particular statutory term may instead be a term of art"
- 要点：法官可能对一个词的通常含义有分歧，或对某个词是否是有特定含义的专门术语有分歧。

### E5

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Some judges have suggested certain forms of legislative history are more reliable than others, with committee reports providing stronger evidence of Congress's understanding of the law than individual floor statements from non-sponsors, for example."
- 要点：一些法官认为不同立法史材料可靠程度不同，例如委员会报告比非提案议员的个人发言更能证明国会的理解。

### E6

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Even a statutory provision that at first appears unambiguous can engender significant difficulties when applied in the real world."
- 要点：初看毫无歧义的条文，用到现实中也可能产生很大困难。

### E7

- 位置：Canons of Construction — Justifications: Disrepute, Rehabilitation, and Empirical Studies
- 原文："He compiled a table of “thrusts” and “parries” that purported to demonstrate that for every canon, there was an opposing canon on the same point." / "parries are truly contradictory, arguing that many of his pairs instead represent two halves of one rule, the thrust giving the general rule, and the parry, the exception or condition."
- 要点：美国学者 Llewellyn 曾列表说明每条解释准则都有一条相反的准则；也有学者和法官质疑这些对子是否真的矛盾，认为很多是一条规则的两半：一般规则和例外。
