# O'Dea 等人 2021：Preferred reporting items for systematic reviews and meta-analyses in ecology and evolutionary biology: a PRISMA extension

- 资料：`odea2021preferred`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1111/brv.12721)
- 版本：Biological Reviews 96(5): 1695–1722，2021，CC BY-NC 4.0 开放获取。原文文本取自 Europe PMC（PMC8518748）。这是 PRISMA 报告指南面向生态学和进化生物学的扩展版 PRISMA-EcoEvo 1.0。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Because of these risks, meta‐analyses are increasingly conducted as part of systematic reviews, which use structured, transparent, and reproducible methods to collate and summarise evidence."
- 要点：因为元分析有这些风险，它越来越多地作为系统综述的一部分来做；系统综述用结构化、透明、可重复的方法收集和汇总证据。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："For readers to determine whether the conclusions from a systematic review or meta‐analysis should be trusted – and to be able to build upon the review – authors need to report what they did, why they did it, and what they found."
- 要点：读者要判断系统综述或元分析的结论能不能信、能不能在它的基础上继续做，作者就要报告做了什么、为什么这样做、发现了什么。

### E3

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："we surveyed 208 researchers with relevant experience (as authors, reviewers, or editors), and conducted detailed evaluations of 102 systematic review and meta‐analysis papers published between 2010 and 2019. Reporting quality was far below optimal and approximately normally distributed."
- 要点：作者调查了 208 位有相关经验的研究者，详细评估了 2010–2019 年发表的 102 篇系统综述和元分析，发现报告质量远不理想。

### E4

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："The minority of assessed papers that referenced a guideline (~16%) showed substantially higher reporting quality than average"
- 要点：被评估的论文里，引用了报告指南的少数论文（约 16%）报告质量明显高于平均水平。

### E5

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："PRISMA‐EcoEvo is a checklist of 27 main items that, when applicable, should be reported in systematic review and meta‐analysis publications summarising primary research in ecology and evolutionary biology."
- 要点：PRISMA-EcoEvo 是一份有 27 个主要条目的清单，生态学和进化生物学里汇总原始研究的系统综述和元分析，适用时都应报告这些条目。

### E6

- 位置：Table 1，Review registration，3.1
- 原文："Register review aims, hypotheses (if applicable), and methods in a time‐stamped and publicly accessible archive and provide a link to the registration in the methods section of the manuscript. Ideally registration occurs before the search, but it can be done at any stage before data analysis"
- 要点：把综述的目标、假设（如有）和方法登记在带时间戳、公开可查的存档里，并在方法部分给出链接；最好在检索前登记，最晚也要在数据分析前。
