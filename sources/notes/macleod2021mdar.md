# Macleod 等人 2021：The MDAR (Materials Design Analysis Reporting) Framework for transparent reporting in the life sciences

- 资料：`macleod2021mdar`，[原文（PubMed Central）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8092464/)，DOI [10.1073/pnas.2103238118](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2103238118)；配套的 [MDAR Framework](https://osf.io/xfpn4/)、[Checklist](https://osf.io/bj3mu/)、[Elaboration 文件](https://osf.io/xzy4s/) 存放在开放科学框架（OSF）上
- 版本：PNAS 118(17):e2103238118，2021 年，开放获取（CC BY 4.0），2026-10-08 读取。作者是多家出版机构（Nature Portfolio、Cell Press、Science、PLOS、eLife、Wiley）的编辑和开放科学中心（Center for Open Science，美国）的人员。Elaboration 文件是 2020 年的 V4 版。位置写小标题或 Elaboration 的节号。

### E1

- 位置：正文，Devising a Harmonized Reporting Framework
- 原文："The scope of the MDAR Framework was the life sciences, and within this our project had a strong focus on information that is relevant for exploratory laboratory research" / "The MDAR Framework is not comprehensive and should not supersede any existing reporting standards."
- 要点：MDAR 的范围是生命科学，重点放在探索性实验室研究需要报告的信息上；它不求全面，也不取代已有的专门报告标准。

### E2

- 位置：正文，开头和 Competing interest statement
- 原文："Our MDAR working group came together in late 2017 with the intention to develop a policy framework to promote transparent reporting" / "Several members of the working group are employed by publishing organizations and they acknowledge that their participation does not commit their organization to endorse the MDAR Framework."
- 要点：MDAR 工作组 2017 年底组建，目的是制定一套促进透明报告的期刊政策框架；成员多在出版机构工作，但参与不代表其机构一定采用 MDAR。

### E3

- 位置：Elaboration §2.4 Sample definition and replicates
- 原文："Number of times the experiment was replicated in the laboratory: when describing the results of an experiment, state how many times the experiment was independently performed in the laboratory."
- 要点：报告实验结果时，写明这个实验在实验室里独立做了几次。

### E4

- 位置：Elaboration §2.4
- 原文："define how many replicates were performed and whether they are biological (derived from different experimental units or subjects) or technical (multiple contemporary measurements from the same experimental unit or subject) replicates."
- 要点：写明做了几个重复，以及它们是生物学重复（来自不同的实验单位）还是技术重复（同一实验单位同时测多次）。

### E5

- 位置：Elaboration §2.4，Best practice
- 原文："Samples must be unambiguously described including a clear definition of the unit of study. In studies using model organisms, cell lines, primary cell cultures, plants or microbes, the unit of study is the smallest object that could be randomly and independently assigned to an intervention."
- 要点：要明确定义研究单位。用模式生物、细胞系、原代细胞、植物或微生物的研究里，研究单位是能被随机、独立地分到某个处理的最小对象。

### E6

- 位置：Elaboration §2.4，Best practice
- 原文："The groups being compared, including control groups should be clearly defined. If no control group has been used, the rationale for this should be stated."
- 要点：要清楚定义比较的各组，包括对照组；没有设对照组的，要说明理由。

### E7

- 位置：Elaboration §3.2 Statistics，Small sample sizes
- 原文："When technical replicates are reported, error and significance measures reflect the experimental variability, not the variability of the biological process; it is misleading not to state this clearly."
- 要点：如果报告的是技术重复，误差和显著性反映的是实验操作的波动，不是生物过程本身的波动；不说清楚这一点会误导读者。

### E8

- 位置：Elaboration §2.3（列在"活体动物研究"的要求下），Randomization
- 原文："Whether samples are randomly assigned to experimental groups, to processing order, or to positions in a multiwell device may influence experimental outcome. Ideally, data should be collected randomly or the samples appropriately blocked."
- 要点：样品是否随机分到各组、随机安排处理顺序、随机放在多孔板的位置，都可能影响结果；理想情况下随机收集数据，或者对样品合理分区组。

### E9

- 位置：Elaboration §2.3，Inclusion/exclusion criteria
- 原文："Investigators should define the criteria for identifying and dealing with outliers before running the experiments to prevent confirmatory bias"
- 要点：在做实验之前就定好怎样识别和处理离群值，防止只留下支持预期的数据。

### E10

- 位置：Elaboration §1.4 Cell materials（Cell lines），Best practice
- 原文："researchers should check their cell lines stocks against the list of commonly misidentified cell lines maintained by the International Cell Line Authentication Committee" / "Best practice requires that cell lines from the ICLAC Register of Misidentified Cell Lines are not used and that all other cell lines are routinely authenticated."
- 要点：研究者应当把手上的细胞系和国际细胞系鉴定委员会（ICLAC）维护的常见错认细胞系名单对照；最佳做法是不用名单上的细胞系，其他细胞系定期做鉴定。
