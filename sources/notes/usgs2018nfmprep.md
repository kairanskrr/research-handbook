# 美国地质调查局 2018：Preparations for Water Sampling（《水质数据采集国家野外手册》A1 章）

- 资料：`usgs2018nfmprep`，[原文 PDF](https://pubs.usgs.gov/tm/09/a1/tm9a1.pdf)，https://doi.org/10.3133/tm9A1
- 版本：美国地质调查局（United States Geological Survey，USGS）Techniques and Methods, book 9, chapter A1，2018 年 11 月修订版。这是 USGS 给本局水质野外人员用的官方规程，美国政府出版物，可免费阅读。位置写 PDF 页码（从封面算起）。

### E1

- 位置：§1.1，PDF 第 11 页
- 原文："Fundamental to water-quality sampling is the fact that the analytical results can be no better than the sample on which the analysis was performed."
- 要点：分析结果的好坏，不可能超过被分析的那个样品本身。

### E2

- 位置：§2.1 Site Access and Permission to Collect Data，PDF 第 14 页
- 原文："USGS policy specifies that permission must be secured before entering public or private property to select sampling sites, install wells, or collect samples" / "The USGS preferred business practice is to obtain permission in writing; however, oral permission may be used if it is documented by a USGS representative."
- 要点：USGS 规定，进入公有或私有土地选点、打井或采样之前，必须先取得许可；最好是书面许可，口头许可要由 USGS 人员记录下来。

### E3

- 位置：§2.2，PDF 第 14 页
- 原文："In most bodies of flowing or still water, a single sampling site or point does not adequately represent the entire stream segment with respect to physical properties, chemical constituents, or biological communities."
- 要点：在大多数流动或静止的水体里，一个采样点不足以代表整段河流的物理性质、化学成分或生物群落。

### E4

- 位置：§2.2，PDF 第 14 页
- 原文："Verify that sampling transects can be accessed during all typical and extreme flow and weather conditions."
- 要点：选点时要确认，在一般和极端的水流、天气条件下都能到达采样断面。

### E5

- 位置：§2.0 Site Selection，PDF 第 13 页
- 原文："Considerations for site selection include the appropriate quantity and locations of sampling sites, data-quality objectives, types of data and samples to be collected, site accessibility, safety, and project budget."
- 要点：选点要考虑：采样点的数量和位置、数据质量目标、要采的数据和样品类型、能否到达、安全和预算。

### E6

- 位置：§2.2.1，PDF 第 15 页
- 原文："Note that the field-measurement profile is a useful guideline, but might not be representative of chemical homogeneity for the analytes of interest; therefore, this assumption should be tested during the early phases of data collection."
- 要点：先在断面上测一轮现场指标（温度、电导率等）是有用的参考，但不一定代表目标物质也混合均匀；这个假设要在采样初期检验。

### E7

- 位置：§2.2.1，PDF 第 15 页
- 原文："CAUTION: Any stream, including an ephemeral or intermittent stream, can rapidly become too deep or too swift to wade safely."
- 要点：任何河流，包括季节性、间歇性的小河，都可能迅速变得太深太急，无法安全涉水。

### E8

- 位置：§3.0，PDF 第 18 页
- 原文："Planning for data management starts before water-quality samples are collected."
- 要点：数据管理的计划在采样之前就要开始。

### E9

- 位置：§3.0，PDF 第 18 页
- 原文："Original records are defined as those data, metadata, and other information that are initially recorded in the field or laboratory." / "Regardless of whether the original records are paper or electronic, they must be archived"
- 要点：原始记录指最初在野外或实验室记下的数据、元数据和其他信息；不论纸质还是电子的，都必须存档。

### E10

- 位置：§4.2 Quality-Assurance Plan，PDF 第 25 页
- 原文："Blanks should be collected before the onset of environmental sampling to identify potential bias issues early in the project, and then on a regular schedule during field sampling."
- 要点：空白样要在正式采样开始前就采，以便早发现偏差问题；之后在野外采样期间定期采。

### E11

- 位置：§4.2 Quality-Assurance Plan，PDF 第 25 页
- 原文："Replicates of environmental samples are collected to estimate natural variability in the aquatic system as well as variability in sample collection, sample processing, and chemical analysis."
- 要点：采平行样，是为了估计水体本身的自然变化，以及采样、样品处理和化学分析带来的变化。

### E12

- 位置：表 1-4（续），PDF 第 27 页
- 原文："Calibrate meters and multiparameter sondes prior to sample trip to test correct operation and function."
- 要点：出发采样前校准仪表和多参数探头，检验它们工作正常。

### E13

- 位置：表 1-4，PDF 第 26 页
- 原文："Plan to have at least one experienced and trained field person on the field team at all times."
- 要点：野外小组里任何时候都要至少有一名有经验、受过训练的人。

### E14

- 位置：§5.1.1 Job Hazard Analysis，PDF 第 28 页
- 原文："A JHA is a technique that focuses on job tasks as a means to identify hazards before they occur."
- 要点：作业危害分析（Job Hazard Analysis）按一项项具体工作，在危险发生之前把它们找出来。

### E15

- 位置：表 1-5，PDF 第 28 页
- 原文："Implement an office call-in procedure every night to ensure someone knows everything is okay. Ensure someone will begin rescue efforts if the required call is not received."
- 要点：每晚向办公室报平安；约定好没接到电话就有人启动救援。

### E16

- 位置：§5.2，PDF 第 31 页
- 原文："The sketch map should contain enough detail so that the site can be found again by a person who has never visited it."
- 要点：现场草图要详细到一个从没去过的人也能凭它再找到这个点。

### E17

- 位置：§4.0 The Project Work Plan，PDF 第 24 页
- 原文："Among the many components of a USGS project work plan are (1) scope and timeline with budget; (2) SOPs for all planned data-collection activities and technical methods that will be implemented; (3) QAP to assess and document the quality of the data; (4) Data-Management Plan for collection, review, and approval of the data products; and (5) plans for analyzing and publishing the data."
- 要点：USGS 的项目工作计划包括：范围、时间表和预算；各项采集工作的标准操作程序；质量保证计划；数据管理计划；分析和发表计划。

### E18

- 位置：表 1-4（续），PDF 第 27 页
- 原文："Prepare bottle labels with site information, sample types, medium codes, and so forth."
- 要点：出发前准备好样品瓶标签，写上站点信息、样品类型、介质代码等。
