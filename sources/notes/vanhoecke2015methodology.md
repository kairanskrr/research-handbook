# Van Hoecke 2015：Methodology of Comparative Legal Research

- 资料：`vanhoecke2015methodology`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.5553/REM/.000010)
- 版本：Law and Method（荷兰 Boom 出版社的开放获取法学方法期刊）2015 年 12 月，出版社网页全文（标注 open access），2026-10-08 读取。作者时任英国伦敦玛丽女王大学比较法教授、比利时根特大学法理学与比较法教授。位置写文章小节号。文章讨论的是学术性的比较法研究，主要比较各国国内法律体系。

### E1

- 位置：§4 How does one compare?
- 原文："On the basis of these writings, we may distinguish six different methods for comparative research: the functional method, the structural method, the analytical method, the law-in-context method, the historical method and the common-core method."
- 要点：作者归纳出比较法研究的六种方法：功能法、结构法、分析法、语境法、历史法、共同核心法。

### E2

- 位置：§7 Conclusion
- 原文："It has to be repeated: the choice of method or level for comparison will mainly depend on the research question(s) guiding the research project."
- 要点：选用哪种比较方法、在哪个层面比较，主要取决于研究问题。

### E3

- 位置：§2 Choice of legal systems to be compared
- 原文："The wording of the research question will be the main criterion for the choice of legal systems to be compared."
- 要点：选哪些法律体系来比较，主要看研究问题怎么问。

### E4

- 位置：§2 Choice of legal systems to be compared
- 原文："Even when one’s knowledge of languages will be the prime reason for choosing or eliminating some country, the researcher should be able to give good reasons why her choice is acceptable from a scholarly point of view."
- 要点：即使主要因为会哪种语言而选某个国家，也要给出学术上站得住的理由。

### E5

- 位置：§2 Choice of legal systems to be compared
- 原文："for a thorough comparative research a good reading knowledge of the local language" / "is an absolute requirement" / "it is highly recommended to check whether one’s own interpretation is correct and whether there have been any recent changes in that law, by contacting a local scholar who speaks English or another language the researcher masters."
- 要点：深入的比较研究必须能读当地语言；只能读英文资料时，强烈建议联系当地学者，核对自己的理解是否正确、那里的法律最近有没有改。

### E6

- 位置：§3 What has to be compared?
- 原文："In all cases, however, comparison should never stop at the level of legislation, and even not at the level of case law, as the social reality may be more different than similar rules suggest (and sometimes more similar than different rules would suggest)."
- 要点：比较不应停在立法层面，甚至不应停在判例层面，因为社会现实可能比规则显示的更不同（有时更相同）。

### E7

- 位置：§6 Tertium comparationis
- 原文："We should not look at a foreign legal system with the eyes and doctrinal framework of our own legal system, but try to transcend it, by using external ‘neutral’ elements for comparing legal systems"
- 要点：不要用本国法律体系的眼光和学理框架看外国法，要设法超越它，用外部的"中立"要素来比较。

### E8

- 位置：§7 Conclusion
- 原文："Hence, just copying foreign law could hardly be considered to be a ‘method’. It is rather the typical example of lack of method in comparative law."
- 要点：照搬外国法算不上方法，反而是比较法缺乏方法的典型。

### E9

- 位置：§4.5 The historical method
- 原文："Fully understanding the law as it functions today in some society, is only possible when one knows where it comes from and why it is as it is today."
- 要点：要完全理解一个社会今天的法律怎样运作，必须知道它从哪里来、为什么是现在这样。

### E10

- 位置：§4 How does one compare?
- 原文："Comparative researchers should become professionals rather than be tourists."
- 要点：比较法研究者应当做专业人士，而不是只看到"那边不一样"的游客。

### E11

- 位置：§7 Conclusion
- 原文："The functional method is looking at the actual societal problem (e.g., a train accident) and the way this is solved in different jurisdictions"
- 要点：功能法看的是一个实际的社会问题（例如火车事故），以及不同法域怎样解决它。

### E12

- 位置：§5.3 Law in action vs law in the books
- 原文："in any substantive research in comparative law, both legislation and case law will have to be studied, as, in all legal systems, common law and civil law alike, they are both of decisive importance for knowing the law."
- 要点：任何实质性的比较法研究都要同时研究立法和判例，因为在所有法律体系里，无论普通法还是大陆法系，二者对弄清法律都有决定性作用。
