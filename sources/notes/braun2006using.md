# Braun & Clarke 2006：Using thematic analysis in psychology

- 资料：`braun2006using`，[作者存档版本（UWE 机构库）](https://uwe-repository.worktribe.com/output/1043060)
- 版本：Qualitative Research in Psychology 3(2), 77–101；本次读取的是 UWE 机构库中的作者修订稿（经 Internet Archive 存档的 `eprints.uwe.ac.uk/11735/2/thematic_analysis_revised_-_final.pdf`）。页码以该稿为准。

### E1

- 位置：What is thematic analysis?
- 原文："Thematic analysis is a method for identifying, analysing, and reporting patterns (themes) within data."
- 要点：主题分析是在数据中识别、分析和报告模式（主题）的方法。

### E2

- 位置：Inductive vs theoretical thematic analysis
- 原文："An inductive approach means the themes identified are strongly linked to the data themselves" / "researchers cannot free themselves of their theoretical and epistemological commitments, and data are not coded in an epistemological vacuum."
- 要点：归纳式分析中，主题与数据本身紧密相连；但研究者无法摆脱自己的理论和认识论立场，编码不是在真空中进行的。

### E3

- 位置：Inductive vs theoretical thematic analysis
- 原文："This form of thematic analysis tends to provide less a rich description of the data overall, and more a detailed analysis of some aspect of the data."
- 要点：理论驱动（演绎式）的主题分析对数据整体的描述较少，而是对数据的某个方面做细致分析。

### E4

- 位置：What counts as a theme?
- 原文："Ideally there will be a number of instances of the theme across the data set, but more instances do not necessarily mean the theme itself is more crucial." / "So researcher judgement is necessary to determine what a theme is."
- 要点：一个主题最好在数据中多次出现，但出现次数多不等于更重要；什么算主题需要研究者判断，没有固定比例。

### E5

- 位置：Phase 1，Transcription of verbal data
- 原文："recognised as an interpretative act, where meanings are created, rather than simply a mechanical one of putting spoken sounds on paper"
- 要点：转录是一种诠释行为，会产生意义，而不只是把声音机械地写到纸上。

### E6

- 位置：Phase 1，Transcription of verbal data
- 原文："If your data have already been, or will be, transcribed for you, it is important that you spend more time familiarising yourself with the data, and also check the transcripts back against the original audio recordings for" [...] "(as should always be done)."
- 要点：如果转录是别人替你做的，你要花更多时间熟悉数据，并对照原始录音检查转录稿；这一步无论如何都应该做。

### E7

- 位置：Phase 1: familiarising yourself with your data
- 原文："During this phase, it is a good idea to start taking notes or marking ideas for coding that you will then go back to in subsequent phases."
- 要点：熟悉数据阶段就开始记笔记、标出编码想法，供后续阶段回看。

### E8

- 位置：Table 1: Phases of Thematic Analysis
- 原文："Familiarising yourself with your data: 2. Generating initial codes:" / "3. Searching for themes: 4. Reviewing themes:" / "5. Defining and naming themes:" / "6. Producing the report:"
- 要点：主题分析分六个阶段：熟悉数据、生成初始编码、寻找主题、复查主题、定义和命名主题、撰写报告。

### E9

- 位置：Phase 2: generating initial codes
- 原文："However, your coded data differs from the units of analysis (your themes) which are (often) broader."
- 要点：编码和主题不同：主题通常更宽，是分析的单位。

### E10

- 位置：Phase 2: generating initial codes
- 原文："It is important to retain accounts which depart from the dominant story in the analysis, so do not ignore these in your coding."
- 要点：要保留偏离主流说法的叙述，编码时不要忽略它们。

### E11

- 位置：Phase 4: reviewing themes
- 原文："Data within themes should cohere together meaningfully, while there should be clear and identifiable distinctions between themes."
- 要点：同一主题内的数据要有意义地聚在一起，不同主题之间要有清楚的区别。

### E12

- 位置：Phase 5: defining and naming themes
- 原文："One test for this is to see whether you can describe the scope and content of each theme in a couple of sentences."
- 要点：检验主题是否定义清楚：能否用几句话说清每个主题的范围和内容。

### E13

- 位置：Phase 6: producing the report
- 原文："Choose particularly vivid examples, or extracts which capture the essence of the point you are demonstrating, without unnecessary complexity." / "Extracts need to be embedded within an analytic narrative that compelling illustrates the story that you are telling about your data"
- 要点：选生动、能抓住要点的引文；引文要嵌在分析叙述之中。

### E14

- 位置：Potential pitfalls
- 原文："Thematic analysis is not just a collection of extracts strung together with little or no analytic narrative."
- 要点：主题分析不是把引文串起来、几乎不加分析。

### E15

- 位置：Potential pitfalls
- 原文："A second, associated, pitfall is the using of the data collection questions (such as from an interview schedule) as the" [...] "In such a case, no analytic work has been done to identify themes across the entire data set, or make sense of the patterning of responses."
- 要点：常见陷阱：把访谈提纲里的问题直接当作主题报告，这等于没有做分析。

### E16

- 位置：Potential pitfalls
- 原文："A weak or unconvincing analysis can also stem from a failure to provide adequate examples from the data" / "it is important not to misrepresent them as an overarching theme."
- 要点：分析不令人信服的一个原因是数据例子不够；少数个例可以有意思，但不要把它们说成贯穿全体的主题。

### E17

- 位置：Potential pitfalls
- 原文："A weak analysis does not appear to consider other obvious alternative readings of the data, or fails to consider variation (and even contradiction) in the account that is produced."
- 要点：弱的分析不考虑数据的其他明显解读，也不考虑叙述中的差异和矛盾。

### E18

- 位置：What is thematic analysis?
- 原文："it denies the active role the researcher always plays in identifying patterns/themes, selecting which are of interest, and reporting them to the readers"
- 要点：说主题从数据中"浮现"，否认了研究者在识别、选择和报告主题中始终起的主动作用。

### E19

- 位置：What makes good thematic analysis?
- 原文："what you say you are doing needs to match up with what you actually do."
- 要点：你说你做了什么，必须和你实际做的一致。
