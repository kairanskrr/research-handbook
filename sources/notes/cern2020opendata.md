# CERN 2020：CERN Open Data Policy for the LHC Experiments

- 资料：`cern2020opendata`，[原文](https://opendata.cern.ch/docs/cern-open-data-policy-for-lhc-experiments)
- 版本：CERN 开放数据政策工作组，2020-12-11 发布于 CERN Open Data Portal。

### E1

- 位置：Published Results (Level 1) Policy
- 原文："Peer-reviewed publications represent the primary scientific output from the experiments. In compliance with the CERN Open Access Policy, all such publications are available with Open Access"
- 要点：同行评审论文是实验的主要成果，按 CERN 开放获取政策全部免费公开。

### E2

- 位置：Published Results (Level 1) Policy
- 原文："the experiments will make public additional information and data at the time of publication, stored in collaboration with portals such as HEPData"
- 要点：发表论文时，实验组会同时公开补充信息和数据，存放在 HEPData 等平台上。

### E3

- 位置：Reconstructed Data (Level 3) Policy
- 原文："with the aim to commence data releases within five years of the conclusion of the run period."
- 要点：重建后的数据，目标是在运行期结束后五年内开始公开。

### E4

- 位置：Reconstructed Data (Level 3) Policy
- 原文："External authors should be aware that they will not have access to the vast amount of tacit knowledge built up within the LHC collaborations"
- 要点：合作组外的人用开放数据时要知道：合作组内部多年积累的大量隐性知识，他们拿不到。

### E5

- 位置：Reconstructed Data (Level 3) Policy
- 原文："Any scientific claims in such publications are the responsibility of their authors and not of the experiments."
- 要点：用开放数据发表的论文，其中的科学结论由作者自己负责，不代表实验组。

### E6

- 位置：Raw Data (Level 4) Policy
- 原文："It is not practically possible to make the full raw data-set from the LHC experiments usable in a meaningful way outside the collaborations."
- 要点：LHC 的全部原始数据在合作组外实际上没法有意义地使用。
