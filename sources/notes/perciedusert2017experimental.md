# Percie du Sert 等人 2017：The Experimental Design Assistant

- 资料：`perciedusert2017experimental`，[原文](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.2003779)
- 版本：PLoS Biology 15(9): e2003779, 2017，开放获取，2026-10-06 读取。介绍英国 NC3Rs 的免费在线实验设计工具。作者都参与了该工具的开发。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："The EDA (https://eda.nc3rs.org.uk) is a web-based application with an integrated website, which guides researchers through the process of designing animal experiments; the output includes a diagram that improves the transparency of the experimental plan. The resource is freely available"
- 要点：实验设计助手（Experimental Design Assistant，EDA）是一个网页应用，一步步引导研究者设计动物实验，输出一张让实验计划更透明的图；免费使用。

### E2

- 位置：The EDA improves the reliability of experimental results and analysis
- 原文："The system generates a randomisation sequence for the experiment, which takes into account any blocking factors included in the design and provides dedicated functionalities, such as support for blinding and sample size calculation"
- 要点：它能生成考虑了区组因素的随机序列，并支持盲法安排和样本量计算。

### E3

- 位置：The EDA improves the reliability of experimental results and analysis
- 原文："For example, it helps researchers to identify variables that could confound the outcome and provides advice on how to include them in the randomisation and the statistical analysis."
- 要点：它帮研究者找出可能混杂结果的变量，并建议怎样把它们纳入随机和统计分析。

### E4

- 位置：The EDA improves the reliability of experimental results and analysis
- 原文："Button, Ioannidis and colleagues estimated the average power in neuroscience animal studies to be around 20%. This constitutes a high risk of missing a genuine effect (a false negative), because only 1 in 5 experiments would have a chance of detecting an effect of the magnitude reported."
- 要点：文中引用 Button、Ioannidis 等人的估计：神经科学动物研究的平均统计功效约 20%，即只有五分之一的实验有机会测出所报告大小的效应。

### E5

- 位置：The EDA enables an effective assessment of the experimental plans
- 原文："The system is not designed to replace specialist statistical advice but can facilitate it."
- 要点：这个工具不能代替统计专家的建议，但能帮你更好地向统计专家求助。
