# Mays & Pope 2000：Qualitative research in health care: Assessing quality in qualitative research

- 资料：`mays2000assessing`，[PubMed Central 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1117321/)
- 版本：BMJ 320(7226): 50–52，2000。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Summary points
- 原文："Qualitative methods are now widely used and increasingly accepted in health research, but quality in qualitative research is a mystery to many health services researchers"
- 要点：（2000 年时）质性方法在健康研究中已广泛使用，但很多卫生服务研究者不清楚怎样判断质性研究的质量。

### E2

- 位置：Two opposing views
- 原文："There has been considerable debate over whether qualitative and quantitative methods can and should be assessed according to the same quality criteria."
- 要点：质性研究和量化研究能否、应否用同一套质量标准评价，一直有很大争论。

### E3

- 位置：Separate and different: the antirealist position
- 原文："Advocates of the antirealist position argue that qualitative research represents a distinctive paradigm and as such it cannot and should not be judged by conventional measures of validity, generalisability, and reliability."
- 要点：一派认为质性研究是独特的范式，不能也不应该用效度、可推广性、信度这些传统标准来评判。

### E4

- 位置：Summary points
- 原文："Quality in qualitative research can be assessed with the same broad concepts of validity and relevance used for quantitative research, but these need to be operationalised differently to take into account the distinctive goals of qualitative research"
- 要点：作者的立场：可以用和量化研究相同的"效度"和"相关性"概念来评价质性研究，但具体做法要按质性研究的目标调整。

### E5

- 位置：Triangulation
- 原文："Triangulation compares the results from either two or more different methods of data collection (for example, interviews and observation) or, more simply, two or more data sources"
- 要点：三角互证：比较两种以上数据收集方法（如访谈和观察）或两个以上数据来源的结果。

### E6

- 位置：Reflexivity
- 原文："Reflexivity means sensitivity to the ways in which the researcher and the research process have shaped the collected data, including the role of prior assumptions and experience"
- 要点：反身性：意识到研究者本人和研究过程怎样塑造了收集到的数据，包括研究者原有的假设和经历。

### E7

- 位置：Reflexivity
- 原文："The effects of personal characteristics such as age, sex, social class, and professional status (doctor, nurse, physiotherapist, sociologist, etc) on the data collected and on the “distance” between the researcher and those researched also needs to be discussed."
- 要点：研究者的年龄、性别、社会阶层和职业身份（医生、护士、理疗师等）对数据和对研究者与被研究者之间"距离"的影响，也要讨论。

### E8

- 位置：Attention to negative cases
- 原文："a long established tactic for improving the quality of explanations in qualitative research is to search for, and discuss, elements in the data that contradict, or seem to contradict, the emerging explanation"
- 要点：主动寻找并讨论与正在形成的解释相矛盾的数据，是提高质性研究解释质量的老办法。

### E9

- 位置：Respondent validation
- 原文："it is better to think of respondent validation as part of a process of error reduction which also generates further original data, which in turn requires interpretation."
- 要点：请受访者核对研究者的解释，最好看作减少错误的一个环节，它产生的新数据本身也需要解释，而不是"最终检验"。

### E10

- 位置：Assessing the validity of qualitative research
- 原文："There are no mechanical or “easy” solutions to limit the likelihood that there will be errors in qualitative research."
- 要点：没有机械的、"简单"的办法能保证质性研究不出错。

### E11

- 位置：Clear exposition of methods of data collection and analysis
- 原文："the written account should include sufficient data to allow the reader to judge whether the interpretation proffered is adequately supported by the data."
- 要点：报告里要给出足够的原始资料，让读者能判断作者的解释是否有数据支撑。
