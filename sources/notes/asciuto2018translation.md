# 约克大学英语与相关文学系：Ways of reading a translation

- 资料：`asciuto2018translation`，[原文](https://www.york.ac.uk/english/about/writing-at-york/writing-resources/ways-of-reading-translation/)
- 版本：英国约克大学英语系写作资源页面，Nicoletta Asciuto 撰写，2018 年 5 月，免费公开；2026 年 10 月读取

### E1

- 位置：Parallel text editions
- 原文："do prefer editions with a parallel text in original."
- 要点：能选的话，优先用附原文对照的版本。

### E2

- 位置：Benefits of close-reading a translation
- 原文："it is sometimes interesting to look at different translations of the same text to see how much they differ."
- 要点：对比同一文本的不同译本，看它们差别多大。

### E3

- 位置：The work of the translator
- 原文："translators’ names are normally well noticeable and visible in the book cover or frontispiece and should always be acknowledged when referencing the edition you are using for your essays."
- 要点：引用所用版本时，要写明译者。

### E4

- 位置：The work of the translator
- 原文："When you are commenting on a specific word choice in English, you should always bear in mind you are reading a translation, which is a product of a translator’s decision, rather than the author’s."
- 要点：评论译本的用词时，记住这是译者的选择，不是作者的。

### E5

- 位置：The work of the translator
- 原文："being able to double check your text with a parallel text will be extremely useful when close reading a translation; it will enable you to make more precise claims with regard to the use of both source and target languages."
- 要点：有对照原文，细读译本时能对原文和译文的用语做出更精确的论断。
