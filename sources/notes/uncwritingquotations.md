# 北卡罗来纳大学教堂山分校写作中心：Quotations

- 资料：`uncwritingquotations`，[原文](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/quotations/)
- 版本：写作中心免费讲义（CC BY-NC-ND 4.0）；2026 年 10 月读取

### E1

- 位置：When should I quote?
- 原文："papers analyzing literature may rely heavily on direct quotations of the text"
- 要点：文学分析论文可能大量依赖对文本的直接引用。

### E2

- 位置：When should I quote?，Discussing specific arguments or ideas
- 原文："If it is especially important that you formulate a counterargument to this claim, then you might wish to quote the part of the statement that you find questionable and establish a dialogue between yourself and John Doe"
- 要点：要反驳某位学者的说法时，引出你质疑的那部分原话，和他"对话"。

### E3

- 位置：How do I set up and follow up a quotation?
- 原文："Provide context for each quotation." / "Attribute each quotation to its source." / "Explain the significance of the quotation." / "Provide a citation for the quotation."
- 要点：引文四步：交代语境、说明出处是谁、解释意义、给出引注。

### E4

- 位置：How much should I quote?，Excerpt those fragments carefully!
- 原文："Misquoting misrepresents the ideas of others."
- 要点：断章取义会歪曲别人的意思。

### E5

- 位置：How much should I quote?，Use block quotations sparingly
- 原文："you should use block quotations only when you fear that omitting any words will destroy the integrity of the passage."
- 要点：只有删掉任何词都会破坏段落完整性时，才用大段引文。

### E6

- 位置：How do I embed a quotation into a sentence?
- 原文："In general, avoid leaving quotes as sentences unto themselves."
- 要点：不要让引文单独成句、悬在那里。
