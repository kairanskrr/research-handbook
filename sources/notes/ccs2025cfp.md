# ACM CCS 2025：Call for Papers

- 资料：`ccs2025cfp`，[原文](https://www.sigsac.org/ccs/CCS2025/call-for-papers/)
- 版本：第 32 届 ACM 计算机与通信安全会议（CCS 2025，ACM SIGSAC 主办）征稿启事，2026-10-08 读取。位置写网页小标题。只作为安全方向一个会议某一年的例子。

### E1

- 位置：Call for Papers，开头
- 原文："CCS has two review cycles in 2025." / "Minor revision: Papers in this category are considered to be promising but need some minor additional work (e.g., minor experiments, proofs to minor lemmas)."
- 要点：2025 年分两轮审稿；结果之一是"小修"：论文有希望，但需要补少量工作（如小实验、次要引理的证明）。

### E2

- 位置：Call for Papers，开头
- 原文："Theoretical papers must make a convincing case for the relevance of their results to practice. Authors are encouraged to write the abstract and introduction of their paper in a way that makes the results accessible and compelling to a general computer-security researcher."
- 要点：理论论文必须说明结果和实践的关系；摘要和引言要让一般的安全研究者也读得懂、被吸引。

### E3

- 位置：Call for Papers，评审标准
- 原文："All submitted papers will be evaluated based on their merits, particularly their importance to practical aspects of computer and communications security and privacy, novelty, quality of execution, and presentation. For papers that might raise ethical concerns, authors are expected to convince reviewers that proper procedures (such as IRB approval or responsible disclosure) have been followed, and due diligence has been made to minimize potential harm."
- 要点：按对实际安全和隐私问题的重要性、新颖性、执行质量和表达评审；可能有伦理问题的论文，要让审稿人相信已经走了适当程序（如伦理审查批准、负责任披露），并尽力减少可能的伤害。

### E4

- 位置：格式要求
- 原文："no more than 12 pages long, excluding the bibliography, well-marked appendices, and supplementary material. Note that reviewers are not required to read the appendices or any supplementary material."
- 要点：正文不超过 12 页（参考文献、附录和补充材料不计）；审稿人不必读附录和补充材料。

### E5

- 位置：Providing Artifacts at Submission Time
- 原文："Submissions whose claimed contributions rely on artifacts (e.g., code, models, data sets) are expected to make these accessible to the reviewers, unless there are good reasons not to, in which case these reasons must be mentioned in the submission."
- 要点：贡献依赖制品（代码、模型、数据集）的投稿，审稿时就要让审稿人能拿到；有正当理由不能提供的，要在投稿中说明。
