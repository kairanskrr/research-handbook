# Clancy 等 2014：Survey of Academic Field Experiences (SAFE): Trainees Report Harassment and Assault

- 资料：`clancy2014survey`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0102172)
- 版本：PLOS ONE 9(7): e102172，2014，开放获取。读取的是 journals.plos.org 网页全文。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："We conducted an internet-based survey of field scientists (N = 666) to characterize these experiences."
- 要点：作者对野外科学工作者做了网上调查，共 666 人作答。

### E2

- 位置：Results，Respondent characteristics
- 原文："Nearly half of the study participants self-identified as anthropologists" / "geologists (N = 29, 4.4%)"
- 要点：受访者近一半是人类学者，约四分之一是考古学者；地质学者只有 29 人（4.4%）。

### E3

- 位置：Results，Respondent characteristics
- 原文："a majority of respondents were from the United States (N = 498/666, 74.8%)"
- 要点：约四分之三的受访者来自美国。

### E4

- 位置：Methods，Survey Recruitment
- 原文："the first, aimed at biological anthropologists between February 21st and April 12th 2013 (N = 124); and the second from April 13th to May 10th 2013 (N = 542)"
- 要点：调查在 2013 年分两轮进行。

### E5

- 位置：Results
- 原文："Fewer than half of survey respondents recalled ever encountering a code of conduct at any of the field sites at which they had worked (N = 251/666, 37.7%)."
- 要点：只有 37.7% 的受访者记得在工作过的野外站点见过行为准则。

### E6

- 位置：Results
- 原文："Among survey respondents, harassment and assault at field sites were overwhelmingly aimed at trainees (students at all stages and postdocs) and employees"
- 要点：在受访者中，野外的骚扰和侵害绝大多数针对学生、博士后和雇员。

### E7

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："Given the retrospective, snowball sampling methodology, our study is not able to determine the prevalence of these negative experiences within or across disciplines"
- 要点：作者说明，这种回顾式、滚雪球抽样的调查不能用来估计各学科的发生率。

### E8

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："Trainees sometimes work at field sites supervised by organizations not affiliated with their home institution." / "can raise additional complexities in establishing norms of behavior in the absence of an explicit, enforced policy."
- 要点：学生有时在不属于本校管理的野外站点工作；如果没有明确、执行的政策，行为规范就更难确立。

### E9

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Few respondents were aware of mechanisms to report incidents; most who did report were unsatisfied with the outcome."
- 要点：很少有受访者知道怎样举报；举报过的人多数对结果不满意。
