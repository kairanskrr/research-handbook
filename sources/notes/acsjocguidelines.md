# The Journal of Organic Chemistry：Author Guidelines（作者须知）

- 资料：`acsjocguidelines`，[原文](https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=joceah)
- 版本：美国化学会《有机化学杂志》（The Journal of Organic Chemistry，JOC）作者须知网页，页面标注最后更新 2026-10-05，2026-10-06 读取。网页不分页，位置写小标题。只代表这一本期刊的要求。

### E1

- 位置：Compound Characterization Data
- 原文："For new compounds, evidence adequate to establish both identity and degree of purity (homogeneity) must be provided."
- 要点：新化合物必须提供足以确定"它是什么"（结构）和"有多纯"的证据。

### E2

- 位置：Compound Characterization Data
- 原文："JOC requires that purity be documented compound-by-compound, with copies of spectra or chromatograms, elemental analysis, or quantitative NMR or chromatographic integration data."
- 要点：JOC 要求逐个化合物记录纯度，用谱图或色谱图、元素分析、定量核磁或色谱积分数据。

### E3

- 位置：NMR / 鉴定新化合物
- 原文："Evidence for documenting the identity of new compounds should include both proton and carbon NMR data and either MS accurate mass (HRMS) or elemental analysis data."
- 要点：鉴定新化合物，要有氢谱和碳谱核磁数据，再加高分辨质谱或元素分析之一。

### E4

- 位置：Purity
- 原文："When primarily synthetic work is reported, JOC does not require that a certain minimum level of purity be met for the reported compounds, but it does require that the purity level that has been attained be faithfully documented. When new or known synthesized compounds are the study materials for physical measurements or bioassays, a purity level of at least 95% needs to be documented."
- 要点：以合成为主的工作，JOC 不设最低纯度，但要如实记录达到的纯度；化合物要拿去做物理测量或生物测试时，要证明纯度至少 95%。

### E5

- 位置：Purity
- 原文："A narrow melting point range is not sufficient by itself to document the purity of a new compound. MS accurate mass (HRMS) data may be used to support a molecular formula assignment but cannot serve to document compound purity."
- 要点：熔程窄本身不足以证明新化合物纯；高分辨质谱能支持分子式，但不能证明纯度。

### E6

- 位置：Elemental Analysis and Accurate Mass Measurement
- 原文："Elemental analysis Found values for carbon, hydrogen, and nitrogen should be within 0.4% of the Calcd values for the proposed formula."
- 要点：元素分析的碳、氢、氮实测值应与理论值相差在 0.4% 以内。

### E7

- 位置：Experimental Section
- 原文："The editors remind authors that it is unethical to modify reported data or spectra, for example to correct spectral baselines or remove solvent or impurity peaks."
- 要点：修改报告的数据或谱图（如校正基线、去掉溶剂峰或杂质峰）是不道德的。

### E8

- 位置：Purity / NMR 谱图
- 原文："It is not acceptable to use peak-editing software or other means to suppress or obscure peaks arising from impurities"
- 要点：不能用修峰软件等手段压低或遮盖杂质峰。

### E9

- 位置：Synthesis Experiments
- 原文："All reported yields should represent weighed amounts of isolated and purified products and must be reported in the Experimental Section as both weights and percentages."
- 要点：报告的产率应是分离纯化后称重得到的，实验部分要同时给出质量和百分比。

### E10

- 位置：Synthesis Experiments
- 原文："The Editors would like to point out that reported yields above 95%, isomer ratios above 200:1, and enantiomeric excesses above 99% typically aren't realistic without further explanation."
- 要点：编辑提醒，产率超过 95%、异构体比例超过 200:1、对映体过量超过 99%，没有进一步解释的话通常不现实。

### E11

- 位置：Experimental Section/Computational Methods
- 原文："Authors have the flexibility to place the experimental content in the main text (Experimental Section), in the Supporting Information, or a combination of both as it best supports the manuscript, so long as the information is accurate and complete." / "These sections should describe methods in sufficient detail to permit repetition of the work by others."
- 要点：实验内容可以放在正文实验部分、支持信息或两者，只要准确完整；这些部分要写得足够详细，让别人能重复。

### E12

- 位置：NMR（原始数据）
- 原文："Authors are requested to furnish primary NMR data files (FID files, acquisition data, processing parameters). All original primary NMR data supporting a submission should be retained and provided if requested."
- 要点：JOC 要求作者提供核磁原始数据文件（FID 文件、采集数据、处理参数）；所有原始核磁数据要保存，被要求时提供。

### E13

- 位置：Computational Data
- 原文："The level of theory, basis set, and relevant input parameters should be identified along with the specific program used. The data should include Z-matrices or Cartesian coordinates, grid size (for DFT calculations), and computed total energies of target or optimized structures."
- 要点：计算部分要写明理论方法、基组、输入参数和所用程序，并给出坐标、格点大小和计算得到的总能量。

### E14

- 位置：Preprints（Editorial Policies 部分）
- 原文："Authors are allowed to deposit an initial version of their manuscript in a preprint repository such as ChemRxiv, arXiv, bioRxiv, or the applicable repository for their discipline prior to submission. Authors must note any use of a preprint repository in the cover letter, include a link to the preprint"
- 要点：JOC 允许投稿前把初稿放到 ChemRxiv 等预印本平台，但要在投稿信里说明并给出链接。

### E15

- 位置：Preprints（Editorial Policies 部分）
- 原文："Authors are discouraged from posting a preprint or a revised version of the manuscript as a preprint after the manuscript has been submitted to the journal without consulting with the Editor-in-Chief."
- 要点：投稿之后，不先问主编就发预印本或发修改版预印本，JOC 不鼓励。

### E16

- 位置：Emphasis on Chemical Safety
- 原文："Authors must emphasize any unexpected, new, and/or significant hazards or risks associated with the reported work. This information should be in the Experimental Section of a full article and included in the main text of a letter."
- 要点：ACS 自 2021 年起在各刊作者须知中加入安全要求：作者必须突出说明工作中意外的、新的或重大的危险，写在完整论文的实验部分或快报正文里。

### E17

- 位置：Emphasis on Chemical Safety
- 原文："In 2021, in support of ACS's core value of "Professionalism, Safety, and Ethics," ACS Publications added a Safety Considerations requirement to the Author Guidelines of every ACS journal"
- 要点：2021 年，ACS 出版部给旗下每本期刊的作者须知都加了"安全考虑"要求。

### E18

- 位置：Compound Characterization Data
- 原文："If required data cannot be obtained (a compound is too insoluble to record a carbon NMR, or too unstable to obtain a good elemental analysis, etc.), the reason for the absence of the data should be noted in the Experimental Section"
- 要点：拿不到某项要求的数据（如太难溶测不了碳谱），要在实验部分说明原因。
