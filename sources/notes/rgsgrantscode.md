# 英国皇家地理学会：Code of Practice for the Grants Programme

- 资料：`rgsgrantscode`，[原文](https://www.rgs.org/in-the-field/grants/guidance-for-society-grant-applicants/code-of-practice-for-the-grants-programme)
- 版本：英国皇家地理学会（与英国地理学家协会合并，Royal Geographical Society with the Institute of British Geographers，RGS-IBG）资助项目的行为守则网页。官网对脚本拒绝访问，读取的是 Internet Archive 2025-04-29 快照。它是该学会**资助项目**的守则，其伦理条款也适用于在学会会议上报告或在学会期刊发表的研究（见 E1）。位置写段落内容。

### E1

- 位置：伦理条款段首
- 原文："The following considerations should therefore apply to all research supported by the Society, whether through financial support, or implicit support through presentation at the Society, Research Group conferences, or other Society events or publication in Society journals:"
- 要点：下列伦理要求适用于学会支持的所有研究，包括资助的、在学会及其研究小组会议上报告的、在学会期刊发表的。

### E2

- 位置：伦理条款
- 原文："Accurate reporting of findings, and a commitment to enabling others to replicate results where possible."
- 要点：准确报告结果，并尽可能让别人能够重复出结果。

### E3

- 位置：伦理条款
- 原文："Research projects should be approved by a research ethics committee in the host country, wherever this exists, even if ethics approval has already been obtained by the researcher’s home institution."
- 要点：只要东道国有研究伦理委员会，项目就应经其批准，即使已经通过了研究者本单位的伦理审查。

### E4

- 位置：伦理条款
- 原文："Local partners, communities and research participants should be included throughout the research process, from planning, to implementation, to post-study feedback and evaluation."
- 要点：当地合作者、社区和参与者应参与研究全过程，从计划、实施到研究后的反馈和评估。

### E5

- 位置：伦理条款
- 原文："Local partners should be recognised in data ownership and authorship of publications."
- 要点：当地合作者应在数据所有权和论文署名上得到承认。

### E6

- 位置：可持续研究条款
- 原文："Recognising that fieldwork is integral to geographical research and that this will commonly involve travel, this travel and the choice of destination(s) visited should in turn be clearly justified."
- 要点：野外工作是地理研究的组成部分，常涉及出行；出行和目的地的选择要有清楚的理由。

### E7

- 位置：伦理条款
- 原文："The review process should be proportional to the likely risk (for example, research on vulnerable groups or at-risk populations demands more careful attention than other forms of research)."
- 要点：审查力度应与可能的风险相称，例如研究弱势或高风险人群要更加小心。
