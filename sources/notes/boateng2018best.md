# Boateng 等人 2018：Best Practices for Developing and Validating Scales for Health, Social, and Behavioral Research: A Primer

- 资料：`boateng2018best`，[原文（PubMed Central 开放获取）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6004510/)
- 版本：Frontiers in Public Health 6:149（2018），开放获取。位置写步骤和小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Scales are a manifestation of latent constructs; they measure behaviors, attitudes, and hypothetical scenarios we expect to exist as a result of our theoretical understanding of the world, but cannot assess directly"
- 要点：量表是潜在构念的外在表现，测的是按理论应当存在、却无法直接测量的行为、态度等。

### E2

- 位置：Scale development overview
- 原文："There are three phases to creating a rigorous scale—item development, scale development, and scale evaluation" / "The last phase, scale evaluation, requires: (7) tests of dimensionality, (8) tests of reliability, and (9) tests of validity."
- 要点：编制量表分三个阶段（编题、形成量表、评价量表）九个步骤；最后阶段要检验维度、信度、效度。

### E3

- 位置：Step 1, Domain identification
- 原文："the domain being examined should be decided upon and defined before any item activity" / "Where there is a similar instrument in existence, you need to justify why the development of a new instrument is appropriate and how it will differ from existing instruments."
- 要点：动手编题之前先定义要测的领域（构念）；已有类似量表时，要说明为什么还要新编、和已有量表有什么不同。

### E4

- 位置：Step 1, Item generation
- 原文："It is considered best practice to combine both deductive and inductive methods to both define the domain and identify the questions to assess it."
- 要点：最佳做法是把演绎（从文献和已有量表出发）和归纳（从访谈、焦点小组等目标人群的回答出发）两种编题方法结合起来。

### E5

- 位置：Step 1, Item generation
- 原文："Kline and Schinka et al. note that the initial pool of items developed should be at minimum twice as long as the desired final scale"
- 要点：初始题库至少应是最终量表题数的两倍。

### E6

- 位置：Step 1, Item generation
- 原文："items should be worded simply and unambiguously. Items should not be offensive or potentially biased in terms of social identity, i.e., gender, religion, ethnicity, race, economic status, or sexual orientation"
- 要点：题目措辞要简单、无歧义，不能冒犯或偏向某种社会身份。

### E7

- 位置：Step 1, Item generation（常见陷阱）
- 原文："One pitfall in the identification of domain and item generation is the improper conceptualization and definition of the domain(s)." / "Caution should also be taken to avoid construct underrepresentation, which is when a scale does not capture important aspects of a construct because its focus is too narrow"
- 要点：常见陷阱是构念定义不当；还要避免"构念代表不足"，即量表关注面太窄，漏掉构念的重要方面。

### E8

- 位置：Step 2, Evaluation by experts
- 原文："Ideally, one should combine expert and target population judgment." / "These expert judges should be independent of those who developed the item pool." / "Multiple judges have been used (typically ranging from 5 to 7)"
- 要点：内容效度最好由专家和目标人群共同评判；专家应独立于编题的人，常用 5 到 7 位。

### E9

- 位置：Step 3, Cognitive interviews
- 原文："Cognitive interviewing entails the administration of draft survey questions to target populations and then asking the respondents to verbalize the mental process entailed in providing such answers"
- 要点：认知访谈：让目标人群作答草稿题目，再请他们说出作答时的思考过程。

### E10

- 位置：Step 3, Cognitive interviews
- 原文："A range of 5–15 interviews in two to three rounds, or until saturation, or relatively few new insights emerge is considered ideal for pre-testing" / "Outcomes of cognitive interviews should always be reported, along with solutions used to remedy the situation."
- 要点：预测试做 5–15 次访谈、分两到三轮，或做到不再有新发现为止；认知访谈的结果和相应修改都要报告。

### E11

- 位置：Step 4, Establishing the sample size
- 原文："The rule of thumb has been at least 10 participants for each scale item" / "In sum, there is no single item-ratio that works for all survey development scenarios."
- 要点：常见经验法则是每道题至少 10 人，但没有一个适用于所有情况的人数与题数比例。

### E12

- 位置：Step 4, Determining the type of data to use
- 原文："the ideal procedure is to develop the scale on sample A, whether cross-sectional or longitudinal, and then test it on an independent sample B."
- 要点：理想做法是在样本 A 上编制量表，再在独立的样本 B 上检验。

### E13

- 位置：Step 5, Inter-item and item-total correlations
- 原文："However, the adjusted item-total correlation, which examines the correlation between the item and the sum score of the rest of the items excluding itself is preferred" / "Items with very low adjusted item-total correlations (< 0.30) are less desirable and could be a cue for potential deletion from the tentative scale."
- 要点：题目分析中优先看"校正后的题总相关"（该题与其余题总分的相关）；低于 0.30 的题可以考虑删除。

### E14

- 位置：Step 6, Extraction of factors
- 原文："A number of scales developed stop at this phase and jump to tests of reliability, but the factors extracted at this point only provide a hypothetical structure of the scale."
- 要点：不少量表做完探索性因子提取就直接算信度，但这时得到的因子只是假设的结构，还需要检验。

### E15

- 位置：Step 6, Extraction of factors
- 原文："Where feasible, researchers could also assess the optimal number of factors to be drawn from the list of items using either parallel analysis"
- 要点：条件允许时，可以用平行分析等方法确定保留几个因子。

### E16

- 位置：Step 7, Confirmatory factor analysis
- 原文："These techniques include the chi-square test of exact fit, Root Mean Square Error of Approximation (RMSEA ≤ 0.06), Tucker Lewis Index (TLI ≥ 0.95), Comparative Fit Index (CFI ≥ 0.95), Standardized Root Mean Square Residual (SRMR ≤ 0.08)"
- 要点：作者列出的验证性因子分析常用拟合指标和阈值。

### E17

- 位置：Step 8, Cronbach's alpha
- 原文："An alpha coefficient of 0.70 has often been regarded as an acceptable threshold for reliability" / "reliability statistics such as Raykov's rho, ordinal alpha, and Revelle's beta, which are debated to have improvements over Cronbach's alpha, are beginning to gain acceptance."
- 要点：0.70 常被当作 α 系数的可接受线；一些被认为改进了 α 的信度指标正逐渐被接受。

### E18

- 位置：Step 8, Test–retest reliability
- 原文："The test–retest reliability, also known as the coefficient of stability, is used to assess the degree to which the participants' performance is repeatable, i.e., how consistent their sum scores are across time"
- 要点：重测信度看同一批人隔一段时间再测，总分是否一致。

### E19

- 位置：Step 9, Tests of validity
- 原文："validation is an ongoing process that starts with the identification and definition of the domain of study (Step 1) and continues to its generalizability with other constructs (Step 9)"
- 要点：效度验证是一个持续过程，从定义构念开始，一直延续到与其他构念的关系检验。

### E20

- 位置：Step 9, Construct validity
- 原文："Evidence of convergent validity of a construct can be provided by the extent to which the newly developed scale correlates highly with other variables designed to measure the same construct" / "The newly developed construct can be invalidated by too high correlations with other tests which are intended to differ in their measurements"
- 要点：聚合效度：新量表应与测同一构念的其他工具高相关；区分效度：与本应不同的构念相关过高，会推翻新量表。

### E21

- 位置：Step 9, Construct validity（已知组比较）
- 原文："we found households with E. coli present in their drinking water had higher mean water insecurity scores than households that had no E. coli in drinking water."
- 要点：已知组比较的例子：饮用水含大肠杆菌的家庭，水不安全量表得分更高，与已有知识一致。

### E22

- 位置：Step 9, Criterion validity
- 原文："Predictive validity can be estimated by examining the association between the scale scores and the criterion in question."
- 要点：预测效度通过量表分数与相应效标的关联来估计。
