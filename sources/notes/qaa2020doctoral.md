# QAA 2020：Characteristics Statement: Doctoral Degree

- 资料：`qaa2020doctoral`，[原文 PDF](https://www.qaa.ac.uk/docs/qaa/quality-code/doctoral-degree-characteristics-statement-2020.pdf)
- 版本：英国高等教育质量保障署（Quality Assurance Agency for Higher Education，QAA）《博士学位特征说明》第三版，2020 年 2 月。描述英国博士学位的特征；它是帮助学校设计课程的参考文件，不属于对英国高校的强制要求。位置写章节和页码。

### E1

- 位置：About this Statement，第 1 页
- 原文："Characteristics Statements sit alongside these resources to help providers develop courses and refine curricula but are not part of the regulated requirements for higher education providers in the UK."
- 要点：特征说明帮助学校设计和完善课程，但不属于对英国高校的强制性要求。

### E2

- 位置：2.1 Category 1: Subject specialist doctorates，第 6 页
- 原文："Practical work, such as in the creative and performing arts, may well form part of a candidate's output. The form of artefacts and outputs of a practical nature, sometimes involving multimedia, relates to the candidate's subject area rather than to the form of degree programme."
- 要点：创意和表演艺术等领域，实践作品可以是博士成果的一部分；作品和实践成果的形式取决于学科，而不是学位项目的类型。

### E3

- 位置：2.1 Category 1: Subject specialist doctorates，第 6 页
- 原文："In the final assessment, candidates are assessed on their thesis, portfolio, artefact or composition (the latter two normally, and the portfolio sometimes, are accompanied by a critical commentary on the work), and by an oral examination. A minimum of two examiners are usually present at the oral examination, usually one internal and a minimum of one external to the higher education provider."
- 要点：最终考核看论文、作品集、作品或作曲（后两者通常、作品集有时附一篇对作品的批判性评述），再加口头答辩；答辩通常至少两位评审，一般一位校内、至少一位校外。

### E4

- 位置：2.1 Category 3: Professional and practice-based (or practitioner) doctorates，第 8 页
- 原文："For example, in the performing arts, the output involves a written component, which complements the practice-based element (this may be shorter than the traditional PhD thesis, and includes both reflection and context), and one or more other artefacts, such as a novel (for creative writing), a portfolio of work (for art and design), or one or more performance pieces (for theatre studies, dance or music)."
- 要点：例如表演艺术，成果包括与实践部分互补的书面部分（可能比传统博士论文短，包含反思和背景定位），以及一件或多件作品：小说（创意写作）、作品集（艺术与设计）、一个或多个演出（戏剧、舞蹈、音乐）。

### E5

- 位置：4.2 Submission，第 15 页
- 原文："In creative arts, as already mentioned, the 'thesis' may take the form of an artefact and a commentary, as is appropriate for the field of study. Most doctoral examiners are looking for work that is original in nature, makes a contribution to knowledge in the subject and is of peer-reviewed publishable quality in that subject: this may refer to elements of the thesis rather than the complete work."
- 要点：创意艺术领域，"论文"可以是作品加评述；多数评审要看的是：有原创性、对本学科知识有贡献、达到可经同行评议发表的水平（可以指论文的部分内容）。

### E6

- 位置：4.3 Final assessment，第 15 页
- 原文："Whether a candidate is being examined on the basis of a 'traditional' thesis, portfolio, artefact(s), clinical practice or other output, the body of work presented demonstrates the research question and provides a critical evaluation of the extent to which it has been addressed."
- 要点：无论提交的是传统论文、作品集、作品还是其他成果，整体材料都要展示研究问题，并批判性地评估这个问题被回答到了什么程度。
