# ICSE 2026：Research Track Call for Papers

- 资料：`icse2026cfp`，[原文](https://conf.researchr.org/track/icse-2026/icse-2026-research-track)
- 版本：第 48 届国际软件工程大会（International Conference on Software Engineering，ICSE）2026 研究论文征稿启事，会议官网（researchr），2026-10-08 读取。网页，位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Research Areas
- 原文："ICSE welcomes submissions addressing topics across the full spectrum of Software Engineering, being inclusive of quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-methods research. Topics of interest include the following and are grouped into the following nine research areas."
- 要点：ICSE 欢迎软件工程各方面的投稿，定量、定性和混合方法研究都收；主题分为九个研究领域。

### E2

- 位置：Research Areas（AI for Software Engineering、Analytics、Testing and Analysis 等领域列表）
- 原文："AI for Software Engineering" / "Mining software repositories, including version control systems, issue tracking systems, software ecosystems, configurations, app stores, communication platforms, and novel software engineering data sources, to generate insights through various research methods" / "Human and Social Aspects" / "Formal methods and model checking (excluding solutions focusing solely on hardware)"
- 要点：九个领域举例：用人工智能辅助软件工程；挖掘版本库、缺陷跟踪系统等软件数据；人和社会层面；形式化方法和模型检查（只针对硬件的不算）也在范围内。

### E3

- 位置：开头说明；Dual Submission Cycles
- 原文："ICSE 2026 will follow a dual deadline structure introduced in 2024." / "Author response period (3 days)"
- 要点：ICSE 2026 沿用 2024 年起的两轮投稿制；每轮都有 3 天的作者回复期。

### E4

- 位置：Review Criteria
- 原文："i) Novelty :" / "ii) Rigor :" / "iii) Relevance :" / "v) Presentation :" / "Verifiability and Transparency : The extent to which the paper includes sufficient information to understand how an innovation works; to understand how data was obtained, analyzed, and interpreted; and how the paper supports independent verification or replication of the paper’s claimed contributions. Any artifacts attached to or linked from the paper will be checked by one reviewer."
- 要点：ICSE 2026 的五条评审标准之一是"可验证与透明"：读者能否看懂创新怎样工作、数据怎样获得和分析，论文是否支持独立验证或重复；论文附带或链接的制品会有一位审稿人检查。其余四条是新颖性、严谨性、相关性、表达。

### E5

- 位置：Review Criteria；Revisions
- 原文："The outcome of each paper will be one of the following Accept, Revision, Reject." / "Revised papers will be examined by the same set of reviewers. An unsatisfactory revised paper will be rejected. Authors are given approximately 4 weeks to submit the revised papers."
- 要点：结果有三种：录用、修改、拒稿。拿到"修改"的论文约有 4 周修改，由原审稿人再审，改得不满意仍会被拒。

### E6

- 位置：Re-submissions of Rejected Papers
- 原文："Authors of papers that receive a REJECT decision in the first submission cycle are strongly discouraged from re-submitting it to the second submission cycle."
- 要点：第一轮被拒的论文，强烈不建议在第二轮重投。

### E7

- 位置：Submission Process
- 原文："All submissions must not exceed 10 pages for the main text, inclusive of all figures, tables, appendices, etc. Two more pages containing only references are permitted ."
- 要点：正文不超过 10 页（含图表和附录），另可加 2 页只放参考文献。

### E8

- 位置：Submission Process（双盲）
- 原文："The ICSE 2026 Research Track will employ a double-anonymous review process." / "All references to the author’s prior work should be in the third person." / "While authors have the right to upload preprints on ArXiV or similar sites, they must avoid specifying that the manuscript was submitted to ICSE 2026."
- 要点：采用双向匿名评审；引用自己以前的工作用第三人称；可以在 arXiv 等处发预印本，但不要写明投给了 ICSE 2026。

### E9

- 位置：Submission Process（同时投稿）
- 原文："papers submitted to ICSE 2026 must not have been published elsewhere and must not be under review or submitted for review elsewhere whilst under consideration for ICSE 2026"
- 要点：投给 ICSE 2026 的论文不能已在别处发表，审稿期间也不能同时投别处。

### E10

- 位置：Submission Process（涉及人的研究）
- 原文："If the research involves human participants/subjects, the authors must adhere to the ACM Publications Policy on Research Involving Human Participants and Subjects ."
- 要点：研究涉及人类参与者时，必须遵守 ACM 关于涉及人类参与者研究的出版政策。

### E11

- 位置：Submission Process（期刊扩展）
- 原文："IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering, ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology and ICSE 2026" / "aimed at reducing community effort in reviewing journal extensions of conference papers and allowing authors to get faster and more consistent feedback"
- 要点：ICSE 2026 与两本期刊 IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering 和 ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology 合作，减少审稿会议论文期刊扩展版的重复工作。

### E12

- 位置：Open Science Policy
- 原文："Note that sharing research artifacts is not mandatory for submission or acceptance. However, sharing is expected to be the default, and non-sharing needs to be justified. We recognize that reproducibility or replicability is not a goal in qualitative research" / "to make their artifact available to the program committee (via upload of supplemental material or a link to an anonymous repository)"
- 要点：ICSE 2026 不强制分享制品，但默认要分享，不分享要说明理由；定性研究不以可重复为目标。投稿时可通过补充材料或匿名仓库链接把制品交给审稿人。

### E13

- 位置：开头 UPDATE 6/29/2025
- 原文："ICSE will again have a Shadow Program Committee for the second cycle to train the next generation of reviewers through deliberate practice with specific guidance and feedback. This program is open to PhD students, post-docs, new faculty members and industry practitioners"
- 要点：ICSE 2026 第二轮设有"影子程序委员会"，让博士生、博士后、新教员等在指导下练习审稿。

### E14

- 位置：Call for Papers 开头
- 原文："The International Conference on Software Engineering (ICSE) is the premier forum for presenting and discussing the most recent and significant technical research contributions in the field of Software Engineering."
- 要点：ICSE 自称是软件工程领域发表和讨论最新、最重要技术研究成果的首要场所。
