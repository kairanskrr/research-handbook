# Greenberg & Buxton 2008：Usability Evaluation Considered Harmful (Some of the Time)

- 资料：`greenberg2008usability`，[作者主页版本](https://www.billbuxton.com/usabilityHarmful.pdf)
- 版本：CHI 2008 论文集，pp. 111–120，DOI 10.1145/1357054.1357074；第二作者 Bill Buxton 个人网站上的 PDF，2026-10-08 读取，pdftotext 提取。位置写节名。这是一篇观点文章；CHI 2026 的审稿指南把它列为"什么是好的 CHI 论文"这场争论的参考读物。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："The choice of evaluation methodology – if any – must arise from and be appropriate for the actual problem or research question under consideration."
- 要点：用什么评估方法（甚至要不要评估），必须从具体的问题或研究问题出发，并与之相称。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："If done during early stage design, it can mute creative ideas that do not conform to current interface norms."
- 要点：在设计早期做可用性评估，可能压制不符合现有界面惯例的创意。

### E3

- 位置：Usability Evaluation as Weak Science › Existence Proofs Instead of Risky Hypothesis Testing
- 原文："In contrast, the existence proof as used in HCI is confirmative hypothesis testing, for the evaluator is seeking confirmatory evidence. This safe test produces only weak validations of an interface technique."
- 要点：人机交互里常见的"存在性证明"（找到一个场景，证明新技术在那里更好）是在找确认性证据，只能给出很弱的验证。

### E4

- 位置：What To Do（第四条）
- 原文："For really novel innovations, existence proofs are likely appropriate. For mainstream systems or modest variations of established ideas, we should likely favor risky hypothesis testing."
- 要点：真正新颖的创新，用存在性证明可能就合适；主流系统或对成熟想法的小改动，应更多用"冒险的"假设检验，也就是认真尝试推翻它。

### E5

- 位置：Usability Evaluation as Weak Science › Existence Proofs Instead of Risky Hypothesis Testing
- 原文："Rigorous science also demands replication, and the same should be true in CHI"
- 要点：严谨的科学要求复现，人机交互也一样。

### E6

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Clearly, usability evaluation is valuable for many situations, as it often helps validate both research ideas and products at varying stages in its lifecycle." / "We believe that the community should continue to evaluate usability for many but not all interface development situations."
- 要点：作者自己也认为可用性评估在很多情况下有价值，社区应当在多数（但不是全部）情况下继续做。

### E7

- 位置：Related Work
- 原文："Shumin Zhai challenged his position by saying that in spite" / "of the concerns, our evaluation methods are better than doing nothing at all"
- 要点：Zhai 反驳"评估暴政"的批评：尽管有种种顾虑，现有的评估方法总比什么都不做好。

### E8

- 位置：What To Do
- 原文："ACM UIST emphasizes novel systems, interaction techniques, and algorithms while evaluation is desired, it is not required if the design is inspiring and well argued"
- 要点：据作者 2008 年的描述，UIST 看重新系统、新交互技术和算法；希望有评估，但设计有启发性、论证充分时不强求。
