# Lisée, Larivière & Archambault 2008：Conference proceedings as a source of scientific information: A bibliometric analysis

- 资料：`lisee2008conference`，[原文](https://crctcs.openum.ca/files/sites/60/2015/10/Proceedings.pdf)
- 版本：作者所在机构（蒙特利尔大学 Larivière 教席网站）公开的论文 PDF；正式发表于 Journal of the American Society for Information Science and Technology 59(11): 1776–1784, 2008，DOI 10.1002/asi.20888。

### E1

- 位置：Methods
- 原文："Data were collected using the Thomson Scientific Science Citation Index (SCI), Social Sciences Citation Index (SSCI), and Arts and Humanities Citation Index (AHCI) CD-ROMs, covering the years 1980 through 2005. Only references made by articles, notes, and review articles were considered in the study."
- 要点：数据来自 Thomson（今 Web of Science）三个引文索引 1980–2005 年的期刊论文，只统计这些论文的参考文献里有多少是会议论文。

### E2

- 位置：Results，Figure 6 图题及说明文字
- 原文："FIG. 6. Natural sciences and engineering subfields with the highest percentage of references made to proceedings, 1980–2005." / "In NSE (Figure 6), these subfields are computers (19.6%), followed by other engineering subfields such as electrical engineering & electronics (13.1%), civil engineering (11.5%), nuclear technology (11.2%), miscellaneous engineering & technology (10.3%), aerospace technology (8.9%), industrial engineering (8.5%), and mechanical engineering (8%)."
- 要点：Figure 6 列的是 1980–2005 年会议论文占参考文献比例最高的自然科学和工程子领域：计算机 19.6% 最高，机械工程 8% 也在其中。

### E3

- 位置：Results
- 原文："Of all the documents cited in Thomson Scientific’s CD-ROM databases in 2005, approximately 1.7% consists of proceedings in the NSE and 2.5% in the SSH."
- 要点：2005 年，自然科学和工程论文引用的全部文献里，会议论文约占 1.7%（社会科学和人文约 2.5%）。
