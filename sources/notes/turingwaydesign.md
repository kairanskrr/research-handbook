# The Turing Way：Guide for Project Design

- 资料：`turingwaydesign`，[Overview of Project Design](https://book.the-turing-way.org/project-design/pd-overview)、[Getting Started Checklist](https://book.the-turing-way.org/project-design/pd-overview/pd-checklist)、[Project Management Methodologies](https://book.the-turing-way.org/project-design/project-management-methodologies/overview)
- 版本：The Turing Way 社区编写的开放手册（CC-BY 4.0），网页，2026-10-05 读取。位置写页面和小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Overview of Project Design，Learning from Mistakes（研究者回顾的项目设计遗憾）
- 原文："Not advocating for clearer goals and success criteria from the beginning."
- 要点：研究者回顾的第一条遗憾：没有一开始就争取更清晰的目标和成功标准。

### E2

- 位置：同上
- 原文："Not setting short- and long-term milestones"
- 要点：遗憾之一：没有设定短期和长期里程碑。

### E3

- 位置：同上
- 原文："Trying to plan too much at the beginning and never getting started."
- 要点：另一条相反的遗憾：一开始计划过多，结果迟迟没有动手。

### E4

- 位置：同上
- 原文："Not properly taking into account the degree to which requirements will change throughout a project - which happens a lot in academia - and the effect this has on designs that then also need to change."
- 要点：没有充分考虑需求在项目中会变化（学术研究中很常见），以及设计也得跟着变。

### E5

- 位置：Getting Started Checklist，Timeline & Milestones
- 原文："Establish a project timeline with key milestones, output, and deadlines. [...] Break down the project into manageable phases or tasks. [...] Useful documentation: Gantt charts, roadmaps, or project boards."
- 要点：建立包含关键里程碑、产出和截止日期的时间线；把项目拆成可管理的阶段或任务；甘特图、路线图、项目看板是有用的文档形式。

### E6

- 位置：Getting Started Checklist，Aims & Values
- 原文："Define the main research questions and objectives."
- 要点：项目开始时先定义主要研究问题和目标。

### E7

- 位置：Project Management Methodologies，Summary
- 原文："Research projects are often characterised by uncertainty, evolving objectives, and a need for flexibility."
- 要点：研究项目的特点是不确定、目标会演变、需要灵活性。

### E8

- 位置：Project Management Methodologies，比较表中 Waterfall 一行
- 原文："Less suitable for research due to its rigid structure and difficulty in accommodating changes once the process has begun."
- 要点：瀑布式（按固定顺序一步步执行的）管理方法结构僵硬、开始后难以调整，不太适合研究。
