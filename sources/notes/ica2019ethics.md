# 国际传播学会 2019：ICA Code of Ethics

- 资料：`ica2019ethics`，[原文 PDF](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.icahdq.org/resource/resmgr/governance_documents/ica.code.of.ethics.may2019.pdf)
- 版本：国际传播学会（International Communication Association，ICA）伦理准则，2019 年 5 月通过，4 页。官网 PDF 对脚本返回拒绝访问，读的是 Internet Archive 2024-09-01 的快照（web.archive.org/web/20240901124205/...），内容是同一份 PDF。位置写条款编号。

### E1

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："Ethical guidelines must be sensitive to institutional, legal, and cultural contexts that may vary among nations and regions."
- 要点：伦理准则要顾及各国、各地区不同的机构、法律和文化环境。

### E2

- 位置：1.1 Rights of research participants
- 原文："Participation in communication research studies should include the voluntary and informed consent of participants (or, where appropriate, assent)."
- 要点：参加传播研究应当基于参与者自愿的知情同意（适当时，如对儿童，取得其本人的"赞同"）。

### E3

- 位置：1.1 Rights of research participants
- 原文："ICA members are expected to uphold the research values of autonomy, beneficence, and justice, and where applicable, to abide by their local institutional ethics and review boards."
- 要点：会员要坚持自主、有益、公正的研究价值，并在适用时遵守所在机构伦理审查委员会的要求。

### E4

- 位置：2.2 Transparency and disclosure
- 原文："While ICA supports the transparency of open data initiatives, it recognizes the need to protect the privacy and rights of data subjects. Researchers should take care to prevent the re-identification of anonymized data."
- 要点：支持开放数据，但要保护数据主体的隐私和权利；研究者要防止匿名数据被重新识别出身份。

### E5

- 位置：2.1 Honest attribution and authorship
- 原文："the published author list should accurately reflect individual contributions."
- 要点：作者名单要准确反映每个人的贡献。

### E6

- 位置：2.2 Transparency and disclosure
- 原文："Authors should be transparent and disclose where and how their datasets, writings, and research have previously appeared at the time of submission of research for review as well as publication or presentation. This includes submissions to conferences as well as journal publications."
- 要点：投稿、发表或报告时，要说明数据、文字和研究之前在哪里、以什么形式出现过，会议投稿和期刊论文都包括在内。

### E7

- 位置：1.1 Rights of research participants
- 原文："ICA expects researchers to take care to address the specific needs of vulnerable populations in their research, such as children, refugees, people who are mentally unwell, etc."
- 要点：研究涉及儿童、难民、精神疾病患者等脆弱人群时，要照顾他们的特殊需要。

### E8

- 位置：2.3 Fair use 及脚注 8
- 原文："Fair use is the right to use copyrighted material without permission or compensation where the social and cultural benefits are greater than the costs to the copyright holder." [...] "It should be noted that all members should follow their national policies regarding fair use."
- 要点：合理使用指社会文化收益大于版权人成本时，无需许可或付费即可使用受版权保护的材料；各会员要遵守本国关于合理使用的规定。
