# Geddes 1990：How the Cases You Choose Affect the Answers You Get

- 资料：`geddes1990cases`，[加州大学伯克利分校政府研究所工作论文 90-13（eScholarship 开放获取）](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/9r45g86c)
- 版本：工作论文版（1990），扫描件，文字为光学字符识别结果；正式版发表于 Political Analysis 2: 131–150（1990）。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："One of our most durable conventions is the" / "selection of cases for study on the dependent variable. That is, if we want to understand something, for example, revolution, we select one or more occurrences and subject them to scrutiny"
- 要点：比较政治学最持久的惯例之一是按因变量选案例：想理解革命，就挑几场革命来研究。

### E2

- 位置：开头
- 原文："All graduate students learn in the statistics courses forced upon them that selection on the dependent variable is forbidden,"
- 要点：研究生都在统计课上学过"不能按因变量选案例"，但很少人记得为什么。

### E3

- 位置：The Nature of the Problem
- 原文："The crux of the difficulty that arises when cases are selected on the dependent variable is that if one studies only countries A and B, one can collect only half the information needed, namely what A and B have in common."
- 要点：按因变量选案例的症结：只研究结果相同的 A 和 B，只能拿到一半信息，即 A 和 B 有什么共同点。

### E4

- 位置：The Nature of the Problem
- 原文："The only things that can actually be explained using a sample selected on the dependent variable are differences among the selected cases."
- 要点：按因变量选出的样本，真正能解释的只有入选案例之间的差异。

### E5

- 位置：A Straightforward Case of Selection on the Dependent Variable
- 原文："But the conclusion does not follow. Perhaps there are other countries which suppress labor to the same or greater degree and which have failed to prosper economically."
- 要点：从几个高速增长的新兴工业化国家都压制劳工，推不出压制劳工促进增长：也许别的国家同样压制劳工却没有发展起来。

### E6

- 位置：A Straightforward Case of Selection on the Dependent Variable
- 原文："When one looks at the relationship between labor repression and growth for a larger sample of cases which includes slow-growing countries as well as fast, the apparent relationship between labor repression and growth disappears."
- 要点：把增长慢的国家也纳入更大的样本后，劳工压制和增长之间的表面关系消失了。
