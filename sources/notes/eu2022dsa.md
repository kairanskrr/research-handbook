# 欧盟 2022：《数字服务法》（Regulation (EU) 2022/2065）

- 资料：`eu2022dsa`，[原文（EUR-Lex）](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065)
- 版本：欧洲议会和欧盟理事会 2022 年 10 月 19 日通过的《数字服务法》（Digital Services Act，DSA），刊于欧盟官方公报 L 277，2022 年 10 月 27 日。EUR-Lex 对脚本返回空页面，读的是 Internet Archive 2023-01-17 的快照（web.archive.org/web/20230117003750/...），为官方公报英文 HTML 全文。只摘录第 40 条（数据访问与审查）。位置写条款号。

### E1

- 位置：Article 40(4)
- 原文："provide access to data to vetted researchers who meet the requirements in paragraph 8 of this Article, for the sole purpose of conducting research that contributes to the detection, identification and understanding of systemic risks in the Union"
- 要点：经主管机构的合理请求，超大型在线平台和超大型搜索引擎要向"经审核的研究者"提供数据，用途仅限于发现、识别和理解欧盟内的系统性风险的研究。

### E2

- 位置：Article 40(8)
- 原文："(a) they are affiliated to a research organisation as defined in Article 2, point (1), of Directive (EU) 2019/790; (b) they are independent from commercial interests; (c) their application discloses the funding of the research;" [...] "(g) they have committed themselves to making their research results publicly available free of charge"
- 要点：成为"经审核的研究者"的条件包括：隶属研究机构、独立于商业利益、申请中披露经费来源、能满足数据安全和保密要求，并承诺免费公开研究结果等。

### E3

- 位置：Article 40(12)
- 原文："shall give access without undue delay to data, including, where technically possible, to real-time data, provided that the data is publicly accessible in their online interface by researchers"
- 要点：对平台界面上本来就公开可见的数据，平台要及时向符合部分条件的研究者（包括非营利组织的研究者）提供访问，技术上可行时包括实时数据。

### E4

- 位置：Article 40(13)
- 原文："The Commission shall, after consulting the Board, adopt delegated acts supplementing this Regulation by laying down the technical conditions under which providers of very large online platforms or of very large online search engines are to share data pursuant to paragraphs 1 and 4 and the purposes for which the data may be used."
- 要点：数据共享的技术条件和用途，由欧盟委员会另行通过授权法规具体规定。
