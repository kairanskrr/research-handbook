# Environmental Science & Technology：Author Guidelines

- 资料：`estauthorguidelines`，[原文](https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=esthag)
- 版本：美国化学会（American Chemical Society，ACS）期刊《Environmental Science & Technology》（ES&T）的作者指南网页，2026 年 10 月 6 日读取。期刊规定会更新，以当时页面为准。

### E1

- 位置：Journal Scope
- 原文："ES&T strives to publish novel research of scientific significance and high environmental importance. Insufficient novelty is common reason manuscripts are declined."
- 要点：ES&T 要发表有科学意义、环境上重要的新研究；新意不足是常见的拒稿原因。

### E2

- 位置：Journal Scope
- 原文："Manuscripts that report data of a routine nature or that address topics that are already well understood will not be considered."
- 要点：只报告常规数据、或研究的问题已经很清楚的稿件，ES&T 不考虑。

### E3

- 位置：Journal Scope
- 原文："Whenever possible, research on new measurement technologies should include results with authentic environmental samples, and evaluations should be performed under environmentally realistic conditions."
- 要点：新测量技术的研究，尽可能要有真实环境样品的结果，并在符合实际环境的条件下评估。

### E4

- 位置：Journal Scope，要点列表
- 原文："Studies should provide novel insights into relevant environmental processes or mechanisms, i.e., studies that only describe the outcomes of standard laboratory or field tests or commercially available products are not appropriate for publication in ES&T."
- 要点：只描述标准实验室或现场测试结果、或商业产品表现的研究，不适合在 ES&T 发表。

### E5

- 位置：Journal Scope，要点列表
- 原文："An assessment of uncertainty or sensitivity analysis should be included in reported data where applicable, with adequate quality assurance/quality control reported. New analytical methods should be thoroughly developed and quality ensured."
- 要点：适用时要报告不确定度评估或敏感性分析，并报告充分的质量保证和质量控制。

### E6

- 位置：Journal Scope，要点列表
- 原文："Manuscripts should include evaluations performed under environmentally realistic conditions and concentrations.  Concentrations should be reported as measured and not nominal."
- 要点：要在符合实际环境的条件和浓度下评估；浓度要报告实测值，不是名义值。

### E7

- 位置：Journal Scope，要点列表
- 原文："The scope of the reported data in ambient monitoring studies must yield conclusions applicable to more than the particular site or time when the data were taken"
- 要点：环境监测研究的结论，要能适用于采样地点和时间以外。

### E8

- 位置：Journal Scope，要点列表
- 原文："Manuscripts that emphasize chemical engineering, new catalysts synthesis or analytical chemistry must provide a concise argument that the research represents an advance that is of primary importance to the environmental field and include comparison to state-of-the-art approaches where appropriate."
- 要点：偏化学工程、催化剂合成或分析化学的稿件，要说明它对环境领域有首要意义，并在适当时和最先进的方法比较。

### E9

- 位置：Journal Scope，要点列表
- 原文："Manuscripts on novel materials (e.g., sorbents or catalysts) should demonstrate the materials have been tested under at least one environmental condition using relevant concentrations of target contaminant."
- 要点：新材料（如吸附剂、催化剂）的稿件，至少要在一种环境条件下、用有实际意义的污染物浓度测试过。

### E10

- 位置：Article Types，Research Articles
- 原文："Research Articles (length limit: 7,000 words)."
- 要点：研究论文字数上限 7000 词。

### E11

- 位置：Article Types，Research Articles 之后
- 原文："Manuscripts that report on initial findings of an urgent nature may be submitted to Environmental Science & Technology Letters (ES&TL)."
- 要点：紧迫的初步发现可以投姊妹刊 ES&T Letters。

### E12

- 位置：Cover Letter
- 原文："A substantial fraction of submissions to ES&T are not sent out for review because an editor concludes that the manuscript does not meet the journal's standards for novelty, scientific merit, or environmental importance. The cover letter is your opportunity to convince the editor that this is not the case."
- 要点：ES&T 有相当一部分投稿没有送审，因为编辑认为它在新意、科学价值或环境重要性上不够；投稿信是说服编辑的机会。

### E13

- 位置：Experimental Section
- 原文："Authors must emphasize any unexpected, new, and/or significant hazards or risks associated with the reported work and this safety information must be included in the Materials and Methods section."
- 要点：研究中意外、新的或重大的危险和风险，必须在材料与方法部分写明。

### E14

- 位置：Research Data Policy，Database Deposition
- 原文："ES&T requires for all published articles that authors make materials, data, and protocols available to readers through deposition in a public database."
- 要点：ES&T 要求所有发表的论文把材料、数据和实验方案存入公共数据库，供读者获取。

### E15

- 位置：Research Data Policy，Computer Codes
- 原文："When computer codes are developed or used and are an essential part of a manuscript, sufficient detail must be given, either within the paper or in the SI. Types of languages that are used in the computer codes, compiler/interpreter, and operating system with a specific version must be provided or properly cited."
- 要点：代码是论文核心部分时，要在正文或补充材料中给出足够细节，包括语言、编译器或解释器、操作系统的具体版本。

### E16

- 位置：Prior Publication Policy
- 原文："Posting of pre-prints to a pre-print server such as ChemRxiv, bioRXiv, arXiv, or applicable repository for their discipline where a DOI is issued, before the manuscript is accepted for publication is considered acceptable but requires citing of the pre-print."
- 要点：录用前把预印本发到 ChemRxiv、bioRxiv、arXiv 等有 DOI 的平台是可以的，但要引用该预印本。

### E17

- 位置：Theses/Dissertations
- 原文："Authors submitting material to the journal that has been used in their thesis/dissertation must either deposit their thesis/dissertation in a publicly available repository that provides a DOI or other identifier [...] or delay posting of the thesis/dissertation or remove the material from the Internet until the final paper is published"
- 要点：投稿内容用在学位论文里的，要么把学位论文放进有 DOI 的公开仓库（当作预印本），要么在论文正式发表前推迟或撤下学位论文的网上版本。

### E18

- 位置：Ethical Use of Animals or Human Participants in Research
- 原文："For animal and human studies, the ethics committee that approved the experimental procedure must be identified and the case number provided, as described in the ACS Ethical Guidelines to Publication of Chemical Research. Research involving human participants/samples should also include a statement confirming that the samples/data were obtained through the informed consent of the donors/participants."
- 要点：涉及动物或人的研究，要写明批准实验的伦理委员会和批件编号；涉及人的样品或数据，要声明是在知情同意下获得的。
