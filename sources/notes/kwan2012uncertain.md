# Kwan 2012：The Uncertain Geographic Context Problem

- 资料：`kwan2012uncertain`，[原文 PDF（作者主页）](http://meipokwan.org/Paper/Kwan_UGCoP_2012.pdf)，doi:10.1080/00045608.2012.687349
- 版本：Annals of the Association of American Geographers 102(5):958–968（2012）。读取的是作者个人网站上的出版社版本 PDF（Internet Archive 快照）。作者是地理学者关美宝（Mei-Po Kwan），文章以邻里效应和环境健康研究为出发点。位置写页码。

### E1

- 位置：第 958–959 页
- 原文："results about these effects can be affected by the zoning scheme or geographic scale of the areal units used [...] This is the well-known modifiable areal unit problem (MAUP), which has received much attention to date."
- 要点：用面状单元数据研究某种影响时，结果会受分区方案或地理尺度的影响，这就是可塑性面积单元问题（MAUP）。

### E2

- 位置：第 959 页
- 原文："findings about the effects of area-based attributes could be affected by how contextual units or neighborhoods are geographically delineated and the extent to which these areal units deviate from the “true causally relevant” geographic context"
- 要点：另一个问题是：结论会受"邻里"等背景单元怎样划定、以及它们偏离真正起作用的地理背景多远的影响。

### E3

- 位置：第 959 页
- 原文："It arises because of the spatial uncertainty in the actual areas that exert contextual influences on the individuals being studied and the temporal uncertainty in the timing and duration in which individuals experienced these contextual influences."
- 要点：这个"不确定的地理背景问题"（UGCoP）来自两方面：不知道到底是哪片区域在影响一个人（空间不确定），也不知道这个人何时、多久受到影响（时间不确定）。

### E4

- 位置：第 959 页
- 原文："It is perhaps a major reason why research findings concerning the effects of social and physical environments on health behaviors and outcomes are often inconsistent, given that past studies on the same issue (e.g., obesity) often used different contextual units"
- 要点：同一问题（如肥胖）的研究常用不同的背景单元，这可能是环境影响健康的研究结论常常不一致的一个主要原因。

### E5

- 位置：第 960 页
- 原文："defined neighborhood around each participant’s home as a 1-km or 3-km circular zone (Berke et al. 2007), as a 1-km road network buffer (Frank et al. 2005)"
- 要点：例子：不同研究把"邻里"定义为住处周围 1 公里或 3 公里的圆、1 公里路网缓冲区等，各不相同。

### E6

- 位置：第 963 页
- 原文："an explicit statement about what contextual units will be used, how well they approximate the true geographic context, and what temporal attributes of individuals and contextual influences will be taken into account by these contextual units should be given."
- 要点：研究应明确说明用什么背景单元、它们离真实的地理背景有多近、考虑了哪些时间因素。

### E7

- 位置：第 963–964 页
- 原文："the model with the best fit is not necessarily the one that uses the true geographic context. [...] This means that appropriateness of contextual units cannot be justified using model fit as a criterion."
- 要点：拟合最好的模型不一定用的是真实的地理背景，所以不能用模型拟合程度来证明背景单元选得对。

### E8

- 位置：第 964 页
- 原文："An activity space is the area containing all locations that an individual visits as a result of his or her daily activities and travel"
- 要点：活动空间是一个人因日常活动和出行而到达的所有地点构成的区域；作者认为它可能比行政区更接近真实的地理背景。

### E9

- 位置：第 966 页注 2
- 原文："A major concern with collecting GPS data in health research is participants’ privacy and data confidentiality, because it could be possible to identify a person’s identity through reverse geocoding if data are not handled carefully."
- 要点：收集 GPS 数据的主要顾虑是隐私和保密：处理不当时，可以通过反向地理编码（由坐标查出地址）认出一个人。

### E10

- 位置：第 966 页注 2
- 原文："Further, no maps or displays of the home or activity sites visited by participants or their daily paths can be printed or disseminated."
- 要点：作者举的阿巴拉契亚无烟烟草研究中，参与者的住处、去过的地点和日常路径，一律不得印成地图或公开展示。

### E11

- 位置：第 966 页注 2
- 原文："In countries without strict regulations and in situations where people provide information without knowing or agreeing to its use for research purposes (e.g., social networking Web sites such as Facebook Places, Twitter, and Foursquare), there might be no informed consent and human subject protection protocol and issues of privacy violation can be a serious concern."
- 要点：在监管不严的国家，或人们并不知道、也没同意信息被用于研究的场合（如社交网站签到），可能根本没有知情同意和受试者保护程序，侵犯隐私的问题可能很严重。
