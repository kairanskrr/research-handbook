# Epstein & King 2002：The Rules of Inference

- 资料：`epstein2002rules`，[原文 PDF（作者 Gary King 主页）](https://gking.harvard.edu/files/gking/files/rules.pdf)
- 版本：University of Chicago Law Review 第 69 卷第 1 期（2002 年冬），第 1–133 页。两位作者是美国的政治学兼法学学者（Epstein）和政治学者（King）。文章讨论的是**美国法律评论**里的实证研究。位置写文章的部分编号（Part）和小节。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："law professors have in fact been conducting research that is empirical--that is, learning about the world using quantitative data or qualitative information--for almost as long as they have been conducting research."
- 要点：作者把"实证"定义为用定量数据或定性信息认识世界；按这个定义，法学教授做实证研究由来已久。

### E2

- 位置：Part I（The Extent of Methodological Problems in Empirical Research）
- 原文："We thus began by casting the net very widely, reading all 231 articles published in all American law reviews between 1990 and 2000"
- 要点：作者的调查从 1990–2000 年美国所有法律评论中标题含"empirical"的 231 篇文章读起（此外还读了六家顶级法律评论 1995–2000 年的实证文章等）。

### E3

- 位置：Part I
- 原文："every one we have read thus far--every single one--violates at least one of the rules we discuss in the balance of this Article."
- 要点：作者读过的每一篇，都至少违反了文中讨论的一条推断规则。

### E4

- 位置：引言
- 原文："While a Ph.D. is taught to subject his or her favored hypothesis to every conceivable test and data source, seeking out all possible evidence against his or her theory, an attorney is taught to amass all the evidence for his or her hypothesis and distract attention from anything that might be seen as contradictory information."
- 要点：博士训练让人用一切检验去挑战自己偏爱的假设；律师训练让人只收集支持己方的证据、转移对反面信息的注意。

### E5

- 位置：Part II.B（General Guidelines）
- 原文："Good empirical work adheres to the replication standard: another researcher should be able to understand, evaluate, build on, and reproduce the research without any additional information from the author."
- 要点：好的实证研究要满足"可复现标准"：别人不需要向作者要额外信息，就能理解、评价、扩展并重做这项研究。

### E6

- 位置：Part VIII（Selecting Observations）
- 原文："(1) identify the population of interest; (2) collect as much data as is feasible; (3) record the process by which data come to be observed; and (4) collect data in a manner that avoids selection bias."
- 要点：选择观察对象的四条规则：确定目标总体；尽可能多收集数据；记录数据是怎样被观察到的；避免选择偏差。

### E7

- 位置：Part VIII.D
- 原文："If this is so, then any inferences Kerr reaches solely on the basis of published opinions will be biased, and that bias is in a predictable direction: he will overestimate the effect of Chevron."
- 要点：如果法官有选择地公开判决书，那么只看已公开判决得出的结论会有偏差（例子中会高估某一判例规则的作用）。

### E8

- 位置：Part II.B
- 原文："The point is not to qualify every statement--for example, by changing "I am certain" to "I am fairly certain"--but rather to estimate the degree of uncertainty inherent in each conclusion and to report this estimate along with every conclusion."
- 要点：不是在每句话前加"比较确定"，而是估计每个结论的不确定程度，并和结论一起报告。

### E9

- 位置：Part II.B
- 原文："one useful course of action is to find the weakest link in the chain of reasoning--the part of the argument that rests on the least empirical evidence or that is most vulnerable to attack."
- 要点：定性研究难以量化不确定性时，可以找出推理链上最弱的一环：证据最少、最容易被攻击的那部分。

### E10

- 位置：Part II.B
- 原文："A third sign is the legal community's refusal to subject articles submitted to some of its most prestigious outlets to any form of blind peer review, preferring instead to leave the refereeing task to law students."
- 要点：作者批评美国法学界最有声望的一些刊物不做任何形式的匿名同行评审，而是由法学院学生决定录用。

### E11

- 位置：Part II.A（The Goals）
- 原文："But possible is different than conclusive, especially since Sklansky provides no argument or evidence about this."
- 要点：作者评论一篇只挑了 11 个"显眼"判例的研究：结论有可能成立，但"可能"不等于"确定"，因为作者没有说明挑选方式会不会影响结果。

### E12

- 位置：Part IV.A（Contribute to a Scholarly Literature）
- 原文："making connections with what has come before helps scholars avoid mistakes, skip arduous reinventions of existing ideas, and find additional observable implications of their theories."
- 要点：和已有研究建立联系，能避免错误、避免重复发明已有的想法。

### E13

- 位置：Part II.B
- 原文："Expect everything you do to be scrutinized, any rival explanation you have not explored to be tested, and every possible way you could be proven wrong to be dissected."
- 要点：要预想到发表后每一步都会被审视，没考虑过的其他解释会被检验。

### E14

- 位置：Part VII.A（Measurement）
- 原文："As a rule, then, human judgment should be removed as much as possible from measurement or, when judgment is necessary, the rules underlying the judgments should be clarified enough to make them wholly transparent to other researchers."
- 要点：测量中尽量去掉人的主观判断；必须判断时，要把判断规则写清楚，让别的研究者完全看得懂。

### E15

- 位置：引言
- 原文："a large fraction of legal scholarship makes at least some claims about the world based on observation or experience."
- 要点：很大一部分法学研究，都至少提出了一些基于观察或经验的、关于现实世界的主张。

### E16

- 位置：Part IX.E（Develop Standards for Data Archiving）
- 原文："Investigators coding cases would deposit their data sets, complete coding rules, and the precise connections between their numerical data and the original cases from which they were coded."
- 要点：对判例做编码的研究者，应公开数据集、完整的编码规则，以及每个数字和原始判例之间的对应关系。

### E17

- 位置：Part VII.A
- 原文："asking someone to identify his or her motive is one of the worst methods of measuring motive."
- 要点：直接问人"你为什么这样做"，是测量动机最差的方法之一（作者举的例子是问大法官投票是否受政治偏好影响）。

### E18

- 位置：Part VII.A
- 原文："So, instead of (or sometimes in addition to) asking respondents to answer research questions directly, it is usually better to look for revealed preferences, which are consequences of theories of motive that are directly observable in real behavior."
- 要点：与其（或除了）直接问受访者，通常更好的办法是看能在真实行为中直接观察到的"显示偏好"。

### E19

- 位置：Part IX.D（Move to an Alternative Model of Scholarly Journal Management）
- 原文："In the law world, students run and edit their school's flagship journal (for example, The University of Chicago Law Review, the Yale Law Journal), though they often consult informally with faculty before making decisions about particular articles."
- 要点：在美国法学界，各校的旗舰期刊由学生运营和编辑，学生做决定前常非正式地征求教师意见。

### E20

- 位置：Part VII.A
- 原文："the justices would say no, that they vote on the basis of some neutral principle(s)."
- 要点：如果问美国最高法院大法官投票是否受政治偏好影响，他们会说不是，说自己依据的是中立原则。
