# Card & DellaVigna 2013：Nine Facts about Top Journals in Economics

- 资料：`card2013nine`，[NBER 工作论文版 PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w18665/w18665.pdf)；正式版发表于 Journal of Economic Literature 51(1)
- 版本：NBER Working Paper 18665（2013 年 1 月）。数据截至 2012 年。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 1 段
- 原文："Publications in the top journals have a powerful influence on the direction of research in economics, on the career paths of young researchers, and on the pay of academic economists."
- 要点：在顶级期刊发表，深刻影响经济学的研究方向、年轻学者的职业道路和学术经济学家的收入。

### E2

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 1 段
- 原文："In this paper we present a descriptive overview of trends among the papers published in the "top-5" economics journals: the American Economic Review (AER), Econometrica (EMA), the Journal of Political Economy (JPE), the Quarterly Journal of Economics (QJE), and the Review of Economic Studies (RES)."
- 要点："五大期刊"指 AER、Econometrica、JPE、QJE 和 Review of Economic Studies。

### E3

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 2 段
- 原文："The combination of rising submissions and falling publications led to a sharp fall in the aggregate acceptance rate, from around 15% in 1980 to 6% today."
- 要点：投稿增加、发表减少，五大期刊总体接收率从 1980 年约 15% 降到 2012 年前后约 6%。

### E4

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 2 段
- 原文："Fifth, the number of authors per paper has increased monotonically from 1.3 in 1970 to 2.3 in 2012, partly offsetting the decrease in the number of articles published per year."
- 要点：每篇论文的平均作者数从 1970 年的 1.3 人增加到 2012 年的 2.3 人。
