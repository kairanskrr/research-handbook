# Verma 2014：Editorial Expression of Concern: Experimental evidence of massive-scale emotional contagion through social networks

- 资料：`verma2014editorial`，[原文（PubMed Central）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4115552/)
- 版本：《美国国家科学院院刊》（PNAS）第 111 卷第 29 期，第 10779 页，DOI 10.1073/pnas.1412583111。主编 Inder M. Verma 针对 Kramer 等 2014 年的 Facebook 情绪传染实验发表的编辑关注声明。全文只有一段，位置写"正文"。

### E1

- 位置：正文
- 原文："Questions have been raised about the principles of informed consent and opportunity to opt out in connection with the research in this paper."
- 要点：这项研究在知情同意和让参与者选择退出这两条原则上受到质疑。

### E2

- 位置：正文
- 原文："Because this experiment was conducted by Facebook, Inc. for internal purposes, the Cornell University IRB [Institutional Review Board] determined that the project did not fall under Cornell's Human Research Protection Program."
- 要点：作者称，因为实验是 Facebook 公司为内部目的做的，康奈尔大学的伦理审查委员会认定该项目不归学校的人类研究保护计划管。

### E3

- 位置：正文
- 原文："Adherence to the Common Rule is PNAS policy, but as a private company Facebook was under no obligation to conform to the provisions of the Common Rule when it collected the data used by the authors, and the Common Rule does not preclude their use of the data."
- 要点：遵守美国保护人类受试者的"共同规则"是 PNAS 的政策；但 Facebook 作为私营公司收集数据时没有义务遵守这套规则。

### E4

- 位置：正文
- 原文："It is nevertheless a matter of concern that the collection of the data by Facebook may have involved practices that were not fully consistent with the principles of obtaining informed consent and allowing participants to opt out."
- 要点：尽管如此，期刊仍然关切：Facebook 收集数据的做法可能不完全符合取得知情同意、允许参与者退出的原则。
