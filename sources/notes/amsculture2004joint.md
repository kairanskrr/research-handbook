# AMS 2004：The Culture of Research and Scholarship in Mathematics: Joint Research and Its Publication

- 资料：`amsculture2004joint`，[原文](https://www.ams.org/profession/leaders/CultureStatement04.pdf)
- 版本：美国数学会（American Mathematical Society，AMS）职业委员会 2004 年声明，一页 PDF，2026-10-06 读取。

### E1

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："In most areas of mathematics, joint research is a sharing of ideas and skills that cannot be attributed to the individuals separately. The roles of researchers are seldom differentiated (in the way they are in laboratory sciences, for example)."
- 要点：在数学的大多数领域，合作研究是想法和技能的共享，无法拆分归到个人；研究者的角色很少像实验科学那样分工。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Joint work in mathematics almost always involves a small number of researchers contributing equally to a research project."
- 要点：数学里的合作几乎总是少数几个人平等地贡献。

### E3

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："For this reason, mathematicians traditionally list authors on joint papers in alphabetical order."
- 要点：因此数学家传统上按姓氏字母顺序排列合作论文的作者。

### E4

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："An analysis of journal articles with at least one U.S. based author shows that nearly half were jointly authored. Of these, more than 75% listed the authors in alphabetical order. In pure mathematics, nearly all joint papers (over 90%) list authors alphabetically."
- 要点：对至少有一位美国作者的期刊论文的分析：近一半是合著；其中超过 75% 按字母排序；纯数学的合著论文超过 90% 按字母排序。

### E5

- 位置：第 4 段
- 原文："In areas of mathematics that are more closely associated to such areas, the culture and traditions may blend together."
- 要点：和大型合作学科联系更紧的数学方向，署名文化可能混合（不一定按字母排序）。

### E6

- 位置：第 5 段
- 原文："While these traditions are well-known to mathematicians, they are often misunderstood by other scholars whose traditions differ. Occasionally, this works against young mathematicians--especially those with names near the end of the alphabet."
- 要点：其他学科的人常误解这一传统，有时对年轻数学家不利，尤其是姓氏字母靠后的人。

### E7

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Determining which person contributed which ideas is often meaningless because the ideas grow from complex discussions among all partners."
- 要点：区分谁贡献了哪个想法往往没有意义，因为想法是在所有合作者的讨论中长出来的。
