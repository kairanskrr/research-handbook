# 英国社会法律研究协会 2021：Statement of Principles of Ethical Research Practice

- 资料：`slsa2021ethics`，[原文 PDF](https://slsa.ac.uk/images/slsadownloads/SLSA_Board_2021/SLSA_Ethics_Statement_-_September_2021.pdf)（学会网站已改版，原链接失效，用 Internet Archive 2025-01-23 快照读取）
- 版本：英国社会法律研究协会（Socio-Legal Studies Association，SLSA，英国研究"法律在社会中如何运作"的学者组织）的研究伦理原则，2001 年制定，2009、2021 年修订。位置写条款编号。

### E1

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："this Ethics Statement seeks not to impose a single model of ethical practice"
- 要点：这份声明不强加单一的伦理模式，而是提供会员在研究中应考虑的一般原则。

### E2

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："as the SLSA does not award ethical approval, it has no legal responsibility where SLSA members have acted unethically."
- 要点：学会本身不做伦理审批。

### E3

- 位置：7.5.1（Gatekeepers）
- 原文："In these situations, members should adhere to the principle of obtaining informed consent directly from research participants to whom access is required, while at the same time taking account of the gatekeeper's interest."
- 要点：通过"把门人"（例如法院、律所的负责人）进入研究场所时，仍要直接取得每位参与者本人的知情同意。

### E4

- 位置：8.1.1（Confidentiality）
- 原文："data about or from a research participant, for instance statements made by an interviewee, are to be treated as confidential and, in the case of attributable statements, should not be attributed to the interviewee without their permission."
- 要点：受访者说的话要保密；未经本人许可，不得把能认出是谁说的话归到他名下。

### E5

- 位置：8.1.2
- 原文："Socio-legal researchers should not give unrealistic guarantees of confidentiality and anonymity."
- 要点：不要对保密和匿名做不现实的保证。

### E6

- 位置：8.1.2
- 原文："In drafting and concluding confidentiality agreements researchers should consider that in some cases they may be subject to legal requirements to disclose information obtained during the course of the research which pertains to serious criminal offences being committed (see below principle 8.3)."
- 要点：订立保密协议时要考虑：涉及严重犯罪的信息，研究者在某些情况下可能负有法定披露义务。

### E7

- 位置：8.2.2
- 原文："Where potential informants and research participants possess a combination of attributes which make them readily identifiable it may be difficult to disguise their identity without introducing an unacceptably large measure of distortion into the data."
- 要点：参与者的几个特征组合在一起就很容易认出是谁时，很难在不严重扭曲数据的前提下隐去身份。

### E8

- 位置：8.2.2
- 原文："In circumstances where it is difficult to protect the anonymity of informants and research participants, they should be informed of this fact in advance where possible."
- 要点：难以保证匿名时，要尽可能事先告知参与者。

### E9

- 位置：8.3.1
- 原文："Socio-legal researchers should be aware of the legal status of researcher/subject confidentiality, where the relevant jurisdiction(s) provide clear rules on this, including those where fieldwork is being carried out."
- 要点：要了解研究者与受访者之间的保密关系在相关法域（包括田野所在地）的法律地位。

### E10

- 位置：8.3.1
- 原文："the socio-legal researcher may be required to give evidence or reveal documents, which may make it impossible for socio-legal researchers to keep certain information confidential without breaking the law."
- 要点：研究者可能被要求出庭作证或交出文件，这时某些信息无法在不违法的前提下保密。

### E11

- 位置：6.3
- 原文："In some cases, where the public interest suggests otherwise and particularly where power is being abused by those being researched, obligations of trust and protection, for instance, through protecting the anonymity of research participants, may weigh less heavily."
- 要点：在被研究者滥用权力等涉及公共利益的情况下，保护匿名等义务的分量可能减轻。

### E12

- 位置：6.3
- 原文："Nevertheless, these obligations should not be discarded lightly"
- 要点：但这些义务不能轻易放弃。

### E13

- 位置：8.6.1（Data collection and storage）
- 原文："Researchers should ensure the security of their data at all stages of research and that their data is managed in line with the data management policies of their institution."
- 要点：研究全过程要保证数据安全，并按所在机构的数据管理政策管理数据。
