# Raibert 1985：Good Writing

- 资料：`raibert1985good`，[原文](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/~pausch/Randy/Randy/raibert.htm)
- 版本：1985 年 1 月短文，CMU 网页转载。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："all that remains is to write bad stuff, and to revise the bad stuff until it is good."
- 要点：好文字的公式：先写出坏东西，再把它改好。

### E2

- 位置：Good Writing is Bad Writing That Was Rewritten
- 原文："This process may take several passes over the same words, sentences, and paragraphs, but a dozen or so passes is typical."
- 要点：几乎所有好文字一开始都是坏的，通常要改十几遍。

### E3

- 位置：Spill the Beans Fast
- 原文："You should summarize your whole story at the very beginning of your paper, without holding anything back."
- 要点：研究论文不是推理小说，一开始就把整个故事（包括发现了什么）讲出来。

### E4

- 位置：Trust Your Readers
- 原文："When you get comments back from your readers, trust what they tell you."
- 要点：读者说哪里看不懂，就相信他；不要争辩自己写得很清楚，而是改写那一部分。

### E5

- 位置：How to Get Unstuck
- 原文："When this occurs, it is likely that you don't have a clear idea what you want to say, or you don't fully understand some of the things you planned to explain."
- 要点：写不下去，往往是因为还没想清楚要说什么，或没完全理解要解释的东西；解释清楚比做出来需要更多理解。

### E6

- 位置：Husband Your Readers
- 原文："Most readers are only effective for one reading anyway, because they know too much about what you are trying to say by the time they attempt a second pass."
- 要点：大多数读者只有第一遍有效，第二遍时已经知道你想说什么了，所以要珍惜读者。

### E7

- 位置：Good Writing is Bad Writing That Was Rewritten
- 原文："Try to have one main idea for each paragraph."
- 要点：每段一个主要意思，用主题句点明。
