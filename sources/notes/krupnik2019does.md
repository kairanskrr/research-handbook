# Krupnik 等人 2019：Does Size Matter? A Critical Review of Meta-analysis in Agronomy

- 资料：`krupnik2019does`，[原文（剑桥大学出版社，开放获取）](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0014479719000012)，DOI [10.1017/S0014479719000012](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0014479719000012)
- 版本：Experimental Agriculture 55(2):200–229, 2019，开放获取（CC BY 4.0），2026-10-08 从出版社下载 PDF。作者来自国际玉米小麦改良中心（CIMMYT）、法国农业国际合作研究发展中心（CIRAD）、美国加州大学圣克鲁兹分校。是对有机农业和保护性农业元分析的评论文章，不是报告规范。位置写小节。

### E1

- 位置：Summary
- 原文："This paper consequently offers three suggestions for how scientists and readers of scientific literature can more carefully evaluate meta-analyses. First, the ways in which papers and data are collected should be critically assessed. Second, the justification of research questions, framing of farming systems, and the scales at which research results are extrapolated and discussed should be carefully evaluated."
- 要点：作者给出更仔细评判元分析的三条建议。第一，审视论文和数据是怎么收集的；第二，审视研究问题的理由、对耕作系统的界定，以及结果外推和讨论的尺度。

### E2

- 位置：Summary
- 原文："Third, when applied to strongly politicized topics situated in an arena of scientific debate, as is the case with OA and CA, more" [...] "conservative interpretations of meta-analytical results that recognize the socially and politically embedded nature of agricultural research is are needed."
- 要点：第三，用于有机农业、保护性农业这类高度政治化、科学上有争论的话题时，对元分析结果要解读得更保守，承认农业研究嵌在社会和政治之中。

### E3

- 位置：Conclusions
- 原文："Small-plot and research station-based experiments may not be representative of whole field or whole farm functioning"
- 要点：小区试验和试验站试验不一定代表整块田或整个农场的运转。

### E4

- 位置：Conclusions
- 原文："The presentation of `global' average results that are decoupled from the context-specific and diverse qualities of farming systems is unlikely to meaningfully inform policy and investment decisions, nor inform ways to improve farmer practice."
- 要点：脱离具体、多样的耕作系统而给出的"全球"平均结果，难以真正指导政策、投资决策或农民改进做法。

### E5

- 位置：Conclusions
- 原文："Our case studies indicate that scientists designing meta-analyses in agronomy grapple with challenges related to the simplification of farmers' complex and variable crop management practices into categories that can be analysed statistically."
- 要点：做农学元分析的人要面对一个难题：把农民复杂多变的作物管理做法简化成能统计分析的类别。

### E6

- 位置：Summary
- 原文："This paper describes the ways in which meta-analysis has been applied to support claims and counter-claims regarding two topics widely debated in agricultural research, namely organic agriculture (OA) and conservation agriculture (CA)."
- 要点：文章考察元分析怎样被用来支持农业研究中两个争议很大的话题（有机农业、保护性农业）上的正反主张。
