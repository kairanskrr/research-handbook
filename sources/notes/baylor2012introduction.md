# 贝勒大学口述历史研究所 2012：Introduction to Oral History（网上工作坊讲义）

- 资料：`baylor2012introduction`，[原网址（已失效）](https://www.baylor.edu/content/services/document.php/66420.pdf)，读自 Internet Archive 2013 年的快照（`https://web.archive.org/web/2013id_/http://www.baylor.edu/content/services/document.php/<编号>.pdf`，编号 66419–66440）
- 版本：美国贝勒大学口述历史研究所（Baylor University Institute for Oral History）"Workshop on the Web: Introduction to Oral History"，每个主题一页 PDF，版权页写 2012 年。2026-10-06 读取。位置写页面标题。注意：讲义写于 2012 年，其中关于具体设备和存储介质（如光盘）的建议已经过时；本文只引用其中不依赖具体设备的原则。

### E1

- 位置：Discovering oral history: What is it?
- 原文："Oral history is a sound recording of historical information, obtained through an interview that preserves a person's life history or eyewitness account of a past experience"
- 要点：口述历史是通过访谈得到的、记录历史信息的录音，保存一个人的生平或对过去经历的亲历讲述。

### E2

- 位置：Discovering oral history: What is it?
- 原文："Two strengths distinguish oral history interviewing: 1) subjectivity, which allows interviewers to ask not only, What happened?, but also, How did you feel about what happened?, and 2) the partnership of co-creation, which invites narrators to interpret and analyze their personal experiences through their own points of view and in their own words."
- 要点：口述历史访谈有两个长处：一是主观性，不仅能问"发生了什么"，还能问"你当时怎么看"；二是共同创作，请受访者用自己的视角和语言解释自己的经历。

### E3

- 位置：Understanding oral history: Why do it?
- 原文："Eyewitnesses to events contribute various viewpoints and perspectives that fill in the gaps in documented history, sometimes correcting or even contradicting the written record."
- 要点：亲历者提供多种视角，填补文献记载的空白，有时还会纠正甚至推翻书面记录。

### E4

- 位置：Discovering oral history: What is it?
- 原文："seek out people who may otherwise leave behind little or no material record for future generations and ask questions that may have never before been asked about a topic or event"
- 要点：口述历史研究者会去找那些可能不会留下什么书面记录的人，问以前没人问过的问题。

### E5

- 位置：Planning a project: Where to begin?
- 原文："Make sure that oral history is the best way to gather the information you seek. Are there people you can reach who can and will tell you what you want to know?"
- 要点：先确认口述历史是获取所需信息的最好办法：有没有你能找到、而且能够也愿意告诉你的人？

### E6

- 位置：Planning a project: Where to begin?
- 原文："Talk to the archivist of the depository to which you will donate your project. Ask what recording formats the depository accepts, what legal agreements are required, and what accompanying materials may be helpful"
- 要点：和将要接收项目的收藏机构的档案人员谈：他们接受什么录音格式、需要哪些法律协议、需要哪些附带材料。

### E7

- 位置：Focusing & researching a topic
- 原文："can be readily researched" / "matches with available narrators" / "can be completed in the allotted time" / "has potential for providing otherwise undocumented information"
- 要点：选题标准包括：便于做背景研究、有合适的受访者、在规定时间内能完成、有可能提供别处没有记载的信息。

### E8

- 位置：Focusing & researching a topic
- 原文："To create informed questions that prompt storytelling" / "To clarify names of people and places mentioned in an interview" / "Between interviews (to clarify and verify)" / "After an interview (for validity and accuracy)"
- 要点：背景研究是为了问出能引出故事的问题、弄清访谈中提到的人名地名；访谈之间做研究是为了澄清和核实，访谈之后做研究是为了检验有效性和准确性。

### E9

- 位置：Selecting narrators
- 原文："Aim for a representative sample of people who can share insights from various perspectives."
- 要点：受访者要能从多种视角讲述，力求有代表性。

### E10

- 位置：Selecting narrators
- 原文："Whenever possible, visit with narrators before the actual recording session to assess the extent of their experience with the topic, their recall ability, and their physical stamina." / "You may conclude that several brief interviews will accomplish more than a single long one because they tire easily."
- 要点：尽可能在正式录音前先拜访受访者，了解他们的相关经历、记忆力和体力；对容易疲劳的人，几次短访谈可能比一次长访谈收获更多。

### E11

- 位置：Selecting narrators
- 原文："The presence of an additional person may discourage probing questions and inhibit candid responses."
- 要点：旁边多一个人，可能让访谈者不好深问，也让受访者不敢坦率回答。

### E12

- 位置：Creating an interview outline
- 原文："Writing out specific questions may hinder candid responses, prevent your following up for details, and impose a rigid agenda on what should be an open, flexible exchange."
- 要点：把问题一字一句写死，会妨碍坦率回答和追问细节，让本该开放灵活的交流变得死板；提纲用关键词列出话题即可。

### E13

- 位置：Composing questions
- 原文："Avoid loaded and leading questions that reveal your biases and suggest you have already formed an opinion of what the answer might be. Not: Wasn't Sandtown a poor, hard place to grow up in? But: Tell me how you felt about the place where you grew up."
- 要点：避免带倾向、带诱导的问题。例如不问"桑镇是不是个又穷又苦的地方？"，而问"说说你对自己长大的地方有什么感受"。

### E14

- 位置：Composing questions
- 原文："Ask one question at a time." / "Use the silent question. Keep quiet and wait."
- 要点：一次只问一个问题；用"沉默的提问"：不说话，等。

### E15

- 位置：Getting the story
- 原文："Give the narrator time to answer each question fully and finish her/his train of thought, then just sit quietly for a few moments. Chances are excellent that the narrator will think of something else to add. Silence is an integral, important part of the oral history interview process."
- 要点：让受访者把话说完，然后安静地坐一会儿，对方很可能还会补充。沉默是访谈中重要的一部分。

### E16

- 位置：Getting the story
- 原文："Keep your feedback quiet, being aware that your sounds can override the narrator's voice during the recording." / "indicating neither agreement nor disagreement"
- 要点：回应要轻，你的声音会盖过录音里受访者的声音；回应要中立，不表示赞同或反对。

### E17

- 位置：Getting the story
- 原文："Rather than disrupt the narrator's train of thought by asking for spellings of unusual words, jot down a phonetic spelling and a clue to its place in the story, then after the interview ask for the correct spelling."
- 要点：不要为了问生僻词怎么写打断对方，先按读音记下，访谈后再问正确写法。

### E18

- 位置：Getting the story
- 原文："Give the narrator a chance to think through difficult subjects."
- 要点：碰到难谈的话题，给受访者时间想清楚。

### E19

- 位置：Getting the story
- 原文："Challenge accounts that you think may be inaccurate , but do not question the narrator's memory or honesty. If you feel you must, refer to other accounts or interpretations you know, asking the narrator for a response or clarification."
- 要点：可以质疑你认为不准确的说法，但不要质疑受访者的记忆或诚实；可以提出你知道的其他说法，请对方回应或澄清。

### E20

- 位置：Getting the story
- 原文："information or switching the recorder off and on. Assure the narrator that sensitive information may be restricted."
- 要点：避免"不录音的话"，不要把录音机关了又开；告诉受访者敏感内容可以限制公开。

### E21

- 位置：Getting the story
- 原文："Sixty to ninety minutes is a good average length for an interview. Concentration diminishes if the interview becomes lengthy."
- 要点：一次访谈 60 到 90 分钟比较合适，太长注意力会下降。

### E22

- 位置：Getting the story
- 原文："Promise only what you actually will do, such as returning to visit again or furnishing copies of recordings or transcripts."
- 要点：只承诺你真会做的事，例如再来拜访、送录音或转录稿副本。

### E23

- 位置：Choosing digital recorders
- 原文："Choose solid-state recorders that create uncompressed PCM WAV or AIFF audio files of CD quality (16-bit, 44.1kHz sampling rate) or better. Avoid digital voice recorders that create highly compressed audio files in proprietary formats"
- 要点：选能录无压缩 WAV 或 AIFF 文件、达到 CD 音质（16 位、44.1 千赫）或更好的录音机；避免录成高度压缩、专有格式文件的录音笔。

### E24

- 位置：Choosing digital recorders
- 原文："Compare recordings made with the internal microphone and an external microphone and choose the setup that works best for your recorder in your unique interview setting." / "Before you go, practice. Before you begin, test. When you are done, protect your recording."
- 要点：比较内置话筒和外接话筒的录音效果，选最适合访谈环境的；出发前练习，开始前测试，结束后保护好录音。

### E25

- 位置：Making contact & setting up
- 原文："Be aware of extraneous noises that will be picked up by the microphone--chiming clocks, humming refrigerators, clattering dishes, et cetera" / "Record both your voices, play back the test recording, and adjust the microphone placement and recorder settings as needed."
- 要点：留意会被录进去的杂音，如钟声、冰箱嗡嗡声、碗碟声；开始前把双方的声音试录一段，回放，再调整话筒位置和设置。

### E26

- 位置：Preparing legal documents
- 原文："Through a contract or deed of gift, narrators agree to donate their interviews to the interviewer, the interviewer's sponsoring organization, or the designated depository." / "Ask your chosen oral history depository what type of donor agreement it prefers."
- 要点：受访者通过合同或捐赠契据（deed of gift），把访谈捐给访谈者、主办机构或指定收藏机构；用哪种协议，问收藏机构。

### E27

- 位置：Preparing legal documents
- 原文："the narrator must transfer copyright ownership to the individual or organization sponsoring the project. When the interviewer is someone other than the designated copyright holder, the interviewer must also transfer copyright to the sponsor."
- 要点：（美国做法）受访者要把版权转让给主办项目的个人或机构；访谈者如果不是指定的版权持有人，也要把自己的版权转给主办方。

### E28

- 位置：Preparing legal documents
- 原文："Ideally, a release form should be signed before an interview series begins."
- 要点：理想情况下，授权书在一系列访谈开始之前就签好。

### E29

- 位置：Preparing legal documents
- 原文："It is very difficult to enforce restrictions that are linked to the duration of a lifetime, so ask the narrator to specify a particular future date to end the restriction."
- 要点：以"在世期间"为期限的限制很难执行，请受访者写明一个具体的解禁日期。

### E30

- 位置：Transcribing oral histories
- 原文："The approximate time required to transcribe one recorded hour is 10-12 hours, depending upon the quality of the sound recording and complexity of the interview."
- 要点：（该讲义 2012 年的估计）转录一小时录音约需 10 到 12 小时，取决于录音质量和访谈复杂程度。

### E31

- 位置：Transcribing oral histories
- 原文："a transcript cannot help but distort what is actually on the sound recording--sarcasm may go unnoticed, for example." / "Many oral historians believe that researchers should listen to the recordings themselves, making their own interpretive judgments rather than relying on the transcript."
- 要点：转录稿必然会失真，例如听不出讽刺语气；很多口述历史研究者认为，研究者应该自己听录音来判断，而不是只靠转录稿。

### E32

- 位置：Transcribing oral histories
- 原文："change as little as possible. The narrator's word choice, including grammar and speech patterns, should be accurately represented."
- 要点：转录时尽量少改，准确保留受访者的用词、语法和说话习惯。

### E33

- 位置：Transcribing oral histories
- 原文："Narrators may preview the draft transcript and provide spellings of proper names and clarification of misunderstood information, resulting in a more accurate account."
- 要点：请受访者预览转录初稿，可以补上专有名词的写法、澄清听错的地方，让记录更准确。

### E34

- 位置：Indexing oral histories
- 原文："A first step toward making an oral history useful for future editing is to create an index or log of the recording, providing the location of subjects on the tape or digital file by minute and second." / "Index soon after the interview."
- 要点：给录音做索引：按分秒标出每个话题在录音中的位置；访谈后尽快做。

### E35

- 位置：Protecting & preserving recordings
- 原文："create consistent file and folder names so that they can be easily recognized and retrieved."
- 要点：文件和文件夹统一命名，便于识别和查找。

### E36

- 位置：Protecting & preserving recordings
- 原文："A list of correctly spelled names and terms mentioned in the interview" / "An explanation of any significant background sounds, equipment problems, or other issues affecting the recording or interview content" / "Research notes, interview outlines, and correspondence with the narrator"
- 要点：录音交出去时要附上：访谈中人名和术语的正确写法清单；对明显背景音、设备故障等问题的说明；研究笔记、访谈提纲和与受访者的通信。

### E37

- 位置：Critiquing & citing oral histories
- 原文："Like any other primary document, an oral history must be explored for validity and accuracy." / "How are the narrator's stories like and different from other versions of the topic? Why?" / "Based on what this interview uncovers, what additional research do I need to do to learn more about the topic or verify these stories?"
- 要点：口述历史和其他一手资料一样，要检验有效性和准确性：受访者的说法和其他版本有何异同、为什么；还要做哪些研究来核实。

### E38

- 位置：Critiquing & citing oral histories
- 原文："Did I ask open-ended questions? leading questions? good follow-up questions?"
- 要点：访谈后听自己的录音，自查：问的是开放式问题吗？有没有诱导性问题？追问得好吗？

### E39

- 位置：Critiquing & citing oral histories
- 原文："Oral histories are usually considered unpublished primary materials." / "provide sufficient information to lead the interested reader to the physical location of the cited source"
- 要点：口述历史通常被视为未出版的一手资料；引用时要给足信息，让读者能找到原件所在的地方。

### E40

- 位置：Reaching the public
- 原文："A signed legal-release form transferring copyright is a must for those considering the creation of public programs. Ethical practice requires you to tell the narrator that the recording might be used in a production or publication."
- 要点：要做面向公众的作品，必须有转让版权的签字授权书；伦理上必须告诉受访者录音可能用于作品或出版。

### E41

- 位置：Using digital media
- 原文："Transfer the unedited, original audio recordings to a computer hard drive as soon as possible." / "Remember LOCKSS: Lots of copies keep stuff safe."
- 要点：尽快把未剪辑的原始录音转到电脑硬盘；记住"多份拷贝保平安"。

### E42

- 位置：Establishing ethical relationships
- 原文："You may be the only person who records your narrators' stories, so take time to include their memories on subjects beyond your own immediate interests."
- 要点：你可能是唯一记录这位受访者故事的人，所以也要记录你眼前研究兴趣之外的回忆。
