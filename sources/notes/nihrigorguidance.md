# NIH：Guidance: Rigor and Reproducibility in Grant Applications

- 资料：`nihrigorguidance`，[原文](https://grants.nih.gov/policy/reproducibility/guidance.htm)（原网站拦截脚本访问，读取自 [Internet Archive 快照](https://web.archive.org/web/2024/https://grants.nih.gov/policy/reproducibility/guidance.htm)）
- 版本：美国国立卫生研究院（National Institutes of Health，NIH）资助申请指南网页，2026-10-06 读取。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Rigorous experimental design for robust and unbiased results
- 原文："Scientific rigor is the strict application of the scientific method to ensure robust and unbiased experimental design, methodology, analysis, interpretation and reporting of results."
- 要点：科学严谨性指严格运用科学方法，确保实验设计、方法、分析、解释和结果报告稳健、无偏。

### E2

- 位置：Research Grants and Mentored Career Development Awards
- 原文："NIH research grant and career development award application instructions and review language focus on four key areas" / "The rigor of the prior research" / "Rigorous experimental design for robust and unbiased results" / "Consideration of relevant biological variables" / "Authentication of key biological and/or chemical resources"
- 要点：NIH 的申请说明和评审标准关注四个方面：前期研究的严谨性、严谨的实验设计、考虑相关的生物学变量、关键生物和化学资源的鉴定。

### E3

- 位置：Consideration of relevant biological variables
- 原文："NIH expects that sex as a biological variable will be factored into research designs, analyses, and reporting in vertebrate animal and human studies."
- 要点：NIH 要求在脊椎动物和人体研究的设计、分析和报告中考虑性别这一生物学变量。

### E4

- 位置：Authentication of key biological and/or chemical resources
- 原文："Key biological and/or chemical resources include, but are not limited to, cell lines, specialty chemicals, antibodies and other biologics." / "The quality of resources used to conduct research is critical to the ability to reproduce the results."
- 要点：关键生物和化学资源包括细胞系、特殊化学品、抗体和其他生物制品等；这些资源的质量对结果能否复现至关重要。

### E5

- 位置：Consideration of relevant biological variables
- 原文："Biological variables, such as sex, age, weight, and underlying health conditions, are often critical factors affecting health or disease."
- 要点：性别、年龄、体重、基础健康状况等生物学变量，常常是影响健康或疾病的关键因素。
