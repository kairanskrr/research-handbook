# Thabane 等人 2010：A tutorial on pilot studies: the what, why and how

- 资料：`thabane2010tutorial`，[原文（PubMed Central）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2824145/)，DOI [10.1186/1471-2288-10-1](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2288-10-1)
- 版本：BMC Medical Research Methodology 10:1, 2010，开放获取（CC BY 2.0），2026-10-07 经 PubMed Central 读取。讨论的是临床三期试验之前的预研究，但关于"预研究为可行性服务、事先写好成功标准、不要从小样本里读效果"的道理可以推广。位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："the main goal of pilot studies is to assess feasibility so as to avoid potentially disastrous consequences of embarking on a large study"
- 要点：预研究的主要目的是评估可行性，避免贸然开始一项大研究而带来灾难性后果。

### E2

- 位置：§3 Reasons for Conducting Pilot Studies
- 原文："In general, the rationale for a pilot study can be grouped under several broad classifications - process, resources, management and scientific" / "Process: This assesses the feasibility of the steps that need to take place as part of the main study. Examples include determining recruitment rates, retention rates, etc." / "Resources: This deals with assessing time and budget problems that can occur during the main study."
- 要点：做预研究的理由可分为四类：流程（正式研究的各个步骤能否走通，如招募率、保留率）、资源（时间和经费会不会出问题）、管理（人员和数据管理）、科学（安全性、剂量、效果及其波动）。

### E3

- 位置：§6 How to Interpret the Results of a Pilot Study: Criteria for Success
- 原文："It is always important to state the criteria for success of a pilot study. The criteria should be based on the primary feasibility objectives." / "In general, the outcome of a pilot study can be one of the following: (i) Stop - main study not feasible; (ii) Continue, but modify protocol - feasible with modifications; (iii) Continue without modifications, but monitor closely - feasible with close monitoring and (iv) Continue without modifications - feasible as is."
- 要点：一定要写明预研究的成功标准，并根据主要的可行性目标来定。预研究的结果一般是四种之一：停止；修改方案后继续；不改方案但密切监控着继续；照原方案继续。

### E4

- 位置：§6，POET 预研究的例子
- 原文："at least 70% of all eligible patients can be recruited" / "complete follow-up in at least 95% of all recruited subjects."
- 要点：例子：一项多中心试验的预研究，事先把成功标准定成具体数字，例如至少招到 70% 的合格患者、至少 95% 的受试者完成随访。

### E5

- 位置：§5 Challenges of and Common Misconceptions about Pilot Studies
- 原文："it can be dangerous to use pilot studies to estimate treatment effects, as such estimates may be unrealistic/biased because of the limited sample sizes."
- 要点：用预研究估计干预效果是危险的：样本少，估计可能不现实、有偏。

### E6

- 位置：§5
- 原文："Quite often the emphasis is wrongly placed on statistical significance, not on feasibility - which is the main focus of the pilot study." / "there are no clear feasibility objectives; no clear analytic plans; and certainly no clear criteria for success of feasibility."
- 要点：常见的错误是把重点放在统计显著性上，而不是可行性上；作者审阅伦理申请时发现，多数预研究没有明确的可行性目标、分析计划和成功标准。

### E7

- 位置：§7 Frequently asked questions
- 原文："Emphasis should not be placed on statistical significance when pilot studies are not powered to detect minimal clinically important differences." / "underpowered studies (with no statistically significant results) are inconclusive, not negative"
- 要点：预研究的样本量不足以检出有意义的差异时，不应强调统计显著性；样本不足的研究没有显著结果，只说明"没有定论"，不说明"没有效果"。

### E8

- 位置：§7 Frequently asked questions
- 原文："a pilot study that shows the main study is not likely to be feasible is not a failed (pilot) study. In fact, it is a success - because you avoided wasting scarce resources on a study destined for failure!"
- 要点：预研究显示正式研究不太可行，并不是预研究失败了，反而是成功：它让你没有把有限的资源浪费在注定失败的研究上。

### E9

- 位置：§5（样本量）
- 原文："It is important that the sample for a pilot be representative of the target study population. It should also be based on the same inclusion/exclusion criteria as the main study. As a rule of thumb, a pilot study should be large enough to provide useful information about the aspects that are being assessed for feasibility."
- 要点：预研究的样本要能代表目标人群，并使用与正式研究相同的纳入和排除标准；经验上，规模要大到能为所评估的可行性方面提供有用信息。

### E10

- 位置：§5（样本量与合并数据）
- 原文："We recommend if any pooling of pilot and main study data is considered, this should be planned beforehand, described clearly in the protocol with clear discussion of the statistical consequences and methods."
- 要点：如果考虑把预研究和正式研究的数据合并，必须事先计划，并在研究方案里写清统计上的后果和方法。

### E11

- 位置：§5
- 原文："None of the above arguments qualifies as sound reasons for calling a study a pilot." / "if a trainee or researcher is assigned a project as a pilot it is important to discuss how the results will inform the planning of the main study."
- 要点："规模小""只是学生项目""经费只够这么多"都不是把一项研究称为预研究的正当理由；被安排做预研究时，要讨论清楚它的结果将怎样服务于正式研究的规划。

### E12

- 位置：§8 Ethical Aspects of Pilot Studies
- 原文："the disclosure of their purpose to study participants requires special wording - that informs them of the definition of a pilot study, the feasibility objectives of the study, and also clearly defines the criteria for success of feasibility."
- 要点：向参与者说明预研究目的时要专门措辞：告诉他们什么是预研究、这次的可行性目标是什么、可行性的成功标准是什么。
