# Lacoste 等 2022：On-Farm Experimentation to transform global agriculture

- 资料：`lacoste2022onfarm`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43016-021-00424-4)
- 版本：Nature Food 3: 11–18，2022（观点文章）。原文文本取自英国格洛斯特大学机构库存放的作者终稿（经同行评审后的定稿，限学术研究使用）。作者来自澳大利亚、法国、英国、美国、加拿大、阿根廷、中国等国的研究机构。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：OFE embeds research in farm management
- 原文："OFE is defined as an innovation process that brings agricultural stakeholders together around mutually beneficial experimentation to support farmers’ own management decisions."
- 要点：农场实验（On-Farm Experimentation）被定义为一种创新过程：让农业各方围绕互惠的试验聚在一起，支持农民自己的管理决策。

### E2

- 位置：OFE embeds research in farm management
- 原文："First, OFE research occurs in farmers’ own fields and at scales that are meaningful to them, rather than in small experimental plots that are designed externally. Second, the private interests of farmers and of other OFE participants are explicitly acknowledged as a prerequisite to negotiate their alignment and build productive relationships."
- 要点：一是在农民自己的田里、以对他们有意义的尺度做，而不是外部设计的小区；二是明确承认农民和其他参与者的私人利益，作为协调、建立合作关系的前提。

### E3

- 位置：Progressing experimentation
- 原文："Spatial and temporal variations in crop and livestock production are far greater than trial treatment effects"
- 要点：作物和畜牧生产在空间和时间上的变异，远大于试验处理的效应。

### E4

- 位置：Progressing experimentation
- 原文："Furthermore, the statistical significance criteria used by scientists provide no indication as to the scope, meaningfulness or local usefulness of results, leaving to farmers the difficult and risky task of adapting recommendations"
- 要点：科学家用的统计显著性标准，说明不了结果的适用范围、实际意义和在当地是否有用，把调整建议这件困难又有风险的事留给了农民。

### E5

- 位置：Figure 2 图注
- 原文："Experimenting at the field scale may involve straightforward assessments of variation, especially in smallholder and subsistence farming, but also because farmers may attach low priority to statistical results and replications."
- 要点：在整块田尺度上做试验，可能只做简单的变异评估，尤其是小农和自给农业，也因为农民可能不太看重统计结果和重复。
