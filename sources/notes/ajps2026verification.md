# American Journal of Political Science：AJPS Verification Policy

- 资料：`ajps2026verification`，[原文网页](https://ajps.org/ajps-verification-policy/)
- 版本：美国政治学刊（American Journal of Political Science，AJPS，美国中西部政治学会主办）官网的核验政策页面，2026 年 10 月访问；页面注明相关文件"即将更新"。位置写段落。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："The corresponding author of a manuscript that is accepted for publication in the American Journal of Political Science must provide materials that are sufficient to enable interested researchers to verify all of the analytic results that are reported in the text and supporting materials."
- 要点：论文被接收后，通讯作者必须提供足够的材料，让别人能核验正文和附录中的全部分析结果。

### E2

- 位置：第 1 段
- 原文："All verification files must be stored in a Dataset within the AJPS Dataverse, on the Harvard Dataverse Network."
- 要点：核验文件必须存放在哈佛 Dataverse 上的 AJPS 专区。

### E3

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："The “American Journal of Political Science Quantitative Data Verification Checklist” and “American Journal of Political Science Qualitative Data Verification Checklist” are designed to help authors ensure that they have provided all necessary files."
- 要点：期刊分别为定量数据和定性数据准备了核验清单。

### E4

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："When the final draft of the manuscript is submitted, the materials will be verified to confirm that they do, in fact, reproduce the analytic results reported in the article. Publication in the American Journal of Political Science is contingent upon provision of complete verification materials and successful verification of their content."
- 要点：提交终稿时，期刊会核验材料是否真能重现文中结果；能否发表取决于材料齐全并通过核验。

### E5

- 位置：第 4 段
- 原文："AJPS articles that do not contain empirical data analyses are not subject to this verification policy."
- 要点：不含经验数据分析的论文不适用这项政策。

### E6

- 位置：第 4 段
- 原文："All other verification materials (e.g., software commands, etc.) still must be provided. The primary reasons for such exemptions are restricted access to datasets and human subjects protection."
- 要点：数据可以申请豁免，但其他核验材料（如程序命令）仍必须提供；豁免的主要理由是数据获取受限和保护人类受试者。
