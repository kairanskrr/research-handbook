# Schünemann et al. (eds.) 2013：GRADE Handbook

- 资料：`schunemann2013grade`，[在线版](https://gdt.gradepro.org/app/handbook/handbook.html)
- 版本：Handbook for grading the quality of evidence and the strength of recommendations using the GRADE approach，2013 年 10 月更新版，GRADE 工作组，免费在线阅读。手册用"质量"（quality）一词，后来的文献多改称"确定性"（certainty）。

### E1

- 位置：§5，Table 5.1
- 原文："High We are very confident that the true effect lies close to that of the estimate of the effect." / "Low Our confidence in the effect estimate is limited: The true effect may be substantially different from the estimate of the effect." / "Very Low We have very little confidence in the effect estimate: The true effect is likely to be substantially different from the estimate of effect"
- 要点：高＝很有把握真实效应接近估计值；低＝把握有限，真实效应可能与估计值差别很大；极低＝几乎没有把握，真实效应很可能与估计值差别很大。（"中"介于两者之间。）

### E2

- 位置：§5.1
- 原文："The GRADE approach to rating the quality of evidence begins with the study design (trials or observational studies) and then addresses five reasons to possibly rate down the quality of evidence and three to possibly rate up the quality."
- 要点：GRADE 先看研究设计（试验还是观察性研究），再看五个可能降级的理由和三个可能升级的理由。

### E3

- 位置：§5，Table 5.1 之后
- 原文："Quality of evidence is a continuum; any discrete categorisation involves some degree of arbitrariness."
- 要点：证据质量本是连续的，分成几级难免有些武断。

### E4

- 位置：§5.3.1
- 原文："When outcomes are subjective it is important to be cautious when considering upgrading because of observed large effects. This is especially true when outcome assessors were aware which group study subjects belonged to (i.e. were not blinded)."
- 要点：结局是主观指标时，尤其是评估者知道分组（没有盲法）时，不要轻易因为效应很大而升级。
