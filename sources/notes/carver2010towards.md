# Carver 2010：Towards Reporting Guidelines for Experimental Replications: A Proposal

- 资料：`carver2010towards`，[原文](https://carver.cs.ua.edu/ReplicationGuidelines.htm)，本地 PDF `papers/pdf/carver2010towards.pdf`
- 版本：RESER 2010（第一届软件工程复现研究研讨会）论文，4 页。SIGSOFT 复现研究标准的部分条目取自此文。

### E1

- 位置：§2.3
- 原文："One of the main benefits of an experimental replication is that it provides researchers with the ability to confirm, refute, or deepen the conclusions drawn from an earlier study."
- 要点：复现研究的主要价值：让研究者能证实、推翻或深化先前研究的结论。

### E2

- 位置：§3.1.2，Level of interaction with original experimenters
- 原文："If the replication is external (i.e. the original researchers are not involved), the level of interaction the replicators had with the original experimenter should be reported. This interaction could range from none (i.e. simply read the paper) to a lot (i.e. original experimenter acted as consultants)."
- 要点：外部复现要报告与原作者的互动程度，从"只读了论文"到"原作者充当顾问"都有可能。

### E3

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："A major difference in the viewpoints taken by these two papers regards the level of interaction between replicating researchers and the original researchers. Without going through the whole debate here, there are legitimate issues on both sides."
- 要点：关于复现者与原作者应该有多少互动，学界有两种对立观点，双方都有合理之处。

### E4

- 位置：§3.1.2，Changes to the original experiment
- 原文："Any changes made to the design, participants, artifacts, procedures, data collected and/or analysis techniques should be discussed along with the motivation for the change."
- 要点：对设计、参与者、制品、流程、所收集数据或分析方法的任何改动，都要写出来，并说明改动的理由。

### E5

- 位置：§3.1.3
- 原文："To make the comparison explicit, it is also important to have a section specifically devoted to comparing the results of the replication with the results of the original study."
- 要点：要专门用一节把复现结果和原研究结果做明确对比。

### E6

- 位置：§3.1.3，Differences in results
- 原文："results from the replication that did not coincide with the results from the original study. Authors should also discuss how changes made to the experimental design (Section 3.1.2) may have caused these differences."
- 要点：对比时要列出与原研究不一致的结果，并讨论自己做的改动是否可能导致了这些差异。

### E7

- 位置：§3.1.4
- 原文："By combining conclusions from the original study with conclusions from the replication, the authors should be able to provide insights that would not have been evident from either study individually."
- 要点：最后要把原研究和复现的结论放在一起，得出单看任何一项研究都看不出的认识。

### E8

- 位置：§3.3 Adaptations for shorter papers
- 原文："The recommendation is to provide all of the information, but with less detail. Comparisons between the original study and the replication can be summarized in tables rather than fully described in the text."
- 要点：篇幅有限（如会议论文）时，仍要给出全部各类信息，只是写得简略些；原研究和复现的对比可以用表格概括。

### E9

- 位置：§3.3 Adaptations for shorter papers
- 原文："If information must be omitted, authors should ensure that the most important details, i.e. those details that relate to changes or differences between the original study and the replication, should be included in the report."
- 要点：不得不删减时，最不能删的是与原研究之间的改动和差异。

### E10

- 位置：§2.3
- 原文："one interesting method use by two studies was to present the results of the replication and the results of the original study in a summary table for easy reference"
- 要点：有的复现论文把复现结果和原研究结果并排放在一张汇总表里，便于对照。

### E11

- 位置：§3.2
- 原文："it is important for the author to place the results into the context of the entire family of studies. Conclusion should be drawn based on knowledge gained by analyzing the results of all studies."
- 要点：如果原研究已经被复现过多次（一个"复现家族"），要把本次结果放到整个家族中看，基于全部研究下结论。
