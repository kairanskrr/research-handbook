# Klein 与 Roodman 2005：Blind Analysis in Nuclear and Particle Physics

- 资料：`klein2005blind`，[原文](https://inspirehep.net/files/123bf8d8ec8f80e60be89076c65dbdbd)
- 版本：投稿给 Annual Review of Nuclear and Particle Science 的预印本，SLAC-PUB-12051（斯坦福直线加速器中心报告，美国能源部资助），INSPIRE-HEP 免费提供。正式版：Annu. Rev. Nucl. Part. Sci. 55 (2005) 141–163。位置写预印本小节号。

### E1

- 位置：§2 Experimenter's Bias and the Motivations for Blind Analyses
- 原文："The crucial difference between experimenter's bias and any other bias in a measurement is that the size of an experimenter's bias cannot be estimated. Thus the only available approach is to use a methodology that prevents or suppresses it."
- 要点：实验者偏差和其他偏差的关键区别是：它的大小没法估计，所以只能靠方法去预防或压制。

### E2

- 位置：§2
- 原文："If the decision to stop analyzing and publish relies on the value of the result--in particular how close it adheres to the experimentalist's preconceptions-- the result may be biased toward the preconceived value." / "It is also probably the most common kind of bias found in nuclear or particle physics."
- 要点：如果"什么时候停止分析、拿去发表"取决于结果是否接近预想值，结果就会偏向预想值（"停止偏差"）。作者认为这大概是核物理和粒子物理中最常见的一种偏差。

### E3

- 位置：§1 Introduction（光速测量的例子）
- 原文："One striking feature is the 17 km/sec shift between the series of experiments from 1930-1940 and later determinations." / "the investigator searches for the source or sources of such errors, and continues to search until he gets a result close to the accepted value. Then he stops!"
- 要点：1930—1940 年的一系列光速测量和后来的测定相差 17 千米/秒。一份事后分析推测：研究者一直找误差来源，直到结果接近公认值才停下。

### E4

- 位置：§1
- 原文："Although the effect is not striking, the measurements do tend to cluster nearer the prior published averages than the `final' value." / "that would not necessarily mean that the experimental results were biased"
- 要点：作者查了四个量的历史测量，发现测量值倾向于靠近当时已发表的平均值，而不是后来的最终值；但作者也说明，这不一定说明有实验者偏差，也可能有别的原因。

### E5

- 位置：§2
- 原文："While blind analysis techniques may not be feasible or necessary in all measurements, as a general rule the possibility of experimenter's bias should be considered in all experiments."
- 要点：盲分析不一定在所有测量里都可行或必要，但每个实验都应考虑实验者偏差的可能。

### E6

- 位置：§2
- 原文："In general, it is crucial that the blind analysis technique be designed as simply and narrowly as possible."
- 要点：盲分析方案要设计得尽量简单、只遮住尽量少的东西。

### E7

- 位置：§2
- 原文："It is also important to realize that blind analyses solve one and only one problem, the influence of experimenter's bias on the measurement." / "either a correction based on a measurement or the inclusion of the bias as a systematic uncertainty still needs to be made, whether the analysis has been done blindly or not."
- 要点：盲分析只解决实验者偏差这一个问题；方法和仪器带来的其他偏差，不论盲不盲，都还要修正或计入系统不确定度。

### E8

- 位置：§2
- 原文："One useful principle that may be adopted is that the publication simply describe the full analysis procedure, in this case to be explicit about which selections were applied after the unblinding."
- 要点：揭盲后如果加了选择条件，一个有用的原则是在论文里完整描述分析过程，明确写出哪些选择是揭盲后加的。

### E9

- 位置：§2
- 原文："There is no reason to publish a result known to be wrong, just because the analysis was done blindly."
- 要点：不能因为做了盲分析，就去发表一个已知是错的结果。

### E10

- 位置：§2
- 原文："Ideally, an experiment should consider such situations in advance, to prepare for such cases."
- 要点：理想情况下，揭盲后可能出现的意外情况要提前想好怎么处理。

### E11

- 位置：§2
- 原文："Collaborations can require that the decision to unblind a result be made as a part of the internal review process, with the consultation or approval of a wider sub-group, and not by the data analysts alone."
- 要点：合作组可以规定，揭盲的决定要在内部审查中、由更大的小组商量或批准，而不是由做分析的人自己决定。

### E12

- 位置：§2
- 原文："Typical objections to the use of blind analysis techniques are that it slows the pace of data analysis, that certain aspects of the analysis become difficult, and that unexpected phenomena can only be found by full exploration of the data. The latter issue is a serious one."
- 要点：对盲分析的常见反对理由：拖慢分析、某些环节变难、意外现象只有把数据全看一遍才能发现。作者承认最后一条是个严重问题。

### E13

- 位置：§2
- 原文："certain cross-checks may only be possible after the blind procedure has been removed."
- 要点：有些交叉检验只有揭盲之后才能做。

### E14

- 位置：§3.2 Hidden Answer Methods
- 原文："In general the hidden answer method works best for experiments measuring a single precise parameter, when that parameter does not depend directly on the number of observed events."
- 要点：隐藏答案法最适合精密测量单个参数、且这个参数不直接取决于观测到的事件数的实验。

### E15

- 位置：§3.2.1 Hidden Detector Parameters（Irvine 引力实验）
- 原文："they kept the value of their near mass known only to 1%--the exact mass known only to someone outside their collaboration." / "Subsequent improvements to the analysis were made and later published, but they nevertheless published the measurement made before these improvements were made."
- 要点：桌面引力实验的例子：近端质量块的准确质量只有合作组外的人知道，组内只知道到 1%；后来分析有改进，但他们仍然发表了改进前的测量结果。

### E16

- 位置：§3.2.2 Hidden Offset
- 原文："the competing groups should begin with different seeds for the pseudo-random generation of the hidden offset C and the hidden sign."
- 要点：同一实验里有几组独立分析时，各组用不同的随机种子生成隐藏偏移量和符号，彼此也看不到对方的结果。

### E17

- 位置：§3.4
- 原文："a blindness scheme is not intended to keep experimenters from looking at the data, but to keep the results of the analysis from influencing the analysis itself."
- 要点：盲分析不是不让实验者看数据，而是不让分析结果反过来影响分析本身。

### E18

- 位置：§1
- 原文："Other examples often cited as possible evidence of bias in measurements are the unusually low χ2 values found in global fits to data sets"
- 要点：全局拟合里卡方值异常地低（各测量彼此吻合得"太好"），常被当作测量可能有偏差的迹象之一。

### E19

- 位置：§4 Conclusion（引 Feynman）
- 原文："The first principle is that you must not fool yourself - and you are the easiest person to fool."
- 要点：作者引用费曼：第一条原则是不能骗自己，而自己是最容易被骗的人。
