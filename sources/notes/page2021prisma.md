# Page et al. 2021：The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for reporting systematic reviews

- 资料：`page2021prisma`，[BMJ](https://www.bmj.com/content/372/bmj.n71)（CC BY 4.0）
- 版本：BMJ 2021;372:n71。页码为 PDF 文件的页序。

### E1

- 位置：PDF 第 2 页
- 原文："The PRISMA 2020 checklist includes seven sections with 27 items, some of which include sub-items"
- 要点：PRISMA 2020 是一份报告清单：7 个部分，27 项。

### E2

- 位置：PDF 第 2 页
- 原文："PRISMA 2020 is not intended to guide systematic review conduct, for which comprehensive resources are available. However, familiarity with PRISMA 2020 is useful when planning and conducting systematic reviews to ensure that all recommended information is captured."
- 要点：PRISMA 规范的是"怎么报告"，不是"怎么做"；但计划和执行时熟悉它，能保证该记录的信息都记下来。

### E3

- 位置：表 1 第 7 项，PDF 第 4 页
- 原文："Present the full search strategies for all databases, registers and websites, including any filters and limits used."
- 要点：要给出所有数据库、网站的完整检索式，包括用了哪些过滤条件和限制。

### E4

- 位置：表 1 第 16a 项，PDF 第 4 页
- 原文："Describe the results of the search and selection process, from the number of records identified in the search to the number of studies included in the review, ideally using a flow diagram"
- 要点：报告从检索到最终纳入的每一步各有多少篇，最好画流程图（PRISMA 流程图）。

### E5

- 位置：与 2009 版相比的主要变化，PDF 第 3 页
- 原文："Modification of the 'Study selection' item in the Methods section to emphasise the reporting of how many reviewers screened each record and each report retrieved, whether they worked independently, and if applicable, details of automation tools used in the process"
- 要点：要报告每篇文献由几个人筛选、是否独立筛选，以及是否用了自动化工具。
