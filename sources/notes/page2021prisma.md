# Page et al. 2021：The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for reporting systematic reviews

- 资料：`page2021prisma`，[BMJ](https://www.bmj.com/content/372/bmj.n71)（CC BY 4.0）
- 版本：BMJ 2021;372:n71。页码为 PDF 文件的页序。

### E1

- 位置：PDF 第 2 页
- 原文："The PRISMA 2020 checklist includes seven sections with 27 items, some of which include sub-items"
- 要点：PRISMA 2020 是一份报告清单：7 个部分，27 项。

### E2

- 位置：PDF 第 2 页
- 原文："PRISMA 2020 is not intended to guide systematic review conduct, for which comprehensive resources are available. However, familiarity with PRISMA 2020 is useful when planning and conducting systematic reviews to ensure that all recommended information is captured."
- 要点：PRISMA 规范的是"怎么报告"，不是"怎么做"；但计划和执行时熟悉它，能保证该记录的信息都记下来。

### E3

- 位置：表 1 第 7 项，PDF 第 4 页
- 原文："Present the full search strategies for all databases, registers and websites, including any filters and limits used."
- 要点：要给出所有数据库、网站的完整检索式，包括用了哪些过滤条件和限制。

### E4

- 位置：表 1 第 16a 项，PDF 第 4 页
- 原文："Describe the results of the search and selection process, from the number of records identified in the search to the number of studies included in the review, ideally using a flow diagram"
- 要点：报告从检索到最终纳入的每一步各有多少篇，最好画流程图（PRISMA 流程图）。

### E5

- 位置：与 2009 版相比的主要变化，PDF 第 3 页
- 原文："Modification of the 'Study selection' item in the Methods section to emphasise the reporting of how many reviewers screened each record and each report retrieved, whether they worked independently, and if applicable, details of automation tools used in the process"
- 要点：要报告每篇文献由几个人筛选、是否独立筛选，以及是否用了自动化工具。

### E6

- 位置：表 1 第 24a 项
- 原文："Provide registration information for the review, including register name and registration number, or state that the review was not registered."
- 要点：报告登记信息：登记平台名称和登记号；没有登记也要说明。

### E7

- 位置：表 1 第 24b、24c 项
- 原文："Indicate where the review protocol can be accessed, or state that a protocol was not prepared." / "Describe and explain any amendments to information provided at registration or in the protocol."
- 要点：说明方案在哪里可以看到（没写方案也要说明）；对登记或方案内容做过的任何修改，都要说明并解释。

### E8

- 位置：表 1 第 12 项
- 原文："Specify for each outcome the effect measure(s) (e.g. risk ratio, mean difference) used in the synthesis or presentation of results."
- 要点：每个结局用了什么效应指标（如风险比、均数差），都要写明。

### E9

- 位置：表 1 第 13d 项
- 原文："If meta-analysis was performed, describe the model(s), method(s) to identify the presence and extent of statistical heterogeneity, and software package(s) used."
- 要点：做了元分析，要写明用的模型、判断异质性有无和大小的方法、用的软件。

### E10

- 位置：表 1 第 13e、13f 项
- 原文："Describe any methods used to explore possible causes of heterogeneity among study results (e.g. subgroup analysis, meta-regression)." / "Describe any sensitivity analyses conducted to assess robustness of the synthesised results."
- 要点：写明探索异质性来源的方法（亚组分析、元回归）和敏感性分析。

### E11

- 位置：表 1 第 14、15 项
- 原文："Describe any methods used to assess risk of bias due to missing results in a synthesis (arising from reporting biases)." / "Describe any methods used to assess certainty (or confidence) in the body of evidence for an outcome."
- 要点：写明怎样评估报告偏倚造成的结果缺失，以及怎样评估每个结局证据的确定性。

### E12

- 位置：表 1 第 18 项
- 原文："Present assessments of risk of bias for each included study."
- 要点：给出每项纳入研究的偏倚风险评价。

### E13

- 位置：表 1 第 20b 项
- 原文："If meta-analysis was done, present for each the summary estimate and its precision (e.g. confidence/credible interval) and measures of statistical heterogeneity."
- 要点：每个元分析都要报告合并估计、它的精确度（置信区间等）和异质性指标。

### E14

- 位置：表 1 第 27 项
- 原文："Report which of the following are publicly available and where they can be found: template data collection forms; data extracted from included studies; data used for all analyses; analytic code; any other materials used in the review."
- 要点：说明数据提取表模板、提取的数据、分析数据、分析代码等是否公开、在哪里。

### E15

- 位置：引言，PDF 第 2 页
- 原文："Terminology used to describe particular review processes has also evolved, as in the shift from assessing "quality" to assessing "certainty" in the body of evidence."
- 要点：术语在变化：对整体证据，从评"质量"改为评"确定性"。

### E16

- 位置：PDF 第 2 页
- 原文："PRISMA 2020 is not intended to guide systematic review conduct, for which comprehensive resources are available."
- 要点：PRISMA 2020 规范怎么报告，不指导怎么做。
