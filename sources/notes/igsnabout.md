# IGSN e.V.：About IGSNs

- 资料：`igsnabout`，[原文](https://www.igsn.org/about-igsns)
- 版本：IGSN e.V.（管理"国际通用样品编号"的非营利组织，2011 年成立，与 DataCite 合作）官网页面，2026-10-06 读取。

### E1

- 位置：页面首段
- 原文："Major problems for achieving this include incomplete documentation of samples in publications, use of ambiguous sample names, and the lack of web-accessible catalogues that allow finding out about existing samples and their archiving location."
- 要点：样品数据难以长期利用的主要问题是：论文里样品记录不完整、样品名称有歧义、找不到样品存放在哪里。

### E2

- 位置：页面首段之后
- 原文："The IGSN ID is a globally unique, persistent, domain-agnostic identifier for material samples."
- 要点：IGSN 是给实物样品用的全球唯一、永久的编号。

### E3

- 位置：页面首段之后
- 原文："When an IGSN is assigned to a sample, the sample can be tracked and traced from that moment until the sample is analysed and the research is published."
- 要点：样品一旦有了 IGSN，从采集到分析、发表都能被追踪。

### E4

- 位置：Facilitating the use of samples
- 原文："it becomes important at the nexus of the big challenges facing humanity and unique samples that can never be taken again. It is also important for the reproducibility of research."
- 要点：有些样品再也采不到了，共享和追踪它们对可复现很重要。

### E5

- 位置：页面首段之后
- 原文："Even though IGSN had its roots in geosciences, it really is now for all sample types from all disciplines."
- 要点：IGSN 起源于地球科学，现在面向所有学科的样品。
