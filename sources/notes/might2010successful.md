# Might 2010：3 qualities of successful Ph.D. students: Perseverance, tenacity and cogency

- 资料：`might2010successful`，[原文](https://matt.might.net/articles/successful-phd-students/)
- 版本：作者主页。页面未注明日期，Internet Archive 最早快照为 2010-08-15。

### E1

- 位置：What doesn't matter
- 原文："qualities like brilliance and quick-thinking are irrelevant in Ph.D. school."
- 要点：聪明、反应快这类品质，对读博并不关键。

### E2

- 位置：Perseverance
- 原文："This is the point (2-3 years in) where attrition peaks."
- 要点：到了要真正推进知识边界的阶段（读博两三年时），没有现成的考试和步骤可循，退出的人最多。

### E3

- 位置：Perseverance
- 原文："To survive this period, you have to be willing to fail from the moment you wake to the moment your head hits the pillow."
- 要点：要熬过这个阶段，就得愿意从早到晚不断失败。

### E4

- 位置：Perseverance
- 原文："The skill you accrete during this trauma is the ability to imagine plausible solutions, and to estimate the likelihood that an approach will work."
- 要点：在反复失败中积累的能力，是想出可行方案、判断一个方法有多大可能成功。

### E5

- 位置：Perseverance
- 原文："Since acceptance rates at good venues range between 8% and 25%, most or all of your papers will be rejected." / "If you stick with it long enough and work at it hard enough, you will."
- 要点：好的会议和期刊接收率低，大部分论文会被拒；坚持足够久、足够努力，终究会发表。

### E6

- 位置：Perseverance
- 原文："This phase of the Ph.D. demands perseverance--in the face of uncertainty, in the face of rejection and in the face of frustration."
- 要点：这一阶段需要毅力：面对不确定、面对被拒、面对挫败。
