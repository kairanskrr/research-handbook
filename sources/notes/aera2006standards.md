# 美国教育研究协会 2006：Standards for Reporting on Empirical Social Science Research in AERA Publications

- 资料：`aera2006standards`，[原文 PDF](https://www.aera.net/Portals/38/docs/12ERv35n6_Standard4Report%20.pdf)
- 版本：AERA 理事会 2006 年 6 月通过，发表于 Educational Researcher 35(6): 33–40；AERA 官网免费提供 PDF。位置写章节和条目编号。

### E1

- 位置：引言
- 原文："They cover, but are not limited to, what are commonly called qualitative and quantitative methods."
- 要点：标准适用于通常所说的定性方法和定量方法，但不限于此。

### E2

- 位置：引言
- 原文："[...] acceptability cannot be determined by using a checklist. In a given case, there may be a sound professional reason why a particular standard is inapplicable."
- 要点：一篇报告是否合格，不能用清单来判定；某项标准在具体研究里可能有正当的专业理由不适用。

### E3

- 位置：Preamble to Standards
- 原文："Two overarching principles underlie the development of these reporting standards: the sufficiency of the warrants and the transparency of the report."
- 要点：标准背后有两条总原则：结论要有充分的依据，报告要透明。

### E4

- 位置：2. Design and Logic
- 原文："Some studies are concerned with drawing causal inferences regarding intended treatments, such as estimating the effects of reducing class size on student achievement. Other studies are concerned with describing particular occurrences and the meanings people give them in a single setting, such as how early literacy is taught and understood in a particular preschool."
- 要点：教育研究的设计很多样：有的要做因果推断（如缩小班额对成绩的影响），有的描述某一个场景里发生了什么、人们怎样理解（如某所幼儿园怎样教早期读写）。

### E5

- 位置：3.1.b
- 原文："Descriptions should include how access, selection, and consent of participants were addressed; how rapport was established; what roles were taken by the researcher (e.g., interviewer, observer) and participants (e.g., respondent, informant) in the data collection; and what significant changes, if any, in relationships and roles of researcher and participants occurred over time."
- 要点：报告要写清怎样进入现场、怎样选择参与者并取得同意、怎样建立信任关系、研究者和参与者各担任什么角色，以及这些关系和角色后来有没有重大变化。

### E6

- 位置：3.1.d
- 原文："When an intervention or treatment is implemented, the intervention or treatment should be described in sufficient detail so that its key features can be identified and used to account for results, and be compared with related interventions or treatments. Similarly, features of control or comparison groups should be described"
- 要点：实施了干预时，要把干预描述得足够详细；对照组或比较组的情况也要描述。

### E7

- 位置：5.8
- 原文："Any considerations that arose in the data collection and processing (e.g., attrition, missing data, ceiling or floor effects, deviations from standard administration of instruments, suspected cheating) that might compromise the validity of the statistical analysis or inferences should be reported."
- 要点：数据收集和处理中出现的、可能影响结论的情况都要报告，例如样本流失、缺失数据、天花板或地板效应、没有按标准程序施测、疑似作弊。

### E8

- 位置：5.10
- 原文："An index of the quantitative relation between variables (an effect size of some kind such as a treatment effect, a regression coefficient, or an odds ratio)"
- 要点：每个关键统计结果都要报告效应量（如干预效应、回归系数、比值比）。

### E9

- 位置：5.10
- 原文："An indication of the uncertainty of that index of effect (such as a standard error or a confidence interval)."
- 要点：还要报告效应量的不确定性，例如标准误或置信区间。

### E10

- 位置：5.10
- 原文："A qualitative interpretation of the index of the effect that describes its meaningfulness in terms of the questions the study was intended to answer."
- 要点：还要用文字解释这个效应量对研究问题意味着什么。

### E11

- 位置：5.12
- 原文："The evidence that serves as a warrant for each claim should be presented. The sources of evidence and the strength and variety of evidence supporting each claim should be described. Qualifications and conditions should be specified; significant counter-examples should be reported."
- 要点：（定性研究）每个论断都要给出支撑证据，说明证据的来源、强度和多样性；写明限定条件，报告重要的反例。

### E12

- 位置：5.12
- 原文："Speculations that go beyond the available evidence should be clearly represented as such."
- 要点：超出现有证据的推测要明确标出来。

### E13

- 位置：6.2
- 原文："When generalization is intended, the author should make clear the intended scope of generalization of the findings of the study."
- 要点：想推广结论时，要写清推广到什么范围。

### E14

- 位置：7.1
- 原文："Study approval in accordance with an Institutional Review Board (IRB) should be stated."
- 要点：要在论文里写明研究经过了机构审查委员会的批准。

### E15

- 位置：7.2
- 原文："Even when direct identifying information is not used, inadvertent disclosure of research participants can happen indirectly."
- 要点：即使不写直接的身份信息，参与者的身份也可能间接泄露。

### E16

- 位置：3. Sources of Evidence
- 原文："Since the role of the researcher and the relationship between the researcher and the participants can influence the data collected, this relationship is addressed in descriptions of sources of evidence."
- 要点：研究者的角色、研究者和参与者的关系会影响收集到的数据，所以要在描述证据来源时交代这层关系。
