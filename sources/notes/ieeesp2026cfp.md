# IEEE S&P 2026：Call for Papers

- 资料：`ieeesp2026cfp`，[原文](https://sp2026.ieee-security.org/cfpapers.html)
- 版本：第 47 届 IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy（2026 年 5 月）征稿启事，2026-10-08 读取。位置写网页小标题。只作为安全方向一个顶级会议某一年的例子。

### E1

- 位置：Systematization of Knowledge Papers
- 原文："we solicit systematization of knowledge (SoK) papers that evaluate, systematize, and contextualize existing knowledge" / "Survey papers without such insights are not appropriate and may be rejected without full review." / "they will be accepted based on their treatment of existing work and value to the community, and not based on any new research results they may contain"
- 要点：会议专门征集"知识系统化"（SoK）论文：评估、梳理已有知识并把它放进脉络里；没有新见解的普通综述不合适，可能不经完整审稿就被拒；SoK 论文按它对已有工作的处理和对社区的价值录用，而不是按新的研究结果。

### E2

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Theoretical papers must make a convincing case for the relevance of their results to practice."
- 要点：理论论文必须令人信服地说明结果和实践的关系。

### E3

- 位置：Ethics Considerations
- 原文："All the papers must use a separate and well-marked section titled “Ethics considerations” at the end of their paper to make the relevant disclosures. If there are no Ethics considerations, the body text of the section should be “None”."
- 要点：所有论文都必须在文末设一个单独的"伦理考量"小节；没有伦理问题，也要写"无"。

### E4

- 位置：Research Ethics Committee
- 原文："IEEE S&P 2026 has a research ethics committee (REC) that will check papers flagged by reviewers as potentially including ethically fraught research." / "may suggest to the PC Chairs rejection of a paper on ethical grounds"
- 要点：会议设研究伦理委员会，检查被审稿人标记为可能有伦理问题的论文，可以建议以伦理理由拒稿。

### E5

- 位置：Ethical Considerations for Vulnerability Disclosure
- 原文："the version of the paper submitted for review must discuss in detail the steps the authors have taken or plan to take to address these vulnerabilities." / "Authors are required to disclose vulnerabilities no later than the rebuttal deadline."
- 要点：投稿版本必须详细讨论作者已经或计划怎样处理所发现的漏洞；最晚在作者回复截止前完成披露。

### E6

- 位置：Ethical Considerations for Human Subjects Research
- 原文："that analyze data derived from human subjects (even anonymized data)" / "Disclose whether the research received an approval or waiver from each of the authors’ institutional ethics review boards (e.g., IRB) if applicable."
- 要点：分析来自人的数据（即使已匿名）的研究，要说明是否得到每位作者所在机构伦理审查委员会的批准或豁免。

### E7

- 位置：Public Meta-Reviews
- 原文："all accepted papers will be published with a meta-review (< 500 words) in the final PDF that lists: (a) the reasons the PC decided to accept the paper and (b) concerns the PC has with the paper."
- 要点：录用论文的最终版本里会附一份公开的综合审稿意见，写明录用理由和委员会仍有的顾虑。

### E8

- 位置：Page Limit and Formatting
- 原文："Submitted papers may include up to 13 pages of text and up to 5 pages for references and appendices, totaling no more than 18 pages." / "Reviewers are not required to read appendices."
- 要点：正文最多 13 页，参考文献和附录最多 5 页，共不超过 18 页；审稿人不必读附录。

### E9

- 位置：Submission Deadlines & Decisions，Reject
- 原文："Rejected papers must wait for one year, from the date of original submission, to resubmit to IEEE S&P."
- 要点：被拒论文要从原投稿日起等满一年才能再投该会议。

### E10

- 位置：Artifacts
- 原文："Papers are strongly encouraged to provide artifact repositories that are anonymized as described above. Theoretical papers are strongly encouraged to submit the proofs as artifacts on such repositories at paper submission time"
- 要点：强烈鼓励投稿时提供匿名的制品仓库；理论论文强烈建议投稿时就把证明作为制品提交。

### E11

- 位置：Important Dates
- 原文："Paper submission deadline: June 5, 2025" / "Paper submission deadline: November 13, 2025"
- 要点：2026 年这届有两个投稿截止日。

### E12

- 位置：Public Meta-Reviews
- 原文："there is no conditional acceptance so papers submitted will be reviewed as is and accepted based on the material that was submitted at the paper submission deadline."
- 要点：没有"有条件录用"，按截稿时提交的材料原样审稿和决定。

### E13

- 位置：开头与 Topics of interest
- 原文："Papers may present advances in the theory, design, implementation, analysis, verification, or empirical evaluation and measurement of secure systems." / "Network security and measurement" / "User studies for security and privacy"
- 要点：论文可以是安全系统的理论、设计、实现、分析、验证，或实证评估和测量；主题包括网络安全与测量、面向安全和隐私的用户研究等。

### E14

- 位置：Ethical Considerations for Vulnerability Disclosure
- 原文："Submitted papers should not include full CVE identifiers in order to preserve the anonymity of the submission."
- 要点：为了保持匿名，投稿版不要写完整的 CVE 漏洞编号。

### E15

- 位置：Anonymous Submission
- 原文："When preparing the artifacts repository authors should take extra care to not include authors’ information in the repository or artifacts content, so as not to break the anonymity of the paper submission."
- 要点：准备制品仓库时，不要在仓库或制品内容里留下作者信息，以免破坏匿名。
