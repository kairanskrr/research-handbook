# 美国大学艺术协会 2014：Standards for the Practice of Art History

- 资料：`caa2014standards`，[原文](https://www.collegeart.org/standards-and-guidelines/guidelines/art-history-ethics)
- 版本：CAA 理事会 2014 年 10 月 26 日通过，取代 1973 年起的《艺术史学者伦理准则》。网页。位置写章节编号。

### E1

- 位置：II. Research and Publishing
- 原文："Art historians are responsible for carefully documenting their findings and then making available to others their sources, evidence, and data. They must guard against misrepresenting evidence and against plagiarism."
- 要点：艺术史学者要仔细记录发现，向他人提供资料、证据和数据，不歪曲证据、不抄袭。

### E2

- 位置：II. Research and Publishing
- 原文："Scholarly integrity demands an awareness of personal and cultural bias and openness to issues of difference as these may inflect methodology and analysis."
- 要点：学术诚信要求意识到个人和文化偏见。

### E3

- 位置：II.B.1
- 原文："It is a maxim of scholarship that authors should be scrupulous in crediting sources, not only of ideas and textual material but also of photographs,"
- 要点：不仅要注明观点和文字的来源，也要注明照片的来源。

### E4

- 位置：II.B.1
- 原文："Comments made by other art historians on the mounts of drawings, on the backs of photographs, recorded in museum dossiers, or reported orally should be cited when relevant to one"
- 要点：写在素描衬板上、照片背面、博物馆档案里的其他学者的意见，乃至口头告知的意见，相关时都应引用。

### E5

- 位置：II.B.2
- 原文："It is, however, the responsibility of art historians working in the living art traditions to deposit original or copies of all field data related to said publications [...] The art historian is responsible for depositing some form of any published material within the community."
- 要点：研究在世艺术传统的学者，要把与发表相关的田野资料原件或副本存放到当地社区的机构，并把发表成果送回社区。

### E6

- 位置：II.B.2
- 原文："All too often, art historians fail to consider the host community, provide the published results of their research or archive field data with the host community or elsewhere."
- 要点：艺术史学者常常没有考虑东道社区，没有把成果或田野资料留给社区。

### E7

- 位置：II.A.1
- 原文："An art historian has the moral obligation to share the discovery of primary source material with his or her colleagues and serious students. [...] The finder should seek to publish research within three years,"
- 要点：发现一手资料有道德义务与同行分享；发现者应争取三年内发表。

### E8

- 位置：II.A.4
- 原文："An art historian may also influence archival custodians to reserve material, even though it may be ten or more years before a book can be published. These practices are improper professional conduct."
- 要点：影响档案保管者为自己预留资料、阻止他人使用，是不当行为。

### E9

- 位置：IV.C.2.c
- 原文："The tactful but outright refusal of gifts from artists may be frustrating, but such practice insures integrity of the scholarly process."
- 要点：婉拒艺术家赠送的作品，能保证学术过程的公正（发表可能抬高作品价格）。

### E10

- 位置：II.E
- 原文："Especially in digital media, the understanding of fair use and intellectual property is rapidly evolving, so it is particularly important to seek the most current resources possible."
- 要点：合理使用和知识产权的理解在快速变化，要找最新的资料。

### E11

- 位置：III.B.1
- 原文："providing that the student is given full credit for what he or she has done."
- 要点：师生合作发表时，学生的贡献要得到充分认可。

### E12

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："These Standards do not at the present time include provisions for their enforcement."
- 要点：这份标准目前没有强制执行条款。

### E13

- 位置：II.F.1
- 原文："There is nothing professionally unethical about an author submitting a manuscript to more than one publisher simultaneously as long as the author notifies the publishers that he or she has made multiple submissions."
- 要点：书稿同时投给多家出版社并不违反职业伦理，只要告知各家。

### E14

- 位置：I.C.1
- 原文："guidelines for evaluating academic publications in a world in which art-history monograph publishing opportunities are diminished"
- 要点：CAA 的终身教职标准涉及"艺术史专著出版机会减少"背景下怎样评价学术成果。
