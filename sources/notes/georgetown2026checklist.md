# Georgetown Law Writing Center 2026：Checklist for Scholarly Writing

- 资料：`georgetown2026checklist`，[原文 PDF（美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心，免费）](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/academics/wp-content/uploads/sites/58/2026/10/Checklist-for-Scholarly-Writing_fcd.pdf)
- 版本：美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心讲义，Alexandra Kotter 2026 年修订，多处引 Volokh 的 Academic Legal Writing。写给**美国法学院学生**。位置写清单的部分编号。

### E1

- 位置：I. Pre-Writing and Planning
- 原文："Tip: If you can't state your thesis in one to two sentences with reasons to support your main claim, your paper isn't ready to draft."
- 要点：如果不能用一两句话说出论点及其主要理由，论文还没到动笔的时候。

### E2

- 位置：II. Structure and Organization
- 原文："Is there a comprehensive roadmap of the entire paper?"
- 要点：引言里要有覆盖全文的路线图。

### E3

- 位置：II. Structure and Organization
- 原文："Are all points of view accurately presented and responded to?" / "Have you directly addressed counterarguments?"
- 要点：各方观点要准确呈现并回应；要直接处理反对论证。

### E4

- 位置：III. Analysis
- 原文："Is the writing analytical and creative, offering new insights, rather than providing a description?" / "If you can't answer, you're likely summarizing, not analyzing."
- 要点：写作应当是分析性的、提供新见解，而不是描述；讲义建议每段后问"那又怎样"，答不上来多半是在概述而不是分析。
