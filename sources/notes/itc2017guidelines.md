# 国际测验委员会 2017：ITC Guidelines for Translating and Adapting Tests (Second Edition)

- 资料：`itc2017guidelines`，[原文 PDF（国际测验委员会官网）](https://www.intestcom.org/files/guideline_test_adaptation_2ed.pdf)
- 版本：第二版，v2.4；国际测验委员会（International Test Commission）官网免费提供。位置写指南编号。

### E1

- 位置：Summary
- 原文："The 18 guidelines are organized into six categories to facilitate their use: Pre-condition (3), test development (5), confirmation (4), administration (2), scoring and interpretation (2), and documentation (2)."
- 要点：18 条指南分六类：前提、编制、确认、施测、计分与解释、文档。

### E2

- 位置：PC-1 (1)
- 原文："Obtain the necessary permission from the holder of the intellectual property rights relating to the test before carrying out any adaptation."
- 要点：改编之前先取得量表知识产权持有人的许可。

### E3

- 位置：PC-2 (2), Explanation
- 原文："This guideline requires that what is assessed should be understood in the same way across language and cultural groups, and this is the foundation of valid cross-cultural comparisons."
- 要点：所测的东西在不同语言和文化群体中要被同样理解，这是跨文化比较成立的基础。

### E4

- 位置：TD-2 (5), Explanation
- 原文："focusing on functional rather than on literal equivalence" / "both designs have flaws, and so rarely would these two designs provide sufficient evidence to validate a translated and adapted test." / "The design too often results in a target language version of the test which maximizes the ease of back translation, but sometimes produces a rather awkward target language version of the test."
- 要点：翻译追求功能等值而非字面等值；正译和回译两种设计都有缺陷，单靠它们很少足以证明译本有效；只做回译常得到"便于回译、但读起来别扭"的译本。

### E5

- 位置：TD-2 (5), Explanation
- 原文："In this approach, a third independent translator or an expert panel identifies and resolves any discrepancies between alternative forward translations, and reconciles them into a single version."
- 要点：双人翻译加协调：两份独立正译，由第三位独立译者或专家组找出并解决分歧，合成一个版本。

### E6

- 位置：TD-5 (8)
- 原文："Collect pilot data on the adapted test to enable item analysis, reliability assessment and small-scale validity studies so that any necessary revisions to the adapted test can be made." / "an item analysis using at least a modest sample size (e.g., 100) can provide much-needed data about the functioning of particular test items."
- 要点：改编后的量表要先收集试测数据，做题目分析、信度和小规模效度研究，再修订；题目分析用一个中等规模的样本（例如 100 人）就能提供很有用的信息。

### E7

- 位置：C-3 (11), Explanation
- 原文："The norms, validity evidence and reliability evidence of a test in its source language version do not automatically apply to other possible adaptations of the test into different cultures and languages."
- 要点：原语言版本的常模、效度和信度证据，不会自动适用于其他语言和文化的改编版。

### E8

- 位置：SSI-2 (16)
- 原文："Only compare scores across populations when the level of invariance has been established on the scale on which scores are reported."
- 要点：只有在报告分数所用的量尺上确立了相应程度的不变性，才能跨群体比较分数。

### E9

- 位置：Doc-1 (17)
- 原文："Provide technical documentation of any changes, including an account of the evidence obtained to support equivalence, when a test is adapted for use in another population."
- 要点：改编量表时，要用技术文档记录所有改动，以及支持等值性的证据。
