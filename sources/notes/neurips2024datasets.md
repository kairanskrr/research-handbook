# NeurIPS 2024：Datasets and Benchmarks Track 征稿启事

- 资料：`neurips2024datasets`，[原文](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2024/CallForDatasetsBenchmarks)
- 版本：NeurIPS 2024 数据集与基准分会征稿页，2026-10-17 读取。
- 说明：机器学习方向顶级会议为数据集和基准论文专设的分会。本页含提交材料要求和审稿时考虑的因素。各年要求会变，以当年页面为准；2025 年的变化见 `neurips2025datasets`。

### E1

- 位置：开头段
- 原文："On the other hand, they do require additional specific checks, such as a proper description of how the data was collected, whether they show intrinsic bias, and whether they will remain accessible."
- 要点：数据集需要专门的检查：数据怎样收集的描述是否恰当，是否有内在偏差，以后是否还能获取。

### E2

- 位置：CRITERIA
- 原文："A key criterion is accessibility: datasets should be available and accessible, i.e. the data can be found and obtained without a personal request to the PI, and any required code should be open source."
- 要点：关键标准之一是可获取：不用私下找负责人要就能找到并拿到数据，所需代码开源。

### E3

- 位置：SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS
- 原文："Dataset documentation and intended uses. Recommended documentation frameworks include datasheets for datasets, dataset nutrition labels, data statements for NLP, data cards, and accountability frameworks." / "Hosting, licensing, and maintenance plan."
- 要点：新数据集的补充材料必须有数据集文档和预期用途（推荐数据表等文档框架），以及托管、许可和维护计划。

### E4

- 位置：SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS
- 原文："Long-term preservation: It must be clear that the dataset will be available for a long time, either by uploading to a data repository or by explaining how the authors themselves will ensure this" / "Explicit license: Authors must choose a license, ideally a CC license for datasets, or an open source license for code"
- 要点：要说明数据集能长期获取（上传到数据仓库，或说明作者怎样保证）；必须选定许可证，数据集最好用知识共享（CC）许可，代码用开源许可。

### E5

- 位置：SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS
- 原文："Highly recommended: a persistent dereferenceable identifier (e.g. a DOI minted by a data repository or a prefix on identifiers.org) for datasets"
- 要点：强烈建议给数据集一个可解析的永久标识符，例如数据仓库分配的 DOI。

### E6

- 位置：SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS
- 原文："For benchmarks, the supplementary materials must ensure that all results are easily reproducible."
- 要点：基准论文的补充材料必须保证所有结果都容易复现。

### E7

- 位置：REVIEWING AND SELECTION PROCESS，All submissions
- 原文："Utility and quality of the submission: Impact, originality, novelty, relevance to the NeurIPS community will all be considered."
- 要点：审稿考虑用处和质量：影响、原创性、新颖性、与社区的相关性。

### E8

- 位置：REVIEWING AND SELECTION PROCESS，Dataset submissions
- 原文："Sufficient detail must be provided on how the data was collected and organized, what kind of information it contains" [...] "and how it will be made available and maintained."
- 要点：文档完整性：要足够详细地说明数据怎样收集和组织、包含什么信息、将怎样提供和维护。

### E9

- 位置：REVIEWING AND SELECTION PROCESS，Dataset submissions
- 原文："Licensing and access: Per NeurIPS ethics guidelines, authors should provide licenses for any datasets released. These should consider the intended use and limitations of the dataset, and develop licenses and terms of use to prevent misuse or inappropriate use."
- 要点：发布的数据集要有许可证；许可和使用条款应考虑预期用途和局限，防止滥用。

### E10

- 位置：REVIEWING AND SELECTION PROCESS，Dataset submissions
- 原文："datasets should minimize the exposure of any personally identifiable information, unless informed consent from those individuals is provided to do so. Any paper that chooses to create a dataset with real data of real people should ask for the explicit consent of participants, or explain why they were unable to do so."
- 要点：数据集应尽量少暴露能识别个人的信息；用真实的人的真实数据建数据集，要征得明确同意，或说明为什么做不到。

### E11

- 位置：REVIEWING AND SELECTION PROCESS，Dataset submissions
- 原文："Note that, if your submission includes publicly available datasets (e.g. as part of a larger benchmark), you should also check these datasets for ethical issues. You remain responsible for the ethical implications of including existing datasets or other data sources in your work."
- 要点：把公开数据集收进自己的基准时，也要检查它们的伦理问题；收进来的数据的伦理后果仍由你负责。

### E12

- 位置：FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS，credentialized access
- 原文："This will be possible on the condition that a credentialization is necessary for the public good (e.g. because of ethically sensitive medical data)" / "This should be seen as an exceptional measure, and NOT as a way to limit access to data for other reasons"
- 要点：只有出于公共利益确有必要时（如伦理敏感的医疗数据），才可以要求用户先取得资格再访问；这是例外，不能借此限制获取。

### E13

- 位置：FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS，Where should I submit it?
- 原文："If the main contribution is a new dataset, benchmark, or other work that falls into the scope of the track (see above), then it is ideally reviewed accordingly." / "the reviewing procedures of the main conference are focused on algorithmic advances, analysis, and applications, while the reviewing in this track is equally stringent but designed to properly assess datasets and benchmarks."
- 要点：主要贡献是新数据集或基准时，最好投这个分会；主会审稿关注算法、分析和应用，这个分会同样严格，但按数据集和基准的标准审。

### E14

- 位置：开头段
- 原文："For instance, datasets can often not be reviewed in a double-blind fashion, and hence full anonymization will not be required."
- 要点：数据集常常无法双盲评审，所以不要求完全匿名。

### E15

- 位置：REVIEWING AND SELECTION PROCESS，Dataset submissions
- 原文："Legal compliance: For datasets, authors should ensure awareness and compliance with regional legal requirements."
- 要点：作者要了解并遵守所在地区对数据的法律要求。
