# ACM 2021：ACM Publications Policy on Research Involving Human Participants and Subjects

- 资料：`acm2021humanparticipants`，[原文](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/research-involving-human-participants-and-subjects)
- 版本：ACM（美国计算机协会）出版委员会 2021 年 8 月 15 日批准的政策。acm.org 对脚本返回 403，本条取自 Internet Archive 2025-12-02 对官方页面的存档（https://web.archive.org/web/20251202211942/https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/research-involving-human-participants-and-subjects）。

### E1

- 位置：标题下
- 原文："Approved by ACM Publications Board on August 15, 2021"
- 要点：ACM 出版委员会 2021 年 8 月 15 日批准。

### E2

- 位置：Policy Statement
- 原文："All authors conducting research involving human participants and subjects must meet appropriate ethical and legal standards guiding such research. In particular, ACM authors must ensure that their human research planning, conduct, and reporting are consistent with their local governing laws and regulations and the general principles detailed below." / "adhering to the principle of informed consent"
- 要点：做涉及人的研究的作者必须符合相应的伦理和法律标准；研究的计划、实施和报告都要符合当地法规和政策列出的原则，原则之一是知情同意。

### E3

- 位置：Scope of Policy
- 原文："This policy applies to all ACM Publications, including ACM Sponsored, co-Sponsored, or In-Cooperation Conferences, International Conference Proceedings Series (ICPS) proceedings, journals, magazines, books, newsletters"
- 要点：适用于所有 ACM 出版物，包括 ACM 主办、合办或合作的会议、期刊等。

### E4

- 位置：Roles and Responsibilities，Authors
- 原文："Where local ethical review boards are required, authors are responsible for having their research reviewed and approved by such boards." / "All ACM Authors must be prepared to provide documentary evidence to ACM that they have adhered to local ethical and legal standards, as ACM may require documentary evidence of such approval at any time following submission of the Work and prior to or after publication of the Work."
- 要点：当地要求伦理审查的，作者负责让研究通过审查批准；作者要准备好向 ACM 提供书面证明，ACM 在投稿后、发表前后任何时候都可能要。

### E5

- 位置：Policy Statement
- 原文："Individual ACM Publications, such as an ACM Journal or Conference Proceeding, may specify a more restrictive policy than required by local legal requirements"
- 要点：具体的 ACM 期刊或会议可以定更严的政策。

### E6

- 位置：Violations and Penalties
- 原文："a Level 2-5 penalty may be assessed" / "a "Note of Concern" on the Work's citation page or a Retraction of the published Work in the ACM Digital Library"
- 要点：违反政策可受处罚，包括在论文页面加"关注说明"或撤稿。
