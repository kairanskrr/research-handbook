# Rana et al. 2024：Standardizing Substrate Selection: A Strategy toward Unbiased Evaluation of Reaction Generality

- 资料：`rana2024standardizing`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.3c01638)（[PubMed Central 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11046462/)）
- 版本：ACS Central Science 2024, 10(4), 899–906，CC BY 4.0 开放获取。德国明斯特大学 Glorius 课题组。原文文本取自 Europe PMC 全文 XML。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："The underlining reason is their subjection to substantial biases, namely, selection and reporting bias (Figure 1C)." / "The former—selection bias—can be explained by the chemist's prioritization of substrates that are expected to give higher yields or are easily accessible. The latter—reporting bias—is given, as most publications do not report unsuccessful experiments or low-yielding results that constitute negative data."
- 要点：底物范围表有两种偏倚：选择偏倚（优先挑预计产率高、容易得到的底物）和报告偏倚（多数论文不报告失败或低产率的结果）。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："However, due to the combinatorial nature of chemistry, even the best scope will always be incomplete."
- 要点：化学结构组合太多，再好的底物范围也总是不完整的。

### E3

- 位置：Results and Discussion（结果讨论末段）
- 原文："We must state clearly that negative results are equally important in delineating the limits of applicability of a newly reported reaction and identifying potential room for further methodological improvements."
- 要点：负面结果对划清新反应的适用边界、找到改进空间同样重要。

### E4

- 位置：Results and Discussion（结果讨论末段）
- 原文："This originates from researchers being hesitant to share unsuccessful examples, which might negatively impact their chances of publication."
- 要点：很多失败例子没有发表，是因为研究者担心影响发表。

### E5

- 位置：Conclusions
- 原文："To demonstrate the broad applicability of a reaction and enable quick adoption in academia and industry, a scope does not necessarily need to be enlarged but broadened in diversity and cleansed of biases."
- 要点：要证明反应适用面广，底物范围不一定要更大，而要更多样、去掉偏倚。

### E6

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："On this note, our group previously developed the robustness screen, which provides a simple way to assess the functional group tolerance of a reaction." / "This screen measures the impact of standardized additives on the reaction outcome and therefore allows an approximation of the applicability and limits of any given report."
- 要点：该课题组此前提出"稳健性筛查"：往反应里加入一组标准化的添加剂，看它们对结果的影响，从而估计反应能耐受哪些官能团、适用边界在哪里。
