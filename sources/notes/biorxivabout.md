# bioRxiv：About bioRxiv

- 资料：`biorxivabout`，[原文](https://www.biorxiv.org/about-biorxiv)（原网站拦截脚本访问，读取自 [Internet Archive 快照](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://www.biorxiv.org/about-biorxiv)）
- 版本：bioRxiv 官方说明页，2026-10-06 读取。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：About bioRxiv
- 原文："bioRxiv (pronounced "bio-archive") is a free online archive and distribution service for unpublished preprints in the life sciences."
- 要点：bioRxiv 是生命科学领域免费的在线预印本存档和发布平台，收的是尚未正式发表的论文。

### E2

- 位置：About bioRxiv
- 原文："Articles are not peer-reviewed, edited, or typeset before being posted online."
- 要点：文章上线前不经过同行评审、编辑或排版。

### E3

- 位置：About bioRxiv
- 原文："An article may be posted prior to, or concurrently with, submission to a journal but should not be posted if it has already been accepted for publication by a journal."
- 要点：可以在投期刊之前或同时发到 bioRxiv；已被期刊接收的文章不应再发。

### E4

- 位置：About bioRxiv
- 原文："Once posted on bioRxiv, articles are citable and therefore cannot be removed."
- 要点：文章一旦发布就可以被引用，因此不能撤下。

### E5

- 位置：JOURNAL PREPRINT POLICIES
- 原文："Most research journals allow posting on preprint servers such as bioRxiv prior to publication." / "Authors should consult these lists and other sources of information before posting on bioRxiv."
- 要点：大多数期刊允许发表前在 bioRxiv 等预印本平台发布；作者发布前应查清目标期刊的政策。

### E6

- 位置：About bioRxiv
- 原文："By posting preprints on bioRxiv, authors are able to make their findings immediately available to the scientific community and receive feedback on draft manuscripts before they are submitted to journals."
- 要点：发预印本可以让成果立刻被同行看到，并在投期刊之前得到反馈。
