# 美国环境保护署 2006：Guidance on Systematic Planning Using the Data Quality Objectives Process (EPA QA/G-4)

- 资料：`epa2006dqo`，[原文 PDF](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2015-06/documents/g4-final.pdf)
- 版本：EPA/240/B-06/001，2006 年 2 月。美国环境保护署（Environmental Protection Agency，EPA）质量体系系列指南之一。位置写 PDF 页码（从封面算起）。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction，PDF 第 11 页
- 原文："Unless some form of planning is conducted prior to investing the necessary time and resources to collect data; the chances can be unacceptably high that these data will not meet specific project needs."
- 要点：采样前不做计划，收来的数据满足不了项目需要的可能性高得不可接受。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction，PDF 第 16 页
- 原文："The DQO Process is the Agency's recommendation when data are to be used to make some type of decision (e.g., compliance or non-compliance with a standard) or estimation (e.g., ascertain the mean concentration level of a contaminant)."
- 要点：数据要用来做决定（例如是否达标）或做估计（例如污染物平均浓度）时，EPA 推荐用数据质量目标流程来做计划。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction，PDF 第 16 页
- 原文："The Triad Approach, developed by EPA's Technology Innovation Program, combines systematic planning with more recent technology advancements, such as techniques that allow for results of early sampling to inform the direction of future sampling."
- 要点：EPA 的 Triad 方法把系统规划和新技术结合，让早期采样的结果指导后续采样。

### E4

- 位置：Introduction，PDF 第 17～18 页，Figure 2
- 原文："The DQO Process consists of seven iterative steps that are documented in Figure 2." / "Step 1. State the Problem. [...] Step 2. Identify the Goal of the Study. [...] Step 3. Identify Information Inputs. [...] Step 4. Define the Boundaries of the Study [...] Step 5. Develop the Analytic Approach. [...] Step 6. Specify Performance or Acceptance Criteria [...] Step 7. Develop the Plan for Obtaining Data"
- 要点：数据质量目标流程有七步，可以反复：说明问题、确定研究目标、确定需要哪些信息、划定研究边界、确定分析方法、规定性能或验收标准、制定获取数据的计划。

### E5

- 位置：Chapter 1，PDF 第 26 页
- 原文："The DQO planning team is typically composed of the project manager, technical staff, data users, and stakeholders."
- 要点：做计划的团队通常包括项目负责人、技术人员、数据使用者和利益相关方。

### E6

- 位置：Chapter 4，PDF 第 41 页
- 原文："When the target population consists of continuous media, such as air, water, or soil, the sampling unit must be defined as some area, volume, or mass that may be selected from the target population."
- 要点：研究对象是空气、水、土壤这类连续介质时，必须把采样单元定义成一定的面积、体积或质量。

### E7

- 位置：Chapter 6，PDF 第 57 页
- 原文："In general, sampling error is much larger than measurement error and consequently needs a larger proportion of resources to control."
- 要点：一般来说，采样误差远大于测量误差，因此需要投入更多资源去控制。

### E8

- 位置：Chapter 6，PDF 第 60 页
- 原文："You can never totally eliminate the possibility that you will make a decision error from your data when performing a statistical hypothesis test."
- 要点：用数据做统计检验时，永远无法完全排除做出错误决定的可能。

### E9

- 位置：Chapter 7，PDF 第 83 页
- 原文："if you expect spatial or temporal variability in the data to be very high, you may wish to consider designs that use less expensive and less precise analytical methods, so that you can focus your resources on collecting a larger number of samples over space and time"
- 要点：如果预计空间或时间上的变化很大，可以考虑用便宜、精度低一些的分析方法，把资源集中到在更多地点和时间采更多样品上。

### E10

- 位置：Chapter 3，PDF 第 38 页
- 原文："If you can address your problem in part through the use of an existing data set, then you should inquire about its quality assurance and control information to assess whether the data will satisfy your needs."
- 要点：用已有数据时，要先查它的质量保证和质量控制信息，判断它是否满足需要。

### E11

- 位置：Introduction，PDF 第 12 页
- 原文："The process uses a common-sense approach to ensure that the level of documentation and rigor of effort in planning is commensurate with the intended use of the information and the available resources."
- 要点：计划的详细程度和严格程度，要和数据的用途、可用的资源相称。

### E12

- 位置：Chapter 7，PDF 第 83 页
- 原文："In a probability-based sampling design, each possible sampling unit has a known probability of being selected, and only those sampling units selected will provide data for the study. In a judgmental sampling design, the sampling units are not assigned a known probability of being selected, but rather, are selected at the discretion of the person in charge of the sampling effort."
- 要点：概率抽样设计里，每个可能的采样单元被抽中的概率是已知的；判断抽样设计里，采样单元由负责采样的人凭判断挑选，被选中的概率未知。

### E13

- 位置：Chapter 7，PDF 第 83 页
- 原文："Examples of common probability-based sampling approaches include simple random sampling, stratified sampling, and systematic and grid sampling. Probability-based sampling allows you to draw quantitative conclusions about the target population, while also properly expressing uncertainty in these conclusions"
- 要点：常见的概率抽样有简单随机抽样、分层抽样、系统抽样和网格抽样；概率抽样让你能对目标总体下定量结论，并恰当表达结论的不确定性。

### E14

- 位置：Chapter 7，PDF 第 83 页
- 原文："However, judgmental sampling designs will not allow you to characterize uncertainty properly. As a result, the outcome of statistical analysis on data collected through judgmental sampling cannot be used to make any type of scientifically-defensible probabilistic statements about the target population."
- 要点：判断抽样无法恰当刻画不确定性；用判断抽样得到的数据做统计分析，不能对目标总体作出站得住的概率性陈述。

### E15

- 位置：Chapter 7，PDF 第 84 页
- 原文："if the spatial properties of a target population (e.g., an area of soil to be characterized) indicate that different subareas of the population have different underlying characteristics (e.g., different portions of the area of interest had different prior uses), then utilizing a stratified sampling design with strata that correspond to these distinct subareas will help ensure proper representation of all characteristics associated with the given population."
- 要点：如果研究区不同部分性质不同（例如土地过去用途不同），按这些部分分层抽样，能保证各部分都有代表。
