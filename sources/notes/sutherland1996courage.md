# Sutherland 1996：Technology and Courage

- 资料：`sutherland1996courage`，[第三方托管 PDF](https://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~ravenben/ucsb/courage-IS.pdf)
- 版本：Sun Microsystems Laboratories Perspectives 96-1，1996 年 4 月。封面注明："Unlimited copying without fee is permitted provided that the copies are not made nor distributed for direct commercial advantage"（以本地提取文本为准）。

### E1

- 位置：§1.1 What is Courage?
- 原文："Courage is what it takes to overcome fear. Fear is an emotion appropriate to perceived risk. Thus, to exhibit courage one must both perceive a risk and proceed in spite of it."
- 要点：勇气是克服恐惧所需要的东西；表现出勇气，就是看到了风险，仍然继续前进。

### E2

- 位置：§2.6 Encouragement in Business
- 原文："If you don't fail regularly you are not trying hard enough things. The trouble, of course, is that it is emotionally much harder to restart after a failure because the risks seem clearer."
- 要点：如果你不经常失败，说明你尝试的事情还不够难；困难在于，失败后重新开始在情感上更难，因为风险看得更清楚了。

### E3

- 位置：§2.2 Formal Mechanisms 末尾
- 原文："My advice for a new graduate student seeking to get started in research is to join an ongoing research group." / "But it matters far less what a new student does than that he do something."
- 要点：给新研究生的建议：加入一个正在运转的研究组；做什么远没有"开始做点什么"重要。

### E4

- 位置：§2.2 Formal Mechanisms
- 原文："Working together with colleagues reduces the labor required from each participant and makes the learning experience more pleasurable. Finally, working in a group reduces the perceived risk inherent in the new material."
- 要点：和同伴一起学习，能减轻每个人的负担、让学习更愉快，还能降低面对新东西时感到的风险。

### E5

- 位置：§2.5 In Academia, it's Hard to Stop
- 原文："Academia seems to me deficient in mechanisms to help people stop old and stale projects."
- 要点：学术界缺少帮人停止陈旧项目的机制；停下来同样需要勇气。

### E6

- 位置：§3.1 Courage to Start
- 原文："Recognizing that it takes courage to get started may help in identifying the excuses you have as excuses, and not reasons."
- 要点：意识到"开始"本身需要勇气，有助于看清自己的拖延理由只是借口。

### E7

- 位置：§3.3 Overcoming Risks
- 原文："A warm-up project is very helpful in getting any new research going. Do something fairly easy and carry it all the way through from beginning to end." / "a warm-up project not only teaches, but also encourages."
- 要点：开始新研究时，先做一个比较容易的热身项目，并从头到尾做完；它既让你学到东西，也给你勇气。

### E8

- 位置：§3.4 Procedures
- 原文："By breaking the task into smaller tasks through emphasizing that only the first dish need be washed or the first expense need be recorded, I reduce my estimate of risk."
- 要点：把大任务拆成小步骤（"先洗第一个盘子"），可以降低对风险的估计，更容易开始。
