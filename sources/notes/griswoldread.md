# Griswold：How to Read an Engineering Research Paper

- 资料：`griswoldread`，[原文](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~wgg/CSE210/howtoread.html)
- 版本：UCSD CSE 210 课程网页，年份待确认

### E1

- 位置：正文，"What are the motivations for this work?"
- 原文："The people problem is the benefits that are desired in the world at large; for example some issue of quality of life, such as saved time or increased safety. The second part is the technical problem, which is: why doesn't the people problem have a trivial solution?"
- 要点：读论文先找动机，动机分两层："人的问题"（现实中想要的好处）和"技术问题"（为什么这个问题没有简单的解法）。

### E2

- 位置：正文，"What are the motivations for this work?"
- 原文："Finally, the motivation and statement of the problem are distilled into a research question, the question that the paper sets out to answer. This might be more focused than the problem stated at the outset. Oftentimes, one or more of these elements are not explicitly stated, making your job more difficult."
- 要点：动机和问题最终被提炼成研究问题；这些要素常常没有明说，需要读者自己找出来。

### E3

- 位置：正文，"What is the work's evaluation of the proposed solution?"
- 原文："An idea alone is usually not adequate for publication of a research paper. This is the concrete engagement of the research question. What argument, implementation, and/or experiment makes the case for the value of the ideas?"
- 要点：要问论文用什么论证、实现或实验来证明想法的价值。

### E4

- 位置：正文，"What is your analysis of the identified problem, idea and evaluation?"
- 原文："Is this a good idea? What flaws do you perceive in the work? What are the most interesting points made? What are the most controversial ideas or points made?"
- 要点：读者要有自己的判断：想法好不好、有什么缺陷、最有意思和最有争议的地方在哪里。

### E5

- 位置：正文，"What is your take-away message from this paper?"
- 原文："Sum up the main implication of the paper from your perspective. This is useful for very quick review and refreshing your memory. It also forces you to try to identify the essence of the work."
- 要点：从自己的角度总结论文最主要的启示，既便于日后回顾，也逼自己抓住本质。

### E6

- 位置：正文，读论文的方法
- 原文："I recommend that you keep a notebook on all the papers you read, or mark-up the papers themselves. [...] In practice, you are not done reading a paper until you can answer all the questions."
- 要点：为读过的每篇论文做笔记或批注；答不出上述所有问题，就还没读完。

### E7

- 位置：正文，读论文的方法
- 原文："If you find that filling out this form doesn't work for you, you can try writing a 250 word abstract of the paper--not rewriting the abstract at the front of the paper, but your abstract, capturing the above five issues from your perspective."
- 要点：可以为论文写一段 250 词的"自己的摘要"，不是改写原摘要，而是从自己的角度概括动机、方案、评估等要点。

### E8

- 位置：正文，读论文的方法
- 原文："Taking time to writing down questions you have about the paper will often surface thoughts that were not initially articulated. Perhaps the paper was vague on key issues, or ignored issues that you think are important."
- 要点：把自己的疑问写下来，常能引出原本没想清楚的想法，例如论文在关键问题上含糊，或忽略了重要问题。
