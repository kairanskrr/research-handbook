# Easterday, Rees Lewis & Gerber 2014：Design-Based Research Process: Problems, Phases, and Applications

- 资料：`easterday2014design`，[原文 PDF（国际学习科学学会机构库）](https://repository.isls.org/bitstream/1/1130/1/317-324.pdf)，DOI [10.22318/icls2014.317](https://doi.org/10.22318/icls2014.317)
- 版本：第 11 届国际学习科学大会（ICLS 2014）论文集第 317–324 页，国际学习科学学会（International Society of the Learning Sciences，ISLS）机构库免费提供；本次经 Internet Archive 2025-08-05 快照读取。位置写小标题和论文集页码。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction，第 317 页
- 原文："There is general agreement that DBR should generate effective educational interventions and useful theory"
- 要点：学界普遍同意，基于设计的研究应同时产出有效的教育干预和有用的理论。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction，第 317 页
- 原文："The classical model of research and development, that is, basic research leading to applied research, leading to development, leading to products, does not work well (Stokes 1997). Alternatively, design, unguided by theory, is likely to be incremental and haphazard."
- 要点：作者认为"基础研究→应用研究→开发→产品"的线性模式效果不好；而没有理论指导的设计，又容易零敲碎打、碰运气。

### E3

- 位置：Problem 1，第 317 页
- 原文："The first problem is the uncertainty about the phases of DBR--the process typically looks different depending on who conducts it. There seems to be no accepted precisely described DBR process at the level of specificity dedicated to other methodologies such as experiments or grounded theory."
- 要点：基于设计的研究没有公认的、像实验或扎根理论那样具体的流程；不同人做出来样子不同。

### E4

- 位置：Design Process，第 319 页
- 原文："The design and DBR processes consist of 6 iterative phases in which designers: focus the problem, understand the problem, define goals, conceive the outline of a solution, build the solution, and test the solution (Figure 1)."
- 要点：作者提出的流程有六个迭代阶段：聚焦问题、理解问题、确定目标、构思方案、做出方案、检验方案。

### E5

- 位置：Focus，第 319 页
- 原文："In the focus phase, designers bound the audience, topic, and scope of the project. The audience specifies whom the product serves, including learners and the other stakeholders affected, such as parents or the community."
- 要点：聚焦阶段要划定服务对象（学习者以及家长、社区等受影响的人）、主题和范围。

### E6

- 位置：Understand，第 319 页
- 原文："In the understand phase, designers study learners, domains, contexts and existing solutions."
- 要点：理解阶段要研究学习者、学科内容、具体环境和已有的解决办法。

### E7

- 位置：Define，第 320 页
- 原文："In the define phase, designers set goals and assessments."
- 要点：确定阶段要定下目标和评估方式。

### E8

- 位置：Define，第 320 页
- 原文："Only after the goal has been defined can a design be said to succeed or fail."
- 要点：只有先定下目标，才谈得上设计成功还是失败。

### E9

- 位置：Conceive，第 320 页
- 原文："In this phase, the designer has not committed to implementing the design in a given medium, but rather creates a non-functional, symbolic or graphical representation that allows the designer to conceptually analyze the solution by determining the components of the design and how they might work together."
- 要点：构思阶段先不动手做成品，而是画出方案的示意，分析它由哪些部分组成、各部分怎样配合起作用。

### E10

- 位置：Build，第 320 页
- 原文："This implementation can be of lower or higher fidelity depending on the stage of the project and the question that the designer wants to test"
- 要点：做出来的原型可以粗糙也可以精细，取决于项目阶段和要检验的问题。

### E11

- 位置：Test，第 320–321 页
- 原文："Testing often uses formative evaluation, which may not establish causality to the extent possible in controlled, randomized experiments, but which can quickly reject bad designs."
- 要点：检验常用形成性评估，它不能像随机对照实验那样确立因果，但能很快淘汰不好的设计。

### E12

- 位置：Iteration，第 321 页
- 原文："Rather than design an entire intervention and discover only at the end that it does not work, iterative design argues for quickly building low fidelity prototypes, testing them, and re-designing--gradually evolving the intervention over time."
- 要点：不要把整套干预做完才发现不管用；应快速做粗糙原型、检验、再设计，让干预逐步演化。

### E13

- 位置：The DBR Process Includes Recursively Nested Research Processes，第 322 页
- 原文："For example, a DBR study of a journalism curriculum might conduct a qualitative study about learners' media practices in the understand phase, or a controlled randomized test of the curriculum in the test phase."
- 要点：基于设计的研究里可以嵌套其他研究，例如在理解阶段做一项定性研究，在检验阶段做一项随机对照检验。

### E14

- 位置：Stage Dependent Search，第 322 页
- 原文："So in the early stages of a project, researchers should focus on low-fidelity prototyping and collecting the minimal amount of data needed to quickly reject failure and identify potential successes."
- 要点：项目早期应集中做粗糙原型，只收集足以快速淘汰失败方案、找出有希望方案的最少数据。

### E15

- 位置：Stage Dependent Search，第 322 页
- 原文："Once researchers have a plausible, well-grounded theory and an implementation with some evidence of success, they can conduct randomized controlled experiments to verify the efficacy of the theory and intervention. If researchers use randomized, controlled, experiments at the beginning stages of a complex design problem, they are likely to waste resources verifying a bad design."
- 要点：有了站得住的理论和初步成功的实施之后，再用随机对照实验验证效果；一开始就做随机对照实验，很可能浪费资源去验证一个不好的设计。

### E16

- 位置：Stage Dependent Search，第 322 页
- 原文："Likewise, if researchers never advance beyond theory building and radically novel designs, they are unlikely to provide strong evidence for the efficacy of an intervention or principle."
- 要点：反过来，如果一直停留在建构理论和做新设计，就很难为干预或原则的效果提供有力证据。

### E17

- 位置：Problem 3 resolution，第 322 页
- 原文："By incorporating scientific processes, DBR produces theories connected to the literature and more rigorously tests interventions."
- 要点：基于设计的研究和一般设计工作的区别：它纳入科学研究过程，产出与已有文献相连的理论，并更严格地检验干预。

### E18

- 位置：Better Design，第 322 页
- 原文："For example, when planning DBR projects, thinking about the test phase has prevented us from jumping to formal evaluation too early or dwelling in theory building too long."
- 要点：规划时想清楚检验阶段，帮作者避免了过早做正式评估，也避免了在理论建构上停留太久。

### E19

- 位置：Communicating Research Process，第 323 页
- 原文："In publishing research and grant applications, the phases more concisely communicate the past history or future plans of a DBR project."
- 要点：在论文和资助申请里，用阶段来组织，能更简洁地说清项目做过什么、打算做什么。

### E20

- 位置：Stage Dependent Search，第 322 页
- 原文："As researchers identify promising prototypes they can focus on theory building with qualitative methods to better understand the issues a design might address and the mechanism through which it affects learning."
- 要点：找到有希望的原型后，可以用定性方法集中建构理论，弄清设计要解决的问题以及它影响学习的机制。
