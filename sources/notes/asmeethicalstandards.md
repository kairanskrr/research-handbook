# ASME：Ethical Standards（出版伦理标准）

- 资料：`asmeethicalstandards`，[原文](https://www.asme.org/publications-submissions/proceedings/submission-review-and-acceptance/ethical-standards)
- 版本：美国机械工程师学会（ASME）网站会议论文集投稿栏目下的"Ethical Standards"页面，2026-10-06 读取。页面说明这些标准大部分取自美国化学会（ACS）的出版伦理指南。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Ethical Obligations of Authors
- 原文："A submitted manuscript shall contain detail and reference to public sources of information sufficient to permit the author's peers to repeat the work or otherwise verify its accuracy."
- 要点：投稿要写出足够的细节和公开资料来源，让同行能重做或核实。

### E2

- 位置：Ethical Obligations of Authors
- 原文："Fragmentation of research papers shall be avoided."
- 要点：避免把研究拆成碎片发表；做了一系列相关工作的人，应让每篇论文完整讲清研究的某一方面。

### E3

- 位置：Ethical Obligations of Authors
- 原文："It is unethical for an author to submit for review more than one paper describing essentially the same research or project to more than one journal of primary publication."
- 要点：把基本相同的研究同时投给多家首发期刊是不道德的。

### E4

- 位置：Ethical Obligations of Authors
- 原文："The authors should reveal to the editor any potential conflict of interest, e.g., a consulting or financial interest in a company that might be affected by publication of the results contained in a manuscript."
- 要点：要向编辑披露潜在利益冲突，例如在可能受论文结果影响的公司有咨询关系或经济利益。

### E5

- 位置：Ethical Obligations of Authors
- 原文："The authors should ensure that no contractual relations or proprietary considerations exist that would affect the publication of information in a submitted manuscript."
- 要点：作者要确保没有合同关系或专有权方面的考虑会妨碍论文内容的发表。

### E6

- 位置：Ethical Obligations of Authors
- 原文："To protect the integrity of authorship, only persons who have significantly contributed to the research or project and manuscript preparation shall be listed as co-authors."
- 要点：只有对研究和论文撰写都有重要贡献的人才能列为合著者。
