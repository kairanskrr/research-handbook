# 美国历史学会 2021：Guidelines for Acknowledgment of Collaborators

- 资料：`aha2021collaborators`，[原文（Internet Archive 快照）](https://web.archive.org/web/20240601010410/https://www.historians.org/jobs-and-professional-development/statements-standards-and-guidelines-of-the-discipline/guidelines-for-acknowledgment-of-collaborators)
- 版本：美国历史学会（AHA）2021 年指南。官网对脚本访问返回 403，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2024-06-01 的快照（旧网址 `historians.org/jobs-and-professional-development/statements-standards-and-guidelines-of-the-discipline/guidelines-for-acknowledgment-of-collaborators`）。指南只有一段。

### E1

- 位置：正文
- 原文："At a time when more historians are working collaboratively to produce monographs, scholarly editions, articles, exhibitions, websites, textbooks and pedagogical materials, and other genres of scholarship, the AHA affirms the importance of fully and publicly recognizing and crediting the work of all contributors."
- 要点：合作完成专著、学术版本、文章、展览、网站、教材的历史学家越来越多；AHA 强调要充分、公开地承认每位贡献者。

### E2

- 位置：正文
- 原文："For example, depending on the project, it might be appropriate for contributors to be listed in an order corresponding to their degree of contribution, or for them to be listed in alphabetical order."
- 要点：署名顺序没有统一规定，视项目而定：可以按贡献大小排，也可以按字母顺序排。
