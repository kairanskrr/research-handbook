# Davies & Gray 2015：Don't let spurious accusations of pseudoreplication limit our ability to learn from natural experiments (and other messy kinds of ecological monitoring)

- 资料：`davies2015pseudoreplication`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.1782)
- 版本：Ecology and Evolution 5(22): 5295–5304，2015，CC BY 开放获取。原文文本取自 Europe PMC（PMC6102510）。这是一篇观点文章，附有作者通过个人网络、生态学邮件列表和推特发放的在线问卷（103 份回复）。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Pseudoreplication is defined as the use of inferential statistics to test for treatment effects where treatments are not replicated and/or replicates are not statistically independent."
- 要点：伪重复指处理没有真正重复、或重复之间在统计上不独立，却仍用推断统计来检验处理效应。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："The concept of pseudoreplication is being applied too dogmatically and often leads to rejection during review."
- 要点：作者认为，伪重复这个概念被用得太教条，常导致论文在审稿时被拒。

### E3

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："These include carefully defining hypotheses and the population of interest, acknowledging the limits of statistical inference and using statistical approaches including nesting and random effects."
- 要点：生态学者的应对办法包括：仔细界定假设和所关心的总体，承认统计推断的局限，使用嵌套、随机效应等统计方法。

### E4

- 位置：Conclusion
- 原文："we argue that accusations of pseudoreplication should not be made without appropriate effort to demonstrate the accusation is statistically valid and ecologically meaningful."
- 要点：作者主张，审稿人指责伪重复之前，要先证明这个指责在统计上成立、在生态学上有意义。

### E5

- 位置：Methods and Results: Is it Just us?
- 原文："There was much interest in the topic, and 103 responses were collected which revealed the following key findings: Fifty‐eight percent of respondents had faced a research question where they felt pseudoreplication was an unavoidable issue"
- 要点：作者的在线问卷收到 103 份回复，其中 58% 的人遇到过他们觉得伪重复无法避免的研究问题。

### E6

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："It is a genuine but controversial issue in ecology particularly in the case of costly landscape‐scale manipulations, behavioral studies where ethics or other concerns may limit sample sizes, ad hoc monitoring data, and the analysis of natural experiments where chance events occur at a single site."
- 要点：伪重复在生态学里确实存在但有争议，尤其是在昂贵的景观尺度操纵试验、受伦理等限制样本量的行为研究、临时监测数据，以及偶然事件只发生在一个地点的自然实验中。

### E7

- 位置：Discussion，Question 1
- 原文："The issue of simple pseudoreplication is very frequent where researchers are trying to understand the effects of unusual individual events such as a flood (Friedman et al. 1996), windstorm (Nagel et al. 2006), wildfire (e.g., Maltby et al. 1990; Johnstone and Kasischke 2005), or volcanic eruption (del Moral and Lacher 2005)."
- 要点：研究洪水、风暴、野火、火山喷发这类单次事件的影响时，简单伪重复问题非常常见。

### E8

- 位置：Discussion，Question 1
- 原文："Strictly this is correct as with, for example, a single disturbance and with monitoring plots located on either side of the disturbance's boundary, one cannot statistically separate spatial effects from “treatment” effects."
- 要点：只有一次干扰、监测样地分布在干扰边界两侧时，严格说无法在统计上把空间差异和"处理"效应分开。

### E9

- 位置：Discussion，Question 1
- 原文："More widely, effects may in part be caused by confounding underlying variables such as variation in soil type, soil moisture, aspect, or elevation. We think, however, that the issue of confounded effects should be firmly separated from the issue of pseudoreplication"
- 要点：差异也可能部分来自土壤类型、湿度、坡向、海拔等混杂变量；作者认为混杂问题要和伪重复问题严格分开。

### E10

- 位置：Discussion，Question 1（作者自己的围栏例子）
- 原文："However, our paper drew a clear line between what we can infer as statisticians and what we can interpret as ecologists."
- 要点：作者在论文里把"统计上能推断什么"和"作为生态学者能解释什么"清楚地分开。

### E11

- 位置：Discussion，Question 1（作者自己的围栏例子）
- 原文："In our case, we took pains to ensure our grazed and ungrazed plots were close together, located on the same soil type, at the same elevation, and on the same aspect."
- 要点：作者特意让放牧和不放牧的样地离得近，并处在同一土壤类型、同一海拔、同一坡向上，以减少混杂。

### E12

- 位置：Discussion，Question 1
- 原文："Reviewers should feel at liberty to ask authors to acknowledge the potential issue, to describe any mitigating action they took in their methods, to be cautious in their interpretations, and to phrase conclusions as new hypotheses."
- 要点：审稿人可以要求作者：承认潜在问题，在方法里写明采取了哪些缓解措施，谨慎解释结果，把结论写成新的假设。
