# 出版伦理委员会 2019：COPE Discussion Document: Authorship（第 2 版）

- 资料：`cope2019authorship`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.24318/cope.2019.3.3)
- 版本：2019 年 9 月第 2 版（第 1 版 2014 年 6 月）。官网对脚本返回 403，本条取自 Internet Archive 2025-05-03 对官方 PDF（publicationethics.org/files/COPE_DD_A4_Authorship_SEPT19_SCREEN_AW.pdf）的存档。

### E1

- 位置：Guidance for editors，第 7 页
- 原文："When authorship disputes arise, submissions can be halted in the review or publication process until the dispute is resolved. Editors are not in a position to fairly adjudicate disputes, nor should they capitulate to threats and bullying by the authors involved in those disputes."
- 要点：出现署名纠纷时，稿件可以在审稿或出版环节暂停，直到纠纷解决；编辑没有条件公正裁决纠纷。

### E2

- 位置：Pre-publication authorship disputes，第 8 页
- 原文："When there is an unresolved question about authorship or competing interests in a submitted paper, the editor should delay the review and production processes until a satisfactory resolution of all unresolved issues has been reached."
- 要点：在审稿件的署名问题没解决前，编辑应推迟审稿和出版。

### E3

- 位置：Post-publication authorship disputes，第 9 页
- 原文："Changes in authorship order, or addition or removal of an author require consent of all listed authors; editors should have in place some mechanism such as a requirement for all authors to agree in writing to the proposed change."
- 要点：发表后改作者顺序或增删作者，要全部署名作者同意，例如书面同意。

### E4

- 位置：Guidance for editors，第 7 页
- 原文："When a paper has already been published and an authorship dispute emerges, the editor has an obligation to correct the scientific record through an erratum, corrigendum, or, in extreme situations, an editorial expression of concern or retraction."
- 要点：已发表论文出现署名纠纷，编辑有义务通过勘误、更正，极端情况下通过编辑关注声明或撤稿来修正记录。

### E5

- 位置：Considerations for academic and research institutions，第 11 页
- 原文："Individuals who may be vulnerable to harassment intimidation, threats or other forms of coercion, such as students and junior faculty, need an institutional environment in which they can bring their concerns about research and authorship to the administration without fear of retribution. Institutional offices of research integrity and grants administration are an important part of a system to educate new researchers and protect whistleblowers."
- 要点：学生和初级教师需要能向机构管理部门反映研究和署名问题而不怕报复的环境；机构的科研诚信办公室是保护举报人的一环。
