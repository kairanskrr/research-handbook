# 约克大学英语与相关文学系：Close reading

- 资料：`yorkclosereading`，[原文](https://www.york.ac.uk/english/about/writing-at-york/writing-resources/close-reading/)
- 版本：英国约克大学英语系写作资源页面，免费公开；2026 年 10 月读取

### E1

- 位置：开头（引 Showalter）
- 原文："...slow reading, a deliberate attempt to detach ourselves from the magical power of story-telling and pay attention to language, imagery, allusion, intertextuality, syntax and form."
- 要点：细读是慢读：摆脱故事的吸引，关注语言、意象、典故、互文、句法和形式。

### E2

- 位置：开头
- 原文："It’s not reading between the lines, but reading further and further into the lines"
- 要点：细读不是读"言外之意"，而是往字句里越读越深。

### E3

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Accurate transcription of quotations is, for some, the first and last rule of close reading."
- 要点：准确抄录引文是细读的头条规则。

### E4

- 位置：Close, not closed readings
- 原文："Close reading has been criticised for being divorced from context and for pulling away from the historical and political engagements of the literary text."
- 要点：细读被批评脱离语境、远离文本的历史和政治关联。

### E5

- 位置：Close, not closed readings
- 原文："we are looking for close readings, not closed readings."
- 要点：要的是细读（close），不是封闭的阅读（closed）。

### E6

- 位置：Close, not closed readings
- 原文："don’t simply list everything you have found; craft it into an argument"
- 要点：不要罗列发现，要组织成论证。

### E7

- 位置：Close, not closed readings
- 原文："Your analysis of your quotation should be twice as long as the quotation itself."
- 要点："二倍规则"：对引文的分析应是引文长度的两倍。

### E8

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Work from the actual text in front of you, not from a sort of mental paraphrase of what the text says."
- 要点：从眼前的原文出发，而不是从脑子里的转述出发。
