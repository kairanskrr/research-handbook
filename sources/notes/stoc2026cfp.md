# STOC 2026：Call for Papers

- 资料：`stoc2026cfp`，[原文](https://acm-stoc.org/stoc2026/stoc2026-cfp.html)
- 版本：第 58 届 ACM 计算理论研讨会（STOC 2026，由 ACM SIGACT 主办）征稿启事，2026-10-08 读取。位置写网页小标题。只作为理论方向一个顶级会议某一年的例子。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Papers presenting new and original research on the theory of computation are sought." / "Papers that extend the reach of the theory of computing, or raise important problems that can benefit from theoretical investigation and analysis, are encouraged."
- 要点：征集计算理论的原创研究；鼓励扩展计算理论边界、或提出能从理论分析中受益的重要问题的论文。

### E2

- 位置：Submission format
- 原文："There is no page limit and authors are encouraged to use the" / "of their paper as the submission."
- 要点：投稿没有页数上限，鼓励直接投"完整版"。

### E3

- 位置：Submission format
- 原文："Each submission should contain within the initial twelve pages following the title page a clear presentation of the merits of the paper, including a discussion of the paper's importance within the context of prior work and a description of the key technical and conceptual ideas used to achieve its main claims." / "This part of the submission should be addressed to a broad spectrum of theoretical computer science researchers, not solely to experts in the subarea."
- 要点：标题页之后的前 12 页要讲清论文的价值：放在已有工作中有多重要、主要结论靠哪些关键技术和概念想法；这部分要写给广泛的理论计算机研究者，而不只是小方向的专家。

### E4

- 位置：Submission format
- 原文："Proofs must be provided that can enable the main mathematical claims of the paper to be fully verified." / "material other than the abstract, table of contents, and the first twelve pages will be read at the committee's discretion."
- 要点：必须提供能完整核验主要数学结论的证明；摘要、目录和前 12 页之外的内容，委员会自行决定读不读。

### E5

- 位置：Submission format，双盲
- 原文："STOC 2026 will use double-blind reviewing" / "Nothing should be done in the name of anonymity that weakens the submission or makes the job of reviewing the paper more difficult." / "authors may post drafts of their papers on the web, submit them to arXiv, and give talks on their research ideas."
- 要点：采用双盲审稿，但不能为了匿名削弱论文或给审稿添麻烦；作者仍可在网上和 arXiv 发草稿、做报告。

### E6

- 位置：Submission Instructions
- 原文："It is expected that authors of accepted papers will make their full papers, with proofs, publicly available on arXiv, ECCC, or a similar preprint service by the camera-ready deadline."
- 要点：录用论文的作者要在定稿截止前把带证明的完整版公开到 arXiv、ECCC 等预印本平台。

### E7

- 位置：Prior and Simultaneous Submissions
- 原文："The conference will follow SIGACT's policy on prior publication and simultaneous submissions." / "SIGACT policy does not allow simultaneous submissions of the same (or essentially the same) material to another conference with published proceedings." / "The only exception to this policy are prior or simultaneous publications appearing in the Science and Nature journals."
- 要点：遵循 SIGACT 关于先前发表和同时投稿的政策：不允许把相同或基本相同的内容同时投给另一个有论文集的会议；唯一例外是 Science 和 Nature 上的发表。

### E8

- 位置：Dates and other relevant information，Camera-ready versions
- 原文："The page limit is 12 pages"
- 要点：录用后的定稿版限 12 页。

### E9

- 位置：Recommended Best Practices for References
- 原文："Authors are asked to avoid "et al." in citations in favor of an equal mention of all authors' surnames."
- 要点：引用时请不要用"et al."，而是平等列出所有作者的姓。

### E10

- 位置：开头，Typical but not exclusive topics of interest
- 原文："algorithms and data structures, computational complexity, randomness in computing, algorithmic graph theory and combinatorics, analysis of Boolean functions, approximation algorithms, cryptography, computational learning theory, continuous and discrete optimization, economics and computation, parallel and distributed algorithms, quantum computing"
- 要点：典型主题包括算法与数据结构、计算复杂性、计算中的随机性、近似算法、密码学、计算学习理论、优化、经济与计算、并行与分布式算法、量子计算等。

### E11

- 位置：Dates and other relevant information
- 原文："Paper submission deadline: Tuesday, November 4, 2025, 4:59pm EST." / "There is no separate abstract submission deadline." / "Notification date: by email on or before February 1, 2026."
- 要点：STOC 2026 的投稿截止为 2025 年 11 月 4 日，没有单独的摘要截止，2026 年 2 月 1 日前通知结果。
