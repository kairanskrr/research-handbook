# Kallio 等人 2016：Systematic methodological review: developing a framework for a qualitative semi-structured interview guide

- 资料：`kallio2016systematic`，[作者接受稿（University of Salford 机构库）](https://salford-repository.worktribe.com/output/1402105/systematic-methodological-review-developing-a-framework-for-a-qualitative-semi-structured-interview-guide)
- 版本：Journal of Advanced Nursing 72(12), 2954–2965；本次读取的是 Salford 机构库中的作者接受稿（Accepted Article，经 Internet Archive 存档）。

### E1

- 位置：Results，Formulating the preliminary semi-structured interview guide
- 原文："A semi-structured interview guide consisted of two levels of questions: main themes and follow-up questions."
- 要点：半结构化访谈提纲有两层问题：主要话题和追问。

### E2

- 位置：Results，Formulating the preliminary semi-structured interview guide
- 原文："After that the order of the main themes could move from the lighter ones to more emotional and in-depth ones"
- 要点：主要话题可以从轻松的开始，再过渡到更情绪化、更深入的话题。

### E3

- 位置：Results，Formulating the preliminary semi-structured interview guide
- 原文："Pre-designed follow-up questions could be beneficial in increasing the consistency of the subjects covered by interviews carried out by different interviewers" / "As a spontaneous follow-up question, the interviewer could ask participants to expand on some particular point that came up in the interview, by asking for more information"
- 要点：事先设计好的追问能让不同访谈者覆盖的内容更一致；临场追问可以请参与者就某一点多讲一些。

### E4

- 位置：Results，Formulating the preliminary semi-structured interview guide
- 原文："Examples of verbal probes included repeating the participant's point, expressing interest with verbal agreement" / "Non-verbal probing referred to remaining silent and allowing the participant to think aloud"
- 要点：言语式追问包括复述参与者的话、用附和表示兴趣；非言语式追问指保持沉默，让参与者边想边说。

### E5

- 位置：Results，Pilot testing of the interview guide
- 原文："the pilot test of the interview guide could be conducted using three different techniques: internal testing, expert assessment and field-testing."
- 要点：访谈提纲的预试有三种方式：研究团队内部试、请外部专家评、找潜在参与者实地试。

### E6

- 位置：Results，Pilot testing of the interview guide
- 原文："This technique could provide critical information about the interview guide in general, for instance removing ambiguities and inappropriate leading questions"
- 要点：内部预试能帮助去掉含糊的问题和不恰当的诱导性问题。

### E7

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："Spontaneous follow-up questions can also create an ethical dilemma, as some ethical review boards want to assess every interview question in advance"
- 要点：临场追问也可能带来伦理上的两难，因为有些伦理审查委员会要求事先审查每一个访谈问题。
