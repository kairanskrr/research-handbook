# Georgetown Law Writing Center 2020：Choosing a Successful Paper Topic

- 资料：`georgetown2020topic`，[原文 PDF（美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心，免费）](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Handout_3-Kaplan_Woods_Choosing_a_Successful_Paper_Topic.pdf)
- 版本：美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心讲义，Lauren H. Uhlig 2012 年初稿，Jane Kaplan 和 Devlin Woods 2020 年修订。写给**美国法学院学生**写研讨课论文、学生评注（note）和学术文章。共 4 页，位置写讲义的小标题。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Your topic must interest both you and your audience. Furthermore, it must be manageable and thesis worthy (i.e., the topic allows for a thesis that is original and significant)."
- 要点：题目要让自己和读者都感兴趣，规模可控，并且"值得立论"：能支撑一个原创、有意义的论点。

### E2

- 位置：1. Choose a General Topic Area
- 原文："Are there recent developments in the law or new cases that pique your interest?"
- 要点：找题目时留意法律的新发展和新判决。

### E3

- 位置：2. Narrow Your Topic to a Manageable Scope
- 原文："Do you want to examine the application of a law or issue in one jurisdiction? Do you want to challenge the current legal definition of a problem or legal term? Do you want to compare one issue to another issue?"
- 要点：缩小题目的几种角度：看一条法律在一个法域的适用；挑战某个问题或法律术语的现行定义；把一个问题和另一个比较。

### E4

- 位置：2. Narrow Your Topic to a Manageable Scope
- 原文："Even if you are narrowing your topic from a very broad topic to something more manageable, do not narrow your topic too much at the very beginning. Your narrower topic should still be broad enough such that you are able to come up with several thesis statements."
- 要点：一开始不要把题目缩得太窄；缩小后的题目仍应宽到能想出几个不同的论点。

### E5

- 位置：3. Pick a Thesis
- 原文："You are only preempted when someone has published a paper with the exact same thesis and defends it for the exact same reasons, or if the facts you have based your thesis on change (i.e., a new case comes out that changes your entire argument)."
- 要点：只有当别人已发表了论点完全相同、理由也完全相同的文章，或你的论点所依据的事实变了（例如出了新判决），你的题目才算被"抢先"。

### E6

- 位置：3. Pick a Thesis
- 原文："Ask yourself: so, what? What is significant about your thesis? Why should the reader be interested?"
- 要点：问自己"那又怎样"：论点有什么意义，读者为什么要关心。
