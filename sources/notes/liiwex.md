# Cornell LII Wex：法律词条（ratio decidendi、obiter dictum、distinguish、precedent、stare decisis、persuasive authority、civil law）

- 资料：`liiwex`，美国康奈尔大学法学院法律信息研究所（Legal Information Institute，LII）的免费法律词典 Wex。读取的词条：[ratio decidendi](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/ratio_decidendi)、[obiter dictum](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/obiter_dictum)、[distinguish](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/distinguish)、[precedent](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/precedent)、[stare decisis](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/stare_decisis)、[persuasive authority](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/persuasive_authority)、[civil law](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/civil_law)
- 版本：2026-10-06 读取；各词条末尾注明了最近审阅时间（2020–2025 年不等）。Wex 由美国编写，举例多为**美国**法院。位置写词条名。

### E1

- 位置：ratio decidendi（2023 年 8 月审阅）
- 原文："The term refers to a key factual point or chain of reasoning in a case that drives the final judgment." / "To determine the ratio decidendi of a case, courts look to the material facts of the earlier case and the law used to justify the decision on those facts."
- 要点：判决理由（ratio decidendi）指案件中推动最终判决的关键事实点或推理链；确定它时，法院看先前案件的重要事实，以及用来在这些事实上论证判决的法律。

### E2

- 位置：ratio decidendi
- 原文："Ratio decidendi is the basis for a court decision and creates binding precedent. Ratio decidendi differs from dictum which describes unnecessary observations that have no force as precedent."
- 要点：判决理由是判决的基础，形成有约束力的先例；附带意见（dictum）是不必要的评论，没有先例效力。

### E3

- 位置：obiter dictum（2020 年 7 月审阅）
- 原文："A comment, suggestion, or observation made by a judge in an opinion that is not necessary to resolve the case, and as such, it is not legally binding on other courts but may still be cited as persuasive authority in future litigation." / "A dissenting opinion is also generally considered obiter dictum."
- 要点：附带意见是法官在判决中所作、对解决本案并非必要的评论，对其他法院没有约束力，但以后可作为有说服力的依据引用；反对意见一般也视为附带意见。

### E4

- 位置：obiter dictum
- 原文："Legal scholars commonly disagree as to what exactly constitutes dicta as opposed to statements of binding precedent or authority in a given case."
- 要点：在具体案件里，哪些话是附带意见、哪些是有约束力的先例，法学者常有分歧。

### E5

- 位置：obiter dictum
- 原文："Dicta are frequently incorporated in later opinions and sometimes even serve as the basis of those opinions."
- 要点：附带意见常被后来的判决吸收，有时甚至成为后来判决的基础。

### E6

- 位置：distinguish（2021 年 6 月审阅）
- 原文："A party usually distinguishes one case from another as part of the argument that because of a certain distinction, the court’s decision in the first case should not apply as precedent ( stare decisis ) to the second case, despite any factual similarities between the cases."
- 要点：区分（distinguish）：论证两个案件之间存在某个差别，因此前案判决不应作为先例适用于后案，即使两案事实有相似之处。

### E7

- 位置：precedent（2024 年 3 月审阅）
- 原文："Therefore, a prior decision serves as precedent only for issues, given the particular facts, that the court explicitly considered in reaching its decision."
- 要点：先前判决只对法院在作出判决时明确考虑过的问题（在其特定事实下）构成先例。

### E8

- 位置：persuasive authority
- 原文："Whether a court decision is persuasive authority or mandatory authority depends on the rank and jurisdiction of the courts involved."
- 要点：一个判决是有约束力的依据还是只有说服力的依据，取决于相关法院的级别和管辖范围。

### E9

- 位置：civil law（2025 年 7 月审阅）
- 原文："As a legal system, civil law refers to a framework based primarily on written codes and statutes , rather than common law , which is based on judicial decisions or precedent . In civil law systems, judges play a more investigative role, focusing on applying the law to the facts rather than interpreting prior rulings ."
- 要点：作为法律体系，大陆法系（civil law）主要以成文法典和制定法为基础，而普通法以判决或先例为基础；在大陆法系中，法官更多扮演调查者角色，重在把法律适用于事实，而不是解释先前的裁决。

### E10

- 位置：stare decisis
- 原文："The previous deciding-court must have binding authority over the court; otherwise, the previous decision is merely persuasive authority ."
- 要点：遵循先例原则下，先前作出判决的法院必须对本院有约束权威，否则先前判决只有说服力。
