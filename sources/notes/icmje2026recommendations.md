# ICMJE 2026：Recommendations for the Conduct, Reporting, Editing, and Publication of Scholarly Work in Medical Journals

- 资料：`icmje2026recommendations`，[原文 PDF](https://www.icmje.org/icmje-recommendations.pdf)
- 版本：国际医学期刊编辑委员会（International Committee of Medical Journal Editors，ICMJE），2026 年 1 月更新版。位置写章节号。

### E1

- 位置：§I.B Who Should Use the Recommendations?
- 原文："Many non-ICMJE journals voluntarily use these recommendations. The ICMJE encourages that use but has no authority to monitor or enforce it. In all cases, authors should use these recommendations along with individual journals’ instructions to authors."
- 要点：很多非 ICMJE 成员期刊也自愿采用这套建议，但 ICMJE 无权强制；作者要把它和具体期刊的投稿须知一起用。

### E2

- 位置：§II.A.2 Who Is an Author?
- 原文："1. Substantial contributions to the conception or design of the work; or the acquisition, analysis, or interpretation of data for the work; AND 2. Drafting the work or reviewing it critically for important intellectual content; AND 3. Final approval of the version to be published; AND 4. Agreement to be accountable for all aspects of the work"
- 要点：作者的四条标准：对构思设计或数据获取、分析、解释有实质贡献；起草或批判性修改；批准最终版本；同意对工作的各方面负责。四条之间是"并且"。

### E3

- 位置：§II.A.2 Who Is an Author?
- 原文："All those designated as authors should meet all four criteria for authorship, and all who meet the four criteria should be identified as authors."
- 要点：署名的人要满足全部四条；满足四条的人都应该署名。

### E4

- 位置：§II.A.2 Who Is an Author?
- 原文："The individuals who conduct the work are responsible for identifying who meets these criteria and ideally should do so when planning the work, making modifications as appropriate as the work progresses."
- 要点：由做研究的人自己判断谁符合标准，最好在计划阶段就定下来，随进展调整。

### E5

- 位置：§II.A.2 Who Is an Author?
- 原文："If agreement cannot be reached about who qualifies for authorship, the institution(s) where the work was performed, not the journal editor, should be asked to investigate."
- 要点：署名有争议时，由研究所在机构调查，而不是期刊编辑。

### E6

- 位置：§II.A.2 Who Is an Author?（大型多作者团队）
- 原文："When a large multi-author group has conducted the work, the group ideally should decide who will be an author before the work is started and confirm who is an author before submitting the manuscript for publication."
- 要点：大型多作者团队最好在工作开始前就决定谁署名，投稿前再确认。

### E7

- 位置：§II.A.3 Non-Author Contributors
- 原文："Examples of activities [...] that alone (without other contributions) do not qualify a contributor for authorship are acquisition of funding; general supervision of a research group or general administrative support; and writing assistance, technical editing, language editing, and proofreading."
- 要点：只拿到经费、只做一般性指导或行政支持、只做写作协助和语言润色，单凭这些不够署名。

### E8

- 位置：§II.A.3 Non-Author Contributors
- 原文："Those whose contributions do not justify authorship may be acknowledged individually or together as a group under a single heading (e.g., “Clinical Investigators” or “Participating Investigators”), and their contributions should be specified"
- 要点：不够署名的贡献者可以单独致谢，也可以用"临床研究者"等集体名义致谢，并写明各自的贡献。

### E9

- 位置：§II.B.1 Participants
- 原文："The potential for conflict of interest and bias exists when professional judgment concerning a primary interest (such as patients' welfare or the validity of research) may be influenced by a secondary interest (such as financial gain). Perceptions of conflict of interest are as important as actual conflicts of interest."
- 要点：利益冲突指专业判断（如病人福祉、研究可靠性）可能受次要利益（如经济收益）影响；"看起来有冲突"和真有冲突一样重要。

### E10

- 位置：§II.B.1 Participants
- 原文："Financial relationships (such as employment, consultancies, stock ownership or options, honoraria, patents, and paid expert testimony) are the most easily identifiable, the ones most often judged to represent potential conflicts of interest"
- 要点：雇佣、顾问、持股、酬金、专利、有偿专家证词等经济关系，最容易识别，也最常被认为构成潜在利益冲突。

### E11

- 位置：§II.E Protection of Research Participants
- 原文："All investigators should ensure that the planning, conduct, and reporting of human research are in accordance with the Helsinki Declaration as revised in 2024"
- 要点：涉及人的研究，计划、实施和报告都要符合 2024 年修订的《赫尔辛基宣言》。

### E12

- 位置：§III.B Scientific Misconduct
- 原文："Some people consider failure to publish the results of clinical trials and other human studies a form of scientific misconduct."
- 要点：有人认为不发表临床试验和其他人体研究的结果是一种学术不端。

### E13

- 位置：§III.D.3 Preprints
- 原文："Authors should become familiar with the policies of journals they wish to submit their work to prior to posting work on a preprint server."
- 要点：发预印本之前，先了解目标期刊对预印本的政策。

### E14

- 位置：§III.D.3.b Submitting Manuscripts That Are in Preprint Archives to a Peer-Reviewed Journal
- 原文："Authors should inform a journal if the work submitted to the journal has been posted on a preprint server and provide a link to the preprint"
- 要点：投稿时要告诉期刊这篇工作已经发过预印本，并给出链接。

### E15

- 位置：§III.L.1 Registration
- 原文："Briefly, the ICMJE recommends that all medical journal editors require, registration of clinical trials in a public trials registry at or before the time of first patient consent for enrollment as a condition of consideration for publication."
- 要点：ICMJE 建议期刊把"在第一位病人签署入组同意时或之前完成公开登记"作为考虑发表的前提条件。

### E16

- 位置：§III.L.1 Registration
- 原文："The ICMJE defines a clinical trial as any research project that prospectively assigns people or a group of people to an intervention, with or without concurrent comparison or control groups, to study the relationship between a health-related intervention and a health outcome."
- 要点：临床试验的定义：前瞻性地把人分配到某种干预，研究健康相关干预和健康结局的关系；有没有对照组都算。

### E17

- 位置：§III.L.1 Registration
- 原文："Health-related interventions are those used to modify a biomedical or health-related outcome; examples include drugs, surgical procedures, devices, behavioral treatments, educational programs, dietary interventions, quality improvement interventions, and process-of-care changes."
- 要点：干预的范围很广：药物、手术、器械，也包括行为治疗、教育项目、饮食干预、质量改进和诊疗流程改变。

### E18

- 位置：§III.L.1 Registration
- 原文："Approval to conduct a study from an independent local, regional, or national review body (e.g., ethics committee, institutional review board) does not fulfill the ICMJE requirement for prospective clinical trial registration."
- 要点：伦理委员会批准不等于完成了试验登记。

### E19

- 位置：§III.L.1 Registration
- 原文："The purpose of clinical trial registration is to prevent selective publication and selective reporting of research outcomes, to prevent unnecessary duplication of research effort, to help patients and the public know what trials are planned or ongoing into which they might want to enroll"
- 要点：登记是为了防止选择性发表和选择性报告结局、避免不必要的重复研究、让病人和公众知道有哪些试验可以参加。

### E20

- 位置：§III.L.1 Registration
- 原文："Retrospective registration, for example at the time of manuscript submission, meets none of these purposes."
- 要点：事后补登记（比如投稿时才登记）达不到任何一个目的。

### E21

- 位置：§III.L.1 Registration
- 原文："the ICMJE encourages registration of research with non-trial designs, but because the exposure or intervention in non-trial research is not dictated by the researchers, the ICMJE does not require it."
- 要点：观察性研究等非试验设计，ICMJE 鼓励登记但不强制。

### E22

- 位置：§III.L.3 Data Sharing
- 原文："1. As of 1 July 2018 manuscripts submitted to ICMJE journals that report the results of clinical trials must contain a data sharing statement as described below. 2. Clinical trials that begin enrolling participants on or after 1 January 2019 must include a data sharing plan in the trial’s registration."
- 要点：2018 年 7 月 1 日起，投到 ICMJE 期刊的临床试验论文必须有数据共享声明；2019 年 1 月 1 日及以后开始入组的试验，登记时就要写数据共享计划。

### E23

- 位置：§III.L.3 Data Sharing
- 原文："Data sharing statements must indicate the following: whether individual deidentified participant data (including data dictionaries) will be shared (“undecided” is not an acceptable answer); what data in particular will be shared; whether additional, related documents will be available (e.g., study protocol, statistical analysis plan, etc.); when the data will become available and for how long; by what access criteria data will be shared"
- 要点：数据共享声明要写清：是否共享去标识化的个体数据（不能写"未定"）、共享哪些数据、是否提供方案和统计分析计划等文件、何时开放、开放多久、按什么条件获取。

### E24

- 位置：§IV.A.2 Reporting Guidelines
- 原文："Journals are encouraged to ask authors to follow these guidelines because they help authors describe the study in enough detail for it to be evaluated by editors, reviewers, readers, and other [...] researchers evaluating the medical literature."
- 要点：鼓励期刊要求作者遵循报告规范，因为规范帮助作者把研究写得足够详细，便于编辑、审稿人和读者评价。

### E25

- 位置：§IV.A.2 Reporting Guidelines
- 原文："examples include CONSORT (www.consort-spirit.org/) for randomized trials, STROBE for observational studies (http://strobe-statement.org/), PRISMA for systematic reviews and meta-analyses (http:// prisma-statement.org/), and STARD for studies of diagnostic accuracy"
- 要点：常用报告规范：随机试验用 CONSORT，观察性研究用 STROBE，系统综述和荟萃分析用 PRISMA，诊断准确性研究用 STARD。

### E26

- 位置：II.A.2 Who Is an Author?
- 原文："All those designated as authors should meet all four criteria for authorship, and all who meet the four criteria should be identified as authors. Those who do not meet all four criteria should be acknowledged"
- 要点：作者都应满足全部四个条件；满足四个条件的人都应列为作者；不满足的写进致谢。

### E27

- 位置：II.A.2 Who Is an Author?
- 原文："The criteria used to determine the order in which [...] authors are listed on the byline may vary, and are to be decided collectively by the author group and not by editors."
- 要点：作者排序的标准可以不同，由作者们共同决定，不由编辑决定。

### E28

- 位置：II.A.2 Who Is an Author?
- 原文："The corresponding author is the one individual who takes primary responsibility for communication with the journal during the manuscript submission, peer-review, and publication process."
- 要点：通讯作者是在投稿、审稿和发表过程中主要负责和期刊沟通的那个人。

### E29

- 位置：II.A.4 Artificial Intelligence (AI)–Assisted Technology
- 原文："Chatbots (such as ChatGPT) should not be listed as authors because they cannot be responsible for the accuracy, integrity, and originality of the work, and these responsibilities are required for authorship"
- 要点：聊天机器人不能列为作者，因为它不能对工作的准确、诚信和原创负责，而这些责任是署名的前提。

### E30

- 位置：II.A.4 Artificial Intelligence (AI)–Assisted Technology
- 原文："For example, if AI was used for writing assistance, describe this in the acknowledgment section (see Section II.A.3). If AI was used for data collection, analysis, or figure generation, authors should describe this use in the methods"
- 要点：用人工智能辅助写作，在致谢里说明；用于收集数据、分析或作图，在方法部分说明。

### E31

- 位置：II.A.4 Artificial Intelligence (AI)–Assisted Technology
- 原文："Authors should carefully review and edit the result because AI can generate authoritative-sounding output that can be incorrect, incomplete, or biased."
- 要点：人工智能的输出听起来很权威，却可能错误、不完整或有偏差，作者要仔细审查和修改。

### E32

- 位置：IV.A.3.d Methods
- 原文："Authors who used AI technology to conduct the study should describe its use in the Methods section in sufficient detail to enable replication of the approach, including the tool used, version, and prompts where applicable."
- 要点：用人工智能做研究的，在方法部分写清楚到别人能重复的程度，包括工具、版本，适用时还有提示词。

### E33

- 位置：V.A Use of AI by Authors
- 原文："Nondisclosure of AI use may require corrective action and may be construed as misconduct in some circumstances"
- 要点：不披露人工智能的使用，可能需要更正，某些情况下可能被认定为不端。
