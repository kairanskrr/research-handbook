# Mülhopt 等人 2018：Characterization of Nanoparticle Batch-To-Batch Variability

- 资料：`mulhopt2018characterization`，[原文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5977325/)
- 版本：Nanomaterials 8(5):311（2018），开放获取（CC BY 4.0），读取的是 PubMed Central 全文。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："The work described here represents over three years of effort across 14 European laboratories to assess the reproducibility of nanoparticle properties produced by the same and modified synthesis routes"
- 要点：这项工作由欧洲 14 个实验室历时三年多完成，评估用相同或改动过的合成路线制得的纳米颗粒，其性质能否重复。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："For 46 different batches of the selected NMs, all physicochemical descriptors as prioritized by the OECD have been fully characterized."
- 要点：他们对 46 个批次的纳米材料，完整表征了经济合作与发展组织（OECD）列为优先的全部理化指标。

### E3

- 位置：1. Introduction
- 原文："Current industrial and laboratory processes for synthesis of NMs give rise to quite significant variability from batch-to-batch"
- 要点：目前工业和实验室的纳米材料合成工艺，批次之间差异相当显著。

### E4

- 位置：1. Introduction
- 原文："There is also the question of how ‘representative’ a single batch is of the NM properties across multiple batches."
- 要点：还有一个问题：单个批次能在多大程度上代表多个批次的性质。

### E5

- 位置：1. Introduction
- 原文："Indeed, synthesis route is well known to affect the properties, including toxicity, of resulting NMs, as has been shown for silica, ceria, silver and many other NMs."
- 要点：合成路线会影响纳米材料的性质（包括毒性），二氧化硅、氧化铈、银等多种材料都已有报道。

### E6

- 位置：4. Discussion（末尾的技术建议）
- 原文："As the NMs are frequently agglomerated or even aggregated, the particle size distributions should be measured by several methods such as DLS and/or SMPS/electrospray and TEM to minimize the variability of the determined size due to technical limitations."
- 要点：纳米材料常常团聚，粒径分布应当用几种方法测（如动态光散射、扫描电迁移率粒径谱仪/电喷雾、透射电镜），减少单一技术局限带来的偏差。

### E7

- 位置：4. Discussion（末尾的总结）
- 原文："To summarize, whereas the variability of a number of parameters suggested by the OECD WPMN is rather low that is, the values are rather stable and reproducible such as the zeta potential and size others for example, impurities are less well controlled."
- 要点：总的来看，ζ 电位、粒径等参数批次间比较稳定，杂质等参数控制得不够好。
