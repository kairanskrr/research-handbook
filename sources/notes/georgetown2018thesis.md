# Georgetown Law Writing Center 2018：Developing a Thesis Statement

- 资料：`georgetown2018thesis`，[原文 PDF（美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心，免费）](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/Updated-Developing-a-Thesis-Statement-Handout.pdf)
- 版本：美国乔治城大学法学院写作中心讲义，作者 Shelley Lambert、Marguerite McLamb、Greg Malloy，2018 年。写给**美国法学院学生**。思路部分基于 Fajans & Falk 的 Scholarly Writing for Law Students。位置写讲义的小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Basics About Thesis Statements
- 原文："A thesis statement is an original, supportable hypothesis or assertion about a topic. The thesis targets a specific point or aspect of the law, articulates a problem, and ideally attempts to resolve it."
- 要点：论点陈述是关于题目的、原创的、可以得到支持的假设或主张；它针对法律的某一具体方面，指出一个问题，最好还试图解决它。

### E2

- 位置：Basics About Thesis Statements
- 原文："try a device such as the "should ... because ..." thesis formulation; argue that a certain result should happen because of particular reasons."
- 要点：可以用"应当……因为……"的句式起草论点：论证某个结果应当发生，并给出理由。

### E3

- 位置：Basics About Thesis Statements
- 原文："As you develop your argument and construct your paper, test your thesis against known and hypothetical situations, and modify the thesis to further refine or strengthen your proposition."
- 要点：写作过程中，用已知情形和假设情形检验论点，并据此修改论点。

### E4

- 位置：Possible Approaches for Thesis Statements
- 原文："Arguments from precedent assert that precedent is binding or should be extended, adopted, or overruled." / "Interpretive arguments examine the language of constitutions, statutes, and regulations." / "Normative arguments assert that a certain rule or result is good, justified by morality, social policy, economics, or justice between the parties." / "Institutional arguments examine the appropriateness of the roles of the judiciary, legislature, or executive or address the administrative impact of a certain result."
- 要点：四类论证：基于先例的（先例有约束力，或应被扩展、采纳、推翻）；解释性的（考察宪法、制定法、法规的文字）；规范性的（某规则或结果是好的，理由来自道德、社会政策、经济或当事人之间的公平）；制度性的（司法、立法、行政各自角色是否恰当，或某结果的行政影响）。

### E5

- 位置：Possible Approaches for Thesis Statements
- 原文："Identify and resolve inconsistencies, logical errors, and omissions."
- 要点：找出并解决不一致、逻辑错误和遗漏，是形成论点的一种途径。

### E6

- 位置：Sample Thesis Statements
- 原文："This Note argues that, based solely on prior case law, particularly Circuit City, the Pyett court should have held that an arbitration clause is enforceable against an employee regardless of the employee's union status."
- 要点：论点示例：仅根据先前判例（特别是 Circuit City 案），Pyett 案的法院本应认定仲裁条款对雇员有效，无论雇员是否属于工会。
