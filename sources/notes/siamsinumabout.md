# SIAM：SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis（SINUM）期刊介绍页

- 资料：`siamsinumabout`，[原文](https://www.siam.org/publications/journals/siam-journal-on-numerical-analysis-sinum)
- 版本：美国工业与应用数学学会（Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics，SIAM）的期刊介绍网页。官网对脚本返回验证页，文本取自 Internet Archive 2024-06-13 的快照。只代表这一本期刊的范围说明，不是详细的审稿标准。位置写段落。

### E1

- 位置：期刊简介
- 原文："SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis (SINUM) contains research articles on the development and analysis of numerical methods. Topics include the rigorous study of convergence of algorithms, their accuracy, their stability, and their computational complexity."
- 要点：SINUM 刊登发展和分析数值方法的论文，主题包括对算法收敛性、精度、稳定性和计算复杂度的严格研究。

### E2

- 位置：期刊简介
- 原文："Also included are results in mathematical analysis that contribute to algorithm analysis, and computational results that demonstrate algorithm behavior and applicability."
- 要点：也刊登有助于算法分析的数学分析结果，以及展示算法表现和适用性的计算结果。
