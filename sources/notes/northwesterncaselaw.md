# Northwestern Pritzker Legal Research Center：Researching Case Law

- 资料：`northwesterncaselaw`，[原文网页：概览页](https://library.law.northwestern.edu/cases/overview)、[Finding Cases 页](https://library.law.northwestern.edu/cases/finding)
- 版本：美国西北大学普利兹克法学院法律研究中心（法学图书馆）的判例检索指南，2026-10-06 读取。讲的是在**美国**用 Westlaw、Lexis、Bloomberg Law 三个商业数据库查判例。位置写网页名称。与同一指南的"Determining if Case Law is Still Good"页（key：`northwesterngoodlaw`）是同一份指南的不同页面。

### E1

- 位置：Researching Case Law（概览页）
- 原文："Keep records of your work throughout the process. Make notes of where you searched and your results from each step." / "This will keep you from having to repeat steps and will help you recall what search terms you used. If you cannot find a definitive answer, you will be also able to report on what you did find and where you looked."
- 要点：全程记录：在哪里查过、每一步结果如何。这样不用重复，也记得用过哪些检索词；找不到确定答案时，也能报告找到了什么、查过哪里。

### E2

- 位置：Finding Cases
- 原文："Unlike searching for secondary sources, you really want to make sure you find every case that may be relevant to your argument (either for or against)."
- 要点：查判例与查二手资料不同：要确保找全所有可能相关的判例，无论支持还是反对你的论点。

### E3

- 位置：Finding Cases
- 原文："Secondary sources can lead you to the leading cases, plus related propositions that you need to show to bridge to your main claim."
- 要点：二手资料可以把你引向主导判例，以及支撑主要主张所需的相关命题。

### E4

- 位置：Finding Cases — One Good Case Method
- 原文："Looking back: What cases are cited by the court in your One Good Case?" / "Looking forward: Using a citator such as Key Cite/Citing References (Westlaw) and Shepard’s (Lexis), what later cases interpret the cases you intend to rely on?" / "Looking out: Topical organizations (headnotes) categorize all published decisions, so a digest will give you good coverage of the universe of cases that exist."
- 要点："一个好判例"法：往回看（这个判例引了哪些判例）；往前看（用引证工具查后来哪些判例解释了它）；往外看（用按主题分类的判例摘要体系覆盖全部已公开判决）。

### E5

- 位置：Finding Cases — When Are You Done?
- 原文："The prevailing description is that when you keep seeing the same cases over and over again without anything new, you’re done."
- 要点：通行的说法是：反复看到同样的判例、不再出现新的，就可以停了。

### E6

- 位置：Finding Cases — When Are You Done?
- 原文："Use at least one source/search that involves an editor attempting to assemble like cases together." / "Use at least one online Boolean search to see if cases exist that were not identified in your first source or were too recent to be indexed in any other system you used."
- 要点：至少用一种由编辑把同类判例归在一起的资料，再至少做一次布尔检索（用 AND、OR 等逻辑词组合检索词），以发现第一种资料漏掉的或太新还没被编入的判例。

### E7

- 位置：Finding Cases — When Are You Done?
- 原文："Read entirely every case that might provide new cases, that you might cite or that your opponent might cite, and that is from the highest court (within reason depending on how many exist)."
- 要点：凡是可能引出新判例、你可能引用或对方可能引用、来自最高一级法院的判例，都要通读全文（数量多时在合理范围内）。

### E8

- 位置：Researching Case Law（概览页）
- 原文："What overarching principles can you synthesize into a framework to demonstrate to a court, even if the framework is not specifically articulated by courts?"
- 要点：思考能否把判例综合成一个统领性的框架，即使法院自己没有明确这样表述过。

### E9

- 位置：Finding Cases
- 原文："Annotated codes provide many key cases that interpret a statute. However, they generally are not 100% comprehensive."
- 要点：附注法典会列出解释某条法律的许多关键判例，但一般并不完整。
