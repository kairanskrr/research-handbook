# 美国土木工程师学会 2020：ASCE Code of Ethics

- 资料：`asce2020code`，[原文 PDF](https://www.asce.org/-/media/asce-images-and-files/career-and-growth/ethics/documents/asce-code-ethics.pdf)
- 版本：美国土木工程师学会（American Society of Civil Engineers，ASCE）伦理准则 PDF（共 3 页，按五类"利益相关方"列出责任），最近一次更新是 2020 年 10 月 26 日（见 E10，出自 ASCE 官网的准则介绍页 https://www.asce.org/career-growth/ethics/code-of-ethics）。位置写条款编号。

### E1

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："In the case of a conflict between ethical responsibilities, the five stakeholders are listed in the order of priority. There is no priority of responsibilities within a given stakeholder group with the exception that 1a. takes precedence over all other responsibilities."
- 要点：各项责任冲突时，五类利益相关方按列出的顺序排优先级；第 1a 条高于其他一切责任。

### E2

- 位置：1. Society，1a
- 原文："first and foremost, protect the health, safety, and welfare of the public;"
- 要点：首先保护公众的健康、安全和福祉。

### E3

- 位置：1. Society，1c
- 原文："express professional opinions truthfully and only when founded on adequate knowledge and honest conviction;"
- 要点：只在有充分知识和真实信念时，才如实发表专业意见。

### E4

- 位置：1. Society，1g
- 原文："acknowledge the diverse historical, social, and cultural needs of the community, and incorporate these considerations in their work;"
- 要点：承认社区在历史、社会和文化上的不同需要，并把这些考虑纳入工作。

### E5

- 位置：2. Natural and Built Environment，2b
- 原文："consider and balance societal, environmental, and economic impacts, along with opportunities for improvement, in their work;"
- 要点：工作中要考虑并平衡社会、环境和经济影响。

### E6

- 位置：4. Clients and Employers，4b
- 原文："make clear to clients and employers any real, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest;"
- 要点：向客户和雇主讲清任何实际的、潜在的或可能被认为存在的利益冲突。

### E7

- 位置：4. Clients and Employers，4e
- 原文："keep clients' and employers' identified proprietary information confidential;"
- 要点：对客户和雇主指明的专有信息保密。

### E8

- 位置：4. Clients and Employers，4f
- 原文："perform services only in areas of their competence;"
- 要点：只在自己胜任的领域提供服务。

### E9

- 位置：第 1 页脚注
- 原文："This Code does not establish a standard of care, nor should it be interpreted as such."
- 要点：这份准则不确立法律意义上的注意标准。

### E10

- 位置：ASCE 官网 Code of Ethics 介绍页
- 原文："First adopted in 1914, the ASCE Code of Ethics is the model for professional conduct for ASCE members. The Code of Ethics was most recently updated on October 26, 2020."
- 要点：ASCE 伦理准则 1914 年首次通过，是 ASCE 会员的职业行为范本，最近一次更新在 2020 年 10 月 26 日。

### E11

- 位置：Preamble
- 原文："Members of The American Society of Civil Engineers conduct themselves with integrity and professionalism, and above all else protect and advance the health, safety, and welfare of the public through the practice of Civil Engineering."
- 要点：序言要求 ASCE 会员诚信、专业，首先保护和促进公众的健康、安全和福祉。

### E12

- 位置：Preamble；1. Society, a
- 原文："In the case of a conflict between ethical responsibilities, the five stakeholders are listed in the order of priority. There is no priority of responsibilities within a given stakeholder group with the exception that 1a. takes precedence over all other responsibilities." / "a. first and foremost, protect the health, safety, and welfare of the public;"
- 要点：伦理责任冲突时，五类相关方按列出的顺序排优先级；第 1a 条"首先保护公众的健康、安全和福祉"高于其他所有责任。

### E13

- 位置：1. Society, h
- 原文："consider the capabilities, limitations, and implications of current and emerging technologies when part of their work"
- 要点：工作中用到现有或新兴技术时，要考虑它们的能力、局限和影响。

### E14

- 位置：4. Clients and employers, b、c
- 原文："make clear to clients and employers any real, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest;" / "communicate in a timely manner to clients and employers any risks and limitations related to their work;"
- 要点：向委托方和雇主说清任何实际的、潜在的或可能被认为存在的利益冲突；及时告知工作的风险和局限。

### E15

- 位置：4. Clients and employers, e、f
- 原文："keep clients’ and employers’ identified proprietary information confidential;" / "perform services only in areas of their competence;"
- 要点：对委托方和雇主指明的专有信息保密；只在自己能胜任的领域提供服务。

### E16

- 位置：5. Peers, a、b、c
- 原文："only take credit for professional work they have personally completed;" / "provide attribution for the work of others;" / "foster health and safety in the workplace;"
- 要点：只把自己亲自完成的工作算作自己的功劳；注明别人的贡献；在工作场所促进健康和安全。
