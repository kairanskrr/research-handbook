# Black 2024：Writing a good scientific paper

- 资料：`black2024writing`，[原文](https://perceiving-systems.blog/en/news/writing-a-good-scientific-paper)
- 版本：Perceiving Systems 博客文章，2024。网页，位置写小标题。原文以计算机视觉为例，作者认为主题适用于各学科。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："First impressions matter. By the end of your intro, the reviewer has likely already decided whether they like the paper or not."
- 要点：读完引言时，审稿人多半已决定喜不喜欢这篇论文；引言之后很难挽回。

### E2

- 位置：The Nugget
- 原文："The nugget is not your technical contribution. I repeat: it is not your technical contribution. It is the insight that leads to your contribution."
- 要点：论文要讲清那个"关键洞见"：它不是技术贡献本身，而是让问题变得可解、从而带来贡献的新看法。

### E3

- 位置：How to tell a story: Goal, Problem, Solution
- 原文："Also, a paper should tell one story (it can have subparts). If you have two big ideas and you put them in one paper, people will only remember one of them."
- 要点：一篇论文讲一个故事；把两个大想法放进一篇，读者只会记住一个。

### E4

- 位置：Number one writing tip: use fewer words…
- 原文："Write it early. Very early. You can write an intro before you have any results."
- 要点：尽早写一个粗糙的初稿，甚至在有结果之前就写引言；先抓逻辑结构，语法以后再改。

### E5

- 位置：Number one writing tip: use fewer words…
- 原文："All good papers start as bad papers. They get written and rewritten."
- 要点：好论文都是从坏论文改出来的；改短几乎总能改好。

### E6

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："The best way to write a clear paper is to write a talk first."
- 要点：先准备一个报告，或者把工作讲给朋友听，用讲解的顺序来组织引言和论文。

### E7

- 位置：How to tell a story: Goal, Problem, Solution
- 原文："Memorize this: Goal, Problem, Solution."
- 要点：用"目标—障碍—解决"的节奏讲科研故事：先让读者想要这个目标，再说明是什么挡住了它，最后给出你的办法。
