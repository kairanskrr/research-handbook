# 美国语言学会 1992：Human Subjects in Linguistic Research

- 资料：`lsa1992humansubjects`，[原文](https://www.lsadc.org/human_subjects_in_linguistic_research)
- 版本：1992 年 5 月由美国语言学会执行委员会通过

### E1

- 位置：开头说明
- 原文："Members who do research with human subjects have expressed concern that the Federal Policy on the Protection of the Human Subjects does not always apply to linguistic research."
- 要点：这份声明的背景：美国联邦人类受试者保护政策不一定适合语言研究。

### E2

- 位置：正文第 1 段
- 原文："Studies of a human language often depend upon a continuing relation with speakers of the language."
- 要点：研究一种语言，常常依赖和说这种语言的人保持长期关系。

### E3

- 位置：正文第 3 段
- 原文："Those who participate in such a work often do so with pride in their command of their language and may wish to be known for their contribution. Not to disclose their names would do them a disservice."
- 要点：参与者常以自己掌握这门语言为荣，可能希望被署名；不公开名字反而对不起他们。

### E4

- 位置：正文末段
- 原文："Such considerations make it difficult to apply general rules in a mechanical way."
- 要点：因此很难机械地套用一般规则。

### E5

- 位置：正文第 1 段
- 原文："Studies of a human language often depend upon a continuing relation with speakers of the language. Such a relation comes to be defined as much by the speakers as by the linguist. Their patterns of life govern when work can be done."
- 要点：语言研究常依赖和发音人的长期关系；这种关系由发音人和语言学者共同界定，什么时候能工作由他们的生活节奏决定。
