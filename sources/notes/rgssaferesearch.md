# 英国皇家地理学会：Safe, responsible and ethical research

- 资料：`rgssaferesearch`，[原文](https://www.rgs.org/exploration/grants/guidance-for-society-grant-applicants/safe-responsible-and-ethical-research/)
- 版本：英国皇家地理学会（RGS-IBG）给资助申请人的安全与伦理说明网页。官网对脚本拒绝访问，读取的是 Internet Archive 2025-11-06 快照。位置写段落内容。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Grant recipients must obtain their own institution’s health and safety and ethical approval before funding can be transferred."
- 要点：获资助者必须先取得本单位的健康安全审批和伦理审批，资金才会拨付。

### E2

- 位置：风险评估段
- 原文："Your risk assessment must not be generic, but be designed around the specific risks you may face depending on the location of your project and the nature of your research."
- 要点：风险评估不能套模板，要针对项目地点和研究性质可能遇到的具体风险来写。

### E3

- 位置：伦理评估段
- 原文："For example, could your work offend the cultural sensitivities of the people you are working among? Have you gained appropriate consent from, or on behalf of, participants or others affected by the research?"
- 要点：申请前要想清楚：研究会不会冒犯当地人的文化禁忌？是否取得了参与者或其他受影响者的恰当同意？
