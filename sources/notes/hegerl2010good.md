# Hegerl 等人 2010：Good Practice Guidance Paper on Detection and Attribution Related to Anthropogenic Climate Change

- 资料：`hegerl2010good`，[原文](https://archive.ipcc.ch/pdf/supporting-material/expert-meeting-detection-anthropogenic-2009-09.pdf)
- 版本：政府间气候变化专门委员会（IPCC）第一、第二工作组 2009 年 9 月日内瓦"检测与归因"专家会议报告（Stocker 等编，2010，IPCC 第一工作组技术支持组出版）中的《检测与归因良好做法指导文件》，核心写作组为 Hegerl 等 8 人。IPCC 官网原链接已失效，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2024-10-14 对该官方 PDF 的存档（web.archive.org/web/20241014205739/https://archive.ipcc.ch/pdf/supporting-material/expert-meeting-detection-anthropogenic-2009-09.pdf），pdftotext 提取。报告封面说明：会议是 IPCC 工作计划的一部分，但不代表工作组或专委会认可其建议，也未经 IPCC 正式评审。位置写节号和页码（页码按页脚"IPCC Expert Meeting on Detection and Attribution - N"）。

### E2

- 位置：Executive Summary，第 1 页
- 原文："It seeks to clarify methods, definitions and terminology across the two working groups and is intended as a guide for future IPCC Lead Authors. This paper also outlines guidelines for how to assess the relative quality of studies and provides recommendations for good practice in detection and attribution studies."
- 要点：文件写给今后的 IPCC 主要作者，统一两个工作组的方法、定义和术语，并给出评判研究质量的准则和检测归因研究的良好做法建议。

### E3

- 位置：§1 Definitions，第 2 页
- 原文："Detection of change is defined as the process of demonstrating that climate or a system affected by climate has changed in some defined statistical sense without providing a reason for that change." / "Attribution is defined as the process of evaluating the relative contributions of multiple causal factors to a change or event with an assignment of statistical confidence. The process of attribution requires the detection of a change in the observed variable"
- 要点："检测"是证明气候或受气候影响的系统在某种统计意义上发生了变化，但不说明原因；"归因"是评估多个原因各自对变化的贡献，并给出统计信度。归因的前提是先检测到变化。

### E4

- 位置：§1 Definitions，第 2 页
- 原文："A confounding factor is one that affects the variable or system of interest but is not explicitly accounted for in the design of a study." / "Confounding factors could therefore lead to erroneous conclusions about cause-effect relationships."
- 要点：混杂因素指影响所研究的变量或系统、但研究设计里没有明确考虑的因素；它可能让人对因果关系得出错误结论。

### E5

- 位置：§2 Methods，II. Multi-Step Attribution，第 3 页
- 原文："An example would be the multi-step attribution of declining marine calcification to rising atmospheric carbon dioxide (i.e., changes in marine calcification are attributed to changes in ocean chemistry, which is in a separate step attributed to changes in atmospheric carbon dioxide; see Box 2.2)." / "Authors should clearly state when a multi-step attribution has been made." / "It is recommended that the component assessments (or steps) be made explicitly (each with its own level of confidence) and that an overall assessment of the combined result be made. The overall assessment will generally be similar to or weaker than the weakest step."
- 要点：多步归因的例子：把海洋生物钙化下降归因于海水化学变化，再在另一步里把海水化学变化归因于大气二氧化碳增加。做了多步归因要明说；每一步单独评估、各给信度，再给总体评估；总体结论一般不会强于最弱的那一步。

### E6

- 位置：§2 Methods，第 4 页
- 原文："Regardless of the method used, authors should specifically state the causal factor to which a particular change is being attributed" / "Confidence in assessments will be increased when attribution of change to a causal factor is robustly quantified and when there is firm understanding of the processes (`process knowledge') that are involved in a proposed causal link"
- 要点：不管用哪种方法，都要写明把变化归因于哪个原因；归因有稳健的定量结果、对因果链条中的过程有扎实理解时，信度更高。

### E7

- 位置：§2 Methods，第 5 页
- 原文："Where models are used in attribution, a model's ability to properly represent the relevant causal link should be assessed." / "Confidence in attribution will also be influenced by the extent to which the study considers other possible external forcings and drivers, confounding factors and also observational data limitations."
- 要点：用模式做归因时，要评估模式能否正确表现相关的因果联系；研究在多大程度上考虑了其他可能的外部强迫和驱动因素、混杂因素和观测数据的局限，也影响归因的信度。

### E8

- 位置：§2 Methods，第 5 页
- 原文："For transparency and reproducibility it is essential that all steps taken in attribution approaches are documented."
- 要点：为了透明和可重复，归因的每一步都必须记录下来。
