# Carlini 2026：How to win a best paper award (or, an opinionated take on how to do important research that matters)

- 资料：`carlini2026best`，[原文](https://nicholas.carlini.com/writing/2026/how-to-win-a-best-paper-award.html)
- 版本：作者博客，2026-03-09。作者在文中注明"for my field! yours may differ."

### E1

- 位置：Coming up with your best-paper-worthy idea › Pick your ideas for impact
- 原文："Some people go into research with the objective of writing a conference paper. Don't do that. You need to go into research with the objective of discovering something interesting, important, and new. The paper follows as a natural consequence."
- 要点：做研究的目标不是写一篇论文，而是发现有意思、重要、新的东西；论文是自然的结果。

### E2

- 位置：While doing the best-paper-worthy research › Put in an unreasonable amount of effort
- 原文："Every experiment should be as precisely performed as you can manage. Run multiple trials. Control for confounders. If there's a question a skeptical reader might ask about your setup, run the experiment that answers it before they ask."
- 要点：每个实验都要尽可能精确：多次重复、控制混杂因素；预见到持怀疑态度的读者会问什么，提前用实验回答。

### E3

- 位置：Coming up with your best-paper-worthy idea › Have good taste for problems
- 原文："The single most important skill to develop for high-impact research is good taste in what problems are worth solving."
- 要点：做出高影响力研究最重要的技能，是判断哪些问题值得解决的品味。

### E4

- 位置：同上
- 原文："They write papers that are technically correct but that no one reads, because they never asked whether the question was worth answering."
- 要点：品味不足的人写出技术上正确却没人读的论文，因为从没问过这个问题值不值得回答。

### E5

- 位置：同上
- 原文："But if I had to summarize it in one sentence, it would be that taste comes from practicing the skill of research, keeping your focus always on identifying what works and what doesn't."
- 要点：品味来自做研究的练习，并始终关注"什么行得通、什么行不通"。

### E6

- 位置：Ignore all the papers
- 原文："Once you've read everything, the second step is to forget it all. The reason is simple: everything that's already been done has already been done."
- 要点：广泛阅读之后，要能跳出已有工作的框架，否则想不出新东西。

### E7

- 位置：Ignore all the papers
- 原文："These are directions where someone (usually someone famous!) published an influential paper that got something critical wrong with the approach, methodology, or evaluation... and then the rest of the field followed along the same wrong direction without thinking critically about the initial choice, because that's just how the field does things."
- 要点：每个领域都有"坏想法"：有影响的早期论文在方法或评估上犯了关键错误，后来者不加思考地跟着走。

### E8

- 位置：Do something only you can do
- 原文："So the magnitude of your contribution is judged, in a very real sense, by counting the months between when you publish, and when the next person would have."
- 要点：贡献的大小，某种意义上取决于你比"下一个会做出它的人"早了几个月。

### E9

- 位置：Do something only you can do
- 原文："I look for areas where, when I read the work, I want to scream. Areas where you keep asking yourself "why is everyone doing this obviously wrong?""
- 要点：一个信号：读某个方向的论文时忍不住想问"为什么大家都在用明显错误的方式做"。

### E10

- 位置：Find your comparative advantage
- 原文："Because this space is high-dimensional, it's highly likely you are the best in the world at some specific corner of it. Find that corner."
- 要点：技能的组合空间维度很高，你很可能在某个具体角落是最强的；找到那个角落。

### E11

- 位置：Get lucky: you're well suited to solve important problems
- 原文："I feel pretty strongly that your overall impact is the product of your skill in some area, multiplied by how important that area is. If you're very good at a less "important" problem, you'll contribute more than working on something you're not very good at."
- 要点：影响力约等于"你在某方面的能力 × 这个方向的重要性"；在不那么热门但你很擅长的问题上，贡献可能比做不擅长的热门问题更大。

### E12

- 位置：同上
- 原文："But second, fortunately, if you're in an area that's not the "hot" one, you'll have a much easier time standing out."
- 要点：不在最热门的方向，反而更容易脱颖而出。

### E13

- 位置：Pick your ideas for impact
- 原文："At some point the marginal hour spent trying to find a good idea is less valuable than just working on ... anything."
- 要点：找想法花到一定程度后，多想一小时不如先动手做点什么。

### E14

- 位置：While doing the best-paper-worthy research › Kill papers that are not working
- 原文："The best way I've found to minimize wasted time is to start with the sub-problem most likely to fail."
- 要点：先做最可能失败的子问题，尽早知道想法行不行。

### E15

- 位置：While doing the best-paper-worthy research › Re-prioritize projects ruthlessly
- 原文："new ideas always sound more exciting than ones you've been working on for a few months, so it's easy to get distracted by some shiny new problem just because it's new."
- 要点：新想法总比做了几个月的旧想法显得更诱人，容易仅仅因为新就分心。
