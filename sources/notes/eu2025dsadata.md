# 欧盟委员会 2025：《数字服务法》数据访问授权条例（Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2050）

- 资料：`eu2025dsadata`，[原文（EUR-Lex）](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2025/2050/oj)
- 版本：欧盟委员会 2025 年 7 月 1 日通过，刊于欧盟官方公报 L 辑 2025/2050，2025 年 10 月 9 日。EUR-Lex 对脚本返回空页面，读的是欧盟出版局（publications.europa.eu，CELEX 32025R2050）提供的同一份官方公报英文 XHTML 全文。位置写条款号。

### E1

- 位置：标题
- 原文："COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) 2025/2050 of 1 July 2025 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 of the European Parliament and of the Council by laying down the technical conditions and procedures under which providers of very large online platforms and of very large online search engines are to share data with vetted researchers"
- 要点：欧盟委员会 2025 年 7 月 1 日通过的授权条例，补充《数字服务法》，规定超大型在线平台和超大型搜索引擎向经审核研究者共享数据的技术条件和程序。

### E2

- 位置：公报页眉；Article 16
- 原文："Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2025/2050 9.10.2025" / "This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union"
- 要点：刊登于 2025 年 10 月 9 日的官方公报，在刊登后第 20 天生效。

### E3

- 位置：Article 3(1), (2)(b), (5)
- 原文："1. The Commission shall establish and host a DSA data access portal." / "serve as the central digital point for information on the data access process and facilitate the information exchanges pursuant to this Regulation among applicant researchers, vetted researchers, data providers and Digital Services Coordinators" / "To participate in the data access process, applicant researchers shall have an account on the DSA data access portal."
- 要点：欧盟委员会建立并托管"DSA 数据访问门户"，作为数据访问流程的中心信息点；申请的研究者要在门户上开账户才能参加流程。

### E4

- 位置：Article 2(1)
- 原文："'data access application' means the information and relevant documentation submitted by applicant researchers to the Digital Services Coordinator of establishment or the Digital Services Coordinator of the Member State of the research organisation, to which the principal researcher is affiliated, to obtain the status of 'vetted researcher'"
- 要点：数据访问申请提交给设立地数字服务协调员，或主要研究者所属研究机构所在成员国的数字服务协调员，以取得"经审核研究者"身份。

### E5

- 位置：Article 7(1), (2)
- 原文："Within 80 working days from the submission of a data access application, the Digital Services Coordinator of establishment" [...] "shall decide whether a reasoned request can be formulated" [...] "formulate a reasoned request, submit it to the data provider and notify the principal researcher of the submission of the reasoned request" [...] "inform the principal researcher of the reasons why the reasoned request could not be formulated" / "Where, in duly justified cases, the Digital Services Coordinator of establishment needs additional time to formulate a reasoned request, it shall notify the principal researcher as soon as possible and shall indicate the reasons for the delay as well as a new date"
- 要点：设立地数字服务协调员要在申请提交后 80 个工作日内决定能否形成合理请求：能的就提交给平台并通知主要研究者；不能的，要告诉主要研究者理由。确有正当理由需要更多时间的，要尽快通知主要研究者延误原因和新的日期。

### E6

- 位置：Article 6(4)(c)
- 原文："a DSA data catalogue, which describes the data assets, that may be accessed for the purposes set out in Article 40(4) of Regulation EU 2022/2065, as well as their data structure and metadata"
- 要点：平台要在自己的界面上公开数据目录，说明可按第 40 条第 4 款申请访问的数据资产及其结构和元数据。
