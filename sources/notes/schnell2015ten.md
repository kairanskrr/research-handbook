# Schnell 2015：Ten Simple Rules for a Computational Biologist's Laboratory Notebook

- 资料：`schnell2015ten`，[原文](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1004385)
- 版本：PLoS Computational Biology 11(9): e1004385, 2015，开放获取（CC BY），2026-10-06 读取。本文面向只做"干实验"（计算）的生物学家，但记录原则来自实验室笔记本的通行做法。位置写规则编号。

### E1

- 位置：引言
- 原文："It is an organizational tool and memory aid, which serves as the primary record of scientific research and activity for all scientists. It also serves as a legal record of ownership of the ideas and results obtained by a scientist."
- 要点：实验记录本是组织工具和备忘录，是科研活动的主要记录，也是想法和结果归属的法律记录。

### E2

- 位置：Rule 1
- 原文："In the absence of an institutional policy, your research group should have a policy to explain to all team members the process for daily record-keeping and maintaining laboratory records."
- 要点：机构没有记录本政策时，课题组也应有自己的规定，告诉每个成员怎样日常记录、怎样保管记录。

### E3

- 位置：Rule 4
- 原文："Each lab notebook entry should be written immediately after the activity or work was performed."
- 要点：每条记录都应在做完之后立即写。

### E4

- 位置：Rule 5
- 原文："If you make a mistake in your paper lab notebook, put a thin line through the mistake and write the new information next to it. Never erase or destroy an entry, because errors or mistakes are an important part of the scientific process."
- 要点：纸质记录本写错了，用一条细线划掉，在旁边写正确信息；永远不要擦掉或销毁记录，错误也是科研过程的一部分。

### E5

- 位置：Rule 8
- 原文："For each lab notebook entry, clearly indicate who did what work and who was present for a discussion or in silico experiment; this is particularly important for collaborative projects."
- 要点：每条记录都写清谁做了什么、讨论时谁在场；合作项目尤其重要。

### E6

- 位置：Rule 8
- 原文："It is important to note that lab notebooks can be subpoenaed as evidence in cases of scientific misconduct by your institution or governmental funding agencies."
- 要点：发生科研不端调查时，机构或政府资助机构可以调取实验记录本作为证据。

### E7

- 位置：Rule 6
- 原文："You should also store the raw data for every figure."
- 要点：每张图的原始数据都要保存。

### E8

- 位置：引言
- 原文："Here, I present the best practices (summarized as ten rules) for keeping a lab notebook in computational biology, for scientists pursuing exclusively “dry” research."
- 要点：本文的十条规则面向只做"干"研究（计算研究）的生物学家。

### E9

- 位置：Rule 3
- 原文："If you rely on your recollection to remember a good idea, a suggestion during a meeting, or an important step in your data analysis or model simulation, you can find yourself in a situation where you no longer remember that critical thought."
- 要点：靠记忆记住好想法、会上的建议或分析中的关键步骤，很可能到时候就想不起来了。

### E10

- 位置：Rule 5
- 原文："The most logical organization of a lab notebook is chronological. Each entry should contain the date it was made and subject of the entry."
- 要点：记录本按时间顺序组织最合理，每条记录都要写日期和主题。

### E11

- 位置：Rule 5
- 原文："Never erase or destroy an entry, because errors or mistakes are an important part of the scientific process."
- 要点：永远不要擦掉或销毁记录，因为错误本身也是科研过程的重要部分。
