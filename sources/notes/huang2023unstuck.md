# Huang 2023：How to get unstuck?（推文串）

- 资料：`huang2023unstuck`，[原推文](https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1717381151508054029)，[Thread Reader 镜像](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1717381151508054029.html)
- 版本：Jia-Bin Huang 推文串，2023-10-26，共 11 条；经 Thread Reader 镜像读取于 2026-10-05。位置写推文中的小标题。

### E1

- 位置：第 1 条推文
- 原文："In most research projects, ~99% of the time your experiments DO NOT work."
- 要点：大多数研究项目里，实验绝大多数时候都不成功。

### E2

- 位置：*Show HOW it failed*
- 原文："Here is the input and our expected results. But here are our current results."
- 要点：向别人求助时，要具体展示输入、预期结果和实际结果，而不是只说"不行"。

### E3

- 位置：*Simplify the tasks until it works*
- 原文："Progressively make your tasks simpler until the tasks become so trivial that they must "work"."
- 要点：逐步简化任务，直到简单到"必然能成功"，再从那里往回加难度。

### E4

- 位置：*Introduce privileged information*
- 原文："Adding privileged information is a powerful trick to make progress."
- 要点：要同时估计 A 和 B 时，先假设已知其中一个，只解另一个；加入"特权信息"是推进的有力技巧。

### E5

- 位置：*Debug the idea*
- 原文："When you debug all your code/experiments and still find it not working, take a step back and debug the idea."
- 要点：代码和实验都查过仍然不行，就退一步检查想法本身。

### E6

- 位置：*Iterate fast*
- 原文："Find toy examples and proxy tasks so you can have a short turnaround time."
- 要点：用玩具例子和代理任务缩短周转时间；迭代越快，进展越多。

### E7

- 位置：*Help your mentors help you*
- 原文："Your mentors have more experience than you and are willing to help. But you have to help them help you."
- 要点：导师有经验也愿意帮忙，但你得让他们帮得上：讲清为什么做实验、怎么做的、看到了什么。

### E8

- 位置：*Make your experiments easy to understand*
- 原文："If you only verbally describe why/how/what you did, it's difficult for others to fully understand the process."
- 要点：只口头描述，别人很难完全理解；要把流程画出来、把细节记下来。
