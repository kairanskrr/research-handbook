# Hamming 1986：You and Your Research

- 资料：`hamming1986you`，[原文](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/~robins/YouAndYourResearch.html)
- 版本：1986 年 3 月 7 日 Bellcore 演讲，J. F. Kaiser 整理的文字稿（网页无小标题，位置按演讲中的话题描述）。

### E1

- 位置：演讲正文，"important problems" 一段（Bell Labs 午餐桌的故事之后）
- 原文："If you do not work on an important problem, it's unlikely you'll do important work. It's perfectly obvious."
- 要点：不做重要的问题，就不太可能做出重要的工作。

### E2

- 位置：同上
- 原文："They are not important problems because we do not have an attack. It's not the consequence that makes a problem important, it is that you have a reasonable attack."
- 要点：时间旅行、反重力这类问题不算"重要问题"，因为没有可行的切入办法；问题重要不在于结果有多大，而在于你有合理的攻克途径。

### E3

- 位置：演讲正文，讲 "ambiguity" 一段
- 原文："Great scientists tolerate ambiguity very well. They believe the theory enough to go ahead; they doubt it enough to notice the errors and faults so they can step forward and create the new replacement theory."
- 要点：优秀的科学家既相信自己的理论到足以推进，又怀疑它到足以发现错误。

### E4

- 位置：同上
- 原文："Darwin writes in his autobiography that he found it necessary to write down every piece of evidence which appeared to contradict his beliefs because otherwise they would disappear from his mind."
- 要点：达尔文会把每条似乎与自己信念矛盾的证据都写下来，否则它们会从脑中消失。

### E5

- 位置：演讲正文，讲把计算机编程问题当作一类问题来解决的一段
- 原文："You should do your job in such a fashion that others can build on top of it, so they will indeed say, ``Yes, I've stood on so and so's shoulders and I saw further.'' The essence of science is cumulative."
- 要点：做研究要让别人能在你的工作上继续往上建；科学的本质是积累。

### E6

- 位置：同上
- 原文："Instead of attacking isolated problems, I made the resolution that I would never again solve an isolated problem except as characteristic of a class."
- 要点：不孤立地解决一个问题，而是把它当作一类问题的代表来解决。

### E7

- 位置：演讲正文，讲 "selling" 一段
- 原文："I have now come down to a topic which is very distasteful; it is not sufficient to do a job, you have to sell it. [...] But the fact is everyone is busy with their own work."
- 要点：做完工作还不够，还要把它讲清楚、讲出去；因为别人都忙于自己的工作。

### E8

- 位置：同上
- 原文："The average scientist, so far as I can make out, spends almost all his time working on problems which he believes will not be important and he also doesn't believe that they will lead to important problems."
- 要点：一般的科学家几乎把所有时间花在自己都不认为重要、也不认为会通向重要问题的事情上。

### E9

- 位置：演讲正文，"Great Thoughts Time" 一段
- 原文："I finally adopted what I called ``Great Thoughts Time.'' When I went to lunch Friday noon, I would only discuss great thoughts after that."
- 要点：Hamming 每周五午饭后专门留出时间，只思考大问题（例如计算机将如何改变科学）。

### E10

- 位置：演讲正文，"Great Thoughts Time" 之后
- 原文："Most great scientists know many important problems. They have something between 10 and 20 important problems for which they are looking for an attack. And when they see a new idea come up, one hears them say ``Well that bears on this problem.''"
- 要点：优秀的科学家心里装着 10 到 20 个重要问题，一直在找攻克途径；看到新想法时，能立刻意识到它和哪个问题有关。

### E11

> 已作废：与 [E18](#e18) 重复（原文被 E18 完整包含）。

- 位置：演讲正文，"door open or the door closed" 一段
- 原文："He who works with the door open gets all kinds of interruptions, but he also occasionally gets clues as to what the world is and what might be important."
- 要点：开着门工作会被打扰，但也会不时得到"什么可能重要"的线索；关着门的人短期更高效，长期却可能做偏。

### E12

- 位置：演讲正文，讲 "ambiguity" 和 "emotional commitment" 之间
- 原文："Great contributions are rarely done by adding another decimal place."
- 要点：重大贡献很少来自"再多算一位小数"式的改进。

### E13

- 位置：演讲正文，讲把问题当作一类问题来解决的一段
- 原文："By changing a problem slightly you can often do great work rather than merely good work."
- 要点：把问题稍微换个提法，常常能把"不错的工作"变成"重大的工作"。

### E14

- 位置：讲 Bode 的话（"compound interest"）
- 原文："Knowledge and productivity are like compound interest." / "Given two people with exactly the same ability, the one person who manages day in and day out to get in one more hour of thinking will be tremendously more productive over a lifetime."
- 要点：知识和产出像复利。能力相同的两个人，每天多思考一小时的那个，一生的产出会多得多。

### E15

> 已作废：与 [E1](#e1) 重复（原文被 E1 完整包含）。

- 位置：讲"important problems"的部分
- 原文："If you do not work on an important problem, it's unlikely you'll do important work."
- 要点：不做重要的问题，就不太可能做出重要的工作。

### E16

- 位置：讲 Clogston 与 Shannon 的部分
- 原文："One of the characteristics of successful scientists is having courage. Once you get your courage up and believe that you can do important problems, then you can. If you think you can't, almost surely you are not going to."
- 要点：成功的科学家有勇气；相信自己能做重要问题，才可能做到；认为自己不行，几乎肯定做不到。

### E17

- 位置：总结部分
- 原文："Friday afternoons for years - great thoughts only - means that I committed 10% of my time trying to understand the bigger problems in the field, i.e. what was and what was not important."
- 要点：Hamming 多年把每周五下午留给"大问题"，用 10% 的时间思考领域里什么重要、什么不重要。

### E18

- 位置：讲"door open / door closed"的部分
- 原文："if you have the door to your office closed, you get more work done today and tomorrow, and you are more productive than most. But 10 years later somehow you don't know quite know what problems are worth working on" / "He who works with the door open gets all kinds of interruptions, but he also occasionally gets clues as to what the world is and what might be important."
- 要点：关门工作，短期效率高，但十年后可能不知道什么问题值得做；开门工作会被打扰，但能得到"什么重要"的线索。

### E19

- 位置：讲 drive 的部分
- 原文："Just hard work is not enough - it must be applied sensibly."
- 要点：光努力不够，努力要用对地方。

### E20

> 已作废：与 [E3](#e3) 重复（原文被 E3 完整包含）。

- 位置：讲 ambiguity 的部分
- 原文："They believe the theory enough to go ahead; they doubt it enough to notice the errors and faults so they can step forward and create the new replacement theory."
- 要点：优秀的科学家能容忍不确定：足够相信，才能往前走；足够怀疑，才能发现错误。

### E21

- 位置：问答部分
- 原文："Somewhere around every seven years make a significant, if not complete, shift in your field."
- 要点：大约每七年在自己的领域内做一次较大的方向调整，避免想法枯竭。

### E22

- 位置：讲 luck 的部分
- 原文："The prepared mind sooner or later finds something important and does it."
- 要点：有准备的头脑迟早会发现并做成重要的事。具体做成哪件是运气，做成某件不是。

### E23

- 位置：讲 emotional commitment 的部分
- 原文："Most great scientists are completely committed to their problem. Those who don't become committed seldom produce outstanding, first-class work."
- 要点：大多数伟大的科学家完全投入自己的问题；不投入的人很少做出一流工作。
