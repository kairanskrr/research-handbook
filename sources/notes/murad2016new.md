# Murad et al. 2016：New evidence pyramid

- 资料：`murad2016new`，[Evidence-Based Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1136/ebmed-2016-110401)，[PMC 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4975798/)（CC BY-NC 4.0）
- 版本：Evid Based Med 2016;21(4):125–127。

### E1

- 位置：正文第 2 段
- 原文："Various versions of the evidence pyramid have been described, but all of them focused on showing weaker study designs in the bottom (basic science and case series), followed by case–control and cohort studies in the middle, then randomised controlled trials (RCTs), and at the very top, systematic reviews and meta-analysis."
- 要点：传统证据金字塔：底部是基础研究和病例系列，中间是病例对照和队列研究，往上是随机对照试验，最顶端是系统综述和荟萃分析。

### E2

- 位置：Rationale for modification 1
- 原文："Study design alone appears to be insufficient on its own as a surrogate for risk of bias. Certain methodological limitations of a study, imprecision, inconsistency and indirectness, were factors independent from study design and can affect the quality of evidence derived from any study design."
- 要点：只看研究设计不足以判断偏倚风险；方法学缺陷、不精确、不一致、间接性等因素与设计无关，会影响任何设计的证据质量。

### E3

- 位置：Rationale for modification 1
- 原文："Hence, despite the fact of having five RCTs, such evidence should not be rated high in any pyramid. The quality of evidence can also be rated up."
- 要点：即使有五个随机试验，方法有缺陷时证据也不该评为高；反过来，证据质量也可以上调。

### E4

- 位置：Rationale for modification 1
- 原文："we are quite certain about the benefits of hip replacement in a patient with disabling hip osteoarthritis. Although not tested in RCTs, the quality of this evidence is rated up despite the study design (non-randomised observational studies)."
- 要点：髋关节置换的获益没有经过随机试验检验，但证据质量仍被上调。

### E5

- 位置：Rationale for modification 2
- 原文："In other words, a meta-analysis of well-conducted RCTs at low risk of bias cannot be equated with a meta-analysis of observational studies at higher risk of bias."
- 要点：低偏倚风险随机试验的荟萃分析，和高偏倚风险观察性研究的荟萃分析，不能等同看待。

### E6

- 位置：Rationale for modification 2
- 原文："Therefore, the second modification to the pyramid is to remove systematic reviews from the top of the pyramid and use them as a lens through which other types of studies should be seen (ie, appraised and applied)."
- 要点：作者建议把系统综述从金字塔顶端拿掉，当作审视和应用其他研究的"透镜"。

### E7

- 位置：Rationale for modification 2
- 原文："The systematic review (the process of selecting the studies) and meta-analysis (the statistical aggregation that produces a single effect size) are tools to consume and apply the evidence by stakeholders."
- 要点：系统综述是挑选研究的过程；荟萃分析是把多项研究的结果用统计方法合并成一个效应量。
