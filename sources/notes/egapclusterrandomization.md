# EGAP 方法指南：10 Things You Need to Know About Cluster Randomization

- 资料：`egapclusterrandomization`，[原文网页](https://methods.egap.org/guides/data-collection/cluster-randomization_en.html)
- 版本："治理与政治证据网络"（EGAP）方法指南，作者 Jake Bowers、Ashlea Rundlett、Jasper Cooper、Anna Wilke；2026 年 10 月访问。位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§1 What clustering is
- 原文："Cluster randomized experiments allocate treatments to groups, but measure outcomes at the level of the individuals that compose the groups."
- 要点：整群随机实验把干预分配给群体，却在群体内的个人层面测量结果。

### E2

- 位置：§2 Why clustering can matter I: information reduction
- 原文："If 200 observations arose from 10 clusters with 20 individuals within each cluster, where ICC = .5, such that 50% of the variation could be attributed to cluster-to-cluster differences (and not to differences within a cluster), Kish’s formula would suggest that we have an effective sample size of about 19 observations, instead of 200."
- 要点：200 个观测来自 10 个群、每群 20 人，组内相关系数为 0.5 时，有效样本量只有约 19，而不是 200。

### E3

- 位置：§3 What to do about information reduction
- 原文："When we ignore the clustered-assignment, the standard error is too small: we are over-confident about the amount of information provided to us by the experiment."
- 要点：分析时忽略整群分配，标准误会偏小，高估了实验提供的信息。
