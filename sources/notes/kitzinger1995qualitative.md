# Kitzinger 1995：Qualitative research: Introducing focus groups

- 资料：`kitzinger1995qualitative`，[原文（PubMed Central，扫描版 PDF）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2550365/)
- 版本：BMJ 1995;311(7000):299–302，"定性研究"七篇系列的第五篇，医学与社会学。PubMed Central 上是扫描页，下面的原文取自扫描 PDF 的文字层；文字识别把个别词连在一起（如 "ofthe"），摘录时补回了空格，其余逐字照录。位置写期刊页码。

### E1

- 位置：Rationale and uses of focus groups，第 299 页
- 原文："Focus groups are a form of group interview that capitalises on communication between research participants in order to generate data. Although group interviews are often used simply as a quick and convenient way to collect data from several people simultaneously, focus groups explicitly use group interaction as part of the method."
- 要点：焦点小组是利用参与者之间的交流来产生数据的小组访谈；它和"一次问好几个人"的小组访谈不同，明确把组内互动当作方法的一部分。

### E2

- 位置：Rationale and uses of focus groups，第 299 页
- 原文："The method is particularly useful for exploring people's knowledge and experiences and can be used to examine not only what people think but how they think and why they think that way."
- 要点：焦点小组特别适合探索人们的知识和经历，不只看人们怎么想，还看他们怎样想、为什么这样想。

### E3

- 位置：方框 Some potential sampling advantages with focus groups，第 299 页
- 原文："Do not discriminate against people who cannot read or write" / "Can encourage participation from those who are reluctant to be interviewed on their own"
- 要点：焦点小组不排斥不会读写的人，也能让不愿意单独接受访谈的人参与进来。

### E4

- 位置：Rationale and uses of focus groups，第 300 页
- 原文："The downside of such group dynamics is that the articulation of group norms may silence individual voices of dissent. The presence of other research participants also compromises the confidentiality of the research session."
- 要点：小组动态的坏处是，群体规范可能压住个人的不同意见；其他参与者在场，也让访谈的保密打了折扣。

### E5

- 位置：Rationale and uses of focus groups，第 300 页
- 原文："Group work can actively facilitate the discussion of taboo topics because the less inhibited members of the group break the ice for shyer participants."
- 要点：小组也可能反过来帮助讨论禁忌话题：放得开的成员先开口，替害羞的人打破僵局。

### E6

- 位置：Sampling and group composition，第 300 页
- 原文："Focus group studies can consist of anything between half a dozen to over fifty groups, depending on the aims of the project and the resources available. Most studies involve just a few groups, and some combine this method with other data collection techniques."
- 要点：一项研究做几组到五十多组不等，取决于目的和资源；多数研究只做几组，有的和其他数据收集方法结合。

### E7

- 位置：Sampling and group composition，第 300 页
- 原文："Focus group discussion of a questionnaire is ideal for testing the phrasing of questions and is also useful in explaining or exploring survey results."
- 要点：让焦点小组讨论问卷，很适合检验题目措辞，也能用来解释或深挖问卷结果。

### E8

- 位置：Sampling and group composition，第 300 页
- 原文："Most researchers recommend aiming for homogeneity within each group in order to capitalise on people's shared experiences. However, it can also be advantageous to bring together a diverse group (for example, from a range of professions) to maximise exploration of different perspectives within a group setting. However, it is important to be aware of how hierarchy within the group may affect the data"
- 要点：多数研究者建议组内同质，以利用共同经历；也可以有意混合不同背景的人来扩大视角，但要留意组内的上下级关系会影响数据。

### E9

- 位置：Sampling and group composition，第 300 页
- 原文："The groups can be "naturally occurring" (for example, people who work together) or may be drawn together specifically for the research. Using preexisting groups allows observation of fragments of interactions that approximate to naturally occurring data"
- 要点：小组可以是本来就存在的（如同事），也可以专为研究召集；用现成的小组，能观察到接近自然状态的互动片段。

### E10

- 位置：Running the groups，第 301 页
- 原文："The ideal group size is between four and eight people. Sessions may last one to two hours (or extend into a whole afternoon or a series of meetings)."
- 要点：Kitzinger 认为理想的小组人数是 4 到 8 人，一次一到两小时。

### E11

- 位置：Running the groups，第 301 页
- 原文："The facilitator should explain that the aim of focus groups is to encourage people to talk to each other rather than to address themselves to the researcher." / "Later on in the session, however, the researcher can adopt a more interventionist style: urging debate to continue beyond the stage it might otherwise have ended and encouraging the group to discuss the inconsistencies both between participants and within their own thinking."
- 要点：主持人要说明讨论的目的是让大家彼此交谈，而不是对着研究者说；后半段可以更主动，推动争论继续，请大家讨论彼此之间和自己想法中的矛盾。

### E12

- 位置：Running the groups，第 301 页
- 原文："Differences between individual one off interviews have to be analysed by the researchers through armchair theorising; differences between members of focus groups should be explored in situ with the help of the research participants."
- 要点：单独访谈之间的分歧只能由研究者事后推想；焦点小组成员之间的分歧可以当场请参与者一起弄清楚。

### E13

- 位置：Running the groups，第 301 页
- 原文："A common exercise consists of presenting the group with a series of statements on large cards." / "The final layout of the cards is less important than the discussion that it generates."
- 要点：常用的小组练习是让大家一起给写在卡片上的说法分类排序；重要的是排序引起的讨论，而不是最后的排法。

### E14

- 位置：Running the groups，第 301 页
- 原文："Finally, it may be beneficial to present research participants with a brief questionnaire, or the opportunity to speak to the researcher privately, giving each one the opportunity to record private comments after the group session has been completed."
- 要点：讨论结束后，可以发一份简短问卷或安排私下交谈，让每个人有机会留下不便在组里说的意见。

### E15

- 位置：Running the groups，第 301 页
- 原文："Ideally the group discussions should be tape recorded and transcribed. If this is not possible then it is vital to take careful notes"
- 要点：小组讨论最好录音并转录；做不到时，一定要仔细记笔记。

### E16

- 位置：Analysis and writing up，第 301 页
- 原文："In general, it is not appropriate to give percentages in reports of focus group data, and it is important to try to distinguish between individual opinions expressed in spite of the group from the actual group consensus. As in all qualitative analysis, deviant case analysis is important"
- 要点：焦点小组报告一般不宜给百分比；要区分顶着小组压力说出的个人意见和真正的小组共识；和其他定性分析一样，要分析不合主流的个案。

### E17

- 位置：Analysis and writing up，第 301–302 页
- 原文："The only distinct feature of working with focus group data is the need to indicate the impact of the group dynamic and analyse the sessions in ways that take full advantage of the interaction between research participants. In coding the script of a group discussion, it is worth using special categories for certain types of narrative, such as jokes and anecdotes, and types of interaction, such as "questions," "deferring to the opinion of others," "censorship," or "changes of mind.""
- 要点：分析焦点小组数据唯一特别之处，是要说明小组动态的影响、利用好参与者之间的互动；编码时可以给玩笑、轶事等叙述类型，以及提问、附和他人、压制、改变主意等互动类型设专门的编码。

### E18

- 位置：Analysis and writing up，第 302 页
- 原文："A focus group research report that is true to its data should also usually include at least some illustrations of the talk between participants, rather than simply presenting isolated quotations taken out of context."
- 要点：忠于数据的焦点小组报告，应当至少展示一些参与者之间的对话，而不是只摆出脱离上下文的单句引语。

### E19

- 位置：Conclusion，第 302 页
- 原文："Interviews may be more appropriate for tapping into individual biographies, but focus groups are more suitable for examining how knowledge, and more importantly, ideas, develop and operate within a given cultural context. Questionnaires are more appropriate for obtaining quantitative information and explaining how many people hold a certain (pre-defined) opinion; focus groups are better for exploring exactly how those opinions are constructed."
- 要点：了解个人经历更适合用访谈；看知识和想法怎样在特定文化环境中形成和运作，更适合用焦点小组；问卷适合回答"多少人持某种看法"，焦点小组适合探索这些看法是怎样形成的。
