# CIOMS 2016：International Ethical Guidelines for Health-related Research Involving Humans

- 资料：`cioms2016ethical`，[原文 PDF](https://cioms.ch/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/WEB-CIOMS-EthicalGuidelines.pdf)
- 版本：国际医学科学组织理事会（Council for International Organizations of Medical Sciences，CIOMS）与世界卫生组织合作编写，2016 年第 4 版。位置写准则编号和小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Preface, Scope of the 2016 version
- 原文："The Working Group decided to broaden the scope of the 2002 Guidelines from “biomedical research” to “health-related research”."
- 要点：2016 版把适用范围从"生物医学研究"扩大到"健康相关研究"。

### E2

- 位置：Preface, Scope of the 2016 version
- 原文："The current scope is confined to the classic activities that fall under health-related research with humans, such as observational research, clinical trials, biobanking and epidemiological studies."
- 要点：现行范围包括观察性研究、临床试验、生物样本库和流行病学研究等经典的涉人健康研究。

### E3

- 位置：Preface
- 原文："the Working Group decided to merge the CIOMS Guidelines for Biomedical Research with the CIOMS Guidelines for Epidemiological Research."
- 要点：2016 版把生物医学研究准则和流行病学研究准则合并为一份。

### E4

- 位置：Guideline 1: Scientific and social value and respect for rights
- 原文："The ethical justification for undertaking health-related research involving humans is its scientific and social value"
- 要点：开展涉人健康研究的伦理理由，在于它的科学价值和社会价值。

### E5

- 位置：Guideline 1
- 原文："Scientific and social value cannot legitimate subjecting study participants or host communities to mistreatment, or injustice."
- 要点：科学和社会价值再大，也不能成为亏待参与者或所在社区的理由。

### E6

- 位置：Guideline 4 评注，Risks to groups
- 原文："research results in certain fields (for example, epidemiology, genetics, and sociology) may present risks to the interests of communities, societies, families, or racially or ethnically defined groups."
- 要点：流行病学、遗传学、社会学等领域的研究结果，可能损害社区、家庭或某个种族、民族群体的利益。

### E7

- 位置：Guideline 4 评注，Risks to groups
- 原文："Research results could therefore stigmatize a group or expose its members to discrimination."
- 要点：研究结果可能让一个群体被污名化，或让其成员遭受歧视。

### E8

- 位置：Guideline 7: Community engagement
- 原文："Researchers, sponsors, health authorities and relevant institutions should engage potential participants and communities in a meaningful participatory process that involves them in an early and sustained manner in the design, development, implementation, design of the informed consent process and monitoring of research, and in the dissemination of its results."
- 要点：研究者等各方应让潜在参与者和社区及早、持续地参与研究的设计、实施、知情同意流程设计、监督和结果传播。

### E9

- 位置：Guideline 7 评注
- 原文："community engagement is a means of ensuring the relevance of proposed research to the affected community, as well as its acceptance by the community."
- 要点：社区参与能保证研究对社区有意义，也让社区接受这项研究。

### E10

- 位置：Guideline 7 评注
- 原文："For instance, when community leaders are men only, researchers should actively include the views of women, as well."
- 要点：社区领袖全是男性时，研究者要主动听取女性的意见。

### E11

- 位置：Guideline 7 评注
- 原文："The research protocol or other documents submitted to the research ethics committee should include a description of the plan for community engagement"
- 要点：交给伦理委员会的研究方案里，要写社区参与计划。

### E12

- 位置：Guideline 12: Collection, storage and use of data in health-related research
- 原文："When data are used that were collected in the context of routine clinical care, an informed opt-out procedure must be used."
- 要点：使用日常诊疗中收集的数据，必须提供知情退出的途径。

### E13

- 位置：Guideline 12
- 原文："1) the research would not be feasible or practicable to carry out without the waiver; and 2) the research has important social value; and 3) the research poses no more than minimal risks to participants or to the group to which the participant belongs."
- 要点：使用以前为其他目的收集、没有取得研究同意的数据时，伦理委员会可以免除个人知情同意，条件是：不免除就做不了；研究有重要社会价值；对参与者及其所属群体的风险不超过最低风险。

### E14

- 位置：Guideline 12
- 原文："Custodians of the data must arrange to protect the confidentiality of the information linked to the data, by sharing only anonymised or coded data with researchers"
- 要点：数据保管方只能向研究者提供匿名化或编码后的数据，保护保密性。

### E15

- 位置：Guideline 12
- 原文："Data from low-resource settings should only be collected and stored in collaboration with local health authorities."
- 要点：在资源匮乏地区收集和保存数据，应与当地卫生部门合作。

### E16

- 位置：Guideline 21: Cluster randomized trials 评注
- 原文："In this research design, groups of individuals (clusters), communities, hospitals, or units of a health facility are randomized to different interventions."
- 要点：整群随机试验随机分配的是人群、社区、医院或科室，而不是个人。

### E17

- 位置：Guideline 21
- 原文："determine whether requiring informed consent and allowing refusal to consent may invalidate or compromise the research results"
- 要点：整群随机试验开始前，要判断要求知情同意、允许拒绝是否会破坏研究结果。

### E18

- 位置：Preface, Scope of the 2016 version
- 原文："The Working Group also acknowledged that there is no clear distinction between the ethics of social science research, behavioural studies, public health surveillance and the ethics of other research activities."
- 要点：工作组承认，社会科学研究、行为研究、公共卫生监测的伦理，和其他研究的伦理之间没有清楚的界线。
