# Hicks 2014：Advice on reviewing papers

- 资料：`hicks2014reviewing`，[原文](http://www.pl-enthusiast.net/2014/08/21/advice-reviewing-papers/)
- 版本：博客文章，The PL Enthusiast，2014-08-21。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Reviews serve two purposes. They judge whether research is well done, and they give feedback to the authors to improve their research."
- 要点：审稿有两个作用：判断研究做得好不好，以及给作者反馈帮助改进。

### E2

- 位置：Judging the work
- 原文："There are four high-level things you are judging about the paper: Is this an interesting/useful idea or study? Is it new, or is it significantly overlapping with prior work? Are the conclusions drawn in the paper correct? Is the paper sufficiently accessible/well-written that others will understand it?"
- 要点：审稿主要判断四件事：是否有意思/有用、是否新、结论是否正确、是否写得让人看得懂。

### E3

- 位置：Start with a summary
- 原文："Write a concise, judgement-free summary of the paper before the judgement and justification."
- 要点：审稿意见先写一段不带评判的简短摘要，再给判断和理由。

### E4

- 位置：Scores abstract text
- 原文："You should view the text as primary, and the scores as an abstraction of the text, not a complement to it."
- 要点：文字是主体，分数只是文字的概括；作者读了文字应该能猜出分数。

### E5

- 位置：Put the most important parts first
- 原文："make sure the key parts of your argument appear at the top. Supporting details should be afterward."
- 要点：最关键的论点放在最前面，支撑细节放后面。

### E6

- 位置：Be respectful
- 原文："Be respectful of the authors, even if the paper fails on every count. Criticize the paper, not the people. Avoid hyperbole."
- 要点：即使论文处处不行也要尊重作者；批评论文，不批评人；不夸张。

### E7

- 位置：Confidentiality
- 原文："Submissions (double-blind or not) are made in confidence. Do not reveal that a paper has been submitted somewhere. Do not pass them on to others."
- 要点：投稿内容是保密的：不透露、不转给别人。

### E8

- 位置：Judging the work
- 原文："On the other hand, a proposed solution may overlap with a prior solution, but still provide new ideas and useful data, and therefore enjoy some novelty."
- 要点：和已有工作有重叠，不等于没有新意；如果提供了新想法和有用的数据，仍然有一定新颖性。

### E9

- 位置：Interesting or useful?
- 原文："Making such a decision requires some sense of the values of the venue/community you are reviewing for."
- 要点：判断论文是否够格，要了解该会议/期刊和所在学术社区的标准。

### E10

- 位置：Interesting or useful?
- 原文："Ultimately, the collective judgment of all reviewers will determine whether the paper is good enough; it’s more art than science."
- 要点：最终结果由全体审稿人共同判断，这更像艺术而非科学。

### E11

- 位置：Reading the paper
- 原文："Therefore, a useful strategy in reviewing papers is to read the introduction and conclusions carefully, along with the related work, and then skim the middle parts."
- 要点：一种审稿策略：仔细读引言、结论和相关工作，中间部分可以略读；如果前两项标准不过关，就不必深挖细节。

### E12

- 位置：Suggestions for improvement
- 原文："You’ve already sunk a few hours into reading the paper; why not spend another 30 minutes to an hour helping the authors make their paper even better?"
- 要点：已经花了几小时读论文，再多花半小时到一小时给出改进建议，很值得。
