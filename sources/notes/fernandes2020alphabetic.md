# Fernandes & Cortez 2020：Alphabetic order of authors in scholarly publications

- 资料：`fernandes2020alphabetic`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11192-020-03686-0)
- 版本：Scientometrics 125:2773–2792（2020）。本文读的是第一作者主页上的投稿稿 https://www.di.uminho.pt/~jmf/PUBLI/papers/2020-sciento.pdf ，和正式发表版可能有出入。数据取自 Scopus，2020 年 5 月下载。

### E1

- 位置：§3.1（数据集），第 5–6 页
- 原文："Dataset 1 (DS1) is related with all papers that were published in a prestigious journal of a subject area" / "This situation occurred, for example, for #2 (Arts & Humanities), due to its wide scope. The selected journal is Nous, which appears in the 14th position."
- 要点：在"艺术与人文"这个领域，作者选作代表的期刊是哲学期刊 Noûs（第一个数据集按每个领域选一本有声望的期刊）。

### E2

- 位置：结论
- 原文："three fields show a strong tendency to have their authors ordered alphabetically: Economics; Mathematics; and Business, Management & Accounting." / "The fields of Social Sciences; Physics & Astronomy; Biochemistry, Genetics & Molecular Biology; Earth & Planetary Sciences; and Arts & Humanities have a moderate tendency."
- 要点：经济学、数学、商业管理三个领域强烈倾向按字母给作者排序；社会科学、物理与天文、生物化学、地球科学和艺术与人文等五个领域有中等程度的倾向。

### E3

- 位置：§3.1（数据集），第 5–6 页；表 3
- 原文："Dataset 2 (DS2) contains the metadata" / "the DS2 sample includes recent articles, published in the years of 2018, 2019 and 2020." / "Nous The Philosophical Review" / "Ethics" / "Nous-Supplement: Philosophical Issues" / "British Journal for the Philosophy of Science" / "Philosophy and Phenomenological Research" / "Philosophical Quarterly Mind" / "Philosophical Studies" / "Table 3 Selected journals for DS2."
- 要点：第二个数据集取每个领域几本顶尖期刊 2018–2020 年的论文；表 3 里"艺术与人文"一栏列的是 Noûs、The Philosophical Review、Ethics、Philosophical Issues、British Journal for the Philosophy of Science、Philosophy and Phenomenological Research、Philosophical Quarterly、Mind、Philosophical Studies 九本哲学期刊。

### E4

- 位置：§5（局限）
- 原文："dataset only considers one journal for each subject area (with one exception)." / "Therefore, the results for DS1 should be seen with some caution"
- 要点：第一个数据集每个领域只取一本期刊，作者提醒对它的结果要谨慎看待。
