# Stol, Ralph & Fitzgerald 2016：Grounded Theory in Software Engineering Research: A Critical Review and Guidelines

- 资料：`stol2016grounded`，[原文（利默里克大学机构库作者版）](https://hdl.handle.net/10344/5396)
- 版本：ICSE 2016 论文，pp. 120–131，DOI 10.1145/2884781.2884833。机构库版本，许可 CC BY-NC-SA 1.0。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："The goal of GT is to generate theory rather than test or validate existing theory. GT is suitable for investigating questions such as what's going on here?"
- 要点：扎根理论的目标是生成理论，而不是检验已有理论；适合回答"这里到底发生了什么"这类问题。

### E2

- 位置：§2.1 Key Components of Grounded Theory
- 原文："Grounded Theory refers to a method of inductively generating theory from data" / "GT studies often focus on unstructured text (e.g. interview transcripts, documents, field notes); however, they may also include structured text, diagrams and images, and even quantitative data"
- 要点：扎根理论是从数据中归纳生成理论的方法；数据多为访谈转录、文档、现场笔记等非结构化文本，也可以包括结构化文本、图表甚至定量数据。

### E3

- 位置：§2.3 Versions of Grounded Theory
- 原文："It is now widely acknowledged that there are at least three main streams of GT" / "Glaser's GT (classic or Glaserian GT); Strauss and Corbin's GT (Straussian GT); and Charmaz's constructivist GT."
- 要点：扎根理论至少有三个主要流派：Glaser 的经典扎根理论、Strauss 和 Corbin 的扎根理论、Charmaz 的建构主义扎根理论。

### E4

- 位置：§2.3 Versions of Grounded Theory
- 原文："Classic GT can be characterized as having a strong focus on emergence (of research questions, of codes, of theory), whereas Straussian GT meticulously suggests a set of 'mini-steps.'" / "Constructivist GT emphasizes understanding and acknowledges that data, interpretations, and resulting theory depend on the researcher's view."
- 要点：经典扎根理论强调"自然浮现"（研究问题、编码、理论都从数据中浮现）；Strauss 版规定了一系列细小步骤；建构主义版强调理解，承认数据解读和理论都取决于研究者的视角。

### E5

- 位置：Table 1，Research question 一行
- 原文："The researcher starts with an 'area of interest.'" / "Defining a RQ a priori is considered 'forcing'" / "Research question may be defined upfront, derived from the literature or suggested by a colleague; RQ is often broad and open-ended." / "Research begins with "initial research questions," which evolve throughout the study"
- 要点：经典版从"感兴趣的领域"出发，事先定研究问题被视为"强加"；Strauss 版可以事先定一个宽泛开放的问题；建构主义版从"初始研究问题"开始，问题在研究中演变。

### E6

- 位置：Table 1，Role of the literature 一行
- 原文："An extensive literature review should be delayed until after the theory is emerging to prevent the influence of existing concepts on the emerging theory." / "The literature may be consulted throughout the process, as concepts from the literature may be used if applicable" / "Acknowledges not only Glaser's reasons for delaying the literature review but also the impracticality of this strategy. Charmaz highlights the need to tailor a literature review to fit the purpose of the GT study"
- 要点：经典版主张理论浮现之后再做全面的文献综述；Strauss 版允许全程查阅文献；建构主义版承认推迟文献综述的理由，也承认它不现实，主张按研究目的裁剪文献综述。

### E7

- 位置：§2.1，Limit exposure to literature
- 原文："A major reason to limit study of the literature is to prevent the researcher from testing existing theories, or thinking in terms of established concepts."
- 要点：限制阅读文献的主要原因，是防止研究者变成在检验已有理论，或用现成概念去想问题。

### E8

- 位置：Table 1，Coding procedures 一行（建构主义扎根理论）
- 原文："Initial coding: examining data word-by-word, line-by-line or incident-by-incident to make sense of the text without injecting the researcher's assumptions, biases, motivations." / "Focused coding: selecting categories from the most frequent or important codes, and using them to categorize the data; does not require a single core category or variable." / "Theoretical coding: specifying the relationship between categories to integrate them into a cohesive theory."
- 要点：建构主义扎根理论的编码分三步：初始编码（逐词、逐行或逐事件地理解数据，不掺入研究者的假设）；聚焦编码（从最常见或最重要的编码中选出类别，用来归类数据，不要求只有一个核心类别）；理论编码（说明类别之间的关系，整合成一个连贯的理论）。

### E9

- 位置：Table 1，Coding procedures 一行（经典与 Strauss 版）
- 原文："Open coding: 'fracturing' of the data" / "Open coding: generation of 'categories' and how they vary dimensionally." / "Selective coding: delimiting coding to only those variables that relate to one (or in some cases, several) core variables to establish a parsimonious theory." / "Axial coding: putting back data in new ways after open coding by identifying relationships between categories; this is effectively Glaser's theoretical coding." / "Selective coding: deciding on the central category that all major categories can link to"
- 要点：两派都从开放编码开始。经典版的选择性编码只保留与核心变量相关的编码；Strauss 版有"轴心编码"（找出类别之间的关系，相当于 Glaser 的理论编码），其选择性编码是确定一个所有主要类别都能连上的中心类别。两派对同一术语的用法不同。

### E10

- 位置：§2.1，Immediate and continuous data analysis / Theoretical sampling
- 原文："The researcher begins analyzing data immediately and does not finish collecting data before beginning analysis—data collection and analysis are simultaneous" / "The researcher identifies further data sources based on gaps in the emerging theory or to further explore unsaturated concepts."
- 要点：研究者拿到数据就开始分析，不等数据收集完；收集和分析同时进行。理论抽样：根据正在形成的理论中的空缺，或为进一步探索尚未饱和的概念，确定下一步找什么数据。

### E11

- 位置：§2.1，Coding
- 原文："The researcher does not classify data into a preconceived coding scheme, or infer categories from logically deduced hypotheses"
- 要点：编码时不把数据归入事先定好的编码方案，也不从演绎出的假设推出类别。

### E12

- 位置：§2.1，Memoing
- 原文："The researcher writes memos (e.g. notes, diagrams, sketches) to elaborate categories as they emerge, describe preliminary properties and relationships between categories, and identify gaps" / "if the researcher skips this stage, he is not doing grounded theory"
- 要点：研究者写备忘录（笔记、图、草图），用来充实正在浮现的类别、描述类别的初步属性和相互关系、找出空缺。Glaser 认为跳过这一步就不是扎根理论。

### E13

- 位置：§2.1，Constant comparison
- 原文："From the start of the study, the researcher constantly compares data, memos, codes and categories"
- 要点：从研究一开始，研究者就不断地比较数据、备忘录、编码和类别。

### E14

- 位置：§2.1，Theoretical saturation
- 原文："Theoretical saturation refers to the point at which a theory's components are well supported and new data is no longer triggering revisions or reinterpretations of the theory"
- 要点：理论饱和指理论的各个部分都有充分支持，新数据不再引起理论的修改或重新解释。

### E15

- 位置：§2.1，Cohesive theory
- 原文："The researcher attempts to move beyond superficial categories and develop a cohesive theory of the studied phenomenon."
- 要点：研究者要超越表面的类别，对所研究的现象发展出一个连贯的理论。

### E16

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："We carefully and systematically selected 98 articles that mention GT, of which 52 explicitly claim to use GT, with the other 46 using GT techniques only. Only 16 articles provide detailed accounts of their research procedures."
- 要点：在 98 篇提到扎根理论的软件工程期刊论文中，52 篇明确声称用了扎根理论，46 篇只用了其中的技巧；只有 16 篇详细交代了研究过程。

### E17

- 位置：§4.4 Few "GT" Studies Generate Theory
- 原文："Eight articles presented contributions that were clearly cohesive theories consisting of constructs and relationships" / "Together these roles provide an explanation for the "social" process of selforganization in agile teams, and as such they go beyond a mere taxonomy of roles." / "We observe that studies that produce a 'set of themes' rather than a theory tend only to borrow discrete practices from GT—what we call grounded theory à la carte."
- 要点：52 篇中只有 8 篇明确给出了由概念和关系构成的连贯理论。Hoda 等人提出的六种角色解释了敏捷团队如何自组织，因此超出了单纯的角色分类。只产出"一组主题"的研究往往只是零散借用扎根理论的做法，作者称之为"点菜式扎根理论"。

### E18

- 位置：§4.2 Many Studies Present Little to No Detail
- 原文："One article claimed theoretical sampling, but instead of collecting additional data to further investigate as of yet unsaturated concepts in the emerging theory, a number of case companies were selected seemingly a priori"
- 要点：有论文自称做了理论抽样，实际却是事先选好了几家公司，而不是根据尚未饱和的概念去补充数据。

### E19

- 位置：§5.1 Method Slurring
- 原文："If a study does not involve simultaneous data collection and analysis, constant comparison, coding, memoing and theory development, it is not a grounded theory study." / "base analysis on seed categories or preconceived analytical frameworks, they are not using grounded theory."
- 要点：没有同时收集与分析、持续比较、编码、备忘录和理论发展的研究，不是扎根理论研究；先按已有理论分类数据、从"种子类别"或预设框架出发分析的，也不是。

### E20

- 位置：§5.2 Considerations for Conducting and Reporting Grounded Theory
- 原文："Research cannot be reconstructed as GT at write-up." / "researchers should describe their implementation of GT, not GT in principle." / "If techniques have been borrowed from the grounded theory literature, researchers should simply state that those techniques have been used without discussing GT."
- 要点：研究不能到写论文时才"重新包装"成扎根理论；要写自己具体怎么做的，而不是介绍扎根理论的原理；只借用了技巧的，直接说用了哪些技巧，不要扯上扎根理论。

### E21

- 位置：§5.2
- 原文："Anyone considering a GT study should read several books before even deciding whether GT is the right method, let alone beginning data collection." / "Good introductions are available for classic GT, Straussian GT, and constructivist GT."
- 要点：考虑做扎根理论的人，在决定是否采用之前，就应该先读几本专著，更不用说开始收集数据之前。三个流派各有入门著作（文中分别引 Glaser 1978 与 Glaser & Strauss 1967、Corbin & Strauss 2015、Charmaz 2014）。

### E22

- 位置：§5.1 Method Slurring
- 原文："Grounded Theory is not the only valid method of either analyzing predominately qualitative data or generating theories."
- 要点：扎根理论不是分析定性数据或生成理论的唯一合法方法。

### E23

- 位置：§5.3 Challenges in Doing Grounded Theory Research in Software Engineering
- 原文："one strategy is to choose an explicit primary data source (e.g. interviews) and theoretically sample from the remaining data based on leads arising from the primary data source." / "Participants' post-hoc reconstructions of how and why they performed certain actions are less reliable than, for example, their accounts of their current frustrations or enduring values." / "Source code, commit logs, project management data and direct observation allow the researcher to triangulate many interviewee claims."
- 要点：受访者事后对自己为何、如何做某事的重构，不如他们对当前困扰或长期价值观的陈述可靠。软件场景数据多而杂，一个办法是选定一个主要数据源（如访谈），再按它提供的线索从其余数据中做理论抽样；源代码、提交记录、项目管理数据和直接观察可以用来核对受访者的说法。

### E24

- 位置：Figure 5，Grounded Theory Data Collection and Analysis
- 原文："Describe your theoretical sampling with examples." / "When did you stop collecting data? Describe how theoretical saturation became apparent."
- 要点：报告时要举例说明理论抽样怎么做的，说明何时停止收集数据、怎样判断达到了理论饱和。

### E25

- 位置：§1 Introduction；§6 Conclusion
- 原文："There is growing awareness that Software Engineering (SE) research must consider social, cultural and human aspects of software construction" / "We believe that grounded theory offers a highly suitable methodology to address social, cultural and human aspects in software engineering"
- 要点：软件工程研究越来越重视软件开发中的社会、文化和人的因素；作者认为扎根理论很适合研究这些方面。

### E26

- 位置：§4.3 Many Studies Ignore GT Variants
- 原文："Of the 52 articles that claim to use grounded theory, 39 did not acknowledge the existence of different variants." / "Two articles claim or cite classic GT but use axial coding, a Straussian practice"
- 要点：52 篇声称用扎根理论的论文中，39 篇没有提到存在不同流派；有两篇声称用经典版，却用了 Strauss 版的轴心编码。

### E27

- 位置：§4.1 Grounded Theory "Use" is Ambiguous
- 原文："In three cases, the authors developed a set of preliminary categories, which were then combined with a "grounded theory approach"—starting with a classification from the literature is highly suspect"
- 要点：有三篇论文先定好一组初步类别，再"结合扎根理论方法"；从文献中的分类出发，是非常可疑的做法。

### E28

- 位置：§4.2 Many Studies Present Little to No Detail
- 原文："Besides being incomplete, it also suggests coding happens in three distinct phases, which is not what Glaser or Strauss had in mind."
- 要点：把扎根理论简述为"三个编码阶段"不仅不完整，还暗示编码分成三个截然分开的阶段，这并不是 Glaser 或 Strauss 的本意。
