# 英国口述历史学会：Looking after yourself（访谈者的自我照顾）

- 资料：`ohs2026wellbeing`，[原文](https://ohs.org.uk/looking-after-yourself/)
- 版本：英国口述历史学会（Oral History Society，OHS）网页，未注明日期，2026-10-06 读取。

### E1

- 位置：正文，关于日程
- 原文："try to avoid doing more than one interview a day"
- 要点：尽量一天不做超过一次访谈。

### E2

- 位置：正文，关于人身安全
- 原文："it is crucially important to tell someone who you are going to see, where and when, and at what sort of time you expect to be back from an interview"
- 要点：出门访谈前，一定告诉别人你去见谁、在哪里、什么时间，预计几点回来。

### E3

- 位置：正文，关于情绪支持
- 原文："If you anticipate that an interview may be distressing in the nature of the interview topic, it can be helpful to talk to someone in advance about how they have dealt with a similar situation"
- 要点：预计话题会令人痛苦时，事先找有类似经验的人谈谈他们是怎么应对的。

### E4

- 位置：正文，关于情绪支持
- 原文："Can you also identify someone who is willing for you to talk to them about your emotions and reactions after the interview, if you have been upset by what you have heard"
- 要点：事先找好访谈后可以倾诉的人，以防你被听到的内容影响情绪。

### E5

- 位置：正文，关于访谈以外的人员
- 原文："People archiving and digitising material will listen to sections of the recordings, and those cataloguing interviews will also be listening in detail, as will transcribers." / "think carefully in advance if there is there a way to build in breaks away from material (especially if the content is traumatic)"
- 要点：做存档、数字化、编目和转录的人也要细听录音，同样会受影响；事先安排好离开材料的休息，内容涉及创伤时尤其如此。
