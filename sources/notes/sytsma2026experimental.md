# Sytsma, Reuter & Willemsen 2026：Experimental Philosophy（斯坦福哲学百科全书）

- 资料：`sytsma2026experimental`，[原文](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/experimental-philosophy/)
- 版本：《斯坦福哲学百科全书》（Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy，免费在线）2026 年 4 月 8 日发布的新条目，作者 Justin Sytsma、Kevin Reuter、Pascale Willemsen，取代了此前由别的作者撰写的同名条目。位置写节号。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Experimental philosophy (or “x-phi”) is a methodological naturalist approach to philosophy that is distinguished by practitioners employing empirical methods for philosophical purposes."
- 要点：实验哲学（x-phi）的特点是用实证方法达到哲学目的。

### E2

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Most notably, this includes that experimentalists have often aimed to study “intuitions” concerning concepts of philosophical interest, usually by investigating the judgments of laypeople (or “the folk”)."
- 要点：最突出的主题是研究人们对哲学概念的"直觉"，通常调查普通人的判断。

### E3

- 位置：开头
- 原文："In line with this target, there is also a correspondingly prominent method in x-phi—the use of questionnaires soliciting judgments about short stories (or “vignettes”) based on philosophical thought experiments."
- 要点：相应的主要方法是问卷：给参与者看根据哲学思想实验改写的短故事（情境短文），请他们作判断。

### E4

- 位置：§2 Methods in Experimental Philosophy
- 原文："This will focus on the two approaches most common in the sub-discipline—questionnaire methods (§2.2) and corpus-linguistic methods (§2.3)—before concluding with a brief survey of the broader range found in the literature (§2.4)."
- 要点：这个分支最常用的两类方法是问卷法和语料库语言学方法，此外还有很多其他方法。

### E5

- 位置：§2.1 Basics of Experimental-Philosophical Research
- 原文："As such, it (ideally) begins with formulating arguments that engage with the surrounding philosophical literature."
- 要点：实验哲学本质上是做哲学，理想情况下从提出与已有哲学文献对话的论证开始。

### E6

- 位置：§2.1 Basics of Experimental-Philosophical Research
- 原文："Empirical premises in those arguments—premises involving claims about how our world is—then form the entry point to standard descriptions of the quantitative empirical research process, such as one would find in discussions of methods in experimental psychology."
- 要点：论证中的经验前提（关于世界实际如何的主张）是进入定量实证研究流程的入口，这套流程和实验心理学方法论里讲的一样。

### E7

- 位置：§2.1 Basics of Experimental-Philosophical Research
- 原文："Typically, the interpretation step will involve connecting the empirical results back to the philosophical arguments generating the research questions. And this will require that researchers consider the limitations of their studies in the context of the broader arguments they make."
- 要点：解释结果时，要把实证结果接回提出研究问题的哲学论证，并在更大的论证中考虑研究的局限。

### E8

- 位置：§1.2 Systematic Empirical Observation
- 原文："For instance, typical sample sizes have increased and statistical reporting has improved as experimental philosophy has developed, with some early studies falling short of contemporary standards in these regards."
- 要点：随着实验哲学的发展，样本量变大、统计报告变好；一些早期研究达不到今天的标准。

### E9

- 位置：§1.2 Systematic Empirical Observation
- 原文："Open science came about in part in response to the replication crisis in psychology (Open Science Collaboration 2015) and involves preregistering study designs, hypotheses, and predictions, as well as making raw data and code for statistical analyses available online."
- 要点：近年实验哲学越来越走向开放科学：预注册研究设计、假设和预测，在线公开原始数据和分析代码；开放科学部分源于心理学的可重复性危机。

### E10

- 位置：§1.2 Systematic Empirical Observation
- 原文："The key point is that in comparison to the types of informal observation Jackson suggests, the methods that practitioners of experimental philosophy adopt are designed to minimize the risk of drawing conclusions based on idiosyncratic or biased responses."
- 要点：和非正式地问问学生相比，实验哲学的方法是为了尽量避免根据个别或有偏的回答下结论。

### E11

- 位置：§1.2 Systematic Empirical Observation
- 原文："This might include taking steps to help ensure that students are answering independently from one another, such as by using individual questionnaires rather than a show of hands;"
- 要点：例如即使在课堂发问卷，也要让学生独立作答（发个人问卷而不是举手）。

### E12

- 位置：§4.1.3 Amateur psychology?
- 原文："Following Machery (2017: §5.1), this can be referred to as the amateur psychology objection, echoing Williamson (2010) who disparagingly refers to practitioners as “amateur experimentalists” and experimental philosophy as “imitation psychology”."
- 要点：对实验哲学的一种批评是"业余心理学"：批评者认为它做得粗糙。

### E13

- 位置：§4.1.3 Amateur psychology?
- 原文："Indeed, in recent years, experimental philosophers have called on empirical tools to investigate key methodological concerns for the practice, such as the adequacy of statistical reporting (Colombo et al. 2018), the occurrence of p-hacking (Stuart et al. 2019), and the replicability of findings (Cova, Strickland, et al. 2021). On each of these metrics, experimental philosophy fares rather better than the critical remarks would suggest."
- 要点：实验哲学家用实证方法检查了自己领域的统计报告、p-hacking 和可重复性；条目作者认为结果比批评者说的要好。

### E14

- 位置：§4.2 Divisions within Experimental Philosophy
- 原文："Thus, research in the positive program aims to bolster the practice of calling on intuitions as evidence in philosophy, most often by putting the surveying of intuitions on a firmer empirical footing."
- 要点："正面纲领"想加强以直觉为哲学证据的做法，主要是让调查直觉有更牢靠的实证基础。

### E15

- 位置：§4.2 Divisions within Experimental Philosophy
- 原文："By contrast, research in the negative program aims to undermine the standard practice, often by targeting underlying assumptions for the method of cases."
- 要点："负面纲领"则想动摇这一标准做法，常针对"案例法"背后的假设。

### E16

- 位置：§4.3 The Restrictionist Challenge
- 原文："The restrictionist challenge is driven by a body of empirical results that indicate that some of the case judgments called on in the armchair literature vary along dimensions that are not philosophically relevant to those judgments."
- 要点："限制论挑战"依据的是一批实证结果：书斋哲学文献中使用的某些案例判断，会随一些和哲学无关的因素而变化。

### E17

- 位置：§4.2 Divisions within Experimental Philosophy
- 原文："Explanatory experimental philosophy plausibly covers a notable majority of the work that has been done in the field (Knobe 2016)."
- 要点：解释型实验哲学（研究直觉本身、解释已发现的效应）可能占已有工作的大部分。

### E18

- 位置：§2.2 Questionnaires Involving Vignettes
- 原文："Thus, the method of cases is often treated as a central component of analytic philosophy. Vignette studies can be seen as the experimentalists’ analog of this method, allowing researchers to investigate case judgments in a systematic way"
- 要点："案例法"常被视为分析哲学的核心组成部分；情境短文研究可看作实验哲学家对这一方法的对应做法，用来系统地研究人们对案例的判断。
