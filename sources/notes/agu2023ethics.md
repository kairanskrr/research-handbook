# 美国地球物理学会 2023：AGU Scientific Integrity and Professional Ethics

- 资料：`agu2023ethics`，[原文](https://www.agu.org/-/media/Files/Learn-About-AGU/AGU_Scientific_Integrity_and_Professional_Ethics_Policy_document.pdf)
- 版本：美国地球物理学会（American Geophysical Union，AGU）《科学诚信与职业伦理政策》，2023 年更新版 PDF。

### E1

- 位置：I. Executive Summary
- 原文："The AGU Scientific Integrity and Professional Ethics Policy is a set of principles and practices for professional behavior regarding the practice, learning, training, publishing, and communication of science which governs all AGU members, staff, volunteers, and non-members participating in AGU sponsored programs and activities."
- 要点：这份政策约束 AGU 会员、员工、志愿者，以及参加 AGU 活动的非会员；它是一个学会的政策，不是全学科的法规。

### E2

- 位置：II. Preamble
- 原文："One of the goals of AGU is to educate and reinforce positive behavior in the scientific and learning environment, including the classroom, field settings, laboratory, industry, and professional meetings."
- 要点：AGU 明确把野外（field settings）列为要求良好行为的场合之一，和课堂、实验室、会议并列。

### E3

- 位置：III.D Harassment, Bullying, and Discrimination
- 原文："We affirm that discrimination, harassment (including sexual harassment), or bullying in any scientific or learning environment is unacceptable, and constitutes scientific misconduct under the AGU Scientific Integrity and Professional Ethics Policy."
- 要点：在 AGU 的政策里，歧视、骚扰（含性骚扰）和霸凌属于科研不端。

### E4

- 位置：III.B Responsibilities，第 3 条 Research Methods
- 原文："Members will employ research methods to the best of their understanding and ability, base conclusions on critical analysis of the evidence, and report findings and interpretations fully, accurately, and objectively, including characterization of uncertainties."
- 要点：AGU 要求完整、准确、客观地报告发现和解释，包括对不确定性的刻画。

### E5

- 位置：III.B Responsibilities，第 5 条 Research Findings
- 原文："Members will share data and findings openly and promptly, as soon as they have had an opportunity to establish intellectual property rights, if appropriate."
- 要点：AGU 要求会员公开、及时地共享数据和发现。

### E6

- 位置：III.B Responsibilities，第 15 条 Stewardship of the Earth
- 原文："Members have an ethical obligation to responsibly, accurately, and clearly inform the public about natural resources, hazards, and other geoscience phenomena of importance to the well-being of Earth and society."
- 要点：AGU 认为会员有伦理义务，向公众负责、准确、清楚地说明自然资源、灾害等地学现象。

### E7

- 位置：VI.C Ethical Obligations of Authors and Contributors，第 2 条
- 原文："Include sufficient detail and reference to sources of information in a manuscript to permit the author's peers to repeat the work. If there are any limitations on use of or access to data, these must be clearly identified."
- 要点：论文要写到同行能重复这项工作的程度；数据使用或获取有限制的，必须写清。

### E8

- 位置：VI.C，第 4 条
- 原文："Carefully document methodology, assumptions, and uncertainty."
- 要点：认真记录方法、假设和不确定性。

### E9

- 位置：VI.C，第 10 条
- 原文："Include as coauthors only those persons who have made significant scientific contributions to the work, and determine order of authorship in a manner appropriate to the contribution. Pay careful attention to inclusion and appropriate attribution of student work."
- 要点：只把有重要科学贡献的人列为合作者，按贡献排序；特别注意学生工作的署名和归属。

### E10

- 位置：VI.E Ethical Obligations of Authors Publishing for the Public，第 2 条
- 原文："Announce a discovery to the public only when the experimental, statistical, or theoretical support for it is of sufficient strength to warrant publication in the scientific literature."
- 要点：只有当证据强到足以在学术文献上发表时，才向公众宣布发现。

### E11

- 位置：V. Scientific Misconduct
- 原文："Scientific misconduct does not include errors of judgment; honest errors in the recording, selection, or analysis of data; or differences in opinions involving the interpretation of data. Fabrication does not include documented use of modeling or statistical techniques."
- 要点：诚实的错误和解释上的分歧不算不端；有记录地使用建模或统计方法不算捏造。

### E12

- 位置：VI.A Overview
- 原文："In Earth and space science, the growth of global collaborations, the public immediacy of many research results, and the implications for individuals and societies result in major ethical responsibilities for Editors, authors, reviewers, and the AGU Board and Council."
- 要点：AGU 认为，全球合作增多、很多结果直接关系公众和社会，使地球与空间科学的编辑、作者、审稿人负有重大伦理责任。

### E13

- 位置：VI.B Ethical Obligations of Editors，第 6 条
- 原文："Results that are at variance with the dominant paradigm, as well as null results, should be given full and equal consideration based upon the criteria of importance, originality, clarity, and relevance."
- 要点：AGU 要求编辑对与主流观点不一致的结果和零结果同等对待。
