# Rutner & Schonfeld 2012：Supporting the Changing Research Practices of Historians

- 资料：`rutner2012supporting`，[原文 PDF](https://sr.ithaka.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/supporting-the-changing-research-practices-of-historians.pdf)
- 版本：Ithaka S+R（一家非营利研究咨询机构）2012 年 12 月 10 日发布的最终报告，基于对美国 39 位历史学家和历史学博士生的访谈。页码为报告页脚页码。注意：样本不具代表性（见 E9），反映的是 2012 年前后的情况。

### E1

- 位置：Methodology，第 6 页
- 原文："For the second phase of this study, Ithaka S+R interviewed thirty-nine practicing academic historians and graduate students about their work practices."
- 要点：研究访谈了 39 位在职历史学者和研究生，了解他们的工作方式。

### E2

- 位置：Gathering and Using Primary Sources，第 11 页
- 原文："The widespread use of digital cameras and other scanning equipment to capture source materials is perhaps the single most significant shift in research practices among historians"
- 要点：用数码相机和扫描设备采集资料，可能是历史学家研究方式中最重大的一个变化。

### E3

- 位置：Gathering and Using Primary Sources，第 12 页
- 原文："Scholars rely on complex file structures and good memories to access their files once home from the archive."
- 要点：从档案馆回来后，学者只能靠复杂的文件夹结构和好记性来找照片。

### E4

- 位置：Gathering and Using Primary Sources，第 12 页
- 原文："One interviewee includes call slips in her photographs, which stated the name of the archive and the collection, so that she could always orient herself to the source."
- 要点：一位受访者拍照时把调阅单一起拍进去，上面写着档案馆和收藏名，以后总能知道照片出自哪里。

### E5

- 位置：Digital Research Methods, Collaboration, and Communication（GIS 部分），第 31 页
- 原文："This type of work can rarely be undertaken by a lone history scholar, and requires new ways of working collaboratively."
- 要点：地理信息系统（GIS）这类数字工作很少能由一位历史学者单独完成，需要新的合作方式。

### E6

- 位置：Promotion and Tenure，第 36 页
- 原文："The monograph remains the centerpiece of the tenure process for these historians."
- 要点：对受访的这些历史学者来说，专著仍是终身教职评审的核心。

### E7

- 位置：Promotion and Tenure，第 36 页
- 原文："In most cases, digital scholarship work is seen as a part of or a supplement to the monograph."
- 要点：多数情况下，数字学术成果被看作专著的一部分或补充。

### E8

- 位置：Scholarly Communication，第 33 页
- 原文："This seems to be a significant change from the typical “lone scholar” approach to historical research."
- 要点：有人用博客公开方法上的尝试、请同行讨论改进；报告认为这和历史研究典型的"孤身学者"（lone scholar）做法相比，是重大变化。

### E9

- 位置：Methodology，第 6 页
- 原文："Still, this sample of historians is not meant to be perfectly representative of the history community."
- 要点：报告自己说明，样本并不完全代表整个历史学界。

### E10

- 位置：Gathering and Using Primary Sources，第 12 页
- 原文："Scholars were able to spend time in the reading room photographing the collections, and would often postpone viewing the images until they returned home from the trip. This was notable in that some historians reported that they no longer engage intellectually with the sources while in the archive; these trips have become more of a collection mission."
- 要点：学者在阅览室里大量拍照，常常回家后才看照片；有人说在档案馆里已不再细读和思考资料，查档变成了"采集任务"。

### E11

- 位置：Gathering and Using Primary Sources，第 13 页
- 原文："These digital photographs clearly add value to the research process, but working with them effectively and efficiently remains a struggle for most."
- 要点：数码照片对研究有帮助，但多数人还没找到高效管理和使用它们的办法。
