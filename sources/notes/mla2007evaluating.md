# 美国现代语言协会 2007：Report of the MLA Task Force on Evaluating Scholarship for Tenure and Promotion

- 资料：`mla2007evaluating`，[原文](https://www.mla.org/content/download/3362/file/taskforcereport0608.pdf)
- 版本：2006 年 12 月完成，刊于 Profession 2007；美国现代语言协会（Modern Language Association，MLA）。原文文本中行末断词的连字符已合并

### E1

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："spring 2005 online survey of 1,339 departments in 734 institutions across the United States"
- 要点：调查对象：2005 年春，美国 734 所院校的 1,339 个英语系和外语系。

### E2

- 位置：Survey Methodology
- 原文："While these departments and institutions do not constitute a sample representative of all four-year United States colleges and universities"
- 要点：作答的院系不代表美国所有四年制高校。

### E3

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："Indeed, 88.9% of departments in Carnegie Doctorategranting, 44.4% in Carnegie Master's, and 48% in Carnegie Baccalaureate institutions now rank publication of a monograph "very important" or "important" for tenure."
- 要点：博士授予院校 88.9%、硕士授予院校 44.4%、本科院校 48% 的作答院系，把出版专著列为终身教职"非常重要"或"重要"。（原文 Doctorategranting 为 PDF 转文本时丢失连字符）

### E4

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："This expectation is even higher in doctorate-granting institutions, where 49.8% of departments now demand progress toward a second book."
- 要点：博士授予院校中 49.8% 的院系要求第二本书有进展。

### E5

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："only 1.6% of responding departments rated refereed journal articles "not important" in tenure and promotion decisions."
- 要点：只有 1.6% 的作答院系认为同行评审期刊论文"不重要"。

### E6

- 位置：§4
- 原文："Scholarly journals have an advantage that books--with their longer lead time to publication--lack: they provide a forum for new ideas and research and for a timely exchange of argument."
- 要点：期刊比书出版周期短，便于及时交流观点。

### E7

- 位置：§5 Evaluating Collaboration
- 原文："is deeply embedded in the practices of humanities scholarship, including the processes of evaluation for tenure and promotion."
- 要点："一人一作"的独立研究深植于人文学科的做法和评价中。

### E8

- 位置：§4
- 原文："In our view, a body of essays or articles in peer-reviewed journals can demonstrate the quality of scholarly work as well as or, in some cases, better than a monograph of similar length."
- 要点：工作组认为，一组同行评审论文可以和专著一样、甚至更好地体现学术质量。

### E9

- 位置：§6
- 原文："The monograph as the gold standard for tenure dossiers is a relatively recent development"
- 要点：专著成为终身教职的"金标准"是比较晚近的事。

### E10

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："scholarly editions by 20% of departments"
- 要点：20% 的作答院系认为学术版本在终身教职评审中"不重要"。

### E11

- 位置：§1
- 原文："the expectation that the dissertation would be published after revision made it easier for departments to demand a monograph of tenure candidates."
- 要点：人们预期博士论文修改后出版，这让院系更容易要求候选人出专著。
