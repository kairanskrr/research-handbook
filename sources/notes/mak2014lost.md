# Mak、Evaniew 和 Ghert 2014：Lost in translation: animal models and clinical trials in cancer treatment

- 资料：`mak2014lost`，[原文（PubMed Central）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3902221/)
- 版本：American Journal of Translational Research 6(2):114–118，2014 年。一篇简短的叙述性综述（不是系统综述），文中数字多引自其他文献。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："However, the average rate of successful translation from animal models to clinical cancer trials is less than 8%."
- 要点：作者称，从动物模型成功转化到癌症临床试验的平均比例不到 8%。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Despite successful pre-clinical testing, 85% of early clinical trials for novel drugs fail; of those that survive through to phase III, only half become approved for clinical use"
- 要点：作者引用的数字：临床前试验成功的新药，早期临床试验有 85% 失败；走到三期的，只有一半获批。

### E3

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Animal models are limited in their ability to mimic the extremely complex process of human carcinogenesis, physiology and progression."
- 要点：动物模型难以模拟人类癌症发生、生理和进展的复杂过程。

### E4

- 位置：Limitations of animal models in cancer research
- 原文："Indeed, animal studies seem to overestimate by about 30% the likelihood that a treatment will be effective because negative results are often unpublished"
- 要点：作者引用的研究认为，因为阴性结果常常不发表，动物研究对疗效可能性的估计大约高估了 30%。

### E5

- 位置：Limitations of animal models in cancer research
- 原文："The failure to translate from animals to humans is likely due in part to poor methodology and failure of the models to accurately mimic the human disease condition."
- 要点：从动物到人转化失败，部分原因可能是方法学差，部分原因是模型不能准确模拟人类疾病。

### E6

- 位置：Limitations of animal models in cancer research
- 原文："It has been recommended that therapeutic agents should not only be evaluated in rodents, but also in higher animal species, and that randomization and outcomes assessor blinding should be performed. In addition, experiments should be designed in both genders and in different age groups of animals and all data, both positive and negative, should be published"
- 要点：文中转述的建议：不只在啮齿类、也在更高等的动物上评估；做随机和结局评估者盲法；两种性别、不同年龄的动物都要纳入；阳性和阴性数据都发表。

### E7

- 位置：Notable examples of failed clinical cancer trials initiated due to successful animal models
- 原文："In the first human clinical trials of TGN1412, the drug caused catastrophic systemic organ failure in patients, despite being administered at a sub-clinical dose that was 500 times lower than the dose found safe in animal studies"
- 要点：TGN1412 在首次人体试验中引起灾难性的全身器官衰竭，而给药剂量比动物研究中认为安全的剂量低 500 倍。

### E8

- 位置：Notable examples of failed clinical cancer trials initiated due to successful animal models
- 原文："IPI-926 treated mice with the advanced brain tumors gained a fivefold increase in survival" / "However, IPI-926 showed no effect compared to placebo in the human trial"
- 要点：IPI-926 让晚期脑肿瘤小鼠的生存期提高了五倍，但在人体试验中和安慰剂没有差别。

### E9

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Experimental discoveries are thought to begin at “the bench” with basic research, progress through pre-clinical animal studies, then show therapeutic efficacy in human clinical trials."
- 要点："从实验台到病床"的设想：发现从基础研究开始，经过临床前动物研究，再在人体临床试验中显示疗效。

### E10

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Both the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and Health Canada require that animal tests be conducted before humans are exposed to a new molecular entity"
- 要点：美国食品药品监督管理局和加拿大卫生部都要求，新分子实体用于人体之前先做动物试验。

### E11

- 位置：Critical re-evaluation of animal models and alternative strategies
- 原文："These include epidemiological studies, autopsies, in vitro studies, in silico computer modelling"
- 要点：替代动物临床前研究的方法包括流行病学研究、尸检、体外研究、计算机建模等（文中还提到器官芯片和微流控芯片）。
