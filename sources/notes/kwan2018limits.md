# Kwan 2018：The Limits of the Neighborhood Effect

- 资料：`kwan2018limits`，[原文 PDF（作者主页）](http://meipokwan.org/Paper/Kwan_2018_AAAG.pdf)，doi:10.1080/24694452.2018.1453777
- 版本：Annals of the American Association of Geographers 108(6):1482–1490（2018），题为 The Limits of the Neighborhood Effect: Contextual Uncertainties in Geographic, Environmental Health, and Social Science Research。读取的是作者个人网站上的出版社版本 PDF（Internet Archive 快照）。位置写页码。

### E1

- 位置：第 1486 页
- 原文："The common spatial units of analysis in geographic, environmental health, and social science research are the census tract, census block group, and postcode area, whereas the common temporal units of analysis are the day, month, and year. [...] Because the values of the contextual variables derived using different spatial and temporal units might be different, research results might differ when different units of analysis were used."
- 要点：常用的空间分析单元是人口普查区、普查街区组、邮编区，时间单元是日、月、年；换用不同单元算出的变量值可能不同，结论也就可能不同。

### E2

- 位置：第 1486 页
- 原文："which refers to the problem that, in the analysis of the relationships between area- or zone-based variables, research findings could be influenced by the geographic scale or the zoning scheme of the areal units for which data are organized [...] These two components of the MAUP are referred to as the scale effect and the zoning effect"
- 要点：可塑性面积单元问题（MAUP）指分析面状单元变量之间的关系时，结果会受数据所用单元的地理尺度或分区方案影响；两部分分别叫尺度效应和分区效应。

### E3

- 位置：第 1486 页
- 原文："The MTUP refers to the problem that findings about the effects of contextual variables on individual behaviors or outcomes could be affected by how data are organized with respect to the temporal dimension."
- 要点：可塑性时间单元问题（MTUP）指结论会受数据在时间上怎样组织（如按天还是按月汇总）的影响。

### E4

- 位置：第 1488 页
- 原文："enhancing exposure measures with qualitative information about people’s perceptions and interpretations of their environments seem helpful for mitigating the UGCoP and selective daily mobility bias"
- 要点：在暴露测量中加入人们怎样感知和理解自己环境的定性信息，似乎有助于减轻不确定的地理背景问题。
