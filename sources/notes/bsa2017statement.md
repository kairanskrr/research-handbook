# 英国社会学会 2017：Statement of Ethical Practice

- 资料：`bsa2017statement`，[原文 PDF](https://www.britsoc.co.uk/media/24310/bsa_statement_of_ethical_practice.pdf)
- 版本：英国社会学会（British Sociological Association，BSA）《伦理实践声明》，2017 年版（更新自 2002 年起草、2004 年发布的版本），共 10 页。位置写声明的段落编号。

### E1

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："The statement does not, therefore, provide a set of recipes for resolving ethical choices or dilemmas (in the context of potentially conflicting interests of the various parties involved), but recognizes that it may be necessary to make such choices, drawing on fundamental principles"
- 要点：声明不提供解决伦理难题的现成"配方"，而是承认研究者可能需要依据基本原则自己做出选择。

### E2

- 位置：第 13 段
- 原文："Because sociologists study the relatively powerless as well as those more powerful than themselves, research relationships are frequently characterised by disparities of power and status. Despite this, research relationships should be characterised, whenever possible, by trust and integrity."
- 要点：社会学者既研究相对无权的人，也研究比自己更有权势的人，研究关系里常有权力和地位的差距；即便如此，也应尽量建立在信任和诚信之上。

### E3

- 位置：第 15 段（Covert Research）
- 原文："However, covert methods violate the principles of informed consent and may invade the privacy of those being studied." / "Participant or non-participant observation in non-public spaces or experimental manipulation of research participants without their knowledge should be resorted to only where it is impossible to use other methods to obtain essential data."
- 要点：隐蔽研究违背知情同意原则，可能侵犯隐私；在非公开场所做不告知的观察，只有在用其他方法无法获得必要数据时才可采用。

### E4

- 位置：第 19 段
- 原文："it may be necessary for the obtaining of consent to be regarded, not as a once-andfor-all prior event, but as a process, subject to renegotiation over time. In addition, particular care may need to be taken during periods of prolonged fieldwork where it is easy for research participants to forget that they are being studied."
- 要点：在田野研究里，同意可能要被看作随时间重新协商的过程，而不是事先一次了结；长期田野工作中，参与者很容易忘记自己正在被研究，要格外注意。

### E5

- 位置：第 20 段
- 原文："In some situations access to a research setting is gained via a ‘gatekeeper’. In these situations members should adhere to the principle of obtaining informed consent directly from the research participants to whom access is required, while at the same time taking account of the gatekeepers’ interest."
- 要点：通过"把关人"（如机构负责人）进入研究现场时，仍要直接取得研究参与者本人的知情同意，同时考虑把关人的利益。

### E6

- 位置：第 27 段
- 原文："Sociologists should be careful, on the one hand, not to give unrealistic guarantees of confidentiality and, on the other, not to permit communication of research films or records to audiences other than those to which the research participants have agreed."
- 要点：不要做不切实际的保密承诺；研究影像和记录不得给参与者同意范围以外的人看。

### E7

- 位置：第 31 段
- 原文："Potential informants and research participants, especially those possessing a combination of attributes that make them readily identifiable, may need to be reminded that it can be difficult to disguise their identity without introducing an unacceptably large measure of distortion into the data. In some situations, presenting findings by way of hypothetical composite and/or partially fictionalised case studies can address this issue."
- 要点：特征组合让人很容易被认出的参与者，可能需要提醒他们：不把数据改得面目全非，就很难掩盖身份。有时可以用假设的合成案例或部分虚构的案例来呈现结果。

### E8

- 位置：第 32 段
- 原文："Where there is a likelihood that data may be shared with other researchers, the potential uses to which the data might be put must be discussed with research participants and their consent obtained for the future use of the material."
- 要点：数据有可能和其他研究者共享时，必须和参与者讨论数据可能的用途，并取得他们对今后使用的同意。

### E9

- 位置：第 38 段
- 原文："Research data given in confidence do not enjoy legal privilege; that is they may be liable to subpoena by a court and research participants should be informed of this."
- 要点：在保密承诺下得到的研究数据没有法律特免权，可能被法院传唤调取，应告知参与者。

### E10

- 位置：第 29 段
- 原文："Special care should be taken where research participants are particularly vulnerable by virtue of factors such as age, disability, their physical or mental health."
- 要点：参与者因年龄、残障、身心健康等原因特别脆弱时，要格外小心。

### E11

- 位置：第 30 段
- 原文："Research involving children requires particular care. The consent of the child should be sought in addition to that of the parent."
- 要点：涉及儿童的研究需要特别小心；除父母同意外，还要征求孩子本人的同意。

### E12

- 位置：第 10 段
- 原文："Social researchers face a range of potential risks to their safety. Safety issues need to be considered in the design and conduct of social research projects and procedures should be adopted to reduce the risk to researchers, including, importantly, any research assistants employed."
- 要点：社会研究者自身面临各种安全风险；设计和开展研究时要考虑安全，采取措施降低研究者（包括雇用的研究助理）的风险。

### E13

- 位置：第 5 段
- 原文："Sociologists should note that there are national laws and administrative regulations (for example Data Protection Acts, the Human Rights Act, copyright and libel laws) which may affect the conduct of their research, data distribution and storage, publication, rights of research subjects, of sponsors and employers etc."
- 要点：国家法律和行政法规（如数据保护法、人权法、版权和诽谤法）会影响研究的开展、数据的分发和存储、发表等。

### E14

- 位置：第 59 段
- 原文："Sociologists should seek to distribute their research findings/share their analyses as widely as possible and, where required, make their research data available to other researchers via appropriate archives."
- 要点：应尽量广泛地传播研究成果；有要求时，通过合适的档案库把研究数据提供给其他研究者。
