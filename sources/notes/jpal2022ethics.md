# J-PAL 研究资源：Ethical conduct of randomized evaluations

- 资料：`jpal2022ethics`，[原文网页](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/resource/ethical-conduct-randomized-evaluations)
- 版本：J-PAL 研究资源页，作者 Laura Feeney、Sarah Kopper、Anja Sautmann，2022 年 10 月更新；2026 年 10 月访问。页面自述作者不是伦理学家、内容不求全面。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction: Ethical framework
- 原文："IRB review cannot substitute for the researcher’s responsibility to consider the ethical implications of their research and ask themselves as well as members of the communities they work in if they are comfortable with their research protocols."
- 要点：伦理审查委员会的审查不能代替研究者自己的伦理责任；研究者要问自己，也要问所在社区的人，是否认可研究方案。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction: Ethical framework
- 原文："A feature that is important to the research design, such as randomly drawing payment amounts, may appear unethical or unfair to field staff."
- 要点：对研究设计很重要的做法（如随机决定发多少钱），在一线工作人员看来可能不道德或不公平。

### E3

- 位置：How research ethics contribute to research quality
- 原文："Carefully consulting and including feedback and consent from local partners, community members, and government in research design and implementation is critical to conducting high-quality, ethical research"
- 要点：在设计和实施中征询当地伙伴、社区成员和政府的意见与同意，是做出高质量、合乎伦理的研究的关键。

### E4

- 位置：Beneficence principle: “Do not harm” and “minimize risks, maximize benefits”
- 原文："For example, a study that is so poorly designed that the results are not credible has no benefit and would thus fail a risk/benefit ratio assessment because it would expose individuals to the burdens of participation without any knowledge gains (Kukla 2007)."
- 要点：设计差到结果不可信的研究没有收益，让人承担参与的负担却得不到知识，过不了风险收益评估。

### E5

- 位置：Implementing the “do not harm” principle in practice
- 原文："Do not deny participants an intervention, treatment, or services they would otherwise be entitled to receive."
- 要点：不要剥夺参与者本来有权得到的干预、治疗或服务。

### E6

- 位置：Implementing the justice principle in practice
- 原文："Some intervention designs, such as phase-in designs, allow for greater justice in the sense that no one is permanently denied access (Glennerster & Takavarasha 2013)."
- 要点：分批推行等设计让任何人都不会被永久排除在外，更符合公正原则。

### E7

- 位置：Implementing the “minimal risk” principle in practice
- 原文："Exposure of sensitive or private data is the most common risk in social science studies, and research protocols should carefully guard against this risk."
- 要点：敏感或隐私数据泄露是社会科学研究最常见的风险。

### E8

- 位置：Implementing respect for persons in practice
- 原文："It should not be exploitatively low but should not be so high as to potentially undermine participants’ abilities to make a rational decision about the risks and benefits of the study"
- 要点：给参与者的补偿不能低到剥削，也不能高到影响他们理性判断参与的风险和收益。
