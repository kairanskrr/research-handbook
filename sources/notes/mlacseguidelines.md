# 美国现代语言协会学术版本委员会：Guidelines for Editors of Scholarly Editions

- 资料：`mlacseguidelines`，[原文](https://www.mla.org/Resources/Guidelines-and-Data/Reports-and-Professional-Guidelines/Guidelines-for-Editors-of-Scholarly-Editions)
- 版本：MLA 学术版本委员会（Committee on Scholarly Editions）官网页面，页面未标修订日期；2026 年 10 月读取

### E1

- 位置：1.1 Principles
- 原文："The scholarly edition's basic task is to present a reliable text: scholarly editions make clear what they promise and keep their promises."
- 要点：学术版本的基本任务是给出可靠的文本：说清承诺什么，并做到。

### E2

- 位置：1.1 Principles
- 原文："A scholarly edition commonly includes appropriate textual apparatus or notes documenting alterations and variant readings of the text, including alterations by the author, intervening editors, or the editor of this edition."
- 要点：学术版本通常附校勘记，记录文本的改动和异文。

### E3

- 位置：1.1 Principles
- 原文："editors of scholarly editions establish and follow a proofreading plan that serves to ensure the accuracy of the materials presented."
- 要点：编者要制定并执行校对计划。
