# Halim：Prof Halim's PhD Tips

- 资料：`halimtips`，[原文](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~stevenha/myresearch/phd_tips.html)
- 版本：作者主页网页，持续更新（含 2024 年的修订），2026-10-05 读取。位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：The Advantages of Taking Graduate Studies
- 原文："However, always be careful as leaking too much ideas (pro-tip: discuss your *already published* paper(s) instead) can jeopardize your own PhD"
- 要点：与外部研究者合作通常是好事，但要小心过多透露尚未发表的想法；可以优先讨论已发表的论文。

### E2

- 位置：The Advantages of Taking Graduate Studies
- 原文："If your 'collaborators' decide not to acknowledge you as the original inventor of that idea, then you lost an idea, or even a PhD thesis because it is already taken by your 'collaborators'..."
- 要点：如果"合作者"不承认你是想法的提出者，你可能失去这个想法，甚至失去博士论文题目。

### E3

- 位置：Expected Soft Skills
- 原文："Frequent contact with your advisor will help to reduce the problems around this issue."
- 要点：做研究计划时要避免走错方向、浪费时间，和导师保持频繁联系有助于减少这类问题。

### E4

- 位置：Miscellaneous Things
- 原文："Typically, your advisor (especially if one has high status in the department) is very busy and thus, their time is precious. [...] Make sure to use the contact time as optimally as you can."
- 要点：导师通常很忙，要尽量用好和导师见面的时间。
