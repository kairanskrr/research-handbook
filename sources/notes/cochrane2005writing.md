# Cochrane 2005：Writing Tips for Ph.D. Students

- 资料：`cochrane2005writing`，[原文 PDF](https://www.johnhcochrane.com/s/phd_paper_writing.pdf)
- 版本：2005 年 6 月 8 日版，作者主页公开。位置写章节号和标题。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Organization
- 原文："Organize the paper in "triangular" or "newspaper" style, not in "joke" or "novel" style."
- 要点：论文要像新闻稿那样把最重要的结论放在最前面，而不是像笑话或小说那样把"包袱"留到最后。

### E2

- 位置：§1 Organization
- 原文："A good paper is not a travelogue of your search process. We don't care how you came to figure out the right answer. We don't care about the hundreds of things you tried that did not work."
- 要点：论文不是探索过程的游记，读者不关心你试过而失败的几百种做法。

### E3

- 位置：§2 Writing / General points
- 原文："Document your work. A fellow graduate student must be able to sit down with your paper and all alone reproduce every number in it from instructions given in the paper, and any print or web appendices."
- 要点：要把工作记录清楚：同学只凭论文和附录，就能独立复现文中的每一个数字。

### E4

- 位置：§3 Tips for empirical work
- 原文："What are the three most important things for empirical work? Identification, Identification, Identification. Describe your identification strategy clearly."
- 要点：实证研究最重要的三件事都是"识别"。要把识别策略讲清楚。

### E5

- 位置：§3 Tips for empirical work
- 原文："Describe what economic mechanism caused the dispersion in your right hand variables. No, God does not hand us true natural experiments very often."
- 要点：要说清楚是什么经济机制造成了解释变量的变化；真正的自然实验并不常有。

### E6

- 位置：§3 Tips for empirical work
- 原文："Consider carefully what controls should and should not be in the regression. Most papers have far too many right hand variables."
- 要点：要仔细想哪些控制变量该放、哪些不该放；多数论文放的解释变量太多了。

### E7

- 位置：§3 Tips for empirical work
- 原文："Explain the economic significance of your results. Explain the economic magnitude of the central numbers, not just their statistical significance. Especially in large panel data sets even the tiniest of effects is "statistically significant.""
- 要点：要解释结果的经济意义和大小，而不只是统计显著性；样本很大时，再小的效应也会"统计显著"。

### E8

- 位置：§4 Seminar presentations
- 原文："You don't need any literature review or motivation in a seminar. Just get to the point."
- 要点：研讨会报告不需要文献综述和铺垫，直接讲重点。

### E9

- 位置：§4 Seminar presentations
- 原文："Listen to the questions, all the way to the end, then count to three before answering."
- 要点：听完整个问题，数到三再回答。

### E10

- 位置：§5 Conclusion
- 原文："Most good economists spend at least 50% of the time they put into any project on writing."
- 要点：作者认为，好的经济学家在每个项目上至少一半时间花在写作上。

### E11

- 位置：§1 Organization / Theory
- 原文："In most papers, the "main result" is empirical. There may be some theory or a model, but if you (or the editor!) ask "does this paper expand our knowledge of economic theory?," the answer is "no." The theory is there to help understand the empirical work."
- 要点：作者认为，多数论文的主要结果是实证的；其中的理论或模型是为了帮助理解实证结果，因此只需写到读者理解实证所必需的程度。

### E12

- 位置：§4 Seminar presentations
- 原文："The seminar then bogs down as people start asking questions about the previewed results" / "I timed the finance workshop last winter quarter and not one paper got to the main results in under an hour!"
- 要点：报告中听众会随时插话提问，讲得没有条理就会陷入纠缠；作者统计过一个学期的金融研讨会，没有一篇论文在一小时内讲到主要结果。
