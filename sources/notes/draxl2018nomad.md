# Draxl & Scheffler 2018：NOMAD: The FAIR Concept for Big-Data-Driven Materials Science

- 资料：`draxl2018nomad`，[原文（arXiv）](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.05039)
- 版本：arXiv:1805.05039 预印本；正式发表于 MRS Bulletin 43, 676–682（2018）。文中描述的是 2018 年时 NOMAD 的做法，现行规则以 NOMAD 官网为准。预印本无页内小节编号，位置写段落小标题。

### E1

- 位置：引言
- 原文："Having a closer look, however, one realizes that such studies are inefficiently exploited, so far, as only a tiny amount of the information that is contained in all the computed data is being used."
- 要点：高通量筛选算了成千上万个结构，但计算数据里的信息只有极小一部分被用上。

### E2

- 位置：引言
- 原文："Unfortunately, besides a few numbers, tables or graphs that appear in the resulting publication, the wealth of other information contained in the full research work is typically disregarded or even deleted."
- 要点：除了写进论文的几个数字、表格和图，研究中产生的大量其他信息通常被丢弃，甚至删掉。这一点对理论和实验研究都适用。

### E3

- 位置：引言
- 原文："throughput screening initiatives (for example, Refs. 1,2,3,4,5,6), significantly boosted by the Materials Genome Initiative7,8 of the White House, meet this issue by computing the properties of many thousands of structures."
- 要点：高通量筛选项目（受美国"材料基因组计划"推动）通过计算成千上万个结构的性质来寻找新材料。

### E4

- 位置：The NOMAD Repository
- 原文："NOMAD keeps scientific data for at least 10 years for free. That way, NOMAD also facilitates research groups to organize their data for their own groups and to share and exchange their results between two or more groups, and to recall what was actually done some years ago."
- 要点：按 2018 年的描述，NOMAD 免费保存科研数据至少 10 年，也方便课题组整理自己的数据、和别的组交换结果、回忆几年前到底做了什么。

### E5

- 位置：The NOMAD Repository
- 原文："Results are accepted in their raw format as produced by the underlying code. The only condition is that the list of authors is provided, and code and code version can be retrieved from the uploaded files, and the input and output files have to be complete."
- 要点：NOMAD 接收计算程序直接输出的原始格式；条件是写明作者、能从文件中查到程序及其版本、输入输出文件完整。

### E6

- 位置：The NOMAD Repository
- 原文："Data can be restricted to the owner or made available to other people (selected by the owner). After a maximum period of three years though, all data become open access."
- 要点：数据可以先只给自己或指定的人看，但最长三年后全部公开。

### E7

- 位置：Summary and outlook
- 原文："Another crucial issue is the quality of experimental samples, which is often not sufficiently known or characterized. In fact, the sample's quality depends very much on its history."
- 要点：实验数据的另一个关键问题是样品质量常常没有充分了解或表征；样品质量很大程度上取决于它的"经历"。

### E8

- 位置：Summary and outlook
- 原文："Metadata about the experiment, the sample and the data must be defined if the experimental data is re-used or combined with other data."
- 要点：实验数据要被再利用或和其他数据合并，必须定义好关于实验、样品和数据的元数据。
