# 英国特许考古学家协会 2020：Standard and guidance for the creation, compilation, transfer and deposition of archaeological archives

- 资料：`cifa2020archives`，[PDF](https://www.archaeologists.net/sites/default/files/2023-11/CIfA-SandG-Archaeological-Archives-2020.pdf)
- 版本：CIfA 2014 年 12 月首发、2020 年 10 月更新的考古档案标准和指南。原文说明它针对英国和马恩岛的工作。位置写节号。

### E1

- 位置：Definition of archaeological archives
- 原文："The archaeological archive is defined as all parts of the archaeological record, including the finds, samples and digital records as well as the written, drawn and photographic documentation."
- 要点：考古档案包括考古记录的全部：出土物、样品、数字记录，以及文字、图纸和照片记录。

### E2

- 位置：Purpose of archaeological archives
- 原文："The archive from an archaeological project is the key to understanding any published interpretations of the results."
- 要点：项目档案是理解已发表解释的钥匙。

### E3

- 位置：3.1.1
- 原文："A project has not been completed until the archive has been transferred successfully and is fully accessible for consultation"
- 要点：档案成功移交并能完全查阅之前，项目不算完成。

### E4

- 位置：3.3.1、3.3.2
- 原文："Project specifications, research designs or similar documents should include a project-specific Selection Strategy and a Data Management Plan." / "Project specifications, research designs or similar documents should identify the repository where the archive will be deposited for long-term curation."
- 要点：项目文件要包含针对本项目的筛选策略和数据管理计划，并写明档案最终存放的收藏机构。

### E5

- 位置：3.4.3
- 原文："A consistent, well-documented terminology should be used in making all paper and digital records."
- 要点：所有纸质和数字记录都要用一致、有说明的术语。

### E6

- 位置：3.4.4
- 原文："All drawings and photographs should be marked with the project identifier, and where relevant have a key to identify the subject, scale, location and orientation."
- 要点：所有图纸和照片都要标上项目编号，并在适用时标明对象、比例尺、位置和方向。

### E7

- 位置：3.4.7
- 原文："Security copies of all digital material should be created and managed as appropriate."
- 要点：所有数字材料都要做备份。

### E8

- 位置：3.4.8
- 原文："Finds and other materials should be collected, sorted, cleaned, marked or labelled, recorded and packed in accordance with recognised standards."
- 要点：出土物要按公认标准收集、分类、清洗、标记、登记和包装。

### E9

- 位置：3.4.15
- 原文："Project reports (including grey literature and OASIS and other on-line entries) and publications must identify the archive repository and provide the accession number issued by the repository or, if this is not available, the site code."
- 要点：项目报告（包括灰色文献和在线登记）和出版物必须写明档案存放机构和入藏编号，没有编号就写遗址代码。

### E10

- 位置：3.8.1
- 原文："The proposed recipient museum or other approved repository must be contacted at the project planning stage and arrangements for deposition of the material archive should be detailed in the specification and/or project design."
- 要点：项目计划阶段就必须联系接收档案的博物馆或机构，并在设计书里写清实物档案的入藏安排。

### E11

- 位置：3.8.2
- 原文："In England, Wales, Northern Ireland and the Isle of Man ownership of objects rests with the landowner, except where other law overrides."
- 要点：在英格兰、威尔士、北爱尔兰和马恩岛，出土物一般归土地所有者（另有法律规定的除外）。

### E12

- 位置：1 Introduction
- 原文："This document provides guidance for work carried out in the United Kingdom and Isle of Man, although the principles will apply elsewhere."
- 要点：本文件针对英国和马恩岛，但原则在别处也适用。

### E13

- 位置：3.4.14
- 原文："Health and Safety takes priority over archaeological requirements."
- 要点：健康和安全优先于考古要求。
