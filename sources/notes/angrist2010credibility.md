# Angrist & Pischke 2010：The Credibility Revolution in Empirical Economics

- 资料：`angrist2010credibility`，[NBER 工作论文版 PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w15794/w15794.pdf)；正式版发表于 Journal of Economic Perspectives 24(2)，[期刊页面](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.24.2.3)
- 版本：NBER Working Paper 15794（2010 年 3 月）。期刊 PDF 被网站拦截，摘录依据工作论文版；位置写小节标题。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："As we see it, the credibility revolution in empirical work can be traced to the rise of a design-based approach that emphasizes the identification of causal effects. Design-based studies typically feature either real or natural experiments and are distinguished by their prima facie credibility and by the attention investigators devote to making the case for a causal interpretation of the findings their designs generate."
- 要点：作者认为，实证经济学的"可信性革命"来自"以研究设计为本"的做法：重视因果效应的识别，通常借助真实实验或自然实验，并花大力气论证结果为什么可以作因果解释。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Design-based studies are most often found in the microeconomic fields of Development, Education, Environment, Labor, Health, and Public Finance, but are still rare in Industrial Organization and Macroeconomics."
- 要点：以设计为本的研究多见于发展、教育、环境、劳动、健康、公共财政等微观领域；在产业组织和宏观经济学中仍然少见（2010 年时的判断）。

### E3

- 位置：引言
- 原文："But experiments are time consuming, expensive, and may not always be practical. It's difficult to imagine a randomized trial to evaluate the effect of immigrants on the economy of the host country. However, human institutions or the forces of nature can step into the breach with informative natural or quasi-experiments."
- 要点：实验耗时、昂贵，有时做不了（例如研究移民对经济的影响）；这时可以借助制度或自然造成的"自然实验""准实验"。

### E4

- 位置：Better Research Design
- 原文："The econometric methods that feature most prominently in quasi-experimental studies are instrumental variables, regression discontinuity methods, and differences-in-differences-style policy analysis."
- 要点：准实验研究最常用的三种计量方法：工具变量、断点回归、双重差分。

### E5

- 位置：Better Research Design
- 原文："When using instrumental variables, for example, it's no longer enough to mechanically invoke a simultaneous equations framework, labeling some variables endogenous and others exogenous, without substantially justifying the exclusion restrictions and as-goodas-randomly-assigned assumptions that make instruments valid."
- 要点：用工具变量时，不能只是给变量贴上"内生""外生"的标签，必须充分论证工具变量成立所依赖的假设：它只通过关心的渠道影响结果（排他性约束），且它的取值近似随机。

### E6

- 位置：Better Research Design
- 原文："The regression discontinuity approach can be used when people are divided into groups based on a certain cutoff score, with those just above or just below the cutoff suddenly becoming eligible for a different treatment."
- 要点：断点回归适用于按某个分数线分组的情形：刚好在线上和线下的人，突然得到不同的处理。

### E7

- 位置：Better Research Design
- 原文："The key assumption that drives regression discontinuity estimation of causal effects is that individuals are otherwise similar on either side of the discontinuity"
- 要点：断点回归的关键假设：分界线两侧的人，除了是否受到处理，其他方面相似。

### E8

- 位置：Better Research Design
- 原文："Differences-in-differences policy analysis typically compares the evolution of outcomes in groups affected more and less by a policy change."
- 要点：双重差分比较受政策影响较大和较小的两组人，在政策前后结果变化的差异。

### E9

- 位置：Whither Sensitivity Analysis?
- 原文："When using instrumental variables, we look at whether the instrument might have causal effects on the outcome in ways other than through the channel of interest" / "With differences-in-differences, we look for group-specific trends, since such trends can invalidate a comparison of changes across groups. In a regression discontinuity design, we look at factors like bunching at the cutoff point, which might suggest that the cutoff directly influenced behavior."
- 要点：每种设计有自己要重点检查的漏洞：工具变量看它是否经由别的渠道影响结果；双重差分看两组原本是否就有不同的趋势；断点回归看人们是否扎堆在分界线附近（说明有人在操纵）。

### E10

- 位置：More Transparent Discussion of Research Design
- 原文："The query "What's your identification strategy?" and others like it are now heard routinely at empirical workshops and seminars."
- 要点："你的识别策略是什么？"已经是实证研讨会上的常规提问。

### E11

- 位置：More Transparent Discussion of Research Design
- 原文："Good designs have a beneficial side effect: they typically lend themselves to a simple explanation of empirical methods and a straightforward presentation of results."
- 要点：好的研究设计还有一个好处：方法容易讲清楚，结果可以直接呈现。

### E12

- 位置：The Leamer Critique and His Proposed Remedies（教育生产函数一段）
- 原文："In what was arguably one of the better studies of the period, Summers and Wolfe (1977) report only the final results of an exhaustive specification search in their evaluation of the effect of school resources on achievement."
- 要点：举例说明"设定搜索"：试遍各种回归设定，只报告最后选定的结果。

### E13

- 位置：Whither Sensitivity Analysis?
- 原文："Wide-net searches of this kind offer little basis for a causal interpretation."
- 要点：撒大网式地跑大量回归、看哪个变量显著，不能为因果解释提供多少依据。

### E14

- 位置：Mad About Macro
- 原文："Many macroeconomists have abandoned traditional empirical work entirely, focusing instead on "computational experiments," as described in this journal by Kydland and Prescott (1996)." / "In a computational experiment, researchers choose a question, build a (theoretical) model economy, "calibrate" the model so that its behavior mimics the real economy along some key statistical dimensions, and then run a computational experiment by changing model parameters (for example, tax rates or the money supply rule) to address the original question." / "Whatever might be said in defense of this framework as a tool for clarifying the implications of economic models, it produces no direct evidence on the magnitude or existence of causal effects."
- 要点：很多宏观经济学家放弃了传统实证，转向"计算实验"：建一个理论模型经济，"校准"参数使它在关键统计特征上像真实经济，再改变政策参数看结果。作者认为它能澄清模型含义，但不直接提供因果效应的证据。

### E15

- 位置：Industrial Disorganization
- 原文："A good structural model might tell us something about economic mechanisms as well as causal effects. But if the information about mechanisms is to be worth anything, the structural estimates should line up with those derived under weaker assumptions."
- 要点：好的结构模型能同时告诉我们机制和因果效应；但它的估计应当和假设更弱的方法得到的结果对得上。

### E16

- 位置：Has the Research Design Pendulum Swung Too Far?
- 原文："The first counterrevolutionary charge raises the question of external validity--the concern that evidence from a given experimental or quasi-experimental research design has little predictive value beyond the context of the original experiment. The second charge is that experimentalists are playing small ball while big questions go unanswered."
- 要点：对设计派的两大批评：一是外部效度（一个实验的结论换个环境可能不成立）；二是只做容易识别的小问题，放过了大问题。

### E17

- 位置：Has the Research Design Pendulum Swung Too Far? / External Validity
- 原文："A constructive response to the specificity of a given research design is to look for more evidence, so that a more general picture begins to emerge."
- 要点：作者的回应：单个研究有局限，就去积累更多证据，让更一般的图景逐渐显现。

### E18

- 位置：Other Empirical Work in the Age of Heavy Metal
- 原文："Indeed, we would be the first to admit that a well-done observational study can be more credible and persuasive than a poorly executed randomized trial."
- 要点：做得好的观察性研究，可能比执行糟糕的随机试验更可信。

### E19

- 位置：Industrial Disorganization
- 原文："We find the empirical results generated by a good research design more compelling than the conclusions derived from a good theory, but we also hope to see industrial organization move towards stronger and more transparent identification strategies in a structural framework."
- 要点：作者更信服好的研究设计得出的实证结果，而不是好理论推出的结论；同时希望结构模型也采用更强、更透明的识别策略。

### E20

- 位置：Has the Research Design Pendulum Swung Too Far? / External Validity
- 原文："The criticism here--made by a number of authors including Heckman (1997); Rosenzweig and Wolpin (2000); Heckman and Urzua (2009); and Deaton (2009)--is that in the quest for internal validity, design-based studies have become narrow or idiosyncratic."
- 要点：Heckman、Rosenzweig 和 Wolpin、Deaton 等人批评：设计派为了追求内部效度，研究变得狭窄、特殊。

### E21

- 位置：Industrial Disorganization
- 原文："The first estimates a demand system for the product in question" / "Next, researchers postulate a model of market conduct, say, Bertrand–Nash price-based competition between different brands or products." / "Finally, industry behavior is simulated with and without the merger of interest."
- 要点：产业组织中分析兼并的主流结构方法分三步：先估计需求，再假设企业之间怎样竞争，最后模拟有无兼并时的行业表现。
