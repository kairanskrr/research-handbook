# 美国科研诚信办公室：Whistleblowers（Handling Misconduct - Complainant）

- 资料：`oriwhistleblowers`，[原文](https://ori.hhs.gov/whistleblowers)
- 版本：美国卫生与公众服务部科研诚信办公室（Office of Research Integrity，ORI）网页，适用于美国公共卫生署（PHS）资助的研究；页面未标日期，2026-10-08 读取。

### E1

- 位置：Handling Misconduct - Complainant，第 1 段
- 原文："The complainant (whistleblower) is an essential element in the effort to protect the integrity of PHS supported research because researchers do not call attention to their own misconduct." / "Prior to making an allegation of research misconduct a complainant should carefully study the policy established by their institution for responding to such allegations to: determine what information should be included in the allegation, to whom the allegation should be reported, what protections are provided for the complainant, and what role the complainant will play in the ensuing proceedings."
- 要点：这一页讲的是美国公共卫生署（PHS）资助的研究。举报科研不端之前，先仔细读所在机构处理这类举报的规定：要提供什么信息、向谁报告、举报人有什么保护、之后扮演什么角色。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 段
- 原文："Research misconduct allegations should be made to the institutional official named to receive such allegations in the institutional policy for responding to research misconduct allegations."
- 要点：举报应交给机构规定里指定接收举报的负责人。

### E3

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："A good faith allegation is made with the honest belief that research misconduct may have occurred. An allegation is not in good faith if it is made with reckless disregard for or willful ignorance of facts that would disprove the allegation."
- 要点：善意举报是真诚相信可能发生了不端；无视或故意不看能推翻指控的事实，就不算善意。

### E4

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："the PHS Policies on Research Misconduct (42 C.F.R. 93.300) obligates institutions to" / "take all reasonable and practical steps to protect the positions and reputations of good faith complainants, witnesses and committee members and protect them from retaliation by respondents and other institutional members."
- 要点：美国公共卫生署的规定要求机构采取合理措施，保护善意举报人、证人的职位和名誉，防止报复。

### E5

- 位置：第 4 段
- 原文："it is the responsibility of the investigative body and ORI, not the complainant, to ensure that the allegation is thoroughly and competently investigated to resolution."
- 要点：把指控查清楚是调查机构和 ORI 的责任，不是举报人的责任。
