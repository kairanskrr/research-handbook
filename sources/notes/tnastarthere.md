# 英国国家档案馆：Start here（研究入门页面）

- 资料：`tnastarthere`，[原文](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/start-here/)
- 版本：英国国家档案馆（The National Archives，位于伦敦 Kew）官网"Help with your research > Start here"栏目下的几个页面：How to use archives、What are archives?、What's online、What's not online。网页未标日期，2026-10-06 读取。按英国开放政府许可（Open Government Licence v3.0）公开。位置写页面标题。

### E1

- 位置：What are archives?，Is an archive like a library?
- 原文："Library books are arranged by subject and author, whereas information in archives is arranged according to the person or organisation that created it. This means that you will probably need to look at records from more than one source, or more than one archive, as you gather information."
- 要点：图书馆按主题和作者排书，档案则按产生它的个人或机构排列；所以收集信息时，你很可能要看多个来源、多个档案馆的记录。

### E2

- 位置：How to use archives，Start at home
- 原文："If the archive has an online catalogue, take some time to find out how to use it. Don’t expect it to be like Google – a simple keyword search will not always work"
- 要点：花时间学会用档案馆的在线目录；它不像 Google，简单的关键词检索不一定管用。

### E3

- 位置：How to use archives，In an archive
- 原文："Recognise both the limitations and potential of the archive – you may not find exactly what you are looking for, but you could find new sources you didn’t know existed"
- 要点：档案既有局限也有潜力：可能找不到想要的，却可能发现没想到的新资料。

### E4

- 位置：What's online
- 原文："Many of our most popular records are available online. This is over 5% of what we have and this figure keeps growing."
- 要点：英国国家档案馆上线的记录占馆藏的 5% 多一点（网页原话，未注日期）。

### E5

- 位置：What's online
- 原文："Other records that we hold have been digitised by commercial partners like Ancestry and Findmypast. You will need to visit their websites to view them – and there will usually be a charge."
- 要点：部分记录由商业公司数字化，要到它们的网站看，通常收费。

### E6

- 位置：What's not online
- 原文："Looking for records that are not online can be a time-consuming process and, as with any search in archival records, there is never a guarantee of success."
- 要点：找没上线的记录很费时间；任何档案检索都不保证能找到。

### E7

- 位置：What's not online
- 原文："You can also search records of other archives using filters in Discovery, our catalogue."
- 要点：英国国家档案馆的目录 Discovery 也能检索其他档案馆的记录。

### E8

- 位置：What's not online
- 原文："Remember to bring the correct ID, as you will need a reader’s ticket to see original documents"
- 要点：看原件需要读者证，去之前要带好规定的身份证件。
