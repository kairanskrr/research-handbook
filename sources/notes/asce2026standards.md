# ASCE：Codes and Standards

- 资料：`asce2026standards`，[原文](https://www.asce.org/publications-and-news/codes-and-standards)
- 版本：美国土木工程师学会（ASCE）"规范与标准"网页，读取于 2026-10-06。

### E1

- 位置：页面正文第 1 段
- 原文："ASCE Standards provide technical guidelines for promoting safety, reliability, productivity, and efficiency in civil engineering. Many of our standards are referenced by model building codes and adopted by state and local jurisdictions."
- 要点：ASCE 标准为土木工程的安全、可靠、效率提供技术准则；许多 ASCE 标准被示范建筑规范引用，并被美国各州和地方采纳。

### E2

- 位置：Get involved with Standards
- 原文："Accredited by the American National Standards Institute (ANSI), ASCE has a rigorous and formal process overseen by the Codes & Standards Committee (CSC). Standards are created or updated by a balanced, volunteer standards committee, followed by a public review period."
- 要点：ASCE 的标准制定流程经美国国家标准学会（ANSI）认可；标准由各方代表均衡的志愿者委员会制定或更新，之后有公众审阅期。

### E3

- 位置：Get involved with Standards
- 原文："All standards are updated or reaffirmed at least every 5 years."
- 要点：所有 ASCE 标准至少每 5 年更新或重新确认一次。

### E4

- 位置：Submit a proposal for a new standard；Attend a standards committee meeting
- 原文："Anyone can submit a proposal to change or create a standard." / "Standards committee meetings are open for anyone to attend."
- 要点：任何人都可以提议修改或新建标准；标准委员会会议对任何人开放。

### E5

- 位置：Comment on a draft standard
- 原文："ASCE conducts a minimum 45-day Public Comment period on any new, revised, or reaffirmed standard."
- 要点：新建、修订或重新确认的标准，都有至少 45 天的公众意见期。
