# 美国有效教育策略信息中心 2022：What Works Clearinghouse Procedures and Standards Handbook, Version 5.0

- 资料：`wwc2022handbook`，[原文 PDF](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Docs/referenceresources/Final_WWC-HandbookVer5_0-0-508.pdf)
- 版本：WWC 2022008REV，2022 年 8 月发布、2022 年 12 月修订。美国教育部教育科学研究所（Institute of Education Sciences，IES）下属"有效教育策略信息中心"（What Works Clearinghouse，WWC）的审查手册，属于公有领域。位置写章节标题。

### E1

- 位置：Chapter I（WWC 简介）
- 原文："addresses the need for credible, succinct information by identifying existing research in education, assessing the quality of this research, and summarizing and disseminating the evidence from studies that meet WWC standards."
- 要点：WWC 找出已有教育研究，评估其质量，再汇总和传播达到 WWC 标准的研究证据。

### E2

- 位置：Phase 3: Synthesize and report results
- 原文："Based on the WWC's confidence in the strength of the research design and execution of the design, eligible findings from the study will receive one of three research ratings: Meets WWC Standards Without Reservations, Meets WWC Standards With Reservations, or Does Not Meet WWC Standards."
- 要点：按研究设计的强度和执行质量，研究结果被评为三级之一：无保留地达标、有保留地达标、不达标。

### E3

- 位置：Table 3. What Works Clearinghouse research ratings
- 原文："This rating is reserved for findings based on a strong research design that is well-executed. This rating therefore provides the highest degree of confidence that the intervention caused the observed effect."
- 要点：最高一级只给设计强、执行好的研究，表示最有把握是干预造成了观察到的效果。

### E4

- 位置：Table 3
- 原文："Because of natural limitations in research designs or because of circumstances around execution of a design, findings that receive this rating do not sufficiently rule out that something other than the intervention caused the observed effect."
- 要点："有保留地达标"的研究，因设计本身的局限或执行中的情况，不能充分排除是干预以外的因素造成了效果。

### E5

- 位置：Study eligibility（研究设计要求）
- 原文："The study must use one of four research designs: randomized controlled trial (RCT), including RCTs that estimate a complier average causal effect (CACE); quasiexperimental design (QED); regression discontinuity design (RDD); or single-case design (SCD)."
- 要点：能进入 WWC 审查的研究必须是四种设计之一：随机对照试验、准实验设计、断点回归设计、单一被试设计。

### E6

- 位置：Baseline equivalence（基线等同）
- 原文："When a study is a QED, study authors do not have this level of control over assignment to conditions. For this reason, authors of QEDs must demonstrate baseline equivalence of the intervention and comparison conditions on relevant covariates, such as outcome pretest, and if needed address differences between conditions by applying an acceptable statistical adjustment."
- 要点：准实验研究无法控制分组，所以作者必须证明干预组和比较组在干预前（例如前测成绩上）是相当的，必要时用可接受的统计方法调整差异。

### E7

- 位置：Baseline equivalence
- 原文："Therefore, the highest possible WWC research rating for QEDs, and for RCTs and RDDs with a high risk of bias due to compositional change, is Meets WWC Standards With Reservations."
- 要点：准实验研究，以及因样本组成变化而偏差风险高的随机试验和断点回归研究，最高只能评为"有保留地达标"。

### E8

- 位置：Attrition（样本流失）
- 原文："Attrition refers to the lack of outcome data for participants who were assigned to either the intervention or the comparison group."
- 要点：样本流失指已分到干预组或比较组的参与者，最后缺少结果数据。

### E9

- 位置：Attrition
- 原文："Participants are not available when outcome measurements were assessed. For example, a student might be absent the day that a posttest was given."
- 要点：流失的来源之一：测量结果那天参与者不在，例如后测那天学生缺课。

### E10

- 位置：Attrition
- 原文："High attrition could signal that the participants for whom outcomes are measured at the end of the study are too different from participants who were initially included in the study."
- 要点：流失率高，可能说明最后测到结果的参与者和最初纳入的参与者差别太大。

### E11

- 位置：Confounding factors
- 原文："Intervention or comparison group contains a single unit, such as teacher, classroom, school, or district (also known as an N = 1 confounding factor)."
- 要点：干预组或比较组只含一个老师、一个班、一所学校或一个学区时，构成"N=1 混杂因素"。

### E12

- 位置：Confounding factors
- 原文："However, if the study is conducted in one school but students within the school are assigned to intervention and comparison conditions, one school will not be a confounding factor because it is aligned with both study's conditions."
- 要点：如果研究只在一所学校做，但校内学生被分到干预组和比较组，这所学校就不算混杂因素，因为两组都在里面。

### E13

- 位置：Confounding factors
- 原文："For instance, if an intervention group of students in grade 3 and a comparison group of students in grade 3 are observed during different academic years, then time could be a confounding factor."
- 要点：例如干预组和比较组是不同学年的三年级学生，时间就可能成为混杂因素。

### E14

- 位置：Major technical and procedural changes between versions 5.0 and 4.1
- 原文："This procedure reflects the WWC's concern that potentially meaningful differences in effect sizes can be obtained from measures created by intervention developers or study authors, and that these measures may not be as informative to policymakers and practitioners as independent measures."
- 要点：WWC 担心由干预开发者或研究作者自己编的测验可能得出明显不同的效应量，对决策者和实践者的参考价值不如独立的测验。

### E15

- 位置：Chapter III
- 原文："Randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and quasi-experimental designs (QEDs) are the most common research designs reviewed by the WWC. Some of these studies assign individuals to intervention and comparison groups, whereas others assign clusters of individuals, such as entire schools of students."
- 要点：WWC 审查最多的是随机对照试验和准实验；有的按个人分组，有的按群体（例如整所学校）分组。

### E16

- 位置：WWC research ratings and the Standards for Excellence in Education Research
- 原文："WWC research ratings focus primarily on the validity of education study findings for causal inferences."
- 要点：WWC 的评级主要看研究结果能不能支持因果推断。
