# PLDI 2026：PACMPL Issue PLDI 2026 Call for Papers

- 资料：`pldi2026cfp`，[原文](https://pldi26.sigplan.org/track/pldi-2026-papers)
- 版本：ACM SIGPLAN（美国计算机协会程序设计语言分会）程序设计语言设计与实现会议（Programming Language Design and Implementation，PLDI）2026 研究论文征稿启事及所附双盲评审问答，会议官网，2026-10-08 读取。网页，位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Call for Papers 开头；Scope
- 原文："PACMPL Issue PLDI 2026 seeks contributions on all aspects of programming languages research, broadly construed, including design, implementation, theory, applications, and performance." / "Novel system designs, thorough empirical work, well-motivated theoretical results, and new application areas are all in scope for PLDI."
- 要点：PLDI 2026 征集程序语言研究的各个方面：设计、实现、理论、应用和性能；新的系统设计、扎实的实证工作、有充分动机的理论结果、新的应用领域都在范围内。

### E2

- 位置：Call for Papers 开头
- 原文："Authors of papers published in PACMPL Issue PLDI 2026 will be invited – but not required – to present their work in the PLDI conference in June 2026"
- 要点：PLDI 2026 的论文发表在期刊 PACMPL（Proceedings of the ACM on Programming Languages）的 PLDI 专刊上，作者受邀（但不强制）到 2026 年 6 月的会议上报告。

### E3

- 位置：Evaluation Criteria and Process
- 原文："Reviewers will evaluate submissions for accuracy, significance, originality, and clarity." / "Authors of empirical papers are encouraged to consider the seven categories of the SIGPLAN Empirical Evaluation Guidelines when preparing submissions."
- 要点：审稿看准确性、重要性、原创性和清晰度；鼓励实证论文的作者按 SIGPLAN 实证评估指南的七个类别准备投稿。

### E4

- 位置：Evaluation Criteria and Process
- 原文："The selection of papers will be made in two rounds of reviewing." / "At the end of the first round, the Review Committee will conditionally accept a subset of the submissions and all other submissions will be rejected." / "The same reviewers as in the first round will then assess how the revision requests have been acted upon by the authors." / "Revisions that fail to adequately address the reviewers’ original concerns will result in rejection."
- 要点：分两轮审稿：第一轮结束时有条件录用一部分、其余拒稿；第二轮由原审稿人检查修改，没解决原有问题的仍会被拒。

### E5

- 位置：Evaluation Criteria and Process
- 原文："Authors will be given several days to compose a written response to the reviews received in the first round – e.g., to correct errors and clarify technical concerns."
- 要点：第一轮后作者有几天时间写书面回复，例如纠正错误、澄清技术疑问。

### E6

- 位置：Double-Blind Reviewing
- 原文："Author names and affiliations must be omitted from submissions." / "Any supplementary material must also be anonymized." / "The Review Committee will only learn the identities of authors of accepted papers following the second round of reviewing."
- 要点：投稿要去掉作者姓名和单位，补充材料也要匿名；审稿委员会到第二轮结束后才知道录用论文的作者是谁。

### E7

- 位置：FAQ on Double-Blind Reviewing，What exactly do I have to do to anonymize my paper?
- 原文："For example, if your paper is the first to describe a system with a well-known name or codename, or you use a personally-identifiable naming convention for your work, then use a different name for your submission (which you may indicate has been changed for the purposes of double-blind reviewing)."
- 要点：如果论文首次介绍一个名字广为人知的系统，或系统命名能认出作者，投稿时要换一个名字，并可注明是为双盲而改的。

### E8

- 位置：FAQ on Double-Blind Reviewing，Am I allowed to post my (non-blinded) paper ...?
- 原文："Submit work previously discussed at an informal workshop, previously posted on arXiv or a similar site" / "Publicize your work on major mailing lists used by the community (because potential reviewers likely read these lists)."
- 要点：可以投在非正式研讨会讲过、或在 arXiv 上发过的工作；但不要在本领域的主要邮件列表上宣传，因为审稿人很可能会看到。

### E9

- 位置：Declaring Conflicts
- 原文："Listing spurious conflicts can be grounds for rejection."
- 要点：申报不实的利益冲突（借此排除审稿人）可能导致拒稿。

### E10

- 位置：Formatting Requirements
- 原文："Each paper should have no more than 20 pages of text, excluding bibliography, using the ACM Proceedings format."
- 要点：正文不超过 20 页，不含参考文献。

### E11

- 位置：Supplementary Material
- 原文："Reviewers are not required to examine the supplementary material but may refer to it if they would like to find further evidence supporting the claims in the paper." / "All appendices must be submitted as supplemental material, not as part of the main submission PDF."
- 要点：审稿人不必看补充材料，只在想找更多证据时参考；附录都要作为补充材料提交，不放在正文 PDF 里。

### E12

- 位置：Artifact Evaluation and Artifact Intent
- 原文："Artifact Evaluation is run by a separate committee whose task is to assess how well the artifacts support the work described in the papers." / "Artifact submission is voluntary but encouraged. The results of the artifact evaluation process will not influence the final decision regarding the papers."
- 要点：制品评估由另一个委员会负责，看制品在多大程度上支持论文的工作；提交制品是自愿的，评估结果不影响论文是否录用。

### E13

- 位置：Artifact Evaluation and Artifact Intent
- 原文："At paper submission time, authors will be asked whether they intend to submit an artifact for evaluation, and to provide an explanation if they do not intend to do so."
- 要点：PLDI 2026 新规：投稿时要说明是否打算提交制品，不打算的要解释原因。
