# Landis 等人 2012：A call for transparent reporting to optimize the predictive value of preclinical research

- 资料：`landis2012call`，[原文（PubMed Central 作者稿）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3511845/)
- 版本：Nature 490(7419):187–191，2012 年。美国国家神经疾病与卒中研究所（NINDS）2012 年 6 月研讨会的建议。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："The main workshop recommendation is that at a minimum studies should report on sample-size estimation, whether and how animals were randomized, whether investigators were blind to the treatment, and the handling of data."
- 要点：研讨会的主要建议：动物研究至少要报告样本量怎么估计、是否以及怎样随机分组、研究者是否对处理设盲、数据怎样处理。

### E2

- 位置：Widespread deficiencies in methods reporting
- 原文："A review of 100 articles published in Cancer Research in 2010 revealed that only 28% of papers reported that animals were randomly allocated to treatment groups, just 2% of papers reported that observers were blinded to treatment, and none stated the methods used to determine the number of animals per group"
- 要点：一项对 2010 年《Cancer Research》100 篇论文的回顾：28% 报告了随机分组，2% 报告了观察者设盲，没有一篇说明每组动物数怎么定的。

### E3

- 位置：Widespread deficiencies in methods reporting
- 原文："Problems related to inadequate study design surfaced early in the stroke research community, as investigators tried to understand why multiple clinical trials based on positive results in animal studies ultimately failed. Part of the problem is, of course, that no animal model can fully reproduce all the features of human stroke."
- 要点：卒中领域较早发现这个问题：多项基于动物阳性结果的临床试验失败了。原因之一是没有一种动物模型能完全再现人类卒中。

### E4

- 位置：Widespread deficiencies in methods reporting
- 原文："publications that included information on randomization, concealment of group allocation, or blinded assessment of outcomes reported significantly smaller effect sizes of NXY-059 in comparison to studies lacking this information"
- 要点：对药物 NXY-059 卒中动物研究的系统综述和荟萃分析：报告了随机、分组隐藏或盲法评估的论文，效应量明显小于没报告这些信息的论文。

### E5

- 位置：Widespread deficiencies in methods reporting
- 原文："In certain cases, a series of poorly designed studies, obscured by deficient reporting, may, in aggregate, serve erroneously as the scientific rationale for large, expensive and ultimately unsuccessful clinical trials. Such trials may unnecessarily expose patients to potentially harmful agents"
- 要点：设计差、报告又不清的一系列研究，可能合起来错误地成为大型、昂贵、最终失败的临床试验的依据，让病人不必要地暴露于可能有害的药物。

### E6

- 位置：Box 1, Blinding
- 原文："Allocation concealment: the investigator should be unaware of the group to which the next animal taken from a cage will be allocated."
- 要点：分组隐藏：研究者不应知道从笼里取出的下一只动物会被分到哪一组。

### E7

- 位置：Randomization and blinding
- 原文："Attention to these details will avoid mistaking batch effects for treatment effects (for example, dividing samples from a large study into multiple lots, which are then processed separately)."
- 要点：样品处理和数据收集也要随机或分区组安排，避免把批次效应误当成处理效应。

### E8

- 位置：Sample-size estimation
- 原文："It is unethical, however, to perform underpowered experiments with insufficient numbers of animals that have little prospect of detecting meaningful differences between groups."
- 要点：动物数太少、几乎不可能检测到有意义差异的实验，同样不合伦理。

### E9

- 位置：Interim data analysis
- 原文："If the results are statistically significant in favour of the working hypothesis, the study is terminated and a paper is written. If the results look ‘promising’ but are not statistically significant, additional data are collected." / "Therefore, sample size and rules for stopping data collection should be defined in advance and properly reported."
- 要点：结果显著就停、不显著就再加数据，会造成大量假阳性；样本量和停止收集数据的规则要事先定好并报告。

### E10

- 位置：Ad hoc exclusion of data
- 原文："For example, a treatment may appear effective if it kills off the weakest or most severely affected animals whose fates are then not reported."
- 要点：各组动物流失不同会造成偏倚：一种处理如果杀死了最弱的动物而又不报告，看起来反而像是有效。

### E11

- 位置：Retrospective primary end-point selection
- 原文："False-positive conclusions arising from such practices can be avoided by specifying a primary end point before the study is undertaken, the time(s) at which the end point will be assessed, and the method(s) of analysis."
- 要点：研究开始前就指定主要终点、评估时间和分析方法，可以避免事后挑终点带来的假阳性。

### E12

- 位置：An important note about exploratory experiments
- 原文："Such exploratory testing is frequently conducted using a small sample size, does not have a primary outcome and is often unblinded. However, because such experiments are likely to be subject to many of the limitations described above, they should be viewed as hypothesis-generating experiments and interpreted as such."
- 要点：早期探索性实验常常样本小、没有主要结局、不设盲；它们只能当作"提出假设"的实验来解读。

### E13

- 位置：The path to implementation
- 原文："There is a widely accepted belief that the scientific community, promotions committees, funding agencies and journals favour positive outcomes, an impression that can lead to bias"
- 要点：普遍认为科学界、晋升委员会、资助机构和期刊偏爱阳性结果，这会导致偏倚。

### E14

- 位置：The path to implementation
- 原文："although few animal studies report on randomization, blinding or sample-size determination, most describe compliance with animal regulations, which is required by journals"
- 要点：很少有动物研究报告随机、盲法或样本量依据，但大多数都写了遵守动物法规，因为期刊要求写。

### E15

- 位置：Small effect sizes
- 原文："reporting how often the particular experiment was performed as a means to control for a general tendency to publish only the best results would strengthen the validity of experimental results."
- 要点：报告某个实验一共做了几次，可以抵消"只发表最好那次结果"的倾向。

### E16

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："The US National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke convened major stakeholders in June 2012 to discuss how to improve the methodological reporting of animal studies in grant applications and publications."
- 要点：这些建议来自美国国家神经疾病与卒中研究所 2012 年 6 月召集的研讨会，讨论怎样改进基金申请和论文中动物研究方法的报告。

### E17

- 位置：An important note about exploratory experiments
- 原文："Potential discoveries arising from the exploratory phase of the research should be supported by follow-up, hypothesis-testing experiments that take into consideration and adequately report on the core standards detailed above"
- 要点：探索阶段的发现，要用按核心标准设计和报告的后续验证性实验来支持。
