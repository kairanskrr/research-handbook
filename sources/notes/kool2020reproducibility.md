# Kool、Andersson、Giller 2020：Reproducibility and external validity of on-farm experimental research in Africa

- 资料：`kool2020reproducibility`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0014479720000174)
- 版本：Experimental Agriculture 56: 587–607，2020，CC BY 开放获取。作者检索了 1986—2015 年发表的撒哈拉以南非洲农场试验论文，最后详细分析了 172 篇。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Material and Methods
- 原文："We selected a timespan from 1986 to 2015 [...] This resulted in 172 papers which were used in our detailed analyses"
- 要点：作者检索 1986—2015 年的文献，筛选后详细分析了 172 篇撒哈拉以南非洲的农场田间试验论文。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："it is often difficult or impossible to reproduce the experimental design. Nutrient management, weed management and crop information are best described, whereas land preparation, field history and management of pests and water are rarely described."
- 要点：根据论文里给出的信息，常常很难甚至无法重现试验设计。养分管理、除草和作物信息写得最好；整地、田块历史、病虫害和水分管理很少写。

### E3

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："on-farm experimental studies often compare treatments to a ‘farmer practice’ reference or control treatment which is assumed to be widely and uniformly practiced and known to the reader."
- 要点：农场试验常用"农民常规做法"当对照，却默认这种做法到处一样、读者也都知道。

### E4

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Most do not explicitly define the research population and/or environment in which (they expect) the experimental findings to work."
- 要点：被分析的研究大多没有明确说出，结论适用于哪些农户、哪些环境。

### E5

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Research stations represent a highly controlled experimental environment, where single or multiple treatments and their interactions can be investigated holding other variables constant. In farmers’ fields, researchers have much less control over the experimental situation, which is embedded in a much wider and more variable bio-physical and socio-economic environment."
- 要点：试验站是高度受控的环境，可以固定其他变量来研究处理；在农民的田里，研究者能控制的少得多，试验处在更大、更多变的自然和社会经济环境中。

### E6

- 位置：Material and Methods，Analysis of the number of experimental locations
- 原文："Although studies are often multi-seasonal (generally covering 2 to 3 seasons)"
- 要点：被分析的农场试验常常跨多个生长季，一般是 2 到 3 季。

### E7

- 位置：Conclusion
- 原文："we propose that experimental studies should minimally address: (1) under what conditions the treatment effect occurs and (2) for whom the experimental findings are relevant. In addition, we advocate for more transparency about the selection criteria used for research site, farmer and field selection and a reflection on the possible implications of such selection for external validity."
- 要点：作者建议，试验研究至少要回答：处理效应在什么条件下出现；结论对谁有用。还要公开选地点、选农户、选田块的标准，并想一想这种选择会怎样影响结论的推广。

### E8

- 位置：Results，reproducibility
- 原文："A number of studies give rather unclear and vague descriptions of experimental practices."
- 要点：不少研究对田间操作的描述含糊不清（例如只写"必要时除草"）。

### E9

- 位置：Results，Crop information
- 原文："The category ‘crop information’ reflects the yield-defining factors, with the variables ‘varieties’, ‘planting date’, ‘plant spacing’ and ‘mono-/intercrop, crop rotation’. [...] Only 10% of the studies described all four factors. Most studies provide information on the plant spacing (76%) and the variety used (69%) but fewer mention the planting date (41%)."
- 要点：只有 10% 的研究把作物信息的四项（品种、播种日期、株行距、单作间作和轮作）都写全了；76% 写了株行距，69% 写了品种，只有 41% 写了播种日期。

### E10

- 位置：Results，farmer practice 的描述
- 原文："In none of these examples do the authors provide details on how this was done. Such uninformative descriptions provide insufficient information to make the experiments reproducible."
- 要点：像"按农民常规做法管理"这类描述没有交代具体怎么做，信息不足，无法重现试验。

### E11

- 位置：Discussion，external validity
- 原文："Interestingly, Franke et al. (2010) found that farmers copied management methods from some of the experimental treatments, and sometimes competed with the researcher-managed plots."
- 要点：有研究发现，农民会照搬某些试验处理的管理办法，有时还和研究者管理的小区"比赛"。

### E12

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："we propose that experimental studies should minimally address: (1) under what conditions the treatment effect occurs and (2) for whom the experimental findings are relevant. In addition, we advocate for more transparency about the selection criteria used for research site, farmer and field selection"
- 要点：试验研究至少要回答：处理效应在什么条件下出现、结论对谁有用；并公开选地点、农户、田块的标准。

### E13

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Research stations represent a highly controlled experimental environment, where single or multiple treatments and their interactions can be investigated holding other variables constant. In farmers’ fields, researchers have much less control over the experimental situation"
- 要点：试验站是高度受控的环境；在农民田里，研究者能控制的少得多。
