# ACM 2025：ACM Policy on Authorship

- 资料：`acm2025authorship`，[原文](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/new-acm-policy-on-authorship)（官网对脚本返回 403，用的是 Internet Archive 2025-12-15 的存档：https://web.archive.org/web/20251215000740/https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/new-acm-policy-on-authorship）
- 版本：网页，页面标注 "Updated on September 16, 2025"。

### E1

- 位置：Scope of Policy
- 原文："This policy applies to all submitted, accepted, and published articles and abstracts in all ACM Publication venues, including ACM journals, ACM conferences, ICPS conferences, ACM magazines, and ACM books."
- 要点：这份署名政策适用于 ACM 旗下所有期刊、会议、杂志和图书。

### E2

- 位置：Criteria for Authorship
- 原文："Anyone listed as author on an ACM submission must meet all the following criteria:" / "They are an identifiable human being." / "They have made substantial intellectual contributions to some components of the original Work described in the manuscript, such as contributing to the conception, design, and analysis of the study reported on in the Work and participating in the drafting and/or revision of the manuscript." / "They take full responsibility for all content in the published Works."
- 要点：ACM 投稿上的每位作者都要同时满足：是可识别的真人；对研究的某些部分（如构思、设计、分析）有实质性的智力贡献，并参与起草或修改稿件；对发表内容负全部责任。

### E3

- 位置：Criteria for Authorship，Note
- 原文："All individuals who meet the above criteria should be listed as authors on the Work. The practices of gift authorship, guest authorship, ghost authorship" [...] "and purchased authorship are clear violations of ACM Publications Policy"
- 要点：符合条件的人都应署名；赠送署名、挂名、代写不署名、买卖署名都明确违反 ACM 出版政策。

### E4

- 位置：Criteria for Authorship
- 原文："Those who contributed to a Work, but whose contribution does not rise to the level of authorship, may be acknowledged at the end of the Work, before the Bibliography, with explicitly described roles, preferably using the roles found in the CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy)."
- 要点：有贡献但不够署名的人，可以在文末、参考文献之前致谢，写明各自的角色，最好用 CRediT（贡献者角色分类）里的角色名。

### E5

- 位置：Criteria for Authorship
- 原文："Generative AI tools and technologies, such as ChatGPT, may not be listed as authors of an ACM published Work. The use of generative AI tools and technologies to create content is permitted but must be fully disclosed in the Work."
- 要点：生成式人工智能工具不能列为作者；用它生成内容是允许的，但必须在论文里完整披露。
