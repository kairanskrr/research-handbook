# Runeson & Höst 2009：Guidelines for conducting and reporting case study research in software engineering

- 资料：`runeson2009guidelines`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10664-008-9102-8)（开放获取）
- 版本：Empirical Software Engineering 14(2):131–164，本地 PDF `papers/pdf/runeson2009guidelines.pdf`。页码为期刊页码。
- 说明：这里只用它对效度（validity）的分类和定义。作者说明这一分类也与软件工程对照实验中常用的分类相近。

### E1

- 位置：§5.2.3 Validity，第 153 页
- 原文："The validity of a study denotes the trustworthiness of the results, to what extent the results are true and not biased by the researchers’ subjective point of view."
- 要点：效度指结果的可信程度：结果在多大程度上是真的，没有被研究者的主观看法带偏。

### E2

- 位置：§5.2.3，第 153 页
- 原文："It is, of course, too late to consider the validity during the analysis. The validity must be addressed during all previous phases of the case study."
- 要点：到分析阶段才考虑效度就太晚了，要在之前的每个阶段都考虑。

### E3

- 位置：§5.2.3，Construct validity
- 原文："This aspect of validity reflect to what extent the operational measures that are studied really represent what the researcher have in mind and what is investigated according to the research questions."
- 要点：构念效度：你实际测量的东西，是否真的代表你想研究的概念。

### E4

- 位置：§5.2.3，Internal validity
- 原文："When the researcher is investigating whether one factor affects an investigated factor there is a risk that the investigated factor is also affected by a third factor."
- 要点：内部效度：研究 A 是否影响 B 时，B 可能也受第三个因素影响；没发现或没控制这个因素，就威胁内部效度。

### E5

- 位置：§5.2.3，External validity
- 原文："This aspect of validity is concerned with to what extent it is possible to generalize the findings, and to what extent the findings are of interest to other people outside the investigated case."
- 要点：外部效度：研究发现能在多大程度上推广到所研究的对象之外。
