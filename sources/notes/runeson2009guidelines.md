# Runeson & Höst 2009：Guidelines for conducting and reporting case study research in software engineering

- 资料：`runeson2009guidelines`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10664-008-9102-8)（开放获取）
- 版本：Empirical Software Engineering 14(2):131–164，本地 PDF `papers/pdf/runeson2009guidelines.pdf`。页码为期刊页码。
- 说明：这里只用它对效度（validity）的分类和定义。作者说明这一分类也与软件工程对照实验中常用的分类相近。

### E1

- 位置：§5.2.3 Validity，第 153 页
- 原文："The validity of a study denotes the trustworthiness of the results, to what extent the results are true and not biased by the researchers’ subjective point of view."
- 要点：效度指结果的可信程度：结果在多大程度上是真的，没有被研究者的主观看法带偏。

### E2

- 位置：§5.2.3，第 153 页
- 原文："It is, of course, too late to consider the validity during the analysis. The validity must be addressed during all previous phases of the case study."
- 要点：到分析阶段才考虑效度就太晚了，要在之前的每个阶段都考虑。

### E3

- 位置：§5.2.3，Construct validity
- 原文："This aspect of validity reflect to what extent the operational measures that are studied really represent what the researcher have in mind and what is investigated according to the research questions."
- 要点：构念效度：你实际测量的东西，是否真的代表你想研究的概念。

### E4

- 位置：§5.2.3，Internal validity
- 原文："When the researcher is investigating whether one factor affects an investigated factor there is a risk that the investigated factor is also affected by a third factor."
- 要点：内部效度：研究 A 是否影响 B 时，B 可能也受第三个因素影响；没发现或没控制这个因素，就威胁内部效度。

### E5

- 位置：§5.2.3，External validity
- 原文："This aspect of validity is concerned with to what extent it is possible to generalize the findings, and to what extent the findings are of interest to other people outside the investigated case."
- 要点：外部效度：研究发现能在多大程度上推广到所研究的对象之外。

### E6

- 位置：§4.2 Interviews
- 原文："the interview questions are based on the formulated research questions (but they are of course not formulated in the same way)."
- 要点：访谈问题从研究问题推出来，但不是照搬研究问题的措辞。

### E7

- 位置：§4.2 Interviews
- 原文："In a semi-structured interview, questions are planned, but they are not necessarily asked in the same order as they are listed." / "Additionally, semi-structured interviews allow for improvisation and exploration of the studied objects."
- 要点：半结构化访谈事先规划问题，但不一定按顺序问；它允许临场发挥和探索。

### E8

- 位置：§4.2 Interviews
- 原文："Questions can be open, i.e. allowing and inviting a broad range of answers and issues from the interviewed subject, or closed offering a limited set of alternative answers."
- 要点：问题可以是开放的（欢迎各种回答），也可以是封闭的（只给几个选项）。

### E9

- 位置：§4.2 Interviews
- 原文："First the researcher presents the objectives of the interview and the case study, and explains how the data from the interview will be used. Then a set of introductory questions are asked about the background etc. of the subject, which are relatively simple to answer."
- 要点：访谈开始时先介绍目的和数据用途，再问一些容易回答的背景问题。

### E10

- 位置：§4.2 Interviews
- 原文："It is not recommended to start the interview with these questions or to introduce them before a climate of trust has been obtained. It is recommended that the major findings are summarized by the researcher towards the end of the interview, in order to get feedback and avoid misunderstandings."
- 要点：敏感问题不要放在开头，要等建立信任之后再问；访谈快结束时，研究者应总结主要收获，请对方反馈，避免误解。

### E11

- 位置：§4.2 Interviews
- 原文："The funnel model begins with open questions and moves towards more specific ones."
- 要点：漏斗式访谈从开放问题开始，逐步转向具体问题。

### E12

- 位置：§4.2 Interviews
- 原文："During the interview sessions it is recommended to record the discussion in a suitable audio or video format. Even if notes are taken, it is in many cases hard to record all details, and it is impossible to know what is important to record during the interview."
- 要点：建议录音或录像；只靠笔记很难记下所有细节，而且访谈当时无法知道什么是重要的。

### E13

- 位置：§4.2 Interviews
- 原文："This is a time consuming task, but in many cases new insights are made during the transcription, and it is therefore not recommended that this task is conducted by anyone else than the researcher. In some cases it may be advantageous to have the transcripts reviewed by the interview subject."
- 要点：转录费时，但常常在转录中产生新想法，所以建议研究者自己转录；有时可以请受访者审阅转录稿。

### E14

- 位置：§4.2 Interviews
- 原文："it is recommended to select subjects based on" [...] "differences instead of trying to replicate similarities" / "The number of interviewees has to be decided during the study. One criterion for when sufficient interviews are conducted is “saturation”, i.e. when no new information or viewpoint is gained from new subjects"
- 要点：按差异而不是相似性选择受访者；访谈人数要在研究过程中决定，一个标准是"饱和"：新的受访者不再带来新信息或新观点。

### E15

- 位置：§5.2 Qualitative Data Analysis
- 原文："Analysis must be carried out in parallel with the data collection since the approach is flexible and that new insights are found during the analysis."
- 要点：定性分析要和数据收集同时进行，因为分析中会产生新的认识，需要据此收集新数据。

### E16

- 位置：§5.2 Qualitative Data Analysis
- 原文："In order to reduce bias by individual researchers, the analysis benefits from being conducted by multiple researchers."
- 要点：由多位研究者分析，可以减少个人偏见。

### E17

- 位置：§5.2.1 General Techniques for Analysis
- 原文："The data can, for example be organized in a table where the rows represent codes of interest and the columns represent interview subjects."
- 要点：可以把编码后的数据整理成表格：行是编码，列是受访者。

### E18

- 位置：§3.3 Ethical considerations
- 原文："Participants must not necessarily agree in the outcome of the analysis, but feeding back the analysis results increases the validity of the study."
- 要点：参与者不一定要同意分析结论，但把分析结果反馈给他们能提高研究的效度。

### E19

- 位置：§1 Introduction，第 132 页
- 原文："The term “case study” appears every now and then in the title of software engineering research papers. However, the presented studies range from very ambitious and well organized studies in the field, to small toy examples that claim to be case studies."
- 要点：标题里叫"案例研究"的论文，从认真组织的实地研究到自称案例研究的小玩具例子都有。

### E20

- 位置：§2.1，第 134 页
- 原文："More strictly, a case study is purely observational while action research is focused on and involved in the change process."
- 要点：严格说，案例研究只观察；行动研究则参与并推动变化。

### E21

- 位置：§2.1，第 134 页
- 原文："We prefer to consider ethnographic studies as a specialized type of case studies with focus on cultural practices (Easterbrook et al. 2008) or long duration studies with large amounts of participant-observer data (Klein and Myers 1999)."
- 要点：作者把民族志看作案例研究的一种特殊类型：关注文化实践，或持续时间长、有大量参与式观察数据。

### E22

- 位置：§2.2，第 136 页
- 原文："Case studies are by definition conducted in real world settings, and thus have a high degree of realism, mostly at the expense of the level of control."
- 要点：案例研究在真实环境中进行，真实性高，代价通常是控制程度低。

### E23

- 位置：§2.2，第 137 页
- 原文："Hence, a case study will never provide conclusions with statistical significance. On the contrary, many different kinds of evidence, figures, statements, documents, are linked together to support a strong and relevant conclusion."
- 要点：案例研究不会给出有统计显著性的结论，而是把数字、陈述、文档等多种证据连起来支撑结论。

### E24

- 位置：§2.2，第 137 页
- 原文："its conclusions are based on a clear chain of evidence, whether qualitative or quantitative, collected from multiple sources in a planned and consistent manner"
- 要点：案例研究的关键特征之一：结论建立在清楚的证据链上，证据按计划、一致地从多个来源收集。

### E25

- 位置：§2.4，第 138 页
- 原文："However, there is a limit to the flexibility; the case study should have specific objectives set out from the beginning. If the objectives change, it is a new case study rather than a change to the existing one"
- 要点：案例研究设计灵活，但目标要从一开始定下；目标变了，就是一项新的案例研究。

### E26

- 位置：§2.5 Definitions，第 138 页
- 原文："The case is referred to as the object of the study (e.g. a project), and it contains one or more units of analysis (e.g. subprojects)."
- 要点：案例是研究对象（如一个项目），其中包含一个或多个分析单元（如子项目）。

### E27

- 位置：§3.1，第 139 页
- 原文："Objective—what to achieve? & The case—what is studied? & Theory—frame of reference & Research questions—what to know? & Methods—how to collect data? & Selection strategy—where to seek data?"
- 要点：案例研究计划至少包含：目标、案例、理论（参照框架）、研究问题、数据收集方法、选择策略。

### E28

- 位置：§3.1，第 139 页
- 原文："Studies on “toy programs” or similarly are of course excluded due to its lack of real-life context. Yin (2003) distinguishes between holistic case studies, where the case is studied as a whole, and embedded case studies where multiple units of analysis are studied within a case"
- 要点：在玩具程序上做的研究不算案例研究，因为没有真实环境；整体式案例研究把案例当一个整体研究，嵌入式案例研究在一个案例中研究多个分析单元。

### E29

- 位置：§3.1，第 140 页
- 原文："In case studies, the case and the units of analysis should be selected intentionally. This is in contrast to surveys and experiments, where subjects are sampled from a population to which the results are intended to be generalized."
- 要点：案例和分析单元要有意识地挑选，而不是像调查和实验那样从总体中抽样。

### E30

- 位置：§3.1，第 140 页
- 原文："The purpose of the selection may be to study a case that is expected to be “typical”, “critical”, “revelatory” or “unique” in some respect [...] However, in practice, many cases are selected based on availability"
- 要点：可以按"典型""关键""揭示性""独特"来选案例；但实际上很多案例是按能否接触到来选的。

### E31

- 位置：§3.2 Case Study Protocol，第 141 页
- 原文："The case study protocol is a container for the design decisions on the case study as well as field procedures for its carrying through. The protocol is a continuously changed document that is updated when the plans for the case study are changed."
- 要点：案例研究方案记录设计决策和现场操作程序，是随计划变化不断更新的文档。

### E32

- 位置：§3.2，第 141 页
- 原文："Firstly, it serves as a guide when conducting the data collection, and in that way prevents the researcher from missing to collect data that were planned to be collected. [...] Thirdly, other researchers and relevant people may review it in order to give feedback on the plans. [...] Finally, it can serve as a log or diary where all conducted data collection and analysis is recorded together with change decisions based on the flexible nature of the research. [...] In order to keep track of changes during the research project, the protocol should be kept under some form of version control."
- 要点：方案能指导数据收集、防止漏收计划中的数据；可以请其他研究者和相关人员审阅并反馈；还可以当作日志，记录所有数据收集、分析和调整决定；要做版本管理以记录变化。

### E33

- 位置：§3.2，第 141 页
- 原文："Case study protocols cannot be published in extenso since they contain confidential information. However, parts of the protocol can be published, such as interview instruments"
- 要点：方案含保密信息，不能全文公开，但访谈提纲等部分可以公开。

### E34

- 位置：§3.3 Ethical Considerations，第 142 页
- 原文："In software engineering, case studies often include dealing with confidential information in an organization. If it is not clear from the beginning how this kind of information is handled and who is responsible for accepting what information to publish, there may be problems later on."
- 要点：案例研究常接触组织的保密信息；如果一开始没说清怎么处理、谁来批准发表什么，后面就会出问题。

### E35

- 位置：§3.3，第 142 页
- 原文："It may be tempting for the researcher to collect data e.g. through indirect or independent data collection methods, without asking for consent. However, the ethical standards must be maintained for the long term trust in software engineering research."
- 要点：用间接方法收集数据时，可能想跳过征得同意，但为了长期信任必须守住伦理标准。

### E36

- 位置：§3.3，第 143 页
- 原文："Issues on confidentiality and publication should also be regulated in a contract between the researcher and the studied organization. However, not only can information be sensitive when leaking outside a company. Data collected from and opinions stated by individual employees may be sensitive if presented e.g. to their managers"
- 要点：保密和发表问题要在研究者与组织之间的合同里约定；员工个人的数据和意见如果被呈给其上级，也可能是敏感的。

### E37

- 位置：§3.3，第 143 页
- 原文："Companies may not know academic practices for publication and dissemination, and must hence be explicitly informed about those."
- 要点：企业可能不了解学术发表的惯例，必须明确告诉它们。

### E38

- 位置：§3.3，第 143 页
- 原文："However, it is important to remember that it is not always sufficient to remove names of companies or individuals. They may be identified by their characteristics if they are selected from a small set of people or companies."
- 要点：只去掉公司名和人名不一定够；对象来自很小的群体时，可能凭特征被认出来。

### E39

- 位置：§3.3，第 143 页
- 原文："Results may be sensitive to a company, e.g. by revealing deficiencies in their software engineering practices, or if their product comes out last in a comparison (Amschler Andrews and Pradhan 2001). The chance that this may occur must be discussed upfront and made clear to the participants of the case study."
- 要点：结果可能对企业不利（如暴露实践中的缺陷）；这种可能要事先讨论并说清楚。

### E40

- 位置：§3.3，第 143 页
- 原文："It is preferable to make the inducements explicit, i.e. specify what the incentives are for the participants. Thereby the inducement’s role in threatening the validity of the study may also be analyzed."
- 要点：参与的好处（激励）最好明确写出，这样也能分析它对效度的影响。

### E41

- 位置：§3.3，第 143 页
- 原文："Firstly, transcript of interviews and observations should be sent back to the participants to enable correction of raw data. Secondly, analyses should be presented to them in order to maintain their trust in the research."
- 要点：把访谈和观察的转录稿发回参与者修正，并向他们展示分析结果，以维持信任。

### E42

- 位置：§3.3，第 143–144 页（作者自己三项研究的例子）
- 原文："These agreements state that the university researchers are obliged to have publications approved by representatives of the companies before they are published, and that raw data must not be spread to any but those signing the contract. [...] we were explicitly told that raw numbers of defects could not be published, but percentages over phases could, which was acceptable for the research purposes."
- 要点：作者的例子：保密协议规定论文发表前须经企业代表批准、原始数据不外传；企业不允许公布缺陷原始数量，但允许公布各阶段百分比，这对研究目的已经够用。

### E43

- 位置：§4.1，第 144 页
- 原文："It is important to use several data sources in a case study in order to limit the effects of one interpretation of one single data source. If the same conclusion can be drawn from several sources of information, i.e. triangulation (Section 2.2), this conclusion is stronger than a conclusion based a single source."
- 要点：用多个数据来源，限制单一来源单一解读的影响；多个来源得出同一结论（三角验证）时，结论更强。

### E44

- 位置：§2.2，第 136 页
- 原文："Data (source) triangulation—using more than one data source or collecting the same data at different occasions. [...] Observer triangulation—using more than one observer in the study. [...] Theory triangulation—using alternative theories or viewpoints."
- 要点：三角验证的几种形式：数据来源、观察者、方法、理论（视角）。

### E45

- 位置：§4.1，第 145 页
- 原文："Third degree: Independent analysis of work artifacts where already available and sometimes compiled data is used. [...] For third degree methods it should also be noticed that the data has been collected and recorded for another purpose than that of the research study"
- 要点：第三级方法分析已有的工作产物（文档、缺陷报告等）；要注意这些数据原本是为别的目的记录的。

### E46

- 位置：§4.2 Interviews，第 146–147 页
- 原文："Semi-structured interviews are common in case studies. [...] One criterion for when sufficient interviews are conducted is “saturation”, i.e. when no new information or viewpoint is gained from new subjects"
- 要点：半结构化访谈在案例研究中常见；"饱和"（新访谈对象不再带来新信息或观点）是访谈够不够的一个判断标准。

### E47

- 位置：§4.2，第 147 页
- 原文："This means that it is good to try to involve different roles, personalities, etc in the interview."
- 要点：访谈对象要覆盖不同角色、不同性格的人。

### E48

- 位置：§4.3 Observations，第 148 页
- 原文："Further, it is particularly relevant to use observations, where it is suspected that there is a deviation between an “official” view of matters and the “real” case"
- 要点：怀疑"官方说法"和"实际情况"有出入时，特别适合用观察。

### E49

- 位置：§4.3，第 148 页
- 原文："The observer spent 1 week with an XP team, taking part in everyday activities, including pair programming, i.e. an approach like Case 1 above. Data collected consisted of field notes, audio recordings of meetings and discussions, photographs and copies of artifacts."
- 要点：Sharp & Robinson 的例子：观察者和一个极限编程团队一起待了一周，参与日常活动；数据包括现场笔记、会议录音、照片和工作产物副本。

### E50

- 位置：§5.1，第 150–151 页
- 原文："The basic objective of the analysis is to derive conclusions from the data, keeping a clear chain of evidence. The chain of evidence means [...] that a reader should be able to follow the derivation of results and conclusions from the collected data"
- 要点：证据链指读者能顺着从收集的数据一路追到结果和结论。

### E51

- 位置：§5.2，第 151 页
- 原文："Negative case analysis tries to find alternative explanations that reject the hypotheses."
- 要点：反例分析：主动寻找能推翻假设的其他解释。

### E52

- 位置：§5.2，第 151 页
- 原文："First the data is coded, which means that parts of the text can be given a code representing a certain theme, area, construct, etc."
- 要点：定性分析第一步是编码：给文本片段打上代表某个主题或概念的标签。

### E53

- 位置：§5.2，第 152 页（作者的 XP 研究例子）
- 原文："Each statement in the transcribed interviews was given a unique identification, and classified by two researchers."
- 要点：例子：转录稿中的每条陈述都有唯一编号，由两名研究者分类。

### E54

- 位置：§5.2，第 153 页
- 原文："This means, for example, in all cases that a pre-planned approach for analysis must be applied, all decisions taken by the researcher must be recorded, all versions of instrumentation must be kept, links between data, codes, and memos must be explicitly recorded in documentation, etc."
- 要点：定性分析要有结构：事先计划分析方法，记录所有决定，保留访谈提纲等工具的所有版本，明确记录数据、编码和备忘之间的联系。

### E55

- 位置：§5.2，第 153 页
- 原文："To our experience editing approaches and template approaches are most suitable in software engineering case studies. It is hard to present and obtain a clear chain of evidence in informal immersion approaches."
- 要点：作者的经验：编辑式（编码在分析中产生）和模板式（事先定好较多编码）分析最适合软件工程案例研究；全凭直觉的沉浸式分析很难给出清楚的证据链。

### E56

- 位置：§5.2.3，第 154 页
- 原文："There is no population from which a statistically representative sample has been drawn. However, for case studies, the intention is to enable analytical generalization where the results are extended to cases which have common characteristics and hence for which the findings are relevant, i.e. defining a theory."
- 要点：案例研究没有统计意义上的代表性样本；它追求分析性推广：把结果推广到具有共同特征的其他案例，也就是形成理论。

### E57

- 位置：§5.2.3，第 154 页
- 原文："Internal validity: This aspect of validity is of concern when causal relations are examined."
- 要点：内部效度只在考察因果关系时才需要关心。

### E58

- 位置：§5.2.3，第 154 页
- 原文："Reliability: This aspect is concerned with to what extent the data and the analysis are dependent on the specific researchers."
- 要点：可靠性：数据和分析在多大程度上依赖于具体是哪个研究者在做。

### E59

- 位置：§5.2.3，第 154 页
- 原文："Examples of ways to improve validity are triangulation, developing and maintaining a detailed case study protocol, having designs, protocols, etc. reviewed by peer researchers, having collected data and obtained results reviewed by case subjects, spending sufficient time with the case, and giving sufficient concern to analysis of “negative cases”, i.e. looking for theories that contradict your findings."
- 要点：提高效度的做法：三角验证、维护详细方案、请同行评审设计、请研究对象审阅数据和结果、在案例中待足够长时间、认真分析反例。

### E60

- 位置：§6.1，第 155 页
- 原文："Describing the case might be more sensitive, since this might enable identification of the case or its subjects. For example, “a large telecommunications company in Sweden” is most probably a branch of the Ericsson Corporation."
- 要点：描述案例时可能暴露身份，例如"瑞典一家大型电信公司"几乎就是爱立信。

### E61

- 位置：§6.1，第 155 页
- 原文："Internal characteristics, like size of the studied unit, average age of the personnel, etc may be more interesting than external characteristics like domain and turnover."
- 要点：描述案例时，内部特征（单元规模、人员平均年龄等）可能比外部特征（行业、营业额）更有用，也更不易暴露身份。

### E62

- 位置：§6.1，第 155 页
- 原文："If they are changed substantially over the course of the study, this should be reported to help understanding the case."

### E63

- 位置：§6.1，第 156 页
- 原文："Providing a “history of the inquiry” requires a level of substantially more detail than pure reporting of used methodologies, e.g. “we launched a case study using semi-structured interviews”."
- 要点：报告要给出"研究过程的历史"：谁在什么时候做了什么，而不只是一句"我们用半结构化访谈做了案例研究"。

### E64

- 位置：§6.1，第 156 页
- 原文："However, to establish trust in the study, the reader needs relevant snapshots from the data that support the conclusions. These snapshots may be in the form of e.g. citations (typical or special statements), pictures, or narratives with anonymized subjects."
- 要点：为了让读者信任结论，要给出支撑结论的数据片段，如引语、图片或匿名化的叙述。

### E65

- 位置：§5.2.3，第 153 页
- 原文："Some researchers have argued for having a different classification scheme for flexible design studies (credibility, transferability, dependability, confirmability), while we prefer to operationalize this scheme for flexible design studies, instead of changing the terms (Robson 2002)."
