# Stagge 等人 2019：Assessing data availability and research reproducibility in hydrology and water resources

- 资料：`stagge2019assessing`，[原文](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201930)
- 版本：Scientific Data 6: 190030，开放获取。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："We used the tool to assess 360 of the 1,989 articles published by six hydrology and water resources journals in 2017."
- 要点：作者用自己设计的调查工具，评估了六种水文与水资源期刊 2017 年发表的 1,989 篇论文中的 360 篇。

### E2

- 位置：Results
- 原文："70.3% of the 360 sampled articles stated some materials were available, but we could only access 48.6% of those materials online" / "Only 5.6% of sampled articles made data, model/code, and directions publicly available while just 1.1% of sampled articles made artifacts available and were fully reproduced."
- 要点：360 篇抽样论文中，70.3% 声称有材料可得，但其中只有 48.6% 的材料能在网上拿到；只有 5.6% 公开了数据、模型或代码以及使用说明；只有 1.1% 既公开了材料又被完整复现。

### E3

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："We estimated, with 95% confidence, that results might be reproduced for only 0.6% to 6.8% of all 1,989 articles."
- 要点：作者估计，这 1,989 篇论文中能复现结果的比例在 0.6% 到 6.8% 之间（95% 置信区间）。

### E4

- 位置：Results，Reproducibility of Results
- 原文："A common issue across cases where we did not generate results was that folder and file locations were hard-coded to work on the author’s computer."
- 要点：没能跑出结果的案例中，常见问题是文件夹和文件路径写死成只在作者电脑上有效。

### E5

- 位置：Discussion，Authors
- 原文："our results showed that, for all journals, the number of sampled articles with code/data/software was consistently 2 to 3 times higher than the number of articles that provided directions"
- 要点：六种期刊里，提供了代码、数据或软件的抽样论文，数量都是提供了使用说明的论文的 2 到 3 倍。

### E6

- 位置：Results，Reproducibility and Journal Policies
- 原文："Stronger journal data availability policies and making open data commitments tend to produce higher rates of artifact availability and result reproducibility. However, there is significant variation among these journals"
- 要点：期刊的数据可得性政策越强，材料可得率和可复现率往往越高，但期刊之间差异很大。

### E7

- 位置：Results，Reproducibility and Journal Policies
- 原文："HESS and WRR, which require data availability statements, had lower percentages of articles that made artifacts available and more papers that direct readers to the authors or third parties for data, models, or code"
- 要点：要求写数据可得性声明的两种期刊（HESS 和 WRR），公开材料的论文比例反而较低，更多论文让读者去找作者或第三方要数据、模型或代码。

### E8

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："we reproduced results for only a small fraction of articles (1.6% of tested articles) using their available artifacts. We estimated, with 95% confidence, that results might be reproduced for only 0.6% to 6.8% of all 1,989 articles."
- 要点：在抽查的论文中，只有 1.6% 能用公开材料复现结果；推算到全部 1989 篇，可复现的比例在 0.6%–6.8% 之间（95% 置信区间）。

### E9

- 位置：Results
- 原文："70.3% of the 360 sampled articles stated some materials were available, but we could only access 48.6% of those materials online"
- 要点：360 篇里 70.3% 声称有材料可用，但其中只有 48.6% 能在网上真正拿到。

### E10

- 位置：Results
- 原文："Hydrology and Earth Systems Sciences (HESS) and Water Resources Research (WRR) had high percentages of articles where materials were only available by first author request (38.5–40.2%)."
- 要点：在《水文与地球系统科学》（HESS）和《水资源研究》（WRR）这两种期刊 2017 年的抽查论文里，38.5%–40.2% 的论文材料只能"向第一作者索取"。

### E11

- 位置：Results
- 原文："Other cases pointed to general data gateways, like the USGS streamgauge network, with no further details, or required expensive or proprietary software."
- 要点：另一些论文只指向一个笼统的数据门户（如美国地质调查局的水文站网），没有具体说明；或依赖昂贵的商业软件。

### E12

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："for all journals, the number of sampled articles with code/data/software was consistently 2 to 3 times higher than the number of articles that provided directions"
- 要点：在六种期刊中，提供了代码或数据的抽查论文数，都是提供了使用说明的论文数的 2 到 3 倍。

### E13

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："Authors can use the survey tool as a checklist to self-assess the availability of data, models, code, and directions and reproducibility of their work before submitting work for publication."
- 要点：作者可以在投稿前用这套问卷当清单自查。

### E14

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："Authors who bundled their code and data together in a single GitHub repository further allowed us to download the entire project, with a higher likelihood that code pointed to valid file directories."
- 要点：把代码和数据打包在同一个仓库里，复现者更容易拿到整个项目，路径也更可能有效。
