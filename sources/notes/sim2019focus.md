# Sim & Waterfield 2019：Focus group methodology: some ethical challenges

- 资料：`sim2019focus`，[原文](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11135-019-00914-5)
- 版本：Quality & Quantity 53:3003–3022（2019），开放获取（知识共享署名许可 4.0）。社会研究与健康研究的方法伦理。2026-10-17 读的期刊网页全文，位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§1.1 Definition of a focus group
- 原文："A focus group can be defined broadly as ‘a type of group discussion about a topic under the guidance of a trained group moderator’ (Stewart 2018: p. 687)."
- 要点：焦点小组可以宽泛地定义为：在受过训练的主持人引导下，围绕一个话题进行的小组讨论。

### E2

- 位置：§1.1 Definition of a focus group
- 原文："It is recommended that data collection, and subsequent analysis, should take account of both the dialogue and the interaction that has occurred within the group"
- 要点：数据收集和分析都应同时考虑组内的对话内容和互动。

### E3

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Focus group methodology generates distinct ethical challenges that do not correspond fully to those raised by one-to-one interviews."
- 要点：焦点小组带来的伦理问题和一对一访谈不完全相同。

### E4

- 位置：§2.1 Disclosure and consent
- 原文："This applies even more in focus group research, because what takes place in the group depends in part upon other participants, who may spontaneously raise issues not necessarily intended, or predicted, by the moderator."
- 要点：焦点小组里发生什么，部分取决于其他参与者，他们可能临时提起主持人没打算、也没预料到的话题，所以很难事先向参与者说清会发生什么。

### E5

- 位置：§2.2 Consent as revocable
- 原文："Withdrawing from a group discussion is a very public and potentially disruptive act that an individual may find hard to perform."
- 要点：从小组讨论中途退出，是当众进行、可能打断讨论的举动，个人可能很难做到。

### E6

- 位置：§2.2 Consent as revocable
- 原文："Probably the only way to resolve this problem is to make it explicit in the consent process that whilst a participant can withdraw from the group, and may ask that his or her data are not quoted when the study is reported, withdrawing data prior to analysis is simply not possible."
- 要点：作者建议在知情同意时讲明：可以退出小组，也可以要求报告中不引用自己的话，但分析前撤回自己的数据是做不到的，因为它和别人的发言交织在一起。

### E7

- 位置：§3.1 Deductive disclosure and internal confidentiality
- 原文："Clearly, external confidentiality can normally be assured by the researcher, as he or she is in control of what is reported from the study. Internal confidentiality relies on adherence to ground rules and observance of aspects of the consent process, over both of which the researcher has much more limited control."
- 要点：研究者能保证"外部保密"（自己报告什么由自己控制）；"内部保密"（组员会不会往外说）靠组员遵守规则，研究者能控制的很有限。

### E8

- 位置：§3.1 Deductive disclosure and internal confidentiality
- 原文："This assists in preserving anonymity, but if participants are known to one another, their anonymity is clearly harder to maintain."
- 要点：参与者彼此不认识有助于匿名；互相认识时，匿名就更难维持。

### E9

- 位置：§3.1 Deductive disclosure and internal confidentiality
- 原文："In particular, it is much harder to ‘withdraw’ a comment in the public context of a focus group than in a one-to-one interview (Carey and Asbury 2012)."
- 要点：在焦点小组这种公开场合，说出口的话比在一对一访谈里更难"收回"。

### E10

- 位置：§3.1 Deductive disclosure and internal confidentiality
- 原文："The moderator can also minimize deductive disclosure, by omitting certain information about participants in a report, by attributing quotations to categories of participants rather than pseudonymized individuals, or by altering other information that is potentially identifying."
- 要点：为防止别人拼凑信息认出参与者，可以在报告中省略部分信息、把引语归到某类参与者而不是某个化名个人，或改动其他可能暴露身份的信息。

### E11

- 位置：§5 Practical strategies，The consent process
- 原文："Thus, certain assurances can be given, though these can strictly only relate to those aspects of the focus group over which the researcher has a degree of influence (Carey and Asbury 2012)."
- 要点：研究者可以给出一些保证，但严格说只能限于自己能影响的那部分。

### E12

- 位置：§5 Practical strategies，Briefing
- 原文："The researcher can emphasize the public nature of a focus group and the need for confidentiality and anonymity, and provide guidance on subjects that might be felt unsuitable to be raised during the discussion"
- 要点：讨论开始前的说明环节，可以强调焦点小组是公开场合、大家需要相互保密和匿名，并提示哪些话题不适合在组里提起。

### E13

- 位置：§5 Practical strategies，Conduct of the focus group
- 原文："he or she can nonetheless try to ensure that all members of the group have an opportunity to contribute to the discussion, without being under pressure to do so if unwilling, and that one or more individuals do not dominate the group. Additionally, the moderator can be alert to distress, breaches of confidentiality, or over-disclosure, and seek to turn the discussion in another direction if appropriate."
- 要点：主持人要让每个人都有机会发言、但不逼不愿说的人，不让一两个人主导；还要留意有人难受、泄露别人隐私或说得过多，必要时把话题引开。

### E14

- 位置：§5 Practical strategies，Conduct of the focus group
- 原文："If sensitive or emotionally difficult topics have been discussed, it may be helpful to finish the discussion on a more neutral or positive note"
- 要点：讨论过敏感或让人难受的话题后，最好用一个中性或积极的话题收尾。

### E15

- 位置：§5 Practical strategies，Debriefing
- 原文："The moderator can stay in the room for a while at the end of the focus group, or otherwise make him- or herself available, to give individual participants the opportunity to address any issues or concerns"
- 要点：结束后主持人可以多留一会儿，让个别参与者有机会单独说出顾虑。
