# Jacobs, Büthe 等 2021：The Qualitative Transparency Deliberations: Insights and Implications

- 资料：`jacobs2021qualitative`，[作者接受稿（White Rose 机构库）](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/158221/)
- 版本：作者接受稿（2020 年 2 月 SSRN 版总述文章），存于谢菲尔德大学等机构的 White Rose 开放库，标明可免费用于个人研究学习；正式版发表于 Perspectives on Politics 19(1): 171–208，2021。"定性研究透明度审议"（Qualitative Transparency Deliberations，QTD）由美国政治学会定性与多方法研究分会发起。位置写接受稿的小标题和页码。

### E1

- 位置：开头，第 5 页
- 原文："the recent push for overarching transparency norms and requirements in the discipline [...] has provoked serious concern among qualitative scholars."
- 要点：近年学科推动统一的透明规范和要求，在定性研究者中引起了严重担忧。

### E2

- 位置：开头，第 5 页
- 原文："A public letter signed by over 1100 political scientists in the fall of 2015 called for a delay to the implementation of the JETS"
- 要点：2015 年秋，1100 多位政治学者签名公开信，要求推迟实施期刊编辑透明声明。

### E3

- 位置：开头，第 5 页
- 原文："which, as of March 2019, has been signed by 27 political science journals."
- 要点：截至 2019 年 3 月，有 27 本政治学期刊签署了期刊编辑透明声明。

### E4

- 位置：开头，第 5 页
- 原文："qualitative research traditions appeared to many to be losing ground to quantitative methods on a number of fronts, including in the pages of leading journals."
- 要点：在不少人看来，定性研究传统在多个方面（包括顶级期刊版面）正在输给定量方法。

### E5

- 位置：开头，第 6 页
- 原文："Several hundred political scientists took part in the deliberations"
- 要点：数百位政治学者参与了这次审议。

### E6

- 位置：开头，第 7 页（主要发现）
- 原文："there exists no single "meta-standard" of research transparency that can operate coherently across all logics of qualitative inquiry;"
- 要点：不存在一个能适用于所有定性研究路数的统一透明标准。

### E7

- 位置：开头，第 7 页（主要发现）
- 原文："sharing some source materials is seen as an intellectually valuable practice within many qualitative research traditions; however, uniform and maximalist data-sharing requirements would be highly problematic for ethical, practical, and epistemological reasons;"
- 要点：共享部分原始材料在很多定性传统里被认为有价值；但统一的、最大化的数据共享要求，在伦理、实际和认识论上都有很大问题。

### E8

- 位置：开头，第 7 页（主要发现）
- 原文："researchers' ethical obligations to protect human participants and their communities ought to take priority over the sharing of information with research consumers."
- 要点：保护参与者及其社区的伦理义务，应优先于向读者共享信息。

### E9

- 位置：开头，第 7 页（广泛共识）
- 原文："the process through which the evidence used in a study was generated; [...] the analytic process through which the scholar arrived at conclusions; and [...] the risks faced by human participants in a study and the steps taken to protect them and their communities."
- 要点：各定性研究群体广泛同意，发表时要详细说明三件事：证据是怎样产生的；怎样分析得出结论；参与者面临什么风险、采取了哪些保护措施。

### E10

- 位置：Costs and Risks，第 17 页
- 原文："In communities under close government surveillance, phrases used, events referenced, or even the date and time of an interview may be sufficient to reveal interlocutors' identities."
- 要点：在受政府严密监控的社区，用词、提到的事件，甚至访谈日期时间，都可能暴露受访者身份。

### E11

- 位置：Costs and Risks，第 18 页
- 原文："What may seem like a low-risk disclosure today might become high risk in the future, as political and social conditions change."
- 要点：今天看似低风险的公开，可能随着政治和社会条件变化变成高风险。

### E12

- 位置：Costs and Risks，第 19 页
- 原文："those who do take part are less likely to provide candid responses if they know that full transcripts will be made publicly available."
- 要点：受访者如果知道访谈全文会被公开，就不太可能坦率回答。

### E13

- 位置：Costs and Risks，第 19 页
- 原文："data-sharing is not a binary, "all or nothing" choice. Scholars might be able to mitigate many of the associated costs, for instance, by providing access to a select set of documents or transcript passages that are especially informative about an empirical claim."
- 要点：数据共享不是"全有或全无"；可以只提供对某个论断最关键的几份文件或几段访谈摘录。

### E14

- 位置：Implications for Research Practice，第 24 页
- 原文："there appears to be a fundamental divide between qualitative researchers who see value in at least some logics and practices of transparency, on the one hand, and qualitative researchers who reject the very concept of transparency as incompatible with their understanding of knowledge production, on the other hand."
- 要点：定性研究者之间有根本分歧：一方认为透明至少部分有价值，另一方（主要是诠释学派）认为"透明"这个概念本身与他们对知识生产的理解不相容。

### E15

- 位置：Implications for Research Practice，第 24 页
- 原文："there was universal agreement that the sharing of qualitative data should not be uniformly required, given the considerable costs and risks of data-sharing for some forms of research."
- 要点：所有工作组一致认为，不应统一要求共享定性数据。

### E16

- 位置：Implications for Research Practice，第 25 页
- 原文："scholars should routinely provide sufficient information about the location of publicly available sources to ensure that others can find them, and should specify the particular parts of any source that are being drawn upon (e.g., by including page numbers)."
- 要点：引用公开文献档案时，要给出足够的位置信息让别人能找到，并具体到所用部分（如页码）。

### E17

- 位置：Implications for Research Practice，第 25 页
- 原文："when quoting from a response to an interview question, sharing the complete response in order to provide wider context" / "where transcripts cannot be shared, reporting the number of interviews consistent and inconsistent with a proposed hypothesis;"
- 要点：引用访谈回答时，给出这个问题的完整回答以提供上下文；不能共享访谈全文时，报告有多少次访谈与假设相符、多少次不符。

### E18

- 位置：Implications for Research Practice，第 25 页
- 原文："the use of Annotation for Transparent Inquiry, a technology developed by the Qualitative Data Repository at Syracuse University, that allows researchers to layer a citation, analytical note, source excerpt, and possible link to a source over the relevant passage in the article text."
- 要点：可以用雪城大学定性数据仓库开发的"透明探究注释"技术，在论文相关段落上叠加引文、分析说明、原文摘录和原始资料链接。

### E19

- 位置：Implications for Research Practice，第 25 页
- 原文："there is a clear consensus on the vital importance of providing readers with detailed accounts of how the evidence used in a study was generated."
- 要点：各定性研究传统明确一致：要向读者详细说明研究所用证据是怎样产生的。

### E20

- 位置：Implications for Research Practice，第 26 页
- 原文："providing information not just about the production of evidence that they themselves generated but also about the origins of sources that pre-date the study, such as textual materials, making explicit the scholar's critical use of his/her sources;"
- 要点：不仅说明自己产生的证据，也说明研究之前就存在的资料（如文本材料）的来历，讲明自己怎样批判地使用这些资料。

### E21

- 位置：Implications for Research Practice，第 26 页
- 原文："providing an interview table containing key metadata for all interviews conducted;"
- 要点：提供一张访谈表，列出所有访谈的关键信息。

### E22

- 位置：Implications for Research Practice，第 26 页
- 原文："specifying, for small-n analysis, what was known about the cases at the time of their selection and/or identify those cases that were almost chosen for analysis but ultimately not included;"
- 要点：少数案例的分析，要说明选案例时已经知道案例的哪些情况，和/或指出差点入选、最后没选的案例。

### E23

- 位置：Implications for Research Practice，第 27 页
- 原文："noting the steps taken to challenge one's own premises or early hunches in the course of a project;" / "reporting when initial hypotheses were dropped or modified — or when new hypotheses were developed — in light of the evidence;" / "explicitness about whether an analysis aims for generalization beyond the cases being examined;"
- 要点：分析过程的透明包括：说明自己采取了哪些步骤挑战最初的前提和直觉；报告哪些假设因证据被放弃、修改或新提出；说清分析是否要推广到所研究的案例之外。

### E24

- 位置：Potential Benefits of Transparency，第 15 页
- 原文："formalizing certain aspects of qualitative analysis — such as background beliefs and the probative value of evidence — can make it even clearer how conclusions have been derived from an array of observations and make it easier for readers to evaluate empirical findings."
- 要点：研究过程追踪的工作组指出，把背景信念、证据证明力等环节形式化，能更清楚地展示结论怎样得出，便于读者评价。

### E25

- 位置：Costs and Risks，第 20 页
- 原文："Excessively long and complex transparency appendices, for instance, may obscure the most important features for readers to attend to. Similarly, methods featuring extremely high levels of analytic explicitness — such as formal, Bayesian process tracing — may generate less readable and comprehensible text than do more informal, narrative approaches."
- 要点：过长过繁的透明附录可能遮住最重要的内容；形式化的贝叶斯过程追踪这类极度明确的方法，写出来可能不如叙事方式好读。
