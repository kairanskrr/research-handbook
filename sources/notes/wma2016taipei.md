# WMA 2016：Declaration of Taipei on Ethical Considerations regarding Health Databases and Biobanks

- 资料：`wma2016taipei`，[原文](https://www.wma.net/policies-post/wma-declaration-of-taipei-on-ethical-considerations-regarding-health-databases-and-biobanks/)
- 版本：世界医学会（World Medical Association，WMA）2002 年通过，2016 年 10 月第 67 届大会（台北）修订。位置写段落号。

### E1

- 位置：第 3 段
- 原文："This Declaration is intended to cover the collection, storage and use of identifiable data and biological material beyond the individual care of patients."
- 要点：宣言管的是超出个人诊疗之外、对可识别的数据和生物材料的收集、保存和使用。

### E2

- 位置：第 4 段
- 原文："A Biobank is a collection of biological material and associated data."
- 要点：生物样本库是生物材料及其相关数据的集合。

### E3

- 位置：第 12 段
- 原文："If the data or biological material are collected and stored in a Health Database or a Biobank for multiple and indefinite uses, consent is only valid if the concerned individuals have been adequately informed about:"
- 要点：样本存入生物样本库用于多种、不确定的未来用途时，只有把目的、风险、获取规则、隐私保护、治理安排等讲清楚，同意才有效。

### E4

- 位置：第 12 段
- 原文："That in case the data and material are made non-identifiable the individual may not be able to know what is done with their data/material and that they will not have the option of withdrawing their consent;"
- 要点：要告诉对方：样本一旦去标识，本人可能无法知道它被怎样使用，也无法再撤回同意。

### E5

- 位置：第 15 段
- 原文："Individuals have the right, at any time and without reprisal, to alter their consent or to ask for their identifiable data to be withdrawn from the Health Database and their biological material to be withdrawn from a Biobank."
- 要点：个人可以随时更改同意，或要求撤出自己可识别的数据和生物材料，不受任何不利后果。

### E6

- 位置：第 19 段
- 原文："In addition the ethics committee must approve use of data and biological material and check whether the consent given at the time of collection is sufficient for the planned use or if other measures have to be taken to protect the donor."
- 要点：伦理委员会要批准数据和生物材料的使用，并检查采集时的同意是否覆盖计划中的用途。

### E7

- 位置：第 6 段
- 原文："Physicians must consider the ethical, legal and regulatory norms and standards for Health Database and Biobanks in their own countries as well as applicable international norms and standards."
- 要点：要同时考虑本国的伦理、法律和监管规定以及适用的国际规范。

### E8

- 位置：第 21 段
- 原文："Criteria and procedures concerning the access to and the sharing of the health data or biological material including the systematic use of Material Transfer Agreement (MTA) when necessary;"
- 要点：治理安排要规定数据和生物材料的获取与共享规则，必要时系统地使用材料转移协议。

### E9

- 位置：第 7 段
- 原文："Consistent with the mandate of WMA, the Declaration is addressed primarily to physicians. The WMA encourages others who are involved in using data or biological material in Health Databases and Biobanks to adopt these principles."
- 要点：宣言主要写给医生，但鼓励所有使用健康数据库和生物样本库的人遵守。
