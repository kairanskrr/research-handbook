# Zhang 2014：Ten Simple Rules for Writing Research Papers

- 资料：`zhang2014ten`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003453)
- 版本：PLOS Computational Biology 10(1): e1003453，开放获取。页码为 PDF 页码。

### E1

- 位置：Rule 1，第 1 页
- 原文："Never separate writing a paper from the underlying research."
- 要点：写论文和做研究不能分开；设计项目时就带上论文提纲。

### E2

- 位置：Rule 1，第 1 页
- 原文："use writing as a tool to reassess the overall project, reevaluate the logic of the experiments, and examine the validity of the results during the research."
- 要点：在研究过程中用写作来重新审视项目、实验逻辑和结果的有效性。

### E3

- 位置：Rule 2，第 1 页
- 原文："By this metric, less is more"
- 要点：以论文对领域的潜在影响为准绳，少而重要的论文比多而不重要的论文更好。

### E4

- 位置：Rule 8，第 2 页
- 原文："do not exaggerate or belittle the significance of the results and the elegance of the methods developed."
- 要点：修改时对自己的工作客观诚实，既不夸大也不贬低。

### E5

- 位置：Rule 8，第 2 页
- 原文："put yourself completely in the shoes of a referee and scrutinize all the pieces"
- 要点：修改时完全站在审稿人的角度检查：意义、逻辑、结果和结论是否正确、组织和呈现。可以把稿子放一两天再回头读，当作别人写的来挑毛病。

### E6

- 位置：Rule 9，第 2 页
- 原文："It is wise to anticipate the possible questions and critiques the referees may raise and preemptively address their concerns before submission."
- 要点：投稿前先在身边"试水"：请同事、合作者提意见，在组会或系里报告，预先解决审稿人可能提的问题。

### E7

- 位置：Rule 3，第 1 页
- 原文："This issue translates to choosing a target audience, as well as an appropriate journal, to cast the main messages to."
- 要点：动笔前先选定读者对象（以及相应的期刊），它决定论文的组织方式和细节程度。

### E8

- 位置：Rule 1: Make It a Driving Force
- 原文："Never separate writing a paper from the underlying research. [...] Therefore, design a project with an ultimate paper firmly in mind. Include an outline of the paper in the initial project design documents to help form the research objectives, determine the logical flow of the experiments, and organize the materials and data to be used."
- 要点：设计项目时就心里装着最终的论文；在项目最初的设计文档里放一份论文提纲，用它来确定研究目标、实验的逻辑顺序，以及要准备的材料和数据。

### E9

- 位置：Rule 1: Make It a Driving Force
- 原文："Furthermore, use writing as a tool to reassess the overall project, reevaluate the logic of the experiments, and examine the validity of the results during the research. As a result, the overall research may need to be adjusted, the project design may be revised, new methods may be devised, and new data may be collected."
- 要点：研究过程中用写作重新审视整个项目；结果可能是调整研究、修改项目设计、设计新方法或收集新数据。计划要跟着改。

### E10

- 位置：Rule 4: Be Logical
- 原文："An effective tactic to help develop a sound logical flow is to imaginatively create a set of figures and tables, which will ultimately be developed from experimental results, and order them in a logical way based on the information flow through the experiments."
- 要点：先想象论文里会有哪些图表，按实验之间的信息流排好顺序，用来理清逻辑。

### E11

- 位置：Rule 4: Be Logical
- 原文："In addition, these figures and tables, as well as the threading logical flow, may be used to direct or organize research activities, reinforcing Rule 1."
- 要点：这些设想中的图表和贯穿它们的逻辑，可以直接用来指挥和组织研究工作。

### E12

- 位置：Rule 5: Be Thorough and Make It Complete
- 原文："Completeness is a cornerstone for a research paper [...] First, important and relevant aspects of a hypothesis pursued in the research should be discussed with detailed supporting data."
- 要点：完整性是论文的基石：所研究假设的重要方面，都要有详细的支撑数据。这可以作为判断"做完了没有"的依据。
