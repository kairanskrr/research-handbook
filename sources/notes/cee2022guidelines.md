# 环境证据协作组 2022：Guidelines and Standards for Evidence Synthesis in Environmental Management

- 资料：`cee2022guidelines`，[原文](https://environmentalevidence.org/information-for-authors/)
- 版本：Collaboration for Environmental Evidence（环境证据协作组），Version 5.1，2022（编者 Pullin、Frampton、Livoreil、Petrokofsky）。官网有访问验证，原文经互联网档案馆（Internet Archive）2026 年 5 月的快照读取：Section 1（页面注 "Last updated: July 3rd 2023"）、Section 9（页面注 "Last updated: September 25th 2022"）。位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§1.1 The CEE registration, submission and deposition process
- 原文："Please note that CEE does not accept manuscripts of unregistered Evidence Syntheses (i.e. those without a previously registered and/or published protocol) nor does it accept retrospective protocols or registration of already completed Evidence Syntheses."
- 要点：CEE 不接受没有事先注册或发表研究方案的证据综合，也不接受事后补写的方案或已经做完的综述再来注册。

### E2

- 位置：§9.2 Reporting conduct of evidence syntheses
- 原文："CEE standards require a high level of reporting of the conduct of evidence syntheses so as to ensure high transparency and repeatability allowing others to test replicability of findings." / "CEE now recommends using the RepOrting standards for Systematic Evidence Syntheses (ROSES) checklist (Haddaway et al. in press) as this will be used by editors and peer reviewers when appraising reports."
- 要点：CEE 要求把综述的做法报告得很详细，透明、可重复，让别人能检验结果；推荐用 ROSES 报告清单，编辑和审稿人评审时也用它。

### E3

- 位置：§9.1 The interpretation of evidence syntheses
- 原文："Where reviews are inconclusive because there is insufficient evidence, it is important not to confuse 'no evidence of an effect' with 'evidence of no effect'."
- 要点：证据不足、综述没有定论时，不要把"没有证据表明有效应"混同为"有证据表明没有效应"。

### E4

- 位置：§9.1.1 Limitations of an evidence synthesis
- 原文："Publication bias : statistically significant results are more prone to be published than non significant ones."
- 要点：发表偏倚：统计显著的结果比不显著的更容易发表；这类偏倚应在综述的局限里考虑和报告。

### E5

- 位置：§9.3 Reporting findings of evidence syntheses
- 原文："Systematic Reviews (not SMs) assess the strength of a causal inference (Hill 1971). Aspects that may be reported in the conclusion section include: 1. The quality/reliability of the included studies. 2. The relevance/external validity of the included studies. 3. The size and statistical significance of the observed effects. 4. The consistency of the effects across studies or sites"
- 要点：系统综述（不包括系统图谱）评估因果推断的强弱；结论里可报告：纳入研究的质量和可靠性、相关性和外部效度、效应的大小和统计显著性、效应在不同研究或地点之间是否一致等。

### E6

- 位置：§9.3.1 Implications for policy and practice
- 原文："Authors should therefore highlight where the evidence is likely to be applicable and equally importantly where it may not be applicable with reference to variation between studies and study characteristics"
- 要点：作者应指出证据可能在哪里适用，同样重要的是指出它可能在哪里不适用。
