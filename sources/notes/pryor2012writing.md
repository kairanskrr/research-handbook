# Pryor：Guidelines on Writing a Philosophy Paper

- 资料：`pryor2012writing`，[原文](https://www.jimpryor.net/teaching/guidelines/writing.html)
- 版本：Jim Pryor（美国纽约大学哲学教师）个人主页上的写作指南，页面标注最后更新于 2012 年 9 月 6 日。写给上本科哲学课的学生（5–6 页的课程论文）。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："A philosophy paper consists of the reasoned defense of some claim"
- 要点：哲学论文就是为某个主张做有理由的辩护。

### E2

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："Your paper must offer an argument. It can't consist in the mere report of your opinions, nor in a mere report of the opinions of the philosophers we discuss."
- 要点：论文必须给出论证；只报告自己的看法、或只复述哲学家的看法都不够。

### E3

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："It usually begins by putting some thesis or argument on the table for consideration. Then it goes on to do one or two of the following:"
- 要点：哲学论文通常先摆出一个论题或论证，再做一两件事（见 E4）。

### E4

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："Criticize that argument; or show that certain arguments for the thesis are no good"
- 要点：可做的事之一：批评这个论证，或说明支持论题的某些论证不成立。同一列表还包括：反驳别人对它的批评、给出支持理由、给出反例、比较两种对立观点、讨论论题的推论、根据反驳修正论题等。

### E5

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："A good philosophy paper is modest and makes a small point; but it makes that point clearly and straightforwardly, and it offers good reasons in support of it"
- 要点：好的哲学论文是谦虚的，只讲一个小观点，但讲得清楚直接，并给出好的理由。

### E6

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："People very often attempt to accomplish too much in a philosophy paper. The usual result of this is a paper that's hard to read, and which is full of inadequately defended and poorly explained claims."
- 要点：人们常想在一篇论文里做太多，结果论文难读，满是没辩护好、没解释清的主张。

### E7

- 位置：Early Stages / Make an outline
- 原文："I find that making an outline is at least 80% of the work of writing a good philosophy paper."
- 要点：Pryor 认为写提纲至少占写好一篇哲学论文 80% 的工作。

### E8

- 位置：Write a Draft / Use simple prose
- 原文："Don't shoot for literary elegance. Use simple, straightforward prose. Keep your sentences and paragraphs short."
- 要点：不要追求文采；用简单直接的文字，句子和段落要短。

### E9

- 位置：Make the structure of your paper obvious
- 原文："If you say "P. Thus Q." then you are claiming that P is a good reason to accept Q. You had better be right."
- 要点：写"P，所以 Q"就是在声称 P 是接受 Q 的好理由；连接词要用对。

### E10

- 位置：Use plenty of examples and definitions
- 原文："You should always make it clear how you understand these notions, even if they are familiar from everyday discourse."
- 要点：论证里的核心概念，即使是日常用语，也要说清你怎么理解它。

### E11

- 位置：Don't vary your vocabulary just for the sake of variety
- 原文："If you call something "X" at the start of your paper, call it "X" all the way through."
- 要点：同一个东西从头到尾用同一个名字，不要为了变化换词。

### E12

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："Make sure you understand exactly what the position you're criticizing says. Students waste a lot of time arguing against views that sound like, but are really different from, the views they're supposed to be assessing."
- 要点：先弄准你要批评的立场到底说什么；学生常在攻击一个听起来相似、其实不同的观点。

### E13

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："But don't treat the philosopher or the views you're discussing as stupid. If they were stupid, we wouldn't be looking at them."
- 要点：不要把你讨论的哲学家或观点当成愚蠢的。

### E14

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："Try to figure out what reasonable position the philosopher could have had in mind, and direct your arguments against that."
- 要点：设想这位哲学家可能持有的合理立场，再针对它展开论证。

### E15

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："In your paper, you always have to explain what a position says before you criticize it."
- 要点：批评一个立场之前，必须先解释它说了什么。

### E16

- 位置：Anticipate objections
- 原文："Don't be afraid of mentioning objections to your own thesis. It is better to bring up an objection yourself than to hope your reader won't think of it."
- 要点：不要怕提出对自己论题的反驳；自己提出来，比指望读者想不到要好。

### E17

- 位置：Anticipate objections
- 原文："Of course, there's often no way to deal with all the objections someone might raise; so concentrate on the ones that seem strongest or most pressing."
- 要点：不可能回应所有反驳，集中回应最强、最紧要的那些。

### E18

- 位置：What happens if you're stuck?
- 原文："Your paper doesn't always have to provide a definite solution to a problem, or a straight yes or no answer to a question."
- 要点：论文不一定要给出明确的解决方案或简单的是/否回答。

### E19

- 位置：What happens if you're stuck?
- 原文："It's okay to change your thesis to one you can defend."
- 要点：发现论证有修不好的问题时，可以把论题改成你能辩护的那个。

### E20

- 位置：Rewrite, and Keep Rewriting
- 原文："You should count on writing many drafts of your paper. At least 3 or 4!!"
- 要点：要准备写很多稿，至少三四稿。

### E21

- 位置：Minor Points / Beginning your paper
- 原文："Don't begin with a sentence like "Down through the ages, mankind has pondered the problem of..." There's no need to warm up to your topic."
- 要点：开头不要用"自古以来人类就在思考……"这类句子热身，第一句就切入正题。

### E22

- 位置：Minor Points / Beginning your paper
- 原文："Dictionaries aren't good philosophical authorities."
- 要点：词典不是好的哲学权威，不要用"词典定义"开头。

### E23

- 位置：How You'll Be Graded
- 原文："We do not judge your paper by whether we agree with its conclusion."
- 要点：评判论文不看是否同意它的结论（而看论证得好不好）。

### E24

- 位置：Minor Points / Secondary readings
- 原文："You shouldn't need to use these secondary readings when writing your papers."
- 要点：在 Pryor 的本科课程里，写课程论文不需要用课外的二手文献。（这一点只适用于课程论文。）

### E25

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："Sometimes you'll need to argue for your interpretation of X's view, by citing passages which support your interpretation. It is permissible for you to discuss a view you think a philosopher might have held, or should have held, though you can't find any direct evidence of that view in the text. When you do this, though, you should explicitly say so."
- 要点：有时要引用原文段落来论证你对某位哲学家观点的解读；可以讨论他"可能持有"或"应当持有"、但文本中找不到直接证据的观点，但必须明说。
