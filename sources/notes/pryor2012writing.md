# Pryor：Guidelines on Writing a Philosophy Paper

- 资料：`pryor2012writing`，[原文](https://www.jimpryor.net/teaching/guidelines/writing.html)
- 版本：Jim Pryor（美国纽约大学哲学教师）个人主页上的写作指南，页面标注最后更新于 2012 年 9 月 6 日。写给上本科哲学课的学生（5–6 页的课程论文）。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："A philosophy paper consists of the reasoned defense of some claim"
- 要点：哲学论文就是为某个主张做有理由的辩护。

### E2

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："Your paper must offer an argument. It can't consist in the mere report of your opinions, nor in a mere report of the opinions of the philosophers we discuss."
- 要点：论文必须给出论证；只报告自己的看法、或只复述哲学家的看法都不够。

### E3

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："It usually begins by putting some thesis or argument on the table for consideration. Then it goes on to do one or two of the following:"
- 要点：哲学论文通常先摆出一个论题或论证，再做一两件事（见 E4）。

### E4

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："Criticize that argument; or show that certain arguments for the thesis are no good"
- 要点：可做的事之一：批评这个论证，或说明支持论题的某些论证不成立。同一列表还包括：反驳别人对它的批评、给出支持理由、给出反例、比较两种对立观点、讨论论题的推论、根据反驳修正论题等。

### E5

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："A good philosophy paper is modest and makes a small point; but it makes that point clearly and straightforwardly, and it offers good reasons in support of it"
- 要点：好的哲学论文是谦虚的，只讲一个小观点，但讲得清楚直接，并给出好的理由。

### E6

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："People very often attempt to accomplish too much in a philosophy paper. The usual result of this is a paper that's hard to read, and which is full of inadequately defended and poorly explained claims."
- 要点：人们常想在一篇论文里做太多，结果论文难读，满是没辩护好、没解释清的主张。

### E7

- 位置：Early Stages / Make an outline
- 原文："I find that making an outline is at least 80% of the work of writing a good philosophy paper."
- 要点：Pryor 认为写提纲至少占写好一篇哲学论文 80% 的工作。

### E8

- 位置：Write a Draft / Use simple prose
- 原文："Don't shoot for literary elegance. Use simple, straightforward prose. Keep your sentences and paragraphs short."
- 要点：不要追求文采；用简单直接的文字，句子和段落要短。

### E9

- 位置：Make the structure of your paper obvious
- 原文："If you say "P. Thus Q." then you are claiming that P is a good reason to accept Q. You had better be right."
- 要点：写"P，所以 Q"就是在声称 P 是接受 Q 的好理由；连接词要用对。

### E10

- 位置：Use plenty of examples and definitions
- 原文："You should always make it clear how you understand these notions, even if they are familiar from everyday discourse."
- 要点：论证里的核心概念，即使是日常用语，也要说清你怎么理解它。

### E11

- 位置：Don't vary your vocabulary just for the sake of variety
- 原文："If you call something "X" at the start of your paper, call it "X" all the way through."
- 要点：同一个东西从头到尾用同一个名字，不要为了变化换词。

### E12

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："Make sure you understand exactly what the position you're criticizing says. Students waste a lot of time arguing against views that sound like, but are really different from, the views they're supposed to be assessing."
- 要点：先弄准你要批评的立场到底说什么；学生常在攻击一个听起来相似、其实不同的观点。

### E13

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："But don't treat the philosopher or the views you're discussing as stupid. If they were stupid, we wouldn't be looking at them."
- 要点：不要把你讨论的哲学家或观点当成愚蠢的。

### E14

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："Try to figure out what reasonable position the philosopher could have had in mind, and direct your arguments against that."
- 要点：设想这位哲学家可能持有的合理立场，再针对它展开论证。

### E15

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："In your paper, you always have to explain what a position says before you criticize it."
- 要点：批评一个立场之前，必须先解释它说了什么。

### E16

- 位置：Anticipate objections
- 原文："Don't be afraid of mentioning objections to your own thesis. It is better to bring up an objection yourself than to hope your reader won't think of it."
- 要点：不要怕提出对自己论题的反驳；自己提出来，比指望读者想不到要好。

### E17

- 位置：Anticipate objections
- 原文："Of course, there's often no way to deal with all the objections someone might raise; so concentrate on the ones that seem strongest or most pressing."
- 要点：不可能回应所有反驳，集中回应最强、最紧要的那些。

### E18

- 位置：What happens if you're stuck?
- 原文："Your paper doesn't always have to provide a definite solution to a problem, or a straight yes or no answer to a question."
- 要点：论文不一定要给出明确的解决方案或简单的是/否回答。

### E19

- 位置：What happens if you're stuck?
- 原文："It's okay to change your thesis to one you can defend."
- 要点：发现论证有修不好的问题时，可以把论题改成你能辩护的那个。

### E20

- 位置：Rewrite, and Keep Rewriting
- 原文："You should count on writing many drafts of your paper. At least 3 or 4!!"
- 要点：要准备写很多稿，至少三四稿。

### E21

- 位置：Minor Points / Beginning your paper
- 原文："Don't begin with a sentence like "Down through the ages, mankind has pondered the problem of..." There's no need to warm up to your topic."
- 要点：开头不要用"自古以来人类就在思考……"这类句子热身，第一句就切入正题。

### E22

- 位置：Minor Points / Beginning your paper
- 原文："Dictionaries aren't good philosophical authorities."
- 要点：词典不是好的哲学权威，不要用"词典定义"开头。

### E23

- 位置：How You'll Be Graded
- 原文："We do not judge your paper by whether we agree with its conclusion."
- 要点：评判论文不看是否同意它的结论（而看论证得好不好）。

### E24

- 位置：Minor Points / Secondary readings
- 原文："You shouldn't need to use these secondary readings when writing your papers."
- 要点：在 Pryor 的本科课程里，写课程论文不需要用课外的二手文献。（这一点只适用于课程论文。）

### E25

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："Sometimes you'll need to argue for your interpretation of X's view, by citing passages which support your interpretation. It is permissible for you to discuss a view you think a philosopher might have held, or should have held, though you can't find any direct evidence of that view in the text. When you do this, though, you should explicitly say so."
- 要点：有时要引用原文段落来论证你对某位哲学家观点的解读；可以讨论他"可能持有"或"应当持有"、但文本中找不到直接证据的观点，但必须明说。

### E26

- 位置：What Does One Do in a Philosophy Paper?
- 原文："You should assume that your audience does not already accept your position; and you should treat your paper as an attempt to persuade such an audience. Hence, don't start with assumptions which your opponents are sure to reject."
- 要点：假定读者还不接受你的立场，把论文当作说服这样的读者；所以不要从对手肯定拒绝的假设出发。

### E27

- 位置：What happens if you're stuck?
- 原文："Many excellent philosophy papers don't offer straight yes or no answers. Sometimes they argue that the question needs to be clarified, or that certain further questions need to be raised. Sometimes they argue that certain assumptions of the question need to be challenged."
- 要点：很多优秀论文不给"是/否"的答案，而是论证问题需要澄清、需要提出进一步的问题，或问题的某些假设需要挑战。

### E28

- 位置：What happens if you're stuck?
- 原文："Sometimes they argue that certain answers to the question are too easy, that is, they won't work."
- 要点：也可以论证某些答案太轻巧、行不通。

### E29

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："Then ask yourself: Are X's arguments good ones? Are his assumptions clearly stated? Are they plausible? Are they reasonable starting-points for X's argument, or ought he have provided some independent argument for them?"
- 要点：评估别人的观点时问：论证好不好？假设说清了吗？可信吗？是合理的出发点，还是本该另外论证？

### E30

- 位置：Be concise, but explain yourself fully
- 原文："Formulate the central problem or question you wish to address at the beginning of your paper, and keep it in mind at all times. Make it clear what the problem is, and why it is a problem."
- 要点：在论文开头写出要处理的核心问题，始终记着它；说清问题是什么、为什么是个问题。

### E31

- 位置：Make an outline
- 原文："It also helps ensure that you're in a position to say what your main argument or criticism is, before you sit down to write a full draft of your paper. When students get stuck writing, it's often because they haven't yet figured out what they're trying to say."
- 要点：提纲确保你动笔前能说出主论证或主批评；写不下去常常是因为还没想清楚要说什么。

### E32

- 位置：Quotations
- 原文："If the quoted passage contains an argument, reconstruct the argument in more explicit, straightforward terms."
- 要点：引文里有论证，就用更明确直接的话把论证重构出来。

### E33

- 位置：Anticipate objections
- 原文："For instance, if you object to some philosopher's view, don't assume he would immediately admit defeat. Imagine what his comeback might be. How would you handle that comeback?"
- 要点：反对某位哲学家时，别以为对方会马上认输；设想他会怎样回击，你又怎样应对。

### E34

- 位置：Your paper should do some philosophical work
- 原文："Philosopher X assumes A and argues from there to B. B seems unattractive to me. Philosopher X just assumes A and doesn't give any argument for it. I don't think A is true. So I can just reject A and thereby avoid B."
- 要点：常见的弱论证模式：X 假定 A 推出 B；我不喜欢 B，X 没论证 A，我不认为 A 为真，所以拒绝 A。

### E35

- 位置：Your paper should do some philosophical work
- 原文："She could have argued that B doesn't really follow from A, after all. Or she could have presented reasons for thinking that A is false. Or she could have argued that assuming A is an illegitimate move to make in a debate about whether B is true."
- 要点：更有意思的做法：论证 B 其实推不出来；给出 A 为假的理由；或论证在关于 B 的争论里假定 A 是不正当的。

### E36

- 位置：Discuss the issues with others
- 原文："It's even more valuable to talk to each other about what you want to argue in your paper. When you have your ideas worked out well enough that you can explain them to someone else, verbally, then you're ready to sit down and start making an outline."
- 要点：和别人讨论你要论证什么；能口头讲给别人听了，才可以开始写提纲。

### E37

- 位置：Rewrite, and Keep Rewriting
- 原文："Reading the paper out loud can help you notice holes in your reasoning, digressions, and unclear prose."
- 要点：把论文读出声，能帮你发现推理漏洞、跑题和不清楚的文字。

### E38

- 位置：Rewrite, and Keep Rewriting
- 原文："When you're revising a draft, it's much more important to work on the draft's structure and overall clarity, than it is to clean up a word or a phrase here or there."
- 要点：修改时，结构和整体清晰度比零星改词重要得多。

### E39

- 位置：Be concise, but explain yourself fully
- 原文："And he's mean, so he's not going to read your paper charitably. (For example, if something you say admits of more than one interpretation, he's going to assume you meant the less plausible thing.)"
- 要点：假想读者"刻薄"：一句话有多种理解，他就按不太合理的那种理解。

### E40

- 位置：Make the structure of your paper obvious
- 原文："make it explicit when you're reporting your own view and when you're reporting the views of some philosopher you're discussing. The reader should never be in doubt about whose claims you're presenting in a given paragraph."
- 要点：明确哪里是自己的观点、哪里是转述别人；读者任何时候都不该搞不清某段是谁的主张。

### E41

- 位置：What happens if you're stuck?
- 原文："It's perfectly okay to say that their strengths and weaknesses seem to be roughly equally balanced. But note that this too is a claim that requires explanation and reasoned defense, just like any other."
- 要点：可以说两种立场势均力敌，但这本身也是需要论证的主张。

### E42

- 位置：Rewrite, and Keep Rewriting
- 原文："Make sure every sentence in your draft does useful work. Get rid of any which don't."
- 要点：每句话都要有用，没用的删掉。

### E43

- 位置：How You'll Be Graded
- 原文："Is the structure of your paper clear? For instance, is it clear what parts of your paper are expository, and what parts are your own positive contribution?"
- 要点：结构要清楚：哪些部分是介绍，哪些是你自己的正面贡献。

### E44

- 位置：Use plenty of examples and definitions
- 原文："So everything turns here on what the author means by "person." The author should be explicit about how he is using this notion."
- 要点：（"胎儿是人；杀人是错的；所以杀胎儿是错的"一例）一切取决于"人"指什么；作者要明说自己怎样用这个概念。

### E45

- 位置：What happens if you're stuck?
- 原文："If there's some problem with your argument which you can't fix, try to figure out why you can't fix it. It's okay to change your thesis to one you can defend."
- 要点：论证有修不好的问题，先想清楚为什么修不好；可以把论题改成你能辩护的。

### E46

- 位置：Presenting and assessing the views of others
- 原文："Use your imagination. Try to figure out what reasonable position the philosopher could have had in mind, and direct your arguments against that."
- 要点：设想对方可能持有的合理立场，针对它来论证。

### E47

- 位置：Make the structure of your paper obvious
- 原文："Don't throw in a "thus" or a "therefore" to make your train of thought sound better-argued than it really is."
- 要点：不要随手加"所以""因此"，让思路显得比实际更有论证。

### E48

- 位置：Rewrite, and Keep Rewriting
- 原文："If you can, show your draft to your friends or to other students in the class, and get their comments and advice."
- 要点：可以的话，把草稿给朋友或同学看，听取意见。

### E49

- 位置：What happens if you're stuck?
- 原文："If something in a view you're examining is unclear to you, don't gloss it over. Call attention to the unclarity. Suggest several different ways of understanding the view."
- 要点：所考察的观点有不清楚之处，不要含糊带过；指出来，给出几种可能的理解。
