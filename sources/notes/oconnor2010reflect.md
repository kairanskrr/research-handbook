# O'Connor 等人 2010：The REFLECT statement: Methods and processes of creating reporting guidelines for randomized controlled trials for livestock and food safety

- 资料：`oconnor2010reflect`，[原文 PDF](https://www.aasv.org/shap/issues/v18n1/v18n1p18.pdf)
- 版本：Journal of Swine Health and Production 18(1):18–26，2010 年。美国猪兽医协会（AASV）官网免费提供全文。同一声明同时发表在另外四本期刊。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："The conduct of randomized controlled trials in livestock with production, health, and food-safety outcomes presents unique challenges that may not be adequately reported in trial reports."
- 要点：以生产、健康和食品安全为结局的家畜随机对照试验有独特的难点，这些难点在试验报告里可能写得不够。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："The consensus meeting resulted in the production of the REFLECT (Reporting Guidelines for Randomized Controlled Trials) statement for livestock and food safety (LFS) and 22-item checklist. Fourteen items were modified from the CONSORT checklist, and an additional sub-item was proposed to address challenge trials."
- 要点：共识会议产出了面向家畜和食品安全的 REFLECT 声明和 22 项清单；其中 14 项改自人体试验的 CONSORT 清单，另加一个子项专门针对攻毒试验。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction（正文第一部分）
- 原文："The differences include two types of "participants" (the animals' owners-managers who consent to participation in a trial, and the animals who are the actual study subjects), the common use of clustered study designs, the use of a deliberate challenge to animals with infectious agents in some trials (aka challenge trials), and non-clinical outcomes (eg, production indices). These differences make the direct use of the CONSORT statement challenging."
- 要点：家畜试验和人体试验的不同：有两类"参与者"（同意参加的动物主人或管理者，和真正被研究的动物）；常用整群设计；有的试验故意让动物感染病原（攻毒试验）；结局常是非临床指标（如生产指标）。这些不同使 CONSORT 难以直接套用。

### E4

- 位置：Results
- 原文："There is a need for clear identification of the unit of allocation of the intervention and the unit of assessment and inference. Interventions can be allocated at any level of the organizational structure and the outcome assessed at the same or lower level."
- 要点：要写清干预分配到哪个单位、在哪个单位测量和推断。干预可以分配到组织结构的任何一层，结局在同一层或更低一层测量。

### E5

- 位置：Results
- 原文："In livestock trials, non-independence of observations can arise because animals are often housed and managed in groups. Animals housed together have something more in common than animals housed separately, as they share the same microclimate, ration, health-management procedures, etc."
- 要点：家畜常成群饲养和管理，同栏动物共享小气候、日粮、保健管理等，彼此更相似，所以观测值不独立。

### E6

- 位置：Results
- 原文："Failure to properly account for non-independence of the data in the statistical analysis results in a violation of the association of independence that underlies many statistical procedures."
- 要点：统计分析不处理数据的不独立性，就违反了很多统计方法所依赖的独立性假设。

### E7

- 位置：Results
- 原文："For example, swine may be studied within pens, within barns, within sites, and within production companies."
- 要点：举例：猪可以按栏、圈舍、场点、生产公司一层层组织。

### E8

- 位置：Results
- 原文："Livestock trials with production, health, and food-safety outcomes are frequently conducted in research settings in which experimental challenge of trial animals (often with pathogenic organisms) is under the control of the researcher."
- 要点：这类家畜试验常在研究设施里进行，由研究者控制对试验动物的人工攻毒（多用病原体）。

### E9

- 位置：Results
- 原文："For example, a study may allocate udder halves to receive the treatment, therefore the allocation unit is the udder half; however, the outcome may be measured on the individual teat, ie, the outcome unit."
- 要点：例子：处理分配到半侧乳房（分配单位），结局却在单个乳头上测量（结局单位）。

### E10

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："As an example of the impact of animal housing, a feedlot veterinarian may expect a different outcome from a vaccine allocated to individual animals, compared to group-level application. Similarly, a challenge study that used 100 times the normal dose of Salmonella to induce Salmonella shedding may have questionable external validity."
- 要点：疫苗按单头动物分配和按群施用，结果可能不同；用正常剂量 100 倍的沙门氏菌攻毒，结论能否推广到现实就值得怀疑。

### E11

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："Although the REFLECT statement directly applies to reporting of studies, it may also be consulted and useful in the design and conduct stages of a trial."
- 要点：REFLECT 直接用于报告，但在设计和实施阶段也可以拿来参考。

### E12

- 位置：Introduction（正文第一部分）
- 原文："The issue of inferior quality of veterinary RCT reports was first raised in editorials and commentaries in veterinary journals in the early to mid-1990's." / "Recently, several systematic reviews of therapeutic, preventive, and food-safety trials in livestock species have highlighted the need for better reporting."
- 要点：兽医随机试验报告质量差的问题，1990 年代早中期就在兽医期刊的社论中提出；后来几篇针对家畜治疗、预防和食品安全试验的系统综述也指出报告需要改进。

### E13

- 位置：Table 3（定义）
- 原文："The owner-manager of the study facility who consented to participate in the trial."
- 要点：REFLECT 里"参与者"（participant）专指同意参加试验的饲养设施主人或管理者。

### E14

- 位置：首页脚注（Note）
- 原文："The REFLECT statement is also published in Journal of Food Protection, Journal of Veterinary Internal Medicine, Zoonoses and Public Health, Preventive Veterinary Medicine, and Journal of Swine Health and Production."
- 要点：REFLECT 声明同时发表在五本期刊上。

### E15

- 位置：Results
- 原文："In addition, the use of "participant" in the original CONSORT statement was limited to refer only to animals' owners-managers, who consent to participation in the trial. The term "study unit" was preferred for the units within the study."
- 要点：REFLECT 把 CONSORT 里的"参与者"限定为同意参加试验的动物主人或管理者；研究中的动物等单位称为"研究单位"（study unit）。

### E16

- 位置：Table 2，Item 3（Participants）
- 原文："Eligibility criteria for owner/managers and study units at each level of the organizational structure, and the settings and locations where the data were collected."
- 要点：要报告主人或管理者以及各层研究单位的入选标准，以及收集数据的场所和地点。
