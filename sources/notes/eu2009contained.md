# 欧盟 2009：Directive 2009/41/EC on the contained use of genetically modified micro-organisms

- 资料：`eu2009contained`，[原文（EUR-Lex）](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32009L0041)
- 版本：欧洲议会和欧盟理事会 2009 年 5 月 6 日的指令 2009/41/EC（关于基因修饰微生物的封闭使用），刊于《欧盟官方公报》L 125/75。EUR-Lex 对脚本访问不返回内容，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2026-03-16 对 EUR-Lex 官方 HTML 页的快照（https://web.archive.org/web/20260316002227/https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32009L0041）。位置写条款号。

### E1

- 位置：Article 1
- 原文："This Directive lays down common measures for the contained use of genetically modified micro-organisms with a view to protecting human health and the environment."
- 要点：这份指令规定基因修饰微生物"封闭使用"的共同措施，目的是保护人的健康和环境。

### E2

- 位置：Article 2(c)
- 原文："'contained use' means any activity in which micro-organisms are genetically modified or in which such GMMs are cultured, stored, transported, destroyed, disposed of or used in any other way, and for which specific containment measures are used to limit their contact with, and to provide a high level of safety for, the general population and the environment;"
- 要点：封闭使用指对微生物做基因修饰，或培养、保存、运输、销毁、处置、以其他方式使用这些基因修饰微生物，并用专门的防护措施限制它们和公众、环境接触的活动。

### E3

- 位置：Article 4(2)–(3)
- 原文："To that end, the user shall carry out an assessment of the contained uses as regards the risks to human health and the environment that those contained uses may pose" / "The assessment referred to in paragraph 2 shall result in the final classification of the contained uses in four classes"
- 要点：使用者要评估封闭使用对人的健康和环境可能带来的风险，评估的结果是把活动归入四个类别之一，并据此确定防护等级。

### E4

- 位置：Article 4(3)
- 原文："activities of no or negligible risk, that is to say activities for which level 1 containment is appropriate to protect human health and the environment." / "activities of high risk, that is to say activities for which level 4 containment is appropriate to protect human health and the environment."
- 要点：第 1 类是无风险或风险可忽略的活动，用 1 级防护；第 4 类是高风险活动，用 4 级防护（中间是低风险的第 2 类和中等风险的第 3 类）。

### E5

- 位置：Article 4(4)
- 原文："Where there is doubt as to which class is appropriate for the proposed contained use, the more stringent protective measures shall be applied"
- 要点：拿不准归哪一类时，采用更严格的防护措施。

### E6

- 位置：Article 9(2)
- 原文："A class 3 or higher class of contained use may not proceed without the prior consent of the competent authority"
- 要点：第 3 类及以上的封闭使用，未经主管机构事先同意不得开展。
