# 哈佛学院写作中心：A Brief Guide to Writing the English Paper

- 资料：`syba2008englishpaper`，[原文](https://writingproject.fas.harvard.edu/files/hwp/files/bg_writing_english.pdf)
- 版本：哈佛学院写作中心"简明指南"系列，Michelle Syba 撰写，2008 年版权；原网址对命令行访问返回 403，经 Internet Archive 2024 年 11 月快照读取 PDF。原文为两栏排版，文本按栏抽取

### E1

- 位置：The Challenges of Writing About English Literature
- 原文："When you perform a close reading, then, you observe how form and content interact."
- 要点：细读就是观察形式和内容怎样相互作用。

### E2

- 位置：The Challenges of Writing About English Literature
- 原文："When you close read, take an inductive approach. Start by observing particular details in the text, such as a repeated image or word, an unexpected development, or even a contradiction."
- 要点：细读用归纳法：从具体细节出发，如重复的意象或词、意外的转折、甚至矛盾。

### E3

- 位置：The Challenges of Writing About English Literature
- 原文："If you can make a case about how an apparently trivial detail reveals something significant about the text, then your paper will have a thought-provoking thesis to argue."
- 要点：如果能论证一个看似琐碎的细节揭示了文本的重要之处，论文就有了引人思考的论点。

### E4

- 位置：Common Types of English Papers
- 原文："Others, however, will ask you to read two or more texts in relation to each other, or to consider a text in light of claims made by other scholars and critics."
- 要点：有的论文要把多个文本放在一起读，或参照其他学者和批评家的观点来看一个文本。

### E5

- 位置：Common Types of English Papers，A theoretically-informed close reading
- 原文："Critical theories provide focus for your analysis; if “abjection” is the guiding concept for your paper, you should focus on the scenes in the novel that are most relevant to the concept."
- 要点：理论给分析提供焦点；以某个概念为指导，就集中分析和这个概念最相关的场景。

### E6

- 位置：Common Types of English Papers，A historically-informed close reading
- 原文："The tracts could help you to show not only how Robinson Crusoe draws on Puritan narrative conventions, but also—more significantly—how the novel revises those conventions."
- 要点：用历史文献（如清教徒小册子）不只是说明小说借用了什么惯例，更重要的是说明小说怎样改写了这些惯例。

### E7

- 位置：Example #1: Close Reading Prose，第 2 步
- 原文："This should be a list not just of page numbers but of quotations."
- 要点：收集证据时，清单里不只记页码，还要抄下引文。

### E8

- 位置：Example #1: Close Reading Prose
- 原文："If a scene or an object is depicted repeatedly, it is probably important for the book as a whole. In most cases, it will be depicted in different ways that complicate or conflict with each other."
- 要点：反复出现的场景或物件多半重要，而且每次的写法常常互相复杂化甚至冲突。

### E9

- 位置：Example #2: Close Reading Poetry，第 3 步
- 原文："Your thesis won’t simply point out the development, however; it will make an argument about the development’s significance."
- 要点：论点不只是指出一个变化，而要论证这个变化有什么意义。

### E10

- 位置：Example #2，对论点的说明
- 原文："The thesis identifies a tension between two moments in the poem and doesn’t try to tidy up or argue away that tension." / "Weird complications tend to offer richer material than complications easily overcome and tensions tidily reconciled."
- 要点：好论点指出张力，不试图把它抹平；怪异的复杂之处，比容易克服的矛盾提供更丰富的材料。

### E11

- 位置：Example #2，对论点的说明
- 原文："The thesis isn’t particularly combative or controversial. Instead, it offers a way of understanding the poem that wouldn’t be apparent to a first-time reader"
- 要点：论点不必好斗或制造争议，而是给出初读者看不出来的理解方式。

### E12

- 位置：Questions to Ask as You Read a Text 之后
- 原文："In some single-author courses you might work with manuscript drafts and biography, and thereby have sources with which to speculate about an author’s intentions. More often, however, you will not have enough evidence to speculate intelligently about the author’s intentions. In the absence of such evidence, orient your claims towards the text."
- 要点：有手稿和传记材料时可以推测作者意图；更常见的是没有足够证据，这时把论断指向文本。

### E13

- 位置：Tips and Conventions，Avoid plot summary
- 原文："A paper that recounts what happened in a novel (or a play)—or that analyzes selected scenes in the same order they occur in the novel—is letting the novel’s author rather than the paper’s author structure the paper’s argument."
- 要点：复述情节，或按小说顺序逐个分析场景，等于让小说作者而不是论文作者来安排论证结构。

### E14

- 位置：Tips and Conventions，Avoid plot summary
- 原文："One rule of thumb is to begin with the most obvious piece of evidence and move progressively to the least obvious piece of evidence."
- 要点：一条经验法则：从最明显的证据开始，逐步推进到最不明显的证据。

### E15

- 位置：Tips and Conventions，Avoid basing your argument on opinion
- 原文："Textual evidence and not personal conviction should be the basis of your thesis and argument."
- 要点：论点和论证的基础是文本证据，不是个人信念。

### E16

- 位置：Tips and Conventions，Focus on speakers, not authors
- 原文："first-person poems and narratives have a “speaker” or “narrator” who should not be confused with the author."
- 要点：第一人称诗歌和叙事里的"说话人"或"叙述者"，不要和作者混为一谈。

### E17

- 位置：Tips and Conventions，Use block quotations appropriately
- 原文："When you use a block quotation, make sure that it is rich enough to reward extended analysis (which should be at least as long as the quotation itself)."
- 要点：用大段引文时，要确保它值得展开分析，分析至少和引文一样长。

### E18

- 位置：Tips and Conventions，Use block quotations appropriately
- 原文："averting the stagnant paper structure in which a thesis is followed by a list of illustrative examples."
- 要点：避免"论点 + 一串例子"的停滞结构。

### E19

- 位置：Common Types of English Papers，A research paper
- 原文："When reading articles, make sure that they have been peer reviewed"
- 要点：读研究文章时，确认它们经过同行评审。

### E20

- 位置：Example #1，第 4 步
- 原文："In your paper, you need not account for every single usage of the word “heart”; select the examples that are most in tension with the primary meaning"
- 要点：不必交代每一处用法，挑和主要含义张力最大的例子。
