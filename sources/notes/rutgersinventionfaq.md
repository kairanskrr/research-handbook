# Rutgers Office for Research：Inventions & Commercialization FAQs

- 资料：`rutgersinventionfaq`，[原文](https://research.rutgers.edu/faculty-staff/inventions-commercialization/faqs)
- 版本：美国罗格斯大学研究办公室技术转移常见问题网页，2026-10-06 读取。位置写问题标题。内容按美国法律和该校政策写成。

### E1

- 位置：When should I submit an invention disclosure?
- 原文："If an invention is disclosed to the Office for Research after it has been publicly disseminated in an enabling manner, then patent rights outside of the United States are not available."
- 要点：发明一旦以"足以让人实施"的方式公开，再去申请，就拿不到美国以外的专利权。

### E2

- 位置：When should I submit an invention disclosure?
- 原文："Furthermore, if a patent application is not filed within one year of a public disclosure, U.S. rights can be lost as well."
- 要点：公开后一年内不申请，美国的专利权也可能丢失。

### E3

- 位置：What if I want to publish my results?
- 原文："Given sufficient notice, we will work with you to obtain the patent protection you need without delaying publication." / "Please be aware that the Office for Research will never prevent you from publishing or presenting your research."
- 要点：只要提前告知，技术转移办公室会配合在不推迟发表的前提下申请专利，并且承诺绝不阻止研究者发表或报告。

### E4

- 位置：What constitutes a public disclosure?
- 原文："A public disclosure is a non-confidential communication from the inventor to the public of the existence of an invention. The disclosure must also be “enabling", meaning that it must give enough information to someone "of ordinary skill in the art" to practice the invention without undue experimentation."
- 要点："公开披露"指非保密地向公众说明发明，而且内容足以让本领域普通技术人员不经过过多试验就能实施。

### E5

- 位置：What constitutes a public disclosure? — Oral disclosures
- 原文："if copies of presentation are distributed at a presentation in which an invention is disclosed, this is a public disclosure. However, even if there are no handouts, an audience member taking notes on the invention would constitute a public disclosure."
- 要点：报告时发了讲义算公开；即使没发讲义，有听众记了笔记也算公开。会议报告、系里的讨论班、论文答辩都可能构成公开。

### E6

- 位置：面向企业合作方的问题
- 原文："In general, universities are not the most ideal places to keep information confidential, as one of the main duties of researchers is to publish."
- 要点：大学不是保密的理想场所，因为研究者的主要职责之一就是发表。
