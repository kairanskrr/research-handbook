# Bayliss 和 Marshall 2022：Radiocarbon Dating and Chronological Modelling: Guidelines and Best Practice

- 资料：`bayliss2022radiocarbon`，英格兰历史遗产局（Historic England）官网 PDF：https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/radiocarbon-dating-chronological-modelling/heag312-radiocarbon-dating/ （官网对脚本访问返回 403 时，用 Internet Archive 存档：https://web.archive.org/web/20220926101250/https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/radiocarbon-dating-chronological-modelling/heag312-radiocarbon-dating/）
- 版本：Alex Bayliss、Peter Marshall 编写，英格兰历史遗产局 2022 年 9 月出版。原文说明它针对英格兰考古项目，应与测年实验室和建模专家的具体建议结合使用。位置写节号。

### E1

- 位置：3.2.2 Archaeological criteria for identifying suitable samples
- 原文："The association between the datable material and the archaeological activity that is of interest is paramount (Waterbolk 1971)." / "The basis of this inference, and its security, must be specifically considered for every potential sample."
- 要点：被测材料和你关心的考古活动之间的关联最重要；每个候选样品都要专门考虑这种推断的依据和可靠程度。

### E2

- 位置：3.2.2 Archaeological criteria for identifying suitable samples
- 原文："Radiocarbon [...] dating does not date contexts, it dates samples. So, the calibrated radiocarbon dates can only be constrained using the stratigraphic sequence of contexts if the dated samples were freshly deposited in the contexts from which they were recovered."
- 要点：放射性碳测年测的是样品，不是地层单位；只有样品是新鲜沉积进它所在单位的，才能用地层序列去约束测年结果。

### E3

- 位置：3.2.2 Archaeological criteria for identifying suitable samples
- 原文："The golden rule is that every potential sample should be considered residual unless there is a plausible argument showing that it was freshly deposited in the context from which it was recovered."
- 要点："黄金法则"：除非有合理论证说明样品是新鲜沉积的，否则每个候选样品都要当作残留物看待。

### E4

- 位置：3.2.2，Single-entity dating
- 原文："This is a strategy that minimises the risk that the submitted sample will contain residual or reworked material, by dating material that certainly derives from a single organism (e.g. a single cereal grain)."
- 要点：单个个体测年：只测确定来自同一个生物体的材料（如一粒谷物），把样品里混入残留物的风险降到最低。

### E5

- 位置：3.2.3 Scientific criteria for identifying suitable samples，Old-wood effect
- 原文："By far the most common source of error of this type is the old-wood effect, where dates are obtained on wood or charcoal from a long-lived tree." / "the carbon from the centre of a 300-year-old oak tree will be 300 years old while that tree is still growing" / "twigs or the outer rings of the tree (a single years' growth is optimal, but the number of growth rings to bark should be recorded if a single growth-ring cannot be isolated)," / "if twigs are not available, samples have to be taken from short-lived species (e.g. Corylus avellana) or branch-wood, although in this case a wood-offset of a few decades cannot be ruled out"
- 要点：最常见的误差来源是"老木效应"：测的是长寿树木的木头或木炭；一棵 300 年的橡树，树心的碳在树还活着时就已有 300 年。木头和木炭样品应当是细枝或树的外圈年轮等；没有细枝时，取短寿命树种（如欧洲榛）或枝条，但仍不能排除几十年的偏差。

### E6

- 位置：3.3 Statistical simulation and sample selection
- 原文："Sometimes suitable samples are not available, and the temptation to submit inferior material for dating should be resisted. Dates on such samples almost always mislead more than they inform, and hamper the understanding of past chronologies"
- 要点：没有合适的样品时，不要勉强送次等材料去测年；这类数据误导多于帮助。

### E7

- 位置：Summary
- 原文："It gives practical advice on the application of these methods within archaeological projects in England. It should be used in conjunction with advice given by radiocarbon laboratories and modelling specialists on specific projects"
- 要点：这份指南针对英格兰的考古项目给出实用建议，具体项目要结合测年实验室和建模专家的建议使用。

### E8

- 位置：2.2.1–2.2.2 之后的 "Key facts: Bayesian Chronological Modelling"
- 原文："Statistical methods are required to handle relationships between dated samples. Bayesian statistics enable calibrated radiocarbon dates to be combined with other information we might have about a chronological problem, producing posterior beliefs that take account of all the evidence. Prior beliefs can be simply that all the dated samples are from a single site or associated with the same kind of pottery, but could include relative sequences provided by stratigraphy, seriation or the growth-rings in wood or charcoal."
- 要点：处理测年样品之间的关系需要统计方法；贝叶斯统计把校正后的放射性碳年代和我们对这个年代问题已知的其他信息结合起来，得到考虑了全部证据的"后验"结果。"先验"信息可以只是"样品都来自同一遗址"，也可以包括地层、序列排比或树木年轮给出的先后顺序。

### E9

- 位置：Appendix，Relevant software，(b) Flexible Bayesian Chronological Modelling
- 原文："A variety of freely-downloadable software is available for radiocarbon calibration, Bayesian Chronological Modelling, and dietary reconstruction." / "BCal -- on-line program available at http://bcal. shef.ac.uk/" / "OxCal -- on-line and downloadable versions available from https://c14.arch.ox.ac.uk/OxCal"
- 要点：有多种可免费下载的软件可用于校正和贝叶斯年代建模；"灵活的贝叶斯年代建模"一类列了 BCal（在线程序）和 OxCal（有在线版和下载版）。

### E10

- 位置：2.2.2 之后
- 原文："It aims to provide enough understanding of the principles employed to enable archaeologists to collaborate actively with their specialist modellers. It cannot be emphasised enough that modelling is a collaborative exercise that relies essentially on the skills, experience and understanding of participating archaeologists."
- 要点：这段介绍只是让考古学家懂得原理，以便和建模专家合作；建模是合作的工作，离不开参与的考古学家的技能、经验和理解。
