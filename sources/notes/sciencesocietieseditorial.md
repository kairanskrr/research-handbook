# 美国农学会、美国作物科学学会、美国土壤科学学会：Editorial Policies

- 资料：`sciencesocietieseditorial`，[原文](https://www.sciencesocieties.org/publications/journals/author-resources/editorial-policies)
- 版本：网页，页面注明 "Last updated June 30, 2026"，2026-10-06 访问。三家学会（ASA、CSSA、SSSA）期刊的编辑政策，含数据政策、预印本政策和作者伦理声明。位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Data Policy
- 原文："The Data Policy of ASA, CSSA, and SSSA expects the storage and availability of data necessary to understand and evaluate phenomena reported in our publications. Data should be stored or archived in domain repositories that are recognized widely and available to the community."
- 要点：三家学会期刊要求保存并提供理解和评价论文结果所需的数据，数据应存放在公认的、学界可用的领域数据仓库里。

### E2

- 位置：Data Policy
- 原文："Data not stored in such a repository should be available upon request to the authors."
- 要点：没放进数据仓库的数据，应能向作者索取。

### E3

- 位置：Prior Publication
- 原文："Posting of preprints to a not-for-profit preprint server is considered acceptable but requires citing of the preprint."
- 要点：可以把预印本发到非营利的预印本平台，但投稿时要引用这份预印本。

### E4

- 位置：Ethics，Statement of Ethics for Authors
- 原文："Honor the rights and accomplishments of others and properly credit the work and ideas of others."
- 要点：作者要尊重他人的权利和成果，恰当地承认他人的工作和想法。

### E5

- 位置：Ethics，Statement of Ethics for Authors
- 原文："Demonstrate social responsibility in scientific and professional practice, by considering whom their scientific and professional activities benefit, and whom they neglect."
- 要点：作者要承担社会责任：想一想自己的研究让谁受益、忽视了谁。
