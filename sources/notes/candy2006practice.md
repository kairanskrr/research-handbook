# Candy 2006：Practice Based Research: A Guide

- 资料：`candy2006practice`，[原文 PDF](https://www.creativityandcognition.com/resources/PBR%20Guide-1.1-2006.pdf)（官网直链对脚本无响应，原文文本取自 Internet Archive 快照 `web.archive.org/web/2020id_/` 下的同一 PDF）
- 版本：澳大利亚悉尼科技大学（University of Technology, Sydney）创造力与认知工作室（Creativity & Cognition Studios）报告 CCS Report 2006-V1.0，2006 年 11 月，作者 Linda Candy。面向研究生，讲"基于实践的研究"博士论文。位置写小标题和页码。

### E1

- 位置：Summary，第 1 页
- 原文："If a creative artefact is the basis of the contribution to knowledge, the research is practice-based. 2. If the research leads primarily to new understandings about practice, it is practice-led."
- 要点：实践相关研究分两种：创作成果是知识贡献的基础，叫"基于实践的研究"（practice-based）；研究主要带来对实践的新理解，叫"实践引导的研究"（practice-led）。

### E2

- 位置：Summary，第 1 页
- 原文："Whilst the significance and context of the claims are described in words, a full understanding can only be obtained with direct reference to the outcomes."
- 要点：基于实践的研究，主张的意义和背景用文字说明，但完整理解必须直接看成果本身。

### E3

- 位置：Practice Led Research，第 3 页
- 原文："In a doctoral thesis, the results of practice-led research may be fully described in text form without the inclusion of a creative outcome. [...] Such research includes practice as an integral part of its method and often falls within the general area of action research."
- 要点：实践引导的研究，博士论文可以完全用文字写，不必附创作作品；它常落在行动研究的大范围里。

### E4

- 位置：Practice and Research，第 2 页
- 原文："However, this kind of research is, for the most part, directed towards the individual’s particular goals of the time rather than seeking to add to our shared store of knowledge in a more general sense."
- 要点：从业者日常的"研究"多是为个人当下的目标，而不是为人类共享的知识库添加东西；这是它和研究的区别。

### E5

- 位置：Practice and Research，第 2 页
- 原文："However, the outcomes of practice must be accompanied by documentation of the research process, as well as some form of textual analysis or explanation to support its position and to demonstrate critical reflection."
- 要点：实践成果必须配上研究过程的记录，以及文字分析或说明，以支撑其立场、展示批判性反思。

### E6

- 位置：Practice-Based Research，第 3 页
- 原文："Practice-based doctoral submissions must include a substantial contextualisation of the creative work."
- 要点：基于实践的博士论文必须对创作作品做充分的背景定位。

### E7

- 位置：Practice and Research，第 2 页
- 原文："It must define a series of research questions or problems that will be addressed in the course of the research. [...] It must specify a research context for the questions or problems to be addressed. [...] It must specify the research methods for addressing and answering the research questions or problems."
- 要点：英国艺术与人文研究委员会（当时叫 AHRB）2000 年的定义：研究必须有研究问题、研究背景和研究方法三要素（Candy 转述）。

### E8

- 位置：The PhD and Knowledge，第 5 页
- 原文："That new knowledge is expected to have two characteristics: it is shared and it can be verified or challenged."
- 要点：博士层次的新知识要有两个特征：可以分享，可以被核验或质疑。

### E9

- 位置：The PhD and Knowledge，Preliminaries，第 5 页
- 原文："That we have achieved new implicit or tacit knowledge is clearly not relevant because, by definition, it is not shared."
- 要点：只存在于个人身上、没有分享出来的默会知识，不算博士研究意义上的知识。

### E10

- 位置：Questions and Answers，Q4，第 8 页
- 原文："Keep a written record either in the form of an online diary or blog, or a hand written notebook. [...] Do not be tempted to only review and reflect on an ad hoc basis. Build it in to your timetable."
- 要点：记录反思：用在线日记、博客或手写笔记本持续记录；反思要排进日程，不要只是想起来才做。

### E11

- 位置：Questions and Answers，Q5，第 9 页
- 原文："The artefact is not an explanation in itself: - it requires linguistic description that relates the development and nature of the artefact to understandings about creative process"
- 要点：作品本身不是解释，需要文字描述把作品的发展和性质，与对创作过程的理解联系起来。

### E12

- 位置：Questions and Answers，Q1，第 8 页
- 原文："Do not quote someone’s reference to someone else’s work: always go to the original text."
- 要点：文献综述不要转引别人对别人工作的引用，一定要回到原文。

### E13

- 位置：Ethical Considerations，第 9 页
- 原文："Researchers must obtain the informed consent of persons participating in research before the research begins."
- 要点：研究涉及人时，开始前必须取得参与者的知情同意。

### E14

- 位置：Domain Differences in Practice-based and Practice-led Research，第 3 页
- 原文："In design research, for example, where the nature of practice is a major research topic and is often conducted by research specialists rather than design practitioners, the emphasis is on achieving new knowledge about the nature of practice and how to improve it, rather than creating and reflecting on new artefacts. [...] By contrast, in the visual arts, the emphasis is on creative process and the works that are generated from that process: the artefact plays a vital part in the new understandings about practice that arise."
- 要点：不同领域侧重不同：设计研究多研究"实践本身怎样运转、怎样改进"；视觉艺术更重创作过程和作品。

### E15

- 位置：Historical Background，第 4 页
- 原文："It would be required that the thesis, as lodged in the Library, would include a permanent record of any artefacts submitted towards the examination."
- 要点：提交到图书馆的论文要包含所有参与答辩的作品的永久记录。

### E16

- 位置：Practice-Based and Practice-Led Research，第 3 页
- 原文："are often used interchangeably."
- 要点："基于实践"和"实践引导"两个词常被混用。

### E17

- 位置：Thesis Outline，5. New Studies，第 7 页
- 原文："In a practice based PhD an artwork, for example, might be presented for examination. If so, this chapter will illuminate it by explaining, at the very least, what is important and novel"
- 要点：如果作品参与答辩，论文的核心章节要说明作品中什么是重要的、新的。

### E18

- 位置：Practice and Research，第 2 页（转述英国艺术与人文研究委员会 AHRB 2000 年的研究定义）
- 原文："It must define a series of research questions or problems that will be addressed in the course of the research. It must also define its objectives in terms of seeking to enhance knowledge and understanding relating to the questions or problems to be addressed."
- 要点：研究计划第一要素：界定要回答的研究问题，并把目标写成"增进对这些问题的认识和理解"。

### E19

- 位置：Practice and Research，第 2 页（同上）
- 原文："It must specify a research context for the questions or problems to be addressed. It must specify why it is important that these particular questions or problems should be addressed, what other research is being or has been conducted in this area and what particular contribution this project will make"
- 要点：第二要素：说明研究背景——为什么这些问题重要、这个领域已有哪些研究、本项目会有什么独特贡献。

### E20

- 位置：Practice and Research，第 2 页（同上）
- 原文："It must specify the research methods for addressing and answering the research questions or problems. [...] It should also explain the rationale for the chosen research methods and why they provide the most appropriate means by which to answer the research questions."
- 要点：第三要素：说明回答问题的方法，并解释为什么这些方法最合适。

### E21

- 位置：Practice-Based Research，第 3 页
- 原文："Practice-based doctoral submissions must include a substantial contextualisation of the creative work. This critical appraisal or analysis not only clarifies the basis of the claim for the originality and location of the original work, it also provides the basis for a judgement as to whether general scholarly requirements are met."
- 要点：基于实践的博士论文必须对作品做充分的背景定位；这部分批判性评析既说明原创性主张的依据和作品的位置，也是判断是否达到一般学术要求的依据。

### E22

- 位置：Thesis Outline，1. Introduction，第 7 页
- 原文："i.The Problem This is a concise statement of the research question or issue that the thesis addresses. ii.The Context What is the main work that has been done that gives rise to the question and what is its significance? iii.The Method"
- 要点：Candy 建议的论文结构中，引言要简要写出问题、背景、方法（和成果）。

### E23

- 位置：Thesis Outline，1. Introduction，第 7 页
- 原文："The value of these outcomes will be to one or more community (computer scientists, artists, theoreticians etc) and it is important to be clear who they are."
- 要点：成果对哪个或哪些群体有价值，要说清楚。

### E24

- 位置：Thesis Outline，2. State of the Art Review，第 7 页
- 原文："It should be a critical review in the context of the stated research question and related issues. This chapter answers questions such as: Who is doing what? Who has done what? Who first did it or published it? The survey is taken from published papers, research monographs, catalogues etc."
- 要点：现状综述要围绕研究问题做批判性评述，回答"谁在做什么、谁做过什么、谁最先做或发表"；来源包括论文、专著、图录等。

### E25

- 位置：Thesis Outline，2. State of the Art Review，第 7 页
- 原文："It must be based on and refer to primary sources, not textbooks or other such reports on the work of others. It is to be expected that this chapter provides a new structured view of the field of study."
- 要点：综述要依据一手来源，不能依据教科书或别人的转述；这一章应给出对领域的一个新的结构化看法。

### E26

- 位置：Thesis Outline，3. Methodology，第 7 页
- 原文："This is a key chapter that provides a description and justification of the research methods used. Normally, the methods will be selected from known and proven examples."
- 要点：方法章描述并论证所用方法；通常从已知、经过检验的方法中选。

### E27

- 位置：Questions and Answers，Q3，第 8 页
- 原文："1. start with the actual things you have done/will do (as if instructing an assistant) 2. then add a brief description of the origins of the method(s) - sources from which they have been selected 3. if any aspect is not yet justified develop the argument to justify it."
- 要点：写方法：先写实际做了/要做的事（像在指导助手），再简述方法来源，最后只对还没论证的部分补论证。

### E28

- 位置：Questions and Answers，Q3，第 8 页
- 原文："If the argument includes negatives (\"statistics is no good for this\" or \"semiotics does not work in this case\") be sure that you are knowledgeable about what you are rejecting."
- 要点：如果论证里否定某种方法（如"统计在这里没用"），要确定自己懂你所否定的东西。

### E29

- 位置：Questions and Answers，Q4，第 8 页
- 原文："Keep a written record either in the form of an online diary or blog, or a hand written notebook. • Having a notebook with you at all times to record any observations or events during the process of making a work is advisable."
- 要点：用在线日记、博客或手写笔记本记录；建议随身带笔记本，记下创作过程中的观察和事件。

### E30

- 位置：Questions and Answers，Q4，第 8 页
- 原文："If you are collecting audio records, you will probably need to transcribe them at some point and may need to factor that in to your time and effort schedule. • Keep an overview chart to be able to see at a glance what has been recorded and what you plan to record."
- 要点：录音之后多半要转写，要算进时间安排；做一张总览表，一眼看出已记录什么、计划记录什么。

### E31

- 位置：Questions and Answers，Q4，第 8 页
- 原文："It is advisable to designate time for reflection after the events and to record your reactions and emerging thoughts at specified Review points. Do not be tempted to only review and reflect on an ad hoc basis. Build it in to your timetable."
- 要点：在事件之后专门留出反思时间，在指定的回顾点记录反应和新想法；不要只是想起来才反思，要排进日程。

### E32

- 位置：Questions and Answers，Q4，第 8 页
- 原文："Good questions to ask yourself are: - what was proposed, discussed, decided and carried through, - what stumbling blocks arose and how they were addressed…., - whether the ideas were workable, interesting, challenging…. - whether the collaboration worked well or not - reasons for success or otherwise - did the solutions work well, if not why not? - whether there were different viewpoints between you and your collaborating parties - whether lessons were learnt from failures."
- 要点：反思时可以问自己：提出、讨论、决定并执行了什么；遇到什么障碍、怎样解决；想法是否可行、有趣；合作是否顺利；成败原因；方案是否有效；和合作方有无分歧；是否从失败中吸取了教训。

### E33

- 位置：Ethical Considerations，第 8 页
- 原文："The first stage in designing a research project is to prepare a research proposal which outlines the proposed methodology and any ethical issues. Researchers must obtain the informed consent of persons participating in research before the research begins"
- 要点：设计项目的第一步是写研究计划，列出方法和伦理问题；研究开始前必须取得参与者的知情同意。

### E34

- 位置：Domain Differences in Practice-based and Practice-led Research，第 3 页
- 原文："In design research, for example, where the nature of practice is a major research topic and is often conducted by research specialists rather than design practitioners, the emphasis is on achieving new knowledge about the nature of practice and how to improve it, rather than creating and reflecting on new artefacts."
- 要点：设计研究更多研究"设计实践怎样运转、怎样改进"，常由专门的研究者来做，而不是创作并反思新作品。
