# Benchimol et al. 2015：The REporting of studies Conducted using Observational Routinely-collected health Data (RECORD) Statement

- 资料：`benchimol2015record`，[PLOS Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1001885)（CC BY）
- 版本：PLoS Med 12(10): e1001885，2015。位置写小标题或清单条目号。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Routinely collected health data are defined as data collected without specific a priori research questions developed prior to utilization for research"
- 要点：常规收集的健康数据，指收集时并没有事先确定的研究问题、后来才拿来做研究的数据。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："These data sources could include provision of broad resources for research (e.g., disease registries), clinical management (e.g., primary care databases), health system planning (e.g., health administrative data), documentation of clinical care (e.g., electronic health record data repositories), or epidemiological surveillance (e.g., cancer registries and public health reporting data)."
- 要点：这类数据包括疾病登记、基层医疗数据库、卫生行政数据、电子病历库、癌症登记和公共卫生报告数据等。

### E3

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："RECORD was created as an extension to the STROBE statement to address reporting items specific to observational studies using routinely collected health data. RECORD consists of a checklist of 13 items"
- 要点：RECORD 是 STROBE 的扩展，专门针对使用常规收集健康数据的观察性研究，清单有 13 项。

### E4

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Reporting deficiencies include inadequate or missing information concerning coding of exposures and outcomes as well as details of linkage rates of different data sources."
- 要点：这类研究常见的报告缺陷：暴露和结局怎样编码没写清，不同数据源的链接率没写。

### E5

- 位置：RECORD item 7.1
- 原文："A complete list of codes and algorithms used to classify exposures, outcomes, confounders, and effect modifiers should be provided."
- 要点：要给出用来界定暴露、结局、混杂因素和效应修饰因素的全部编码和算法。

### E6

- 位置：RECORD item 6.2
- 原文："Any validation studies of the codes or algorithms used to select the population should be referenced."
- 要点：用来选研究人群的编码或算法，如果有验证研究，要引用。

### E7

- 位置：Introduction 部分的说明
- 原文："Authors also should indicate whether their hypotheses were generated before or after data analysis."
- 要点：作者要说明假设是在分析数据之前还是之后提出的。

### E8

- 位置：RECORD item 19.1
- 原文："Discuss the implications of using data that were not created or collected to answer the specific research question(s). Include discussion of misclassification bias, unmeasured confounding, missing data, and changing eligibility over time"
- 要点：讨论部分要说明：数据本来不是为这个问题收集的，会带来什么影响，包括错误分类偏倚、未测量的混杂、缺失数据、纳入资格随时间变化。

### E9

- 位置：Discussion (Limitations) 部分的说明
- 原文："Administrative data are particularly subject to errors in upcoding or opportunistic coding. For example, when hospital reimbursement is based on complexity of the case mix, hospitals may maximize reimbursement by liberally applying more complex disease codes to patient records"
- 要点：行政数据特别容易出现"往高编码"的问题：医院报销按病例复杂程度计算时，医院可能多用更复杂的疾病编码来多拿报销。

### E10

- 位置：Discussion (Limitations) 部分的说明
- 原文："In addition, changes in coding strategies may impact validity or consistency of data."
- 要点：编码方式的变化会影响数据的有效性和前后一致性。

### E11

- 位置：RECORD item 12.3
- 原文："State whether the study included person-level, institutional-level, or other data linkage across two or more databases. Linkage techniques and methods used to evaluate linkage quality should be provided."
- 要点：如果把两个以上数据库链接起来，要说明在什么层面链接、用什么方法，以及怎样评估链接质量。

### E12

- 位置：RECORD item 22.1
- 原文："Authors should provide information on how to access any supplemental information such as the study protocol, raw data, or programming code."
- 要点：要说明怎样获取研究方案、原始数据或程序代码等补充材料。

### E13

- 位置：Methods (Setting) 部分的说明
- 原文："information should be provided to allow the reader to understand the content and validity of the database and the original reasons why the data were collected."
- 要点：要让读者了解数据库的内容、有效性，以及这些数据当初是为什么收集的。

### E14

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："the RECORD guidelines are not designed to recommend the methods used to conduct research, but rather to improve its reporting"
- 要点：RECORD 不规定研究怎么做，只改进报告。

### E15

- 位置：Methods (Participants) 部分的说明
- 原文："Although the risk of misclassification is amplified in studies using databases containing large populations, such studies offer an opportunity to study rare or uncommon diseases"
- 要点：大人群数据库的错误分类风险会被放大，但它们也让研究罕见病成为可能。
