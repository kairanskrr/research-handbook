# USENIX Security '26：Call for Papers

- 资料：`usenixsec2026cfp`，[原文](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/call-for-papers)
- 版本：USENIX Security Symposium 2026 征稿启事（含 Ethics Guidelines 部分），2026-10-05 读取。位置写网页小标题。只作为安全方向一个顶级会议的例子。

### E1

- 位置：Important Dates，Cycle 1
- 原文："Paper submissions (including artifacts) due: Tuesday, August 26, 2025, 11:59 pm AoE [...] Early reject notification: Tuesday, October 7, 2025 [...] Rebuttal Period: November 6–13, 2025"
- 要点：一年两轮投稿；每轮有截稿、早期拒稿通知、作者回复期（rebuttal）等固定节点。

### E2

- 位置：Submission Policies and Instructions，Executive Summary
- 原文："All papers MUST have a discussion of research ethics."
- 要点：所有投稿都必须讨论研究伦理。

### E3

- 位置：Anonymous Submission
- 原文："While submitted papers must be anonymous, authors may choose to give talks about their work, post a preprint of the paper online, disclose security vulnerabilities to vendors or the public, etc., during the review process."
- 要点：投稿必须匿名，但审稿期间作者仍可做报告、发预印本、向厂商或公众披露漏洞。

### E4

- 位置：Anonymous Submission
- 原文："When referring to your previous work, do so in the third person, as though it were written by someone else."
- 要点：匿名投稿时，引用自己以前的工作要用第三人称，像引用别人的一样。

### E5

- 位置：Ethics Guidelines，Disclosures
- 原文："Publicly disclosing vulnerabilities before they have been privately disclosed to the responsible parties, and hence before they have been mitigated, can therefore expose people to negative outcomes."
- 要点：在私下告知负责方、漏洞被修复之前就公开，会让人受到伤害。

### E6

- 位置：Ethics Guidelines，Disclosures
- 原文："Clearly state to whom you have disclosed vulnerabilities and the outcomes of those disclosures, if any."
- 要点：论文里要写清向谁披露了漏洞、结果如何。

### E7

- 位置：Ethics Guidelines，Is IRB approval a sufficient alternative?
- 原文："IRBs are not expected to understand computer security research well or to know about best practices and community norms in our field, and so IRB approval does not absolve researchers from considering ethical aspects of their work."
- 要点：伦理审查委员会（IRB）批准不等于可以不考虑伦理问题。

### E8

- 位置：Ethics Guidelines
- 原文："sometimes, the most ethical path is not to do the research or not to publish the research after it is complete."
- 要点：有时最合乎伦理的选择是不做这项研究，或做完了不发表。

### E9

- 位置：Ethics Guidelines，When should I start considering Ethics?
- 原文："As early as possible in the research process."
- 要点：伦理问题越早考虑越好。

### E10

- 位置：Ethics Guidelines
- 原文："Authors are strongly encouraged to familiarize themselves with The Menlo Report"
- 要点：会议建议作者熟悉《Menlo 报告》。

### E11

- 位置：Simultaneous Submission and Plagiarism
- 原文："Simultaneous submission of the same work to multiple venues, submission of previously published work, and plagiarism constitute dishonesty or fraud."
- 要点：一稿多投、投已发表的工作、抄袭都属于不诚实或欺诈。

### E12

- 位置：Submission Policies and Instructions，Executive Summary
- 原文："Artifacts must be made available during the reviewing process. If they cannot be made available during review or after publication, the Open Science appendix must explain the reasoning."
- 要点：审稿期间就必须提供制品；做不到要在"开放科学"附录里说明原因。
