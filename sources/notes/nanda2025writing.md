# Nanda 2025：Highly Opinionated Advice on How to Write ML Papers

- 资料：`nanda2025writing`，[原文](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/eJGptPbbFPZGLpjsp/highly-opinionated-advice-on-how-to-write-ml-papers)
- 版本：AI Alignment Forum 文章，2025。网页，位置写小标题。原文面向机器学习论文，本章只取跨学科成立的部分。

### E1

- 位置：TL;DR
- 原文："A narrative is fundamentally about a contribution to our body of knowledge: one to three specific novel claims that fit within a cohesive theme"
- 要点：论文的主线是对知识的贡献：一到三个具体、新颖、围绕同一主题的主张。

### E2

- 位置：The Essence of a Paper / Crafting a Narrative
- 原文："Readers will rarely take away more than a few sentences of content. Choose those sentences carefully."
- 要点：读者很少能带走超过几句话的内容，必须把研究压缩成几条主张，主动舍弃其余细节或放进附录。

### E3

- 位置：The Essence of a Paper / Crafting a Narrative
- 原文："Generally, stronger statements make for more interesting papers, but require higher standards of evidence - resist the temptation to overclaim for clicks!"
- 要点：主张越强越有意思，但需要更高标准的证据；可以按证据强度选择"存在性""普遍性""有保留""限定范围"等不同强度的说法，不要夸大。

### E4

- 位置：The Essence of a Paper / Rigorous Supporting Evidence
- 原文："Assume you've made a mistake - what is that mistake?"
- 要点：主动"攻击"自己的主线：假设自己错了，找出错在哪；不要忽视与主线矛盾的结果，要充分讨论局限。

### E5

- 位置：The Writing Process: Compress then Iteratively Expand
- 原文："A key challenge in paper writing is the illusion of transparency - you have spent months steeped in the context of this research project."
- 要点：作者对项目背景太熟悉，容易以为读者也懂（"透明错觉"），需要尽量多找别人反馈。

### E6

- 位置：TL;DR
- 原文："Write iteratively: Write abstract -> bullet point outline -> introduction -> first full draft -> repeat"
- 要点：迭代地写：摘要 → 要点提纲 → 引言 → 完整初稿 → 重复，每个阶段后反思并找人反馈。

### E7

- 位置：The Essence of a Paper / Novelty
- 原文："be extremely clear about what is and is not novel, especially in the introduction and related work"
- 要点：在引言和相关工作中清楚说明哪些是新的、哪些不是，并引用最相关的工作说明区别。

### E8

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："the process of writing forces you to clarify your own thinking in ways that often reveal gaps or new insights"
- 要点：写作迫使你理清思路，常会暴露漏洞或带来新见解。

### E9

- 位置：The Writing Process / Iteratively Expand
- 原文："A good outline is tight and minimal - every part of it should have a clear role in the overall narrative."
- 要点：好的提纲紧凑精简，每一部分都要在主线中有明确作用；说不出"删掉它会出什么问题"，就删掉。
