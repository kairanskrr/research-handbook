# Putnick & Bornstein 2016：Measurement Invariance Conventions and Reporting: The State of the Art and Future Directions for Psychological Research

- 资料：`putnick2016measurement`，[原文（PubMed Central 作者稿）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5145197/)
- 版本：Developmental Review 41:71–90（2016）；PubMed Central 公开的作者稿。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Measurement noninvariance suggests that a construct has a different structure or meaning to different groups or on different measurement occasions in the same group, and so the construct cannot be meaningfully tested or construed across groups or across time."
- 要点：测量不变性不成立，说明构念在不同组或不同时间点的结构或含义不同，就无法在组间或时间上作有意义的比较。

### E2

- 位置：Abstract
- 原文："Hence, prior to testing mean differences across groups or measurement occasions (e.g., boys and girls, pretest and posttest), or differential relations of the construct across groups, it is essential to assess the invariance of the construct."
- 要点：比较组间（如男孩和女孩）或前后测的均值之前，必须先检验测量不变性。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction（抑郁的例子）
- 原文："In this example, men may score lower than women on the depression scale because they cry less and gain less weight. However, crying and weight gain are not associated with depression in men in the first place."
- 要点：如果哭泣、体重增加只在女性中反映抑郁，男性在量表上得分低可能只是因为哭得少、体重增加少，而不是更不抑郁。

### E4

- 位置：Testing Measurement Invariance
- 原文："(1) configural, equivalence of model form; (2) metric (weak factorial), equivalence of factor loadings; (3) scalar (strong factorial), equivalence of item intercepts or thresholds; and (4) residual (strict or invariant uniqueness), equivalence of items' residuals or unique variances."
- 要点：测量不变性的四步：形态（模型形式相同）、度量（因子载荷相同）、标量（题目截距或阈值相同）、残差（残差方差相同）。

### E5

- 位置：Testing Measurement Invariance（比较组间均值）
- 原文："Once the configural, metric, and scalar invariance steps have been passed, the researcher is free to compare group means on the latent factors"
- 要点：通过形态、度量、标量三步之后，才可以比较组间潜变量均值。

### E6

- 位置：Configural invariance
- 原文："Redefining the construct in any step of invariance testing (e.g., by omitting items or making other post-hoc alterations to the model)" / "exploratory in nature, and results should be interpreted with this concern in mind."
- 要点：在不变性检验中删题、事后改模型，属于数据驱动的探索，解释结果时要考虑这一点。

### E7

- 位置：Introduction（干预研究的例子）
- 原文："the intervention, protocol, or trial could change the way participants interpret the constructs under study."
- 要点：干预本身可能改变参与者对构念的理解，所以前后测之间也需要检验不变性。
