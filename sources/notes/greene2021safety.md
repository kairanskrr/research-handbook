# Greene 等 2021：Safety and Belonging in the Field: A Checklist for Educators

- 资料：`greene2021safety`，[原文](https://go-outdoors.caltech.edu/documents/20636/greene-et-al_2021.pdf)
- 版本：未经同行评审的 EarthArXiv 预印本（2021），作者是英国高校地理、地球与环境科学方向的师生。读取的是加州理工学院网站上转载的预印本 PDF。清单针对的是**教学性质的野外实习**，部分条目也适用于科研野外工作。位置写章节号。

### E1

- 位置：§1 Author Positionality Statement
- 原文："The authors comprise current and former staff and students in UK Higher Education spanning a variety of GEES disciplines."
- 要点：作者都是英国高校地理、地球与环境科学（GEES）方向的现任或前任师生。

### E2

- 位置：§4.2.2 Permission/Ethics
- 原文："Meeting legal requirements is a minimum standard. Check whether any of the sites you plan to visit are protected (this includes biology and environment, not just rocks) and whether your intended activities are permissible."
- 要点：守法只是底线；先查要去的地点是否受保护（不只岩石，也包括生物和环境），计划的活动是否被允许。

### E3

- 位置：§4.2.2 Permission/Ethics
- 原文："Obtain relevant permissions (e.g., site access). Obtain sampling permission and export/import permits, if applicable."
- 要点：办好进入许可、采样许可，需要时办样品进出口许可。

### E4

- 位置：§4.2.2 Permission/Ethics
- 原文："Beyond legality, the fieldwork must also be ethical." / "Whose land is it, are locals involved in your work, and are they mutually benefitting (or at minimum not being harmed) by your visit"
- 要点：除了合法，还要合乎伦理：这是谁的土地，当地人有没有参与，他们有没有受益、至少没有受损。

### E5

- 位置：§4.2.6 Health, Safety, Hazards, and Risks
- 原文："Hazard and risk assessment needs to be checked off by a third party and communicated to all participants prior to the activity commencing so that everyone is familiar with the risks and control measures"
- 要点：危险与风险评估要由第三方审核，并在出发前告诉所有参与者。

### E6

- 位置：§4.2.6 Health, Safety, Hazards, and Risks
- 原文："Investigate local emergency services. Whom do you call in a medical emergency? Where is the nearest hospital?"
- 要点：事先查清当地急救：出事打给谁，最近的医院在哪。

### E7

- 位置：§4.2.6 Health, Safety, Hazards, and Risks
- 原文："Review previous years' incident and near miss forms"
- 要点：查看往年的事故和险情记录。

### E8

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："To guarantee safety and belonging, participants' identities (backgrounds and protected characteristics) must be considered as a part of fieldwork planning and implementation."
- 要点：规划野外工作时要考虑参与者的身份和背景，才能保证每个人的安全。

### E9

- 位置：§4.2.5 Scheduling
- 原文："In addition to factors like seasonal weather and tides, consider religious calendars when scheduling your field dates."
- 要点：定野外日期时，除了季节天气和潮汐，还要考虑参与者的宗教节日。

### E10

- 位置：§4.1.1 Training
- 原文："Physical first aid training for staff (and sometimes students) is standard, but is also insufficient to ensure the health and safety of all participants."
- 要点：急救培训是标准配置，但只靠它不足以保证所有人的安全。
