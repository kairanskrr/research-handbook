# Alon 2009：How To Choose a Good Scientific Problem

- 资料：`alon2009choose`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2009.09.013)（作者实验室主页 PDF：[链接](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/mcb/UriAlon/sites/mcb.UriAlon/files/uploads/nurturing/howtochoosegoodproblem.pdf)）
- 版本：Molecular Cell 35，2009；作者主页托管的"in press"版本，共 3 页。

### E1

- 位置：The Two Dimensions of Problem Choice（第 1 页）
- 原文："The second axis is interest: the increase in knowledge expected from the project. We generally value science that ventures deep into unknown waters. Problems can be ranked in terms of the distance from the known shores, by the amount in which they increase verifiable knowledge."
- 要点：一个问题的价值，可以用它预期能增加多少"可验证的知识"来衡量，也就是离"已知的岸边"有多远。

### E2

- 位置：The Two Dimensions of Problem Choice（第 1 页）
- 原文："We will compare problems by imagining two axes. The first is feasibility--that is, whether a problem is hard or easy, in units such as the expected time to complete the project. This axis is a function of the skills of the researchers and of the technology in the lab."
- 要点：比较问题的第一条轴是可行性：用预计完成时间衡量难易，它取决于研究者的技能和实验室的条件。

### E3

- 位置：同上
- 原文："It is important to remember that problems that are easy on paper are often hard in reality, and that problems that are hard on paper are nearly impossible in reality."
- 要点：纸面上容易的问题，实际往往很难；纸面上难的问题，实际几乎做不成。

### E4

- 位置：同上
- 原文："The second axis is interest: the increase in knowledge expected from the project."
- 要点：第二条轴是"兴趣"，即项目预期能增加多少知识。

### E5

- 位置：同上（第 1 页）
- 原文："many projects in current research are of the easy-but-not-too-interesting variety, also known as ``low-hanging fruit.'' Many other projects in science today are unfortunately both difficult and have low interest, partially stemming from a view that hard equals good."
- 要点：很多项目是"容易但不太有意思"的低垂果实；还有很多项目又难又没意思，部分源于"难就是好"的观念。

### E6

- 位置：同上
- 原文："[If pro]blem A is better on both axes than problem B, one can erase B from the diagram. Applying this criterion to all problems, one is left only with problems for which there are no problems clearly better in both feasibility and interest. These remaining problems are on the Pareto front."
- 要点：如果问题 A 在两条轴上都优于 B，就可以划掉 B；剩下的问题构成"帕累托前沿"（没有任何别的问题在两方面都更好）。方括号内的 "If pro" 在 PDF 中因跨栏断词丢失，按上下文补出。

### E7

- 位置：同上（第 1 页）
- 原文："a beginning graduate student needs a problem that is easy; positive feedback can thus be rapidly provided, bolstering confidence."
- 要点：刚入学的研究生需要一个容易的问题，好尽快得到正反馈、建立信心；之后的问题再往"有意思"的方向移。

### E8

- 位置：Take Your Time（第 1 页）
- 原文："A common mistake made in choosing problems is taking the first problem that comes to mind. Since a typical project takes years even it if seems doable in months, rapid choice leads to much frustration and bitterness in our profession. It takes time to find a good problem, and every week spent in choosing one can save months or years later on."
- 要点：选题的常见错误是抓住第一个想到的问题；一个项目通常要做好几年，草率选择会带来很多挫败；在选题上多花一周，后面可能省下几个月甚至几年。

### E9

- 位置：Take Your Time（第 1–2 页）
- 原文："Do not commit to a problem before 3 months have elapsed. In these 3 months the new student or postdoc reads, discusses, [...] and plans."
- 要点：Alon 实验室的规矩：新学生或博士后入组后 3 个月内不确定课题，先读、讨论、计划。

### E10

- 位置：Take Your Time（第 2 页）
- 原文："The author is under no illusion that everyone is free to choose their own problems, or has the time needed for an extended search."
- 要点：作者承认，并不是每个人都能自由选题，或有时间长时间寻找。

### E11

- 位置：The Subjectivity of the Interest Axis（第 2 页）
- 原文："The confusion is due to the mixing of two voices--one is a loud voice of the interests of those around us, in conferences, in our department, etc. The other is a faint voice in our breast, that says, ``This is interesting to me.''"
- 要点：判断一个问题是否有意思时，有两个声音：周围人（会议、系里）的大声音，和自己心里"这个我觉得有意思"的小声音。

### E12

- 位置：同上
- 原文："One way to help listening to the inner voice is to ask: ``If I was the only person on earth, which of these problems would I work on?''"
- 要点：一个自测问题：如果世界上只有我一个人，我会做这些问题中的哪一个？

### E13

- 位置：同上
- 原文："Another good sign of the inner voice are ideas and questions that come back again and again to your mind for months or years."
- 要点：几个月、几年里反复回到脑中的想法和问题，往往比最近才冒出来的更适合做成项目。

### E14

- 位置：同上
- 原文："In science, the more you interest yourself, the larger the probability that you will interest your audience."
- 要点：你越能让自己感兴趣，就越可能让读者感兴趣。
