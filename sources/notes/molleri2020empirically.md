# Molléri, Petersen & Mendes 2020：An Empirically Evaluated Checklist for Surveys in Software Engineering

- 资料：`molleri2020empirically`，[arXiv:1901.09850](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.09850)
- 版本：arXiv v1（2019-01-28，录用前版本）；正式版发表于 Information and Software Technology 119 (2020) 106240

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："To construct the checklist, we systematically aggregated knowledge from 14 methodological papers supporting survey-based research in software engineering."
- 要点：这份问卷研究检查清单是系统汇总 14 篇软件工程问卷方法论文献得到的。

### E2

- 位置：§2，Instrument validation
- 原文："After design, the ability of the instrument to measure what is intended should be assessed. The most frequently cited approaches for the assessment are P66) piloting, P65) retest, P62) focus groups, and P63) expert or P58) non-expert reviews."
- 要点：问卷设计好后要检验它能否测到想测的东西；最常提到的方法是预调查、重测、焦点小组、专家或非专家评审。

### E3

- 位置：§2，Response management
- 原文："After distribution of the instrument to the selected participants, it is important to observe the response rate (P64) in order to identify the reasons for non-responses (P25)."
- 要点：发出问卷后要观察回应率，找出不回应的原因。

### E4

- 位置：§2，Data analysis
- 原文："An additional suggestion to ensure their reliability is P79) to have more than one item measuring the same variable."
- 要点：为保证可靠性，同一个变量最好用不止一道题来测。

### E5

- 位置：§3.3.1，EQ1 Completeness
- 原文："Among the checklist groups that are more scarcely addressed are: 2) study plan; 3) identify the population; 6) instrument validation; and 8) response management."
- 要点：作者用清单评估了 24 篇问卷论文，做得最少的是：研究计划、界定总体、问卷检验、回收管理。

### E6

- 位置：§4.2.1 Research Practice
- 原文："Very often the demographics of the participants are described, but scarce information is provided regarding the target population."
- 要点：论文常描述了参与者的人口学特征，却很少交代目标总体。

### E7

- 位置：§4.2.1 Research Practice
- 原文："When designing the data collection instrument, one should carefully consider the order (P34) and amount (P36) of questions. Additional sources of data (P69), such as work repositories can provide means to cross-check the results."
- 要点：设计问卷要仔细考虑题目的顺序和数量；工作仓库等其他数据来源可以用来交叉核对问卷结果。

### E8

- 位置：§4.2.1 Research Practice
- 原文："Furthermore, it is also important to report the improvements made as resulting of the instrument validation (P61)."
- 要点：要报告问卷检验后做了哪些改进。

### E9

- 位置：§4.2.1 Research Practice
- 原文："Besides the response rates, it is valuable to report any strategy used to improve responses, such as reminders (P70) or searching for additional databases (P16)."
- 要点：除回应率外，还要报告为提高回应用了哪些办法，如提醒或补充名单来源。

### E10

- 位置：§3.3.2 Evaluation by the corresponding authors
- 原文："As a recommended practice, researchers are encouraged to make additional information (e.g., research diary, questionnaire instrument, ancillary documents) accessible to the target audience."
- 要点：建议公开研究日志、问卷原件和邀请信等辅助文件，弥补论文篇幅限制。
