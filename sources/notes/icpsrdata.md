# ICPSR：关于 ICPSR、存档、引用数据、数据保密（网页）

- 资料：`icpsrdata`，[About](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/pages/about/)、[Deposit Data](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/pages/deposit/index.html)、[Citing Data](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/pages/datamanagement/citations.html)、[Data Confidentiality](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/pages/datamanagement/confidentiality/index.html)
- 版本：美国密歇根大学社会研究所下属的校际政治与社会研究联盟（Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research，ICPSR）官网的四个页面。官网对脚本返回验证页，读取的是 Internet Archive 快照（2025 年 7–9 月）。位置写页面名。

### E1

- 位置：About ICPSR
- 原文："ICPSR is an international consortium of more than 810 academic institutions and research organizations." / "ICPSR maintains a data archive of more than 350,000 files of research in the social and behavioral sciences."
- 要点：ICPSR 是由 810 多家学术和研究机构组成的国际联盟，维护着一个有 35 万多个社会与行为科学研究文件的数据档案库。

### E2

- 位置：Deposit Data，What Should My Deposit Include?
- 原文："Deposits should include all data and documentation necessary to independently read and interpret the data collection"
- 要点：存档的内容应包括别人独立读懂和解释这批数据所需的全部数据和文档。

### E3

- 位置：Deposit Data，Documentation File(s)
- 原文："Documentation files are integral to reuse of a data collection and should thoroughly explain the data collection. Examples of documentation files include codebooks, copies of data collection instruments, user guides, README files, summary statistics, project summaries, and bibliographies of publications using the data."
- 要点：文档是数据能被再利用的关键，例如编码手册、数据收集工具（如问卷）副本、用户指南、README、汇总统计、项目概要、用过这批数据的出版物清单。

### E4

- 位置：Citing Data，How to Cite Data
- 原文："Each citation must include the basic elements that allow a unique dataset to be identified over time: Author Title Distributor Date Version Persistent identifier (such as the Digital Object Identifier, Uniform Resource Name URN, or Handle System)"
- 要点：数据引用必须包含能长期唯一识别数据集的基本要素：作者、标题、发布方、日期、版本、持久标识符（如 DOI）。

### E5

- 位置：Citing Data，How can I let ICPSR know about my publication?
- 原文："Users of ICPSR data are required to send us bibliographic citations for each completed manuscript or thesis abstract."
- 要点：使用 ICPSR 数据的人，须把每篇完成的稿件或学位论文摘要的书目信息发给 ICPSR。

### E6

- 位置：Data Confidentiality，Identifiers
- 原文："Indirect identifiers. These are variables that can be problematic as they may be used together or in conjunction with other information to identify individual respondents." / "Detailed geographic information (e.g., state, county, province, or census tract of residence)" / "Detailed occupational titles" / "Exact dates of events (e.g., birth, death, marriage, divorce)"
- 要点：间接识别变量单独看不暴露身份，但和其他信息组合起来就可能认出具体受访者（例如详细地理信息、所属组织、详细职业、确切的事件日期、详细收入）。

### E7

- 位置：Data Confidentiality，Identifiers
- 原文："If modifications to address identifiers to create a public-use dataset will seriously reduce the analytic utility of the data, ICPSR may release a restricted-use dataset or both public- and restricted-use datasets. Restricted-use datasets retain confidential, identifying information, and are accessible under controlled conditions."
- 要点：如果为了制作公开版数据而处理识别信息会严重损害数据的分析价值，ICPSR 可能发布受限使用版，它保留可识别信息，只在受控条件下开放。

### E8

- 位置：Data Confidentiality，Confidentiality, Informed Consent, and Data Sharing
- 原文："The informed consent must include a statement describing how the confidentiality of subject records will be maintained. However, it is also important that informed consent be written in a way that does not unduly limit an investigator's discretion to share data with the research community."
- 要点：知情同意书必须说明怎样为参与者保密；同时，同意书的写法不应过度限制研究者今后与研究共同体共享数据。

### E9

- 位置：Data Confidentiality，Confidentiality and IRBs
- 原文："Institutional Review Boards (IRBs) take different approaches to secondary analysis of research datasets such as those distributed on ICPSR's website. Some institutions require IRB review of proposals to analyze secondary data. Other institutions provide IRB exemption for projects involving secondary data if the data were acquired from preapproved sources such as ICPSR."
- 要点：各机构的伦理审查委员会对二次分析的处理不同：有的要求审查，有的对来自 ICPSR 等预先认可来源的数据给予豁免。
