# EdWorkingPapers：About EdWorkingPapers

- 资料：`edworkingpapersabout`，[原文](https://edworkingpapers.com/about)
- 版本：网页，2026 年 10 月读取。布朗大学安嫩伯格研究所（Annenberg Institute）主办的教育学工作论文系列的介绍页。

### E1

- 位置：About EdWorkingPapers
- 原文："offers this national working paper series to provide open access to high-quality papers from multiple disciplines and from multiple universities and research organizations on a wide variety of topics related to education."
- 要点：这是一个全国性的教育工作论文系列，开放获取，收录多个学科、多所大学和研究机构的教育研究论文。

### E2

- 位置：About EdWorkingPapers
- 原文："EdWorkingPapers circulates papers prior to publication for comment and discussion; these papers have not gone through a peer review process."
- 要点：论文在正式发表前发布，供评论和讨论；没有经过同行评审。

### E3

- 位置：Scope
- 原文："EdWorkingPapers primarily focuses on new research that holds significant implications for education policy and is oriented toward eventual publication in academic journals."
- 要点：主要收录对教育政策有重要意义、最终打算在学术期刊发表的新研究。
