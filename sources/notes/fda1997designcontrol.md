# 美国 FDA 1997：Design Control Guidance for Medical Device Manufacturers（已撤回）

- 资料：`fda1997designcontrol`，[原文（Internet Archive 快照）](https://web.archive.org/web/20251214094548/https://www.fda.gov/media/116573/download)
- 版本：美国食品药品监督管理局（Food and Drug Administration，FDA）器械与放射健康中心 1997 年 3 月 11 日发布的指南，解释旧版 21 CFR 820.30 的设计控制要求。FDA 官网对脚本访问返回 404，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2025-12-14 的快照。**这份指南已在 2026 年随新的质量管理体系法规实施而撤回**（见 `aabb2026qmsr`），这里只作为理解设计控制思路的历史资料引用。位置写节名和段落小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Section C. Design Input，"Research and Development"段
- 原文："Some manufacturers have difficulty in determining where research ends and development begins." / "It may be reasonable to develop a rapid prototype to explore the feasibility of an idea or design approach, for example, prior to developing design input requirements. But manufacturers should avoid falling into the trap of equating the prototype design with a finished product design."
- 要点：研究和开发的界线不好划。在写设计输入要求之前，先做一个快速原型来探索想法是否可行，是合理的；但不能把原型设计当成成品设计。

### E2

- 位置：Section C. Design Input，"Research and Development"段
- 原文："Prototypes at this stage lack safety features and ancillary functions necessary for a finished product, and are developed under conditions which preclude adequate consideration of product variability due to manufacturing."
- 要点：这个阶段的原型缺少成品必需的安全功能和辅助功能，也没法充分考虑批量制造带来的产品差异。

### E3

- 位置：Section C. Design Input，"Concept Documents versus Design Input"段
- 原文："Even the simplest of terms can have enormous design implications." / "Thus, a concept document may be the starting point for development, but it is not the design input requirement."
- 要点：再简单的说法（原文举的例子是"要便携"）也会牵出大量设计问题；概念文件可以是开发的起点，但不等于设计输入要求。

### E4

- 位置：Section C. Design Input，"Responsibility for Design Input Development"段
- 原文："Medical terminology is appropriate in requirements when the developers and reviewers are familiar with the language, but it is often preferable to translate the concepts into engineering terms at the requirements stage to minimize miscommunication with the development staff."
- 要点：开发和审查的人熟悉医学术语时，需求里可以用医学术语；但在需求阶段把概念翻译成工程语言，往往更能减少和开发人员之间的误解。

### E5

- 位置：Section C. Design Input，开头讨论部分
- 原文："If essential requirements are not identified until validation, expensive redesign and rework may be necessary before a design can be released to production."
- 要点：关键需求要是到确认阶段才发现，可能要付出昂贵的重新设计和返工。

### E6

- 位置：Introduction，设计控制示意图说明
- 原文："In practice, feedback paths would be required between each phase of the process and previous phases, representing the iterative nature of product development."
- 要点：示意图画成先后排列的阶段（"瀑布"式），但实际开发中各阶段之间要来回反馈，产品开发是迭代的。

### E7

- 位置：Introduction，设计控制示意图说明
- 原文："As the figure illustrates, design validation encompasses verification and extends the assessment to address whether devices produced in accordance with the design actually satisfy user needs and intended uses."
- 要点：设计确认包含验证，并进一步评估按设计生产出的器械是否真正满足用户需要和预期用途。

### E8

- 位置：Section B. Design and Development Planning
- 原文："For example, if clinical trials are anticipated, there may be tasks associated with appropriate regulatory requirements."
- 要点：如果预计要做临床试验，计划里就要列入相应的法规任务。

### E9

- 位置：Section F. Design Verification，"Types of Verification Activities"段
- 原文："The basis of verification is a three-pronged approach involving tests, inspections, and analyses."
- 要点：验证的三种基本手段是测试、检查和分析。
