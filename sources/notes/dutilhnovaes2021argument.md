# Dutilh Novaes 2021：Argument and Argumentation（斯坦福哲学百科全书）

- 资料：`dutilhnovaes2021argument`，[原文](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/argument/)
- 版本：《斯坦福哲学百科全书》条目，作者 Catarina Dutilh Novaes；2021 年 7 月 16 日首发。位置写节号。本文件是第 106 号任务的本地摘录。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："Argument is a central concept for philosophy. Philosophers rely heavily on arguments to justify claims"
- 要点：论证是哲学的核心概念；哲学家主要靠论证为主张辩护。

### E2

- 位置：§2.4 Analogy
- 原文："Analogical arguments continue to occupy a central position in philosophical discussions, and a number of the most prominent philosophical arguments of the last decades are analogical arguments"
- 要点：类比论证在哲学讨论中仍居中心位置；近几十年一些最著名的哲学论证就是类比论证（如汤姆森的小提琴家论证、塞尔的中文屋论证）。

### E3

- 位置：§2.4 Analogy
- 原文："thought experiments are often based on analogical principles when seeking to make a point that transcends the thought experiment as such."
- 要点：思想实验想得出超出它本身的结论时，常常依赖类比。

### E4

- 位置：§2.4 Analogy
- 原文："both arguments have been criticized on the grounds that the purported similarity between the source and the target domains is not sufficient to extrapolate the property of the source domain"
- 要点：类比论证的软肋：两边的相似性可能不足以把源情形的性质推到目标情形上。

### E5

- 位置：§3.1 Adversarial and cooperative argumentation
- 原文："Such aggressive practices reflect a vision of argumentation as a kind of competition or battle, where the goal is to "score points" and "beat the opponent"."
- 要点：好斗的做法把论辩看作比赛或战斗，目标是"得分""打败对手"。

### E6

- 位置：§3.1 Adversarial and cooperative argumentation
- 原文："these practices exclude a number of people from participating in argumentative encounters, namely those for whom displays of aggression do not constitute socially acceptable behavior"
- 要点：批评者认为，好斗的论辩会把一些人（对他们来说表现攻击性不被社会接受的人）排除在外。

### E7

- 位置：§3.1 Adversarial and cooperative argumentation
- 原文："adversariality conceived and practiced in different ways need not have the detrimental consequences of more extreme versions of belligerent argumentation."
- 要点：另一些学者认为，以不同方式理解和实践的"对抗性"，不一定有极端好斗论辩的那些坏后果。

### E8

- 位置：§3.1 Adversarial and cooperative argumentation
- 原文："It may be argued for example that actual argumentative encounters ought to be adversarial or cooperative to different degrees, as different types of argumentation are required for different situations"
- 要点：也可以认为，不同场合需要不同程度的对抗或合作。
