# Hopewell et al. 2025：CONSORT 2025 statement: Updated guideline for reporting randomised trials

- 资料：`hopewell2025consort`，[PLOS Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1004587)（CC BY 4.0）
- 版本：PLoS Med 22(4): e1004587，2025 年 4 月 14 日。同一声明同时发表在多家期刊。页码为 PDF 页序。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract，Results，PDF 第 2 页
- 原文："The CONSORT 2025 statement consists of a 30-item checklist of essential items that should be included when reporting the results of a randomised trial and a diagram for documenting the flow of participants through the trial."
- 要点：CONSORT 2025 包括一份 30 项的必报清单，和一张记录受试者在试验中流向的流程图。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction，PDF 第 3 页
- 原文："Randomised trials, when appropriately designed, conducted, analysed, and reported, are generally considered the highest quality evidence in evaluating healthcare interventions."
- 要点：设计、实施、分析和报告都恰当的随机试验，一般被认为是评价医疗干预的最高质量证据。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction，PDF 第 3 页
- 原文："having a clear and transparent trial protocol is important because it prespecifies the methods used in the trial, such as the primary outcome, thereby reducing the likelihood of undeclared post hoc changes"
- 要点：清楚透明的试验方案事先规定了主要结局等方法，减少事后悄悄改动的可能。

### E4

- 位置：Introduction，PDF 第 3 页
- 原文："Similar problems related to the lack of complete and transparent reporting of trial protocols led to the development of the SPIRIT (Standard Protocol Items: Recommendations for Interventional Trials) statement, published in 2013"
- 要点：针对试验方案报告不完整的问题，有专门的方案报告规范 SPIRIT（2013 年首发）。

### E5

- 位置：Main changes to CONSORT 2025，PDF 第 5 页
- 原文："We also restructured the CONSORT checklist, with a new section on open science, which includes items that are conceptually linked, such as trial registration (item 2), where the trial protocol and statistical analysis plan can be accessed (item 3), sharing of de-identified participant level data (item 4), and funding and conflicts of interest (item 5)."
- 要点：2025 版新增"开放科学"部分，包括试验登记、方案和统计分析计划在哪里获取、去标识化个体数据的共享、资金与利益冲突。

### E6

- 位置：Implication and limitations，PDF 第 7 页
- 原文："It is important to note that CONSORT 2025 and SPIRIT 2025 do not include recommendations for designing, conducting, or analysing trials"
- 要点：CONSORT 和 SPIRIT 不讲怎样设计、实施和分析试验。

### E7

- 位置：Implication and limitations，PDF 第 7 页
- 原文："Importantly, however, CONSORT 2025 is not meant to be used as a quality assessment instrument."
- 要点：CONSORT 不是用来给试验质量打分的工具。

### E8

- 位置：表 1 之后的说明段落，PDF 第 9 页
- 原文："We encourage trial authors to detail what was done and to acknowledge if something was not done or was modified, ensuring alignment of information with that reported in the trial protocol, statistical analysis plan, and trial registry."
- 要点：作者要写清做了什么、承认哪些没做或改了，并保证论文与试验方案、统计分析计划和登记记录一致。
