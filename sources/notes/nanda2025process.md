# Nanda 2025：How I Think About My Research Process: Explore, Understand, Distill

- 资料：`nanda2025process`，[原文](https://www.alignmentforum.org/s/5GT3yoYM9gRmMEKqL/p/hjMy4ZxS5ogA9cTYK)
- 版本：AI Alignment Forum 帖子，2025-04-26，2026-10-05 读取。位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Exploration (Stage 2): Gain surface area
- 原文："Your north star is just to gain information - do exploratory experiments, visualise data, follow your curiosity, prioritise moving fast."
- 要点：探索阶段的目标只是获取信息：做探索性实验、把数据可视化、跟着好奇心走、优先求快。

### E2

- 位置：Exploration (Stage 2): Gain surface area
- 原文："Junior researchers often get stuck in the early stages of a project and don’t know what to do next. In my opinion this is because they think they are in the understanding stage, but are actually in the exploration stage."
- 要点：新手在项目早期卡住，常常是因为以为自己该有明确假设（理解阶段），其实还处在探索阶段。

### E3

- 位置：Exploration (Stage 2): Gain surface area
- 原文："Frequently ask yourself “am I getting enough information per unit time?” If you haven’t learned anything recently, shake it up."
- 要点：经常问自己单位时间内得到的信息够不够；最近没学到新东西，就换个做法。

### E4

- 位置：Exploration (Stage 2): Gain surface area
- 原文："A key practical tip is to keep a highlights doc of particularly interesting results, this makes it easier to spot connections"
- 要点：单独记一份"亮点文档"，收集特别有意思的结果，便于发现它们之间的联系。

### E5

- 位置：Understanding (Stage 3): Test Hypotheses
- 原文："This stage begins when you understand the problem domain enough to have some specific hypotheses that you think are interesting - hypotheses you can write down, and have some idea of what evidence you could find to show if they’re true or false."
- 要点：进入聚焦（检验假设）阶段的标志：能写下具体假设，并知道用什么证据判断它真假。

### E6

- 位置：Distillation (Stage 4): Compress, Refine, Communicate
- 原文："Sometimes you’ll discover that actually things are way messier than thought. [...] You may need to go back to understanding or even exploration - this is totally fine and normal, and does not mean you’ve screwed anything up."
- 要点：发现事情比想象复杂时，退回到检验假设甚至探索阶段是正常的，不代表你搞砸了。

### E7

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Take breaks, the total change to productive time is typically positive. Find sustainable ways to work."
- 要点：休息通常让有效工作时间净增加；要找可持续的工作方式。
