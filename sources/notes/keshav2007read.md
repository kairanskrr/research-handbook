# Keshav 2007：How to Read a Paper

- 资料：`keshav2007read`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1145/1273445.1273458)
- 版本：ACM SIGCOMM Computer Communication Review 37(3), 2007；本地 PDF `papers/pdf/keshav2007read.pdf`

### E1

- 位置：§2 The Three-Pass Approach
- 原文："The key idea is that you should read the paper in up to three passes, instead of starting at the beginning and plowing your way to the end. Each pass accomplishes speciﬁc goals and builds upon the previous pass"
- 要点：不要从头到尾硬读，而是最多分三遍读，每一遍有自己的目标，后一遍建立在前一遍之上。

### E2

- 位置：§2.1 The first pass
- 原文："The ﬁrst pass is a quick scan to get a bird’s-eye view of the paper. You can also decide whether you need to do any more passes. This pass should take about ﬁve to ten minutes"
- 要点：第一遍是 5～10 分钟的快速浏览，得到全貌，并决定还要不要继续读。

### E3

- 位置：§2.1 The first pass
- 原文："1. Carefully read the title, abstract, and introduction 2. Read the section and sub-section headings, but ignore everything else 3. Read the conclusions"
- 要点：第一遍只读标题、摘要、引言、各级小标题和结论，再扫一眼参考文献。

### E4

- 位置：§2.1 The first pass
- 原文："At the end of the ﬁrst pass, you should be able to answer the ﬁve Cs: 1. Category: What type of paper is this? [...] 2. Context: Which other papers is it related to? [...] 3. Correctness: Do the assumptions appear to be valid? 4. Contributions: What are the paper’s main contributions? 5. Clarity: Is the paper well written?"
- 要点：第一遍结束时要能回答五个问题：论文类型、与哪些工作相关、假设是否成立、主要贡献、写得是否清楚。

### E5

- 位置：§2.2 The second pass
- 原文："In the second pass, read the paper with greater care, but ignore details such as proofs. It helps to jot down the key points, or to make comments in the margins, as you read."
- 要点：第二遍读得更仔细，但跳过证明等细节；边读边记要点、写批注。

### E6

- 位置：§2.2 The second pass
- 原文："The second pass should take up to an hour. After this pass, you should be able to grasp the content of the paper. You should be able to summarize the main thrust of the paper, with supporting evidence, to someone else."
- 要点：第二遍最多一小时，读完应能向别人概括论文主旨和支撑证据。

### E7

- 位置：§2.3 The third pass
- 原文："The key to the third pass is to attempt to virtually re-implement the paper: that is, making the same assumptions as the authors, re-create the work. By comparing this re-creation with the actual paper, you can easily identify not only a paper’s innovations, but also its hidden failings and assumptions."
- 要点：第三遍的关键是在脑中"重做"这项工作，和原文对比，从而看出创新点，也看出隐藏的缺陷和假设。

### E8

- 位置：§2.3 The third pass
- 原文："This pass can take about four or ﬁve hours for beginners, and about an hour for an experienced reader. At the end of this pass, you should be able to reconstruct the entire structure of the paper from memory, as well as be able to identify its strong and weak points."
- 要点：第三遍新手要四五个小时；读完应能凭记忆重建论文结构，指出优缺点。

### E9

- 位置：§3 Doing a Literature Survey
- 原文："First, use an academic search engine such as Google Scholar or CiteSeer and some well-chosen keywords to ﬁnd three to ﬁve recent papers in the area. Do one pass on each paper to get a sense of the work, then read their related work sections."
- 要点：文献调研第一步：用学术搜索引擎和精选关键词找三到五篇近期论文，各读一遍，再读它们的相关工作部分。

### E10

- 位置：§3 Doing a Literature Survey
- 原文："Otherwise, in the second step, ﬁnd shared citations and repeated author names in the bibliography. These are the key papers and researchers in that area."
- 要点：第二步：在这些论文的参考文献里找被反复引用的论文和反复出现的作者，他们就是该领域的关键论文和关键研究者。

### E11

- 位置：§3 Doing a Literature Survey
- 原文："The third step is to go to the website for these top conferences and look through their recent proceedings. [...] If they all cite a key paper that you did not ﬁnd earlier, obtain and read it, iterating as necessary."
- 要点：第三步：翻阅顶级会议近期的论文集；如果它们都引用了一篇你漏掉的关键论文，就找来读，必要时反复迭代。
