# 中国 2020：《中华人民共和国生物安全法》（全国人大官网英文本）

- 资料：`npc2020biosecurity`，[原文（全国人大英文网站）](https://subsites.chinadaily.com.cn/npc/2020-10/17/c_703568.htm)
- 版本：《中华人民共和国生物安全法》（Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China），2020 年 10 月 17 日第十三届全国人大常委会第二十二次会议通过，2021 年 4 月 15 日起施行。本摘录读的是全国人大英文网站（由中国日报承办）发布的英文本，共 6 页；以后的修订本文没有核对。中文原文以全国人大官网为准。位置写条款号。

### E1

- 位置：主席令第 58 号
- 原文："The Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China , adopted at the 22nd Meeting of the Standing Committee of the Thirteenth National People's Congress of the People's Republic of China on October 17, 2020, is hereby promulgated, and shall come into force as of April 15, 2021."
- 要点：《生物安全法》2020 年 10 月 17 日通过，2021 年 4 月 15 日起施行。

### E2

- 位置：Article 36
- 原文："The state adopts classified management as regards biotechnology research and development activities. These activities shall be classified as high-risk, medium-risk, and low-risk activities according to the degree of potential harm to the public health, industry, agriculture, and ecological environment."
- 要点：国家对生物技术研究、开发活动分类管理，按对公众健康、工业、农业、生态环境的潜在危害程度，分为高风险、中风险、低风险三类。

### E3

- 位置：Article 38
- 原文："High-risk and medium-risk biotechnology research and development activities may be conducted only by the legal persons lawfully established within the territory of China with approval or after being put on record in accordance with law. Risk assessments shall be conducted when carrying out medium-risk and high-risk biotechnology research and development activities"
- 要点：高风险、中风险的生物技术研发活动，只能由在中国境内依法成立的法人组织经批准或备案后开展；开展这两类活动要做风险评估。

### E4

- 位置：Article 45
- 原文："The state adopts pathogenic microorganism laboratory hierarchical management in accordance with the level of biosecurity protection in relation to pathogenic microorganism. Pathogenic microorganism experiments shall be conducted in laboratories at the corresponding levels."
- 要点：国家按生物安全防护水平对病原微生物实验室分等级管理，病原微生物实验要在相应等级的实验室进行。
