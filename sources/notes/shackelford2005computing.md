# Shackelford 等人 2005：Computing Curricula 2005 — The Overview Report

- 资料：`shackelford2005computing`，[原文](https://www.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/education/curricula-recommendations/cc2005-march06final.pdf)
- 版本：ACM、AIS、IEEE 计算机学会联合工作组发布的计算学科课程总报告，2005 年 9 月（2006 年 3 月定稿版 PDF）。位置写小节和页码。

### E1

- 位置：§2.2.1 Before the 1990s，第 9 页
- 原文："For students who wanted to become expert in developing software or with the theoretical aspects of computing, computer science was the obvious choice. For students who wanted to work with hardware, electrical engineering was the clear option."
- 要点：1990 年代以前，想做软件和计算理论的学生选计算机科学，想做硬件的选电气工程。

### E2

- 位置：§2.2.1 Before the 1990s，第 10 页
- 原文："computer scientists and electrical engineers sometimes worked closely together since they were both concerned with developing new technology, were often housed in the same part of the university, and sometimes required each others' help."
- 要点：计算机科学家和电气工程师都在开发新技术，常在大学同一学院，有时需要彼此帮忙，因此合作紧密。

### E3

- 位置：§2.2.1 Before the 1990s，第 10 页
- 原文："With the advent of the microprocessor in the mid-1970s, computer engineering began to emerge from within electrical engineering to become a discipline unto itself."
- 要点：1970 年代中期微处理器出现后，计算机工程从电气工程中分化出来，成为独立学科。

### E4

- 位置：§2.3.1 Computer Engineering，第 13 页
- 原文："Computer engineering is concerned with the design and construction of computers and computer-based systems. It involves the study of hardware, software, communications, and the interaction among them."
- 要点：计算机工程研究计算机和基于计算机的系统的设计与制造，涉及硬件、软件、通信及其相互作用。

### E5

- 位置：§2.2.3 After the 1990s，第 12 页
- 原文："for CE, software attention is focused on hardware devices; for SE, the emphasis is on creating software that satisfies robust real-world requirements; and for CS, software is the currency in which ideas are expressed"
- 要点：计算机工程关注的是硬件设备上的软件；计算机科学则把软件当作表达想法的媒介。

### E6

- 位置：§3.4.1 Computer Engineering，第 30 页
- 原文："At some universities, electrical engineering and computer science reside in the same school, and computer engineering is seen as a natural merging of interests among faculty in these two disciplines."
- 要点：有的大学里电气工程和计算机科学在同一个学院，计算机工程被看成两者兴趣的自然交汇。
