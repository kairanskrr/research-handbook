# 美国地理学家协会 2024：Statement of Professional Ethics

- 资料：`aag2024ethics`，[原文](https://aag.org/statement-of-professional-ethics)
- 版本：美国地理学家协会（American Association of Geographers，AAG）职业伦理声明网页，1998 年通过，最近一次修订为 2024 年 6 月 12 日（见 E18）。2026 年 10 月读取。位置写节号和小标题。

### E1

- 位置：I. Preamble
- 原文："Geographical scholarship spans the natural and social sciences as well as the humanities and arts and is undertaken in many different social and environmental contexts."
- 要点：地理学横跨自然科学、社会科学以及人文和艺术，在许多不同的社会和环境背景下开展。

### E2

- 位置：I. Preamble
- 原文："Members of the AAG are required to abide by AAG’s Professional Conduct Policy and Procedures, and many geographers must also conform to ethical requirements related to research with human subjects as interpreted and enforced by institutions and funders."
- 要点：AAG 会员必须遵守 AAG 的职业行为政策；许多地理学者还要遵守所在机构和资助方执行的人类受试者研究伦理要求。

### E3

- 位置：II. Do Good
- 原文："The principle of respect acknowledges that all geographical knowledge is situated"
- 要点：尊重原则承认，一切地理知识都是"有处境的"（situated），即和产生它的人与场合有关。

### E4

- 位置：II. Respect Research Participants
- 原文："An important sign of respect and care in geographical scholarship involving human subjects is conducting research with, rather than on, participants and avoiding exploitative or extractive research."
- 要点：涉及人的地理研究，尊重的重要标志是"和"参与者一起做研究，而不是"对"参与者做研究，避免剥削性、索取式的研究。

### E5

- 位置：II. Respect Research Participants
- 原文："Geographers should also carefully reflect upon how we represent ourselves, research participants, and places in our research, teaching, and professional life."
- 要点：地理学者要认真反思自己在研究中怎样呈现自己、研究参与者和地方。

### E6

- 位置：III. Do No Harm
- 原文："The obligation to do no harm should supersede other goals of seeking or communicating new knowledge."
- 要点：不造成伤害的义务，高于获取或传播新知识的目标。

### E7

- 位置：III. Recognize Power Hierarchies
- 原文："We frequently occupy powerful positions relative to our research participants, and it is all too easy for us to be unaware of, or to forget, the impact that these power imbalances can have on those affected by geographical research."
- 要点：研究者相对参与者常处于强势地位，很容易意识不到或忘记这种权力不平等对受研究影响者的作用。

### E8

- 位置：IV. Collaborate with Care
- 原文："The scope of collaboration, rights, and responsibilities of those participating, co-authorship, credit, and acknowledgment should be openly and fairly established at the outset."
- 要点：合作范围、各方权利和责任、合著署名、功劳和致谢，应在一开始就公开、公平地商定。

### E9

- 位置：VI. Obtain Informed Consent
- 原文："The consent process should be a part of project design and continue through implementation as an ongoing dialogue and negotiation with research participants."
- 要点：知情同意要纳入项目设计，并在实施过程中作为与参与者持续的对话和协商。

### E10

- 位置：VI. Obtain Institutional Approval
- 原文："Geographers whose research involves humans, based in countries where there is an Institutional Review Board (IRB) or similar process, must obtain institutional approval [...] In countries where there are no IRB processes, geographers, should obtain permission from the communities they will contribute to."
- 要点：所在国家有机构审查委员会（IRB）或类似程序的，涉及人的研究必须获得机构批准；没有这类程序的国家，应取得相关社区的许可。

### E11

- 位置：VI. Take Informed Consent Seriously
- 原文："the right to refuse research goes beyond specific individuals approached through the IRB process, and also includes the right of communities to refuse participation. [...] It is the quality of the consent, not its format, which is relevant."
- 要点：拒绝参与研究的权利不只属于个人，也包括社区整体拒绝参与的权利；知情同意看的是质量，不是形式（不一定要书面签字）。

### E12

- 位置：VI. Share Findings
- 原文："Whenever appropriate, results of research should be shared with research participants, local colleagues, host agencies, and affected persons and communities in a format that is accessible to them."
- 要点：在合适时，应以对方看得懂的形式，把研究结果分享给参与者、当地同行、接待机构和受影响的人与社区。

### E13

- 位置：VI. Make Data Available
- 原文："in some situations, generalization or other measures such as the use of pseudonyms will be necessary to protect privacy, confidentiality, and limit exposure to risks."
- 要点：有些情况下需要对数据做概括化处理或使用化名等措施，以保护隐私和保密、降低风险。

### E14

- 位置：VI. Protect Privacy and Confidentiality
- 原文："(1) automated tracking of the locations and movements of individuals or vehicles; (2) the use of images from satellites, aircraft, UAVs (drones), or ground-based sensors that are of sufficient resolution to identify individuals or vehicles; (3) the use of high resolution geographic location to link data in ways that violate personal confidentiality; and (4) any use of big data that compromises privacy, confidentiality, or violates other ethical principles in this Statement, even when such data is considered publicly available."
- 要点：AAG 列出四类特别容易引发隐私和保密问题、需格外小心的地理空间研究：自动追踪个人或车辆的位置和移动；使用足以认出个人或车辆的卫星、航空、无人机或地面传感器影像；用高分辨率位置去关联数据而泄露个人信息；任何损害隐私的大数据使用，即使数据被认为是公开的。

### E15

- 位置：VII. Disclose Funding Sources
- 原文："All funding sources, affiliations, sponsorships, and partnerships should be fully disclosed in an understandable manner at the time that informed consent is requested from research participants"
- 要点：在征求参与者知情同意时，就要以易懂的方式完整披露所有资金来源、所属机构、赞助方和合作方。

### E16

- 位置：VII. Be Transparent
- 原文："Disclosure of funding sources is required for publication in AAG journals and for presentations at the Annual Meeting of the AAG and at the meetings of its regional divisions."
- 要点：在 AAG 期刊发表、在 AAG 年会及地区分会会议上报告，都必须披露资金来源。

### E17

- 位置：VIII. Weigh Competing Ethical Obligations
- 原文："These considerations may also include geographers’ own safety, especially if they are a member of a marginalized group, or in cases where research participants, funders, or sponsors are in a position of power over the researcher."
- 要点：权衡伦理义务时也要考虑研究者自身的安全，尤其是研究者属于边缘群体，或参与者、资助方对研究者有权力时。

### E18

- 位置：页首
- 原文："Endorsed by the Council of the American Association of Geographers: October 18, 1998; updated April 5, 2005; revised November 1, 2009; revised March 15, 2021; and revised June 12, 2024."
- 要点：声明 1998 年经 AAG 理事会通过，2005、2009、2021、2024 年多次更新修订。
