# 口述历史协会 2019：Archives Principles and Best Practices（口述历史存档最佳实践）

- 资料：`oha2019archiving`，[网页版](https://oralhistory.org/archives-principles-and-best-practices/)，[完整版 PDF](https://oralhistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/OHA-Archives-Principles-and-Best-Practices-Complete-Manual.pdf)
- 版本：美国口述历史协会（Oral History Association，OHA）2019 年 10 月通过，是其 2018 年《原则与最佳实践》的附录，由 OHA 存档最佳实践工作组撰写。读取网页版，2026-10-06。位置写小节名。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction，及脚注 1
- 原文："Some archiving plans may eschew traditional repositories, particularly when working with vulnerable communities, or when inclusion in a repository carries the potential for legal or personal danger." / "Examples include interviews conducted with undocumented immigrants who would be at risk of deportation or survivors of domestic violence or stalking who risk being located and harmed by former abusers."
- 要点：和弱势群体合作，或存进收藏机构可能带来法律或人身危险时，存档计划可以不走传统收藏机构。例如无证移民（有被遣返风险），家暴或跟踪的幸存者（有被施暴者找到的风险）。

### E2

- 位置：Metadata and Description，Overview
- 原文："It is important to collect the following types of information or metadata categories: administrative, descriptive, technical, preservation, and rights and access."
- 要点：要收集五类元数据：管理、描述、技术、保存、权利与查阅。

### E3

- 位置：Metadata and Description，What to Collect
- 原文："Descriptive metadata is information that is necessary for the discovery of oral history interviews and that documents the content and the context of the resource (e.g., the name of the interviewer and narrator, the date and place of the interview, and content information in the form of keywords, summary, and controlled vocabulary)."
- 要点：描述元数据用于让人找到访谈，记录内容和背景，例如访谈者和受访者姓名、访谈日期和地点、关键词、摘要、规范词表。

### E4

- 位置：Preservation，How to Preserve
- 原文："Maintaining a backup is not the same as having a fully vested archival program."
- 要点：有备份不等于有了完整的存档。

### E5

- 位置：Preservation，What to Preserve
- 原文："A legal release signed by the narrator, interviewer, and any others present during the interview."
- 要点：随录音一起保存的文件至少包括：受访者、访谈者和在场其他人签字的法律授权书。

### E6

- 位置：Access，Permissions and Ethical Use
- 原文："Oral histories are co-created by the interviewer and the narrator and copyright is most often considered as belonging to both parties unless or until they sign an agreement to transfer that right."
- 要点：口述历史由访谈者和受访者共同创作，在签协议转让之前，版权通常被认为归双方所有。

### E7

- 位置：Access，Permissions and Ethical Use
- 原文："If a potential donor does not have either recorded verbal or legal release for an interview, it is not recommended that the archive accept the donation"
- 要点：如果捐赠者既没有录下的口头授权，也没有法律授权书，不建议档案馆接收。

### E8

- 位置：Access，Transcription
- 原文："Because human transcribers and automated transcription services are both fallible, it is best practice to review first-draft transcripts for accuracy." / "All transcripts should be reviewed before publishing, carefully checking to ensure that proper names are spelled correctly and that dates reflect what is said on the recording."
- 要点：人工和自动转录都会出错，最佳做法是核对转录初稿；发布前都要审校，特别检查专有名词拼写和日期是否与录音一致。

### E9

- 位置：Access，Transcription
- 原文："In addition to, or in lieu of (if transcription isn’t feasible), a transcript, the interview could be indexed to make it more searchable."
- 要点：转录不可行时，可以用索引代替转录稿，方便检索。

### E10

- 位置：Ownership and Rights Management，Definitions of Ownership
- 原文："These physical carriers can be owned by the narrator, repository, or other third parties, regardless of the owner of the intellectual property."
- 要点：录音载体的所有权和知识产权（版权）可以属于不同的人。

### E11

- 位置：Ownership and Rights Management，Definitions of Ownership
- 原文："Legal realities of ownership of physical property or intellectual property should not be used to alienate narrators from their stories."
- 要点：不能拿法律上的所有权把受访者和他们自己的故事隔开。

### E12

- 位置：Ownership and Rights Management，Narrator Rights in an Archival Relationship
- 原文："In some cases, a narrator’s position on the public’s access and use of their oral history may evolve, resulting in requests for the repository to increase or limit access or to remove the oral history from the collection altogether."
- 要点：受访者对公开的态度可能会变，可能要求扩大或限制查阅，甚至撤出整份访谈。

### E13

- 位置：Appraisal & Accessioning，Accessioning
- 原文："Establish a numbering or naming system to uniquely identify interviews and their components to ensure that materials are unambiguously connected to any information about them."
- 要点：建立编号或命名规则，让每份访谈及其组成部分都有唯一标识，并和相关信息明确对应。

### E14

- 位置：Ownership and Rights Management，Documenting Provenance and Context of Interview
- 原文："Language in contracts and agreements—including informed consent agreements, legal release forms, and deeds of gift—should clearly articulate the type of rights that have been discussed and transferred, and they should also include and reference examples of known and potential uses of oral history interviews."
- 要点：知情同意书、授权书、捐赠契据要写清讨论过、转让了哪些权利，并列出已知和可能的用途。
