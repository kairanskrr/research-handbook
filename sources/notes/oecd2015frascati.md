# OECD 2015：Frascati Manual 2015: Guidelines for Collecting and Reporting Data on Research and Experimental Development

- 资料：`oecd2015frascati`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1787/9789264239012-en)（OECD 官方免费 PDF：[链接](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2015/10/frascati-manual-2015_g1g57dcb/9789264239012-en.pdf)）
- 版本：2015 版。OECD（经济合作与发展组织）制定的研发统计手册，各国统计"什么算研究"都以它为准，适用于所有学科，包括人文社科和艺术。

### E1

- 位置：第 2 章 §2.2，第 2.5 段（第 44 页）
- 原文："Research and experimental development (R&D) comprise creative and systematic work undertaken in order to increase the stock of knowledge [...] and to devise new applications of available knowledge."
- 要点：研究与试验发展的定义：为增加知识存量、或为现有知识设计新应用而进行的创造性、系统性工作。

### E2

- 位置：第 2 章 §2.2，第 2.6–2.7 段（第 44 页）
- 原文："R&D is always aimed at new findings, based on original concepts (and their interpretation) or hypotheses. [...] For an activity to be an R&D activity, it must satisfy five core criteria. [...] The activity must be: novel creative uncertain systematic transferable and/or reproducible."
- 要点：研究总是以新发现为目标；一项活动要算研究，必须同时满足五条标准：新颖、有创造性、结果不确定、系统、可转移和/或可复现。

### E3

- 位置：第 2 章 §2.4，第 2.16 段（第 46 页）
- 原文："Novelty could result from a project to reproduce an existing result that finds potential discrepancies."
- 要点：重复已有结果并发现可能的不一致，也可以算"新颖"。

### E4

- 位置：第 2 章 §2.4，第 2.18 段（第 46 页）
- 原文："At the outset of an R&D project, the kind of outcome and the cost (including time allocation) cannot be precisely determined relative to the goals."
- 要点："不确定"：开始时无法准确知道会得到什么结果、要花多少时间和成本。

### E5

- 位置：第 2 章 §2.4，第 2.19 段（第 46 页）
- 原文："In this context "systematic" means that the R&D is conducted in a planned way, with records kept of both the process followed and the outcome."
- 要点："系统"：有计划地进行，并记录过程和结果。

### E6

- 位置：第 2 章 §2.4，第 2.20 段（第 47 页）
- 原文："An R&D project should result in the potential for the transfer of the new knowledge, ensuring its use and allowing other researchers to reproduce the results as part of their own R&D activities. This includes R&D that has negative results, in the case that an initial hypothesis fails to be confirmed or a product cannot be developed as originally intended."
- 要点："可转移/可复现"：新知识要能传给别人、让别人复现；负面结果（假设没被证实）同样算。

### E7

- 位置：第 2 章 §2.4，第 2.20 段（第 47 页）
- 原文："As the purpose of R&D is to increase of the existing stock of knowledge, the results cannot remain tacit (i.e. remain solely in the minds of the researchers), as they, and the associated knowledge, would be at risk of being lost."
- 要点：研究结果不能只留在研究者脑子里，否则知识会丢失；必须记录并传播。

### E8

- 位置：第 2 章 §2.2，第 2.9 段（第 44–45 页）
- 原文："Basic research is experimental or theoretical work undertaken primarily to acquire new knowledge of the underlying foundations of phenomena and observable facts, without any particular application or use in view. Applied research is original investigation undertaken in order to acquire new knowledge. It is, however, directed primarily towards a specific, practical aim or objective."
- 要点：基础研究为获得关于现象本质的新知识，不以特定应用为目标；应用研究也为获得新知识，但主要指向具体的实际目标。

### E9

- 位置：第 2 章 §2.2，第 2.9 段（第 45 页）
- 原文："Experimental development is systematic work, drawing on knowledge gained from research and practical experience and producing additional knowledge, which is directed to producing new products or processes or to improving existing products or processes."
- 要点：试验发展利用已有知识并产生新知识，目标是做出新产品、新流程或改进现有的。

### E10

- 位置：第 2 章 §2.2，第 2.11 段（第 45 页）
- 原文："The order in which the three types of R&D activity appear is not meant to suggest that basic research leads to applied research and then to experimental development."
- 要点：三类研究不是先后顺序或高低等级，它们之间知识双向流动。
