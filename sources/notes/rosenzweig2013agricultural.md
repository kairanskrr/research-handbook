# Rosenzweig 等人 2013：The Agricultural Model Intercomparison and Improvement Project (AgMIP): Protocols and pilot studies

- 资料：`rosenzweig2013agricultural`，[原文 PDF](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20150002678/downloads/20150002678.pdf)（DOI 10.1016/j.agrformet.2012.09.011）
- 版本：Agricultural and Forest Meteorology 170:166–182，2013。美国国家航空航天局技术报告服务器（NASA Technical Reports Server）公开的期刊排版版本；该服务器对脚本返回 403，经互联网档案馆 2024 年 12 月的快照下载。位置写小节编号。

### E1

- 位置：§3.2 Crop Modeling Team Protocols，目标第 1 条
- 原文："Evaluate models for a range of crops and regions by comparing outputs with observed growth and yield data, including responses to [CO2], temperature, water shortage, water excess, and interactions with management factors."
- 要点：作物建模团队的第一个目标：在多种作物和地区上，拿模型输出和观测到的生长、产量数据比，以此评价模型，包括对二氧化碳浓度、温度、缺水、涝和管理措施的反应。

### E2

- 位置：§3.2 Crop Modeling Team Protocols
- 原文："multiple models for the same crop are initially calibrated using Sentinel Site-specific datasets" / "The primary goal of this activity is to obtain an estimate of crop model uncertainty calculated from the responses of an ensemble of crop models for a given crop and region."
- 要点：同一种作物用多个模型，先用定点站（Sentinel Site）的数据校准；这项工作的主要目的，是用一组模型的不同反应来估计作物模型的不确定性。

### E3

- 位置：§3.2 Crop Modeling Team Protocols
- 原文："Calibration of model parameters and code improvement by the crop modelers are documented."
- 要点：建模者对模型参数的校准和代码的改进都要记录下来。

### E4

- 位置：§5.1 AgMIP protocol examples 中的小麦试点（Obregón, Mexico）
- 原文："Most of the variability in absolute yield response to a given emission scenario was caused by the five crop models rather than by differences in the five GCMs. For example, 88% of the simulated variance in A2 yields was a first-order effect due to variability between crop models (which may be influenced by biases in the baseline calibration), 10% was the first-order effect due to variability between GCMs"
- 要点：在墨西哥奥夫雷贡附近的小麦试点中，产量变化的差异主要来自 5 个作物模型之间，而不是 5 个全球气候模型之间；A2 情景下模拟产量的方差 88% 来自作物模型之间的差异（可能受基准期校准偏差影响），10% 来自气候模型之间的差异。
