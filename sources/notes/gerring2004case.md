# Gerring 2004：What Is a Case Study and What Is It Good For?

- 资料：`gerring2004case`，[作者在波士顿大学个人主页上挂的 PDF（Internet Archive 快照）](https://web.archive.org/web/2016/http://blogs.bu.edu/jgerring/files/2013/06/CasestudyAPSR.pdf)
- 版本：American Political Science Review 98(2): 341–354 的出版社排版 PDF，由作者放在个人主页上；位置写期刊页码。

### E1

- 位置：摘要，第 341 页
- 原文："A "case study," I argue, is best defined as an intensive study of a single unit with an aim to generalize across a larger set of units."
- 要点：案例研究最好定义为：深入研究一个单元，目的是推广到更大的一批单元。

### E2

- 位置：Scope of Proposition，第 348 页
- 原文："Research designs invariably face a choice between knowing more about less and knowing less about more."
- 要点：研究设计总要在"对少数对象知道得多"和"对多数对象知道得少"之间选择。

### E3

- 位置：Unit Homogeneity，第 348 页
- 原文："Single-unit research designs often fall short in their representativeness—the degree to which causal relationships evidenced by that single unit may be assumed to be true for a larger set of (unstudied) units."
- 要点：单案例设计的弱点常在代表性：在这个单元里看到的因果关系，能否认为在更多没研究的单元里也成立。

### E4

- 位置：Causal Insight，第 349 页
- 原文："Ceteris paribus, case studies are more likely to shed light on causal mechanisms and less likely to shed light on true causal effects."
- 要点：在其他条件相同时，案例研究更擅长揭示因果机制，不擅长估计真实的因果效应大小。

### E5

- 位置：Strategy of Research，第 350 页
- 原文："The point is, theory confirmation/disconfirmation is not the case study's strong suit."
- 要点：证实或证伪理论不是案例研究的强项。

### E6

- 位置：Strategy of Research，第 350 页
- 原文："I do not mean to suggest that case studies never serve a confirmatory role. As discussed, evidence drawn from a single unit may disconfirm a necessary or sufficient hypothesis."
- 要点：案例研究并非不能做验证：单个单元的证据可以否定一个"必要"或"充分"条件式的假设。

### E7

- 位置：Conclusion，第 352 页
- 原文："case studies are generally more useful (1) when inferences are descriptive rather than causal, (2) when propositional depth is prized over breadth and boundedness, (3) when (internal) case comparability is given precedence over (external) case representativeness, (4) when insight into causal mechanisms is more important than insight into causal effects, (5) when the causal proposition at issue is invariant rather than probabilistic, (6) when the strategy of research is exploratory, rather than confirmatory, and (7) when useful variance is available for only a single unit or a small number of units."
- 要点：案例研究在七种情况下更有用：描述性推断、重深度、重案例可比性、重因果机制、命题是确定性的、探索性研究、有用的变化只存在于少数单元。

### E8

- 位置：Conclusion，第 353 页
- 原文："A field dominated by case studies may have little need for another case study. A field where cross-unit studies are hegemonic may be desperately in need of in-depth studies focused on single units."
- 要点：一个领域已满是案例研究，可能不再需要又一个；跨单元研究一统天下的领域，可能急需深入的单案例研究。
