# 美国教育科学研究所与国家科学基金会 2013：Common Guidelines for Education Research and Development

- 资料：`ies2013common`，[原文 PDF](https://ies.ed.gov/pdf/commonguidelines.pdf)
- 版本：2013 年 8 月，美国教育部教育科学研究所（IES）和美国国家科学基金会（National Science Foundation，NSF）联合发布。位置写章节标题。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Most simply, the six types of research described in this document form a "pipeline" of evidence that begins with basic and exploratory research, moves to design and development of interventions or strategies, and, for interventions or strategies with initial promise, results in examination of the effectiveness for improving learning or another related education outcome."
- 要点：文件描述的六类研究，最简单地看构成一条证据"管道"：从基础和探索性研究，到干预的设计与开发，再到对有希望的干预检验其效果。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："However, as we describe later in this document, the reality of scientific investigation is more complicated, less orderly, and less linear than such a "pipeline" suggests."
- 要点：但实际的科学研究比"管道"更复杂、更不按顺序、更不线性。

### E3

- 位置：Types of Research
- 原文："Research Type #2: Early-Stage or Exploratory Research examines relationships among important constructs in education and learning to establish logical connections that may form the basis for future interventions or strategies to improve education outcomes. These connections are usually correlational rather than causal."
- 要点：早期或探索性研究考察教育中重要概念之间的关系，为将来设计干预打基础；这些关系通常是相关的，不是因果的。

### E4

- 位置：Types of Research
- 原文："Research projects of this type draw on existing theory and evidence to design and iteratively develop interventions or strategies, including testing individual components to provide feedback in the development process."
- 要点：设计与开发研究依据已有理论和证据，反复迭代地设计、开发干预，包括测试单个组成部分来为开发提供反馈。

### E5

- 位置：Types of Research
- 原文："Research Type #4: Efficacy Research allows for testing of a strategy or intervention under "ideal" circumstances, including with a higher level of support or developer involvement than would be the case under normal circumstances."
- 要点：效力研究在"理想"条件下检验干预，例如开发者提供比平时更多的支持。

### E6

- 位置：Types of Research
- 原文："Research Type #5: Effectiveness Research examines effectiveness of a strategy or intervention under circumstances that would typically prevail in the target context."
- 要点：有效性研究在目标场景的常规条件下检验干预的效果。

### E7

- 位置：Types of Research
- 原文："Research Type #6: Scale-up Research examines effectiveness in a wide range of populations, contexts, and circumstances, without substantial developer involvement in implementation or evaluation."
- 要点：推广研究在广泛的人群、场景和条件下检验效果，开发者不大量参与实施或评估。

### E8

- 位置：Purpose of Design and Development Research（Table 1）
- 原文："(3) Collection of data on the feasibility of implementing the solution(s) in typical delivery settings by intended users; and (4) Conducting a pilot study to examine the promise of generating the intended outcomes."
- 要点：设计与开发研究通常包括：收集在常规环境中由预期使用者实施的可行性数据，以及做一项预研究看它有没有希望产生预期效果。

### E9

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："In contrast, ED concentrates its investments on developing and testing the effectiveness of well-defined curricula, programs, and practices that could be implemented by schools."
- 要点：美国教育部（ED）的投入集中在开发和检验学校可以实施的课程、项目和做法的效果上。
