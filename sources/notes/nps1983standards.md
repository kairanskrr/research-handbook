# 美国内政部 1983：Secretary of the Interior's Standards and Guidelines for Archeology and Historic Preservation

- 资料：`nps1983standards`，[美国国家公园管理局 PDF](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/historicpreservation/upload/standards-guidelines-archeology-historic-preservation.pdf)
- 版本：美国内政部长发布的考古与历史保护标准和指南，1983 年 9 月 29 日刊登于《联邦公报》（Federal Register，第 48 卷第 190 期）。适用于美国联邦层面的历史保护工作。本摘录只用"考古记录"（Archeological Documentation）标准和指南部分。PDF 为扫描件转文字，个别字有识别错误，引文照录。

### E1

- 位置：Standards for Archeological Documentation, Standard II
- 原文："Methods and techniques should be selected so that the results may be verified if necessary. Non-destructive techniques should be used whenever appropriate."
- 要点：方法的选择要让结果在必要时可以核实；适当时就用非破坏性技术。

### E2

- 位置：Guidelines, Documentation Plan, Research Design
- 原文："Archeological documentation can be carried out only after defining explicit goals and a methodology for reaching them."
- 要点：只有先定出明确的目标和达到目标的方法，才能开展考古记录工作。

### E3

- 位置：Guidelines, Documentation Plan
- 原文："Archeological investigations seldom are able to collect and record all possible data."
- 要点：考古调查很少能收集和记录所有可能的数据。

### E4

- 位置：Guidelines, Documentation Plan
- 原文："Responsiveness to the concerns of local groups (e.g., Native American groups with ties to specific properties) that was built into survey and evaluation phases of the preservation plan, should be maintained in archeological investigation, since such activity usually involves site disturbance."
- 要点：在调查和评估阶段对当地群体（如与遗址有联系的美洲原住民群体）关切的回应，到发掘阶段也要保持，因为发掘通常会扰动遗址。

### E5

- 位置：Guidelines, Field Studies
- 原文："The implementation of the research design in the field must be flexible enough to accommodate the discovery of new or unexpected data classes or properties, or changing field conditions."
- 要点：在野外执行研究设计要有足够弹性，以应对新的或意外的发现，以及变化的现场条件。

### E6

- 位置：Guidelines, Field Studies
- 原文："It is thus desirable to maintain some consistency in choice of sampling designs throughout multiple phases of work at the same property."
- 要点：同一遗址的多个阶段工作，最好保持抽样设计的一致。

### E7

- 位置：Guidelines, Field Studies
- 原文："Destructive methods should not be applied to portions or elements of the property if nondestructive methods are practical."
- 要点：能用非破坏方法时，就不要用破坏性方法。

### E8

- 位置：Guidelines, Field Studies
- 原文："The field operation may recover data that may not be fully analyzed; this data, as well as the data analyzed, should be recorded and preserved in a way to facilitate future"
- 要点：野外收集的数据不一定都会被分析；分析过的和没分析的都要记录、保存好，方便将来使用。

### E9

- 位置：Guidelines, Field Studies
- 原文："Field records should be maintained in a manner that permits independent interpretation in so far as possible." / "Record-keeping should be standardized in format and level of detail."
- 要点：野外记录要尽量让别人能独立解读；记录的格式和详细程度要统一。

### E10

- 位置：Guidelines, Reporting Results
- 原文："Some individual property information, such as specific locational data, may be highly sensitive to disclosure, because of the threat of vandalism."
- 要点：具体位置等信息可能非常敏感，公开后有被盗掘破坏的风险。

### E11

- 位置：Guidelines, Curation
- 原文："Curation of important archeological specimens and records should be provided for in the development of any archeological program or project."
- 要点：任何考古项目在规划时就要安排好重要标本和记录的保管。
