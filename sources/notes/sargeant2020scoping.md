# Sargeant & O'Connor 2020：Scoping Reviews, Systematic Reviews, and Meta-Analysis: Applications in Veterinary Medicine

- 资料：`sargeant2020scoping`，[PMC 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6997489/)，DOI [10.3389/fvets.2020.00011](https://doi.org/10.3389/fvets.2020.00011)
- 版本：Frontiers in Veterinary Science 7:11，2020 年，开放获取（CC BY 4.0），2026-10-08 经 Europe PMC 全文接口读取。两位作者分别在加拿大圭尔夫大学和美国艾奥瓦州立大学兽医学院。是方法综述，不是学会指南或法规。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Systematic Reviews
- 原文："The planned approach for each of the steps is first described in a protocol, which should be completed prior to starting the actual review." / "Any deviations from the protocol should be acknowledged and justified in the final systematic review report or publication."
- 要点：系统综述每一步打算怎么做，先写进研究方案，方案要在综述开始前完成；实际做的和方案不一样的地方，要在最终报告里说明并给出理由。

### E2

- 位置：Systematic Reviews
- 原文："Protocols may be published prior to starting the review on websites such as PROSPERO (https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/prospero/) or SYREAF (www.SYREAF.org), posted to University repositories, or submitted as supplementary materials with systematic review publications."
- 要点：研究方案可以在综述开始前发布到 PROSPERO 或 SYREAF 等网站、放进大学的机构库，或作为补充材料随论文提交。

### E3

- 位置：Define the review question
- 原文："In veterinary medicine, systematic review questions generally fall into one of four question types: descriptive questions, intervention questions, exposure or etiology questions, or diagnostic test accuracy evaluations."
- 要点：兽医学的系统综述问题一般分四类：描述性问题（如患病率）、干预问题、暴露或病因问题、诊断试验准确性。

### E4

- 位置：Conduct a comprehensive search for studies
- 原文："Searching the veterinary literature can be challenging, in that search filters used in human healthcare literature searches are lacking for veterinary medicine and reporting of key features in titles and abstracts may be poor" / "It is recommended that the search include multiple databases."
- 要点：检索兽医文献有难度：人医常用的检索过滤器在兽医学里缺乏，标题和摘要里关键特征也常写得不全；建议检索多个数据库。

### E5

- 位置：Scoping Reviews，Selecting relevant studies（系统综述一节说筛选按同样的程序）
- 原文："To reduce the potential for selection bias in the identification of relevant literature, it is standard practice for relevance screening to be undertaken in duplicate by two reviewers working independently, with any disagreements resolved by consensus." / "screening follows the same procedures as was described previously for a scoping review"
- 要点：为了减少选择偏倚，标准做法是由两位评审者各自独立筛选文献，分歧协商解决；系统综述的筛选按同样的程序。

### E6

- 位置：Collect data from relevant studies
- 原文："To reduce the potential for misclassification, data extraction should be conducted by two reviewers working independently with disagreements resolved by consensus"
- 要点：为减少错误归类，数据提取应由两位评审者独立完成，分歧协商解决（作者也列了单人提取加第二人核对等替代做法）。

### E7

- 位置：Assess risk of bias in relevant studies
- 原文："At this stage of the review, it is not appropriate to remove studies from a review based on the risk of bias, unless one or more trial design features are included a priori in the eligibility criteria" / "Validated tools for assessing risk of bias are available for different study designs"
- 要点：评估偏倚风险这一步，不应因为偏倚风险高就把研究剔除，除非事先把某些设计特征写进了纳入标准；不同研究设计有各自经过验证的偏倚风险评估工具。

### E8

- 位置：Synthesize the results（发表偏倚）
- 原文："Publication bias is a potential concern whenever research results are considered in decision-making." / "a study of conference proceedings abstracts for swine and cattle vaccine trials found that <10% of the studies were subsequently published"
- 要点：只要拿研究结果做决策，发表偏倚就是一个潜在问题。兽医学里很多研究没有在同行评议期刊上发表：一项研究发现，猪和牛疫苗试验的会议摘要中，后来正式发表的不到 10%。

### E9

- 位置：Present the results
- 原文："The methods and results of a systematic reviews should be reported in sufficient detail that the reader can evaluate the potential for bias." / "The Preferred Reporting of Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-analyses (PRISMA) guidelines provide recommendations for the items of information and the level of detail that should be included in a systematic review report"
- 要点：系统综述的方法和结果要报告得足够详细，让读者能评估可能的偏倚；PRISMA 指南规定了综述报告应包含哪些信息、写到多细。

### E10

- 位置：Interpret the results
- 原文："One approach to evaluating the quality of evidence is by using the Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development and Evaluation (GRADE)" / "The overall quality of evidence is determined to be high, medium, low, or very low."
- 要点：评价证据质量的一种做法是 GRADE（推荐分级的评估、制定与评价），把总体证据质量定为高、中、低、很低四级。
