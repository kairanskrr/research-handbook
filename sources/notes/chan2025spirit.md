# Chan et al. 2025：SPIRIT 2025 statement: updated guideline for protocols of randomised trials

- 资料：`chan2025spirit`，[BMJ](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2024-081477)；开放获取全文见 [PubMed Central PMC12035670](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12035670/)
- 版本：BMJ 2025;389:e081477。同一声明同时发表在 BMJ、JAMA、Lancet、Nature Medicine 和 PLOS Medicine。网页版无页码，位置写小标题或清单条目编号。

### E1

- 位置：Table 1，item 20
- 原文："Strategies for achieving adequate participant enrolment to reach target sample size"
- 要点：方案要写明怎样招够目标样本量。

### E2

- 位置：Table 1，item 21b
- 原文："To reduce predictability of a random sequence, other details of any planned restriction (eg, blocking) should be provided in a separate document that is unavailable to those who enrol participants or assign interventions"
- 要点：为降低随机序列的可预测性，区组等限制的细节应写在单独文件里，不让负责入组和分配的人看到。

### E3

- 位置：Table 1，item 24c
- 原文："If blinded, circumstances under which unblinding is permissible, and procedure for revealing a participant’s allocated intervention during the trial"
- 要点：设盲的试验，方案要写明什么情况下允许揭盲、怎样揭开某位受试者的分组。

### E4

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："As the most important record of planned methods and conduct, a well written protocol has a key role in promoting consistent and rigorous execution by the trial team."
- 要点：方案是计划方法和实施过程最重要的记录，写得好的方案能促使团队一致、严谨地执行试验。

### E5

- 位置：Updated SPIRIT 2025 statement
- 原文："The SPIRIT 2025 statement comprises a checklist of 34 minimum protocol items (table 1) and a diagram illustrating the schedule of enrolment, interventions, and assessments (fig 1)."
- 要点：SPIRIT 2025 包括 34 项试验方案最低限度条目，和一张入组、干预、评估时间安排图。

### E6

- 位置：Table 1，item 32a
- 原文："Who will obtain informed consent or assent from potential trial participants or authorised proxies, and how"
- 要点：方案要写明由谁、怎样向受试者或其授权代理人取得知情同意。

### E7

- 位置：Definition of a randomised trial protocol
- 原文："The full protocol must be submitted for approval by an REC/IRB before enrolling participants."
- 要点：完整方案必须在入组受试者之前提交研究伦理委员会批准。

### E8

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Many trial protocols do not adequately describe important elements including the primary outcomes, treatment allocation methods, use of blinding, measurement of adverse events, sample size calculations, data analysis methods" / "Gaps in protocol content can lead to avoidable protocol amendments"
- 要点：很多方案没写清主要结局、分配方法、盲法、不良事件测量、样本量计算、分析方法等；方案内容有漏洞会导致本可避免的方案修订。

### E9

- 位置：Table 1，item 28a
- 原文："Composition of data monitoring committee (DMC); summary of its role and reporting structure; statement of whether it is independent from the sponsor and funder" / "Alternatively, an explanation of why a DMC is not needed"
- 要点：方案要写明数据监查委员会的组成、职责、汇报关系、是否独立于申办方和资助方；不设的话要解释为什么不需要。

### E10

- 位置：Table 1，item 28b
- 原文："Explanation of any interim analyses and stopping guidelines, including who will have access to these interim results and make the final decision to terminate the trial"
- 要点：方案要说明期中分析和终止规则，包括谁能看期中结果、谁最终决定终止试验。

### E11

- 位置：Table 1，item 25b
- 原文："Plans to promote participant retention and complete follow-up, including list of any outcome data to be collected for participants who discontinue or deviate from intervention protocols"
- 要点：方案要写明怎样留住受试者、完成随访，包括对中途停止或偏离干预的人仍要收集哪些结局数据。

### E12

- 位置：Definition of a randomised trial protocol
- 原文："every protocol version should contain a transparent audit trail documenting the dates and descriptions of changes. Important protocol amendments should be reported to REC/IRBs and trial registries as they occur"
- 要点：每个方案版本都要有透明的修改记录（日期和内容）；重要修订要及时报告伦理委员会和登记库。
