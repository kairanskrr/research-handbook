# 美国语言学会 2019：LSA Ethics Statement

- 资料：`lsa2019ethics`，[原文](https://www.lsadc.org/lsa_ethics_statement)
- 版本：2019 年 7 月通过，2023 年 11 月随活动行为准则更新；美国语言学会（Linguistic Society of America）

### E1

- 位置：§1 Introduction
- 原文："This statement is not meant to replace formal research ethics oversight, nor is it meant to provide an exhaustive code of conduct."
- 要点：这份声明不取代正式的伦理审查，也不是一份完整的行为守则。

### E2

- 位置：§3 Participants
- 原文："Even if an institutional ethics committee deems that a research project is exempt from obtaining the committee’s approval, linguists should do everything in their power and in their capacities to ensure that their research follows the core principles of respect for persons"
- 要点：即使机构伦理委员会认为项目可以免审，语言学者也要尽力遵守尊重个人等核心原则。

### E3

- 位置：§3 Participants
- 原文："Obtaining informed consent from participants and ensuring that any individual’s participation in research is completely voluntary at every stage."
- 要点：要取得参与者的知情同意，并确保每个阶段的参与都完全自愿。

### E4

- 位置：§3 Participants
- 原文："Research participants have the right to control whether their actions are recorded in such a way that they can be connected with their personal identity."
- 要点：参与者有权决定自己的言行被记录后，能不能和本人身份联系起来。

### E5

- 位置：§3 Participants
- 原文："Considering carefully whether some kind of compensation to participants is appropriate, be it remuneration for time and effort, formal recognition of their contribution to the creation or documentation of knowledge"
- 要点：要认真考虑给参与者什么回报：报酬、对其贡献的正式承认等。

### E6

- 位置：§3 Communities and the public
- 原文："In many communities, responsibility for linguistic and cultural knowledge is viewed as collective, so that individual community members are not in a position to consent to share materials with outsiders"
- 要点：在许多社区，语言和文化知识被看作集体的，个人无权单独同意把材料分享给外人。

### E7

- 位置：§3 Communities and the public
- 原文："linguists should comply with a community’s wishes regarding access, archiving, and distribution of results."
- 要点：在资料的查阅、存档和结果的传播上，要遵从社区的意愿。

### E8

- 位置：§3 Communities and the public
- 原文："community involvement sought from the earliest stages of project planning."
- 要点：从项目规划的最早阶段就要请社区参与。

### E9

- 位置：§3 Communities and the public
- 原文："returning, wherever feasible, data and knowledge gained to the community for stewardship. Not all data or knowledge collected with the aid of linguists or on behalf of linguistic scholarship belongs to linguists."
- 要点：尽可能把获得的数据和知识交还社区保管；语言学者参与收集的数据不都属于语言学者。

### E10

- 位置：§3 Communities and the public
- 原文："Considering carefully the impact of what is shared through publication, specifically whether this could affect the communities’ social status or relationship with political structures."
- 要点：发表前要考虑公开的内容会不会影响社区的社会地位或与政治机构的关系。

### E11

- 位置：§4 Colleagues and the discipline
- 原文："Reporting results with rigor and integrity (including not fabricating or falsifying data, engaging in p-hacking, etc.)."
- 要点：报告结果要严谨诚实，不捏造、不篡改数据，不做 p 值操纵。

### E12

- 位置：§4 Colleagues and the discipline
- 原文："Citing carefully in one’s work the original sources of ideas, descriptions, and data."
- 要点：要仔细引用观点、描述和数据的原始来源。

### E13

- 位置：§4 Colleagues and the discipline
- 原文："Promoting, wherever appropriate and possible, data sharing, replication, and reproducibility, as well as archiving in repositories that are accessible."
- 要点：在合适且可能时，推动数据共享、重复研究和可复现，并存入可访问的资料库。

### E14

- 位置：§4 Students
- 原文："Properly acknowledging any and all contributions of students and other collaborators to our research"
- 要点：要恰当承认学生和其他合作者对研究的全部贡献。

### E15

- 位置：§3 Communities and the public
- 原文："Considering carefully the use of technologies that they may be involved in developing, including their potential role in social and political decision-making processes"
- 要点：参与开发语言技术的人，要考虑技术在社会和政治决策中的作用。

### E16

- 位置：§3 Participants
- 原文："Anonymous observations of public behavior, which often cannot involve full knowledge of the potential consequences, should be thoroughly vetted with research ethics boards"
- 要点：对公共场合行为的匿名观察，要经过伦理委员会的充分审查。

### E17

- 位置：页尾
- 原文："Approved July of 2019; updated with revised LSA Code of Conduct for Events in November 2023"
- 要点：版本信息：2019 年 7 月通过，2023 年 11 月更新。
