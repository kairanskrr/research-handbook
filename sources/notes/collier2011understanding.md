### E1

- 位置：引言，第 823 页
- 原文："it is defined as the systematic examination of diagnostic evidence selected and analyzed in light of research questions and hypotheses posed by the investigator."
- 要点：过程追踪是按照研究者提出的研究问题和假设，系统地挑选、分析有诊断力的证据。

### E2

- 位置：Teaching Exercises，第 823–824 页
- 原文："This new formulation of process tracing is accompanied by online teaching exercises" / "The Sherlock Holmes story "Silver Blaze" (posted online with the exercises) serves as the basis for an exercise, and also as a running example in parts of the following presentation."
- 要点：文章配有在线教学练习；福尔摩斯故事《银色马》既是一个练习，也是正文的贯穿例子。

### E3

- 位置：Descriptive Inference，第 824 页
- 原文："the descriptive component of process tracing begins not with observing change or sequence, but rather with taking good snapshots at a series of specific moments."
- 要点：过程追踪的描述部分，从在一系列具体时刻拍好"快照"开始，而不是一上来就看变化和顺序。

### E4

- 位置：Causal Inference，第 825 页
- 原文："Careful, analytically informed specification of hypotheses is essential both in selecting and interpreting pieces of evidence, and in weighing them against one another."
- 要点：仔细、有理论依据地写清假设，是挑选、解读和权衡证据的前提。

### E5

- 位置：Causal Inference，第 825 页
- 原文："The distinctions in table 1 are a useful heuristic, but should not be taken rigidly. The decision to treat a given piece of evidence as the basis for one of the four tests can depend on the researcher's prior knowledge, the assumptions that underlie the study, and the specific formulation of the hypothesis."
- 要点：四种检验的区分是有用的经验法则，不能僵化；一条证据算哪种检验，取决于研究者的已有知识、研究假定和假设的具体表述。

### E6

- 位置：Straw-in-the-Wind Tests，第 826 页
- 原文："Straw-in-the-wind tests thus provide neither a necessary nor a sufficient criterion for accepting or rejecting a hypothesis, and they only slightly weaken rival hypotheses."
- 要点：风中稻草检验既非必要也非充分，只略微削弱竞争假设。

### E7

- 位置：Hoop Tests，第 826 页
- 原文："The hypothesis must "jump through the hoop" to remain under consideration, but passing the test does not by itself affirm the hypothesis." / "Hoop tests do not confirm a hypothesis, but they can eliminate it."
- 要点：假设必须"跳过圈"才能继续留在考虑范围内，但通过本身不证实它；环形检验不能证实假设，但能淘汰它。

### E8

- 位置：Smoking-Gun Tests，第 827 页
- 原文："a suspect who is caught holding a smoking gun is presumed guilty. However, those with no smoking gun may not be innocent. In other words, this provides a sufficient but not necessary criterion for accepting the causal inference."
- 要点：手拿冒烟的枪的嫌疑人被推定有罪，但没拿枪的人不一定无辜；确凿证据检验是充分而非必要的标准。

### E9

- 位置：Doubly Decisive Tests，第 827 页
- 原文："single tests that accomplish this are rare in social science, but this leverage may be achieved by combining multiple tests, which together support one explanation and eliminate all others."
- 要点：单个检验就达到双重决定性的情况在社会科学中很少，但可以把多个检验组合起来，共同支持一个解释、排除其他解释。

### E10

- 位置：Doubly Decisive Tests，第 827 页
- 原文："Put simply, when the investigator has eliminated all plausible alternatives, the remaining scenario must be the correct one."
- 要点：排除法：排除所有可信的替代解释后，剩下的就是正确的。

### E11

- 位置：Conclusion，第 828 页
- 原文："Doubts may arise as to which causal-inference test is appropriate. The analysis may face standard problems of missing variables. Measurement error can be an issue, and probabilistic relationships are harder to address than in quantitative research."
- 要点：过程追踪本身也有严重问题：用哪种检验可能有疑问，可能漏掉变量，有测量误差，概率性关系比定量研究更难处理。

### E12

- 位置：Conclusion，第 828–829 页
- 原文："It can therefore be productive to start with a good narrative or with a timeline that lists the sequence of events. One can then explore the causal ideas embedded in the narratives, consider the kinds of evidence"
- 要点：可以先写一份好的叙述或列出事件顺序的时间线，再从中找出因果想法，考虑需要什么证据。
