# Perez 2024：Tips for Empirical Alignment Research

- 资料：`perez2024tips`，[原文](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/dZFpEdKyb9Bf4xYn7/tips-for-empirical-alignment-research)
- 版本：AI Alignment Forum 帖子，2024-02-29，2026-10-05 读取。位置写网页小标题。本文面向语言模型实验研究，这里只摘跨学科成立的部分。

### E1

- 位置：General Mindset Tips
- 原文："What matters is whether or not you’re learning about a problem. If we’re learning, we’re winning, even if the experiment didn’t “work.”"
- 要点：关键是有没有在了解问题；只要在学到东西，实验"没成功"也是收获。

### E2

- 位置：General Mindset Tips
- 原文："If you’re hitting diminishing returns on the project, then it’s totally fine and great to switch projects"
- 要点：项目收益明显递减时，换项目完全可以。

### E3

- 位置：Three modes of research（Exploratory phase）
- 原文："Run some quick experiments to prototype various ideas, using the workflow described earlier (anything you can run in a day or less, e.g. preferring faster-to-implement/run experiments)."
- 要点：探索阶段先用一天内能跑完的快速实验给各种想法做原型。

### E4

- 位置：Three modes of research（Writing phase）
- 原文："I’ll often just start a doc as I’m running experiments, and then add info about the experimental results as they come in (e.g. various plots/stats, and my observations/takeaways about the results"
- 要点：边做实验边开一份文档，结果一出来就记下图表、统计和自己的观察结论。

### E5

- 位置：Work Habits
- 原文："Lots of researchers burn out by working in unsustainable ways (also since the object-level work can be hard and goals ill-defined), or by developing an aversion to what they do, and it’s really good to avoid this (also, burn out just sucks)."
- 要点：许多研究者因工作方式不可持续而倦怠，要主动避免。

### E6

- 位置：Tactical Research Tips & Approach（Always be thinking about what the best next experiment you run should be）
- 原文："When you show experimental results (in meetings or in slack), you should also include discussion of your proposed next possible steps immediately after (and proposed prioritization)."
- 要点：展示结果时，紧接着提出你建议的下一步及优先级；做实验的人最了解结果，最适合先提方案。

### E7

- 位置：What success generally looks like
- 原文："Noticing when you’re going slowly and quickly and shamelessly asking for help"
- 要点：发现自己进展慢时，及时、不难为情地求助。

### E8

- 位置：What success generally looks like
- 原文："whether you’re transparent/communicative about issues you’re facing"
- 要点：好合作者会主动、透明地说出自己遇到的问题。
