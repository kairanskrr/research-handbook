# Albert & Wager 2003：How to handle authorship disputes: a guide for new researchers

- 资料：`albert2003authorship`，[原文](https://web.archive.org/web/20241128073038/https://publicationethics.org/files/2003pdf12_0.pdf)（出版伦理委员会 The COPE Report 2003，第 32–34 页）
- 版本：出版伦理委员会（Committee on Publication Ethics）官网托管的 PDF；官网对脚本返回 403，本条取自 Internet Archive 2024-11-28 对同一官方文件的存档。

### E1

- 位置：How to handle authorship disputes when they occur，(a) Disputes
- 原文："In such cases you need to negotiate with the people involved." / "Support this with evidence, such as laboratory notebooks, manuscripts, ICMJE statement, Instructions to Authors etc. If you remain unhappy with your supervisor's decision, you may consider an appeal to someone more senior, such as the departmental head or dean. But you should do this in exceptional circumstances only - and make sure your supervisor knows what you are intending to do."
- 要点：属于解释分歧的署名纠纷，先和当事人协商，拿实验记录、稿件、署名规范等证据说话；仍不服导师的决定，可以向系主任或院长申诉，但只在特殊情况下这样做，并让导师知道。

### E2

- 位置：What you can do if authorship issues are not resolved
- 原文："If you discover this only after publication you may contact the journal and ask for a correction." / "You could contact the journal but an editor is unlikely to add your name without the agreement of the other authors."
- 要点：发表后才发现自己被违愿署名，可以请期刊更正；被漏掉时可以联系期刊，但没有其他作者同意，编辑不太可能加上你的名字。

### E3

- 位置：Key concepts in authorship，Appeals
- 原文："However most editors are reluctant to get involved in disputes about omitted authors since they do not have enough information to judge such cases." / "Similarly, COPE only hears cases submitted by journal editors and is not an appeal body for cases of disputed authorship."
- 要点：多数编辑不愿介入漏署作者的纠纷，因为他们没有足够信息判断；出版伦理委员会只受理期刊编辑提交的案例，不是署名纠纷的申诉机构。
