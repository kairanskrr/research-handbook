# Nature Methods：Aims & Scope

- 资料：`naturemethodsaims`，[原文](https://www.nature.com/nmeth/aims)
- 版本：《自然—方法》（Nature Methods，施普林格·自然出版）期刊官网的办刊宗旨页，2026-10-08 读取。

### E1

- 位置：Aims & Scope 第 3 段
- 原文："To enhance the practical relevance of each paper, the description of the method must be accompanied by strong validation, an application to an important biological question and results illustrating its performance in comparison to available approaches."
- 要点：方法论文要有充分的验证，要用在一个重要的生物学问题上，还要给出和现有方法比较性能的结果。

### E2

- 位置：Aims & Scope 第 3 段
- 原文："Papers are selected for publication based on broad interest, thorough assessments of methodological performance and comprehensive technical descriptions that facilitate immediate application."
- 要点：选稿看三点：读者面广，对方法性能有全面评估，技术描述完整、别人能马上用上。
