# 英国口述历史学会 2019：Is your oral history legal and ethical?（法律与伦理指南）

- 资料：`ohs2019legal`，[总页](https://www.ohs.org.uk/advice/ethical-and-legal/)，以及分页：[准备](https://ohs.org.uk/legal-and-ethical-advice/legal-and-ethical-preparation/)、[第一次接触](https://ohs.org.uk/legal-and-ethical-advice/legal-and-ethical-1st-approach/)、[访谈中](https://ohs.org.uk/legal-and-ethical-advice/legal-and-ethical-during-interview/)、[访谈后](https://ohs.org.uk/legal-and-ethical-advice/legal-and-ethical-after/)、[存档](https://ohs.org.uk/legal-and-ethical-advice/legal-and-ethical-archiving/)
- 版本：英国口述历史学会（Oral History Society，OHS）的网上指南，总页注明"© Oral History Society, 2019"，取代 2012 年等早期版本；适用于英国四个地区和英国法律。2026-10-06 读取。位置写分页名。

### E1

- 位置：总页
- 原文："This guide is intended as no more than guidance and does not constitute formal legal advice. If you need legal advice you should consult a solicitor."
- 要点：本指南只是指导，不构成正式法律意见；需要法律意见应咨询律师。

### E2

- 位置：准备（Preparation）
- 原文："Interviewers working in a UK institution of higher or further education, or whose interviewees are either employees or currently patients of the National Health Service (NHS) will be required to submit their research proposal to an ethics committee."
- 要点：在英国高校工作的访谈者，或受访者是英国国家医疗服务体系雇员或病人的，研究计划要提交伦理委员会。

### E3

- 位置：准备（Preparation），引英国数据档案馆（UK Data Archive）列出的责任
- 原文："a duty of confidentiality (though not necessarily anonymity) towards informants and participants"
- 要点：对受访者有保密的责任，但不一定是匿名。

### E4

- 位置：准备（Preparation）
- 原文："archives which may be willing to accept the recordings should be contacted in advance to check what documentation is required"
- 要点：事先联系可能接收录音的档案馆，问清需要哪些文件。

### E5

- 位置：准备（Preparation）
- 原文："OHS advice is to seek parental or carer consent for interviewing children under the age of sixteen, in addition to the consent of the child where they are aged thirteen or over."
- 要点：OHS 建议，访谈 16 岁以下的孩子要取得父母或监护人同意；13 岁及以上的孩子本人也要同意。

### E6

- 位置：第一次接触（1st approach）
- 原文："An Interview Participation Agreement should be completed before the interview begins" / "An Interview Recording Agreement which covers copyright and access conditions should be completed after the interview has been finished." / "but which should be shown to and explained to an interviewee before any recording begins"
- 要点：OHS 用两份文件：访谈前签"参与协议"；访谈结束后签涵盖版权和查阅条件的"录音协议"，但录音协议要在开录前就给受访者看过、讲解过。

### E7

- 位置：第一次接触（1st approach）
- 原文："Without a signed Recording Agreement contacting people at a later date is usually very time-consuming and often impossible if interviewees or interviewers have died or moved away."
- 要点：没有签好的录音协议，日后再去找人通常很费时；如果受访者或访谈者已去世或搬走，往往根本找不到。

### E8

- 位置：第一次接触（1st approach）
- 原文："Both interviewer and interviewee have copyrights in the recording"
- 要点：（英国法律下）访谈者和受访者对录音都有版权。

### E9

- 位置：访谈中（During interview）
- 原文："If for any reason an interviewee asks that the interview be ended or broken off and resumed at some later date, that preference should be respected." / "it is often helpful before the interview begins to agree a time to finish the recording session, or perhaps to arrange to have a break."
- 要点：受访者要求结束或中断、改天再继续，要尊重；访谈前先约好结束时间或中间休息，往往有帮助。

### E10

- 位置：访谈中（During interview）
- 原文："They should record these instances (with time codes) on an Interview Sensitivity Review Form as the interview is being summarised (or transcribed) and then subsequently bring any concerns to the attention of interviewees and project leaders so that everyone involved can weigh up the likely risks of disclosure involved."
- 要点：对可能构成诽谤或泄露他人敏感个人信息的内容，在做摘要或转录时连同时间码记到"敏感内容审查表"上，再提请受访者和项目负责人一起权衡公开的风险。

### E11

- 位置：访谈中（During interview）
- 原文："Interviewers, and project staff/volunteers processing interview data, should treat the content of the interview as confidential until a Recording Agreement or other access agreement has been finalised."
- 要点：录音协议或其他查阅协议定下来之前，访谈者和处理资料的人员都要对访谈内容保密。

### E12

- 位置：访谈后（After）
- 原文："an interviewee owns copyright in his or her recorded words spoken on the recording and that this can be retained or assigned (passed) to someone else"
- 要点：受访者对自己在录音中说的话拥有版权，可以保留，也可以转让给别人。

### E13

- 位置：访谈后（After）
- 原文："Any closure or restrictions on access or use that the interviewee may wish to make should be clearly described (with time codes and with dates when any embargoes come to an end)."
- 要点：受访者要求的封存或使用限制要写清楚，标上时间码和解禁日期。

### E14

- 位置：访谈后（After）
- 原文："Interviewers or project managers may also like to offer interviewees the opportunity to check the summary or transcript though this is not always a universal or necessary practice."
- 要点：可以请受访者核对摘要或转录稿，但这并非普遍或必需的做法。

### E15

- 位置：存档（Archiving）
- 原文："Places of deposit tend to avoid the acquisition of oral history interviews which are not accompanied by documentation including provenance, availability for use, and copyright status"
- 要点：收藏机构倾向于不接收缺少来源、可用范围、版权状态等文件的口述历史访谈。

### E16

- 位置：存档（Archiving）
- 原文："For digitisation projects involving online web access to oral history interviews, as far as possible interviewees should be re-contacted to confirm their agreement to this kind of access."
- 要点：把访谈数字化放到网上时，应尽量重新联系受访者，确认他们同意这种公开方式。

### E17

- 位置：存档（Archiving）
- 原文："Interviews placed on the internet should always be accompanied by terms and conditions for use."
- 要点：放到网上的访谈要附上使用条款。

### E18

- 位置：存档（Archiving）
- 原文："Publicly-funded organisations and places of deposit should also be aware of Freedom of Information legislation."
- 要点：公共资金资助的机构和收藏机构要注意（英国的）信息自由法。
