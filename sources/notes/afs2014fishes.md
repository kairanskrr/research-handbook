# 美国渔业学会等 2014：Guidelines for the Use of Fishes in Research

- 资料：`afs2014fishes`，[原文](https://fisheries.org/policy-media/science-guidelines/guidelines-for-the-use-of-fishes-in-research/)
- 版本：2014 年版。由美国渔业学会（American Fisheries Society，AFS）、美国鱼类学和爬虫学会、美国渔业研究生物学家协会的联合委员会编写。官网对脚本返回 403，原文文本取自 Internet Archive 2025-05-18 的网页快照（网页即指南全文）。位置写章节号。

### E1

- 位置：2.3 Statistical Design
- 原文："A true “replicate” is the smallest experimental unit to which a treatment can be applied independently. Pseudoreplication can result from wrongly treating multiple samples from one experimental unit as multiple experimental units or from using experimental units that are not statistically independent"
- 要点：真正的"重复"是能独立施加处理的最小实验单位。把一个实验单位里的多个样品当成多个实验单位，或者用了统计上不独立的实验单位，都会造成伪重复。

### E2

- 位置：2.3 Statistical Design
- 原文："In all cases, studies should be designed to use the fewest animals necessary to reliably answer the questions posed."
- 要点：任何情况下，研究都应设计成用能可靠回答问题的最少数量的鱼。

### E3

- 位置：1. Introduction
- 原文："All United States institutions that use vertebrate animals for research, teaching, research training, and biological testing are required to create an IACUC to oversee and evaluate all aspects of the institution’s animal care and use program."
- 要点：在美国，用脊椎动物做研究、教学或检测的机构都必须设立动物护理和使用委员会（IACUC），监督本机构对动物的照料和使用。鱼也是脊椎动物。

### E4

- 位置：3. Statutory Requirements and Regulatory Bodies
- 原文："The investigator must have knowledge of all regulations pertaining to the animals under study, as well as to biosecurity issues, and must obtain all permits necessary for carrying out proposed studies"
- 要点：研究者必须了解与研究动物和生物安全有关的全部法规，并取得开展研究所需的全部许可。

### E5

- 位置：3.1 International Regulations and Guidelines
- 原文："Investigators working outside of the United States should comply with all wildlife regulations of the country in which the research is being performed."
- 要点：在美国以外做研究，要遵守研究所在国家的野生动物法规。

### E6

- 位置：Final Disposition of Experimental Animals
- 原文："Fish collected in the wild and brought into the laboratory for experimental or teaching purposes should never be returned to the environment according to the general consensus among agencies granting scientific collecting permits."
- 要点：从野外采来、带进实验室做实验或教学的鱼，按发放科研采集许可机构的普遍共识，绝不应再放回野外。
