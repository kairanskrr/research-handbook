# Kitchenham & Charters 2007：Guidelines for performing Systematic Literature Reviews in Software Engineering

- 资料：`kitchenham2007guidelines`，[原文 PDF](https://legacyfileshare.elsevier.com/promis_misc/525444systematicreviewsguide.pdf)
- 版本：技术报告 EBSE-2007-01，v2.3。页码为 PDF 文件的页序。

### E1

- 位置：§1，PDF 第 11 页
- 原文："However, unless a literature review is thorough and fair, it is of little scientific value."
- 要点：文献综述如果不全面、不公正，就没有多少科学价值。这是要求综述"系统化"的出发点。

### E2

- 位置：§2，PDF 第 11 页
- 原文："A systematic literature review (often referred to as a systematic review) is a means of identifying, evaluating and interpreting all available research relevant to a particular research question, or topic area, or phenomenon of interest."
- 要点：系统综述的定义：针对一个具体问题，找出、评估并解读**全部**相关研究。

### E3

- 位置：§2.5.1，PDF 第 12 页
- 原文："If, during the initial examination of a domain prior to commissioning a systematic review, it is discovered that very little evidence is likely to exist or that the topic is very broad then a systematic mapping study may be a more appropriate exercise than a systematic review. A systematic mapping study allows the evidence in a domain to be plotted at a high level of granularity. This allows for the identification of evidence clusters and evidence deserts to direct the focus of future systematic reviews and to identify areas for more primary studies to be conducted."
- 要点：证据很少或主题很宽时，先做映射研究更合适。映射研究能找出研究扎堆和空白的地方，为后续的系统综述和新研究指明方向。

### E4

- 位置：§4，PDF 第 14 页
- 原文："This document summarises the stages in a systematic review into three main phases: Planning the Review, Conducting the Review, Reporting the Review."
- 要点：系统综述分三个阶段：规划、执行、报告。

### E5

- 位置：§5.1，PDF 第 14–15 页
- 原文："Prior to undertaking a systematic review it is necessary to confirm the need for such a review."
- 要点：动手之前，先确认这个综述确实需要做，例如检查是否已有类似的综述。

### E6

- 位置：§5.3.1，PDF 第 17 页
- 原文："The most important activity during planning is to formulate the research question(s)."
- 要点：规划阶段最重要的是把研究问题想清楚。

### E7

- 位置：§5.4，PDF 第 20 页
- 原文："A pre-defined protocol is necessary to reduce the possibility of researcher bias. For example, without a protocol, it is possible that the selection of individual studies or the analysis may be driven by researcher expectations."
- 要点：事先写好研究方案，是为了防止研究者按自己的预期去挑论文、做分析。方案包括背景、研究问题、检索策略、筛选标准和流程、质量评估、数据提取和综合方法等。

### E8

- 位置：§5.4，PDF 第 21 页
- 原文："It is usually helpful to pilot the selection criteria on a subset of primary studies."
- 要点：筛选标准定好后，先在一小批论文上试用。

### E9

- 位置：§5.5，PDF 第 21 页
- 原文："The protocol is a critical element of any systematic review."
- 要点：研究方案是系统综述的关键，执行前应该请人评审。

### E10

- 位置：§6.1.2，PDF 第 23 页
- 原文："Publication bias refers to the problem that positive results are more likely to be published than negative results."
- 要点：发表偏差：正面结果比负面结果更容易发表，只查正式发表的文献会让结论偏乐观。

### E11

- 位置：§6.2.2，PDF 第 27 页
- 原文："Initially, selection criteria should be interpreted liberally, so that unless a study identified by the electronic and hand searches can be clearly excluded based on title and abstract, a full copy should be obtained."
- 要点：筛选分多轮。第一轮要宽松：只凭标题和摘要不能明确排除的，都要找来全文。原文还引用了一句：软件工程论文的摘要质量太差，不能只靠摘要，还要看结论。

### E12

- 位置：§6.2.3，PDF 第 28 页
- 原文："When two or more researchers assess each paper, agreement between researchers can be measured using the Cohen Kappa statistic. [...] Alternatively, individual researchers can apply a test-retest approach, and re-evaluate a random sample of the primary studies found after initial screening to check the consistency of their inclusion/exclusion decisions."
- 要点：多人筛选时用 Cohen's Kappa 衡量一致性；一个人做时，可以隔一段时间重新判断一批随机抽取的论文，检查前后是否一致。

### E13

- 位置：§6.3，PDF 第 27 页
- 原文："In addition to general inclusion/exclusion criteria, it is considered critical to assess the 'quality' of primary studies"
- 要点：除了纳入/排除标准，还要评估每篇被纳入研究的质量，用于进一步筛选，或解释不同研究结果为什么不一样。

### E14

- 位置：§6.3.4，PDF 第 37 页
- 原文："It is tempting to assume that because something wasn't reported, it wasn't done. This assumption may be incorrect. Researchers should attempt to obtain more information from the authors of the study."
- 要点：论文没写某件事，不等于没做。可以联系原作者确认。

### E15

- 位置：§6.4.3，PDF 第 40–41 页
- 原文："Whenever feasible, data extraction should be performed independently by two or more researchers. [...] For single researchers such as PhD students, other checking techniques must be used. For example supervisors could perform data extraction on a random sample of the primary studies and their results cross-checked with those of the student."
- 要点：数据提取最好由两人以上独立完成。一个人做（例如博士生）时，可以请导师对随机抽取的一部分论文也做一遍提取，再交叉核对。

### E16

- 位置：§6.5，PDF 第 41 页
- 原文："Data synthesis involves collating and summarising the results of the included primary studies. Synthesis can be descriptive (non-quantitative). However, it is sometimes possible to complement a descriptive synthesis with a quantitative summary. Using statistical techniques to obtain a quantitative synthesis is referred to as meta-analysis."
- 要点：综合就是把纳入研究的结果汇总起来。可以是描述性的，也可以加上定量汇总；用统计方法做定量综合叫元分析。
