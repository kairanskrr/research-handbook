# 英格兰历史遗产局 2015：Management of Research Projects in the Historic Environment (MoRPHE) Project Managers' Guide

- 资料：`historicengland2015morphe`，[官网页面](https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/morphe-project-managers-guide/)（官网有访问验证时，可用 Internet Archive 存档：https://web.archive.org/web/2022/https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/morphe-project-managers-guide/heag024-morphe-managers-guide/）
- 版本：英格兰历史遗产局（Historic England）2015 年 4 月 30 日发布，Edmund Lee 编写。原文说明，由该局资助的研究项目必须遵循本指南，其他人可作良好做法参考。位置写节号。

### E1

- 位置：Summary
- 原文："Research and R&D projects funded by Historic England will be required as a condition of grant or contract to follow this guidance."
- 要点：英格兰历史遗产局资助的研究项目，必须遵循本指南（资助或合同条件）。

### E2

- 位置：1.1 The MoRPHE procedural model
- 原文："Initiation is the project's design Stage." / "Uncertainties, potential problems and opportunities are documented in a Risk Log (see Glossary)."
- 要点："启动"阶段就是设计阶段；不确定性、潜在问题和机会记录在风险日志里。

### E3

- 位置：1.1 The MoRPHE procedural model
- 原文："Review Point R3 is a review, conducted at the conclusion of each Execution Stage, of the project's progress against its Aims and Objectives. The review may generate an Updated Project Design, to be applied to subsequent Execution Stages."
- 要点：每个执行阶段结束时，对照目标检查进展；检查结果可能是一份更新后的项目设计书，用于之后的阶段。

### E4

- 位置：2.5 Execution
- 原文："This flexible approach is consistent with the unpredictable nature of historic-environment research."
- 要点：这种灵活的做法，适合历史环境研究难以预料的特点（例如出现新发现）。

### E5

- 位置：2.5.3 Archive preparation
- 原文："Archive should be selected for inclusion on this basis, and actively compiled and indexed as it is acquired, throughout the project's life cycle."
- 要点：档案要围绕研究问题来选，并在整个项目过程中边产生边整理、边编索引。

### E6

- 位置：2.5.3 Archive preparation
- 原文："The archive is considered complete only at project Closure. It should be capable of independent third-party interrogation, via specific mechanisms as set out explicitly in the Project Design."
- 要点：项目结束时档案才算完整；它应当能让独立的第三方查询核对。

### E7

- 位置：2.5.4 Dissemination
- 原文："Dissemination of results is fundamental to the success of any historic-environment research project."
- 要点：传播成果是历史环境研究项目成功的根本。
