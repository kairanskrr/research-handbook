# Dredze & Wallach 2012：How to Be a Successful PhD Student (in Computer Science (in NLP/ML))

- 资料：`dredze2012successful`，[原文 PDF](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~mdredze/publications/HowtoBeaSuccessfulPhDStudent.1_2.pdf)
- 版本：v1.2（2022-01-25），初版 2012-03-20。页码为 PDF 页序，条目号为原文编号。本地 PDF 的字体编码不标准，原文文本由字符映射还原。

### E1

- 位置：#16 Prioritize，第 4 页
- 原文："It's a good idea to do a time audit if you feel things aren't matching up."
- 要点：觉得时间分配和优先级对不上时，做一次时间审计。

### E2

- 位置：#18 Keep a log，第 4 页
- 原文："Keep a daily log of everything you do and everything you think. It's a good idea to make sure your log is searchable (e.g., plain text or use a note taking program)."
- 要点：每天记下做了什么、想了什么，并且让日志可以搜索。

### E3

- 位置：#20 A social life，第 4 页
- 原文："Take the time to ensure you are happy and healthy, or you won't be as productive or effective in your work life."
- 要点：花时间保证自己快乐和健康，否则工作效率也会下降。

### E4

- 位置：#21 It's okay to get stuck，第 4 页
- 原文："Remember that EVERYONE gets stuck/demoralized/etc."
- 要点：每个人都会卡住、灰心，成功者的区别在于能走出低谷。

### E5

- 位置：#34 Learn when to quit，第 6 页
- 原文："You can learn from failure, but only if you move on. If you find yourself repeatedly lowering your goals and expectations, you aren't making progress."
- 要点：失败能让人学到东西，但前提是继续往前走；如果你在反复降低目标和期望，说明没有进展，该考虑放手，并听取导师的判断。

### E6

- 位置：You and Your Advisor，第 2 页
- 原文："Your relationship with your advisor is the single most important relationship you will have in graduate school."
- 要点：和导师的关系是研究生阶段最重要的关系。

### E7

- 位置：You and Your Advisor，第 2 页
- 原文："Different advisors have different advising styles. Ask them how they run their research group. Talk to their existing students to find out about their advising style and what they expect of their students."
- 要点：导师风格各不相同；直接问导师怎么带组，并向组里的学生了解导师的风格和期望。

### E8

- 位置：#5 Listen to your advisor，第 2 页
- 原文："They won't always be right, but they have been doing research for (in some cases much) longer than you have. (And, if it wasn't clear, your advisor is your boss -- you work for them. When your advisor tells you to do something, do it.)"
- 要点：导师不总是对的，但经验比你多；而且导师是你的上级，导师让你做的事就去做。

### E9

- 位置：#6 Ping your advisor，第 2 页
- 原文："If your adviser hasn't replied to an important email, remind them that you are waiting for their reply. (But first make sure that your email was clear."
- 要点：重要邮件没回复，就提醒导师你在等回复（先确认邮件本身写清楚了）。

### E10

- 位置：#7 Feedback，第 3 页
- 原文："However, feedback is helpful and it doesn't hurt to ask for it more often, e.g., once a semester."
- 要点：至少每年从导师那里得到一次正式反馈；更频繁地主动要反馈，例如每学期一次，也没有坏处。

### E11

- 位置：#8 Meet regularly，第 3 页
- 原文："You should meet with your advisor regularly (e.g., weekly) -- ESPECIALLY if you don't want to."
- 要点：定期（例如每周）和导师见面，越不想见越要见。

### E12

- 位置：#9 Make an agenda，第 3 页
- 原文："If you don't know what you want to talk about, your advisor's unlikely to know either."
- 要点：每次见面前列好议程；你自己不知道要谈什么，导师更不会知道。

### E13

- 位置：#10 Bring results，第 3 页
- 原文："Try to bring results (e.g., graphs, tables, figures) to every meeting."
- 要点：每次见面都带上结果（图、表）。

### E14

- 位置：#11 Start with a summary，第 3 页
- 原文："Your advisor has many research projects. Unlike you, they didn't spend the previous week working on your research project only, and will therefore need to context switch."
- 要点：导师同时管很多项目，需要切换上下文；所以会议开头先总结上次约定的下一步和你这段时间做了什么。

### E15

- 位置：#14 Everyone works differently，第 4 页
- 原文："It's a good idea to discuss these preferences with your advisor so they understand you better and can work with you as effectively as possible."
- 要点：把自己的工作习惯和偏好告诉导师，便于双方更有效地合作。

### E16

- 位置：#17 Make your own "next actions"，第 4 页
- 原文："If you're sitting around waiting for your advisor to tell you what to do next, something is wrong. Find something to do, e.g., read papers."
- 要点：坐等导师告诉你下一步做什么，说明出了问题；要自己找下一步。

### E17

- 位置：#13 Talk to other students，第 4 页
- 原文："This is a good way to learn whether your expectations of yourself and graduate school are realistic, as well as to learn about implementation details, interesting problems and solutions, ways of thinking and problem-solving, and "tricks of the trade"."
- 要点：经常和组内外的同学交流，既能校准自己的期望，也能学到细节、思路和窍门。

### E18

- 位置：#32 First Year，第 6 页
- 原文："As a first year, find a senior student with an interesting research project and offer to help."
- 要点：第一年可以找一位有好项目的高年级同学，主动帮忙，从中学习。

### E19

- 位置：#37 Ask questions，第 7 页
- 原文："Don't be afraid to ask questions and/or ask for help -- you'll learn faster and be more productive than if you try to figure absolutely everything out for yourself."
- 要点：不要怕提问和求助，这比什么都自己摸索学得更快。

### E20

- 位置：#45 Keep track of deadlines，第 8 页
- 原文："If you think you're not on track to meet a deadline, DO tell to your advisor -- they'd rather know sooner than later."
- 要点：觉得赶不上截止日期，一定要告诉导师，越早越好。

### E21

- 位置：#46 Finish writing early，第 8 页
- 原文："Finish your paper (at least) a week before the deadline. [...] it makes it more likely that you'll actually get any useful feedback from your advisor, who probably has several other papers to read before the deadline."
- 要点：至少提前一周写完论文，才有时间得到导师和同事的有用反馈。

### E22

- 位置：#54 Ask for feedback，第 9 页
- 原文："If you give a talk (either a practice talk or a real talk ) ask your audience for feedback on clarity, style, content, presentation, etc."
- 要点：每次报告（包括练习报告）后，向听众要反馈。

### E23

- 位置：Progressing Through Graduate School，第 10 页
- 原文："Don't just speculate and try to compare yourself to others -- ask your advisor."
- 要点：想知道自己进度如何，不要和别人瞎比，直接问导师。
