# 美国化学会 2015：Identifying and Evaluating Hazards in Research Laboratories

- 资料：`acs2015identifying`，[原文](https://www.acs.org/content/dam/acsorg/about/governance/committees/chemicalsafety/publications/identifying-and-evaluating-hazards-in-research-laboratories.pdf)
- 版本：美国化学会化学安全委员会危害识别与评估工作组编写的指南，2015 年。ACS 官网对脚本访问返回拦截页，本摘录读取的是 Internet Archive 2026-10-04 保存的 PDF。

### E1

- 位置：Executive Summary，第 9 页
- 原文："it was prepared in response to a recommendation from CSB to ACS to fill a need for better guidance on hazard assessment."
- 要点：这份指南是应美国化学安全与危害调查委员会（CSB）向 ACS 提出的建议而编写的，用来补上危害评估指导的缺口。

### E2

- 位置：Executive Summary，第 9 页
- 原文："Hazard assessment is a continuous process."
- 要点：危害评估是一个持续的过程。

### E3

- 位置：Executive Summary，第 9 页
- 原文："What-if analysis: An approach that raises a series of questions to help identify things that might go wrong." / "Checklists: A method that tends to be more operational in that it helps researchers remember all of the precautions they are supposed to take."
- 要点：指南介绍的五种工具里包括"如果……会怎样"分析（提一连串问题找出可能出错的地方）和检查清单（帮研究者记住该做的防护）。

### E4

- 位置：1.2 Application，第 11 页
- 原文："This guide was written for researchers without deference to the stage in their careers-- undergraduate students, graduate students, postdoctoral scholars, instructors, PIs, or departmental chairs"
- 要点：这份指南写给所有阶段的研究者，从本科生、研究生到课题负责人和系主任。

### E5

- 位置：6. Change Control，第 27 页
- 原文："Unfortunately, the cause of many accidents and injuries can be traced back to unrecognized changes in the work scope or hazards. Fundamentally, when the work to be performed changes, that change must be evaluated against the current hazard analysis to determine if the hazard analysis continues to be sufficient."
- 要点：很多事故都能追溯到没被注意到的变化；工作一变，就要对照现有的危害分析，看它是否仍然够用。

### E6

- 位置：6.1 Recognizing Change，第 27 页
- 原文："Same basic synthesis, but changing the reactant to a compound with an additional functional group;" / "Scale up;" / "New person on team or losing someone with experience;"
- 要点：值得重新评估的变化举例：同一合成换了多一个官能团的反应物、放大规模、组里来了新人或有经验的人离开等。

### E7

- 位置：5.3，第 26 页
- 原文："Involving multiple people in a review (students, laboratory workers with varying levels of experience, peers, and support staff) is a good defense against unconscious incompetence."
- 要点：让多人（学生、经验不同的实验人员、同事和支持人员）一起审查，是防止"不知道自己不知道"的好办法。

### E8

- 位置：5.3，第 26 页
- 原文："Discuss previous incidents and near misses." / "Maintain open lines of communication--talk about safety in research meetings."
- 要点：讨论以前的事故和未遂事件；在组会上谈安全。

### E9

- 位置：4.3 Researcher and Laboratory Worker，第 24 页
- 原文："Researchers have a right and a responsibility to ask challenging and clarifying questions to ensure the scope of work and all hazards and controls are well understood before beginning an experiment or research protocol."
- 要点：研究人员有权利也有责任在开始实验前提出质疑和澄清问题，确保弄清工作范围、所有危害和防护措施。
