# AEA RCT Registry：说明、注册指南与常见问题

- 资料：`aea2026rct`，[About the AEA RCT Registry](https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/policies/rct-registry)；[注册指南](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/site/instructions)；[常见问题](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/site/faq)；[首页](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/)
- 版本：2026 年 10 月 6 日读取的网页。

### E1

- 位置：About the AEA RCT Registry
- 原文："It is the policy of the AEA journals that all work involving field experiments must be registered prior to submission for publication. (Laboratory experiments do not need to be registered at this time.) Researchers may register during any phase of the trial—forthcoming, ongoing, or completed—but are encouraged to register early."
- 要点：AEA 期刊要求所有田野实验在投稿前登记；实验室实验目前不要求。试验的任何阶段都可以登记，但鼓励尽早。

### E2

- 位置：About the AEA RCT Registry
- 原文："Registration is free and requires a minimal amount of information, allowing for amendments and additional material, such as reports, articles, data, and code, to be provided at any point during the life of the study. Version history is maintained."
- 要点：登记免费，所需信息很少；之后可以随时修改、补充材料，修改历史会被保留。

### E3

- 位置：About the AEA RCT Registry
- 原文："Select fields can be hidden/embargoed until the trial is completed so as to not influence survey respondents while the survey is in the field."
- 要点：部分字段可以在试验结束前隐藏，以免影响正在接受调查的人。

### E4

- 位置：Registration Guidelines，Required Information
- 原文："To register a trial, you must complete the following fields:" / "Outcomes (End Points)" / "Experimental Design (Public)" / "Planned Number of Observations"
- 要点：登记必填项包括：结果变量、实验设计、计划样本量等。

### E5

- 位置：Registration Guidelines
- 原文："When a trial is registered before the start of its intervention, it is marked as a pre-registered trial with an orange clock icon."
- 要点：在干预开始前登记的试验，会被标为"预注册"。

### E6

- 位置：Registration Guidelines，Registration Review Criteria
- 原文："The criteria is not intended to review the quality of the research, nor the ethics of the research."
- 要点：平台对登记的审核不评判研究质量，也不评判研究伦理。

### E7

- 位置：首页
- 原文："AEA RCT Registry currently lists 12869 studies with locations in 172 countries."
- 要点：读取时平台登记了 12869 项研究，分布在 172 个国家。
