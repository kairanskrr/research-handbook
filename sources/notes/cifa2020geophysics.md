# 英国特许考古学家协会 2020：Standard and guidance for archaeological geophysical survey

- 资料：`cifa2020geophysics`，[PDF](https://www.archaeologists.net/sites/default/files/2023-11/CIfA-SandG-Geophysical-Survey-2020.pdf)
- 版本：CIfA 2014 年 12 月首发、2020 年 10 月小幅更新的考古地球物理勘探标准和指南。位置写节号或附录名。

### E1

- 位置：Definition of geophysical survey
- 原文："Archaeological geophysical survey uses non-intrusive and non-destructive techniques to determine the presence or absence of anomalies likely to be caused by archaeological features, structures or deposits"
- 要点：考古地球物理勘探用非侵入、非破坏的技术，判断有没有可能由考古遗存引起的异常。

### E2

- 位置：Annex 2: Report contents, Results
- 原文："Since the cause of anomalies often cannot be unambiguously determined based on geophysical measurements alone, the text should also be clear about the degree of uncertainty pertaining to inferences drawn from the results."
- 要点：单凭物探数据往往无法确定异常的成因，报告要写清推断的不确定程度。

### E3

- 位置：Annex 1: Field techniques
- 原文："the requirement of a Section 42 Licence for survey over scheduled monuments and other protected places"
- 要点：这份英国指南提到，对列级古迹（scheduled monuments）等受保护地点做物探，需要"第 42 条许可"（Section 42 Licence）。
