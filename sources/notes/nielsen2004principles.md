# Nielsen 2004：Principles of Effective Research

- 资料：`nielsen2004principles`，[Internet Archive 快照](https://web.archive.org/web/20250105211800/https://michaelnielsen.org/blog/principles-of-effective-research/)（原地址 2026-10 返回 404）
- 版本：Technical note，2004 年 7 月。位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Self-discipline
- 原文："The first factor is having clarity about what one wants to achieve, why one wants to achieve it, and how to go about achieving it. [...] Conversely, I think the main cause of aimlessness and procrastination is when you lack clarity on one or more of these points."
- 要点：清楚"要做成什么、为什么、怎么做"是自律的第一要素；拖延和漫无目的，主要源于其中某点不清楚。

### E2

- 位置：Self-discipline
- 原文："He was shocked to discover that after factoring in all the other activities he engaged in each day – interruptions, email, surfing the net, the phone, fruitless meetings, chatting with friends, and so on – he was averaging only half an hour of research per day."
- 要点：有人用秒表记录研究时间，发现扣除各种打断后每天平均只做了半小时研究。如实记录时间是改变的基础。

### E3

- 位置：The skills of the problem-solver
- 原文："For the most part, it’s better to be doing something, rather than nothing, provided, of course, that you set time aside frequently for reflection and reconsideration of your goals."
- 要点：大多数时候动起来比停着好，前提是经常留出时间反思和重新审视目标。

### E4

- 位置：The skills of the problem-solver
- 原文："One of the most common mistakes made by researchers is to hold on very closely to a particular problem formulation. [...] if you can find a related problem, or reformulate a problem in a way that permits you to move forward, that is progress."
- 要点：常见错误是死守问题的某一种表述；找到相关问题或换一种表述让自己能往前走，就是进展。

### E5

- 位置：Self-discipline
- 原文："Researchers are typically under little immediate social pressure to produce research results. [...] The key is to be accountable to other people. Some simple ways of achieving such accountability are to take on students, to collaborate with colleagues, or to set up mentoring relationships with colleagues."
- 要点：研究者通常没有多少产出研究成果的即时外部压力；自律的关键之一是对他人负责；与同事合作、建立指导关系都能带来这种约束。

### E6

- 位置：开头
- 原文："We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act but a habit."
- 要点：文章的出发点：卓越不是一次行为，而是一种习惯。

### E7

- 位置：Integrating research into the rest of your life
- 原文："Motivation and desire alone are not enough. You also need to have the rest of your life in order to be an effective researcher. Make sure you’re fit. Look after your health."
- 要点：只有热情不够，还要把生活的其他部分安排好：保持健康，照顾家人，享受生活。

### E8

- 位置：Integrating research into the rest of your life
- 原文："while the neglect paid short-term dividends in increased productivity, over the total period of writing the book I believe it probably cost me productivity"
- 要点：作者曾为写书忽视健康和人际关系，短期产出增加了，但整体来看可能反而降低了产出。

### E9

- 位置：Self-discipline
- 原文："It is a tempting but ultimately counterproductive fallacy to believe that self-discipline is merely a matter of will" / "The key is to be accountable to other people."
- 要点：自律不只是意志力问题；关键之一是让自己对别人负责，例如带学生、与人合作、建立指导关系。

### E10

- 位置：Aspects of research: self-development and the creative process
- 原文："However, some people end up obsessed with writing as many papers as possible, as quickly as possible. While the short-term rewards of this are attractive (jobs, grants, reputation and prizes), the long-term costs are significant. In particular, it can lead to stagnation, and plateauing as a researcher."
- 要点：只顾尽快多发论文，短期回报诱人，但长期代价是停滞。要在产出和自我提升之间保持平衡。

### E11

- 位置：Developing research strengths
- 原文："you want to develop unique combinations of abilities" / "The few who do put in this effort often achieve spectacular results."
- 要点：培养独特的能力组合；新领域融合两个旧领域时，少数肯系统学习另一领域的人，常常取得惊人的成果。

### E12

- 位置：Developing research strengths
- 原文："You are almost certainly better off reading deeply in the ten most important papers of a research field than you are skimming the top five hundred."
- 要点：精读一个领域最重要的十篇论文，几乎肯定比浏览五百篇更有用。

### E13

- 位置：Develop a high-quality research environment
- 原文："improving your environment is something anyone can do; students, in particular, often underestimate the magnitude of the changes they can bring about."
- 要点：任何人都能改善自己的研究环境，例如组织讨论班、读书会；学生常常低估自己能带来的改变。

### E14

- 位置：Vision
- 原文："A good vision answers questions like: What sort of researcher would I like to become?"
- 要点：好的长期目标要回答"我想成为怎样的研究者"等问题。

### E15

- 位置：Developing research strengths
- 原文："(a) quickly skimming a great deal of work, to keep track of what is known, and what sort of problems people are thinking about, and (b) based on that skimming, picking a dozen or so papers each year to read deeply"
- 要点：跟进领域的办法：大量快速浏览，了解已知什么、别人在想什么问题；再从中每年挑十几篇精读。
