# APA：Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts 期刊页面和投稿说明

- 资料：`apa2024aesthetics`，[原文](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/aca)（美国心理学会第 10 分会的官方期刊）
- 版本：网页。原站对脚本访问返回拦截页，本条摘录取自 Internet Archive 2024-07-07 的快照（http://web.archive.org/web/20240707072656/https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/aca）。

### E1

- 位置：页面开头；Journal scope statement
- 原文："This journal is a publication of APA Division 10 (Society for the Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts)" / "To that end, we publish manuscripts presenting original empirical research and papers that synthesize and evaluate extant research that relate to the psychology of aesthetics, creativity, and the arts." / "Generally, qualitative work, case studies, essays, interviews, biographical profiles, and literature reviews are discouraged."
- 要点：该刊是美国心理学会第 10 分会的期刊；刊发原创实证研究和综合评价已有研究的论文；一般不鼓励质性研究、案例研究、随笔、访谈、传记和文献综述。

### E2

- 位置：Submission guidelines，Registered Reports
- 原文："This is a partial manuscript that includes the introduction and methods section of the manuscript (i.e., the hypotheses, rationale for the study, experimental design, and methodological details including power analyses for sample size, statistical analysis pipeline, and any pilot data)." / "In this stage, the manuscript will be reviewed for significance and rationale for the study as well as the rigor and clarity of the methodological approach. If the partial manuscript is accepted, this amounts to provisional acceptance of the full report regardless of the outcome of the study."
- 要点：接收注册报告：先投引言和方法（假设、实验设计、用于确定样本量的统计功效分析、分析流程），审查研究意义和方法的严谨、清楚；这一步通过，无论结果如何都原则上接收。

### E3

- 位置：Submission guidelines，Ethical Principles
- 原文："APA expects authors to have their data available throughout the editorial review process and for at least 5 years after the date of publication."
- 要点：APA 要求作者在审稿期间和发表后至少 5 年内能提供数据。

### E4

- 位置：EDI Efforts，Inclusive reporting standards
- 原文："Participant sample descriptions (recommended)" / "Constraints on Generality (COG) statements (recommended)"
- 要点：建议描述被试样本，建议写"结论推广范围的限制"声明。
