# NeurIPS 2025：Policy on the Use of Large Language Models

- 资料：`neurips2025llm`，[原文](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/LLM)
- 版本：NeurIPS 2025 关于使用大语言模型的政策网页（分作者和审稿人两部分），2026-10-07 读取。位置写网页小标题。只作为机器学习方向一个顶级会议的例子，其他会议和期刊的规定可能不同。

### E1

- 位置：NeurIPS 2025 LLM Policy for Reviewers
- 原文："You must keep everything relating to the review process confidential. Do not use ideas, code, or results from the submissions in your own work until they become publicly available. Do not talk about or share submissions with anyone or any LLMs"
- 要点：审稿过程的一切都要保密；稿件公开之前，不得在自己的工作中使用其中的想法、代码或结果；不得和任何人或任何大模型谈论或分享投稿。

### E2

- 位置：NeurIPS 2025 LLM Policy for Reviewers，FAQ 2
- 原文："You may only use ideas, code, or results from submissions (1) after they become publicly available, and (2) if the author has granted the appropriate permission/license that covers your intended usage."
- 要点：只有在稿件公开之后、且作者授予的许可涵盖你的用途时，才能使用稿件中的想法、代码或结果。

### E3

- 位置：NeurIPS 2025 LLM Policy for Reviewers，FAQ 4
- 原文："You can use resources (e.g. publications on Google Scholar, Wikipedia articles, interactions with LLMs and/or human experts without sharing the paper submissions) to enhance your understanding of certain concepts and to check the grammaticality and phrasing of your written review."
- 要点：审稿人可以在不分享稿件的前提下，借助文献、维基百科、大模型或专家弄懂概念，并检查审稿意见的语法和措辞。

### E4

- 位置：NeurIPS 2025 LLM Policy for Reviewers，FAQ 4
- 原文："Please remember that you are responsible for the quality and accuracy of your submitted review regardless of any tools, resources, or other help you used to construct the final review."
- 要点：不管用了什么工具或帮助，审稿意见的质量和准确性都由审稿人负责。

### E5

- 位置：LLM Policy for Authors
- 原文："The use of LLMs in implementing the method should be described in the experimental setup section (or equivalent) if it is an important, original, or non-standard component of the approach."
- 要点：大模型如果是方法中重要、原创或非标准的组成部分，要在实验设置部分写清楚。

### E6

- 位置：LLM Policy for Authors，FAQ 2
- 原文："Only humans are eligible to be authors. You, as an author, are fully responsible for all the content in your paper, including text, figures, and methodology, regardless of what tools (e.g., LLMs) you have used."
- 要点：只有人能当作者；不管用了什么工具，作者都要对论文的全部内容负责。

### E7

- 位置：LLM Policy for Authors
- 原文："If an investigation determines a violation, NeurIPS reserves the right to revoke the paper's publication status. Examples of such violations include content that is not scientifically sound (e.g. using references generated by an LLM without conducting the due diligence to verify correctness, existence and appropriateness in the manuscript’s context)"
- 要点：违规可能被撤销发表资格；例子之一是用大模型生成的参考文献，却没有核实它是否存在、是否正确、是否切题。

### E8

- 位置：LLM Policy for Authors，FAQ 3
- 原文："No, if the LLM is used only for writing, editing, or formatting purposes and does not impact the core methodology, scientific rigorousness, or originality of the research, declaration is not required."
- 要点：只用大模型润色、编辑或排版，不影响方法、严谨性和原创性的，不需要在论文里声明。

### E9

- 位置：LLM Policy for Authors
- 原文："Some (including free) tools may retain input data for further model training purposes, so authors should exercise caution to account for individual privacy considerations."
- 要点：有些工具会保留输入的数据用于训练，要注意个人隐私。

### E10

- 位置：LLM Policy for Reviewers
- 原文："You must keep everything relating to the review process confidential." / "Do not talk about or share submissions with anyone or any LLMs"
- 要点：审稿时一切保密，不要和任何人或任何大模型讨论、分享投稿内容。
