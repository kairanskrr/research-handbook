# 美国政治学会 2012：A Guide to Professional Ethics in Political Science (2nd ed., revised 2012)

- 资料：`apsa2012ethics`，[原文 PDF](https://www.apsanet.org/Portals/54/APSA%20Files/publications/ethicsguideweb.pdf)
- 版本：美国政治学会（American Political Science Association，APSA）职业伦理、权利与自由委员会编，第二版，2012 年修订；APSA 官网免费提供 PDF。位置写条款编号。

### E1

- 位置：§III.A, 第 6 条
- 原文："Researchers have an ethical obligation to facilitate the evaluation of their [...] knowledge claims through data access, production transparency, and analytic transparency so that their work can be tested or replicated."
- 要点：研究者有伦理义务，通过数据获取、生产透明、分析透明三方面，让别人能检验和重复自己基于证据的结论。

### E2

- 位置：§III.A, 6.1 Data access
- 原文："If these are data they [...] themselves generated or collected, researchers should provide access to those data or explain why they cannot."
- 要点：自己生成或收集的数据，要提供获取途径，或者说明为什么不能提供。

### E3

- 位置：§III.A, 6.2 Production transparency
- 原文："Researchers providing access to data they themselves generated or collected, should offer a full account of the procedures used to collect or generate the data."
- 要点：提供自己收集的数据时，要完整说明数据是怎样收集或生成的。

### E4

- 位置：§III.A, 6.3 Analytic Transparency
- 原文："Researchers making evidence-based knowledge claims should provide a full account of how they draw their analytic conclusions from the data, i.e., clearly explicate the links connecting data to conclusions."
- 要点：要完整说明怎样从数据得出结论，讲清数据和结论之间的联系。

### E5

- 位置：§III.A, 6.4
- 原文："Scholars may be exempted from Data Access and Production Transparency in order to (A) address well-founded privacy and confidentiality concerns, including abiding by relevant human subjects regulation; and/or (B) comply with relevant and applicable laws, including copyright."
- 要点：出于有充分理由的隐私和保密考虑（包括遵守人类受试者法规），或为遵守法律（包括版权），可以免除数据获取和生产透明的义务。

### E6

- 位置：§III.A, 6.6
- 原文："scholars may postpone data access and production transparency for one year after publication of evidence-based knowledge claims relying on those data"
- 要点：收集数据的人有优先使用权，可以在发表后推迟一年再公开数据。

### E7

- 位置：§III.B, 10.1
- 原文："Teachers cannot represent themselves as authors of independent student research; and research assistance, paid or unpaid, requires full acknowledgement."
- 要点：教师不能把学生的独立研究署成自己的；研究助理工作无论是否有报酬，都必须充分致谢。

### E8

- 位置：§III.B, 10.2
- 原文："As advisers, faculty members are not entitled to claim joint authorship with a student of a thesis or dissertation."
- 要点：导师无权要求在学生的学位论文上共同署名。
