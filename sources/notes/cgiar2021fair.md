# 国际农业研究磋商组织 2021：CGIAR Open and FAIR Data Assets Policy

- 资料：`cgiar2021fair`，[原文 PDF](https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/3c39bf16-b35d-4faa-b509-b29d045a9144/content)
- 版本：2021 年 4 月 16 日起生效，取代 2013 年的开放获取与数据管理政策。国际农业研究磋商组织（CGIAR）是一个由多个国际农业研究中心组成的全球合作组织，本政策适用于它的各研究中心和合作伙伴。位置写条款号。

### E1

- 位置：1.1 CGIAR's Commitment
- 原文："CGIAR adheres to the principle that the results of its research and development activities are international public goods and is committed to their widespread dissemination and use to achieve the maximum impact to advantage the poor, especially smallholder farmers in developing countries."
- 要点：CGIAR 把研究成果看作国际公共物品，致力于广泛传播，让穷人、尤其是发展中国家的小农受益。

### E2

- 位置：1.1 CGIAR's Commitment
- 原文："CGIAR is therefore committed to sharing outputs of its research that are as open as possible and always Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable (FAIR)"
- 要点：CGIAR 承诺研究产出尽可能开放，并且始终可查找、可获取、可互操作、可复用（FAIR）。

### E3

- 位置：Annex 2 iv. Datasets and databases
- 原文："Datasets (and any relevant data collection and analysis tools) shall, subject to any additional donor requirements, be deposited in a suitable repository and made Open and FAIR as soon as possible and in any event within 12 months of completion of the data collection or appropriate project milestone, or within 6 months of publication of the information products underpinned by that data, whichever is sooner."
- 要点：数据集要存入合适的仓库并开放：最迟在数据收集完成（或项目节点）后 12 个月内，或在用到这些数据的成果发表后 6 个月内，以先到者为准。

### E4

- 位置：4.1 c) ii
- 原文："Data Management Plans shall be developed at project formulation to ensure that data assets conform to accepted interoperability standards (e.g. ontologies, controlled vocabularies)."
- 要点：项目立项时就要写数据管理计划，保证数据符合公认的互操作标准（例如本体、受控词表）。

### E5

- 位置：4.2
- 原文："Data assets shall be managed responsibly, with due regard to privacy and ethical considerations"
- 要点：数据要负责任地管理，顾及隐私和伦理。

### E6

- 位置：4.1 b) ii
- 原文："Data assets may only be published without Open Access if they are associated with a Restricted Use Agreement or Limited Exclusivity Agreement (both as defined in Annex 1), or other contractual obligation to retain confidentiality."
- 要点：只有在有限制使用协议、有限排他协议或其他保密合同义务时，数据才可以不开放。
