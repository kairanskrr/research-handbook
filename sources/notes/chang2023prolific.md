# Chang & Yeh 2023：Prolific philosophy authors' preferences regarding scientific collaboration, journal levels, and author role

- 资料：`chang2023prolific`，[原文](https://hdl.handle.net/2142/117350)
- 版本：iConference 2023 会议海报，伊利诺伊大学 IDEALS 机构库公开的条目。本文只读了公开摘要。样本是 2011–2020 年在哲学期刊发文最多的 100 位作者，其中 28 位主要在心理学领域工作。

### E1

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："By analyzing articles published by the 100 most prolific authors in terms of the number of articles published in philosophy journals between 2011 and 2020, this study confirmed that most philosophy researchers tend-ed not to perform collaborative research, with 56.5% of the 3,773 analyzed articles being single-authored articles."
- 要点：分析 2011–2020 年在哲学期刊发文最多的 100 位作者的 3,773 篇文章，56.5% 是单人署名。

### E2

- 位置：摘要
- 原文："Moreover, not all the authors were affiliated with philosophy-related institutes; specifically, 28 of the authors primarily worked within psycholog-ical fields and had a higher interest in coauthorship and journals with higher levels of prestige than the 72 philosophy authors."
- 要点：这 100 人里有 28 位主要在心理学领域工作，他们比另外 72 位哲学作者更倾向合著。
