# Vinitsky：Expectation Setting

- 资料：`vinitskyexpectation`，[原文](https://www.eugenevinitsky.com/posts/expectation-setting)
- 版本：作者主页文章，年份待确认，2026-10-05 读取。这是一位导师写给自己组内学生的期望说明，位置写网页小标题。

### E1

- 位置：开头
- 原文："It is quite hard to understand what a successful first year or two of graduate school looks like. This document is intended to help you set a standard so that you are neither too hard on yourself, stressed out about meeting some imaginary expectation that you could not possibly achieve, nor too easy on yourself because you misunderstand how much hard work is required."
- 要点：新生很难知道"做得好"是什么样；写明期望，是为了让学生既不因想象中的标准过度焦虑，也不因误判工作量而太松懈。

### E2

- 位置：开头
- 原文："On the other hand, if you're struggling to hit these expectations we should have a chat to figure out what's going on and find a way to help you!"
- 要点：达不到期望时，应该和导师谈一谈，一起找原因和办法。

### E3

- 位置：Year one / Meetings
- 原文："I can’t read your mind and it’s not super-productive for me to sit and read your research notebook with you so it’d be good if you have your research in some kind of digestible form"
- 要点：导师读不了你的心，也不适合在会上陪你翻研究笔记；要把进展整理成易读的形式，例如提前一天发给导师的文档。

### E4

- 位置：Year Three
- 原文："By this point you're setting the direction, not me."
- 要点：到第三年，研究方向应主要由学生来定，而不是导师。
