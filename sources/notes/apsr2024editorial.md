# APSR 编辑部：American Political Science Review Editorial Report: Executive Summary (Fall 2023)

- 资料：`apsr2024editorial`，[原文](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/CFCD95D2AF8F273A6CA9D1ABAD66BD47/core-reader)
- 版本：Political Science Today 4(1): 56–60, 2024 年 2 月，DOI 10.1017/psj.2024.21；改编自 2023 年 10 月提交给 APSR 编委会的完整报告。剑桥大学出版社网站公开全文。

### E1

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："The current editorial team began their editorship on June 1, 2020."
- 要点：这份报告的数据来自 2020 年 6 月起任职的编辑团队。

### E2

- 位置：Executive Summary
- 原文："We have reduced the median days from submission to first invitation for peer review from 13 (prior team) to 10; from submission to reject after peer review from 84 (prior team) to 74.5; and from submission to invitation to revise after peer review from 129.5 (prior team) to 89. Our median days from submission to desk reject stands at 10"
- 要点：投稿到审后拒稿的中位数 74.5 天，到审后邀请修改 89 天，到不送审直接拒稿 10 天。

### E3

- 位置：Summary of Submissions and Acceptance Decisions
- 原文："Our team’s overall final acceptance rate for all submissions that were initially submitted and have a final decision by our team is 7.9%."
- 要点：7.9% 是本届编辑团队任内首次投稿、且由本团队做出最终决定的全部稿件的总录用率，不是某一年的录用率。

### E4

- 位置：Workflow and Turnaround Times，Table 1 Submissions, Decisions, and Turnaround Times 的注
- 原文："Note: The table includes manuscripts submitted between 07/01/2008 and 07/31/2023."
- 要点：审稿时长表收的是 2008 年 7 月 1 日到 2023 年 7 月 31 日投来的稿件（按历届编辑团队分列），数据截至 2023 年 7 月底。
