# Papoutsoglou 等人 2020：Enabling reusability of plant phenomic datasets with MIAPPE 1.1

- 资料：`papoutsoglou2020enabling`，[原文](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.16544)
- 版本：New Phytologist 227: 260–273，2020，CC BY 开放获取。原文文本取自斯洛文尼亚机构仓库 DiRROS 存放的出版社 PDF。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："the reuse and meta-analyses of phenotyping data are particularly challenging due to the heterogeneity of this domain that encompasses many types of experimental sites (field, glasshouse, controlled environment), plants (crops, forest trees), collected data (images, physical measurements, chemical assays, molecular biology assays), and experimental designs (factors being tested, timing, field layouts, etc.)."
- 要点：植物表型数据很难复用和汇总分析，因为差异太大：试验场所（田间、温室、人工气候室）、植物（作物、林木）、数据类型、试验设计（因素、时间、田间布局）都各不相同。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Because the original experimental data followed no standardisation guidelines, the authors had to manually assemble detailed metadata during the preprocessing of the data from descriptions in unstructured text."
- 要点：一个复用马铃薯田间试验数据的例子：原始数据没有按任何标准整理，复用者只好从零散的文字描述里手工拼出元数据。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："MIAPPE (Minimum Information About a Plant Phenotyping Experiment), the first and so far only community metadata standard for the plant phenotyping domain"
- 要点：MIAPPE（植物表型试验最少信息标准）是植物表型领域第一个、也是（截至该文）唯一的社区元数据标准。

### E4

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："to minimise the chance of a researcher missing important information in the documentation of an experiment"
- 要点：MIAPPE 的指导原则之一，是尽量减少研究者在记录试验时漏掉重要信息。

### E5

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："(i) the extension of MIAPPE to accommodate a wider range of use cases (including those relevant to perennial and woody plants)"
- 要点：1.1 版把适用范围扩展到更多情形，包括多年生植物和木本植物。

### E6

- 位置：Results，MIAPPE 1.1 的 Event 概念
- 原文："describes a discrete occurrence at a specific time that affected the whole study or one or more observation units, which can be the application of a field/glasshouse practice (e.g. planting, fungicide application) or an unpredictable happening (e.g. rainfall, pathogen attack)."
- 要点：MIAPPE 里的"事件"记录在某个时间点影响整个试验或部分观测单位的事情，可以是田间或温室操作（播种、施杀菌剂），也可以是意外（降雨、病原菌侵染）。

### E7

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："Four key components are needed from research communities to meet these requirements: metadata standards which list the fields required for interpreting the data from a given experimental domain;"
- 要点：要让数据可查找、可获取、可互操作、可复用，研究社区需要四样东西，第一样是元数据标准：列出解读某类试验数据必需的字段。
