# Sargeant 等人 2016：Methods and Processes of Developing the STROBE-Vet Statement

- 资料：`sargeant2016strobevet`，[PMC 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5115188/)
- 版本：Journal of Veterinary Internal Medicine 30(6):1887–1895，2016 年，开放获取（CC BY-NC）。同一文章同时发表在另外四本期刊。位置写小标题。

### E1

- 位置：Abstract，Objective
- 原文："To develop an extension of the STROBE (Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology) statement that addresses unique reporting requirements for observational studies in veterinary medicine related to health, production, welfare, and food safety."
- 要点：STROBE-Vet 是 STROBE（加强流行病学观察性研究报告）的兽医扩展版，针对兽医学中关于健康、生产、福利和食品安全的观察性研究。

### E2

- 位置：Introduction（第一段）
- 原文："Thus, observational studies may be used to estimate the prevalence or incidence of a condition, to investigate the distribution of conditions in time and space, to explore risk factors and compare management options, to create explanatory models, or to evaluate diagnostic test accuracy."
- 要点：兽医观察性研究可用来估计患病率或发病率、研究疾病的时空分布、探索风险因素和比较管理方式、建立解释模型、评价诊断试验的准确性。

### E3

- 位置：Introduction（第一段）
- 原文："Observational studies are a common methodological approach in veterinary research"
- 要点：观察性研究是兽医研究中常见的方法。

### E4

- 位置：Results（scope）
- 原文："Studies involving human health outcomes related to animal exposure were considered outside the scope of this initiative. For these studies, the original STROBE statement would be the appropriate guideline to use."
- 要点：以人的健康为结局、研究动物暴露的研究不在 STROBE-Vet 范围内，应使用原版 STROBE。

### E5

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："However, in veterinary medicine, many observational studies do not adhere strictly to one of these three classical designs, and large population cohort studies are rare."
- 要点：兽医学里很多观察性研究并不严格属于队列、病例对照、横断面三种经典设计之一，大规模人群队列研究很少。

### E6

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："In veterinary medicine, there generally are two components to the concept of “participant:” the owner/manager of the animals included in the study population and the animals themselves." / "a footnote was added to note this point and to recommend that relevant information concerning both types of “participants” should be reported."
- 要点：兽医研究里"参与者"一般有两层：动物的主人或管理者，以及动物本身；清单加了脚注，建议两类"参与者"的相关信息都要报告。

### E7

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："It is common in veterinary medicine, particularly in livestock and shelter medicine (where companion animals are kenneled), for animals to be housed or managed in groups." / "It is necessary to account for any non‐independence of the observational units in the design, sampling strategy, and statistical analysis to avoid violating the assumption of independence underlying many statistical procedures."
- 要点：兽医学里动物成群饲养或管理很常见，尤其是家畜和收容所医学；设计、抽样和统计分析都要处理观测单位之间的不独立。

### E8

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："The non‐independence of observational units may be hierarchical, for instance animals within pens, pens within barns, barns within same‐owner facilities. However, this is not always the case." / "non‐independence can also result from repeated samples taken over time from the same animal or facility."
- 要点：不独立可能是层级式的（动物在栏里、栏在圈舍里、圈舍在同一主人的设施里），但并非总是如此；同一动物或设施多次采样也会造成不独立。

### E9

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："An example of an addition is item 7 (variables), which now calls for the specification of the putative causal structure (with a causal diagram being highly encouraged) for all hypothesis‐driven studies."
- 要点：新增要求：所有检验假设的研究都要说明设想的因果结构，强烈鼓励画因果图。

### E10

- 位置：Discussion
- 原文："With numbered subitems, the transparency item addresses sources of funding, conflicts of interest, authors' roles, ethical approval (animal, human, or data use, as applicable), and the use of any quality standards."
- 要点：最后一项扩展为"透明度"：资金来源、利益冲突、作者分工、伦理审批（动物、人或数据使用）、采用的质量标准。

### E11

- 位置：Introduction
- 原文："We strongly recommend that the STROBE‐Vet checklist be used in conjunction with the explanation and elaboration document for all observational studies related to animal health, production, welfare, or food safety outcomes."
- 要点：作者强烈建议所有涉及动物健康、生产、福利或食品安全结局的观察性研究，把 STROBE-Vet 清单和解释文件一起使用。
