# IEEE SA: Develop Standards

- Source: `ieeesadevelop`, [original](https://standards.ieee.org/develop/)
- Version: Web page of the IEEE Standards Association (IEEE SA), read on 2026-10-06.

### E1

- Location: Top of page
- Quote: "Standards drive technological innovation, fuel growth of global markets, expand consumer choice, support interoperability and help protect public health and safety."
- Key point: IEEE SA holds that standards drive technological innovation, support interoperability between different vendors' equipment, and help protect public health and safety.

### E2

- Location: Top of page
- Quote: "IEEE Standards are developed using a time-tested, effective and trusted process. This process is broken down into a six stage life cycle."
- Key point: IEEE standards are developed through an established process divided into a six-stage life cycle.

### E3

- Location: Our Principles — IEEE Standards Development Principles
- Quote: "These principles provide a community for voluntary cooperation among interested parties and stakeholders, enable technical excellence, global interoperability, and innovation to foster economic growth and societal prosperity."
- Key point: Standards development relies on voluntary cooperation among interested parties; its principles include direct participation, due process, broad consensus, balance, transparency, and openness.

### E4

- Location: Standards Board Committees
- Quote: "Patent Committee (PatCom) – Recommends updates to the IEEE SA patent policy, provides educational material related to the patent policy, and reviews conformity with the patent policy procedures."
- Key point: IEEE SA has a Patent Committee responsible for patent policy; the relationship between patents and standards is a formal issue in standards development.
