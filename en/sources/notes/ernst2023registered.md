# Ernst & Baldassarre 2023: Registered Reports in Software Engineering

- Source: `ernst2023registered`, [original](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03649)
- Version: arXiv preprint v1 (February 2023); published version in Empirical Software Engineering 28(2):55, 2023.

### E1

- Location: abstract
- Quote: "In software engineering research, registered reports were first introduced in the International Conference on Mining Software Repositories (MSR) in 2020. They are now established in three conferences and two pre-eminent journals, including this one (EMSE)."
- Key point: In software engineering, Registered Reports were first introduced at the International Conference on Mining Software Repositories (MSR) in 2020; by 2023 they had been adopted by three conferences and two journals (including Empirical Software Engineering).

### E2

- Location: §2.1
- Quote: "Many journals now support the format, with the Empirical Software Engineering Journal (EMSE) supporting them as of 2020." / "As of 2022, the ACM journal Transactions on Software" [...] "Engineering and Methodology (TOSEM) hosts a Registered Paper initiative"
- Key point: The Empirical Software Engineering journal has supported Registered Reports since 2020; since 2022, the ACM journal TOSEM (Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology) has had a "Registered Paper" initiative.

### E3

- Location: §3
- Quote: "The modification from the typical RR approach at the Empirical Software Engineering Journal (EMSE) is that Stage 1 is managed as a conference track. Current options include the International Conference on Mining Software Repositories, the International Conference on Software Maintenance and Evolution, and the Empirical Software Engineering and Measurement Conference."
- Key point: At the Empirical Software Engineering journal, Stage 1 is reviewed by a dedicated conference track, currently MSR, the International Conference on Software Maintenance and Evolution (ICSME), or the Empirical Software Engineering and Measurement conference (ESEM); the full Stage 2 paper is submitted to the journal.
