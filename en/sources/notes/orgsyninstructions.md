# Organic Syntheses: Instructions for Authors

- Source: `orgsyninstructions`, [original](https://www.orgsyn.org/instructions.aspx)
- Version: Instructions for Authors page on the website of Organic Syntheses (a journal of synthetic methods, founded 1921, free to read). Read 2026-10-06. The page is not paginated; locations give subheadings or paragraph content.

### E1

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "All organic chemists have experienced frustration at one time or another when attempting to repeat reactions based on experimental procedures found in journal articles."
- Key point: Nearly every organic chemist has been frustrated trying to repeat a reaction from a journal's experimental procedure and failing.

### E2

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "To ensure reproducibility, Organic Syntheses requires experimental procedures written with considerably more detail as compared to the typical procedures found in other journals and in the "Supporting Information" sections of papers. In addition, each Organic Syntheses procedure is carefully "checked" for reproducibility in the laboratory of a member of the Board of Editors."
- Key point: To ensure reproducibility, the journal requires procedures in far more detail than typical journals and Supporting Information, and every procedure is checked by repeating it in the lab of a Board member.

### E3

- Location: Checking of procedures
- Quote: "At least two successful runs of each step must be completed, at least one of which must be on exactly the submitting author's scale"
- Key point: During checking, each step must be run successfully at least twice, at least once on exactly the submitting author's scale.

### E4

- Location: Checking of procedures
- Quote: "In general, the yields and levels of selectivity obtained by the checking authors must be no less than ca. 5% below that reported by the submitting authors."
- Key point: The checkers' yields and selectivity should generally be no more than about 5% below those reported by the submitting authors.

### E5

- Location: Checking of procedures
- Quote: "the Organic Syntheses editor and their coworker(s) are now credited as "Checking Authors" on all articles and should be included in the list of authors in citations to published Organic Syntheses articles."
- Key point: The checking editor and coworkers are now credited as "Checking Authors" and should be included in the author list when the article is cited.

### E6

- Location: Scale / reproducibility paragraph
- Quote: "it is required that each reaction or experimental operation will have been carried out at least twice on exactly the scale described in the procedure" / "It is recommended that the reaction(s) and operations be tested by a coworker not been involved in the project as an "internal check""
- Key point: Before submission, each reaction or operation must have been run at least twice at exactly the stated scale; an "internal check" by a colleague not involved in the project is recommended.

### E7

- Location: Scale / reproducibility paragraph
- Quote: "A range in yield over several runs of more than 10% may indicate that there are variables affecting the reaction that have not been identified"
- Key point: A yield range of more than 10% across runs may mean there are unidentified variables affecting the reaction.

### E8

- Location: Common Omissions
- Quote: "The source of all starting materials and reagents must be provided and the purity of commercial reagents should be included."
- Key point: Give the source of all starting materials and reagents, and the purity of commercial reagents.

### E9

- Location: Common Omissions
- Quote: "Room temperature and vacuum pressures must be defined explicitly."
- Key point: Define room temperature and vacuum pressures explicitly.

### E10

- Location: Reaction procedure
- Quote: "For reactions carried out at room temperature provide the temperature in °C (i.e., do not report as "rt")."
- Key point: For room-temperature reactions, give the temperature in °C; do not just write "rt."

### E11

- Location: Reaction procedure
- Quote: "Describe the appearance of the reaction mixture (color, homogeneous or not, etc.) and describe all significant changes in appearance during the course of the reaction (color changes, gas evolution, appearance of solids, exotherms, etc.)."
- Key point: Describe the appearance of the reaction mixture and all significant changes during the reaction (color, gas evolution, precipitation, exotherms, etc.).

### E12

- Location: Characterization (product purity)
- Quote: "provide analytical data establishing that the purity of the isolated product is at least 97%. Note that this data should be obtained for the material on which the yield of the reaction is based, not for a sample that has been subjected to additional purification"
- Key point: The journal requires the isolated product to be at least 97% pure, with purity data from the material on which the yield is based, not from a separately purified sample.

### E13

- Location: Safety (required wording of Note 1)
- Quote: "Prior to performing each reaction, a thorough hazard analysis and risk assessment should be carried out with regard to each chemical substance and experimental operation on the scale planned and in the context of the laboratory where the procedures will be carried out."
- Key point: Before each reaction, carry out a thorough hazard analysis and risk assessment for each chemical and operation, at the planned scale and under the conditions of your lab.

### E14

- Location: Common Omissions
- Quote: "if a product of one step is used in the next step without purification, then a Note is required describing the purification of a sample and characterization data for both the purified sample and the crude material used in the next step."
- Key point: When a product is used in the next step without purification, add a Note describing how to purify a sample, with characterization data for both the purified sample and the crude material.

### E15

- Location: Reaction Procedure
- Quote: "Indicate the order of addition and time for addition of all reagents and how each is added (via syringe, addition funnel, etc.). Indicate the temperature of the reaction mixture (preferably internal temperature)."
- Key point: State the order, time, and method of addition for every reagent (syringe, addition funnel, etc.), and the temperature of the reaction mixture, preferably internal.

### E16

- Location: Reaction Procedure
- Quote: "Be careful to describe clearly all cooling and warming cycles, including initial and final temperatures and the time interval involved."
- Key point: Describe all cooling and warming cycles clearly, with initial and final temperatures and the time involved.

### E17

- Location: Reaction Procedure
- Quote: "Indicate how the reaction can be monitored to determine the extent of conversion of reactants to products. In the case of reactions monitored by TLC, provide details in a Note, including eluent, Rf values, and method of visualization."
- Key point: State how to monitor the reaction's progress; for thin-layer chromatography (TLC), give the eluent, Rf values, and visualization method.

### E18

- Location: Reaction Workup
- Quote: "Describe the composition and volume of quenching agent, and time and temperature for addition." / "For extractions, the number of washes and the volume of each should be indicated as well as the size of the separatory funnel. For concentration of solutions after workup, indicate the method and pressure and temperature used."
- Key point: Report the composition, volume, and addition time and temperature of the quenching agent; the number and volume of extraction washes and the size of the separatory funnel; and the method, pressure, and temperature of concentration.

### E19

- Location: Reagents (significant figures for amounts and solution concentrations)
- Quote: "Likewise, "5.00 equiv" implies that an amount of excess reagent must be controlled to a precision of 0.01 equiv. The concentration of solutions should be expressed in terms of molarity or normality, and not percent"
- Key point: Use sensible significant figures: "5.00 equiv" implies control to 0.01 equiv. Express solution concentrations as molarity or normality, not percentages.

### E20

- Location: Scale / reproducibility paragraph
- Quote: "In any case where a reagent is employed in significant excess (e.g., more than 1.5 equiv), a Note should be included explaining why an excess of that reagent is necessary. If possible, the Note should indicate the effect of using amounts of reagent less than that specified in the procedure."
- Key point: When a reagent is used in significant excess (e.g., more than 1.5 equiv), explain why, ideally noting what happens with less.

### E21

- Location: Characterization (purity evidence)
- Quote: "quantitative NMR (qNMR), GC, or HPLC analyses involving measurements versus an internal standard will also be accepted."
- Key point: Purity measured by quantitative NMR (qNMR), GC, or HPLC against an internal standard is also accepted.

### E22

- Location: Scale / reproducibility paragraph
- Quote: "It is appropriate to report the weight, yield, and purity of the product of each step in a procedure as a range. Yields should be rounded off to the nearest percent"
- Key point: Weight, yield, and purity of each step's product may be reported as a range; round yields to the nearest percent.

### E23

- Location: Common Omissions
- Quote: "The amount of product generated in a prior step must be more than or equal to the amount used as starting material in a subsequent step."
- Key point: The amount of product made in a prior step must be at least the amount used as starting material in the next step.

### E24

- Location: Discussion section
- Quote: "The scope and limitations of the method should be discussed, and it is generally desirable to include a table of examples." / "Competing methods for accomplishing the same overall transformation should be described and referenced."
- Key point: The discussion should cover the method's scope and limitations, ideally with a table of examples, and describe and cite competing methods for the same transformation.

### E25

- Location: Common Omissions
- Quote: "Full details on procedures for purification must be provided"
- Key point: Purification procedures must be given in full.
