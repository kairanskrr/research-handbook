# Lacoste et al. 2022: On-Farm Experimentation to transform global agriculture

- Source: `lacoste2022onfarm`, [full text](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43016-021-00424-4)
- Version: Nature Food 3: 11–18, 2022 (perspective article). Text taken from the author's accepted manuscript (final peer-reviewed version, for academic research use only) in the institutional repository of the University of Gloucestershire, UK. The authors are from research institutions in Australia, France, the UK, the US, Canada, Argentina, China, and elsewhere. Locations give the subheading.

### E1

- Location: OFE embeds research in farm management
- Quote: "OFE is defined as an innovation process that brings agricultural stakeholders together around mutually beneficial experimentation to support farmers’ own management decisions."
- Key point: On-Farm Experimentation (OFE) is defined as an innovation process that brings agricultural stakeholders together around mutually beneficial experimentation to support farmers' own management decisions.

### E2

- Location: OFE embeds research in farm management
- Quote: "First, OFE research occurs in farmers’ own fields and at scales that are meaningful to them, rather than in small experimental plots that are designed externally. Second, the private interests of farmers and of other OFE participants are explicitly acknowledged as a prerequisite to negotiate their alignment and build productive relationships."
- Key point: First, OFE happens in farmers' own fields and at scales meaningful to them, rather than in small plots designed externally; second, it explicitly acknowledges the private interests of farmers and other participants as the basis for coordination and partnership.

### E3

- Location: Progressing experimentation
- Quote: "Spatial and temporal variations in crop and livestock production are far greater than trial treatment effects"
- Key point: Spatial and temporal variation in crop and livestock production is far greater than trial treatment effects.

### E4

- Location: Progressing experimentation
- Quote: "Furthermore, the statistical significance criteria used by scientists provide no indication as to the scope, meaningfulness or local usefulness of results, leaving to farmers the difficult and risky task of adapting recommendations"
- Key point: The statistical significance criteria scientists use say nothing about the scope, meaningfulness, or local usefulness of results, leaving the difficult and risky job of adapting recommendations to farmers.

### E5

- Location: Figure 2 caption
- Quote: "Experimenting at the field scale may involve straightforward assessments of variation, especially in smallholder and subsistence farming, but also because farmers may attach low priority to statistical results and replications."
- Key point: Field-scale experimentation may involve only straightforward assessments of variation, especially in smallholder and subsistence farming, and also because farmers may place less weight on statistics and replication.
