# AIAA: Editorial Policy Statement on Numerical and Experimental Accuracy

- Source: `aiaaeditorialpolicy`, [original](https://aiaa.org/EditorialPolicyStatement)
- Version: web page of the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA), accessed 2026-10-06. Locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: opening
- Quote: "The AIAA journals will not accept for publication any manuscript reporting (1) numerical solutions of an engineering problem that fails to adequately address the accuracy of the computed results or (2) experimental results unless the accuracy ofthe data is adequately presented."
- Key point: AIAA journals do not accept numerical papers that fail to adequately address the accuracy of computed results, or experimental papers that fail to adequately state the accuracy of the data.

### E2

- Location: opening
- Quote: "The implementation of this policy statement will be at the discretion of the Editor-in-Chief of each journal and the Associate Editor evaluating the submitted manuscript."
- Key point: How the policy is applied is at the discretion of each journal's Editor-in-Chief and the Associate Editor handling the manuscript.

### E3

- Location: Experimental Accuracy, Estimation of Bias Uncertainty
- Quote: "The bias uncertainty of the results (systematic component of the unexplained variance) should be estimated. In addition, the method of estimation should be described."
- Key point: Estimate the bias uncertainty (systematic component) and describe the method of estimation.

### E4

- Location: Experimental Accuracy, Estimation of Precision Uncertainty
- Quote: "The repeatability of the results (random component of the unexplained variance) should be quantified. Also, the method of determining the repeatability should be specified."
- Key point: Quantify the repeatability of results (random component) and describe how it was determined.

### E5

- Location: Experimental Accuracy, Statement of Total Uncertainty
- Quote: "Authors should identify any known potential sources of bias and precision uncertainty that have not been explicitly included in the estimates of total uncertainty."
- Key point: Identify any known sources of bias and precision uncertainty that were not included in the total uncertainty estimate.

### E6

- Location: Experimental Accuracy, Statement of Experimental Design
- Quote: "the experimental conditions and order of acquisition should be presented or concisely described."
- Key point: Present the experimental conditions and order of data acquisition, so others can assess uncertainty, repeat the experiment, or compare with simulations.

### E7

- Location: Numerical Accuracy, Statement of Code Verification Activities
- Quote: "If commercially available computer codes are an important element in the simulations presented, references for publicly available codetesting should be given."
- Key point: If commercial codes are an important element of the simulations, give references to publicly available code testing.

### E8

- Location: Experimental Accuracy, Coverage Factor
- Quote: "The coverage factor used for all expanded uncertainty estimates should be specified."
- Key point: Specify the coverage factor used for all expanded uncertainty estimates.

### E9

- Location: Estimation of Precision Uncertainty
- Quote: "The repeatability of the results (random component of the unexplained variance) should be quantified. Also, the method of determining the repeatability should be specified. Unusual results, whether explained or unexplained, should be identified."
- Key point: Quantify the repeatability of results and describe the method; point out anomalous results, whether or not they can be explained.

### E10

- Location: Coverage Factor
- Quote: "The coverage factor used for all expanded uncertainty estimates should be specified. That is, the author(s) should associate a specific level of confidence with each uncertainty value"
- Key point: Specify the coverage factor for all expanded uncertainty estimates, that is, associate a specific level of confidence with each uncertainty.
