# APA: Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts, journal page and submission guidelines

- Source: `apa2024aesthetics`, [original](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/aca) (official journal of Division 10 of the American Psychological Association)
- Version: web page. The site returned a block page to scripted access, so these excerpts come from the Internet Archive snapshot of 2024-07-07 (http://web.archive.org/web/20240707072656/https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/aca).

### E1

- Location: top of page; Journal scope statement
- Quote: "This journal is a publication of APA Division 10 (Society for the Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts)" / "To that end, we publish manuscripts presenting original empirical research and papers that synthesize and evaluate extant research that relate to the psychology of aesthetics, creativity, and the arts." / "Generally, qualitative work, case studies, essays, interviews, biographical profiles, and literature reviews are discouraged."
- Key point: The journal belongs to APA Division 10; it publishes original empirical research and papers that synthesize existing research; qualitative research, case studies, essays, interviews, biographies, and literature reviews are generally discouraged.

### E2

- Location: Submission guidelines, Registered Reports
- Quote: "This is a partial manuscript that includes the introduction and methods section of the manuscript (i.e., the hypotheses, rationale for the study, experimental design, and methodological details including power analyses for sample size, statistical analysis pipeline, and any pilot data)." / "In this stage, the manuscript will be reviewed for significance and rationale for the study as well as the rigor and clarity of the methodological approach. If the partial manuscript is accepted, this amounts to provisional acceptance of the full report regardless of the outcome of the study."
- Key point: The journal accepts Registered Reports: you first submit the introduction and methods (hypotheses, experimental design, the power analysis used to set the sample size, and the analysis plan), which are reviewed for significance and methodological rigor and clarity; if this stage is passed, the paper is accepted in principle regardless of the results.

### E3

- Location: Submission guidelines, Ethical Principles
- Quote: "APA expects authors to have their data available throughout the editorial review process and for at least 5 years after the date of publication."
- Key point: APA expects authors to have their data available during review and for at least 5 years after publication.

### E4

- Location: EDI Efforts, Inclusive reporting standards
- Quote: "Participant sample descriptions (recommended)" / "Constraints on Generality (COG) statements (recommended)"
- Key point: Participant sample descriptions are recommended, as are Constraints on Generality statements.
