# Studia Logica: Aims and Scope

- Source: `studialogicaaims`, [original](https://link.springer.com/journal/11225/aims-and-scope)
- Version: The "Aims and scope" web page of Studia Logica (founded in Poland in 1953, published by Springer). The original site has script verification, so the text is taken from the Internet Archive copy of 2025-12-03 https://web.archive.org/web/20251203224712/https://link.springer.com/journal/11225/aims-and-scope . It speaks only for this journal.

### E1

- Location: Aims and scope, paragraph 3
- Quote: "The key criterion for acceptance of papers to be published in Studia Logica will not be the scope of presented research but its method: they are required to contain significant and original results concerning formal systems and their properties."
- Key point: The key criterion for acceptance is not the subject matter of the research but its method: papers must contain significant, original results about formal systems and their properties.

### E2

- Location: Aims and scope, paragraph 3
- Quote: "Studia Logica strive for a balance between mathematical techniques and philosophical relevance."
- Key point: The journal aims to strike a balance between mathematical technique and philosophical relevance.
