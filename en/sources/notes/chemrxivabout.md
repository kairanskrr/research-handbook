# ChemRxiv: About ChemRxiv and FAQs for ChemRxiv Authors

- Source: `chemrxivabout`, [About](https://chemrxiv.org/engage/assets/public/chemrxiv/term/about.htm), [Author FAQ](https://chemrxiv.org/engage/assets/public/chemrxiv/term/author-faq.htm)
- Version: Two information pages on the ChemRxiv website. The site returns 403 to scripted access, so this excerpt was read from Internet Archive snapshots (About: 2025-08-12; Author FAQ: 2025-10-15).

### E1

- Location: About, paragraph 1
- Quote: "ChemRxiv (pronounced 'chem-archive') is a free submission, distribution, and archive service for unpublished preprints in chemistry and related areas."
- Key point: ChemRxiv is a free submission, distribution, and archive service for unpublished preprints in chemistry and related areas.

### E2

- Location: About, screening statement
- Quote: "We conduct a basic screening for plagiarism, offensive language, non-scientific, and inappropriate content, and we will exclude materials that may pose a health or security risk if identified. Beyond this screening, the files are unedited and are not peer reviewed."
- Key point: ChemRxiv only does basic screening (for plagiarism, offensive language, non-scientific and inappropriate content, and material that may pose a health or security risk); beyond that, there is no editing and no peer review.

### E3

- Location: About, Scope
- Quote: "Analytical chemistry" / "Organic chemistry" / "Physical chemistry" / "Theoretical and computational chemistry"
- Key point: At submission you choose from a set of categories, including analytical chemistry, organic chemistry, physical chemistry, and theoretical and computational chemistry (16 in total).

### E4

- Location: About, The ChemRxiv partnership
- Quote: "ChemRxiv is co-owned and collaboratively managed by the American Chemical Society (ACS)"
- Key point: ChemRxiv is co-owned and collaboratively managed by chemical societies including the American Chemical Society (the same paragraph also lists the Chinese Chemical Society, the Chemical Society of Japan, the German Chemical Society, and the Royal Society of Chemistry).

### E5

- Location: Author FAQ, How is the content screened?
- Quote: "This process is carried out by PhD chemists, but it is not considered peer review. We make no assessment of the accuracy, completeness, or importance of the science presented."
- Key point: Screening is done by PhD chemists but is not peer review; it does not assess whether the content is accurate, complete, or important.

### E6

- Location: Author FAQ, Can I retract my content on ChemRxiv?
- Quote: "Individual pieces of research content posted to ChemRxiv are citable and part of the scholarly record, so they cannot be removed from the site except in extremely rare circumstances."
- Key point: Content posted to ChemRxiv is citable and part of the scholarly record, and except in extremely rare cases it cannot be removed.

### E7

- Location: Author FAQ, journal policies
- Quote: "Although most journals accept content posted as a preprint prior to submission, some journals or publishers may place restrictions on the posting of certain prepublication content. We recommend that you check the policies of any publishers to which you intend to submit your work for formal publication, in advance of uploading content to ChemRxiv."
- Key point: ChemRxiv says most journals accept manuscripts posted as preprints before submission, but some restrict this; it recommends checking the target publisher's policy before uploading.
