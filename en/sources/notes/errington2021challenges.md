# Errington et al. 2021: Challenges for assessing replicability in preclinical cancer biology

- Source: `errington2021challenges`, [original](https://elifesciences.org/articles/67995) (read from an [Internet Archive snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://elifesciences.org/articles/67995))
- Version: eLife 10: e67995, 2021, open access (CC BY), accessed 2026-10-06. Reports the obstacles met during replications in the Reproducibility Project: Cancer Biology. Locations give the subheading.

### E1

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "none of the 193 experiments were described in sufficient detail in the original paper to enable us to design protocols to repeat the experiments, so we had to seek clarifications from the original authors."
- Key point: None of the 193 experiments planned for replication was described in the original paper in enough detail to design a protocol from it; every one required asking the original authors for details.

### E2

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "Third, once experimental work started, 67% of the peer-reviewed protocols required modifications to complete the research and just 41% of those modifications could be implemented."
- Key point: Once experimental work started, 67% of the peer-reviewed protocols needed modifications to complete the research, and only 41% of those modifications could be implemented.

### E3

- Location: Challenges during experiments and peer review
- Quote: "Sometimes just minor modifications were needed (e.g., changing antibody concentrations or blocking reagents to detect the protein of interest during a Western blot assay)."
- Key point: Sometimes only minor modifications were needed, e.g. changing antibody concentrations or blocking reagents to detect the protein of interest in a Western blot.

### E4

- Location: The duration of the different phases in the project
- Quote: "The gap between the start of experimental work and final data delivery was 90 weeks"
- Key point: In this project, the time from the start of experimental work to final data delivery averaged 90 weeks.

### E5

- Location: Independent development of replication protocols
- Quote: "We coded if we requested a key reagent (i.e., cell lines, plasmids, model organisms, antibodies) that was not available commercially or in a repository. We requested key reagents for 136 of the 193 experiments (70%)"
- Key point: For 136 of the 193 experiments (70%), the project had to request key reagents (cell lines, plasmids, model organisms, antibodies) from the original authors because they were not available commercially or in a repository.

### E6

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "the data needed to compute effect sizes and conduct power analyses was publicly accessible for just 4 of 193 experiments. Moreover, despite contacting the authors of the original papers, we were unable to obtain these data for 68% of the experiments."
- Key point: The data needed to compute effect sizes were publicly accessible for only 4 of the 193 experiments; even after contacting the original authors, the data could not be obtained for 68% of the experiments.

### E7

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "None of the 193 experiments was described completely enough to design a replication protocol without requesting clarifying details from the original authors."
- Key point: None of the 193 experiments was described completely enough to design a replication protocol without requesting clarifying details from the original authors.

### E8

- Location: Requesting assistance from original authors
- Quote: "For example, a request for specific protocol details was responded with ‘a standard procedure was used.’"
- Key point: For example, a request for specific protocol details was answered only with "a standard procedure was used."
