# Cohen et al. 2016: STARD 2015 explanation and elaboration (BMJ Open)

- Source: `cohen2016stard`, [Original (PubMed Central)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5128957/)
- Version: Cohen JF, Korevaar DA, Altman DG, et al., "STARD 2015 guidelines for reporting diagnostic accuracy studies: explanation and elaboration," BMJ Open 2016;6(11):e012799, DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2016-012799. Open access under CC BY-NC 4.0. This excerpt used the PubMed Central full text retrieved via NCBI E-utilities. STARD 2015 is the reporting guideline for primary diagnostic accuracy studies.

### E1

- Location: Introduction, Table 1 Key STARD terminology
- Quote: "Index test The test under evaluation Target condition The disease or condition that the index test is expected to detect Clinical reference standard The best available method for establishing the presence or absence of the target condition."
- Key point: The index test is the test under evaluation; the target condition is the disease or condition the index test is meant to detect; the reference standard is the best available method for establishing whether the target condition is present.

### E2

- Location: Introduction, Table 1 Key STARD terminology
- Quote: "Sensitivity Proportion of those with the target condition who test positive with the index test Specificity Proportion of those without the target condition who test negative with the index test"
- Key point: Sensitivity is the proportion of people with the target condition who test positive on the index test; specificity is the proportion of people without it who test negative.
