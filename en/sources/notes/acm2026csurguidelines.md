# ACM Computing Surveys: Author Guidelines

- Source: `acm2026csurguidelines`, [original page](https://dl.acm.org/journal/csur/author-guidelines)
- Version: The ACM website blocks scripted access; the content below was checked against an [Internet Archive snapshot from 2026-07-11](http://web.archive.org/web/20260711105913/https://dl.acm.org/journal/csur/author-guidelines).
- Background: ACM Computing Surveys is the leading survey journal in computer science.

### E1

- Location: Types of Papers, Long Survey Paper
- Quote: "A paper that summarizes and organizes recent research results in a novel way that integrates and adds understanding to work in the field. A survey article assumes a general knowledge of the area; it emphasizes the classification of the existing literature, developing a perspective on the area, and evaluating trends."
- Key point: A survey paper summarizes and organizes recent research in a novel way that integrates and adds understanding to the field. It assumes readers already have a basic knowledge of the field, and focuses on classifying the existing literature, developing a perspective on the field, and evaluating trends.

### E2

- Location: Types of Papers, Tutorial Paper
- Quote: "A paper that organises and introduces work in the field. A tutorial paper assumes its audience is inexpert; it emphasizes the basic concepts of the field and provides concrete example that embody these concepts."
- Key point: A tutorial differs from a survey: it assumes an inexpert audience, emphasizes the basic concepts of the field, and gives concrete examples.

### E3

- Location: Types of Papers, Short Survey paper
- Quote: "Short survey papers are perspective pieces/essays that are well-focused and written by a leader in the field that usually presents a personal point of view critiquing widespread notions pertaining to a field."
- Key point: Short surveys are perspective pieces written by a leader in the field, usually presenting a personal point of view critical of prevailing views in the field.

### E4

- Location: Paper Length
- Quote: "Long survey papers must not exceed 35 pages, including references, when formatted using the Surveys style."
- Key point: Long surveys must not exceed 35 pages, including references.
