# World Health Organization: Trial registration (International Clinical Trials Registry Platform web page)

- Source: `whotrialregistration`, [original](https://www.who.int/tools/clinical-trials-registry-platform/network/trial-registration)
- Version: Web page of the World Health Organization (WHO) International Clinical Trials Registry Platform (ICTRP), accessed October 2026. Locations give the subheading.

### E1

- Location: How to register a trial
- Quote: "You cannot register a trial with WHO. The WHO ICTRP is not a clinical trials registry." / "To register a trial, submit the details directly to any one of the Primary Registries in the WHO Registry Network"
- Key point: WHO is not itself a registry, and you cannot register a trial with WHO; submit it directly to any one of the Primary Registries in the WHO Registry Network.

### E2

- Location: How to register a trial
- Quote: "it is only necessary for your trial to be registered once, in either a Primary Registry in the WHO Registry Network or an ICMJE approved registry."
- Key point: To meet WHO's transparency and publication requirements, a trial needs to be registered only once, in either a WHO Primary Registry or an ICMJE-approved registry.

### E3

- Location: How to register a trial, NOTE
- Quote: "Regulatory, legal, ethical, funding and other requirements for oversight and conduct of clinical trials differ from country to country."
- Key point: Regulatory, legal, ethical, funding, and other requirements for clinical trials differ from country to country.
