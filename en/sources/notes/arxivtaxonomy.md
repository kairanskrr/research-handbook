# arXiv: Category Taxonomy

- Source: `arxivtaxonomy`, [original](https://arxiv.org/category_taxonomy)
- Version: arXiv category taxonomy, read 2026-10-06.

### E1

- Location: hep-ex (High Energy Physics - Experiment)
- Quote: "Results from high-energy/particle physics experiments and prospects for future experimental results"
- Key point: The high-energy physics experiment category covers results from particle physics experiments.

### E2

- Location: hep-th (High Energy Physics - Theory)
- Quote: "Formal aspects of quantum field theory. String theory, supersymmetry and supergravity."
- Key point: The high-energy physics theory category covers formal quantum field theory, string theory, and related topics.

### E3

- Location: hep-ph (High Energy Physics - Phenomenology)
- Quote: "Theoretical particle physics and its interrelation with experiment."
- Key point: The phenomenology category: theoretical particle physics and its interrelation with experiment.

### E4

- Location: hep-lat (High Energy Physics - Lattice)
- Quote: "Lattice field theory. Phenomenology from lattice field theory. Algorithms for lattice field theory. Hardware for lattice field theory."
- Key point: The lattice category: lattice field theory, including its algorithms and dedicated hardware.

### E5

- Location: physics.comp-ph (Computational Physics)
- Quote: "All aspects of computational science applied to physics."
- Key point: The computational physics category: all aspects of computational science applied to physics.

### E6

- Location: astro-ph.IM (Instrumentation and Methods for Astrophysics)
- Quote: "Detector and telescope design, experiment proposals. Laboratory Astrophysics. Methods for data analysis, statistical methods. Software, database design"
- Key point: The astrophysics instrumentation and methods category: detector and telescope design, data analysis methods, software, and more.

### E7

- Location: cond-mat.mtrl-sci (Materials Science)
- Quote: "Techniques, synthesis, characterization, structure. Structural phase transitions, mechanical properties, phonons. Defects, adsorbates, interfaces"
- Key point: arXiv has a materials science category, cond-mat.mtrl-sci, covering techniques, synthesis, characterization, structure, phase transitions, mechanical properties, defects, interfaces, and more.

### E8

- Location: cond-mat.mes-hall (Mesoscale and Nanoscale Physics)
- Quote: "Semiconducting nanostructures: quantum dots, wires, and wells. Single electronics, spintronics, 2d electron gases, quantum Hall effect, nanotubes, graphene, plasmonic nanostructures"
- Key point: arXiv's mesoscale and nanoscale physics category, cond-mat.mes-hall, covers quantum dots, nanotubes, graphene, plasmonic nanostructures, and similar topics.
