# REF 2019/02: Panel criteria and working methods (REF 2021)

- Source: `ref2019panel`, [original PDF](https://2021.ref.ac.uk/media/1450/ref-2019_02-panel-criteria-and-working-methods.pdf)
- Version: Panel criteria and working methods for REF 2021, REF 2019/02, including the October 2020 revisions. Main Panel D covers the arts and humanities, including Unit of Assessment 32 (Art and Design: History, Practice and Theory) and Unit of Assessment 33 (Music, Drama, Dance, Performing Arts, Film and Screen Studies). Locations are given by paragraph number.

### E1

- Location: Main Panel D, para. 264
- Quote: "In all cases where the role of the researcher, or the research process, is not evident within the submitted output, submitting units are strongly encouraged to submit a statement of up to 300 words."
- Key point: Wherever the researcher's role or the research process is not evident in the output, submitting units are strongly encouraged to submit a statement of up to 300 words.

### E2

- Location: Main Panel D, para. 266
- Quote: "the question and/or issues being explored, the process of discovery, methods and/or methodologies, the creative and/or intellectual context or literature review upon which the work draws, or challenges or critiques"
- Key point: The statement should explain the research process: the question being explored, the process of discovery, methods, and the creative or intellectual context (or literature review) the work draws on.

### E3

- Location: Main Panel D, para. 266
- Quote: "the findings, discoveries or creative outcomes of that process"
- Key point: Explain the research insights: the findings, discoveries, or creative outcomes of the process.

### E4

- Location: Main Panel D, para. 266
- Quote: "how and where the insights or discoveries were ‘effectively shared’"
- Key point: Explain the dissemination: where and how the insights were "effectively shared."

### E5

- Location: Main Panel D, para. 267
- Quote: "The objective is to enable panels to assess the originality, significance and rigour of the research. The sub-panels will ignore any additional material that includes evaluative commentary on the perceived quality of a research output."
- Key point: The aim is to let panels assess originality, significance, and rigour; panels ignore any material that comments on the output's own quality.

### E6

- Location: Unit of Assessment 32, para. 157
- Quote: "It is anticipated that outputs will span a range of texts, edited publications and creative practices, as well as artefacts, events and curatorial outputs."
- Key point: The Art and Design unit expects outputs to include texts, edited publications, creative practices, artefacts, events, and curatorial outputs.

### E7

- Location: Unit of Assessment 33, para. 160
- Quote: "It anticipates that outputs will span a range of artefacts, creative practices, curatorial outputs, edited publications, recordings and writings."
- Key point: The unit covering music, drama, and related fields expects outputs to include artefacts, creative practices, curatorial outputs, edited publications, recordings, and writings.

### E8

- Location: Main Panel D, para. 210
- Quote: "that has also been a performance or recording; or an exhibition where the research may be curatorial"
- Key point: One output can be several types at once, for example a "composition" that has also been a performance or recording, or an exhibition where the research is curatorial.

### E9

- Location: para. 265
- Quote: "As a single item, with a 300-word supporting statement. • As a multi-component output, with a 300-word supporting statement. • As a single item, supported by contextual information (previously called a ‘portfolio’)."
- Key point: Main Panel D (arts and humanities) lets outputs whose research role or process is not evident be submitted in several ways: a single item with a 300-word supporting statement; a multi-component output with a 300-word statement; a single item with contextual information (previously called a "portfolio"); or a multi-component output with contextual information.

### E10

- Location: para. 265
- Quote: "Both the item and the contextual information may include moving image, sonic, visual or other digital media or written text, as appropriate, to enable the panel to access the research dimensions of the work and to assess its significance, originality and rigour."
- Key point: Both the item and the contextual information may include moving image, sound, visual or other digital media, or written text, so the panel can access the research dimensions of the work and assess its significance, originality, and rigour.

### E11

- Location: para. 266
- Quote: "the research process – the question and/or issues being explored, the process of discovery, methods and/or methodologies, the creative and/or intellectual context or literature review upon which the work draws, or challenges or critiques"
- Key point: The research process covers the questions explored, the process of discovery, methods, the creative or intellectual context or literature review the work draws on, or the challenges and critiques it poses.

### E12

- Location: para. 267
- Quote: "The sub-panels will ignore any additional material that includes evaluative commentary on the perceived quality of a research output."
- Key point: Sub-panels ignore any additional material that comments on the perceived quality of the output.

### E13

- Location: para. 260 (Main Panel C supplementary criteria)
- Quote: "submissions should include an explanatory presentation of the building, design or intervention in an easily-handled paper-based format (for example, a PDF which could include photographs, figures or diagrams) sufficient to allow the panel both to understand the output without visiting it, and to make a judgement of its research contribution."
- Key point: Main Panel C asks that practice-based outputs (a building, design, or intervention) come with an explanatory presentation in an easily handled paper-based format (for example a PDF with photographs, figures, or diagrams), so panels can understand the output and judge its research contribution without a site visit.
