# OSDI '26: Call for Papers

- Source: `osdi2026cfp`, [original](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26/call-for-papers)
- Version: Call for papers of the 20th USENIX Symposium on Operating Systems Design and Implementation (OSDI '26, July 2026), read 2026-10-08. Locations give page subheadings. Used only as one year's example from a top systems conference.

### E1

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "OSDI emphasizes innovative research and quantified or insightful experiences in systems design and implementation."
- Key point: OSDI emphasizes innovative research and quantified or insightful experiences in systems design and implementation.

### E2

- Location: Submitting a Paper
- Quote: "Submissions will be judged on novelty, significance, interest, clarity, relevance, and correctness. An excellent paper will:" / "Motivate a significant problem" / "Propose an interesting and compelling solution" / "Demonstrate the practicality and benefits of the solution"
- Key point: Submissions are judged on novelty, significance, interest, clarity, relevance, and correctness; an excellent paper motivates a significant problem, proposes a compelling solution, and demonstrates that it is practical and beneficial.

### E3

- Location: Deadline and Submission Instructions
- Quote: "Papers should be the right length, which may be less than 12 single-spaced pages. Reviewers will be encouraged to down-rank overly padded papers. However, papers of 6 pages or less are unlikely to receive full consideration."
- Key point: The limit is 12 pages; papers should be the right length, overly padded papers will be down-ranked, but papers of 6 pages or less are unlikely to receive full consideration.

### E4

- Location: Submission Tracks
- Quote: "OSDI '26 offers two reviewing tracks, the Research track (comparable to previous OSDIs) and the Operational Systems track." / "Submissions to the Operational Systems track should describe the design, implementation, analysis, and experience of operational systems." / "We encourage submissions that disprove or strengthen existing assumptions, deepen the understanding of existing problems, and validate known techniques at new scales or in new environments."
- Key point: There are two reviewing tracks, Research and Operational Systems; the Operational Systems track covers the design, implementation, analysis, and experience of deployed systems, and welcomes work that disproves or strengthens existing assumptions or validates known techniques at new scale or in new environments.

### E5

- Location: Opening, main changes this year
- Quote: "Removes the author response period" / "Replaces the revise-and-resubmit process with a conditional accept decision"
- Key point: OSDI '26 removed the author response period and replaced revise-and-resubmit with a conditional accept decision.

### E6

- Location: Submitting a Paper, preprints
- Quote: "technical reports, talks, and social media posts, is permitted. However, your OSDI submission must use an anonymized name for your project or system that differs from any used in such contexts."
- Key point: Prior posting on arXiv, in technical reports, talks, and social media is permitted, but the submission must use a different, anonymized name for the project or system.

### E7

- Location: Deadline and Submission Instructions, supplementary material
- Quote: "PC members are not required to read supplementary material when reviewing the paper, so each paper should stand alone without it."
- Key point: Reviewers are not required to read supplementary material, so the paper must stand alone.

### E8

- Location: Conditional accept
- Quote: "This decision indicates a heavyweight shepherding process in which a paper will be rejected unless the authors make a set of changes determined by the program committee during the review period."
- Key point: A conditional accept means the paper goes through heavyweight shepherding and will still be rejected unless the authors make the changes set by the program committee.
