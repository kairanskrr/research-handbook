# Lachat et al. 2016: STROBE-nut: An Extension of the STROBE Statement

- Source: `lachat2016strobenut`, [PLoS Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1002036) (CC BY)
- Version: PLoS Med 13(6): e1002036, 2016. The extension of STROBE for nutritional epidemiology.

### E1

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "In total, 24 recommendations for nutritional epidemiology were added to the STROBE checklist."
- Key point: In total, 24 recommendations for nutritional epidemiology were added to the STROBE checklist.

### E2

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "Methods to assess dietary intake include (i) food frequency questionnaires, principally used to assess long-term average intakes; (ii) 24-hour recall as a memory-based short-term dietary assessment method; (iii) a food diary, which prospectively collects dietary intake data"
- Key point: Methods for assessing dietary intake include food frequency questionnaires, used mainly for long-term average intake; 24-hour recall, a short-term memory-based method; and prospectively kept food diaries, among others.

### E3

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "Only a few ideal reference measures are currently available, i.e., stable isotopes like doubly labeled water for energy intake, the recovery biomarker 24-hour urinary nitrogen excretion for protein intake, and 24-hour urinary potassium excretion for potassium intake. For practical reasons, however, nonideal reference measures such as 24-hour dietary recalls or food records are often used."
- Key point: Only a few ideal reference measures exist, such as doubly labeled water (energy), 24-hour urinary nitrogen (protein), and 24-hour urinary potassium (potassium); in practice, less-than-ideal references such as 24-hour recalls or food records are often used.

### E4

- Location: nut-9
- Quote: "It should be clear how misreporting (including under- and overreporting) was defined and addressed in the analysis. Potential selection bias due to exclusion of misreporters should be assessed by comparing participant characteristics"
- Key point: State how misreporting (under- and overreporting) was defined and handled in the analysis; excluding misreporters may introduce selection bias, so compare participant characteristics with and without the exclusions.

### E5

- Location: nut-12.2
- Quote: "Adjustment for total energy or energy from food can also mitigate the dietary assessment measurement error"
- Key point: Adjusting for total energy or energy from food can also mitigate measurement error in dietary assessment.

### E6

- Location: nut-8.6
- Quote: "Describe and reference the validation study of the dietary or nutritional assessment method, including the reference method(s) used, when it was conducted, and in which population."
- Key point: Describe and reference the validation study of the dietary assessment method, including the reference method, when it was conducted, and in which population.
