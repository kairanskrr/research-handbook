# Wilkinson et al. 2016: The FAIR Guiding Principles for scientific data management and stewardship

- Source: `wilkinson2016fair`, [original](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618)
- Version: Scientific Data 3, 160018 (2016), open access (CC BY 4.0).

### E1

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "Distinct from peer initiatives that focus on the human scholar, the FAIR Principles put specific emphasis on enhancing the ability of machines to automatically find and use the data, in addition to supporting its reuse by individuals."
- Key point: Besides supporting reuse by people, the FAIR Principles put specific emphasis on enabling machines to find and use data automatically.

### E2

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "This article describes four foundational principles—Findability, Accessibility, Interoperability, and Reusability"
- Key point: FAIR stands for four foundational principles: Findability, Accessibility, Interoperability, and Reusability.

### E3

- Location: Box 2: The FAIR Guiding Principles
- Quote: "F1. (meta)data are assigned a globally unique and persistent identifier"
- Key point: Data and metadata are assigned a globally unique and persistent identifier.

### E4

- Location: Box 2: The FAIR Guiding Principles
- Quote: "R1.1. (meta)data are released with a clear and accessible data usage license" / "R1.2. (meta)data are associated with detailed provenance"
- Key point: Release data with a clear usage license and detailed provenance (how the data were produced).

### E5

- Location: The FAIR Guiding Principles in detail
- Quote: "One such example is highly sensitive or personally-identifiable data, where publication of rich metadata to facilitate discovery, including clear rules regarding the process for accessing the data, provides a high degree of ‘FAIRness’ even in the absence of FAIR publication of the data itself."
- Key point: FAIR does not mean fully open: even when sensitive data themselves are not public, publishing rich metadata and clear access rules can give a high degree of FAIRness.

### E6

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "A diverse set of stakeholders—representing academia, industry, funding agencies, and scholarly publishers—have come together to design and jointly endorse a concise and measureable set of principles that we refer to as the FAIR Data Principles."
- Key point: A diverse set of stakeholders from academia, industry, funding agencies, and scholarly publishers jointly designed and endorsed a concise, measurable set of principles called the FAIR Data Principles.
