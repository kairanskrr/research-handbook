# Might 2010: 3 qualities of successful Ph.D. students: Perseverance, tenacity and cogency

- Source: `might2010successful`, [full text](https://matt.might.net/articles/successful-phd-students/)
- Version: author's website. The page is undated; the earliest Internet Archive snapshot is from 2010-08-15.

### E1

- Location: What doesn't matter
- Quote: "qualities like brilliance and quick-thinking are irrelevant in Ph.D. school."
- Key point: Qualities like brilliance and quick thinking are not what matters in a PhD.

### E2

- Location: Perseverance
- Quote: "This is the point (2-3 years in) where attrition peaks."
- Key point: Once you have to actually push the boundary of knowledge (two to three years in), there are no set exams or steps to follow, and this is where attrition peaks.

### E3

- Location: Perseverance
- Quote: "To survive this period, you have to be willing to fail from the moment you wake to the moment your head hits the pillow."
- Key point: To survive this period, you have to be willing to fail from the moment you wake up to the moment you go to sleep.

### E4

- Location: Perseverance
- Quote: "The skill you accrete during this trauma is the ability to imagine plausible solutions, and to estimate the likelihood that an approach will work."
- Key point: The skill you build through repeated failure is the ability to imagine plausible solutions and estimate how likely an approach is to work.

### E5

- Location: Perseverance
- Quote: "Since acceptance rates at good venues range between 8% and 25%, most or all of your papers will be rejected." / "If you stick with it long enough and work at it hard enough, you will."
- Key point: Good venues have low acceptance rates, so most of your papers will be rejected; if you stick with it long enough and work hard enough, you will publish.

### E6

- Location: Perseverance
- Quote: "This phase of the Ph.D. demands perseverance--in the face of uncertainty, in the face of rejection and in the face of frustration."
- Key point: This phase demands perseverance in the face of uncertainty, rejection, and frustration.
