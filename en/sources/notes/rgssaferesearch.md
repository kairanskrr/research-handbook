# Royal Geographical Society: Safe, responsible and ethical research

- Source: `rgssaferesearch`, [original](https://www.rgs.org/exploration/grants/guidance-for-society-grant-applicants/safe-responsible-and-ethical-research/)
- Version: Web page of the Royal Geographical Society (RGS-IBG) with safety and ethics guidance for grant applicants. The official site blocks scripts, so we read the Internet Archive snapshot of 2025-11-06. Locations describe the paragraph content.

### E1

- Location: opening
- Quote: "Grant recipients must obtain their own institution’s health and safety and ethical approval before funding can be transferred."
- Key point: Grant recipients must obtain their own institution's health and safety approval and ethics approval before funds are transferred.

### E2

- Location: risk assessment paragraph
- Quote: "Your risk assessment must not be generic, but be designed around the specific risks you may face depending on the location of your project and the nature of your research."
- Key point: A risk assessment must not be generic; write it around the specific risks you may face given the project's location and the nature of the research.

### E3

- Location: ethics assessment paragraph
- Quote: "For example, could your work offend the cultural sensitivities of the people you are working among? Have you gained appropriate consent from, or on behalf of, participants or others affected by the research?"
- Key point: Before applying, think it through: could the work offend the cultural sensitivities of local people? Have you gained appropriate consent from participants or others affected by the research?
