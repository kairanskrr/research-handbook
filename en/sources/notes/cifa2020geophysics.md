# Chartered Institute for Archaeologists 2020: Standard and guidance for archaeological geophysical survey

- Source: `cifa2020geophysics`, [PDF](https://www.archaeologists.net/sites/default/files/2023-11/CIfA-SandG-Geophysical-Survey-2020.pdf)
- Version: CIfA's standard and guidance for archaeological geophysical survey, first issued December 2014 with minor updates in October 2020. Locations give section numbers or appendix names.

### E1

- Location: Definition of geophysical survey
- Quote: "Archaeological geophysical survey uses non-intrusive and non-destructive techniques to determine the presence or absence of anomalies likely to be caused by archaeological features, structures or deposits"
- Key point: Archaeological geophysical survey uses non-intrusive, non-destructive techniques to determine whether there are anomalies likely to be caused by archaeological remains.

### E2

- Location: Annex 2: Report contents, Results
- Quote: "Since the cause of anomalies often cannot be unambiguously determined based on geophysical measurements alone, the text should also be clear about the degree of uncertainty pertaining to inferences drawn from the results."
- Key point: Geophysical data alone often cannot determine what caused an anomaly, so the report must be clear about how uncertain its inferences are.

### E3

- Location: Annex 1: Field techniques
- Quote: "the requirement of a Section 42 Licence for survey over scheduled monuments and other protected places"
- Key point: This UK guidance notes that surveying scheduled monuments and other protected places requires a Section 42 Licence.
