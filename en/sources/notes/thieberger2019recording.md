# Thieberger 2019: Recording Audio and keeping track of recordings (PARADISEC training handout)

- Source: `thieberger2019recording`, PDF handout on the PARADISEC website: https://www.paradisec.org.au/blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Honiara-Audio.pdf
- Version: Slide handout by Nick Thieberger (PARADISEC, University of Melbourne), posted on the PARADISEC website in August 2019 (the file name indicates it was used for a training session in Honiara, Solomon Islands). This is training material, not the archive's formal deposit requirements. Locations give slide titles.

### E1

- Location: Slides "Record with NO compression"; "Basics"
- Quote: "Do NOT record in mp3 format!" / "Minimally 44.1 kHz, 16bit WAV"
- Key point: Do not record in mp3; use WAV at a minimum of 44.1 kHz and 16 bits.

### E2

- Location: Slide "Set levels"
- Quote: "If you can control input levels" / "Do not use automatic limiting" / "Set recording to peak around -12"
- Key point: When you can adjust the input level, do not use automatic limiting; set recording peaks at around -12.

### E3

- Location: Slides "Machinery"; "Recording"
- Quote: "Monitor audio recordings through headphones" / "Avoid background noise if possible" / "Move away from the wind and use a windsock" / "Keep the mic as close to the speaker as possible" / "Use a mic stand"
- Key point: Monitor with headphones; avoid background noise as much as possible; avoid wind and use a windscreen; keep the microphone as close to the speaker as possible; use a microphone stand.

### E4

- Location: Slide "Know your equipment"
- Quote: "BEFORE you need to use it!" / "Read the manual" / "Make test recordings"
- Key point: Get to know your equipment before using it: read the manual and do test recordings.
