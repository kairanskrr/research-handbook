# Kwan 2018: The Limits of the Neighborhood Effect

- Source: `kwan2018limits`, [PDF (author's website)](http://meipokwan.org/Paper/Kwan_2018_AAAG.pdf), doi:10.1080/24694452.2018.1453777
- Version: Annals of the American Association of Geographers 108(6):1482–1490 (2018), titled The Limits of the Neighborhood Effect: Contextual Uncertainties in Geographic, Environmental Health, and Social Science Research. We read the publisher's version PDF hosted on the author's personal website (Internet Archive snapshot). Locations give page numbers.

### E1

- Location: p. 1486
- Quote: "The common spatial units of analysis in geographic, environmental health, and social science research are the census tract, census block group, and postcode area, whereas the common temporal units of analysis are the day, month, and year. [...] Because the values of the contextual variables derived using different spatial and temporal units might be different, research results might differ when different units of analysis were used."
- Key point: Common spatial units of analysis are the census tract, census block group, and postcode area; common temporal units are the day, month, and year. Variables computed with different units can take different values, so the findings can differ too.

### E2

- Location: p. 1486
- Quote: "which refers to the problem that, in the analysis of the relationships between area- or zone-based variables, research findings could be influenced by the geographic scale or the zoning scheme of the areal units for which data are organized [...] These two components of the MAUP are referred to as the scale effect and the zoning effect"
- Key point: The modifiable areal unit problem (MAUP) is that, when analyzing relationships between area-based variables, findings can be influenced by the geographic scale or zoning scheme of the units used; the two parts are called the scale effect and the zoning effect.

### E3

- Location: p. 1486
- Quote: "The MTUP refers to the problem that findings about the effects of contextual variables on individual behaviors or outcomes could be affected by how data are organized with respect to the temporal dimension."
- Key point: The modifiable temporal unit problem (MTUP) is that findings can be affected by how data are organized in time (e.g., aggregated by day or by month).

### E4

- Location: p. 1488
- Quote: "enhancing exposure measures with qualitative information about people’s perceptions and interpretations of their environments seem helpful for mitigating the UGCoP and selective daily mobility bias"
- Key point: Enhancing exposure measures with qualitative information about how people perceive and interpret their environments seems helpful for mitigating the uncertain geographic context problem.
