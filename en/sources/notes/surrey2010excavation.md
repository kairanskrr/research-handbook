# Surrey, England 2010: Surrey Archaeological Society *Excavation Recording Manual*

- Source: `surrey2010excavation`, [original (PDF on the Surrey Archaeological Society website)](https://d10.surreyarchaeology.org.uk/sites/surreyarchaeology.org.uk/files/Excavation%20Recording%20Manual.pdf)
- Version: Surrey Archaeological Society, *Excavation Recording Manual*, with a foreword dated May 2010 by David Bird, chair of the Research Committee; PDF (29 pages) publicly available on the society's website. This is a local archaeological society's recording standard for its members, not a UK-wide standard. Locations give manual page numbers and field names.

### E1

- Location: p. 1, Introduction
- Quote: "The Research Committee wishes to encourage use of a common method of site recording in Surrey." / "The system which is described here has been developed and used over a number of years by Society members who consulted, amongst other sources, the Essex County Council and Museum of London Archaeology Service manuals."
- Key point: The Surrey Archaeological Society wants excavations in the county to use a uniform recording method; the system draws on the manuals of Essex County Council and the Museum of London Archaeology Service.

### E2

- Location: p. 2, Context Record Sheet, General Note and Context Number
- Quote: "Where a field is listed as 'Required', it is not permitted to leave it blank. If there is no information appropriate to enter there, it must at least contain a dash or 'n/a' or ' none'." / "This will be a unique number from a sequence which may be extended from year to year and is issued from the Context Record. It is essential that no duplication of context numbers occurs. The sequence may contain gaps; batches of numbers may conveniently be reserved for different areas of the site."
- Key point: Fields marked "mandatory" must not be left blank; if there is nothing to record, write a dash, n/a, or none. Context numbers are issued from a single register and must never be duplicated; gaps in the numbering are allowed, and blocks of numbers can be reserved for different excavation areas.

### E3

- Location: pp. 2-5, fields of the Context Record Sheet
- Quote: "Matrix description (Colour, Composition, Texture, Inclusions)" / "Stratigraphic Relationships Required" / "It is important to enter reciprocal information on all the appropriate sheets (e.g. if 1 is above 2, then 2 must also be recorded as below 1)." / "Plans; Sections/Elevations/Profiles; Sample Numbers; Photos" / "Interpretation Required"
- Key point: The context record sheet has fields for soil description (color, composition, texture, inclusions), stratigraphic relationships, cross-references to plans/sections/samples/photographs, interpretation, and more; stratigraphic relationships must be entered consistently on every related sheet (if 1 is above 2, then 2's sheet must record that it is below 1).
