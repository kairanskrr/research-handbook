# van Deursen 2013: Some Research Paper Writing Recommendations

- Source: `vandeursen2013writing`, [original](https://avandeursen.com/2013/07/10/research-paper-writing-recommendations/)
- Version: The author's blog post, 2013-07-10. Web page; locations give the subheading.

### E1

- Location: Identify the Contributions
- Quote: "A contribution is anything the world did not know before this paper, but which we now do know thanks to this paper."
- Key point: Definition of a contribution: anything the world did not know before this paper but now knows thanks to it.

### E2

- Location: Identify the Contributions
- Quote: "Each contribution is an outcome, not the process of doing something. Contributions are things, not effort."
- Key point: A contribution is an outcome, not the process or the effort. "Six months of manually analyzing 500,000 commits" is not a contribution; the findings that came out of it are.

### E3

- Location: Structure the Paper
- Quote: "Furthermore, it is essential to separate background material from own contributions."
- Key point: Clearly separate background material from your own contributions, so readers and reviewers can see at a glance what is new.

### E4

- Location: Communicate the Contributions
- Quote: "Thus, contributions should not just be strong, they should also be communicable."
- Key point: Review discussions treat a paper contribution by contribution, so contributions must not only be strong but also communicable, understandable even to non-experts.

### E5

- Location: Share Contributions Early
- Quote: "Therefore, it is crucial to offer help to others, volunteering to read preliminary drafts of papers"
- Key point: Volunteer to read other people's drafts, and they will read yours; help each other.
