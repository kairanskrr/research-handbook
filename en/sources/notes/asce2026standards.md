# ASCE: Codes and Standards

- Source: `asce2026standards`, [original](https://www.asce.org/publications-and-news/codes-and-standards)
- Version: "Codes and Standards" page of the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE), read 2026-10-06.

### E1

- Location: para. 1 of the page body
- Quote: "ASCE Standards provide technical guidelines for promoting safety, reliability, productivity, and efficiency in civil engineering. Many of our standards are referenced by model building codes and adopted by state and local jurisdictions."
- Key point: ASCE standards provide technical guidelines for safety, reliability, and efficiency in civil engineering; many are referenced by model building codes and adopted by US state and local jurisdictions.

### E2

- Location: Get involved with Standards
- Quote: "Accredited by the American National Standards Institute (ANSI), ASCE has a rigorous and formal process overseen by the Codes & Standards Committee (CSC). Standards are created or updated by a balanced, volunteer standards committee, followed by a public review period."
- Key point: ASCE's standards process is accredited by the American National Standards Institute (ANSI); standards are created or updated by balanced volunteer committees, followed by a public review period.

### E3

- Location: Get involved with Standards
- Quote: "All standards are updated or reaffirmed at least every 5 years."
- Key point: All ASCE standards are updated or reaffirmed at least every 5 years.

### E4

- Location: Submit a proposal for a new standard; Attend a standards committee meeting
- Quote: "Anyone can submit a proposal to change or create a standard." / "Standards committee meetings are open for anyone to attend."
- Key point: Anyone can propose changing or creating a standard, and standards committee meetings are open to anyone.

### E5

- Location: Comment on a draft standard
- Quote: "ASCE conducts a minimum 45-day Public Comment period on any new, revised, or reaffirmed standard."
- Key point: Every new, revised, or reaffirmed standard has a public comment period of at least 45 days.
