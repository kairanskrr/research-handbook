# NeurIPS 2025: Call for Position Papers

- Source: `neurips2025position`, [web page](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPositionPapers)
- Version: the NeurIPS 2025 (Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems) call for position track papers, read on 2026-10-17. Locations give the page's subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Top of page (NeurIPS 2025 Call for Position Papers)
- Quote: "We want to hear your ideas. What is the field getting right? Getting wrong? How can our work be used for positive or destructive ends in government, military, medicine, public policy, or law? What are open problems that we should focus more on? Or less?"
- Key point: The call lists questions a position paper can answer: what the field is getting right or wrong, how research can be used, and which open problems deserve more or less attention.

### E2

- Location: Policies and Requirements
- Quote: "Alternative Views: The paper can (if appropriate) include an “Alternative Views” section in the main body of the paper (not in an appendix) that describes and addresses one or more viable (not strawmen) positions that are opposed to the paper’s position."
- Key point: NeurIPS 2025 says a paper "can (if appropriate)" include an "Alternative Views" section, rather than requiring one.

### E3

- Location: Reviewing
- Quote: "Alternative views: The paper describes and addresses one or more viable (not strawmen) positions that are opposed to the paper’s position (if relevant)."
- Key point: The "alternative views" review criterion is qualified with "(if relevant)."

### E4

- Location: Policies and Requirements
- Quote: "Paper Checklist: No checklist is required for Position Papers."
- Key point: Position papers at NeurIPS 2025 do not need to fill in the main track's paper checklist.
