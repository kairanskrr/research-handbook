# Rosenberg et al. 2020: The Next Frontier: Making Research More Reproducible

- Source: `rosenberg2020next`, [original (author's accepted manuscript in the US National Science Foundation public access repository)](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10188458)
- Version: Author's accepted version of an editorial in Journal of Water Resources Planning and Management 146(6): 01820002.

### E1

- Location: main text, after Figure 1
- Quote: "This policy has subsequently been adopted by nearly all of the 30+ journals published by the American Society of Civil Engineers."
- Key point: The Journal of Water Resources Planning and Management's 2018 data availability policy was later adopted by nearly all of the 30+ journals published by ASCE.

### E2

- Location: main text, para. 2
- Quote: "3) Authors cannot share proprietary or sensitive materials or materials containing protected intellectual property; 4) Some workflows use stochastic, high performance computing, big data, or methods with long run times that are too big to share or reproduce bit for bit"
- Key point: Barriers to reproduction include proprietary or sensitive materials, or materials with protected intellectual property, that cannot be shared; and workflows that use stochastic methods, high performance computing, big data, or long run times and are too large to share or reproduce bit for bit.

### E3

- Location: Checklist, Make all materials available in the repository
- Quote: "Specify which code segments produce each figure, table, or other results in the article." / "Remove (or indicate) code references to local paths that are machine (user) specific."
- Key point: Specify which code produces each figure, table, or other result in the paper; remove (or flag) references to local paths that only work on your own machine.

### E4

- Location: Checklist, Make all materials available in the repository
- Quote: "Write directions assuming people who will use the content are technically proficient in the field but not familiar with the data, models, code, software, programming language, methods, or study materials."
- Key point: Write directions assuming the reader is technically proficient in the field but unfamiliar with your data, models, code, software, and methods.

### E5

- Location: Checklist, Make proprietary, private, sensitive, computationally intensive, and stochastic data, models, and code more reproducible
- Quote: "Where possible, make available the input and output data from every step of a workflow that uses proprietary software or is data intensive, requires a long run time, or cannot be rerun by other users."
- Key point: For workflow steps that use proprietary software, are data intensive, run a long time, or cannot be rerun by others, make the input and output data of every step available where possible.

### E6

- Location: main text, after the list of benefits
- Quote: "Each benefit also provides professionals better access to published research and can help narrow the gap between research and practice."
- Key point: Sharing materials also gives engineering professionals better access to published research and can help narrow the gap between research and practice.
