# FOCS 2025: Call for Papers

- Source: `focs2025cfp`, [Original](https://focs.computer.org/2025/call-for-papers/)
- Version: Call for papers of the 66th IEEE Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (FOCS 2025). The official site returned 403 to scripts, so this note is taken from the Internet Archive snapshot of the official page from 2026-09-26 (https://web.archive.org/web/20260926020216/https://focs.computer.org/2025/call-for-papers/). Locations are given as page subheadings. Used only as an example of one theory conference in one year.

### E1

- Location: Overview and Call for Papers
- Quote: "The 66th Annual Symposium on Foundations of Computer Science (FOCS 2025), sponsored by the IEEE Computer Society Technical Committee on Mathematical Foundations of Computing, will be held in Sydney, Australia, December 14-17"
- Key point: FOCS 2025 was sponsored by the IEEE Computer Society Technical Committee on Mathematical Foundations of Computing and held in Sydney, Australia, December 14–17, 2025.

### E2

- Location: Important dates
- Quote: "Submission deadline" / "April 3, 2025 at 8PM ET"
- Key point: The submission deadline was April 3, 2025, at 8 PM US Eastern Time.

### E3

- Location: Submission format
- Quote: "There is no page limit and authors are encouraged to use the "full version" of their paper as the submission." / "The submission should contain, within the initial ten pages following the title page, a clear presentation of the merits of the paper, including a discussion of the paper's importance within the context of prior work and a description of the key technical and conceptual ideas used to achieve its main claims." / "Proofs must be provided which can enable the main mathematical claims of the paper to be fully verified." / "material other than the abstract, references, and the first ten pages will be read at the committee’s discretion"
- Key point: There is no page limit, and authors are encouraged to submit the full version; the first ten pages after the title page must present the paper's merits and key ideas clearly; proofs sufficient to fully verify the main mathematical claims must be included; anything beyond the abstract, references, and first ten pages is read at the committee's discretion.

### E4

- Location: Submission format
- Quote: "FOCS 2025 will use double-blind reviewing" / "Nothing should be done in the name of anonymity that weakens the submission or makes the job of reviewing the paper more difficult."
- Key point: Reviewing is double-blind; nothing done for anonymity should weaken the submission or make reviewing harder.

### E5

- Location: Submission instructions
- Quote: "It is expected that authors of accepted papers will make their full papers, with proofs, publicly available by the camera-ready deadline."
- Key point: Authors of accepted papers are expected to make their full papers, with proofs, publicly available by the camera-ready deadline.

### E6

- Location: Prior and simultaneous submission
- Quote: "The conference will follow SIGACT's policy on prior publication and simultaneous submissions." / "Simultaneous submission of the same (or essentially the same) abstract to FOCS 2025 and to another conference with published proceedings or journal is not allowed."
- Key point: The conference follows SIGACT's policy on prior publication and simultaneous submissions; the same (or essentially the same) work may not be submitted at the same time to another conference with published proceedings or to a journal.
