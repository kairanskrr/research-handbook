# STOC 2026: Call for Papers

- Source: `stoc2026cfp`, [original](https://acm-stoc.org/stoc2026/stoc2026-cfp.html)
- Version: Call for papers of the 58th ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 2026, sponsored by ACM SIGACT), accessed 2026-10-08. Locations give page subheadings. Used only as an example of one top theory conference in one year.

### E1

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "Papers presenting new and original research on the theory of computation are sought." / "Papers that extend the reach of the theory of computing, or raise important problems that can benefit from theoretical investigation and analysis, are encouraged."
- Key point: Solicits original research in the theory of computation; papers that extend the frontiers of the theory of computation, or that raise important problems that can benefit from theoretical analysis, are encouraged.

### E2

- Location: Submission format
- Quote: "There is no page limit and authors are encouraged to use the" / "of their paper as the submission."
- Key point: There is no page limit for submissions, and authors are encouraged to submit the "full version" directly.

### E3

- Location: Submission format
- Quote: "Each submission should contain within the initial twelve pages following the title page a clear presentation of the merits of the paper, including a discussion of the paper's importance within the context of prior work and a description of the key technical and conceptual ideas used to achieve its main claims." / "This part of the submission should be addressed to a broad spectrum of theoretical computer science researchers, not solely to experts in the subarea."
- Key point: The first 12 pages after the title page should make clear the paper's value: its importance in the context of prior work, and the key technical and conceptual ideas behind the main results; this part should be written for a broad audience of theoretical computer science researchers, not only specialists in the subarea.

### E4

- Location: Submission format
- Quote: "Proofs must be provided that can enable the main mathematical claims of the paper to be fully verified." / "material other than the abstract, table of contents, and the first twelve pages will be read at the committee's discretion."
- Key point: Proofs that allow full verification of the main mathematical claims must be provided; the committee reads the abstract, table of contents, and material beyond the first 12 pages at its discretion.

### E5

- Location: Submission format, double-blind
- Quote: "STOC 2026 will use double-blind reviewing" / "Nothing should be done in the name of anonymity that weakens the submission or makes the job of reviewing the paper more difficult." / "authors may post drafts of their papers on the web, submit them to arXiv, and give talks on their research ideas."
- Key point: Review is double-blind, but the paper must not be weakened or reviewing made harder for the sake of anonymity; authors may still post drafts online and on arXiv and give talks.

### E6

- Location: Submission Instructions
- Quote: "It is expected that authors of accepted papers will make their full papers, with proofs, publicly available on arXiv, ECCC, or a similar preprint service by the camera-ready deadline."
- Key point: Authors of accepted papers must make a full version with proofs publicly available on a preprint server such as arXiv or ECCC before the camera-ready deadline.

### E7

- Location: Prior and Simultaneous Submissions
- Quote: "The conference will follow SIGACT's policy on prior publication and simultaneous submissions." / "SIGACT policy does not allow simultaneous submissions of the same (or essentially the same) material to another conference with published proceedings." / "The only exception to this policy are prior or simultaneous publications appearing in the Science and Nature journals."
- Key point: SIGACT's policy on prior publication and simultaneous submission applies: the same or essentially the same content may not be submitted simultaneously to another conference with proceedings; the only exception is publication in Science and Nature.

### E8

- Location: Dates and other relevant information, Camera-ready versions
- Quote: "The page limit is 12 pages"
- Key point: The camera-ready version of an accepted paper is limited to 12 pages.

### E9

- Location: Recommended Best Practices for References
- Quote: "Authors are asked to avoid "et al." in citations in favor of an equal mention of all authors' surnames."
- Key point: When citing, please do not use "et al."; list all authors' surnames equally.

### E10

- Location: Opening, Typical but not exclusive topics of interest
- Quote: "algorithms and data structures, computational complexity, randomness in computing, algorithmic graph theory and combinatorics, analysis of Boolean functions, approximation algorithms, cryptography, computational learning theory, continuous and discrete optimization, economics and computation, parallel and distributed algorithms, quantum computing"
- Key point: Typical topics include algorithms and data structures, computational complexity, randomness in computing, approximation algorithms, cryptography, computational learning theory, optimization, economics and computation, parallel and distributed algorithms, quantum computing, and more.

### E11

- Location: Dates and other relevant information
- Quote: "Paper submission deadline: Tuesday, November 4, 2025, 4:59pm EST." / "There is no separate abstract submission deadline." / "Notification date: by email on or before February 1, 2026."
- Key point: The STOC 2026 submission deadline is November 4, 2025, with no separate abstract deadline; notifications are sent by February 1, 2026.
