# China 2020: Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China (中华人民共和国著作权法) (2020 amendment, English text on the National People's Congress website)

- Source: `npc2020copyright`, [original (National People's Congress English website)](https://subsites.chinadaily.com.cn/npc/2020-11/11/c_674691.htm)
- Version: Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Amending the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China (adopted at the 23rd Meeting of the Standing Committee of the 13th NPC on November 11, 2020, Presidential Order No. 62, in force from June 1, 2021); the web page runs to 3 pages, with the full English text of the amended Copyright Law in the second half. This excerpt uses the English text published on the NPC's English website (run by China Daily), which is marked "Translation for Reference Only"; the authoritative Chinese text is the one on the NPC's official website. Locations give article numbers.

### E1

- Location: Presidential Order No. 62
- Quote: "The Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Amending the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China, adopted at the 23rd Meeting of the Standing Committee of the Thirteenth National People's Congress of the People's Republic of China on November 11, 2020, is hereby promulgated and shall go into effect on June 1, 2021."
- Key point: The decision on the third amendment of the Copyright Law was adopted on November 11, 2020, and took effect on June 1, 2021.

### E2

- Location: amended full text, Article 24, paragraph 1, opening clause
- Quote: "In the following cases, a work may be used without permission of, and without payment of remuneration to the copyright owner, provided that the name or appellation of the author and the title of the work are indicated, the normal use of the work is not affected and the legitimate rights and interests enjoyed by the copyright owner are not unreasonably prejudiced"
- Key point: In the following cases a work may be used without the copyright owner's permission and without paying remuneration, provided that the author's name and the title of the work are given, the normal use of the work is not affected, and the copyright owner's legitimate rights and interests are not unreasonably harmed.

### E3

- Location: amended full text, Article 24, paragraph 1, item (2)
- Quote: "(2) appropriate quotation from a published work of another in one's own work for the purpose of introducing or commenting a certain work, or illustrating a point"
- Key point: Appropriate quotation from another's published work in your own work in order to introduce or comment on a work or to illustrate a point.

### E4

- Location: amended full text, Article 24, paragraph 1, item (6)
- Quote: "(6) translation, adaptation, compilation, broadcasting, or reproduction in a small quantity of copies, of a published work by teachers or scientific researchers for use in classroom teaching or scientific research, provided that such a work shall not be published or distributed"
- Key point: Translation, adaptation, compilation, broadcasting, or reproduction in small quantities of a published work by teachers or scientific researchers for classroom teaching or scientific research, provided it is not published or distributed.

### E5

- Location: amended full text, Article 24, paragraph 1, item (13)
- Quote: "(13) other circumstances as provided by laws and administrative regulations"
- Key point: Article 24 lists 13 cases; the last is "other circumstances as provided by laws and administrative regulations."
