# American Antiquity submission instructions: Preparing your materials (Cambridge University Press website, accessed October 2026)

- Source: `amantiquity2026instructions`, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-antiquity/information/author-instructions/preparing-your-materials
- Version: submission instructions for American Antiquity, the journal of the Society for American Archaeology (SAA), on the website of its publisher, Cambridge University Press, accessed October 2026. The page gives no publication date, and its content may change.

### E1

- Location: References and in-text citations
- Quote: "As of October 2026, the SAA journals use Chicago Manual of Style's Author-Date referencing style." / "In the text, the author's surname, date of publication and, if required, a page number, should be given. For example: "(Sauveterre 1998, 20)"."
- Key point: As of October 2026 (the original says "As of October 2026" without saying when the change took effect), SAA journals use the Chicago Manual of Style's author-date system; in the text, give the author's surname, year of publication, and page number where needed, e.g., (Sauveterre 1998, 20).

### E2

- Location: Authorship and contributorship
- Quote: "All authors listed on any papers submitted to this journal must be in agreement that the authors listed would all be considered authors according to disciplinary norms, and that no authors who would reasonably be considered an author have been excluded."
- Key point: All listed authors must agree that everyone listed counts as an author by disciplinary norms, and that no one who deserves authorship has been left out.

### E3

- Location: CRediT taxonomy for contributors
- Quote: "When submitting a manuscript, the corresponding author will be prompted to provide further details concerning contributions to the manuscript using the CRediT taxonomy ." / "All parties who have contributed to the scholarly work, but do not meet the full criteria for authorship, should be recognised with their contributions described in terms of the CRediT taxonomy."
- Key point: At submission, the corresponding author describes each person's contribution using CRediT (the Contributor Roles Taxonomy); contributors who do not meet authorship criteria should also be acknowledged, with their contributions described using CRediT.
