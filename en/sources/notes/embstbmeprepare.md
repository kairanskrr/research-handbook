# IEEE EMBS: Prepare and Submit Manuscript – IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering

- Source: `embstbmeprepare`, [original (Internet Archive snapshot)](https://web.archive.org/web/20250115120531/https://www.embs.org/tbme/for-authors/prepare-and-submit-manuscript/)
- Version: Author instructions page of *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering* (TBME), published by the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBS). The official site returns 403 to scripts, so this excerpt was read from an Internet Archive snapshot of 2025-01-15; the page may have been updated since. Locations give the section of the page.

### E1

- Location: journal description at the bottom of the page
- Quote: "Papers range from engineering development in methods and techniques with biomedical applications to experimental and clinical investigations with engineering contributions."
- Key point: TBME papers range from engineering development of methods and techniques with biomedical applications to experimental and clinical investigations with an engineering contribution.

### E2

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "Structured abstract (see the Template Instructions) must be less than 250 words and address the following 5 points: objective, methods, results, conclusion, and significance." / "If any of these is not clearly addressed, the manuscript may be returned without review."
- Key point: TBME requires a structured abstract of under 250 words covering five points: objective, methods, results, conclusion, and significance; a manuscript missing any of them may be returned without review.

### E3

- Location: Cover Letter
- Quote: "It must disclose if there are any prior conference proceedings papers related to this work, and include a statement on why you believe the prior conference papers represent different works as compared with the submission to TBME."
- Key point: The cover letter must disclose any prior conference proceedings papers related to the work and state why the authors believe they are distinct from the manuscript submitted to TBME.

### E4

- Location: note after the Cover Letter section
- Quote: "Please note that we accept full papers, which have appeared in preliminary form as conference abstracts for consideration of publication." / "Note however that prior publication in an extensive conference paper may disqualify a manuscript for consideration for publication at TBME."
- Key point: Work that has appeared only as a conference abstract may be submitted to TBME as a full paper; but a previously published, substantial full-length conference paper may make the work ineligible.

### E5

- Location: Letters
- Quote: "Given that the current TBME submission to decision is about 30 days, submission of regular papers is encouraged."
- Key point: According to the page at the time, TBME's time from submission to decision was about 30 days.

### E6

- Location: journal information (Peer Review)
- Quote: "Each published article was reviewed by a minimum of two independent reviewers using a single-anonymous peer review process"
- Key point: Each published TBME article is reviewed by at least two independent reviewers under single-anonymous peer review (reviewers know who the authors are; authors do not know the reviewers).

### E7

- Location: Author Consent Form
- Quote: "The IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society will apply sanctions against authors who are discovered to have made duplicate submissions to other journals."
- Key point: EMBS sanctions authors found to have made duplicate submissions (the page goes on to list: immediate rejection, return of manuscripts under review, and a one-year ban on submitting to the society's journals).

### E8

- Location: Disclosure of Financial Support and Author- and Project-Related Conflicts of Interest
- Quote: "These requirements include financial interests of the author in any company that may be licensing, patenting, or using results from the published work at the time of or prior to the date of manuscript acceptance."
- Key point: Interests to disclose include the author's financial interests in any company that may be licensing, patenting, or using the results of the work.

### E9

- Location: Human and Animal Subjects section
- Quote: "If the published work utilizes human subjects, authors must indicate whether the procedures followed were in accordance with the ethical standards of the responsible committee on human experimentation (institutional and national) and with the Helsinki Declaration of 1975, as revised in 2000."
- Key point: For work involving human subjects, state whether the procedures followed the ethical standards of the responsible institutional and national ethics committees and the Declaration of Helsinki (the page cites the 1975 version, revised in 2000).

### E10

- Location: Human and Animal Subjects section
- Quote: "If the published work utilizes animal subjects (including animal sources for in vitro research), authors must indicate whether the institutional and national guides for the care and use of laboratory animals were followed."
- Key point: For work involving animals (including animal sources for in vitro research), state whether institutional and national guidelines for the care and use of laboratory animals were followed.
