# Nature Sustainability: Content Types

- Source: `natsustaincontent`, [web page](https://www.nature.com/natsustain/content)
- Version: the article-types page of the journal Nature Sustainability, read on 2026-10-17. Locations give the page's subheadings. It speaks only for this journal; other Nature journals have similar but not identical pages.

### E1

- Location: Perspective
- Quote: "A Perspective is intended to provide a forum for authors to discuss models and ideas from a personal viewpoint. They are more forward looking and/or speculative than Reviews and may take a narrower field of view. They may be opinionated but should remain balanced and are intended to stimulate discussion and new approaches."
- Key point: A Perspective discusses models and ideas from a personal viewpoint and is more forward-looking and speculative than a Review; it may be opinionated but should stay balanced, and aims to stimulate discussion and new approaches.

### E2

- Location: Perspective
- Quote: "Perspectives may also advocate a controversial position or present a speculative hypothesis. Two articles advocating opposite sides in a research controversy are normally published as Perspectives."
- Key point: A Perspective may argue a controversial position or put forward a speculative hypothesis; two articles taking opposite sides of a research controversy are normally both published as Perspectives.

### E3

- Location: Perspective
- Quote: "Most Perspective articles are invited by the editors, though we are open to consider proposals."
- Key point: Most Perspectives are invited by the editors, but proposals are considered.

### E4

- Location: Perspective
- Quote: "The synopsis should outline the topics that will be covered, how the material will be organized, include an example of figure (even in sketch form), list any recent, key publications in the area, and state clearly what the contribution of the article is (new model, new conceptual framework, etc.)."
- Key point: An unsolicited proposal starts with a synopsis that outlines the topics, how the material will be organized, an example figure, recent key publications, and the article's contribution (a new model, a new conceptual framework, and so on).

### E5

- Location: Perspective, Format
- Quote: "Main text – up to 5,000 words." / "References – up to 60." / "Perspectives are peer reviewed."
- Key point: The main text is up to 5,000 words with up to 60 references, and Perspectives are peer reviewed.

### E6

- Location: Comment
- Quote: "These pieces are intended to be agenda-setting, authoritative and informed, and can be provocative articles calling for action on timely issues [...] If so, they must road-map a proposed solution in detail, not simply snapshot a problem."
- Key point: A Comment may call for action, but it must lay out a detailed road map to a proposed solution rather than simply describe a problem.
