# Huang 2021: How to cope with paper rejection?

- Source: `huang2021rejection`, [original tweet](https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1443248831102136333), [Thread Reader mirror](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1443248831102136333.html)
- Version: Tweet thread, 2021-09-29, 7 tweets.

### E1

> Withdrawn: duplicates [E6](#e6) (the quote is fully contained in E6).

- Location: Tweet 2 (It’s okay to be upset)
- Quote: "So it's perfectly okay to feel upset. Talk with friends/lab mates/advisor. Take a day off and come back recharged."
- Key point: Feeling upset after a rejection is normal; talk with friends, lab mates, or your advisor, take a day off, and come back.

### E2

- Location: Tweet 4 (It’s not personal)
- Quote: "Don’t take rejection personal. It's about your ideas, implementation, experiments, and writing. Create a to-do list from the reviewers' feedbacks and make sure that those concerns are properly addressed in your revision."
- Key point: Rejection is not personal; turn the reviewers' feedback into a to-do list and address each concern in the revision.

### E3

- Location: Tweet 5 (It doesn't matter much)
- Quote: "Vast majority of the papers won't matter in the long run. Your career will be shaped only by a few good ones."
- Key point: Most papers will not matter in the long run, and your career is shaped by a few good ones; using a rejection to make a paper more solid can be a good thing.
