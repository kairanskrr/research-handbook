# Jacobsson et al. 2022: An open-access database and analysis tool for perovskite solar cells based on the FAIR data principles

- Source: `jacobsson2022open`, [original](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41560-021-00941-3)
- Version: Nature Energy 7, 107–115 (2022; published online 2021-12-13), open access (CC BY 4.0).

### E1

- Location: Main
- Quote: "However, most laboratories are not able to adhere to the FAIR data principles, especially in the applied science fields."
- Key point: The authors argue that most laboratories cannot adhere to the FAIR data principles, especially in the applied sciences.

### E2

- Location: Main
- Quote: "However, the largest hurdle is the diversity and complexity of the datasets involved. For instance, sample properties are often influenced by the sample history."
- Key point: The largest hurdle is the diversity and complexity of the data; for instance, sample properties are often influenced by the sample history (how it was made and handled).

### E3

- Location: Details of the database
- Quote: "We have manually gone through every paper found in the Web of Science with the search phrase ‘perovskite solar’ up to the end of February 2020 (that is, over 15,000 papers). In total, we have manually extracted data for over 42,400 devices."
- Key point: The authors manually went through every paper found in Web of Science with the search phrase "perovskite solar" up to the end of February 2020 (over 15,000 papers) and extracted data for over 42,400 devices.

### E4

- Location: Example uses of the Perovskite Database
- Quote: "A problem faced while developing perovskite solar cells, which is in no way unique for the perovskite field, are cell-to-cell and batch-to-batch variations. Those can be large, thus masking otherwise statistically significant differences."
- Key point: Cell-to-cell and batch-to-batch variations are a problem in perovskite solar cell research (and not only there); they can be large enough to mask otherwise statistically significant differences.

### E5

- Location: Example uses of the Perovskite Database
- Quote: "There are also laboratory-to-laboratory variations, and what appears to make a significant difference in one laboratory may not be relevant in another. This is usually ascribed to undescribed, unexplored, unknown or hidden parameters"
- Key point: There are also laboratory-to-laboratory variations: what makes a significant difference in one lab may not matter in another. This is usually ascribed to undescribed, unexplored, unknown, or "hidden" parameters.

### E6

- Location: Example uses of the Perovskite Database
- Quote: "Those could be things such as glove box volume, precise atmospheric composition during fabrication, minor or unintended variations in precursor stoichiometry, chemical impurities and so on to mention a few hypotheses."
- Key point: Possible hidden parameters the authors mention, as hypotheses, include glove box volume, the precise atmosphere during fabrication, minor or unintended variations in precursor stoichiometry, and chemical impurities.

### E7

- Location: Future expansion of the database
- Quote: "In a typical project, there may be hundreds and sometimes thousands of devices made before the paper is written. Despite this, the average number of devices for which we could extract data was fewer than six per publication with original device data."
- Key point: A typical project may make hundreds or thousands of devices before the paper is written, yet in papers with original device data, the authors could extract data for fewer than six devices per paper on average (a statistic for their corpus).

### E8

- Location: Future expansion of the database
- Quote: "A common pattern is that one parameter is changed in few steps, and for each of those steps data for the best device could be found."
- Key point: A common pattern is that one parameter is changed in a few steps, with data reported only for the best device at each step.

### E9

- Location: Future expansion of the database
- Quote: "Sharing data in this way gives it new life and draws additional attention to the original publication, it is a way to comply with the demands for openness more frequently seen from taxpayers, funding agencies and publishers"
- Key point: Sharing data this way gives it new life, draws more attention to the original paper, and meets the demands for openness from taxpayers, funding agencies, and publishers.
