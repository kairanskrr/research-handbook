# BMES 2021: BMES Code of Ethics

- Source: `bmes2021ethics`, [Original PDF](https://www.bmes.org/hubfs/Files/BMES_Code_of_Ethics_2021_-_Revised_October_2021.pdf)
- Version: Code of Ethics of the Biomedical Engineering Society (BMES), revised October 2021, downloaded 2026-10-06. Locations are given as the code's item numbers.

### E1

- Location: Preamble
- Quote: "This BMES Code of Ethics is not a binding statement of law" / "The principles herein define the specific conduct to which a biomedical engineer should conform to meet the ethical standards of our profession, beyond the requirements set by regulatory bodies."
- Key point: The code is not a statement of law; it sets out the professional ethics biomedical engineers should follow beyond what regulators require.

### E2

- Location: 1.1.1
- Quote: "Assume personal responsibility for understanding the local regulations pertaining to biomedical research and technology development, and adhere to the letter and spirit of applicable regulation"
- Key point: You are personally responsible for understanding local regulations on biomedical research and technology development, and for following both their letter and their spirit.

### E3

- Location: 2.1.3
- Quote: "Keep meticulous, comprehensive, and accurate records throughout the process of discovery and design to prevent errors, increase transparency, and improve reproducibility."
- Key point: Keep meticulous, comprehensive, and accurate records throughout discovery and design, to prevent errors, increase transparency, and improve reproducibility.

### E4

- Location: 2.1.4
- Quote: "Exercise due diligence when studying or developing biomedical technology (e.g., seek review from collaborators or consultants when working outside areas of proficiency, keep up to date on current methods and practices, and undertake thorough background searches)."
- Key point: Exercise due diligence when studying or developing biomedical technology: seek review from collaborators or consultants when working outside your expertise, keep up with current methods, and do a thorough background search.

### E5

- Location: 2.2.2
- Quote: "Strive to make biomedical data and methodology publicly accessible following project completion or proprietary development (subject to privacy constraints on human data)"
- Key point: After a project or proprietary development ends, make data and methods publicly accessible as far as privacy constraints on human data allow.

### E6

- Location: 2.3.1
- Quote: "Treat human subjects as intrinsically valuable rather than instrumental in service of research and development goals"
- Key point: Treat human subjects as intrinsically valuable, not as instruments in service of research and development goals.

### E7

- Location: 2.4.1
- Quote: "Use animals in quantities and in levels of distress that are justified by the potential benefits the biomedical research may have for advancing the public health."
- Key point: The number of animals used and the distress they suffer must be justified by the research's potential benefits for public health.

### E8

- Location: 3.1.2
- Quote: "Design and refine biomedical devices explicitly with the broadest possible range of humans in mind (e.g., spanning age, sex, size, ability, and other fundamental physiological characteristics)."
- Key point: Design devices explicitly for the broadest possible range of people, spanning age, sex, size, ability, and other basic physiological characteristics.

### E9

- Location: 3.2.2
- Quote: "Ensure that artificially intelligent systems and data-driven models developed with the potential for use in public health decisions are validated on their use cases and that their limitations, scope, and data sources are known and clearly defined to practitioners and institutions (e.g., algorithmic medical diagnostics)."
- Key point: AI systems and data-driven models that may be used in public health decisions must be validated on their actual use cases, and their limitations, scope, and data sources must be made clear to practitioners and institutions.

### E10

- Location: 5.1.1
- Quote: "Declare conflicts of interest transparently to relevant parties, both financial and intellectual"
- Key point: Declare conflicts of interest transparently to relevant parties, both financial and intellectual.

### E11

- Location: 5.1.2
- Quote: "Communicate to the media and public with scrupulous honesty (e.g., clearly express warranted levels of confidence in conclusions and put expected efficacy in context) to avoid misleading the public (e.g., giving false hopes for treatment"
- Key point: Communicate with the media and the public with scrupulous honesty: state how confident the conclusions warrant being, put expected efficacy in context, and avoid giving patients false hope.
