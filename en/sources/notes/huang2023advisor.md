# Huang 2023: How to meet with your advisors/mentors? (tweet thread)

- Source: `huang2023advisor`, [original tweet](https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1653614931202195458), [Thread Reader mirror](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1653614931202195458.html)
- Version: Tweet thread by Jia-Bin Huang, 2023-05-03, 8 tweets; read via the Thread Reader mirror on 2026-10-05. Locations give the subheadings in the tweets.

### E1

- Location: *Present results*
- Quote: "Your advisor sees your results for the first time in the meeting? It means they don't have time to understand and think about them."
- Key point: If your advisor sees your results for the first time in the meeting, they have no time to understand and think about them.

### E2

- Location: *Present results*
- Quote: "Share and discuss results async FREQUENTLY."
- Key point: Share and discuss results with your advisor asynchronously and often, rather than saving them up for the weekly meeting.

### E3

- Location: *Make an agenda*
- Quote: "Make an agenda. Manage the meeting to ensure you cover all the topics you want to discuss."
- Key point: Make an agenda and run the meeting yourself so you cover everything you want to discuss.

### E4

- Location: *Start with a summary*
- Quote: "Last week, we agreed to examine hypotheses A and B. I will show my experiments later. In sum, ..."
- Key point: Open with a summary: what you agreed to do last week and what you concluded; show the details later.

### E5

- Location: *Interpret your results*
- Quote: "Contextualize your results."
- Key point: Put results in context: what you did, why, how, what you found, and whether it matched expectations.

### E6

- Location: *Propose next steps*
- Quote: "That's all my progress. Based on what we see in the results, here are the prioritized tasks A, B, C (and why)."
- Key point: Instead of asking "what should I do next," propose prioritized next steps based on the results, with your reasons.

### E7

- Location: *Summarize your meeting*
- Quote: "Summarize the meeting and the next steps mutually agreed on. Send an email/post on slack right away."
- Key point: Right after the meeting, write up and send a summary of the discussion and the mutually agreed next steps.
