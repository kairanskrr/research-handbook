# EdWorkingPapers: About EdWorkingPapers

- Source: `edworkingpapersabout`, [original](https://edworkingpapers.com/about)
- Version: Web page, accessed October 2026. The "About" page of the education working paper series hosted by the Annenberg Institute at Brown University.

### E1

- Location: About EdWorkingPapers
- Quote: "offers this national working paper series to provide open access to high-quality papers from multiple disciplines and from multiple universities and research organizations on a wide variety of topics related to education."
- Key point: A national, open-access working paper series for education research from multiple disciplines, universities, and research organizations.

### E2

- Location: About EdWorkingPapers
- Quote: "EdWorkingPapers circulates papers prior to publication for comment and discussion; these papers have not gone through a peer review process."
- Key point: Papers are circulated before publication for comment and discussion; they have not been peer reviewed.

### E3

- Location: Scope
- Quote: "EdWorkingPapers primarily focuses on new research that holds significant implications for education policy and is oriented toward eventual publication in academic journals."
- Key point: The series mainly features new research with significant implications for education policy that is intended for eventual publication in academic journals.
