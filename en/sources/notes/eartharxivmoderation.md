# EarthArXiv: Moderation Policy

- Source: `eartharxivmoderation`, [original](https://eartharxiv.github.io/moderation.html)
- Version: EarthArXiv moderation policy page, accessed 2026-10-06. Locations give the subheading.

### E1

- Location: What does EarthArXiv accept?
- Quote: "So-called ‘null’ results (i.e. results that do not support a hypothesis)"
- Key point: The submission types EarthArXiv generally accepts include so-called "null" results, i.e. results that do not support a hypothesis.

### E2

- Location: Basic Requirements for Paper Submissions
- Quote: "Your paper clearly states that this is a non-peer reviewed preprint submitted to EarthArXiv"
- Key point: The manuscript must clearly state that it is a non-peer-reviewed preprint submitted to EarthArXiv.

### E3

- Location: What does EarthArXiv accept? (types not accepted)
- Quote: "Theses and course projects (see Thesis Commons as an alternative)"
- Key point: EarthArXiv does not accept theses or course projects.

### E4

- Location: top of the page
- Quote: "When all submission requirements are met, EarthArXiv is typically able to moderate manuscripts within one week of submission."
- Key point: When all submission requirements are met, EarthArXiv typically moderates a manuscript within one week (moderation, not peer review).
