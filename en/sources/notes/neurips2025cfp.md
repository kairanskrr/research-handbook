# NeurIPS 2025: Call for Papers

- Source: `neurips2025cfp`, [original](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers)
- Version: NeurIPS 2025 call for papers, accessed 2026-10-05. Locations give section headings. Used only as an example of one top machine learning conference.

### E1

- Location: list of dates at the top of the page
- Quote: "Abstract submission deadline: May 11, 2025 AoE [...] Full paper submission deadline: May 15, 2025 AOE"
- Key point: The abstract is due first and the full paper a few days later; deadlines are in the Anywhere on Earth (AoE) time zone.

### E2

- Location: Paper Formatting Instructions
- Quote: "The main text of a submitted paper is limited to nine content pages, including all figures and tables."
- Key point: The main text is limited to nine pages, including figures and tables.

### E3

- Location: Paper Checklist
- Quote: "authors are required to complete a paper checklist."
- Key point: Submissions must include a completed paper checklist (covering reproducibility, transparency, ethics, and societal impact).

### E4

- Location: Double-blind Reviewing
- Quote: "All submissions must be anonymized and may not contain any identifying information that may violate the double-blind reviewing policy."
- Key point: Double-blind review: submissions (including supplementary material and code links) must not reveal the authors' identities.

### E5

- Location: Preprints
- Quote: "The existence of non-anonymous preprints (on arXiv or other online repositories, personal websites, social media) will not result in rejection. [...] Reviewers will be instructed not to actively look for such preprints"
- Key point: Non-anonymous preprints on arXiv or elsewhere will not lead to rejection; reviewers are asked not to search for them actively.

### E6

- Location: Preprints
- Quote: "Note that public versions of the submission should not say "Under review at NeurIPS" or similar."
- Key point: Public versions should not say "Under review at NeurIPS" or similar.

### E7

- Location: Dual Submissions
- Quote: "Papers previously presented at workshops are permitted, so long as they did not appear in conference proceedings"
- Key point: Papers presented at workshops without formal proceedings may be submitted; papers that appeared in proceedings count as published.

### E8

- Location: Author Responses
- Quote: "Authors will have one week to view and respond to initial reviews."
- Key point: Authors have one week to read and respond to the initial reviews.

### E9

- Location: Publication of Accepted Submissions
- Quote: "Reviews, meta-reviews, and any discussion with the authors will be made public for accepted papers"
- Key point: Reviews and discussions for accepted papers are made public.

### E10

- Location: Publication of Accepted Submissions
- Quote: "We strongly encourage accompanying code and data to be submitted with accepted papers when appropriate"
- Key point: Authors of accepted papers are strongly encouraged to submit code and data with them.

### E11

- Location: Author Responses
- Quote: "Authors may not submit revisions of their paper or supplemental material, but may post their responses as a discussion in OpenReview."
- Key point: During the response period, authors may not submit a revised paper or supplementary material; they can only post responses as a discussion on the review platform (OpenReview).

### E12

- Location: Author Responses
- Quote: "After the initial response period, authors will be able to respond to any further reviewer/AC questions and comments by posting on the submission's forum page."
- Key point: After the initial response period, authors can still answer follow-up questions from reviewers and area chairs (ACs) on the paper's discussion page.

### E13

- Location: Author Responses
- Quote: "After the notification deadline, accepted and opted-in rejected papers will be made public and open for non-anonymous public commenting. Their anonymous reviews, meta-reviews, author responses and reviewer responses will also be made public."
- Key point: After notification, accepted papers and rejected papers whose authors opt in are made public, together with their reviews, meta-reviews, and author responses.

### E14

- Location: Dual Submissions
- Quote: "The NeurIPS policy on dual submissions applies for the entire duration of the reviewing process."
- Key point: The ban on dual submissions applies for the entire review process.
