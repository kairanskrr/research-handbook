# Hopewell et al. 2025: CONSORT 2025 statement: Updated guideline for reporting randomised trials

- Source: `hopewell2025consort`, [PLOS Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1004587) (CC BY 4.0)
- Version: PLoS Med 22(4): e1004587, 14 April 2025. The same statement was published simultaneously in several journals. Page numbers follow the PDF page order.

### E1

- Location: Abstract, Results, PDF p. 2
- Quote: "The CONSORT 2025 statement consists of a 30-item checklist of essential items that should be included when reporting the results of a randomised trial and a diagram for documenting the flow of participants through the trial."
- Key point: CONSORT 2025 consists of a 30-item checklist of essential items to report and a diagram documenting the flow of participants through the trial.

### E2

- Location: Introduction, PDF p. 3
- Quote: "Randomised trials, when appropriately designed, conducted, analysed, and reported, are generally considered the highest quality evidence in evaluating healthcare interventions."
- Key point: Randomized trials that are appropriately designed, conducted, analyzed, and reported are generally considered the highest-quality evidence for evaluating healthcare interventions.

### E3

- Location: Introduction, PDF p. 3
- Quote: "having a clear and transparent trial protocol is important because it prespecifies the methods used in the trial, such as the primary outcome, thereby reducing the likelihood of undeclared post hoc changes"
- Key point: A clear and transparent trial protocol pre-specifies the methods, such as the primary outcome, reducing the chance of undeclared post hoc changes.

### E4

- Location: Introduction, PDF p. 3
- Quote: "Similar problems related to the lack of complete and transparent reporting of trial protocols led to the development of the SPIRIT (Standard Protocol Items: Recommendations for Interventional Trials) statement, published in 2013"
- Key point: Similar problems with incomplete reporting of trial protocols led to a dedicated protocol reporting guideline, SPIRIT (first published in 2013).

### E5

- Location: Main changes to CONSORT 2025, PDF p. 5
- Quote: "We also restructured the CONSORT checklist, with a new section on open science, which includes items that are conceptually linked, such as trial registration (item 2), where the trial protocol and statistical analysis plan can be accessed (item 3), sharing of de-identified participant level data (item 4), and funding and conflicts of interest (item 5)."
- Key point: The 2025 version adds a new "open science" section covering trial registration, where to find the protocol and statistical analysis plan, sharing of de-identified individual participant data, and funding and conflicts of interest.

### E6

- Location: Implication and limitations, PDF p. 7
- Quote: "It is important to note that CONSORT 2025 and SPIRIT 2025 do not include recommendations for designing, conducting, or analysing trials"
- Key point: CONSORT and SPIRIT do not cover how to design, conduct, or analyze trials.

### E7

- Location: Implication and limitations, PDF p. 7
- Quote: "Importantly, however, CONSORT 2025 is not meant to be used as a quality assessment instrument."
- Key point: CONSORT is not meant to be used as a tool for scoring trial quality.

### E8

- Location: Explanatory paragraph after Table 1, PDF p. 9
- Quote: "We encourage trial authors to detail what was done and to acknowledge if something was not done or was modified, ensuring alignment of information with that reported in the trial protocol, statistical analysis plan, and trial registry."
- Key point: Authors should detail what was done, acknowledge anything not done or modified, and make sure the paper agrees with the trial protocol, statistical analysis plan, and registry record.

### E9

- Location: CONSORT 2025 statement
- Quote: "CONSORT 2025 provides guidance for reporting all randomised trials but focuses on the most common type, the two-group parallel design."
- Key point: CONSORT 2025 applies to all randomized trials but focuses on the most common type, the two-group parallel design.

### E10

- Location: Implication and limitations, PDF p. 7
- Quote: "It is important to note that CONSORT 2025 and SPIRIT 2025 do not include recommendations for designing, conducting, or analysing trials, but nevertheless the recommendations contained here can help researchers in the design, conduct, and analysis of their trial by highlighting key issues to consider."
- Key point: CONSORT and SPIRIT do not cover how to design, conduct, or analyze trials, but the key issues they raise help with design, conduct, and analysis.

### E11

- Location: CONSORT 2025 statement
- Quote: "The CONSORT 2025 statement supersedes the CONSORT 2010 statement, which should no longer be used."
- Key point: CONSORT 2025 supersedes the 2010 version, which should no longer be used.

### E12

- Location: CONSORT 2025 statement
- Quote: "We strongly recommend the CONSORT 2025 statement be used alongside the CONSORT 2025 explanation and elaboration document"
- Key point: The CONSORT 2025 statement is strongly recommended to be used together with its explanation and elaboration document.

### E13

- Location: CONSORT 2025 statement
- Quote: "We also strongly recommend the submission of a completed CONSORT 2025 checklist as part of the manuscript submission process, detailing where in the manuscript checklist items are reported"
- Key point: Submit a completed CONSORT checklist with the manuscript, noting where in the manuscript each item is reported.
