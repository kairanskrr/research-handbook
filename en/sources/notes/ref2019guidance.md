# REF 2019/01: Guidance on submissions (REF 2021)

- Source: `ref2019guidance`, [original PDF](https://2021.ref.ac.uk/media/1447/ref-2019_01-guidance-on-submissions.pdf)
- Version: Guidance on submissions for the 2021 round of the UK Research Excellence Framework (REF), REF 2019/01, including the October 2020 revisions. Published by the four UK higher education funding bodies. Locations are given by annex and paragraph.

### E1

- Location: Annex C, para. 1
- Quote: "For the purposes of the REF, research is defined as a process of investigation leading to new insights, effectively shared."
- Key point: The REF definition of research: a process of investigation leading to new insights, effectively shared.

### E2

- Location: Annex C, para. 2
- Quote: "the invention and generation of ideas, images, performances, artefacts including design, where these lead to new or substantially improved insights;"
- Key point: Research includes the invention and generation of ideas, images, performances, and artefacts including design, where these lead to new or substantially improved insights.

### E3

- Location: Annex B, table note 1.a
- Quote: "For non-text outputs, practice-based outputs or any other output where the research dimensions are not evident within the output/representation of the output itself: a written description of the research process and/or content should be provided."
- Key point: For non-text, practice-based outputs where the research dimensions are not evident in the output itself, include a written description of the research process and/or content.
