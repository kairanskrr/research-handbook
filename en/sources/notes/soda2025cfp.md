# SODA 2025: Submissions

- Source: `soda2025cfp`, [original](https://www.siam.org/conferences-events/siam-conferences/soda25/submissions/)
- Version: Submission page of the ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms (SODA 2025). siam.org returns 403 to scripts, so this entry is taken from an Internet Archive copy of the official page made on 2024-11-06 (https://web.archive.org/web/20241106211859/https://www.siam.org/conferences-events/siam-conferences/soda25/submissions/). The SODA 2026 submission page has no archived copy, so the 2025 page is used. Locations give page subheadings. Used only as an example of one theory conference in one year.

### E1

- Location: Page title
- Quote: "ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms (SODA25)"
- Key point: SODA stands for ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms.

### E2

- Location: Important Submission Information
- Quote: "Submissions are due by 11:59 p.m. on July 5, 2024, anywhere on earth." / "There is a single submission deadline for both registering and submitting a paper; there is not a separate abstract deadline."
- Key point: The deadline was July 5, 2024 (anywhere on Earth); there is a single deadline, with no separate abstract deadline.

### E3

- Location: Submission Format
- Quote: "There is no page limit and authors are encouraged to use the "full version" of their paper as the submission." / "The submission should contain within the initial ten pages following the title page a clear presentation of the merits of the paper" / "The submission should be addressed to a broad spectrum of theoretical computer science and discrete mathematics researchers." / "Proofs must be provided which can enable the main mathematical claims of the paper to be fully verified." / "material other than the abstract, references, and the first ten pages will be read at the committee's discretion"
- Key point: There is no page limit, and full versions are encouraged; the first 10 pages after the title page should make the paper's value clear; write for a broad audience of theoretical computer science and discrete mathematics researchers; proofs that allow full verification of the main mathematical claims must be provided; the committee reads the abstract, references, and material beyond the first 10 pages at its discretion.

### E4

- Location: Anonymous Submissions
- Quote: "The conference will employ a fairly lightweight double-blind reviewing process." / "Nothing should be done in the name of anonymity that weakens the submission or makes the job of reviewing the paper more difficult."
- Key point: Review is double-blind in a "fairly lightweight" form; do not weaken the paper for the sake of anonymity.

### E5

- Location: Simultaneous Submissions
- Quote: "Simultaneous submissions of the results to another conference with a published proceedings is not allowed."
- Key point: The same results may not be submitted at the same time to another conference with proceedings.

### E6

- Location: Simultaneous Submissions
- Quote: "At the time that the author signs SODA's copyright transfer agreement, the author must affirm that no copyright transfer agreement permitting the publication of a similar paper in a journal or elsewhere has been signed."
- Key point: When signing SODA's copyright transfer agreement, authors must state that they have not signed a copyright transfer agreement allowing a similar paper to be published in a journal or elsewhere.
