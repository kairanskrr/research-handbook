# Huang 2023: How to decide what to work on?

- Source: `huang2023decide`, [original tweet](https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1628521223838654464) (read via the [Thread Reader mirror](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1628521223838654464.html))
- Version: Tweet thread, 2023-02-22, 7 tweets.

### E1

- Location: Tweet 2, *Find your position*
- Quote: "Understand your unfair advantages over others, what the community cares about, and how you can best contribute. Avoid crowding the ball (aka chasing the hottest problem in the field)."
- Key point: Understand your unfair advantages over others, what the community cares about, and how you can best contribute; avoid crowding around the hottest problem.

### E2

- Location: Tweet 3, *Identify intrinsic motivation*
- Quote: "Work on problems and real applications that you genuinely care about."
- Key point: Work on problems and real applications you genuinely care about.

### E3

- Location: Tweet 4, *Anticipate disruptive technologies*
- Quote: "Instead of focusing on what everyone is working on NOW, anticipate the major problems LATER when disruptive technologies (e.g., new sensors, algorithms, models, data) emerge."
- Key point: Rather than focusing on what everyone is working on now, anticipate the major problems that will appear when disruptive technologies emerge.

### E4

- Location: Tweet 5, *Imagine success*
- Quote: "At the begging of your project, be an amateur and dream big! Don't be intimidated by all the technical challenges just yet."
- Key point: At the beginning of a project, dream big like an amateur and do not let technical challenges intimidate you yet. (The original's "begging" should read "beginning"; quoted as is.)

### E5

- Location: Tweet 6, *Validate the idea fast*
- Quote: "Not all the ideas will pan out. Design small experiments to validate your hypothesis and assumptions. If you fail, fail FAST!"
- Key point: Not every idea will pan out; design small experiments to test your hypotheses quickly, and if you fail, fail fast.
