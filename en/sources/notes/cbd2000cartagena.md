# Secretariat of the Convention on Biological Diversity 2000: Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety to the Convention on Biological Diversity: Text and Annexes

- Source: `cbd2000cartagena`, [Original PDF](https://www.cbd.int/doc/legal/cartagena-protocol-en.pdf)
- Version: English text of the Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety, adopted January 29, 2000, in force September 11, 2003; the standalone edition published by the Secretariat in Montreal in 2000. Locations are given as article numbers.

### E1

- Location: Article 1
- Quote: "the objective of this Protocol is to contribute to ensuring an adequate level of protection in the field of the safe transfer, handling and use of living modified organisms resulting from modern biotechnology that may have adverse effects on the conservation and sustainable use of biological diversity, taking also into account risks to human health, and specifically focusing on transboundary movements."
- Key point: The Protocol's objective: to help ensure adequate protection in the safe transfer, handling, and use of living modified organisms from modern biotechnology that may harm biological diversity, also taking into account risks to human health, with a focus on transboundary movements.

### E2

- Location: Article 2.1
- Quote: "Each Party shall take necessary and appropriate legal, administrative and other measures to implement its obligations under this Protocol."
- Key point: Each Party must take legal, administrative, and other measures to implement its obligations under the Protocol.

### E3

- Location: Article 3(g)
- Quote: "“Living modified organism” means any living organism that possesses a novel combination of genetic material obtained through the use of modern biotechnology;"
- Key point: A living modified organism is any living organism with a novel combination of genetic material obtained through modern biotechnology.

### E4

- Location: Article 3(i)
- Quote: "“Modern biotechnology” means the application of: a. In vitro nucleic acid techniques, including recombinant deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA) and direct injection of nucleic acid into cells or organelles, or b. Fusion of cells beyond the taxonomic family, that overcome natural physiological reproductive or recombination barriers and that are not techniques used in traditional breeding and selection;"
- Key point: Modern biotechnology means in vitro nucleic acid techniques (including recombinant DNA and direct injection of nucleic acid into cells) or fusion of cells beyond the taxonomic family, which overcome natural reproductive or recombination barriers and are not traditional breeding techniques.

### E5

- Location: Article 3(b)
- Quote: "“Contained use” means any operation, undertaken within a facility, installation or other physical structure, which involves living modified organisms that are controlled by specific measures that effectively limit their contact with, and their impact on, the external environment;"
- Key point: "Contained use" means any operation within a facility or other physical structure involving living modified organisms controlled by specific measures that effectively limit their contact with, and impact on, the external environment.

### E6

- Location: Article 6.2
- Quote: "the provisions of this Protocol with respect to the advance informed agreement procedure shall not apply to the transboundary movement of living modified organisms destined for contained use undertaken in accordance with the standards of the Party of import."
- Key point: The advance informed agreement procedure does not apply to transboundary movements of living modified organisms destined for contained use under the importing Party's standards.

### E7

- Location: Article 7.1
- Quote: "Subject to Articles 5 and 6, the advance informed agreement procedure in Articles 8 to 10 and 12 shall apply prior to the first intentional transboundary movement of living modified organisms for intentional introduction into the environment of the Party of import."
- Key point: The advance informed agreement procedure applies before the first transboundary movement of a living modified organism intended for release into the importing Party's environment.

### E8

- Location: Article 18.2(b)
- Quote: "Living modified organisms that are destined for contained use clearly identifies them as living modified organisms; and specifies any requirements for the safe handling, storage, transport and use, the contact point for further information, including the name and address of the individual and institution to whom the living modified organisms are consigned; and"
- Key point: Documentation accompanying living modified organisms destined for contained use must clearly identify them as such, state requirements for safe handling, storage, transport, and use, and give contact details, including the name and address of the receiving individual and institution.
