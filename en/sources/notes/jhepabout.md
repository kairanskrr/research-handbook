# JHEP: About (Journal of High Energy Physics overview and editorial policy)

- Source: `jhepabout`, [original](https://jhep.sissa.it/jhep/help/JHEP/JHEP_about.jsp)
- Version: The "JHEP about" page of the Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP), read 2026-10-08; the page is undated. The journal is owned by the International School for Advanced Studies (SISSA) in Italy, which runs peer review; Springer publishes it. It represents this one journal only.

### E1

- Location: General; Scope
- Quote: "JHEP encompasses the following areas of theoretical and experimental physics: Collider Physics Underground and Large Array Physics Quantum Field Theory Gauge Field Theories Symmetries String and Brane Theory"
- Key point: JHEP covers many areas of theoretical and experimental physics, including quantum field theory, gauge field theories, and string and brane theory.

### E2

- Location: Editorial policy
- Quote: "The criteria for acceptance are scientific quality, originality and relevance." / "Editors are asked to apply a strict quality filter in selecting contributions for publication."
- Key point: The criteria for acceptance are scientific quality, originality, and relevance; editors are asked to apply a strict quality filter.

### E3

- Location: Editorial policy, machine learning papers
- Quote: "Editors/Referees are asked to apply the same standards of rigour and deductive reasoning that characterize theoretical physics when assessing submissions in the field of machine learning applications in particle physics."
- Key point: When assessing papers on machine learning applications in particle physics, editors and referees apply the same standards of rigor and deductive reasoning that characterize theoretical physics.
