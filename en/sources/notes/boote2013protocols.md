# Boote & Jones 2013: Protocols for AgMIP Crop Model Improvement Teams

- Source: `boote2013protocols`, [Original (PDF on the AgMIP website)](https://agmip.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Protocols-for-AgMIP-Crop-Model-Improvement-Teams.pdf)
- Version: 2013-05-14, 5 pages, published by the Agricultural Model Intercomparison and Improvement Project (AgMIP) on the "Protocols and Reports" page of its website, read 2026-10-08. Organizational recommendations for crop model improvement teams, not a journal article. Locations are given as subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Framework for Proposed Crop Model Improvement Activities
- Quote: "In all cases, it is important to also collect good weather data, soil characteristics, crop and soil measurements in those various experiments designed to collect data to improve crop models or test new production technologies."
- Key point: Any experiment designed to improve crop models or test new production technologies should also collect good weather data, soil characteristics, and crop and soil measurements.

### E2

- Location: Framework for Proposed Crop Model Improvement Activities
- Quote: "Collected data needs to be stored in a way that it is available for the future community of scientists who may see a future need."
- Key point: Store collected data so that future researchers can also access it.
