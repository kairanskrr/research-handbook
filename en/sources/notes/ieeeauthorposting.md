# IEEE Author Center: Post-Publication Policies

- Source: `ieeeauthorposting`, [original](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-journal-author/publishing-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/post-publication-policies/)
- Version: IEEE Author Center web page for journals, read on 2026-10-06. Locations give page subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Article Sharing and Posting Policies — Preprint
- Quote: "Authors who have submitted or plan to submit their articles to IEEE may retain their posted preprints in the following locations:" / "arXiv.org, TechRxiv.org, or any not-for-profit preprint server approved by the Publication Services and Products Board (PSPB)" / "IEEE does not consider this to be a form of prior publication."
- Key point: Before or after submitting to an IEEE journal, authors may keep their preprints on arXiv, TechRxiv, or other approved not-for-profit preprint servers; IEEE does not count this as prior publication.

### E2

- Location: Article Sharing and Posting Policies — Author-submitted article
- Quote: "Upon acceptance, the previously posted article must be replaced with either (1) the full citation to the IEEE work with the DOI, or (2) the accepted version of the article with the DOI and an appropriate copyright notice"
- Key point: Once the paper is accepted, the previously posted version must be replaced with the full citation and DOI, or with the accepted version with the DOI and a copyright notice.

### E3

- Location: Article Sharing and Posting Policies — Published article (VOR)
- Quote: "For articles that are not published under an open access license and that use the standard IEEE Copyright Form, the author may not post the VOR online"
- Key point: For papers that are not open access and use the standard IEEE Copyright Form, authors may not post IEEE's final published version (Version of Record, VOR) online.
