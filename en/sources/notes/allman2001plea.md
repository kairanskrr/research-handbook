# Allman 2001: A Referee's Plea

- Source: `allman2001plea`, [original](https://mallman.net/plea.txt)
- Version: short plain-text essay, written after the author served on networking conference program committees.

### E1

- Location: 2. Sloppy Papers Are Long Shots.
- Quote: "Sloppy papers give the indication of sloppy research."
- Key point: Sloppy papers give the impression of sloppy research.

### E2

- Location: 2. Sloppy Papers Are Long Shots.
- Quote: "Proofread your paper."
- Key point: Proofread before submitting.

### E3

- Location: 7. Answer All The Easy Questions.
- Quote: "If you need a constant for the foo algorithm and you define c=17, I want to know why you chose 17."
- Key point: Answer in the paper the simple questions a reviewer will immediately ask (such as why a parameter has a particular value).

### E4

- Location: 6. Don't Waste My Time III.
- Quote: "Roughly stick to the page limit and the requested format."
- Key point: Stick to the page limit and formatting requirements.
