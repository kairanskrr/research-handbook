# Sage: Music & Science submission guidelines

- Source: `sage2024musicscience`, [original](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/MNS) (an open access journal published by Sage; SEMPRE members get a discount)
- Version: Web page. The site returns 403 to scripts, so this excerpt comes from the Internet Archive snapshot of 2024-01-14 (http://web.archive.org/web/20240114200115/https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/MNS).

### E1

- Location: 4.7 Data
- Quote: "Music & Science requests authors to submit any primary data used in their research articles alongside their article submissions for publication in the online version of the journal, or to provide detailed information in their articles on how the data can be accessed." / "Examples of data types include but are not limited to statistical data files, replication code, text files, audio files, images, videos, appendices, and additional charts and graphs necessary to understand the original research."
- Key point: Authors are asked to submit primary data with the article or give detailed information in the article on how to access it; data include statistical data files, code for reproduction, text, audio, images, video, and so on.

### E2

- Location: 4.7 Data
- Quote: "In making your dataset available, please ensure that it is appropriately labelled (e.g., it should be easy to identify data pertaining to particular variables, etc.) in order that a reviewer or reader is in a position to replicate the analyses that you have undertaken."
- Key point: Label the data clearly so that a reviewer or reader can redo your analysis.

### E3

- Location: 4.1 Peer review policy
- Quote: "Music & Science follows a double-anonymous peer-review process through to the point of publication, at which point the reviewers have the option of remaining anonymous or being named. A minimum of two reviewers are selected to review a submission."
- Key point: Double-anonymous peer review, with at least two reviewers.

### E4

- Location: 4.8 Research ethics and participant consent
- Quote: "Authors are also required to state in the methods section whether participants provided informed consent and whether the consent was written or verbal"
- Key point: State in the methods section whether participants gave informed consent, and whether it was written or verbal.
