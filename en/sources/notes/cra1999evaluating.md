# Patterson, Snyder & Ullman 1999: Evaluating Computer Scientists and Engineers for Promotion and Tenure

- Source: `cra1999evaluating`, [Original](https://cra.org/resources/best-practice-memos/evaluating-computer-scientists-and-engineers-for-promotion-and-tenure/)
- Version: A "best practice memo" of the Computing Research Association (CRA, US), approved by the CRA Board in August 1999; web version read 2026-10-05. Locations give web page subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "conference publication is preferred in the field, and computational artifacts —software, chips, etc. —are a tangible means of conveying ideas and insight."
- Key point: In computing, conference publication is preferred, and artifacts such as software and chips are themselves a tangible way to convey ideas.

### E2

- Location: Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field
- Quote: "The reason conference publication is preferred to journal publication, at least for experimentalists, is the shorter time to print (7 months vs 1-2 years), the opportunity to describe the work before one’s peers at a public presentation, and the more complete level of review (4-5 evaluations per paper compared to 2-3 for an archival journal)"
- Key point: Why experimentalists preferred conferences (in 1999): faster publication (7 months vs. 1–2 years), the chance to present to peers in person, and more reviews per paper (4–5 vs. 2–3).

### E3

- Location: Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field
- Quote: "For experimentalists conference publication is preferred to journal publication, and the premier conferences are generally more selective than the premier journals"
- Key point: For experimentalists, conferences are preferred to journals, and the premier conferences are generally more selective than the premier journals.

### E4

- Location: Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field
- Quote: "Though conference publication is highly regarded in the theoretical community, there is a long tradition of completing, revising, and extending conference papers for submission and publication in archival journals."
- Key point: The theory community also values conferences highly, but has a long tradition of completing and extending conference papers for journal publication.

### E5

- Location: Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field
- Quote: "A key research tradition is to share artifacts with other researchers to the greatest extent possible."
- Key point: A key tradition in experimental research is to share artifacts (software, systems, etc.) with other researchers as fully as possible.

### E6

- Location: Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field (quoting Fred Brooks)
- Quote: "Since anyone can create something new [in a synthetic field], that alone does not establish a contribution. Rather, one must show that the creation is better."
- Key point: Computing is a synthetic field: creating something new does not by itself make a contribution; you must show the creation is better.

### E7

- Location: Computer Science and Engineering —Structure of The Field
- Quote: "Publication in the prestige conferences is inferior to the prestige journals only in having significant page limitations and little time to polish the paper."
- Key point: The only drawbacks of premier conferences compared with premier journals are strict page limits and little time to polish the paper.
