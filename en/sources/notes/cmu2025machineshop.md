# Carnegie Mellon University EHS: Machine Shop Safety - Guideline

- Source: `cmu2025machineshop`, [Original](https://www.cmu.edu/ehs/Workplace-Construction/documents/machine-shop-safety-policy.pdf)
- Version: Machine shop safety guidelines from the Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) office of Carnegie Mellon University (US), 15th edition, revised November 2025. Locations give section numbers. These are one university's rules only.

### E1

- Location: 4.j (student responsibilities)
- Quote: "Before working on machine equipment, students must be trained and demonstrate competency to the shop manager, shop staff and/or qualified faculty."
- Key point: Before using machine equipment, students must be trained and demonstrate competency to the shop manager, shop staff, or qualified faculty.

### E2

- Location: 5.a (training)
- Quote: "Have attended and successfully completed both: 1. The general shop safety course offered by EHS. 2. Specific shop and safety training for each type of machine they operate."
- Key point: Two kinds of training are required: the university's general shop safety course and specific training for each type of machine you operate.

### E3

- Location: 7.a
- Quote: "Always have a partner with you. At least two persons must be in the shop when machine equipment is being used"
- Key point: Never work alone; at least two people must be in the shop when machine equipment is in use.

### E4

- Location: 7.b.iii
- Quote: "Never wear hand protection when using tools with rotating elements or pinch points, such as drills and saws, due to the potential for getting fingers and hands caught in moving parts."
- Key point: Do not wear gloves when using tools with rotating parts or pinch points, such as drills and saws, because fingers and hands can get caught.

### E5

- Location: 7.c
- Quote: "You must ensure that safety guards are in place on moving parts before you start working."
- Key point: Make sure safety guards are in place on moving parts before you start.

### E6

- Location: 7.o
- Quote: "There are limits to what can be built in a given shop and in a given time. You can’t do it safety if you are in a hurry. If it cannot be done safely don’t start it."
- Key point: You can't work safely in a hurry; if a job cannot be done safely, don't start it.

### E7

- Location: 7.t
- Quote: "Ensure power is off, equipment is properly locked out, tagged and safety devices are in place."
- Key point: Before adjusting or repairing a machine, make sure the power is off, the equipment is locked out and tagged, and safety devices are in place.

### E8

- Location: 7.m
- Quote: "Attend all running machines. Never leave a machine to run unattended."
- Key point: Attend every running machine; never leave a machine running unattended.
