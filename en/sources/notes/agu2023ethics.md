# American Geophysical Union 2023: AGU Scientific Integrity and Professional Ethics

- Source: `agu2023ethics`, [original](https://www.agu.org/-/media/Files/Learn-About-AGU/AGU_Scientific_Integrity_and_Professional_Ethics_Policy_document.pdf)
- Version: Scientific Integrity and Professional Ethics Policy of the American Geophysical Union (AGU), 2023 updated PDF.

### E1

- Location: I. Executive Summary
- Quote: "The AGU Scientific Integrity and Professional Ethics Policy is a set of principles and practices for professional behavior regarding the practice, learning, training, publishing, and communication of science which governs all AGU members, staff, volunteers, and non-members participating in AGU sponsored programs and activities."
- Key point: The policy binds AGU members, staff, volunteers, and non-members taking part in AGU activities; it is a society's policy, not a regulation for the whole discipline.

### E2

- Location: II. Preamble
- Quote: "One of the goals of AGU is to educate and reinforce positive behavior in the scientific and learning environment, including the classroom, field settings, laboratory, industry, and professional meetings."
- Key point: AGU explicitly lists field settings, alongside classrooms, laboratories, and meetings, as places where positive behavior is expected.

### E3

- Location: III.D Harassment, Bullying, and Discrimination
- Quote: "We affirm that discrimination, harassment (including sexual harassment), or bullying in any scientific or learning environment is unacceptable, and constitutes scientific misconduct under the AGU Scientific Integrity and Professional Ethics Policy."
- Key point: Under AGU policy, discrimination, harassment (including sexual harassment), and bullying constitute scientific misconduct.

### E4

- Location: III.B Responsibilities, item 3, Research Methods
- Quote: "Members will employ research methods to the best of their understanding and ability, base conclusions on critical analysis of the evidence, and report findings and interpretations fully, accurately, and objectively, including characterization of uncertainties."
- Key point: AGU requires findings and interpretations to be reported fully, accurately, and objectively, including characterization of uncertainty.

### E5

- Location: III.B Responsibilities, item 5, Research Findings
- Quote: "Members will share data and findings openly and promptly, as soon as they have had an opportunity to establish intellectual property rights, if appropriate."
- Key point: AGU requires members to share data and findings openly and promptly.

### E6

- Location: III.B Responsibilities, item 15, Stewardship of the Earth
- Quote: "Members have an ethical obligation to responsibly, accurately, and clearly inform the public about natural resources, hazards, and other geoscience phenomena of importance to the well-being of Earth and society."
- Key point: AGU holds that members have an ethical obligation to inform the public responsibly, accurately, and clearly about natural resources, hazards, and other geoscience phenomena.

### E7

- Location: VI.C Ethical Obligations of Authors and Contributors, item 2
- Quote: "Include sufficient detail and reference to sources of information in a manuscript to permit the author's peers to repeat the work. If there are any limitations on use of or access to data, these must be clearly identified."
- Key point: A manuscript should include enough detail for peers to repeat the work; any limitations on the use of or access to data must be stated.

### E8

- Location: VI.C, item 4
- Quote: "Carefully document methodology, assumptions, and uncertainty."
- Key point: Carefully document methodology, assumptions, and uncertainty.

### E9

- Location: VI.C, item 10
- Quote: "Include as coauthors only those persons who have made significant scientific contributions to the work, and determine order of authorship in a manner appropriate to the contribution. Pay careful attention to inclusion and appropriate attribution of student work."
- Key point: Include as coauthors only those who made significant scientific contributions, order them appropriately, and pay particular attention to the authorship and attribution of students' work.

### E10

- Location: VI.E Ethical Obligations of Authors Publishing for the Public, item 2
- Quote: "Announce a discovery to the public only when the experimental, statistical, or theoretical support for it is of sufficient strength to warrant publication in the scientific literature."
- Key point: Announce a discovery to the public only when the evidence for it is strong enough to warrant publication in the scholarly literature.

### E11

- Location: V. Scientific Misconduct
- Quote: "Scientific misconduct does not include errors of judgment; honest errors in the recording, selection, or analysis of data; or differences in opinions involving the interpretation of data. Fabrication does not include documented use of modeling or statistical techniques."
- Key point: Honest errors and differences in interpretation are not misconduct; documented use of modeling or statistical methods is not fabrication.

### E12

- Location: VI.A Overview
- Quote: "In Earth and space science, the growth of global collaborations, the public immediacy of many research results, and the implications for individuals and societies result in major ethical responsibilities for Editors, authors, reviewers, and the AGU Board and Council."
- Key point: AGU holds that the growth of global collaborations and the direct public relevance of many results give editors, authors, and reviewers in Earth and space science significant ethical responsibilities.

### E13

- Location: VI.B Ethical Obligations of Editors, item 6
- Quote: "Results that are at variance with the dominant paradigm, as well as null results, should be given full and equal consideration based upon the criteria of importance, originality, clarity, and relevance."
- Key point: AGU requires editors to give results at variance with the dominant paradigm, as well as null results, full and equal consideration.
