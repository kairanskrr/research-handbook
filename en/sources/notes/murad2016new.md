# Murad et al. 2016: New evidence pyramid

- Source: `murad2016new`, [Evidence-Based Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1136/ebmed-2016-110401), [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4975798/) (CC BY-NC 4.0)
- Version: Evid Based Med 2016;21(4):125–127.

### E1

- Location: main text, paragraph 2
- Quote: "Various versions of the evidence pyramid have been described, but all of them focused on showing weaker study designs in the bottom (basic science and case series), followed by case–control and cohort studies in the middle, then randomised controlled trials (RCTs), and at the very top, systematic reviews and meta-analysis."
- Key point: The traditional evidence pyramid: basic science and case series at the bottom, case-control and cohort studies in the middle, randomized controlled trials (RCTs) above them, and systematic reviews and meta-analyses at the top.

### E2

- Location: Rationale for modification 1
- Quote: "Study design alone appears to be insufficient on its own as a surrogate for risk of bias. Certain methodological limitations of a study, imprecision, inconsistency and indirectness, were factors independent from study design and can affect the quality of evidence derived from any study design."
- Key point: Study design alone is not a sufficient surrogate for risk of bias; methodological limitations, imprecision, inconsistency, and indirectness are independent of design and affect the quality of evidence from any design.

### E3

- Location: Rationale for modification 1
- Quote: "Hence, despite the fact of having five RCTs, such evidence should not be rated high in any pyramid. The quality of evidence can also be rated up."
- Key point: Even five RCTs should not be rated high-quality evidence if they have methodological flaws; conversely, the quality of evidence can also be rated up.

### E4

- Location: Rationale for modification 1
- Quote: "we are quite certain about the benefits of hip replacement in a patient with disabling hip osteoarthritis. Although not tested in RCTs, the quality of this evidence is rated up despite the study design (non-randomised observational studies)."
- Key point: The benefits of hip replacement have not been tested in RCTs, yet the quality of this evidence is rated up.

### E5

- Location: Rationale for modification 2
- Quote: "In other words, a meta-analysis of well-conducted RCTs at low risk of bias cannot be equated with a meta-analysis of observational studies at higher risk of bias."
- Key point: A meta-analysis of RCTs at low risk of bias cannot be equated with a meta-analysis of observational studies at higher risk of bias.

### E6

- Location: Rationale for modification 2
- Quote: "Therefore, the second modification to the pyramid is to remove systematic reviews from the top of the pyramid and use them as a lens through which other types of studies should be seen (ie, appraised and applied)."
- Key point: The authors propose removing systematic reviews from the top of the pyramid and using them as a "lens" through which other studies are examined and applied.

### E7

- Location: Rationale for modification 2
- Quote: "The systematic review (the process of selecting the studies) and meta-analysis (the statistical aggregation that produces a single effect size) are tools to consume and apply the evidence by stakeholders."
- Key point: The systematic review is the process of selecting studies; the meta-analysis is the statistical aggregation of their results into a single effect size.
