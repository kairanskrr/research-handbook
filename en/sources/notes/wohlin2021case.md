# Wohlin 2021: Case Study Research in Software Engineering — It is a Case, and it is a Study, but is it a Case Study?

- Source: `wohlin2021case`, [original](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infsof.2021.106514) (open access, CC BY 4.0)
- Version: Information and Software Technology 133:106514, 2021. We read the publisher's version, taken from [DiVA](https://bth.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1527888/FULLTEXT01.pdf), the institutional repository of Blekinge Institute of Technology.
- Note: The author examined the 100 most recent papers calling themselves "case studies" in 7 software engineering journals and judged whether they met the definition of a case study.

### E1

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "Close to 50% of the articles investigated were judged as not being case studies according to the definition of a case study."
- Key point: Close to half of the papers examined were judged not to be case studies by the definition.

### E2

- Location: §1 Introduction
- Quote: "Studies of only historical information, for example data or software, in any form does not meet the definitions of a case study."
- Key point: The author holds that studies of only historical information (such as existing data or software) do not meet the definition of a case study.

### E3

- Location: §4 Results
- Quote: "Typical examples claiming to be case studies, but not meeting the definitions are: (1) Evaluations or illustrations on a limited scale, for example, in a laboratory. These could be labelled evaluations or illustrations given their primary objective, and (2) Studies of existing information in open source repositories, defect databases of other database sources. These ought to be labelled as archival analysis."
- Key point: Typical papers that call themselves case studies without meeting the definition: (1) small-scale evaluations or illustrations (for example, in a laboratory), which should be labeled evaluations or illustrations; (2) studies of existing information such as open source repositories or bug databases, which should be labeled archival analysis.

### E4

- Location: §4 Results
- Quote: "Finally, if the investigator participates in the case studied, then it ought to be labelled as action research"
- Key point: When the investigator participates in the case studied, it should be labeled action research.

### E5

- Location: §6 Conclusions
- Quote: "A case study is an empirical investigation of a case, using multiple data collection methods, to study a contemporary phenomenon in its real-life context, and with the investigator(s) not taking an active role in the case investigated."
- Key point: The author's proposed definition: a case study is an empirical investigation of a case, using multiple data collection methods, to study a contemporary phenomenon in its real-life context, with the investigator not taking an active role in the case.

### E6

- Location: §6 Conclusions
- Quote: "We have also emphasised multiple data collection methods to clarify that triangulation is essential. [...] To interview more than one person does not make it a case study."
- Key point: The author stresses multiple data collection methods because triangulation is essential; interviewing more than one person does not make a study a case study.

### E7

- Location: §6 Conclusions
- Quote: "In particular, researchers should provide a reference to the definition used."
- Key point: Researchers should cite the definition they use.
