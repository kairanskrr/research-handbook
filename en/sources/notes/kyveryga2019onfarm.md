# Kyveryga 2019: On-Farm Research: Experimental Approaches, Analytical Frameworks, Case Studies, and Impact

- Source: `kyveryga2019onfarm`, [full text](https://doi.org/10.2134/agronj2019.11.0001)
- Version: Agronomy Journal 111(6), 2019, introduction to a special issue on on-farm research, open access under CC BY-NC-ND. Text taken from the PDF hosted on the website of the author's organization, the Iowa Soybean Association. Locations give the subheading.

### E1

- Location: Precision Agriculture Technology Tools and Farmer Motivation through On-Farm Research
- Quote: "On-farm research has expanded rapidly during the last two decades, especially in developed countries. This increase is attributed to an increase in the wider use of precision agriculture (PA) technologies."
- Key point: On-farm research has expanded rapidly over the last two decades, especially in developed countries; the author attributes this to the wider use of precision agriculture technologies.

### E2

- Location: Precision Agriculture Technology Tools and Farmer Motivation through On-Farm Research
- Quote: "Technologies in the PA tool box such as yield monitoring, Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS), light bars, auto guidance, and variable rate devices enable farmers and researchers to conduct on-farm experiments at a field scale, in multiple locations and at much lower cost."
- Key point: Tools such as yield monitoring, satellite navigation (GNSS), auto guidance, and variable-rate devices let farmers and researchers run on-farm trials at whole-field scale, at many locations, and at much lower cost.

### E3

- Location: Precision Agriculture Technology Tools and Farmer Motivation through On-Farm Research
- Quote: "In-depth interviews of 40 participants of the Nebraska On-Farm Research Network and qualitive summaries of the survey data revealed that farmers conducted on-farm trials mainly because of the potential to increase profit, general curiosity and the need for unbiased results."
- Key point: In one study in the special issue, in-depth interviews with 40 participants of the Nebraska On-Farm Research Network showed that farmers ran on-farm research mainly to increase profit, out of curiosity, and to get unbiased results.

### E4

- Location: Precision Agriculture Technology Tools and Farmer Motivation through On-Farm Research
- Quote: "farmers expressed the desire to be more involved in all phases of conducting an on-farm research project."
- Key point: In the same study, farmers said they wanted to be more involved in all phases of an on-farm research project.

### E5

- Location: Analytical Approaches for On-Farm Trials
- Quote: "One of the critical components of on-farm research is to effectively analyze and summarize study outcomes and communicate results to farmers and agronomists."
- Key point: One critical component of on-farm research is to analyze and summarize the outcomes well and communicate the results to farmers and agronomists.
