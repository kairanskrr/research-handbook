# Bailey et al. 2012: The Menlo Report

- Source: `bailey2012menlo`, local PDF `papers/pdf/bailey2012menlo.pdf`
- Version: IEEE Security & Privacy 10(2): 71-75, 2012. Introduces the Menlo Report, ethical principles for information and communication technology (ICT) research developed under the US Department of Homeland Security.

### E1

- Location: start of p. 71
- Quote: "has exposed gaps between what researchers could do and what they should do, thereby creating ethical challenges"
- Key point: Technological progress has exposed gaps between what researchers could do and what they should do, creating ethical challenges.

### E2

- Location: p. 72
- Quote: "four core ethical principles: three from the original Belmont Report—respect for persons, beneficence, and justice—and an additional principle—respect for law and public interest"
- Key point: Four core ethical principles: respect for persons, beneficence, and justice from the Belmont Report, plus respect for law and public interest.

### E3

- Location: pp. 72-73, respect for persons
- Quote: "research subject participation is voluntary and follows from informed consent" / "the interests of individuals who are impacted by but not targets of research are guarded"
- Key point: Respect for persons: participation is voluntary and follows from informed consent, and the interests of people who are affected by the research but are not its targets are protected.

### E4

- Location: p. 72, beneficence
- Quote: "researchers should avoid harm, maximize probable benefits and minimize probable harms, and systematically assess both risk of harm and benefit."
- Key point: Beneficence: avoid harm, maximize probable benefits and minimize probable harms, and systematically assess both risks and benefits.

### E5

- Location: p. 73, justice
- Quote: "subject selection should be fair, and burdens should be allocated equitably across impacted subjects."
- Key point: Justice: select subjects fairly and allocate burdens equitably among those affected.

### E6

- Location: p. 73, respect for law and public interest
- Quote: "explicitly charges researchers to" / "engage in legal due diligence" / "be transparent in methods and results" / "be accountable for actions"
- Key point: Respect for law and public interest: do legal due diligence, be transparent in methods and results, and be accountable for your actions.

### E7

- Location: p. 74, reference 3
- Quote: "Belmont Report: Ethical Principles and Guidelines for the Protection of Human Subjects of Research"
- Key point: Full title of the Belmont Report: Ethical Principles and Guidelines for the Protection of Human Subjects of Research.
