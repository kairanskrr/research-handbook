# Good 2018: Reflections on the scope of language documentation

- Source: `good2018scope`, [Original](http://hdl.handle.net/10125/24804)
- Version: Language Documentation & Conservation Special Publication 15, pp. 13–21, open access; the author is a linguist at the University at Buffalo, US

### E1

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "Language documentation is understood as the creation, annotation, preservation, and dissemination of transparent records of a language."
- Key point: Language documentation is the creation, annotation, preservation, and dissemination of transparent records of a language.

### E2

- Location: §1
- Quote: "with the collection of naturalistic recordings of underdescribed languages, accompanied by metadata and annotations consisting of time-aligned transcriptions, translations, and morphological analysis, forming the core of most documentary projects."
- Key point: At the core of most documentation projects are naturalistic recordings plus metadata and annotations: time-aligned transcriptions, translations, and morphological analysis.

### E3

- Location: §2.1
- Quote: "can inadvertently lead to the adoption of an assemblyline approach to the task of documentation that is ill suited to local contexts"
- Key point: Treating documentation as an assembly line (record so many hours, transcribe, archive, done) is ill suited to local community contexts.
