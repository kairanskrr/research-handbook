# Chartered Institute for Archaeologists 2020: Standard and guidance for the creation, compilation, transfer and deposition of archaeological archives

- Source: `cifa2020archives`, [PDF](https://www.archaeologists.net/sites/default/files/2023-11/CIfA-SandG-Archaeological-Archives-2020.pdf)
- Version: CIfA's standard and guidance for archaeological archives, first issued December 2014 and updated October 2020. It states that it covers work in the United Kingdom and the Isle of Man. Locations give section numbers.

### E1

- Location: Definition of archaeological archives
- Quote: "The archaeological archive is defined as all parts of the archaeological record, including the finds, samples and digital records as well as the written, drawn and photographic documentation."
- Key point: The archaeological archive covers every part of the archaeological record: finds, samples, digital records, and written, drawn, and photographic documentation.

### E2

- Location: Purpose of archaeological archives
- Quote: "The archive from an archaeological project is the key to understanding any published interpretations of the results."
- Key point: A project's archive is the key to understanding any published interpretation of its results.

### E3

- Location: 3.1.1
- Quote: "A project has not been completed until the archive has been transferred successfully and is fully accessible for consultation"
- Key point: A project is not complete until the archive has been transferred successfully and is fully accessible.

### E4

- Location: 3.3.1, 3.3.2
- Quote: "Project specifications, research designs or similar documents should include a project-specific Selection Strategy and a Data Management Plan." / "Project specifications, research designs or similar documents should identify the repository where the archive will be deposited for long-term curation."
- Key point: Project documents must include a project-specific selection strategy and data management plan, and name the repository that will eventually hold the archive.

### E5

- Location: 3.4.3
- Quote: "A consistent, well-documented terminology should be used in making all paper and digital records."
- Key point: All paper and digital records should use consistent, well-documented terminology.

### E6

- Location: 3.4.4
- Quote: "All drawings and photographs should be marked with the project identifier, and where relevant have a key to identify the subject, scale, location and orientation."
- Key point: All drawings and photographs should be marked with the project identifier and, where relevant, a key to subject, scale, location, and orientation.

### E7

- Location: 3.4.7
- Quote: "Security copies of all digital material should be created and managed as appropriate."
- Key point: Security copies of all digital material should be made.

### E8

- Location: 3.4.8
- Quote: "Finds and other materials should be collected, sorted, cleaned, marked or labelled, recorded and packed in accordance with recognised standards."
- Key point: Finds should be collected, sorted, cleaned, marked, recorded, and packed according to recognized standards.

### E9

- Location: 3.4.15
- Quote: "Project reports (including grey literature and OASIS and other on-line entries) and publications must identify the archive repository and provide the accession number issued by the repository or, if this is not available, the site code."
- Key point: Project reports (including grey literature and online entries) and publications must name the archive repository and give its accession number, or the site code if there is no accession number.

### E10

- Location: 3.8.1
- Quote: "The proposed recipient museum or other approved repository must be contacted at the project planning stage and arrangements for deposition of the material archive should be detailed in the specification and/or project design."
- Key point: The receiving museum or repository must be contacted at the project planning stage, and arrangements for depositing the material archive must be set out in the specification or project design.

### E11

- Location: 3.8.2
- Quote: "In England, Wales, Northern Ireland and the Isle of Man ownership of objects rests with the landowner, except where other law overrides."
- Key point: In England, Wales, Northern Ireland, and the Isle of Man, finds generally belong to the landowner unless other law overrides this.

### E12

- Location: 1 Introduction
- Quote: "This document provides guidance for work carried out in the United Kingdom and Isle of Man, although the principles will apply elsewhere."
- Key point: This document covers the United Kingdom and the Isle of Man, but its principles apply elsewhere too.

### E13

- Location: 3.4.14
- Quote: "Health and Safety takes priority over archaeological requirements."
- Key point: Health and safety takes priority over archaeological requirements.
