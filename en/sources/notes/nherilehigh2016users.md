# NHERI Lehigh 2016: RTMD Experimental Facility User's Guide

- Source: `nherilehigh2016users`, [original](https://lehigh.designsafe-ci.org/media/cms_page_media/95/NHERI%20Lehigh%20RTMD%20EF%20User's%20Guide%202016-11-02_YsuNvbw.pdf)
- Version: user's guide, version of November 2, 2016. The Real-Time Multi-Directional (RTMD) experimental facility at Lehigh University, part of the Natural Hazards Engineering Research Infrastructure (NHERI) funded by the US National Science Foundation, runs large-scale structural tests and hybrid simulations.

### E1

- Location: Preface (paragraph 1)
- Quote: "The types of laboratory simulations and tests enabled by the Lehigh EF include: (1) hybrid simulation (HS) which combines large-scale physical models with computer-based numerical simulation models"
- Key point: One type of test the facility supports is hybrid simulation, which combines large-scale physical models with numerical computer models.

### E2

- Location: §1.6 Configuring an Experiment
- Quote: "All testing proposed by the researchers will need to be reviewed by the RTMD staff prior to testing to ensure the testing protocol does not present a risk to the safety of laboratory staff and researchers of any potential for damaging the equipment." / "The researchers must also include a statement as to what is the expected damage that will occur to the test specimen during each test. No testing will commence until either the Lehigh Equipment Site PI, Co-PI, or Research Scientist of the RTMD has approved in writing the test matrix."
- Key point: All tests proposed by researchers must be reviewed by facility staff beforehand to make sure they don't endanger people or damage equipment; researchers must state what damage they expect the specimen to suffer in each test; testing may not start until the test matrix is approved in writing.

### E3

- Location: §1.6 Configuring an Experiment
- Quote: "No deviation from the test matrix is permitted without the approval of either the NHERI Lehigh EF PI, Co-PI, or RTMD Research Scientist/Engineer."
- Key point: No deviation from the approved test matrix is permitted without approval.

### E4

- Location: §1.6 Configuring an Experiment
- Quote: "Control programs are developed and validated through hydraulics off simulations with the RTMD IT Manager." / "All aspects are tested in a safe manner before any experiment is conducted."
- Key point: Control programs are first developed and validated in hydraulics-off simulation; before the actual test, every aspect is tested in a safe manner.

### E5

- Location: §2.7 Software Policies
- Quote: "software used for any form of testing at the NHERI Lehigh RTMD EF must be validated before placed on the Real-Time Integrated Control System. The algorithms which the software is based on must be shown to be stable."
- Key point: Any software used for testing must be validated before it is placed on the real-time control system, and the algorithms it relies on must be shown to be stable.

### E6

- Location: §1.9.1 Data Description
- Quote: "The researchers will be given training and assistance by the NHERI Lehigh EF IT Systems Manager, but will responsible for uploading all of their data, including: the metadata that describes the experimental setup report; unprocessed experimental data; converted experimental data"
- Key point: Researchers are responsible for uploading all their data, including metadata describing the test setup, raw unprocessed test data, and converted data.

### E7

- Location: §1.9.1 Data Description
- Quote: "All data will be made available raw and uncorrected, as well as in converted forms in engineering units." / "the data will be documented to enable a qualified researcher to reproduce the simulation or test and generate the derived data throughout the lifetime of data repository."
- Key point: Data are made available both raw and uncorrected and converted into engineering units; they are documented well enough for a qualified researcher to rerun the simulation or test and regenerate the derived data.

### E8

- Location: §1.11 Payload Project Protocol
- Quote: "All ongoing and newly funded projects at the NHERI Lehigh Experimental Facility are posted on the site's website to enable researchers to identify potential payload project opportunities."
- Key point: The facility's website lists all ongoing and newly funded projects so that other researchers can find "payload project" opportunities (adding their own research to someone else's test).

### E9

- Location: §5.1 Guidelines for Proposal Preparation
- Quote: "Lehigh's site also recommends that contact be made early in the proposal process with NHERI Lehigh's RTMD EF Operations Manager, in order to aid in planning, scheduling, cost development, etc."
- Key point: Contact the facility's operations manager early in the proposal process for help with planning, scheduling, and cost estimates.

### E10

- Location: §5.3 Required Documentation
- Quote: "the following two primary documents were required for completion prior to the onset of any laboratory activity for an awarded NEES research project from 2004 through 2014." / "Indemnification  Insurance  Payment terms  Termination terms  Intellectual Property rights  Publication rights" / "As of the drafting of this document (4/1/16), there is no research participation agreement template or equipment site policies compliance check document required by NHERI."
- Key point: From 2004 to 2014, funded projects of the Network for Earthquake Engineering Simulation (NEES) had to complete two documents before any laboratory work began; one, the research participation agreement, covered indemnification, insurance, payment, termination, intellectual property, and publication rights. As of April 2016, when this guide was drafted, NHERI did not require such documents (the Lehigh facility reserved the right to introduce them before a project starts).

### E11

- Location: §5.4 Training
- Quote: "A formal on-site training program must be completed satisfactorily prior to any use of NHERI or non-NHERI equipment, including utilization of all ATLSS laboratory equipment."
- Key point: Formal on-site training must be completed before using any equipment.

### E12

- Location: §5.5 Experiment Execution
- Quote: "As a safety precaution, during testing, researchers will be asked to refrain from entering the test area."
- Key point: As a safety precaution, researchers may not enter the test area during testing.
