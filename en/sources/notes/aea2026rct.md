# AEA RCT Registry: About, Registration Guidelines, and FAQ

- Source: `aea2026rct`, [About the AEA RCT Registry](https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/policies/rct-registry); [registration instructions](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/site/instructions); [FAQ](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/site/faq); [home page](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/)
- Version: web pages accessed October 6, 2026.

### E1

- Location: About the AEA RCT Registry
- Quote: "It is the policy of the AEA journals that all work involving field experiments must be registered prior to submission for publication. (Laboratory experiments do not need to be registered at this time.) Researchers may register during any phase of the trial—forthcoming, ongoing, or completed—but are encouraged to register early."
- Key point: AEA journals require all field experiments to be registered before submission; laboratory experiments are currently exempt. Trials can be registered at any stage, but early registration is encouraged.

### E2

- Location: About the AEA RCT Registry
- Quote: "Registration is free and requires a minimal amount of information, allowing for amendments and additional material, such as reports, articles, data, and code, to be provided at any point during the life of the study. Version history is maintained."
- Key point: Registration is free and requires minimal information; it can be amended and supplemented at any time, and the revision history is kept.

### E3

- Location: About the AEA RCT Registry
- Quote: "Select fields can be hidden/embargoed until the trial is completed so as to not influence survey respondents while the survey is in the field."
- Key point: Some fields can be hidden until the trial is completed, so as not to influence respondents while the survey is in the field.

### E4

- Location: Registration Guidelines, Required Information
- Quote: "To register a trial, you must complete the following fields:" / "Outcomes (End Points)" / "Experimental Design (Public)" / "Planned Number of Observations"
- Key point: Required fields include outcomes, experimental design, planned number of observations, and others.

### E5

- Location: Registration Guidelines
- Quote: "When a trial is registered before the start of its intervention, it is marked as a pre-registered trial with an orange clock icon."
- Key point: Trials registered before the intervention starts are marked as "pre-registered."

### E6

- Location: Registration Guidelines, Registration Review Criteria
- Quote: "The criteria is not intended to review the quality of the research, nor the ethics of the research."
- Key point: The registry's review of registrations does not assess the quality of the research or its ethics.

### E7

- Location: home page
- Quote: "AEA RCT Registry currently lists 12869 studies with locations in 172 countries."
- Key point: At the time of access, the registry listed 12,869 studies in 172 countries.
