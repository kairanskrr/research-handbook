# Eisner 2010: Write the Paper First

- Source: `eisnerfirst`, [original](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~jason/advice/write-the-paper-first.html)
- Version: Web page, dated 2010

### E1

- Location: Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — What needs to be done?
- Quote: "Writing the literature review will help you design your experiments. It may influence what datasets you use, what comparisons you do, and what you are trying to prove you can do that other people can't."
- Key point: Writing the literature review helps you design your experiments: what data to use, what to compare against, and what you are trying to show you can do that others cannot.

### E2

- Location: Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — What needs to be done?
- Quote: "However, don't write the lit review first. Write out your own ideas before comparing them with the literature. This increases the chance that you'll find new angles on the problem."
- Key point: But do not write the literature review first; write out your own ideas before comparing them with the literature, which makes you more likely to find a new angle.

### E3

- Location: Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — Is it a good topic?
- Quote: "Show people a draft of the first 2-3 pages before you do any experiments. If they tell you that your basic idea will evoke skepticism or yawns from reviewers, it's better to know that before you waste your time."
- Key point: Before running any experiments, show people a draft of the first 2–3 pages; if they think the basic idea will evoke skepticism or yawns from reviewers, better to know before you waste time.

### E4

- Location: Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — What needs to be done?
- Quote: "Assuming the idea is indeed a good one, then writing a draft makes you sharpen the message of the paper. Then you can figure out what work needs to be done to support exactly that message."
- Key point: Writing a draft forces you to sharpen the message of the paper, and then you can work out what needs to be done to support it.

### E5

- Location: Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — What needs to be done?
- Quote: "Writing the experimental section is possible even before you've done the experiments. Explain the full experimental design. Make empty result tables with row and column headers and explanatory captions."
- Key point: You can write the experimental section before running the experiments: explain the full experimental design and make empty result tables with row and column headers and explanatory captions.

### E6

- Location: Writing is a mechanism for planning what to work on — What needs to be done?
- Quote: "Honest writing may lead you to realize that proving your point requires more work than you'd thought (which is why you'd better write early)."
- Key point: Honest writing may make you realize that proving your point takes more work than you thought, which is why you should write early.

### E7

- Location: The document is a focus for discussion with others
- Quote: "By showing others the draft at this stage, you'll get important feedback before you invest time in the "wrong" work."
- Key point: Showing others the draft at this stage gets you important feedback before you invest time in the "wrong" work.

### E8

- Location: The document is like a code specification
- Quote: "Of course, neither coding nor research is purely top-down—in practice, there's feedback. Just as coding will make you rethink parts of the spec, certainly experimentation will make you revise parts of the paper."
- Key point: Research is not purely top-down; experiments will make you revise the draft, and the plan should be updated accordingly.

### E9

- Location: Writing is the best use of limited time / Clear writing increases your odds of acceptance
- Quote: "Clear motivation and exposition are more important than results for getting your paper accepted."
- Key point: Eisner argues that clear motivation and exposition matter more than results for getting a paper accepted; a confusing paper is fatal, a few missing experiments are not.

### E10

- Location: The document is a focus for discussion with others
- Quote: "Your draft can describe your motivation, formal problem, model, algorithms, and experiments before you actually build anything."
- Key point: Before you build anything, the draft can already describe the motivation, the formal problem, the model, the algorithms, and the experiments, and you can show it to others for feedback.
