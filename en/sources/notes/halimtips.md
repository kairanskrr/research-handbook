# Halim: Prof Halim's PhD Tips

- Source: `halimtips`, [Original](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~stevenha/myresearch/phd_tips.html)
- Version: Web page on the author's website, continually updated (including 2024 revisions), accessed 2026-10-05. Locations are given as page subheadings.

### E1

- Location: The Advantages of Taking Graduate Studies
- Quote: "However, always be careful as leaking too much ideas (pro-tip: discuss your *already published* paper(s) instead) can jeopardize your own PhD"
- Key point: Collaborating with outside researchers is usually good, but be careful not to leak too many unpublished ideas; prefer discussing your already published papers.

### E2

- Location: The Advantages of Taking Graduate Studies
- Quote: "If your 'collaborators' decide not to acknowledge you as the original inventor of that idea, then you lost an idea, or even a PhD thesis because it is already taken by your 'collaborators'..."
- Key point: If your "collaborators" do not acknowledge you as the originator of an idea, you may lose the idea, or even your PhD thesis topic.

### E3

- Location: Expected Soft Skills
- Quote: "Frequent contact with your advisor will help to reduce the problems around this issue."
- Key point: When planning research, avoid going in the wrong direction and wasting time; frequent contact with your advisor helps reduce these problems.

### E4

- Location: Miscellaneous Things
- Quote: "Typically, your advisor (especially if one has high status in the department) is very busy and thus, their time is precious. [...] Make sure to use the contact time as optimally as you can."
- Key point: Advisors are usually very busy, so make the best use of your meeting time with them.
