# IEEE 2026: IEEE Publication Services and Products Board Operations Manual

- Source: `ieee2026pspb`, [original](https://pspb.ieee.org/images/files/files/opsmanual.pdf)
- Version: Operations manual of the IEEE Publication Services and Products Board (PSPB), revised 25 June 2026, PDF downloaded 2026-10-06. Section 2.7 reproduces the full IEEE Code of Ethics (IEEE Policies 7.8, June 2020 version). Locations give section and page numbers.

### E1

- Location: §2.7, reproducing item 1 of the IEEE Code of Ethics (IEEE Policies 7.8), pp. 23-24
- Quote: "to hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public, to strive to comply with ethical design and sustainable development practices, to protect the privacy of others, and to disclose promptly factors that might endanger the public or the environment;"
- Key point: Item 1 of the IEEE Code of Ethics: hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public; comply with ethical design; protect the privacy of others; and promptly disclose factors that might endanger the public or the environment.

### E2

- Location: §2.7, item 3 of the IEEE Code of Ethics, p. 24
- Quote: "to avoid real or perceived conflicts of interest whenever possible, and to disclose them to affected parties when they do exist;"
- Key point: Avoid real or perceived conflicts of interest whenever possible, and disclose them to affected parties when they do exist.

### E3

- Location: §2.7, item 5 of the IEEE Code of Ethics, p. 24
- Quote: "to seek, accept, and offer honest criticism of technical work, to acknowledge and correct errors, to be honest and realistic in stating claims or estimates based on available data, and to credit properly the contributions of others;"
- Key point: Seek, accept, and offer honest technical criticism; acknowledge and correct errors; be honest and realistic in stating claims or estimates based on available data; and properly credit the contributions of others.

### E4

- Location: §8.1.7.F, p. 91
- Quote: "it is common in technical publishing for material to be presented at various stages of its evolution, such as publishing early ideas in a workshop or as a draft article posted as a Preprint (see Subsection 8.1.9.A.2.d), more developed work in a conference, and fully developed contributions as journal or transactions articles."
- Key point: IEEE accepts "evolutionary publication": early ideas in a workshop or preprint, more developed work at a conference, and fully mature results in a journal or Transactions.

### E5

- Location: §8.1.7.F, pp. 91–92
- Quote: "IEEE recognizes the importance of this evolutionary publication process as a significant means of scientific communication and fully supports this publishing paradigm, but IEEE requires that this evolutionary process be fully referenced." / "all the articles published in the periodical contain substantial additional technical material with respect to the conference article or articles of which they represent an evolution."
- Key point: IEEE supports developing conference papers into journal articles but requires full citation of earlier versions, and the journal version must add substantial technical content beyond the conference paper.

### E6

- Location: §8.2.1.A.1, p. 104
- Quote: "Made a significant intellectual contribution to the theoretical development, system or experimental design, prototype development, and/or the analysis and interpretation of data associated with the work contained in the article;"
- Key point: One IEEE authorship condition: a significant intellectual contribution to at least one of theoretical development, system or experimental design, prototype development, or data analysis and interpretation. Prototype development is explicitly listed as a contribution that can qualify for authorship.

### E7

- Location: §8.2.1.A.1, p. 104
- Quote: "Contributed to drafting the article or reviewing and/or revising it for intellectual content; and" / "Approved the final version of the article as accepted for publication, including references."
- Key point: Authorship also requires contributing to drafting or revising the article for intellectual content, and approving the final accepted version (including references). The conditions must all be met.

### E8

- Location: §8.2.1.A.2, p. 105
- Quote: "In articles with multiple authorship, the order of the authors shall be at the discretion of the authors."
- Key point: IEEE sets no rule for author order; the authors decide.

### E9

- Location: §8.2.1.B.3, p. 106
- Quote: "Methods and materials should be described in sufficient detail to permit evaluation and replication."
- Key point: Describe methods and materials in enough detail for others to evaluate and replicate the work.

### E10

- Location: §8.2.1.B.2, p. 106
- Quote: "Financial support of the work being reported and of the authors should be clearly acknowledged in the article, as well as any potential conflict of interest."
- Key point: State clearly in the article the sources of funding and any potential conflicts of interest.

### E11

- Location: §8.2.1.B.9, p. 106
- Quote: "IEEE defines plagiarism as the use of someone else's work (for example, use of text, prior ideas, processes, results, or algorithms) without explicitly acknowledging the original author and/or source."
- Key point: IEEE defines plagiarism as using someone else's text, ideas, processes, results, or algorithms without explicitly acknowledging the original author or source.

### E12

- Location: §8.2.1.B.12, p. 107
- Quote: "If authors have used their own previously published work(s) as a basis for a new submission, they are required to cite the previous work(s) and very briefly indicate how the new submission offers substantive novel contributions beyond those of the previously published work(s)."
- Key point: When a new submission builds on your own published work, you must cite the earlier work and briefly state what substantive new contributions the new submission makes.

### E13

- Location: §8.2.1.B.16, p. 107
- Quote: "An author, in offering an article for presentation at an IEEE conference, or accepting an invitation to present an article, is expected to be present at the meeting to deliver the article." / "IEEE reserves the right to exclude an article from distribution after the conference (e.g., not placed on IEEE Xplore) if the article is not presented at the conference."
- Key point: IEEE expects an author to present a conference paper in person; IEEE may exclude papers not presented from post-conference distribution (e.g., from the IEEE Xplore database).

### E14

- Location: §8.2.1.B.8, p. 106
- Quote: "Authors have an obligation to correct errors promptly."
- Key point: Authors are obliged to correct errors promptly.

### E15

- Location: §4.3.7 TechRxiv Editorial Advisory Board Charter, p. 58
- Quote: "The TechRxiv Editorial Advisory Board is an organizational unit of, and reports to, PSPB." / "is an open, moderated preprint server for unpublished research in general areas of engineering technology and practice, particularly as they relate to the IEEE Field of Interest."
- Key point: TechRxiv is an open preprint server run by IEEE for unpublished research in engineering and technology.
