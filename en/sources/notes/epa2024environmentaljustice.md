# US Environmental Protection Agency: Environmental Justice web page (2024 snapshot)

- Source: `epa2024environmentaljustice`, [original (Internet Archive snapshot of 2024-05-31)](http://web.archive.org/web/20240531090447/https://www.epa.gov/environmentaljustice)
- Version: Snapshot of the EPA environmental justice home page from May 31, 2024. When this project visited the original URL in October 2026 it returned 404 (the page had been taken down), so it can only be read via the snapshot. The definition on the page comes from US federal policy at the time; US policy has changed since.

### E1

- Location: main text, definition paragraph
- Quote: "“Environmental justice” means the just treatment and meaningful involvement of all people, regardless of income, race, color, national origin, Tribal affiliation, or disability, in agency decision-making and other Federal activities that affect human health and the environment"
- Key point: Environmental justice means the just treatment and meaningful involvement of all people, regardless of income, race, color, national origin, Tribal affiliation, or disability, in government decision-making and federal activities that affect human health and the environment.

### E2

- Location: main text, definition paragraph
- Quote: "are fully protected from disproportionate and adverse human health and environmental effects (including risks) and hazards, including those related to climate change, the cumulative impacts of environmental and other burdens"
- Key point: Part of the definition: people are fully protected from disproportionate and adverse human health and environmental effects, including the cumulative impacts of multiple burdens.
