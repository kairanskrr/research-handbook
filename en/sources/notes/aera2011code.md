# American Educational Research Association 2011: Code of Ethics

- Source: `aera2011code`, [original PDF](https://www.aera.net/Portals/38/docs/About_AERA/CodeOfEthics(1).pdf)
- Version: Code of Ethics of the American Educational Research Association (AERA), approved by the AERA Council in February 2011. Locations give standard numbers.

### E1

- Location: Preamble
- Quote: "It has as its primary goal the welfare and protection of the individuals and groups with whom education researchers work."
- Key point: The code's primary goal is the welfare and protection of the individuals and groups with whom education researchers work.

### E2

- Location: 13.04 Informed Consent With Children, (a)
- Quote: "In undertaking research with children, education researchers obtain the consent of a parent or a legally authorized guardian."
- Key point: In research with children, obtain the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

### E3

- Location: 13.04 (b)
- Quote: "In undertaking research with children, education researchers obtain the assent (that is, the agreement) of children to participate, to the extent that the children are capable of providing such assent"
- Key point: To the extent children are capable, also obtain their own assent (agreement) to participate.

### E4

- Location: 13.04 (c)
- Quote: "Education researchers recognize that waivers of consent from a child and a parent or guardian require approval from institutional review boards or, in the absence of such boards, from another authoritative body with expertise on the ethics of research."
- Key point: Waiving the consent of a child and parent requires approval from an Institutional Review Board (or another authority with ethics expertise).

### E5

- Location: 13.01 (d)
- Quote: "In undertaking research with vulnerable populations (e.g., children, youth, special needs students, recent immigrant populations), education researchers take special care to ensure that the voluntary nature of the research is understood and that consent or assent is not coerced."
- Key point: In research with vulnerable populations such as children, youth, and special needs students, take special care that participants understand participation is voluntary and that consent or assent is not coerced.

### E6

- Location: 13.03 Informed Consent of Students and Subordinates
- Quote: "When undertaking research at their own institutions or organizations with research participants who are students or subordinates, education researchers take special care to protect the prospective participants from adverse consequences of declining or withdrawing from participation."
- Key point: When participants are your own students or subordinates at your own institution, take special care to protect them from adverse consequences of declining or withdrawing.

### E7

- Location: 14.02 Dual Relationships
- Quote: "In planning research, education researchers select research participants with whom they have no other relationship (e.g., teacher, supervisor, mentor, or employer). If research opportunities or circumstances require selection of research participants with whom education researchers have another such relationship, researchers take particular care to ensure that consent to participate is voluntary and free of coercion."
- Key point: When planning research, select participants with whom you have no other relationship (such as teacher or supervisor); if that is unavoidable, take special care that consent is voluntary and free of coercion.

### E8

- Location: 13 Informed Consent (general statement)
- Quote: "Education researchers recognize the possibility of undue influence or subtle pressures on research participants that may derive from researchers' expertise or authority, and they take this into account in designing informed consent procedures."
- Key point: Researchers' expertise or authority can create undue influence or subtle pressure on participants; consider this when designing informed consent procedures.

### E9

- Location: 14.06 Data Sharing, (a)
- Quote: "Education researchers share data and pertinent documentation as a regular practice."
- Key point: Share data and pertinent documentation as a regular practice.

### E10

- Location: 14.06 (c)
- Quote: "Education researchers anticipate data sharing as an integral part of a research plan whenever data sharing is feasible."
- Key point: Whenever feasible, plan for data sharing as an integral part of the research plan.

### E11

- Location: 15. Authorship Credit, (c)
- Quote: "Education researchers ensure that principal authorship, authorship order, and other publication credits are based on the relative scientific or professional contributions of the individuals involved, regardless of their status."
- Key point: Principal authorship and authorship order are based on relative contributions, regardless of status.

### E12

- Location: 15 (d)
- Quote: "A student is usually listed as principal author on any multiple-authored publication that substantially derives from the student's dissertation or thesis."
- Key point: A student is usually the first author of a multi-author paper substantially derived from the student's dissertation or thesis.

### E13

- Location: 13.06 Use of Recording Technology
- Quote: "Education researchers obtain informed consent from research participants, students, employees, clients, or others prior to photographing, videotaping, filming, or recording them in any form"
- Key point: Obtain informed consent from participants, students, and others before photographing, videotaping, or audio-recording them.
