# Society for American Archaeology 2023: Editorial Policy, Information for Authors, and Style Guide for American Antiquity, Latin American Antiquity, and Advances in Archaeological Practice

- Source: `saa2023styleguide`, PDF from the website of the Society for American Archaeology (SAA): https://www.saa.org/common/Uploaded%20files/saadocs/Publications/Style%20Guide/saa-style-guide_english_updated_2021_final08023c15928949dabd02faafb269fb1c.pdf (download page https://saa.org/Member/SAAMember/Publications/Style-Guide.aspx )
- Version: Cover says "Revised December 2023" (the file name still has 2021). Applies to SAA's three journals. Note: according to the American Antiquity submission instructions (`amantiquity2026instructions`), as of October 2026 SAA journals use the Chicago Manual of Style author-date system; both are author-date systems, and for details the newer instructions take precedence. Locations give section numbers.

### E1

- Location: 3.4 In-Text Reference Citations; 3.4.1 Simple citation
- Quote: "In-text year citations always immediately follow the name(s) of the author(s)." / "(Wylie 1991) or Wylie (1991)"
- Key point: In-text citation years immediately follow the author's name, e.g. (Wylie 1991): an author-date system.
