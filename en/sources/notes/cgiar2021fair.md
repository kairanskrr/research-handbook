# CGIAR 2021: CGIAR Open and FAIR Data Assets Policy

- Source: `cgiar2021fair`, [Original PDF](https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/3c39bf16-b35d-4faa-b509-b29d045a9144/content)
- Version: In effect from April 16, 2021, replacing the 2013 Open Access and Data Management Policy. CGIAR is a global partnership of international agricultural research centers; this policy applies to its centers and partners. Locations give section numbers.

### E1

- Location: 1.1 CGIAR's Commitment
- Quote: "CGIAR adheres to the principle that the results of its research and development activities are international public goods and is committed to their widespread dissemination and use to achieve the maximum impact to advantage the poor, especially smallholder farmers in developing countries."
- Key point: CGIAR treats its research results as international public goods and is committed to disseminating them widely so that they benefit the poor, especially smallholder farmers in developing countries.

### E2

- Location: 1.1 CGIAR's Commitment
- Quote: "CGIAR is therefore committed to sharing outputs of its research that are as open as possible and always Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable (FAIR)"
- Key point: CGIAR commits to making its research outputs as open as possible and always Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable (FAIR).

### E3

- Location: Annex 2 iv. Datasets and databases
- Quote: "Datasets (and any relevant data collection and analysis tools) shall, subject to any additional donor requirements, be deposited in a suitable repository and made Open and FAIR as soon as possible and in any event within 12 months of completion of the data collection or appropriate project milestone, or within 6 months of publication of the information products underpinned by that data, whichever is sooner."
- Key point: Datasets must be deposited in a suitable repository and made open within 12 months of the end of data collection (or a project milestone) or within 6 months of publishing results that use the data, whichever comes first.

### E4

- Location: 4.1 c) ii
- Quote: "Data Management Plans shall be developed at project formulation to ensure that data assets conform to accepted interoperability standards (e.g. ontologies, controlled vocabularies)."
- Key point: A data management plan must be written when the project is formulated, so that data conform to accepted interoperability standards (e.g., ontologies, controlled vocabularies).

### E5

- Location: 4.2
- Quote: "Data assets shall be managed responsibly, with due regard to privacy and ethical considerations"
- Key point: Data must be managed responsibly, with due regard to privacy and ethics.

### E6

- Location: 4.1 b) ii
- Quote: "Data assets may only be published without Open Access if they are associated with a Restricted Use Agreement or Limited Exclusivity Agreement (both as defined in Annex 1), or other contractual obligation to retain confidentiality."
- Key point: Data may be withheld from open access only under a Restricted Use Agreement, a Limited Exclusivity Agreement, or another contractual obligation of confidentiality.
