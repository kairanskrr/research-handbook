# American Institute of Chemical Engineers: Code of Ethics

- Source: `aichecodeethics`, [original](https://www.aiche.org/about/governance/policies/code-ethics)
- Version: Code of Ethics page on the website of the American Institute of Chemical Engineers (AIChE). The website returned 403 to scripted access, so these excerpts come from the Internet Archive snapshot of 2026-10-04 (https://web.archive.org/web/20261004132831/https://www.aiche.org/about/governance/policies/code-ethics ). The code has no sections, so locations give item numbers.

### E1

- Location: preamble
- Quote: "The Board of Directors of the American Institute of Chemical Engineers adopted this Code of Ethics to which it expects that the professional conduct of its members shall conform, and to which every applicant attests by signing his or her membership application."
- Key point: The code was adopted by the Institute's Board of Directors and binds its members; applicants for membership sign to affirm they will abide by it.

### E2

- Location: item 1
- Quote: "Hold paramount the safety, health and welfare of the public and protect the environment in performance of their professional duties."
- Key point: Hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public and protect the environment in performing professional duties.

### E3

- Location: item 2
- Quote: "Formally advise their employers or clients (and consider further disclosure, if warranted) if they perceive that a consequence of their duties will adversely affect the present or future health or safety of their colleagues or the public."
- Key point: If you perceive that your work will adversely affect the present or future health or safety of colleagues or the public, formally advise your employer or client, and consider further disclosure if warranted.

### E4

- Location: item 3
- Quote: "Accept responsibility for their actions, seek and heed critical review of their work and offer objective criticism of the work of others."
- Key point: Accept responsibility for your actions, seek and heed critical review of your work, and offer objective criticism of others' work.

### E5

- Location: item 4
- Quote: "Issue statements or present information only in an objective and truthful manner."
- Key point: Issue statements and present information only in an objective and truthful manner.

### E6

- Location: item 5
- Quote: "Act in professional matters for each employer or client as faithful agents or trustees, avoiding conflicts of interest and never breaching confidentiality."
- Key point: Act as faithful agents of each employer or client in professional matters, avoiding conflicts of interest and never breaching confidentiality.

### E7

- Location: item 7
- Quote: "Perform professional services only in areas of their competence."
- Key point: Perform professional services only in areas of your competence.
