# Bourne & Korngreen 2006: Ten Simple Rules for Reviewers

- Source: `bourne2006reviewers`, PLoS Computational Biology 2(9): e110, open access
- Version: Journal editorial. Ligatures (fi, fl) in the quotes have been restored to plain letters.

### E1

- Location: Rule 1
- Quote: "Do Not Accept a Review Assignment unless You Can Accomplish the Task in the Requested Timeframe—Learn to Say No"
- Key point: Do not accept a review assignment unless you can finish it on time.

### E2

- Location: Rule 2
- Quote: "Do not take on the review if there is the slightest possibility of conflict of interest."
- Key point: Do not accept a review if there is the slightest possibility of a conflict of interest; if unsure, ask the editor.

### E3

- Location: Rule 3
- Quote: "Support your criticisms or praise with concrete reasons that are well laid out and logical."
- Key point: Support both criticism and praise with concrete, well-organized reasons.

### E4

- Location: Rule 4
- Quote: "It is always beneficial to remember that you are there to help the authors in their work, even if this means rejecting their manuscript."
- Key point: Reviewers are there to help the authors, even when the verdict is rejection.

### E5

- Location: Rule 9
- Quote: "A point-by-point critique is valuable since it is easy to read and to respond to. For each point, indicate how critical it is to your accepting the paper."
- Key point: Give a point-by-point critique, and say for each point how critical it is to acceptance.

### E6

- Location: Rule 9
- Quote: "Provide a rating—fence-sitting prolongs the process unnecessarily."
- Key point: Give a clear recommendation; do not sit on the fence.

### E7

- Location: Rule 9
- Quote: "Further, as passionate as you might be about the subject of the paper, do not push your own opinion or hypotheses."
- Key point: Do not use a review to push your own opinions or hypotheses.

### E8

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "There is certainly a place for papers that, for example, support existing hypotheses, or provide a new or modified interpretation of an existing finding."
- Key point: Novelty is often expected but is not everything: papers that support existing hypotheses or reinterpret existing findings also have value.

### E9

- Location: Rule 10
- Quote: "However, be sure those comments are clearly supported by your review"
- Key point: Confidential comments to the editor must also be supported by the review itself.

### E10

- Location: Rule 6
- Quote: "Including citations missed by the author (not your own) is often a short but effective way to help improve a paper."
- Key point: Adding citations the authors missed (not your own) is a simple, effective way to help.

### E11

- Location: Rule 8
- Quote: "If anonymity is the rule of the journal, do not share the manuscript with colleagues unless the Editor has given the green light."
- Key point: Do not show the manuscript to colleagues without the editor's approval.

### E12

- Location: Rule 10
- Quote: "It is also a good place to indicate your own shortcomings, biases, etc., with regard to the content of the paper"
- Key point: Comments to the editor are a good place to state your own shortcomings and biases regarding the paper's content.
