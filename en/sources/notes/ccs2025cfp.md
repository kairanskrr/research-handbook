# ACM CCS 2025: Call for Papers

- Source: `ccs2025cfp`, [Original](https://www.sigsac.org/ccs/CCS2025/call-for-papers/)
- Version: Call for papers of the 32nd ACM Conference on Computer and Communications Security (CCS 2025, organized by ACM SIGSAC), read 2026-10-08. Locations are given as page subheadings. Used only as an example of one security conference in one year.

### E1

- Location: Call for Papers, opening
- Quote: "CCS has two review cycles in 2025." / "Minor revision: Papers in this category are considered to be promising but need some minor additional work (e.g., minor experiments, proofs to minor lemmas)."
- Key point: In 2025 there are two review cycles; one possible outcome is "minor revision": the paper is promising but needs a little more work (such as minor experiments or proofs of minor lemmas).

### E2

- Location: Call for Papers, opening
- Quote: "Theoretical papers must make a convincing case for the relevance of their results to practice. Authors are encouraged to write the abstract and introduction of their paper in a way that makes the results accessible and compelling to a general computer-security researcher."
- Key point: Theoretical papers must make the case that their results matter in practice; the abstract and introduction should make them accessible and compelling to a general security researcher.

### E3

- Location: Call for Papers, review criteria
- Quote: "All submitted papers will be evaluated based on their merits, particularly their importance to practical aspects of computer and communications security and privacy, novelty, quality of execution, and presentation. For papers that might raise ethical concerns, authors are expected to convince reviewers that proper procedures (such as IRB approval or responsible disclosure) have been followed, and due diligence has been made to minimize potential harm."
- Key point: Papers are judged on their importance to practical security and privacy, novelty, quality of execution, and presentation; papers that may raise ethical concerns must convince reviewers that appropriate procedures were followed (such as ethics review approval and responsible disclosure) and that potential harm was minimized.

### E4

- Location: Formatting requirements
- Quote: "no more than 12 pages long, excluding the bibliography, well-marked appendices, and supplementary material. Note that reviewers are not required to read the appendices or any supplementary material."
- Key point: The main text is limited to 12 pages (excluding bibliography, appendices, and supplementary material); reviewers are not required to read appendices or supplementary material.

### E5

- Location: Providing Artifacts at Submission Time
- Quote: "Submissions whose claimed contributions rely on artifacts (e.g., code, models, data sets) are expected to make these accessible to the reviewers, unless there are good reasons not to, in which case these reasons must be mentioned in the submission."
- Key point: Submissions whose contributions rely on artifacts (code, models, datasets) are expected to make them accessible to reviewers during review; if there are good reasons not to, the submission must state them.
