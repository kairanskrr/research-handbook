# Fernandes & Cortez 2020: Alphabetic order of authors in scholarly publications

- Source: `fernandes2020alphabetic`, [original](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11192-020-03686-0)
- Version: Scientometrics 125:2773–2792 (2020). This project read the submitted version on the first author's homepage, https://www.di.uminho.pt/~jmf/PUBLI/papers/2020-sciento.pdf , which may differ from the published version. Data come from Scopus, downloaded in May 2020.

### E1

- Location: §3.1 (datasets), pp. 5–6
- Quote: "Dataset 1 (DS1) is related with all papers that were published in a prestigious journal of a subject area" / "This situation occurred, for example, for #2 (Arts & Humanities), due to its wide scope. The selected journal is Nous, which appears in the 14th position."
- Key point: For the subject area "Arts and Humanities", the journal the authors chose as representative is the philosophy journal Noûs (the first dataset takes one prestigious journal per subject area).

### E2

- Location: conclusion
- Quote: "three fields show a strong tendency to have their authors ordered alphabetically: Economics; Mathematics; and Business, Management & Accounting." / "The fields of Social Sciences; Physics & Astronomy; Biochemistry, Genetics & Molecular Biology; Earth & Planetary Sciences; and Arts & Humanities have a moderate tendency."
- Key point: Three fields show a strong tendency to order authors alphabetically: Economics; Mathematics; and Business, Management & Accounting. Five fields show a moderate tendency: Social Sciences, Physics & Astronomy, Biochemistry, Earth Sciences, and Arts & Humanities.

### E3

- Location: §3.1 (datasets), pp. 5–6; Table 3
- Quote: "Dataset 2 (DS2) contains the metadata" / "the DS2 sample includes recent articles, published in the years of 2018, 2019 and 2020." / "Nous The Philosophical Review" / "Ethics" / "Nous-Supplement: Philosophical Issues" / "British Journal for the Philosophy of Science" / "Philosophy and Phenomenological Research" / "Philosophical Quarterly Mind" / "Philosophical Studies" / "Table 3 Selected journals for DS2."
- Key point: The second dataset takes papers from 2018–2020 in several top journals per field; the "Arts and Humanities" column of Table 3 lists nine philosophy journals: Noûs, The Philosophical Review, Ethics, Philosophical Issues, British Journal for the Philosophy of Science, Philosophy and Phenomenological Research, Philosophical Quarterly, Mind, and Philosophical Studies.

### E4

- Location: §5 (limitations)
- Quote: "dataset only considers one journal for each subject area (with one exception)." / "Therefore, the results for DS1 should be seen with some caution"
- Key point: The first dataset uses only one journal per subject area, and the authors caution that its results should be read with some caution.
