# Eyring et al. 2016: Overview of the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 (CMIP6) experimental design and organization

- Source: `eyring2016cmip6`, [original](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-9-1937-2016)
- Version: Geoscientific Model Development 9, 1937–1958, 2016, open access. Only the abstract and the first paragraph of the introduction on the journal web page were read.

### E1

- Location: abstract
- Quote: "By coordinating the design and distribution of global climate model simulations of the past, current, and future climate, the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project (CMIP) has become one of the foundational elements of climate science."
- Key point: By coordinating the design and distribution of global climate model simulations of past, present, and future climate, the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project (CMIP) has become one of the foundations of climate science.

### E2

- Location: abstract
- Quote: "The DECK and CMIP historical simulations, together with the use of CMIP data standards, will be the entry cards for models participating in CMIP."
- Key point: To participate in CMIP, a model must run a common set of baseline experiments (the DECK) and the historical simulations, and use the CMIP data standards.

### E3

- Location: abstract
- Quote: "Participation in CMIP6-Endorsed MIPs by individual modelling groups will be at their own discretion and will depend on their scientific interests and priorities."
- Key point: Individual modeling groups decide for themselves which endorsed intercomparison projects to join.

### E4

- Location: §1 Introduction
- Quote: "The objective of CMIP is to better understand past, present, and future climate change in a multi-model context."
- Key point: The objective of CMIP is to understand climate change in a multi-model context.
