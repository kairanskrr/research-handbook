# NDSS 2026: Call for Papers

- Source: `ndss2026cfp`, [original](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss2026/submissions/call-for-papers/)
- Version: call for papers of the Network and Distributed System Security (NDSS) Symposium 2026, accessed 2026-10-08. Locations give section headings. Used only as one example from one security conference in one year.

### E1

- Location: Paper Submission Information, Major Revision
- Quote: "Major Revision: Papers in this category are considered promising but need additional work (e.g., new implementations, experiments, and/or proofs)." / "within six weeks after notification"
- Key point: There is a "Major Revision" outcome: the paper is promising but needs additional work (new implementations, experiments, or proofs) and can be revised and resubmitted within six weeks of notification.

### E2

- Location: Ethical Considerations
- Quote: "Authors must proactively anticipate and address potential negative consequences of both conducting and publishing their research. Submissions may be rejected regardless of scientific merit if they fail to adequately address ethical concerns."
- Key point: Authors must proactively anticipate and address potential negative consequences of conducting and publishing their research; submissions that handle ethical issues poorly may be rejected regardless of scientific merit.

### E3

- Location: Ethical Considerations
- Quote: "IRB exemptions may not be sufficient grounds for proper mitigation of ethical concerns." / "If the paper reports a potentially high-impact vulnerability, the authors should report or at least discuss their plan for responsible disclosure."
- Key point: An institutional review board (IRB) exemption may not be sufficient grounds for addressing ethical concerns; papers reporting high-impact vulnerabilities should report, or at least discuss, their plans for responsible disclosure.

### E4

- Location: Ethical Considerations
- Quote: "Each paper may optionally include an “Ethics Considerations” section immediately preceding the reference section."
- Key point: At NDSS 2026 the "Ethics Considerations" section is optional.

### E5

- Location: Areas/Topics of Interest, SoK papers
- Quote: "Such papers go beyond summarizing previous research (like in surveys); they also include a thorough examination and analysis of existing approaches, identify gaps and limitations"
- Key point: Systematization of Knowledge (SoK) papers go beyond summarizing previous research; they also examine existing approaches in depth and identify gaps and limitations.

### E6

- Location: Paper Formatting
- Quote: "Technical papers submitted for NDSS Symposium must not exceed 13 pages, excluding the “Ethics Considerations” section, references, or appendices"
- Key point: The main text must not exceed 13 pages, excluding the ethics considerations section, references, and appendices.

### E7

- Location: Artifact Evaluation
- Quote: "Authors of accepted papers are strongly encouraged to open-source their artifacts and to submit them for an artifact evaluation."
- Key point: Authors of accepted papers are strongly encouraged to open-source their artifacts and submit them for artifact evaluation.

### E8

- Location: Paper Submission Information
- Quote: "NDSS Symposium 2026 will have two review cycles: a summer submission cycle and a fall submission cycle."
- Key point: The 2026 edition has two submission cycles, summer and fall.
