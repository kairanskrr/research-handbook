# ACM 2021: ACM Publications Policy on Research Involving Human Participants and Subjects

- Source: `acm2021humanparticipants`, [original](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/research-involving-human-participants-and-subjects)
- Version: policy approved by the Publications Board of ACM (Association for Computing Machinery) on August 15, 2021. acm.org returned 403 to scripted access, so this note uses the Internet Archive snapshot of the official page from 2025-12-02 (https://web.archive.org/web/20251202211942/https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/research-involving-human-participants-and-subjects).

### E1

- Location: below the title
- Quote: "Approved by ACM Publications Board on August 15, 2021"
- Key point: Approved by the ACM Publications Board on August 15, 2021.

### E2

- Location: Policy Statement
- Quote: "All authors conducting research involving human participants and subjects must meet appropriate ethical and legal standards guiding such research. In particular, ACM authors must ensure that their human research planning, conduct, and reporting are consistent with their local governing laws and regulations and the general principles detailed below." / "adhering to the principle of informed consent"
- Key point: Authors conducting research involving human participants must meet appropriate ethical and legal standards; planning, conducting, and reporting the research must follow the principles set out in local regulations and policies, one of which is informed consent.

### E3

- Location: Scope of Policy
- Quote: "This policy applies to all ACM Publications, including ACM Sponsored, co-Sponsored, or In-Cooperation Conferences, International Conference Proceedings Series (ICPS) proceedings, journals, magazines, books, newsletters"
- Key point: The policy applies to all ACM publications, including ACM sponsored, co-sponsored, and in-cooperation conferences, journals, and others.

### E4

- Location: Roles and Responsibilities, Authors
- Quote: "Where local ethical review boards are required, authors are responsible for having their research reviewed and approved by such boards." / "All ACM Authors must be prepared to provide documentary evidence to ACM that they have adhered to local ethical and legal standards, as ACM may require documentary evidence of such approval at any time following submission of the Work and prior to or after publication of the Work."
- Key point: Where local ethics review is required, authors are responsible for having their research reviewed and approved; authors must be ready to provide written documentation to ACM, which may request it at any time after submission, before or after publication.

### E5

- Location: Policy Statement
- Quote: "Individual ACM Publications, such as an ACM Journal or Conference Proceeding, may specify a more restrictive policy than required by local legal requirements"
- Key point: Individual ACM journals or conferences may set a more restrictive policy.

### E6

- Location: Violations and Penalties
- Quote: "a Level 2-5 penalty may be assessed" / "a "Note of Concern" on the Work's citation page or a Retraction of the published Work in the ACM Digital Library"
- Key point: Violations can be penalized, including a "Note of Concern" on the work's citation page or retraction.
