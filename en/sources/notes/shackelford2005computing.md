# Shackelford et al. 2005: Computing Curricula 2005 — The Overview Report

- Source: `shackelford2005computing`, [original](https://www.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/education/curricula-recommendations/cc2005-march06final.pdf)
- Version: Overview report on computing curricula by a joint task force of the ACM, AIS, and the IEEE Computer Society, September 2005 (final PDF of March 2006). Locations give sections and page numbers.

### E1

- Location: §2.2.1 Before the 1990s, p. 9
- Quote: "For students who wanted to become expert in developing software or with the theoretical aspects of computing, computer science was the obvious choice. For students who wanted to work with hardware, electrical engineering was the clear option."
- Key point: Before the 1990s, students who wanted to work on software or the theory of computing chose computer science, and those who wanted to work on hardware chose electrical engineering.

### E2

- Location: §2.2.1 Before the 1990s, p. 10
- Quote: "computer scientists and electrical engineers sometimes worked closely together since they were both concerned with developing new technology, were often housed in the same part of the university, and sometimes required each others' help."
- Key point: Computer scientists and electrical engineers worked closely together because both developed new technology, were often in the same part of the university, and sometimes needed each other's help.

### E3

- Location: §2.2.1 Before the 1990s, p. 10
- Quote: "With the advent of the microprocessor in the mid-1970s, computer engineering began to emerge from within electrical engineering to become a discipline unto itself."
- Key point: With the arrival of the microprocessor in the mid-1970s, computer engineering emerged from electrical engineering as a discipline of its own.

### E4

- Location: §2.3.1 Computer Engineering, p. 13
- Quote: "Computer engineering is concerned with the design and construction of computers and computer-based systems. It involves the study of hardware, software, communications, and the interaction among them."
- Key point: Computer engineering is concerned with the design and construction of computers and computer-based systems, involving hardware, software, communications, and their interaction.

### E5

- Location: §2.2.3 After the 1990s, p. 12
- Quote: "for CE, software attention is focused on hardware devices; for SE, the emphasis is on creating software that satisfies robust real-world requirements; and for CS, software is the currency in which ideas are expressed"
- Key point: In computer engineering, attention to software focuses on hardware devices; in computer science, software is the currency in which ideas are expressed.

### E6

- Location: §3.4.1 Computer Engineering, p. 30
- Quote: "At some universities, electrical engineering and computer science reside in the same school, and computer engineering is seen as a natural merging of interests among faculty in these two disciplines."
- Key point: At some universities, electrical engineering and computer science are in the same school, and computer engineering is seen as a natural meeting of their interests.
