# CABI Agriculture and Bioscience: Evidence Synthesis submission requirements

- Source: `cabiagbioevidencesynthesis`, [Original](https://cabiagbio.biomedcentral.com/submission-guidelines/preparing-your-manuscript/evidence-synthesis)
- Version: The description of the Evidence Synthesis article type in the submission guidelines of CABI Agriculture and Bioscience, an open access journal published by CABI. The original URL now redirects to the publisher's journal home page; the text was read from an Internet Archive snapshot of May 27, 2025.

### E1

- Location: Evidence Synthesis, article type description
- Quote: "These differ from traditional literature reviews in that they should report on structured evidence syntheses (such as systematic reviews or systematic maps) that follow an a-priori protocol."
- Key point: These articles differ from traditional literature reviews: they report structured evidence syntheses that follow an a priori protocol, such as systematic reviews or systematic maps.

### E2

- Location: Evidence Synthesis, manuscript requirements
- Quote: "All evidence synthesis submissions must be accompanied by a 'RepOrting standards for Systematic Evidence Syntheses' (ROSES) form."
- Key point: Every evidence synthesis submission must include a ROSES (RepOrting standards for Systematic Evidence Syntheses) form.

### E3

- Location: Evidence Synthesis, Protocol registration in the Abstract
- Quote: "We expect systematic reviews and maps to have followed an a-priori protocol (methodology) and recommend that this is made publically available prior to starting your evidence synthesis." / "agriRxiv, the preprint service for agriculture and allied sciences, for example, accepts evidence synthesis protocols."
- Key point: The journal expects systematic reviews and maps to follow an a priori protocol and recommends making it public before starting; for example, agriRxiv, the preprint service for agriculture, accepts evidence synthesis protocols.
