# US Code of Federal Regulations 21 CFR 820.30 Design controls (2023 edition, since removed)

- Source: `cfr2023designcontrols`, [Original PDF](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2023-title21-vol8/pdf/CFR-2023-title21-vol8-sec820-30.pdf)
- Version: US Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) Title 21, section 820.30, edition of April 1, 2023, from the US Government Publishing Office's govinfo website. This is the "design controls" provision of the old Quality System Regulation; since the new Quality Management System Regulation took effect, §820.30 in the current edition is marked "[Reserved]" (see `cfr2026qmsr`). Locations are given as paragraph numbers.

### E1

- Location: §820.30(a)
- Quote: "Each manufacturer of any class III or class II device, and the class I devices listed in paragraph (a)(2) of this section, shall establish and maintain procedures to control the design of the device in order to ensure that specified design requirements are met."
- Key point: The old rule required manufacturers of Class III, Class II, and some Class I devices to establish design control procedures to ensure specified design requirements are met.

### E2

- Location: §820.30(c) Design input
- Quote: "Each manufacturer shall establish and maintain procedures to ensure that the design requirements relating to a device are appropriate and address the intended use of the device, including the needs of the user and patient."
- Key point: Design input: design requirements must be appropriate and address the device's intended use, including the needs of users and patients.

### E3

- Location: §820.30(e) Design review
- Quote: "The procedures shall ensure that participants at each design review include representatives of all functions concerned with the design stage being reviewed and an individual(s) who does not have direct responsibility for the design stage being reviewed, as well as any specialists needed."
- Key point: Each design review must include representatives of all functions concerned, an individual without direct responsibility for the stage being reviewed, and any specialists needed.

### E4

- Location: §820.30(f) Design verification
- Quote: "Design verification shall confirm that the design output meets the design input requirements."
- Key point: Design verification confirms that the design output meets the design input requirements.

### E5

- Location: §820.30(g) Design validation
- Quote: "Design validation shall ensure that devices conform to defined user needs and intended uses and shall include testing of production units under actual or simulated use conditions."
- Key point: Design validation ensures that devices conform to defined user needs and intended uses, and includes testing production units under actual or simulated use conditions.

### E6

- Location: §820.30(i) Design changes
- Quote: "Each manufacturer shall establish and maintain procedures for the identification, documentation, validation or where appropriate verification, review, and approval of design changes before their implementation."
- Key point: Design changes must be identified, documented, validated (or verified), reviewed, and approved before implementation.

### E7

- Location: §820.30(j) Design history file
- Quote: "The DHF shall contain or reference the records necessary to demonstrate that the design was developed in accordance with the approved design plan and the requirements of this part."
- Key point: The Design History File (DHF) must contain or reference the records showing that the design was developed according to the approved design plan and the regulation's requirements.
