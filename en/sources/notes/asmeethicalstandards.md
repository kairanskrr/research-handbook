# ASME: Ethical Standards (publication ethics standards)

- Source: `asmeethicalstandards`, [original](https://www.asme.org/publications-submissions/proceedings/submission-review-and-acceptance/ethical-standards)
- Version: "Ethical Standards" page in the proceedings submission section of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) website, read 2026-10-06. The page says most of these standards are taken from the publication ethics guidelines of the American Chemical Society (ACS). Locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Ethical Obligations of Authors
- Quote: "A submitted manuscript shall contain detail and reference to public sources of information sufficient to permit the author's peers to repeat the work or otherwise verify its accuracy."
- Key point: A manuscript must give enough detail and references to public sources for peers to repeat the work or verify it.

### E2

- Location: Ethical Obligations of Authors
- Quote: "Fragmentation of research papers shall be avoided."
- Key point: Avoid fragmenting research into many papers; someone doing a series of related studies should make each paper a complete account of one aspect of the work.

### E3

- Location: Ethical Obligations of Authors
- Quote: "It is unethical for an author to submit for review more than one paper describing essentially the same research or project to more than one journal of primary publication."
- Key point: Submitting papers describing essentially the same research to more than one journal of primary publication is unethical.

### E4

- Location: Ethical Obligations of Authors
- Quote: "The authors should reveal to the editor any potential conflict of interest, e.g., a consulting or financial interest in a company that might be affected by publication of the results contained in a manuscript."
- Key point: Disclose potential conflicts of interest to the editor, e.g., a consulting or financial interest in a company that the results might affect.

### E5

- Location: Ethical Obligations of Authors
- Quote: "The authors should ensure that no contractual relations or proprietary considerations exist that would affect the publication of information in a submitted manuscript."
- Key point: Authors should ensure that no contractual relations or proprietary considerations will affect publication of the manuscript's content.

### E6

- Location: Ethical Obligations of Authors
- Quote: "To protect the integrity of authorship, only persons who have significantly contributed to the research or project and manuscript preparation shall be listed as co-authors."
- Key point: Only people who contributed significantly to both the research and the manuscript should be listed as co-authors.
