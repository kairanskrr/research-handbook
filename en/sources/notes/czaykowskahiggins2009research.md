# Czaykowska-Higgins 2009: Research Models, Community Engagement, and Linguistic Fieldwork: Reflections on Working within Canadian Indigenous Communities

- Source: `czaykowskahiggins2009research`, [Original](http://hdl.handle.net/10125/4423)
- Version: Language Documentation & Conservation 3(1): 15–50 (open access); the author is a linguist at the University of Victoria, Canada, and the article draws on her work with Indigenous communities in Canada

### E1

- Location: §4.1 Establishing relationships
- Quote: "the most important prerequisite to and sine qua non of any kind of linguistic fieldwork" [...] "is establishing and maintaining solid, respectful, reciprocal, and trusting working relationships"
- Key point: The author holds that the most important prerequisite for any linguistic fieldwork is establishing and maintaining solid, respectful, reciprocal, and trusting working relationships.

### E2

- Location: §4.1 Establishing relationships
- Quote: "In most Canadian Indigenous communities, for instance, the elders of the community are always central in the research partnership."
- Key point: In most Indigenous communities in Canada, elders are always central to the research partnership.

### E3

- Location: §4.1 Establishing relationships
- Quote: "consultation has to take place regularly and often"
- Key point: Consultation has to happen regularly and often.

### E4

- Location: §4.1 Establishing relationships
- Quote: "it is possible to overlook or underestimate the need and the time needed for common sense and sensitivity."
- Key point: Under pressure to publish or get tenure, it is easy to underestimate the time needed to build relationships.

### E5

- Location: §4.2 Determining roles
- Quote: "those members of the language-using community who work with linguists to document and describe their languages are naturally seen to be researchers and experts in their own right"
- Key point: In community-based language research, community members who document their language with linguists are seen as researchers and experts in their own right.

### E6

- Location: §4.2 Determining roles
- Quote: "the role of a linguist should not" [...] "be that of a knight in shining armor who rides into a community to rescue it."
- Key point: A linguist should not act as a knight in shining armor who rides into a community to rescue it.

### E7

- Location: §4.3 Institutional issues
- Quote: "working out an agreed-upon process for undertaking the research; determining the kinds of protocols that all partners are comfortable with"
- Key point: Outcomes of collaborative research include agreeing on a process for doing the research and on protocols all partners are comfortable with.

### E8

- Location: §4.3 Institutional issues
- Quote: "developing work plans for the various people involved in doing the research; developing Memoranda of Understanding"
- Key point: They also include work plans for everyone involved and memoranda of understanding.
