# American Society of Agronomy, Crop Science Society of America, and Soil Science Society of America 2024: Publications Handbook and Style Manual

- Source: `asa2024style`, [original PDF](https://www.agronomy.org/files/publications/style/style-manual.pdf)
- Version: February 2024 update. Publications handbook and style manual for the journals and books of the American Society of Agronomy (ASA), the Crop Science Society of America (CSSA), and the Soil Science Society of America (SSSA). Locations give chapters.

### E1

- Location: Chapter 4. Statistical Design and Analysis
- Quote: "Included should be any information about blocking, multiple experimental unit sizes (e.g., in split and strip plots), the number of sites and years or independent runs of the experiment, the number of replicates, a description of conditions at field sites and in greenhouse or controlled environmental chambers, and how measurements were made for specific traits."
- Key point: Papers should state the blocking, multiple experimental unit sizes (e.g., split and strip plots), the number of sites and years (or independent runs), the number of replicates, field and greenhouse conditions, and how each trait was measured.

### E2

- Location: Chapter 4. Statistical Design and Analysis
- Quote: "In studies where the experimental units and observational or sampling units were not the same, each should be clearly identified. The number of experimental units used and the number of samples taken from each unit should be clear to the reader."
- Key point: When experimental units and observational (sampling) units differ, identify both clearly; readers should be able to see how many experimental units were used and how many samples were taken from each.

### E3

- Location: Chapter 4. Statistical Design and Analysis
- Quote: "A measure of the precision achieved, either as a standard error or a confidence interval, should be reported for all data on which conclusions are drawn."
- Key point: Report a measure of precision (standard error or confidence interval) for all data on which conclusions are drawn.

### E4

- Location: Chapter 4, Spatial and Temporal Statistical Analyses
- Quote: "Data observed at different points in space and/or time on the same experimental material are often correlated."
- Key point: Data observed at different points in space or time on the same experimental material are often correlated (not independent).

### E5

- Location: Chapter 1, Manuscript Handling
- Quote: "For example, a paper released because it needed another year of data may be resubmitted after those data have been gathered and the results incorporated into the paper."
- Key point: For example, a paper returned because it needed another year of data may be resubmitted once those data are collected and incorporated.

### E6

- Location: Chapter 1, Anonymous Review
- Quote: "Most ASA, CSSA, and SSSA journals use a single-anonymous review process, where the authors do not know the names of the reviewers. Some use a double-anonymous process and also withhold the names of the authors from the reviewers."
- Key point: Most of the three societies' journals use single-anonymous review (authors do not know who the reviewers are); some use double-anonymous review.

### E7

- Location: Chapter 5. Tables
- Quote: "Typically, specify the crop or soil involved, the major variables presented, and the place and year."
- Key point: Table titles should typically state the crop or soil, the major variables, and the place and year.

### E8

- Location: Chapter 1, requirements for Materials and Methods under Research Papers
- Quote: "Give details of unusual experimental designs or statistical methods."
- Key point: Give details of unusual experimental designs or statistical methods.

### E9

- Location: Chapter 4. Statistical Design and Analysis
- Quote: "Readers must be able to understand how the authors designed and conducted their studies so that the results can be judged for validity and also serve as a basis for the design of future research."
- Key point: Readers must be able to understand how the study was designed and conducted, to judge whether the results are valid and to use them as a basis for future research.

### E10

- Location: Chapter 4, Treatment and Experimental Design
- Quote: "Treatment design includes the factors of interest, the levels of each factor, the relationship among the factors (e.g., a factorial treatment structure), and the selection of blocking variables and covariates. Experimental design refers to the method of arranging the experimental units and the method of assigning treatments to the units."
- Key point: Treatment design covers the factors of interest, the levels of each factor, the relationships among factors (e.g., factorial structure), and the choice of blocking variables and covariates; experimental design is how the experimental units are arranged and how treatments are assigned to them.

### E11

- Location: opening of Chapter 4
- Quote: "The reporting of the results from each analysis should include a brief description of the statistical method and a literature citation providing its full detail, verification of the degree to which assumptions have been met, and complete descriptions of sampling design and experimental observations in relation to the efficacy of the statistical analysis."
- Key point: Reporting each analysis should include a brief description of the statistical method with a citation giving full detail, a check of how far the assumptions were met, and a complete description of the sampling design and observations.

### E12

- Location: Chapter 1, requirements for Materials and Methods under Research Papers
- Quote: "In the Materials and Methods section, give enough detail to allow a competent scientist to repeat the experiments, mentally or in fact."
- Key point: The Materials and Methods section should give enough detail for a competent scientist to repeat the experiments, mentally or in fact.

### E13

- Location: Chapter 1, requirements for Materials and Methods under Research Papers
- Quote: "In the materials section, describe the preparation method, equipment, and measurements, using SI-acceptable units."
- Key point: The materials section should describe preparation methods, equipment, and measurements, using SI-acceptable units.

### E14

- Location: Chapter 1, requirements for Materials and Methods under Research Papers
- Quote: "When a product must be identified by trade name, add the name of the manufacturer or a major distributor parenthetically after the first mention of the product."
- Key point: When a product must be identified by trade name, give the manufacturer or a major distributor in parentheses at first mention.
