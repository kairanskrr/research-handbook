# EarthArXiv: About EarthArXiv

- Source: `eartharxivabout`, [Original](https://eartharxiv.org/repository/about/)
- Version: The "About" page of the EarthArXiv website, read 2026-10-06.

### E1

- Location: About EarthArXiv, paragraph 1
- Quote: "Active since 2017, EarthArXiv is a preprint server devoted to open scholarly communication. EarthArXiv publishes articles from all subdomains of Earth Science and related domains of planetary science."
- Key point: Active since 2017, EarthArXiv is a preprint server for all areas of Earth science and related planetary science.

### E2

- Location: About EarthArXiv, paragraph 1
- Quote: "The EarthArXiv platform assigns each submission a Digital Object Identifier (DOI), therefore assigning provenance and making it citable in other scholarly works."
- Key point: Every preprint gets a DOI and can be cited.

### E3

- Location: About EarthArXiv, paragraph 2
- Quote: "EarthArXiv does not evaluate the scientific quality of submissions."
- Key point: EarthArXiv does not evaluate the scientific quality of submissions; there is no peer review.
