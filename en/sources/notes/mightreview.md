# Might: How to peer review scientific work

- Source: `mightreview`, [full text](https://matt.might.net/articles/how-to-peer-review/)
- Version: blog post written for graduate students.

### E1

- Location: opening
- Quote: "Review the manuscript as you would want your own reviewed."
- Key point: The core principle: review the manuscript as you would want your own reviewed.

### E2

- Location: Recognize conflicts
- Quote: "If you're not sure whether you have a conflict, disclose that to the editor or program chair so that they can decide."
- Key point: If you are not sure whether you have a conflict of interest, disclose it to the editor or program chair and let them decide.

### E3

- Location: Don't review like a grad student
- Quote: "Reviews written by graduate students are among the most negative."
- Key point: Reviews written by graduate students are among the most negative; list the paper's strengths before criticizing it.

### E4

- Location: Acknowledge visceral reactions
- Quote: "Acknowledging your gut instinct will help you avoid confirmation bias"
- Key point: Noting your first impression and why you have it helps you avoid confirmation bias (looking only for evidence that supports it).

### E5

- Location: Offer constructive criticism
- Quote: "When you criticize an aspect of a manuscript, offer advice on how to fix it."
- Key point: When you criticize something, also suggest how to fix it.

### E6

- Location: Offer constructive criticism
- Quote: "If you recommend rejection, what specifically could have changed your mind to argue for acceptance?"
- Key point: If you recommend rejection, say specifically what could have changed your mind.

### E7

- Location: Hold the snark
- Quote: "Peer reviews are not the right venue to show off your wit."
- Key point: Peer reviews are not the place to show off your wit.

### E8

- Location: Keep it impersonal
- Quote: "Don't let the track record of the authors sway your opinion in either direction."
- Key point: Don't let the authors' track record sway your opinion in either direction.

### E9

- Location: Anonymize but don't hide
- Quote: "Anonymity in peer review is meant as a shield, not a sword."
- Key point: Anonymity in peer review is a shield, not a sword.

### E10

- Location: Scholarly conflict
- Quote: "I recommend informing the program chair or editor and recusing yourself from discussion of such manuscripts"
- Key point: If a manuscript independently solves a problem you are working on, tell the editor or chair and recuse yourself from discussing it.
