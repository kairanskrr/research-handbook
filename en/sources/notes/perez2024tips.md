# Perez 2024: Tips for Empirical Alignment Research

- Source: `perez2024tips`, [original](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/dZFpEdKyb9Bf4xYn7/tips-for-empirical-alignment-research)
- Version: AI Alignment Forum post, 2024-02-29, read 2026-10-05. Locations give page subheadings. The post addresses experimental research on language models; we excerpt only the parts that hold across fields.

### E1

- Location: General Mindset Tips
- Quote: "What matters is whether or not you’re learning about a problem. If we’re learning, we’re winning, even if the experiment didn’t “work.”"
- Key point: What matters is whether you are learning about the problem; if you are learning, an experiment that "didn't work" is still progress.

### E2

- Location: General Mindset Tips
- Quote: "If you’re hitting diminishing returns on the project, then it’s totally fine and great to switch projects"
- Key point: If a project hits clearly diminishing returns, switching projects is perfectly fine.

### E3

- Location: Three modes of research (Exploratory phase)
- Quote: "Run some quick experiments to prototype various ideas, using the workflow described earlier (anything you can run in a day or less, e.g. preferring faster-to-implement/run experiments)."
- Key point: In the exploration phase, prototype ideas with quick experiments you can run in a day or less.

### E4

- Location: Three modes of research (Writing phase)
- Quote: "I’ll often just start a doc as I’m running experiments, and then add info about the experimental results as they come in (e.g. various plots/stats, and my observations/takeaways about the results"
- Key point: Start a document as you run experiments and add plots, statistics, and your observations and takeaways as results come in.

### E5

- Location: Work Habits
- Quote: "Lots of researchers burn out by working in unsustainable ways (also since the object-level work can be hard and goals ill-defined), or by developing an aversion to what they do, and it’s really good to avoid this (also, burn out just sucks)."
- Key point: Many researchers burn out from unsustainable ways of working; actively guard against it.

### E6

- Location: Tactical Research Tips & Approach (Always be thinking about what the best next experiment you run should be)
- Quote: "When you show experimental results (in meetings or in slack), you should also include discussion of your proposed next possible steps immediately after (and proposed prioritization)."
- Key point: When you show results, follow them immediately with your proposed next steps and their priority; whoever ran the experiment knows the results best and is best placed to propose a plan first.

### E7

- Location: What success generally looks like
- Quote: "Noticing when you’re going slowly and quickly and shamelessly asking for help"
- Key point: Notice when you are going slowly and ask for help quickly and without embarrassment.

### E8

- Location: What success generally looks like
- Quote: "whether you’re transparent/communicative about issues you’re facing"
- Key point: Good collaborators are proactive and transparent about the problems they face.
