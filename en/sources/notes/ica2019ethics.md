# International Communication Association 2019: ICA Code of Ethics

- Source: `ica2019ethics`, [original PDF](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.icahdq.org/resource/resmgr/governance_documents/ica.code.of.ethics.may2019.pdf)
- Version: Code of ethics of the International Communication Association (ICA), adopted May 2019, 4 pages. The official PDF refuses scripted access, so we read the Internet Archive snapshot of 2024-09-01 (web.archive.org/web/20240901124205/...), which is the same PDF. Locations give clause numbers.

### E1

- Location: Preamble
- Quote: "Ethical guidelines must be sensitive to institutional, legal, and cultural contexts that may vary among nations and regions."
- Key point: Ethical guidelines must be sensitive to institutional, legal, and cultural contexts that vary among nations and regions.

### E2

- Location: 1.1 Rights of research participants
- Quote: "Participation in communication research studies should include the voluntary and informed consent of participants (or, where appropriate, assent)."
- Key point: Participation in communication research should rest on participants' voluntary, informed consent (or, where appropriate, as with children, their assent).

### E3

- Location: 1.1 Rights of research participants
- Quote: "ICA members are expected to uphold the research values of autonomy, beneficence, and justice, and where applicable, to abide by their local institutional ethics and review boards."
- Key point: Members are expected to uphold the research values of autonomy, beneficence, and justice and, where applicable, to abide by their local institutional ethics review boards.

### E4

- Location: 2.2 Transparency and disclosure
- Quote: "While ICA supports the transparency of open data initiatives, it recognizes the need to protect the privacy and rights of data subjects. Researchers should take care to prevent the re-identification of anonymized data."
- Key point: ICA supports open data but recognizes the need to protect the privacy and rights of data subjects; researchers should prevent the re-identification of anonymized data.

### E5

- Location: 2.1 Honest attribution and authorship
- Quote: "the published author list should accurately reflect individual contributions."
- Key point: The author list should accurately reflect individual contributions.

### E6

- Location: 2.2 Transparency and disclosure
- Quote: "Authors should be transparent and disclose where and how their datasets, writings, and research have previously appeared at the time of submission of research for review as well as publication or presentation. This includes submissions to conferences as well as journal publications."
- Key point: When submitting, publishing, or presenting, disclose where and in what form your data, writings, and research have appeared before, including conference submissions and journal articles.

### E7

- Location: 1.1 Rights of research participants
- Quote: "ICA expects researchers to take care to address the specific needs of vulnerable populations in their research, such as children, refugees, people who are mentally unwell, etc."
- Key point: Research involving vulnerable populations such as children, refugees, and people who are mentally unwell must address their specific needs.

### E8

- Location: 2.3 Fair use and footnote 8
- Quote: "Fair use is the right to use copyrighted material without permission or compensation where the social and cultural benefits are greater than the costs to the copyright holder." [...] "It should be noted that all members should follow their national policies regarding fair use."
- Key point: Fair use is the right to use copyrighted material without permission or payment when the social and cultural benefits outweigh the costs to the copyright holder; members must follow their own country's fair use rules.
