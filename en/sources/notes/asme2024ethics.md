# ASME: Society Policy P-15.7, Ethics (Code of Ethics of Engineers)

- Source: `asme2024ethics`, [original](https://www.asme.org/getmedia/1d9d64bf-f6a7-4e75-a8f5-0cabd8c04269/P-15-7-Ethics_2.pdf)
- Version: Society Policy P-15.7 of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME), revised October 1, 2024, free PDF on the ASME website. Locations give items.

### E1

- Location: opening
- Quote: "ASME requires ethical practice by each of its members and has adopted the following Code of Ethics of Engineers as referenced in the ASME By-Law B2.1."
- Key point: ASME requires ethical practice of every member and has adopted the Code of Ethics of Engineers.

### E2

- Location: Fundamental Canons, item 1
- Quote: "Engineers shall hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public in the performance of their professional duties."
- Key point: Engineers shall hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public in performing their professional duties.

### E3

- Location: Fundamental Canons, item 2
- Quote: "Engineers shall perform services only in the areas of their competence"
- Key point: Engineers perform services only in areas of their competence.

### E4

- Location: Fundamental Canons, item 4
- Quote: "Engineers shall act in professional matters for each employer or client as faithful agents or trustees and shall avoid conflicts of interest or the appearance of conflicts of interest."
- Key point: Engineers act as faithful agents of their employers or clients and avoid conflicts of interest and the appearance of them.

### E5

- Location: Fundamental Canons, item 5
- Quote: "Engineers shall respect the proprietary information and intellectual property rights of others"
- Key point: Engineers respect the proprietary information and intellectual property rights of others.

### E6

- Location: Fundamental Canons, item 7
- Quote: "Engineers shall issue public statements only in an objective and truthful manner"
- Key point: Engineers issue public statements only in an objective and truthful manner.
