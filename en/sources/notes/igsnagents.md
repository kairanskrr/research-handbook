# IGSN e.V.: IGSN Agents and Registration

- Source: `igsnagents`, [original](https://ev.igsn.org/resources/igsn-agents-and-registration)
- Version: The "IGSN Agents and Registration" page on the IGSN e.V. website, listing the organizations that offer IGSN registration and their DataCite prefixes, read on 2026-10-10. Locations give the organization or list name.

### E1

- Location: Kiel University IGSN Service
- Quote: "The IGSN service is available to all employees and institutions of Kiel University."
- Key point: Kiel University's IGSN service is open to all its employees and institutions, an example of registering through your own institution.

### E2

- Location: GFZ Data Services; Lithodat
- Quote: "GFZ Data Services at the Helmholtz Centre offers an IGSN Registration Service" / "Lithodat Pty Ltd offers IGSN registration and DOI registration."
- Key point: The list also includes data services and companies that offer registration to outside users, such as GFZ Data Services at the Helmholtz Centre and Lithodat Pty Ltd.

### E3

- Location: List of DataCite DOI Prefixes Used for IGSN IDs
- Quote: "10.57777 GEO Big Data Open Platform KIGAM" / "10.57993 United States Geological Survey IGSN" / "10.60494 IGSN ID registration for Geoscience Australia samples data"
- Key point: The list of DataCite prefixes used for IGSN includes organizations such as the Korea Institute of Geoscience and Mineral Resources (KIGAM), the United States Geological Survey, and Geoscience Australia.
