# Society for Ethnomusicology 1998: Position Statement on Ethical Considerations

- Source: `sem1998ethics`, [original](https://www.ethnomusicology.org/page/EthicsStatement) (the official site returns 403 to scripts; text taken from a 2024 Internet Archive snapshot)
- Version: Adopted by the Board of the Society for Ethnomusicology (SEM, based in the US) in 1998. Web page. Locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Field Research
- Quote: "Cultivation of relationships based on informed consent, rights of privacy and confidentiality, and mutual respect."
- Key point: Fieldwork should build relationships based on informed consent, rights of privacy and confidentiality, and mutual respect.

### E2

- Location: Field Research
- Quote: "Sensitivity to proprietary concerns regarding recorded materials, photographs, and other documentation."
- Key point: Be sensitive to ownership concerns about recordings, photographs, and other documentation.

### E3

- Location: Field Research
- Quote: "the responsibilities of field research extend beyond the fieldwork setting and often involve a long-term commitment to the rights and concerns of field consultants and their communities."
- Key point: The responsibilities of field research extend beyond the field site and often involve a long-term commitment to consultants and their communities.

### E4

- Location: Field Research
- Quote: "field research may create or contribute to the basic conditions for future unanticipated, possibly exploitative, uses of recordings and other documentation."
- Key point: Field research may create conditions for unanticipated, possibly exploitative future uses of recordings.

### E5

- Location: Field Research
- Quote: "recognize the need to be informed regarding copyright and other laws pertaining to the ownership of intellectual and cultural property"
- Key point: Be informed about copyright and the laws on ownership of intellectual and cultural property.

### E6

- Location: Publication
- Quote: "give credit to consultants, colleagues, students, and others where appropriate."
- Key point: When publishing, give appropriate credit to consultants, colleagues, students, and others.

### E7

- Location: General
- Quote: "ethical values affirmed by these statements do not necessarily represent those of all practitioners of ethnomusicology everywhere."
- Key point: The Society acknowledges that these ethical values do not necessarily represent all practitioners everywhere.
