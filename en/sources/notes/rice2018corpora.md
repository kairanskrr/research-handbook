# Rice 2018: Reflections on documentary corpora

- Source: `rice2018corpora`, [original](http://hdl.handle.net/10125/24817)
- Version: Language Documentation & Conservation Special Publication 15, pp. 157-172, open access; the author is a linguist at the University of Alberta, Canada

### E1

- Location: §4, p. 163
- Quote: "A real LC is not an archive of available material. It involves the rendering of that material to be machine-readable and query-able."
- Key point: A usable language corpus is not just an archive of material; the material has to be rendered machine-readable and queryable.

### E2

- Location: §5, p. 170
- Quote: "A small, untagged, and unbalanced corpus can still yield tremendous insights into the structure, meaning, and use of a language"
- Key point: Even a small, untagged, unbalanced corpus can yield many insights into the structure, meaning, and use of a language.
