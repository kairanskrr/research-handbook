# American Chemical Society: Research Data Policy

- Source: `acsdatapolicy`, [original](https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/data_policy)
- Version: research data policy web page of the American Chemical Society Publications Division, accessed 2026-10-06. Applies to ACS journals; each journal sets its own policy level.

### E1

- Location: opening
- Quote: "ACS strongly endorses The FAIR Data Principles and believes that where ethically and legally feasible, all research data should be findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable."
- Key point: ACS strongly endorses the FAIR Data Principles: where ethically and legally feasible, all research data should be findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable.

### E2

- Location: ACS Research Data Policy
- Quote: "Each ACS journal will independently determine the level of policy required that reflects the needs of their specific communities."
- Key point: Each ACS journal independently decides which level of the data policy to require.

### E3

- Location: Research Data Policy Levels
- Quote: "Level 1 Public data availability encouraged" / "Level 4 Public data availability required and data will be peer-reviewed; Data Availability Statement required"
- Key point: The policy has four levels, from "public data availability encouraged" (Level 1) to "public data availability required, data peer-reviewed with the manuscript, and a Data Availability Statement required" (Level 4).

### E4

- Location: Data Availability Statements
- Quote: "A Data Availability Statement should describe the availability of the data underlying the conclusions drawn in the research article and provide a means of access, where applicable, by linking to the data, preferably through the use of a persistent identifier such as a DOI or an Accession Number assigned by a data repository."
- Key point: A Data Availability Statement should describe where the data underlying the conclusions are and how to access them, preferably with a persistent identifier such as a DOI from a data repository.

### E5

- Location: Data Availability Statements
- Quote: "Although submission of data as part of an article's Supporting Information is acceptable, authors are encouraged to submit data in a format that can be most easily accessed and reused. ACS encourages authors to provide machine-readable data"
- Key point: Putting data in the Supporting Information is acceptable, but ACS encourages providing machine-readable data in the format most easily accessed and reused.
