# Huang 2021: How to come up with research ideas?

- Source: `huang2021ideas`, [original tweet](https://twitter.com/jbhuang0604/status/1423499757591400448) (read via the [Thread Reader mirror](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1423499757591400448.html))
- Version: Tweet thread, 2021-08-06, 9 tweets. The author is a computer vision researcher, so the examples lean toward that field; only the general practices are excerpted here.

### E1

- Location: Tweet 3, *Relax assumptions*
- Quote: "Identify the underlying assumptions of existing work and try relaxing them to make it work in more unconstrained settings."
- Key point: Identify the assumptions behind existing work and try relaxing them so the method works in less constrained settings.

### E2

- Location: Tweet 4, *Make more assumptions*
- Quote: "Take a general approach and tailor it to your SPECIFIC problem. You can then leverage all the domain knowledge (i.e., make more assumptions) to improve the method."
- Key point: Conversely, take a general method, apply it to a specific problem, and use domain knowledge (more assumptions) to improve it.

### E3

- Location: Tweets 5-6, *Combine two ideas/problems* and *Grab a powerful hammer and find all the nails*
- Quote: "Pay attention to new emerging tools in the community. Apply and adapt them on your problem."
- Key point: Combine two ideas; watch for new tools emerging in the community and apply them to your own problem.

### E4

- Location: Tweet 8, *Stress test the state-of-the-art*
- Quote: "Don't simply run on the fixed, boring benchmark datasets. Try it out on diverse, unconstrained examples and see how it fails. It's a great way to identify limitations of existing work. This is where your work can fill the gap."
- Key point: Stress-test state-of-the-art methods: run them on diverse, unconstrained examples and see how they fail, which reveals the limitations of existing work and the gaps your work can fill.
