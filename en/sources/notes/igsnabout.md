# IGSN e.V.: About IGSNs

- Source: `igsnabout`, [original](https://www.igsn.org/about-igsns)
- Version: Web page of IGSN e.V. (the nonprofit organization that manages the International Generic Sample Number, founded in 2011 and working with DataCite), read on 2026-10-06.

### E1

- Location: First paragraph of the page
- Quote: "Major problems for achieving this include incomplete documentation of samples in publications, use of ambiguous sample names, and the lack of web-accessible catalogues that allow finding out about existing samples and their archiving location."
- Key point: The main obstacles to long-term use of sample data are incomplete documentation of samples in publications, ambiguous sample names, and no way to find where samples are stored.

### E2

- Location: After the first paragraph of the page
- Quote: "The IGSN ID is a globally unique, persistent, domain-agnostic identifier for material samples."
- Key point: IGSN is a globally unique, persistent identifier for material samples.

### E3

- Location: After the first paragraph of the page
- Quote: "When an IGSN is assigned to a sample, the sample can be tracked and traced from that moment until the sample is analysed and the research is published."
- Key point: Once a sample has an IGSN, it can be tracked from collection through analysis to publication.

### E4

- Location: Facilitating the use of samples
- Quote: "it becomes important at the nexus of the big challenges facing humanity and unique samples that can never be taken again. It is also important for the reproducibility of research."
- Key point: Some samples can never be collected again, so sharing and tracking them matters for reproducibility.

### E5

- Location: After the first paragraph of the page
- Quote: "Even though IGSN had its roots in geosciences, it really is now for all sample types from all disciplines."
- Key point: IGSN has its roots in the geosciences but now covers samples from all disciplines.
