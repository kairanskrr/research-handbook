# PRISMA-DTA Checklist (PDF from the official PRISMA website)

- Source: `prismadta2018checklist`, [original (PDF)](https://www.prisma-statement.org/s/PRISMA-DTA-Checklist-xh5j.pdf)
- Version: PRISMA-DTA Checklist on the official website of the PRISMA Executive, 2 pages, downloaded October 2026; the footer says it is adapted from the PRISMA-DTA statement by McInnes et al. 2018 in JAMA. Locations give item numbers.

### E1

- Location: Item 4 (Objectives)
- Quote: "Provide an explicit statement of question(s) being addressed in terms of participants, index test(s), and target condition(s)."
- Key point: State the research question in terms of participants, index test(s), and target condition(s).

### E2

- Location: Item 6 (Eligibility criteria)
- Quote: "Specify study characteristics (participants, setting, index test(s), reference standard(s), target condition(s), and study design)"
- Key point: Eligibility criteria should specify participants, setting, index test(s), reference standard(s), target condition(s), and study design.

### E3

- Location: Item 12 (Risk of bias and applicability)
- Quote: "Describe methods used for assessing risk of bias in individual studies and concerns regarding the applicability to the review question."
- Key point: Describe how you assessed each study's risk of bias and its applicability to the review question.

### E4

- Location: Item 13 (Diagnostic accuracy measures)
- Quote: "State the principal diagnostic accuracy measure(s) reported (e.g. sensitivity, specificity) and state the unit of assessment (e.g. per-patient, per-lesion)."
- Key point: State the principal diagnostic accuracy measures (e.g., sensitivity, specificity) and the unit of assessment (per patient or per lesion).
