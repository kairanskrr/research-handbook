# NeurIPS 2025: Policy on the Use of Large Language Models

- Source: `neurips2025llm`, [original](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/LLM)
- Version: NeurIPS 2025 policy page on the use of large language models (LLMs), with separate parts for authors and reviewers, accessed 2026-10-07. Locations give section headings. Used only as an example of one top machine learning conference; other conferences and journals may have different rules.

### E1

- Location: NeurIPS 2025 LLM Policy for Reviewers
- Quote: "You must keep everything relating to the review process confidential. Do not use ideas, code, or results from the submissions in your own work until they become publicly available. Do not talk about or share submissions with anyone or any LLMs"
- Key point: Everything about the review process is confidential; do not use ideas, code, or results from a submission in your own work until it is public; do not discuss or share submissions with anyone or with any large language model.

### E2

- Location: NeurIPS 2025 LLM Policy for Reviewers, FAQ 2
- Quote: "You may only use ideas, code, or results from submissions (1) after they become publicly available, and (2) if the author has granted the appropriate permission/license that covers your intended usage."
- Key point: You may use ideas, code, or results from a submission only after it becomes public and only if the license the authors grant covers your use.

### E3

- Location: NeurIPS 2025 LLM Policy for Reviewers, FAQ 4
- Quote: "You can use resources (e.g. publications on Google Scholar, Wikipedia articles, interactions with LLMs and/or human experts without sharing the paper submissions) to enhance your understanding of certain concepts and to check the grammaticality and phrasing of your written review."
- Key point: Without sharing the paper, reviewers may use publications, Wikipedia, large language models, or human experts to understand concepts, and to check the grammar and wording of their reviews.

### E4

- Location: NeurIPS 2025 LLM Policy for Reviewers, FAQ 4
- Quote: "Please remember that you are responsible for the quality and accuracy of your submitted review regardless of any tools, resources, or other help you used to construct the final review."
- Key point: Whatever tools or help they use, reviewers are responsible for the quality and accuracy of their reviews.

### E5

- Location: LLM Policy for Authors
- Quote: "The use of LLMs in implementing the method should be described in the experimental setup section (or equivalent) if it is an important, original, or non-standard component of the approach."
- Key point: If a large language model is an important, original, or non-standard component of the method, describe it in the experimental setup section.

### E6

- Location: LLM Policy for Authors, FAQ 2
- Quote: "Only humans are eligible to be authors. You, as an author, are fully responsible for all the content in your paper, including text, figures, and methodology, regardless of what tools (e.g., LLMs) you have used."
- Key point: Only humans can be authors; whatever tools they use, authors are fully responsible for all the content of the paper.

### E7

- Location: LLM Policy for Authors
- Quote: "If an investigation determines a violation, NeurIPS reserves the right to revoke the paper's publication status. Examples of such violations include content that is not scientifically sound (e.g. using references generated by an LLM without conducting the due diligence to verify correctness, existence and appropriateness in the manuscript’s context)"
- Key point: Violations may lead to the paper's publication status being revoked; one example is LLM-generated references that were not checked for whether they exist, are correct, and are relevant.

### E8

- Location: LLM Policy for Authors, FAQ 3
- Quote: "No, if the LLM is used only for writing, editing, or formatting purposes and does not impact the core methodology, scientific rigorousness, or originality of the research, declaration is not required."
- Key point: Using a large language model only for writing, editing, or formatting, without affecting the methodology, rigor, or originality, does not need to be declared in the paper.

### E9

- Location: LLM Policy for Authors
- Quote: "Some (including free) tools may retain input data for further model training purposes, so authors should exercise caution to account for individual privacy considerations."
- Key point: Some tools retain input data for further training, so be careful about personal privacy.

### E10

- Location: LLM Policy for Reviewers
- Quote: "You must keep everything relating to the review process confidential." / "Do not talk about or share submissions with anyone or any LLMs"
- Key point: Everything in the review process is confidential; do not discuss or share submissions with anyone or with any large language model.
