# NIH: Guidance: Rigor and Reproducibility in Grant Applications

- Source: `nihrigorguidance`, [original](https://grants.nih.gov/policy/reproducibility/guidance.htm) (the site blocks script access; read from an [Internet Archive snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/2024/https://grants.nih.gov/policy/reproducibility/guidance.htm))
- Version: grant application guidance page of the US National Institutes of Health (NIH), accessed 2026-10-06. Locations give section headings.

### E1

- Location: Rigorous experimental design for robust and unbiased results
- Quote: "Scientific rigor is the strict application of the scientific method to ensure robust and unbiased experimental design, methodology, analysis, interpretation and reporting of results."
- Key point: Scientific rigor is the strict application of the scientific method to ensure robust and unbiased experimental design, methodology, analysis, interpretation, and reporting of results.

### E2

- Location: Research Grants and Mentored Career Development Awards
- Quote: "NIH research grant and career development award application instructions and review language focus on four key areas" / "The rigor of the prior research" / "Rigorous experimental design for robust and unbiased results" / "Consideration of relevant biological variables" / "Authentication of key biological and/or chemical resources"
- Key point: NIH application instructions and review criteria focus on four areas: rigor of the prior research, rigorous experimental design, consideration of relevant biological variables, and authentication of key biological and chemical resources.

### E3

- Location: Consideration of relevant biological variables
- Quote: "NIH expects that sex as a biological variable will be factored into research designs, analyses, and reporting in vertebrate animal and human studies."
- Key point: NIH expects sex as a biological variable to be factored into the design, analysis, and reporting of vertebrate animal and human studies.

### E4

- Location: Authentication of key biological and/or chemical resources
- Quote: "Key biological and/or chemical resources include, but are not limited to, cell lines, specialty chemicals, antibodies and other biologics." / "The quality of resources used to conduct research is critical to the ability to reproduce the results."
- Key point: Key biological and chemical resources include cell lines, specialty chemicals, antibodies, and other biologics; the quality of these resources is critical to whether results can be reproduced.

### E5

- Location: Consideration of relevant biological variables
- Quote: "Biological variables, such as sex, age, weight, and underlying health conditions, are often critical factors affecting health or disease."
- Key point: Biological variables such as sex, age, weight, and underlying health conditions are often critical factors affecting health or disease.

### E6

- Location: Consideration of relevant biological variables
- Quote: "In particular, sex is a biological variable that is frequently ignored in animal study designs and analyses, leading to an incomplete understanding of potential sex-based differences in basic biological function, disease processes and treatment response." / "Strong justification from the scientific literature, preliminary data or other relevant considerations must be provided for applications proposing to study only one sex."
- Key point: Sex as a biological variable is frequently ignored in animal study designs and analyses, leading to an incomplete understanding of possible sex differences in basic biological function, disease processes, and treatment response; applications that study only one sex must justify this with literature, preliminary data, or other reasons.
