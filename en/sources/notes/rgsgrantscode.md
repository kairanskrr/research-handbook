# Royal Geographical Society: Code of Practice for the Grants Programme

- Source: `rgsgrantscode`, [original](https://www.rgs.org/in-the-field/grants/guidance-for-society-grant-applicants/code-of-practice-for-the-grants-programme)
- Version: Web page with the code of practice for the grants programme of the Royal Geographical Society (with the Institute of British Geographers, RGS-IBG). The official site blocks scripts, so we read the Internet Archive snapshot of 2025-04-29. It is the code for the Society's **grants programme**, but its ethics provisions also apply to research presented at Society conferences or published in Society journals (see E1). Locations describe the paragraph content.

### E1

- Location: opening of the ethics provisions
- Quote: "The following considerations should therefore apply to all research supported by the Society, whether through financial support, or implicit support through presentation at the Society, Research Group conferences, or other Society events or publication in Society journals:"
- Key point: The ethical requirements below apply to all research the Society supports, whether funded, presented at the Society or its Research Group conferences, or published in Society journals.

### E2

- Location: ethics provisions
- Quote: "Accurate reporting of findings, and a commitment to enabling others to replicate results where possible."
- Key point: Report findings accurately, and enable others to replicate results where possible.

### E3

- Location: ethics provisions
- Quote: "Research projects should be approved by a research ethics committee in the host country, wherever this exists, even if ethics approval has already been obtained by the researcher’s home institution."
- Key point: Wherever the host country has a research ethics committee, the project should be approved by it, even if it has already passed ethics review at the researcher's home institution.

### E4

- Location: ethics provisions
- Quote: "Local partners, communities and research participants should be included throughout the research process, from planning, to implementation, to post-study feedback and evaluation."
- Key point: Local partners, communities, and participants should be included throughout the research, from planning and implementation to post-study feedback and evaluation.

### E5

- Location: ethics provisions
- Quote: "Local partners should be recognised in data ownership and authorship of publications."
- Key point: Local partners should be recognized in data ownership and in the authorship of publications.

### E6

- Location: sustainable research provisions
- Quote: "Recognising that fieldwork is integral to geographical research and that this will commonly involve travel, this travel and the choice of destination(s) visited should in turn be clearly justified."
- Key point: Fieldwork is integral to geographical research and often involves travel; the travel and the choice of destinations should be clearly justified.

### E7

- Location: ethics provisions
- Quote: "The review process should be proportional to the likely risk (for example, research on vulnerable groups or at-risk populations demands more careful attention than other forms of research)."
- Key point: The review should be proportional to the likely risk; for example, research on vulnerable or at-risk groups needs more careful attention.
