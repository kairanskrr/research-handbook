# IUPAC FAIR Chemistry Cookbook

- Source: `iupacfaircookbook`, [original](https://iupac.github.io/WFChemCookbook/intro.html)
- Version: Home page of the online cookbook maintained by the WorldFAIR Chemistry project of the International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry (IUPAC), read on 2026-10-06. Default license CC BY-SA 4.0.

### E1

- Location: Welcome, para. 1
- Quote: "Welcome to the IUPAC FAIR Chemistry Cookbook, a self-serve web-based toolbox of interactive demonstrations of community best practices in machine-readable chemical data management."
- Key point: The Cookbook is a self-serve, web-based toolbox of interactive demonstrations of how to manage chemical data in machine-readable form.

### E2

- Location: Welcome, para. 3
- Quote: "The IUPAC FAIR Chemistry Cookbook is focused on working with chemical data that are findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable for machine processing"
- Key point: The Cookbook focuses on chemical data that are findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable (FAIR) for machine processing.

### E3

- Location: Welcome, para. 3
- Quote: "Familiarity with techniques for digitally representing chemical structures and other data components will allow researchers to tap into multiple networked resources for searching and analysis, as well as enable their own data to be more discoverable."
- Key point: Knowing how to represent chemical structures and other data digitally lets researchers search and analyze across networked resources, and makes their own data more discoverable.
