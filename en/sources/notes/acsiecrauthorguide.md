# American Chemical Society: Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research Author Guidelines

- Source: `acsiecrauthorguide`, [original](https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=iecred)
- Version: author guidelines web page of the American Chemical Society (ACS) journal *Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research* (I&EC Research below), marked "Last updated: October 05, 2026", accessed 2026-10-06. It represents this journal's requirements only. Locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Scope of the Journal
- Quote: "papers may deal with process design and development (for example, synthesis and design methods, systems analysis, process control, schemes for data correlation, modeling and scale-up procedures, etc.)"
- Key point: The journal accepts papers on process design and development, including modeling and scale-up methods.

### E2

- Location: Manuscript Types › Topical Categories
- Quote: "Kinetics, Catalysis, and Reaction Engineering; Materials and Interfaces; Process Systems Engineering; Separations; Thermodynamics, Transport, and Fluid Mechanics"
- Key point: The journal is organized into topical sections, including Kinetics, Catalysis, and Reaction Engineering; Materials and Interfaces; Process Systems Engineering; Separations; and Thermodynamics, Transport, and Fluid Mechanics.

### E3

- Location: Scope of the Journal
- Quote: "Papers that simply report data or calculations will be declined for publication."
- Key point: Papers that simply report data or calculations will be declined.

### E4

- Location: Scope of the Journal
- Quote: "Manuscripts most likely to be declined after Editorial review are those with English deficiencies, those lacking conclusions that are clearly significant and novel, and those with contributions that are judged unlikely to attract the interest of a general applied chemistry or chemical engineering readership."
- Key point: Manuscripts most likely to be declined at editorial review are those with English deficiencies, those lacking clearly significant and novel conclusions, and those of too narrow interest.

### E5

- Location: Prior Publication Policy
- Quote: "Posting of the original submitted manuscript to a pre-print server is acceptable. Please note the use of a pre-print server explicitly in the cover letter"
- Key point: Posting the original submitted manuscript to a preprint server is acceptable, but must be stated explicitly in the cover letter.

### E6

- Location: Prior Publication Policy
- Quote: "Publishing material that has been used in reports to research sponsors is acceptable provided one or more of the authors holds the copyright."
- Key point: Material used in reports to research sponsors may be published, provided at least one of the authors holds the copyright.

### E7

- Location: Research Data Policy
- Quote: "All ACS journals strongly encourage authors to make the research data underlying their articles publicly available at the time of publication."
- Key point: All ACS journals strongly encourage authors to make the research data underlying their articles publicly available at publication.

### E8

- Location: Data Requirements
- Quote: "Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research does not have specific data requirements."
- Key point: I&EC Research has no specific data requirements.

### E9

- Location: Safety Considerations
- Quote: "Authors must emphasize any unexpected, new, and/or significant hazards or risks associated with the reported work. This information should be in the Experimental Section of a full article and included in the main text of a communication or letter."
- Key point: Authors must emphasize any unexpected, new, or significant hazards and risks of the work; in full papers, this goes in the Experimental section.

### E10

- Location: Patent Activities and Intellectual Property
- Quote: "Authors are responsible for ensuring that all patent activities and intellectual property issues are satisfactorily resolved prior to first publication (ASAP or in issue). Acceptance and publication will not be delayed for pending or unresolved issues of this nature."
- Key point: Authors must resolve patent and intellectual property issues before first publication; the journal will not delay acceptance or publication for such issues.

### E11

- Location: Conflict of Interest Disclosure
- Quote: "the Corresponding Author must provide a statement on behalf of all authors of the manuscript, describing all potential sources of bias, including affiliations, funding sources, and financial or management relationships, that may constitute conflicts of interest."
- Key point: The corresponding author must provide a statement on behalf of all authors describing all potential sources of bias, including affiliations, funding sources, and financial or management relationships.
