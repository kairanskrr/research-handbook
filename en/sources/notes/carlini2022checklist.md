# Carlini 2022: Research Paper Release Checklist

- Source: `carlini2022checklist`, [Original](https://nicholas.carlini.com/writing/2022/paper-release-checklist.html)
- Version: Author's homepage, 2022-01-30

### E1

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "Every time I make one (or someone tells me a mistake they made that I could easily see myself making) I write it down and add it to a checklist."
- Key point: Every time he makes an avoidable mistake, he writes it down and adds it to a checklist to go through before the next release.

### E2

- Location: Secondary checklist for public releases
- Quote: "All authors are listed on the paper." / "Author names are spelled correctly, with correct affiliations." / "Un-comment the acknowledgments section, and any author contribution section."
- Key point: Before public release, check that all authors are listed, names and affiliations are correct, and the acknowledgments and author contribution sections (often commented out for anonymous submission) are restored.
