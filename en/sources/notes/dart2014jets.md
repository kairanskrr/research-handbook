# DA-RT 2014: Journal Editors' Transparency Statement (JETS)

- Source: `dart2014jets`, [Original web page](https://www.dartstatement.org/2014-journal-editors-statement-jets)
- Version: The joint statement of political science journal editors on Data Access and Research Transparency (DA-RT), issued in 2014; we read the full text and later update log on dartstatement.org (accessed October 2026). Locations give web page paragraphs.

### E1

- Location: Statement text
- Quote: "Transparency requires making visible both the empirical foundation and the logic of inquiry of research. We agree that by January 15, 2016 we will:"
- Key point: Transparency means making visible both the empirical foundation and the logic of inquiry of research; signatory journals committed to implementing the following by January 15, 2016.

### E2

- Location: Statement text, item 1
- Quote: "Require authors to ensure that cited data are available at the time of publication through a trusted digital repository."
- Key point: Require authors to make cited data available at publication through a trusted digital repository.

### E3

- Location: Statement text, item 1
- Quote: "If cited data are restricted (e.g., classified, require confidentiality protections, were obtained under a non-disclosure agreement, or have inherent logistical constraints), authors must notify the editor at the time of submission."
- Key point: If cited data are restricted (classified, needing confidentiality protection, under a non-disclosure agreement, etc.), authors must tell the editor at submission.

### E4

- Location: Statement text, item 2
- Quote: "Require authors to delineate clearly the analytic procedures upon which their published claims rely, and where possible to provide access to all relevant analytic materials."
- Key point: Require authors to set out clearly the analytic procedures their claims rely on and, where possible, provide access to the relevant analytic materials.

### E5

- Location: Statement text, item 3
- Quote: "we will require authors who base their claims on data created by others to reference and cite those data as an intellectual product of value."
- Key point: Authors who use data created by others must cite those data as a valuable intellectual product.

### E6

- Location: Page update log, UPDATE 11/9/2015
- Quote: "Nancy Hirschmann, Mala Htun, Jane Mansbridge, Kathleen Thelen, Lisa Wedeen, and Elisabeth Wood have organized a petition to delay transparency standards."
- Key point: In November 2015, several scholars organized a petition to delay these transparency standards.
