# Björk & Solomon 2013: The publishing delay in scholarly peer-reviewed journals

- Source: `bjork2013publishing`, [Original](https://web.archive.org/web/20131008075840/http://openaccesspublishing.org/oa11/article.pdf)
- Version: Author's self-archived copy (author website openaccesspublishing.org, read via the Internet Archive snapshot of 2013-10-08); published in Journal of Informetrics 7(4): 914-923, 2013, DOI 10.1016/j.joi.2013.09.001.

### E1

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "Using a stratified random sample we studied average publishing delays in 2700 papers published in 135 journals sampled from the Scopus citation index."
- Key point: Using a stratified random sample of 2,700 papers from 135 journals in the Scopus citation index, the study measured average publishing delays.

### E2

- Location: §2 Methods
- Quote: "We decided to merge some of these latter groups based on our subjective assumption of similarity in reviewing culture and publication speed resulting in nine groups. These include arts/humanities, biomedicine, business/economics, chemistry, earth science, engineering, mathematics, physics, and social sciences." / "we selected 20 articles working backward from the last article published in 2012."
- Key point: The authors merged journals into nine groups on their own subjective judgment (including engineering, earth science, and social science), with no finer disciplines; for each journal they took the last 20 articles published in 2012, counting backward.

### E3

- Location: §3 Results
- Quote: "The first time period reflects the delay due to the peer review and revision process used by a journal and the second the length of the publishing process, backlog due to publication page limits and potentially other factors."
- Key point: "Submission to acceptance" reflects the time spent on peer review and revision; "acceptance to publication" reflects the publishing process, backlogs caused by page limits, and similar factors.

### E4

- Location: Appendix table Publication Time in Months by Discipline, Engineering row
- Quote: "Engineering Mean 5.00 4.30"
- Key point: Engineering: mean 5.00 months from submission to acceptance and 4.30 months from acceptance to publication (9.30 months from submission to publication in the table).

### E5

- Location: §4 Discussion
- Quote: "There was also considerably more variation among journals within a discipline and size group than among disciplines and size groups."
- Key point: Journals within the same discipline and size group varied far more than the discipline and size groups varied from each other.

### E6

- Location: Appendix table Publication Time in Months by Discipline, Social Science row
- Quote: "Social Science Mean 6.17 7.93"
- Key point: Social science: mean 6.17 months from submission to acceptance and 7.93 months from acceptance to publication (14.10 months from submission to publication in the table).
