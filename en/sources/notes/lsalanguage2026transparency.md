# Language (journal of the Linguistic Society of America): Research transparency and Preparing your materials

- Source: `lsalanguage2026transparency`, [web page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/language/information/journal-policies/research-transparency)
- Version: two author-guidance pages for *Language*, the flagship journal of the Linguistic Society of America (LSA), on the Cambridge University Press website: Journal policies → Research transparency, and Author instructions → [Preparing your materials](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/language/information/author-instructions/preparing-your-materials); also the [Language page](https://www.linguisticsociety.org/lsa-publications/language/submission-guidelines) on the LSA website. Read 2026-10-08; the pages carry no version date. Locations give the page subheading. These are the current requirements of this one journal only.

### E1

- Location: Research transparency, Data availability
- Quote: "All manuscripts submitted to this journal must contain a Data Availability Statement , explaining where and how readers can access the materials underpinning the research published in the manuscript."
- Key point: Every manuscript submitted to *Language* must include a Data Availability Statement explaining where and how readers can access the materials underlying the research.

### E2

- Location: Research transparency, Data availability
- Quote: "Whenever possible, we also encourage authors to make all data and qualitative materials underpinning their research available to readers through an appropriate repository" / "Appropriate repositories are ones that provide persistent identifiers and have robust preservation policies"
- Key point: Authors are encouraged (not required) to deposit data and qualitative materials in an appropriate repository whenever possible, one that provides persistent identifiers and has a reliable preservation policy.

### E3

- Location: Research transparency, Data availability
- Quote: "If you cannot make your data publicly available due to ethical or legal concerns, please explain this in your Data Availability Statement."
- Key point: If data cannot be made public for ethical or legal reasons, explain this in the Data Availability Statement.

### E4

- Location: Research transparency, Code and model availability
- Quote: "We strongly encourage authors to share any analytical code or models used for the research reported in their manuscript."
- Key point: Authors are strongly encouraged to share analysis code and models.

### E5

- Location: Preparing your materials, Data requirements and referencing
- Quote: "Data sources should be clearly referenced and, if publicly available, cited as such"
- Key point: Data sources should be clearly referenced, and cited formally if publicly available.

### E6

- Location: Preparing your materials, Data requirements and referencing (Large-scale empirical or experimental studies)
- Quote: "articles reporting results of empirical studies such as quantitative analyses or experimental results should allow replication by other researchers based upon the article's contents and references, and possibly Supplementary Materials"
- Key point: Articles reporting quantitative analyses or experimental results should let other researchers replicate them from the article, its references, and supplementary materials.

### E7

- Location: Preparing your materials, Data requirements and referencing (Articles reporting judgements)
- Quote: "Authors of articles reporting judgments (e.g., acceptability or meaning judgments) should be prepared to further substantiate their judgments if called into question by referees or Editors"
- Key point: Authors reporting judgments (e.g., acceptability or meaning judgments) should be prepared to substantiate them further if reviewers or editors ask.

### E8

- Location: Language page on the LSA website
- Quote: "Language is the flagship journal of the Linguistic Society of America." / "Starting in 2026, Language is a fully open access journal."
- Key point: *Language* is the flagship journal of the Linguistic Society of America; starting in 2026 it is fully open access.
