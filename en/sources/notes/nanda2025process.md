# Nanda 2025: How I Think About My Research Process: Explore, Understand, Distill

- Source: `nanda2025process`, [original](https://www.alignmentforum.org/s/5GT3yoYM9gRmMEKqL/p/hjMy4ZxS5ogA9cTYK)
- Version: AI Alignment Forum post, 2025-04-26, accessed 2026-10-05. Locations give the page's section headings.

### E1

- Location: Exploration (Stage 2): Gain surface area
- Quote: "Your north star is just to gain information - do exploratory experiments, visualise data, follow your curiosity, prioritise moving fast."
- Key point: In the exploration stage, the only goal is to gain information: run exploratory experiments, visualize data, follow your curiosity, and prioritize moving fast.

### E2

- Location: Exploration (Stage 2): Gain surface area
- Quote: "Junior researchers often get stuck in the early stages of a project and don’t know what to do next. In my opinion this is because they think they are in the understanding stage, but are actually in the exploration stage."
- Key point: Junior researchers often get stuck early in a project because they think they should already have a clear hypothesis (the understanding stage), when they are actually still in the exploration stage.

### E3

- Location: Exploration (Stage 2): Gain surface area
- Quote: "Frequently ask yourself “am I getting enough information per unit time?” If you haven’t learned anything recently, shake it up."
- Key point: Ask yourself often whether you are getting enough information per unit time; if you haven't learned anything recently, shake things up.

### E4

- Location: Exploration (Stage 2): Gain surface area
- Quote: "A key practical tip is to keep a highlights doc of particularly interesting results, this makes it easier to spot connections"
- Key point: Keep a separate "highlights doc" of particularly interesting results; it makes connections between them easier to spot.

### E5

- Location: Understanding (Stage 3): Test Hypotheses
- Quote: "This stage begins when you understand the problem domain enough to have some specific hypotheses that you think are interesting - hypotheses you can write down, and have some idea of what evidence you could find to show if they’re true or false."
- Key point: You are ready for the focused stage (testing hypotheses) when you can write down specific hypotheses and know what evidence would tell you whether they are true.

### E6

- Location: Distillation (Stage 4): Compress, Refine, Communicate
- Quote: "Sometimes you’ll discover that actually things are way messier than thought. [...] You may need to go back to understanding or even exploration - this is totally fine and normal, and does not mean you’ve screwed anything up."
- Key point: When things turn out messier than you thought, going back to hypothesis testing or even to exploration is normal; it does not mean you messed up.

### E7

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "Take breaks, the total change to productive time is typically positive. Find sustainable ways to work."
- Key point: Breaks usually increase your total productive time; find sustainable ways to work.
