# IUPAC Gold Book: entry "mechanism of a reaction"

- Source: `iupacgoldbookmechanism`, [original](https://doi.org/10.1351/goldbook.M03804)
- Version: Entry in the online International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry (IUPAC) Compendium of Chemical Terminology (Gold Book), 5th edition, version 5.0.0 (2025); its source is given as the IUPAC 1994 recommendations "Glossary of terms used in physical organic chemistry" (Pure and Applied Chemistry 66: 1077, p. 1138). The official site puts a human check on scripted access, so this excerpt reads the Internet Archive copy of the site from 2026-08-10 (web.archive.org/web/20260810025145/https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/M03804). This is a terminology definition, not a journal's review standard.

### E1

- Location: Entry text
- Quote: "An acceptable mechanism of a specified reaction (and there may be a number of such alternative mechanisms not excluded by the evidence) must be consistent with the reaction stoichiometry, the rate law and with all other available experimental data, such as the stereochemical course of the reaction."
- Key point: An acceptable mechanism must be consistent with the reaction stoichiometry, the rate law, and all other available experimental data (such as the stereochemical course of the reaction); and there may be several alternative mechanisms that the evidence does not exclude.

### E2

- Location: Entry text
- Quote: "It should be noted that for many reactions all this information is not available and the suggested mechanism is based on incomplete experimental data."
- Key point: For many reactions not all of this information is available, and the suggested mechanism rests on incomplete experimental data.
