# Cawley 2018: A Guide and Advice for Economists on the U.S. Junior Academic Job Market (2018–2019 Edition)

- Source: `cawley2018guide`, [PDF on the AEA website](https://www.aeaweb.org/content/file?id=869)
- Version: Version of September 21, 2018 (for the 2018-19 job market). Covers only the US academic job market; specific schedules and mechanisms may change from year to year.

### E1

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "The most important determinant of your success on the job market is the quality of your job market paper and how well you present it."
- Key point: The most important determinant of success on the job market is the quality of your job market paper and how well you present it.

### E2

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "You should focus your time and effort towards improving your paper, your interview skills, and your job talk (your 90-minute seminar based on the job market paper)."
- Key point: Spend your time improving the paper, practicing interviews, and preparing the job talk, a 90-minute seminar based on the job market paper.

### E3

- Location: Preparing to Go on the Job Market
- Quote: "The most important indicator of your readiness is whether you will have a good job market paper completed early in the Fall." / "Try to complete your job market paper by the end of summer."
- Key point: The most important sign you are ready for the market is having a good job market paper done early in the fall; aim to finish it by the end of summer.

### E4

- Location: Preparing to Go on the Job Market
- Quote: "Organize an informal workshop with the other graduate students who will be on the job market and practice presenting your job market paper."
- Key point: Organize an informal workshop with fellow students going on the market and practice presenting your job market paper.

### E5

- Location: Interviewing at the ASSA Meetings
- Quote: "Candidates with empirical job market papers should expect a careful examination and detailed discussion of their identification strategy and empirical methods."
- Key point: If your job market paper is empirical, expect close questioning of your identification strategy and empirical methods in interviews.

### E6

- Location: Interviewing at the ASSA Meetings
- Quote: "Interviewers want to make sure that the job candidate is responsible for the idea behind the job market paper and the decisions made in it."
- Key point: Interviewers want to make sure the idea behind the job market paper and the key decisions in it are the candidate's own.

### E7

- Location: Applying for Academic Jobs
- Quote: "The materials you transmit must include a cover letter, your CV, and your job market paper."
- Key point: Academic job applications must include a cover letter, a CV, and the job market paper.
