# Bourne 2005: Ten Simple Rules for Getting Published

- Source: `bourne2005published`, PLoS Computational Biology 1(5): e57, open access
- Version: Journal editorial. Ligatures (fi, fl) in the quotes have been restored to plain letters.

### E1

- Location: Rule 5
- Quote: "The correct response to a paper being rejected or requiring major revision is to listen to the reviewers and respond in an objective, not subjective, manner."
- Key point: When a paper is rejected or needs major revision, the right response is to listen to the reviewers and respond objectively, not emotionally.

### E2

- Location: Rule 5
- Quote: "If reviewers are unanimous about the poor quality of the paper, move on—in virtually all cases, they are right. If they request a major revision, do it and address every point they raise both in your cover letter and through obvious revisions to the text."
- Key point: If reviewers unanimously judge the paper poor, accept it and move on; if they ask for a major revision, address every point in both the cover letter and the revised text.

### E3

- Location: Rule 5
- Quote: "Scientific careers are full of rejection, even for the best scientists."
- Key point: Scientific careers are full of rejection, even for the best scientists.

### E4

- Location: Rule 8
- Quote: "Reviewing other papers will help you write better papers. To start, work with your mentors; have them give you papers they are reviewing and do the first cut at the review"
- Key point: Start reviewing early: drafting first-cut reviews for your mentors helps you write better papers.

### E5

- Location: Rule 9
- Quote: "This will define the form and level of detail and assumed novelty of the work you are doing."
- Key point: Decide early where to submit, because the venue defines the form, level of detail, and expected novelty of the paper.

### E6

- Location: Rule 3
- Quote: "Put your energy into improving the quality of the manuscript before submission."
- Key point: Put your energy into improving the manuscript before submission; a fundamentally flawed manuscript will not get useful reviews.

### E7

- Location: Rule 6
- Quote: "Get a candid opinion by having the paper read by colleagues without a vested interest in the work, including those not directly involved in the topic area."
- Key point: Get a candid opinion from colleagues with no vested interest in the work, including people outside the topic area.

### E8

- Location: Rule 7
- Quote: "Be familiar with the guide to authors and follow it, the editors and reviewers do."
- Key point: Know the guide to authors and follow it.

### E9

- Location: Rule 10
- Quote: "It is better to publish one paper in a quality journal than multiple papers in lesser journals."
- Key point: One paper in a quality journal is better than several in lesser journals.

### E10

- Location: Rule 9
- Quote: "Many journals have a presubmission enquiry system available—use it."
- Key point: Many journals offer presubmission enquiries; use them.
