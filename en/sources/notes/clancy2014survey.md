# Clancy et al. 2014: Survey of Academic Field Experiences (SAFE): Trainees Report Harassment and Assault

- Source: `clancy2014survey`, [Original](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0102172)
- Version: PLOS ONE 9(7): e102172, 2014, open access. We read the full web text on journals.plos.org.

### E1

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "We conducted an internet-based survey of field scientists (N = 666) to characterize these experiences."
- Key point: The authors ran an online survey of field scientists; 666 people responded.

### E2

- Location: Results, Respondent characteristics
- Quote: "Nearly half of the study participants self-identified as anthropologists" / "geologists (N = 29, 4.4%)"
- Key point: Nearly half of respondents identified as anthropologists and about a quarter as archaeologists; only 29 (4.4%) were geologists.

### E3

- Location: Results, Respondent characteristics
- Quote: "a majority of respondents were from the United States (N = 498/666, 74.8%)"
- Key point: About three quarters of respondents were from the United States.

### E4

- Location: Methods, Survey Recruitment
- Quote: "the first, aimed at biological anthropologists between February 21st and April 12th 2013 (N = 124); and the second from April 13th to May 10th 2013 (N = 542)"
- Key point: The survey ran in two rounds in 2013.

### E5

- Location: Results
- Quote: "Fewer than half of survey respondents recalled ever encountering a code of conduct at any of the field sites at which they had worked (N = 251/666, 37.7%)."
- Key point: Only 37.7% of respondents recalled ever seeing a code of conduct at any field site where they had worked.

### E6

- Location: Results
- Quote: "Among survey respondents, harassment and assault at field sites were overwhelmingly aimed at trainees (students at all stages and postdocs) and employees"
- Key point: Among respondents, harassment and assault in the field were overwhelmingly aimed at trainees (students and postdocs) and employees.

### E7

- Location: Discussion
- Quote: "Given the retrospective, snowball sampling methodology, our study is not able to determine the prevalence of these negative experiences within or across disciplines"
- Key point: The authors note that a retrospective, snowball-sampled survey like this cannot estimate prevalence within or across disciplines.

### E8

- Location: Discussion
- Quote: "Trainees sometimes work at field sites supervised by organizations not affiliated with their home institution." / "can raise additional complexities in establishing norms of behavior in the absence of an explicit, enforced policy."
- Key point: Trainees sometimes work at field sites run by organizations other than their home institution; without an explicit, enforced policy, norms of behavior are harder to establish.

### E9

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "Few respondents were aware of mechanisms to report incidents; most who did report were unsatisfied with the outcome."
- Key point: Few respondents knew how to report incidents, and most of those who did report were unsatisfied with the outcome.
