# EarthArXiv: Frequently Asked Questions

- Source: `eartharxivfaq`, [Original](https://eartharxiv.github.io/faq.html)
- Version: EarthArXiv FAQ page (CC BY 2.0), read 2026-10-06. Some answers were written around the time AGU launched its preprint server ESSOAr; the content is undated. Locations give question titles.

### E1

- Location: How might preprints and post-prints (peer reviewed articles) affect citations?
- Quote: "You will be unable to delete the preprint file, but you can update or modify it."
- Key point: A preprint cannot be deleted once posted, only updated.

### E2

- Location: How might preprints and post-prints (peer reviewed articles) affect citations?
- Quote: "The preprint and the peer reviewed article are considered distinct works - thus, unique DOIs - and citations can be made to either."
- Key point: The preprint and the peer-reviewed article are distinct works, each with its own DOI, and either can be cited.

### E3

- Location: How do I know if the journal I am submitting to will not object to a preprint/postprint
- Quote: "we strongly encourage authors to also check the specific copyright and sharing information provided by their journal of choice."
- Key point: Check the target journal's copyright and sharing policy yourself.

### E4

- Location: How do I know if the journal I am submitting to will not object to a preprint/postprint
- Quote: "if you have submitted your manuscript to EarthArXiv after submitting it to a journal, and the journal rejects publication of your manuscript your published preprint will remain public on EarthArXiv."
- Key point: If the journal rejects the paper, the preprint stays public.

### E5

- Location: How do I know if the journal I am submitting to will not object to a preprint/postprint
- Quote: "check that all the journals that your article might cascade through before ultimately being published accept preprints"
- Key point: Before posting a preprint, check every journal the paper might cascade through and confirm that each accepts preprints.

### E6

- Location: Is there now a competition between ESSOAr and EarthArXiv?
- Quote: "The AGU's Earth and Space Science Open Archive (ESSOAr) presents an alternative place to publish."
- Key point: The AGU's Earth and Space Science Open Archive (ESSOAr) is an alternative preprint server.

### E7

- Location: Can I submit related materials along with my paper, e.g. data and code?
- Quote: "EarthArXiv does not currently host the supplementary materials, but we do provide options to link your preprint to materials hosted elsewhere."
- Key point: EarthArXiv does not host supplementary materials; put data and code elsewhere and link to them.
