# China 2024: The Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress concludes its ninth session (news on the National People's Congress English website)

- Source: `npc2024legislature`, [original (National People's Congress English website)](https://subsites.chinadaily.com.cn/npc/2024-04/28/c_983112.htm)
- Version: Xinhua English report "Top legislature concludes standing committee session," reposted on April 28, 2024, on the NPC's English website (run by China Daily). It only says that the session adopted decisions amending three laws, including the Biosecurity Law, without listing which articles changed. Locations give paragraphs.

### E1

- Location: paragraphs 1–2
- Quote: "The Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress (NPC) concluded its ninth session Friday in Beijing. At the closing meeting, lawmakers voted to adopt an academic degrees law, a tariff law, and a decision to amend the Law on the Popularization of Agricultural Technology, the Law on the Protection of Minors, and the Biosecurity Law."
- Key point: At the close of the ninth session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress, lawmakers voted to adopt decisions amending the Agricultural Technology Extension Law, the Law on the Protection of Minors, and the Biosecurity Law (the photo caption dates the closing meeting to April 26, 2024).
