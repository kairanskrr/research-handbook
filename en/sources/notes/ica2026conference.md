# International Communication Association: 76th Annual Conference (ICA26) submission guidelines and submission types

- Source: `ica2026conference`, [submission guidelines](https://www.icahdq.org/page/conf-submission-guidelines), [submission types](https://www.icahdq.org/page/conf-submission-types)
- Version: Two web pages for the 76th Annual Conference of the International Communication Association (ICA) (4-8 June 2026, Cape Town, South Africa). The official site returns a human-verification page to scripts, so we read Internet Archive snapshots: submission guidelines of 2025-10-02 (web.archive.org/web/20251002022040/...), submission types of 2025-09-05 (web.archive.org/web/20250905202618/...). Some text on the submission types page still carries over from the previous conference (e.g., mentions of ICA25). The src file joins the text of both pages, marking page names in square brackets. Locations give the page name and subheading.

### E1

- Location: Submission guidelines, Exclusive Submissions
- Quote: "Each unique paper/proposal may be submitted to only one Division or Interest Group, OR to the theme sessions."
- Key point: Each paper may be submitted to only one Division or Interest Group, or to the theme sessions; no duplicate submissions.

### E2

- Location: Submission guidelines, Exclusive Submissions
- Quote: "Each *submitter* (whomever is entering the individual submission in the system) is limited to three (3) first author peer-reviewed submissions."
- Key point: Each submitter is limited to three first-author peer-reviewed submissions.

### E3

- Location: Submission guidelines, Ethical Considerations
- Quote: "If material in your presentation has been published, presented, or accepted for publication or presentation, this must be disclosed in your paper or proposal and may make your submission ineligible, depending on the Division/Interest Group."
- Key point: Material that has been published, presented, or accepted must be disclosed in the paper; depending on the Division's rules, this may make the submission ineligible.

### E4

- Location: Submission types, Full Paper
- Quote: "Full Paper 8,000 words maximum, not including tables and references, unless Division or Interest Group guidelines state otherwise."
- Key point: Full papers are limited to 8,000 words, not including tables and references, unless Division or Interest Group guidelines say otherwise.

### E5

- Location: Submission types, Preregistration
- Quote: "Preregistration The design and analysis plan for the reported research is preregistered in a public, open-access repository."
- Key point: One submission type is "Preregistration": the design and analysis plan of the research is preregistered in a public, open-access repository.

### E6

- Location: Submission types, Works in Progress
- Quote: "Works in progress reflect ongoing research that has not yet reached full completion stage but has a structure and enough substance (an argument, methods, a reference list, preliminary results, or preliminary conclusions) for review."
- Key point: You can submit a "work in progress": research not yet complete but with enough substance (an argument, methods, references, preliminary results, or preliminary conclusions) to be reviewed.

### E7

- Location: Submission guidelines, Ethical Considerations
- Quote: "If your submission or proposal is accepted for an ICA conference, you have a commitment to register for and attend the conference and perform your assigned role."
- Key point: If your submission is accepted, you are committed to registering, attending, and performing your assigned role.

### E8

- Location: Submission guidelines, Ethical Considerations
- Quote: "Covert fragmented publication (salami strategy) – Occurs when a complex study is broken down into two or more components and each component is analyzed and published as a separate paper."
- Key point: Breaking a complex study into pieces and publishing each as a separate paper without saying so ("salami" publication) is a form of covert self-plagiarism.

### E9

- Location: Submission guidelines, General Guidelines
- Quote: "The main ICA rules are the standards, and then Divisions and Interest Groups can be more strict, so please reach each Div/IG's calls carefully."
- Key point: The main ICA rules are the baseline; Divisions and Interest Groups can be stricter, so read each call carefully.

### E10

- Location: Submission guidelines, Deadline and Conference attendance
- Quote: "All submissions and proposals must be completed online no later than 3 November @ 12:00 noon ICA headquarters time (EDT)." [...] "If your paper or proposal is accepted for presentation at the annual conference, you will be notified in mid-January"
- Key point: The ICA26 deadline is 3 November at 12:00 noon (US Eastern Daylight Time), and acceptance decisions are sent in mid-January of the following year.

### E11

- Location: Submission types, Interdivisional
- Quote: "All submissions will be reviewed by members of a committee comprising reviewers from all 33 divisions and interest groups."
- Key point: Interdivisional submissions are reviewed by a committee of reviewers from all 33 divisions and interest groups (which shows ICA has 33 divisions and interest groups).

### E12

- Location: Submission types, Research Escalator Extended Abstract
- Quote: "The Research Escalator session is a mentorship opportunity for early career scholars, those new to ICA, or scholars with a work-in-progress to receive feedback and support on a project that is being developed"
- Key point: The "Research Escalator" session is a mentorship opportunity for early career scholars, those new to ICA, or scholars with a work in progress to get feedback and support.
