# NHERI@UTexas 2020: Safety Plan

- Source: `nheriutexas2020safety`, [original](https://utexas.designsafe-ci.org/media/filer_public/02/b1/02b1b9f1-9b83-4fe7-ad82-7acc4c959a50/nheriutexas_safety_plan-20200826.pdf)
- Version: Version 2.3, August 2020. Safety manual of the equipment site of the Natural Hazards Engineering Research Infrastructure (NHERI, funded by the US National Science Foundation) at the University of Texas at Austin, which runs field tests with large mobile shaker trucks.

### E1

- Location: §1.1 Management Statement of Safety Policy
- Quote: "Our primary research methodology incorporates the use of large-scale mobile seismic sources, or shaker trucks, in the field at research sites across the country."
- Key point: The site's main research method is using large-scale mobile seismic sources (shaker trucks) at field sites across the United States.

### E2

- Location: §1.1 Management Statement of Safety Policy
- Quote: "A typical NHERI@UTexas project is the culmination of intensive preparation over the course of months or sometimes years. Funding, organization, scheduling and myriad logistical issues can define a strictly limited window of opportunity for successful completion of the project."
- Key point: A typical project at this site is the culmination of months, sometimes years, of preparation; funding, organization, scheduling, and logistics can leave a very narrow window to complete it.

### E3

- Location: §1.7 Job Hazard Analysis
- Quote: "Job hazard analysis is a technique that focuses on job tasks as a way to identify hazards before they occur."
- Key point: Job hazard analysis is a technique that focuses on individual job tasks to identify hazards before they occur.

### E4

- Location: §2.2.1 Safety Preparation Before Field Tests
- Quote: "The Principal Investigator of the user team must perform a survey of the proposed test site to identify all potential hazards of the site and the potential hazards that may be caused by the shaker before field tests, and provide the NHERI@UTexas Project Management Team (PMT) with a description of planned experiments one month before testing commences."
- Key point: Before field testing, the user team's principal investigator must survey the site to identify potential hazards from the site itself and from the shaker trucks, and submit the test plan to the site's project management team one month before testing.

### E5

- Location: §2.2.1 Safety Preparation Before Field Tests
- Quote: "However, the responsibility for the design of the experiment with regards to safety of the test site and structures in and near by the test site rests on the user, and the Principal Investigator of the user team is responsible for obtaining permits for site access and site liability insurance."
- Key point: Responsibility for experiment design regarding the safety of the test site and nearby structures rests with the user; the user team's principal investigator is responsible for site access permits and site liability insurance.

### E6

- Location: §2.2.2 Responsibility In The Field
- Quote: "If vibration-sensitive structures (especially inhabited buildings) are within 50 ft of the shake point, users must consult the owner for permission to shake and are strongly encouraged to perform pre-construction and post-construction photo surveys of these structures."
- Key point: If vibration-sensitive structures (especially inhabited buildings) are within 50 ft of the shake point, users must get the owner's permission, and photographing these structures before and after testing is strongly recommended.

### E7

- Location: §1.12 Safety Self-Inspections
- Quote: "During field operations the Chief Scientist and/or Chief Engineer will conduct a daily "tailgate meeting" safety briefing with all personnel involved in field work."
- Key point: During field operations, the chief scientist or chief engineer holds a daily safety briefing ("tailgate meeting") with all field personnel.

### E8

- Location: §1.12 Safety Self-Inspections
- Quote: "will call an immediate cessation of work and hold a tailgate meeting with all personnel present to determine the corrective action required, and to modify the testing schedule accordingly. A safe and deliberate workflow must always take precedence over schedule concerns."
- Key point: After a misjudgment, accident, equipment failure, or similar event, stop work immediately and hold a meeting to decide the corrective action and adjust the test plan; a safe, sound procedure always takes priority over schedule.

### E9

- Location: §1.12 Safety Self-Inspections
- Quote: "Never make assumptions regarding safety; other people may not be aware that something is wrong or that they are being unsafe."
- Key point: Never make assumptions about safety; others may not realize something is wrong or that they are unsafe.
