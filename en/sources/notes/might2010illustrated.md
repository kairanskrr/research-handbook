# Might 2010: The illustrated guide to a Ph.D.

- Source: `might2010illustrated`, [full text](https://matt.might.net/articles/phd-school-in-pictures/)
- Version: author's website, 2010. A series of pictures showing that a PhD means pushing out a small bump in the boundary of human knowledge.

### E1

- Location: the picture sequence
- Quote: "You push at the boundary for a few years:" / "Until one day, the boundary gives way:"
- Key point: You push at the boundary for a few years, until one day it gives way.

- Location: end of the picture sequence
- Quote: "Of course, the world looks different to you now:" / "So, don't forget the bigger picture:" / "Keep pushing."
- Key point: After your small breakthrough the world looks different to you, but don't forget the bigger picture: keep pushing.
