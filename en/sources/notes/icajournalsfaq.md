# International Communication Association: Journals Frequently Asked Questions

- Source: `icajournalsfaq`, [original web page](https://www.icahdq.org/page/JournalsFAQ)
- Version: Journals FAQ page on the website of the International Communication Association (ICA). The official site returns a human-verification page to scripts, so we read the Internet Archive snapshot of 2025-09-12 (web.archive.org/web/20250912130141/...). Locations give the question headings on the page.

### E1

- Location: How do I submit my research to an ICA journal?
- Quote: "The International Communication Association publishes six major peer reviewed journals: Journal of Communication, Communication Theory, Human Communication Research, Communication, Culture & Critique, Journal of Computer-Mediated Communication, and The Annals of The International Communication Association."
- Key point: ICA publishes six peer-reviewed journals.

### E2

- Location: What style manual do ICA Journals follow?
- Quote: "All submitted manuscripts must be prepared in strict accordance with the 7th edition of the Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association (APA)."
- Key point: Manuscripts for ICA journals must be prepared in strict accordance with the 7th edition of the Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association (APA style).
