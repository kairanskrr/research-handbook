# AERA: Publications FAQs

- Source: `aerapublicationsfaq`, [original](https://www.aera.net/Publications/Publications-FAQs)
- Version: "Publications FAQs" page on the American Educational Research Association website, accessed October 2026.

### E1

- Location: AERA Online Paper Repository — How can I access past conference papers?
- Quote: "AERA’s Online Paper Repository is an open access clearinghouse, with abstracts, of scientific presentations given at AERA annual meetings since 2010." / "AERA does not publish conference proceedings"
- Key point: AERA has an open-access Online Paper Repository with abstracts (many with full papers) of presentations at its annual meetings since 2010; AERA does not publish conference proceedings.

### E2

- Location: AERA Online Paper Repository — Can I submit a paper I presented at the Annual Meeting to a journal for publication consideration?
- Quote: "Authors retain full copyright of their work when uploading a conference paper to AERA’s Online Repository , so they are free to submit it to journals for publication consideration after the annual meeting."
- Key point: Authors keep full copyright of annual meeting papers uploaded to the repository, so they are free to submit them to journals afterward.
