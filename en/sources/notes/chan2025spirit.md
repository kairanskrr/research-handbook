# Chan et al. 2025: SPIRIT 2025 statement: updated guideline for protocols of randomised trials

- Source: `chan2025spirit`, [BMJ](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2024-081477); open-access full text at [PubMed Central PMC12035670](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12035670/)
- Version: BMJ 2025;389:e081477. The same statement was published simultaneously in BMJ, JAMA, Lancet, Nature Medicine, and PLOS Medicine. The web version has no page numbers; locations give the subheading or checklist item number.

### E1

- Location: Table 1, item 20
- Quote: "Strategies for achieving adequate participant enrolment to reach target sample size"
- Key point: The protocol must state the strategies for enrolling enough participants to reach the target sample size.

### E2

- Location: Table 1, item 21b
- Quote: "To reduce predictability of a random sequence, other details of any planned restriction (eg, blocking) should be provided in a separate document that is unavailable to those who enrol participants or assign interventions"
- Key point: To reduce the predictability of the random sequence, details of any restriction such as blocking should go in a separate document that those who enroll participants or assign interventions cannot see.

### E3

- Location: Table 1, item 24c
- Quote: "If blinded, circumstances under which unblinding is permissible, and procedure for revealing a participant’s allocated intervention during the trial"
- Key point: For a blinded trial, the protocol must state when unblinding is permissible and how a participant's allocated intervention is revealed.

### E4

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "As the most important record of planned methods and conduct, a well written protocol has a key role in promoting consistent and rigorous execution by the trial team."
- Key point: The protocol is the most important record of the planned methods and conduct; a well-written protocol helps the team carry out the trial consistently and rigorously.

### E5

- Location: Updated SPIRIT 2025 statement
- Quote: "The SPIRIT 2025 statement comprises a checklist of 34 minimum protocol items (table 1) and a diagram illustrating the schedule of enrolment, interventions, and assessments (fig 1)."
- Key point: SPIRIT 2025 comprises a checklist of 34 minimum protocol items and a diagram of the schedule of enrollment, interventions, and assessments.

### E6

- Location: Table 1, item 32a
- Quote: "Who will obtain informed consent or assent from potential trial participants or authorised proxies, and how"
- Key point: The protocol must state who will obtain informed consent from participants or their authorized proxies, and how.

### E7

- Location: Definition of a randomised trial protocol
- Quote: "The full protocol must be submitted for approval by an REC/IRB before enrolling participants."
- Key point: The full protocol must be approved by a research ethics committee before any participants are enrolled.

### E8

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "Many trial protocols do not adequately describe important elements including the primary outcomes, treatment allocation methods, use of blinding, measurement of adverse events, sample size calculations, data analysis methods" / "Gaps in protocol content can lead to avoidable protocol amendments"
- Key point: Many protocols do not adequately describe the primary outcomes, allocation methods, blinding, measurement of adverse events, sample size calculations, analysis methods, and so on; gaps in protocol content lead to avoidable protocol amendments.

### E9

- Location: Table 1, item 28a
- Quote: "Composition of data monitoring committee (DMC); summary of its role and reporting structure; statement of whether it is independent from the sponsor and funder" / "Alternatively, an explanation of why a DMC is not needed"
- Key point: The protocol must describe the data monitoring committee's composition, role, reporting structure, and whether it is independent of the sponsor and funder; if there is none, it must explain why one is not needed.

### E10

- Location: Table 1, item 28b
- Quote: "Explanation of any interim analyses and stopping guidelines, including who will have access to these interim results and make the final decision to terminate the trial"
- Key point: The protocol must explain any interim analyses and stopping guidelines, including who sees the interim results and who makes the final decision to stop the trial.

### E11

- Location: Table 1, item 25b
- Quote: "Plans to promote participant retention and complete follow-up, including list of any outcome data to be collected for participants who discontinue or deviate from intervention protocols"
- Key point: The protocol must describe plans for participant retention and complete follow-up, including which outcome data will still be collected from participants who discontinue or deviate from the intervention.

### E12

- Location: Definition of a randomised trial protocol
- Quote: "every protocol version should contain a transparent audit trail documenting the dates and descriptions of changes. Important protocol amendments should be reported to REC/IRBs and trial registries as they occur"
- Key point: Every protocol version needs a transparent audit trail of changes (dates and descriptions); important amendments must be reported promptly to ethics committees and trial registries.
