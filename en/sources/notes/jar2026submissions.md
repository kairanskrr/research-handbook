# JAR 2026: Submissions (Basic Requirements and Submission Checklist)

- Source: `jar2026submissions`, [submissions page](https://www.jar-online.net/en/submissions), [basic requirements PDF](https://www.jar-online.net/sites/default/files/2025-12/JAR_submissions_basic-requirements_2026.pdf), [submission checklist PDF](https://www.jar-online.net/sites/default/files/2025-12/JAR_submission_checklist_2026.pdf)
- Version: The 2026 submission requirements and submission checklist of the Journal for Artistic Research (JAR, published by the Society for Artistic Research), plus the notes on its submissions page (read 2026-10-06). They represent this one journal's requirements only.

### E1

- Location: Basic Requirements, Suitability a
- Quote: "the exposition exposes artistic practice as research. This goes beyond simply documenting, describing, or writing about work. It engages with questions and claims about knowledge within practice."
- Key point: A submission must "expose" artistic practice as research: it goes beyond documenting, describing, or writing about the work and engages with questions and claims about knowledge within practice.

### E2

- Location: Basic Requirements, Length
- Quote: "We do not measure suitable length in words, as the journal also accommodates time-based media; instead, we require that a reader be able to access all essential aspects of the exposition in the period of an hour of investigation."
- Key point: JAR does not measure length in words; instead, a reader must be able to access all essential aspects of the exposition in about an hour.

### E3

- Location: Basic Requirements, PhD Research
- Quote: "Simply copying and pasting large sections of the thesis into a journal submission, without contextualization, will not be accepted."
- Key point: Copying and pasting large sections of a PhD thesis into a submission without recontextualizing them will not be accepted.

### E4

- Location: Basic Requirements, PhD Research
- Quote: "We are open to unpublished material emerging during PhD research, while requiring authors to show sensitivity to the difference in readership and intention between thesis and exposition."
- Key point: JAR accepts unpublished material from PhD research but requires authors to be aware that a thesis and an exposition differ in readership and intention.

### E5

- Location: Basic Requirements, Design
- Quote: "The design should support the exposition of practice as research and not simply \"style\" a page."
- Key point: The page design should support the exposition of practice as research, not just "style" the page.

### E6

- Location: Submission checklist, Media files and images
- Quote: "files must be uploaded into the RC rather than embedded using external links, which can break over time. Author's must ensure adequate image/sound/video quality for web display"
- Key point: Media files must be uploaded into the Research Catalogue rather than linked externally, since external links break over time; image, sound, and video quality must be adequate for web display.

### E7

- Location: Submission checklist, Media files and images
- Quote: "Authors should select the appropriate mode of documentation and display for your research (e.g., video vs. images, slide show vs. single image) and test whether media files work on other computers."
- Key point: Authors should choose the right mode of documentation and display for their research (video or images, slide show or single image) and test that media files work on other computers.

### E8

- Location: Submission checklist, Copyright
- Quote: "Copyright: should be secured and noted as needed."
- Key point: Copyright must be secured in advance and noted as needed.

### E9

- Location: Submissions page, General Information
- Quote: "Our format for publishing artistic research, the ‘exposition’, invites authors to combine text, image, film, and audio material on expandable web pages, challenging the dominance of writing in traditional academic research."
- Key point: JAR's format for publishing artistic research is the "exposition," in which authors combine text, image, film, and audio on expandable web pages.

### E10

- Location: Submissions page, General Information
- Quote: "At present, the editorial review process typically takes between eight months and a year from submission to publication."
- Key point: JAR states that the editorial review process currently takes eight months to a year from submission to publication.
