# Chartered Institute for Archaeologists 2023: Standard and universal guidance for archaeological excavation

- Source: `cifa2023excavation`, [Standard PDF](https://www.archaeologists.net/sites/default/files/2024-11/CIfA-Standard-Archaeological-Excavation_2023.pdf), [Universal guidance PDF](https://www.archaeologists.net/sites/default/files/2024-11/CIfA-Universal-Guidance-Archaeological-Excavation_2023.pdf)
- Version: The Standard for archaeological excavation (1 page) and the Universal guidance for archaeological excavation, published by the Chartered Institute for Archaeologists (CIfA) in December 2023. These are professional requirements for its members; each country has its own regulations and guidance. Locations give the document name and clause number.

### E1

- Location: Standard, opening
- Quote: "All archaeological excavations will result in a report, published accounts where appropriate, and a stable, ordered, accessible archive."
- Key point: Every archaeological excavation must produce a report, published accounts where appropriate, and a stable, ordered, accessible archive.

### E2

- Location: Standard, Definition of archaeological excavation
- Quote: "Archaeological excavation involves a programme of intrusive fieldwork with defined research objectives which examines, records and interprets archaeological deposits, features and structures"
- Key point: Archaeological excavation is a program of intrusive fieldwork with defined research objectives that examines, records, and interprets archaeological remains.

### E3

- Location: Universal guidance, 2.4
- Quote: "The proposed project team should have access to suitable expertise and appropriate reference material to assess the significance of remains and any materials recovered"
- Key point: The project team should have access to suitable expertise and reference material to assess the significance of the remains and any materials recovered.

### E4

- Location: Universal guidance, 2.12 (i)
- Quote: "methods of recording, including spatial data collection standards"
- Key point: The project design must state the recording methods, including spatial data collection standards.

### E5

- Location: Universal guidance, 2.12 (o)
- Quote: "staffing (including specialists, both external and in-house)"
- Key point: The project design must state the staffing, including in-house and external specialists.

### E6

- Location: Universal guidance, 5.3
- Quote: "the report should normally contain sufficient data and references to the project archive to permit interpretations to be challenged."
- Key point: The report should contain enough data and references to the project archive for its interpretations to be challenged.

### E7

- Location: Universal guidance, 2.2
- Quote: "The project design should set out the proposed scheme of investigation in enough detail that all relevant parties can understand and agree what will be done"
- Key point: The project design should set out the proposed scheme of investigation in enough detail that all relevant parties can understand and agree what will be done.

### E8

- Location: Universal guidance, 2.12 (l)
- Quote: "archiving strategy, including reference to data management plans, selection strategy and local repository requirements"
- Key point: The project design must include an archiving strategy: data management plans, a selection strategy for what is kept, and local repository requirements.

### E9

- Location: Universal guidance, 2.2
- Quote: "It should provide a benchmark against which the results of the work can be monitored and measured. Templates should therefore be used with care."
- Key point: The project design is the benchmark against which the results of the work are monitored and measured, so use templates with care.

### E10

- Location: Universal guidance, 2.12 (h)
- Quote: "methods of investigation, including environmental sampling and scientific dating strategies, where appropriate"
- Key point: The project design must state the methods of investigation, including environmental sampling and scientific dating strategies where appropriate.

### E11

- Location: Universal guidance, 2.9
- Quote: "Any variations to the project design should be circulated and understood within the project team and agreed in writing by all relevant parties."
- Key point: Any change to the project design must be circulated within the team and agreed in writing by all relevant parties.

### E12

- Location: Universal guidance, 1.4
- Quote: "Due regard should also be given to the jurisdiction-specific guidance on archaeological excavation to ensure the work undertaken is in accordance with the legislation and policy of the country or jurisdiction of the place of work."
- Key point: Work must also follow the guidance, legislation, and policy of the country or jurisdiction where it takes place.

### E13

- Location: Universal guidance, 3.3
- Quote: "consideration should be given to the processing of environmental samples at the fieldwork stage, where appropriate, to support the assessment of significance and preservation."
- Key point: Where appropriate, process environmental samples during fieldwork to support the assessment of the site's significance and preservation.

### E14

- Location: Universal guidance, 3.4
- Quote: "Following (or where suitable, during) fieldwork, the findings should be assessed against the project design to determine the extent to which the research aims have been met or could be met and to identify any new research questions to be incorporated in an updated project design."
- Key point: After fieldwork (or during it, where suitable), assess the findings against the project design: how far the research aims have been or could be met. Identify any new research questions and add them to an updated project design.

### E15

- Location: Universal guidance, 4.5
- Quote: "The report should include a statement of the quantity and perceived quality of the data in the site archive, a statement of the archaeological potential of the data to answer the project research aims, and recommendations for the analysis and data storage and curation requirements."
- Key point: The post-excavation assessment report should state the quantity and perceived quality of the data in the site archive, the data's potential to answer the research aims, and recommendations for further analysis and data retention.

### E16

- Location: Universal guidance, 4.3
- Quote: "If material is identified which would merit further study, the project design should be updated, even if the research aims remain unchanged."
- Key point: If the assessment identifies material that merits further study, update the project design even if the research aims are unchanged.

### E17

- Location: Universal guidance, 5.3
- Quote: "the report should normally contain sufficient data and references to the project archive to permit interpretations to be challenged. Similarly, reports should normally integrate the results of specialist analysis with the site sequence, to ensure that important data are not overlooked"
- Key point: The report should contain enough data and references to the archive for its interpretations to be challenged, and it should integrate specialist analyses with the site's stratigraphic sequence so that important data are not missed.

### E18

- Location: Universal guidance, 5.4
- Quote: "Subject to any reasonable contractual requirements on confidentiality, copies of the report should be submitted to an appropriate national or local record in accordance with the stated timeframe outlined in the project design."
- Key point: Subject to reasonable confidentiality agreements, the report must be submitted to the national or local record within the timeframe set in the project design.

### E19

- Location: Universal guidance, 5.5
- Quote: "The digital report should be made available online to ensure that the results of the project are readily available to support public dissemination, future research and/or decisions about the site or asset."
- Key point: The digital report should be made available online to support public dissemination, future research, and decisions about the site.

### E20

- Location: Universal guidance, 1.5
- Quote: "Departures from the universally applicable or jurisdiction-specific guidance should be undertaken with caution. Clear justification is required, with the reasons formally documented"
- Key point: Depart from the universal or local guidance only with caution, a clear justification, and the reasons formally documented.

### E21

- Location: Universal guidance, 3.2
- Quote: "It should be clear that the nature and scale of post-investigation analyses, publications and the archive will be tailored to the significance and research potential of the assets."
- Key point: The scale of post-excavation analysis, publication, and archiving should match the significance and research potential of the remains.
