# US Environmental Protection Agency 2016: Definition and Procedure for the Determination of the Method Detection Limit, Revision 2

- Source: `epa2016mdl`, [original PDF](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2016-12/documents/mdl-procedure_rev2_12-13-2016.pdf)
- Version: EPA 821-R-16-006, December 2016. A stand-alone version of Revision 2 of the method detection limit procedure in 40 CFR 136 Appendix B (US federal regulations on Clean Water Act analytical methods). Locations give the PDF page.

### E1

- Location: cover note, PDF p. 1
- Quote: "This document contains the text of Revision 2 of the method detection limit procedure from 40 CFR 136 Appendix B; but formatted as a more user friendly stand-alone document."
- Key point: This document is a stand-alone version of Revision 2 of the method detection limit procedure in 40 CFR 136 Appendix B.

### E2

- Location: Definition, PDF p. 3
- Quote: "The method detection limit (MDL) is defined as the minimum measured concentration of a substance that can be reported with 99% confidence that the measured concentration is distinguishable from method blank results."
- Key point: The method detection limit (MDL) is the minimum measured concentration that can be reported with 99% confidence that it is distinguishable from method blank results.

### E3

- Location: Scope and Application, PDF p. 3
- Quote: "It is essential that all sample processing steps used by the laboratory be included in the determination of the method detection limit."
- Key point: All sample processing steps used by the laboratory must be included in determining the method detection limit.

### E4

- Location: Procedure (2)(b), PDF p. 4
- Quote: "Process a minimum of seven spiked samples and seven method blank samples through all steps of the method. The samples used for the MDL must be prepared in at least three batches on three separate calendar dates and analyzed on three separate calendar dates."
- Key point: Process at least seven spiked samples and seven method blanks through all steps of the method, prepared and analyzed in at least three batches on three separate days.

### E5

- Location: Procedure (2)(b), PDF p. 4
- Quote: "Statistical outlier removal procedures should not be used to remove data for the initial MDL determination, since the total number of observations is small and the purpose of the MDL procedure is to capture routine method variability."
- Key point: Do not use statistical outlier removal for the initial MDL determination, because the number of observations is small and the procedure is meant to capture the method's routine variability.

### E6

- Location: Procedure (2)(e), PDF p. 5
- Quote: "Select the greater of MDLs or MDLb as the initial MDL."
- Key point: Calculate one MDL from the spiked samples and one from the method blanks, and take the greater as the initial MDL.

### E7

- Location: Procedure (4)(a), PDF p. 6
- Quote: "At least once every thirteen months, re-calculate MDLs and MDLb from the collected spiked samples and method blank results using the equations in Section 2."
- Key point: At least once every thirteen months, recalculate the MDLs from the accumulated spiked sample and method blank results.
