# University of Chicago Law Review: Submissions

- Source: `uclrsubmissions`, [original web page](https://lawreview.uchicago.edu/submissions)
- Version: Accessed October 6, 2026. The University of Chicago Law Review is a student-edited journal of the University of Chicago Law School in the United States. Locations give the web page subheading.

### E1

- Location: Submissions to The Law Review
- Quote: "accepts submissions through Scholastica."
- Key point: The journal accepts submissions through Scholastica (a submission management platform).

### E2

- Location: Selection
- Quote: "The Law Review occasionally solicits feedback on submissions from scholars who are expert in their field."
- Key point: The journal occasionally asks expert scholars in the field for feedback on submissions.

### E3

- Location: Expedited Review
- Quote: "Please let us know if you have received an offer of publication from another journal and would like us to expedite our review of your article."
- Key point: If you have received an offer of publication from another journal, you can ask the journal to expedite its review.

### E4

- Location: Withdrawal
- Quote: "We appreciate if you are able to let us know that you have decided to withdraw your article from consideration."
- Key point: The journal asks authors to let it know when they decide to withdraw an article.

### E5

- Location: Light Edit Policy
- Quote: "The editors believe strongly that both the argument and the voice of a manuscript should be the creations of the author."
- Key point: The journal practices "light editing": it holds that both the argument and the voice of a manuscript belong to the author.
