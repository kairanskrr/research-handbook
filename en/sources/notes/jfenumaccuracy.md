# Journal of Fluids Engineering: Editorial Policy Statement on the Control of Numerical Accuracy

- Source: `jfenumaccuracy`, [original](https://www.asme.org/wwwasmeorg/media/ResourceFiles/Shop/Journals/JFENumAccuracy.pdf)
- Version: PDF on the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) website containing the Journal of Fluids Engineering's editorial policy statement on the control of numerical accuracy (ten criteria and a shortened version); the same PDF appends the discretization error estimation procedure of Celik et al. 2008 (see `celik2008procedure`). Read 2026-10-06. Locations give item numbers.

### E1

- Location: Shortened statement, first paragraph
- Quote: "The Journal of Fluids Engineering will not consider any paper reporting the numerical solution of a fluids engineering problem that fails to address the task of systematic truncation error testing and accuracy estimation."
- Key point: The Journal of Fluids Engineering will not consider numerical papers that fail to address systematic truncation error testing and accuracy estimation.

### E2

- Location: Item 4
- Quote: "Solutions over a range of significantly different grid resolutions should be presented to demonstrate grid-independent or grid-convergent results."
- Key point: Present solutions on a range of significantly different grid resolutions to demonstrate grid-independent or grid-convergent results.

### E3

- Location: Item 10
- Quote: "Comparison with reliable experimental results is appropriate, provided experimental uncertainty is established. However, “reasonable agreement” with experimental data alone will not be enough to justify a given single-grid calculation, especially if adjustable parameters are involved."
- Key point: Comparison with reliable experimental results is appropriate if the experimental uncertainty is established; but "reasonable agreement" with experiment on a single grid is not enough, especially when there are adjustable parameters.

### E4

- Location: Item 1
- Quote: "It is not enough to state, for example, that the method is based on a “conservative finite-volume formulation,” giving then a reference to a general CFD textbook."
- Key point: Describe the numerical method specifically (including the formal order of accuracy of each term); stating "conservative finite-volume formulation" and citing a general textbook is not enough.

### E5

- Location: Note after the ten items
- Quote: "We recognize that the effort to perform a thorough study of numerical accuracy may be great and that many practical engineering calculations will continue to be performed by first order methods, on a single fixed grid. However, such analyses would not be appropriate for presentation in this archival journal."
- Key point: The editors acknowledge that a thorough accuracy study can take great effort and that many practical engineering calculations will still use first-order methods on a single grid; but such analyses are not appropriate for the journal.

### E6

- Location: Item 5
- Quote: "Stopping criteria for iterative calculations need to be precisely explained. Estimates must be given for the corresponding convergence error."
- Key point: State the stopping criteria for iterative calculations precisely and give estimates of the corresponding convergence error.
