# arXiv: About arXiv

- Source: `arxivabout`, [original](https://info.arxiv.org/about/index.html)
- Version: Official arXiv about page, read 2026-10-06.

### E1

- Location: About arXiv, para. 4
- Quote: "arXiv was founded by Paul Ginsparg in 1991"
- Key point: arXiv was founded by Paul Ginsparg in 1991.

### E2

- Location: About arXiv, last paragraph
- Quote: "Submissions to arXiv are subject to a moderation process that classifies material as topical to the subject area and checks for scholarly value. Material is not peer-reviewed by arXiv"
- Key point: arXiv moderation only checks whether a submission is topical to the subject area and has scholarly value; it is not peer review.

### E3

- Location: About arXiv, last paragraph
- Quote: "the contents of arXiv submissions are wholly the responsibility of the submitter"
- Key point: The contents of arXiv submissions are wholly the submitter's responsibility.
