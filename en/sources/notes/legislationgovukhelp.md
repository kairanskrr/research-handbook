# legislation.gov.uk: Help page (on revised legislation)

- Source: `legislationgovukhelp`, [web page](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/help) (the site returns an empty page to scripts; read via an Internet Archive snapshot of 2026-09-28)
- Version: help page of legislation.gov.uk, the UK's official legislation website run by The National Archives. It concerns updates to **UK** legislation. Locations give the question heading.

### E1

- Location: Q. How will I know if the legislation I am viewing is up to date or if there are changes (e.g. effects or amendments) that have not yet been applied to it?
- Quote: "This will either state that there are no known outstanding changes to the legislation you are viewing or that there are some outstanding changes yet to be applied by the Legislation.gov.uk Editorial Team."
- Key point: When you open revised legislation, the "Changes to legislation" notice at the top of the page tells you whether there are outstanding changes not yet incorporated into the text.

### E2

- Location: Q. How up to date is the revised content on this website?
- Quote: "Our aim is to update revised legislation with new amendments within a maximum of three months of the coming into force date of the new amendments."
- Key point: The site aims to incorporate new amendments into the revised text within a maximum of three months of their coming into force, so the online text may temporarily lag behind the law in force.

### E3

- Location: Q. What legislation is held on legislation.gov.uk?
- Quote: "In some cases we only have the original published (as enacted) version and no revised version."
- Key point: For some legislation the site has only the original published (as enacted) version and no revised version.

### E4

- Location: Q. What legislation is available as revised?
- Quote: "Legislation.gov.uk has revised versions of secondary legislation made in 2014 onwards."
- Key point: The site has revised versions of secondary legislation (e.g., statutory instruments) made in 2014 onwards; for earlier years coverage is only partial.
