# US Office of Research Integrity: Whistleblowers (Handling Misconduct - Complainant)

- Source: `oriwhistleblowers`, [original](https://ori.hhs.gov/whistleblowers)
- Version: Web page of the Office of Research Integrity (ORI), US Department of Health and Human Services; applies to research funded by the US Public Health Service (PHS). Undated; read 2026-10-08.

### E1

- Location: Handling Misconduct - Complainant, paragraph 1
- Quote: "The complainant (whistleblower) is an essential element in the effort to protect the integrity of PHS supported research because researchers do not call attention to their own misconduct." / "Prior to making an allegation of research misconduct a complainant should carefully study the policy established by their institution for responding to such allegations to: determine what information should be included in the allegation, to whom the allegation should be reported, what protections are provided for the complainant, and what role the complainant will play in the ensuing proceedings."
- Key point: This page concerns research funded by the US Public Health Service (PHS). Before reporting research misconduct, read your institution's policies for handling such allegations carefully: what information to provide, whom to report to, what protections whistleblowers have, and what role they play afterward.

### E2

- Location: Paragraph 2
- Quote: "Research misconduct allegations should be made to the institutional official named to receive such allegations in the institutional policy for responding to research misconduct allegations."
- Key point: Make allegations to the institutional official named in the institution's research misconduct policy to receive them.

### E3

- Location: Paragraph 3
- Quote: "A good faith allegation is made with the honest belief that research misconduct may have occurred. An allegation is not in good faith if it is made with reckless disregard for or willful ignorance of facts that would disprove the allegation."
- Key point: A good-faith allegation is made with the honest belief that misconduct may have occurred; an allegation made with reckless disregard for, or willful ignorance of, facts that would disprove it is not in good faith.

### E4

- Location: Paragraph 3
- Quote: "the PHS Policies on Research Misconduct (42 C.F.R. 93.300) obligates institutions to" / "take all reasonable and practical steps to protect the positions and reputations of good faith complainants, witnesses and committee members and protect them from retaliation by respondents and other institutional members."
- Key point: The PHS Policies on Research Misconduct require institutions to take all reasonable and practical steps to protect the positions and reputations of good-faith complainants and witnesses and to protect them from retaliation.

### E5

- Location: Paragraph 4
- Quote: "it is the responsibility of the investigative body and ORI, not the complainant, to ensure that the allegation is thoroughly and competently investigated to resolution."
- Key point: Making sure the allegation is thoroughly investigated is the responsibility of the investigative body and ORI, not of the complainant.
