# Riesberg 2018: Reflections on descriptive and documentary adequacy

- Source: `riesberg2018adequacy`, [original](http://hdl.handle.net/10125/24816)
- Version: Language Documentation & Conservation Special Publication 15, pp. 151-156, open access; the author was then at the University of Cologne, Germany, and the Australian National University

### E1

- Location: p. 152
- Quote: "One such idea is that documentation corpora should contain no elicited material at all"
- Key point: The author criticizes the idea that documentation corpora should contain no elicited material at all.

### E2

- Location: p. 152
- Quote: "this is an interpretation of Himmelmann's demand to separate descriptive and documentary activities which clearly overshoots his goal."
- Key point: The author argues this idea misreads Himmelmann's call to separate documentation from description and overshoots his goal.

### E3

- Location: p. 154
- Quote: "this is not achieved by simply uploading an audio or video file to a digital archive"
- Key point: Simply uploading audio or video files to an archive does not let others check your conclusions.
