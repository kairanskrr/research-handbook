# FSE 2025: Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers

- Source: `fse2025ivr`, [original](https://conf.researchr.org/track/fse-2025/fse-2025-ideas-visions-and-reflections)
- Version: call for papers of the Ideas, Visions and Reflections track at FSE 2025 (the ACM International Conference on the Foundations of Software Engineering); read on 2026-10-17. Locations give the page's headings. It reflects only this one software engineering conference's rules for that year.

### E1

- Location: Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers
- Quote: "Bold visions of new directions that may not yet be supported by solid results but rather by a strong and well-motivated scientific intuition."
- Key point: Vision papers propose new directions that may not yet have solid results behind them, resting instead on a well-motivated scientific intuition.

### E2

- Location: Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers
- Quote: "Bold revisits of current research directions that may be somehow misguided."
- Key point: Reflection papers can boldly revisit current research directions that may have gone astray.

### E3

- Location: Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers
- Quote: "Submissions must clearly motivate and illustrate initial evidence (type 1) or a rationale (types 2 and 3) for changing current practice and/or research in software engineering."
- Key point: Submissions must make clear why current practice or research should change: new-idea papers give initial evidence, vision and reflection papers give a rationale.

### E4

- Location: Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers
- Quote: "Evaluation results are not required for FSE-IVR papers. Preliminary results for submissions of the first type or a sketch of an evaluation plan for submissions of the second type, could help the reviewers understand the scope of the work better."
- Key point: Evaluation results are not required, but preliminary results or a sketch of an evaluation plan help reviewers understand the scope of the work.

### E5

- Location: Out of Scope
- Quote: "A submission should not be an FSE full research submission lacking an evaluation, or a disguised advertisement for previously published results: An FSE research track submission with slightly less evaluation or lower quality is not a good FSE-IVR paper."
- Key point: A full research paper missing its evaluation, or a disguised advertisement for already published results, does not make a good paper for this track.

### E6

- Location: Evaluation
- Quote: "Value: the problem is worth exploring" / "Impact: the potential for disruption of current practice" / "Originality: the novelty of the paper insight" / "Scholarship: appropriate consideration of relevant literature" / "Quality: overall paper quality"
- Key point: All papers are reviewed on value, impact, originality, scholarship (appropriate consideration of related literature) and overall quality.

### E7

- Location: Evaluation
- Quote: "Visions of the future (type 2) and Reflections on the past (type 3) papers will also be evaluated according to:" / "Validity: soundness of the rationale"
- Key point: Vision and reflection papers are also judged on validity, meaning whether their rationale is sound.

### E8

- Location: How to Submit
- Quote: "must not exceed four pages (not including a one-page bibliography)"
- Key point: Papers are limited to four pages, plus one page of references.

### E9

- Location: Call for Ideas, Visions and Reflections Papers
- Quote: "FSE-IVR publications lay the foundation for strong full-paper publications later on."
- Key point: Papers in this track lay the groundwork for later full papers.
