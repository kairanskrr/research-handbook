# Nature Methods: Aims & Scope

- Source: `naturemethodsaims`, [original](https://www.nature.com/nmeth/aims)
- Version: Aims & Scope page on the website of *Nature Methods* (Springer Nature), accessed 2026-10-08.

### E1

- Location: Aims & Scope, paragraph 3
- Quote: "To enhance the practical relevance of each paper, the description of the method must be accompanied by strong validation, an application to an important biological question and results illustrating its performance in comparison to available approaches."
- Key point: A methods paper needs strong validation, an application to an important biological question, and results comparing its performance with existing methods.

### E2

- Location: Aims & Scope, paragraph 3
- Quote: "Papers are selected for publication based on broad interest, thorough assessments of methodological performance and comprehensive technical descriptions that facilitate immediate application."
- Key point: Papers are selected on three grounds: broad interest, a thorough assessment of methodological performance, and a technical description complete enough for others to use the method right away.
