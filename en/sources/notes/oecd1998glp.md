# OECD 1998: OECD Principles on Good Laboratory Practice (as revised in 1997)

- Source: `oecd1998glp`, [original PDF](https://one.oecd.org/document/env/mc/chem(98)17/en/pdf) (the official site returned 403 to scripts; text taken from an Internet Archive snapshot of the same PDF)
- Version: No. 1 in the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) Series on Principles of Good Laboratory Practice and Compliance Monitoring, document ENV/MC/CHEM(98)17, published 1998, freely available. Locations give section numbers.

### E1

- Location: Section I, 2.1
- Quote: "Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) is a quality system concerned with the organisational process and the conditions under which non-clinical health and environmental safety studies are planned, performed, monitored, recorded, archived and reported."
- Key point: Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) is a quality system governing how non-clinical health and environmental safety studies are planned, performed, monitored, recorded, archived, and reported.

### E2

- Location: Section I, 1. Scope
- Quote: "Unless specifically exempted by national legislation, these Principles of Good Laboratory Practice apply to all non-clinical health and environmental safety studies required by regulations for the purpose of registering or licensing pharmaceuticals, pesticides, food and feed additives, cosmetic products, veterinary drug products and similar products, and for the regulation of industrial chemicals."
- Key point: Scope: non-clinical safety studies required by regulations for registering or licensing pharmaceuticals, pesticides, food and feed additives, cosmetics, veterinary drugs, and similar products, and for regulating industrial chemicals (unless exempted by national legislation).

### E3

- Location: Preface
- Quote: "These Principles of GLP were adopted by the OECD Council in 1981, as an Annex to the Council Decision on the Mutual Acceptance of Data in the Assessment of Chemicals"
- Key point: The OECD Council adopted these principles in 1981 as an annex to the Council Decision on the Mutual Acceptance of Data in the Assessment of Chemicals.

### E4

- Location: Foreword
- Quote: "The GLP regulations for non-clinical laboratory studies published by the US Food and Drug Administration in 1976 provided the basis for the work of the Expert Group"
- Key point: The OECD principles are based on the GLP regulations for non-clinical laboratory studies published by the US Food and Drug Administration in 1976.

### E5

- Location: Preface
- Quote: "If individual countries can confidently rely on test data developed in other countries, duplicative testing can be avoided, thereby saving time and resources."
- Key point: One aim is for countries to accept each other's test data, avoiding duplicative testing.

### E6

- Location: Section II, 1.2
- Quote: "The Study Director is the single point of study control and has the responsibility for the overall conduct of the study and for its final report."
- Key point: Each study has one Study Director, who is the single point of study control and responsible for the overall conduct of the study and its final report.

### E7

- Location: Section II, 2.1
- Quote: "This individual(s) should not be involved in the conduct of the study being assured."
- Key point: The person doing quality assurance must not be involved in conducting the study being assured.

### E8

- Location: Section II, 8.1
- Quote: "For each study, a written plan should exist prior to the initiation of the study."
- Key point: Every study needs a written study plan before it begins.

### E9

- Location: Section II, 8.1
- Quote: "Deviations from the study plan should be described, explained, acknowledged and dated in a timely fashion by the Study Director and/or Principal Investigator(s) and maintained with the study raw data."
- Key point: Deviations from the study plan must be described, explained, acknowledged, and dated promptly, and kept with the raw data.

### E10

- Location: Section II, 8.3
- Quote: "All data generated during the conduct of the study should be recorded directly, promptly, accurately, and legibly by the individual entering the data. These entries should be signed or initialled and dated."
- Key point: All data must be recorded directly, promptly, accurately, and legibly by the person entering them, and signed or initialed and dated.

### E11

- Location: Section II, 8.3
- Quote: "Any change in the raw data should be made so as not to obscure the previous entry, should indicate the reason for change and should be dated and signed or initialled by the individual making the change."
- Key point: Changes to raw data must not obscure the previous entry and must give the reason, the date, and the signature of the person making the change.

### E12

- Location: Foreword
- Quote: "Its principles are required to be followed by test facilities carrying out studies to be submitted to national authorities for the purposes of assessment of chemicals and other uses relating to the protection of man and the environment."
- Key point: Test facilities conducting studies to be submitted to national authorities for assessment must follow these principles.

### E13

- Location: Section II, 10.1
- Quote: "a) The study plan, raw data, samples of test and reference items, specimens, and the final report of each study;"
- Key point: The study plan, raw data, samples of test and reference items, specimens, and the final report must be archived for the period set by the authorities.

### E14

- Location: Foreword
- Quote: "Organisation and were adopted by Council on 26th November, 1997 [C(97)186/Final], which formally amended Annex II of the 1981 Council Decision."
- Key point: The revised principles were adopted by the OECD Council on November 26, 1997, replacing the annex to the 1981 Decision.
