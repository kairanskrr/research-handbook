# Kurose: 10 pieces of advice I wish my PhD advisor had given me

- Source: `kuroseadvice`, [PDF](https://www.sigcommedu.web.illinois.edu/advice/kurose_wish_advisor.pdf)
- Version: slide deck, year to be confirmed, 24 pages. Page numbers are PDF page order; "#N" is the advice number on the slide.

### E1

- Location: #7 Learn the process of doing research, p. 16
- Quote: "why your advisor can’t (and shouldn’t) solve (or even define) the problem for you"
- Key point: A graduate student is like an apprentice learning the process of doing research, which is why your advisor can't (and shouldn't) solve, or even define, the problem for you.

### E2

- Location: #9 A community of scholars, p. 18
- Quote: "meet people, listen, collaborate"
- Key point: Become part of the community of scholars: meet people, listen, collaborate. Research discussions with fellow students, paper presentations, and practice talks are all part of this.
