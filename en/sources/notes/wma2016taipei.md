# WMA 2016: Declaration of Taipei on Ethical Considerations regarding Health Databases and Biobanks

- Source: `wma2016taipei`, [original](https://www.wma.net/policies-post/wma-declaration-of-taipei-on-ethical-considerations-regarding-health-databases-and-biobanks/)
- Version: Adopted by the World Medical Association (WMA) in 2002 and revised at the 67th General Assembly in Taipei, October 2016. Locations give the paragraph number.

### E1

- Location: Paragraph 3
- Quote: "This Declaration is intended to cover the collection, storage and use of identifiable data and biological material beyond the individual care of patients."
- Key point: The Declaration covers the collection, storage, and use of identifiable data and biological material beyond the individual care of patients.

### E2

- Location: Paragraph 4
- Quote: "A Biobank is a collection of biological material and associated data."
- Key point: A biobank is a collection of biological material and associated data.

### E3

- Location: Paragraph 12
- Quote: "If the data or biological material are collected and stored in a Health Database or a Biobank for multiple and indefinite uses, consent is only valid if the concerned individuals have been adequately informed about:"
- Key point: When samples are stored in a biobank for multiple and indefinite future uses, consent is valid only if people have been adequately informed about the purpose, risks, access rules, privacy protections, governance arrangements, and so on.

### E4

- Location: Paragraph 12
- Quote: "That in case the data and material are made non-identifiable the individual may not be able to know what is done with their data/material and that they will not have the option of withdrawing their consent;"
- Key point: People must be told that once their data and material are made non-identifiable, they may not be able to know how they are used and will no longer be able to withdraw consent.

### E5

- Location: Paragraph 15
- Quote: "Individuals have the right, at any time and without reprisal, to alter their consent or to ask for their identifiable data to be withdrawn from the Health Database and their biological material to be withdrawn from a Biobank."
- Key point: Individuals may at any time, without reprisal, alter their consent or ask for their identifiable data and biological material to be withdrawn.

### E6

- Location: Paragraph 19
- Quote: "In addition the ethics committee must approve use of data and biological material and check whether the consent given at the time of collection is sufficient for the planned use or if other measures have to be taken to protect the donor."
- Key point: The ethics committee must approve the use of data and biological material and check whether the consent given at collection covers the planned use.

### E7

- Location: Paragraph 6
- Quote: "Physicians must consider the ethical, legal and regulatory norms and standards for Health Database and Biobanks in their own countries as well as applicable international norms and standards."
- Key point: Consider both your own country's ethical, legal, and regulatory norms and the applicable international norms.

### E8

- Location: Paragraph 21
- Quote: "Criteria and procedures concerning the access to and the sharing of the health data or biological material including the systematic use of Material Transfer Agreement (MTA) when necessary;"
- Key point: Governance arrangements must set rules for access to and sharing of data and biological material, using Material Transfer Agreements (MTAs) systematically where necessary.

### E9

- Location: Paragraph 7
- Quote: "Consistent with the mandate of WMA, the Declaration is addressed primarily to physicians. The WMA encourages others who are involved in using data or biological material in Health Databases and Biobanks to adopt these principles."
- Key point: The Declaration is addressed primarily to physicians but encourages everyone who uses health databases and biobanks to adopt its principles.
