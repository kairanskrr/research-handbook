# US Institute of Education Sciences 2018: Celebrating the Launch of the Registry of Efficacy and Effectiveness Studies (REES)

- Source: `ies2018rees`, [original](https://ies.ed.gov/learn/blog/celebrating-launch-registry-efficacy-and-effectiveness-studies-rees)
- Version: IES blog post, 31 October 2018, introducing REES, the study registry developed by the Society for Research on Educational Effectiveness (SREE). Locations give the Q&A subheadings in the post.

### E1

- Location: First paragraph of the main text
- Quote: "REES is a registry of causal impact studies in education developed with a grant from the National Center for Education Research (NCER). REES will increase transparency, improve the replicability of studies, and provide easy access to information about completed and ongoing studies."
- Key point: REES is a registry of causal impact studies in education that aims to increase transparency and replicability and make completed and ongoing studies easy to find.

### E2

- Location: First paragraph of the main text
- Quote: "In the FY 2019 Requests for Applications (RFAs) for the Education Research and Special Education Research Grants Programs, IES recommended that applicants describe a plan for pre-registering their studies in both the project narrative (as part of the research) as well as the data management plan."
- Key point: In its FY 2019 requests for applications for education research grants, IES recommended that applicants describe a preregistration plan in both the project narrative and the data management plan.

### E3

- Location: What kinds of studies can be registered?
- Quote: "It accommodates a range of study designs including randomized controlled trials, quasi-experimental designs, regression discontinuity designs, and single case designs."
- Key point: It accommodates randomized controlled trials, quasi-experimental designs, regression discontinuity designs, single case designs, and other designs.

### E4

- Location: What information should be included in a study entry?
- Quote: "A REES entry includes basic study information and a pre-analysis plan."
- Key point: An entry includes basic study information and a pre-analysis plan.

### E5

- Location: What if a study entry needs to be changed?
- Quote: "All updates to an entry will be time-stamped. Original entries and updated entries will be publically available."
- Key point: Every update to an entry is time-stamped, and both original and updated entries are public.
