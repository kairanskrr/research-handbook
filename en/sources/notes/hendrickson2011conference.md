# Hendrickson et al. 2011: Conference Papers and Prior Publication

- Source: `hendrickson2011conference`, [Original](https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)BE.1943-5592.0000294) (this note was read from the Internet Archive snapshot of 2022-08-14: http://web.archive.org/web/20220814212719/http://ascelibrary.org/doi/10.1061/(ASCE)BE.1943-5592.0000294)
- Version: Journal of Bridge Engineering 16(6), 2011 editorial.

### E1

- Location: Paragraph 1
- Quote: "It has been a long-standing policy of the American Society of Civil Engineers that material published in ASCE journals must not have been published elsewhere previously or subsequently, either by the authors or others."
- Key point: A long-standing policy of the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE): material published in ASCE journals must not have been published elsewhere before or after.

### E2

- Location: Paragraph 2
- Quote: "Although it is reasonable to write a journal article that is based on a conference presentation, ASCE expects that the journal article be significantly different from any paper or report appearing in a published proceeding. “Significantly different” generally means that the journal article contains 50% new content with respect to that in the conference proceeding."
- Key point: You may base a journal article on a conference presentation, but it must be "significantly different" from any paper in a published proceeding, which generally means 50% new content relative to the conference paper.

### E3

- Location: Paragraph 3
- Quote: "With the January 2011 annual meeting, the TRB moved to make the annual conference paper “compendium” available on the Internet to attendees and to anyone willing to pay an access fee. As a result, ASCE now regards the TRB Annual Meeting papers as having been published." / "Authors who are submitting papers to the TRB may opt out of being included in the compendium. Doing so reserves the right to submit a paper to ASCE."
- Key point: From the January 2011 annual meeting, the US Transportation Research Board (TRB) made its annual meeting paper compendium available online, so ASCE now regards TRB Annual Meeting papers as published; authors can opt out of the compendium to keep the right to submit to ASCE journals.

### E4

- Location: Paragraph 4
- Quote: "Although this note specifically refers to the TRB’s annual meeting, the policy is true for any conference that is publishing a proceeding. Papers that are posted online are considered published."
- Key point: The policy applies to any conference that publishes proceedings; papers posted online are considered published.
