# ASME: The ASME Criteria for Interpretation of the Canons

- Source: `asme2012canons`, [original](https://www.asme.org/wwwasmeorg/media/ResourceFiles/AboutASME/Get%20Involved/Advocacy/Policy-Publications/CRITERIA-FOR-INTERPRETATION-OF-THE-CANONSJune2012.pdf)
- Version: Criteria for interpretation of the Fundamental Canons of the Code of Ethics of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME), June 2012 edition, free PDF on the ASME website. Locations give section numbers.

### E1

- Location: 2.f.(1)
- Quote: "Technical Communications for publication (theses, articles, papers, reports, etc.) which are based on research involving more than one individual (including students and supervising faculty, industrial supervisor/researcher, or other co-workers) must recognize all significant contributors. Co-authors listed on proposed and accepted publications should have entered the joint authorship arrangement by mutual consent prior to submittal of the document for publication"
- Key point: Papers, theses, and reports based on research involving more than one person (including students and faculty supervisors, industrial supervisors, or researchers) must recognize all significant contributors; co-authors should agree on authorship before submission.

### E2

- Location: 2.g
- Quote: "Engineers shall not engage in plagiarism. The act of substantially using another's ideas or written materials without due credit and advance notification, is unethical."
- Key point: Do not plagiarize; substantially using someone else's ideas or written material without due credit is unethical.

### E3

- Location: 4.l
- Quote: "Before undertaking work for others in which Engineers may make improvements, plans, designs, inventions, or other records which may justify seeking copyrights, patents, or proprietary rights, Engineers shall enter into positive agreements regarding the rights of respective parties."
- Key point: Before doing work for others that may produce improvements, designs, or inventions eligible for copyright, patents, or proprietary rights, agree explicitly with all parties on who holds which rights.

### E4

- Location: 4.j.(1)
- Quote: "They will not disclose confidential information concerning the business affairs or technical processes of any present or former employer or client or bidder under evaluation without consent, unless required by law or court order."
- Key point: Do not disclose confidential information about the business affairs or technical processes of present or former employers or clients without consent, unless required by law or court order.

### E5

- Location: 4.m
- Quote: "Engineers shall admit their own errors when proven wrong and refrain from distorting or altering the facts to justify their mistakes or decisions."
- Key point: Admit your errors when proven wrong, and do not distort facts to justify your mistakes.

### E6

- Location: 1.c.(3)
- Quote: "Whenever Engineers observe conditions, directly related to their employment, which they believe will endanger public safety or health, they shall inform their supervisor and the proper authority of the situation."
- Key point: If you observe conditions directly related to your work that you believe endanger public safety or health, inform your supervisor and the proper authority.
