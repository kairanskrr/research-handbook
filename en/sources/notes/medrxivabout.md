# medRxiv: About medRxiv

- Source: `medrxivabout`, [About medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/about-medrxiv)
- Version: web page, accessed 2026-10-06.

### E1

- Location: About medRxiv
- Quote: "medRxiv (pronounced "med-archive") is a free online archive and distribution server for complete but unpublished manuscripts (preprints) in the medical, clinical, and related health sciences."
- Key point: medRxiv is a free preprint server for the medical, clinical, and related health sciences.

### E2

- Location: About medRxiv
- Quote: "Preprints are preliminary reports of work that have not been certified by peer review. They should not be relied on to guide clinical practice or health-related behavior and should not be reported in news media as established information."
- Key point: Preprints have not been certified by peer review; they should not be relied on to guide clinical practice or health-related behavior, and news media should not report them as established information.

### E3

- Location: About medRxiv
- Quote: "MedRxiv is not intended for case reports (single or in series), narrative reviews, scoping reviews or systematic reviews without meta-analyses"
- Key point: medRxiv does not accept case reports, narrative reviews, scoping reviews, systematic reviews without meta-analyses, and similar article types.

### E4

- Location: About medRxiv
- Quote: "Once posted on medRxiv, manuscripts receive a digital object identifier (DOI), so are discoverable, citable, and indexed by numerous search engines and third-party services and cannot be removed."
- Key point: Once posted, a manuscript gets a permanent identifier (DOI), is citable and indexed, and cannot be removed.
