# Dettmers 2019: Creativity in Academia

- Source: `dettmers2019creativity`, [Original](https://timdettmers.com/2019/09/03/creativity-in-academia/)
- Version: Author's blog, 2019-09-03

### E1

- Location: Middle of the main text
- Quote: "Expertise is important and a requirement for creativity. You need to be able to understand what are valid ideas and which are not."
- Key point: Expertise is a requirement for creativity: you need to be able to tell which ideas are valid and which are not.

### E2

- Location: Middle of the main text
- Quote: "They feel like failures if their ideas fail. But the process of hammering on ideas and not making any progress is the first part of creativity."
- Key point: Many researchers feel like failures when their ideas fail, but hammering on an idea without making progress is itself the first part of creativity.

### E3

- Location: Middle of the main text
- Quote: "The second step is often abandoning the idea for some time." / "But this step can be a critical element of creativity."
- Key point: The second step is often abandoning the idea for a while, and this step can be a critical element of creativity.

### E4

- Location: Middle of the main text
- Quote: "Instead, it is much more effective to work on many ideas."
- Key point: Working on many ideas at once is much more effective than focusing on just one.

### E5

- Location: Middle of the main text
- Quote: "I think to have a sane creative process, it is essential to acknowledge and even embrace this long-winded exhausting struggle with multiple rounds of failure as an essential part of creativity."
- Key point: A sane creative process requires acknowledging, and even embracing, the long, exhausting struggle with repeated failure as an essential part of creativity.
