# American Journal of Political Science: AJPS Verification Policy

- Source: `ajps2026verification`, [original web page](https://ajps.org/ajps-verification-policy/)
- Version: verification policy page on the website of the American Journal of Political Science (AJPS, published by the Midwest Political Science Association), accessed October 2026; the page notes that the related documents are "being updated." Locations give paragraphs.

### E1

- Location: paragraph 1
- Quote: "The corresponding author of a manuscript that is accepted for publication in the American Journal of Political Science must provide materials that are sufficient to enable interested researchers to verify all of the analytic results that are reported in the text and supporting materials."
- Key point: After acceptance, the corresponding author must provide materials sufficient for others to verify all analytic results in the article and appendix.

### E2

- Location: paragraph 1
- Quote: "All verification files must be stored in a Dataset within the AJPS Dataverse, on the Harvard Dataverse Network."
- Key point: All verification files must be stored in the AJPS Dataverse on the Harvard Dataverse Network.

### E3

- Location: paragraph 2
- Quote: "The “American Journal of Political Science Quantitative Data Verification Checklist” and “American Journal of Political Science Qualitative Data Verification Checklist” are designed to help authors ensure that they have provided all necessary files."
- Key point: The journal provides separate verification checklists for quantitative and qualitative data.

### E4

- Location: paragraph 3
- Quote: "When the final draft of the manuscript is submitted, the materials will be verified to confirm that they do, in fact, reproduce the analytic results reported in the article. Publication in the American Journal of Political Science is contingent upon provision of complete verification materials and successful verification of their content."
- Key point: When the final draft is submitted, the materials are verified to confirm that they actually reproduce the reported results; publication depends on the materials being complete and passing verification.

### E5

- Location: paragraph 4
- Quote: "AJPS articles that do not contain empirical data analyses are not subject to this verification policy."
- Key point: Articles without empirical data analyses are not subject to this policy.

### E6

- Location: paragraph 4
- Quote: "All other verification materials (e.g., software commands, etc.) still must be provided. The primary reasons for such exemptions are restricted access to datasets and human subjects protection."
- Key point: Exemptions can be requested for data, but all other verification materials (such as software commands) must still be provided; the main reasons for exemptions are restricted access to data and protection of human subjects.
