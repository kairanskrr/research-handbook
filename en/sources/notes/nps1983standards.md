# US Department of the Interior 1983: Secretary of the Interior's Standards and Guidelines for Archeology and Historic Preservation

- Source: `nps1983standards`, [US National Park Service PDF](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/historicpreservation/upload/standards-guidelines-archeology-historic-preservation.pdf)
- Version: standards and guidelines for archeology and historic preservation issued by the US Secretary of the Interior, published in the Federal Register (vol. 48, no. 190) on September 29, 1983. They apply to historic preservation work at the US federal level. This excerpt uses only the Archeological Documentation standards and guidelines. The PDF is OCR text from a scan, with occasional recognition errors; quotes are reproduced as is.

### E1

- Location: Standards for Archeological Documentation, Standard II
- Quote: "Methods and techniques should be selected so that the results may be verified if necessary. Non-destructive techniques should be used whenever appropriate."
- Key point: Choose methods so that the results can be verified if necessary; use non-destructive techniques whenever appropriate.

### E2

- Location: Guidelines, Documentation Plan, Research Design
- Quote: "Archeological documentation can be carried out only after defining explicit goals and a methodology for reaching them."
- Key point: Archeological documentation can be carried out only after defining explicit goals and a methodology for reaching them.

### E3

- Location: Guidelines, Documentation Plan
- Quote: "Archeological investigations seldom are able to collect and record all possible data."
- Key point: Archeological investigations seldom can collect and record all possible data.

### E4

- Location: Guidelines, Documentation Plan
- Quote: "Responsiveness to the concerns of local groups (e.g., Native American groups with ties to specific properties) that was built into survey and evaluation phases of the preservation plan, should be maintained in archeological investigation, since such activity usually involves site disturbance."
- Key point: Responsiveness to the concerns of local groups (e.g., Native American groups with ties to the site) built into the survey and evaluation phases should continue into excavation, because excavation usually disturbs the site.

### E5

- Location: Guidelines, Field Studies
- Quote: "The implementation of the research design in the field must be flexible enough to accommodate the discovery of new or unexpected data classes or properties, or changing field conditions."
- Key point: Implementing the research design in the field must be flexible enough to accommodate new or unexpected discoveries and changing field conditions.

### E6

- Location: Guidelines, Field Studies
- Quote: "It is thus desirable to maintain some consistency in choice of sampling designs throughout multiple phases of work at the same property."
- Key point: Across multiple phases of work at the same site, keep the sampling designs reasonably consistent.

### E7

- Location: Guidelines, Field Studies
- Quote: "Destructive methods should not be applied to portions or elements of the property if nondestructive methods are practical."
- Key point: Do not use destructive methods where nondestructive methods are practical.

### E8

- Location: Guidelines, Field Studies
- Quote: "The field operation may recover data that may not be fully analyzed; this data, as well as the data analyzed, should be recorded and preserved in a way to facilitate future"
- Key point: Not all data collected in the field will be analyzed; record and preserve both analyzed and unanalyzed data so they can be used in the future.

### E9

- Location: Guidelines, Field Studies
- Quote: "Field records should be maintained in a manner that permits independent interpretation in so far as possible." / "Record-keeping should be standardized in format and level of detail."
- Key point: Keep field records so that others can interpret them independently as far as possible; standardize their format and level of detail.

### E10

- Location: Guidelines, Reporting Results
- Quote: "Some individual property information, such as specific locational data, may be highly sensitive to disclosure, because of the threat of vandalism."
- Key point: Information such as specific locations can be highly sensitive; disclosing it risks looting and vandalism.

### E11

- Location: Guidelines, Curation
- Quote: "Curation of important archeological specimens and records should be provided for in the development of any archeological program or project."
- Key point: Curation of important specimens and records should be planned when any archeological project is developed.
