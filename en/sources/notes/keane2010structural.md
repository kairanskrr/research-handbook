# Keane 2010: A Structural Perspective on the Experimentalist School

- Source: `keane2010structural`, [original](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.24.2.47)
- Version: Journal of Economic Perspectives 24(2): 47–58, 2010. We read only the public abstract on the American Economic Association's article page, not the full text. It represents the author's view.

### E1

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "My view, like Leamer's, is that there is no way to escape the role of assumptions in statistical work, so our conclusions will always be contingent."
- Key point: The author argues that there is no escaping the role of assumptions in statistical work, so conclusions are always contingent.

### E2

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "In marketing, 1) the structural paradigm is dominant, 2) the data are a lot better than in some fields of economics, and 3) there is great emphasis on external validation."
- Key point: The author says that in marketing the structural paradigm dominates, the data are better, and there is great emphasis on external validation (testing a model on data not used to estimate it).

### E3

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "I emphasize that the ability to do controlled experiments does not obviate the need for theory, and finally I address different approaches to model validation."
- Key point: The author stresses that the ability to run controlled experiments does not remove the need for theory, and the article also discusses different approaches to model validation.
