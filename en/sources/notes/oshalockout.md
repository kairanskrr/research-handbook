# OSHA: Control of Hazardous Energy (Lockout/Tagout) — Overview

- Source: `oshalockout`, [original](https://www.osha.gov/control-hazardous-energy)
- Version: Web page of the US Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), a US government website, read 2026-10-06.

### E1

- Location: What is hazardous energy?
- Quote: "During the servicing and maintenance of machines and equipment, the unexpected startup or release of stored energy can result in serious injury or death to workers."
- Key point: During servicing and maintenance of machines and equipment, unexpected startup or release of stored energy (electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, etc.) can cause serious injury or death.

### E2

- Location: What can be done to control hazardous energy?
- Quote: "Proper lockout/tagout (LOTO) practices and procedures safeguard workers from hazardous energy releases."
- Key point: Proper lockout/tagout (LOTO) practices protect workers from hazardous energy releases.
