# American Society of Civil Engineers 2020: ASCE Code of Ethics

- Source: `asce2020code`, [original PDF](https://www.asce.org/-/media/asce-images-and-files/career-and-growth/ethics/documents/asce-code-ethics.pdf)
- Version: Code of Ethics of the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE), PDF (3 pages, listing responsibilities to five groups of stakeholders), most recently updated October 26, 2020 (see E10, from the code's introduction page on the ASCE website, https://www.asce.org/career-growth/ethics/code-of-ethics). Locations give section numbers.

### E1

- Location: Preamble
- Quote: "In the case of a conflict between ethical responsibilities, the five stakeholders are listed in the order of priority. There is no priority of responsibilities within a given stakeholder group with the exception that 1a. takes precedence over all other responsibilities."
- Key point: When responsibilities conflict, the five stakeholder groups are prioritized in the order listed; item 1a takes precedence over all other responsibilities.

### E2

- Location: 1. Society, 1a
- Quote: "first and foremost, protect the health, safety, and welfare of the public;"
- Key point: First and foremost, protect the health, safety, and welfare of the public.

### E3

- Location: 1. Society, 1c
- Quote: "express professional opinions truthfully and only when founded on adequate knowledge and honest conviction;"
- Key point: Express professional opinions truthfully, and only when founded on adequate knowledge and honest conviction.

### E4

- Location: 1. Society, 1g
- Quote: "acknowledge the diverse historical, social, and cultural needs of the community, and incorporate these considerations in their work;"
- Key point: Acknowledge the diverse historical, social, and cultural needs of the community and incorporate them into your work.

### E5

- Location: 2. Natural and Built Environment, 2b
- Quote: "consider and balance societal, environmental, and economic impacts, along with opportunities for improvement, in their work;"
- Key point: Consider and balance societal, environmental, and economic impacts in your work.

### E6

- Location: 4. Clients and Employers, 4b
- Quote: "make clear to clients and employers any real, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest;"
- Key point: Make clear to clients and employers any real, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest.

### E7

- Location: 4. Clients and Employers, 4e
- Quote: "keep clients' and employers' identified proprietary information confidential;"
- Key point: Keep clients' and employers' identified proprietary information confidential.

### E8

- Location: 4. Clients and Employers, 4f
- Quote: "perform services only in areas of their competence;"
- Key point: Perform services only in areas of your competence.

### E9

- Location: footnote on p. 1
- Quote: "This Code does not establish a standard of care, nor should it be interpreted as such."
- Key point: The code does not establish a legal standard of care.

### E10

- Location: Code of Ethics introduction page on the ASCE website
- Quote: "First adopted in 1914, the ASCE Code of Ethics is the model for professional conduct for ASCE members. The Code of Ethics was most recently updated on October 26, 2020."
- Key point: First adopted in 1914, the ASCE Code of Ethics is the model for professional conduct of ASCE members; it was most recently updated on October 26, 2020.

### E11

- Location: Preamble
- Quote: "Members of The American Society of Civil Engineers conduct themselves with integrity and professionalism, and above all else protect and advance the health, safety, and welfare of the public through the practice of Civil Engineering."
- Key point: The preamble asks ASCE members to act with integrity and professionalism and, above all, to protect and advance the health, safety, and welfare of the public.

### E12

- Location: Preamble; 1. Society, a
- Quote: "In the case of a conflict between ethical responsibilities, the five stakeholders are listed in the order of priority. There is no priority of responsibilities within a given stakeholder group with the exception that 1a. takes precedence over all other responsibilities." / "a. first and foremost, protect the health, safety, and welfare of the public;"
- Key point: When ethical responsibilities conflict, the five stakeholder groups are prioritized in the order listed; item 1a, "first and foremost, protect the health, safety, and welfare of the public," takes precedence over all others.

### E13

- Location: 1. Society, h
- Quote: "consider the capabilities, limitations, and implications of current and emerging technologies when part of their work"
- Key point: When current or emerging technologies are part of your work, consider their capabilities, limitations, and implications.

### E14

- Location: 4. Clients and employers, b and c
- Quote: "make clear to clients and employers any real, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest;" / "communicate in a timely manner to clients and employers any risks and limitations related to their work;"
- Key point: Make clear to clients and employers any real, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest, and communicate the risks and limitations of your work in a timely manner.

### E15

- Location: 4. Clients and employers, e and f
- Quote: "keep clients’ and employers’ identified proprietary information confidential;" / "perform services only in areas of their competence;"
- Key point: Keep clients' and employers' identified proprietary information confidential, and perform services only in areas of your competence.

### E16

- Location: 5. Peers, a, b, and c
- Quote: "only take credit for professional work they have personally completed;" / "provide attribution for the work of others;" / "foster health and safety in the workplace;"
- Key point: Take credit only for work you personally completed, attribute the work of others, and foster health and safety in the workplace.
