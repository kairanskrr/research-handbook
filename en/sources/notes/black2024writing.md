# Black 2024: Writing a good scientific paper

- Source: `black2024writing`, [Original](https://perceiving-systems.blog/en/news/writing-a-good-scientific-paper)
- Version: Perceiving Systems blog post, 2024. Web page; locations are given as subheadings. The original uses computer vision as its example; the author considers the topic relevant to all fields.

### E1

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "First impressions matter. By the end of your intro, the reviewer has likely already decided whether they like the paper or not."
- Key point: By the end of the introduction, the reviewer has likely already decided whether they like the paper; it is hard to win them back after that.

### E2

- Location: The Nugget
- Quote: "The nugget is not your technical contribution. I repeat: it is not your technical contribution. It is the insight that leads to your contribution."
- Key point: A paper must make its key insight (the "nugget") clear: not the technical contribution itself, but the new way of seeing the problem that makes it solvable and leads to the contribution.

### E3

- Location: How to tell a story: Goal, Problem, Solution
- Quote: "Also, a paper should tell one story (it can have subparts). If you have two big ideas and you put them in one paper, people will only remember one of them."
- Key point: A paper should tell one story; if you put two big ideas in one paper, readers will remember only one.

### E4

- Location: Number one writing tip: use fewer words…
- Quote: "Write it early. Very early. You can write an intro before you have any results."
- Key point: Write a rough first draft early, even an introduction before you have results; get the logical structure first and fix the grammar later.

### E5

- Location: Number one writing tip: use fewer words…
- Quote: "All good papers start as bad papers. They get written and rewritten."
- Key point: All good papers start as bad papers; making a paper shorter almost always makes it better.

### E6

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "The best way to write a clear paper is to write a talk first."
- Key point: Prepare a talk first, or explain the work to a friend, and organize the introduction and paper in the order you explained it.

### E7

- Location: How to tell a story: Goal, Problem, Solution
- Quote: "Memorize this: Goal, Problem, Solution."
- Key point: Tell the research story as Goal, Problem, Solution: first make the reader want the goal, then show what stands in the way, then give your solution.
