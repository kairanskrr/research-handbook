# ICML 2025: Call For Position Papers

- Source: `icml2025position`, [original](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPositionPapers)
- Version: call for papers of the position paper track at ICML 2025 (International Conference on Machine Learning); the page footer says it was updated 2025-01-28; read on 2026-10-17. Locations give the page's headings. It reflects only this one machine learning conference's rules for that year.

### E1

- Location: top of page (ICML 2025 Position Paper CFP)
- Quote: "Position papers make an argument for a viewpoint or perspective about what should be done, in contrast to research track papers, which report on advances that have already been accomplished."
- Key point: A position paper argues for a view about what should be done, whereas a research paper reports advances already made.

### E2

- Location: top of page (ICML 2025 Position Paper CFP)
- Quote: "submissions to the position paper track will be judged primarily on whether they present a compelling position that warrants greater exposure within the machine learning community (regardless of whether a reviewer agrees with the position)."
- Key point: Position papers are judged mainly on whether the position is compelling and deserves wider exposure in the community; whether the reviewer agrees with it is not the criterion.

### E3

- Location: top of page (ICML 2025 Position Paper CFP)
- Quote: "The goal of this track is to highlight papers that stimulate (productive, civil) discussion on timely topics that need our community’s input." / "Controversial topics are welcome."
- Key point: The track wants timely topics that stimulate productive, civil discussion, and controversial topics are welcome.

### E4

- Location: top of page (ICML 2025 Position Paper CFP)
- Quote: "Position papers should meet standard ICML expectations for scholarship, including the use of evidence and reasoning to support claims, inclusion of relevant background and context, and the attribution of others’ work via appropriate citations."
- Key point: Position papers must still meet scholarly standards: support claims with evidence and reasoning, give background, and cite others' work properly.

### E5

- Location: Policies and Requirements
- Quote: "The Title should state the position and start with “Position:”." / "The Abstract must identify the paper as a position paper and briefly state the position (e.g., “This position paper argues that <statement of the position>.”)" / "The Introduction must state the position, using bold text."
- Key point: The title states the position, the abstract states it in one sentence, and the introduction states it in bold.

### E6

- Location: Policies and Requirements
- Quote: "These hypothetical paper titles do state a position:" / "\"Position: Stop Research on Psychic Properties of Machine Learning\"" / "while these versions do not:" / "\"Position: A Perspective on Psychic Quantum Atelic Learning\""
- Key point: A title that states a position makes a claim ("stop this kind of research"); a title that only names a topic ("a perspective on some topic") does not.

### E7

- Location: Policies and Requirements
- Quote: "The paper must include an “Alternative Views” section in the main body of the paper (not an appendix) that describes and addresses one or more viable (not strawmen) positions that are opposed to the paper’s position."
- Key point: The main body must include an "Alternative Views" section that describes and responds to one or more viable (not strawman) opposing positions.

### E8

- Location: Policies and Requirements; Reviewing
- Quote: "Papers that describe new research without advocating a position are not responsive to this call and should instead be submitted to the main paper track."
- Key point: Papers that only describe new research without advocating a position do not belong in this track and should go to the main track.

### E9

- Location: Reviewing, Position
- Quote: "Examples include (but are not limited to) an argument in favor of or against a particular research direction (not a particular algorithm or solution), a call to action, a value statement, a policy proposal, or a recommendation for changes to how we conduct and evaluate research."
- Key point: A position can argue for or against a research direction (not a specific algorithm), be a call to action, a value statement, a policy proposal, or a recommendation to change how research is done and evaluated.

### E10

- Location: Reviewing
- Quote: "Support: The paper supports its position with clear reasoning and evidence where appropriate." / "Significance: The paper demonstrates that the topic is important, in terms of scope, impact, timeliness, risks, benefits, etc."
- Key point: Review criteria include support (backing the position with clear reasoning and, where appropriate, evidence) and significance (showing why the topic matters in scope, impact, timeliness, risks and benefits).

### E11

- Location: Reviewing
- Quote: "Discussion potential: The topic is likely to inspire constructive, useful discussion within the ICML community. The reviewer need not agree with the stated position."
- Key point: The "discussion potential" criterion asks whether the topic is likely to spark constructive discussion; reviewers need not agree with the position.

### E12

- Location: Reviewing
- Quote: "Communication quality: The paper is well organized and clearly written." / "Context: The paper includes a discussion of (and citations to) literature and events relevant to the stated position."
- Key point: The criteria also include communication quality (well organized and clearly written) and context (discussing and citing literature and events relevant to the position).

### E13

- Location: Policies and Requirements
- Quote: "The formatting requirements (including 8-page limit), double-blind reviewing, policies (including plagiarism, dual submission, use of generative AI, ethics review, etc.), and deadlines for position papers are identical to those of the main conference"
- Key point: Position papers follow the same formatting (8-page limit), double-blind review, policies and deadlines as the main conference.

### E14

- Location: top of page (ICML 2025 Position Paper CFP)
- Quote: "We encourage you to browse the position papers that were published at ICML 2024 for examples (and ideas to build on, or from which to offer an alternative position)."
- Key point: The call suggests reading the previous year's position papers as examples, and as ideas to build on or argue against.
