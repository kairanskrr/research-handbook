# World Health Organization 2026: One Health (fact sheet)

- Source: `who2026onehealth`, [original](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/one-health)
- Version: World Health Organization (WHO) fact sheet, page dated May 8, 2026, accessed 2026-10-06. Locations give the subheading.

### E1

- Location: Key facts
- Quote: "One Health is an approach to optimize the health of humans, animals, plants and ecosystems by integrating these fields, rather than keeping them separate."
- Key point: One Health is an approach that considers the health of humans, animals, plants, and ecosystems together by integrating these fields, rather than keeping them separate.

### E2

- Location: Key facts
- Quote: "More than 60% of emerging infectious diseases that are reported globally come from animals, both wild and domestic."
- Key point: More than 60% of emerging infectious diseases reported globally come from animals, both wild and domestic.
