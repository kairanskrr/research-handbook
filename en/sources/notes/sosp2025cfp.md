# SOSP 2025: Call for Papers

- Source: `sosp2025cfp`, [original](https://sigops.org/s/conferences/sosp/2025/cfp.html)
- Version: Call for papers of the 31st ACM Symposium on Operating Systems Principles (SOSP 2025), accessed 2026-10-08. Locations give page subheadings. Used only as an example of one top systems conference in one year.

### E1

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "In keeping with SOSP tradition, we will favor work that explores new territory, continues a significant research dialogue, or reflects on experience with or measurements of state-of-the-art implementations."
- Key point: In keeping with SOSP tradition, preference goes to work that opens new areas, continues important research conversations, or reflects on experience with and measurement of state-of-the-art implementations.

### E2

- Location: Submission Criteria
- Quote: "Propose and implement an interesting, compelling solution;" / "Demonstrate the practicality and benefits of the solution;" / "Be accessible to the broader systems community."
- Key point: A good paper proposes and implements a compelling solution, shows that it is practical and beneficial, and is accessible to the wider systems community.

### E3

- Location: Submission Criteria
- Quote: "We encourage submission of groundbreaking work in significant new directions, with the understanding that the evaluation criteria for papers addressing new problems may be different from those continuing a line of work in a more established area."
- Key point: Work that opens new directions is encouraged; papers on new problems may be judged by different standards from papers that continue established directions.

### E4

- Location: Author Response Period
- Quote: "Authors must limit their responses to (a) correcting factual errors in the reviews or (b) directly addressing questions posed by reviewers. In particular, responses must not include new experiments or data, describe additional work completed since submission, or promise additional work to follow."
- Key point: The author response may only correct factual errors in the reviews or directly answer reviewers' questions; it may not add new experiments or data, or promise future work.

### E5

- Location: Format requirements
- Quote: "Submissions may have at most 12 pages of technical content, including all text, figures, tables, etc. Bibliographic references are not included in the 12-page limit."
- Key point: Technical content is limited to 12 pages (including figures and tables), not counting references.

### E6

- Location: Anonymity
- Quote: "If your submission reports on experiences with a system at your institution or organization, you should refer to the system anonymously but describe the properties of the system that are needed to appreciate the work (e.g., size of the user base, volume of requests, etc.)."
- Key point: When reporting experience with your own organization's system, anonymize the system's name, but describe the system characteristics needed to understand the work, such as user scale and request volume.

### E7

- Location: Acceptance
- Quote: "Papers selected by the program committee will receive conditional acceptance, with the final acceptance subject to revisions and approval by a program committee member acting as a shepherd." / "encouraged to supply source code and raw data to help others replicate and understand their results, as part of an artifact evaluation process."
- Key point: Selected papers are first accepted conditionally, and are finally accepted only after a shepherd approves the revisions; authors are encouraged to provide source code and raw data through artifact evaluation.

### E8

- Location: Anonymity
- Quote: "If the name of your project or system is already known to the community (e.g., through arXiv, technical reports, talks, social media posts, or other uses), your SOSP submission must use an anonymized name."
- Key point: If a project or system's name has already been made public in the community, it must be replaced with an anonymous name in the submission.

### E9

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "SOSP takes a broad view of systems and solicits contributions from many fields of systems practice, including: operating systems, file and storage systems, distributed systems, cloud computing, mobile and edge systems, secure and reliable systems, systems aspects of big data and machine learning"
- Key point: SOSP takes a broad view of "systems," soliciting work on operating systems, file and storage systems, distributed systems, cloud computing, mobile and edge systems, secure and reliable systems, systems for big data and machine learning, and more.
