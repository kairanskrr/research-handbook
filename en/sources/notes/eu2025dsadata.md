# European Commission 2025: Delegated Regulation on data access under the Digital Services Act (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2050)

- Source: `eu2025dsadata`, [original (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2025/2050/oj)
- Version: Adopted by the European Commission on 1 July 2025, Official Journal of the European Union L series 2025/2050, 9 October 2025. EUR-Lex returns an empty page to scripts, so this was read from the same Official Journal English XHTML full text provided by the Publications Office of the EU (publications.europa.eu, CELEX 32025R2050). Locations give the article number.

### E1

- Location: title
- Quote: "COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) 2025/2050 of 1 July 2025 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 of the European Parliament and of the Council by laying down the technical conditions and procedures under which providers of very large online platforms and of very large online search engines are to share data with vetted researchers"
- Key point: A delegated regulation adopted by the European Commission on 1 July 2025, supplementing the Digital Services Act by laying down the technical conditions and procedures under which very large online platforms and very large online search engines share data with vetted researchers.

### E2

- Location: Official Journal header; Article 16
- Quote: "Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2025/2050 9.10.2025" / "This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union"
- Key point: Published in the Official Journal on 9 October 2025, it enters into force on the twentieth day following publication.

### E3

- Location: Article 3(1), (2)(b), (5)
- Quote: "1. The Commission shall establish and host a DSA data access portal." / "serve as the central digital point for information on the data access process and facilitate the information exchanges pursuant to this Regulation among applicant researchers, vetted researchers, data providers and Digital Services Coordinators" / "To participate in the data access process, applicant researchers shall have an account on the DSA data access portal."
- Key point: The European Commission establishes and hosts a "DSA data access portal" as the central point for information on the data access process; applicant researchers must open an account on the portal to take part.

### E4

- Location: Article 2(1)
- Quote: "'data access application' means the information and relevant documentation submitted by applicant researchers to the Digital Services Coordinator of establishment or the Digital Services Coordinator of the Member State of the research organisation, to which the principal researcher is affiliated, to obtain the status of 'vetted researcher'"
- Key point: A data access application is submitted to the Digital Services Coordinator of establishment, or to the Digital Services Coordinator of the Member State where the principal researcher's research organization is located, to obtain vetted researcher status.

### E5

- Location: Article 7(1), (2)
- Quote: "Within 80 working days from the submission of a data access application, the Digital Services Coordinator of establishment" [...] "shall decide whether a reasoned request can be formulated" [...] "formulate a reasoned request, submit it to the data provider and notify the principal researcher of the submission of the reasoned request" [...] "inform the principal researcher of the reasons why the reasoned request could not be formulated" / "Where, in duly justified cases, the Digital Services Coordinator of establishment needs additional time to formulate a reasoned request, it shall notify the principal researcher as soon as possible and shall indicate the reasons for the delay as well as a new date"
- Key point: Within 80 working days of an application, the Digital Services Coordinator of establishment must decide whether a reasoned request can be made: if so, it sends the request to the platform and informs the principal researcher; if not, it tells the principal researcher why. If more time is genuinely needed, it must promptly inform the principal researcher of the reason for the delay and the new date.

### E6

- Location: Article 6(4)(c)
- Quote: "a DSA data catalogue, which describes the data assets, that may be accessed for the purposes set out in Article 40(4) of Regulation EU 2022/2065, as well as their data structure and metadata"
- Key point: Platforms must publish on their interfaces a DSA data catalogue describing the data assets that can be requested under Article 40(4), together with their structure and metadata.
