# IEEE S&P 2026: Call for Papers

- Source: `ieeesp2026cfp`, [original](https://sp2026.ieee-security.org/cfpapers.html)
- Version: Call for papers of the 47th IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy (May 2026), read on 2026-10-08. Locations give page subheadings. Used only as an example of one year of one top security conference.

### E1

- Location: Systematization of Knowledge Papers
- Quote: "we solicit systematization of knowledge (SoK) papers that evaluate, systematize, and contextualize existing knowledge" / "Survey papers without such insights are not appropriate and may be rejected without full review." / "they will be accepted based on their treatment of existing work and value to the community, and not based on any new research results they may contain"
- Key point: The conference solicits systematization of knowledge (SoK) papers that evaluate, systematize, and contextualize existing knowledge; ordinary surveys without new insights are not appropriate and may be rejected without full review; SoK papers are accepted for their treatment of existing work and their value to the community, not for new research results.

### E2

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "Theoretical papers must make a convincing case for the relevance of their results to practice."
- Key point: Theoretical papers must make a convincing case for the relevance of their results to practice.

### E3

- Location: Ethics Considerations
- Quote: "All the papers must use a separate and well-marked section titled “Ethics considerations” at the end of their paper to make the relevant disclosures. If there are no Ethics considerations, the body text of the section should be “None”."
- Key point: Every paper must include a separate "Ethics considerations" section at the end; if there are none, the section must say so.

### E4

- Location: Research Ethics Committee
- Quote: "IEEE S&P 2026 has a research ethics committee (REC) that will check papers flagged by reviewers as potentially including ethically fraught research." / "may suggest to the PC Chairs rejection of a paper on ethical grounds"
- Key point: The conference has a research ethics committee that checks papers flagged by reviewers as potentially ethically fraught and may recommend rejection on ethical grounds.

### E5

- Location: Ethical Considerations for Vulnerability Disclosure
- Quote: "the version of the paper submitted for review must discuss in detail the steps the authors have taken or plan to take to address these vulnerabilities." / "Authors are required to disclose vulnerabilities no later than the rebuttal deadline."
- Key point: The submitted version must discuss in detail how the authors have handled or plan to handle any vulnerabilities they found; disclosure must happen no later than the author response deadline.

### E6

- Location: Ethical Considerations for Human Subjects Research
- Quote: "that analyze data derived from human subjects (even anonymized data)" / "Disclose whether the research received an approval or waiver from each of the authors’ institutional ethics review boards (e.g., IRB) if applicable."
- Key point: Research analyzing data from human subjects (even anonymized data) must state whether it received approval or a waiver from each author's institutional ethics review board.

### E7

- Location: Public Meta-Reviews
- Quote: "all accepted papers will be published with a meta-review (< 500 words) in the final PDF that lists: (a) the reasons the PC decided to accept the paper and (b) concerns the PC has with the paper."
- Key point: The final version of each accepted paper includes a public meta-review stating why the committee accepted it and what concerns remain.

### E8

- Location: Page Limit and Formatting
- Quote: "Submitted papers may include up to 13 pages of text and up to 5 pages for references and appendices, totaling no more than 18 pages." / "Reviewers are not required to read appendices."
- Key point: Up to 13 pages of main text plus up to 5 pages of references and appendices, 18 pages in total; reviewers are not required to read appendices.

### E9

- Location: Submission Deadlines & Decisions, Reject
- Quote: "Rejected papers must wait for one year, from the date of original submission, to resubmit to IEEE S&P."
- Key point: Rejected papers must wait one year from the original submission date before resubmitting to the conference.

### E10

- Location: Artifacts
- Quote: "Papers are strongly encouraged to provide artifact repositories that are anonymized as described above. Theoretical papers are strongly encouraged to submit the proofs as artifacts on such repositories at paper submission time"
- Key point: Papers are strongly encouraged to provide anonymized artifact repositories at submission; theoretical papers are strongly encouraged to submit their proofs as artifacts.

### E11

- Location: Important Dates
- Quote: "Paper submission deadline: June 5, 2025" / "Paper submission deadline: November 13, 2025"
- Key point: The 2026 edition has two submission deadlines.

### E12

- Location: Public Meta-Reviews
- Quote: "there is no conditional acceptance so papers submitted will be reviewed as is and accepted based on the material that was submitted at the paper submission deadline."
- Key point: There is no conditional acceptance; papers are reviewed and decided as submitted at the deadline.

### E13

- Location: Opening and Topics of interest
- Quote: "Papers may present advances in the theory, design, implementation, analysis, verification, or empirical evaluation and measurement of secure systems." / "Network security and measurement" / "User studies for security and privacy"
- Key point: Papers may cover the theory, design, implementation, analysis, verification, or empirical evaluation and measurement of secure systems; topics include network security and measurement and user studies for security and privacy.

### E14

- Location: Ethical Considerations for Vulnerability Disclosure
- Quote: "Submitted papers should not include full CVE identifiers in order to preserve the anonymity of the submission."
- Key point: To preserve anonymity, submitted papers should not include full CVE identifiers.

### E15

- Location: Anonymous Submission
- Quote: "When preparing the artifacts repository authors should take extra care to not include authors’ information in the repository or artifacts content, so as not to break the anonymity of the paper submission."
- Key point: When preparing the artifact repository, keep author information out of the repository and its contents so as not to break anonymity.
