# American Sociological Review: submission guidelines

- Source: `asrsubmission`, [Sage submission guidelines PDF](https://www.sagepub.com/docs/default-source/msg/ASR) (web version: https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/ASR)
- Version: Submission guidelines for the American Sociological Review (ASR), flagship journal of the American Sociological Association, published by Sage; downloaded October 6, 2026 (the web version returns a verification page to scripts, so the PDF with the same content was used). Locations give guideline sections.

### E1

- Location: What We Publish
- Quote: "ASR papers tend to combine very strong research designs and analytic methods, general interest framing, and theory development that transcends subfields of sociology. As editors, when we are evaluating whether or not to send a paper out for review, we have three criteria in mind—quality of methods, general sociological appeal, and breadth."
- Key point: ASR papers typically combine very strong research designs and methods, general-interest framing, and theory development that transcends sociology's subfields; editors deciding whether to send a paper out for review look at three things: quality of methods, general appeal to sociology, and breadth.

### E2

- Location: Manuscript Preparation
- Quote: "ASR Articles may not exceed 15,000 words in length including text, references, and footnotes (excluding tables and figures)."
- Key point: ASR articles may not exceed 15,000 words including text, references, and footnotes, excluding tables and figures.

### E3

- Location: Ethics
- Quote: "Submission of a manuscript to another professional journal while it is under review by the ASR is regarded by the ASA as unethical."
- Key point: The American Sociological Association regards submitting a manuscript to another journal while it is under review at ASR as unethical.

### E4

- Location: Anonymized Manuscript
- Quote: "ASR uses anonymous peer reviewers for manuscript evaluation. Delete or rewrite any text that identifies you as the author: when citing your own work, please write “Smith (1992) concluded...,” but do not write “I concluded (Smith 1992)....”"
- Key point: ASR uses anonymous peer review; delete or rewrite text that identifies you, and cite your own work as "Smith (1992) concluded..." rather than "I concluded (Smith 1992)...."

### E5

- Location: Anonymized Manuscript, citation format
- Quote: "Citations in the text should provide the last name of the author(s) and year of publication. Include page numbers for direct quotes or specific passages."
- Key point: In-text citations give the author's last name and year; add page numbers for direct quotes or specific passages (format such as Ramirez and Weiss 1979:239-40).

### E6

- Location: References
- Quote: "References for data sets should include a persistent identifier, such as a Digital Object Identifier (DOI)."
- Key point: References to data sets should include a persistent identifier such as a DOI.

### E7

- Location: Manuscript Submission
- Quote: "ASR does not consider submissions of papers previously rejected at the journal."
- Key point: ASR does not consider papers previously rejected by the journal.
