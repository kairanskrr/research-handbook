# Centre for Reviews and Dissemination: PROSPERO website information pages (2023 archive)

- Source: `crd2023prospero`, [PROSPERO](https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/prospero/); we read the Internet Archive snapshot of January 19, 2023: http://web.archive.org/web/20230119125846/https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/prospero/
- Version: The snapshot contains the text of the home page, "About PROSPERO," the registration guidance, and the eligibility self-check page. The PROSPERO website has since been redesigned; the current pages need scripts to display and could not be read. The official website is authoritative for current rules.

### E1

- Location: Registering a review on PROSPERO
- Quote: "The aim is to provide a comprehensive listing of systematic reviews registered at inception, to help avoid unplanned duplication. By promoting transparency in the process and enabling comparison of reported review findings with what was planned in the protocol PROSPERO also aims to minimise the risk of bias in systematic review."
- Key point: PROSPERO lists systematic reviews registered at inception, to help avoid unplanned duplication and to reduce bias by allowing comparison of what was planned with what was reported.

### E2

- Location: Registering a review on PROSPERO
- Quote: "PROSPERO has been developed and is managed by the Centre for Reviews and Dissemination (CRD) at the University of York and is funded by the UK's National Institute for Health Research (NIHR)."
- Key point: PROSPERO was developed and is managed by the Centre for Reviews and Dissemination at the University of York and funded by the UK's National Institute for Health Research.

### E3

- Location: Inclusion criteria
- Quote: "PROSPERO includes details of any planned or on-going systematic review that has a health related outcome."
- Key point: PROSPERO includes planned or ongoing systematic reviews with a health-related outcome.

### E4

- Location: Requirements for registration
- Quote: "A full protocol should be ready before registering with PROSPERO" / "Submissions must be made before data extraction commences (from October 2019)"
- Key point: Have a full protocol ready before registering; you must submit before data extraction begins (since October 2019).

### E5

- Location: Integrity of data and prospective registration
- Quote: "Reviews should ideally be registered before screening against eligibility criteria commences."
- Key point: Ideally, register before screening against eligibility criteria begins.

### E6

- Location: Requirements for registration, PROSPERO does not accept
- Quote: "Systematic reviews without an outcome of clear relevance to the health of humans" / "Scoping reviews" / "Literature reviews that use a systematic search" / "Systematic reviews assessing sports performance as an outcome"
- Key point: Not accepted: reviews without an outcome clearly relevant to human health, scoping reviews, literature reviews that merely use a systematic search, reviews with sports performance as the outcome, and others.

### E7

- Location: Eligibility self-check page
- Quote: "PROSPERO will not accept registrations unless there is more than one person conducting the review."
- Key point: Registrations are not accepted unless more than one person is conducting the review.

### E8

- Location: Eligibility self-check page
- Quote: "We therefore do not accept registrations from systematic reviews that will not be made available to others e.g. projects that are internal to an organization or company, or masters dissertations if it is known that these will not be shared."
- Key point: Reviews whose results will not be made available are not registered, e.g., internal projects of an organization or master's dissertations known not to be made public.

### E9

- Location: Home page notice
- Quote: "Students: We do not (have the resources to) register reviews done as part of training courses, modules or other 'mini' reviews."
- Key point: "Mini" reviews done for training courses or course modules are not registered.

### E10

- Location: When to register your review
- Quote: "Do not register too early. Your systematic review protocol should be complete before you submit your registration request." / "Registering reviews that are never performed is unhelpful to the research community and may discredit the research team."
- Key point: Do not register too early: finish the protocol first. Registering reviews that are never done does not help the research community and may harm the team's reputation.

### E11

- Location: Other considerations
- Quote: "To avoid duplication of records, Cochrane protocols should not be registered separately with PROSPERO."
- Key point: Cochrane protocols are uploaded automatically and should not be registered separately.
