# AMS: Policy Statement on Ethical Guidelines

- Source: `ams2022ethical`, [original](https://www.ams.org/about-us/governance/policy-statements/sec-ethics)
- Version: Ethical Guidelines of the American Mathematical Society, adopted 2005, revised 2019 and 2022; web page accessed 2026-10-06.

### E1

- Location: I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- Quote: "The knowing presentation of another person's mathematical discovery as one's own constitutes plagiarism and is a serious violation of professional ethics. Plagiarism may occur for any type of work, whether written or oral and whether published or not."
- Key point: Knowingly presenting another person's mathematical discovery as your own is plagiarism, whether written or oral, published or unpublished.

### E2

- Location: I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- Quote: "To give appropriate credit, even to unpublished materials, materials on websites, and announced results (because the knowledge that something is true or false is valuable, however it is obtained);"
- Key point: Give appropriate credit even to unpublished material, material on websites, and announced results.

### E3

- Location: I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- Quote: "To publish full details of results that are announced without unreasonable delay, because claiming a result in advance of its having been achieved with reasonable certainty injures the community by restraining those working toward the same goal;"
- Key point: Once you announce a result, publish the full details without unreasonable delay; claiming a result before you are reasonably sure of it can hinder others working toward the same goal.

### E4

- Location: I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- Quote: "To correct in a timely way or to withdraw work that is erroneous."
- Key point: Correct or withdraw erroneous work in a timely way.

### E5

- Location: I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- Quote: "All the authors listed for a paper, however, must have made a significant contribution to its content, and all who have made such a contribution must be offered the opportunity to be listed as an author."
- Key point: Every listed author must have made a significant contribution to the content, and everyone who made such a contribution must be offered authorship.

### E6

- Location: I. Mathematical Research and its Presentation
- Quote: "A claim of independence may not be based on ignorance of widely disseminated results."
- Key point: A claim of independent discovery may not rest on ignorance of widely disseminated results.

### E7

- Location: II. Social Responsibility of Mathematicians
- Quote: "The appearance of bias in reviewing, refereeing, or in funding decisions must be scrupulously avoided, particularly where decisions may affect one's own research, that of colleagues, or of one's students. When conflicts of interest occur, one should withdraw from the decision-making process."
- Key point: Scrupulously avoid the appearance of bias in reviewing and refereeing; withdraw from decisions where you have a conflict of interest.

### E8

- Location: II. Social Responsibility of Mathematicians
- Quote: "Similarly, a referee's report is normally provided with the understanding that the name of the writer be withheld from certain interested parties, and the referee must be anonymous unless otherwise indicated in advance."
- Key point: Referee reports are normally given on the understanding that the referee's name is withheld from interested parties; referees are anonymous unless otherwise agreed in advance.

### E9

- Location: IV. Publications
- Quote: "Editors are responsible for the timely refereeing of articles and must judge articles by the state of knowledge at the time of submission. Editors should accept a paper for publication only if they are reasonably certain the paper is correct."
- Key point: Editors must judge articles by the state of knowledge at the time of submission, and accept a paper only when reasonably certain it is correct.

### E10

- Location: IV. Publications
- Quote: "Both editors and referees must respect the confidentiality of materials submitted to them unless these materials have previously been made public, and above all may not appropriate to themselves ideas in work submitted to them"
- Key point: Editors and referees must keep submitted material confidential (unless it is already public), and above all must not appropriate ideas from it.

### E11

- Location: IV. Publications
- Quote: "The published article should bear the date on which the manuscript was originally submitted to the journal for publication, together with the dates of any revisions."
- Key point: Published articles should carry the date of original submission and the dates of any revisions.

### E12

- Location: III. Education and Granting of Degrees
- Quote: "In particular, a Ph.D. degree is a certification of both mathematical knowledge and independent achievement in mathematics."
- Key point: A PhD in mathematics certifies both mathematical knowledge and independent achievement in mathematics.

### E13

- Location: III. Education and Granting of Degrees
- Quote: "Mathematicians and organizations involved in advising doctoral students should fully inform them about the employment opportunities, both within and outside academia, that may be available to them upon completion of their degrees."
- Key point: Those advising doctoral students should fully inform them about employment opportunities both within and outside academia.

### E14

- Location: opening (statement about the guidelines)
- Quote: "These are guidelines, not a collection of rigid rules."
- Key point: These are guidelines, not a collection of rigid rules.
