# CERN 2020: CERN Open Data Policy for the LHC Experiments

- Source: `cern2020opendata`, [Original](https://opendata.cern.ch/docs/cern-open-data-policy-for-lhc-experiments)
- Version: CERN Open Data Policy working group, published 2020-12-11 on the CERN Open Data Portal.

### E1

- Location: Published Results (Level 1) Policy
- Quote: "Peer-reviewed publications represent the primary scientific output from the experiments. In compliance with the CERN Open Access Policy, all such publications are available with Open Access"
- Key point: Peer-reviewed publications are the experiments' primary output, and under the CERN Open Access Policy all of them are open access.

### E2

- Location: Published Results (Level 1) Policy
- Quote: "the experiments will make public additional information and data at the time of publication, stored in collaboration with portals such as HEPData"
- Key point: When publishing, the experiments also release additional information and data, stored on portals such as HEPData.

### E3

- Location: Reconstructed Data (Level 3) Policy
- Quote: "with the aim to commence data releases within five years of the conclusion of the run period."
- Key point: For reconstructed data, the aim is to start releases within five years of the end of the run period.

### E4

- Location: Reconstructed Data (Level 3) Policy
- Quote: "External authors should be aware that they will not have access to the vast amount of tacit knowledge built up within the LHC collaborations"
- Key point: People outside the collaborations who use open data should know they lack the vast tacit knowledge built up inside the collaborations over many years.

### E5

- Location: Reconstructed Data (Level 3) Policy
- Quote: "Any scientific claims in such publications are the responsibility of their authors and not of the experiments."
- Key point: Scientific claims in papers that use open data are the responsibility of their authors, not the experiments.

### E6

- Location: Raw Data (Level 4) Policy
- Quote: "It is not practically possible to make the full raw data-set from the LHC experiments usable in a meaningful way outside the collaborations."
- Key point: The full LHC raw data cannot in practice be used meaningfully outside the collaborations.
