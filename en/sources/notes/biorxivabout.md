# bioRxiv: About bioRxiv

- Source: `biorxivabout`, [original](https://www.biorxiv.org/about-biorxiv) (the site blocks scripted access; read from an [Internet Archive snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://www.biorxiv.org/about-biorxiv))
- Version: Official bioRxiv about page, read 2026-10-06. Locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: About bioRxiv
- Quote: "bioRxiv (pronounced "bio-archive") is a free online archive and distribution service for unpublished preprints in the life sciences."
- Key point: bioRxiv is a free online archive and distribution service for unpublished preprints in the life sciences.

### E2

- Location: About bioRxiv
- Quote: "Articles are not peer-reviewed, edited, or typeset before being posted online."
- Key point: Articles are not peer-reviewed, edited, or typeset before being posted.

### E3

- Location: About bioRxiv
- Quote: "An article may be posted prior to, or concurrently with, submission to a journal but should not be posted if it has already been accepted for publication by a journal."
- Key point: An article may be posted before or at the same time as journal submission, but not after it has been accepted by a journal.

### E4

- Location: About bioRxiv
- Quote: "Once posted on bioRxiv, articles are citable and therefore cannot be removed."
- Key point: Once posted, articles are citable and therefore cannot be removed.

### E5

- Location: JOURNAL PREPRINT POLICIES
- Quote: "Most research journals allow posting on preprint servers such as bioRxiv prior to publication." / "Authors should consult these lists and other sources of information before posting on bioRxiv."
- Key point: Most journals allow posting on preprint servers such as bioRxiv before publication; authors should check the target journal's policy before posting.

### E6

- Location: About bioRxiv
- Quote: "By posting preprints on bioRxiv, authors are able to make their findings immediately available to the scientific community and receive feedback on draft manuscripts before they are submitted to journals."
- Key point: Posting a preprint makes findings immediately available to the community and brings feedback before journal submission.
