# ASME: Papers Presented at a Conference

- Source: `asmeconfpapers`, [original](https://www.asme.org/publications-submissions/journals/information-for-authors/journal-guidelines/papers-presented-at-a-conference)
- Version: Author guidelines page for journals of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME), read 2026-10-06. Locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Conferences
- Quote: "ASME currently accepts submissions of papers that have been previously presented at a conference proceeding. Authors are required to identify the conference as part of the submission. A description of modifications made to the paper post-presentation should be included in the Comments box."
- Key point: ASME journals accept papers previously presented at a conference, but you must name the conference and describe the changes made since.

### E2

- Location: Conferences
- Quote: "The review and publication of a paper that was previously presented at a conference is at the discretion of the Journal Editor."
- Key point: Whether such a paper is reviewed and published is at the discretion of the journal editor.

### E3

- Location: ASME Conferences
- Quote: "To avoid dual submission, papers from an ASME conference can only be submitted to a journal after Author Notification of Acceptance."
- Key point: To avoid dual submission, papers from an ASME conference may be submitted to an ASME journal only after the conference acceptance notification.

### E4

- Location: ASME Conferences
- Quote: "Conference papers submitted to an ASME Journal undergo an independent peer-review with the opportunity for the author to provide revision, expansion, and updates based upon the reviews."
- Key point: Conference papers submitted to an ASME journal go through an independent peer review, and authors can revise, expand, and update them based on the reviews.

### E5

- Location: ASME Conferences
- Quote: "A reference to the conference publication should be included as a footnote and reference in the journal paper."
- Key point: The journal version should cite the conference version in a footnote and in the references.
