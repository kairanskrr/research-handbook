# APSR editors: American Political Science Review Editorial Report: Executive Summary (Fall 2023)

- Source: `apsr2024editorial`, [original](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/CFCD95D2AF8F273A6CA9D1ABAD66BD47/core-reader)
- Version: Political Science Today 4(1): 56-60, February 2024, DOI 10.1017/psj.2024.21; adapted from the full report submitted to the APSR editorial board in October 2023. Full text freely available on the Cambridge University Press website.

### E1

- Location: Executive Summary
- Quote: "The current editorial team began their editorship on June 1, 2020."
- Key point: The report's data come from the editorial team that took over in June 2020.

### E2

- Location: Executive Summary
- Quote: "We have reduced the median days from submission to first invitation for peer review from 13 (prior team) to 10; from submission to reject after peer review from 84 (prior team) to 74.5; and from submission to invitation to revise after peer review from 129.5 (prior team) to 89. Our median days from submission to desk reject stands at 10"
- Key point: Median time from submission to rejection after peer review is 74.5 days, to invitation to revise after peer review 89 days, and to desk rejection 10 days.

### E3

- Location: Summary of Submissions and Acceptance Decisions
- Quote: "Our team’s overall final acceptance rate for all submissions that were initially submitted and have a final decision by our team is 7.9%."
- Key point: 7.9% is the team's overall final acceptance rate for all manuscripts first submitted and finally decided during its term, not an acceptance rate for a single year.

### E4

- Location: Workflow and Turnaround Times, note to Table 1 Submissions, Decisions, and Turnaround Times
- Quote: "Note: The table includes manuscripts submitted between 07/01/2008 and 07/31/2023."
- Key point: The turnaround table covers manuscripts submitted from July 1, 2008 to July 31, 2023 (broken down by editorial team), with data through the end of July 2023.
