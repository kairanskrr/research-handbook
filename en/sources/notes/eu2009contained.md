# European Union 2009: Directive 2009/41/EC on the contained use of genetically modified micro-organisms

- Source: `eu2009contained`, [original (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32009L0041)
- Version: Directive 2009/41/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 May 2009 on the contained use of genetically modified micro-organisms, Official Journal of the European Union L 125/75. EUR-Lex returns no content to scripts, so this excerpt was read from an Internet Archive snapshot of 2026-03-16 of the official EUR-Lex HTML page (https://web.archive.org/web/20260316002227/https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32009L0041). Locations give the article number.

### E1

- Location: Article 1
- Quote: "This Directive lays down common measures for the contained use of genetically modified micro-organisms with a view to protecting human health and the environment."
- Key point: The directive lays down common measures for the contained use of genetically modified micro-organisms (GMMs) to protect human health and the environment.

### E2

- Location: Article 2(c)
- Quote: "'contained use' means any activity in which micro-organisms are genetically modified or in which such GMMs are cultured, stored, transported, destroyed, disposed of or used in any other way, and for which specific containment measures are used to limit their contact with, and to provide a high level of safety for, the general population and the environment;"
- Key point: Contained use is any activity in which micro-organisms are genetically modified, or in which such GMMs are cultured, stored, transported, destroyed, disposed of, or otherwise used, with specific containment measures limiting their contact with the general population and the environment.

### E3

- Location: Article 4(2)–(3)
- Quote: "To that end, the user shall carry out an assessment of the contained uses as regards the risks to human health and the environment that those contained uses may pose" / "The assessment referred to in paragraph 2 shall result in the final classification of the contained uses in four classes"
- Key point: The user must assess the risks the contained use may pose to human health and the environment; the assessment places the activity in one of four classes, which determines the containment level.

### E4

- Location: Article 4(3)
- Quote: "activities of no or negligible risk, that is to say activities for which level 1 containment is appropriate to protect human health and the environment." / "activities of high risk, that is to say activities for which level 4 containment is appropriate to protect human health and the environment."
- Key point: Class 1 covers activities of no or negligible risk, with level 1 containment; Class 4 covers high-risk activities, with level 4 containment (in between are Class 2, low risk, and Class 3, moderate risk).

### E5

- Location: Article 4(4)
- Quote: "Where there is doubt as to which class is appropriate for the proposed contained use, the more stringent protective measures shall be applied"
- Key point: Where there is doubt about which class applies, the more stringent protective measures apply.

### E6

- Location: Article 9(2)
- Quote: "A class 3 or higher class of contained use may not proceed without the prior consent of the competent authority"
- Key point: Contained use of Class 3 or higher may not proceed without the prior consent of the competent authority.
