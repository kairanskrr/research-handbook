# NIST: FY 2008 Budget Request Details — National Earthquake Hazards Reduction Program

- Source: `nist2008nehrp`, [original](https://www.nist.gov/congressional-and-legislative-affairs/fy08-national-earthquake-hazards-reduction-program)
- Version: the National Earthquake Hazards Reduction Program (NEHRP) section of the FY2008 budget request of the US National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST); web page created 2009, updated 2025, accessed 2026-10-06. It reflects the situation around 2007-2008.

### E1

- Location: Challenge
- Quote: "Currently, a critical gap exists between the results produced by basic research and the implementation of that knowledge in the field. New construction materials and techniques and building codes and standards do not reflect the current state-of-knowledge."
- Key point: NIST then saw a critical gap between basic research results and their implementation in engineering practice; new construction materials and technologies, building codes, and standards did not reflect current knowledge.

### E2

- Location: Proposed Program; Expected Impacts
- Quote: "Identify implementation gaps between basic research results and design guidance and national model building code provisions;" / "Updated model building codes and standards that incorporate the latest scientific research aimed at reducing the risk to life and property from earthquakes;"
- Key point: NIST's plan included identifying implementation gaps between basic research results and design guidance and national model building code provisions; one expected outcome was updated model building codes and standards that incorporate the latest research.
