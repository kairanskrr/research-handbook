# China 2020: Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China (中华人民共和国生物安全法) (English text on the National People's Congress website)

- Source: `npc2020biosecurity`, [original (National People's Congress English website)](https://subsites.chinadaily.com.cn/npc/2020-10/17/c_703568.htm)
- Version: Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, adopted at the 22nd Meeting of the Standing Committee of the 13th National People's Congress (NPC) on October 17, 2020, in force from April 15, 2021. This excerpt uses the English text (6 pages) published on the NPC's English website (run by China Daily); later amendments were not checked. The authoritative Chinese text is the one on the NPC's official website. Locations give article numbers.

### E1

- Location: Presidential Order No. 58
- Quote: "The Biosecurity Law of the People's Republic of China , adopted at the 22nd Meeting of the Standing Committee of the Thirteenth National People's Congress of the People's Republic of China on October 17, 2020, is hereby promulgated, and shall come into force as of April 15, 2021."
- Key point: The Biosecurity Law was adopted on October 17, 2020, and took effect on April 15, 2021.

### E2

- Location: Article 36
- Quote: "The state adopts classified management as regards biotechnology research and development activities. These activities shall be classified as high-risk, medium-risk, and low-risk activities according to the degree of potential harm to the public health, industry, agriculture, and ecological environment."
- Key point: The state manages biotechnology research and development by category, classifying activities as high-, medium-, or low-risk according to their potential harm to public health, industry, agriculture, and the ecological environment.

### E3

- Location: Article 38
- Quote: "High-risk and medium-risk biotechnology research and development activities may be conducted only by the legal persons lawfully established within the territory of China with approval or after being put on record in accordance with law. Risk assessments shall be conducted when carrying out medium-risk and high-risk biotechnology research and development activities"
- Key point: High- and medium-risk biotechnology research and development may be conducted only by legal persons lawfully established in China, after approval or filing; these activities require a risk assessment.

### E4

- Location: Article 45
- Quote: "The state adopts pathogenic microorganism laboratory hierarchical management in accordance with the level of biosecurity protection in relation to pathogenic microorganism. Pathogenic microorganism experiments shall be conducted in laboratories at the corresponding levels."
- Key point: The state manages pathogenic microorganism laboratories by tiers based on their level of biosecurity protection; experiments with pathogenic microorganisms must be done in a laboratory of the corresponding level.
