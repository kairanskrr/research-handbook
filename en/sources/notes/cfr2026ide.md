# US Code of Federal Regulations 21 CFR Part 812: Investigational Device Exemptions (current version, Subpart A)

- Source: `cfr2026ide`, [Original (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-H/part-812)
- Version: Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR), Title 21, Part 812, Investigational Device Exemptions (IDE), Subpart A (General Provisions), read 2026-10-06 through the eCFR API. Locations are given as paragraph numbers.

### E1

- Location: §812.1(a)
- Quote: "This part provides procedures for the conduct of clinical investigations of devices."
- Key point: Part 812 sets out procedures for conducting clinical investigations of devices.

### E2

- Location: §812.2(a) General
- Quote: "This part applies to all clinical investigations of devices to determine safety and effectiveness, except as provided in paragraph (c) of this section."
- Key point: This part applies to all clinical investigations of devices to determine safety and effectiveness, except as provided in paragraph (c).

### E3

- Location: §812.3(m)
- Quote: "Significant risk device means an investigational device that:" / "Otherwise presents a potential for serious risk to the health, safety, or welfare of a subject."
- Key point: Definition of a "significant risk device": an implant, a life-supporting device, a device of substantial importance in diagnosis or treatment, or any other investigational device that presents a potential for serious risk to a subject's health, safety, or welfare (the first three categories are also conditioned on presenting a potential for serious risk).

### E4

- Location: §812.2(b)(1)(ii)
- Quote: "Obtains IRB approval of the investigation after presenting the reviewing IRB with a brief explanation of why the device is not a significant risk device, and maintains such approval;"
- Key point: Studies of non-significant-risk devices can follow abbreviated requirements; one of them is to give the IRB a brief explanation of why the device is not a significant risk device and obtain its approval.

### E5

- Location: §812.3(o)
- Quote: "Sponsor-investigator means an individual who both initiates and actually conducts, alone or with others, an investigation" / "The obligations of a sponsor-investigator under this part include those of an investigator and those of a sponsor."
- Key point: Someone who both initiates and actually conducts an investigation is a "sponsor-investigator" and carries the obligations of both an investigator and a sponsor.

### E6

- Location: §812.2(c) and (c)(6)
- Quote: "This part, with the exception of § 812.119, does not apply to investigations of the following categories of devices:" / "A device shipped solely for research on or with laboratory animals and labeled in accordance with § 812.5(c)."
- Key point: Apart from §812.119, this part does not apply to devices shipped solely for research with laboratory animals and labeled as required.

### E7

- Location: §812.1(a)
- Quote: "An approved investigational device exemption (IDE) permits a device that otherwise would be required to comply with a performance standard or to have premarket approval to be shipped lawfully for the purpose of conducting investigations of that device." / "good manufacturing practice requirements under section 520(f) except for the requirements found in § 820.10(c), if applicable"
- Key point: An approved investigational device exemption (IDE) lets a device be shipped lawfully for research and exempts it from a range of requirements, including the quality system requirements; §820.10(c) (design and development requirements, where applicable) is not exempted.
