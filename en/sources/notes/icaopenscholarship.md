# International Communication Association: Open Scholarship page

- Source: `icaopenscholarship`, [original web page](https://www.icahdq.org/page/OpenScholarship)
- Version: The "Open Scholarship" page on the website of the International Communication Association (ICA). The official site returns a human-verification page to scripts, so we read the Internet Archive snapshot of 2025-09-18 (web.archive.org/web/20250918203718/...). Locations give the page subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Top of page (introduction signed by Claes de Vreese)
- Quote: "ICA has adopted the term Open Scholarship in recognition of the epistemological and methodological diveristy in our field."
- Key point: ICA uses the term "Open Scholarship" in recognition of the epistemological and methodological diversity of the field.

### E2

- Location: ICA Perspectives on Open Scholarship
- Quote: "Conceptually, communication scholars discussed, for example, whether open science standards of reproducibility and replicability may be at odds with certain types of qualitative scholarship; procedurally, some problematized the additional labor associated with manuscript preparation and review; and from an ethical point of view, some suggested open science practices to be colonial in that they privilege academic voices over the subaltern voices of researched subjects."
- Key point: Debates at the 2020 ICA conference: whether standards of reproducibility and replicability conflict with some qualitative scholarship; the extra workload in preparing and reviewing manuscripts; and whether open science privileges the voices of scholars over those of the people studied.

### E3

- Location: ICA Perspectives on Open Scholarship
- Quote: "Proponents of open science practices suggest that transparency will help ensure robustness in communication scholarship, and opponents argue that these practices privilege some forms of scholarship while diminishing others."
- Key point: Proponents argue that transparency helps ensure robust research; opponents argue that these practices privilege some forms of scholarship while diminishing others.

### E4

- Location: Open Science Badges at ICA
- Quote: "ICA supports Open Science Badges to acknowledge open science practices. These voluntary badges are offered for those who would like to share data and materials and preregister research, and signal to the reader that the content has been made available in a persistent location."
- Key point: ICA supports voluntary Open Science Badges recognizing research that shares data and materials and is preregistered.

### E5

- Location: Availability of Data and Materials Statement
- Quote: "Where ethically feasible, ICA encourages authors to make all data on which the conclusions of the paper rely available to readers. Authors are required to include a Data Availability Statement in their article."
- Key point: Where ethically feasible, ICA encourages authors to make available all data on which a paper's conclusions rely; ICA journals require a Data Availability Statement in every article.

### E6

- Location: Open Science Badges at ICA
- Quote: "During the paper submission process, authors can also mark their desired badges. These badges will appear in the conference print program."
- Key point: Badges can also be requested when submitting to the annual conference, and they appear in the conference program.
