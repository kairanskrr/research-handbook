# Olah & Carter 2017: Research Debt

- Source: `olah2017debt`, [original](https://distill.pub/2017/research-debt/)
- Version: Distill article, 2017. Web page; locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Interpretive Labor
- Quote: "In these one-to-many cases, each member of the audience pays the cost of understanding, even though the cost of explaining stays the same."
- Key point: In one-to-many writing, explaining is paid for once but understanding is paid for by every reader; effort the author spends explaining clearly saves effort for every reader.

### E2

- Location: Clear Thinking
- Quote: "Conversely, if we can’t explain an idea well, that’s often a sign that we don’t understand it as well as we could."
- Key point: If you can't explain an idea well, that is often a sign you don't yet fully understand it.

### E3

- Location: Research Distillation
- Quote: "It’s tempting to think of explaining an idea as just putting a layer of polish on it, but good explanations often involve transforming the idea."
- Key point: Good explanation is not just surface polish; it often means reorganizing and transforming the idea itself.
