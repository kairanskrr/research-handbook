# Kidger 2023: Just know stuff

- Source: `kidger2023know`, [original](https://kidger.site/thoughts/just-know-stuff/)
- Version: Author's blog, 2023. Full title: "Just know stuff. (Or, how to achieve success in a machine learning PhD.)" Its detailed lists are specific to machine learning; we take only the general principles.

### E1

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "Know your field inside-out. Know as much about adjacent fields (in math, statistics, …) as you can." / "possessing a technical depth of knowledge is how you come up with new ideas and learn to recognise bad ones."
- Key point: Know your field inside out and as much about adjacent fields as you can; deep technical knowledge is how you come up with new ideas and learn to recognize bad ones.

### E2

- Location: Closing
- Quote: "Don’t expect to cover all of that in a few months; this is something that should happen over the next few years."
- Key point: Building knowledge takes years, not months.

### E3

- Location: Closing
- Quote: "One never really stops learning."
- Key point: You never really stop learning.
