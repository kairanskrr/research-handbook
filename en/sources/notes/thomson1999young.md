# Thomson 1999: The Young Person's Guide to Writing Economic Theory

- Source: `thomson1999young`, [original PDF](https://static.uni-graz.at/fileadmin/_Persoenliche_Webseite/wendner_ronald/write_a_thesis/Thomson.pdf)
- Version: Journal of Economic Literature 37(1): 157-183, 1999. Read from the journal-typeset PDF provided on the course web page of Professor Ronald Wendner at the University of Graz. The author is an economist at the University of Rochester; the article gives writing advice to PhD students, mainly aimed at formal-model (theory) papers, and represents the author's personal views.

### E1

- Location: §2 General Principles, p. 159
- Quote: "Show that what you did is interesting and has not been done before."
- Key point: Let readers see that what you did is interesting and has not been done before.

### E2

- Location: §5 Writing Proofs, p. 177
- Quote: "Verify the independence of your hypotheses. For each hypothesis in each theorem, check whether you could proceed without it."
- Key point: Check whether your assumptions are independent of one another: for each assumption in each theorem, see whether the proof still goes through without it.
