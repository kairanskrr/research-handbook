# European Union 2022: Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065)

- Source: `eu2022dsa`, [original (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065)
- Version: Digital Services Act (DSA), adopted by the European Parliament and the Council on 19 October 2022, Official Journal of the European Union L 277, 27 October 2022. EUR-Lex returns an empty page to scripts, so this was read from an Internet Archive snapshot of 2023-01-17 (web.archive.org/web/20230117003750/...), the full English HTML text of the Official Journal. Only Article 40 (data access and scrutiny) is excerpted. Locations give the article number.

### E1

- Location: Article 40(4)
- Quote: "provide access to data to vetted researchers who meet the requirements in paragraph 8 of this Article, for the sole purpose of conducting research that contributes to the detection, identification and understanding of systemic risks in the Union"
- Key point: Upon a reasoned request from the competent authority, very large online platforms and very large online search engines must give data access to "vetted researchers", solely for research that contributes to detecting, identifying, and understanding systemic risks in the EU.

### E2

- Location: Article 40(8)
- Quote: "(a) they are affiliated to a research organisation as defined in Article 2, point (1), of Directive (EU) 2019/790; (b) they are independent from commercial interests; (c) their application discloses the funding of the research;" [...] "(g) they have committed themselves to making their research results publicly available free of charge"
- Key point: Conditions for vetted researcher status include affiliation with a research organization, independence from commercial interests, disclosure of funding in the application, the ability to meet data security and confidentiality requirements, and a commitment to make research results publicly available free of charge.

### E3

- Location: Article 40(12)
- Quote: "shall give access without undue delay to data, including, where technically possible, to real-time data, provided that the data is publicly accessible in their online interface by researchers"
- Key point: For data that is already publicly accessible on their interfaces, platforms must give access without undue delay, including real-time data where technically possible, to researchers who meet some of the conditions (including researchers at non-profit organizations).

### E4

- Location: Article 40(13)
- Quote: "The Commission shall, after consulting the Board, adopt delegated acts supplementing this Regulation by laying down the technical conditions under which providers of very large online platforms or of very large online search engines are to share data pursuant to paragraphs 1 and 4 and the purposes for which the data may be used."
- Key point: The technical conditions and purposes of data sharing are to be laid down in separate delegated acts adopted by the European Commission.

### E5

- Location: Article 3, point (n)
- Quote: "'Digital Services Coordinator of establishment' means the Digital Services Coordinator of the Member State where the main establishment of a provider of an intermediary service is located or its legal representative resides or is established"
- Key point: The "Digital Services Coordinator of establishment" is the Digital Services Coordinator of the Member State where the provider has its main establishment (or where its legal representative is located).

### E6

- Location: Article 49(2)
- Quote: "Member States shall designate one of the competent authorities as their Digital Services Coordinator. The Digital Services Coordinator shall be responsible for all matters relating to supervision and enforcement of this Regulation in that Member State"
- Key point: Each Member State designates one of its competent authorities as its Digital Services Coordinator, responsible for all matters of DSA supervision and enforcement in that Member State (unless the Member State has assigned some tasks elsewhere).
