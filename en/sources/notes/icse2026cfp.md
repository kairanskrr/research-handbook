# ICSE 2026: Research Track Call for Papers

- Source: `icse2026cfp`, [original](https://conf.researchr.org/track/icse-2026/icse-2026-research-track)
- Version: Call for research papers of the 48th International Conference on Software Engineering (ICSE) 2026, conference website (researchr), read on 2026-10-08. Web page; locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Research Areas
- Quote: "ICSE welcomes submissions addressing topics across the full spectrum of Software Engineering, being inclusive of quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-methods research. Topics of interest include the following and are grouped into the following nine research areas."
- Key point: ICSE welcomes submissions across all of software engineering and is inclusive of quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-methods research; topics are grouped into nine research areas.

### E2

- Location: Research Areas (list of areas such as AI for Software Engineering, Analytics, Testing and Analysis)
- Quote: "AI for Software Engineering" / "Mining software repositories, including version control systems, issue tracking systems, software ecosystems, configurations, app stores, communication platforms, and novel software engineering data sources, to generate insights through various research methods" / "Human and Social Aspects" / "Formal methods and model checking (excluding solutions focusing solely on hardware)"
- Key point: Examples from the nine areas: AI for software engineering; mining software repositories such as version control and issue tracking systems; human and social aspects; formal methods and model checking (hardware-only work excluded) are also in scope.

### E3

- Location: Opening note; Dual Submission Cycles
- Quote: "ICSE 2026 will follow a dual deadline structure introduced in 2024." / "Author response period (3 days)"
- Key point: ICSE 2026 keeps the dual deadline structure introduced in 2024; each cycle has a 3-day author response period.

### E4

- Location: Review Criteria
- Quote: "i) Novelty :" / "ii) Rigor :" / "iii) Relevance :" / "v) Presentation :" / "Verifiability and Transparency : The extent to which the paper includes sufficient information to understand how an innovation works; to understand how data was obtained, analyzed, and interpreted; and how the paper supports independent verification or replication of the paper’s claimed contributions. Any artifacts attached to or linked from the paper will be checked by one reviewer."
- Key point: One of ICSE 2026's five review criteria is "Verifiability and Transparency": can readers understand how the innovation works and how the data were obtained and analyzed, and does the paper support independent verification or replication; one reviewer checks the artifacts included or linked. The other four are novelty, rigor, relevance, and presentation.

### E5

- Location: Review Criteria; Revisions
- Quote: "The outcome of each paper will be one of the following Accept, Revision, Reject." / "Revised papers will be examined by the same set of reviewers. An unsatisfactory revised paper will be rejected. Authors are given approximately 4 weeks to submit the revised papers."
- Key point: There are three outcomes: Accept, Revision, Reject. Papers given "Revision" get about 4 weeks to revise and are re-reviewed by the same reviewers; unsatisfactory revisions are rejected.

### E6

- Location: Re-submissions of Rejected Papers
- Quote: "Authors of papers that receive a REJECT decision in the first submission cycle are strongly discouraged from re-submitting it to the second submission cycle."
- Key point: Authors of papers rejected in the first cycle are strongly discouraged from resubmitting them in the second.

### E7

- Location: Submission Process
- Quote: "All submissions must not exceed 10 pages for the main text, inclusive of all figures, tables, appendices, etc. Two more pages containing only references are permitted ."
- Key point: The main text may not exceed 10 pages (including figures, tables, and appendices), plus 2 more pages for references only.

### E8

- Location: Submission Process (double-anonymous review)
- Quote: "The ICSE 2026 Research Track will employ a double-anonymous review process." / "All references to the author’s prior work should be in the third person." / "While authors have the right to upload preprints on ArXiV or similar sites, they must avoid specifying that the manuscript was submitted to ICSE 2026."
- Key point: Review is double-anonymous; refer to your own prior work in the third person; you may post preprints on arXiv and elsewhere, but do not say they were submitted to ICSE 2026.

### E9

- Location: Submission Process (concurrent submission)
- Quote: "papers submitted to ICSE 2026 must not have been published elsewhere and must not be under review or submitted for review elsewhere whilst under consideration for ICSE 2026"
- Key point: Papers submitted to ICSE 2026 must not have been published elsewhere or be under review elsewhere while under consideration.

### E10

- Location: Submission Process (research involving people)
- Quote: "If the research involves human participants/subjects, the authors must adhere to the ACM Publications Policy on Research Involving Human Participants and Subjects ."
- Key point: Research involving human participants must follow the ACM Publications Policy on Research Involving Human Participants and Subjects.

### E11

- Location: Submission Process (journal extensions)
- Quote: "IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering, ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology and ICSE 2026" / "aimed at reducing community effort in reviewing journal extensions of conference papers and allowing authors to get faster and more consistent feedback"
- Key point: ICSE 2026 partners with two journals, IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering and ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology, to reduce the effort of reviewing journal extensions of conference papers.

### E12

- Location: Open Science Policy
- Quote: "Note that sharing research artifacts is not mandatory for submission or acceptance. However, sharing is expected to be the default, and non-sharing needs to be justified. We recognize that reproducibility or replicability is not a goal in qualitative research" / "to make their artifact available to the program committee (via upload of supplemental material or a link to an anonymous repository)"
- Key point: ICSE 2026 does not require sharing artifacts, but sharing is expected by default and not sharing must be justified; qualitative research does not aim at replicability. Artifacts can be given to reviewers as supplementary material or through a link to an anonymous repository.

### E13

- Location: Opening UPDATE 6/29/2025
- Quote: "ICSE will again have a Shadow Program Committee for the second cycle to train the next generation of reviewers through deliberate practice with specific guidance and feedback. This program is open to PhD students, post-docs, new faculty members and industry practitioners"
- Key point: ICSE 2026 runs a "Shadow Program Committee" in the second cycle so PhD students, postdocs, new faculty, and others can practice reviewing with guidance.

### E14

- Location: Start of the Call for Papers
- Quote: "The International Conference on Software Engineering (ICSE) is the premier forum for presenting and discussing the most recent and significant technical research contributions in the field of Software Engineering."
- Key point: ICSE describes itself as the premier forum for presenting and discussing the most recent and significant technical research in software engineering.
