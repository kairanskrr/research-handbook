# Nevo & Whinston 2010: Taking the Dogma out of Econometrics

- Source: `nevo2010taking`, [original](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.24.2.69)
- Version: Journal of Economic Perspectives 24(2): 69–82, 2010. We read only the public abstract on the American Economic Association's article page, not the full text. This is a comment responding to Angrist & Pischke 2010 and represents the authors' view.

### E1

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "Structural modeling attempts to use data to identify the parameters of an underlying economic model, based on models of individual choice or aggregate relations derived from them."
- Key point: Structural modeling uses data to identify the parameters of an underlying economic model, built on models of individual choice or the aggregate relations derived from them.

### E2

- Location: Abstract
- Quote: "credible analysis can come in many guises, both structural and nonstructural, and for some questions structural analysis offers important advantages."
- Key point: The authors argue that credible analysis can take many forms, structural and nonstructural, and that for some questions structural analysis offers important advantages.
