# Yale Law Journal: Submissions and Data Preservation Policy

- Source: `yljsubmissions`, [submissions web page](https://www.yalelawjournal.org/submissions); [data preservation policy PDF](https://yalelawjournal.org/files/01KGDP50ZRQVDA2806A9N0DE8C.pdf)
- Version: Accessed October 6, 2026 (the web page mentions the submission period for Volume 136). The Yale Law Journal is a student-edited journal of Yale Law School in the United States. Locations give the web page or PDF subheading.

### E1

- Location: Submissions, Attribution
- Quote: "For language that is taken verbatim from other sources, we require quotation marks and citations that conform to the Bluebook"
- Key point: Verbatim language from other sources requires quotation marks and citations conforming to the Bluebook (the legal citation manual widely used in the US) and the journal's own requirements.

### E2

- Location: Submissions, Attribution
- Quote: "These requirements apply regardless of whether the author is citing their own previously published work."
- Key point: The same quotation marks and citations are required when authors quote their own previously published work.

### E3

- Location: Submissions, Articles and Essays
- Quote: "The Articles & Essays Committee reviews submissions without knowledge of the identity of the authors."
- Key point: The journal reviews Articles and Essays without knowing the authors' identities (anonymous review).

### E4

- Location: Submissions, Articles and Essays
- Quote: "On April 19, 2011, the Yale Law Journal and several peer journals released a joint letter committing to give every author at least seven days to decide whether to accept any offer of publication."
- Key point: In 2011 the journal and several peer journals jointly committed to giving every author at least seven days to decide whether to accept an offer of publication.

### E5

- Location: Submissions, Length
- Quote: "For Articles, we strongly encourage submissions of fewer than 25,000 words, including footnotes (roughly 50 Journal pages)."
- Key point: Articles are strongly encouraged to be under 25,000 words, including footnotes.

### E6

- Location: Data preservation policy PDF, opening
- Quote: "The Yale Law Journal will publish pieces containing original empirical analyses or calculations only if the data and code used in those analyses or calculations are available to our editors throughout our editorial process."
- Key point: Pieces with original empirical analyses are published only if the data and code are available to the editors throughout the editorial process.

### E7

- Location: Data preservation policy PDF, Submission Requirements for Empirical Pieces
- Quote: "you must either (a) upload your data, replication code, and/or README file to the Journal's Dataverse or (b) submit a waiver explaining why your data is confidential, proprietary, or otherwise unable to be shared."
- Key point: For an empirical submission, either upload the data, replication code, and README to the journal's Dataverse, or submit a waiver explaining why the data cannot be shared.
