# USGS 2018: Preparations for Water Sampling (National Field Manual for the Collection of Water-Quality Data, chapter A1)

- Source: `usgs2018nfmprep`, [original PDF](https://pubs.usgs.gov/tm/09/a1/tm9a1.pdf), https://doi.org/10.3133/tm9A1
- Version: United States Geological Survey (USGS) Techniques and Methods, book 9, chapter A1, revised November 2018. This is the official protocol for USGS water-quality field staff; a US government publication, free to read. Locations give the PDF page number (counted from the cover).

### E1

- Location: §1.1, PDF p. 11
- Quote: "Fundamental to water-quality sampling is the fact that the analytical results can be no better than the sample on which the analysis was performed."
- Key point: The analytical results can be no better than the sample that was analyzed.

### E2

- Location: §2.1 Site Access and Permission to Collect Data, PDF p. 14
- Quote: "USGS policy specifies that permission must be secured before entering public or private property to select sampling sites, install wells, or collect samples" / "The USGS preferred business practice is to obtain permission in writing; however, oral permission may be used if it is documented by a USGS representative."
- Key point: USGS policy requires permission before entering public or private land to select sites, install wells, or collect samples; written permission is preferred, and verbal permission must be documented by USGS staff.

### E3

- Location: §2.2, PDF p. 14
- Quote: "In most bodies of flowing or still water, a single sampling site or point does not adequately represent the entire stream segment with respect to physical properties, chemical constituents, or biological communities."
- Key point: In most flowing or still water bodies, a single sampling site does not adequately represent the whole stream segment's physical properties, chemical constituents, or biological communities.

### E4

- Location: §2.2, PDF p. 14
- Quote: "Verify that sampling transects can be accessed during all typical and extreme flow and weather conditions."
- Key point: When selecting sites, verify that the sampling transects can be reached under all typical and extreme flow and weather conditions.

### E5

- Location: §2.0 Site Selection, PDF p. 13
- Quote: "Considerations for site selection include the appropriate quantity and locations of sampling sites, data-quality objectives, types of data and samples to be collected, site accessibility, safety, and project budget."
- Key point: Site selection considers the number and locations of sampling sites, data-quality objectives, the types of data and samples to collect, site accessibility, safety, and budget.

### E6

- Location: §2.2.1, PDF p. 15
- Quote: "Note that the field-measurement profile is a useful guideline, but might not be representative of chemical homogeneity for the analytes of interest; therefore, this assumption should be tested during the early phases of data collection."
- Key point: A first profile of field measurements across the transect (temperature, specific conductance, and so on) is a useful guide but may not show that the analytes of interest are well mixed; test this assumption early in sampling.

### E7

- Location: §2.2.1, PDF p. 15
- Quote: "CAUTION: Any stream, including an ephemeral or intermittent stream, can rapidly become too deep or too swift to wade safely."
- Key point: Any stream, including ephemeral or intermittent ones, can quickly become too deep or too swift to wade safely.

### E8

- Location: §3.0, PDF p. 18
- Quote: "Planning for data management starts before water-quality samples are collected."
- Key point: Planning for data management starts before samples are collected.

### E9

- Location: §3.0, PDF p. 18
- Quote: "Original records are defined as those data, metadata, and other information that are initially recorded in the field or laboratory." / "Regardless of whether the original records are paper or electronic, they must be archived"
- Key point: Original records are the data, metadata, and other information first recorded in the field or laboratory; whether on paper or electronic, they must be archived.

### E10

- Location: §4.2 Quality-Assurance Plan, PDF p. 25
- Quote: "Blanks should be collected before the onset of environmental sampling to identify potential bias issues early in the project, and then on a regular schedule during field sampling."
- Key point: Collect blanks before environmental sampling begins, to catch bias problems early, and then on a regular schedule during field sampling.

### E11

- Location: §4.2 Quality-Assurance Plan, PDF p. 25
- Quote: "Replicates of environmental samples are collected to estimate natural variability in the aquatic system as well as variability in sample collection, sample processing, and chemical analysis."
- Key point: Replicate samples are collected to estimate natural variability in the water body as well as variability from sample collection, processing, and chemical analysis.

### E12

- Location: Table 1-4 (continued), PDF p. 27
- Quote: "Calibrate meters and multiparameter sondes prior to sample trip to test correct operation and function."
- Key point: Calibrate meters and multiparameter sondes before the sampling trip to check that they work correctly.

### E13

- Location: Table 1-4, PDF p. 26
- Quote: "Plan to have at least one experienced and trained field person on the field team at all times."
- Key point: Plan to have at least one experienced, trained person on the field team at all times.

### E14

- Location: §5.1.1 Job Hazard Analysis, PDF p. 28
- Quote: "A JHA is a technique that focuses on job tasks as a means to identify hazards before they occur."
- Key point: A Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) goes through specific job tasks to identify hazards before they occur.

### E15

- Location: Table 1-5, PDF p. 28
- Quote: "Implement an office call-in procedure every night to ensure someone knows everything is okay. Ensure someone will begin rescue efforts if the required call is not received."
- Key point: Call in to the office every night to confirm all is well, and make sure someone starts a rescue if the call does not come.

### E16

- Location: §5.2, PDF p. 31
- Quote: "The sketch map should contain enough detail so that the site can be found again by a person who has never visited it."
- Key point: A site sketch map should be detailed enough that someone who has never been there can find the site again.

### E17

- Location: §4.0 The Project Work Plan, PDF p. 24
- Quote: "Among the many components of a USGS project work plan are (1) scope and timeline with budget; (2) SOPs for all planned data-collection activities and technical methods that will be implemented; (3) QAP to assess and document the quality of the data; (4) Data-Management Plan for collection, review, and approval of the data products; and (5) plans for analyzing and publishing the data."
- Key point: A USGS project work plan includes the scope, timeline, and budget; standard operating procedures for all data-collection activities; a quality assurance plan; a data management plan; and an analysis and publication plan.
