# Abadie 2021: Using Synthetic Controls: Feasibility, Data Requirements, and Methodological Aspects

- Source: `abadie2021using`, [original](https://hdl.handle.net/1721.1/144417)
- Version: Journal of Economic Literature 59(2), 2021, pp. 391–425 (DOI 10.1257/jel.20191450). These excerpts come from the publisher's final version deposited in MIT's open-access repository, DSpace@MIT.

### E1

- Location: §5 Contextual Requirements, Size of the Effect and Volatility of the Outcome
- Quote: "the impact of "small" interventions with effects of a magnitude similar to the volatility of the outcome are difficult to detect. Even a large effect may be difficult to detect if the volatility of the outcome is also large."
- Key point: Effects of a magnitude similar to the volatility of the outcome are hard to detect; when the outcome is very volatile, even a large effect may go unseen.

### E2

- Location: §5 Contextual Requirements, Availability of a Comparison Group
- Quote: "Units that adopt an intervention similar to the one adopted by the unit of interest should not be included in the donor pool because they are affected by the intervention, very much like the unit of interest." / "it is important to restrict the donor pool to units with characteristics that are similar to the affected unit."
- Key point: Units that adopted an intervention similar to the treated unit's should not be in the donor pool; the donor pool should be restricted to units with characteristics similar to the treated unit.

### E3

- Location: §5 Contextual Requirements, Convex Hull Condition
- Quote: "there may not exist a combination of untreated units that provide a credible approximation to the treated units, and the conventional synthetic control estimator should not be used in that case."
- Key point: If no combination of untreated units provides a credible approximation to the treated unit, the conventional synthetic control estimator should not be used.

### E4

- Location: §6 Data Requirements, Sufficient Pre-intervention Information
- Quote: "The credibility of a synthetic control estimator depends in great part on its ability to steadily track the trajectory of the outcome variable for the affected unit before the intervention." / "when designing a synthetic control study, it is of crucial importance to collect information on the affected unit and the donor pool for a large pre-intervention window."
- Key point: The credibility of a synthetic control depends largely on its ability to steadily track the treated unit's outcome trajectory before the intervention; when designing the study, collect data for a long enough pre-intervention period.
