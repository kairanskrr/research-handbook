# Raibert 1985: Good Writing

- Source: `raibert1985good`, [original](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/~pausch/Randy/Randy/raibert.htm)
- Version: Short essay from January 1985, reposted on a CMU web page. Locations are given by section heading.

### E1

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "all that remains is to write bad stuff, and to revise the bad stuff until it is good."
- Key point: The formula for good writing: write bad stuff, then revise it until it is good.

### E2

- Location: Good Writing is Bad Writing That Was Rewritten
- Quote: "This process may take several passes over the same words, sentences, and paragraphs, but a dozen or so passes is typical."
- Key point: Nearly all good writing starts out bad and usually takes a dozen or so passes of revision.

### E3

- Location: Spill the Beans Fast
- Quote: "You should summarize your whole story at the very beginning of your paper, without holding anything back."
- Key point: A research paper is not a mystery novel; summarize the whole story, including what you found, at the very beginning.

### E4

- Location: Trust Your Readers
- Quote: "When you get comments back from your readers, trust what they tell you."
- Key point: When readers say something is unclear, trust them; do not argue that you wrote it clearly, rewrite that part instead.

### E5

- Location: How to Get Unstuck
- Quote: "When this occurs, it is likely that you don't have a clear idea what you want to say, or you don't fully understand some of the things you planned to explain."
- Key point: If you get stuck writing, you probably do not yet have a clear idea of what you want to say, or do not fully understand what you planned to explain; explaining something takes more understanding than doing it.

### E6

- Location: Husband Your Readers
- Quote: "Most readers are only effective for one reading anyway, because they know too much about what you are trying to say by the time they attempt a second pass."
- Key point: Most readers are only effective for one reading, because by the second pass they already know what you are trying to say, so use your readers carefully.

### E7

- Location: Good Writing is Bad Writing That Was Rewritten
- Quote: "Try to have one main idea for each paragraph."
- Key point: Give each paragraph one main idea, stated in a topic sentence.
