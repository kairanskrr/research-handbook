# Society for American Archaeology 2024: SAA Principles of Archaeological Ethics

- Source: `saa2024principles`, [original PDF](https://www.saa.org/common/Uploaded%20files/saadocs/CareerPractice/saa_ethics.pdf)
- Version: The nine principles of archaeological ethics adopted by the Society for American Archaeology (SAA) on March 7, 2024 (first issued 1996, principle 9 added 2016, revised 2024). The PDF contains English and Spanish; this note uses only the English part. Locations give principle numbers.

### E1

- Location: Principle No. 1: Stewardship
- Quote: "Stewardship involves the collaborative management of the archaeological record for the benefit of all people."
- Key point: Stewardship means the collaborative management of the archaeological record for the benefit of all people.

### E2

- Location: Principle No. 1: Stewardship
- Quote: "Archaeologists work for the long-term conservation and protection of the archaeological record, while respecting the rights and beliefs of Indigenous and descendant peoples to the representation of their heritage as manifested in the archaeological record."
- Key point: Archaeologists work for the long-term conservation and protection of the archaeological record while respecting the rights and beliefs of Indigenous and descendant peoples about how their heritage is represented.

### E3

- Location: Principle No. 2: Responsibility
- Quote: "Archaeologists must abide by all laws and regulations in addition to collaborating, to the extent possible, with groups affected by their work to achieve results in a mutually agreeable and beneficial manner."
- Key point: Archaeologists must abide by all laws and, as far as possible, collaborate with groups affected by their work to reach mutually agreeable and beneficial results.

### E4

- Location: Principle No. 3: Commercialization
- Quote: "The commercial sale or authentication of archaeological objects and other data for profit must not be practiced by archaeologists."
- Key point: Archaeologists must not sell archaeological objects for profit or authenticate them commercially.

### E5

- Location: Principle No. 3: Commercialization
- Quote: "framing the archaeological record as an invaluable and nonrenewable resource for telling the story of the past and present."
- Key point: The archaeological record is an invaluable and nonrenewable resource for telling the story of the past and present.

### E6

- Location: Principle No. 4: Public Education and Community Engagement
- Quote: "(3) listen to and incorporate the knowledge and concerns of impacted communities."
- Key point: Archaeologists should listen to and incorporate the knowledge and concerns of impacted communities.

### E7

- Location: Principle No. 5: Preservation of the Archaeological Record
- Quote: "The collections and documentation (digital/physical) generated through archaeological research are part of the archaeological record and as such should be treated in accord with the principles of stewardship."
- Key point: Collections and documentation (digital and physical) generated by archaeological research are themselves part of the archaeological record and should be treated under the principles of stewardship.

### E8

- Location: Principle No. 5: Preservation of the Archaeological Record
- Quote: "Archaeologists have the responsibility to ensure that the record is archived, curated, and published, according to prevailing professional standards."
- Key point: Archaeologists are responsible for ensuring the record is archived, curated, and published according to prevailing professional standards.

### E9

- Location: Principle No. 6: Reporting
- Quote: "Archaeologists have a responsibility to share and publish results and data in a timely and, so much as possible, open access manner."
- Key point: Share and publish results and data in a timely way and, as far as possible, open access.

### E10

- Location: Principle No. 6: Reporting
- Quote: "An interest in preserving and protecting sensitive information about the past and minimizing unintended consequences must be taken into account when publishing and distributing knowledge."
- Key point: Publishing must take into account protecting sensitive information about the past and minimizing unintended consequences.

### E11

- Location: Principle No. 7: Training and Resources
- Quote: "An archaeologist’s education should include research design and planning that prioritizes analysis of existing collections, justifies new fieldwork that maximizes conservation of existing resources, and ensures proper curation and dissemination of results."
- Key point: Archaeological education should include research design that prioritizes analysis of existing collections, justifies new fieldwork that maximizes conservation of existing resources, and ensures proper curation and dissemination of results.

### E12

- Location: Principle No. 7: Training and Resources
- Quote: "Archaeology is a multidisciplinary field that requires a holistic approach to training, research, and professional conduct."
- Key point: Archaeology is a multidisciplinary field that requires a holistic approach to training, research, and professional conduct.

### E13

- Location: Principle No. 5: Preservation of the Archaeological Record
- Quote: "Archaeologists should be cognizant that many descendant and Indigenous communities view the archaeological record as part of their heritage and should be included in the decisions regarding documentation and the pursuit of intellectual knowledge."
- Key point: Many descendant and Indigenous communities see the archaeological record as their heritage and should be included in decisions about documentation and research.
