# WMA 2024: Declaration of Helsinki – Ethical Principles for Medical Research Involving Human Participants

- Source: `wma2024helsinki`, [original](https://www.wma.net/policies-post/wma-declaration-of-helsinki/)
- Version: Revision adopted by the 75th General Assembly of the World Medical Association (WMA), October 2024. The web version has no paragraph numbers, so locations give the subheading.

### E1

- Location: Preamble
- Quote: "The World Medical Association (WMA) has developed the Declaration of Helsinki as a statement of ethical principles for medical research involving human participants, including research using identifiable human material or data."
- Key point: The Declaration of Helsinki states ethical principles for medical research involving human participants, including research using identifiable human material or data.

### E2

- Location: General Principles
- Quote: "These purposes can never take precedence over the rights and interests of individual research participants."
- Key point: The purposes of research can never take precedence over the rights and interests of individual participants.

### E3

- Location: General Principles
- Quote: "Physicians who combine medical research with medical care should involve their patients in research only to the extent that this is justified by its potential preventive, diagnostic or therapeutic value and if the physician has good reason to believe that participation in the research will not adversely affect the health of the patients who serve as research participants."
- Key point: Physicians who combine research with medical care may involve their patients in research only to the extent justified by its potential preventive, diagnostic, or therapeutic value, and only if they have good reason to believe participation will not harm the patients' health.

### E4

- Location: Research Ethics Committees
- Quote: "The protocol must be submitted for consideration, comment, guidance, and approval to the concerned research ethics committee before the research begins."
- Key point: The protocol must be submitted to the research ethics committee for review and approval before the research begins.

### E5

- Location: Research Ethics Committees
- Quote: "When collaborative research is performed internationally, the research protocol must be approved by research ethics committees in both the sponsoring and host countries."
- Key point: For international collaborative research, ethics committees in both the sponsoring and host countries must approve the protocol.

### E6

- Location: Free and Informed Consent
- Quote: "each potential participant must be adequately informed in plain language of the aims, methods, anticipated benefits and potential risks and burdens, qualifications of the researcher, sources of funding, any potential conflicts of interest"
- Key point: Each potential participant must be told in plain language about the aims, methods, anticipated benefits, risks and burdens, the researcher's qualifications, sources of funding, conflicts of interest, and so on.

### E7

- Location: Free and Informed Consent
- Quote: "the physician or another qualified individual must then seek the potential participant’s freely given informed consent, formally documented on paper or electronically."
- Key point: After confirming understanding, seek freely given informed consent and document it formally on paper or electronically.

### E8

- Location: Free and Informed Consent
- Quote: "When seeking informed consent for participation in research the physician or other researcher must be particularly cautious if the potential participant is in a dependent relationship with them or may consent under duress. In such situations, the informed consent must be sought by an appropriately qualified individual who is independent of this relationship."
- Key point: Be especially cautious if the potential participant is in a dependent relationship with the physician or may consent under duress; in that case informed consent should be sought by a qualified person independent of that relationship.

### E9

- Location: Research Registration, Publication, and Dissemination of Results
- Quote: "Medical research involving human participants must be registered in a publicly accessible database before recruitment of the first participant."
- Key point: Medical research involving human participants must be registered in a publicly accessible database before the first participant is recruited.

### E10

- Location: Research Registration, Publication, and Dissemination of Results
- Quote: "Negative and inconclusive as well as positive results must be published or otherwise made publicly available. Sources of funding, institutional affiliations, and conflicts of interest must be declared in the publication."
- Key point: Negative and inconclusive results must be made public just like positive ones; publications must declare funding sources, institutional affiliations, and conflicts of interest.

### E11

- Location: Free and Informed Consent
- Quote: "Physicians or other qualified individuals must obtain free and informed consent from research participants for the collection, processing, storage, and foreseeable secondary use of biological material and identifiable or re-identifiable data."
- Key point: Free and informed consent is required for the collection, processing, and storage of biological material and identifiable (or re-identifiable) data, and for foreseeable secondary use.

### E12

- Location: Free and Informed Consent
- Quote: "A research ethics committee must approve the establishment and monitor ongoing use of such databases and biobanks."
- Key point: A research ethics committee must approve the establishment of such databases and biobanks and monitor their ongoing use.

### E13

- Location: Free and Informed Consent
- Quote: "Where consent is impossible or impracticable to obtain, secondary research on stored data or biological material may be done only after consideration and approval of a research ethics committee."
- Key point: Where consent is impossible or impracticable to obtain, secondary research on stored data or biological material may be done only after review and approval by a research ethics committee.

### E14

- Location: Use of Placebo
- Quote: "The benefits, risks, burdens, and effectiveness of a new intervention must be tested against those of the best proven intervention(s), except in the following circumstances:" / "If no proven intervention exists, the use of placebo, or no intervention, is acceptable"
- Key point: A new intervention must in principle be tested against the best proven intervention; one exception is that a placebo or no intervention may be used as the comparison when no proven intervention exists.

### E15

- Location: Scientific Requirements and Research Protocols
- Quote: "In clinical trials, the protocol must also describe any post-trial provisions."
- Key point: Clinical trial protocols must also describe any post-trial provisions.

### E16

- Location: Research Ethics Committees
- Quote: "Where monitoring is required, the researcher must provide information to the committee and/or competent data and safety monitoring entity, especially about any serious adverse events. No amendment to the protocol may be made without consideration and approval by the committee."
- Key point: Where monitoring is required, researchers must report to the ethics committee and/or the data and safety monitoring body, especially about serious adverse events; any amendment to the protocol needs the ethics committee's review and approval.

### E17

- Location: Risks, Burdens, and Benefits
- Quote: "When the risks and burdens are found to outweigh the potential benefits or when there is conclusive proof of definitive outcomes, physicians and other researchers must assess whether to continue, modify or immediately stop the research."
- Key point: When risks and burdens are found to outweigh potential benefits, or there is conclusive proof of definitive outcomes, researchers must assess whether to continue, modify, or immediately stop the study.
