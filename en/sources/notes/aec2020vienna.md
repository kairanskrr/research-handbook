# AEC et al. 2020: The Vienna Declaration on Artistic Research

- Source: `aec2020vienna`, [original PDF](https://aec-music.eu/media/2021/05/viennadeclarationonarfinalversion24june20_20201216163421.pdf) (publication page: https://aec-music.eu/publication/vienna-declaration-on-artistic-research/)
- Version: final version of June 24, 2020, two pages. A policy document jointly signed by ten European organizations in higher arts education and artistic research, including the Association Européenne des Conservatoires (AEC), addressed to policymakers and funders. Locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "Artistic Research (AR) is practice-based, practice-led research in the arts which has developed rapidly in the last twenty years globally"
- Key point: Artistic research is practice-based, practice-led research in the arts, which has developed rapidly worldwide over the past twenty years.

### E2

- Location: Key features of Artistic Research
- Quote: "Excellent AR is research through means of high level artistic practice and reflection; it is an epistemic inquiry, directed towards increasing knowledge, insight, understanding and skills."
- Key point: Excellent artistic research is research through high-level artistic practice and reflection; it is an epistemic inquiry aimed at increasing knowledge, insight, understanding, and skills.

### E3

- Location: Infrastructure & Access to Funding
- Quote: "AR incorporates many aspects and features that are not, or not solely, text based, such as artefacts, movements and sounds."
- Key point: Artistic research includes many things that are not (or not solely) text-based, such as artefacts, movements, and sounds.

### E4

- Location: Evaluation & Recognition
- Quote: "AR is validated through peer review covering the range of disciplinary competences addressed by the work."
- Key point: Artistic research is validated through peer review covering the range of disciplinary competences the work addresses.

### E5

- Location: Claim for Action
- Quote: "To support and work towards the establishment of AR as an independent category within the Frascati Manual"
- Key point: The signatories call for artistic research to be established as an independent category in the Frascati Manual.

### E6

- Location: Infrastructure & Access to Funding
- Quote: "AR in general does not have equal access to research funding as other fields of research or is not at all eligible to apply for research grants or scholarships."
- Key point: In some countries, artistic research does not have equal access to research funding, or is not eligible to apply at all.

### E7

- Location: Introduction
- Quote: "The Vienna Declaration is intended as a policy document addressing political decision makers, funding bodies,"
- Key point: This is a policy document addressed to policymakers and funding bodies.

### E8

- Location: Key features of Artistic Research
- Quote: "AR is undertaken in all art practice disciplines - including architecture, design, film, photography, fine art, media and digital arts, music and the performing arts"
- Key point: Artistic research takes place in all art practice disciplines: architecture, design, film, photography, fine art, media and digital arts, music, and the performing arts.

### E9

- Location: signatories at the end
- Quote: "AEC, CILECT / GEECT, Culture Action Europe, Cumulus, EAAE, ELIA, EPARM, EQ-Arts, MusiQuE, SAR"
- Key point: The declaration is jointly signed by ten organizations (the original lists only abbreviations); according to its introduction, the signatories include discipline organizations of higher arts education institutions, artistic research organizations, two international quality assurance agencies in the arts, and international policy organizations.
