# WIPO 2025: An International Guide to Patent Case Management for Judges, China chapter 4.1.4

- Source: `wipo2025international`, [original](https://www.wipo.int/patent-judicial-guide/en/full-guide/china/4.1.4)
- Version: Full web text of the 2025 edition by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), accessed 2026-10-11. The China chapter was written by two Chinese judges, Du Weike and He Juan. Locations give the section number.

### E1

- Location: §4.1.4 Guiding case system
- Quote: "Article 18 paragraph 2 of the Law on the Organization of the People's Courts provides that "the Supreme People's Court may publish guiding cases.""
- Key point: Article 18(2) of the Law on the Organization of the People's Courts provides that the Supreme People's Court may publish guiding cases.

### E2

- Location: §4.1.4 Guiding case system (summarizing Articles 9-11 of the 2015 Detailed Rules for Implementing the Provisions on Case Guidance Work)
- Quote: "At all levels, in trials of cases that are similar to the guiding cases issued by the Supreme People's Court in terms of basic facts and the application of the law, the courts shall make decisions by reference to the rules of the decision in the applicable guiding cases." / "Guiding cases shall be cited as reasons for a decision but not as the basis for the decision."
- Key point: Courts at all levels hearing cases whose basic facts and application of law are similar to a Supreme People's Court guiding case shall decide by reference to that case's adjudication rules; guiding cases are cited as part of the reasoning, not as the legal basis of the judgment.
