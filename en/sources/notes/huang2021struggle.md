# Jeff Huang 2021: Behind the scenes: the struggle for each paper

- Source: `huang2021struggle`, [original](https://jeffhuang.com/struggle_for_each_paper/)
- Version: Author's homepage, 2021. The author (a professor at Brown University) looks back on the story behind each of his 30 papers.

### E1

- Location: Opening
- Quote: "But my CV only shows a list of neatly cataloged papers: title, authors, conference. Each one appearing no different from another. But how each paper ended up published is its own story, a story about people and opportunity."
- Key point: The papers on a CV look neatly cataloged, but how each one ended up published is its own story.

### E2

- Location: The story of the first paper
- Quote: "It was a long 3.5 years between when I got that first message and when it was published" / "I was rejected by every Ph.D. program I applied to before this publication"
- Key point: The author's first paper took 3.5 years from start to publication; before it was published, every PhD program he applied to rejected him.

### E3

- Location: Closing reflections
- Quote: "The other thing I noticed is that papers that get the most citations later often got poor reviews or multiple rejections."
- Key point: The papers that later get the most citations often received poor reviews or multiple rejections at first.

### E4

- Location: Closing reflections
- Quote: "But I also think about how my group has encountered a lot of rejection, and keeping up morale was sometimes difficult."
- Key point: Even an established research group faces a lot of rejection, and keeping up morale is not easy.
