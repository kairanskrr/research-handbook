# Society for Ethnomusicology 2008: Position Statement on Ethnographic Research & Institutional Review Boards

- Source: `sem2008irb`, [original](https://www.ethnomusicology.org/page/PS_IRB) (the official site returns 403 to scripts; text taken from a 2024 Internet Archive snapshot)
- Version: Adopted by the SEM Board on January 16, 2008. Addresses institutional review boards (IRBs) at US universities; it cites the US federal "Common Rule" as it stood before the 2018 revision. Web page. Locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: SEM Policy Guidelines for Institutional Review Boards
- Quote: "on anonymity of research subjects and the insistence on destroying documentation at a project’s end are particularly antithetical to the kind of work ethnomusicologists and other ethnographers do."
- Key point: The emphasis of US ethics review rules on anonymity and their insistence on destroying records at the end of a project are at odds with how ethnomusicologists work (SEM's position).

### E2

- Location: SEM Policy Guidelines for Institutional Review Boards
- Quote: "Each local situation dictates the culturally appropriate means of obtaining permission for conducting research and for documenting that research with audio and visual recording media."
- Key point: How to obtain permission to do research and to make audio and video recordings depends on what is culturally appropriate locally.

### E3

- Location: SEM Policy Guidelines for Institutional Review Boards
- Quote: "Research documentation, in the form of field notes, recordings, photographs, videos, and publications, is important not only to the immediate research project but to the historical record and should be archived and preserved"
- Key point: Research documentation such as field notes, recordings, photographs, and videos also matters to the historical record and should be archived and preserved.
