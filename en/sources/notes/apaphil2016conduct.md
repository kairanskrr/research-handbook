# American Philosophical Association 2016: Code of Conduct

- Source: `apaphil2016conduct`, [original](https://www.apaonline.org/page/codeofconduct)
- Version: Code of Conduct of the American Philosophical Association, drafted by a task force chaired by Nancy J. Holland and approved by the Board in 2016. The website blocks scripted access, so the text is taken from an Internet Archive snapshot. Locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: I. Code of Ethics
- Quote: "Respecting the philosophical opinions and traditions of others, without disparaging those who hold positions at odds with one’s own;"
- Key point: One of philosophers' responsibilities in all professional interactions: respect the philosophical opinions and traditions of others, without disparaging those who hold positions at odds with your own.

### E2

- Location: I. Code of Ethics
- Quote: "Maintaining integrity and trust in all professional commitments and interactions; and"
- Key point: Maintain integrity and trust in all professional commitments and interactions.

### E3

- Location: I. Code of Ethics
- Quote: "With the adoption of this Code of Conduct in 2016, the APA reaffirms its commitment to the AAUP statement and to the core value reflected in that statement: academic freedom."
- Key point: By adopting the code, the American Philosophical Association reaffirms its commitment to the American Association of University Professors (AAUP) statement on professional ethics and its core value, academic freedom.
