# arXiv: The arXiv endorsement system

- Source: `arxivendorsement`, [original](https://info.arxiv.org/help/endorsement.html)
- Version: arXiv help page, read 2026-10-06.

### E1

- Location: opening
- Quote: "arXiv requires that users be endorsed before submitting their first paper to arXiv or a new category."
- Key point: Before submitting your first paper to arXiv, or to a new category, you need an endorsement.

### E2

- Location: section on requesting endorsement
- Quote: "A good choice for graduate students would be your thesis advisor or another professor in your department/institution working in your field who is also an active author on arXiv."
- Key point: For graduate students, a good endorser is your thesis advisor or another professor at your institution in your field who is an active arXiv author.

### E3

- Location: section on requesting endorsement
- Quote: "Please note, however, that it is inappropriate to email large numbers of potential endorsers at once, or to repeatedly email the same endorser with a request for endorsement."
- Key point: Do not email large numbers of potential endorsers at once, or repeatedly email the same person.

### E4

- Location: notes for endorsers
- Quote: "The endorsement process is not peer review."
- Key point: Endorsement is not peer review.
