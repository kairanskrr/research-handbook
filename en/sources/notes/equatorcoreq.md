# EQUATOR Network: COREQ entry page

- Source: `equatorcoreq`, [entry in the EQUATOR Network reporting guideline library](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/coreq/)
- Version: Web page, entry last updated 2023-12-06, accessed 2026-10-06. The original COREQ paper (Tong, Sainsbury & Craig 2007) is not open access, so only the EQUATOR entry page is cited.

### E1

- Location: entry title
- Quote: "a 32-item checklist for interviews and focus groups"
- Key point: COREQ is a 32-item checklist for interviews and focus groups.

### E2

- Location: entry information, Full bibliographic reference
- Quote: "Tong A, Sainsbury P, Craig J. Consolidated criteria for reporting qualitative research (COREQ): a 32-item checklist for interviews and focus groups. Int J Qual Health Care. 2007;19(6):349-357."
- Key point: COREQ was published by Tong, Sainsbury, and Craig in 2007 in the International Journal for Quality in Health Care.

### E3

- Location: page sidebar, Reporting guidelines for main study types
- Quote: "Qualitative research SRQR COREQ"
- Key point: EQUATOR lists SRQR and COREQ as the main reporting guidelines for qualitative research.

### E4

- Location: page sidebar, Reporting guidelines for main study types
- Quote: "Quality improvement studies SQUIRE"
- Key point: The main reporting guideline for quality improvement studies is SQUIRE.

### E5

- Location: page sidebar, Reporting guidelines for main study types
