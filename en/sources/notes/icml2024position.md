# ICML 2024: Call For Position Papers

- Source: `icml2024position`, [original](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2024/CallForPositionPapers)
- Version: the call for papers from ICML 2024 (International Conference on Machine Learning), the first year it had a position paper track; read on 2026-10-17. Locations give the page's headings.

### E1

- Location: top of page (ICML 2024 Call For Position Papers)
- Quote: "We invite submissions for a new position paper track at the ICML 2024 conference." / "many important contributions to the field do not fit the typical mold of a traditional conference research paper: instead, they have discussed an ongoing trend, or advocated for a particular viewpoint or perspective on the field."
- Key point: ICML 2024 introduced a new position paper track because many important contributions to the field do not look like traditional research papers; instead they discuss an ongoing trend or argue for a particular view of the field.

### E2

- Location: Reviewing
- Quote: "Position papers will be reviewed by ICML reviewers who explicitly opt in by checking the appropriate box within the OpenReview system."
- Key point: In 2024, position papers were reviewed by reviewers who explicitly opted in to review them.

### E3

- Location: Reviewing
- Quote: "1. Position: [...] 2. Support: [...] 3. Significance: [...] 4. Discussion potential: [...] 5. Communication quality: [...] 6. Context: [...]"
- Key point: The 2024 review criteria had only six items (position, support, significance, discussion potential, communication quality, context), with no separate "alternative views" item yet; the 2025 call added it (see `icml2025position` E7).

### E4

- Location: Policies
- Quote: "position papers must be titled beginning with the phrase “Position Paper: <Paper title>”"
- Key point: In 2024 the only title requirement was to begin with "Position Paper:".
