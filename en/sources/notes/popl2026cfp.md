# POPL 2026: PACMPL Issue POPL 2026 Call for Papers

- Source: `popl2026cfp`, [original](https://popl26.sigplan.org/track/POPL-2026-popl-research-papers)
- Version: Call for research papers for the ACM SIGPLAN Symposium on Principles of Programming Languages (POPL) 2026, from the conference website, read 2026-10-08. Web page; locations give subheadings.

### E1

- Location: Scope
- Quote: "Both theoretical and experimental papers are welcome, on topics ranging from formal frameworks to experience reports. We seek submissions that make principled, enduring contributions to the theory, design, understanding, implementation, or application of programming languages."
- Key point: POPL welcomes both theoretical and experimental papers, from formal frameworks to experience reports, and seeks principled, enduring contributions to the theory, design, understanding, implementation, or application of programming languages.

### E2

- Location: Evaluation Criteria
- Quote: "All papers will be judged on significance, originality, relevance, correctness, and clarity."
- Key point: Papers are judged on significance, originality, relevance, correctness, and clarity.

### E3

- Location: Double-Blind Reviewing
- Quote: "This means that identities of authors will not be made visible to reviewers until after conditional-acceptance decisions have been made, and then only for the conditionally-accepted papers."
- Key point: POPL 2026 uses full double-blind review: reviewers see author identities only after conditional-acceptance decisions, and only for the conditionally accepted papers.

### E4

- Location: Double-Blind Reviewing, Supplementary material
- Quote: "Authors are permitted to provide supplementary material (e.g., detailed proofs, proof scripts, system implementations, or experimental data) along with their submission, which reviewers may (but are not required to) examine."
- Key point: You may submit supplementary material such as detailed proofs, proof scripts, system implementations, or experimental data; reviewers may consult it but are not required to.

### E5

- Location: Double-Blind Reviewing, Dissemination of work under submission
- Quote: "Authors are welcome to disseminate their ideas and post draft versions of their paper(s) on their personal website, institutional repository, or arXiv (reviewers will be asked to turn off arXiv notifications during the review period)." / "nothing should be done in the name of anonymity that weakens the quality of the submission."
- Key point: You may post drafts on your personal website, institutional repository, or arXiv (reviewers are asked to turn off arXiv notifications during review); do not weaken the submission for the sake of anonymity.

### E6

- Location: Evaluation Process
- Quote: "A response must be concise, addressing specific points raised in the reviews; in particular, it must not introduce new technical results."
- Key point: Author responses must be concise and address specific points in the reviews; they must not introduce new technical results.

### E7

- Location: Evaluation Process
- Quote: "To conform with ACM requirements for journal publication, all POPL papers will be conditionally accepted" / "That the changes are sufficient will be confirmed by the original reviewers prior to acceptance to POPL."
- Key point: To meet ACM requirements for journal publication, all POPL papers are first conditionally accepted, and the original reviewers then confirm the revisions.

### E8

- Location: Submission Guidelines
- Quote: "Each paper should have no more than 25 pages of text, excluding bibliography, using the PACMPL format"
- Key point: The main text is limited to 25 pages, excluding references.

### E9

- Location: Artifact Evaluation for Accepted Papers
- Quote: "Artifact submission is strongly encouraged but voluntary and will not influence the final decision regarding the papers."
- Key point: Artifact submission is strongly encouraged but voluntary, and artifact evaluation does not affect acceptance.
