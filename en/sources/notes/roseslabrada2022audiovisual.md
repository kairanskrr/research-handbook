# Rosés Labrada 2022: Audiovisual recording advice (Jacobs Research Funds)

- Source: `roseslabrada2022audiovisual`, https://depts.washington.edu/jacobsf/Documents/Audiovisual_recording_advice.pdf (linked from the Jacobs Research Funds application page https://depts.washington.edu/jacobsf/apply.php )
- Version: Advice for applicants prepared by Jorge Emilio Rosés Labrada for the Jacobs Research Funds (a US fund supporting research on Indigenous languages and anthropology of the Americas), April 2022 version, two pages.

### E1

- Location: para. 2
- Quote: "For audio recording alone, best practice is now to record in an uncompressed format (either .wav or .aiff) at 44.1Khz and 16-bit depth minimally."
- Key point: For audio-only recording, current best practice is an uncompressed format (.wav or .aiff) at a minimum of 44.1 kHz and 16-bit depth.

### E2

- Location: para. 2
- Quote: "An external XLR-connected stereo microphone is the best choice here as the XLR connection minimizes crackling and the stereo quality provides valuable spatial information. However, the kind of mic you choose will depend on the recording situation,"
- Key point: An external XLR-connected stereo microphone is the best choice: the XLR connection minimizes crackling and stereo gives useful spatial information; but which microphone to use depends on the recording situation.

### E3

- Location: para. 3
- Quote: "please note that not all video conferencing platforms can record uncompressed audio files and that you may want to make additional arrangements to have an on-site recording device with the speaker(s)"
- Key point: When working remotely, note that not all video conferencing platforms can record uncompressed audio; you may want to arrange a separate recording device next to the speaker.
