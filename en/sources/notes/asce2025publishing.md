# ASCE Library: Publishing in ASCE Journals (2025 snapshot)

- Source: `asce2025publishing`, [original](https://ascelibrary.org/author-center/journal) (the ASCE site blocks scripted access; this excerpt was read from the Internet Archive snapshot of 2025-05-12: https://web.archive.org/web/20250512155023/https://ascelibrary.org/author-center/journal)
- Version: ASCE journals author guidelines page "Publishing in ASCE Journals," May 2025 snapshot (footer © 1996-2025). The current version may have changed; check the target journal's page when submitting.

### E1

- Location: ASCE Publication Policies > Preprint Servers
- Quote: "ASCE recognizes that it is customary for researchers to post papers on preprint servers prior to peer review. ASCE does not consider papers on preprint severs as previously published, as long as copyright can be transferred to ASCE. Should the paper be accepted, authors must do the following:" / "Post the full citation and DOI of the published paper on the preprint server paper." / "Post the ASCE copyright notice on the preprint server paper. If the author opts for the Open Access option, then the author retains copyright. A note indicating as much should be put on the preprint paper in lieu of the ASCE copyright notice."
- Key point: ASCE does not consider papers on preprint servers as previously published, as long as copyright can still be transferred to ASCE; once the paper is accepted, authors must add the full citation and DOI of the published paper and an ASCE copyright notice to the preprint; open access authors keep copyright and add a corresponding statement instead. ("severs" in the original is a typo on the page.)

### E2

- Location: ASCE Publication Policies > Previously Published Content
- Quote: "ASCE only considers original manuscripts that have not been previously published and are not under consideration with other journals. “Previously published” includes papers in print and available online and may include conference proceeding papers, posters, and preprints on institutional repositories, preprint servers, or other internet sites."
- Key point: The "previously published content" section of the same page says ASCE only considers manuscripts that are unpublished and not under review elsewhere; "previously published" may include conference papers, posters, and preprints on institutional repositories, preprint servers, or other websites.
