# Congressional Research Service 2025: Recent Executive Actions Related to Gain-of-Function Research and Laboratory Biosafety

- Source: `crs2025gainoffunction`, [Original web page](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12554.html) (report number IN12554)
- Version: May 19, 2025. An Insight from the US Congressional Research Service (CRS), a US government work, read through the public mirror on EveryCRSReport.com. It reflects the situation as of May 2025 only.

### E1

- Location: Paragraph 1
- Quote: "There currently is no overarching federal law that provides oversight of laboratory biosafety and biosecurity with enforceable legal penalties."
- Key point: The US currently has no overarching federal law that oversees laboratory biosafety and biosecurity with enforceable legal penalties.

### E2

- Location: Paragraph 1
- Quote: "Privately funded research is generally not covered by federal policy or agency guidance beyond what is pursuant to FSAP."
- Key point: Privately funded research is generally not covered by federal policy or agency guidance, apart from the Federal Select Agent Program.

### E3

- Location: Paragraph 1
- Quote: "In the United States, oversight of the life sciences , in particular laboratory biosafety and biosecurity, is exercised pursuant to a mixture of federal law, federal guidance, and self-governance, dependent on the types of experiments and biological agents being used."
- Key point: US oversight of the life sciences, especially laboratory biosafety and biosecurity, is a mixture of federal law, federal guidance, and self-governance, depending on the type of experiment and the pathogens used.

### E4

- Location: Paragraph 2
- Quote: "It established an oversight and review process for two categories that previously had been overseen by separate policies: dual-use research of concern (DURC) and research on pathogens with enhanced pandemic potential (also known as gain - of - function research ). The 2024 Policy was scheduled to take effect on May 6, 2025, and institutions that receive federal funding had prepared to implement those requirements."
- Key point: The 2024 policy brought dual-use research of concern and research on pathogens with enhanced pandemic potential (gain-of-function research) under a single oversight and review process; it was due to take effect on May 6, 2025.

### E5

- Location: Paragraph 3
- Quote: "On May 5, 2025, the White House issued Executive Order (E.O.) 14292, " Improving the Safety and Security of Biological Research ," which directed OSTP to revise or replace the 2024 Policy within 120 days."
- Key point: Executive Order 14292 of May 5, 2025, directed the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy to revise or replace the 2024 policy within 120 days.

### E6

- Location: Fourth-to-last paragraph
- Quote: "NIH's notice declared that it is rescinding a previous notice, NOT-OD-25-061 , which had described the implementation of the 2024 Policy."
- Key point: NIH rescinded its earlier notice on implementing the 2024 policy.
