# Environmental Science & Technology: Author Guidelines

- Source: `estauthorguidelines`, [original](https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=esthag)
- Version: Author guidelines web page of *Environmental Science & Technology* (ES&T), a journal of the American Chemical Society (ACS), accessed October 6, 2026. Journal policies change; the page as read at that time applies.

### E1

- Location: Journal Scope
- Quote: "ES&T strives to publish novel research of scientific significance and high environmental importance. Insufficient novelty is common reason manuscripts are declined."
- Key point: ES&T aims to publish novel research of scientific significance and high environmental importance; insufficient novelty is a common reason for rejection.

### E2

- Location: Journal Scope
- Quote: "Manuscripts that report data of a routine nature or that address topics that are already well understood will not be considered."
- Key point: ES&T will not consider manuscripts that report routine data or address topics already well understood.

### E3

- Location: Journal Scope
- Quote: "Whenever possible, research on new measurement technologies should include results with authentic environmental samples, and evaluations should be performed under environmentally realistic conditions."
- Key point: Research on new measurement technologies should, whenever possible, include results with authentic environmental samples and be evaluated under environmentally relevant conditions.

### E4

- Location: Journal Scope, bullet list
- Quote: "Studies should provide novel insights into relevant environmental processes or mechanisms, i.e., studies that only describe the outcomes of standard laboratory or field tests or commercially available products are not appropriate for publication in ES&T."
- Key point: Studies that only describe the outcomes of standard laboratory or field tests, or the performance of commercial products, are not suitable for ES&T.

### E5

- Location: Journal Scope, bullet list
- Quote: "An assessment of uncertainty or sensitivity analysis should be included in reported data where applicable, with adequate quality assurance/quality control reported. New analytical methods should be thoroughly developed and quality ensured."
- Key point: Where applicable, report an assessment of uncertainty or a sensitivity analysis, together with adequate quality assurance/quality control.

### E6

- Location: Journal Scope, bullet list
- Quote: "Manuscripts should include evaluations performed under environmentally realistic conditions and concentrations.  Concentrations should be reported as measured and not nominal."
- Key point: Evaluations should be performed under environmentally realistic conditions and concentrations; concentrations should be reported as measured, not nominal, values.

### E7

- Location: Journal Scope, bullet list
- Quote: "The scope of the reported data in ambient monitoring studies must yield conclusions applicable to more than the particular site or time when the data were taken"
- Key point: Conclusions of ambient monitoring studies must apply beyond the particular site and time of sampling.

### E8

- Location: Journal Scope, bullet list
- Quote: "Manuscripts that emphasize chemical engineering, new catalysts synthesis or analytical chemistry must provide a concise argument that the research represents an advance that is of primary importance to the environmental field and include comparison to state-of-the-art approaches where appropriate."
- Key point: Manuscripts emphasizing chemical engineering, catalyst synthesis, or analytical chemistry must argue that the work is of primary importance to the environmental field and, where appropriate, compare against state-of-the-art methods.

### E9

- Location: Journal Scope, bullet list
- Quote: "Manuscripts on novel materials (e.g., sorbents or catalysts) should demonstrate the materials have been tested under at least one environmental condition using relevant concentrations of target contaminant."
- Key point: Manuscripts on novel materials (e.g. sorbents or catalysts) should show that the materials have been tested under at least one environmental condition using environmentally relevant contaminant concentrations.

### E10

- Location: Article Types, Research Articles
- Quote: "Research Articles (length limit: 7,000 words)."
- Key point: Research articles are limited to 7,000 words.

### E11

- Location: Article Types, after Research Articles
- Quote: "Manuscripts that report on initial findings of an urgent nature may be submitted to Environmental Science & Technology Letters (ES&TL)."
- Key point: Initial findings of an urgent nature can be submitted to the sister journal ES&T Letters.

### E12

- Location: Cover Letter
- Quote: "A substantial fraction of submissions to ES&T are not sent out for review because an editor concludes that the manuscript does not meet the journal's standards for novelty, scientific merit, or environmental importance. The cover letter is your opportunity to convince the editor that this is not the case."
- Key point: A substantial fraction of ES&T submissions are not sent for review because an editor judges them insufficient in novelty, scientific merit, or environmental importance; the cover letter is your chance to convince the editor.

### E13

- Location: Experimental Section
- Quote: "Authors must emphasize any unexpected, new, and/or significant hazards or risks associated with the reported work and this safety information must be included in the Materials and Methods section."
- Key point: Any unexpected, new, or significant hazards or risks in the work must be stated in the Materials and Methods section.

### E14

- Location: Research Data Policy, Database Deposition
- Quote: "ES&T requires for all published articles that authors make materials, data, and protocols available to readers through deposition in a public database."
- Key point: ES&T requires that all published articles make materials, data, and protocols available to readers by depositing them in a public database.

### E15

- Location: Research Data Policy, Computer Codes
- Quote: "When computer codes are developed or used and are an essential part of a manuscript, sufficient detail must be given, either within the paper or in the SI. Types of languages that are used in the computer codes, compiler/interpreter, and operating system with a specific version must be provided or properly cited."
- Key point: When code is an essential part of the paper, give sufficient detail in the paper or the Supporting Information, including the specific versions of the language, compiler or interpreter, and operating system.

### E16

- Location: Prior Publication Policy
- Quote: "Posting of pre-prints to a pre-print server such as ChemRxiv, bioRXiv, arXiv, or applicable repository for their discipline where a DOI is issued, before the manuscript is accepted for publication is considered acceptable but requires citing of the pre-print."
- Key point: Posting a preprint before acceptance to a server that issues a DOI, such as ChemRxiv, bioRxiv, or arXiv, is allowed, but the preprint must be cited.

### E17

- Location: Theses/Dissertations
- Quote: "Authors submitting material to the journal that has been used in their thesis/dissertation must either deposit their thesis/dissertation in a publicly available repository that provides a DOI or other identifier [...] or delay posting of the thesis/dissertation or remove the material from the Internet until the final paper is published"
- Key point: If submitted material was used in your thesis or dissertation, either deposit the thesis in a public repository that issues a DOI (treating it as a preprint) or delay or remove the online version of the thesis until the paper is published.

### E18

- Location: Ethical Use of Animals or Human Participants in Research
- Quote: "For animal and human studies, the ethics committee that approved the experimental procedure must be identified and the case number provided, as described in the ACS Ethical Guidelines to Publication of Chemical Research. Research involving human participants/samples should also include a statement confirming that the samples/data were obtained through the informed consent of the donors/participants."
- Key point: For animal and human studies, name the ethics committee that approved the procedures and give the approval number; for human samples or data, state that they were obtained with informed consent.
