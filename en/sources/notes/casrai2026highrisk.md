# CASRAI editorial team 2026: USG Policy for Stopping High-Risk Life Sciences Research: What Changes for Institutions

- Source: `casrai2026highrisk`, [Original web page](https://casrai.org/news/usg-policy-stopping-high-risk-life-sciences-research-institutions-2026)
- Version: Published August 7, 2026. CASRAI is a nonprofit in research administration; this is a news commentary on its website, not the government text; the text is CC BY 4.0. The official documents it mentions (NIH notice NOT-OD-26-101 and the US Department of Health and Human Services joint statement) returned 403 to scripts and have no Internet Archive snapshots, so this project could not read the official originals.

### E1

- Location: TL;DR
- Quote: "On July 28, 2026, NIH, HHS, and OSTP announced the release of the U.S. Government Policy for Stopping High-Risk Life Sciences Research (framework dated July 20, 2026), issued under Section 4 of Executive Order 14292"
- Key point: On July 28, 2026, NIH, the US Department of Health and Human Services, and the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy announced the U.S. Government Policy for Stopping High-Risk Life Sciences Research (framework dated July 20, 2026), issued under Executive Order 14292.

### E2

- Location: TL;DR
- Quote: "The new policy formally replaces the 2024 USG Policy for Oversight of DURC and Pathogens with Enhanced Pandemic Potential (DURC/PEPP)"
- Key point: The new policy formally replaces the 2024 policy on dual use research of concern and pathogens with enhanced pandemic potential (DURC/PEPP).

### E3

- Location: New Institutional Obligations
- Quote: "principal investigators are expected to provide written attestations on research classification before proposal submission."
- Key point: Principal investigators must give written attestations of how their research is classified before submitting a proposal.

### E4

- Location: New Institutional Obligations
- Quote: "If ongoing research may meet the DGOF definition, the policy requires that work stop immediately, with notice to the funding agency within 24 hours."
- Key point: Ongoing research that may meet the definition of dangerous gain-of-function research must stop immediately, with notice to the funding agency within 24 hours.

### E5

- Location: Two Categories of Covered Research
- Quote: "the broad category of routine, low-risk gain-of-function work used throughout ordinary laboratory research is not what the policy targets"
- Key point: The broad category of routine, low-risk gain-of-function work in ordinary lab research is not what the policy targets.
