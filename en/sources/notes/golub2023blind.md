# Golub 2023: Blind analysis in Physics experiments: Is this trip necessary?

- Source: `golub2023blind`, [Original](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13542)
- Version: arXiv:2311.13542v1, 2023, a short comment not peer reviewed. The author is in the physics department at North Carolina State University and responds critically to Klein & Roodman 2005.

### E1

- Location: §2 The case for blind analysis and some counter-arguments
- Quote: "However blind analysis has real costs in that it can severely inhibit the search for systematic errors and can preclude studies of unexpected events that occur in 'blinded' regions of the parameter space."
- Key point: Blind analysis has real costs: it can severely inhibit the search for systematic errors and can preclude studying unexpected events in the "blinded" regions.

- Key point: The author considers introducing blind analysis to solve a problem that may not exist to be overkill.
